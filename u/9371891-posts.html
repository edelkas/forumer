#165364 2 2006-04-16T12:18:55Z zerhino
Episode 51-4 the Lowest Highest Point:

O.K. so you want to get lots of gold fast as you can. Follow the #1 hiscore, naems, route for the beginning, which includes grabbing the first key on the right side. If you can't corner pop across the jump don't worry, pick up speed and jump. Never jump across the middle if you don't have your speed going. Now once you have all the gold at the main chamber you will have from your starting time like 30 secs so go down in the chamber quick. Wallslide down until the drones have moved just enough that you can wall-pop to the block, grab the key and wall pop back to wall pop back up the wall. Rinse and repeat on the other side. At this point you should be running low on time so run down the incline to get your speed and then jump and hit the exit.

I think a friend did this level for me so I never did it and haven't any experience with it. But practice makes perfect and if you are that low on time getting all the gold at the beginning will help you lots. Just look out for the rocket
