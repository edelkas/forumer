#157730 8 2006-03-25T10:51:37Z novam
You can't change the icon of the exe itself, but replace the icon of start menu icons. Save the ico-file where you want, just remember the place.

1. Right-Click on the start menu/quick launch/desktop icon
2. Choose Properties
3. Click "Other icon" (or similar)
4. Select the ico-file
5. OK two times
6. Ready
