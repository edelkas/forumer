I think hate is a perfectly natural way to feel, and not necessarily unhealthy, so long as you don't let it get out of control and make you do something really stupid, like any other really strong emotion that can cloud your judgement.
I think it's human nature to both hate and love, and neither one is really more likely than another.
I think hate is generally how a person feels towards another person, or towards an ideal, or something that represents a person or a group of people. It's also used in a less literal way towards objects, but I think that's a completely different emotion, just as love for a person is completely different than love for an object.
I think hate is often confused with disappointment or contempt or anger or other emotions like that, which are more temporary. I think hate is when you dislike a person so much that you cannot stand to spend time with them, that the thought that you share a universe with them is unpleasant, and that you would be happy if they were to die (regardless of whether you would actually do anything to kill them.)
