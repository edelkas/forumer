I wasn't here yesterday, and I won't be here tomorrow, but I'll take the time to point out that those of you who say nothing can be done are WRONG.

Wrong, wrong, wrong.


Here's what to do:

Navigate Start>Control Panel>Sound.

On the playback tab, right click all entries and select "Disable".

Click OK.



Sound cards are now disabled, and any and all sound-related glitches and lag are beautifully gone.


You won't hear any sound while playing the game, though.


Reverse when done.
