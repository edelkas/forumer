My apologies.

In 3d Studio Max one can create an initial mesh for their character, then add what are practically lines that define the pivot point of the particular section and then the movement afterwards. When connected multiple times you get a bone frame, which is basically a bunch of lines that connect to each other and let the mesh move around in a humanoid manner.

In terms of flash, this would be as if you had a head and a torso, then decided to make a line from the head to where the neck would be, then another line conjoined but seperate of that line to define how the torso moves.

I'm trying to explain this the best I can, perhaps I am simply looking for the concept of a ragdoll.
