There will be one round per month (or as quickly as we can manage), in which each participating member will emerge with a score. Rankings will be kept for the cumulative score. After 10 rounds, the frontrunner will be crowned champion of MARS 2008!

1 (optional)
1a. Make a map. This map must be unique for this tournament. Your goal is to make it difficult but beatable, and above all fun. You will not be competing on your own map, so biasing it towards insider knowledge will not help you.

Quote
Your benchmark demo should not be a speed run, but should not take unnecessary detours.  Simply beat the map in a leisurely but efficient manner. Each should play the map, and may try as many times as they desire before deadline to achieve the highest-scoring demo possible.   • 40 points for the best run in your set.
  • 20 points for the second-best run in your set.
