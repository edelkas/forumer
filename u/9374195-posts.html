RedShift said:
> Just a question, are there any good online tuts for AS 2.0 scripters looking to learn AS 3.0?

Yep.
This is what I used when I was a total newbie to AS3: http://as3.betaruce.com/tut/tut_1/tut_1.html
This has more of the same tutorials: http://as3.betaruce.com/tut/
This will help you migrate AS2 code over to AS3. It'll also give you a heads-up on what's changed: http://livedocs.adobe.com/flex/2/langref/migration.html
Adobe's own documentation will come in handy once you've got started: http://livedocs.adobe.com/flex/2/langref/class-summary.html
Senocular's tips are a great help: http://www.kirupa.com/forum/showthread.php?t=223798

I suggest using google too, as there is loads of great tips and tutorials out there. AS3 is really amazing in what it can do- you'll be surprised.
