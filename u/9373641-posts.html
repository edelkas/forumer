I'm not a member of MInT, but I had to contribute this:
a ninja cupholder for your computers spare 5.25" bay.
By ninja, I mean like Ninja Awesome.

This wouldn't be that hard to do. go find an old 16x CD-ROM drive for $5 at a garage sale, and then take a dremel to it. you could style the tray in to a cup holder like device, and then remove all of the interior components not required for the drive assembly. (By drive assembly I mean the part that makes the tray go in and out - it's composed of a small IC, a motor, and some gears/belts)

I think you could then just plug it in to a computer power supply's +12v / +5v drive dongle, as I think most older drives have independent mechanisms for opening and closing, so they'll do that without a data connection (such as IDE). I suppose you could connect an IDE cable, but if you want to do that I'd leave the innards intact so as to avoid the myriad errors associated with a drive that is missing it's innards.

Then you could paint it pearlescent black with blue accents.

Another idea from Jesse:
a miniature power supply.

I had this idea last summer when it was really hot in my room...
So, in the central US (think places like Utah) people often have Swamp Coolers instead of the better known Air Conditioner. Swamp coolers are very simple: they suck/blow air through dampened cloths, thus cooling the air and adding moisture. perfect for hot and dry environments. You could make a mini version easily: all you'd need is a cheap old ATX power supply, some cooling fans (120mm size is best, the quieter the better. 32dB is probably the highest acceptable sound level.), some kind of thin but absorbent cloth (low thread count linens, perhaps), and a few computer water cooling components (to keep the cloth damp). I think you can figure out how to configure them.

Well, I must say that I do prefer Windows over mac's, but that's far a vast number of reasons that I won't argue. but I must agree with several people above that that I do not like Mac's advertising campaign. I find it a sign of a companies own weakness that the cornerstone of it's advertising it's making fun of their competitors - good advertising campaigns focus on the advantages of your product, not the (often made up and exaggerated) disadvantages of your competitors.

And your comment on people only using Windows because it comes OEM on computers - MacOS comes on Apple computers as well. however, the MacOS only comes on a very limited line of computers, and licensing prohibits you from installing it on a non-Apple computer. I view this as Apple's primary weakness - they're OS is going the way of BetaMax. they do not permit the installation of their OS on other manufacturers devices, and so the selection of machines that run MacOS is limited and lacks the specialized machines that you can obtain within the Windows community.

Is there a machine running MacOS comparable to the TyanPSC? (Tyan Personal Super Computer - runs Windows 2003 Cluster Edition) A machine running MacOS comparable to the massive line of Blade servers? (Most of which run either Windows Server Edition, Windows Cluster Edition, or POSIX) a machine running MacOS comparable to the budget machines made by Acer and many other companies? (virtually all of which run Windows XP Home or Windows Vista Basic)

The examples go on...
