I don't think a theme song would ruin anything. Anyone on here played zwok? A simple and addictive platform game with a beautiful theme that I love and almost never choose to listen to. I think we could pull the same thing off with n easily, so I'm starting the contest. I'm not a pro and I'm definitely an n game newb but I've taken courses in music theory and am a classically trained guitarist.

In regards to the sarcastic comment made about midi, midi isn't bad music, in fact it's not even music. It's the digital equivalent of sheet music. The only reason it sucks is because your computers sound card has bad midi instruments to render it. But there is hope because you can make your own instruments! Now you might be asking, why would we want to use midi and new instruments just to make a song? The answer is in midi you have a simple format to create any type of music you want with near zero deviance from absolute pitch or steady rhythm (unless you want it of course) and at a tiny fraction of the size! It depends a lot on the quality of your file, but about a minute of mp3 is around 900kb while a midi file and it's instrument (there are a few different formats, soundfonts are popular) are pretty much always going to be less than 10kb. Great sound quality, small file sizes, easy portability? Why this sounds like a dream come true! It is.

So here's my first submission for The Way of The Ninja Unofficial Theme Song Contest!

It's a chiptune, which means it sounds like the old contra or castlevania or chrono trigger music, just to keep it old school. It's a midi file rendered by a virtual instrument made of a saw and a square wave with a little equalization processed by a low pass filter and given some artificial reverb. I actually made the midi when I first started playing n as a theme but didn't think of anything to do with it.

Let's say a limit of three (3) songs per person? and the winner is picked by popular vote?

It's on.

Oh! Remember in Mario how the music would get faster when your time was running out? That would be so awesome and so easy!
