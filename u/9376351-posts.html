The PC version is located somewhere in ~/.wine and the Linux version is in ~./adobe. I'm in Windows right now and don't want to reboot just to find the exact locations, so you'll have to look.
Thank you so much!! It worked!! For me it was in: .wine/windows/profiles/*your profile*/application data/macromedia/Flash Player/#SharedObjects/*some random alphanumeric characters*/localhost/*.sol.

So, basically after you find .wine, go through it like it was windows. And just replace it with the one that you copied over from windows.
