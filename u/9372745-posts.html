Lobster Thermidor(for two):


Ingredients:

1 lobster, preferably live
3-4 spines celery, depending on size, medium to small dice
1 medium onion, medium to small dice
1-3 cloves garlic, depending on personal taste, minced
2 T(ablespoon) shallot, minced
1 t(easpoon) fresh lemon juice
1/4#(pound) butter
1/4# flour
1 1/2 C(up) milk
1/2 t cayenne pepper
S+P (salt and Pepper)
1 T parmesan cheese

Method:

Steam or boil lobster, 7-8 minutes if steaming; 10-12 if boiling. Cool in ice water. After lobster is cool, split in half lengthwise. Remove meat from tail and claws, keeping half shell of body whole. The claws don't really matter, you can do whatever you have to to get meat out of them*. Discard all undesirable innards.

Saute onions, garlic, celery and shallots in all of butter, until transparent. Add all of flour, whisking constantly until smooth. Add milk. This is where it gets tricky. Depending on the temperature of the milk, you may need more, maybe not so much. What you'll be looking for is a semi firm mixture, stiffer than pancake batter but not as stiff as, say, cookie dough.

Turn off flame.

Dice lobster meat, add to mixture, add lemon juice, cayenne, s+p. Mix thoroughly.

Scoop mixture into halves of shells, mound it up nice and full, sprinkle with parmesan and bake for about 10-15 minutes at 425f-475f.


Bon appetit!



*For a truly stunning presentation, while claws are still attached to body, carefully cut the very tip of the pincers off. Use shears to cut down the top and bottom of the claw and then around the base off the claw, where the claw meets the knuckle. If done correctly, the sides of the shell of the claw will fold away like a book, and you can carefully pry them away and you should be left with just the meat at the end.
This, however, is a very complicated procedure; one that I am just mastering.
