No, I'm sorry but there isn't. You can watch a quick replay, though, by pressing "R" after completing a level. The only way to show the completion to your friend is to invite him to your house. Or, use a separate game video record program (like FRAPS).

But you can record demos in user-made levels.

Editor manual tells you how to do that, step-by-step.
