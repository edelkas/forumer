#409640 9 2008-02-15T08:42:59Z BuddyLee
Well I think the post misses a simple explanation of how doors work in some cases. When you place an edited trapdoor along a wall, it processes into three distinct cases:
1. The wall on the opposite side is fully blocked (as in most of your examples, such as http://numa.notdot.net/map/112190). In this case, both drones AND N are affected by the trapdoor. Walls on the bottom-left edge will be solid from above yet deadly/glitchy from below, and walls on the top-right edge will be solid from below and deadly/glitchy from above.
2. The wall on the opposite side is fully open (as in the drone through wall examples, such as http://numa.notdot.net/map/96160). Here the bottom-left and top-right edges only matter when touching tiles. The empty edges are projected to tile walls, meaning both drones AND N can go through them (as with empty squares).
3. The wall on the opposite side is not fully blocked, but the tile is not a D tile (an example, http://numa.notdot.net/map/116202). This is (as far as I can tell) the most eloquent and useful door glitch. It ONLY affects drones, while N can pass through without problems.

Possible reasoning/explanation: In the coding of N, there is probably a specific piece of code that tells objects to read other objects (such as doors) before reading tiles, which can potentially override certain functionality. In the case of this glitch, the effects of the trapdoor are projected onto the other walls (don't know why it chose bottom-left and top-right, but that's not too important). So when a drone would be able to go through a tile (only D tiles), the drone can always go through the projected edge. If the edge is blocked it should stop both drones AND N. If the tile doesn't fit those, drones should still be stopped but N needs its own collision detection, so N is unaffected. When trapdoors are activated they act like locked doors, and likewise when locked doors are opened they act as unactivated trapdoors.

I hope this is helpful. It would be somewhat easier to explain with pictures. If anyone has questions or requires clarification about something here I will certainly try.
