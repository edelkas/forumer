33-4 : Soar


1. Just grab the switch to your left. Don't worry about the other one.

2. Next hop onto the bounce block to your left again.

3. The thing should shoot a rocket at you. Once the rocket gets close, quickly wall jump onto the left-most grey block then jump to grab the door switch.

4. Now just jump down to the door. It doesn't matter if you die because you'll still pass the level.
