#39919 24 2004-12-07T17:58:40Z firestorm
I like the timer.

Lots of people at school play on practice mode and then say stuff like "this game's really easy"

It gives you a challenge, and I don't think it should just kill you. If it did, there would be no way to lose and you could theoretically always play the same level over and over again until you get lucky.

Another argument against per-level timers: unless each level had it's own timer, it would still be a bad idea, as no level takes 90 seconds to complete (and one that would would be a really bad level to play) but some levels take nearly no time and some take ages and also, if there was a certain way to beat a level with a low time limit, if you didn't know the special way, you'd be stuck. With the current timer, you can just stock up gold on the previous level so you have enough time to take the long way.

Also, part of the fun in the game is to choose what gold to get and what to leave. If you're too scared of dying to get any gold, you lose, so the timer gives you a choice in how much to risk.

So, 'No, go to hell Brickman'.
