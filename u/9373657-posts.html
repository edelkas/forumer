Short But Sweet
Dedicated to astheoceansblue

What? Explain the guidelines and rules!

SBS is a contest in which you must submit a map that is, well, short but sweet. A short but sweet map is a playable map that is simple in design and completion, yet still fun in gameplay. The tileset is simple or fills a maximum 3 eighths of the map. Enemies can't be very hard to beat, but enough to provoke an easy or easy-medium challenge. Enemies are not above 18 in number. Like I mentioned before, the map should be fun to play, yet easy to complete.

An example of a SBS map is freya, made by astheoceansblue. The tileset, if divided, forms about a quarter of the map (proof!). The enemies are not really too hard, yet still fun to dodge when gold is added into the picture. There are also only 12 enemies, almost more than enough to make the map awesome.

So then, submit a map (maximum 2) following these guidelines. No resubmissions, no submitting of old maps. Contest ends April 7th. We'll need some judges.

Simplicity:x/20 (how simple is the map's tileset or object placement?)
Fun:x/20(Needs no description, does it?)
SBS Feel:x/10(Does the map follow the criteria and give off a short but sweet feeling to it?)
Overall:x/50
(This will be the combined score of Simplicity, fun, and SBS feel. The bigger the number, the better!)

Hiya, folks! I decided to see if I could magically and spontaneously return to N by starting a nice map pack!

The Remix Chain Level Pack

The concept is simple; first, post the link to any of your older NUMA maps (or make one!). Afterwards, "remix" the previous poster's map by taking its concept/ enemy placement/ tileset/ etc. and making a whole new map based partly around it. I'll take the resulting giant mass and convert it into one level pack.

RULES (especially long since I KNOW this idea has a lot of potential for completely massing up)

You can either submit a map to NUMA and post a link here to count as a submission, or you can post a downloadable .txt. .TXT's ONLY! ONLY .TXTs! Soaked in? Good.

If you remix a map and decide to post it on NUMA, label it "[insert title here] remix" and post a link to the original map (also, add a link to this thread).

If you remix a map and put it in a text format, don't just add code. GIVE IT A NAME! PLEASE! OH, AND YOUR USERNAME TOO!

If you post a remix and some other guy had beaten you to it, PLEASE don't complain; deal with it and make a new remixed map (I'm sure you can't have such bad luck that you constantly post your remixes after somebody else beat you to the punch).

NO CALLING DIBS ON ANY CERTAIN MAP.

Please try not to take too much time on your remix map, or else someone will post a remix before you can post your perfect one. Don't worry about being crude, this is just a nice little idea and not a competition.

If you are going to post one of your maps that you want remixed, please also post your remix of the previous poster. If you are going to post a remix of the previous poster's map, please post the map you want remixed!

You cannot remix your own maps.

If you don't like what the remixer has done to your map's idea/enemy placement/ tileset/ etc., move on. This has an element of risk to it, but you should be okay, 'mkay?

Attempt not to post a question about what you can or can't do unless you are ABSOLUTELY POSITIVELY SURE that it's not in the rules.

If you're gonna criticize, don't.Otherwise, make it constructive or get out.

I have no clue when this will end. Don't ask, I'll tell you when it's over.

No posting other people's maps for remixing.

Yes, you CAN post simply tilesets.

You can remix as many maps as you want, but please be nice and give other people a chance to remix.

EXAMPLE
Here's a quick example of how this goes just so that everybody will be clear on what to do...
A posts a map he made. He has a link to the map, which is uploaded on NUMA. He then posts a remix of B's map, titled "[Title of B's map] remix". Instead of having a link to NUMA, he decides to show another alternate route to posting the remix (y'know, just for fun) and posts it here in the form of a .txt file.
