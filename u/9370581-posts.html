Those of you who like Mario games and N (which should be all of you) should check out Donkey Kong for gameboy. It starts out with the four levels of the arcade donkey kong, but there are more than a hundred levels after that, and they all require carrying a key to the exit.

Mario has all of his moves from Mario 64, plus a handstand that repels falling objects. He dies if he falls from too great a height, and he cannot jump on enemies to kill them.

There are also puzzle elements that involve you editing levels, then running time trials through them. It's quite unique.

I've heard N compared to LodeRunner many times, but this is the game that N reminds me of, more than any other.
