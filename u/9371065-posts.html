#248582 30 2006-11-01T11:22:08Z Wannas1693
Well, the main reasons against implementing this idea are that it goes against the main theme of N, and that it would take a lot of additional programming to implement; this is programming that is unneccessary to game that is already fantastic.. The time needed to code such a feature is time that Mare and Raigan probably don't have. It very well may be implemented in N2, but that is no one's decision except the developers. When Nv1.5 is released, feel free to play with the source code and add it to a mod or the like--no one is discouraging you from that.
Another reason that it wasn't implemented, or won't be in version 1.5 is that it doesn't complement the other existing objects very well. For example, bounceblocks and homing launchers can make a challenging map, or mines and chasing enemies. However, adding water, I feel, doesn't present such a new idea: a certain combination of enemies can create the same challenge one would face in a pool of water.

In conclusion, many of these ideas which have been suggested and shot down over the development of N could very well be implemented in the future, in fact they may have been considered when beta versions were tested. However, currently Mare and Raigan have enough on their hands without trying to develop new ideas, many of which do not add a new "dimension of play" or a useful innovation to the game. Again, feel free to program any ideas with the source code--they could turn out to be better ideas than anyone thought! But almost none of these ideas that have been suggested are real innovations to gameplay; Mare and Raigan have likely thought about many of these ideas already and decided against them.

#315977 4 2007-04-26T13:57:44Z Wannas1693
Okay. N poser...

The interface consists of four parts; the posing area, the ninja's settings, the background settings, and the finished product.

The posing area shows a rather elongated black ninja (no matter what colours you have set) with several blue circles and crescent-shaped buttons. A summary is that the circles can be dragged to move the joints around, while the crescents will reverse the joints (eg. elbows or head direction). For sake of detail, I will give a run through of the buttons from the top down, assuming that you are looking at the default pose:
Head direction; head angle; upper back and shoulders; lower back and hips; arms and hands (move closer to bend elbows); elbow directions; legs and feet (same deal with knees); knee directions.
Pointing out the obvious, there are two arms and legs each.

Okay: the ninja (labeled N) interface:
As you might have known, the three sliders are the basic RGB controls. Below and on the left are presets for the colour of the ninja; these, as a matter of fact, correspond to the flavours unlocked in N v1.4. On the right are text boxes if you wish to enter your own values into the RGB settings. Ensure that your values are from 0 to 255. The last function in this interface is a black slider with a text box (the default value is 100). This is the scale slider. You can guess what it does.

The background settings are very simple. Again, there is a set of RGB colour sliders, a few preset backgrounds, and text boxes for your own values.

Your results will appear in the bottom right box. In Windows, use the Printscreen button (to the right of the F1-F12 keys) to capture the screen, and then open up a new window of your favourite imaging program and paste it. Again stating the obvious, make a selection around the ninja, copy again, and paste it into your comic. If you use MSPaint (like me), set your background colour (right click with the dropper tool) to the colour which you are using in N Poser, and then turn transparency on (the options are below the toolbox) and put it in. Note that you may want to move the ninja one pixel below the ground, and then repair the ground to make it touch the ground more realistically.

That should be all. Have fun making your comic. Oh, wait. Try and check your grammar. A decent effort is all we want. For example "How to use N Poser." is not a question. A better rephrasing would be "How do I use N Poser?".
