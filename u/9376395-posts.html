THE GUIDE TO MAKING A DECENT MAP

most of you out there, will want to play a map, not watch a DDA, look at N-ART (though you do see the occasional good one) or play a terrible piece of junk which belongs on a spam site

now, noobs, they submit maps that have lines of launchpads, enemy spamming, mine clustering, crap tiles, and this generates LAG!

im going to put this into sections
if you want to find something type in the code next to the description in ctrl+f

1. what makes a decent map [0001]
2. tiles [0002]
3. ninja placement [0003]
4. exit placement [0004]
5. mastering these techniques [0005]
6. on to gold [0006]
7. enemy alert! [0007]

this is a seperate section for different types of maps
1. guide to making action maps [101]
2. guide to making maps based on difficulty [102]
3. guide to making survival maps [103]
4. guide to making DDA's [104]
5. guide to making concepts [105]



WHAT MAKES A DECENT MAP [0001]

lots of things, aesthetics, placement, flow, design, propulsion etc. master these bunch and you're on your way

seriously guys, its not a challenge to make a decent map.

TILES [0002]

tiles is one of the hardest things to master, but once you master them, there a breeze.

try mixing different tiles, like 2 and 3, to create some cool shapes, also try and stick to a theme, nobody wants to play a map thats sloppy and poor in the tileset.

examine this map: http://nmaps.net/135092
the tiles are controlled, stick to the same theme, look cool and help it flow. thats what tiles are there for.

now examine this map: http://nmaps.net/131333
the tiles are ugly, im not saying its a bad map, im saying it would be a damnsight better if it had nice tiles.

TILES IN SPACES WHERE YOU CANT GET TO

what i mean is, you press H, make the level on the left side, stick some nice looking tiles on the right, KABOOM, you've just made a map look better, as its easier on the eye than just a level on the left, as your eyes would be drawn to the left without it, this helps player's eye relax and flicker between the two.

NINJA PLACEMENT [0003]

this can be extremely tricky, and very easy. easy, as in a symettrical map, cave map, map that has a starting point planned, but oftentimes, when you use another's tileset, you will think, "shit, where am i gonna place the friggin ninja!!" i thought exactly this when i made this map out of AMLT's tileset : http://nmaps.net/135037

so i put him in a crappy spot, this, personally, made me think this map was gonna get 3's, i got off lucky

TILESET MAKING - THE UNOFFICIAL WALKTHROUGH, WRITTEN BY ME (GODENATOR)

Yeah um, lets split this down into sections

1.Themes
2.Length
3.Aesthetics
4.Gameplay

Tilesets should have at least 3 out of 4 of those things

so...

1! dun dun dun...

THEMES:

Themes can make the difference between a good map and a terrible map, a good map, should have a single theme, or a mixture of themes in rare occasions.

you'll notice straight away my one out of two 5s.

A single theme should be a picture, a way that it looks etc.

(I messed that up)
look at Tender.

Them tiles took me 5 minutes, yet they still came out brilliantly (read comments), sometimes it's not time that does it, it's the effort and the idea.

every tileset is different, because everyone should have different themes (unless you have seen a theme and decided to use it).

2! dun dun dun....

LENGTH:

I like lengthy tiles, and i like them to be rather linear, but length is all about personal preference, so don't worry if people see it's too short or too long, ignore them comments.

3! dun dun dun....

AESTHETICS:

A key part of Tilesets is nice aesthetics, if its not nice, it's not good (except in some cases, where the gameplay is superb).

Well, this is easiest in my opinion, it's not hard to make it look good, just keep editing till it does..

and finally

GAMEPLAY:

I can't stress how important it is to have good gameplay, if you're tileset is an N-art, its fine, but you can expect some critisism.

A key to gameplay is FLOW.

you've heard of flow, all maps should have it, it makes it easier and more fun and can sometimes make your jaw drop in races.

The other key to success, is smart-arseness.

you heard me.

You wanna be one, cos a well placed 5 tile on a wall can slow someone down and get em hit by a rocket, trust me, you wanna be a smart-arse

hope this helps
