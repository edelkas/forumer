I've devised two mechanisms for blasting the ninja sideways across an empty map so fast that he'll explode once he hits the wall... on the other side...

Load up that level in debug mode. There are 3 things to do.

1) watch as in a DDA.
2) press Enter with your mouse near the bottom right launch pad. run into launch pad and watch.
3) press Enter again, this time with your mouse above the ledge so you can land on the upper launchpad.

it took around 30 minutes of trial and error to get these thwumps positioned right to make them propel the ninja this fast, but, well, i did it. hopefully this will be useful for mapmakers? I've seen thwump launches that send N up or diagonally real far. but not horizontally.

Using doors instead of tiles also works for creating the tower loop effect. I have a sample map I put together quickly to demonstrate this. It did take some tweaking to get right, but using doors is often much easier to achieve the effect since they only take up space at the very edge of tiles, unlike the tiles. It's easier to keep N alive.

In the map, there is one edited launchpad (power = 2) and a bunch of locked doors. You'll have to approach the launchpad at a medium low speed, and let go of buttons once you're "in".
