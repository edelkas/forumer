1. I'm editing the Anthology pack for consistency, making sure all of the maps we've got are off NUMA, and that we're happy with the final product for Anthology.

3. We need to have a conversation as to whether Ampersand is going to be public or hidden. I love the idea of it being hidden, but the more I think about it, the more I wonder if anyone's going to find it. More than that, I wonder about those people who don't find it - If it's that exclusive, fine, but I don't want people to miss out.

If public, then we just put it at the end of Anthology, and let it go. If hidden, we have two options - Leave off the leading $, or put a link and a note in the middle of Anthology somewhere. I'm not sure which would be easier to discover.

4. LV needs to make the infosheet for Anthology - He has a handful of graphs, and we have histograms for the levels (If you haven't seen them, by all means ask). I want more of this stuff. Images like we had explaining each member's contribution to the Legacy

If we could have a .zip pack with 20 items or so (Also, along with anthology, we could include Legacy, Expanse, and Zenith as seperate packs as they appeared in their original form), I think we'll be well-off.
