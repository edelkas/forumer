MRO
The Map Rater's Organization

This Organization:
Created to rate others maps and get your maps rated, the goal is ONE dedicated map to your MRO team a week.

How this organization works:
There are 10 people per team, each have a quota of ONE map submission a week, and to rate ALL other team members maps by the end of the week. A MRO week starts Sunday and ends Saturday.

Submitting and rating:
One map a week is submitted to this form, with a link of the map and a color of the team you're on. As long as your map submission has those two key things, you can submit however it fits to you. When submitting the map to NUMA it's expected to put two tags, MRO, and your team color. When rating a map submitted to MRO, you should leave comments on the map aswell. Here is an example of of a map submission to this thread:

Team Green
http://numa.notdot.net/map/103252

Rules and regulations:
If you feel you cannot submit a map every week, or rate all of your memebers maps every week, do NOT sign up. I understand that N is just a game, and many things should be prioritized above it, school, work, family etc. If you have something urgent happen PM me and say "I can't submit this week" or whatever it might be. ALL MRO MEMBERS MOST COMMENT IF THEY VOTE. This is to prove that you are active so when I check every week I can tell who is not taking a part of what MRO stands for.

  • The map must be to NUMAs standard of "Hard" or "Difficult"

  • Only ONE submission to the contest per person.

  • Map must be playable (No DDAs)

  • mrgy05
  • 3v!1
  • Mechtradevil
Scoring Guidelines: (Total of 100 Points)
  • 25 Points - Difficulty
  • 25 Points - Aesthetics (Overall map look)
  • 25 Points - Demo (Performance)
  • 15 Points - Object Placement (Flow)
  • 10 Points - Tileset (Flow)
Scoring Standards:

You have to consider what NUMAs norm of "difficult" is, your demo is one of the most crucial part of this contest. Your demo is to impress the judges by showing close calls, flow, and different unique ideas that are incorporated into your map.
