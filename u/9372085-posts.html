Well I see countless threads asking why their userlevels do not show up and people do not take the time to search threads so I thought why not make a thread explaining why. If anyone else has more solutions then feel free to post and ill edit it in

#1- If you have dragged your N loader out of the file that you downloaded to your desktop the userlevels will not work. If you want to play the userlevels, open the N loader in the file you downloaded that contains all your N files and launch N there and it will work. Or, you can drag your userlevels.txt file to your desktop and it will work.

#2- The userlevels might not be in the right format, make sure they are all in the format like this; $levelname#authorname#comments#leveldata#
