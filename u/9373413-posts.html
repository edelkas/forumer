Name: Doppleganger
Description: a drone that copies your current appearence and or weapons and status excluding poison and electrical damage/affliction (with a colour variation)
Weapon: what ever weapon you currently use when it changes into you
Mobile: yes
Speed: same as you with occasional lapses in speed
Defence: half of your current Def or HP
Attack Strength: very weak(unarmed) to High
On Hit: 20% chance to knockdown
9% of self death on transformation
12% chance fire/electrical damage when changes(if damaged)
Resistances:
--fire
Half of yours physical resistance(if unarmed)
- electrical resistance
