#2065 1 2005-02-22T09:29:16Z Lancer
Hostile Intent is, in my opinion, just as good as The Specialists. It's very different from any mod that I've seen on the HL engine because it is fairly slow paced.

Just because it's slow doesn't mean it's any less exciting. On most levels you get the feeling that someone is gonna pop up behind you or around a corner. When you are camping an objective you get the same rush from footsteps coming nearer.

The game has a pretty steep learning curve though. First you have to get used to aiming exclusively with the iron sights. Next you have to worry about who you are shooting at because many of the player models look similar and friendly fire is on by default. You might think that would ruin the game, but it adds a lot to the excitement factor because you have to take a second to look at your target before you shoot. Also, most of the weapons will kill with one or two well aimed shots or bursts which means you have to use cover well or develop very fast reflexes. Last, you can't see how much ammo you have in your magazine. It makes your heart sink when you pop out around a corner to surprise an enemy only to hear a little click. All the features add to the gameplay rather than detract from it.

HI doesn't have a very wide arsenal, but all the weapons are very useful depending on the situation, unlike CS where at least half the weapons are just fluff. One of the best parts though, is the awesome and mature community. Again, unlike CS.

Download it, you won't be disappointed.
