Hey, everyone. As you can guess, I'm new here. This is my first post on this Forum. I just downloaded N yesterday -- It's pretty cool. Making custom levels on it seemed even better, so you can make levels to your liking... And it was. It paid off the work it took to learn how to make levels. I've made a couple of levels, which you can see or try out through the Level Editor. Let me post a quick, simple guide for the few out there who might not know how to do it:

Quick Guide:

1. Enter the Main Menu
2. Press the button with the ~` symbols, or the one with the |\ symbols
3. This is the Level Editor, now press J to clear current background
4. Now, copy the Level Code to the first white box, near the top-right corner, overwriting the current one.
5. Click somewhere else on the screen, and press L to load the Level.
6. The Level will now load, now you have two choices:
7. A - Press P to start playing the Level (HAVE CAPS LOCK ON!) B - Copy the Demo*

*Copy the Demo to the second box, and then click somewhere else on the screen. Afterwards, press Q, and finally, press P to play the Demo. (HAVE CAPS LOCK ON!)
