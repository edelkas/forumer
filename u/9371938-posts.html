Ok, here we go....

1. The ability to choose your victory dance when you beat a map.
2. The ability to adjust your wallsliding speed (pressing down speeds you up, pressing up slows you down).
3. An object you can swing on. Your a ninja right? Why not be able do use a rope to swing on? Or (to make it fair) have an object with a ninja rope coming out of it that you can swing off of (the rope not being very long).
4. Some form of multiplay (this has prolly been suggested before, but I'm new to the scene, so I'm just gunna go for it). Like have it so you and a friend can compete on specially designed maps to see who can finish first.... maybe (and this is a BIIIIIG maybe) via LAN/Internet support. I know this is a stretch, but there are already some flash games that have online multiplayer... one example is Tactics Arena, a Turn-Based Strategy Flash game played Head-to-Head online.
5. A new drone: The harpoon drone! Basically, when it sees you it shoots a harpoon in your direction (which is connected to the drone by a flexible, metal wire). When the harpoon hits a surface, the wire tightens and electricity flows through it for about 1.5 seconds, then the harpoon and wire dissolve. I guess it kinda functions like a laser drone, only a bit different.

Soo... what do you guys think?
