#18223 1 2007-12-01T15:58:23Z thedupli
The goal was to create levelpack with maps capable of testing all the skills that a player needs to have, e.g. precision jumping, speed jumping, fast wall climbing, enemy dodging ...
To achieve that we built maps with different gameplays, some of them are flowy single-route kind, other are multi-routed to test the ability of choosing the faster way to the agd, other are puzzlish in order to see if the player is capable of guessing the right way to play them.
Nearly all the N glitches and tricks have been used, even the less common ones.
The name of the levelpack is anagram of our names.

The other rules in the levelpack were about the enemies:
a) mines couldn't be the primary enemy of a map, but were used only to prevent the player from following a certain route and to make the agd a bit more challenging,
b) every level A of an episode have only one kind of enemy (or no enemy in a couple of cases), each of the following levels add another kind of enemy to the previous one,
c) two maps of the levelpack couldn't have the same combination of enemies.

Enjoy!
