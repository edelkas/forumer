#142879 189 2006-02-13T02:11:52Z bla687
Soharu: Not too bad for a second map, but I have some general tips for you.

1. Avoid putting trap doors under gold. I know there's a couple metanet levels that do it, but it's a rather annoying practice best to be avoided.

2.Test your levels a lot. This level, for example, is impossible. The room with the exit switch can't be escaped, because N doesn't fall fast enough to get through the doorway without bringing the floor guard over the door, making it impossible to leave. Had you tested the level more you probably would have caught that.

3. Work on the look of the level a little more. That one was just a bunch of boxes with little challenges in them, and there was a lot of just empty space. Try to fill things in a bit or make areas smaller, and try to make the tileset look a little more interesting.

There are exceptions to these rules, but generally if you follow these guidelines your levels should be better received by the N community. I would do well to follow these guidelines a little better myself. I've submitted a lot of crap to NUMA. Anyway, keep at it. One of the most important things is practice. Make a level and see how it plays, work with it, play with things, sort of figure out what's fun to play and what's not. A lot of the maps I make I never submit, and there's a lot I've submitted I wish I hadn't.
I hope this helps you with future maps.
