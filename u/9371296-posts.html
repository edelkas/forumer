#154945 14 2006-03-15T14:03:25Z Beo
Good:

- Good Gold Placement, I hate sloppily placed gold and overuse when the gold isn't properly placed. If you're going to use Z-snap, be extremely careful.
- Mines, maps just feel empty without at least a few.
- Planning, what I mean is that you can't make a tileset, slap some enemies on and call it a map. Have drone pathing that makes jumps that little bit trickier, a carefully placed floorguard to complicate an otherwise easy map. A nicely placed mine may force to take a completely different route
- Flow, maps have to have some kind of flow, even if they're action. This word also covers another design aspect, the pacing of the map. Make sure that it's not fast-paced and actiony one second, then slow and careful the next. If your map revolves around quickly changing playing styles, make sure you switch between the two at a steady pace, not at inconsistent intervals. Try to make your map flow between different gameplay styles.
- A variety of objects, it annoys me when maps have gold, mines and rockets [sometimes gausses.] If you're going to do that, make sure it's perfectly streamlined and you can't improve it. It's much better when more gameplay elements are woven together, to avoid mundanity
- Creativity, Freshness, come on, it's no fun playing the same map you've played hundreds of times before under a new disguise. try and innovate, or at least keep it fresh and blend styles together. Puzzle maps are great.
- Intelligence, I don't like playing maps mindlessly. Please make me at least think about which path to choose, how to execute a tricky jump, etc. Don't add hard enemies and think it's fun. Maps where you can choose your own path out of many, where there are several good ones but lots of bad ones so there's still strategy, that is, the freeform maps, are great fun.
- Aesthetics, please keep it neat, clean and stylish. There are endless tileset possibilities, don't stick with the one tile type and overuse pattern and shapes. Keep the style consistent, don't just abruptly change styles. You can make the map flow from one style from to another, just do it carefully and seamlessly

Bad:

- Bad Planning, maps where enemies are just slapped on and not thought about, with no attention to pathing and strategy
- Cluttering, where objects are bunched together and the maps looks ugly because of it.
- Linearity, make there be different paths, unless it's a puzzle where the whole fun is figuring out the one possible path to take.
- Be careful with chainguns, their main function is to force you to take refuge behind something or to keep moving as there is no real way to dodge them. In maps where the tileset and objects are designed to take advantage of this, then they're fine but avoid carelessly placing them anywhere.
- Plainess, use different kinds of tiles, enemies and gameplay and vary your maps because no one likes maps with one type of thing overused. Try to make it exciting and colourful. Never find a cool shape and copy it loads of times. It's just boring and ugly.
- Difficulty Spikes, as someone so aptly put it. When the map suddenly gets harder, or when the difficult bit is at the end and the player has to redo the easy parts over again to get to the part they're having difficulty with. 'There and Back' maps are annoying long, try to avoid any levels being excessively lengthy.


That's all I can think of right now but I'll add later. It's 1:00 am for me right now.

Ingredients:

3 Cloves of Garlic

(Optional) Bayleef

Frying Pan

9. (Optional) If you want to end up with a Bolognese-type sauce, then slice up the Tomatoes and add them. Mix until the whole mixture is reddish.

Suggestions:

Quote: squibbles, 2008-03-29T04:02:03Z
"The only person who this will make look bad is you."

It's grammatically correct. The reason it sounds so weird is that the verb 'look' has other words attached to it, specifically:

"make something look bad."

But because of the word order the "something" (which in this particular sentence is "you") has been moved to the end of the sentence, leaving just "make look bad."

It's the same sort of format as:

"The only person who this will hurt is you."

As for the other question, both are fine in normal speech at least, but if you're writing an very formal essay then I'm not too sure which one.
