#293353 5 2007-02-03T22:13:05Z Fenneth
I'll do my best to help, although I'm not sure how well I can put this into words.

I assume you can get to the bit where the gauss turret shoots at you. (just BTW, it's a lot easier to go over the thwumps than under them)

From there, you have to jump up into the little niche in the left wall and then as quickly as you can jump onto the slope to the right. Here you are safe from the gauss and can take a little breather.

From there, you jump onto the flat surface with the floorguard on it, and then quickly jump again over the top of the floorguard. Then from there get to the exit ASAP.

This is a legitimately tricky level, and you're probably going to have to keep at it for a while. Good luck.

And by the way, on the slopes, make sure when you hit the jump key you're pressing the arrow key in the direction you want to go. If you're not doing that you will jump at a weird angle and probably hit a mine.
