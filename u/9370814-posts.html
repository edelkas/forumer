Basically the way I see it, when you're dead, you can hit the I key or something, get a confirmation message asking if you're sure, the game will exit, then the next time you start it you're given the choice of continuing where you left off, or forfeiting your progress on that chapter so you can start another one if you're just too ticked off with it.
