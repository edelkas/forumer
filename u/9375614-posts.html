#18934 1 2008-01-23T02:54:56Z MaestroOscuro
1) Create the image, with 3 colors (I use Black, Yellow and Red) and the size 124x92.
2) In Gimp or Photoshop, active the grid. Size of the grid, 1px.
3) Count each line as: 5b, 20g, 10r, 7b, 2g, 2b, 5r... than means 5 black, 20 gold, 10 red... and write it to the notepad. (I write the end line with XXX) (Use the zoom if you love your eyes )

Mode A:
4) set the top middle screen for the notepad, and the bottom middle screen for NED Plus.
5) Read the notepad and start putting objects. And be carefull: Is too easy to put something wrong, because you need a high resolution.

Mode B (quick mode. only if you use a laptop):
4) Create a little support for 2 rulers, vertical and horizontal. You need one elastic gum (this is the horizontal guide) and one school ruler (the vertical guide).
5) Print the notepad text.
6) Read the text and do like in this example:
5r: without mooving the rulers, down the mouse (I recommend using an electronic pen) and drag looking the vertical ruler (you know, top to down is the easy way) 5 milimeters (or the size you can use with your monitor and your ruler).
7) After each line, move the school ruler one milimeter.

Try it, and let me see your work
