4 new enemies: Snake droid, conspiracy droid, cannon ball droid and spring droid.

Snake droid
- The Snake droid looks like the classic snake on the old cell phone game. The snake moves by extending one end while deleting the other. It is a dark color with a white eye on the end that is moving forward. The snake is 1/2 a block wide and follows a grid-like path (no curvy turns). The path would be defined in three way: by following a free form path that would loop, by following a path with the same algorithms as a zap droid, or by creating a path that would trap N in a box and slowly working to close it, eventually crushing N. N interacts with the snake droid like he would any block of terrain.
- This should be in the new release of Ngame because it adds a complex dynamic if applied creatively. It could make elevators, moving platforms and doors while also having a simple AI.
- Other versions of the snake droid could have an N eating head or an electric body.
- The first picture shows (first) the snake droid following a freeform path and the (second) picture shows how it could be used as an elevator. The possibilities are endless

Conspiracy droid
- Looks like a yellow version of the homing zap droid.
- Instead of having a line of sight directly in front of droid (like the homing zap droid), the conspiracy droid can sense N in a 90 degree range for maybe around 5 squares in front of it. If N passes though the range of vision, the conspiracy droid starts to chase him. As long as N stays in the line of sight, the conspiracy droid will automatically correct its path to keep following. If N manages to run out of the line of sight, the conspiracy droid will go to the last place it saw N and do a 360 sweep of the area. In addition, the conspiracy droid will send a signal to any other conspiracy droids that pass within a line of sight of the active conspiracy droid, and relay N position. Static security cameras could also be placed through out the level, alerting and conspiracy droid in the vicinity of N presence if he passes trough the area of vision.
- This should be in Ngame because its is a much more advanced version of a homing zap drone, challenging experienced player while also adding the complexity of a group enemy. It also emphasizes the need of ninja stealthiness.
- The center picture shows two conspiracy droids. The bottom one has detected N and is sending his coordinates to the one on top. The above conspiracy droid shows that the area of vision can be obstructed by land. There is also a security camera of the right with a narrow beam.

Cannon ball droid
- Looks like a black circle (the size of a zap droid) with a white circle as an eye and a jet engine attacked to its back
- If N comes within a line of sight, the cannon ball droid will use its jet engine to start blasting toward N. There is no top speed, so it can build up a lot of force before it comes to the player. Because its so massive, it takes some time to start, stop, and change direction. It can bounce off of the wall or other cannon ball droids. It doesnt electrocute players on contact, but relies on its bone crushing inertia to kill the player. If it doesnt have enough force to kill the N, it will knock N off course.
- This should be in Ngame because it would add a comical (and potentially non-lethal) enemy to the game.
- The third picture show the cannon ball droid bouncing off each other

Spring droid
- look like a skinny yellow zap droid with hydraulic piston coming of each end
- The spring droid is a rod that bounces around, trying to hit N. I has hydraulic piston on each end. While in the air, the pistons can move inward and out ward, controlling the spring droids spin, so it can land right for the next bounce. Hypothetically, it could wall jump up chimneys and on bounce blocks. The spring droid tries to crush N while he is on the ground, or hit him off course while he is in the air.
- This should be in Ngame because it is a new and unique character and adds another potentially non-lethal enemy to the game.
- The last picture shows a spring droid bouncing of a piece of terrain.

I hope you like these ideas.
