#15050 1 2007-05-01T10:30:57Z Personman
Hello all,
I have created what I believe to be quite an entertaining map, found here. It is on the one hand an extraordinarily difficult level with many creative and interesting (and quite possibly very frustrating) challenges, and on the other an exploration in N-map form of a theme I doubt has been done before. The theme will be very obvious to some, a fun challenge to figure out for others, and completely unknown to others. For that last category, I've put it in the spoiler box below. Anyway, let me know what you think, how it can be improved, etc, etc. Thanks!
Personman
SpoilerThe level is based on the Fibbonacci series, the numbers obtained by adding the last two values in the series together to get the next one: 1 1 2 3 5 8 13 21 ... This series is represented in the level in many ways: The heights of the segments, the clusters of gold on the top, third, and fourth segments, the clusters of bounce blocks and gold in the third, the spacing of the thwumps, the spacing of the bounce blocks at the bottom, the mines at the bottom... This level was a lot of fun to make!
