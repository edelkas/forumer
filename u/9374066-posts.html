Some levels remind me of Prince of Persia (POP), where the player has to jump over big distances and make very complicated moves. And the time factor is very important. Not just the overall time in which you have to complete one level, but the thing, that you have to do certain parts with perfect timing, e.g. a drone follows you through a small tunnel...
So my number one suggestion is:

1.) slowly closing doors (SCD)

And this certainly isn't hard to program, since N already uses the edge flag for doors. none -> interesting -> solid ... details for the programmers

Since there aren't many objects N can interact with, I would like to see something like

2.) movable obstacles (e.g. a round rock)

which shields from enemys and can be pushed along the way. Can squash N if it approaches too fast.
With (2) one could build levels which have the "puzzle qualities" of POP levels. It would lead to a big variety of usages.

And to push the "puzzle" issue even further

3.) 2-way-buttons (poor name, sorry)

You know, if a button opens one door, but at the same time closes another door, this kind of button can be pressed again, to reverse the effect. This is just one possible sceanrio.

I really like the action part of N too, but it lacks those puzzle qualities - as I said before. I think, these little (in terms of programming effort) additions would improve the game a lot, without changing too much of the original spirit.

Gimme feedback, I'm curious
