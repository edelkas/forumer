Chaingun drones, because they are basically like a laser drone, but 5x better. Instead of shooting 1 line, it shoots bullets around a whole area. Instead of targeting early and giving you time to know where it is going to attack, the chaingun drone retargets itself right before it fires. Also, since it moves slowly, it does not stay in the same place, making it a bit harder, and you cant wait for it to move to an better location because it moves so slow(same is with laser drone, but you cant dodge bullets effectively but you can with lasers)

The only real defence you have against chainguns is hiding behind a wall, or running faster than the bullets, which is not always possible.
