Jump Pad Teleporters

Ok, I finally looked into the idea of using jump pads as makeshift teleporters. And it turns out, that with some trial-and-error, any type of teleporter you want to make is relatively easy to do. See the attached level for some examples.

The first frame after the ninja leaves a jump pad, he doesn't interact with other objects (gold, mines, other jump pads, drones, etc.). You should place a braking jump pad where the ninja is on the second frame after the launch. Or more likely, place the braking pad where you want him to go, then adjust the numbers of the first one.

The ninja will still die if he gets imbedded in a tile, even on the first frame after launch. The giant puff of smoke occurs when the ninja's feet touch ground; I have one example of this in the included level.

EDIT:
Turn off caps lock when you're about to hit a launch pad and watch it frame by frame to get a better idea of how the "teleporting" works.
