Stats

Rate of Fire: Slow -- Semi-auto
Accuracy: Medium
Fire Power: Medium - High
Air Time: Fast
Weight: Small
Clip Size: Small -- Magazine
Dual Wield: Yes

Uses

Suggest only useing it at far ranges. It will not do well up close if your enemy has a better weapon. Other uses could be just a back up if you have no other option.

Cons: Not so good close up, slow rate of fire

------------------------Plasma Pistol

Stats

Rate of Fire: Medium -- Semi-auto
Accuracy: Medium
Fire Power: Low
Air Time: Medium
Weight: Small
Clip Size: Varies -- Battery
Dual Wield: Yes

Uses

Used for taking out peoples sheilds quickly. Then either charging, shooting, then punching them. You could also shoot them then pull out another weapon and shoot them in the head(noob move). My Opinion

I always get caught off guard with it. If i charge it up and wait/walk for some one, no one shows up. So usually shoot it into the wall. I hardly ever find it any way.

Pros: Can take out vehicles, drains peoples sheilds
Cons: Weak, easy to dodge, overheating

Stats

Rate of Fire: Fast -- Auto
Accuracy: Low
Fire Power: Medium
Air Time: Fast
Weight: Medium
Clip Size: Large -- magazine

It depletes your enemies shields and the SMG penetrates the flesh.

Pros: Fast rate of fire, large clip size, extremely good close up
Cons: Alot of recoil, not good on its own, low power, larger spray

Rate of Fire: Medium -- Auto
Accuracy: Medium
Fire Power: Medium - High
Air Time: Medium
Weight: Medium
Clip Size: Medium -- magazine
Dual Wield: Yes
