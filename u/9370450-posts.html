#25288 2 2004-07-27T12:11:08Z Vlad
For future reference, a good way to get ideas on how to pass a tough part is by checking the high score replays (which appear to be down at the moment...).

For the thwumps, though, they're not all that bad once you get the hang of them. Basically, the idea is that you want to start the next thwump moving when you jump to the current one. The way to do this is when you jump to the next thwump, jump just high enough so that the one above the one you're landing on starts to move. Depending on the difference in height between them, this will either require a tap of the jump key or a longer press.

#26348 3 2004-08-27T19:56:16Z Vlad
Also, you could also run all the way to the left, wait until the sweeper is just about to hit you, then jump straight up. The incline over your head will push you into the button and give you a cilantro forward momentum. Once you're in the air, hold right. If you waited long enough, the sweeper will pass under you as you jump and you'll be able to outrun it. This also allows you to get a head start on your return trip while the sweeper is still headed to the left.
