Quote: Unreality, December 20, 2007 10:10 am
But I really haven't recompiled n. The n_v14.swf is not got from decompilation.
Imagine n_v14.exe is actually a file with content like "xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx[n_v14.swf]". Just trim off the "x" and you will get n_v14.swf
I'm not exactly adept in this field, but it is my understanding that the process of reducing an .exe to a .swf is a form of decompiling. From http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decompiler :

"The term "decompiler" is most commonly applied to a program which translates executable programs (the output from a compiler) into source code in a (relatively) high level language which, when compiled, will produce an executable whose behavior is the same as the original executable program."

i.e. Executable to source code (eg .swf) is a form of decompiling.

As I stated earlier, this may not be a problem if Metanet gives you written permission to reverse engineer n WHICH you give proof of in your mod package.
