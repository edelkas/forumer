#459895 3 2008-08-11T15:01:08Z Conen__TG_
Im Not Exactly listed as one of the "Great Mapmakers" on NUMA, however I know the name of the game.

Firstly, when you are thinking about Joining NUMA, take a look at the site and peek at a few maps. Also a new mapmaker should look to visit topics like these in the forums.

Now to address a few of the topics above;
1. Spamming - Using many more objects then needed.
Everybody should take into consideration the players that have computers held together with string and a window fan (Nice to meet you all). Not all of our computers can handle too much in a map. Spammy Mines, or spammy one-ways etc. usually will not even add to a maps gameplay, but it will only hurt it. I learned this the hard way. Now It's said that the look of a map is important, and alot of spamming of one-ways and mines would make the map look more fluid, but remember: Quality > Looks! Make sure to playtest all levels in your game to make sure they aren't laggy before submitting them to NUMA
2. Clustering Objects - Forming a cloud of objects when there only needs to be maybe 1 or 2 of those objects.
The Biggest example is bounce block clouding (Or the BBC(I Just made that up, don't use it noobs nobody will know what you are talking about)). Clustering as well as spamming can cause lag on slower computers. The effectiveness of a single bounce block can show impressive understanding of not only the behavior of the bounce block, but as well as the behavior of the objects around it.
3. Checking your map - On the confirm map submission screen, click the thumbnail and look to see everything is there.
Also as previously stated, playtest maps in your own game to make sure there are no lag issues.
4. Tile Laying - Creating the tileset for your map.
The Tumbnail of a map is important, because people on NUMA (Unlike me) will determine whether or not it is worth their viewing time judging by the look of the thumbnail. Make sure that the tiles on your map though, don't make areas too restrictive or cramped. Also if you have a firm grasp of the enemies in the map, the tiles can help to compliment their behavior.

Just a few tips for now.

P.S. Dont come to me for NReality tips cause I have NOOOOOOO IDEAAA what the hell that stuff is about.
