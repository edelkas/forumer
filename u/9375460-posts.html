Spoiler: Deadly Trios
There are so many great players out there, but how do you set them apart from each other, how do you decide who is the best?


Perhaps this tournament will help answer that question.

The Trios
Teams will be in trios. Once everyone has signed up, trios will be randonly assigned. The leader of each trio will initially be the person in the trio who signed up first. However, after each map the leader will be the player who has scored the most respect points within the trio. This leader will be responsible for making sure the other members of their trio submit their scores and, if necessary, share routes.

Format
The ideal number of trios is 5 which would make the tournament 6 rounds in length. Each round will last a week, in which time everyone will play a map on which their points will be decided. The first two rounds will be for trios to accumulate points and for leaders to be made clear judging on how many respect points they have accumulated. There will be a map for each round and in the later rounds they will increase in difficulty. Late-joiners will either replace someone who drops out or if there are enough late-joiners within the first two rounds (not knockout rounds) they will become a new trio. In the event that a trio member drops out and there are no late-joiners at the current time, the trio can ask someone to fill the place from outside the contest.
