I love N+ and I think generally it's a big improvement on the flash version, but there are a couple of issues with it:

1) Previous times don't seem to be recorded properly. Times are saved, but when an episode is beaten again they aren't shown on the end of episode time screen (the continue screen), they are just shown as --. This is really annoying if you're trying to beat your own times as you can't compare them.

2) The camera in multiplayer is bad. Even if you die the camera still snaps to your location meaning a lot of the time you can't see what other players are doing.

3) You can't move or alter the zoom on the camera when watching replays. When I'm sitting on my couch and just playing from aprox 10 feet away the ninja is tiny on the full level mode, making replays almost pointless.

4) The game automatically takes you to the next episode after beating one. Maybe this was intentional to try and keep players in the game, but tbh I find it quite irritating a lot of the time. Surely there should be a 'back to main menu' option after beating an episode, rather than just continue to the next one.

Maybe these could be fixed with a patch, or maybe no-one else thinks they need fixing? Any feeback would be great
