#25004 8 2004-07-16T01:55:10Z BlueShadow
Thank you for being interested

is my favourite DDA map

It's not hard to make a map like that, but you need to plan ahead quite a bit - get a general idea of how you want it to look and the order you want N to visit different areas of the screen. However, lots of it comes down to trying the level after adding any obstacle, and seeing where he ends up, then adding the next step. One of the hardest things to judge is adding enemies (not just thwumps); because of the way the AI works, adding a gauss turret later in the run can affect the reaction time of a homing turret earlier in the run (something which could kill N in DDA3)

All in all, I must have spent 4/5 hours solid on each map, but I split that up into 2 hour slots on different days (I just got bored after a couple of hours, actually)

And actually, it was Warfreak2 who got me started on DA levels - with a map called Lazy N. He also reminded me that too many jump pads in a DDA level look naff - the fun is the falling and the being pushed.

Hope these comments help
