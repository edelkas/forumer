Hello. New here, but not new to N.

I found this game a few years ago when I was a Junior in high school. Someone in my class found N on TheGN.com and me, him, and one other kid played that almost all the time in our computer class, trying to compete to see who could beat more levels by the end of the school year. For a while, I didn't play the game, and I sorta forgot about it, until I saw the IGN article about N+, and found a link to the site to download the game. I didn't know the game was downloadable, nor did I know about the level editor and the fact that there are a ton more levels than on TheGN's flash arcade.

I gotta say, it's about the best free game I've ever played. I was joking around when I first discovered the game and said, "this game is so good, I'd pay for it if it was for gameboy or something," and now what do ya know? It's gonna be on the DS.

I've messed around with the level editor a bit, my first level was real simple, just feeling out the editor. My 2nd level can be real easy or real hard, depending on how hard you look for something. Neither of them are that great.

1st level: Crossfire

2nd level: It's a Trap!
