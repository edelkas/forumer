First off, its a B-sides album. You should only buy these if you know all of the songs they have ever made beat for beat, you can appreciate them so much more. This is one of the most exciting and interesting albums I have heard in a long time. I like it more than Demon Days, personally (probably because I overlistened DD to hell and back).
