#270097 6 2006-12-14T20:29:59Z repeat2341
Sure-fire way past the launchpad area (*spoiler*):

Distract all three zap drones:
Usually, the drones will come out so that one is headed right at you, another right at you but one line higher, and the third away. Let the one coming right for you trigger and go for a second, then jump (but not into the gold) to trigger the upper one and clear the lower one. Make sure that when the upper one flys into the corner and goes down you are not on the lower level (by, naturally, getting the gold in the upper alcove), and it will turn right and out of your hair. The third one will probably be in the launchpad shaft by now. Trigger it to come back down toward the lower column during one of its turnarounds in the alcoves along the right side of that shaft. Then you can either jump over it while it's turning right, or trigger it right and then jump. Yay! No drones in the launchpad column, and it's wayyy harder if there are. Too hard for me.

The launchpad... I launchpad and hold a hard left so I bonk my head into the railing under the left side and slide up into the right wall while the thwump triggers; then I jump in behind it and jump out at the last second, holding left, to get thwump-launched into the spot just under the upper thwump. Then, I jump to land on top of that thwump (the thwump launch will kill you on the drone), sneak under the drone and around the next one, and stare shamefacedly at the column with the two thwumps I can't seem to get down.

EDIT: Yay, I made it by practicing the area with NLE. White unlocked, time to get down, oh yeah, very funky now.

You have to jump from the far column back into the near just when the near thwump passes you. Otherwise it's easy, unless (like me) your computer frameskips pretty bad on this level.

You're talking about double- and triple- jumping. Search for threads about that. Search for a thread by me called "Jump School." There's also a series of levels that are designed to teach you to pull that kind of stuff off. I'm not where I can get the links right now.

Basically, you tap jump just before hitting a surface, and then jump again as soon as you do. A triple jump is the same, but bouncing off a flat onto an incline, off of a wall, and into the air.

Bounce block double- and triple- jumping are also required skills, as are corner kicking and watching high scores.
