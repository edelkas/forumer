An interesting tidbit about nicotine!

I'm sure all of you know that there are receptors in your brain. When nicotine is introduced to that chemical environment, certain receptors become designated to receive that chemical, and certain brain cells become destined to carry the chemical to these receptors. When someone is addicted, more and more receptors and brain cells are used to get more and more of the nicotine. Soon, when a person is heavily addicted to the drug, brain cells and receptors are /created/ to take in as much of the nicotine as possible. For a very long time, people thought that brain cells and the like could not be created.
