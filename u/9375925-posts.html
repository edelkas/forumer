So I'm beginning to put together a map pack. I've been a fan of N for years, playing on and off. I've recently gotten a few of my compadres hooked on the game, and so we are all competing to finish. Meanwhile, I have been trying to construct some nasty levels.

These are not intended to be easy, nor are they intended to be go-for-all-the-gold levels (for the most part). However, I do realize that as a map-maker it is hard to judge exactly what is playable and what is not. That is where you come in.

I've played each of these levels and have not been able to finish. However, that does not mean they are unplayable. I'm not all that great, and I certainly am not setting any high scores or world records. Nor are these the best levels known to man. I just feel that they are interesting and challenging, and they seem viable (if not exactly reasonable). So I hope you all give them a shot, let me know what spots need working on and which you like most. I will be compiling them all into Episodes and then releasing a map pack in the near future.
