You copy a level data code, you open Ned and CLICK on the level data box so that the CURSOR goes there, then you paste the code. Remember, the CURSOR is still in the box. So, to avoid it, just do what LittleViking said, CLICK OUTSIDE the box AFTER pasting the code and BEFORE pressing 'L'
