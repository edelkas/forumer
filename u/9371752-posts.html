Go to Gold Saucer.

Amass a considerable amount of GP (Racing chocobos is good for this), then head off to the Battle Arena, where you, well, battle. You need all the GP because you'll lose it fast in your constant battling, and the BP you win will reset to zero when you leave the Battle Arena. The Omnislash manual can be purchased for 32,000 BP (I've read it can be bought on the first disk for twice the price, but I don't know how accurate that is). Cloud can learn Omnislash by using the manual from the Item menu outside of battle after he's learned all of his previous limit breaks up to his second level 3.

Alternatively, you can just finish the game and in the final, story-driven duel I suppose you can say you've "obtained" Omnislash.

Also, Galbalan from Wanders from Ys III is the hardest final boss I've yet fought, though granted it's an Action/RPG (reading through the topic here). I see all these Square titles as answers for hardest final boss and a part of me dies. Fun games, but all easy.
