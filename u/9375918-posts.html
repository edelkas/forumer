Hmm... You seem to be very motivated and aware of what you need to accomplish during your years in High School. I pretty much screwed myself over so I'll probably end up going to some CSU or the community college (insert cringe).

The jump from 10th to 11th grade seemed like a whole different school entirely, to me. Honors English 10 was nothing compared to AP English 11, same goes with APUSH in comparison to World History.

As for extracurriculars... If you have the motivation and drive to handle those, try to get them out of the way during the first two years of High School, 'cause in your Junior and Senior years, you'll have the heavier workload to deal with. I tend to go through the school day and end up having to take a nap when I get home, and this is without any extracurricular activities.

Another suggestion I have for you... If you plan on going to a four year college, try to get through Calculus in the math area... If you're anything like me you'll do fine in Algebra 1, Geometry and Algebra 2. It's Trig/Pre-calculus where I crash and burn. So, if you find yourself having issues early on, get a tutor. And make sure you understand what you're doing.

And take the AP English and History tests in your Junior year... and the AP Gov't and English ones in your Senior year, those tests are essentially money saved if you pass them.

P.S. Don't worry bout the social anxieties of entering High School, it's wasted breath if you ask me, cause High School really isn't all that different than Junior High, just with more people
