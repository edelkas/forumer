At my school, a question is filtering around that has many students- and teachers- pondering.

"Would you rather be a ninja or a pirate?"

While at first a simple question, this takes a lot of thought. Ninjas tend to be more methodical and reserved, and more of lone wolf types, where a pirate is never without his mates, enjoying life to the fullest.

So, I finally came up with the idea of a ninja v. pirate N level!

This, at first, is nothing new. There are plenty of ninja/pirate levels on NUMA. But i have some requests in my level that can only be met by the most capable and creative mapmaker. Do you have what it takes to complete...


The ninja-pirate challenge?

Here are the requirements:

1. Must have some resemblence to a pirate ship or locale, in which the ninja steals the gold.
2. The gate cannot be on the pirate ship. It must be on either a separate boat or some other entity, because the ninja must escape with the gold.
3. The gold must be required in beating the level- it cannot be out of the way, and it must be guarded.
4. N should have to neutralize enemies on board the ship. And these enemies must include some zap drones.

Here are some extra requirements, to really give the level the extra pop.
1. Swash and Buckle: make N go through some sweet jumps and loops to defeat those nasty pirates
2. Cannons off the bow are always a nice touch.
3. Water is good too.
4. Once N gets the gold, and goes to the exit, he should shoot out of a launchpad to fly to the door.
5. One thing I wanted to try was to change the pirate flag. At the beginning of the level, there should be a flag that looks like the Jolly Roger, made out of doors. There should be a switch that unlocks all of those doors while simultaneously turning on a group of them that says, "N."
