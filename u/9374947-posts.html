First Scenario: So you start out in an empty level, right? The only objects in there are a floating elevator and gold pieces that seemed to have been placed by a mindless child with ADD. Suddenly, a block appears. Then another. Eventually you've got a staircase leading up to the elevator/button. The thing is, the stairs stop right before you would be able to reach either of them.

Second Scenario: Now you've actually got a level and a clear path to the button, and elevator. You begin to proceed to the button, but as you do, a drone pops out of nowhere. Latching onto the wall above you, you barely avoid it. This gives you time to look around. Doors with buttons are being placed in your way, mines are being laid and drones are beginning to follow a pattern that leads straight to you. It's a battle of the wits.

Third Scenario: Everything that just happened in the second scenario is the same, except now whatever has been doing this seems to be going crazy and blocking his own drones, destroying his own mines, and unlocking his own doors. What's going on?!

So what I'm doing here is giving you the features of the mode in extremely horrid stories.: P

The first scenario is you and a partner trying to get to the other level by one of the players being N, and the other in NED, creating and destroying obstacles for you.

The second is you versus the NED user. He can place mines to detour you or hopefully kill you, Drones to hunt you down, and doors to keep you busy while he attempts to kill you. However, everything is limited on his side by either a money system or load system. Both users must go about their ways as smart as possible.

The third option is two NED users and N. The NED users use their abilities to either stop or help you.

There you go, new friends, enemies and acquaintances... You can call me West, by the way if my screen-name is too complicated...
