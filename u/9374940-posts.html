#17953 1 2007-11-18T07:00:05Z Tapp
Now yes, clifty has done the exact same thing, but I wanted to get deeper into the propulsion. There are several main types of propulsion, from the method of launch pads, to the expert use of a glitch (such as thwump death propulsion to glitched locked door propulsion.). First, is launch pads. I personally don't like launch pad propulsion, but it's okay if you want to use them. Simply point and click and you've got yourself propulsion. When you create a launch pad, a code is written into the text box. It reads 
2^X position, Y position, X speed, Y speed!
Let's focus on the final two numbers. They define the speed and direction of the launch pad. The first one is the speed at which it shoots you right (a negative number shoots you left), while the second is the speed at which it shoots you down (a negative number shoots you up). The exact speed is the number, times ten in pixels per step (or frame for you non-programmers ). That of couse means that a value of 72 will shoot you from the left side to the right side in one step while a value of 52 on the Y speed will do the same for vertical speed. These numbers can also be altered to give you more precise angles than 45 degree angles. So the possibilities are virtually limitless. If left facing or up facing thwumps are placed halfway inside a wall, they start reversing once you touch them and they never stop. This can be very useful. The same thing can be done with thwumps facing down or right, but they have to be moved one pixel out of the box. Put plants in hand, aim at the ground and press E to plant them
Grapes and berries make great farming use, as they re-grow every 3 or so days
Build a net early and catch butterflies whenever possible. I've tripled my carrying ability so far by doing so.
Be careful when placing storages, as they catch on fire very easily
Driving boats in to the beach can get them permanently stuck
Build all tools you possibly can as early as you can, they can make the job a LOT easier
If being chased, run in to the water
Plant a line down the centre of the island using berries/grapes, and make a note of which direction to follow it to find the camp
Any major exploration should also be noted with a trail of berries/grapes
Make a torch once you have a fire, since it never goes out and can be used to re-light the fire
Do a lot of berry/grape planting until 200, and then you can grow lumber too
Don't hunt large animals like sheep and turtles too early, large pieces of fried meat are often needed for trades with the natives
Attacking rocks and trees (and various other objects) is probably the fastest way to get supplies of that kind
Instead of building storages, start a few building projects and dump supplies in to them when available. A well is good for useless pebbles.
If you are on an island with lions, get a fence or tent canvas between you and them, get as close as you can without getting mauled, and hack away.
