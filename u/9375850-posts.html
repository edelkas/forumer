Just checking back in to see how everything is going. i am saddened to see how things have transpired.

Over the years i have seen this happen with a plethora of businesses' products (we'll focus on game developers for the sake of the current topic). I am very familiar with how the business world works and in that respect i completely understand that the world cannot stop to polish a game that has for the most part netted it's base revenue for your company (I say base simply because additional content would yield additional income, which brings me to my next point.).

HOWEVER, truly great games and developers are the ones that break from this mold. Sure you have another project that needs to get done, but what many developers and certainly not publishers understand is that unresolved issues and quick-stop support do stick into consumer's minds when future products are released. On the same hand really well done titles that have the support they need often become memorable classics and will only help to grow your brand (Company) and boost sales for the next title. Don't believe me? Ask Valve. Ask ID. Ask Epic. They all started out as small developers and never lost focus of this. Video games are a form of art, and as with other forms of art, quality is a very important factor.

The thing that puts typical businesses aside from great businesses is their willingness to do what is necessary; to get things done properly. Quality is something that is in relatively short supply in this country as of late, and those who put forth the necessary effort are historically better off in the end; rewarded by stronger sales and more return customers.

This game was a major hit with Xbox Live, and has added to an already strong following. I think the signs are all around, and with the exposure and success granted by XBL i think that alienating your customer base here is at best a negative move. You have people ready to throw money at you, and all the game needs is a bit of maintenance and additional content; the latter of which is already out there thanks to your fans and just needs to be put together for download.

It was not my intention to lecture or express doubt for your intentions, and i apologize if you are a bit upset. I simply wanted to share some of my past experience and insight. It's upsetting to see good things fall short of their true potential, and as a businessman and gamer of 20+ years i wanted to express my concerns.

I wish you good luck with your future products and will continue to watch with a hopeful demeanor.
