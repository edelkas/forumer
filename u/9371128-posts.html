Presented by Unreality, Atob, Evil_bob, and Kkstrong

...:::The Premise:::...

In this competition, mappers will pit their best skills among one another to make their best NReality maps possible. Each week a theme will be chosen, the participants must make a map based on the theme. The top three maps that score highest will be placed on hold, to go into an NReality column to be released in NReality Version 7. Unlike a tournament, however, players will not be eliminated every round, because this isn't a ladder tournament. This is a 5-week competition. Every week is a new round.

The top 3 maps from each week will be arranged into a total of 3 standard N episodes. The levels may be altered slightly as too change difficulty but not in a way that the concept and layout is effected. These 3 episodes will be included in a column in the release of NReality version 7. Note that the levels in the column will be available for highscoring.

...:::The Rules:::...


1. NReality mods must be present
2. You will have one week to make the map using the specified theme. You may use other concepts and objects so long as the theme of the week is prominent in your map.
3. You may submit as many maps as you would like per week.
4. You must submit the map to NUMA. If you wish to delist for privacy, you may. In this case PM the link to one of the judges.
5. Maps must include the NUMA tag "TNCC".
6. No old maps allowed
7. Playable maps only. You may not make DDAs, survivals, or N-arts.
8. Collaborations are allowed if desired -- to ensure that we get the best possible maps.
9. If you collaborate one week, you may also submit other maps to the competition that are not collaborated on, or collaborated with a different partner.
10. If a tie for 3rd occurs then there will be 4 winners that week
11. If a map is edited after the deadline, but during the judging stages, the map is automatically disqualified. However, maps may be edited up until the judging phase.
12. There is no limit on number of entrants.
13. Atob, Kkstrong and I each judge each of the maps.
14. We may throw some twists into the rounds.
15. ALL Judge rulings are final.

...:::The Prizes:::...
Each week we will have 3 prizes. At the end of the 5 weeks, the person with the most points will be invited to continue mapping for the column.

1st - That weeks map in the column and 3 points
2nd - That weeks map in the column and 2 points
3rd - That weeks map in the column and 1 point

...:::The Judging:::...

NOTE: Judging criteria has changed and may be subject to more change. Keep these in mind while mapping and you should still be ok.

10/50 - Concept - How original are you. Extra point for finding never before seen uses of the NReality.
10/50 - Gameplay - Enemy placement, difficulty, fun and amount of lag.
10/50 - Aesthetics - How it looks
10/50 - Highscore-ablity - Does it provide ample amounts of innovation, routes, and challenge.
10/50 - Episodic - How well cold the map fit in an episode or column. Does the gold represent a good investment, has the user considered the length of an episode in general.

If I have failed to mention something, please just say and it will be resolved as soon as possible.
