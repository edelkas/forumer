I found an odd glitch with a thump while experimenting. Place a thwump so that it faces horizontially, with only half of it above the bottom border. When the thwump is activated, it may - and I don't think I've managed to make it work except in one case - it may go in the opposite direction of its blue side and go on into infinity. Since only half the thwump is exposed, if the ninja is pushed against the wall, he'll fly straight up, but if you use a bounce block as well, he'll go in the other direction. I'm going to try to use this in a DDA.

This has probably been found and exploited, but I wanted to put that out there. (By the way, great guide, I was trying to make a couple DDA's and both were kind of nooby. Not terribly, mind you, but still nooby. Lots and lots'a launchpads. Now I know better!)
