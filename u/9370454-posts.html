#42319 20 2004-12-24T22:02:00Z Stud_Bagel
It's already possible to play like this, if you have a fairly new video card.

First, get Powerstrip. Install it, run it, and right click on the taskbar icon. Click Color Profiles, then Configure. Find the "Invert Gamma Ramp" option and click it. Now your whole screen is inverted, all the time. Whee!

If it doesn't work, your video card is probably too old to do it, or Powerstrip just doesn't know how to make it happen with your hardware. I have a Radeon 8500 and it works for me.
