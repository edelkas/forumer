I've been working on a game (in Java, since I've never used ActionScript) using some of the collision detection explored in the N tutorials (specifically, the first tutorial, since I'm not using a grid-based system). It's all been working great, but I'm not sure how to deal with an object that simultaneously collides with two other objects.

For example, I'm trying to add in boxes, which the player can push around by running into them. They work a lot of the time, but since the boxes react to collisions with both the player and the environment (i.e. platforms, right now), situations where it touches both don't work.

Say a box is standing still on a platform. Right now, if the player lands on top of it, the box will project away from the player, moving it down into the wall. It will then project out of the wall and back into the player. Since gravity continues to act on the player, the box goes further into the player until it is projected to either side. This is actually looks decent, but I would rather be able to test for this situation and have the player stand on top of the box in that case (i.e. the player is projected away from the box, rather than the box being projected away from the player).

I think this situation is similar to one in N, when a non-deadly side of a thwump is pushing the ninja. Multiple collisions can occur if the thwump pushes the ninja into a wall, and causes the ninja to die. How was that situation tested for in N?

I am also thinking about implementing moving platforms, which would pose similar problems. The player will still be projected out of the moving platforms, just as with any other platform. However, I am not sure how to deal with a situation where the player is stuck in between two platforms. Like with the box, the player will project out of one platform into the other, then project out of that other platform into the first one.

Sorry about the long post, but I would really appreciate any help on this topic.
