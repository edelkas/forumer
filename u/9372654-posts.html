In the mean time...

Many of you claim that in order to believe God you first need proof. But this pressuposes that your mind is the final arbiter of truth. But Proverbs 1:7 says that "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge." In other words, God is the one who determines truth, not you. You don't put God on trial, He puts you on trial. You don't learn or discover truth apart from God, but rather God reveals it to you.

Therefore the question of "where's the proof" is silly. God is the standard of proof because if His existence is not presupposed then you cannot even have a standard for proof. Some of you are thinking, "Well your presupposing that the Bible is true when you say all of this." EXACTLY. Everyone presupposes something. I presuppose the contents of the Bible because it was revealed to us by a God who cannot lie (Titus 1:2, Heb. 6). You presuppose that man is the authority. But why should we trust man's mind? Where is the consensus concerning truth among men? There is no consensus at any level, in any culture. At the end of the book of Judges we read about how the people of Israel were living in opposition to God and doing what is "right in their own eyes". This is total anarchy, and yet this is autonomous human reasoning---thinking that man's mind is the authority? Which mind? And whose epistemology?

There are 5 types of proof in epistemology. I will prove right now that the only proof that is not self-defeating is the one that presupposes the Bible as true. The first four presuppose autonomous human reasoning. (By the way, if anyone wants me to prove that everyone has presuppositions, I can do that quite easily too.)

1. Irrational proofs --- this actually isn't a proof at all, its just an avoiding of the issue by saying that there is no truth and therefore no proof and therefore no need to prove anything. This is self defeating because "there is no truth" is being asserted as a true statement. (Claiming is there is no truth is called skepticism, by the way) Or perhaps you say that there is truth, its just subjective. But is this true for EVERYONE? Now your trying to say that all truth is subjective is universally true! And so that too is self-defeating. So hopefully you can see why claiming "there is no proof and no need to prove anything" is worthless. Something is true, but what?

2. Pragmatic proofs --- another attempt to skirt around the issue. Pragmatism holds that whatever works best is true. Well this assumes that we know what works best and that we know what goals to pursue. Moreoever, what works best for some may not work best for others. Or even if it did work best for everyone at a present time, how do we know that it will work best for everyone in the future? So pragmatism reduces to subjectivism and therefore skepticism. (see irrational proofs)

3. Empiricist proofs --- "all knowledge is gained through sense experience". The problem is that "all knowledge is gained through sense experience" is an idea. Ideas cannot be perceived (experienced by the senses). Therefore according to empiricism's own criteria for epistemic justification it cannot justify itself. That was easy...

4. Rational proofs --- "knowledge is gained by deducing from foundational or a priori knowledge". The obvious response is, where did this foundational/a priori knowledge come from? And who determines that something is a priori/properly basic? All forms of foundationalism reduce to subjectivism and therefore skepticism.

5. Transcendental proofs --- These proofs seek to know what the preconditions for the intelligibility of the human experience are. Let us consider logic. Logic is necessary to make sense of language, to even think properly. But why is there logic? Why can we trust it? Logic presupposes truth and the uniformity of nature. Logic is a way to relate things, furthermore, and must be contained in one's mind. It is not something that can be perceived or deduced (to deduce it would be to assume it already!) Logic has to reside in a mind. So in what mind was logic originally contained? It would have to be the mind of a being who is by nature logical. And sure enough, God says in His word that He cannot lie. For someone to lie something must be true, and Jesus said, "I am the way, the TRUTH, and the life, no man comes unto the Father except by me". God is truth, therefore He is logic. Logic resides in His being and His mind, and He granted mankind the ability to reason logically because we are created in His image (Gen. 1).

"THE FEAR OF THE LORD IS THE BEGINNING OF KNOWLEDGE" - Prov. 1:7

The best proof for the Christian God is that without Him you can't prove anything!

That was a quick intro to proofs, so ask questions.
