I was experimenting some, and now I have come to the critic part, where error and trial is needed, and a very time-absorbing such.

I was trying to get a explination to the drone's code, and what I got was this:


6^(x coordinate), (y coordinate), (way of movement), (chase or not), (what kind of drone), (start direction)!
Where the (...) is replaced with numbers.

X and Y coordinates is easy, just where the drones appear on the map.

Way of movement:
0=right, follows the wall (clockwise)
1=left, follows the wall (counter clockwise)
2=dumb, right
3=dumb, left
4=dumb, alt. (right first)
5=random (or same as 4, but start with left?)
Every higher number will be replaced with one of these when you load it.
chase:
0=regular (don't chase)
1=chase, if you make a laser drone chase, it rotates instead (clockwise, I don't really know how to make it rotate counter clockwise), and never stops. If you make a chaindrone chase, it also rotates, but it stops and it's not very useable.

start direction:
0=right
1=down
2=left
3=up
Every higher will be replaced with one of these when you load it.
