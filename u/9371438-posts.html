FREIKS MAPLE CARAMEL SLICE

Ingredients

200Grams of chocolate buiscuits (un-iced) crushed finely
1/2cup dessicated coconut
200G butter, melted
100G dark eating chocolate, melted

CARAMEL
395g can sweetened condenced milk
60g butter
2tablespoons maple syrup


METHOD

greace 19x29cm ractangular slice pan: line base and two long sides with baking paper, extending paper 2cm above edge of pan.

combine buiscuit crumbs, coconut and butter in a large bowl. press over base of prepared pan, cover. refrigerate until firm. spreadhot maple caramel over crumb layer, cover; refrigerate until firm. Spread combined chocolate and oil over slice:leave to set

MAPLE CARAMEL METHOD
Combine ingredients in medium saucepan; whisk over heat untill butter melts simmer, whisking constantly, about 8 minutes or until the mixture thickens and is dark golden brown.

MAKES ABOUT 24
