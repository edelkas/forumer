Multi-Way Thwumps
Multi-Way thwumps are different to normal thwumps, as they move toward N no matter what way he is facing it! This will make things a tad more difficult and will make good close encounters in races or DDA's!

An add-on to this idea is when it collides with something it stays there! Until it detects N again! Then it does the same thing.
