In the help forums, there are a lot of posts like this: "Help! I can't get past lvl so and so and episode so and so..."
First, on hard maps you (usually) can't succeed the first time.
Second, you need to actually TRY the lvl multiple times. If you try once or twice and it dosen't work, just try again.
Some people have beaten the game so it is obviously possible. N is a game that requires patience. And when you get fed up with trying to beat a lvl play some other game and come back to N later. Or even better, tackle another column.
Third, it is possible that you just can't get through a lvl. That means you aren't taking the right path or you lost too much time in the previous episodes(but if you read the posts at least 70% of them are people that are trying to say: This lvl is HARD). THEN you can post the famous "I can't beat this lvl." But first watch the highscorers demos. That should help.
