My Top 10 Door Related Requests for N+ and N 1.5

1. Switches which can be toggled on and off, so a door can be repeatedly opened and closed

2. Having more than one switch operate a single door

3. Better NED allowing movement of doors by dragging and dropping doors, as opposed to deleting a door using its switch, and remaking it, which can be a nightmare if there are many switches in a single tile area

4. For the trap door switch: When pressed the switch becomes grey, just like the locked door switch

5. Doors which span more than one tile, imagine 5 tile long doors, they could really help with the feeling of scale in some levels

6. Timed doors, which open only after a pre-defined delay

7. Doors which are opened only after many switches have been pressed

8. Diagonally placable doors

9. Doors which require all the gold in a room to be collected before they open

10. Differently coloured trigger lines in NED for every single door
