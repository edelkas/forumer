I reached my hand under the couch, tentatively at first, then deeper. Encountering no marbles, I pulled my hand out in disappointment.

I remember the image vividly, and I suspect I always will. It was a slim hand, with tapered fingers - a woman's hand. It was gnarled and wrinkled, as if aged, and it was dead black. Not black as in African, black as in dead. Of course, back then, I didn't know that corpses blacken as they decompose, so I didn't know what the black meant.

The hand reached out to me as far as it could, which was just up to the wrist. Then it retreated under the couch. Then it emerged again, this time pushing with it a little crumpled up, plastic bag with a logo on it I didn't recognize. It waited, as if expecting me to take the bag. Then, when I didn't, it pulled the bag back under the couch and was gone.

I got up, walked down to the kitchen, and told my Mommy what had happened.

The problem is that a game that reliant on both Multiplayer and Player Balancing wouldn't go over too well with a Developer.

The person building the Dungeon, the DM, would have to be limited in some ways, and rewarded in others.
What if he just made crummy dungeons, where all the "phat lewt" was easy to grab? Then you could just pimp out your character and massacre anything in your path. Obviously, you would want to be able to go to other Dungeons with your Hero, and adventure there. What fun is it for all three parties (Protagonists, Antagonists, and DM) if some dude can hop on the server and totally own everything?

Setting a level requirement/limit could help, but you could still easily "twink" your hero by having a friend build a Dungeon filled with chests that you could easily get to.

The Protagonist would be the easiest to work with, I suppose. Just like every old RPG ever made. I think the interface that best fits would be either "Oblivion" by Bethesda or "Might and Magic: Dark Messiah" by whoever did that. With level requirements to join a dungeon, things could still be fair, and work out rather well.

Antagonist would be another problem.
Obviously, you wouldn't want to hang out in a dungeon and wait for people to join so you can be killed by them kill them. You could end up waiting for a long time, and get little reward. AI Heroes would work, but then you have to figure out how you control their power and the like. If it were up to the DM, once again he could power level someone if he was so inclined, and you could dungeon hop (Once again, all that work to (If done so legitimately) level up, you would HAVE to allow players to play on other dungeons).
Although being an antagonist would be fun, developing a fair, balanced game would be extremely tough, and likely easy to exploit.
And what about incentive for people playing as creeps to guard chests, other than the fact that it makes heroes more powerful. Wouldn't they want some sort of reward?

So, the best way I can think of to prevent exploitation would be "League" style maps. The only way to be able to Map on these servers would be to have enough kudos and then apply to one of the developers. Sorry to be such a negative guy towards this (I've had tons of ideas about games much like this, so dont think I'm being biased or anything), but eventually you have to realize how much work would have to go into something this big to keep things even remotely fair/plausible of an idea.
