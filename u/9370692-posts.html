Plese note that this is not an official competition. It is just something that I thought would be cool. Personally I love seing good DDA's. I have both the 2nd best and the 4th best maps on NUMA and they are both DDA's. One of them was first till maximo's came along. Congrats maximo.

Anywho. What I propose is a reasonably short competition in which the competitiors (you) try to create the best DDA possible to impress the judge (me). Basically you must submit your map to NUMA and add a post here to it. I will judge them and give you the results within (hopefully) a week after competition completion.

Each entrant may submit 2 maps. This is the limit for this competition. If it goes well then I may do it again with more submissions.

Unique ideas are welcome however there are some things that I will mark down on.

1. Excessive jump pad use.
2. Excessive gold delays. THis can cause massive lag.
3. The 'occassional death' syndrome. Hint: Avoid using chainguns.
4. Dying after completing the level. This is just a personal nitpick cause I love to see the DDA fully completed with a nice pose or two.

Scoring System:

10 Points - Close Calls. The more close calls the better.
10 Points - Complexity. Complex DDA's such as maximo's recent DDA make them much more enjoyable to watch.
5 Points - Lag. No Lag - 5 Points. Excessive Lag - 0 Points.
5 Points - Map Layout. DDA's with good map layouts are definitely better than ones with seemingly random tiles thrown in.
5 Points - Death. N Never Dies - 5 Points. N dies frequently - 0 Points.
3 Points - Judges Tilt. 3 Points for maps that I think really deserve it.
2 Points - Life after Completion. N survives - 2 points. N dies instantly - 1 points.

Rules:

1. All maps must be submitted to NUMA with a related post in this thread by the New Year US Standard Time. (11:59 PM, 31st Dec 2004)
2. All maps must be complete DDA's.
3. All maps must be originally created within the alloted period unless they do not yet have 5 ratings on NUMA.
4. Judge's decisions are final.
5. Each entrant may submit no more than 2 DDAs.

All other traditional rules, i.e. hidden objects, non-moving drones, etc. are allowed.

Good Lcuk to everyone and get DDAing.
