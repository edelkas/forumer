Hello ,

This is what I call the Storm Cloud. It is a vicious enemy in the game and is on a track to go back and forth across a room. If you're too slow,you get caught inside of its deadly lightning bolts and die a painful electric death. the lightning bolts hit the ground almost instantly, and a shock is sent through the ninja frying his insides until he dies! So you must be quick to pass this enemy and you must move swiftly and carefully. It does not just harm the ninja, but the ninja can use it to an advantage. He can jump on it and use it as a tool to cross gaps.
