Hello all - this is EdS25.

I downloaded N on Aug 12 - heard about it when playing Cave Story (a lot of people mentioned N as being the only better freeware game available which of course sparked my curiousity.)

N completely took over my life for two solid weeks. I wish even a small fraction of the games I pay ridiculous amounts of money for were this enthralling. Played it every waking moment (yes I have a job - you do the math.) Played til my hands were completely cramped and I could still see the high score screen when I closed my eyes. I dreamt about it. I beat every episode but 88 (MTI - big surprise there right?) and was stuck there for several days. But in the late evening of the 26th, 2 weeks to the day after first downloading it, I finally beat MTI. Ep 89 went pretty smooth after that.

I decompressed for a few days, but have tried quite a few user levels since. My favorite levels are usually "jumping puzzle" ones where it's just you versus the level itself. I generally got too frustrated in the rocket/turret levels. Although nowadays, now that I'm much more accustomed to the timing, I'm surprised I used to have as much trouble as I did.

Finally, yesterday I decided it was time for me to get a high score. I picked a good candidate from the list - something with a big differential in scores, hoping to get in the top 20. Ep 62 - Level 1. Well, to my surprise I found myself climbing the ranks and even (momentarily - basically until someone sees this post) beat trib4l by 1.1 seconds! Woot! I know it won't last - and it's only 1 level out of 500, but it felt good nonetheless.

Good luck to everyone out there (in N and otherwise)
