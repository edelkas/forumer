Ok, the new MapSwap program has a huge loop hole. I don't know if I should be posting this here, but oh well, this looks like the spot for it.

MapSwap (for those who are unaware) is a clever program where you post a incomplete map, and in return you get another incomplete map, with credit to do whatever you want with it.

However with my last several map swaps that I have posted, I have received back a spam map, with nothing really in the map data box, thus the person submitting this has posted nothing and received a map. So these people have stolen maps.

I hope somebody reads this.

MapSwap was good. Most things in our world were good. Until they were spoiled by those few people who want more than they can give.
