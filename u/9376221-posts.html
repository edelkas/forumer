I like pretty much everything in the game, so frankly I've got to go for minor stuff:

1) Quit control [Q] prior to level reload - not a big problem in normal play, but some of the user levels (especially N Art) are huge. It's irritating to have to watch a level you're tired of slowly, slowly reload in front of your eyes, before you can escape. I know you could simply close N, but that just seems... less than subtle.

2) Ghost demos - the possibility of playing the game against a semi-transparent demo playing in the background. Since the level terrain itself doesn't change, the only thing you'd actually need to animate would be Ghost-N. It was mentioned that animating the Ghost-enemies could be a problem, as there might be confusion between Live and Ghost, but I'd suggest not animating the Ghost-enemies. After all, if the Ghost-N suddenly splatters into a thousand pieces, it's fairly obvious what's happened. Ditto for riding on thwumps.
[DOUBLE EDIT]
Oops. Just spotted this in that 'Things not to post' thread. Humbug. Guess I'll go for user episodes too, instead.

3) 45 degree one-way blocks - I always felt this was missing: a smooth surface is both elegant and handy for speed in levels; having to gouge a triangle out of a ramp feels clumsy, and inefficient.

Most of this was probably mentioned before, but I'm willing to add my name to the lists.

I've checked back through the majority of this thread and, sure enough, all three suggestions were not just mentioned in the forum, but actually in previous posts here. Ah well, it's an addition to the next Favourites Listing. When LV posts one...
