In my opinion, it's all about learning to stay cool. The more frustrated you are, the less chance there is that you will complete the level. When i first started playing, i gave up at episode 01 multiple times... but then i realised that it's all about patience. When you die near the end of a level, instead of being frustrated about dying so close to the end, be happy about the fact that you managed to get so close and will probably be able to get there again. In other words, don't "expect" to beat every level in less than 10 tries... or 100 for that matter.
