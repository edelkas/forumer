#115681 10 2005-11-17T19:24:27Z germy
It took me forever to be able to do that! I must have spent 2 hours trying to get the timing down right. And it is about timing, not luck. I can usually get it about 1 in 15 - 20 tries.

First you have to jump into the first tunnel just right so you don't get hung up on the top or bottom corner, just barely tap the jump button.

Then you have to jump into the tunnel going down so that the upper curve kind of shoots you down faster. You'll know when you've done that right because it feels like you jumped "down" the tunnel.

Then you have to time your "change direction skid" so that you don't go too far or too short. Just enough to barely touch the trap door button.

Then you have to jump and slide along the top of the tunnel, so that you drop down quickly under the first drone. Be sure not to stick to the wall on the way down.

Then run like heck!

4 simple steps that have to completed just right to get close to the upper high scores. The first two jumps are really important. Really pisses you off when you actually do it and carelessy hit a mine because you are rushing to get a high score... But very satisfying none the less.
