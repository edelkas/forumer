Hello everyone.

I'm new here. I just spent some time reviewing the many, many posts on ideas for N gameplay, and so I hope you people don't get mad at me for posting another thread along these lines. There is the possibility that I missed a few ideas, so if these are redundant, please don't hate me. I may not have read all of the posts from the beginning of this forum's creation, but at least I read the bulletin.

So, here are a few ideas that came to mind which may not

A) Ruin the simplicity of N

or

B) Violate the pre-stated bulletin

or

C) Radically alter the gameplay of N (Perhaps this is restating A. I'm not sure.)

Let's see what you all think.

1. Doors which reset themselves (close) after being opened, after a pre-determined period of time. They would be controlled by a switch which also resets. Obviously, level design needs to be a consideration to not make this feature too unforgiving.

2. Sections of walls, floors, ceilings, or even areas suspended between platforms that are electrified (charged with lethal blue energy) for timed intervals. Has anyone played Strider 2 for Genesis? It was a horrible game, but this kind of feature did appear there, and the feature itself is not horrible. Depending on what else is going on around these areas, this can really raise the difficulty bar on maneuvering.

3. Non-gameplay related- a thumbnail sketch of levels that have been completed when you are picking which mission you want to play- have you ever wanted to go back and play a level but you couldn't remember exactly which mission set it was located in? I have. So, that would be a nice user friendly thing that R&M could do. When your mouse is hovering over the mission, a set of thumbnail pics could appear off to the side. Would that take up too much memory? I don't know.

4. Just came to me. Homing mines- when you get too close, they start to move toward N, at 1/3 the speed of a homing missile perhaps. When you jump outside of their 'detection radius', they remain stationary. Now that one might be too much, but let's see what you all think.

Any comments?
