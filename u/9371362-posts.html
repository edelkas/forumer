After a very quick search, here's a couple of threads which should help you:-

http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=6096


A reccomendation from someone who completed it for the first time last night: When the first thump heads left, run right (behind it), and jump up, drawing the second thump to the right. Then go back to the left wall, and wait for the bottom thump to head back, and then jump once below the first mine, then between mines 2 and 3 - if you catch the wall on the descent from this jump, you should go just high enough to get the fourth thump to head right. From here, when the 4th thump returns, jump on it, and in doing this you will draw out thump 5. Wait until Thump 4 is on the left hand side of thump 5, and then jump onto thump 5. use this to then repeat what you did for thumps 4 and 5 on thumps 6 and 7, and then jump to safety. You can figure the last bit out by your self because it isn't all that difficult.

That was probably isn't the quickest, but I found it to be the easiest.

The key with this level is being smooth with your jumps and landings, and a bit of luck at the bottom. You can't afford to land on corners, or be too slow running to the next jump etc.
