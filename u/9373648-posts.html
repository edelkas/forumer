I'd like to add something to your post Twistkill.
Articles.
A, an, and the.
A is replaced with an if there is a vowel after the word a. EX: An apple.
The is used if you don't want to use an or a. EX: An elephant = The elephant. The is also used if you want to be more specific.
A, well it's a lot like an, except "a" is used if there is a consanant (I think that's spelled wrong) after it. EX: A dog ran by my house. You wouldn't say an dog ran by my house, since dog begins with a consanant, not a vowel.
That's about it.
