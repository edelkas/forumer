If you don't have the patience, think about it this way. Every episode is a level. Episode 23 would be Level 23. No more 23-0, 23-1, 23-2, etc. Every time you reach the exit is a checkpoint, and the end of the level is a save point. You can't save anywhere else other than the save points, so in order to get to the next level, you need to finish the one you're on. Hope this helps out a bit.

Back to reality... Everyone gets stuck in the middle of an episode and has to restart the episode. I get stuck on the last level of most episodes and have to do every level before it again and again and again. You just can't give up.
