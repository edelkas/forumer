"Take the leap!" he insists, but you can't see him anymore.
A tug at your pant leg, and you turn around. There sits a small goat, munching on a piece of a carrot.
You hear a loud explosion from outside the building. The glass around you shatters. Meanwhile, the goat is ramming it's horns against you. You think it is trying to direct you towards the stairs. You follow the goat.
It begins to walk up the stairs to the penthouse level, Floor 222. Then gradually, as walk up the stairs, you notice the goat becoming more and more upright, until eventually it stand 7 feet tall, with large horns.
"I am Goatman", it bellows as he picks you up and begins to run. It's breath is heavy and warm, yet strangely comforting.
"Today is the apocalypse my friend. But we will be safe."
By now he had almost got to the 222nd floor. You had never been there before, even though you worked in the office below it.
The fire escape door was closed as we got to the penthouse.
"I am Goatman. Let me in!", it yelled. The door slowly opened...

You look around. Three large cakes sit on a long cedar table, and around them sit four... well, you think that they might be human. One's got scars all over her face, and a air hostess uniform. Kitty rolls her eyes, "But we are safe, so it is okay." Another loud explosion goes off, this time in the building next door. Rubble hits the windows, but they do not shatter. "You're a bit shell-shocked", whispers Goatman into your ear. He pulls out a vial from underneath his fleece. It's small and transparent. "Drinks this, it'll make you feel better", you hastily grab the bottle and gulp it down; it tastes like rotten lemon. Goatman pulls out a leather arm chair from underneath the table, and insists you sit. You start to feel dizzy, then fall asleep, as Gustav pulls out a large map and scrolls it onto the table.

You wake up. It's dark and noisy, the sound of gravel crunching under tyres. And there's Timmy fast asleep, huddled in a ball, in the corner across from you. 'Watch yourself there!", Gustav chuckles as he follows a trail with his finger on a map. "Turn left... now." Kitty swerves the van into a small farmyard. Most of the crops are dead. You turn back to Timmy, and kneel down. "Timmy," you say in a childish voice, "Wakey wakey!" His eyes slowly open, their red and sore. "We're here."

You caught up with Gustav, "But... we're in... London! How are we going to..." Your voice drowned away, as you noticed the car disappear, then appear again. Gustav chuckled, "Trust me, my friend, that is a very fast van."

You all got out of the van. It was twilight, 3 AM according to the clock towering above you. Gustav pointed towards a house, "That is where my friends are."

Gustav was in a large armchair, wailing in agony, while his two friends obsessed over over him. One gave you a glance then returned to Gustav. "What happened?", you question. "He's tired. He just did something no one's supposed to do" You were confused. "He opened a riff.", she pointed to the kitchen. You walked in.
