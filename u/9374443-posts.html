After reading all the suggestions on this post, I seriously think that the best idea is for voting to be integrated into comments. I don't think that it should be required to comment just so you can vote; it's already been pointed out that that would lead to useless posts full of gibberish. I think though that it's still the easiest way to show who's voted what. Even if you don't put anything in your comment, a post would still appear below the map saying:
[Superguh007 Rating: 3.5]
[ ]
That way, a pop-up window wouldn't be necessary to show the history of the votes, you could just look.

This wouldn't completely stop multiaccounting, but it would make it much more obvious if say, joejohn20 kept rating every single one of an author's maps a five, and had no maps of his own. Plus, it would be much more conveniant in terms of helping mapmakers improve. Personally, I don't want ratings of five with nobody telling me why. To me, that's almost as bad as having my maps sniped.
