rather than trying to come to a solution the hard way, first ask yourself why you are loosing the connection so quickly. Have you tried other games using the Wifi router in your house? It may be that your router needs to be adjusted. If you are sharing the signal, it could cause conflicts. Go to the computer that is connected to the router and go to the router settings page .. usually the address is on the bottom of the router or in the manual. if you dont have it, just search the web by typing in your model number and this address http://192.168.1.1/ it should ask you for login and pass. search for the defaults online. usually its something like admin/admin or admin/blank

once you get to your router settings, search for a place where you can change the frequency from AUTO to usually 1 of 12 bands. Select maybe 11 or something obscure. Save settings and try the PSP again.

I had problems like this before and this solved many issues forme.. Let me know if this helped?
