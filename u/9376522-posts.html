I agree with LittleViking completely. Bounce Blocks are pretty much broken. Sometimes, you'll see the Ninja hugging the wall when you're against them, but he'll be a few pixels away, and facing the wrong direction!

The first level of Chapter 19 has got to be the worst in the game. It's entirely Bounce Blocks and Mines! Have fun trying to wall jump off those correctly! I've beat the entire game, and this one took three or four times longer than anything else.

Also, in "Plus" graphics at least, Laser-Drones are broken. Several times, their lasers have disappeared midshot, but if you wander into where the beam should be, you'll still end up dead. Annoying? Very.

And then there's little changes that I dislike - Timed Mines are worthless, Floor Drones are slow, and Thumps are also now terribly annoying to jump off of.

Also -> One way Floor/Walls are glitchy. You'll kind of teleport through them, if you're close enough.

Also, besides the one terribly frustrating level that I mentioned earlier, none of the game really presents a challenge to anybody that's beaten even one Column of N.

And finally, the Map-Editor is very annoying for left-handed people.
