In my hundreds of attempts, I've discovered several things anyone may find useful. I call the columns 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, from left to right.



- Turning right when you begin will never net the high score now. I have scored around 148-149 by that method, though, so if you're gunning for second or lower, give it a shot.

- Hopping to the second platform and then jumping along the ceiling is the fastest way to get all the way left, and if you do it well, you will land on the forward slope of the first gold mound (right side of chute 3) and possibly be shot across the entire way. I didn't do this in my replay because it tends to make you go too fast, and you get the drones in the hot spots (see the end of the post).

- Once you're in the bottom left corner, you could jump up chute 2 instead of chute 3 (I jump up chute 3), but you might have to contend with the drone that rose from chute 3 if you didn't activate it hopping across the gold mounds from start. In the end, chute 3 is better. If you ascend chute 2, you need to turn around to collect the gold. It is possible to barely evade the drone as you rise through chute 2, however, you then need to undergo a serious course correction in midair to fall through chute 3, and then you still need to turn around to collect the gold. Not wise.

- What you do in the bottom left corner determines most of the rest of the game. If you jump over the drone that begins on the floor, odds are you will catch the drone that descends chute 5 and lure it across row where the gold is (I do). If you then hustle up chute 3, you will probably find it at the bottom of chute 2 when you want to fall. However, if you allow it to turn right when it reaches the bottom and move counter-clockwise around the edge of the screen, you will probably find it at the top of chute 4 when you want to ascend.



I may have attempted this level more than a thousand times; it is the only one I play. I have done some very outlandish things, most, if not all of them far faster than my replay, and they all eventually end in death. Hot spots include trying to descend chute 2 in the beginning, ascending chute 4, and trying to escape the second switch room.

Good luck.
