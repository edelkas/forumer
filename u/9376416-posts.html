Terribly disappointed.

Reason #1
Buggy bouncy blocks breaks the game. That reason alone makes it a terrible N+ game.

Reason #2

Reason #3
Oh, they have a gold count in the Statistic screen. Sweet. Then i'll do all episodes trying to get all gold that are possible to get, for the sake of a high score. Some levels are easy to beat, but extremely hard to get all gold. And i did it on all episodes, except for 3 or 4 levels where it's practically impossible to get, or i'm not skillful enough. It was a GREAT challenge.

And so, reason #3 sounds like a good reason so far, right? WRONG. Every gold you pick up are added on the stats, INCLUDING THE ONES YOU PICK UP BEFORE YOU DIE. Therefore, you can just pick up 100, die, restart level, pick up another 100 = This will count as you picking up 200 gold. The game simply does not reset your gold count back to the number prior the start of the level. RIDICULOUS!

That's not a highscore! You can just grind gold this way to easily get a high gold count.

Reason #4
Music is terrible. No, more than that, it's extremely annoying when your remaining time is less than 120 seconds.
