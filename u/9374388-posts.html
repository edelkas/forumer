Hi, Dusty here. I made a coupla levels, and I'd like some reviews before submitting them to NUMA. I'm working on another. Here's the basics of them:

The Golden Age: First N-level I made. Basically, playable N-art; lots of cool shapes. Kinda pointless but there is something satisfying about getting all that gold.

Namesake: Again, same thing, less gold but with a tileset saying "Dusty96" and a smilie face out of gold.

Far-Flung: A level with a bunch of cloud shaped tiles and a coupla birds/balloons made out of gold. As the title and content implies, you're up in the sky. Not very long to complete, but kinda fun.

Tributary: A text tileset I made for the person that referred me to N (Greg from Kongregate.) Text says "Thanks to Greg for telling me about N." Lots of gold, so don't play this if you've got a slow computer.

Supersize: I think this is one of my greatest accomplishments. It's a hollowed-out picture of the Ninja, maybe 200 times as big as the real Ninja is, with 3 hollowed-out mines that you can't touch (200 times as big as the real mines) and 2 pieces of gold (one you can collect, one you can't) and an exit switch (the blue part is a lot of zap drones, I wish I could figure out how to make them stay put). Play it to see what I mean.

Optical Delusion: A sort of checkerboard level. 2 thwumps, some launchpads you should NOT touch from the side, and some gold. 4 doors surrounding the exit switch, too. Look at the level as a whole, and figure out why it's called Optical Delusion..

Dodge 'em: This one is an edited version of a friend's (Hypronelite) level. The tileset is the same, except for the maze part with the door. And, the enemies and gold are all mine; it had too many enemies so I stripped the whole thing of all its objects. I have the old version if you want it, just ask me.

Cross Country: A little level where you have to dodge the mines and get the gold if you can. I should have put some more mines on the lower level; once you get down there, if your jump key is working you can beat it. Expect an updated level soon.

Madness Interactive: I named this level "Madness Interactive" as both a pun on the game and a literal depiction of the level. Madness, indeed.. It's so hard to decide; do you get the scattered gold, get one switch and go for the room of gold, get all the switches and go for the room of gold, strip the whole thing of its gold, or just trigger the switch and get the heck outta there? You decide. It's harder than it looks..


Anyway, those are all my levels. Code is enclosed in the document (don't worry, it's not a computer worm) Put it into userlevels.txt and enjoy!
