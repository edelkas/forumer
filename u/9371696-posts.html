DATFQP:

Q: What does DATFQP mean?
A: Don't Ask The Following Questions Please.

Q: These maps seem familiar.
A: Is that a question?

Q: Why do these maps seem familiar?
A: That's more like it. All of the maps are on NUMA, but some didn't make it to the levelpack and some were Episized for the levelpack.

Q: What does Episized mean?
A: It means they were made more episodic. Don't tell me you don't know what episodic means.

Q: Why don't you delete them from NUMA?
A: I probably am. I'm going to keep the rated ones and my favorite ones, though.

Q: Can you save one that I like?
A: Go vote for it, and if it gets rated it will definetly stay. If you did that, leave a comment there or PM me and I'll try my best.

Q: What if I can't find it?
A: All the maps have the same name on NUMA and in the levelpack, but I re-arranged them for the levelpack.

Q: Why did you make a levelpack anyway? You haven't been making maps long enough and you haven't made enough maps yet.
A: Honestly, I was bored. I have MMBS, or Map Maker's Block Syndrome. Because I have nothing better to do. I wanted to put some of my unrated maps out into the open.

Q: This is horrible. Are you trying to offend the other levelpacks and their makers?
A: No. I would never dream of competing of something so great as the Legacy or the Camisade Collection. I don't even consider myself a good map maker. I am decent at best. I just wanted to put my maps into the open.

Q: If you don't think you're good, then why should I play your maps?
A: Because decent is better than half of the stuff you see on NUMA.

Q: I have a great idea for one of these maps!
A: Again, not a question.

Q: What if I have a great idea for one of these maps?
A: Better. Post it here and I'll consider it.

Q: Why did you make this big, long, FAQ?
A: I didn't. I made a big, long, DATFQP.

Q: Why did you make a big, long, DATFQP?
A: Honestly, I was bored. Because I wanted to infuse humor to make you like the maps more/ Because I have nothing better to do/ Because I wanted to be original.

Q: I have seen FAQs on levelpacks before.
A: This isn't Jeopardy.

Q: How is a FAQ on a level pack original?
A: Its not. But a DATFQP is.
