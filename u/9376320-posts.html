Creators Island - N Edition
<Work In Progess>

This is a W.I.P thread about my upcoming game Creators Island - N Edition!

At the moment there is no demo, but there will be soon, hopefully...

In my game, the idea is simple, run around trying to get to the end of the level. There will be at least 10 official levels that, by completing them, will give you a special code that will unlock a trophy in the Trophy Room! But wait, there's more... Included with my game will be... A Level Editor! This will allow you to create your own levels and share them with people across the world!

Features:
  • Single Player Action
  • Level Editor
  • Trophy Room
  • Level Sharing
  • Single-Computer 2 Player Racing (Un-Decided)
When the game and level editor are up and running, I will open a contest. The top 10 user made levels will be picked and used as official levels. This will happen when the bugs have been sorted out and what-not.

Until then everyone!
