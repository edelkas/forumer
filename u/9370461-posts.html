#24669 13 2004-07-11T20:14:12Z kyle
pillory is 52-2, and I have to say, I didn't find it all that hard. I'm not a spectacular player either. The bounce blocks are not impossible to control, just tricky - you have to make sure not to move in a way that will cause you to stay in the same place. If you move against the direction of force of the bounce blocks (i.e. into them for too long), you will stay in the same place (their acceleration and yours will cancel out). If you don't have any momentum when you hit them, you'll also stay in the same place (they won't displace enough to leave an opening, and you'll just be moving against them). If you experiment, its not really impossible at all to get through. There are other harder levels with overlapping bounce blocks, so you'll need to figure out how to deal with them for those, too. Going down the middle column on 52-2 was also frustrating at first, but actually quite rewarding once I managed to do it consistently.

This is the easiest way if you want to actually land and let the block stablize before moving on to the next block, and your jumps are always too high.
