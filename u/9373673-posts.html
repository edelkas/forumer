First off, sorry for the topic description - there just wasn't enough space.

OK, the idea of this contest is to get people to submit a map that they think is suitable for highscoring with their fastest demo for that map (put SOME effort in). They recieve the crown for their map (as they have the fastest demo). Then, other folks get to try and beat the first demo. If they do then they capture the crown from the map-maker. Crowns vary in value depending on the level of competition (more competition=harder to capture=greater value). The person with the highest value of all their crowns added together wins when the competition ends (undecided so far).

So, in rules:
-RULES-
1-No spammy maps (an obvious one)

2-No frame by frame (please point me towards suspects)

3-The speed of the demo is measured in userlevels (this wont affect the time difference if the same amount of gold is collected in both levels, and im expecting solid AGDs)

4-The value of each crown depends on how many owners it has had (so if the crown started with 99turtle99, the map-maker, then went to usaswim, then ended up with Mr_Lim, it's value would be 3)

5-Demos are submitted as .txt files in this thread in userlevels format (this makes it far easier to check your times)

6-For every 5 seconds you beat the previous demo by, you add an extra point of value to that crown (this is to stop you from just beating the demo, letting some n00b beat that, then pwning it with an extra two points - you get good value doing that straight off!)

7-You can only submit your own maps (collabs are fine)

8-You may submit your map with your demo at any time

9-If you're caught cheating, you'll be disqualified (so be subtle )

10-The overall winner gets to put something in their sig saying "I'm better than all these people at highscoring", with no arguments (you might want to only list the more prestigious highscorers)
-RULES-

I'll submit a map with a demo soon, probably in the next 24 hours, but please, start it off yourself!
If there is anything amiss, please inform me (e.g. a missing but necessary rule)
