While the N game is great how it is, there's always room for perfection. Some things that would be awesome (In my point of view, which is probabaly going to be shot down by the veteran members) are:

-Double Jumping-
To get in those hard to reach places or to dodge a guided missle while already in mid-air.

-Ninja Gadgets-
This could include many things, like a grappling hook to get somewhere beyond jumping (With a limit on how far, of course. No cheating.) or a probe to lure enemies away from their posts. Or maybe even a special magnet to get nearby gold. (even though gold isn't magnetic)

-Powerups-
A speed boost to get past a floorchaser hot on your tail, a jump boost to dodge those pesky ninja-seeking missles. Maybe even invincibility to stick it to those stupid drones. Or, for your sneaky ninja, invisibility, to get past the enemies wiht a bit more of a thrill. Or maybe just without the imment death.

-Added modes-
Extreme mode: No gold, no time. Just you, 5 lives, th elevel, and a helluva lot of badass enemies, many times stronger.

Test mode: No time restraints, no enemy restraints, no ninja-seeking rockets, no ninja-chasing blue things, no gauss drones. Nothing. Get a feel for the level. Or just screw around. Your choice.


-Alternate levels-
Imagine: No enemies, no gold, no time, nothing. It's like heaven, but not. For the level is made to confuse your brain on how to get to the end.


These are just some things that I think would be awesome in N. Please post some of your own suggestions. Constructive criticism welcome.
