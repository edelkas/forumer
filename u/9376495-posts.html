Here are my favorite qualities...

-Non linear gameplay: This really fills out the map and leaves the player some figuring to do. A map can be made less linear just by putting gold in hard to reach places. Its not hard, introduce freedom into your maps.
-AGDs: If a map is made for AGD, you can either speedrun (the comparitively easy option) or AGD (which is usually harder) which lets the player gather enough gold to set the difficulty just right.
-Zap Drones: Usually introduce rhythm into otherwise bland maps, which adds to the gameplay.
-Thwumps: Seriously, its hard not to use these well.

And here are my least favorite...

-Enemy spamming: Its not fun, the enemies are squandered, and it just increases the loading time. Puh-lease.
-Chainguns: Very few people use them consistently effectively in their maps.
-Short maps: Whats the charm of a map that only lasts 300 frames?
-Star Trek Doors: Really, you dont need them in most situations. Their only use imo is to make the map easier, its sorta like cutting a corner rather than redesigning the map for a better flow.

> People take a screenshot from an n level (this could be existing or made solely for this purpose)
> They try and get the ninja doing the most interesting thing possible.
> They send the pic here, and after judging, the best pic here wins a ded from the judges.

> The screenshot can be as big as this site permits, or small enough to form an avatar, your choice.
> Spamming one enemy over and over again is not allowed, this shouldnt look anything like a conventional N-art, but look more like an actual playable level.
> Any objects and tiles can be used in the screenshot. However, colour and background mods are not allowed, otherwise people would just post insanely garish images. Any modded objects (ie alarm drones, rotating laser drones are allowed.

> Each N-art must have a title, and most likely youll want to add a caption to add it it. try to make it short and sweet tho, we dont want massive reams of text overshadowing the picture

The N-arts wont be judged for a while, just fill this thread with any (good ) screenshots for now. its not really meant to be a formal contest with deadlines, just an opportunity for you guys to show off your creativity.

Upon finding his way through the forest, the mechanical voice booms down again, telling him he has surpassed the first trial. He then tells you that these trials are necessary for him to prove that you are worthy to wield the MindMachine against your enemies. He has kept the secret in a secret institute a few hundred yards away from the outskirts of the forest. Naturally, you have to find a way to break in.

Naturally, Thattius had to do the noble thing and pressed the self destruct button. The entire Institute started to crumble around them, whilst the MindMachine cursed at itself for having a self destruct button in the first place.
