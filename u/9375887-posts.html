Name: 4 For All
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 02
Difficulty: 6/10
Description: Each Player has 2 mini parts which they must navigate through to hit a switch, and then head back. If all players make it back they can hit their switches which unlock one of the final door gates.



Name: Box 9
Type: 2 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 2P Coop 02
Difficulty: 6/10
Description: 9 different co-op puzzles in 1 map. Make your way through each of the 9 boxes by working together.



Name: 20 Switches
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 04
Difficulty: 7/10
Description: Hit the 20 switches to open your path to the exit. Use the underground bunker to recollect yourselves. Its going to take teamwork to distract the rockets while the others get switches. Due to online lag, this level is more difficult than it should be.



Name: Block Drop
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 03
Difficulty: 6/10
Description: Player 2 and 4 must lure a horizontal thwamp which players 1 and 3 can land on. If they dont land on this block, they go splat (landing on a block prevents splatter). On the way down the top players will lure a vertical falling block which the side players must use to wall jump off to hit a switch which opens doors for the players above. Hurry though because the doors must be opened before the thwamp sees and crushes the top players in the 1 way hallway. Each switch opens the cross players gate. This means all 4 players must be in sync, or close to it.



Name: Block Ridin
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 05
Difficulty: 6/10
Description: 1 Player must walk back and forther through a laser to avoid death. While doing so he must lure thwamps from the ceiling which side players can use to wall jump up to the next level. As players wall jump to the next level, the center player is givin access to a thwamp which he can ride across a pit of mines.



Name: Bulletproof
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 02
Difficulty: 4/10
Description: 1 player spawns right beside the exit, and its open. The problem is there are drones navigating their way around preventing him from reaching it. The drones are gone after 10 seconds. In order for the player to survive for 10 seconds he must have his 3 allies each take a bullet from the gauss turret which is directly above him. If all three players take 1 bullet, and the player below jump ropes the rotating lasers, he will be able to make the exit after the drones are done their rounds



Name: Card Race
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 03
Difficulty: 7/10
Description: 4 sections. Each player must navigate through their section, hit a switch, and make it back alive. To top it off, a push drone is making a 1 way trip around the level and he is required by every player to push them through and back to their sections. Each player navigates around a suit from a deck of playing cards. Each section provides different challenges



Name: Catch Em
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 01
Difficulty: 4/10
Description: Each player runs forward, unlocking a chase drone. The only way to win the level is to trap every chase drone; otherwise they will make their way to your exit and block you. To trap them, you must rely on your ally below to hit his switch at the appropriate time. The last player requires 2 players to help catch both drones after him.



Name: Cliffhanger
Type: Solo
Episode Name: Extreme 01
Difficulty: 11/10
Description: Time your jumps around the mines and navigate from one side to the other. Mines are placed in extremely ridiculous locations, making the jumps nearly impossible. Not for the faint of heart.



Name: Crossover
Type: 4 Player Co-op
Episode Name: 4P Coop 04
Difficulty: 7/10
Description: In the center of the map is a hellish pool of bad guys. Once released from their prison, their is no way to survive in the center. Unfortunately, you MUST release them to advance, as their release switch is on top of the locked gate switch you need. Once released, there is no passing through the center. Each player must crossover diagonally from one another at the exact same moment in order to make sure the switch is pushed, and nobody dies. Camping at spawn will only result in a quick death once the switch is hit by another player. The only way to survive the center is to be in it, and on your way out as its unleashed. Once complete, players must make their way around the level (where terrible lag begins to occur ) and then fly back through the center, hitting the switch and making it into the ext, 1 player must sacrifice themselves here to hit the switch



Name: Cube Zero
Type: Solo
Episode Name: Cube Pack 1
Difficulty: 2/10
Description: Based off the movie Cube (IMDB Link). Traps, fake switches, hidden switches, fake exits and more. This is a puzzle map. Not difficult but might take you a while to figure it out. Its just like the movie, some rooms are safe, and others are death traps.
