I normally just lurk around this place, but I thought I'd stick my oar in here. Having been seriously clinically depressed and having been sectioned into a mental hospital to prevent suicide, I have some knowledge of this.
The suicidal state, for me personally, was nothing to do with feeling "down." It was more of a 'reasoned' argument that the world would be a better place if I was gone. Everyone has dark places in their mind; I was stuck there.
So, big deal, emo kid, yadda yadda.
The problem with my case was that I found no reason for me to live and no benefit to anybody if I did live. The only thing that stopped me was the knowledge that people around me would be destroyed, even though the person they loved was in fact just a fake persona I had set up because the real me was too horrific (not true, but what I thought at the time).
And that, in a nutshell, is the problem with depression - the mind will always, always be able to turn a situation around until it is negative. For example:
"I have friends! Yay!"
"no you don't, they are only pretending to be your friends because they are nice people and don't want to hurt you. In reality they can't stand the sight of you, and can't wait to be away from you."

The brain is cunning when it goes wrong. And that's the point - in real depression the brain HAS gone wrong. There is a physical problem. It's like breaking your leg. My brain does not produce enough serotonin, and this causes it to malfunction. Anybody who doesn't accept this is wrong (Winjet, Decestation).
God will help you? Yeah, sure. Just like God heals a broken leg in a day. Oh, he doesn't do that? Then he doesn't help this either.
