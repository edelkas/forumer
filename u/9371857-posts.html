OK here's what I did. I'll try to go into as much detail as I can. In the small path above the thwump that protects the exit panel use the ramp at the end to give your walljump a boost (just a double jump will do, not a triple). I assume you know what I mean by that. Walljump up the wall three times. On the third one, go to the left, you should jump over the topmost rocket this way. Land on the launchpad in the very middle and go up to the left, above the gold cluster. Use that launchpad to get to the exit. The rest is up to you to figure out. Hope it helps.
