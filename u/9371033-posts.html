#89316 23 2005-08-08T21:36:50Z Chris_Street
I know the author of The Spirit Engine personally, and I also know the guy who created the music for the same game. Needless to say, I did not steal any music from TSE. However, the music in BOTH Zone Runner games isn't original; I borrowed it from www.modarchives.com

I'm glad some of you like my Zone Runner games. The concept came almost by accident. Back in December 2002, I challenged a friend to make a game in a week. A sort of "small competition" if you will. Anyway, my game (Zone Runner 1) was voted the best and got very high scores around the internet. Personally, out of any freeware game I've played, Zone Runner 1 is my favourite. The music does help the addictiveness, and I chose it very carefully.

For those of you who are wondering, the Zone Runner Compilation can now be found at www.circy.cjb.net as the original URL is now no longer around.

Regarding critisism about the graphics, well, it's my personal style. I like double outlined graphics, and I'm fully aware that it's a style that, like Marmite, you'll love or you'll hate. In addition, the engine for both games could be improved. I know that, but then again, for ZR1, I only had a week to make it. Zone Runner 2 took five months, and I'm actually looking into the possibility of a Zone Runner 3.

Currently however, I'm working on the sequel to one of my games released in February 2004; that being Mr Stump's Dentures 2. Again, there's a double outline being used in the concept, but its more of a traditional platform game.

Anyway, I found this forum thread via Google, and I'm happy that my game is being talked about in a positive way. I have no idea if N was inspired by the Zone Runner Compilation; I actually doubt if it was, personally, it seems a bit too different.

If you're having problems with music, you need to extract all the folders from the main zip file and make sure that BASS.DLL is in the same directory as the game itself. Or you could put it in c:/windows/system

If you have any questions or want to know any passwords (for Zone Runner 2, you'll have to search for the INI and edit it) give me an email: circx@hotmail.com

PS: Zone Runner 2 came out May 2003, and The Spirit Engine came out waaay afterwards... so how could I steal any music from it?

Wow this topic still going? I haven't been here for a while I'm feeling generous, so I'll give you the passwords and stuff for both games. Actually, ZR2 doesn't have a password feature, it requires INI modification... but if you fiddle with the INI you'll gain access to all 30 levels

Zone Runner 1 Passwords
PASSWORD for LEVEL 2: 465532
PASSWORD for LEVEL 3: 427651

I have to admit that LEVEL 3 is a bit naff compared to the rest of the game... my competition deadline was running out!


Zone Runner 2 INI Modification:
(You might want to make a back-up of this in case you muck anything up)

1. Find zone2.ini in c:/windows. Either search for it using the search feature or manually find it.

2. Open the INI up with Notepad

3. Delete everything inside, and replace with the following:

[]
ZONE1=8342
ZONE2=5907
ZONE3=2061

4. Save the INI, and hey presto! You have access to all the levels and hidden secrets

Have fun
