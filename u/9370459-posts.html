Something I discovered while playing on cubish. (37-3?)

If a seeker zap drone starts tracking you, and then you close a trap door, it will move directly through the closed trap door. It will not pick a player up through the trapped door (I don't think, I didn't test it though), nor will it move through the door if it is simply doing its route.

Basically, if it starts tracking you, and you close the door, it won't do anything.
