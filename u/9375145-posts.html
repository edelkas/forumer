I was playing Zwok for a while under a variety of names Speccyteccy, wingo and i owzn ur base mostly, but found it too easy with games not lasting very long, so started to play as a lazy without logging in.

Advantages:

Nobody knows who you are, and also you get a new number each game.
Youre shorter than an expert can make a big difference!
Lack of powerful weapons seriously improves your accuracy and tactics
Game last longer
If everyone did this (and thats what Im advocating!) then theres less chance of getting wiped by a lucky shot turning it into more of a game of skill

Disadvantages:

No bouncer, cluster, brick or spannerz
You dont know which side youre on until the game starts when Im logged in, I keep my finger on the forward button so I hit the ground running.

Bots

Just because the chat control is there doesnt mean there are no bots. Theyre easy to spot though:

They all have ordinary names mostly first names, but some obvious nicknames too.
They always throw
They never leave
They never run far
Theyre rubbish and have a tendency for friendly fire!

Leaving the game

If anything screws up with the game - i.e. you start your turn before the trajectory paths have cleared or you remain in a game even when youre dead, then just quit the game and start again.

Id also suggest leaving the game at the first sign of any deliberate friendly fire (bots excepted).

Tactics

The most important thing as a team is to spread out so if your team is numbered 1 to 3 starting from the edge of the screen, then 1 generally should move right to the edge, 2 then runs a little way forward and 3 runs as far as possible.

Keep moving! Ive played so many games where I run far every turn but the opponents still aim at where I was.

I believe the chant watch and learn should be taken very literally every shot should be a lesson was it too high, too low?

Watch your opponents people generally tend to play predictably i.e. if they charge theyll probably do the same next turn, likewise if they throw a particular weapon.

Retreat charging is a great tactic, but its also important to know when to retreat. I love maybe charging twice so by the third the opponents think Im about to make a mad dash into their territory so I dont. If, however, its just you left, then go for it if you can get between or past your opponents, you can often get them to kill themselves very satisfying!

Safe Area most levels have areas particularly the brow of a hill that should be safe from their own team so just because you start out at the front doesnt mean one of your team mates wont overtake you, so try and avoid low shots that might hurt your team mates.

Also, if one of your team mates is charging, try not to throw short into their path!

If two people are charging and the one at home dies, then number 2 really should retreat theres little advantage in two players entering enemy territory!

Similarly, when using certain weapons bouncers and clusters in particular choose your shots carefully to avoid any nasty ricochets! Sometimes its safer to go for the percentage shot throw a roller.

Bloc Media

I paid a visit to these guys to discuss a project Im involved in we met the guy who owns the company and I told him about my addiction to Zwok. I think he was quite surprised to find a 37 year old playing a game aimed at kids. I told him no to underestimate the game I think its one of the best computer games ever combing several skills:

Split second timing to get the most steps in and still have time to shoot

Accuracy I reckon I could hit anyone (providing they dont move!) with a full(ish) screen length flat throw at least 50% of the time. Lobs are harder, but improving.

Pattern matching to be able to (almost subconsciously) take in all the movements of both your team and opponents and to use that information to predict what will happen next and to then make your move accordingly.

Psychology trying to figure out your opponents ability in order to predict whether to bluff, double bluff, triple bluff, etc., etc.!

When the game came out, I automatically assumed it would be a precursor to a Playstation game, so I asked him. He just smiled and said that he couldnt say anything. Im guessing the ability to choose your team mates would be a great addition.

Its also one of the funniest games ever theres not much that genuinely makes me lol, but Zwok does. And its here that I have to thank you lot Ive had some cracking games with most of you and many, many laughs and I look forward to many, many more.
