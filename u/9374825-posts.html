In this RP you play as a pirate. You either comand a ship, or be commanded by someone else. There is no real story, you kinda just make it up as you go along.

Battle System:
If it's you and an NPC, then you attack, the enemy attacks. You attack, the enemy attacks. If it's two-on-one, then the two ships on the same team attack once each, then the ship by themselves attacks twice. If it's three-on-one, then it's just the same, except the three on the same team attack once each, and the ship by themselves attack thrice.

Okay, now I'll explain...

Type:
The type is the type of ship your comand, of course. It can be either light, medium or heavy. Light goes faster, has less HP and does less damage. High goes slower, has more HP and does heavy damage!

Level:
This is the level of your ship. View the EXP Table to see how much EXP you need to gain a level. You start at level 0.

EXP:
This is obvious, isn't it?

Damage:
The damage your ship does. The higher your type, the more damage but the slower your go.

Hull&Sail HP:
This is the HP of your Hull and Sails. When your Hull is 0 then you are defeated. Your ship blows up. The lower your sails are, the lower your speed. You don't exactly calculate your speed, but just make sure you go slower than usual when your sails are low. And the lowre your speed, the higher chance the enemy has of hitting your ship.

Skills:
These are your ships Skills/Abilities. Call them whatever you like, they do the same thing. Your skills have a cool down. A cool down is the amount of your posts you have to wait before you can use that skill again. Don't make them too high, but don't make them too low.
