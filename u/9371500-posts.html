for coffeefreak (page 7) and other people stuck on 88.4, as i see its a popular topic:

just found out about the n forums, if you havent beaten it yet...heres a few tips on 88.4:
fall down the right corridor and land on the angle right under the up-facing thwump for
riding the thwump at the bottom, it helps a lot and saves loads of time if you dont have
much left from the jumping puzzle. IMO, if youve made it to 88.4, then you know a bit
about precision jumping (dont worry, this jump is easy for an experienced player), so
you will not need the button to the left. wall jumping off the top thwump's back after it
goes to the left is the easiest way to get thru that section (I found that waiting, and
jumping over it again makes the next part a tad easier). Lure the next thwump up and
jump down, hit the left facing angle and give the jump key a light tap, when you land
jump up again asap to avoid the thwump. now jump along the small angled bits until you
hit the ledge. Sorry-but thats as far as i can take you at the moment...im stuck there
but i did find checking the lowest posted high score to be VERY helpful.
good luck!
