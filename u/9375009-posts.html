The Walls

The walls are closing in on me,
I have missed my chance to escape,
They move towards me slowly,
Along the ground they scrape.

I'd seen them kill others before,
I never thought that they'd get me,
Soon I won't live anymore
At least I will be free

I thought that I was much stronger,
I thought that I would live and thrive
I thought that I'd last longer,
I thought that I'd survive.

I am divided by the walls,
From all the others in the room,
Although they can't see them crawl,
Slowly towards my doom.

The walls are so close to me now,
I can not see anyone else,
They try to help, making vows,
To me their words are false.

If it was true I could get out,
I would have done it long ago,
I tried forever, I don't doubt,
I know that I'm trapped though.

The ice doesn't make the walls stop,
It makes me become blind and deaf,
Ice caused me to start the drop,
and ice will end it with death.

I can feel the walls coming,
They will break me into debris,
I am going to get high one last time,
So I won't feel them crush me.

The walls are closing in on me,
I've gone blind and deaf to escape,
I can't see them move so slowly,
I can't hear them as they scrape.
