Click the "~" like he said, at the top left corner of your keyboard, under the F1-12's. After that it should be pretty easy, it tells you what to click, make your lvl and then click your home key. Click the "\" button, or the "~" button, and you'll be back at the debug menu. Copy the numbers and symbols from the upper text box, and save them to a document. Whenever you want to play that lvl, copy the data, and paste it in the upper text box, and click "L". When it finishes loading click "P".
