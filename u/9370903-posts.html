Game Name: Sepp-U-Ku (The samurai art of ritual suicide)

- Several game modes including
-Online play for each of these modes except for single player
-Race: Up to 4 players race in maps designed specifically for racing
-Coop: Up to 4 Players work together useing there skills and weapons to get through levels designed specifically for coop.
-Single Player: Play through the game alone
-Deathmatch: Up to 8 samuraiand ninjas fight in an all out deathmatch
-Team Deathmatch: Two teams of 4 fight to the death. ( Ninjas vs Samurai)
Gameplay:

-Coop and Single Player(GamePlay): At the begining of the game you pick your class between Samurai and Ninja,. Then
you pick one weapon (3 per class. One brute force weapon, One transportation weapon,
and One Distance weapon ). And you pick one basic skill to Develope. At the end of each
map if your score is high enough (more on scoring later) you can pick to develope a skill to
upgrade or save your points. .
-Classes (Skills and Weapons) NOTE: WHILE WEAPONS WILL GIVE YOU AN ADVANTAGE THEY WILL NOT BE REQUIRED TO BEAT ANY OF THE LEVELS.
-Samurai: Srong Brutal warrior. He is slower than the ninja but more powerfull
-Weapons:
- Katana: A long sword that is very lethal
-Can be used to stab and/or slice enamies.
-Can be used to stick into the wall while sliding to slow down and enventually
stop yourself
-Can be used to deflect bullets lightsaber style

-Kyogetsu-shoge: A hook type weapon attached to a long chain.
-Can be used to attach to walls and swing
-Can be used to throw down to teammates and swing them up to a higher
location
-Kozuka Blade: Small knives used as a throwing weapon
-Can be used to pin enamies agaisnt a wall.
-Can be used to deflect ai by spinning them in the alternate direction
-Can be used to hit switches if hit just right.

-Skills:
Still devloping but will be class specific and not give you to much of an advantage.

-Ninja- Quick, swift and silent, he is the stealthier of the two but he can't take a lickin like the Samurai.
-Weapons:
-Naginata: This Japanese halberd was often used by sohei (priest soldiers). Yet still an excellent weapon for the ninja
-Can be used to stab and/or slice enamies.
- Can be stuck into the wall and then can be stood on or can swing from it.
-Can be stuck into the ground to give extra lift when jumping

-Nekote: These small devises were worn around the ends of the fingers.
- Can be used to climb onto any surface.
- Can be used to attach to an enemy unnoticed and get a free ride

-Shuriken: Ninja shuriken were hand released blades thrown at the enemy for distraction purposes.
-Can be used to distract the enemy
-Can be used to hit switches if aimed correctly
-Can be used to to make ladders into the wall.

-Skills:
Still devloping but will be class specific and not give you to much of an advantage.

- Race, Dm and Team Dm (Gameplay): These modes will feature all of the above elements but also include the ability to kill other players around you with your
weapons in creative ways
