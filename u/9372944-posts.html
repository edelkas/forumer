There is still racism. There will always be racism. Everyone is at least slightly racist. You don't have to be an extremist "don't get on my bus" idiot to be a racist. Racism today is, however, a result of some slightly different processes. I'm going to use racism in the U.S. (since I'm an ignorant American ) as an example. When slavery and segregation still existed in the U.S., they were a result of white supremacism and the readily available slave forces of Africa. The African race was introduced into the American culture because people thought whites were better than blacks. The civil-rights-era concept of racism and segregation is long gone now. There are almost no loud and opinionated racists anymore. But racism still exists, because of the perceptions of the populace. People sometimes think poorly of blacks because they see many undesirable blacks, be they homeless or criminal. That doesn't mean that all criminals and homeless are black, it's just the way it looks sometimes. It's the same thing with how all Jews are seen to be rich, because many rich people are Jews. It's not outright racism due to a simple concept of skin color, it's internal racism based on societal interactions.

One would hope that most people would look past internal race and prejudice when making such decisions. Unfortunately, it doesn't work like that for everyone. Some people will think, "Oh, Obama is black, he probably comes from a dysfunctional family and will screw up the country. I'm not voting for him." Others would think, "Oh, he's the same race as me, and since I know I'm a good person, he must be too." It seems to be less of a problem with younger generations though. In the end, however, racism still exists, and anyone who would say it doesn't is being too optimistic.
