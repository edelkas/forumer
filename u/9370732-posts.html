#68410 127 2005-05-22T03:57:23Z great_sea
96-4 can be annoying. Really its not too hard, you just have to stay _focused_ the entire time you play. The whole way through, make sure to pay attention and see what path the drones are taking. It starts out with three surface-follower drones and one that just goes clockwise(or counter-clockwise). Unlocking the right side brings into play three more surface followers and one more clockwise drone.

This is the way I do the level:

From the start, there is one surface-following drone heading to you on the floor level. Immediately get the switch to your left (obviously), then jump over the first hump onto the leftward facing slope. This first jump is tricky, but I find that it will make things easier later on. You will need to jump over the zap drone just before/while he heads down into that little pit. If you do it fast enough, it is very easy to get up to the top level (just jump against the [1] slope against the wall and glide past the two drones on the opposite wall coming down). Now jump over to the right and you will end up behind a surface-follower drone that is now moving to the top. Get the switch when he gets out of the way, and then simply follow behind him as he goes along. I prefer to stop and wait on the right side of the mine on top of the final hump, and simply jump over the first drone heading toward me. While in the air, move over to the left against the wall (so that you are falling toward the drone that you have been following.) Fall just until you have room to wall jump over to the right side _under_ another drone that was just started moving up the wall. Then shift mid-air back to the left top avoid another drone that starting coming up the left wall. From here simply follow that one drone over to the switch, and then follow him out of there. The first two drones heading toward you will be easy to avoid. Just wait a second before heading up top and they will be right next to eachother, and you can simply jump over them. There may or may not be one more drone coming down the left wall at this point, depending on when you hit the switch, but if he is coming down simply wall jump out from under him over to the right side and then back again as you head up the wall.

Once again, follow your original drone over to the right. Now the other side is unlocked, so right before you go through the little passage-way to the right side, you will need to avoid a surface follower drone on the floor (easiest way, once again, is to sit next to a mine on top of a hump and jump straight up over him). From here you just need to make it to the finish. The easiest way is to take your time, carefully noting which threatening drones are surface-followers and which are not, so that you know how to avoid them before they get to you. I find it is easiest to simply stand on the next to the mine on the right-most hump, and avoid a couple drones, and then jump down as soon as there is an opening.

I find that the easiest way to avoid the top surface follower drones is to simply stand still and let them pass. The best way to avoid clockwise/counterclockwise drones is to jump over them from the top of a hump. The best way to avoid the bottom surface-follower drones is to either jump on a slope before they get to the pit and then jump from the slope over them to the next slope (the next slope that faces the same way as the original one, that is), or to stand next to a mine on top of a hump and jump over it as it passes. You will have to really consider how you want to pass each drone, based on how much room you have and what other drones are heading your way that you will need to avoid.

Sorry for not going into detail on the last stretch, but things really can change depending on the timing of your getting the switches, etc. Just take your time when you need to, but don't be afraid to make a couple risky jumps from slope to slope, over drones, etc. I hope this helps! 96-4 was one of the harder levels in the 90's, but I know you can do it if you just keep on pressin' on.
