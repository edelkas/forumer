So probably none of you know of my site, Xbox Evolved http://www.xboxevolved.com but I just recently conduted an interview with Raigan Burns and Mare Sheppard from Metanet Software, and Nick Waanders of Slick Entertainment. You can go to the front page and find the interview by clicking on teh image or the link on the right of the site.

I conducted the interview, because being that I get to do all kinds of super slick stuff like play games early, I happened to come across N+, and well while I can't say much about it atm, it is a really fantastic game so far. I've been working on my own levels (which suck) and I'm about half way through the entire game so far. I happen to come across some of the levels that were selected in the SUBLiME contest and they are great. Not only do they look and play much better than they do on the PC, but they recreated them pretty much exact on XBLA.

Specifically, I played Eeyore's "In Soviet Russia, N plays you" which is very tricky, thanks to the bombs, but nice nontheless, jph w.'s Grilled Eel Guts, which has a very appropiate name I think for the way the level looks, and of course Triple T's "Press Start"! Very original.

Oh there is ONE MORE level that I can think of..Anothny A I hate you. Ninja in the City is perfectly executed, with how the gold and pads are set up, but not only that, on the very bottom I kept having problems. Screw those laser drones. I eventually got through the level after like 20 tries, but congrats once again to you guys, and hopefully we can all share levels together on Live later this year!

The interview: http://xboxevolved.e-mpire.com/article/N+/4198.html
