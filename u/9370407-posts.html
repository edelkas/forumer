#22656 6 2004-04-28T13:31:04Z Khalad
Calling it a bug implies it being harmful some how. I think it's a pretty neat consequence of the simple physics model used in the game. There are other ways to get higher jumps than normal, too, though this is the most extreme I've seen. If you jump while going up an incline, you'll get an extra boost. And if you time it right and jump at the beginning of a ramp up, you'll jump even higher still.

I figure it's cause of how everything's modeled by forces. If you already have an upward component to your velocity vector, it's natural that an upward force will accelerate you even faster upwards. Going up an incline moves you upward, so if you jump quickly before you slow down too much, you get a higher-than-normal jump. If you just hit a jump pad, you're moving up even faster, so wall-jumping will shoot you up like a gun.

At least, that's my explanation based on what Mare and Raigan have said about the physics model in N. I could be wrong.
