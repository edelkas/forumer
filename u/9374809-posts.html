What I'm wondering is where do I find the adapters for the controllers. I've been wanting to play N with a controller to.

There are 2 types of console controllers that can be easily used with a PC.


The first is an Xbox 360 wired controller; these have USB plugs on the end, and MOST current Windows or Vista machines will instantly recognize them if they're plugged into a USB port on your PC. Even older machines (like Celerons) will do it.


The 2nd is a PS2 controller. RadioShack sells an adaptor you can use to plug your PS2 controller into your PC.... I *think* via a USB port. Im told it's about $12-15 in price.

You can try looking it up online, but their site is a bit screwy for that; I had to have someone actually SHOW me where it was before I saw it there.

Either option is great for PC gaming.... better than PC-specific controllers, usually.


A 360 controller is pricey, though, be aware of that.



And dont forget, N by itself does not actually recognize any controller; you need a profiler program like those mentioned above to actually map the keys to the buttons.
