Upon falling back into reality, Thattius finds that he is still in the cell in which he began his epic journey. Thattius desperately searches around his cell to find the ventilation shaft he thought he had used. After quite a long time of searching, there is no opening to be found. Instead, Thattius found a letter posted where the vent was supposed to be. Curious, he reads the letter:

"Congratulations on surviving in the Mindmachine. In case your insightful mind deceives you, you have already passed the mental preparation. You have proven yourself worthy of our elite group. Now remember carefully as I describe your way out. There should be two metal and two plastic poles holding your bed. Take the two metal poles and stick it where you found this note. They should be strong enough to dig a hole to the outside. We shall be waiting.
-The Order of the Elite"

As Thattius grabs the poles to make his getaway, two guards with electrically charged truncheons spot him and begin to chase Thattius. Luckily enough, the corrupt government feeds the two guards too much and they are much too big to completely chase Thattius. When the daybreak hits Thattius, the guard towers begin their attack. Dodging bullets, and long-distance laser rays, they attempt to kill the runaway "criminal". Thattius uses the barricades and anything he finds to shield himself from the oncoming danger. Disappointed by not finding anybody waiting for him, Thattius runs off into the nearby woods for shelter. Once in the calm of the forest, Thattius finds several rockets coming his way. One look straight and Thattius finds booby traps set all around. Quickly running here and there, He safely avoids all of the rockets. Then, the rockets begin to fire again. Thattius seeks shelter from the raining fire and spots a small alcove in the midst of a tree trunk. He dashes to the safe spot and awaits what will happen next.

As the explosions and ammunitions stop, Thattius looks out from his shelter. Surprised, he finds three men in cloaks waiting for him, with rocket launchers in their hands. "What the fuck was that for?!!??!?" Thattius blurted out. Still silent, the three cloaked men seemingly float to the other side of the forest, with Thattius running to keep up. Along the path that the cloaked men flow through, there are traps set in the trees, probably for catching animals of some sort. Still, they are quite dangerous and Thattius tries his best to avoid them. At the edge of the forest, they turn around and face Thattius. "Who are you?" Thattius shouted, still huffing and puffing. One man steps forward and gold letters appear on his hood.

"Carnax..." Thattius read. Carnax responded "Yes...You have passed the first part of the physical preparation. Now, you must test your mettle in a real situation." "So me running out of a prison, dodging your rockets, and following you through a booby trapped forest wasn't a real situation?!!?" Thattuis angrily answered. The two men with Carnax disappeared into thin air, while Carnax remained. "I am part of the Order of the Elite, here to guide newcomers on our ways. We all share a common goal. Yet, only the best can join. May you prove your abilities now." Suddenly, the forest was wavering, but Thattius and Carnax were still. The world was morphing around them. Soon enough, they were in the midst of a cavern. As Thattius began to refocus his attention to Carnax, he noticed Carnax was multiplying and changing into some type of electrified machine. "Find the exit, before I can catch you." was said in unison by the images of Carnax. Without thinking, Thattius ran from end to end of the cavern searching for a key that opened the golden door beneath him.

After running through the seeming endless cavern always with Carnax on his tail, Thattius finds a blue switch marked exit. Relieved, he hits it and heads back to where he began. Without a second thought, Thattius jumps down into the door in the ground. Again, a certain tingle occurs in Thattius's body. The world begins to morph around them again, except they are at the edge of the forest, exactly where they were before, at the edge of the forest. Congratulations... a distant voice of Carnax boomed. Then, as if in a mirage, another cloaked man appeared. The gold letters Supreme Annihilator appeared on the hood. You have passed the evasion test, Thattius. Supreme Annihilator spoke. Now you must pass the survival test. As with Carnax, Supreme Annihilator began to change form and multiply. However, one thing was different. Supreme Annihilator's images each took on a different form and the world around them did not change. Test your mettle in the sky. Supreme Annihilator said as Thattius felt as if he were flying. One look down and both of them were above the ground. Live!! No mercy! echoed the many voices of Supreme Annihilator.

Congratulations, Thattius... The voice called out as Thattius finished collecting the last bit of gold and flew towards the safe zone. Soon enough, as Thattius hit the safe zone, he was teleported back to solid ground. Unsurprised, Thattius stared blankly into the emptiness before him as the last cloaked man materialized before him. Rage..... the man said. I shall now test your mobility. Thattius and Rage manifested in a narrow cliff. RUN!! Rage shouted as he morphed into an evil divine being. Thattius scaled the mountain as quickly as he could. Rage thundered and fired at Thattius. Luckily, Thattius made it back down safely as well. I'm impressed, said Rage, it has been a while since we have had such a promising candidate. Carnax and Supreme Annihilator appeared next to Rage. The three said in unison, Welcome to The Order of the Elite.
