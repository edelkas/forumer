I was at my friends place recently, and we were up most of the night, making random N levels. And then I had to go to the toilet. Anyway, long story short, when I got back from the toilet, I sat back down, and I noticed my friend was playing N upside down.

It turns out, he had gotten bored, and felt like stuffing around. He found the option to turn the screen up-side down in the graphics driver settings for rotating the screen. It was really funny to watch, and is really confusing, because left moves right, and right, left. I had a go myself, but I wasn't too good at it.

By the end of the night, he had gotten to about level 5-0 or somewhere close to that, so it is possible.

I was thinking, maybe for the people that don't have computers that are too hot, that N should come with the option of playing Upside-down ? Or maybe as an unlockable mode. Or even, when you beat a level, you can then play it upside down?

I just thought I'd post this, because it was really fun, and others might also enjoy it. If you have a good enough computer, I would suggest you try it out. You might have fun .

Sorry if this has been posted before. I didn't see anything about it.

Ok. As I was playing through the story mode, I got to a level where my laggy jumping made it almost impossible. When I say laggy jumping, I mean, when I jump in the air, the game would stop for less than a second, and then continue. But in that time, N would move a LOT.

A cilantro later, I was listening to iTunes, and I decided to play N again. I know normally, you would suggest that CLOSING all the other programs will help, I completely ignored this suggestion . I played N, and it worked flawlessly. I was stumped at the time. But I decided to close all the running programs, and see if it was still fine. As soon as I closed iTunes, LAG!!!. So now, whenever I want to play N, I just open iTunes, play a song, any song, and leave it playing. Now, the only lag I get, is when the song changes ( the song will stop playing ).

If you have this same problem, get iTunes, and try it out. You have to actually PLAY a song. Not just have it running. This may or may not work for everyone, and you may not need iTunes. I might try it with some other music players.

Good luck .
