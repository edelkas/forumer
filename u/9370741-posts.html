Yeah, Soldat rox! I play it now and then, but i'm actually a Quake 3 player. I like Soldat's physics though and the other parts of the gameplay too. I just discovered N and it reminded me of Soldat.

I'd like to be able to use my mouse to determine in which direction my ninja is facing, just like in Soldat. I'm used to using my left hand for strafing/steering/jumping and my right hand for aiming (FPS style), which is why i like the controls in Soldat.

Quake 3 has a mod(ification) called Defrag that's about pure skill in moving/jumping/flying and using weapons to make jumps and climb walls. There are tons of Q3 maps that are dedicated to these trick jumps, in freestyle and also many defrag runs (like the time trial in N). I consider Q3 to have the best physics of any FPS game ever made, but this also sparked my interest in games like Soldat and N that have very nice physics.
