Ahem. Just what the topic says. I had this random crazy idea while I was playing one of the really annoying N levels with about 730 mines and as many drones: zero gravity. I told you it was a crazy idea.

The basics are the screen stops being stationary and rotates to accommodate (sp?) the fact that the Ninja is no longer stuck on what used to be the floor, and there is, of course, no gravity. Drones are not affected by this, continuing to go around in their same old loops.
I don't know how this would be attained, as in how you'd get it (unlockable feature? map-specific little checkbox in Ned? a new object that you have to touch to toggle gravity on or off in that level?), but the gravity toggle switch seems the most reasonable.

How this changes gameplay is that now the Ninja has to push himself off of walls--he can walk on surfaces, but not for long because by running he effectively pushes himself off anyway. (Running is jumping at an angle so gravity keeps you on the ground.) This comes with an obvious downside, because if you get stuck out in the open you're screwed and have to either wait for a drone or missile to come along, or kill yourself. How much lag this would add to the game I don't know, but somebody just gimme some feedback on how this sounds.

Good? Bad? Utterly retarded?
