On the other hand

After a night of heavy smoking, the worst you'll get is a a little bit of a depressing feeling (This has to do with your endorphins and how you're low on them since you unleashed a lot of endorphins the night before by laughing your ass off half the time). But once you start to wake up you'll start to piece together what exactly happened and in what order. You can't remember because the THC is still in your system attached to your brain.
