a quick explanation of vectors for morons/n00bs/whatever

flash animations are (in general, see NB) created in vectors. this means that they can be resized with no loss of quality. therefore, it would not effect Ns playability (or make it "lo-res") to add a fullscreen button/control/whatever

NB: bitmaps can be imported into flash, but if they are vectors are not applied (because its a bitmap. its made out of pixels, not vectors).

hope that helps.
yes please for FS

Yeah, basically vector defines an image as shapes, not pixel colors and placement. You can draw a circle with radius 3 inside of a circle of radius 7. The output will be the same quality no matter how large it's rendered. This is why company logos are designed in vector. You need a FedEx or Nike letterhead that is an inch wide, but you also need a good quality logo 10 feet wide on the side of your trucks.
