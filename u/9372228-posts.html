Like Klablizzy said, start of experimenting with maps. I ussually start a map with some idea in my head of what the map will be like, but sometimes I might need a little Inspiriation...I look around top-rated for this.

Now, when making the map, first, I will try and make a cool looking original tileset with lots of free space and posibilities. Sometimes sinple tilesets are the best, but sometimes people want something in-depth and complex to look at. Next place objects in nice looking places which suit the map. Don't worry to much about fancyness In the way the gold is placed, but what I do suggest, is that you put It just a little way out of the common path. This way, people will want to show of thier skills and all-gold it. Usually determination to get an all-gold makes people play the level obsessivley. Then when they have the all-gold they will be satisfied, and realise how addicted they were to the level. This makes them rate high. When placing enemys, place them in sensible spots, for instance, don't place a rocket where it is just going to explode straight away. Playtest it through, and find a good place where the rocket is a threat. Same for all other enemys.

So basically, just fiddle around with N-ed until you get something you like, and I am sure you will get further support, advice and constructive critisism on NUMA.
