# Metanet at Forumer

This repo contains an archive of the original golden-era Metanet forums at Forumer, active between 2004 and 2008. Generated with ad-hoc tool [tapascrape](https://github.com/edelkas/tapascrape).

## History

The [original Metanet forums](https://web.archive.org/web/20080224224438/http://metanet.2.forumer.com/) were created by Keron Cyst in [April 2004](https://web.archive.org/web/20040430180647/http://www.harveycartel.org/metanet/news.html), and were active during most of the peak of the popularity of N.

In September 2008, the community migrated to self-hosted boards at [The Real N](https://web.archive.org/web/20080924171509/http://forum.therealn.com/), starting from scratch since Forumer didn't provide any way to migrate the data or export the chats. This meant leaving everything behind.

After a few further domain changes (old guards will remember Eastern Viking) the community landed on [NinjaRobotYeti](https://web.archive.org/web/20110128091247/http://ninjarobotyeti.com/) in 2010 (see the forums [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110714184311/http://forum.ninjarobotyeti.com/)). In 2013 the boards were relocated again to their current destination, at [Droni.es](https://droni.es/).

In the meantime, the old forums remained safely archived at **http://metanet.2.forumer.com**. However, Forumer was acquired by [CrowdGather](https://techcrunch.com/2011/04/15/forum-network-crowdgather-acquires-free-forum-host-forumer/) in 2011, who also acquired [Yuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuku), another message board platform, the same year, eventually merging their platforms.

Everything remained calm until 2013, when the old boards suddenly disappeared. They had been nuked by the new staff because the forums had been infringing their TOS for 5 years, since the boards had moved and the front page was instead linking users to the new domain. I personally contacted the new Yuku staff in hopes of recovering them before they were lost and put them in touch with Keron, and they were kind enough to go through and [transition the boards](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/metanetfr/we-re-back-t24455.html) to the new Yuku domain.

Later, in 2016, the Yuku division was acquired by [Tapatalk](https://www.tapatalk.com/), this time through the mediation of [Kablizzy](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/metanetfr/here-i-sit-once-again-t24462.html). As of today, the boards remain [archived](https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/metanetfr/) at Tapatalk, and in fact they remain open. However, there's no telling how long this will last until they are gone for good, which prompted this archival project.

## Archival

Unfortunately, each of the acquisitions (Forumer -> Yuku -> Tapatalk) involved internal migrations on which data was lost, notably every single **attachment** and most **images**, as well as Forumer-specific features that weren't supported by the generic Tapatalk platform. Since the data hadn't been ported over to the community-hosted boards either, a lot of that is gone for good. Another disadvantage of the new boards is that the themes were lost and the styling is terrible.

The present archive was generated by scraping the current Tapatalk boards via their [mobiquo API](https://www.tapatalk.com/api/api_section.php?id=4), used by their mobile app. This provides most information in their database efficiently, and even allows to recover the raw [BBCode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBCode) source of the posts, instead of the broken HTML presently rendered by the Tapatalk boards.

However, the API doesn't contain everything in the boards, so this was enriched by also scraping the HTML boards themselves. This provides user signatures and titles ("ranks"), group names, avatars, and even some posts the API fails to recover.

The archive was then further complemented by scraping every single **WaybackMachine** snapshot of the old forums, around **52k** of them. You can download this data [here](<https://mega.nz/file/AVw1FaYa#R3pKvB7pwbEcVBXZ6cl8vkaeMESbONst6TtHLN49qOE>), a 7-zipped 21MB file that unpacks to 3.3GB. This provided plenty of additional user profile information not present in Tapatalk, the complete user list, topic descriptions, most emoticons and many avatars, new topics and posts, and even some attachments that had been archived.

The posts source was then post-processed / cleaned in order to eliminate errors caused from the data migrations, such as corrupt BB tags. The same process was done to user signatures. Finally, external resources, mostly images (avatars, smileys, embedded images in signatures and posts, etc) were also archived systematically, some from their original hosts, and most recovered from the WaybackMachine (from dead hosts such as ImageShack, Tinypic, etc).

All the information was combined and linked in a queryable SQLite database which you can also download [here](https://mega.nz/file/Nc5SgQ4Y#0ufDbf-EeBU_C9oT7Gj8jQ1Mvj8DkVGWbzX1whC5zjo).

## The site

The static site was generated from the [SQL database](https://mega.nz/file/Nc5SgQ4Y#0ufDbf-EeBU_C9oT7Gj8jQ1Mvj8DkVGWbzX1whC5zjo) mentioned above. It provides a clean, compact way to browse the forums. It combines the Tapatalk information with everything recovered from the WaybackMachine. Each forum and topic is rendered in a single HTML page, ditto for users' post lists.

Internal links and old **Forumer links** were all remapped so clicking on them browses the forums correctly. Links to the old [numa.notdot.net](https://web.archive.org/web/20050830061418/http://numa.notdot.net/) domain were remapped to working [nmaps.net](https://www.nmaps.net/) links as well. Post **quotes** were backtracked whenever possible so they too are clickable.
