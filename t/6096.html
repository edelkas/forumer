#120919 3 2005-12-06T18:59:28Z Aries_Griff
Trib4l, your ned version of 18-2 didn't work for me. So for 71-4, you just need to grab the switch which isn't hard to get to. Then you need to jump into the little piece of vertical wall and walljump off of it quickly before you wallslide on the wall. Then jump onto the ledge on the upper right, and wall jump onto the bounceblocks and across, avoiding drones and the rocket as you go. It's not too hard, just practice. 18-2 is tough, but I think the way I do it is easiest. When you first fall, jump over to get on top of the thwump that moves when you first start when it starts moving back to the right slowly. You may need to start walking to stay on top of it. The thwump above the first should trigger when you jump onto the first one. So when the second thwump comes back slowly, then hop (don't do a full jump, you'll get killed by the third thwump) onto that second thwump. Then do a full jump onto the third thwump as it comes back to trigger the fourth thwump. Then repeat the full jumps to trigger each thwump in order and land on them working your way up. Once you reach the second thwump from the top, you can jump out of the pit. You can jump all the way across either pit once you're at the top, but I recommend just going for the switch and the door. It's tricky, but with enough practice, all it requires is knowing when to jump and when to tap left or right to stay on the thwumps.
