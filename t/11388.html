I've been thinking about this competition idea for a while now...

Rules-
1. Must be a dda (can be hold left, right, or nothing)
1. Map must be in a 10x10 or smaller area (if the ninja goes anywhere outside that 10x10 area the map does not count)
2. You can use as many objects and tiles within the 10x10 area as you want
3. You can edit anything in the level data manually
4. Can include glitches (this isnt one of those things where if it's not used in metanet it cant be used here)
5. Cannot be an infinite dda
5. Um yea thats about it

Ideal map would have-
1. As many close calls as possible
2. Different types of propulsion
3. More than just one rocket (and also not just two or more rockets in the same spot)
4. Gold delay wont effect your score much... but it will be used as a tie-breaker if needed (less gold is better)
5. Oh and cheating death is always good... might add a bonus point or two

Scoring-

Length: X/10
Creativity: X/10

NOTE: Total score will be X/50

You suggest, I'll listen and agree or disagree
