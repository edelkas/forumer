Messageboard Games Guidelines:[/u]
  • Try not to post in more than 4 or 5 games at once. This is because then the "Last Posts" will be plastered with your name all over it, and you may shove popular games of the front page.
  • Don't create stupid games or games that have already been made. It's annoying, and they will be closed anyway.
  • Don't be stupid and corrupt good games with rape, ponies, or anything else that's inapporiate or idiotic. Some of my favorite games were closed because of this. And it's just stupid anyway.
  • Don't create games that faciliate spamming, and don't post extremely long or multiple posts. For obvious reasons.
  • Try to stay on the low with the cursing. If you want to curse, go into B&T.
  • And finally, don't flame or personally attack a member. Light jokes are fine, but doing this is wrong in all of the forums, and will result in a warn, suspension, or in extreme cases, a ban.

Rules and regulations for starting a new MG thread (thanks kisler):
  • First, look among the threads that are on the first two pages to see if a similar idea has been done before and the game is still going. If it is, don't start the same thing again.
  • Search the planned title of your thread. An older thread may have been done before. If search results come up, check to see if the thread is locked. It was locked for a reason, so you shouldn't try your idea.
  • Then, analyze your idea. If your game facilitates spamming (as in multiple or useless posts) it would be wise not to create the game, as it will most likely be closed.
  • If your game passed the above criteria, go ahead and create your game.
