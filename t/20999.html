#397501 2 2008-01-06T03:06:07Z Skyline
It was a dark afternoon, and the deep amber grain that stretched beyond was not lit up in glory as it usually was at this time. The clouds seemed to percolate and swirl around in the sky, as if planning something and preparing. Curious, I stepped into the outdoors and took a look around. And while I was heading down the dirt road a little ways, a sudden gust blew me off my feet. I fell back, stumbled back up, and turned my head toward the coming storm. My clothes were dusted in a fine layer of dirt, and I was dazed. Suddenly, another torrent of air, this time with downpours of rain. Then, in the distance, a sound of a train, roaring across the plains. As I turned toward the train tracks, I spotted no upcoming train. A tree branch flew through the chaos and nearly struck me down where I stood. The wind intensified, and with it, the screaming wind nearly deafened me. In a burst of adrenaline, I sprinted through the turmoil, barely seeing, until I reached my front door. The basement was completely dark, and I huddled next to the cold concrete as the tornado ripped through my beloved home. I fell into unconciousness.
I awoke on a pile of shattered timber, gazing into the bright stars. It felt like a dream, at least at first. And as I lay there, thinking nothing and feeling nothing, a sudden burst of reality came to me. I was ruined. I was lost. I was gone. There was nothing left, and there would be no more.
