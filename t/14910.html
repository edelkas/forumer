Tileset Madness Competition - Take 2.[/u]

For anyone who is fairly new or just had their head in a cave about a year ago, the original TCM was quite successful. Amlt requested today that I try to recreate that awesomeness. So, here we are. Everyone enjoy...

The Rules[/u]

This is a competition in which you create N art out of only tiles. As a map cannot be submitted to Numa without at least 1 object, you must add one object to your map, and I'd like it to be either hidden by the tiles that make up the level border, or far outside the border, such that it cannot be seen (preferably the former).
The maximum number of submissions by one user is 5.
That's pretty much it, although there is one other rule. If a map is deleted from Numa, it is automatically stricken from the competition. I will be checking Numa periodically to make sure this rule is conformed to.
If a submission is created using N-art Maker (and I should expect a sort of decency out of all of you regarding this sort of thing), it will not be accepted as a viable entry for the competition. I do this because, if it's not your work, then why should I accept it? It's like plagiarism.
Maps must have been submitted on or after today, December 30. So if you had a masterpiece in the past, too bad.

The Team[/u]

Amlt - Judge
Mr Mongoose - Judge
Jakel - Judge
Spiker - Back-up Judge
Ultima - Math-checker

The Judging[/b]

Each judge will rate each tileset out of 100 points.
Thumbnail of tileset: Out of 50 points
Full image of tileset: Out of 50 points
The sum of all 3 judges' total scores for each map will be tabulated to determine the final standings.

The Deadline

The deadline for the competition will be Thursday, February 1, at 12:00 AM GMT sharp. This will (most likely) not be extended for any reason. But if the competition is really nice and vibrant and I decide it needs a bit more time, I may. Also, if anyone comes up with a plausible reason for me to extend it, I'll consider it.
