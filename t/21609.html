Changing Enemies 3 : The Future

The point of this contest is to make the best maps using only the enemies specified to that week. What I mean by this, is that I will decide a type of enemy and you will have to make a map using only that type of enemy.


The Rules:

1. No using any other enemies, except the one/ones specified.
2. Mines dont count as enemies, so you can use mines with any other type of enemy. But I may choose mines, and you will have to make a map of all mines. (I think that is fair.)
3. You will have one week to make the map using the specified type of enemy.
4. You can not make DDAs, survivals, or N-arts.
5. After one week of map-making, the lowest scored mapmaker will be eliminated.
6. Only one map per week.
7. Unlimited amount of spots.
8. Judges each judge all of the maps.
9. You don't have to submit the map to NUMA.
10. NO OLD MAPS ALLOWED.
11. Judges can enter the competition, but cannot (obviously) judge their own map.
12. Unlike the seasons before, you will have a week to make the map throughout the whole competition. The deadline length will remain the same.
13. People that don't make a map will automatically be eliminated. With no second chances. Don't join unless you are dedicated to making a map on time.
14. Depending on how many people sign up, will decide how the elimination numbers work.
15. I may throw some twists into the rounds. IE: I may specify the category of map for that week.
16. If you are a judge, you better be willing to do your judging each and every week.
17. I'm going to cut down the number of rounds this year due to last years lack of participance.


Scoring System:

Aesthetics: ?/5
Amount of fun: ?/10
How well you used the enemy: ?/10

Total: ?/25


Prizes: Let me know if you are into making sig tags for this competition.

1st Place: A sig tag and a dedication.
2nd Place: A sig tag or dedication.
3rd Place: A dedication.
