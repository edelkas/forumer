I also think offering it in two or three strengths would be good. I reccommend a) Just below N's normal maximum running speed, so you just need a decent run up to break through; b) A little above max running speed, so you need some form of artificial acceleration (such as falling from a great height, or downhill slopes) and c) the speed N falls at from a fairly low jump.
