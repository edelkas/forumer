Hello and welcome to the No Block Tile Contest, where you can use any tile except a block tile. You can do anything as long as the map does not include a block tile. Inspiration for the contest is from ShadowWolf.

Judges

Sept
ShadowWolf
Guiseppi
Evil Bob (BackUp)

Judging System

10 Points for how fun.
10 Points for originality/creativity.
10 points for use of objects/tiles.
-
30 Points for an overall score.

Rules

1. Maps cannot not contain a whole block tile.

2. Maps have to be submitted to NUMA.

3. Anytype of map can be submitted, except N-Art.

4. You can only submit a highest of three maps.

5. Maps have to be submitted from February, 19 to March, 24 and one old map.

6. You most post a link here to your submissions.
