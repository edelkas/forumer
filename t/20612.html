Uncle Blizzy's Create-A-Sig Contest!

Calling all imagiers! Uncle Blizzy is back for the winter, and needs a brand new signature to commemorate the occasion! Starting today, I will be accepting submissions for both a signature and an avatar - Sets are preferred to single signatures, but avatars are not required. At the end of this year, I will choose a winner, and that person will win themselves a... well, we'll keep that a secret for now.

Here are the rules;
  • All Signatures must be posted by December 31st at 23:59 PM, GMT
  • Each Signature must fit the forums' maximum signature image limits (600 x 200), which can also be found below.
  • Each participant may submit no more than twice.
  • Avatars are optional, and considered "Extra Credit."
  • Any style signature is acceptable - There are literally no rules for colour-scheme or images. However, all images should ideally say "Kablizzy" on them.
Signature Size Limitations -



If there are any questions, feel free to post them here! With that in mind, go!
