Actually, you can set it up like so.

Both players CAN cross sides, but what good would that do? So don't, though you can.
If the ball lands on the net and stays, its still in game, and you could jump and hit it still.
Tons of rules permitting what can and can't go down.
Hitting the ball at certain velocities and angles changes its trajectory and speed.
Obstacles, enemies, and mines CAN be activated if you wish.
The net should bounce the ball back only at the top, the bottom lets the ball go and counts as a fault.
If items are to be placed by the opponents, it can only be UNDER the tile net range.
Ninjas start on their side, against the middle of the side walls, to prevent early death.
The ball spawns on top of the net for a serve and for posession; First to touch it gets the first ball to be their serve for that serve.
For each serve after the first, the ball is placed in the center of the servers box, in the tile net area, and held there until it's hit.
