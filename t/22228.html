I made 2 levels and saved the game data into the userlevels file. But when I went to play them one of the objects appears off the playable area of the screen. One level its a gauss turret that kills you when you land on the bottom floor. In the other level its the button for the exit door. Any idea why this is happening? #427291 2 2008-04-12T00:07:00Z max_ride
They're called Nan'd objects, they happen by corruptions in the code. They usually result when you save something into a userlevels file without a "#" sign at the end of the code. Or, at least that is what happens to me.

In order to get rid of them, you have to search the code for the "NaN" and erase that, and then go back into editor and delete the object. That's how I do.
#427293 3 2008-04-12T00:09:24Z PsychoSnail
