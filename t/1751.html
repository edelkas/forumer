So, I guess that we are starting another Chasing the Fox Contest, open to all. This will run much like the first.

I am posting 10 links to NUMA maps and various challenges on each of them worth a given number of points. When you complete a challange (besides a time trial challenge) and post the completion in form of a demo OR a screenshot (when applicable) on this thread, you are awarded the points and the challenge is crossed off the list. This is first come, first served basis only!

Not all of the challenges will involve beating the maps. Some are simply complete the task and post a demo or even a screenshot to prove it. Some of the challenges will seem extremely stupid. The point is to play my maps and get a good feel for them.

On any of those challenges that do not require you to beat the level, getting the challenge points and completing it will earn a bonus 1 to your score. Being the first to complete more than one challenge in a single post earns a bonus of the number of challenges completed. If someone has done one of two challanges you post completions for, then you only get credit for the newly beat one. If you happen to win the completion Time Trial or Best Death with your demo, the extra bonus comes as well.

Example: Someone has completed Needles for 1 point. You post an All-Gold completion. That earns 4 points for an all-gold completion, 2 points for all gold, and 2 points for being the first to win two different challenges with one post. Total for this demo is 8.

Strategy hint: To cut down on the bonuses that some people might potentially earn, quickly post demos completing easier tasks.

Taking demos that are posted on NUMA previous to the beginning of the contest (and I have kept track of them) is ILLEGAL and will result in disqulification.

The contest will begin when we post judges and maps in the thread.
