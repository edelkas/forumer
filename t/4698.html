Rules
These are the rules for adding a map to 'The Epic'...

* Each map must play right- to- left, with the ninja starting on the far right, and the exit being on the far left.
* Each map MUST contain, on the far right, the two columns that were on the far- left of the previous map. Thus, the series will play like a single, very long level.
* Every level must bear some resemblance to something real- ish. No pretty patterns, or metanet style levels. Try to incorporate gravitiy in most, if not all, of the level design.
* All level descriptions must link back to this thread and you must link to the map in this thread. The description should always involve- usually further- the vague plot of the thing. Nothing gory or too serious.
* You are not allowed to create 2 consecutive 'epic' maps.
* Nothing too insanely hard.
* No more 'inside a building' type levels; the idea of The Epic is realistic landscapes, and the enterior of a bulding- type levels never seem to fit that. A cityscape or something might be allright, or the odd small shack, but no office blocks. And no buidlings that take up the majority of a map.
* You must post your map within 5 days of it becoming your turn. If not, it'll go to the next person.
* If I judge a level 'not worthy' of The Epic, it won't be included in the semi- official list. There will be no arguing with me- if I think it sucks, it won't be in The Epic. Breaking the rules slightly may, on occasion, be acceptable to me. But ask first, because if I don't like it, then I'm afraid you won't be in this.
* No making obscure, mixed references involving fat female hamsters. They can be taken the wrong way.

The Queing System
It's pretty much first come, first served. Stick your name down, and wait your turn. I will PM you if you don't realise you're up. You have a week to make your map... if you don't complete it by then, your turn goes to the next person in the que. You can re- enter the que as many times as you like.
VIP's are people who've been waiting a long time, somehow skipped, ignored, or not iinformed that it's their turn- and have the right to re-enter at pretty much the top of the que if they ever return to this thread.

Rules
These are the rules for adding a map to 'The Epic'...

* Each map must play right- to- left, with the ninja starting on the far right, and the exit being on the far left.
* Each map MUST contain, on the far right, the two columns that were on the far- left of the previous map. Thus, the series will play like a single, very long level.
* Every level must bear some resemblance to something real- ish. No pretty patterns, or metanet style levels. Try to incorporate gravitiy in most, if not all, of the level design.
* All level descriptions must link back to this thread and you must link to the map in this thread. The description should always involve- usually further- the vague plot of the thing. Nothing gory or too serious.
* You are not allowed to create 2 consecutive 'epic' maps.
* Nothing too insanely hard.
* No more 'inside a building' type levels; the idea of The Epic is realistic landscapes, and the enterior of a bulding- type levels never seem to fit that. A cityscape or something might be allright, or the odd small shack, but no office blocks. And no buidlings that take up the majority of a map.
* You must post your map within 5 days of it becoming your turn. If not, it'll go to the next person.
* If I judge a level 'not worthy' of The Epic, it won't be included in the semi- official list. There will be no arguing with me- if I think it sucks, it won't be in The Epic. Breaking the rules slightly may, on occasion, be acceptable to me. But ask first, because if I don't like it, then I'm afraid you won't be in this.
* No making obscure, mixed references involving fat female hamsters. They can be taken the wrong way.
