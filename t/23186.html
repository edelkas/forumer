Yes.

I don't particularly understand the question. The universe contains the cosmos by definition, and it is a chaotic system - tiny changes now could have massive effects later. I'm guessing your question is 'Is the universe ordered or is it in chaos?' I can answer that one.

The universe is an ordered system, obeying physical rules (most of it... black holes are a special case, but it is likely that there are physical rules governing them, we just don't know what they are yet). However, the Universe is a chaotic system - meaning, as stated above, that tiny changes in today's system can have profound effects on the state of the system in the distant future. There are no particular consequences to this, other than that we should be fairly confident that the universe won't turn into a giant blancmange any time soon - the timescale of chaos is too large for it to make much difference to our mayfly lives. It does make tracking incoming meteors/comets a bit more interesting, though...
