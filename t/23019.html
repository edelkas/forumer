#449221 8 2008-06-28T00:35:03Z incluye
Or you could do this:
Go to the leveldata and find !5^[number]^[number]. The [number]s are the X and Y coordinates.
If you want your thwump coming out of the ground (moving up) subtract 1 from the second [number].
If you want your thwump coming out of the right wall (moving left) subtract 1 from the first [number].
Due to N's arrangement, thwumps placed halfway into the left wall or the ceiling without Z-snapping will move.
