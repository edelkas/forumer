TUMIKI Fighters for Mac
TUMIKI Fighters for Windows

This has been passively mentioned in game threads for other Kenta Cho games, but now that it's been released for Mac, I must say, TUMIKI's pretty fun.

For those of you who don't know, this game's another shoot-em-up. You drive this little plane, and you shoot up other planes with the Z key, which will then cause the planes to fall down. The small planes die immediately, and if you catch it it'll stick to your plane and fire against the enemy when you're firing. It also gives you points while you're firing. However, one shot from a bullet and it's destroyed, so use the X key to pull the pieces in temporarily.

Your main plane flies on the left side of the screen, and can be destroyed by one bullet, so it pays to gather up pieces for shielding, if nothing else

Larger ships tend to fire more projectiles at you, and are also composed of multiple pieces which break off as their health bars(lower right of screen) run out. Again, catch the pieces to get them working on your side, but they'll be destroyed by other bullets.

At the end of each stage, you'll confront a boss plane, which is usually composed of four or more pieces which take a long time to destroy, and fire *lots* of particles at you. Yes, you can catch the pieces as they fall off too

When you destroy the final boss piece, it'll fall down, but you can't catch it. It'll explode, and then your plane will take off all its acquired pieces, and speed off towards the next stage.

Here are two screenshots:





It's a really fun game, so I suggest everyone go download it.

Finally, for you veterans, I noticed everyone was stuck on Level 3. I don't know why. I know it's a pretty difficult thing, as I've seen, but you really need to start wisely positioning and rotating your pieces before you catch them. The boss is rather tricky, but possible.

My high score is getting to the last piece of the Level 4 boss
