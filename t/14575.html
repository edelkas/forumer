Ever watch the title screen and see a level you want to play, but haven't unlocked/don't know where it is? Now you can play it from the title screen. Just press (~) like you would to open Ned, and press P to play it like you would play an edited level. When you die you can go back to edit and reset the objects.

Enjoy,
