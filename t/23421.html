#458443 3 2008-08-05T07:26:42Z TearsOfTheSaints
Quote: Numby, August 05, 2008 07:17 am
You need to collect gold, which gives you time. You start each episode with 90 seconds.

Yes, what he said.

You start each episode with 90.00 seconds (showing on the top left of your screen). During each level you collect gold, or in your case, you don't collect gold. If you collect gold during your race to the door, your total gold collected (which equals 2 seconds each), will be added to the time it took you to complete the level. Now, once you go to level 01 of that same episode, you start with the time you finished level 00.

Example:

Start with 90.00
Finish Level 00 with 103.25
Start Level 01 with 103.25
Finish Level 01 with 156.75
Start Level 02 with 156.75...etc.

Once you've completed the whole episode (Levels 00 through 04), you will have a total time. This time will be your score, or highscore.
