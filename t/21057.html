As of today, we have about 42% percent Preps (...which consists of "Upper Preps" "Lower Preps), 30% Smart People (..."Nerds", "Slightly out People", and Other Smart People), and 23% Others (Emo/Goth/Scene/Druggies/etc/etc/etc, Losers/Dorks/Geeks, Gangsters/95% of black people and New people and, last and least, Retards).

1- Upper Preps Dressed in latest clothing, drive nice cars, good-looking, athletic (for the guys, at least), and may not be smart. Very active social life.
2 - Lower Preps Dressed in latest clothes, nice cars, good-looking, but not necessarily athletic and dont hang out with the Upper Preps. They are more schoolwork oriented and you can find them a couple in your honors/AP classes. Active social life.
3 - Nerds Smart people that dont especially care for the latest in clothing and stuff; found mostly in honors/AP classes. Highly school-oriented. Many academic clubs rely on them for survival.
4 - Slightly out People Smart people that arent exactly preps, but arent nerds. They are your smart people that go to dances. They have an active social life.They are in honors/AP classes.
5 - Other Smart People Smart people that are just plain weird or that dont apply themselves academically. You can find them in a variety of classes including normal classes, honors classes, and AP classes.
6 - Emo/Goth/Scene/Druggies/etc I think you guys know who you are if you fit into this category. They are mostly found in normal level classes and few honors/applied classes.
7 - Losers/Dorks/Geeks If you are unpopular, NOT smart, and oftentimes ugly, you are in this category. These guys are usually at the normal level.
8 - Gangsters - I dont want to elaborate on this one, for the sake of being politically correct.
9 - New People New people that havent found their place in the social ladder. Most will never go higher than the Lower Prep level.
10 - Retards I have to be politically correct, so, uh...yeah.
