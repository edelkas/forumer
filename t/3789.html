#77809 3 2005-06-23T19:49:23Z brickman
Sure, its possible to not do it all at once. Here's what you do:
1) play some levels
2) actually BEAT one or two so you're in the middle of an episode, or this is useless
3) when you need to stop, enter boss mode and shrink it
4) forget its there entirely until you're ready to come back. As long as you don't shut down (and hibernate/standby should mean you don't have a reason to) and don't close n it'll still be there a week later, at that exact level, halfway through the level if you want.
