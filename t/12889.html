I use Oldblivion too. The reason it runs a separate exe is that it uses DirectX 8 shaders that aren't built into Oblivion at all, in place of the DirectX 9 shaders. When the game requests a shader, Oldblivion intercepts it, translates it into an older algorithm, and sends the new request to the video card. If you have an older video card, Oldblivion works wonders.
