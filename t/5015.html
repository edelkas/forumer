On the topic of the matrix and the spoon:
I was so bored one time that I actually analyzed the spoon thing and how Neo was able to freeze bullets, and after a while I actually figured out a logical explanation. The program makes you be whatever your perception of you is/what you "know" you are (Morpheus said this and then demonstrated it in the training sessions). The way to manipulate all the stuff is simply to convince yourself that the spoon is part of you that you can control, or that you can jump between buildings, or whatever. Once you do that, the program adjusts itself to the fact that you can do that, and now bending the spoon or leaping 50 foot gaps is as simple as opening a door. "There is no spoon. You have to realize that when you bend the spoon you are really bending yourself." Its a bit harder with bullets, since you have to convince the program that they're a part of you during the time between firing and hitting, but if you can't handle it I suppose there's always the slightly less awe-inspiring alternative of just making your skin bulletproof.
Pretty deep for something that most people will never bother to think about and just dismiss as nice-sounding stuff made up randomly for the sake of letting the protagonist be learning to understand something.
