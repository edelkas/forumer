Happy New Year and Congratulations to all of the Dronies Winners!

Today, it has finished. When we began on The Legacy Project over two years ago, we had wanted for the pack to be a hundred maps to signify maximo's return to the community - Something that, even then, was all-but-unheard of. But as myself and LittleViking and nevermore and tktktk joined, we wanted to do something a bit more grandiose. So, we shot for 500 maps, 100 from each of us. But after some deliberation, we wanted something more long-lasting than just a single pack of a few hundred maps. And so, it was decided that The Legacy Project would consist of 1000 maps total, spread over three packs - 400 for the initial release, and 300 for each subsequent release. And today, the final portion of the puzzle is finished. And to start 2008 off, we have a number of treats for you all.

For those of you who have been unaware of Legacy's goings-on, let us fill you in!

First and foremost, we would like to announce three more new additions to the Legacy Team; Sendy, Barabajagal, and astheoceansblue! As busy as our schedules are, as a team, we decided that we need some more new faces in Legacy - With Sweep gone, and with nevermore and Lucidium entirely MIA this year - and with the rest of us plugging away through school and work and life in general - We needed some additional mapping power to the final of the three packs. So, we decided that, for Zenith, we needed some fresh ideas and some amazing mappers, and that, we have gotten. As of today, astheocreansblue, Barabajagal, and Sendy will all be helping work on projects for the team on a permanent basis. These three have single-handedly been setting mapping standards for years now, and I am proud to say that they have now officially joined The Legacy Team for all projects, current and future. These will probably be the last of the Legacy additions for some time, so enjoy them as you would enjoy a savory steak.

Secondly, today marks the end of the Dronies 2007! Voting has ended, and the results are up in the Legacy Subforum for your perusal.

Thirdly, I would like to re-announce InsomNicon III! Our very, very tentative date is January 4th, but we might have some last-minute changes to that date, but don't expect that to be cemented at all until we're certain of our schedules.

Fourthly, I would like to announce our next big project: The Legacy Compilation! Over the coming months, The Legacy Team will be re-organizing all of the maps from the three Legacy packs, and creating a gigantic, 1000-map bonanza for everyone's enjoyment! We are still in discussions over the specifics, but the end result will be all three Legacy Packs combined into one!

Next, I am proud to announce the long-awaited dedication maps for Caero, Lord_Day, and Cerberus! We have been saving all of our big guns for today, and we felt that hanging on to the dedication maps until today would likely be the best move for publicity purposes. We thought that these three should get as much exposure as possible, as they were the best of the best during Legacy's Expanse contest. So, without further ado, you can find the dedication maps here;

Caero
Lord_Day
Cerberus

And finally, the coup de grace. The moment that many of you have been waiting an entire year for. Today, we are launching Zenith, the final 300-map installment to our massive 1000-map anthology! Zenith adds 300 levels to the existing 700, all made by eleven of The Legacy Team's thirteen members - tktktk, maximo, sweep, Barabajagal, atob, Sendy, blue_tetris, KinGAleX, LittleViking, krusch, and myself - As well as the map made by Caero for his magnificent showing during the Legacy: Expanse contest. Zenith is meant as the final expansion pack to the Legacy project, continuing in the same vein as the Origins pack and the Expanse pack, with new ideas, new map styles, and hopefully, some brand-new perspectives upon the game. Over the past year, we nine have tirelessly played and re-played through the pack, tweaking and balancing, and arranging the pack to be in tip-top shape for you all to enjoy.

Download Zenith

Alongside Zenith, we are proud to present the Legacy: Zenith Contest! Like the Legacy: Origins Contest and the Legacy: Expanse Contest, the Legacy: Zenith is designed to provide incentive for you all to further enjoy the levels that we provide. The contest is a race, and a challenge, to find the fastest, most skilled and most dedicated players here. The first 20 people to complete Legacy will be awarded and recognized for their accomplishment. Please, please, please read all of the rules before entering, and follow the submission rules included here;

Contest Rules

Contest Sumbission: TheLegacyProject@gmail.com

Contest Deadline: March 1, 2008

Contest Rules:

- Once you complete the Legacy: Zenith pack in its entirety, you will submit your N userdata.sol file by e-mail to The Legacy Team for review. For help finding your .sol file, please consult the first post of this thread.

- E-mails must be marked "Legacy: Zenith Submission" in the subject line, along with your N username. (Ex. Legacy: Zenith Submission - Kablizzy)

- All Submissions Must include completed Demo data from each of the 300 Zenith levels. MAKE SURE YOUR DEMO DATA IS COMPLETE. Incomplete submissions may result in disqualification.

- Please be aware that your .sol file contains your username and password. The Legacy Team will not use, store, or even look at this information. If you are still not comfortable submitting a file with your password in it, you can change your info before submitting. Inside the Configure menu, set your username as LegacySubmission and your password as dummy (with the e-mail address left blank) and press "change user." You can then submit without any private information being sent.

- In the case that your .sol file is very large, you can upload your file to iHud.com and e-mail the link given to you to The Legacy Team. iHud is a free file-hosting service that allows for files up to 50 MB in size without requiring users to register.
