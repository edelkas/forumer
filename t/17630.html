#326853 2 2007-06-03T03:35:57Z Hendor
Whenever I play a level and get a highscore on it, I must exit the level and press Submit for it to appear on the boards.

Whenever I play an episode and score it, N will automatically submit it and it will appear on the boards. It should tell you this upon completion of the episode in a little message box that says something like "New Online Highscore... Submitting..." In addition, if you achieve a level score while in the process of playing an episode, it will be automatically submitted in the same fashion as above.

I hope I have been helpful, good day.
