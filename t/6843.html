#132894 2 2006-01-16T12:44:58Z iangb
you can't really 'save' a level in-game, as such. Instead just copy the data from the top textbox of Ned into a notepad file, and save the file. To load your level again, paste that data back into the top box, CLICK ON THE MAIN WINDOW (not in a textbox, or when you load the level you will type an L in the level data, corrupting it), and hit L. That will re-load the level for playing.

S in Ned doesn't save the game to disk - it just copies the data to the Clipboard so from there it's a simple matter of ofening notepad and selecting 'Paste'.
