Sweep had an idea for a nation vs. nation knock-out or possibly league tournament.

It'll encorporate both map making and highscoring. It probably won't launch until July, but I'd like to get an idea of the number of countries entering so I can build the format around it.

Basically, everyone is gonna team up as their home-nations. Four people per nation. Two map-makers, two highscorers. Some nations will have too many people for this format, such as maybe the USA, in which case we'll split it South, North, East and West or something. Other nations may not have enough, in which case nations will be combined, as appropriately as possible (eg, Germany and Austria go together). Also, nations are allowed to use substitutes if they decide that's easiest for them.

Each nation also needs a leader.

So, if you guys could say if you're interested, state your nation, and possibly mention anyone else that you know from your nation that could be interested. Oh, and to clarify, this should be the nation you either live in or were born in or belong to in some very strong way.

Oh, and if anyone has any ideas for how to do this thing, please suggest!
