For the original topic, click here.

If you'd rather not read through those three pages, Then here's a summary of sorts.

The Gold Counter[/u]
A while back, I suggested the idea of a gold counter, a device in the configure menu that would tally up all of the gold that you collected throughout all of the episodes that Mare and Raigan put together.
Originally, I was pursuing something like this:

The numbers would represent the pieces collected out of the total throughout all the episodes.

The Reward[/u]
It was pointed out that something like this would just be another pointless statistic, and that there's nothing to gain by knowing how much gold you've collected. So I suggested that a reward could be given to someone that took the time to collect all the gold.
theEpic then suggested that the reward could be a semi-transparent gold trail for the ninja when he ran, which was well recieved.

Complications and their Solutions[/u]
It then occured to me that if someone who had already beaten n were to pursue this reward, it would be very hard for them to find the gold that they haven't collected yet. Imagine that you've only one piece of gold left that you haven't collected. How would you find that piece of gold amidst all 500 levels in the game? My solution was this:

The outline of an episode would turn golden after all of the possible gold* in that episode was collected.

Something like this could also be added to hasten the process.


*Maestro also brought up a good point, saying that some gold isn't gettable, like in 01-0. This was probably the biggest flaw in the whole idea, but Condog found a solution.
To work around this problem, you would have to subtract the number of impossible gold pieces from the counter itself, so the total amnt. of gold shown in the gold counter would be less than the actual number of gold in the game. (This is kinda confusing. I'll gladly elaborate if you'd like.)

How it would Work[/u]
The counter would increase by 1 the moment you collect a piece of gold. the number would reset itself to what it was when you started the level if you die.
For example, I start 00-0 for the first time. the counter displays [0/93]. I get 3 pieces of gold, then die. the counter would have increased to three, only to return to 0 when the level is reset. If I had completed the level after collecting those three pieces, the counter would then display three from then on, until I successfully collected any other pieces of gold in that level.
Needless to say, the same piece of gold cannot be collected twice.

That's about it. If I've forgotten anything, please let me know. Feel free to post additional ideas in this thread.
