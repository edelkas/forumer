#178078 2 2006-05-20T12:48:04Z Ngaged
Welcome! To watch demos in N, you must...

1. Load a map in N's debug mode.
2. Get your demo data for that map from somewhere, like NUMA.
3. Go back to N, and clear out all of the data in the lower box.
4. Paste your demo data into that box.
5. Press 3.
6. Press Q.
7. Press P to enjoy your demo.

Hope this helps!
