hey,
that's a known bug -- you can see other examples of that kind of trick in the first set of episodes, one of the levels has boxes made of one-way platforms which were _supposed_ to trap you, but which you can evade. it's mentioned in a thread somewhere..

we decided to leave it in because (a) we honestly have _no_ idea how it's happening, and ( we like to encourage people to experiment as much as possible, and try things; such as launching off of bounce blocks or by getting squished between a thwump and a slanted ceiling.

we're getting the automatic highscore validation up this week; this will detect people who cheat by uploading false scores and/or replays. THAT kind of cheating we won't permit. some of the broken highscores currently up are likely the result of bugs in the 1.3 release and not purposeful cheating.

we kind of like this type of emergent behaviour -- things which weren't planned being discovered. we consider it "fair" since everyone is operating under the same set of rules.

sorry to dissapoint you,
raigan
