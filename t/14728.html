The notation I would prefer here is that 0.99 repeating = 1- (pronounced "one minus." The minus should be super-scripted). This is the limit as a number approaches 1. 1- is so close to 1 that, in most cases, it can be treated as 1. In reality, 0.99 repeating doesn't equal 1, it equals 1-. But generally, 1- can simply be called 1.

(The only exception to 1- = 1 would be certain fractions. 5 / (1 - 1) is undefined because you can't divide by zero. 5 / (1 - 1-) is five over a very small number, and it equals infinity.)
