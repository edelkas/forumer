I was sifting through some maps the other day and I came across my episodes. If you don't remember, they were short-lived and they fizzled out pretty fast. But I decided that it was high time that I polished them up, and add some more maps to them. So here we are, the first volume of many, from 00 to 03.

From 00 to the end of 01 was done a long time ago, and some of them are in my maps on Numa. But there were maps that I removed for the obvious fact that they sucked.

02 was done just recently, when I felt uninspired. So, I went to Numa, and found some old tktktk and LV maps to inspire me. You'll see the influence from both authors. There's also a reference to Barabajagal's episodic map "Glacier."
