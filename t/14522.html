Changing Enemies.

The point of this contest is to make the best maps using only the enemies specified to that week. What I mean by this, is that I will decide a type of enemy and you will have to make a map using only that type of enemy. (This is sort of like the One-Type enemy contest except you dont get to choose the enemy.)
The rules to this competition are simple.

1. No using any other enemies, except the one/ones specified.
2. Mines dont count as enemies, so you can use mines with any other type of enemy. But I may choose mines, and you will have to make a map of all mines. (I think that is fair.)
3. You will have one week to make the map using the specified type of enemy.
4. You can not make DDAs or survivals.
5. After one week of map-making, the lowest scored mapmaker will be eliminated.
6. Only one map per week.
7. There will be only 20 spots open to begin with. First come first serve.
8. There will be 2 other judges. We will each judge all of the maps.
9. You don't have to submit the map to NUMA.
10. NO OLD MAPS ALLOWED. (Thanks Deja for reminding me.)
11. Judges can enter the competition, but cannot (obviously) judge their own map.
12. In the first week three people will get eliminated, the next 2 rounds two people will go home.
13. Every other week you will have one day less to make the map.
14. People that don't make a map will automatically be eliminated, plus the bottom 3, 2 or 1 people will also be eliminated.

The scoring system will be based off of a thirty-five point system. What we judge on are as follows...

Tileset: ?/5

Amount of fun: ?/10

How well you used the enemy: ?/10

The creativity of the enemy used: ?/10

The prizes are as follows:

1st Place: A sig tag and a dedication.

2nd Place: A dedication.

3rd Place: A pat on the good ol' back.

Hopefully no one has done this contest before. I hope for the best.
-Max_Ride
