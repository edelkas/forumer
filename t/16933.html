#312944 2 2007-04-14T02:32:10Z Shark
Follow these steps:
  1. open Ned by hitting ~.
  2. Hit 'E' on your keyboard.
  3. Hit the delete button on your keyboard.
  4. While holding E, drag your mouse around. You should now be making solid 1x1 blocks. Explore the other tiles by hitting the numbers 1-9.
  5. To place objects, hit the 'Insert' button.
