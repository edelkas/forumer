#397079 7 2008-01-05T04:43:38Z southpaw
Here are a couple things you can do (sorry if you already said you did them I was kinda confused reading through your first post):

1. Plug the speakers into your mp3 player to make sure that when plugged into another source they play in stereo. If they don't play in stereo still then it is a problem with the actual speakers and not your computer.

2. Check your volume settings. It may be that the balance for the Master volume got moved over to the right speaker. If that's the case just move the marker back into the middle.

3. If the above two are not the problem then it's probably something to do with your computer (either a setting or problem with the speaker jack itself). It seems that it may be the computer, seeing as how when you plug your headphones into the computer they don't play in stereo either.

That's all I can think of. Hope that helps.
