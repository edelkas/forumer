hey,
one big thing is that there are usually a few "winning" paths through the map -- if you watch the highscores for that level, you can see how other people are beating it, and try to do the same thing.

OR, you can find a different way.

if i remember that level correctly, it has gauss turrets inside and two chaingun drones -- these sort of levels you either have to be very patient (get one switch, then run to safety before being shot at) or very foolish (try to get all the switches at once and hope the shots miss).

also, depending on how fast your computer is, it might be a bit harder -- if it's running less than perfectly smooth, it will be a lot harder to time things...

raigan
p.s - as someone else mentioned in another thread, i think the key to beating N is to not go crazy when you're stuck, just go read a book or play basketball -- usually when you come back, a level that you've been trying for 2 hours will be beating in 2 or 3 tries just because you're relaxed
