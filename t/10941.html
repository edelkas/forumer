Gate 88 is a rather awesome RTS game. In the game, you pilot a green spaceship, and face off against other people across the Internet or the computer. You build turrets and factories around your central command center to shoot down fighters while researching components for your ship and your buildings.

Pros:
  • For everybody to play: Windows, Mac and Linux are all supported!
  • Large amount of control over what you're doing without being super-complicated
  • People who will smack you around then tell you why.
Cons:
  • Extreme violence and shooting
  • Occasional lag
  • Computer practice mode is way different than multiplayer
Gate 88 is just a great game to play. It's the reason Eli never comes here anymore. Get hooked today!
