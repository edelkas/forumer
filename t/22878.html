#444156 25 2008-06-13T05:08:56Z OneSevenNine
Smilies really irk me, for the reason that (for example) on the internet does not actually have the same meaning as a : and a / next to each other, as does not have the same meaning as a ;, a -, and a ) next to each other. These groups of symbols stand for different things than the smilies we use to represent them; and different sets of smilies alter the meaning of the symbols. To illustrate how this can lead to confusion: the text smilies : / and : | I often use interchangably, but these forums represent them as and . Trying to use those interchangably will lead to confusion, I guarantee. These smilies represent a very narrow range of emotions, while the internet culture has molded the pure text symbols to have a meaning of their own unconcerned with what the text supposedly "says." I mean, how many times do you actually laugh as you type "lol?" lol stands for "Laugh out Loud" but you don't actually have to be laughing to use it. Same thing with the smilies. You may or may not be surprised as you type , but since the forums converted that colon and "o" into a Smilie, the reader of whatever message assumes you are; but if the reader had seen a colon and an "o" rather than the smilie, he would've reacted to it differently.

Sticking to text ensures that there one and only one set of "standard" smilies gets used, and less meanings get distorted unintentionally as a result.
