The idea of this competition is to make a map using the bare bones of a pre-existing map.
Spoiler: The IdeaTo strip a map down to its bare bones, you remove all tiles, all bounce-blocks, all one-ways, all normal, locked, and trap doors, all exits, and all ninjas. In other words, you will be left with the enemies and the gold. If you have no idea what I'm talking about, I have the bare bones of "Being For The Benifit Of Mr. Kite" below. Next, add a ninja, an exit, tiles, bounce-blocks, one-ways, normal, locked, and trap doors to make a playable map. You have a "Bare Bones" map.

Spoiler: The RulesThese are the 5 steps to making a "Bare Bones" map:
1. Select a map from numa to use for this contest. You may choose your own map. If you don't, you may have to get permission from the mapmaker.
2. PM me a link to the map you would like to use. If it is a usable map for this competition, I will strip it down to its bare bones and PM you back. An example of an unusable map is "On Route 666".
3. Create the map. Do NOT remove any remaining objects or cover them with tiles. If you do, you will be disqualified. When you have finished creating the map, submit it to numa with a tag "bare-bones".
4. Post a link to the map in this thread.
5. Await judging.
Spoiler: The JudgingI will be the judge of this contest. If anybod else wants to judge, PM me or post in this thread. I can have up to three total judges.
?/25 - Does the tileset look good with the rest of the map?
?/25 - Do the enemies work with the rest of the map?
?/25 - Is the gold placement good? (No, do not add or move gold. The gold must work with the rest of the map.)
?/25 - Is the map fun?
