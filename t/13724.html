Episode 0-00-00-01: Successfully run left three spaces.

Episode 0-00-00-03: Hold down jump throughout this level.
Episode 0-00-00-04: Hold jump for .75 seconds, then let it go.
Episode 0-00-00-05: Hold down left as you fall.
Episode 0-00-00-06: Land on the launch pad.
...etc.
And if you screw any one of those up, you get to do it again.
