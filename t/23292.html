Cloud Chamber (SpecialTrigger+ChaseAI)
http://nmaps.net/132939

Restart NReality (a mod of N, download @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Special Trigger and chasing Pathing AI

To add "Special Trigger" to a door switch, append
^startTriggers(separated by "."),stopTriggers(separated by "."),startSpecialTriggers(separated by "."),stopSpecialTriggers(separated by ".") to the door object

To make the object special triggerable, append
^^waitForTrigger,startTriggers(separated by "."),stopTriggers(separated by "."),startSpecialTriggers(separated by "."),stopSpecialTriggers(separated by ".")

zap drone -> specialOn -> seeker drone
seeker drone -> specialOff -> zap drone

rotating laser drone:
specialOn-> keep rotating
specialOff->stop rotating

Chasing Pathing (Path ID 9) : append the following to the drones/objects
^^^,9,direction,speed
The drone/object will chase the ninja at all time

Such as the rocky drone in this game:
!6^84,84,2,0,121,0^****,6^,9,0,1!
