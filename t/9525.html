#8559 1 2006-05-08T08:11:26Z bhauth
OK, here's the deal. Choose a map on NUMA - any map made by someone else - and choose a half of it - top, bottom, left, or right. Delete everything on the half you chose up to but not including the center line. Now, fill in the half you deleted (you can also change the center line) with new tiles and objects.

Try to stick to modifying the half you chose, although small modifications to the half left alone are OK if they keep the spirit intact.

You can put the ninja and the exit/switch anywhere you want. You can do anything you want with doors and switches that have one part in the half you deleted, but again, try to keep the spirit of the non-deleted half intact with the stuff you put in it.

Be sure to link to the original map.

Want to judge? Post or pm me. Want to submit? Choose a map, make your new map, submit to NUMA, and post here. Be sure to link to the original map!

Once there are enough judges I'll distribute entries between them.

Judging criteria:
Gameplay: 5 points.
Fit with original level: 3 points.
Appearance: 2 points.

Judges can award tenths of points.
