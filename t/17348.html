... Do I even have to say anything?

In my opinion, the game is to be one of the best in the entire PS2 library in terms of Action, Graphics, and almost everything else. The game runs continuously off the disc with virtually no loading times, which gives the sense of 1 gigantic level, the graphics are quite good despite the PS2's limitations, there's almost a constant 60 FPS framerate, the action (and puzzle-solving) and gameplay is virtually unrivaled, and the story is engrossing. For every single PS2 owner here: If you do not buy this game or the original, there is something wrong with you.

I have beaten it on Mortal (Easy), Spartan (Normal), and God (Hard) mode, and I am currently trying to beat it on Titan (Very Hard) mode. If you need any tips on which weapons to upgrade first, any combat hints, or any help with any of the puzzles, just ask me here.

#324061 19 2007-05-25T04:51:01Z Twistkill
While we're still on the topic of the age limit of M-rated games, I personally think that the ESRB doesn't realize how desensitized the younger kids have become to violence and sex as it is.

Oh, lemme share one of my best moments of God of War 2 I've had so far:

I got over a 2,600 hit combo on the final battle with Zeus on God Mode with the combined effort of the Urn of Prometheus (Granting me infinite Rage of the Titans) and simply holding down the square button while it was active. You must have Level 5 Athena's Blades to perform it. See, holding down the square button during Rage of the Titans actually deals little damage, but it hits very rapidly in a wide arc, so you can rack up an insanely large combo on really tough enemies like Cyclops or the bosses like Euryale or Perseus.

In addition, when you try to constantly attack while Kratos is in his normal state, if he gets hit, then he stumbles and stops his attacks. However, Rage of the Titans actually reduces the damage of all hits and you do not stumble if you're hit while it is active. Some htis are even completely negated. Plus, during the entirety of the time it is enabled, the time limit you have between your attacks to continue a combo doesn't end until you deactivate it.
