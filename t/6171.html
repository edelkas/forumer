I used to play photoshop tennis. Basically, it's where one person makes a picture, then another person edits the picture in an amusing way, then the next person edits the new picture in a new way and so on (ad infinitum).

Why can't we do this with maps?

The rules: you can edit the last map posted in whatever way you want, as long as you can still see parts of the previous map in your new map. You must post your new map in a txt file. You must never skip a map, and you don't need permission to post up a new one. You can change something very small, or the majority of the whole thing. Simple. I'll look over the whole operation to make sure we all play nice, but I'm sure it'll be fine.

Ok, I'll post up the first map. It's one I made quite a while back, but never submitted to NUMA because I never thought it was good enough. Some of you will recognise it from the "Should I submit this map to NUMA?" thread. Remember the rules: edit whatever you want, and as much as you want. Don't be afraid, just go for it.

Oh, and thanks to this thread for a similar idea that inspired me
