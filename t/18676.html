AQA is a text service - you text them a question and they text you the answer. Yesterday i texted this -
Quote: me @ to AQA
Does this sentence make sense - 'Buffalo buffalo Buffalo buffalo buffalo, buffalo Buffalo buffalo.'?
And they replied
Quote: AQA
AQA: No, the sentence does not make sense. There is an inconsistency in the capital letters with no relevant punctuation to accomodate them.

Now, im pretty sure im right, so the next day (today) i texted
Quote: Me @ to AQA
Yesterday i asked "Does this sentence make sense - 'Buffalo buffalo Buffalo buffalo buffalo, buffalo Buffalo buffalo.'?" and you responded "AQA: No, the sentence does not make sense. There is an inconsistency in the capital letters with no relevant punctuation to accomodate them.", however i think you failed to take into account the fact that 'buffalo' can be the name of a city, and therefore would be capitalized. So i ask you to respond again with this in mind, and also remebering that 'buffalo' can also be an action. Thank you.
And, after 38 minutes (which is an unbelievable amount of time for AQA) i recieved this
Quote: AQA @ 38 minutes later
AQA: Sorry. Buffalo (New York) buffalo (animals) Buffalo (NY) buffalo (animals) buffalo (verb: bully) buffalo (bully) Buffalo (NY) buffalo (animals). Phew.

w00t!

Lol. Still, its fairly cool - i defeated a massive corporation (im pretty sure its owned by Virgin) plus managed to get AQA to say 'Phew'. That made my day. Just thought id share .

(BTW, anyone else ever use one of these? Either AQA or something of that ilk?)
