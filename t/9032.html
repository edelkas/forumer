I think that it is bulky and inconvenient. Things like this never catch on - like that eye-toy game. Very cool and innovative, but no real games were produced for it.

Remember that really cool controllers have been around since the NES, and even before. Companies have tried to make an interesting peice of hardware for their console, but they are too proprietary and inconvenient. An excellent example is the Nintendo Zapper - neat hardware but how many games could you use it with? Duck Hunt and a few others that are quite obscure. There were pointing devices for the SNES, but they are obscure as well. There was a glove device (not hardly this advanced) for the NES - I think that it was only for just one game.

This looks like it will go the path of the eye-toy: good hardware, crappy games. This might be a better controller than even the revolution controler, but the revolution has so many other factors that will make it successful.
