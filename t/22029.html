#422491 7 2008-03-28T00:15:31Z Guest
Call of Duty 4. Immensely fun. Yes, one would think that first person shooting is boring, but COD4 is different. There are loads of different types of guns, and an insane amount of customizations. Each customization you make changes the performance. Plus, there are all types of guns, from assault rifles to sniper rifles to handguns to machine guns to pistols to submachine guns. There are things called "perks" which change your gameplay. Perks are unlocked as you level up. Also, the maps are beautiful. Each map has a different method of play, but are not uniform in this one way. A certain map may be meant for sniping, but does allow for a good deal of room to room fighting. Finally, there are many types of gameplay, some new and some classics.

It truly is an amazing game.
