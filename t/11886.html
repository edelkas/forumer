#215164 3 2006-08-13T19:44:14Z Twistkill
I'm actually gonna write a description unlike pembie...

Boss mode is that the N gameplay area is covered up, so if you are playing this game at work and your boss comes over, you just hit the boss key and it's hidden.

Another advantage of this is that it saves CPU power, so instead of closing N and losing your current process during an episode or making a map, etc. you just hit the boss key.

One last advantage: Whenever a rocket is chasing N or when a laser fires at N in debug mode, if you hit P to go the edit main menu, those noises loop infinitely. Hitting the boss key stops those sounds from looping, and you can keep your progress in whatever you're doing.

So you see, boss mode is quite handy.
