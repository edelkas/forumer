The exploit you're speaking of is at the beginning of: 343 Guilty Spark, the level in which you first encouter the flood. As the Pelican drops you off, immediately jump back in (as best you can) and throw all of your grenades. The pelican shold now take off with you inside. Do not move, and it will cary you to some higher ground. There's nothing here, a few enemies and such, and you miss out some of the level.

Although there are no actual 'secret' levels, there are plenty of bugs/exploits/easter eggs to play around with. More so in Halo 2.

Just look up Halo faq/walkthrough on google to find out more.

#183798 12 2006-06-02T23:34:32Z Sgt. Poptarts
These are the level in order:

Piller of Autumn ( A ship that you must escape from, or die trying).
Halo (Halo- very luscious place that was made by the forerunners, also a weapon).
Truth and Reconciliation (Dark level where Sniper Rifles are friendly).
Silent Cartographer (A big place where big things happen).
Assault on the control room (First Snowy Level).
343 Guilty Spark (freaky level).
The Library (Very long spooky level).
Two Betrayles (Assault on the Control Room Backwards(second snowy level)).
Keyes (Truth and Reconciliation type level with little light).
The Maw (Pillar of Autumn after it bites the dust).

This makes it a little easier for you to figure out what the levels are.
