#374727 2 2007-10-27T02:58:55Z Atilla
You already get it like that on the user levels menu, so I'll assume you mean your PB should be automatically written to the text file instead of your .sol. That would have been impossible for v1.4 because the version of Flash which was used wouldn't let you write to anything but a .sol. It's possible for v1.5, but I'm not sure I'd support it. If you're playing through the user levels menu to check that the times are OK on an episodic level pack, for instance, you would need to keep a spare copy without the demos. Or clear all the demos manually. Not to mention that if you've been playing a pack or something and want to give it to someone else, they'll end up with all your PBs unless you manually delete them.
