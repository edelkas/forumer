#381929 3 2007-11-22T20:43:34Z TheWigginator
You have a NaN. If you load the level data you will see that there is the word NaN at the end. This stands for Not a Number. It probably apopeared because you pressed L to load the level or R to reset it while your cursor was in the text box still. To stop that happening make sure you click out of the text box before you press L or R. To delete the rocket turret you must delete the bit of code that is for the rocket turret. It is right at the end: 444!10^300,NaN
If you delete that the turret should disappear.

I hope that helps...
