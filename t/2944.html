Quote
-Please read all rules below before you join, they might seem complicated, however they really are not too much-


A man had put out fliers around town as to his soon passing from this world. It reads:
Quote:
People of Jian Ye,
I have been ill for quite some time now, and I am afraid my passing is near, and it saddens me that even in my long years I have yet to find an heir for my posessions. I have no sons or daughters, and the only relatives I know are dimwitted and lack any real intelligence at all.

I offer anyone who is willing to come to my estate and participate in a little game to see which one of you is fit to become my heir. If you should succeed, you will not regret it.
Signed,
Sima Zhang

OOC:
This miniquest is open for all players to participate, and there is no risk of death. If you are interested in playing, just show up to the house in Jian Ye.

Mini-Game Rules
Many of you may have played this game before, however we will play this game IC-ly in this mini quest. The game is called Mafia, and here is how it works:

There are four types of characters:
1. The Mafia (may be 1-3 Players depending on how many people play)
2. The Doctor (1 Player)
3. The Spy (1 Player)
4. The Civilians (everyone else)
(You cannot choose your role, I will assign them randomly)

After everyone joins, you will be PMd your role. You are not to tell anyone what you are via PM, however during the game you may "claim" to be any character, however the other players have the right weither to believe you are telling the truth or not.

Here is how the game is played:

1. The Mafia discuss via PM together who their target will be, and the maifa leader will pm me their final decision on who they have chosen to kill that round.

2. The Doctor will PM me who he/she wants to save from death that round (inclucing his/herself).

3. The Spy will PM me one person he/she wants to check on to see of they are the mafia or not.

Once all three roles are complete, the round will end and I will notify everyone publically of who was killed. If the doctor happens to choose to save the same person that the mafia chose to kill, then that person will still be alive.

Once you are dead, you cannot speak or play in the game any longer, and you automatically lose. After I have told everyone who has been killed, each of you may discuss amongst yourselves of who you think is the mafia. All discussions must happen pubically. We will be using the honesty policy, so please to not cheat and discuss via PM.

After everyone discusses, you will all vote on one person who you choose to execute that round. When you come to a decision on who you will choose to execute, you will put it in all caps and type I Vote "Tiao Fu" OFF, Final Decision!

If alot of players may choose many different people they want to kick off, I will choose the top two choices, and have you all vote between the two of who you want to kick out.

Once that person is executed, the round will start again, untill either the Mafia or Citizens win. Who ever is left at the end of the game will be the heir(s).

If you have any questions, please PM me. Thanks and Good Luck!

EDIT: Signups will end as soon as I feel we have enough players so join soon, the bigger the game the better!
