How to make Popcorn!

First, go out and get some popcorn. These products can usually be found at supermarkets and some drug stores. After you do that, take it home, open the box and tear through the plastic wrap surrounding the bag. Unfold the bag and place it in the microwave (if you don't have one, then you should stop reading right now and go out and buy one) and close the door. Set the microwave on *High* and put the time for 1:50 to 2:00 minutes and start it. When it is finished, open up the fully expanded bag of popcorn and empty the contents into a large bowl. Seasonings such as butter, salt, cinnamon, etc. are optional. Enjoy!
