Wander over to Gamehippo and check out Lyle in Cube Sector (or, alternately, visit the author's website). It's a NES-style platformer that bears a very strong resemblence to Metroid. It's also a bit like Cave Story, but without the story*. Rather than weilding a proper weapon, however, Lyle can only destroy enemies by lobbing the cubes that are mysteriously scattered around the landscape at them. There are, of course, powerups for extra cube-hurling abilities, more health and so on. The game's a bit short, but still fun. And the last boss is rather amusing.

*Oh no! Your kitty has been kidnapped by malevolent forces! Or possibly just a kindly old woman wrapped up against the cold. It's so hard to tell with cowled robes that hide the face entirely. Nothing will do but for Lyle to rush off and rescue the poor thing, of course. This is rather optimistic of him, since he starts without any offensive capabilities whatsoever and virtually every creature other than the cat is hostile (which is, perhaps, why Lyle is so desperate to recover it). I wonder why he lives alone in the middle of a moster-ridden dungeon, anyway.
