OK, so, we all know what jump-only means don't we?

*everybody nods*

So, everybody who participates in this contest has to make a jump-only map, you don't have to submit it to Nmaps.net, but it's easier to view it if you do, as my crappy computer wont download files, and gets screwed up when I don't copy from Nmaps.net.


Now there will be 3 judges (PM me if you want to be one), and the judging system in the first round will work alot like X-Factor, each judge will decide if it is descent or not (based on the judging guidelines, as seen below), and say either "yes" or "no".

The judging will be balanced on the amount of no and yes recieved. (can somebody give me the correct grammar for that sentence please?)


There will be 4 rounds (Round 1 beginning on Sunday), and each round the judges will examine the map that the contestant has made, maps must be submitted at least the day before the round (this is GB time by the way).

You can submit your map in multiple ways, the first (and easiest) way of doing this is to submit it to Nmaps.net, and post the link on the thread.

The second way is to PM all of the judges with the code for the map (note that they will not appreciate this), and wait for a reply.

The third and final way is the hardest, but I understand that people might not (for some reason) be able to post links or PM, if this is the case, post the level code in a text file


Judging guidelines, total score out of 20 (round 2 and above)

Aesthetics /5
Amount of space used /5 (bigger resulting in more points)
Enemies used /6 (Discluding mines and thwumps, as thwumps are objects)
Flow /4 (no flow, bad flow, good flow, brilliant flow)

Round dates and guidelines

Round 1 - Sunday 21st of September - Read above
Round 2 - Wednesday 24th of September - Same as first
Round 3 - Saturday 27th of September - Quarter finals, Contestants MUST submit their maps to Nmaps.net AND post it here, Judging will be done by Judging guidelines (exceptions might be made for some people)
Round 4 - Tuesday 30th of September - Same as above
Final - Sunday 5th of October - The 2 contestants battle it out by each making a jump-only map, pleasing the judges, and playing a random map that was made in the competition, DIFFERENT GUIDELINES APPLY, READ BELOW

on the Final, contestants will get points for the time they took on the map that they were given (Read above), Say they're finishing time was 101.34, they will get 10 points (rounded) and points for the quality of the map (Read juging guideline), the maps will be specially chosen by me, so that they are equal length, hopefully the difficulty will be the same too, points will be deducted for failed attempts, except the first try.

I hope I've made this clear.

Finally, number of contestants, 20 at the max please, applies close by Saturday, so hurry up,

on the first round, 10 contestants go through, on the second, seven, on the quarter finals we get rid of 3 and in the semi-finals 2.

Exceptions can be made if we don't have enough contestants.

happy no-jumping

have fun!
