On Friday, I wasn't on this forum because I went to Six Flags in New Jersey. We left from the school at around 8:00 AM and we arrived at about 10:30 PM. It was free because we did a fundraiser for the tickets. (26 people) We left at 7:30 PM and got back to school at 10:00 PM.

I only went on 2 rollercoasters, but it was worth it. I went on Superman first, and it was FUN. You lay flat and as if you're flying around like Superman, (hence the name) and there was one loop/corkscrew that scared me, but the rest was fun. You sat down as normal, then it hydrauliclly lifted you up into the flat position when it was time to go. My first rollercoaster in 10 years!

The second one was Nitro. It was even better than Superman! It was a regular sit-down rollercoaster, but the first drop of the ride had to be at least 650 ft. straight down! Insanity! We had to have been going over 90 MPH. The rest of the ride was fast, and the turns were tight and fluid. I want to go on it again!

Those were the only 2 I went on. I regret it, but read the bottom paragraph. There was one rollercoaster named Kingda Ka that launched you straight up into the air well over 900ft. It turned over a hill for 1 second, and then went into a spiraling curve all the way to the bottom. OVER 128 MPH IN 3 SECONDS!!!! I didn't go on it, but the guys I hung out with said they were so high at the peak they could shake hands with God.

I'll probably go again this summer because I have relatives who live in New Jersey, and Six Flags is practically in their backyard. I want to try American Scream Machine, Nitro (again), Medusa, (they recently redid it), and Superman again!

Has anyone else gone to Six Flags, Universal Studios, etc? And what rides did you go on?
