Next time you have a problem, try asking it in the N Help subforum which was made for these kind of requests. This High Scores forum is where we post about the highscores for certain levels from the game.

Anyways, you couldn't have known that, so here is a map with two routes. The red route requires a double jump off of the bounce block and the green route requires you to jump of the side of one of the bounceblocks on the way up.

Oh, if you don't know how to do a "double jump", I posted how in this thread. Here's the excerpt:

Quote
Large Jumps off of Bounce Blocks (see level 10-2): These jumps are actually two jumps. They require you to land on the bounce block following a fairly big fall. It will sag under you and will then pop back up. The goal is to jump right when the ninja gets near the very bottom of the "sag", and then once again as soon as possible after. You should shoot upwards quite quickly as you have actually jumped twice, one of those times off of a bounceblock that was moving up quickly. These can be done vertically(off the top) and horizontally(off the side). They are actually relatively easy to do and most highscorers can do them 99% of the time given a large enough fall before hitting the bounce block.

Good Luck!
