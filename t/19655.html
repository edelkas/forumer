My features:
-Transparency.
-Different gradients and designs.
-Can do: Sigs, sigbars, avvies 100px. or less, and maybe wall paper, sand sig sets.

Cons:
Quality may not reach professional level, but's that's a risk you would have to take.

Examples:
-The sigset I use is 100% made by me.
-Mocaism sigbar is made by me.

Add any more details if you want your order to be a little more specific.
