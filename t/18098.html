Here's a simple tournament to divert ourselves from all of the recent map-making/highscoring competitions: name the N levels.
Objective
It is based off the messageboard game here. The judge (me for first round) posts a number of partial screenshots of levels from:
  • The game
  • The userlevels in the game
PM the current judge with guesses for the identities of as many of the levels there as possible. If you cannot find/remember a specific level number or its name, a rough description will do. For obvious reasons, don't post your guesses in this thread.

At the end of a week, the person who has the most correct guesses becomes the winner and takes over the judging for the next week.
Rules
Not many. Submit a numbered list, ideally with episode/level numbers for N levels, userlevel numbers for userlevels, as well as their names. If you can't recall the level number but know what the level is, just submit a rough verbal description of the level, and the judge will decide if you know it or not.

You are allowed multiple PMs, though please re-submit all of the guesses each time. Just saves the judges a bit of effort.

Sign up in this thread. You can join any week you like.
Judges
At the end of a week, the person who has the most correct guesses becomes the winner and takes over the judging for the next week. If that person declines the position, then it goes to the 2nd place, etc. In the event of a tie, either the top scorers collaborate, or they must decide upon a single person to take charge.

At the beginning of each new week, the judge will make a bunch of partial screenshots, arranging them roughly in order for easiest to hardest, pasting them onto one image, and then attaching it to this thread along with the announcement that the week has started. Keep in mind not to make all of them too easy or too difficult, and try to avoid ambiguity.
