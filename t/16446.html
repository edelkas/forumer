I just came up with this idea. What if instead of "5 more ratings required for a public rating", why not have "Rate this map to see the rating and comments"?

This would mean that you must rate the map in order to view the public rating and comments.

I like this idea. Some maps that reach the top rated tend to disappear instantly because some people think it isn't worthy to be top rated so they generally snipe it or rate very low. I think we should learn to be honest citizens and rate what we believe the map deserves. For top rated, it would show which maps we have already rated and there would be a Suggestions page, which already exists, that recommends some good maps. The newest page would stay the same.

Think of it like a coffee taste testing. Usually, people are blindfolded and have no idea what they are doing. They are handed a sample of different brands of coffee. Sometimes, cheaper brands score higher than well known brands such as Folgers or Maxwell's House. Same thing goes here.
