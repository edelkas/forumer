"Things Fall Apart" by Chinua Achebe is about a man in one African tribe of I think 7 or 8, dominated in his life by fear and anger, due to his hardened resolve to never be like his lazy, super-lax father. Eventually, through a lot of event-buildup, Okonkwo (that guy, yeah) commits suicide over how pathetic his tribe has become (in converting to Christianity, that is) over 7 years of exile from a fatal accident he didn't mean to cause. It's a pretty weird book.
