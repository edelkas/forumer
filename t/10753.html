i know the game is supposed to be totally skill based and all, but i just dont like the idea of dying so easily. in levels that require huge stages, youd get through a large amount of them, and are about to get to the end, then die, and have to start all over again. (this could only be a side part of it, maybe noob mode or something) but i thought it would be pretty cool to have a health system...maybe like 3 hits, and stuff like missiles and lasers would take two hits, large falls 2, but floor guards and drones 1.. i agree this would make the game a lot easier, but it would relieve a lot of frustration, IMO. either this, or there could just be a checkpoint system, like a key you can press, to establish a checkpoint *once* in the level. however, thats not the only suggestion i have.

to add more enemies to the game, there could be things like:
timed mines (activated by even the slightest movement), of a different color, say green, who blink a set amount of times (consistent number would be best, so it isnt random), then explode, with a contained explosion. perhaps a little larger than the red ones we already see. your character would die from the explosion if he is in any part of the fire resulting, of im thinking a circle design. the cool part of these mines would be that you can pass through them when they havent detonated yet, which would allow for time to run.

i also thought it would be cool to change the thwomps around a bit too. what i had in mind was have them be able to crush you in either direction, say, on the platform side (top), or the electric side (bottom). that way, you could jump on one, and speed into the air, and leap off at a high speed. also, it would make for an interesting level to have slow approaching electric sides, too. (basically, be able to switch the speeds and directions around)

ive got more ideas, ill tell them later... k ttyl all
