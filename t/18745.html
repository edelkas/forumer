I'm pretty new to the game of Go and was wondering if anyone here plays and would like to plays a game or two.

If anyone's wondering what on earth Go is, it's an ancient Chinese (i think) game mostly popular today in china, japan and korea. It involves the placing of white and black stones onto a board (made of a grid of lines) with th intention of capturing as much territory as possible with your coloured stones. It can be a very complicated and diverse game, and is considered more complicated than chess. If you want to know more, check out the wiki page!
