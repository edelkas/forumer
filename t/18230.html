Okay, I know what you're thinking, "oh no, another noob map pack." But this, however, has not, like a noob level pack, been rushed. Nor is it something that is so small it could hardly be called a pack. No, it is a full column of levels, plus a few bonus levels, just for kicks. I've been working on this for weeks, but keep in mind when I started it I had just started to grasp how to make a good level.

I call it A Beginning because I think of it as the start of my mapmaking career.

Also, this is my first try at this, so please, be gentle.

p.s. I'd just like to mention I've read the forum rules, Jeff Killian's (sorry if I spelled that wrong) guide to mapmaking, and a lot of the stupid posts noobs make.

Without further ado, the pack!
