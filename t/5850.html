*cheering in the crowd*

The Daily Llama and I have decided to host a new series as partners known as The N Show!

Rules
Here are the rules for The N Show:
  • The theme of the map must follow the previous map, for example: "N just got home from an exhausting day at work" is the theme. Wouldn't it make more sense to have "But wait! His things are missing!" as a theme rather than "So he took his dog for a walk"?
  • If The Daily Llama or I don't think an entry map is good enough, or "in sync" with The N Show, it won't be accepted.
  • The point is to have a good theme with an enjoyable level. No insanely hard maps, and no uber easy ones.
  • This is meant to be a truthful series. Since a fair portion of this is going to be inside-a-building type levels, we'd prefer not to have floating buildings, floating cars, etc. Try to stay as close to realness as possible.
  • You have 5 days to post your map after it becomes your turn. If it isn't received, the next person in line moves to the front.
  • Maps must be submitted in NUMA with the prefix: "The N Show Ep #"
  • Maps must link to this thread, and this thread to the map.
  • 2 or more consecutive entries are not allowed.
Queuing system
We have decided that the best possible queuing system was that of "The Epic". We have taken the queuing system from there.

It's pretty much first come, first serve. The first person to post is first in the list, then so on and so forth. You will be PMed if it is 3 days within your queue, in case you have forgotten. If you don't get finished by the 5th day after your turn is up, then the next person in the queue is up. You can re- enter the queue as many times as you like.

    Ep 2- Traffic (Sketch)
    Ep 3- Bad (and Final) Day at Work (Ngaged)
