I know it's been said before, but I think guns would be really enriching. You could use them to solve puzzles. Here's how I'm envisioning it:

You control the ninja with left, right, as before. Jump is up. Down is crouch. You could jump higher with crouch jump, or a running crouch jump.

You aim and shoot with the mouse, but no cursor on the screen. He can shoot in 360 degrees. If you've ever played Dark Castle on a really old Mac, you'll know what I'm talking about.

With most guns, you have to eyeball it. Some guns have a laser aiming function. Some doors can be opened only by shooting them. Ammo is very limited, but you can pick up more. You can kill yourself with rockets and other powerful weapons, but you can also propel yourself. The best jump you can do is a running crouch rocket jump. You can dampen your fall by shooting the ground sometimes. You could have a heat beam that makes rockets change direction and follow the hot spot you've created. Which could be how a door is opened if you don't personally have a rocket launcher.

Different enemies have different hit points, maybe denoted by their color? Some are invincible. Maybe if you kill one with a weapon, you get that weapon? Maybe gold = time AND ammo?

Powerups, etc. A powerup could let you maybe take a 100% drop, which might be the only way to get to a door to unlock it. Or it might let you run a corridor and take 5 rocket hits and still get to a trigger.

Teleporters! Different kinds. It might look like a vertical line, like a regular door, but you come out of a vertical line somewhere else on the screen. Depending on the orientation, you can come out with speed. You could have a series of them set up in parallel. You enter one, and fall from the top of the screen, and enter another one at the bottom, much faster, repeat. You gain momentum and come out of the last one at light speed. Could be dangerous. Plus, the ends aren't marked, so in a room with a bunch of telporters, if you pick the wrong one, you might get ejected into a minefield. Or if you don't open a hit a switch first, you might get a one way ticket into a locked room with a drone.

Blah blah blah. I'm done.

#22483 6 2004-04-21T19:35:41Z Guest
hey,
the similarity between some of your comments and some of our ideas is uncanny

we actually have a rope-simulation working right now, using the n-engine: you can wrap rope around any of the tiles, and pull one end and the other end moves, etc.. BUT.. this may or may not make it into the next game. but we'll definitely use it eventually. the hardest part is really making the interface easy and fun -- i.e we haven't yet thought of a control system which allows the player (a) a wide range of "expresiveness" (i.e they can do many different things) and ( a small/simple set of keys. ideally, the player should be able to aim/throw, shorten/lengthen, swing, and jump off of the ninja-rope, all in a fast/action-y way. this is something we're going to figure out eventually.

also, the jump-cache idea is something we've never though of -- i.e we _could_ make 50ms or so of "leeway". i don't know though.. this seems like cheating! i realize how hard it is to make certain jumps (esp. off of rounded tiles) but.. timing is the essence of the game. still, it's a good idea to keep in mind.

the problem with the jump-flipping is (as you seem to have guessed) it's hard to make it look right all the time. like.. due to the nature of the controls it's very hard to predict what the player is going to do, and there are certain cases where it would look really weird.

anyway.. the idea of ninja-themed weapons has also occurred to us. but.. we'd prefer to sort of find a unique take on them (i.e instead of the player just being able to run around and kill things, which every other game does). we're still thinking though.
