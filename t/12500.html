ok, heres my idea for a series, if i can get ppl to do it, then it will commence, i have no real set number of levels to be made, or number of authors, but thats part of the plan...

i got the idea largely in part from Kamkazi's Underworld Series, which i took part in...the simpler explaination is basically the same thing, except underwater, but more rules will apply of course...

long version, very detailed, read carefully
instead of descending straight down, makers are free to go in whatever direction they please, as long as it follows the rules(to be stated later), this allows multiple people to make levels at a time, speeding it up and preventing a loss of interest...
there is a guidline to this of course, my level(the first one) would include above water land, its the only one that may have land, unless you use a floating island(yes, floating, or youll screw up levels below you), on top of that, for the first, say 2-ish(2 is probably about right, we dont want too much open ocea) levels, the ocean floor must decend at an angle(more then just a couple grid line, say, minimum 10 or so), so the basic layout would look like this... x=map... _means no map
xxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
_xxxxxxxxxxxxxx

etc, horizontal length has not been determined...and has no real limit as of now...

the ocean floor, once it levels off, would not require to be at all flat, one could include mountains or chasms, but within the level(unless you want to continue the chasm to the level below, not recomended, although not a bad idea)

thats basically it on the descrip, any questions, ask me

rules, revised for simplicity
-it will be 3 deep, the top containing the water level, the middle containing sea life(or w/e you can come up with) the lower with the sea floor
-only action maps, this is not negotiable
-glitches are free to use
-no large caves, this is not Underworld, although i might allow a cave that spanned back under the first level
-floating islands only, or youll screw up the levels below you
-multi-lvl chasms in the floor are allowed, as in, multi level vertical(id like to see one actually)
-no tiles that affect levels around yours, unless you plan on making those levels, this excludes the sea floor, cuz that kinda has to have tiles

well, im done, its kinda long, i realize, but its also kinda complicated, any questions or suggestions, or if you want to join, post it here...ill determine who participates, im just trying to ensure the maps are good...
