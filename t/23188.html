To ensure NReality works:

1. Clear all your temporary internet files or cache of all of your browsers (firefox + ie + opera etc)

2. Restart NReality

3. Wait for the top left "Transparent Update finished loading" text disappear

4. then the newest transparent update is loaded, you can feel free to play the games
