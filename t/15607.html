#292590 2 2007-02-01T22:49:01Z scythe33
One of the biggest mistakes people make with this level is to move slowly after the drones are released. This is wrong; you should move quickly. You want the first drone to chase you towards the place where you got the switch, and you want the second drone to chase you toward the place where the first drone was originally trapped. This will hold them off long enough for you to get away.
The second hard part is getting back up the right wall--again, rather than jump slowly, jump fast, and remember you don't have to be hanging on the wall-just close to it-to jump off of it. It's very hard to get used to, and i should know.
This is the "optimal" route; adjust it if a drone gets in the way.
