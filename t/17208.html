Well, first off this should be in N help, not in Developers' Tutorials.
As for the question, to play it in debug mode, go to the debug menu, press R if your objects aren't in the right places, press 1, and then press P. Make sure you have capslock on, or it will be stuck in frame by frame mode.
To exit playing mode, just press ~.

For any more questions, just consult the manual.
