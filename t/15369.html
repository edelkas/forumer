Make a Dragon Contest
Brought to you by the N.F.P.O.D. (est. 2006, Fimision)

Update: TheAdster wins! he, Pheidippides, and fingersonthefrets shall all recieve prizes

As many of you know, the N.F.P.O.D., or NUMAers For the Preservation Of Dragons, has been working to re-inundate NUMA with dragon maps. These beautiful and majestic creatures, whose role in society has been largely ignored till now, are finally being recognized as the amazing beings they are. The N.F.P.O.D. has decided to bring the dragon protection front to you. This is your chance to thank these graceful creatures for all their hard work, and make NUMA a better place at the same time. Here follows the rules
of the official tournament:
  • All maps must be playable.
  • The deadline is February 16 20th, eastern time, 6 o'clock

  • New maps only.
  • There is a limit of two maps per person, although this may be waived at a later date.
  • Judges may submit maps.

  • The maps will be judged as follows:

    1. Incorporation of Dragons: 50/100
    2. Game-play: 50/100

  • The prizes will be as follows:

    1. An official sig-tag and dedication by yours truly
    2. An official sig-tag
    3. An official sig-tag
