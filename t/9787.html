This contest is a series of four survival maps each including ways to dodge a string of missiles.

This is how it goes.

1. Load the map in Ned (Seriously. Use Ned.) and press "P".

2. You will then be shot at by a string of thirty missiles, which soon turn into one easily-avoidable missile.

3. Try to avoid them as long as possible with as much style as possible.

4. Have as much fun as possible.

This contest is for those who want to build up on missle dodging (And dodging strings of missiles that n00bs put in their maps)
