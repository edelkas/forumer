This is the official Sign-up for our V:tM Feral Streets chronicle. In order to participate in the RPG, you'll have to follow the following guidelines for signup:

1. Create a character, using this template sheet

2. No, you can't play a Baali, or a Kiyasid, or a True Brujah, or anything of that sort. Things like 8th Generation Vampires are going to have to have a *very* good reason (Dominate isn't one of 'em), as well as stats above 4. If your character is a hobo with Firearms and wits of 5, it ain't gonna fly. Use common sense, and don't try to pull one over on me. I've munchkined the system, too.

3. Try not to use funky colors. I did, and it looks like refried ass. Thanks.

In order to create the character, use an image-editing program (For instance, Paint or Photoshop), and follow the character creation guide outlined here. Between that, and this site, you should have all the information you need. If you're already familiar with Vampire: The Masquerade character creation guidelines, then by all means, go for it (We're using 3rd edition for this, by the way).

To edit your character sheet, simply use the pencil tool or the paintbucket tool (Be careful, some things don't want to fill well). Use the Lettering tool to fill in the top-most stuff, and the discipline names, background names, and merits/flaws.

If you want a very quick-start to the game, here you go.

Step 1. What was your character before she became a vampire? a Hairstylist? a Homemaker? A Military Officer?

Step 2. What clan is your character? A clan is a vampire's undead family, their tradition, and their way of unlife. Clan information can be found here. Since we are playing a Camarilla Chronicle, it would do good for you to try and associate yourself with the Camarilla clans - Brujah, Gangrel, Malkavian, Nosferatu, Toreador, Tremere, Ventrue, Lasombra Antitribu, or Tzimisce Antitribu. Playing as an Assamite, Follower of Set, Giovanni, or Ravnos is possible, but less likely.

Step 3. What is your character's true nature? Is she a caregiver, a rogue? What drives her? This reflects the character's innermost thoughts and desires. What is your character's demeanor? What does she show to others? Does she put on a gallant front, is she a show-off?

Step 4. Attributes - Prioritize the three categories: Physical, Social, Mental. Your character automatically begins with one dot in each attribute (Unless you're playing a Nosferatu, who starts with 0 in Appearance). When the prioritizing of columns is done, you have 7 points to spend in your first-choice column, 5 points to spend in the second, and 3 points to spend in the third.

Step 5. Select Abilities - As above, prioritize Talents, Skills, Knowledges. You have 13 points to spend in the your primary column, 9 to spend in your secondary, and 5 to spend in your tertiary. No Ability may be higher than 3 at this stage.

Step 6. Choose your Backgrounds (Found here). You may spend 5 points in Backgrounds.

Step 7. Distribute 7 points amongst your three Virtues. Your character begins with one dot in each Virtue.

Step 8. Choose Disciplines (Found on the left, here. You may only choose clan disciplines at this stage, which can be discovered within the corresponding Clan section here. You may spend three points in Disciplines.

Step 9. Your Humanity is equal to your Conscience + Self-Control. Your willpower is equal to your courage. Your blood pool begins at 10 (Unless you took the background 'Generation,' in which case, your Blood Pool is raised by 1 for each point of Generation).

Step 10 (Optional). Choose Merits / Flaws (Found here). These help flesh out your character. Flaws

Step 11. Spend Freebie Points (15). Costs are as follows:

Attribute - 5 per dot.
Ability - 2 per dot.
Discipline - 7 per dot.
Background - 1 per dot.
Virtue - 2 per dot.
Humanity - 1 per dot.
Willpower - 1 per dot.

Step 12. Finalize. Record your Generation (13 - 1 for each point of the Background 'Generation' you took), and declare a Haven (Somewhere in and/or around New York).

And from there, you're ready to play! When you post, make sure you include the character sheet as an attachment to your post. All Character creation will need to be approved by either myself, or Blue_Tetris.

If you have any questions, feel free to post here (Only if it's really important, I'd like to keep this thread clean), or PM one of us.
