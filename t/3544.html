I just thought it might be interesting to share some of the ideas we've had that seemed cool at the time, but just really didn't work out how you'd hoped. If things go well, we may even be able to offer suggestions to each other to make these things work.

For example, a map I recently tried to put together consisted of four corridors; two horizontal, two vertical. In these corridors, you had to hide behind a moving line of thwumps or drones to protect you from a line of chaingun drones as you dodged mines and hit the buttons to open the next corridor. The result would have been sort of a semi-race level, and I thought it would have been pretty cool. The problem came when I realized that thwumps didn't block line of sight to shooting enemies. After that, I pretty much had to scrap the project.

So, has anyone else had similar problems, brining a concept off the drawing board?
