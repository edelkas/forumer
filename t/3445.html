#71381 3 2005-06-01T02:08:51Z brickman
A moving wall is basically a thwump that moves backwards and ignores EVERYTHING behind it, including tles and the level border. Once activated (and they're activated by touching or almost touching them) they never stop moving (and thus remove themselves, if you can survive that long). 86-3 is the only "official" level with any (and its one of the usermade levels), but many userlevels used the concept too. Their main use is to force the player to either move quickly or be squished, although they have some other uses (like the bottom row of 86-3). Making one that moves right is simple, but other directions are for some reason extreemely hard to do (requiring off the grid placing accurate to a few pixels).

#71474 5 2005-06-01T14:24:03Z nevermore
Alright, here goes. A rightward moving backwards thwump is easily placed in the wall. Same with moving down - just place it facing up in a full wall.
Moving left and up is more complicated. The easiest way to do it is to manually change the code. So, stick a thwump facing right into a wall. Go back to the debug menu, and find the last piece of code. The first two numbers after the [^] are the coordinates. Take the first one, and subtract one from it.
For one that moves up, place one facing down and subtract one from the second number after the [^].
