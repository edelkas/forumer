this game is the shiznit, with realistic attributes like cap sizes, can selection, locations from all over Europe, NYC and Melbourne. Mostly trains. Other attributes include dripping, time limit (How long it takes for the police to get you) and bombing points. I did have an old account, but I started again. The levels of "Power" are: Toy, Rookie, Advanced, Expert, Famous, World Famous, and King. you start as a toy, and your power ranking increases with the number of bombpoints you recieve from each bombing. a bomb isn't a missile; rather a large piece of graffiti writing. features all colours of Montana cans, including the shock cans, Black cans, writer team colours (sig. colours by can2, klark kent, smash, the rest of em ) caps included are: german outlines (Skinny) New York (Average) Gold Fatcap(Large) Pink Fatcap (The largest to date). You must register, verify, and start from scratch in a battle to the top. speaking of battles, they are held occasionally. If you win, you gane a large sum of bomb points. You also win a trophy next to your name on the users list. Post your coments.

http://www.bombtheworld.net/
