Spotlight

Well, everyone, if you have been looking at my maps on NUMA, you must have seen that my maps have numbers in front of them which indicate the presence of episodes. And that's exactly what it is. Well just stay on and check here regularly for more episodes.

These are some really good maps, with some really great things in there. There are a few things that you might want to keep in mind. It feels like some of these maps could use a tiny bit more play-testing, there are a few things that feel a little off. It isn't big, and it's hard to describe, but it's the kind of things that in my mind move maps from a 5 to a 4.5. Also, beware of making filler maps just to boost your total. For example, 1-4 seemed kinda filler-like to me. Make a standard, make sure all your maps meet that standard, and don't be afraid to delete one or two if you need to.

Great start, I'm looking forward to more.
