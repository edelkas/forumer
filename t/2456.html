Now, I can't recall when you said you're releasing n's source code, maybe when 1.4 comes out or maybe not, but that doesn't matter. When you do, its natural that people will grab it and turn it into 20 different versions of n with additional objects, enemies and moves. This is a good thing. But the source, as we all know, is terrible. This is bad. This means making modified versions will be harder, and less will be made with fewer changes and take longer to do them. This is very bad. Although someone will undoubtedly just make a version with clean code, by the time they finish others will be well into their own mods and won't want to start over.

What you should do, in my opinion, is get someone to clean up the code before you release it. I don't want you to do it, because you've (hopefully) got other important projects to work on/a real life. Instead, you should give it to someone on this forum who meets two requirements:
A) is able to make n's code much more efficient and logical
can be trusted to fix the code and give it back without doing anything else with it, especially making his own mods or distributing it

I would suggest for this task a moderator, preferably Tsukatu. We know he has inhuman programming prowess, and I'm pretty sure he's responsible enough to not distribute it until you're done. Once its edited, it will be given back to you, and you can not only distribute it when you're ready but also use it as a much less CPU-intensive base for future updates (if and when they come). When the mod community gets their hands on it, the results will be much better and run better.
