So, just a quick update for those who haven't seen it yet. Spore has finally been announced for the 7'th of september, about a year later than the original date. However, this time they got around to make a video saying it, so I feel pretty confident about them keeping their promise this time . There was an update in platforms as well it seems, and if nothing else happens, it'll come out on: PC, Mac, DS, PSP and cellphones, though in various different formats.

For those of you who haven't heard of the game (if that even is possible by now), it's a survival/strategy/civilization/sandbox-like game with Will Wright at the helm (maker of the Sims). Here is a quite old quite long gameplay demonstration (there's a longer version still looming around somewhere, but that one's mostly boring). A newer 14-minute demo can be found at Gametrailers. I prefer the longer review for not skipping through two of five phases, but it can be quite slow at times.
