#129241 3 2006-01-02T15:17:06Z Levinous
When your map is finished, go into your 'N' folder, and open 'userlevels.txt'. At the very bottom of it, you take a new space and input the following:

$[your map's name]#netfisch#[your map's type]#[your level data (001^875)]#

Don't forget the extra # at the end.
