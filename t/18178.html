Who can make a challenging level WITHOUT USING ENEMIES? This will challenge your creativity and mapmaking abilities. The rules are as follows:

1. No enemies WHATSOEVER. That means no droids, no rockets, no cannons, no chainguns, no laser thingies, no mines, and no floorguards. Any level containing any of these objects will be given an instant score of ZERO and removed.

2. No DDAs. The level should be a challenge. Once again, any DDA will be removed and given a score of zero.

3. Gold, doors, and switches are allowed, but remember, the idea behind this contest is to make a MINIMALIST level. Any level that overuses these will lose design points.

4. Nothing that is already on NUMA. I'm doing this to see new levels, not to look at some cookie cutter design. I can find your NUMA level if I want it.

5. I NEED 2 JUDGES. The judges will be chosen through an intense selecting process (the first two people who ask me by PM.) Note that judges will not be allowed to submit levels, naturally.

...GO!

JULY 6TH Edit: Mines are allowed, but SPARINGLY. Remeber, the name of this contest is minimalism. Also, I forgot thwumps. They are prohibited too. Also, I decided to add a stipulation: ONE ENEMY PER LEVEL! Please remember that the level should not be centered around running as fast as possible to avoid a drone, but should be a level that requires THOUGHT and STRATEGY. Stay true to the heart of N: planning and skill, not holding the run button until your finger bleeds.

Need one more judge!

July 10th EDIT: GRADING RUBRIC!!! Here it is:

Challenge: Not too easy, but not impossible. Something that takes more than a few tries to get. []/25.
Minimalisim: Less is more. Make every object count. []/25.
Innovation: Is it something new? Does it benefit mapmakers of the future? []/25.
Awesomisity: Duh. []/25.
