how do i download player-made maps?
#31684 2 2004-10-11T17:22:52Z Guest
Log on to and copy the map data. Start up N, and hit the ~ key to enter debug mode. Once there, delete the data in the upper right text box, and paste the map data you copied into that box. Be sure to clock the mouse outside the text box, and then Hit the L key to load the data. Make sure the caps lock button is on, and then hit P to play the level. If you need anymore help, send me a personal message, and I'll be glad to help ya.
