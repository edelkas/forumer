#134817 4 2006-01-22T22:58:20Z Maniac
HeartView already discovered this glitch. I forget the URL of his map submission, though.

Basically, the reason this happens is that, when a seeker drone sees you, it automatically calculates what terrain it will hit at the end of its "rush" and then rushes forward until it hits it. If it hits the ninja on the way, great!

Anyway, when the ninja triggers the trapdoor, the seeker drone doesn't update its view of the world - in its mind, it still thinks the far wall is as far as it will go, so it will then go through the trapdoor. After rushing, it then "comes to its senses" and goes back and forth in its little prison.

Another corollary of this "feature" is that if a seeker drone heads towards you and would hit a door at the end, and the door is then open when it's done, it will stop temporarily and then go through.
