#13795 1 2007-01-31T01:20:37Z epigone
Imagine this hypothetical situation. It's a thousand, maybe ten thousand years from now. Life on Earth has continued, remarkably untouched by cosmic accidents, global warming, overpopulation, and the various other problems our world currently faces. Through evolution (or God, take your pick) another species of senient beings has come into existence. Assume that this new species has roughly the same capabilities as humans, they are not overly intelligent, not incredible animalistic killing machines either.

I'm asking you to debate what humans would do if another sentient species were come to existence. Would humans enslave this race, if so why? Out of fear, jealousy? To take advantage of them and use them as a work force? Or would humans befriend this new species, mutually co-existing. Would this be a synergistic relationship, or would it simply be two different species existing, ignoring eachother? Would humans decare war on the species? What would happen then?

Please do not come here to debate the likelihood of this happening. If you want to debate the processes of evolution, go to the thread for that. If you want to debate what Earth (or any interstellar colony) will be like far into the future, create a thread for that. Also do not take into consideration the incredible advancements of technology that may have occured in this time frame, thus dramtically altering the scenario. Only debate the aforementioned scenario as a general idea.

- Fundamentalist groups would declare that mankind was formed in the image of God and is the only truly sentient species, and thus that the new race is either 1. fabricated by scientists as part of their sinister agenda to force Darwinism on the world and destroy all moral standards and/or 2. not really sapient and thus has no rights.

- Bigots would declare that the new race is inferior to humans, can't be trusted and should be either exterminated or subjugated - just like they did with other races in colonial times, really.

- The two above groups would attempt to subjugate or destroy the new species

- Someone would create a Sapient Species Rights Group and attempt to subjugate or destroy the two above groups.

- Antitheists would claim that it demonstrated the corruptness and arrogance of religions and attempt to stamp them out, which is equivalent to attempting to douse a fire by throwing lighter fluid on it.

- Someone would claim we should leave the new species in isolation and not corrupt their culture with ours.

- Scientists would acquire a few volunteers from/abduct members of the new species, depending on whether or not we could communicate, and start running intelligence tests, brain scans, sociological/psychological/cultural studies, and so on.

- Conservative governments would present the newcomers as a potential threat, urge caution in dealing with them, and send large numbers of heavily armed men to stand suspiciously close to the new species' houses.

- Liberal governments would advocate welcoming them with open arms, entering into a joyous fellowship with our fellow sapient beings, spreading peace and love throughout the universe, and so on, and so forth, before getting bogged down in red tape while trying to enact laws to protect the new species.

- If we can communicate with the other species and they are similar to us, someone would start a cross-species romance, inciting another huge argument over the sanctity of marriage and whether "a man and a woman" only refers to human men and women, or adult males and females of either sapient species.

- Lawyers would get very rich while being paid to argue about whether non-humans have rights and can become citizens.

- The term "specieism" would be introduced.

- Some people would claim they were really a member of the new species born in a human body, or some such.

- Others would adopt fashions from the new species.

- Someone would claim the new species fulfills some ancient prophecy, and furthermore that if everyone doesn't listen to what I say they'll be struck down with holy fire.


In short, everyone would get very upset and run around like headless chooks.

In my opinion, it'd be something like this.

first, scientists / nasa / other similair program discovers alien race.

second, military hushes any rumors, then continues investigation with their scientists.

next, the military either deems the new species a global threat, decides it is time to inform government in order to start peace talks, or highly classified research continues for many many years, eventually leading to one of the former two choices,

then, international governments informed, people informed, either international war effort or highly controversial peace-talks begin.

This is where it gets messy.
as scythe33 said above, some people will argue for subjugation, some for annihilation, some will argue the aliens equal rights, others will say we should not interfere.


what I think the outcome depends on is the level of advancement in the alien species. If they are very primitive, chances are the areas in which they live will be restricted, but squatters will move in and slowly grab up the land from the weaker species, until they're either exterminated, or forced onto small reservations. (sound familiar?)

if the aliens are about equal, (unlikely) things will probably be pretty balanced at first, but surely tensions will grow, and there will be wars, hatred, mass killings, etcetera, leading most likely to peace and equality after years of hardship and strife.

if the aliens are more advanced then us, we will be perfectly happy to alley with the aliens, but that of course relies on their being willing to do this.

That's all I feel like saying, and it covers only a fraction of the possibilities.
