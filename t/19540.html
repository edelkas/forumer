#17341 1 2007-09-27T07:27:29Z LittleViking
TheRealN.com has been going strong for almost a full year now, and we want to celebrate that anniversary in style. That is to say, we want to celebrate with a new style. And we want the talented artists here in the Metanet community to show us how it should be done. Starting now, and going until Tuesday, October 16, we're asking you to submit your designs for the site to us. We're looking for entries in the form of pictures only; no CSS or HTML necessary. (If you'd rather work with CSS and HTML, feel free to; but it's not expected or required.) You have complete artistic freedom for this. Entries can be subtle changes from the current design or total rehauls grayscale or colorful, with any layout you choose. Each person can submit as many as 3 entries. That way, you can try out a few different styles, while still focusing your efforts into each one. You can post or PM your entries. If you choose PM, send to LittleViking001. I'd rather see you post them, however. There won't be a public poll to choose a winner, so posting entries will be the only way to get feedback from the forumers. A winner will be chosen by the Real N admins: blue_tetris, Kablizzy, and LittleViking.

Things to keep in mind:
  • The comic sizes vary, so don't form your design around any one strip. Your design should leave a little flexibility here.
  • The design should have space for both the comic and a blog entry. It should also be easy to adapt for the Articles, Links, About, etc. pages.
  • If you want to incorporate ad space into your design, use the dimensions provided by Project Wonderful.
  • Any height is fine, but the image shouldn't be wider than about 950 pixels. If you're using 1024x768 as a monitor resolution, you should be able to see your full design without scrolling horizontally.
Prizes for the winners will be announced later on. One prize pack will be available through the mail, and one will be online-only for people who aren't able to give out a mailing address.

Any design selected is subject to modification, to meet the webmasters' desires and for ease of coding. In general, designs will not be heavily modified. The winning design (as well as all other designs) may be used for other The Real N sites; i.e. the Legacy website, upcoming games and projects. Artists will be contacted before their design is used. The artist will be credited in the site's respective About page and compensated with prizes. If you wish to enter but can't meet the deadline, contact LittleViking before the 16th and an exception might be made. Absolutely no entries will be accepted after Monday, October 22.
