Well, first it was any number of things. When I was young, I gave myself the moniker "yoop". I don't know where that came from. As I got older, "yoop" was thrown out, and various words that I thought sounded cool took its place. "cipher" was considered, but I didn't much like it. Eventually I saw "blade" used as a name, so for myself, I stuck "scythe" onto it, making "scytheblade". When I first discovered razoric.com (which is now either defunct or very changed from what it was originally), the character |DG|H4x0r on the mario/resident evil video gave me the idea of adding _hax onto the end. It became scytheblade_hax. I still use the _hax ending for character names in games like Diablo, where you can have more than one character per account.
When I took up Diablo, scytheblade_hax wouldn't fit in the account name selection. I dropped it to scythe, which was taken, so I added 33, trying to keep my username under eight characters for some reason I no longer remember-otherwise it would have been 333. This was all well and good-I named my first character scytheblade_hax, and kept using it. When I came to the battle.net forums in late 2003, the name attached to me was now "Scythe33". I played around with the capitalization, variously "Scythe33", "ScYtHe33", and "scythe33". It wasn't until a few years later that I finally decided it looked more "stylish" with a lowercase s. So, "scythe33" it was, and it shall remain, because I came up with a pretty awesome name.
"pirate_ninja_jesus" was a bit more spontaneous. We were all changing our nicknames on IRC one day, and I went to this from uncyclopedia. It stuck. End of story.
