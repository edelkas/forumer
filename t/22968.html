#447006 4 2008-06-21T04:55:47Z PsychoSnail
On Windows computers, press the Print Scr button to take a screenshot of the whole screen, or Alt+PrintScr to take a screenshot of just the active window. This puts the screenshot on the clipboard, so you need to launch an image editor such as Paint and paste it there. This can be done for anything on Windows, by the way.
