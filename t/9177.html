This way was by far the easiest/simplest way for me when it came to beating this level, as long as you don't care about gold (there is about an overall loss of 20 seconds), and I hope this example is not complex but, rather, easily understood and copied in the N world.

The thwumps are represented by the the letter "t" in brackets ([t]|), the blue bar (|)represents the dangerous/killing/crushing/shocking side of them, the horizontally pointing arrows (> and <) point the way in which the thwumps are currently traveling, the vertically pointed arrows (v and ^) show whether you are jumping up or falling down (used only if the example might otherwise be lacking in clarity), the dashes (------) are empty air (annoying but necessary for my picture to work), "N" is your guy, and every "n" represents a point in your suggested flightpath.

Step 1: jump straight up, holding the jump button as long as possible. Upon falling, don't move horizontally at all, you should have triggered the first four thwumps' paths on your side and the other side so that their position looks like this, approximately.

[t]|
[t]|
[t]|
--n----->[t]|
--v----------->[t]|
--+----------------->[t]|
--^----------------------->[t]|
--n
_N____

Step 2: wait until the thwumps on the other side, in their stairstep order, get close, the approximate distance is shown below, after which you jump up as shown, pushing off the wall at the height of your jump (make sure not to hit your head on the 5th/6th thwump) and more up than out, which should then trigger several above blocks above you to the right, although only the first four block need play a part in beating the level.

[t]|
[t]|
[t]|
-N-------------------------------|[t]<
--n----------------------- |[t]<
---n---------------- |[t]<
---n-----------|[t]<
--n
_n_____

Step 3: now that the first four thwumps traveling left have hit the wall and are now traveling right, and the thwumps after the first four have been triggered and are out of your way follow the flight plan which will end with you sitting on top of one thwump and being pushed by another (a "stairway" that is quite safe and involves no work to stay on while traveling).

[t]|-----------n
[t]|-------n-----n
[t]|----n-----------n
[t]|--n----------------n
----n--------------------n
--n------------------------n----------------------------------->[t]|
-n----------------------------n--------------------------->[t]|
n-------------------------------n------------------->[t]|
-----------------------------------n------->[t]|
----------------------------->|[t]--n
-------------------------------->|[t]N
------------------------------------>|[t]
---------------------------------------->|[t]

_-_____

Step 4: Approximately in the middle of the level your "stairway" will meet an mirror image "stairway" that is coming towards you that threaten to crush you between your "stairway" of thwumps that you are riding on and it unless you jump over the "stairway" on the right and land back on your original one in the same safe position of being on top of one thwump and being pushed by another to the right toward the door key that you were in before your evasive jump. About 3/4 of the way to the right hand wall, jump off and get the key, then wait for your "stairway" to reset back to it's original position against the wall right above you. As soon as the first thwump that makes up the four thwump "stairway" hits the wall, repeat Steps 1-4, the same ones that got you the door key on the opposite wall, exactly and in the same order, using them to get safely back to the door, which should now be open.

I hope this helps you beat the level. Good luck!
