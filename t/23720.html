I was extremely bored last night, so I friend and I spend an hour or two on YouTube. It started off with "we're bored and comedians are funny, so let's YouTube 'robin williams' or something," and that led to a video of Robin Williams trolling American Idol, which led to "best of simon cowell" and finally to "worst american idol" and "worst x factor".

While most of them were just bad, I found a few that are hilariously terrible (the classic William Hung clip not included).

Double Shock
This may be my favorite American Idol related video of all time. From this clip, I don't know whether I like Simon Cowell better for his commentary or Ryan Seacrest for finding these two and encouraging them.
My favorite moment has to be when the second one is singing, and the first guy and Seacrest are right outside. The first guy says something like, "it sounds like he's doing really good, probably because he kept practicing the whole time we were waiting," and Ryan Seacrest is just staring at him and trying to keep a straight face, like "_ mmhmm."
