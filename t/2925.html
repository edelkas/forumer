First, see the newcomers thing. On the main page, it's a little button that says "first time here" or something. Then see the strongbad emails, the real meat of the website. Trust me, you'll get hooked.

Basically I created this thread to introduce y'all to this site (that haven't already seen it), and to talk about alla the crazy stuff that happens. It's mostly updated weekly, especially the strongbad emails.
