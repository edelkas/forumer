#10328 1 2006-07-29T13:56:20Z L3X
N has almost totally lost my interest, i never play any more. School is becoming more important, and the high score tables are pointless anyway. Whats the point in highscoring, when as soon as a new 0th runner comes along, you have to look at them with at least some suspicion? When i was new to N i assumed everything was legit, but after being in the scene for a while, the amount of namecalling, accusations, and actual cheating is just too much to bother with. Whats the point in spending sometimes hours on a level? There is none anymore, a run cleaner than yours can be faked by someone with some free time and a lack of a sense for what is fundamentally right or wrong. So i turned my attention to innovating, and finding new routes. But even that has lost its interest, it became nothing but a systematic hunt for a better option, the analytical 'brute forcing' of a level to squeeze those frames out of it in an optimised fashion. Finally for me, was total time. I flicked through all the levels, constantly improving them, going through the whole game quite a few times over the period of a month, but now im barely behind johnny (with your total time, you truly are a legend of highscoring, much admiration for that) and i've lost my interest in that too. So its over for me, i cant be bothered playing.

Thanks for the fun times everyone, and the friendly competition with those of you i respect shall forever be a sweet memory to me. It certainly has been a good way to pass 5 months of free time. Expect me to be dropping in occasionally, and to perhaps play 1.5 when it comes out.
