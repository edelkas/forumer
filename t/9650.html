#175419 4 2006-05-13T23:22:11Z Twistkill
Episode 5 is the hardest episode in the first column, in my opinion.

I don't know of any tutorials, but I can give you a few tips.

1. The highscores. I don't watch them that often, but they can be helpful sometimes. Don't worry about how fast or amazing the run is, just try to mimic their path at your own speed.

2. Walljumping. I assume you know how to walljump, but you have to time them right so you can be good at the game. Try jumping at them from the edge of a slope to get up the wall fast, or try jumping back and forth between 2 walls, if available. If you just have to traverse on really big wall, you must hold the jump button to get the biggest jump, and then jump as soon as you hit the wall. (Before you start to wallslide)

3. Thwumps. They can be elevators to high areas or transports across dangerous areas if you manage to jump on top of them. When they're moving fast horizontally, you must run with them because they're not considered a solid edge.

4. Gold. Try to get plenty of gold. You might think it's pointless to waste 6 seconds getting 10 pieces of gold, but every piece of gold gives you an extra 2 seconds, so they are helpful in the long run.

5. Don't hesitate. You must be aggressive in jumping and running. 1 wasted second could mean death. Always be alert.

That's some of the advice I could come up with off the top of my head. Hope this helps you.

#177625 20 2006-05-19T05:20:45Z Ryuk
1. Try doing all the other levels in different columns. Once you know for sure that you're stuck, go back and do the first few. You'll be surprised in yourself.

2. Try, try again. I know it's difficult at first, but if you spend a while on an episode, you're bound to get it. It took me at least 3 weeks to beat MTI, episode 88 level 4, but after a while, I got it.

3. Try to make a routine. Try to figure out which way would be the best way to go and keep practicing that way. Don't go for a completely random way every time.

4. Don't get mad. Trust me, getting mad won't help you at all. You may get frustrated throughout the game. If you do, just walk away from your computer. Killing your ninja 30 times after getting mad won't help. It's useless and somewhat immature.

5. Listen to other people. Chances are that if they've beaten the game and they actually check the level, they may have some excellent insight.

Most of all, try to have fun. It's only a game, remember that. Hope this helps!

#178621 31 2006-05-21T15:20:19Z jtbandes
I've beaten the game too. Here are some tips:
(You can please quote me on these if other people ask for help)

Jtbandes's 10 Tips for Beating N:
1. Breathe.
2. Breathe in AND out.
3. Try not to get frustrated or angry. If you do, take a break. Sometimes even a break as long as a day or more.
4. Watch the highscores. They have good routes. If some of the higher ones are over your head, watch the last few highscores.
5. If you feel you are really stuck on a level, pick a different column and go on. I think many people, including myself, skipped to the 90s when they hit MTI (88-4). Come back to the other level later.
6. HAVE FUN! Really, that's the whole point of a game, not to get mad at it. Learn to have fun while beating levels.
7. Practice, practice, practice. I often am seen picking a random episode that I already beat and playing it again to practice. Even the first column.
8. While playing a difficult level, devise a route that works as you go. Follow that route the same way each time, and each time you will get a little further and add on to your route. This also work with a fixed route, when you can devise a technique for each part of the route.
9. Believe in yourself. I don't consider myself an extremely good player, but I still managed to beat the game. If I can, so can you.
10. Don't give up!

Good luck!
