That idea came to my mind just a minute ago: Why shouldn't you be able to color the Ninja the way you want him to?

You could color each arm, each leg, the head and the body in a different Color to your likes. These could and should be stored in the replays as well (It's only about 50 letters at most!), so when playing back a replay (Online Highscore or Ned Demo, doesn't matter) you would see the Ninja colored the way the demo's player has him.

Why? This would give the replays an identification factor, it could make the game and the replays more individual. That way, you could just watch a replay and say "hey, that's *insertusername*". I guess that's a good way of putting a special sign onto a replay without using any words or so.
