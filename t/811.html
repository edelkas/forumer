I am reading the reviews of N and every one of them says the coolest thing about it is the superb animation of the ninja. A good way to complement this without an extreme change to gameplay is introducing a stunt button. It only has an effect while the ninja is airborne; pressing it during a jump makes the ninja do cool flips and somersaults, (as well as slowing the clock during the jump?). However, those who overindulge in acrobatics had best beware, as failure to land a stunt can have backbreaking consequences.

So, what do you think? In my opinion, stunts would be a seamless addition to N's atmosphere.
