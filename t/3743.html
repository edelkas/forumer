Some pass it by as just another Diablo/2 clone, but I say give it a chance. I'm hooked on episode 1 right now, waiting for money to get episodes 2, 3, and 4. Anyways, the game is basically a Diablo-style game, but there isn't randomly generated dungeons, lots of large areas to explore, many items to collect and monsters to slay... and episode 1 is free, so there's no reason not to try it out. Just don't mistake it for a pure Diablo clone... it's more than that.
