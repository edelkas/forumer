1) Unless they're moving right, you have to place them very precisely with the grid off. Its tricky.

2) Rockets only go through doors as a bug (unless the door's still open from n running through). Its almost completely unpredictable and its only function is to ruin perfectly good maps. Unless you're making a KRA DDA, in which case don't count on it working the first 50000 times anyways.

3) If you touch a launchpad in the same frame that you are "killed" in debug mode, you will survive it. You survive in normal mode too, but become a "ghost" and can't interact with anything, including the exit, so you might as well be dead

4) Don't. Its not worth it. It requires excessive manual code alteration and requires the player to load two maps consequetively to get it to work, and the first will show the fake tiles anyways. Unless you mean just the crazy NaNed "buggy" tiles that fling you across the map or kill you at random. For that you just need to insert a few random characters into the tile data.
