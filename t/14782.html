I'm sorry if this has been asked before, but what is the point of boss mode?
#276361 2 2006-12-24T06:57:39Z LittleViking
The main idea of boss mode is that if you're playing N at work and your boss walks by, you can press that button and your boss won't see the game you're playing. It also lowers CPU usage in case you want to do other things while playing N, and if you encounter the Ned bug where sounds repeat forever, going to boss mode will stop the sound.
