The following is a comparison between Clinton and Obama for the democratic ticket.

I don't really understand completely why there is so much hate directed at Hillary Clinton; while I'm sure a lot of it is based on actual dislike for her politics, I know a lot of it is almost... instinctual. I think for some reason we all know we're supposed to hate her, without knowing why. I personally think she'd be better at foreign policy than Barack, though both would be worlds better than G.W. Though, it just comes down to the fact that, because of this widespread hate, Hillary would be much too divisive to our country's politics, when what we need right now is for our politics to heal. I've even heard some democrats saying that they'd like her to be elected just because they know it'd piss off a lot of conservatives... that's just wrong. That's just horrible. I think that she'd be a fine president, but one thing I object to is the fact that she's been in the pocket of the insurance industry for a while now, and her new health care plan, while well-intentioned, goes about things the wrong way by basically guaranteeing insurance companies even more money.
I've been following Barack Obama's career for a while, since before he became a candidate. I even wrote an article for my high-school newspaper two years ago about him, because I was pretty impressed. I think he's likely sold out a little, during his presidential run... let me rephrase that: he's likely sold out a lot. I wish there was a reasonable, intelligent person who could run for president without relying on advisers and speechwriters constantly. But I still think he's the best bet, and he's impressed me by staying out of the flame-war that the media seems to want him to be in against Hillary. He might not be as helpful for our international image as Clinton would be (from what I can tell, she's already generally well-liked overseas), but I think he would do some good in improving relations.

Overall, I support Obama, the main tipping-point being the fact that President Hillary would be much too unhealthy for our current political climate.
