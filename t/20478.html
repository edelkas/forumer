The Story



The year is 2040. Communism has broken out in the United States. The old government has been
taken over by world domination psychos. Laws have gotten impossibly strict. Immediate execution is to anyone who disobeys. There is one outlaw in the US. He has never been captured. He is now wanted dead or alive. The communists devise a plan to capture him as he has also cause a lot of trouble to the government trying to destroy it.


A loved one is currently in the communist's captivity. Now his mission is to break in and escape with the person.

Where you come in



I am the outlaw/spy.
You are the constraints.

You basically tell what happens on my mission and I decide what to do.
I control the outlaw, you control everything outside of me.



Power


I have two laser guns, two extra nuclear batteries, and a compact laser blaster(for mass destruction, just in case.)


Communist soldiers have simalar weapons but slightly more advanced.



What happened so far



I started my mission as follows:

I drive my hovercar without a license plate 1409 miles across a few states to the headquarters of the communists where the hostage is in captivity.
I'm currently in Nevada, my destination is Vermont.
