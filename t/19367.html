Week Two

For Why Must It Be So Shiney?, by Maximo, you have to collect any 4 gold.

A little note for future reference: If you collect more gold than the competition requires of you, 3 seconds will be deducted off of your time per extra gold piece.

--

About

Every week players will compete against each other in trying to get the fastest possible no-gold demos on a set three maps posted here every Wednesday. Each player has a week in which to perfect their no-gold runs, and submit their demos to me via Personal Message on the forum. Any demos received after 11:59PM (GMT) Tuesday will be ignored.

The fastest three demos I receive for each map will be put here, and the creators of each demo will get a score; 1st place gets 3 points, 2nd gets 2 points, and 3rd gets 1 point. The highest possible score you can receive in a single week is 9 points (excluding improvements).
Improvements on existing demos can get you up to 3 points. If the fastest demo on a map was 86.875, and you improved it by 0.025-0.050, you would receive 1 point, if you improved it by 0.075-0.100 you would receive 2 points, and if you improved it by 0.125 or more you would receive the maximum 3 points. You can not submit improvements to your own demos.

--

Rules
  • Please only submit userlevel-friendly demos. Either copy the demo straight from your userlevels, or put the map data before the demo data (without spaces). Any non-userlevels friendly demos will be ignored.
  • Only submit honest demos - Speed-hacking, fbfing, demo editing, and any other forms of cheating demos are strictly forbidden. If I believe you are found to be cheating, you will be removed from the competition, your score and all previous demos received from you will be removed.
  • Copying someone elses demo is forbidden. A submitted demo must be your own. This is why I want the demos PM'd to me, rather than letting everyone see them.
