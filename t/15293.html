... and it's FREAKIN' AMAZING. "Tetris-like" is the vaguest possible description of it; you're given a bunch of blocks with numbers on them, and you must add (combining two blocks gives a new block with their sum) them near blocks which already have that sum to destroy them. Blocks slowly rise from the bottom.

The game sports online highscores. Check out the maniac in 1st place This is easily going on my list of high-quality freeware games.
