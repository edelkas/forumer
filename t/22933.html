Now, I wasn't sure where to post this, and I couldn't find a 'NUMA Suggestions' topic in any recent activity, so I decided to just say this in a new topic. I'm sorry if that's morbidly wrong or something.

So here's my idea for NUMA. I'm not sure if it's been brought up before, but I think that we should be able to subscribe to our favorite authors, like on YouTube. This way, we could have a box on our Profile page (or any other sensible location) very similar to the one that shows your recent maps on the Profile page. There could also be a "Subscribers" and "Subscriptions" tab on each author's home page, also reflecting those of YouTube, but, of course, abiding to N(ew)MA's layout.

I believe that this idea is sensible because it saves a lot of time. I like to check in on my favorite authors to see if I missed anything, but that requires navigating their Profile page. I'm not saying that's a devastating task, but wouldn't it be so much easier to just subscribe to the author and go straight to their map from the box on your own Profile page?

Let me know what you all think. Comments and critique are very much appreciated.

Thanks for your time!

~Kimmie~

#446247 17 2008-06-19T03:57:26Z southpaw
This has been suggested numerous times before (once or twice by myself in fact, though I used the deviantArt comparison).

The main argument seems to be split into two ideas:
Argument #1: There are already RSS feeds that NUMA supports that allow you to "subscribe" to your favorite authors, and they update as soon as the page updates.
Argument #2: The majority of NUMA users have no idea how to use an RSS feed and would rather the same idea be integrated with NUMA.

Polls show that the majority of NUMA users agree with Argument #2. You can view the poll by clicking here.

Personally I think it is a wonderful idea, but Arachnid has said that there would be some time before he could think about implementing it, as for the last month his life has been about getting NUMA up and running and needs to take some time for his personal life. I've suggested the idea to him directly but more in the sense of Favouriting Authors, just like Favouriting maps. There wouldn't be automatic updates, but you could have a list of your favourite authors that you could check often. Automatic updates could come soon afterward though.

Anyway, it's been discussed with Arachnid directly and that's the story so far. I do believe he is considering implementing this idea into the site, it'll just be a while.
