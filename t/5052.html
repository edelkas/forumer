#105419 2 2005-10-04T05:50:04Z nevermore
For your first problem - Placing an enemy will affect other enemies. Something to do with lag.
Because of this, I suggest that from now on when you make DDAs, place all of your enemies before everything else.

Second problem - When you're pushing the [L] button, you're still inside of the level data textbox. Click outside of it so that you're not selecting it, and then push L. Because you're still selecting it, you actually type L into the box, and that messes with the map data.
