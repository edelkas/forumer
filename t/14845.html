Who are your top 5 favorite highscorers?

I'll start the list off with my personal top 5:

1. Afterthought
2. blobglob
3. pootify
4. clux
5. Melchoir

Very tough to shorten the list down to just 5, Thundermute, Unsane and Knu would have been my 6, 7 and 8.

I forget if it was pootify or blobglob that first did the jumping off walls in small areas to turn around faster. Pootify invented the thwump corner boost and was a genius innovator, even though he only really stuck to a single episode (56, his scores are still around).

blobglob plays in such a unique way, you'll see 19 carbon copies of a route and you'll see blobglob's route, done completely differently but just as fast. He's one of my favorite players to play against because he always rises to the occasion and pulls out things you'd never think of.

clux can take a level that everyone has thought was closed, and revolutionise it. He's perhaps the greatest player around today.

Melchoir invented the corner jump, he has the greatest taste in levels and he loves making up routes to the most open levels, stuff where you wouldn't even know where to begin.

And Afterthought, well.
