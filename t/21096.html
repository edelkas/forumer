For All Non-Americans

Hamburger Helper is just the pasta noodles and a seasoning packet. You make it either healthy or disgusting - it's the same as a box of seasoned rice. It's all dry and non-perishable. You add the water, and decide whether or not to add any extra sauce. The seasoning packet and water make quite a good mix in almost all of the Hamburger Helper boxes, though.

The "Flavor" on the front of the box simply denotes what type os seasoning is inside.
They're great when you fix them up yourself.
