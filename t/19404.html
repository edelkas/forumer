The Rule:

All of your posts must have a dyslexic quality to it. For example:

I you were to type "Godzilla hates me" you could replace a few letters and voila!

"9oPz!lle ha+a5 Ea"

In case you need an explanation: The "d" in Godzilla to a dyslexic person might look like a "p", or the "t" would look like a "+" symbol, and better yet, the "m" could look like and "E". It's all in the dyslexia. But please, instead of just posting random stuff, make it funny and talk to people about different things.
