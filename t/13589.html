To record it...

1. get the data.
2. Load it or if you have it already, reset it.
3. Press 1, then P, then play the level.
4. Once you have finished press 2.
5. Copy it to where you want.


To test it...
1. Get the demo.
2. Press 3.
3. Then press Q, then P (make sure you have CAPS LOCK ON)
4. Finished.
