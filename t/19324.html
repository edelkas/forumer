Attention! Attention!

I have decided to recruit for a new type of map pack: a tileset pack. The object is to make excellent tilesets: never want for a tileset again, just grab one from the pack and build a level.
Once the mappack is completed, it will be open for people to use. If you want to make a level from any tileset, just ask.

The name of this pack is Mosaicist--I've decided to call it Mosaicism instead so those who make tilesets are known as the Mosaicists. And here's the sig bar for the members, thanks to Animator:

Please post in this thread with your intents for joining, and the number of tilesets you expect to make. You must name each of your tilesets.

Post them in userlevels format in a .txt file. I will compile them into one big .txt file, which can be found here as levels accumulate.

You may submit one or two tilesets from NUMA, but I want the majority of maps to be completely original.

If you submit a map from a tileset in this pack, put a link to this thread on the level description.

Regards,
incluye
