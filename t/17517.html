#325396 21 2007-05-29T17:49:24Z Spawn of Yanni
Those games, such as Worms, are very different from N. For one, they have MUCH larger playing arenas. Which means that even randomly generated maps are guaranteed to be playable, since they have a lot of free space. N doesn't have that much space to begin with.

Also, N is far more complex in terms of contents of a map. A randomly generated map refers only to terrain being created. In N, as well as having ground being created, you need to have a ninja, a door and a key to even make it playable. So when randomly generating a map, you need to hope that none of those things end up inside the ground, or that the key or door don't end up in an area in which the ninja can't enter.

Furthermore, even if the door and key are in space, they might be right next to the ninja, which won't make for a very enjoyable map. In addition to all this, there are enemies to be placed if you want to have a decent map. These can also end up in the ground or in an area in which they have no chance of killing the ninja. And, finally, you can't even consider having doors and such, because chances are they will just end up somewhere in the air. So, I'm for the random map selector, but no map generator, thank you very much.
