MindScape is a flash game on MINDistortion.com, by Manuel Fallman.

An interesting platformer game with an interesting physics system, items to find, medals to earn, and rabbits. The game contains three different areas which you must travel through as you try to escape what a rabbit has told you is 'a delusional state of mind'. Good luck.
