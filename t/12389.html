Copy a frame and then "Paste in Place" for the next frame. I think it's CTRL + Shift + V, or something like that. Anyways it's in a dropdown menu at the top, probably under Edit.

This'll paste the copied entity in its exact same location for the frame it's being pasted into as the frame it was copied from.
