After the One-Life Challenge and the Total Game Score thread, here comes a new challenge : I called it the "Finish the Game Challenge" ! (What a name... If you have a better idea for the name tell it to me ) [Edit : recalled "The Complete a Column Challenge"]

The concept is easy : you just have to complete every levels from 00-0 to 99-4 in the main game ! Then you come here and you post your time (you can note the time you took for each column, too) (edit : the time it took IRL not in the game, if you didn't understand !)

So just don't go for the golds or you'll lose time (I know, it's hard because some people will feel like going for a 0th ^^)

Have fun !

Another edit : please don't use the version of N who loads map faster ^^
