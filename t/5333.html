#4874 1 2005-10-25T03:32:35Z LittleViking
The idea is simple: Post a map and someone will create a demo for it.
Here are the rules.

1. Every map gets a demo.
2. Only three maps can be up at one time. The small number should help avoid confusion and losing maps in the thread.
3. To avoid death of the thread if three impossible-to-beat maps are up, there will be a time limit before they're eliminated. After one week with no demo, a map is taken off the list of three. (This takes precedence over rule one, obviously.)
4. Anyone can post maps. This won't be like the rating thread where you'd have to demo a map before you could post one. I'm sure the new players want demos for their maps too.
5. Only one map per author can be on the list at a time.

Ideally, people will post maps that don't have any demos on them already, but you can also post a map with a specific goal attached.
For the people who do the demos, you can leave comments about the map here if choose. It would definitely be appreciated.

Edit: Added rule 5.
Edit: Just to be safe, and since I didn't mention it before, demoing a map implies a completion demo. You can't just make a quick demo with the ninja dying to get a map off the list.
