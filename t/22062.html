#19741 1 2008-03-30T02:42:31Z Sting::Ray
I was wondering if (in N v1.5) there could be an addition made to allow easy creation of custom episodes, not just levels. Like, along with the "userlevels" button, there would be a "mods" button under "configure". That way, you could choose a mod, and it would take the current episode screen (00-99) and replace it with the amount of episodes in the mod.

Example:

1. You download Legacy 'Origins', newly packaged as a mod (I'll talk about that in the next paragraph)
2. You open N v1.5
3. You find the "mods" button under "configure"
4. You press said "mods" button
5. You scroll through your list of mods, just like userlevels, and pick "Legacy: Origins"
6. You press the "main menu" button at bottom right hand side of screen
7. You press "play"
8. You view Legacy 'Origins' episodes 00-59, get personal bests on them, replays, etc.

Each mod could be contained in a folder.

In the folder, the mod has a 'manifest' of some sort, which has the name, author and description (which would appear in N when you selected it in the list of mods)

From there, using the "robust file-based system", you would have all the level files, perhaps condensed into episodes files for ease of modding the mod.

Also, on the support end, you would add a 'mods' section of NUMA.

Please comment about this; especially whether it's even possible with M&R's current system.
