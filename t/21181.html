#402285 2 2008-01-20T10:25:54Z Weisslenny0
It's quite simple, really.

Up at the top of the screen, you'll see a button that reads "My Controls". Click this. On that page, look to the left. Under 'Personal Profile' is the text "Edit Signature" (http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?act=UserCP&CODE=22). Then scroll down and just type stuff into the text box. Press "update my signature" when you're done, and you will have a signature.

If you're unsure of other things, like adding colors, pictures, and whatever, don't hesitate to PM me (click the "PM" button under my name) and I'll be more than happy to answer. Enjoy!
