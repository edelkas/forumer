#64730 8 2005-05-10T02:58:47Z Daddaluma
A few things to keep in mind.

The most successful NTTS maps are relatively easy to beat. You don't want to have any week where it's too hard/frustrating to even beat the level that no one even bothers trying to compete on things like style and creativity.

A good NTTS map usually offers some freedom so people have a lot of choice in their routes and can be more creative. This is not set in stone of course. And it doesn't mean the level can't be linear or narrow or have an obvious path.

Just look at how much everyone likes trying to get the level 00-0 high score. It's super easy, but there's tons of routes and tricks to it. That kind of level makes for a great NTTS competition.
