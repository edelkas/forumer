#188112 12 2006-06-12T00:14:17Z nevermore
Not ONE person has succesfully explained this to our poor help requester.

Okay. In the game, when you hit an enemy (specifically a rocket, a mine, a thwump, a drone or a floorguard) at the exact same time as hitting a launchpad, you will survive the "death." This is known to most of us as "Cheating Death." In the real game mode, after this happens the only thing you will be able to interact with is the terrain - you will go through all objects and enemies. However, if this happens in debug mode you can continue to play through the level normally, and so this trick is used in many DDAs.

what do u do? Like put 3 bounce pads in 1 place and 3 bounce pads in another?

Nope, you just modify one or both of the last two numbers in the code for the launchpad. Depending on how you do it, this can give you a launchpad that launches you, but only a few pixels, sticking you to it; a launchpad that is simply more powerful than normal; or a teleporter, which is really just a launchpad that makes you go so fast that you are in a completely different place in the next frame.

But this is no place for a teleporter discussion, there are already too many of those threads. Just post in the tutorial thread linked in an above post if you have any questions.

And yes, cheating death happens when you hit an enemy and a launchpad in the EXACT SAME frame.
