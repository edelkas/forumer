#113339 2 2005-11-09T02:24:18Z Maniac
This takes some work.

Basically, the ninja needs to be moving into the trapdoors at a relatively high speed. After that, you need to *very* delicately move the trigger until it works. I can't really say much else.

Don't overuse it though, the trapdoors can take up space in your DDA and restrict movement.
