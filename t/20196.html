#380436 5 2007-11-18T04:28:29Z Ditocoaf
Idk... this is in the same vein as enemy-interaction; it doesn't seem to fit in with the style of N. Currently, there is no way that enemies can help you. To take it further: enemies don't do anything but kill you. (They have various ways of moving, and various ways of extending themselves to kill you, but the principle is intact: they kill you. that's it, no freezing you or doing anything else to you, no effecting eachother or anything else.)

This would create more depth to the game, but part of what makes N so fun is it's simplicity. The aspects of this game, taken in its parts, are very few, and very simple. It's the infinite combinations and arrangements, which create whole new concepts in themselves, that make it so big, and so limitless. Anything else added to this game would have to uphold that principle, and add to the existing patterns, rather than breaking them.

(The one obvious exception to this rule is if R&M decide to change a basic aspect of the game, and it's awesome.)
