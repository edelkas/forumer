#154303 14 2006-03-13T08:15:24Z tktktk
I think exact timing like "1.25 seconds" would be too hard to keep track of. Here's the best explanation I can give:

1. Hold right (or left) so that you're on horizontal ground and against a vertical wall.

2. Hold right (or left) for the duration of this tutorial.

3. Press the jump button twice as quickly as possible, and hold it the second time.

4. Briefly release the jump button just before you come into contact with the wall.

5. Immediately press and hold the jump button again right as you come into contact with the wall.

6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 as needed. Remember, if you time step 4 or 5 incorrectly it will result in a significant decrease in momentum.
