That's 112 589 990.6842624 km, or roughly one-hundred-and-twelve million kilometres.

One Astronomical Unit (AU) is the mean distance between the Earth and Sun, and is given (by Wikipedia) as "about one-hundred-and-fifty million kilometres", which is off of our estimate by about thirty-eight million kilometres.
