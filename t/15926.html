This is just one giant mountain of frustration from me, that's been building up over the last couple of weeks. There are two vague areas of fake people whom I hate. The first is people who aren't true to themselves; people who put on acts every day, every word that comes out of their mouths is to project an image of someone else. Someone they're not. The second kind of fake person, the kind of person I'm angrier with now, are fake nice people. People who act like they're your friend, or at least on friendly terms with you. People who say bad things about you behind your back. These people are generally manipulative, arrogant, and deceptive. Or at least they have been, in my experience.

For example, I was talking with an aquaintence, someone I thought I was on friendly terms with, today. She's a friend of one of my friends, and we got to talking. I don't really want to get in to the specifics, but we made friendly conversation for a while, we talked about current goings on at our school, it was all a very pleasant experience. We talked about something that had been bothering me lately, something I was feeling down about, to a certain extent. I realized midway through this part of the conversation that this is what she had come to me to talk about. I ended up explaining to her why I was upset, and how I was then currently feeling. She gave me a complement, one that took me off guard a bit, but one that I appreciated greatly. Overall, a pleasant experience. So I thought anyways.

One of my friends talked to her later that day, talking about me, in which she remarked simply: "I don't really like him." This statement in and of itself would make very little difference to my life, and I would probably have ignored it, were it not for the element of deception, and of manipulation. It felt like she was being nice to improve my thoughts of her, to make me think that she's a better person. Well, it fucking worked. For half a day. I thought she was just... actually nice. I felt, in a weird way, kinda backstabbed by her when I found out about all this shit.

Fake nice people are just a giant object of hate for me. I can't fucking stand them.

I just felt like venting my anger here, hope you guys don't mind.
