#135579 10 2006-01-24T23:23:49Z Atilla
Maps are put in the playable or non-playable sections purely based on their category. You chose to classify your map as N-Art, which is a non-playable category (look at some of the other N-Art). It should be in action, even though it has a theme.

Some of the problems with the level are:

There's loads of gold stacked in the top left corner for no good reason.

There are mines caddishly hidden behind gold. This requires the player to be psychic rather than skillful, especially since the placing is rather inconsistant.

There is gold which adds little to the playability of the level. Gold should generally be used as an incentive for doing things the difficult way, or a reward for doing something well (such as jumping from the very edge of a platform).

There's a large, empty bit with no enemies and no flow. This is not always a problem, but in this case it makes part of the level a bit boring.

Possibly a bit drone-heavy. It could cause lag on slow computers.

Still, it's not too bad for your first level.
