Code name? Radical. Just sounds cooler .

Part One: What's Radical?

Let's travel back in time three months. Summer. Yes, the season with the warm weather, free time, and pretty colors. I, having some summer fun and free time, decide to open N on a whim to make some levels. Suddenly, I'm struck by this abstract, creative spirit. At this point, it's clear to me that I wish to make a map pack.

Fast forward about three months.
Enter four great guys who I can't manage to thank enough:
Krusch, Baraba, Aju, and Sept
Four who kept the pack a secret (despite my idiocy), tested and critiqued my levels, and drove and supported me through this.

Radical is a map pack, made for your enjoyment. A thank you pack, if you will.
50 levels of my efforts, all for you to have fun playing. Enjoy, guys.

Part one ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------OVER


Part Two: A little bit about Radical

A'ight, let's get something straight here before we get down to playing these levels. There are fifty levels in here. About 125 were created in total. 75 were wiped out, destroyed. All 50 have undergone at least one coat of revisions. These levels are not just a little time consumer, I put a lot of time and effort in them.

Before you go, OMGZ YOU'RE COPYING KRUSHC, realize that they are NOT rip-offs. Why would krusch, aju, or baraba support or enjoy them if they were? Play them, and you'll soon realize that they are not ripoffs.

Aju's favorites list: 1-0, 7-4, 8-4, 1-1, 4-0, 6-0, 6-1, 9-3, 8-2, 0-2.

My favorites: 00-2, 02-1, 04-0, 04-1, 04-4, 05-1, 06-1, 07-4, 08-2, 09-2.

A most curious levelpack.

I *love* the graphical style of it. It reminds me of construction, building, DIY and workbenches. It gives the levels a haphazard, fun and spontaneous quality.

I do feel you could have had some more variation worked in one way or the other. As some have pointed out it all felt quite similar, but it's still a joy to play all the same, with all the levels being of uniform difficulty (or should that be ease).

Overall: Fun, memorable, a little too random in places (the level names spring to mind here)
