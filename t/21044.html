I dont know if you have any examples where you live, but for me there is an obvious one;

Drug dealers.

In Beijing all the 'aggressive' (as opposed to passive) drug dealers are black. By this i mean that the drug dealers who are on the streets or in bars and clubs activly pushing drugs are black. There are white people dealing drugs in Beijing, but they deal only to those who know them, and they dont try and get sales from strangers.
I have never ever witnessed a low-level chinese drug dealer. Obviously the chinese do most of the growing in china, but they sell it on in bulk to non-chinese. They never involve themselves in the actual distribution to drug-takers.

It seems to me that this situation is caused by discrimination, and discrimination is caused by this situation - catch-22 stlye.

Let me explain - blacks currently dominate the market on drug dealing (specifically nigerians, but other african nationalitys work for them too), and anyone who wants to buy drugs in Beijing will talk to a black person to do it. This means that if any other races of people try and sell drugs, they will have less succsess than blacks, because people wont immediatly presume they are dealers. They will have to find all the sales themselves, rather than some coming to them.

Also, the Nigerians controll the specific areas that are known to drug takers - particular bars are used solely for the purpose of selling and taking drugs, and in these bars it would be impossible for a non-black to sell.

Thirdly, Nigerians activly bring in other Nigerains (generally under false pretenses) and coerce them into sellng drugs. No-other race currently does that in Beijing.


This, clearly, causes the government to discriminate against blacks - it is extrememly difficult for a nigerian to get a visa for more than one month, for example, and occasionally they will do a raid on the particular areas in beijing where drugs are sold, focusing wholly on blacks.

Example.



Are situations like this common? Or is Beijing, as the capital of the worlds most advanced third-world country, extermemly bad for it?

And, more importantly; Do you think that the fact that blacks are controlling the drug market in Beijing means that it is fair.. no, reasonable to commit such clearly discriminant acts as happened in the drug raids? Do you think there is any other way the government could deal with the problem?

Personally i think they need to stop supply before they have a chance at stopping the street sellers - for a week or so after the raids you wouldnt see a black face in Sanlitun (the main drinking area), but now there are as many drug dealers as before, albeit mostly new faces.

They deported a handfull of dealers (generally under immigration charges - most of the Nigerains sell their passports when they arrive), but more are arriving all the time.
And unless they stop the drugs from arriving in Beijing, then no matter how many dealers they arrest, they will never stop them from returning in stength.



---

BTW, the people of Bejing are not at all racist - on the very rare occasion that they see an african they treat them as a sort of specatacle, but not in anything of a negative way. It is just the police and government that have problems with them.
