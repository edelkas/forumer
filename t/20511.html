#386607 39 2007-12-05T16:13:05Z blue_tetris
In pigments, the primary colors are red, yellow, and blue. The secondary colors are orange, violet, and green. These are only the primary colors because of how they mix, and the purity of the color compared to its lightness and saturation. Yellow pigments have high saturation, while greens and reds have poor saturations and will mix to a duller color. That's why, when you mix green and red paints, you get brown and not gray; brown is just a dark shade of yellow.

In light, the primary colors are red, blue, and green. The secondary colors are yellow, cyan, and magenta. Growing up, I used to have this green light out back by a shed in our yard with another standing lamp out in the yard, further away from it. If I held my hand between the light and the wall of the shed, a magenta-colored shadow was cast upon the shed. It was an illusion of the eye, but it was phenomenal to look at. It appeared quite vividly to be magenta--the precise hue.
