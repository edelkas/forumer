I see it as something that could work well with more 'high-speed' levels...perhaps a level that required you to stay ahead of enemies more than out-maneuvering them (maybe in a spiral or something). To avoid it screwing up old levels it could require some sort of ramp or strip (maybe not even visible, just a 'wall-run' zone that is set in the level editor). Doesn't seem like it would be too game-changing, just a matter of converting the velocity that normally pushes you away from a wall into upwards movement.
I don't think it would make a level easier necessarily, it would just be one more thing to allow you to maintain smooth movement as you make it through a room. Wall jumping works great, but it seems like it could add some cool variety, especially when you have to get out of the way of a bot by staying on one side of a wall but still maintain your upward movement.
