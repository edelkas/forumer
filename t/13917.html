Comet, Butterfly, and Sword.

[obligatory introduction]
THIS IS THE FUCKING COOLEST MOVIE EVER!!!!!!!!!!!!!
[/obligatory introduction]

I absolutely guarantee you that you will say, on at least half a dozen occassions, "what the FUCK!?" while watching this movie.

What is this movie, you ask? It's ridiculous, that's what it is. It's fucking ridiculous.

My favorite scene is when Sing attacks a government agent and his men by falling out of a tree, putting his feet into his bow in mid-air, launching himself from the goddamned bow because it fucking works that way, and then rockets past the agent's men as they literally fucking explode around him for no reason other than that he flew by them.
And who could forget that scene where Sister Ko turns her dress into a goddamned composite longbow and a pair of arrows and fucking launches those very same twisted-up pieces of soft, cushy silk at a bunch of guys on horses, killing them instantly.
Oh, and how 'bout that scene where the big bad guy is defeated with a rubber ball.

Other wierd shit... God, there's so much of it...
Ninjas fly by twirling their shuriken like fucking helicopter blades.
Sister Ko beheads everyone using a narrow strip of cloth.
Some jackass playing with a rubber ball misses the ball in his attempt to catch it, and so he clicks his heels together to spontaneously change his direction in mid-fucking-air.

This movie is artistic.
It's basically a huge kick in science's balls.

See it.
After I saw that first scene that I just described, with the launching of the self from self's own longbow in the middle of a fall, I could not stop laughing for at least twenty minutes. I was on the floor. I had to rewind the movie about that much to see what the hell I missed.
