#375180 3 2007-10-29T01:37:37Z scythe33

The black part is relatively straightforward. The blue part, though, is weird. Slide on the wall as soon as you can so the ninja slows all the way down, then press right, and try and make him fall in between the bounceblock and the wall without hitting the mines. It's frustrating, but possible. It'll take a few tries. If you really can't pull it off, try sliding before the mine right above the left bounceblock and jumping onto the bounceblock. Then go down.
The drone might catch you, though.
