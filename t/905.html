No, N2 will not be shareware.

We have a number of rantings expressing our views in our various interviews () and our corporate whitepapers ().

Check there, and the for an idea of our plans for upcoming features.



Quote: FireBall, 2004-09-13T07:22:47Z
N II is going to have 3D Texture's and some anti-alias and to make a panel in N II it is a polygon Panel

It will most likely NOT have 3D textures, because we have emphasized many times that N's graphics are the way they are for a whole mess of reasons, including these:
-the minimalist style allows gameplay to attain the focus
-minimalism references that state of enlightenment found by a player who has followed the way of N

Which isn't to say the graphics in N2 will be the same as those in N; we just know they won't be crazy different.

there already is anti-aliasing, inherant in the flash player, which we will keep 'cause it looks nice. Although, whether we're using Flash again is as yet undecided.

N is a tile based game. At the moment, we don't really see a reason to change it to a poly-based game; however, we are in the middle of developing a polygon engine for other games.

We will most likely release the editor with N2, since I can't think of any reason not to.

One last caution -- we're not yet finished with N1, so we won't be getting to N2 for a while yet. What I've said here could certainly change!
