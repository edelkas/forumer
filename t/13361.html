#245022 2 2006-10-23T19:02:33Z Mongy
It's not really a "glitch." It's just that adding that gauss changes the firing order. You see, not all the enemies fire at the same time. They each target you and fire one at a time. Adding another gauss screws up the order because the gauss will fire first, since it was the last thing placed. Therefore, the other enemies while fire later than they were supposed to, and the DDA is screwed up.

I messed up a really good DDA once like this because I foolishly added an extra rocket to it. It screwed the whole thing up, and unfortunately I hadn't saved. I lost the whole thing
