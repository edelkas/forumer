Chosen Destiny

Have you ever looked over your shoulder because you thought someone was watching? Have you ever felt like someone was following you? You might want to check your back again, your instincts might be right.

Chapter 1

You have to breath, come on! shouted the doctor over the shuffle of medical staff and equipment. The woman on the bed was breathing in short, quick gasps, in and out hurriedly as the pressure and pain accumulated.
Come on now, one more big push, one more come on! the doctor urged as the baby started to slide out. The woman took one big breath and pushed with all her might. The nurse at her side squeezed her hand a little harder and held her breath with the woman. The rest of the baby slid out and the doctor quickly flicked it on the foot to get it to cry. Its a boy! he proudly announced as he cut the umbilical cord. He handed the newly born boy to a nurse and he was taken close to the new mother so she could see her creation.
When she said she was done, the nurse took the baby away to have its health checked. The woman looked at the doctor as tears streamed down her smiling face, but the doctor was busy updating his records, and failed to return the smile. When the doctor was done he exited the room and entered the washroom. He washed his hands and took off his mask and operating hat. He went into his office and sat down at his desk.
He powered up his computer and sat back as the operating system loaded. His phone started to ring, Beep beep beep. He ignored it at first and continued waiting for his computer to finish loading. Beep beep beep, the phone rang again. He glanced at it and ignored it once again. The computer finished loading as the phone rang a third time, Beep beep beep. He hit the speaker button.
Doctor Lewis speaking.
Hello Doctor, how are you today, the man on the phone replied.
I'm a little tired but good, may I ask whos calling?
An old friend, replied the man in a flat voice.
Oh, of course. I was expecting a call today, but that was fast.
We had an angel tell us of the birth, how is he doing?
The subject, hes fine. Started crying right up with a flick on the heel. My nurse said he was in good health on her initial inspections.
Excellent, Excellent. Richard will be happy to hear his new subject is alive and well. We are already sending a couple more angels over to make sure everything goes as planned.
Fine by me.
Has she named him yet? I can only hope it is a strong name, the man said, with a smirk on the end.
Why yes, his name is Ricky Joseph Forte.
Not the best, but it will suffice. Well doctor, send us a copy of all his files when you get them, and make sure no one sees you copying them. I have to go now, Richard is expecting me to come talk about our subjects. With that the phone clicked off and the doctor turned to his computer. He jiggled the mouse to turn off the screen saver. He moved the cursor on the screen over to the icon to load up the hospitals database. He double clicked and a window popped up asking for a password. He input the password elohim. The computer screen went black for a second and then a screen with a list of every person ever born in the hospital appeared. He clicked on the button labeled new entry while he reached over to the phone to call the nurse.
He pressed the speaker button and dialed the head nurses number. His attention turned to the computer as he typed in the new entry. His fingers skillfully covered the keyboard and the words Forte, Ricky Joseph appeared on the screen. The nurse answered the phone, Nurse Trudy, how may I help you?
Hi Trudy, Lewis here, I need the record for the subject copied and brought to my office right away.
Already have them copied sir. Ill bring them down to you personally.
Good, good, your always a step ahead of me arent you Trudy, He said in a friendly tone, Oh, and would you inform the receptionist that a few more angels are in route.
Certainly sir. With that the doctor hung up the phone and turned back his computer. By now he had entered the babys birthday into the computer, July 15, 2004.
The doctor turned around in his chair and looked out of the window. The July mornings sunshine was pouring through the window. The old oak tree outside the window cast crooked shadows into the room as the light filtered through its tangle of branches and leaves. The doctor heard a bird calling to its mate in a high-pitched chirp. He felt the warmth of the sun wash over him and for this moment, he forgot what he was taking part in.
Just then there was a knock at the door, and the doctor snapped out of his daydream. Who is it? he inquired and turned around in his chair to face the door. Trudy, Came the reply, I have the subjects records with me.
Come on in, Lewis said as he turned to the computer and opened his email inbox. Trudy opened the door and brought the documents over to the doctors desk. She set the packet of papers down in front of him and turned to sit in one of the armchairs. The doctor didnt look up but continued to read his emails. The nurse coughed to get his attention and the doctor looked up. He smiled at her and thanked her for her speedy completion of his task. He then proceeded to flip through the pages of information on the subject.
Every thing is here, thanks again. You may go now. The doctor said, Also please inform me when the angels arrive.
Your welcome and I will, the nurse said as she stood. She turned and headed out of the room, paying careful attention to close the door behind her. Lewis got up and headed over to the door and locked it. He then went over to his desk and picked up the babys information. He took it to his fax machine and proceeded to fax the papers to the man from the phone call.

At the time there were only a handful of rooms in use, as there is only one of the Omega Projects currently in progress.  The importance of this project, and the amount of people assigned to it has made it hard, if not impossible, for Destiny Ops to start and run any more projects.

Anywhere, he could have gone anywhere.

Off a cliff, off a bridge, into a wall, anywhere, Chris said panicking.
Oh no, Jess said, a great revelation had just come to her, I know where he is.
For Gods sake, where?
Chris did he go into the bathroom and get something from under the sink? Jess said more worried than before.
Yes, but I couldnt see, it was too dark.  Why, whats under there?

His father, what? he said confused.
The man that has been his father since before he was born, she said, not wanting to believe it.
Oh no, he cant.  How could he? Chris said, finally understanding what Jess had already figured out.
He got the gun from underneath the sink, and he is going to go kill Richard.

Stop Ricky, we know who you are.  You can not get away! the guard called out.  It meant nothing to Ricky.  He sprinted through the double doors and up the staircase.  Before he reached the top his gun was in his hand, the safety switched off.

All for science Ricky, youre a man of science you should understand.

Congratulations again, you and Rich really pulled one out of your hat this time.
