#233607 19 2006-09-26T03:24:08Z Atilla
Not necessarily. You forget that WoW and suchlike are actually programmed in a suitable language, rather than Flash, which struggles with complex games.

Also, I dislike this idea. I think it would make level design painfully difficult in many cases, and it would make several map styles nearly impossible. For example, what if I want to force the player to jump while being chased by a floorguard? I can't put mines on the floor because they'll blow the floorguard up. Likewise for avoiding zap drones/thwumps in a minefield. Half the levels would come down to getting most of the enemies to wipe each other out before waltzing casually to the exit, and I don't think that really suits N. Robotology, maybe... and the different language would also (hopefully) negate the lag problems.
