#121895 4 2005-12-10T21:30:38Z Aries_Griff
For me there were a few tricky parts to this level. One, being down on the bottom, not getting killed by the floor guards is hard. That just takes skill and practice. But beyond that, getting back through the middle part on the way back up has some tricky jumps. My advice for that is to either fall off to the side of where you're jumping and wall slide that edge, then jump for some of those jumps, and for the short jump in the middle, I recommend getting on the corner, then tap right so you start tilting over the edge, then tap the jump button to make that hop so you don't hit the piece that hangs down. From there it's easy street.
