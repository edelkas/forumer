Well, I saw in anothing thread that someone had mentioned having different colored ninjas be unlockable with their own specific strengths. I thought this was pretty cool, but I didn't think the idea of different ninjas was all that fitting with N.

However, it would fit very nicely to just have different colored suits that the ninja could unlock. I don't know how many have read/researched at all about real ninja, but most wore dark blue/green/red/grey (the reasoning being that at night, in shadows, cook cloth is noticably darker than the surrounding shadows; also, as for red, it may have been used to frighten enemies because of the connection to blood etc.) So it would be very fitting that you be unable to unlock different colored outfits that imbue different strengths (higher jumping, but slower running etc.) Of course this could be made not to count towards highscores etc... I personally feel that this would be really cool to have
