#302797 3 2007-03-05T22:59:20Z Hendor
I would recommend Attilas too. Other than that, here are a couple "moves" that people frequently use. I've also put a level where the top highscore incorporates it in brackets:

Perpendicular jump (see 88-1): This is when the ninja runs up a slope and then springs up much higher than you can normally jump. Heres how you do it: Let's say you're running right and there is a slope ahead. You hold right and run up onto the slope. Right at the instant you want to jump, release right and hit jump. There's this little glitch that sometimes causes you to skid instead of jumping after you release right, but after some practise, you can avoid this most times.

Corner Jump (see 90-1): Well, not much to this. Just skim the corner and press jump. There is only about 1 frame where pressing jump will actually make you do a big corner jump, so these are still a large part luck, even for the best of players.

Large Jumps off of Bounce Blocks (see 10-2): These jumps are actually two jumps. They require you to land on the bounce block following a fairly big fall. It will sag under him and will then pop back up. The goal is to jump right when the ninja gets near the very bottom of the "sag", and then once again as soon as possible after. These can be done vertically(off the top) and horizontally(off the side). They are actually relatively easy to do and most highscorers can do them 99% of the time given a large enough fall before hitting the bounce block.

If you think of any others I can explain them, but I think thats good for now.
