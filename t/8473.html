#157511 3 2006-03-24T20:23:46Z iangb
Weird Noob indeed.

In the main menu of N, press the \ key. That's the one to the left of Z. If that doesn't work, try @. That will get you into Ned. Ned is the level editor.
Have a look at the files readme.txt, and editor_manual.txt to find out how to use Ned.

Which button you should press depends on which language your keyboard is based on.
I'm from sweden, and I have to press the "" key to get to debug menu. Once you're there, you press "e" or "t" to get to ned. (I would recommend using T). You can also try "~" if you're not from Norway, and have to use the AE key.
