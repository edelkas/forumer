If anyone has played an online riddler such as God Tower, notpr0n, or especially Dumb: The Game, you'll find these riddles to be (somewhat of) a daunting task. I assure you, I don't intend these riddles to be incredibly hard, but not always will your own knowledge be enough to solve. Get together and put your own analysis' to find the correct answer.

RULES:
- Any discussion of the riddle is permitted.
- Hints will be distributed over time.
- First person to PM the answer and explanation gets the credit. The answer and explanation are to be judged by myself, and as long as you stay within reasonable distance of the answer and theory, you won't be rejected.
- In the event of a team entry, send a single PM naming all involved in the solution.
- If I drop a big hint in IRC, I'll post here. Otherwise, it'll only spread by word-of-mouth.

TIPS:
- Think too small, and you'll miss a clue. Think too big, and you'll catch yourself a red herring. Take breaks while doing a puzzle to get fresh views on what you're doing.
- The title is ALWAYS a hint. It could be one word. It could be the whole thing. Hell, there might even be multiple hints. Again, watch out for herrings.

With that said, lets start the show. Good luck to you all!
