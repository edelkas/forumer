#177522 2 2006-05-19T00:32:51Z Atilla
Do you have a working NUMA account? If not, create one by opening N, going to the configure menu, filling in the boxes in the bottom right, and clicking "Create new user".

Once you have an account, go to NUMA and sign in. Then click on "Submit map". Fill in the level description, name, type, and so on. For the level data, use the raw data N produces (NOT "$Name#Author#Type#Raw data#" like you would in your userlevels.txt).
