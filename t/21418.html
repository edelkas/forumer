Sweep, it depends. Do you have a cheap black sheet? If so, you can quickly make a ninja costume with a pair of very sharp scissors and a bit of duct tape. Leave your elbows and forearms bare-too much work for too little effort-put on black shoes if you can, and any sort of black glove.

Do not, period, double up the thickness of the sheet. Try and let layers overlap as little as possible. This might not be easy at the knee and hip, though.
