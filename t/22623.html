I moved my Alienware Area-51 (in a glossy Aurora chassis) from a dank corner under my desk (duh) to on top of my desk and cleaned out my video cards and air filters and fans. I reconnected my mouse, keyboard, miscellaneous cables and such back up after moving my PC from a ground position to an elevated, non-dusty position.

I punched in my nifty network key and got internet working nicely (one "bar" quicker according to 2Wire), too.

After gutting my chips, air filters, and removing roughly 4oz of dust.
FOUR. OUNCES. OF. DUST.
Do you have any idea how much dust that is? Thats a lot of freaking dust!

But even though it's nothing fancy, I'm proud of myself, because I did all of this solo.

I can finally feel my front-facing illuminated fans blowing air into my PC, now. I can also hear the healthy whurl of my fans, and I can see a paper on the wall behind my PC actually move due to air displacement from my two rear fans.

Huzzah!
