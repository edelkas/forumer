Quote: Chase, 2007-12-11T15:18:16Z
Hello. Just to forewarn those who haven't recieved it yet, a virus is spreading around Windows Live Messenger users fast! Via the use of .zip files posing as pictures.

Whatever you do, do not save .zip files to your computer via MSN Messenger, Windows Live Messenger, or any IM program you have.

The issue isn't with saving .zips. Firstly, since this isn't to do with an execution vulnerability in Messenger, saving it won't infect your computer. Secondly, opening the .zip won't infect your computer eitherZIPs don't execute (unless you're using a malicious or broken application to open them). According to the article, the .zip contains two files which aren't actually images, but instead a .exe and a .pif. These are what will spread the virus.

I think the real message to take away from this is to be wary of executables (.exe/.pif/.com) and file types you don't recognise.

Also, when you think about it, ".zip" is a funny sounding name.
