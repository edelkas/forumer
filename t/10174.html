So, I recently caught up with an old friend on mine. A friend who, whilst we were playing gamecube, casually remarked to me that one could unlock gigabowser if he beat even match 51 with bowser himself.

He also remarked something about beating the left masterhand in less than 1.5 minutes and earning him as a secret character, but I've finished the whole two hand thing in less than 50 seconds using almost solely gannondorf's up + a smash move, so that's rubbish.

(Although, one _can_ fight the left master hand if one removes 150% damage on the right masterhand, on difficulty normal, in the normal game mode. (Classic.) However, while I've never done this without looseing a life, I'm fairly sure it's not meant to unlock any other characters, since it _does_ give you about an 80,000 point bonus.)

Okay. Anyway. So, I see this as a promising sign that maybe, just maybe, giga bowser could be unlocked...
He's a different character to the normal bowser. Most of his moves are the same (but not all), he looks _similar_ (but not entirely the same, especially around the head) and this is all rather remiscent of many, many 'unlockable' pairs in the game- link/ young link, marth/ roy, mario/ doctor mario, ect.

The differences arise in, say, giga bowsers down + a move resulting in a small ice flurry totally unlike bowsers (which freezes characters), and his smash moves setting the characters on fire, which is utterly unlike bowser.

So, I say that the concept that he could be unlocked in this method is not entirely ludicrous, especially since my game has room for exactly two more characters on the table. (On either side of the bottom row.) And so I set to trying to defeat event match 51 with bowser.

Problem is, I can't do it. I can beat giga boswer himself, about 1/4 of the time, by using plenty of fire breathing and up + b on him, followed by down + b-ing him once he gets over 130% damage. Occasionally he'll try to stomp me over the side and commit suicide, and that'll help, too.

No, he's not the problem. Gannondorf + Mewtwo are. Try as I might, I can't defeat them- I'll always just get chucked off the stage, and it's rare that I face them when I have more than one life. (And when I do, I get sorely owned anyway.)

So, this thread- for all of you super smash bros. fans- is twofold...

#1- Any advice on beating mewtwo + gannondorf with bowser?
#2- Come help me prove/ disprove giga bowser as an unlockable character!

And if he can't be unlocked this way, well then, Nintendo are just evil to create an entirely new character that plays exactly like normal ones, but can't be played from the players perspective.

(My one problem with this theory is that All Star mode- fighting all of the characters in the game, once unlocked- would be obsolete if you could unlock any more characters. But there's always a chance, right?)

Oh yeah- and, I've beaten all- star mode on normal. Contrary to what some cheat websites say, this achieves nada. So boo to them.
