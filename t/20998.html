#397362 5 2008-01-05T21:05:59Z Borealis
Nothing sucks more than a set of old rusty strings, or ones that cut your hands to pieces. I too use Martin strings for my acoustic, but for my electrics I'm using good ol' Ernie Balls. And by that I don't mean my testicles. I have lighter strings on my Les Paul, which are 9-46. They are called "hybrid slinky" and they work well for a bluesy rock tone. My reason for using a slightly thicker gauge at the top is that they cope better with odd tunings, whereas a 9-42 tends to bottom out at drop C.
