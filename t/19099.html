Halo 3 is coming in just about one month, and I'm all hyped up over it. Why? Is it because of the awesome single player campaign, and four player online co-op? Hell no- I rarely ever played Single Player or Co-Op on the first two games. Well, maybe I'm dying to play online multiplayer? Kinda, but it's Halo 2 all over again, so why bother? Then it's gotta be the vehicles, right? Well, the Brute Chopper gets me happy, as I love ramming people all day, but no.

It's the game editors. I fell in love with Halo 2 (not Halo:CE, mind you) because of all the ways you could customize gametypes. I've made a bunch of crap with my friends, and we play a lot of them when we LAN up. Now, Halo 3 is going to be the new hotness of customizable FPS's. It just barely beats Far Cry's map MAKER (not just an editor, you can forge the land). With crazy new gametypes and tons of ways to customize them (300% game speed, anyone?), and the new map editor, for all your spawnpoint/object/vehicle/weapon-placing needs, the sky's the limit.

If anyone intends on getting Halo 3, expect me to have a lot of nifty stuff going on in my File Share. If we have a large enough group, we can have our own custom games going on. Who's gonna have H3?
