Are you tired of RPG's you have to pay for? What about ones you don't have to, but the game is just downright meaningless? Do you play Maple Story or Flyff, wondering what's left to do after achieving that high level you worked 6 months for? Here's where it ends.

Space Cowboy is an MMO that goes beyond the RPG. You still get PvE, PvP, Clans, Parties, Block Lists, Skills, Stats, Classes, and even choose your Alliance. What's the catch? You're in a ship. 360 by 360 degrees of free-flying freedom in one of four types of ships, tons of items, and a reason to be there. It's -FUN-.

Grinding: 12 Hours of basic grinding nets you at LEAST a level 20. Congrats, you can now start a clan and you have a nice area unlocked to fly in. If you want grinding, level 30 and above is where you want to be.

Choose your playing style: Do you wanna kill monsters and do missions? Go ahead. Challenge random players to dogfights and improve your record? Find a partner. Create or join a brigade and start a war (Note: You can have over 30v30 battles here.) with another brigade? How about just a simple formation vs. formation battle? Launch away. Hell, even get in on the Nation Wars. Gain fame and improve your rank by shooting the enemy forces out of the sky, and even move into their territory and take down their mothership!

Flying is easily controlled, and the fansite explains a whole lot of how to build your stats and such. Join up!


I fly a Level 30+ I-Gear Jet, and I'm on the B.C.U. Alliance. Join B.C.U. If you wanna balance the Nations, or join A.N.I. and be my enemy. Choose wisely!
