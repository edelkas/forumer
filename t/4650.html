#95486 5 2005-08-30T14:22:19Z nevermore
Corner jumps rely on two things. The angle at which you hit the block, and the timing of your jump. If you hit the block at the wrong angle, the corner jump will not work. If you don't jump at the right time, the corner jump will not work.

I'm sure we all know how to jump. It's just timing.
However, the tricky bit is the angle. To succesfully pull of a corner jump, you need to be on the corner of the block for at least two frames. Practice just jumping towards the corner of a block, and play it frame by frame to see if the ninja's on it for two frames. Practice the angle required to get the ninja on the corner for two frames. Once you've got that down, the timing comes in.
You need to jump on the first frame of those two frames. This is why a corner jump will not work if you are only on it for one frame - on the next frame you're not touching the block, so you have nowhere to jump from.

Does that help?
