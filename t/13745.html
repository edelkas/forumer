#253718 8 2006-11-12T22:43:01Z Tsukatu
It's pretty simple stuff. You don't really have to complete everything in a level before going down to the next one.
Eat the things with the dots in 'em to grow big and strong. You'll notice that portions of your body have lit up dots. You'll see them on other creatures you'll find, too. To kill a creature, eat all of its glowing dots (they'll become dull grey and the creature will become pissed off (orange), or will get scared and try to run (blue)). Get the red doodad to go down one level (progress in the game) or the blue doodad to go up (backwards).

You can actually just ignore everything and hunt the red things at each level. You'll face that last boss (a looooong snake with a bunch of glowy dots; eat his, don't let him eat yours; his movement will become slower with each segment of his body lost), who will let out a yellow dot in addition to the normal dots you get for killing a creature. Once you eat the yellow dot, you lose control of your organism and you'll go back to the very beginning with a new, different organism.

Tips I'd give:
Eat every dot you can find floating around. It'll give you plenty of body segments, so you can take a whole lot of hits before you go down. That and it will make you look badass.
For efficiency's sake, at least 75% of your movement should be charging (holding down the left mouse button). Stop charging momentarily to quickly change direction, and then continue charging again. When you're fighting another organism, at least 90% of your movement should be charging.
Before you go into a level that has a bunch of enemies, try to have as few of your segments lit up (from eating) as possible, because you'll take an awful lot of time to digest segments you eat off of other creatures if you come in with a full stomach. You want to be able to eat as frequently as possible.
The revolving organisms you'll find get hostile if they're in bunches, but if you stalk them around a bit and attack one while it's away from the others, it will panic and run away instead of attacking you, making it muuuch easier to kill.
You know those big organisms? Like, the frickin' huge ones that take up the screen? When you eat a bit of one of those guys, they'll release these tiny little sperm-shaped bastards, which will try to eat bits of you. And the thing is, you can't kill them and get that food back, so it's lost forever. The best thing to do when attacking one of those gargantuan organisms is to eat a piece and then run like hell until the little bastards all die behind you. Then come back in for more.
Stay away from the mouth of every creature. I know it sounds obvious, but it's easy to forget that the creature has a mouth. For most of them, it's the circle with the three triangles in it.
After you beat the game as a snake, you'll come back as a circle. You'll notice that your constituent bits rotate when you're charging. Use this when you're fighting. Basically, rotate the dulled portions of your body so that they're facing the enemy, charge right at him, eat a piece of his body, and then rotate your body around again and move off of him. Repeat.
When you beat the boss as the circle, you get another yellow thing and come back as a snake. Then again as a circle. After that, I got bored, so I'm not sure if there's another organism you can be if you're patient enough.
