#356458 2 2007-08-17T18:59:43Z Animator
If you want to know, read the read-me.
IT EXPLAINS ALL...
The read-me will say that Caps is pause.
When caps is on, you can play it.

How to see others:
Get the userlevel data on the map that you want to play.
Press Ctrl + A on the letters (or code.)
Press Ctrl + C to copy it.
Now, you may either:
a) Place it in the userlevels txt. and play it WITHY time...
or..
b) Enter userlevels in N and place it in the white box.

That's all.
