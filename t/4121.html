#126114 8 2005-12-23T01:25:32Z Aries_Griff
(Aries's Easy Way)

The first part is all luck, until you get the timing down. If you walk to each side to trigger the thwump and land on top of it when it first comes out, then drop to the otherside, if you do that quickly, you can fall right through the part with the zap drone.(I still rely a lot on luck)

Then grab the switch on the right and wait for the floor guard to come over, then jump over it at the inclined ceiling part with momentum, so you can hit the ground running and jump into the middle right section pretty easily.

Getting to the third square isn't hard, I like to use the first two squares and wall jump up their sides quickly when the drones are on the other sides. Then come the thwumps. There are two approaches to getting up that I found work a fair percentage of the time. (1) The first is to lure the first thwump over by hopping from your square youre standing on, then once it's coming back, about when the first thwump is directly overhead, jump so that you trigger the top thwump, and when you're coming down from that jump, the first thwump will trigger too. If you do this right, there will be a sizeable gap between the two. If the gap isn't big enough, keep jumping and you should be able to get the top one to trigger and the bottom one to stay, and then you can hop and trigger the bottom when they are a good distance apart. Once you have a gap between them you want the first thwump to be coming back, and jump onto the right square where the thwump just passed over, and the top thwump should trigger. If you're fast, you can let the top thwump go over you, and jump before the bottom one hits you or the top thwump comes back. This is tricky, but it works. (2) The second way, my preferred way, is to lure the first thwump out by hopping, then when it is almost back to where it started, jump high so you trigger both thwumps. The idea on this one is to get the two thwumps to trigger as close together as possible. Then jump onto the right block as soon as the first thwump clears it, then immediately jump up onto the right wall. If you did this correctly, you should have a fair amount of clearance on the thwumps. I say jump onto the wall instead of the block with the drone on it, because sometimes the drone can be sitting waiting for you if you just walljump after clearing the thwumps without looking. Method 2 has a high percentage of success for me, and I'd recommend it.

Once you're on top, it's easier on the way down. Just drop onto the square above the thwumps and get to the right wall(you can run off the square into the wall if the drone isn't close to you). Wallslide the wall until you trigger the top thwump. Then hop up(small hop) and grab the wall again so it doesn't kill you, and then slide until the bottom one triggers, and just hop off the wall, and you should clear the bottom thwump and the top one should still be going back. Land on that square in the middle of the area safely below the thwumps and you're fine. Just drop down to the floor of that little area(the fall wont kill you). This part is easy in my opinion.

Then avoid the floor guard on the way to the middle left area (should'nt be hard, just wait for it). Then I recommend jumping onto the right wall, then jumping from the wall to the middle jump pad. If you hit the middle one, it is really easy to land on the middle bounceblock, and then jump to the terminal switch. Piece of cake. Getting down shouldn't be too hard, you can just get back on the middle bounceblock and fall to the little floor space between jump pads, and you wont die. Or you can get to the lowest bounceblock, and walk off the left side and go hard right so you wallslide the wall safely down.

From there, you may or may not have to dodge the floorguard, depending on where he is. The way up isn't too bad, just don't get ahead of yourself, because the space between mines gets smaller at the top. Once you're at the top, you face a tricky set of mines. The way I always do the mines that are directly above and below is tricky for me, but is the most consistent way for me. I jump straight, full into the ceiling, so my ninja almost hovers at the top, and then on the way down I hold right to clear the bottom mine. It's tricky, but if you just try jumping at the ceiling, you should see the timing. The second set of mines is easy because it is staggered, just jump to victory and watch your step.

[/easy way]

Hope that helps, I spent a while replaying the level to find the easy way.
