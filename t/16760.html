Let the map making tips start!

TIPS:
Have an idea of what you want to do before you start.
Try to balance the time you spend on the tileset with the time you spend on the objects.
Always playtest your map.
Try to stick to a theme throughout.
Do not overuse objects (EX: gold), it just slows down the load time and makes the map ugly.

#314333 12 2007-04-19T21:13:07Z usaswim
  1. Make it look very attractive
  2. Make it not too easy, nor too hard
  3. Add gold, unless the theme is no gold, in clumps
  4. Tileset is crucial. If you spent under 5 minutes on the tileset it will probably not look nice
  5. Always playtest
  6. Make sure you reset your objects
DDAs
  1. Press 1 before you playtest it
  2. Add enemies first
  3. Don't use random z-snap clumping
  4. Don't overuse gold or mines
  5. Don't make lines and lines of launchpads
  6. Use a variety of propulsion
  7. Make lots of close calls
Races
  1. Make it very flowy
  2. Don't overuse launchpads (exception of no-jump races)
  3. Add an adequate amount of gold and mines (if necessary)
  4. Don't make too many close calls
  5. Don't overuse enemies (especially machine-gun drones)
  6. Don't make tunnel races (where the floor-ceiling is 1 unit) involving lots of 4 and 8 tiles.
For everything else... check here
