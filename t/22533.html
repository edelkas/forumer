#434698 2 2008-05-09T02:38:50Z Atilla
Neither. It's the quality of the work which matters, rather than which medium is used to produce it. Furthermore, the two are not mutually exclusive - a lot of people do a sketch by hand, then digitally ink and colour it. Even if you sketch and ink by hand, you'll probably need to give it a minor digital touch-up to get a crisp picture, unless you have a really good scanner or you're planning to do oil paintings or something else which doesn't use hard lines. Also, badly-scanned art looks extremely slipshod, so if you don't have adequate scanning facilities or you don't know how to touch up a scan, you're probably better off going entirely digital.
