I think a lot of people are missing the true concept of this idea with their easy-way-around-having-to-make-a-new-object suggestions. One time launch pads would make you bounce up one time, and after that, there would be no object where the launch pad previously was.

This would make for a very cool and puzzly object. Think of all the unique level ideas you could come up that limit you to one bounce per launch pad. There could be opportunities for strategizing and formulating.

To be quite honest, I think it's a cool idea.
