Game Modes: Different modes, such as survival where you're in a survival map with enemies, and you have to survive for as long as you can. Stationary laser: This laser has a beam which tracks N, and like the gauss cannon, tries to lock on. When it does, it follows N in a beam.
Lifts: When N walks onto it, it goes down/up.
HP: health.
Difficulty levels: In lower difficulties, enemies are dumber and slower, they do less damage, N is faster.
Mines: They can kill enemies.
Nonlethal weapons: They can push enemies backwards, or blow up a single mine. Limited shots.
