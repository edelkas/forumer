I don't know if this is... particularly unusual or not... but I know that when I play N while listening to certain songs, I play better than usual.

In fact, there's one song I find works like a charm- after having died on a level for 25+ times, I'll tend to play this song... and tend to have beaten the level before the song is out... This song is,

Cannon in D- Pachebell

Anyway, so, I thought it might be interesting to compile a playlist of music to play N by- not the songs that N players necessarily enjoy, but the ones they find help them play better. And to add them to the playlist.

So far, we have...

The way we get by- Spoon
