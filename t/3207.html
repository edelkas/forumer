#67427 5 2005-05-17T21:37:35Z nevermore
Alright, I think it's actually much easier not to get the switches that open the doors above the exit. It reduces playing time. So, assuming you've seen the highscores, you know you have to quickly jump into the tunnels holding the switch and exit switch, and get back out without letting the rocket hit you. It's really easier than it looks, if you know a few tricks. This way seems the most harmless:

1. Drop down to the right and get the first switch inside of the alcove.
2. Run to the left towards the rocket launcher, and jump when you hit the slope.
3. Control your jump so that you end up next to the wall right under the exit switch.
4. Walljump off, and land on the slope to the right. Here's the hard part:
5. Slide a bit down the little slope, letting the rocket pass above your head and hit the wall.
6. Jump up and open the switch that lets you get to the door.
7. Travel down and into the door. Congratulations!
