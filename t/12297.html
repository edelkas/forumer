Oh- and, for the interested-
Episode Themes
0: Sherlock Holmes story titles
1: Relating to nursery rhymes
2: Focusing on launch pads
(Except for 'crashed cable car', but the launch pads are entirely necessary to complete the level, so I figured it'd fit anyway.)
3: 'The Epic' style, 5 interconnecting medieval- themed levels. The decorations on the top corners vary, but the sides still overlap and create what is essentially one long level.
4: One level, only rotated and- for the last variant- inverted.
5: Focusing on floor guards
6: Levels 'carved' into a solid tileset, rather than vice versa.
(The first level in here isn't as tight as the rest, but it's still mainly 'e' tiles and still cavern style, so it fits.)
7: Song titles from brit alternative- turned- pop group Gomez.
8: This one's pretty subtle. All of the levels are, in some way, devided into two different sections with different themes, tilesetwise.
9: All of these could belong in different catagories.
*Variant on 'order, order'
*Variant on 'crashed cable car'
*Variant on the 4th level- inverted and rotated
*Doesn't quite fit, but is missing a quarter on the lower left, and has a Sherlock Holmes title- works with 'the adventure of the missing three- quarters'.
