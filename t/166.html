I don't think I'm the only one to prepare for the online Rankings, so I decided to let us share our strats to be prepared for the great moment. Anyway, this is my Episode 20 Strat. I've coem close to 260, and I know my 20-2 still sucks.

20-0: This should not be hard. Just go right left right and into the goal. Try to jump as close as possible to keep as much momentum as possible, turn around and jump off at the walls as soon as possible, and jump far on the last platform, turning in midair and running left when landign on it to save some time.

20-1: Rather obvious, drop doen to the bottom right area, drop down the left shaft, onto the slope, do a small jump, land before the other slope, and climb it in 2 not full power jump, collecting all gold pieces and barely making it to the switch. Drop left without touchign the ground, hit the goal and break your legs =D

20-2: There's a 3+ seconds shortcut right at the start. Jump left, walljump off, you'll barely make it onto a Round piece. run up a tad and jump, over the mine and back onto the main course. It's tricky, but it saves 3 or so seconds. Quickly climb the left side, and take the bottom switch. Activate the Goal switch, and take the 5 gold pieces below the goal area, climb the right wall and make it into the goal jumping barely behind the zap drone. If done quickly, you might be able to outrun the drone here.

20-3: Image attached. Anyway, jump right, onto the edge, going over, but stearing left. You should try to use the next edge on the left side to slow you down enough to make the drop down without actualy having to land on it. Jump down and land to the right of the single bottom mine. Might be possible to land left of it, I'm not sure. Anyway, jump to the gold area, collecting all gold in a single jump, walljump and drop. Take the path to the door as shown on the pic. You can jump over the mine in the first jump, too, but it's slower, but easier.

Edit: Just found another way of getting down, it's jumping over teh first edge, landing on the bottom edge, sliding off it while not dieing and slowing down, and land to the right of the single mine. Very tricky, though.

20-4: Another ultra shortcut here. Jump into the first wave, then jump into the 2nd move, landing on the downside slope to gain speed. Jump at the bottom without touching the next wave and you should make it all the way over to the uphill side of the 2nd move to the left of the jumppad. Run down the slope, run up the next wave and jump LEFT off it, then steer right in midair. If you jump right off it, you gain less heigth. Anyway, the Zap Drone should just have passed(that's why we jump into the first wave, great way of timing this), so walljump off the white block, on top of it and jump left. Collect both gold pieces and jump over the wave and into the downhill slope on the left wall, running down it, activating the switch and jumping off the wave to the right. Land on the 2nd to top white block, jump against the top one, walljump, collect the gold piece while making it over the first wave. Just tap jump the last wave and hit the goal.
