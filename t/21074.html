Spoiler: The story so far:
"10 seconds till armageddon!" screams the business man, as he falls from the 221st floor. His body slowly twirls in the air. You peer down through the broken glass.
"Take the leap!" he insists, but you can't see him anymore.
A tug at your pant leg, and you turn around. There sits a small goat, munching on a piece of a carrot.
You hear a loud explosion from outside the building. The glass around you shatters. Meanwhile, the goat is ramming it's horns against you. You think it is trying to direct you towards the stairs. You follow the goat.
It begins to walk up the stairs to the penthouse level, Floor 222. Then gradually, as walk up the stairs, you notice the goat becoming more and more upright, until eventually it stand 7 feet tall, with large horns.
"I am Goatman", it bellows as he picks you up and begins to run. It's breath is heavy and warm, yet strangely comforting.
"Today is the apocalypse my friend. But we will be safe."
By now he had almost got to the 222nd floor. You had never been there before, even though you worked in the office below it.
The fire escape door was closed as we got to the penthouse.
"I am Goatman. Let me in!", it yelled. The door slowly opened...

You look around. Three large cakes sit on a long cedar table, and around them sit four... well, you think that they might be human. One's got scars all over her face, and a air hostess uniform. Her pink hair twirls into a large beehive shaped style, although at some points it looks more like spaghetti. Two men sit beside her, one fat with a large mustache, the other thin and boney. And a small child sits whimpering in a oversized chair at the end of the table. You can hear screams from down below. You run to a window and peer down.
"Welcome!", says the fat man in a harsh-yet-cheery voice. "I am Gustav, and these are my friends Kitty and Pearl." The thin one waves at you. "Oh, and this is Timmy. He's a bit shy." The kid shudders a bit. "You've met Goatman. Now..." Gustav stands up upon the table. You notice his hands are covered with small bugs. "Today is the apocalypse my friends..." Kitty rolls her eyes, "But we are safe, so it is okay." Another loud explosion goes off, this time in the building next door. Rubble hits the windows, but they do not shatter. "You're a bit shell-shocked", whispers Goatman into your ear. He pulls out a vial from underneath his fleece. It's small and transparent. "Drinks this, it'll make you feel better", you hastily grab the bottle and gulp it down; it tastes like rotten lemon. Goatman pulls out a leather arm chair from underneath the table, and insists you sit. You start to feel dizzy, then fall asleep, as Gustav pulls out a large map and scrolls it onto the table.

You wake up. It's dark and noisy, the sound of gravel crunching under tyres. And there's Timmy fast asleep, huddled in a ball, in the corner across from you. 'Won't be long now.", yells Gustav from the side seat. Kitty's driving. You stand up and your head hits the roof. 'Watch yourself there!", Gustav chuckles as he follows a trail with his finger on a map. "Turn left... now." Kitty swerves the van into a small farmyard. Most of the crops are dead. You look out the window, between Gustav and Kitty. There's a large building up ahead, looks like it might've been an energy reactor or something once. But it's windows are smashed in, and there's graffiti all over it. "That's where we're going," Gustav stated. A moments silence; "Would you mind waking up Timmy?". You turn back to Timmy, and kneel down. "Timmy," you say in a childish voice, "Wakey wakey!" His eyes slowly open, their red and sore. "We're here."

You walk in to the building. It smells. There's pigeon shit everywhere. At the back of the building, Goatman and Pearl sit a desk, examining files. You look up, it's as if the roof didn't exist. The building was everlastingly tall. You could see the remnants of old floors, poking out of the walls. Gustav gestures towards a seat, next to Goatman's. You sit down, and the chair slowly creaks. It's midday. Gustav paces around the desk. "Tomorrow, my friends, is the end. Yet it is okay, because we will be safe... BUT! That is no reason to stop, we have much to do, in not much time." He triumphantly stood, smug. "Firstly we must get to my home land, Romania, and visit some friends of mine. No time to waste!" He began rushing to the car, the others followed. You caught up with Gustav, "But... we're in... London! How are we going to..." Your voice drowned away, as you noticed the car disappear, then appear again. Gustav chuckled, "Trust me, my friend, that is a very fast van."

You got into the back of the van. It was hot and humid. Following you in were Timmy, Goatman, Pearl, and Kitty. And then you realized that Pearl and Kitty had been gone for quite some time. "Where were you guys?", you exclaimed in a slightly sarcastic manner. "Well, it all began in..." Kitty quickly covered Pearl's mouth. He was struggling. "To cut a long story short, me and Pearl had better things to do than bother about world. More important things." You giggled at Kitty's words. "Not those kind of things!" , Pearl hastily spat out of his mouth. Kitty simply rolled her eyes. "We're here! Fast van, isn't it my friends?", Gustav's grin virtually hit you in the head. You all got out of the van. It was twilight, 3 AM according to the clock towering above you. Gustav pointed towards a house, "That is where my friends are."

You walked in. It was musty, with flickering light bulbs and old rugs. Two people - a brother and sister - welcomed you in. You were poured coffee, drunk it, then were told to go to sleep. "Big day tomorrow, eh? End of the world and all.", Gustav joked. Two hours later you woke up to the sound of pain. You opened your door, and could see them. Gustav was in a large armchair, wailing in agony, while his two friends obsessed over over him. One gave you a glance then returned to Gustav. "What happened?", you question. "He's tired. He just did something no one's supposed to do" You were confused. "He opened a riff.", she pointed to the kitchen. You walked in. Apart from the linomat floor, covered with coffee grinds and the occasional ant, there was nothing. You stood there for a while. Then you noticed it, the cupboard. It was black, and tall. You opened it and looked inside. It was if you had seen nothingness.

Kitty pulled you away. "Ach! Don't look in there!" She shook you a bit, then you came to your senses. "Sorry.", you mumble. Gustav is panting in the background. "It's okay,", Kitty comfortingly rubbed your shoulder. "That, is why we're safe." She pointed at the cupboard. "That is why Gustav is tired." "Kitty,", it was Pearl. "Quick Kitty!" You turned around. Gustav's mouth was overflowing with bugs; it was if he was rotting. His skin was rapidly changing color. "He's dying,", somehow Goatman had appeared behind me. "Within an hour, Gustav will be gone...So better say your goodbyes now!" Timmy walked down the stairs; red eyes, teary, hot face, loud breathing. "Timmy, I have something to tell you", Kitty spoke in her nicest voice. "Gustav is... is..." "What?", Timmy thought out loud. "Your father."

"What?! My father?!", his cheeks were red. You went up to comfort him, but he pushed you away. Timmy was angry. Suddenly the boy who had been shy and whimpering, had become a firey temper, outraged. "And you knew, he knew! All along?" "Yes Timmy,", Kitty was trying to calm him down. "But...", tears was rapidly welting at his eyes. "Why didn't you...tell me? Why didn't you...", he fainted. His body began shaking, as if he were having a fit. "What's happening?", you asked. "He's changing,", Goatman spoke as he looked curiously at Timmy's vibrating body. "Long story short, Gustav knew he was going to die. And so he asked me to guard Timmy when this happens." You looked at Goatman, confused. "Everybody,", proclaimed Goatman, "Timmy ain't going to be the Timmy we know from now on." Timmy was lying there, serenely. "But right now, we REALLY need to go... So, into the cupboard everyone!" You slumped Timmy over your shoulder,then jumped through the door.
