#308892 7 2007-03-30T20:44:29Z pajammin
Once you think you like your levels, press Home and then '~' or '\'. Then, make sure you have Caps Lock on, and press P to play your level. Then, just use your regular keys to play. If you die, you can press '~' or '\' to go back to the main Ned page, then R to reset, and P to play it again, or, you can drag your cursor over a desired spot on your level while you ninja is dead, and press Enter (or Return, whatever it's labeled on your keyboard) to respawn and continue playing.

If you're confused, just post again, and I'll be willing to break it down some more for you.

~Kimmie~
