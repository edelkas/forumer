#120401 9 2005-12-04T17:50:25Z Raigan
we met someone from steam at the GDC and offered to let them distribute N as a free download, just to promote steam.. but we never heard anything back from them.

up until we heard about darwinia on steam, we thought it was a pretty cool thing: a great distribution system that lets developers have more power by cutting out the need for publishers.

BUT, then we found out that, apparently, if you distribute on steam then you can't distribute in other internet-based ways. this really, really sucks; the majority of our users are probably not going to have steam installed, but it would be nice to _also_ be on steam. we just don't understand why valve would make this thing that seems to shift the balance of power towards developers, and then go acting like an evil publisher by imposing constraints on developers.

as far as we're concerned, all they're doing to introversion is using them as leverage to get more people to install steam. i understand that valve want people to have a reason to install steam, but it just seems really heavy-handed and one-sided: they could instead have offered a discounted version of darwinia (i.e $15 w/steam, $20 from the introversion website) or SOMETHING. but this all-or-nothing seems like they don't care at all about developers aside from how many fans they have who are willing to install steam in order to get their new game.
