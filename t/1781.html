#40329 4 2004-12-10T08:51:05Z Guest
When you start N, on the main menu, there should be an option entitled "Highscores."

Go in there, and it will show every episode in the game. Click on 29, and then, in the box that says "Episode scores", it will, after a few seconds, show the names of the highscores for that episode. Click on the top one, and it will replay the entire episode - 29-0 through 29-4 in order.

If you just want to watch 29-3, then on the box at the top that says [view scores], click on level 3, and then click on one of the names on the left, it'll replay that highscore.
