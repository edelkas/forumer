I had trouble with this level when I first got here, but once you get the hang of it it's quite simple and very fun. The way I try to do it is by going either to the left or right end of the map (usually right) and walljump off of the lowest bounceblock onto the next higher one and the next higher one..until I get to the platform I want to reach. It's almost like wallclimbing, but you've got to be pretty fast to do it.

Watch the highscores to see how it is done.
