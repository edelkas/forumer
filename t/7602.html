how to I get my levels to appear on my [user levels] list? I know how to create a level but how do i get it on that list?
#145257 2 2006-02-18T10:51:08Z ZZ9
if you read userlevels.txt is shall answer your question better but....


Take a few new lines at the end of userlevels.txt and then type $(What you want to name your level)#(your name)#(what type of level it is)#(level data)#

Don't forget the # at the end. Once you have done this your level should appear in your user levels list.
