If the character really does have the faith of the 6 sixes being rolled, why would he want God to help him? If he had that much faith, he would now that he should be spending his prayers on more important issues. But what if he didn't really have the faith to God doing such an action? Isn't that a bit small minded to want every person to live? If you make sure every person lives, that would most likely go against any great plan that may be laid out. Example 3. In this example, the narrator is just plain wrong. He says that prayer has exactly no effect on Cancer. Yet it is documented many times around the world that people who pray often find the strength to keep fighting the cancer. And it is known that a healthy outlook on the world is extremely important to your health. Example 4 now. I can't believe that this is being used in the video. The passage itself says that God help us for what we ask for in heaven. One must also consider that why would God need to give the person a new leg? If the person was fine and surviving? Why then? And this brings up another problem. Humans are extremely self centered. The person with only one leg is asking for a new leg. So why is that person ignoring how they lost the leg? Couldn't they be spending their time giving speeches about the prevention of the loss of leg? Yet they just want a quick fix. Not exactly the correct Christian outlook. As for the last minutes of the video, I lost a lot of respect for the author. Thus far, they had at least been making solid points and rather unbiased about the views. Yet the subject moves to how smart and logical people should think religious people are idiots for their beliefs. Talk about not being accepting of others.
