Welcome, Perimetric.

As a beginner, it's alright to use jump-pads in your DDA's, but as you get more experienced, you should rather go without them and find other methods of movin' N around. The same applies to gold delay. Also, you should try not have too many unnecessary objects as the load time is uber long.

Of course, though, you'll get better at map-making (and especially DDA's if that's the direction you're going). Next time you want your maps noticed, it's always a good idea to post them at the Map-Rating Thread. You'll probably get more attention there. And if you're looking for a list of some of the best DDA's ever made, click this. It may help.

#415791 4 2008-03-11T06:59:04Z Nexx
Hello Perimetric.

A few pointers: Don't spam objects (like the mines near your name and in the level - just use enough to get the job done), and try to create more close calls (you've got a few good ones there) and otherwise keep it exciting (for example, the ride up on the thwump is boring).

Try posting your map(s) at NUMA. That's what it's there for. In the words of the admins, if everyone posted their maps in this forum, it would be swamped.

Also, you use the Map-Rating Thread by posting your map at NUMA first, and then linking to it in the Map-Rating Thread. Make sure you rate the last person's map before posting.

Hope that helped, too.

#415888 7 2008-03-11T17:14:14Z Mae
1. Create a new user (go to Configure on N home page).
2. Activate your account with your email.
3. Go to NUMA and log on with the user you created.
4. Click "Submit a Map".
5. Enter the map data into the box required, and the rest is pretty self-explanitory.
