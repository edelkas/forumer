#117203 3 2005-11-23T05:45:28Z KinGAleX
If you put a map in your userlevels file which has many weird characters in the title, that can happen. Say someone has a map called "Check out these characters #$#@$%^@@#$!@#$". N will read those $s as the beginning of a new map, and will then nullify everything after it. Try renaming any maps with weird characters like that, or else it's something else.
