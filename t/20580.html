Welcome to Ninjas - Red vs. Blue

First off, I'll explain the rules.
  • There is a player move 'rota', the players move in order, if one player doesn't respond in 24 hours, he's missed his go. If the player doesn't respond for 3 days, he gets removed.
  • Attacking uses one of 4 moves, 2 equipped weapons, A grenade, or a melee attack.
  • Damage is calculated depending on weapon used, even for melee attack.
  • Once you attack/defend/use an item, you cannot move.
  • Depending on your skill point spend, you can boost skills.
  • You earn skill points per kill.
  • If you die that turn, you will respawn next turn your team has.
  • Weapons are scattered around the map, use them wisely, and change to create killer combos.
  • When you sign up, you don't choose a team, I choose the teams once signup is complete.
  • When signing up, post your skill points spendage, you start with 5 to spend.
Skills
Skills enable you to move farther, do more damage, or jump higher. It all depends on how you spend. The list are as follows.

Power
Speed
Metabolism

One skill point is earned per kill. So choose wisely. Speed is how many tiles you can move, metabolism is how many squares you can jump, and Power is the chance of a critical hit.

Weapons
As is Halo, the weapons are from the games, the weapon selection are from Halo 1 and 2 only. This is due to the lack of sprites Each weapon comes with full ammo, if you die, those weapons disappear off the map. Weapons are as follows.

Plasma Rifle - 100 battery power, loses 1 per 5 bullets fired, loses 10 if overheated, a ratio of 5/10 for overheating. Takes 4 hits to kill.
Plasma Pistol - 100 battery power, loses 1 per 1 bullet, loses 10 per charge bullet, charge takes a turn. Takes 6 hits to kill, or 1 charge and 2 normal.
Beam Rifle - Battery of 100, loses 5 per bullet, 20 lost if overheat. 6/10 chance. Takes two hits to kill.
Fuel Rod Gun - Battery of 100, loses 10 per bullet. Takes 1 hit to kill.
Standard Rifle - Magazine of 60, comes with another 120. Takes 5 hits to kill.
Magnum - Magazine of 6, comes with another 18. Takes 7 hits to kill.
Sniper Rifle - Magazine of 4, comes with another 12, 8x zoom. Takes two hits to kill.
Rocket Launcher - Magazine of 2, comes with another 4. 2x zoom. OHKO
Energy Sword - Infinite ammo. OHKO.
HE Grenade - Find around the map. Depends on range with damage.
Plasma Grenade - Find around the map, sticks to targets. Depends on range with damage.

Current list
