#330788 2 2007-06-13T08:52:03Z George
NaN stands for 'Not a Number'. The often occurs when you load a level with the cursor still in the textbox. By adding an extra L, it disrupts the data and turns it into an NaN.

In order to get rid of it, all you have to do is get rid of all the data from the end until you reach an exclamation mark.

Sometimes NaN's are purposely used to do certain things not normally possible. For example, a NaN can be used on chaingun and laser drones to make them still. They will still attack you, but they will act exactly like a gauss turret - stationary.

Also, a very large ninja can be created using a NaN. Rather pointless, and I've never done it myself, but it seems very... interesting.
