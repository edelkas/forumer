I don't think that this has been suggested before...

The ratings system should be... *thinks of an analogy* like figure skating. And like lots of other sports.

The way the system works, the top and bottom votes are ignored in calculating the average score. That way it is much harder for a map to be sniped by a low score. You could set it so that, say, after 5 votes the top and bottom votes are ignored, after 15 the top/bottom two votes, etc etc. That way the more votes a map gets the more the average consensus is shown rather than the opinions of few voting snipers heavilly affecting the rating - either for better or for worse.

... I think that it could really work well.
