I think we can all mostly agree that with a rating system that uses averages it is easy to throw the rating of a map with just a few votes, regardless of the quality of the map. It also means that one doesn't have to play the map to make a fair judgment, which is where snipers come into play.

Now, there have been quite a few attempts at changing the voting system to get rid of snipers, and for the most part they have been good ideas but with many flaws. I think this is mostly because some people have created systems which are far too simple that it isn't really rating a map anymore, or they become far too complex because the person has many ideas that -could- stop sniping, but the sheer amount of ideas put together make the system too complex and in the end there are many flaws.

I think I have found a voting system that takes power away from a sniper and still allows a top rated system that will almost be completely unchanged.

To start, let's imagine that instead of using a rating system that averages scores let us use a rating system that adds scores. Let me explain a little more. Averaging the score of a map puts power into those who want to abuse a system that decreases the "value" of a map. For example, someone rates a map a 4 and another person rates the map a 0. the average score for the map is now 2, when it could easily be a 3.5 or 4. This means someone has just decreased the "value" of that map by rating it low.

With this new system, rating a 0 is not of importance, because it would essentially be a No Rate (NR). Instead of averaging the scores I suggest that we add the scores. If a person rates a map a 5 and another person rates the map a 2 the score will be 7. this means you can only add to the value of a players map, rather than decrease the value of a players map.

so the options are as follows: 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5. All maps in this new voting system will be rated and compared to all other maps in terms of total score rather than average score. this kind of comparison is just as good and just as effective. The higher your score overall the higher it is on the rating scale. This is how the current system works, and this is how this new system works.

Now, on to the Top Rated page, because that's where this kind of cuts out the benefit of having an average rating. But I have a solution. Top Rated will have a minimum standard that will automatically put a map up there if it has received at least a score of 23 in its first five votes (that's two +4's and three +5's). Then the Top Rated page will also be ordered by date that they were added to the page, rather than average rating. I would say that as long as a map has made it to the Top Rated page it should stay there indefinitely. This means that the Top Rated page could cycle through new maps all the time and let people see what is new and fresh and great rather than AfternoonDragonStep04 on the front page always.

I'm very tired as of now but I wanted to get this up and see what people thought of it. I'm sorry if this idea has been thought of before, I'm just too lazy to search for the same idea...

I'll explain in more detail, possibly with picture, if people would like me to.

There's a few problems I can see with this system:
- Sniping is still possible if you get in early. A single 0 vote in the first 5 on a map can stop it from getting into the top rated page - ever.
- Ratings represent how long a map has been around more than anything else - older maps have had more opportunity to accumulate votes.
- Maps on the top rated page are more likely to get votes, so being 'top rated' is self-reinforcing.
