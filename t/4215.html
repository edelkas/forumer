#3839 1 2005-07-27T17:58:41Z shoftee
OK. Here's the idea:
  • Invert Selection:
    All of the selected tiles are replaced with their opposites.
    In other words: The full parts become empty, and the empty - full.
  • Flip Selection:
    All of the selected tiles are flipped around their x and/or y axes. Flipping around both axes is the same as rotating 180 degrees.
  • Rotate Selection:
    ... obvious I think...
  • Transparency:
    OK, this is the one thing that I want in Ned more than anything else. With this little option turned on, when you move/copy the selection, only the non-empty tiles are moved/copied. This is VERY, and I mean VERY, useful when you want to put large shapes close to each other. I've needed this so many times that I've lost the count!
