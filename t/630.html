Good for a first try, I've seen worse from "experienced" mapmakers. Two things, though: 1) There's no point to the tunnels leading to the switch. Mabey if you put some zap drones which you have to use timing to avoid, or better yet put a gauss turret inside each one of those isolated wall tiles (it will shoot outward in all directions from there, and looks pretty cool). I went in there fully expecting a handful of thwumps to shoot out of the walls too. 2) you need to have put a mine underneath the hole you jump through when you reach the turret, or else you can just skip it entirely by going left while falling.
