#136330 3 2006-01-27T10:18:00Z Ambidextrous Pineapple
it's hard to explain bounceblocks. It'll make sense when you get used to it, in a weird sort of way.

When you land ON a bounce block, if you do not do anything, it will not sink very low. The key to having it sink low (and thus be able to jump higher) is to move to one side when you land, preferably the opposite side of the direction you want to travel. That will make it sink lower.

As to when to jump, timing is everything, and cannot be taught. try to jump off of it a few milliseconds after it's reached its lowest, so that its upwards momenteum can be added to yours.

Also, to jump extremely high, land and sink on a bounceblock, then instead of single jumping, quickly tap jump twice. This is a "double jump", and propells you to extreme heights. This can also be done off the side of a bounceblock.

Edit: more tips:

For "chimney jumping" where you have a space of one block and walls on either side of you (for example 2-2), you can use naem's method to propell you faster. Press one shift key, and then the other. REMEMBER not to press the other shift key while still holding the first. This takes some getting used to, but it can be faster.

Also for chimney jumping, when i was a real newb at the game, i thought that you go faster chimney jumping by holding one direction and jumping. You don't. Just get into the chimney and tap shift as fast as you can. There IS a small space in the middle where you can't jump off of anything, so make sure you're on a side before jumping.

wall jumping is just another acquired technique - you will learn how to gague your upwards momenteum for the situation. EG: if there is a mine on the wall, you need to have SOME momenteum, but not so much that you crash into the mine.
