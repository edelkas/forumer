You should never rate maps this way...

1. It's too hard for you, so you give it a random rating.
I think if a map is too hard for you, too hard for you to enjoy it, I think you shouldn't rate it at all. You can't play the map properly, so where will your number come from?

2. Rate a map from first looks.
A map looks ugly and messy, that doesn't mean it's bad. Play it first!

3. The author made lousy maps before.
As I said, play it first!

4. Personal reasons.
You hate the author, so you rate it low. This happens sometimes, it's just dumb. You rate the map, not the author.

5. The map is highly rated, so you rated it low.
I think this happens sometimes. A map is on the top list, you feel jealous and so you rate it a bit low. Like a 2.5 or a 2, just to take it off the top list. You shouldn't do this because the author really worked hard to make a nice map, and because of his hard work, it hit the top. Then you go and take away his happiness just because your maps aren't as good as his, imagine if that was you.

6. You're an idiot.
You rate it 0 because.... you're an idiot. I think you should just go away.




I'm not really good at righting these kinds of things, I think. Hope people read this and actually follow it. Tell me if I'm missing anything here.
