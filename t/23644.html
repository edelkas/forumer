WEEK FOUR THEME: OBJECT MODS


-With this weeks theme you must make a map showing the wonderful things that objects can do. You are allowed to make action, puzzle, or race maps in order to prove your knowledge of what a object can do.
-You may use any object mods and you may use any other mod you see fit as long as the map fits the theme of object mods to the best of your ability. object mods can accomplish any number of things, and of course using a mod never before seen will score you bonus points.
-Objects are defined as anything that are not an enemy, a ninja or a door. However, this week, we are redfining objects as anything except drones and ninjas.

On a side note, if possible, when you put your link here, which you must do to enter the map in the comp. , please post at least verbally what mods you used or the actual code of it. This is not required, but it will grealy ease the judging process.

Also a demo would be helpful if included on NReality. This is optional

This week's round ends Sunday, 11:59pm Pacific standard time (PST = GMT -8).
  • *PST = 11:59 pm sunday, August 31th
    *GMT = 6:59 am monday, September 1st
    * Hong Kong = 2:20 pm monday, September 1st
Any maps submitted after this deadline will not be accepted, no exceptions.

submit your maps here.
