The more perceptive amongst you may have noticed NUMA just sprouted a 'Get Firefox' banner along the side (noticeable, but hopefully not annoying). After seeing the dismal browser stats for NUMA when I checked the logs, it occurred to me that making people here even aware of the existence of Firefox might be a good idea.
For those that don't know, Firefox is a web-browser, just as Internet Explorer is. The difference is, Firefox is much better, both from an end-user's point-of-view and a site designer's . It comes with popup blocking and tabbed browsing built in, and IMHO those two features alone make it worth choosing over IE. From a web-designer's point of view, Firefox is far easier to write pages for, too.

Plus, NUMA looks much better in Firefox. I did my best to make it as nice as possible in IE, but due to IE's broken rendering engine, I just couldn't. So if you load Firefox, you'll notice that the title banner spans the full width of the page, for example.

Also, with Firefox 1.0 preview out, with built in RSS support, and NUMA soon having RSS support, you'll be able to be kept up to date on the newest NUMA levels (all or just selected categories or authors) all the time - you can even have a folder of bookmarks containing the latest levels, that automatically keeps itself up to date.

</plug>
