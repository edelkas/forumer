The competition: Make the most infuriatingly difficult map that you can complete.

A submission consists of one map together with two all-gold demos demonstrating that you have what it takes to complete your own level any time you like. The demos will be kept secret.

After all the maps are submitted, the elimination round begins. Eliminating a map is simple; All you have to do is submit an all-gold demo. Anyone can submit a demo, even if they didn't submit a map.

Last map standing wins!

Rules

To reduce the number of idiotic submissions that a contest of this kind naturally invites, I propose the following rules:

Rules for the two submitted demos:

1) The two demos must be distinct, but follow the same route. This eliminates (hopefully) the possibility of making the demo first and then building the level around it.
2) The demos must be all gold and shorter than 2000 frames. In other words, your level must be difficult, but short.
3) Your demos must be made in user levels mode. Any hint that you have been using frame by frame mode or splicing multiple demos together and you will be disqualified. Note that the demos should include the level data as well as the demo. You can submit the demos at any time by pm to me (monopole).

Rules for the levels:

1) Any obvious noob qualities are not allowed. So if you're thinking about putting 50 rockets and 20 chaingun drones into your level, think again. Levels with lag will not be accepted. I reserve the right to not accept a level because no one would ever want to play it. If you think your level would get less than 3 ninjas on numa, don't submit as I probably wont accept it. Remember that people are going to be forced to play these levels! I've attached a level with demo to give you an idea what I'm looking for.
2) Somewhat buggy levels are acceptable, but remember that the bugs must work in user levels mode. Excessive use of bugs may disqualify the level if it is too annoying to play.
3) You must submit the map to NUMA on the day the competition starts (see below). The map must be completely you own work. You may only submit one map so make it good!

Rules for the elimination round:

1) Demos must be all gold and created in user levels mode. Cheating will result in disqualification. Demos must be submitted to numa. They may be of any length, but beware that it is sometimes difficult to submit very long demos to numa.
2) After the demo is submitted to numa it should be pm'd to me so that I can announce that one of the levels has been eliminated.
3) In the event that there is a stalemate, the demos of the remaining levels will be released.

Assuming there is interest, I will start the competition on July 1st . All the maps should be submitted to numa on this day, but please submit the two demos to me before this time so that I can tell you if the map is acceptable. The contest will last as long as it takes for all but one map to be eliminated. Let me know if you would be interested in judging. Unfortunately, judges will not be able to join in the mayhem.
