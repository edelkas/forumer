#70828 8 2005-05-30T16:57:29Z brickman
I hate people who respond to any stuck person with "watch the highscores". 95% of the time, if you go to the highscores you'll find that instead of showing you the easy way they show the "three times as hard but it shaves 1.725 seconds off your time so the top 10 or so people all did it" way, and if you look at the bottom ones you either get the "you're gonna be hurting for time when you get to the end of the epsidoe" way or the "I was totally clueless and just muscled my way through" way, neither of which are gonna necessarily make things easy.

For 7-0 it is possible to use the jumppad near the start to quickly get to the top, and you kind of need to distract the chaingun for that top button. For 7-1, you want to alternate between the side blocks and the middle ones near the top in order to get enough height. On 7-2, make sure you take the time to get on the side of the circle that your destination is on BEFORE jumping or you'll have trouble, and ignore the clock. On 7-3, I think it may be easier if you get all the buttons and then when getting the exit switch just jump across and go up through the other door. 7-4 is easy if you don't get yourself trapped in the exit switch room and approach the exit from above.
