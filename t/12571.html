#228515 9 2006-09-14T17:12:26Z Twistkill
Quote: GhostfaceN, September 14, 2006 01:00 pm
sorry i have another question. on the options menu there something that say tab button "god mode". i press tab but there is nothing like a god mode or something.
That is not "god mode" as you said, it's called boss mode. Boss mode is that the N gameplay area is covered up, so if you are playing this game at work and your boss comes over, you just hit the boss key and it's hidden. (Or if you're supposed to be doing your homework and your parents come over. )

Another advantage of this is that it saves CPU power, so instead of closing N and losing your current progress during an episode or making a map, etc. you just hit the boss key.

One last advantage: Whenever a rocket is chasing N or when a laser fires at N in debug mode, if you hit P to go the edit main menu, those noises loop infinitely. Hitting the boss key stops those sounds from looping, and you can keep your progress in whatever you're doing.

So you see, boss mode is quite handy.
