Hi,
First of all, since this post will bump the previous beta thread, we should start with:

You can download a beta version of the next release (1.4) at this thread:
http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=2651


We just wanted to update everyone on what's going on, i.e why 1.4 is still not released

As we previously mentioned, we were forced to cut some pretty significant features (all based on filesystem support) from 1.4 because none of the available products did what we wanted: allowed us to read/write files on Mac and PC without making the game choppy or laggy

We had planned on cutting those features and getting things done as much as we could this week, then releasing 1.4.

However, we've been talking with a 3rd-party developer group who may let us use a beta version of its new Mac/PC app. We've used a PC-only product of theirs and it runs N just as smoothly as the current projector They seem very sympathetic and interested in letting us use their product for N v1.4. They also clearly have the technical abilities to make their product run N as fast as possible.


So, we're going to wait for a week or two before releasing the "real" 1.4 so that we can hopefully take advantage of this and re-include all of the features we had to abandon. We don't want to be too optimistic and say this will happen for sure, though it certainly seems quite promising

Anyway... we're sorry for the continued delay, please bear with us!

Thanks,
Mare and Raigan
