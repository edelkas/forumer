#457915 7 2008-08-03T19:17:38Z T3chno
Hm, looks like my old stuff in some way. My thoughts:
*Get rid of scan lines. All of them. They are so 2007.
*Keep brush use to a limit. Your background should not be 100% brushes.
*Use new techniques in your sigs, such as smudging, C4Ds, etc.
*Keep colors varied. All red is consistent, but it's better to have variations of red.
*Depth. All you did was paste a render and put scan lines on them. No. You have to make them part of the sig.
*Blur tool: To add more depth to the sig, blur parts of a sig to make it seem like it's farther away.
*Follow tutorials: Depending on what imaging program you have, you can find loads of tuts by Googling. If you have Photoshop, bonzai collecting numerouss tutorial links here.

Hope to some more work from you. The best way to improve is to keep making sigs and experimenting.
