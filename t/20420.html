#18167 1 2007-11-28T02:54:40Z incluye
Don't worry, folks, I won't be leaving in the middle of the compilation.


So, write a chapter, make a map.


Yes, you read this correctly. I want people to help me write a book. The levels will be released on NUMA with the chapter in the level description.
Why?



Post in the thread to sign up. I'll make a list of every member, in order. When it's your turn (like the forum worm) you will have up to five days to write a chapter and make your map. If you think that's an awful short time, let me know. If you think it's far too long, tell me.

Once you've written the chapter, post it in the same .txt file as your map. The next person will read the chapter and add their own, continuing the storyline.

I will be the master editor for the writing. Maps, too, will be judged--if your map is rejected, you have the choice whether to make another or not. I would like two people to help me judge, and we can fill in if anyone's map is not accepted and they don't want to make another one.

Acceptable level types: action, race, puzzle, tileset.

If possible, I would like it if the novel is sort of about N's personal life outside his frantic enemy-dodging job.

Good luck.


~incluye
