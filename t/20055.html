Well, since I'd dare not revive a threat - nor would I revive one full of obsolete facts regarding the game, here it is!
http://sco.gpotato.com/
Since the last thread regarding this game was posted, major updates have occurred. The game is no longer buggy, the client is bug-free (from what I've heard and experienced), and the graphics have been upped a bit - specifically the way particle effects look.

In a nutshell:
-Combat occurs within your ship - a customizable ship ala` Cowboy Bebop (fans of the show will immediately sense a reference to it; both in name and in gameplay/style). Your ship is your main attribute within the game, and it is the only thing that can determine your skills.
-Your pilot is simply an avatar for roleplaying purposes. It is also the means by which you move around stations which you land in - to buy things or sell things to/from shops, to make repairs to your ship, or to buy things from other players.
-The enemies range from ground-tanks, to enemy ships, to floating eggs of demonic space-creatures.
-Combat is fast-paced, and live-action. Fans of the game Freelancer will feel right at home.
-Skills come in a few distinct flavors: Buff, Attack, Heal, Defend. Buffs do just that - they buff you. Attack skills enhance damage output and sometimes directly effect weapons/the enemy. Heals heal... duh. And Defense skills reduce damage to you.
-You can craft your own items out of loot.
-You can repaint your ship, and even buy new ships (different ships for each of four classes).
-ALL classes have similar basic and advanced weapons. Within their skills is where they truly differ. The four classes are:
B-GEAR: Basic fighter. Can combat in the air and against ground targets with equal precision.
I-Gear: Sniper. Focuses on long-range homing missile attacks. They can also use skills to make it so enemies are unable to detect their attacks. They are mainly air fighters.
M-GEAR: Medic/Buffer. Their combat skills aren't amazing, but they can heal, defend, and buff.
A-GEAR: Shapeshifting ship. It can freely switch between aircraft and tank, allowing it to combat ground targets and air targets with easy. Very, very thick armor, but speed lacks. It has a long-range cannon.
-All weapons, armor pieces, ship components, and even ship color can be changed with new parts from the shop.

...Did I mention it's completely free?

Anyone else play this great game besides me? I'm still within my first dozen levels or so, so I'm nothing impressive, of course.
