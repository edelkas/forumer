This is how to put maps onto the forum. Easy steps.

1.Go to Notebook in your start menu

2.Inside, Type in:

$Name of map#Maker of Map#Type of Map#Map code#
/\
2a.Copy and Paste code where the Map Code is /|\
|
3. Your notebook text should look like this:

Example: $Crazy map from me#Hard map making guy#Action#00000000100000100001000001000 [Dont try this code. It wont work]

4.Click on 'File' then 'Save as...'

5.Pick where to save your text and name your text file

6.Make a post and below where you type your post, theres a section called File Attachments.

7.Click 'Browse...' and find where you saved your text and double-click on it

8.To make sure your text works, go to your text and highlight THE WHOLE TEXT and paste it into the Map Data in your N game.

9.Press L to load the map and press P to play [Dont forget to press Caps Lock to unfreeze your map!]

10. If it works, Enjoy your sharable map!!!
