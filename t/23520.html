#460881 6 2008-08-16T00:18:57Z southpaw
Step 1: Get an image hosting website account. I recommend Photobucket. That way you're not limited to one image per post, and image file size isn't an issue either.

Step 2: Get GIMP or Paint.net. Figure out how to use it.

Step 3: Go to deviantArt.com and find the Resources on the left. You can download brushes such as Inner Universe for GIMP. Nothing is available for Paint.net though, unfortunately.

Step 4: Make stuff and post it in this thread often. People here will give you feedback about how to make your stuff better and more awesome.


So yeah. Those are my suggestions.
