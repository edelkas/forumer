#17279 1 2007-09-20T20:36:21Z nevermore
We don't submit maps on numa to be judged - at least those of us in our right mind shouldn't want our products to just be held under the public eye disdainfully. NUMA is a place for improvement. Just as one comes to an art class to work on their skill, everyone's map-making abilities are always works in progress. We need to learn to respect the creative and recreative process and lower our standards - how can we expect people to churn out amazing things time after time just for our satisfaction? If you dislike a map explain why and be specific, and always stay constructive.

I think this is the cure for sniping.
