This is an rpg based on my favorite robot Omicron 419 (he came before my nickname).
He was built to lead the Defenders to victory in times of need, and in this such case the cyclopian Evil Eyes are attacking. The Evil Eyes are lizard-like ghouls from the SpawnWorld, a planet that has for centuries been hidden in a nebula. There are so far (I haven't made new ones up yet,) five classes, Delta which is a Med-Bot, Alpha the regular ground artillery, Epsilon which is a sniper, Omega a bazooka packing heavy-trooper, and Omicron which is an all around class with a jet pack. This game has no stats so it is simple. I hope you enjoy it.

k. The defenders are an army of robots that protect home 1 (the planet in the rpg). You can be any class (and perhaps soon you can be the enemy) and can come to scheduled battles (ex. terrain: mountains, 5:00 east coast time on april 4). I decided not to complicate things with stats so noobs to rpgs could play. Attacks aren't "rolled," but determined by a "dungeon master," in this case me. Or if you'd prefer we can have stats and "dungeon dice."home 1 is 85% mechanic, and almost every citizen is a robot. space travel is possible and there is life out there (aliens I mean). their government is not to much different from a demodracy, except some politicians are pre-voted because they might have been the best possible choice. recently spawnworld was discovered, and soon after their fleets came to invade. the defenders were drafted back into the army, and had to defend the planet (duh). Your job as the player is to attack the enemy, knocking their reinforcements down one-by-one. certain jobs get certain missions, like if you were a delta you would have to go heal soldiers and if you were omega you would have to destroy tanks and such.
