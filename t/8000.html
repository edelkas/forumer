Pretty much anything given away by the code that could be called a secret only makes a map worse.

You have to understand that...there are over 35 thousand maps on NUMA, already far more than any one player will play, and enough that most don't even get reviewed. With so many maps, if you want your map to get played, it has to be pretty good. For a map to be good enough to get played, you need to look at the some of the high ranked maps and see what they do, understand some of the physics engine N uses, and spend enough time on your map to not only finish it, but test it and revise it a few times.
