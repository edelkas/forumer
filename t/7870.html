Music soothes the savage beast.

This competition revolves around making a map depicting the logo of your favourite band or singer ( if he/she/they don't have one, choose another). The map can be any category of level.

First three people to want to be can be the judges along with me. We will rate you on your levels by comparison to its logo, enjoyment of the level (this does not count for N art, incomplete levels, or a simple tileset), and effort in making the level.

Different awards will be:
1:Best Overall
2:Best DDA
3:Best incomplete
4:Best N Art
5:Best Action
6:Best Race
7:Best Puzzle
8:Best Test Level
9:Best Incomplete
10:Best effort
11:Best Ressemblance To Logo

Maximum 3 entries per user. The map submitting will end at March 6, 7:00 PM (I'll update to change the date if I need to).

Have Fun!
