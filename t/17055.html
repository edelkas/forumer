A friend of mine, who is part of Acqualia Software Has just finished his latest project - Picturesque. Now I'm telling you, this thing is just amazing! A simple image editor for 'batch beautifying' them for the web - and it fulfills all this and more. Here is a simple example. To create my reflectioned logo, I would usually have to open PS, duplicate layer, blah blah blah, until now! Using a simple pallete of 6 different effects, it is a very handy tool.
