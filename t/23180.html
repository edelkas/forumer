#20798 1 2008-07-14T21:26:30Z Skyline
The Weather Channel and its cheap jazz music blares. My sister walks in with a disgusted look on her face. Apparently, I stole her turn on the computer. She was playing solitaire for the past two hours. Apparently, I'm a "JERK". APPARENTLY, I CAN'T HEAR HER BITCH ABOUT ME IN THE GODDAMNED ROOM RIGHT NEXT TO ME. Summer.

I have a house on an island in Florida. Which really isn't a bad thing, because I do enjoy it. But when you're trapped down here with your horribly irritating father, your not-so-bad mother, and two boring sisters, it's not as great as it sounds.

So we go to the beach. Again. And again. And again. And goddamned again. Now, the past few weeks haven't been as bad because my older sister came down with her boyfriend, and they were great to hang out with. But with them having recently flown out of here, I'm standing in the sand and staring at my younger sister complaining about whatever the hell she has to complain about. I do admit, I enjoy her company sometimes. But today at the beach, she's afraid to go in the water. Now, the complete isolation from my friends has lead to a lot of growing tension over the past two weeks, so I don't start out being the happiest camper here. But with my family reluctant to do anything but stare at the sand and sit on their asses, I get fed up. This may sound very selfish and unreasonable of me, but as I said, I'm not exactly happy to start out with. So I walk. Away from everyone.

My feet are burning in the sand, but I could care less. I keep walking with my eyes at my feet, and I don't stop until I'm tempted by the ocean. So I go in and swim for a while in the water. This seems to relax me, so I get out and start walking back towards the familiar group of beach umbrellas, only a speck on the horizon. After walking for a bit, my mother's figure appears. She approaches me, and then proceeds immediately to yell at me for going in the water out of her watch. Now, keep in mind, I was in about five feet of ocean with calm waves and no rip tides or any of that shit. I ignore the "you should know better"'s and just walk back to dry off.

I feel lonely. I feel abandoned. I feel like I'm wasting my life. I want adventure, or something exciting. I want a better thrill than going mini golfing, which is as exciting as it seems to get around here. I just want something different. The threat of school and its obligations is looming. I need to work on my saxophone and train for cross country. And of course, summer reading. But all I want to do is just run the fuck away to somewhere else and live without any worries or work, and just do what I enjoy. I throw this idea away, but it still seems to linger in my mind. Basically, this island is driving me to the goddamned brink. Although there isn't anything inherently tragic or bad in my life, I feel like I need to make this post and complain like some self-centered prick. And you know what, that sounds about right. I feel justified. But I'm still not satisfied.

I may post more, but feel free to comment or do whatever you please.
