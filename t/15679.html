Good morning! Welcome to week six.

This week, the theme will be No Active Enemies. Maps with no active enemies are mostly maps with mines, or jumper puzzles. But this can also extend to race maps, and maps intended to convey a specific purpose (For instance, N-Art). I would like for these to be playable, since N-Art that can be done in less than 24 hours is probably gonna be pretty terrible anyway, but the gist is that there should not be any enemies that seek you out. No Gausses, no rocket launchers, no drones, no floor guards, and most certainly no thwumps.

Announcements:
- Okay, we're up on time this week!
- Submission Deadline 5 A.M. MST (GMT-7), on Tuesday, February 6th.
- I will have last week's .txt file up shortly.

The Monday Night Numa Map League is here to encourage mapmakers of all skill levels to engage in a ritual, of sorts, and to participate in something to call their own. Each week, participants will make a single map - The only stipulation? It has to be Monday. After each Monday ends, all of that week's maps will be compiled in a file, and attached to this thread. The MNNM is an ongoing project, but will have mapping 'seasons,' of an as-yet determined time frame.

To become a member, please post in the MNNM Sign-Up thread declaring your intentions to join the League. You will be assigned a member number, and be given a signature tag for use in your forum signature.

From there, simply open up NED, and begin mapping!

Each week, mapmakers will abide by a given theme for their maps, and post those maps to NUMA. This theme could be purely aesthetic, conceptual, rather specific, or very loose. Each Monday, a new theme will be posted, and league members will have until the end of that night to post their map. Once the map is posted to NUMA, each member must post a link in this thread, and at the end of each week, the maps will be compiled in the next week's thread for everyone's enjoyment! Maps from previous weeks are attached to this thread.

Each map posted to NUMA should be formatted with the Week Number and Theme. For example,
