All those feathers and their low density makes them reach their terminal velocity very quickly (when dropped with their wings died to their sides, of course). Determine what that is for each chicken, and brand the number into them where it's easily visible. Bring at least two with you wherever you go, as well as a stopwatch. That way, if you encounter a pit or a ditch of indeterminable depth, simply drop the chicken and start the stopwatch. Multiply the time it takes to *thud* at the bottom by the number you branded onto it, and that's the depth!
Chickens are excellent for spelunking.

(Note: if the pit is long enough, you may also want to factor in the speed of sound.)
