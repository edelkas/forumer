I was having mapmaker's block, so I did a couple episodes to help me come up with ideas. Right now its only 2 episodes, but I intend to add on. They all are 00 and 01 are all easy completions, but I won't say the same thing for all gold.
00 Intro
00-0
Pretty basic, but good practice for the corner jump.
00-1
Another simplistic level. A couple of reverse jumps to get the gold but still fairly easy. The first two levels are best for speedy all gold runs.
00-2
Just a mix of a bunch of different sections. Depending on how much gold you get it ranges in difficulty. The is a bug in the userlevels where the top left corner tile mutates and is a 'ghost' tile, but this really doesn't affect the gameplay. If your playing these levels in debug mode get this one here at the bottom of the txt file.
00-3
A challenging introduction of the curved tile for beginners. Also nicely set up for speed runs, but it takes a good deal of skill with curved jumping.
00-4
Two high jumps to get the gold on the underside and a for a high score run try to collect all the gold in the small passage while Keeping the Rocket Alive. Quite hard to pull off.
01 Drones
01-0
Great for practicing dodging drones passing by on the 1 tile.
01-1
Not much to say about this, pretty basic.
01-2
A tricky chaingun dodger and just plain fun to bounce around in.
01-3
Probably my favorite tileset of the pack so far. Rather hard to get all the gold.
01-4
Completion is very easy, all you have to do is build up speed so you don't get stuck in the door switch area. All gold is hard with all the lasers and chainguns firing at you.
