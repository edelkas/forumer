= The Hybrid Competition =
Welcome.

Hybrids. A lot of them have been seeing these parts recently.

And now there's a competition to celebrate them!

This is a chance to show your creativity. Take two level types. Merge them. Simple.

Rules
- Only two map types may be merged. But don't fret! I'm not limiting you to the normal level styles!

- Merging can work as you please. Styles can be merged entirely, or have one half one style and one half the other.
- Only one non-playable map style can be used per map.
- The map must be playable. However, this does not mean the Ninja can't be fired out of a KRADDA into an action level.
- No old maps.
- 2 Maps per author. May be upped later.
- Map deadline 25th of March.
- Judging will start then and be done for 1st April (or else heads will roll)
- Judges can submit.
- No glitched objects, enemies or tiles.
- No multiroute DDAs, or multi exit maps.
- Maps should be submitted to NUMA, with the description as follows:
Quote: Map Description
For M37GE! [Optional link]
[Map Style/Map Style]
[What you please]

Judging
I will need two (minimum) extra judges. Each judge shall judge all th maps, and a mean average will be taken for the final results.

Backup (To rate judges' maps) - jbaker

Quote: Judging Criteria
O (Originality) - Were the two level types mixed an original idea? Or was it something recycled such as Race/Action or boring such as two types of puzzles? [/10]
Q (Quality) - Were the two level types merged well? Or does the contrast make you feel ill? [/10]
F (Fun Factor) - Is it a great laugh to play? Or do things such as insane easiness or difficulty or repetitiveness hinder it? [/10]
R (Replay Value) - Wannanothergo? [/10]
N (NUMA Rating) - What'd you rate it on NUMA? 4 marks for every whole Ninja, 2 for a half, ergo [/20]
