EDIT: Since this problem has been fixed and kinglibai (me) has been banned, I supposed this thread isn't worth reading. Anyways, read it if you like.

Some people in NUMA like to cause trouble in any way they could. This includes excessive spamming, submitting worthless maps, and so on. But the scam that is the most damaging is deleting other people's maps.

You're probably wondering, 'That's not possible'. Well, it is.

When you delete your own map, you first have to press the delete button. Then you have to press the confirm button.

The delete screen URL looks like this:http://numa.notdot.net/map/999999/delete

The confirm URL looks like this:http://numa.notdot.net/map/999999/delete?confirm=yes

The problem is that you don't have to first go to the delete screen, you can be linked directly to the confirm screen . Once you click that link, your map is gone.

You also have to be on the account that made the map for that to work. This means that you have to somehow get the owner to click the link. If you try to delete someone else's map, it will say something like "Of course you can't delete other people's maps! What do you take us for, idiots?"

The obvious way around that is by posting the link as a comment on the map. Nine out of ten times, the victim gets scammed and accidently deletes his own map. When you think about it, there's really no other place to put this malicious link. By putting it on the forums, or anywhere else on the Net, the map's owner has little chance of comming to the map. If you're like most people, you'd check on your best maps every once in a while, and probably won't realize what the link does.

I first fell for this when someone did this to me, making me delete a map I just made. A week later, I fell for it again, deleting a DDA I spent hours on. I frantically hit BACK, but sadly, it was gone.

So then I tried the trick on a few other maps, and it worked. Even the best mapmakers didn't see it comming. After a few more times, after I was threatened with suspension by several mods, and I decided to type up this post.

To avoid getting scammed, simply look at the URL before blindly clicking it. I don't want any more people falling for this trick. Feel free to comment!

I found a way to escape tinyurl scams!
1. Right-click on link.
2. Select "Copy Link Location" (or the like)
3. Paste to anywhere.
4. Add "preview." (without the quotes).
The URL should now look like this:
http://preview.tinyurl.com/abc123 <- Link leads error page. Don't bother clicking.
Now, copy/paste to your browser, and go!
You'll get to the tinyURL preview page which lets you see the link to that URL!
Alternatively, go here...
http://tinyurl.com/preview.php
and enable previews.
