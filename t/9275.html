3 WORDS OR LESS
Being a competition of inspiration; relations; things

Dedicated to cheesegod for not being annoyed at how identical this is to his thread.

Feedback on quality of competition appreciated.

How To Win
(or, Judge, Jury, and Excecutioner)

The aim of this game is to win. This can be done by making the best map. The person who judges which the best map is, is the person who has last made the map picked to be the best map by the previous judge.

Winning is good, because it always you both a fuzzy feeling of superiority, and makes you a judge yourself. Which is good, because it always you to endow others with warm fuzzy feelings of superiority, gives you a powertrip, and always opens up the opportunity of being bribed by competitors.

The Judge
(or, The Boney King of Nowhere)

Upon having won, you are now a judge for the next round. The first thing that you must do is set down the theme for the next series of maps, using a maximum of three words.
The more obscure and open to interpretation your theme is, the better. Your words should serve more as a source of inspiration than a set of guidelines for the next map.

Acceptable- but not hugely wonderful- examples are, for instance,
Floating Islands
Flowy race maps
Chaingun drones

The sorts of examples that fill me with giddy joy are ones such as
Professor Moriarty
Radiohead
Genre: Horror

After this, you simply pick your favourite map and bestow unto them your previous position. From this point, feel free to re- enter the competition.

Round Commencement
(or, Your Time Is Up)

Each round lasts untill the judge has picked a map- and, thus, the next judge. After this point, no new maps can be made untill the new judge posts with the next topic.

A judge cannot pick a map untill either
  • 5 maps have been entered into the competition
  • 5 days have passed since the round has begun
However, maps may continue to be submitted after these criteria have been fulfilled- up untill the point that a winner has been picked.

Rules for Entry
(or, No No No No No No No No)

The rules are very basic.
Your map must relate, in some way, to the topic given. If the way in which it relates is trivial (for instance, a boss map of a large squid for the topic of "Professor Moriarty") you must provide a description of exactly how it relates (for instance, "Professor Moriarty is the arch nemesis of Sherlock Holmes, on whom the books are based. This squid thing is the arch nemesis of N, on whom the game is based.)
Your map must be submitted to NUMA.
Your map must be a playable map- Action, Puzzle, or Race.
For obvious reasons, you cannot submit a map when you are a judge.

And that's it.
