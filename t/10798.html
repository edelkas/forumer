I'm not sure where this goes but I'm not submitting it to Numa either.....

One day N had a call from the Ministry of Sewage that he had to clean out the alternative pipe that lead to the city's water supply; because the current one had germs and bacteria in the water.

Spoiler Warning!! (Answers in white.)


Step 1. (Note: The boxes at the top check off for each step.) Close the current pipeline by jumping on to the bounce block trigger. Head off by collecting all the gold and opening the new pipeline. Run to the right to clear some dirt for the new pipe....

Step 2. Jump and land on the one-way,Tip: Do not touch the plans for the new pipe!! close the travel pipeway to the city's line so bacteria doesn't contaminate the supply. Jump up in Fbf to get to the top of the new pipe, again clearing some dirt. Travel down, into the city's control system for water.

Step 3. Squeezzzzzzzze into the control booth, activating the switches. Tip: Fall from a high height into the booth.

Step 4. Climb up the sewage pipeline and then touch the special control panel. (Near the booth.) BE CAREFUL!! After you've activated the button to let water flowing, you will be standing under the the newest water pipeline!! Jump up to saftey over the booth!

Step 5. When you see water flowing in the newly created pipeline, you have succeeded in your sewer-pipeline-water-city-job-cleaner-mission!!!

Have fun!!!
