This thread is to post maps that you are very proud of, and you want to be in the next N- probably in the user levels list. You may post up to 4 of your own, and 2 links to other peoples maps you feel definately deserve to be included.

After this, some of us enthusiastic folk will filter through the maps, cutting out a few maps from the lsit. We will cut out things such as
  • Overly difficult levels
  • Levels with bad loading times
  • DDA's that do not work every single time
  • Levels that are undeniably crap
And then, we'll present the list to Mare and Raigan, and they can go through some quick tests, and stick them (hopefully!) on the next N.

The reason for doing this, is so that some of the maps that you feel are your best may be included. Also, this way, hopefully not too many *great* maps will be overlooked in the- well- huge NUMA archive.

Cheers! Thanks, Mare and Raigan, for letting this thread go ahead, and create more work (later on) for you two...
