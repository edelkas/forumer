I just found a good roguelike a few days ago, called TOME. It has a huge selection of classes, races, and race modifiers (every one of the dozens of races can be four types of undead or a barbarian or hermit or regular). There's also slews of unique monsters and artifacts. And then there's fates which randomly place challenges or princesses or artifacts on a level--you can even be "fated to die" (meaning that if you haven't leveled up your character yet, you WILL probably die). So far my best character is a level 11 troll barbarian swordsman who has already killed two uniques, rescued two princesses, finished two quests, and found three unique artifacts (and several non-unique but specially enchanted items. I threw away a sword of demon slaying and a mace of slay undead to use a magic dagger that hurls ice bolts at a point where in most games I'm beginning to ammass my first steel armor), and thats by level 11. I can only imagine how cool it will be by level 50 when I've moved on to non-fighte classes, like possesors or mimics. There's so many options. . . if you have ever enjoyed a roguelike, you'll probably do well to play this one.
