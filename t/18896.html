#355312 2 2007-08-14T17:00:00Z AMomentLikeThis
Open the userlevels.txt file.

For the maps you want to put in there, put this: $[Map Name]#[Author]#[Type (ie: Action)]#[Map Data (the map code)]#.

Also, make sure theres a &userdata= at the top of the text file, otherwise N can't read it.
