#15442 1 2007-05-30T05:39:23Z Mosh
Launchpads that face a horizontal angle (left and right, respectively) don't do much for N's running speed. Sure, they offer a momentary boost, but only so much. Not really enough to help N scale a wall, even if placed a single tile from the sloped-corner of the wall.

Solution?

Why not add a run-speed object? I'm assuming it would have the same placement-flexibility as a Launchpad, but instead of causing a fixed velocity boost that restarts at Velocity-Zero, why not simply multiply the velocity of the Ninja? It would allow seamless speed boost in all directions, and offer an alternative - not a replacement - for the Launchpad.

Action levels would easily take advantage of the quick-response and "Guarenteed Boost" capability of launchpads, and race levels would most likely take advantage of the "Fast moving gets even faster" capability of the Velocity-Boosters.

Possibly, even, they'd have multiple different types of Velocity-Multipliers. A stripe on the object image, a color change - whatever could be used to identify the amount of velocity multiplication - whatever it would be, it would add insurmountable diversity, in a very positive way, to the gameplay. Possibly x1.5, x2, x2.5, and x5 Velocity boosters could be implemented.

They wouldn't be direction-specific, either. Simple pass through one and your velocity is increased. Maybe take a half-second graduated curve in the increase of velocity, so it isn't a sudden jerk in speed, and there you have it. Increased velocity based on current velocity.

What do you think?

Cheers,
~Mosh
