#96361 3 2005-09-02T01:36:57Z Guest
i think the key is to walljump while you're still moving up. there's certainly a timing that you just need to "get".. jumping adds to your upward velocity, so if you jump when you've got some upward velocity you'll go faster.. if you wait too long between jumps, you'll be moving down and the jump power will be cancelled by your downward movement.

i hope that makes sense..
