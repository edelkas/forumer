The date is Saturday, 20th of may 2006. And two unrelated unvents occur.

1. Scientists unexpectadly discover that the earth will be destroyed by a meteor in 2 months.
2. An enterprising young man by the name of "Thomas de Louvre" has started up a small hotel in England called "Hotel Acapelypso". It's a very nice hotel indeed, filled with potted palms, cool, darkly coloured tiles and lush, red carpets. The rooms are slightly scare in their furnishings, but the main lounges and dining room are very comfortable and friendly- the sort of environment that encourages friendly talk between strangers, and sparks intimacies between people whom had never previously met.

And so, in this comfortable haven nestled away from the harshness of the world, we lay our scene. Our scene of the last two months of this world, when the planet learns of it's approaching doom and is the end slowly nears. Our scene, with the sole set being this small hotel, where people go to stay and await the end.

A little information...

This is sort of a refined version of "Meteor", which was an RPG that sort of failed about a year ago. Except, that the plot is not run by one person- this RPG is run by everyone, and the world will adjust from the news that each individual character brings in.

So, come into the hotel in as many guises as you like- I'll take the role of Thomas de Louvre and a handful of others- and discuss things, talk, debate, on all topics, but as though the world outside is progressing the way that it is.

Explain plans, talk of love and life, and we'll see what happens between our characters- which may come and go as they will- during the last two months of our world. Enjoy!

Now... who are our first guests?
