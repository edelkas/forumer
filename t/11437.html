Ok. This is pretty simple, but you can only get involved if you have a music library on your computer and an app that can select a track at random.

User A answers a question, User B posts the track they get for the answer (chosen on shuffle or random by their music player, it should be first track they get), and they also post a translation of what the answer means.

Let me demonstrate.

Question: What will I be remembered for?

Artist: Jeff Buckley
Track: Last Goodbye
Translation: Sweep will be remembered for his last goodbye.

Often the answers in this can be quite funny, sometimes they don't make any sense, sometimes they seem to be oddly truthful. Try your best to translate them logically but, er, innovatively.
