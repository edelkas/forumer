#2782 1 2005-05-08T13:29:43Z brickman
Well, the oficial version of 1.4 is gonna be out soon, and the unofficial release has had its testing time. And for the most part, it is excellent. But there's one thing that should be changed: you need a quick way to play user levels.

Now don't get me wrong. The addition of a way to play levels on a timer is great. But currently the only way to play these levels is to edit the text document, and that takes a lot longer than just pasting the data was in previous versions. Of course, we can still do levels like that in debug mode, but doesn't that defeat the purpose? We need a quick addition to the user level page of a temporary level insertion thing: a button that brings up its own level data and author boxes, which you just fill in once to play the level without actually adding it to the list. You can play it untill you win/give up, then just exit and its done--just like in debug, but with the timer and automatic recording.



Also, as long as I have your attention, could you guys make it so that n interacts with objects after hitting the exit in user levels? Thats the way they were built. Many of my levels and undoubtedly many others were built with mini thwump-DDAs as a "reward" for finishing, and this way they never happen and people just wonder "Okay, whats that thwump and those tiles doing there at the bottom of sprint?" and the like. And there's countless ones where n now falls through a oneway or bounceblock floor and goes splat.
