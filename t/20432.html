I use the name 'Invictus' from a poem of the same name. I'm not a poet, but I've always like this poem. I work for the Sheriff's Dept and this is one of many poems that we use as motivational motto's in our training philosophy.


Invictus

Out of the night that covers me,
Black as the pit from hole to hole,
I thank whatever gods may be
For my unconquerable soul.

In the fell clutch of circumstance
I have not winced nor cried aloud.
Under the bludgeonings of chance,
My head is bloody, but unbowed.

Beyond this place of wrath and tears
Looms but the horror of the shade.
Yet the menace of the years find,
And ye shall find me unafraid.

It matters not how high the gate,
How charged with punishments the scroll,
I am the master of my fate.
I am the captain of my soul.

William Ernest Henley. 18491903
