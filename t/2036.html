Instant download

This game, with brilliant vector graphics similar to Kenta Cho's shoot-em-ups, has you controlling a ship which can move in all directions in a box-shaped arena where you destroy numerous enemies and confront massive bosses (sometimes more than one simultaneously) every 10 stages See how far you can get in the half-hour provided

Unfortunately, the creator of Area 2048 botched up menu navigation Play the game and try to just access Hard Mode to see what I mean.

Gameplay:

The top-left shows your main statistics.
L = Level. There are many levels, each with 10 stages called "scenes" (bosses are in every tenth scene).
W = Weapon. This is your ship's special ability. [More on this below.]

First Boss:



Default Key Setup:
Arrow keys move your ship in 8 directions.
Use Z to launch your standard, nostalgic projectile barrage When an enemy is nearby (including bosses) and you push Z, your ship will track the enemy and will continuously fire at it until it is destroyed (notice the first screenshot), or unless you stray too far away from it and it comes out of tracking.
When the target is destroyed, you lose tracking and will have to let go and push Z again in order to track the next enemy

The W shows a bar for X. Hold X to make the screen zoom out and trigger the invincibility square which appears at the beginning and end of a scene. Holding Z while holding X (sorry, Castaway, for the complex fingerwork involved ) will have your ship launch off a huge barrage of projectiles, easily more than twice as much as your normal shooting, but it drains the W bar. W can accumulate up to 4 bars

The top-right shows you (white) and your enemy/enemies (red).

The bottom-left shows how much time you have left and how many enemies are remaining.

You can push P to pause the game at [just about] any time.

Projectiles do not disappear before they touch the end of the arena However, you can touch the border and live. Touching enemies kills you.
