let x=1
x-x=x-x
thats true rite?
factorise LHS with difference of 2 squares
(x-x)(x+x)=x-x
factorise RHS by taking out x
(x-x)(x+x)=x(x-x)
divide both sides by (x-x)
x+x=x
2x=x
therefore 2=1
QED
