It's amazing to see how many mistakes Sony has made with the PS3, and how much bad press there is out there as a result. This timeline that I found a few weeks ago chronicles a lot of the blunders surrounding the console (it's the first two posts in the thread). Basically, this is my thought process when I look at the PS3:

The high price of the PS3 means a lower install base.
Lower install base means lower game sales.
High development costs combined with low game sales means low profit for developers.
Low profit means the PS3 won't see many exclusive titles.

At that point it becomes a vicious cycle. Fewer exclusives plus a continued high price (Sony can't drop the price very easily due to the $200-$300 hit it's taking on each console sold) means continued low install base, meaning continued low game sales, and so on and so forth. In order to break this cycle, Sony is going to have to part with a lot of money to either secure exclusives or lower the price. Either way, the company certainly has its work cut out for it.
