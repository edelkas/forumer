#236905 2 2006-10-03T23:22:18Z Twistkill
I think this may be the solution:

Right-click anywhere in the N window when it's open. Click on settings, and then click on the tab that has a folder on it. (That's Local Storage, which is what your problem is, right?) Slide it all the way to the right, and then click close. Then your data should be saved automatically whenever you play.
