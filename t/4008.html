#82789 5 2005-07-11T22:16:42Z Raigan (guest)
hi,
we think it's really cool that so many new and weird things were "found"; at the same time, there are certain levels which i wish we had made _after_ having seen the things that could be done

but it's really awesome to watch the highscores and see the crazy weird things that can be done, it's really impressive! i hope that all of our games will reward people for being inventive and trying strange stuff.

DDAs are also really cool -- does anyone know who first invented them?

about the launchpads, it was easier to do things the way it is now (a raw direction vector) instead of having a number code (i.e 1, 2, 3, 4) which would then have to get translated into a direction vector. but we didn't ever think of messing with that.

i don't know.. going through all the user levels was really cool as we got to see so much creativity -- one thing i remember well is a level with just a single rocket launcher.. i thought it was impossible until i realised you had to get the rocket to chase you around! i don't know.. it just seemed like such a cool idea.

raigan
p.s - about the floor guard, you'll recall that earlier versions of ned had you choose a "starting direction", even though it made no difference; i'm afraid that the 1 is just the starting direction param.. if i remember correctly.
