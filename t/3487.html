#72343 5 2005-06-04T22:01:59Z brickman
I liked HL2. A lot. Only thing I really didn't like was the occasionally slow pacing (although that sort of didn't ruin things too much), how hard of a time I had with getting used to the vehicles (which greatly contributed to problem #1), and the ending.
I think that it could have done with another chapter or two, but I wasn't really left saying "man, only __ chapters, what a ripoff." And any length gripes I could have would have to be quelled by the sheer number of people making mods to extend its life and the free expansion thing they're making (Aftermath). What it could really do with is a BETTER ENDING. I mean, I played HL1, and when I got to the second half of Zen after beating the giant spider/headcrab mother boss, I literally had to use god mode for the rest of the game because it was THAT HARD (and this was on easy mode, I don't even remember if I had the patience to REACH that boss on hard), and while I do think it was too hard I know that every inch of that would have been extreemely rewarding if I had been capable of doing it. And the final boss and ending sequence were cool. In HL2, I did the entire citadel without really breaking a sweat thanks to the insanely powerful upgraded ggun and the chargers which gave you 200% health and full armor every time. I didn't even really enjoy it, since I wasn't being forced to treat the ggun as a ggun as much as an electric gun (all I had to do was charge at them until I was within the arbitrary primary fire range, and one or two blasts killed a group of six). Aside from grabbing energy balls I only used secondary fire once or twice. And the very end. . . even if you're not going to give us the boss we were expecting, give us something more climactic than a pair of gunships (which would actually have been pretty climactic anywhere else in the game, but here it was just "grab energy ball from generator next to you, turn 20 degrees, shoot" repeated about 5 times for the pair).

As for Ravenholm, I'll quote what I said right before Keron told us to split the topic:
Quote
For some reason Ravenholm was not all that scary for me. I mean, I'm sure it was a little scary, but ten times greater than the fear was the feeling that "This is so sweet. . ." as I chopped groups of zombies in half at once with sawblades and smashed them with cars and set them on fire and (despite not being the least bit bloody or extreemely effective I always liked this one) just pummeling them over and over with a paint can until it stops moving. But when I came out of that door and watched those zombies playing up on the roof, I literally said out loud "You gotta be kidding me. Flying sombies?", but in a good way as in "cool". Of course they don't really fly, but it looked enough like it. I suppose the scariest part would be when you're calling the elevator, of course. "I'll just make sure they don't get in this door". . .
So basically Ravenholm was perhaps the most fun part of the game, because every inch was just violent fun with you thinking "now how am I going to mutilate this next group of zombies" every time you enter a new room. I guess it actually was a bit scarier near the end than I let on in that post, mostly in the parts where fast zombies did roof assaults and that map where you go through an entire wing of th city just to be told you're going the wrong way (mainly because of the respawning zombies).
The vehicle parts. . . well, I have to address them seperately. The airboat chapter, once I was used to steering, was supremely awesome (both the boat segments and the indoor ones, actually) until the part where they attach a gun to it. Now this should make things more fun in theory, but I am yet to master the art of moving at high speed with one hand and aiming a machinegun (which is a weapon that intrinsically requires you to keep the crosshairs centered on any strong target without allowing them to stray, which is impossible when you're moving) with the other. I simply couldn't do it. So I ended up creeping through it, always making sure to stop or almost stop the boat before doing any fighting that involves an opposing force stronger than three combine footsoldiers, and just going past any combat I could while just shooting wildly in hopes of knocking out one or two of my dozen adversaries as I pass to save health. It was slow paced and not too fun, but it was generally much more effective than anything else. And it made the scene with two armored cars in an open area and the final fight with the chopper hard and more than a bit annoying. The car section was basically the same as the second part of the boat one but slightly better due to the obvious differences between the gauss gun and he machinegun and because you had to do a lot of footwork too without worrying about ammo at all. And because you could just floor it through a lot more if you wanted.
My favorite sections were the first airboat part, Ravenholm and everything between getting the pheropods and the end of the "Nova Prospect" chapter (basically, the entire part with antlion allies plus the brief segment where you're seperated from them, because its so beautifally orchestrated to make it still feel like they're helping/coming for you). In no particular order.

As for mods, I can't find too many that are finished. I would love for people to post some finished (or at least fully playable without any huge problems, I don't really care if its in its 27th testing stage or not) mods, preferably some single player ones.

#72522 7 2005-06-05T14:40:40Z brickman
Hey, I don't think that the HL1 ending was lacking at ALL. The last few levels were so intense I couldn't even finish them all, leading up to not one but two bosses who are both bigger than you and BOTH could be viewed as important (hey, try to tell me taking down the haedcrab mother is not going to help anyone) and who both make you feel awesome when you beat them. Definitely didn't leave you feeling like you were shortchanged. And the ending cinematic, in my opinion, was actually a pretty good ending, leaving off at the perfect spot--it leaves you with just enough questions. The HL2 one, however, is the oppoasite--no bosses, no crazy difficulty, no coolness, no feeling of accomplishment, and that ending accomplishes pretty much NOTHING. The idea was for it to be the opposite in the sense that the first was an entirely organic area, where you knew you weren't getting any more ammo while the second was an entirely metal environment with infinite ammo, but it ended up being opposites in the wrong ways.

But lets change the subject to something more people LIKED. Like, all the "imaginative" things you did in Ravenholm. One of my favorite things to do was to gfet out the gravity gun when I enter a room with one of the fan traps, and instead of turning them on like normal just give them a jolt whenever someone gets near. Sure, turning it on is a bit easier, but this way was much more fun and could be used to keep that one that flies off from breaking. Another interesting thing I did was that when I got to the part where Gregori sends you a lift, instead of just standing my ground up there against the zombies I jumped down onto the street (taking some damage, but there's medkits on the other side) and then let the zombies pursue me through the streets and the building as I made my way back up. Not only was it cool, it wa also effective--I got up there again at the same time that the lift did, and the zombies can't get through the trapdoor, so you just did the whole challenge without wasting a single bullet of ammo and with only one fall's worth of damage, which the meds on the other side can more than handle. Its also really fun spawning a lot of rebel and zombie npcs to have either a "watch someone else go through ravenholm" situation or just some huge battles. If only spawning important npcs like alyx and gragori worked right. . .
