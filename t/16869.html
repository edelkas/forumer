#312165 10 2007-04-11T16:00:09Z apg
Not too bad for a first map. Better than my first, that's for sure. The main problem was lack of variety.

Also, there were two things you might not have noticed:
  1. You don't have to open all locked doors to get to the exit switch.
  2. If the hidden trap door on the bottom was supposed to prevent you from exiting, it doesn't work - you can still touch the exit through the trap door and win.
(Basically, try not to use those kind of 'death traps', because restarting the map just for not finding a hidden trap door is generally annoying. )
