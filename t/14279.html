#266038 2 2006-12-07T17:32:39Z wedgie
If you right click in the game window when its loaded and click on settings, you will have a small window come up. If you click on the tab with a folder on it then you will get to the flash player settings. If the slide bar on here is to the extreme left then that will mean that flash wont be able to save anything onto your computer, including your saves. If you slide this to the right then it will allow the game to save its progress.

If the bar in here isn't all the way to the left, then I have no idea what the problem is, sorry. Its usually just a case of what I said before.

EDIT: Actually, if its just stopped saving on the seventh column, then it could be that your storage tab is only set to allow for a small amount of information to be saved and that its save limit has been reached once you got to the seventh column.
