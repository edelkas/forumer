Answer the poll, for a little experiment. There are many reasons a person may not rate someone's map, and that hurts the flow of NUMA, as explained here:

http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?sho...ndpost&p=460542

And so, for each option, here's what you should do to improve.

1. A map can be messy or sloppy, but that doesn't define the actual map in perspective. You could play this map, and it might result in an overly fun/hilarious situation. If the gameplay wasn't good, then rate it, but always explain comments. Comments are just as important as rates, because it tells what you should improve. Harsh criticisms or not, you should always take it in mind.

2. You -should- care, really. The unrated situation is a self-feeding paradox. If you don't rate (and/or comment), this will indicate that either the regular mapmaker doesn't know what is wrong and will continue creating such atrocious maps (thus causing good maps going away from the attention they deserve), the mapmaker will embrace it as good intentions and will continue (still causing the good maps to go down), or the mapmaker will just quit (which means we would have lost a potential member to our forums), and they all don't end that well. If you took the time to do these comments and rates, you might have inspired a future great mapmaker to improve themselves, thus causing an even more contribution of such professionally well done maps to NUMA.

3. Some people have this problem, really. Worried about such rates, that they forget that NUMA is not a 'get-rated-highly-and-get-popular' community. NUMA is not a competition (which is really what it has all boiled down to). Some people take this too seriously, and will go to no lengths to get rated (which explains the sudden jumps in sniping, multi-accounting, and maybe even no ratings). Just a word in advice: It's just a game. It's not real. You will not win anything of worth if your map becomes the top rated of all of NUMA. Not even dronies count here; It's digital, you're not goona print the thing and hang it, are you? Psh.

4. Rating specific authors is nice, because you get to enjoy your maps with 99.9% certainty that you'll like it. But you need to cut some slack for those other maps, too, you know. They make it with -your- intentions to rate and play.

5. Like I said in poll explanation #2, it all bares down to competition. Snipes lower rates, and that's bad to do. Rate honestly, people. Even it is the person you least enjoy, rate honestly. Maybe you might improve them in a turn for the better.

6. Now, this goes down in either competition, a grudge, or jealousy. If it is a competition, please refer to poll explanation #3. If it is a grudge, then you should just put that aside, man. An eye for an eye would make the world blind. Ghandi said that, and Ghandi ain't never wrong. Just rate the map, and put your hatred aside. You'll be friendly and happy again. Such anger should never become like this. If it is jealousy, then don't be jealous, man. The mapmaker never did anything to you, so why hate him? Rate and comment honestly, man, and all is well.

7. Pardon the bad pun. Explain if you have a different reason.
