I have already posted these in a thread but got told i should post them in an entirely new thread instead. So here it is, edited largely so that hopefully you will like them more.

Some Ideas:
1. As an unlockable secret, Gravity can be toggled ONLY in practise mode, making the game harder or easier. I know this has already been brushed upon, but i believe it would be an excellent feature. Because it is onyl in practise mode it woudl simply be used for fun, and nothing more. This would not change gameplay because the actual game would be the same as normal.

2. Short animations when you complete a group of levels or column. For instance this could be an example of some incredibly nifty N work done by someone with high skills. Or EVEN part of a story so that when you have completed the game you have a full sub story of an N adventure. Which can be watched in the options menu or something.

3. Levels which are completely dark. BUT N has an area of light around him wherever he goes so you can see what is immediately around you. Also the screen only becomes dark when you being playing. While the screen is paused just after loading before you start it, you can see the whole level to give you an idea of where to go. Though this idea will probably be looked down upon because it could be seen as changign gameply. However, it could be used in unlockable Extra levels not involved in the actual game. This woudl reduce it's impact on gameplay.

4. Possibly a way to upload your own custom backgrounds for N; you could have anything, but it only affects your N, everybody else has their own background, or the default if they chose not to add a background.

5. Blocks which you can only stand on a certain number of times. After which they either crumble or simply disappear. They could go different colours when stood on to show how many touches the have left. Or maybe blocks which Zap you when you stand on them for too long.

6. When sliding down a verticle wall, if you press the arrow towards the wall again then the ninja will grab onto the wall harder so that he stays in one place and doesnt slide down any further. Until you press the opposite arrow once that is. This is developing the sliding move, more than adding a new one.

7. The amount of Gold you collect or levels you complete changes your status as a ninja. E.g. From Amateur to Confident to Pro etc. etc. finally Enlightenment. This would give people somehtign else to aim for. Also it might make N glow different colours, depending on how close he is to enlightenment. Like No glow to begin with but then eventually a multi layered Golden glow. This is slightly similar to Flavours. the glow would be transparant so you arent put off when playing.
ALSO while on the subject of gold, maybe you can buy flavours with the gold you have, and the red scarf mentioned in a thread etc.


8. You can fall as far as you want onto the bouncy blocks!!!! They act as a cushion to your fall. It isnt right that you can only fall onto them from as far as you fall onto the floor.


Oh by the way, im pretty sure most of these have been mentioned in less detail, but i wanted to build upon them.
Don't call me a N00b as if i didnt already know about it.

More ideas to come if you are friendly!!!!
