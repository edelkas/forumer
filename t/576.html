If you look up at the top of this forum, you will see a two page thread about weapons. I have also seen a thread somewhere about network play. So I had an idea: competitive swordplay 2 player. I know, I'm not the first to think of this, but I'm gonna back it up with an idea of how it works.
First, each player has a "health" bar, which takes the place of the time bar but goes dwon with hits instead of time. Each starts with 10 full points (ten secs). Gold randomly appears somewhere every 15 seconds and heals whoever gets it 2 points.
Each player has two attack buttons added to the movement ones (fortunately, the n movement system is simple enough that this won't cause problems if two ppl use one keyboard). The first button is stab, second slash. On the groundd, stab does 1 damage fast, slash does 2 but is slower. In the air, slash does 1.5 in front of you and stab does 3 BELOW YOU, but only if you hit it while going down. Thus, in order to win you have to always stay above your opponenet and off the ground, encouraging people to still use n's physics instead of just swordplay.
In order to take full advantage of n's physics, you couldn't just do dm. Mabey additional modes like one where one player starts with a 3 second health bar and no sword and the other is hooked up to a 20 second timer (chase), or where each player's sword starts in the middle of the map and they must get to it fast to gain an advantage over their opponent (race).
People would be able to make their own maps for this, and specify the modes it works for. It would allso come with one or two maps per mode. The players would obviously be different colors (or mabey just a floating number above their head).

What do you think? It may be a lot of work, but its the only multiplayer mode that would do justice to the n engine.
