#48362 4 2005-01-28T20:50:24Z Eli
Yup, I'm there.

I don't like it. There's too much of a 'production cascade' -- a situation in which production starts slow, then quickly becomes excessive. Once this happens, gold from excessive-production provinces can also be invested in causing excessive production in newly conquered provinces.

A production cascade is not always a problem in itself, but in this game, huge armies make things extremely difficult, especially with that action limit. Gold/production, if not used on armies, can do these things:
1. Increase production. This is bad for obvious reasons.
2. Levy taxes. This is bad for the same reasons, but also because it doesn't even help you much in the long run, and drains your influence as well.
3. Build fortifications. This is bad because it just makes the game drag out longer and huge armies even more worthless.

The AI is terrible, as well. In one game vs. the AI, I built up production in 5 territories, then conquered the world in one fell swoop. They should have realized that if there was an Emperor in a 5-land area, he was pretty darn powerful. By the end, my stupidly huge army had about 5000 upkeep.

Basically, even with humans, the best strategy is to look tough, while you're actually farming and building culture and sending diplomats to prepare for your onslaught, which is no fun anyway.

There aren't even any settings you can change to make the game more fun.
