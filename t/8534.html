#170248 10 2006-05-01T02:01:43Z Ben Miff
Hrm. Tiles...

It really depends on the map type you're building. I tend to build maps using just the E's and D's first since I do a lot of drone maps; once the drone paths are out of the way, then you can start working on the tiles themselves. Of course, if you're doing anything with gauss turrets or laser drones that can only target a certain area, then using the other tiles to start with is perfectly fine. As for more decorative maps, it's important to try and get the tiles to fit in with an overall theme as well as just looking decent. Generally it's best to try and avoid mixing curves and angles (4 and 8's with the rest of the numbers), although larger "bowl" shapes will still use the angles. Also, when using angles, you'll need to choose how fractured you want the ground and walls; it's best not to mix a highly fractured landscape (such as this) with a smooth landscape (such as this). The only other thing I can think of is that thwumps don't tend to mix well with curves, and also to remember that these are more guidelines than rules; there'll always be exceptions that'll work.
