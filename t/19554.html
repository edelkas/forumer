#17355 1 2007-09-28T20:40:41Z Frogs
I was looking at this thumbnail, and I noticed that in the instances where the bounceblocks were half-snapped one on top of another, it appeared as if it was just a single elongated bounceblock. I got an idea; couldn't we just "stretch" bounceblocks to different sizes? The increments would be the height and length of one square on the grid in the tile and object editor mode, unless you wanted to use c-snap or z-snap, which would enable you to stretch them more precisely. You would, of course, still be able to place them at their normal size. An example of a way to use this would be to leave a corridor around the outside edge of the map, and the middle would be taken up by one giant bounceblock. The physics would be different (?) then if you were to cover that same area with regular sized bounce blocks.

There's also the idea of the bounceblock editor, where you could cut corridors or "hidey-holes" through and out of a stretched bounceblock. Your thoughts?
