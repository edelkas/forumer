#300858 3 2007-02-25T03:15:24Z Twistkill
... *points to avy and sig*

Okay, when you enter the room, look near the back. You'll see 2 statues with a glowing-type thing next to them. This (hopefully obviously, by now) means that you can move them around. Move each of them in front of the 2 spawning points of the Harpies, and voila! However, they will be constantly attacking you while you do this, so you will have to regrab the statues every time they make a hit because your animation will stop.

But wait! For a huge bunch of red orbs: Use Medusa's Gaze on the Harpies while they're flying around, before you plug the spawn points. Whenever you freeze one, it will fall to the ground and break, giving you a 30 orb bonus for breaking the Harpy using Medusa's Gaze. Repeat this many, many times until the game will not allow you to take anymore. There is a health and magic alternating chest behind each statue, so you can continue this for a while. When the game puts a cap on your orbs, or when you've run out of magic, plug up the spawn points.
