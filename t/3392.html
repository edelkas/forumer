Ok... What about a program that manipulates the order of objects in the map?

Take me for example, I generally put gold down on a map last, but I prefer the enemies to move in front of the gold. If you had some kind of batch processing tool you could stick all your maps in it and give the object Z orders (i.e. the apparent depths) a uniform look throughout your levels.

Of couse, you could do it manually, but such a simple operation would be great to have automated. Just drag and drop the order of priority you want different object types.

Some other uses: Making enemies appear behind mines (an effect I quite like). Making sure gold appears over trap switches and that exit switches appear over trap or normal switches.

It could also remove nans or do other manipulations such as making supa-pads and maybe even a teleport setter-upper? And a thwump bug maker etc..

I'm still not 100% sure this is a good idea but it's interesting..
