#248433 6 2006-10-31T23:01:31Z Jagermeister
Okay, here goes my advice for 89-3 "Entice" by tktktk


The main technique used in this map is the ability to attract the "hunter" drones to manipulate your situation so that you can grab the necessary items without getting yourself killed. However, I'm pretty sure that you already knew this. There is a very easy way to get the drones to the top, which is the hardest part, in my opinion. When the hunter drones are on the bottom level and you are protected by the platforms you stand on, just "mimic" their path until you are in open to be hunted. As soon as you enter this area jump the gap, and you should be even in midair with the drone right when you take off from your jump. Just clear the gap and he'll barely miss you and fly to the top.

After you go to the bottom and retrieve the item (without attracting enemies down there), spend this time attracting the enemies down to the bottom level WHILE YOU ARE IN the bottom level. It is so much easier doing it from here than "clearing gaps" while attracting, trust me. If you run out of space before you get them all in, retreat to the far right into the "drone-free" zone that they cannot enter. Go back into the lair to finish attracting them all.

Once this is done, run to the top, get the key, go back to the middle, and do all that without attracting them (it's very simple, really. Just don't go out into the "open" when they are out there). Then, hop in the doorway and celebrate. Then get ready for Mt. Doom.
