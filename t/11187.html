#202068 10 2006-07-13T07:19:39Z Calcy
It's a hardware that looks like a CD player but connects through your USB port to your computer. When it's connected, every time you play N you can play in frame-by-frame, even in the regular game. You'll get a passcode and a URL on Metanet's site once you beat the game, and you can use those to enter your home address. You'll receive the FbF machine in about 5 business days.

It's quite handy.
