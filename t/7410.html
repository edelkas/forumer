Today is a glorious day!

...For today, it is here. For months now, we have had a secret that we have been keeping from all of you. Around the forums, and around IRC, we have been dropping hints of "It's coming," and other assorted hints that most of you have probably missed. But now, it is here, and the veil shall be pulled back, to reveal what it is that has been coming, and why you absolutely need to be interested.

It all began as Maximo's brainchild. Back in early October, 2005, Maximo decided to comemmorate his official return to the forums with a bit of a treat. After a bit of work on the treat, he spoke to LittleViking001 about it, and LV quickly jumped onto the bandwagon. From there, nevermore, tktktk, sweep, and Kablizzy were asked to join the project, as well, to which we all graciously accepted. We all began working on this thing, and for the past five months, we have been collaborating with each other daily and nightly, working tirelessly to get this thing up and running. And now, it is finally here.

Now, some of you may have seen us planning for this. Mostly on IRC, during the early stages of planning, you may have noticed the "Legacy" channel that LV and Kablizzy and nevermore and Maximo tend to frequent. We masqueraded this as our staff channel, which, to an extent, was true, since all six of us are staff members. In reality, it was our planning channel for this project, and why we guarded it so fervently. So, back to the topic at hand. Over the past 5 months, we have worked every night on this project. We have slaved and tweaked, and we feel as though, after all this work, that we are finished, that the Legacy Project is finally ready for launch.

...So, what is the Legacy project, you ask? The Legacy Project consists of Six main members: Maximo, LittleViking001, tktktk, nevermore, Sweep, and Kablizzy. We six have - over the past five months - been working on a level-pack for N. Five of us have been working on the levels, while Sweep has been providing playtesting and website development. This level pack, which we have entitled "Legacy," consists of 400 unique maps, arranged in 8 columns of 10 episodes each. Each episode consists of five maps, arranged in the same way as the original Metanet levels. In addition, the play-style of each of the levels is reminiscent of the original Metanet levels, to which each of us have added our own particular flair and flavor to the project, and each level has our own unique touch to it. It has taken us almost half a year to complete this project, but it will all, in the end, be well worth it.

We hope you've enjoyed all of the preperation over the Legacy Project, but we hope you enjoy the project itself that much more. So, without further ado, visit the Legacy Website, and have fun!
