Not every 0 vote is a sniper, and, to be fair, not every 5 vote is valid ("reverse sniping" is just as valid of a problem as sniping is). Point is that people will vote how they want to vote and there is nothing you can do to have the server stop them.

About the only way you could deter them (not stop, just slow them down) is to put a cap on the number of votes allowed per hour or something similar to that. Most people that are going to spam votes for someone else would be deterred by only being able to put in 5 votes in an hour (for example).
