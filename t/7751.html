#147460 2 2006-02-23T10:43:53Z Jiggerjaw
Tip: Push button really fast
No kidding, really. Just push jump thrice in succession as fast as your fingers will let you. I've never heard of going too fast, because a perfect triple jump is:
Jump/frame/jump/frame/jump/jump/jump/jump/
What I mean by that is only .050 seconds separates the first and last jump.
You'll get it eventually, and you'll find it's really not that tough. Plus, just to tell you, there was already a topic about this by -naem- in complaints. Do a search next time.
#147492 3 2006-02-23T14:51:36Z Maniac
It's really quite difficult to do, and unnecessary for the mastery of N. But if you really want to learn how...

Run towards the slope. Jump once, hit the slope, jump again, hit the wall, and jump again. This will propel you upward at a blistering speed.
