No way man. Big creatures are good, but how are you going to win with one 7/7 on the sixth or seventh turn? You need a way to get damage through to the opponent. That's why you go for the 5/5 trampler. If you can get a third turn 5/5 with trample, you are set. compare to Phyrexian Negator. Although the drawbacks seem similar, the negator can be attacking on the fourth turn, (or second, if you have say, dark ritual.) And then you can demolish anything they'll have out. (Ideally nothing, saving you your permanents, but even if they have a couple of small chump-blockers, you can sack some lands and trample over them, causing major headaches.) With the 7/7, you'll take out a creature a turn, sure, but you'll get no damage through unless you can destroy each of their individual creatures.
Besides, you can't tap that to add green to your mana pool the turn it comes into play. And that's why a forest is better.
