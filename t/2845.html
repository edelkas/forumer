I don't know if any of you have used Xfire, but Xfire is a free gaming tool that automatically keeps track of when and where gamers are playing PC games online and lets their friends join them easily. It doesn't matter which online game your friends are playing, which server browser they are using, or which gaming service they're playing on; Xfire is intelligent enough to recognize where your friends are at any time. For Single Player games, it just notifies the buddies that you are playing that particular game. A great thing about Xfire is that it logs all of the games that you play onto your profile and shows how many hours you play each game.

Enter N. I play this game religiously, like many of you do I'm sure. I play this game more than any PC game out there, and if N was supported, I would show the world my love for N. All that needs to be done is to support here:

http://www.xfire.com/xf/modules.php?name=F...ewtopic&t=20933

Implementing N into Xfire stats is VERY easy, but it wont get supported unless it gets feedback. I know a lot of you dont have Xfire and probably wont get it, but seriously consider helping this. Among other things, it could help show the world what they are missing.

Thank you.
