I beat Brain n-bolism, in fact its rather easy.
I'll post a demo when I get home (im at school ), but for now I can give you a few hints:
Run to the right at the beginning. Before you get out of the corridor, hit space and hold it and right and hold until you hit the wall. Jump down, and jump over the thwump. Play with the bounce blocks at the bottom until you get the door button. Then juma s far to the right as you can, and get over the thwump. Time it right (start with about 55.000 left) jumping up the wall, and the drones should get out of your way, so you can jump and get the switch. Then jump on the top right corner of the bbouncy block to get to the door.
It can take crazy timing in some parts, but it's really not that hard.
