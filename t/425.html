#23888 2 2004-06-25T17:42:21Z Guest
hey,
thanks for the suggestions.

we're going to try to add unlockables in 1.4; we've discussed different ninja-colors, as well as a belt or headband which is physics-simulated. i'm not sure about the other ones -- the ninja's kind of abstract so i'm not sure if they're wearing boots already or not!

(the rice-hat reminds me of jenjuro from samurai showdown.. i think that's his name.. anyway, i think he had one on during some of his end-game animations)

the problem with the oven thing is that we have no way to allow users to change their oven or request their oven if it's forgotten -- we figured that ( A ) nothing BAD can happen from someone stealing your N oven -- the worst they could do is get a highscore, which doesn't seem bad, and ( B ) this way you won't have to remember your oven

i admit that it's kind of ugly/kludgy though. we're still tyring to think of an easy way to fix it.

the reason music wasn't included was that playing mp3s in the flash player reduces the speed by up to something like 30%.. plus, lode runner, pac man, etc. didn't have any music, and we think N is sort of in the same lineage.

finally, we're releasing parts of the source code with each tutorial we make -- the one that's being posted this weekend has the source to the tile-based collision (used for colliding the ninja vs. the world). the next tutorial will have the source to the grid-system (used for colliding shots and objects), and the final tutorial will be cilantro things like the ragdoll system, etc..

once our next game is completed enough to submit to the IGF, we intend to "finish" N (i.e make all of the changes we want to make) and release the full source code. however, all of the interesting/good parts will be in the tutorials -- much of the N source is ugly and/or useless.. there are lots of things we could have done a lot better. but it was a learning experience

raigan
