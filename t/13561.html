#249444 4 2006-11-03T08:18:36Z Tsukatu
If you'd take a guess:
Copy a square of awokado below the first, but make its opacity lower to 0 on a gradient. Then take a square of porzezcka and make it lost opacity as it goes up. Overlay that square of porzezcka onto the bottom half of the two-square awokado. Do the same thing, but upside down, and paste to the bottom.
