Your link would better be described as "1-vote remaining" maps. I use that link a lot.

An alternative unrated maps link would be http://http//numa.notdot.net/browse?rated=false
This would sort with the newest maps first but just skip over the rated ones.

Another version is http://http//numa.notdot.net/browse?rated=false&sort=random
This is probably a much better than the newest unrated maps.

#287312 5 2007-01-19T00:04:37Z Winjet
These aren't too complicated once you get used to the links. "rated=false" takes you to maps that aren't rated.

And "rated=false&sort=votes&order=desc" will take you to maps that need only one more vote, then 2, then 3...

Edit: Nevermind the "order=desc". Like KA said, it's default. But "order=asc" will flip it around.
