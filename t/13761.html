#254263 8 2006-11-13T23:49:25Z Atilla
I think the current system, with .5 increments, is fine. Ratings are very subjective, and they simply don't have that level of precision. Can you say with certainty that you will enjoy a map which is rated 4.4 more than one which is rated 4.3? Or that the map with 4.4 is better? I think not. Heck, as like as not, the difference is probably just due to who actually rated it. Some people prefer certain types of map, some are more lenient than others, and some might have just been in a good mood at the time.

Also, we already have complaints of "Argh! My map dropped by the smallest possible increment! Who sniped it?!" I'd prefer not to have people whining because their rating dropped by 0.1 when someone gave it a 4.

Also, I don't like the idea of anonymous comments spam and flaming, for reasons which I hope are obvious.
