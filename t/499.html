I think there should be more puzzle levels, just like everyone else. Not literal puzzels, like that early one where the first part was a bunch of rooms made out of oneway walls, but levels where you have to figure out how to win and planning is more important than excecution.
I also think there should be a few episodes devoted to race levels, like the very first one. Since there's online highscores where everyone's clamoring for a great time, it makes perfect sense to have levels designed mainly for time trials. They have lots of gold, many paths, are open ended, and have an infinite number of ways to solve it which vary in effectiveness. Or any combination of the above. But the important thing: no enemies, mines only to block paths. Thus, the entire challenge is finishing faster.
What I don't want to see: lotsa gunner drones, laser drones without plenty of room, super hard levels, levels which require lightning reflexes. We already have enough of that.

Also, organise the columns in some way or another, like all episodes in one new column have are more similar than they are to the ones in the next column.
