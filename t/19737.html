#371665 4 2007-10-13T13:34:45Z George
Although it isn't recommended, it is possible with key remapping, which means you can set multiple keys to work as the shift key. In that case, you can jump with both Left Shift and the Up arrow. But it's not a good idea if you're going to combine that method with high scoring, which would most likely be the case anyway.

You could also try what I do. I prefer switching shift keys when chimney jumping. With my configuration, my left pinky is on the Left Shift, but when I need to chimney jump, I use my right hand on the Right Shift, letting go of the arrow keys (seeing as you don't need them anyway). Depending on the type of keyboard you have, it may or may not work.
