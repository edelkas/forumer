As the title of this topic states, Off The Rails is a simple (and fun) little game where you play as two little green Mexican bean-like things in the Old West, who must pump their little handcart across a series of tracks.

You must repeatedly mash the left and right arrow keys to move the cart across the track, up is to jump, down is to brake, and space is to switch between backwards and forwards.

It's really fun, and very addicting .
