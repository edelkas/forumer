This thread is for our musicians. If you can play music (Which I think most if not all of you). If you do more than one (Play and Compose, for instance), please sign in to both threads. In this thread, make sure you list what instruments/what kinds of music you can play, for ease of reference.

Please, in this thread, only post once. If you have questions, pose them in general discussion.

Now, for you guys, what you'll be doing for the project is playing the music. You guys will each be doing an individual piece as well as a group piece, and if you have time and motivation to do more, then we'll do more. The key to this whole project is contact. Keep in close contact with me, whether via PM, E-mail, IM, or through the subforum here - We need to be able to stay on-track, and we all need to be able to set aside the time to get this project completed.

The first thing you should do, as a musician, is sign in here! Once we know how many composers, musicians, and editors we have, we'll divide you guys into groups of at least one composer, one musician, and one editor each.

The second thing you'll want to do, once we get groups assigned, is get in touch with your composer in their composer thread, and start thinking about what kinds of tunes we're going to be composing in the groups, and then once that gets done, we'll start recording individual parts, and the editors will mesh them together.

Thirdly, you guys will need to keep in touch with me to be sure your individual projects are flowing along nicely. I'll be trying to give you guys themes to follow for the pieces, but it's up to you guys to be sure everything is on-beat and it's going to be up to the editors to make sure it all sounds good. I'll allow for a lot of creative freedoms for these things, but I don't want the songs to be dance mixes, or anything like that. This stuff needs to be very up-to-par with my expectations, and if it isn't I'll let you guys know what's going on in my head so that we can get together and fix things.

Fourthly, for your individual projects, ask me as many questions as you need to, and then just play. I know that we have a harp player, and really, a piece with just the harp would be freakin' amazing to me. Same with just a piano. Also, if you think your instrument might be lacking, lemme know. I'll hook you up with one of the editors to give you a drumbeat to work with, or hook you up with one of the composers to give you a part to play - Anything that I can do to make this easier on you, I'll do.

And finally, it's your job to keep in touch with the other members of the group, and to make sure things are moving along nicely.

Beyond that, keep active, keep in touch with me, and welcome!
