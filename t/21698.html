Part 1- The Lighting and the Gun

The lightning outside is getting worse. Everyone except Danny and Jose are staying in watching the bolts smash to the ground. Danny was outside, no-one knew why, but he was drawing his signature weapon, a Barrett M99, and slowly aiming at the house in which Gary and his friends were (ironically) playing cluedo.

Suddenly, someone shouted an ear-piercing scream "DANNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!!! NO!".It was Jose (better know as JOOZ MEN DEADS) who was screaming at Danny. This was not because Danny was about to shoot someone, but because the gun wasn't loaded and it would bring the cops without accomplishing anything. As Jose was telling Danny this, a bolt of lighting struck the ground directly next to them. They made a decision, they had to get to higher ground...

Only Julio Julius had noticed the gun, and took action at once. He looked around. Suddenly, he spots a box in the sky. Using his magnificent double jump, he lands atop the mysterious box. Inside it? A hot curry power up. Julio had been hungry and he quickly eats it away. All of a sudden, Benjamin sprints past the box with a giant banana.

Benjamin, eating the enlarged plant of the family Musaceae that had suddenly appeared in his hand, gains 0.073 grams of Riboflavin, giving him enough power to spring onto the mysterious box. He attacks Julio, too busy feasting to realize what's going on, and Julio falls down, far, far into the depths below...

Danny loaded his gun, and fired twice. The first shot missed completely, the second did a lot more damage, as Gary fell to the floor, dying...

The lightning continued regardless...

Part 2- Next morning...

Obviously, Danny was in hiding from the others, and Benjamin and Danny were looking for him. Everyone else was looking after Gary. Julio's curry power up had hepled propel him back up, er, if you know what I mean. Suddenly Benjamin heard a noise behind him. Benjamin spun around. It was the Grim Reaper. *cue the music* Benjamin pointed him Gary's way, but noticed that Gary wasn't yet dead! (Of course he had severe internal injuries and didn't have long to live.) The dying man was trying to say something and Benjamin had to stop him before the others found out about their secret. But it was too late. For with Gary's last breath, he began speaking, but then Benjamin covered Gary's mouth and the secret went unheard. Suddenly an ambulance arrived and two men loaded Gary onto a stretcher, brought him into the back, and drove off. The Grim Reaper disappeared. But Danny knew something the others didn't. He reloaded his gun...
