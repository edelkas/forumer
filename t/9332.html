Okay. Here's the deal. We're starting up a game. I'm sure everyone here has seen, or at least heard of, the show, American Idol. Well, for the rare case who hasn't, it's a reality tv show where they gather the best, and the worst, singers from around the nation and have them come sing on national television. There are three judges who rate each performance and every so often kick someone out of the contest. Over a number of months the contestants are narrowed down until you have people that can sing -really- well. Then they pick a winner.

Well. We've decided we're doing a smaller, more friendly but still as comical, version of this here on our beloved forums.

The judges are going to be:

B_T
Kablizzy
Myself

We're planning on doing this over Ventrilo and recording it, so you must have a working microphone. Now, I know you may think, "Well I don't want to embarrass myself in front of everyone." Here's the thing, more people than you will be embarrasing themselves, and really, it just makes the whole thing more fun.

SO! If you want to be a contestant, which everyone should, sign up in this thread and we'll try to get it off the ground as soon as possible.

Also, as a side note, you can make a file of you singing and submit it to us. If you do it this way we will record our opinions and comments of your submission that way. Both that, and actually coming onto Ventrilo, are both acceptable forms of entry, but for now just sign up.

To get ventrilo go to http://www.ventrilo.com/ and download it there.

If you have any questions about Ventrilo, or how to use it, get onto IRC and message me about it. Or you can also just PM me here on the boards. I'm on there quite a bit and will be able to answer your questions to help you get it up and running.
