Breakdown of Numa Browsing
http://numa.notdot.net/browse?
sort=created / lastupdated / rating / votes
category=[category number here]
author=trib4lmaniac
order=asc/desc
name=Mount Doom
count=[results per page]

Concatonate them however you like.

http://numa.notdot.net/browse?sort=rating&order=desc&count=3
Shows the top 3 rated maps

http://numa.notdot.net/browse?sort=lastupd...der=asc&count=1
Shows the oldest map that remains unmodified ever.
