Welcome to my guide on CS:Source, this will only show you through the basics of playing the game, not installing it, or Steam beforehand.

What the game is exactly...
It's a multiplayer FPS (First person shooter) game, where you join servers of people who will be your opponents. There are two teams, Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists, each gets a different selection of weapons.


Getting to know the controls[/i]
Counter-Strike: Source uses the typical WASD control system, with the keys in a similar layout to the arrow keys. These are for moving around, but not actually aiming, that's what the mouse does, it lets you look around and shoot people. Left clicking fires the weapon, Right clicking uses the Secondary Function (E.g. M4A1 Carbine, adding Silencer), scrolling the mouse wheel will put you in weapon select mode, highlight the weapon you want, and click to change to it.

A couple of extras that'll be necessary...
E = Use, for defusing bombs, opening doors.
Q = Change to last held weapon, so if you had a pistol out, and changed to your rifle, Q would pull the pistol out again.
Ctrl = Crouch, Essential for accuracy, crouching makes it easier to hit people.
Spacebar = Jump, jump around a bit, it's fun! Pressing ctrl while jumping helps you onto higher ledges.
T = Spray, this lets you use your spraypaint image, it 'leaves your mark' on people you killed etc.
F = Flashlight, good for dark levels, press it to switch on, press again to switch off.
Z, X and C = Voice commands, choose a number after pressing one to use that command.
Y = General chat, talk to everyone in the server using this.
U = Team chat, talk to your team only using this.
B = Buy weapons, this lets you get good guns, navigate the buy menu with the mouse or numbers, remember that money doesn't grow on trees, I'll explain how to find out how much you have later.

Right, now the controls are over with, time for the interface...

Image here

Up in the top left is your radar, this will let you find your team mates, but will only show up enemies when you or your friends can visibly see them or are being shot at by them on screen.

Bottom left to bottom right is health, armour, Round time, Ammo (Current Clip | Spare Bullets) and Cash (Above ammo).

Time to explain all the different mods, and a few tips and tricks

Never buy Nightvision, it's useless, and not worth using tbh.

Always buy a Defuse Kit if you're CT, it means it's easier to defuse the bomb.

Zombies!, that'll be the zombie mod, the teams are humans (blue) vs. zombies (red), humans occassionally have infinite ammo, but zombies have around 50x more health and have increased speed, but only a knife.

I can't buy weapons here, and mario sounds are playing!, you're in gungame, the objective here is to kill, and kill some more, so you level up through the weapons. One kill means one level, and knifing gets you a bonus level.

What is this map, it's full of pyramids..., Surfing time! Hold strafe down towards the triangle (D /\ A) and use the mouse to direct yourself. It's good fun.

A few more things[/i]

On de_ maps, the objective is to either plant the bomb (T) or defuse it if it is planted (CT).
On cs_ maps, the objective is to rescue hostages (CT), or stop the CT team (T)

Weapons are usually more expensive depending on popularity or power. A Machine Gun is more expensive than a pistol say.

If you're still confused, send me a PM
