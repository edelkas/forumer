Topics aren't maps - there's a reason why I was against that "topic rating" system someone suggested a while back, the reason being exactly this. Don't make a topic in the hope that someone will see it and immediately give you +50 respect points, make a topic if you think that others will actually find it interesting. I guess the point that would cover most of your other problems would be - don't try so damn hard, man. This place is just like any group of friends. A large majority of members chose imaging. Some go for coding. Find something, anything, you're even mildly good at, and work on that. Hell, show it off.

Oh, and the N IRC channel is a great place to chat with a lot of the forum members. You should hang around there.
