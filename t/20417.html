A cab becomes involved in an accident. Two cab companies serve the city the accident occurred in: The "Green," which operates 85% of the city's cabs, and the "Blue," which operates the remaining 15%. A witness identifies the involved cab as Blue. When the court tests the reliability of the witness under circumstances similar to those on the night of the accident, he correctly identifies the color of a cab 80% of the time and misidentifies it the other 20%.

What is the probability that the cab involved in an accident was Blue, as the witness stated?

I really have no idea where to start. All I can think of is just to randomly add and/or subtract the %s and see what I get. (It's no trick-question, BTW; there is a definite numerical answer. Supposedly.)
