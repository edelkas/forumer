#104043 4 2005-09-27T07:14:14Z TornadoTK
Glad to see someone checked it out.

You earn a LOT more bounty past level 10. Just look for Bonuses as you play. I don't think I'm accurate, but..

Blue = Clean kills. Either accuracy, combo, or damage taken.
Purple = Double kill or large damage.
Gold = Chain two kills in one combo.
Red = Headshot (Head hitbox is actually the neck)

Also, don't be afraid to get cheesy. Use the rifle to killsteal in the mansion corridor, dagger-stun the people you know can't safe fall out of it and blast them on the floor, and when in doubt, get some HP/AP. As for items, learn your strategy while playing.

Pistol - Long-to-Medium range, less accuracy required.
Revolver - Medium Range, more accuracy required.
Shotgun - Only for close range players, or koreans.
SMG's - No accuracy? Get close and spray away.
Rifle - Accurate, and somewhat strong. Great in corridors.
Rockets - Only strong when used on multiple enemies at once.
MG's - Haven't reached level 25 yet.

Frag: Useful in close-quarters and smoking your enemy out.
Flash: Use only during getaways, or to confuse moshpit gunners.
Smoke: ...I don't see what this does. Ever.
HP Repair: Great boost of HP in Team games.
AP Repair: Almost as useful, but HP always comes first.

Learn the game, learn the weaknesses.
