#21002 1 2008-08-03T00:25:23Z The Kitchen Sink
Increasing guards' size can have adverse effects on their placement; when one attempts to load the code, it changes the guard's location on the Y axis to compensate for the size (so that the bottom of the guard will still touch the bottom). When going into the editor, it reverts changes, but keeps the coordinates intact, which leads to newly located guards, moved upwards. Adding the code again and loading will again compensate, moving the guard even further up. Furthermore, attempting to move the guard manually in the editor, while helping, still does not completely eradicate the changes; when exiting out of the editor, the guards will be moved back up, though to a lesser amount.

As for the new types of guards, Unreality didn't change the compensating for them, so they still go upwards regardless of orientation, meaning it's impossible to use bigger guards that are orientated differently unless you either manually code-edit their location on the y-axis or predict where they will arrive after the code-compensation. Inconvenient at best.

I don't resize guards much: I was merely documenting the use for the wiki when I discovered this...but a bug is a bug, and it would be nice if it were fixed.
