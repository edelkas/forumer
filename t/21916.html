#419137 3 2008-03-20T14:41:22Z Pikman
A .txt will work, but a .doc might not because Mac users won't be able to access the file unless they have Word:Mac.

At the top of the file, put:
&userdata=

Then, do this for each level:
$level_name#author_name#description#level_data#

Level data is the raw data straight from the editor.

If you upload with Word Wrap on, you get a symbol at the end of the line that looks like an arrow coming from the text and pointing downward, standing for a new line. Getting rid of those takes a long time.

When you're ready to post the file, there's a bar here that says "File Attachments". Then hit [Browse], find the file, and click [Add Reply] to attach it to the post.
