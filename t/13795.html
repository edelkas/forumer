#254793 9 2006-11-15T01:08:13Z iangb
Because we make a 'ph' sound in 'phooey', not a 'f' sound. They really are different, but we can't differentiate between them easilly, in the same way that 'r' and 'l' hard to distinguish for someone of asian upbringing.

Try saying 'fish'. Note at the start of the word your front upper teeth are over your lower lip.
Now say 'phooey'. Either your teeth will be behind your lower lip, or you won't be using your teeth at all - you'll be using your upper and lower lips alone to make the sound. We don't easilly notice the difference, but it's there.
