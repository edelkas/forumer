#89119 52 2005-08-07T22:29:04Z brickman
Yay, someone cares. Ok, here goes:

Drill Man
In this game, you play as a little robot with drills for hands (looking like a megaman character, not some crazy chrome tank). Instead of the focus being to make tunnels, however, this game plays in the same vein as games like n and jumper, and the main application for your drills is to cling to and "walk" on walls and ceilings. While on a wall he can move up and down, let go or jump off, and while on a ceiling he can just move to the sides or drop down. There are of course enemies, some of whom can be killed by drilling them from a specific direction and some who can't be killed at all. Terrain includes walls, hard walls (that you can't cling to), breakable blocks (comeon, he's got a drill--why not), spikes&mines, thin platform (which you can stand on or hang from and switch between those positions at will) and similar things. It would be divided into episodes which may or may not require you to finish in one go, and every 5/10 episodes would be a "boss" episode where you spend the first four "levels" fleeing from a ninja (both as a throwback to n and because he makes a good boss) and then fight him on the fifth, with a very specific attack pattern involving running from side to side with his sword, leaping over you throwing shurikens, then jumping between the walls hurling more shurikens between jumps, then jumping to a position above you and doing a "power thrust" straight down, immediately after which you land a blow. Every time you see him he moves faster and throws more shurikens. (Yes, the boss is VERY fleshed out). The rest of the levels would involve avoiding and occasionally (though not usually) skewering the enemies while traversing walls, floors and ceilings to the exit, with gold in there as a bonus. Controll is still pretty simple, wiht buttons for jumping, running, one drill button and the up and down (only for aiming the drill).

How does that sound?
