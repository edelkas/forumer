#126998 7 2005-12-26T04:20:20Z astro
PNGs are totally lossless, so the picture quality will be unchanged. However, they can't be animated, and they are larger than equivalent JPEGs or GIFs.

GIF = animation
PNG = small/high resolution graphics (including diagrams, etc.)
JPEG = photos
