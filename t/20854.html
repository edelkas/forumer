Those are called "Pips". They are like little badges that represent how much you've posted.
You can only acquire them by posting. Do not spam to get them - Pips do not earn you any privileges on the forum. They are also not representative of one's rank. Anyone can get pips - more pips do not make you better than anyone else.
I hope that helped.
