I don't think you can love and hate something at the same time. I just... I think if you do, you're not properly using one or both rems. Hate signifies an extreme distaste. When you hate something, you want to remove its object from existence. If you don't, then you don't really hate it, you're just using the word "hate" in a lesser manner.

Love is just as extreme. If you love something, you want it to be with you forever (the opposite of removing from existence immediately), or until yopu stop loving it.

People just use the words hate and love too liberally; they throw them around to define things that they "like" or "dislike".
