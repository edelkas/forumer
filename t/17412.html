#15293 1 2007-05-19T21:12:36Z Street_Wolf
Hey all,

I was just trying to make a prisoner map a few minutes ago, and an idea popped into my head. What if there was a new, harmless, drone that wandered around. The reason for this drone would be to actually activate door switches. This could add several new scenarios such as surviving until a locked door is opened by the drone, racing to the exit, but the drone will put a hidden door closed in front of the exit if you don't hurry, or even a floor destroying drone that slowly picks away at ground that you have to stand on (locked doors) until finally it's all gone.

I know a lot of these ideas could be used if a second physical N was added (suggestion from a different thread) but this would eliminate the lag problem of another character with physics.

Anyway, I know that a lot of people don't want anythign actually added to the game, but I think this might change things since it's not just another way to die. It would add the element of timing to a lot of maps.
