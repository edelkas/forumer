Q: Metanet's working on another game?!
A: Yep. Popularly dubbed TNG ("The Next Game"), people have been ranting over it for months now, even though it won't be released most likely until late 2008, because N+ has taken longer then expected. For up to date news, check the new blog: metablog.

Q: What will it have?
A: As it seems, it will involve nothing but robots (including the protagonist), a projectile weapon for the protagonist (in layman's terminology, a gun), and even more extensive depth in physicsin fact, supposedly most navigation throughout a level will occur through rope-swinging or a similar method. It'll also have a level editor, as always.

Q: What is it being coded in?
A: OpenGL and C++.

Q: Sweet!
A: Oh, and it'll co$t.

Q: What?! / Yes, a chance to contribute to such a great company!!
A: Of course! It has already been declared long ago that Metanet's games will be alternating from freeware to shareware to freeware; N is their first game and it's free, so Robotology will be shareware, their next game afterwards will be free, etc. Nothing's stopping you from donating as it currently is, tho'...
