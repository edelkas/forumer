I refer mostly to the Christian God, since I am well aware that many other deities have humourous tendencies.

Anyway, it's like this this. My college recently had a seminar in which several speakers discussed humour and religion. I was not able to attend, unfortunately, but I am somewhat intrigued by the topic.

Promotional materials for the seminar boasted the line "Christianity is a faith best held lightly, lest we become self-righteous." So, the first thing I wish to discuss is just how much levity is appropriate to your faith (or lack of faith, for that matter). Should philosophical matters be considered with the utmost gravity at all times? Does God mind if you watch the Life of Brian? Is saying "Jesus H. Christ on a bicycle!" when you're shocked acceptable? Is it OK to for atheists to say "Good gods!", or should they take their disbelief more seriously?

Secondly, does God have a sense of humour? A sense of humour is generally regarded as a good trait, and man is supposed to be the image of God, so it's not unreasonable to suppose He has one. This begs the further question of whether it just means He understands our Python-watching tendencies, or whether God enjoys a hearty laugh as well. If so, what does He find amusing?*
