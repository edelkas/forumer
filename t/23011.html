How do i get inside 3 rows of blounceblocks?
#448630 2 2008-06-25T21:03:33Z wedgie
Hit them with as much momentum as possible, i.e. hit them from as much of a hight as possible with as much left or right movement as possible. Then either continue to hold in the direction you are travelling to filter through the blocks, or after you drop through the first set and a block to your side stops you, let it spring you in the opposite direction to fall through the last layers.

Remember that blocks barely move when you are stationary. The more you are moving when you are touching the blocks, the more the blocks will displace.
