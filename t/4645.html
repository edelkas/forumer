I'm sick of people complaining about these. We need to discuss and define them, and probably should have a long time ago.

Double-posting. Double-posting occurs when you make a post, then make another post two minutes later when you should have edited instead. Perfectly understandlable that someone who's new to forums would miss the edit button. If you see this happen, simply tell them to edit their post next time. Now, most of the time, when I see someone apologizing for a double-post, they're not doing what I would consider a double-post. If you were the last person to post in your personal maps thread and you post again several days later, it's not a double-post. If you were the last person to post in a very old discussion, and now you have something new to add, you're not double-posting. Even if you just want to bring attention to a thread that hasn't been touched in a few days, you're not double-posting. Like I said earlier, a double-post occurs right after the initial post; when you should have edited instead of posting again.

Thread resurrections. Why do you people hate these so much? It's not like it does any harm. Also, there's no such thing as a ten-day resurrection. Or a five-day resurrection, or a two-week resurrection. A resurrection is a couple of months or more. Falsely calling out a necromancer is no better than spam, especially considering how many posts I see like this that add nothing to the conversation.
