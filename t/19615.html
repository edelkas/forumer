The N poetry awards


Ok... I've been thinking about this for a while... I hope it's not to noobish

I've noticed on NUMA that their are several maps accompanied by poems. The map fits the poem, the poem suits the map.

One of my favorite examples of this is Eclectic Sex Hoodie by Mechtradevil.


So here's my plan: five poems are submitted by five different authors (they have to be okayed by me first, just incase). I will submit one too, leaving four spaces (I will allow more if needed, though they'll have to be good:D ). Please submit your poems ASAP. You cannot be a poet and a mapmaker...

Anyway, then, once these poems are all submitted, mapmakers will get to work on making maps appropriate to the poems, using the same names, same subject matter, but on N. Mapmakers will submit their maps through NUMA, posting the link on this page, and also linking this thread in it's NUMA description.

!!All maps will have to be submitted three weeks after all poems have been handed in!! (date will be confirmed)

Then the poets will judge the maps on: relevance to poem (50%), inerpretation of poem (25%) and of course enjoyability (25%). And whoever has the highest marks for each poem will win prizes...

Which brings me to the part you've all been waiting for... PRIZES!

Their will be a first second and third for each poem map.

All the first place winning maps will become part of a mappack, get a free sig, and a remix of their winning map

All second place winning maps will get a dedication and a free sig

All third place winning maps will get a free sig

Also, all poets/judges will get a special sig labelled 'N poet'

For a year and a day
You have been less alive than

Hopefully this gets in! PS- this poem isn't about me!

The Walls

The walls are closing in on me,
I have missed my chance to escape,
They move towards me slowly,
Along the ground they scrape.

I'd seen them kill others before,
I never thought that they'd get me,
Soon I won't live anymore
At least I will be free

I thought that I was much stronger,
I thought that I would live and thrive
I thought that I'd last longer,
I thought that I'd survive.

I am divided by the walls,
From all the others in the room,
Although they can't see them crawl,
Slowly towards my doom.

The walls are so close to me now,
I can not see anyone else,
They try to help, making vows,
To me their words are false.

If it was true I could get out,
I would have done it long ago,
I tried forever, I don't doubt,
I know that I'm trapped though.

The ice doesn't make the walls stop,
It makes me become blind and deaf,
Ice caused me to start the drop,
and ice will end it with death.

I can feel the walls coming,
They will break me into debris,
I am going to get high one last time,
So I won't feel them crush me.

The walls are closing in on me,
I've gone blind and deaf to escape,
I can't see them move so slowly,
I can't hear them as they scrape.

Made for Life

Subtle is the difference between
Our love of the dead
Between the bright of sky,
The color of rotting fruits; pleasantly warm, corrupted.
Crawling in the air
You can taste it,
An autumnal fever of mourning
Begone!

Slow is the cathedral scene remarked on by
Our dying fathers,
Breaking fingers grip the obtuse;
The sunbird ruffles her feathers humidly, we pretend she is crying.
Crawling in the air
You can taste it,
An autumnal fever of mourning
Begone!

Sanguine we are blessed to attend this famous funeral
Our beauty within her blood; blasphemed-sharp, intrinsic.
Betwixt bulrushes and flamed copper pipes
The atmosphere descends like shattered wombs, priests cradling deceased gold.
Crawling in the air
You can taste it,
An autumnal fever of mourning
Begone!
