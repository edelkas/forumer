I was thinking... why do I play N? I fail and get nothing, I win and get nothing too.
So, what about making level ups or power ups?
Lets say, when you get 1000 gold, you get a flavor or a pose. Or you can buy poses and flavors, also.
And to make things fair, you can only levelup and get gold in N levels. And you must think "so I make the level 1 a thousand times and im rich! hahahaha". But no. You could only win gold playing the colum the level you are. For example, you are level 3, you can only play the third collum (20-30 levels). If you play harder levels, gold would multiply, for example. You are level 1 and play a collum-2 map. You get 200 gold, but since it is collum-2 you get a 0.5 multiplier. Hurray! You get 300 gold.
This would make N rpg-ish, for lovers like me. And this wouldnt change N much since you could not buy things like "fly" or "super jump", cause that would ruin the simplicity of N.
I hope you like this idea, based on the others ideas I read.

Also I support the idea that says that if you for example die 10000 times by gravity you get something.
