#385964 5 2007-12-04T01:25:21Z krusch
We shouldn't be doing this until the year is over, but:

Arcade Fire - Neon Bible - I've always loved Arcade Fire. While I can't *really* say I think this album is one of '07's best, I did enjoy it immensely, so it makes the list.

Coheed and Cambria - Good Apollo, I'm Burning Star IV, Volume 2: No World for Tomorrow - Absolutely awesome prog rock/metal album.

Crippled Black Phoenix - A Love of Shared Disasters - If just for the third and fourth songs, this makes the list.

Do Make Say Think - You, You're a History in Rust - Cute and creative post-rock.

Dream Theater - Systematic Chaos - If it weren't for this album, they wouldn't be touring and I wouldn't be seeing them. Woo!

Eluvium - Copia - Magnum opus?

Grizzly Bear - Friend EP - I wish they would release more NEW material, rather than remixing their old stuff. *sigh*. Still a great EP.

jesu/Eluvium - Split - Two of my favourite artists! Absolutely great.

Minus the Bear - Planet of Ice - My least favourite of theirs, which says more about the band than the album.

Pelican - City of Echoes - A nice post-metal release.

Stars of the Lid - And Their Refinement of the Decline - Sleepy.


I highlighted the best ones.
