Okay, so I found an old issue of a school magazine a good friend was in, while he was in the third grade. All of the 3rd grade students wrote poetry for the magazine, and all of them got published. Most were pretty lukewarm... cheesy, yet not really that amusing or even that bad.

My Poem About Rocks
Rocks, Rocks, Rocks
Some rocks are small,
Some rocks are in a ball.
Goodbye, I am being turned into cement.
