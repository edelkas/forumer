"Rage", for those of you who don't know it, is a music video program which airs through the night and into the morning on most Fridays 'round where I live. It's the insomniac's dream- music video upon music video to squander away the small hours.

Anyway, I figured that it could be fun to do a hypothetical guest programming of a much shortened version of the epicness which is Rage, to help some suitably bored others shoot off some time. So, make your own mini- programs, and I'll be indebted to you and be glad of something to do during the night.

Anyway, there's no need to introduce every video- just shove in some chunks interspersed with a bit of idle banter and we have our own version of Rage- "Milk Anger"- right here on the metanet forums! And don't label the links- it's nicer not to know what's coming up. A better way to waste time, too.
