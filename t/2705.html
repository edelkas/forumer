Q: How do I access the N level editor / Ned?
A: At the main menu of the game, before any sounds are played, push tilde (it's ~, under Esc).

Q: Erm, my keyboard doesn't have that button.
A: Oogh... well, here are some others M&R worked out. Hope you can find at least one of 'em: , | (that's not an I or small 'l'; that's a vertical slash which on most keyboards is used by pressing Backslash while holding Shift), ^, , @, and '

Q: How does level-saving/loading work?
A: Data for custom levels, as of our current version of Ned, needs to be copied & pasted into .TXTs (the most popular of all word-processing filetypes here). The data appears in the top-right box of Ned.

Q: Why does the game go frame-by-frame when I click while playing a custom level?
A: Ned's a debug mode so the programmers could see events in a level occur by frames. Turn on Caps Lock to play normally.

Q: My progress doesnt save when I play N. How do I fix this?
A:
1. Right-click anywhere in the window that N is running in, and click on Settings.
2. Then click on the tab that has a folder on it. The slider that is there is for Local Storage.
3. Make sure the slider is all the way to the right, which is an unlimited amount of storage.

You also have to beat the whole episode in a row to unlock the next one. It saves your episode scores, but not your progress on the episodes. (Thanks to Shark)

Q: I keep hearing this stuff about teleports. What are those?
A: Teleports are edited launchpads, created by code editing. LittleViking's step-by-step tutorial makes you skilled with teleports in no time.

Q: How do I make backwards-moving Thwumps, like in 86-3?
A: Insert them half-way into the wall. Unless they're facing left, you need to adjust their placement with the z-snap to make it work. This means moving them one pixel out from the wall.

Q: How can I post my levels to the forums? The data becomes corrupted when I post it.
A: The forums' Word Wrap messes up those long strings of level data. Avoid this by saving your levels in Notepad and attaching the .txt instead (the button is located right above Add Reply). Or, even better, submit the level to NUMA and link to it.
