This has probably been asked a million time but I couldn't find it. Any way as I look through DDAs most of them say how many frames it has. I know that in debug mode if caps lock is off and you press play every time you click N will move a frame. But suppose you have a really bug DDA that has over 2000 frame you would undoubtedly lose count and it would be really annoying clicking that much. #381350 2 2007-11-20T23:44:21Z Kablizzy
When you start a DDA, you press '1' to start a demo recording. When the DDA is completed, the demo data will appear in the bottom box. You count the first number in front of the colon, and voila! Frame count.
#381992 3 2007-11-23T00:01:55Z scythe33
I know it's kind of obvious, but I've actually messed this up, so remember to press '1' before you press 'P'.
You can do the same thing when playtesting your level in debug mode. Comparatively, 1000 frames is a short level, 2000 frames is medium-long, and anything above 3000 frames is a very long level.
