#147537 2 2006-02-23T18:37:05Z Maniac
Well, you probably searched manually instead of using the Search function on the boards, because this and 88-4 are the two most-asked about rooms in N.

Anyway, this room slaughters a lot of people because it's the first to use a really effective combination of a rocket and mines, as you've seen. The idea here is to pay more attention to the mines than the rocket. The rocket can't move as fast as you can, so as long as you run away from it, you're fine. The mines are more of a pain, as they won't move out of your way.

I'd also be cautious on the sides of the arena, it'd stink to nearly get the thing done and then die to gravity
