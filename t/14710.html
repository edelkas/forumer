I don't visit numa much at all so I really have no clue if this is an already done idea or if it's even the most commonly available map type on numa, but here's the idea.

It's sort of a puzzle map, though it has arcade qualities in the manual dexterity required to complete it. It wouldn't be compatible with userlevels mode in N. The idea would be, when you first begin the level, you kill your ninja. Then, using the ctrl 'drag ninja towards mouse' thang, you would navigate the ninja through the level using the mouse. Game over would happen if the ninja's body explodes, on a mine or just whacking it into something too hard.

There could be a potential for small, tricky maps with no breathing room and corridors too small for the ninja to normally traverse. There could be the option of being able to 'throw' the ninja over certain parts of the level by letting go of control. I don't know if the rocket follows the dead body or not (look how well researched my idea is!), but there could be a more open level where you have to keep the ninja from exploding AND keep a rocket alive. A few cool options, a different way of playing N, hilarity when you bump the corpse into walls too hard and blood flies everywhere. Fun for the whole family!
