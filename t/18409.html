This is most certainly the most horrible dream that I have ever had.
It started when I was at my house and 2 policemen came in and started beating me with police batons. I asked them, "What is happening? Why are you doing this?" The man responded with, "We don't need a reason." He then used the end of his baton to break all of my teeth out.

Then they dragged my out to the police car, which was a pickup truck and stapled (yes, stapled) my hands to the back of the truck. When the truck started moving I tried to keep up with it, but it became too fast and I was being dragged across the pavement.

By the time we arrived at the jail, most of my lower body was very bloody and mangled. They took me into a jail cell and fixed my hands to a chain attached to the ceiling. This chain was short enough so that my feet were not touching the floor. When I looked at my surroundings I was to witness hell-like scenes. I remember specifically: There was about an inch of blood covering the whole floor and there were numerous piles of dismembered body parts and heaps of mangled bodies. I could see jail cells filled with children - schoolchildren - being burned alive by a man wearing a welder's mask with a flame thrower. There was another cell that contained a man that was chopping up babies on a workbench.

To the cell adjacent to mine, there was a woman suspended in the same way that I was suspended. 2 men went into her cell and began skinning her while she was alive. I remember this part of the dream most vividly - I remember every detail about how they cut her skin and peeled it down. I was glad when she finally died so that she would not scream anymore.

I don't know what happened after that, but I remember being home after all of this happened. I was sitting on my bed and my parents were in the other room talking about me. I remember that all four of my limbs were amputated and my skin was burned all over. I heard my parents saying "he screams in his sleep, and he cries while he is awake. He pulls his hair out constantly. We are going to have him put away somewhere that he won't hurt himself.

This is a recurring dream and I have had the same dream many times over the last few years, as well as many other dreams with the same theme or characters - especially the flamethrower man, he at least makes a cameo appearance in all of my dreams.

There are always the same important elements: policemen/authority, children in jail, babies being killed, torture, and self mutilation. I have researched the meaning of all of these symbols and they actually have a sort of profound meaning in my life.


It is very rare for me to have pleasant dreams, it has only happened a few times in the last 5 years or so. I dream somewhat often, but it is always a nightmare like this. I have researched this and the fact that I always have nightmares explains a lot of things about my life - there are good reasons for me to have nightmares all the time.

It's far more likely that you're getting the deja-vu effect, which is basically your mind rewriting its past memories. Happens all the time. It's one of the reasons witness testimonies are so unreliable - people can be convinced that they saw some variation of what they really did see if you hint them enough.
