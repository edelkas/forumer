==Week 1==
This weeks theme is Canyons. Make a map that has a canyon aspect to it. The entries are due Sunday, September 16th, 2007 by midnight. If all entries are in before the deadline the next round will start, so that way there isn't a huge delay.

Welcome to the FIVER competition, where the object of the game is to get a five on a single map... the twist? oh right, the twist. Get a perfect five from three of the five judges... heh heh heh...


Here are the rules:

1. NO OLD MAPS. Anybody found using an old map will not be considered for judging.
2. You may submit your maps here in this thread, either as a link to NUMA or a *.txt file.
3. Each week you will be judged on the overall level, like on NUMA. But remember that each judge may be particular about certain things, so it is important to consider gameplay, tileset, object placement, enemy placement, difficulty, etc when making your map.
4. The judges will rate the map according to what they would rate it on NUMA.
5. Those who recieve a 5 from at least three of the five judges will win the grand prize. The is an ongoing contest. Each round is 8 weeks. If nobody has recieved at least three 5's then we revert to the lower prize table (see below).
6. Each week will be a different theme. The themes will be according to style, such as action, puzzle, etc.
7. N art and tilesets are not allowed (for obvious reasons), but DDA's are. If the theme happens to be "Prisoner Maps" and you make a DDA based off a prisoner map then be my guest and submit it.


Contestants are encouraged to spend time on their maps.

I will need 5 judges who will be able to judge each and every week. There will be one extra judge (me) who will rate the judges maps. Contestants are unlimited. You may join at any point in the competition.

This is an ongoing competition. Those who may recieve fives from at least three of the judges will recieve the grand prize but the contest will continue, so in this way the competiton doesn't end.
Also, the rounds will be in 8 week intervals. Each week will have a different theme you will have to follow. At the end of the eight weeks the prizes are given out to those who scored the highest. But the next week a new 8 week round starts again, allowing those who didn't make it on the last round to try again. And so it continues.


The Grand Prize:
1 Dedication map
Your choice of (1) a sigatar set or (2) a second dedication map

The Prize Table:
1st place - Your choice of a DED or a sigatar set. You also get a sig bar saying you got first.
2nd place - Your choice of a DED or a sigatar set
3rd place - A DED

For those of you confused about the prize table:
The grand prize is only for those who receive three 5's. The Prize Table is for the rounds. If a person gets three 5's in the 4th week they get the grand prize, and can still compete in the contest. At the end of the eight weeks the scores are tallied up and the prizes given to 1st, 2nd and 3rd place are determined by the Prize Table. If you have won the grand prize you are eligible to win it again in consecutive rounds, as well as eligible to win any of the prizes from the Prize Table at the end of each round.
