Seriously. We all get into moods. When you're 0-8 years old, moods last for ten minutes. When you're 9-12, they last for an hour or two. When you're 13-20, they last for four or five hours. When you're 21-30, they last for a day or two--sometimes three. When you're 31-50, they last for a week or more. When you're 51-60, they only last for a day. When you're 61-70, they only last for an hour. When you're over 71, you pretty much have the same mood all the time. Then, you eventually die and have no mood at all.

Just know that everything you experience will pass--the good and the bad. Enjoy every emotion you have. Enjoy your highs and your lows, and you'll succeed in life.
