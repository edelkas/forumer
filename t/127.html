hey,

about the highscores/episodes/etc: we'll think about it. it's something we're discussing..

one argument for episodes is that some levels are trivial -- they take a couple seconds to beat. a "high score" on such a level is meaningless.

secondly, i remember playing loderunner -- i'd often just skip levels until i found one i liked/could beat. this sort of made each level kind of "cheap". episodes force the player to invest a bit more effort in order to progress in the game.. and they add a certain amount of nervousness as you reach the last level of the episode -- you don't want to stop playing cause then you'll have to restart the episode next time, but you're SOOO frustrated, etc...

i don't know -- if everyone wants to voice your opinions that would be great.

personally i just like the episodes because they package the levels into chunks that aren't TOO small (i.e a single level) but can still be beaten in less that 30min. (i've beaten the entire game in <2 hours; but, to be fair, about an hour of that was just for the final episode


about your "luck" complaint.. well, i don't know. to be honest i never thought of that as luck -- the shooting enemies all use completely non-random AI which is based only on your current position. you can definitely use their behaviour againt them -- for instance, you can do a small-jump to get in sight of a laser drone, and they'll shoot at you -- which now gives you a couple seconds to run without having to worry about them.

we sort of consider N a puzzle game because beating a level isn't always about timing and skill, it's about finding a "solution" to the map -- so for enemy-laden maps, you often have to find routes that are devious in order to stay hidden as much as possible.

also, each of the enemies can be easily evaded on their own, so try to avoid situations where you have many together.

it _is_ true that some of the maps require you to run like crazy and "hope" you don't get shot, but in reality what determines if you get shot is you. you just need to find the tricks

when the online highscores are up, you'll be able to see solutions to all the levels, so hopefully this will give you ideas. most of the time we've tried to allow for multiple "solutions" to each level, so you don't feel forced to do a certain thing.


i definitely know what you mean though -- for instance e9m0 (i _think_.. it's been a while) has two chaingunners, a rocket, a gauss turret, and two chase-drones. when you start that level, you get killed instantly for a while. but gradually you learn good "starting moves" -- i.e combos of running+jumping which will get you to the next area of the map alive. and once you get there, you die. but after a while, you learn some good moves from THAT area, etc.. it's sort of like chess.

anyway, we'd really like to know what people think -- part of our problem is that we really like hard games (i.e crimsonland, SMB3) which aren't easily beaten, but which are totally beatable. i personally find myself more addicted to games i know i CAN beat, but which i can't beat for sure all the time. the other part of the problem is that we have a couple months more experiance than anyone else so it's hard for us to judge the difficulty of the levels sometimes
