#260295 7 2006-11-27T04:16:07Z wedgie
In sections where you have solid mines, use the quarter snap instead of no snap. This way, you will still have the same affect to look at, with a solid dark red colour, but you will be using the least amount of mines possible to fill that area. This is what i had to do on my N art 'My hero', as i was having similar problems with my submission.
I can just see many different sections where you could do this to save objects. Just fill the centre with 1/4 snap then use no snap on the edges. Sorted.
