#138298 2 2006-02-01T17:59:37Z BSBen
Copy your Nv14b.sol file and put it on your disk/jumpdrive/whatever. then put it in the computer you want to send it to. then put it in the

C:\Documents and Settings\[your computer account]\Application Data\Macromedia\Flash Player\#SharedObjects\[random letters and numbers]\localhost

folder.

There you go.
