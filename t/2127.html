So I was searching around on gamehippo, looking for something to occupy my weekend, and I stumbled upon this. I am posting to the description game because, sadly, there's some Japaneese filename issues that require a patch linked to in that description, or Japanese language support, and unfortunately that patch doesn't work on some computers (sorry for wasting your time if it doesn't for you). In the game, you control a ghost who can take control of any enemy (well, any enemy that isn't a turret or a boss) by jumping on it and pressing z and possess it to do his bidding until it dies or he discards it by pressing c. Either one removes the enemy and reverts Obake to ghost form. While in control of an enemy only the enemy takes damage, not Obake himself, but they usually go down pretty quick if you aren't careful and its not always easy to get another host. While controlling an enemy, the player can perform an enemy-specific attack with the z key and can often do more interesting things than jumping with the jump key, such as limited flying, hovering, teleportation, or inverting gravity (for himself). The game is a bit easy, but fun enough for that not to be a problem and with plenty of secrets.

The real fun comes from the enemies, though. There's no shortage of interesting guys to take control of, including fire breathing elves, giant fireball-spraying robots, pogo-stick guys, jellyfish that climb walls, trident-wielding jetpackers, witches, tanks, suits of armor, and robots that can toss their heads and invert gravity in midair (a personal favorite). Name an attack, its probably in there. The levels vary greatly as well, including autoscrollers (and not just one direction either), caves, outdoor areas, horizontal only levels, flashlight levels grab 6 keys levels, and of course the standard icy platform levels. Theres levels making good use of almost every host body's abilities (or at least parts of levels), and you can go back to previous levels with new enemies to get to new secret areas or just for fun (although you have to make sure it doesn't die, or you're stuck with the enemies at hand).

There's only a few gripes. First, the bosses, up until the actual fight with the other ghost, are way too easy (in the too short vein more than the too pathetic vein, a bit more health would do them good). Some of the enemies are almost entirely useless, and I spent a bit too much time going to previous levels to grab a good host body to start the next one with. Also, many of the enemies don't use the attacks against you that they use when posessed, and while thats a relief with the statues, for everyone else its a shame. The overall difficulty is once again begging to enter this list, although I never really had a problem with it I'm sure a community such as this might.

Still, its great, and if you can play it, DO!
