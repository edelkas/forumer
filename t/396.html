Hey, I've been playing this game like mad and thought of a few things that might be add to the gameplay. I like the game as it is really, i.e. out-manuvering instead of outgunning your enemy, mix of puzzle and platformer, etc.

Anyway, here are some things I thought might be cool.

Water - Could be placed like regular terrain, to create some very odd situations (like water on the ceiling, or in floating "buckets") Water would slow down everything that is in it. This could be really bad for N, since slowing down would cost him time (and simulate "drowning" in the process). It would also allow for some neat new graphics effects, like lasers making steam bubbles as they pass through. N should probably be able to jump higher in water too.

Ice - Maybe a cliche' feature, but it seems that slippery floors and walls could add some real challenges to the game.

Electric Bot - Flys around shooting lightning bolts either straight down, or in a (counter)clockwise fashion. If it goes in water, the entire pool of water becomes deadly.

I was going to say colored terrain too, to make themed levels either, but color seems to be one of the most requested features.
