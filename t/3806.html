this is moving to read. I really never sat back and thought about why people respect me, but reading this and what people say I can pretty well tell I am. (if you can't tell I'm trying hard not to be egotistic amidst the praise). I think part of it is Mount Doom and Flipside. Flipside was the real launch to fame on the forums. Before that it was all Marcross Dreams (man I miss that guy, and you need to go play his maps) and saucy soldat. Flipside at the time was renowned as the best map ever by the higher ups at the time. That started my carreer as a map maker. Before that I had done door eerie clones, some really really bad maps called robbery I-V (please don't make me remember more than once, they sucked hard core) and just not good maps. Flipside really simplified everything and was just clean. Since then that has been my overriding theme in maps, clean and simple (hence all the semetry, low object counts, not a lot of enemies, small maps, all simple and fluid). Then I made Mount Doom. Man oh man did that caus a stir. not right when I released it, which is funny, but after I spent a month with LV trying to beat it and we finally did I was set. People noticed the map because me and LV wouldn't stop talking about it and it quickly became peoples favorite map. That is still apparent since so many of you lobbied for it to be the last map of the user column. Since then my maps have been met with overwhellming support. Sure I miss a couple of times (and you guys let me know) but my ratings are all very very high. I mean I have 200 maps with an average rating of 4... that if you don't know is absurd beyond all reason. So in conclusion my respect was built on my maps, then I ended up being a very reasonable guy with some good insights (at least I think they are good) and then got my good in the forums. Then I was the only guy who was able to hold my own in the debate era of the forum (hold my own for my view, the rest of you who were against me did just dandy, and no this is not me starting this back up). So yea, that's my story for those of you who missed my rising.
