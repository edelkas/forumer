CONTEST OVER
Yay!! Another original contest!! w00t!!
You are to make a level pack that uses the same tileset for each map and contains 1 of each of the specified types.
Judging begins: January 8th
Judging ends: January 11th
============
The rules

All maps must use the EXACT SAME TILESET
Your level pack must contain 1 and only 1 of each of these types of maps:
  • action
  • puzzle
  • race
  • DDA
Each of the maps must be submitted to NUMA with a link to this contest on it
Put links to all of your maps in ONE SINGLE post
Judges cannot submit maps

=================
The Rewards

1st- Sig Tag & 2 dedications
2nd- Sig Tag & dedication
3rd- Sig Tag

Your entires will be judged on...

Appeal of tileset (/10)
Flow of Race (/10) [I know that making a flowy race out of a multi-use tileset it hard- you might want to make parts of the tileset specifically for the race, and then block off those parts in the other maps if you need to]
Difficulty of Action (/10) [not too hard, not too easy]
Challengeingness (that's a new one) of Puzzle (/10)
"Thrill" of DDA (/10)

So a total of 50 points.

Your score will be the average of the judge's ratings. If you are a judge, your maps will only be rated by me and the other judge.
