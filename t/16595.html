Okay, I wanted to make a competition, and finally came out with a somewhat fun (in my opinion) idea for one. It goes as follows:

I will find a playable tileset, made by any author, and it will be the tileset of the week for the entrants to make a map out of. Only one map per user! I would like for there to be 20 entrants, and then applications will cease. Every week, four people will be knocked out of the competition. I will have a link to the tileset here, and I will post a link to every map made every week by every user.

Crucial Rules!!
You may NOT edit the tileset, as I will pick tilesets that are easy to use. If the tileset is chopped, cropped, or edited in any sort of way, you will be asked to resubmit the map correctly.
If you fail to submit your entry to NUMA within the week deadline, you will be disqualified.


Disqualification Steps:

Week 1: 20 people.
Week 2: 16 people.
Week 3: 12 people.
Week 4: 8 people.
Week 5: 6 people.
Week 6: 5 people.
Week 7: 4 people.
Week 8: 3 people.
Week 9: 2 people.
Week 10: Champion is announced.

Prizes:

First Prize: Sig tag (who wants to make it?), five dedication maps.
Second prize: Sig tag (who wants to make it?), three dedication maps.
Third prize: Sig tag (who wants to make it?), a dedication map.
