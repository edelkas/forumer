I now post just a few minutes after our amazing race on PGR2 - I do hope you enjoy the story

--------------------

Time: 8:40pm GMT, 02nd of November 2006
Place: Nuremberg race track.
Racers: 2002 R34 Skyline GT-R (me) v. 2002 Subaru STi (Sis)....

We are both amazing drivers.
Both of our Kudos rank's is higher than just about anyone's around here.
We've proven it time after time, in any weather, at any track.
But the at Nuremberg?
In the rain?
Now that's shear hell.

The start was not good, I short shifted and ended up immediately behind.
The racing itself was no better.
Half way through, I was losing and keep sliding into the dirt many times.
33.065 behind at the time check in the middle of the race.
Very, very bad.
She was doing better than me and kept the racing line so perfect, the gap kept climbing.
Then she got cocky.
At the famous Carrousel turn, she slipped and went off-road.
Time fell fast after that.

20 seconds.
15 seconds.
10 seconds.

Then at about 3/4 through the track, it all slowed down.
She took a corner well - I took a corner well.
She got the right run out of a corner - I got the right run out of a corner.
Clipping apexes and putting down parallel black steaks on the track.
We were totally equal, 10 seconds apart.
Then, out of the blue, her brakes locked up in one of the hairpin turns.
She slid, but held it well and came screaming and sliding out of the corner.
Then the final corner came.
She went very, very wide.
I kept superbly tight.
Plus, I was closing.
Fast.

Then on the 1 mile home straight, with her 0.765 secs in front, our hearts racing as fast as the cars themselves...
She tried to block...
Tried to stop me from taking the lead...
She missed.
And traction went.
Totally.

We both shouted "NOOOO!!!!!"
That moment, time stood still.
Forever.
All I saw was a blue flash go across form left to right infront of me.
Sideways.
2 seconds from the finish she had span out and off the road.
And crashed head on into the barrier.
I won just 1.56 in front.
From 40 odd seconds behind to just 1 second in front to win the race.
An awesome gaming moment that we will remember forever...

#251095 13 2006-11-07T05:29:47Z TornadoTK
I think that my most favored gaming moment would have to be in Counter Strike: Source. Normally, I could care less about this game, but this was just the marvel of the day.

Map of de_dust2

Game is 6 on 5, in the Counter-Terrorist's favor. I'm on the Terrorist team, and my whole team goes down to Bombsite A, while I go up towards B. In the previous rounds, we we're doing that same plan they've been doing, so I feel like not dying with my team for once. My whole team gets wiped and drops the bomb. 6v1.

I look out the door from B to A, and it's clear. I run out, and a mob of CT's come rushing at me. I run back to the middle, and see MORE CT's. I run into the tunnels in hopes that they all rush me like a bunch of lemmings. Well, I got what I wanted. I went up the stairs and chucked a flashbang at my feet just in time to see it go off when all six guys were up the stairs and shooting at me. With everyone blind, I start moving around the map in my head, and leave towards B. I drop a smoke, and wait a few seconds for the footsteps. They throw a flash at me, so I take a few shots with my D.Eagle and run away again, but I nailed a guy on his way out. Grabbed the P90 and ran after tossing my frag grenade in. 5v1.

I weave around the map, from B to CT spawn to where my team died. I grab the bomb, head near T spawn, back through the mid, back through tunnels, and back to B. Peek my head out, nobody's home, and I plant. I immediately switch to my flashbang and wait. Then, the footsteps. Some from the tunnel, some from the door. I chuck it out the door, and when it blows, I run out and kill two guys, taking their equipment. 3v1.

The CT's are at B and the bomb is still ticking. Two guys rush me at the same time I throw a newfound flashbang, and it bounces off of his head, and blinds the three of us. I start spraying my gun in small circles, and sure enough, nailed 'em both. 1v1.

...

The bomb.

I immediately chuck a grenade through the hole in the wall (high part of the wall, just under the marker at B on the map).

Clink-clink.

Clink.

Boom.

Terrorists win.

When my team respawns, everyone flips out about how they lost their money by betting on CT (the in-game, money, of course), and how close the other guy was to defusing the bomb, and how the grenade just bounced off of his head after hitting the walls, then blew up in his face.

Ish happens.
