#58567 16 2005-04-05T20:33:58Z Sendy
Reasons to play N:

1) It's fun! Controlling N is very dreamlike, you feel a lot of control over the character, and the environment is very 'real' and interactive.

2) It's fun! Dying can often STILL get laughs out of me. You'd have thought that after 558658909085 deaths it would get old, but it never does. Maybe it's just my sadistic sense of humour :>

3) It's simple/complex. Enough said. Simple rules, complex combinations and results - just like any true classic game. And there are so many tricks and moves that it seems like there's always room for improvement in terms of how quick you beat a level, etc...

4) It's fun... Because you can make your own levels. Including a level editor is always a way to get my attention.

5) It's a challenge that harkens back to the 8-bit days of gaming. To have the odds stacked up against you (at times) gives a huge adrenaline rush.

Being free is not a reason. If it were to be priceware I'd still buy it, provided the price didn't take the p*ss!
