#13682 1 2007-01-24T22:46:31Z Viridovix
I have decided to introduce a new type of image requesting to metanet. Hopefully, this will benefit everyone, including the artist.

What you do is:

- Request the item

- The artist makes it

- YOU make the artist something back! ( And it can't be something you whip up in 5 minutes either)



I have noticed that random, un-active members come in here and request sigs, then leave like nothing happened. I think I speak for most of us when I say, we would like a little more appreciation than the automatic "thanks".

With this new method, they'll have to think twice about requesting something. No more silly requests with no purpose. For me, and most likely many other graphics artists, we don't do out best when we know the user won't even use it.

Let's revolutionize the way we do things here. Let's give it purpose. Let's make it better.
