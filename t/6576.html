#129065 3 2006-01-01T16:01:55Z Levinous
The different flavours are unlocked on how many columns overall you have beaten. So you don't get pink for the first column and green for the second, you get pink for any one column you've beaten and green for any two columns you've beaten. Vice versa goes for the rest of the flavours.
