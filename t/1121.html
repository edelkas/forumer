To anyone who's interested, the following are thanks to John Hausserman:

All of these riddles follow the same rules:
- Humans always tell the truth.
- Vampires always lie.
- Werewolves lie to humans, tell the truth to vampires, and don't even talk to other werewolves.
- There does not have to be every kind of species in a given situation. That is, just because there are 3 people doesn't mean that there is one of every kind... it could be something like one human and two werewolves, or they could all be vampires.
- Unless otherwises stated, they know who everyone else is, but you don't.

What are Bill, Bob, and Barry? Explain your reasoning.

ANSWER:

Let's see if that works:
For Bob to be Human, two things have to be true...
1) If Bob is a Werewolf, Bill must be a Vampire.
2) Bob must know who Bill is.
Let's check #2 first, because #1 can get confusing quickly.
Bob knows he is Human. Knowing this, and knowing that Bill is lying to him, there are 2 possibilities:
2a) Bill is a Vampire.
2b) Bill is a Werewolf.
Bob has no way of telling which is true, so he actually doesn't know what Bill is, and therefore Bob isn't human, because he says an untrue statement, which also means Bill isn't a Werewolf, which then makes him a Vampire.
So about now, Bob could be just about anything, for all we know.
Let's examine the possibility that he is a Vampire:
If Bob is a Vampire, he must NOT know Bill is a Vampire... but he knows Bill is lying, and only one thing can lie to a Vampire: another Vampire. So Bob isn't a Vampire.
Let's see if he's Human: Once again, he isn't Human, because he still has no way of knowing if Bill is a Werewolf or a Vampire. Either way Bill could lie to him.
Therefore Bob must be a Werewolf. Let's make sure this works:
If Bob is a Werewolf, and he knows Bill is lying to him, does he know what Bill is? Yes, because only a Vampire can lie to a Werewolf. So he is telling the truth when he says, "I know what you are," which is what he would do to a Vampire, being a Werewolf and all. Also, his conditional is true: if he is a Werewolf (which he is), Bill is a Vampire (which he is).
