Well, VijchtiDoodah of Soldat Forums recently showed me this excellent webcomic... Gone With the Blastwave. It is set in a generic post-apocalyptic universe, with 3 teams against each other, lost in a large city. It is in no way serious, however - the humor is great.

But the biggest thing I noticed about this comic is how stylish the drawings are. He makes them using Photoshop... and some of them almost look like oil paintings. Currently, there is only 37 comics made in a 2 year period, which is a very unfortunate slow update speed, because this is a very good comic with an amazing art style that I love to look at.

There's some blood and several curse words, but nothing serious enough to warrant B&T.

Any comments on it?
