Let's play hypotheticals. Let's assume that it really works. The life of all humans has just been extended by 40 years.
What happens next?
I thought it was an interesting question. If we look at some math:
Traditionally, aging starts around 35 (though the body is by no means exhausted at this point, and indeed it's well before halfway through the human lifespan). If the 50% claim holds for that marker, it moves up to 53. Of course, school still ends, for all intents and purposes, by 25 or so. That increases the gap between out-of-college and the end of athletic viability in most sports from 10 years to 28. Certainly, that'll be noticeable.

But how would we even distribute the stuff? Current supplements are known not to be sufficient-25 mg supplements don't raise the plasma level to what was effective in mice. Will this cause something akin to Dune, where everyone is effectively hooked on melange? Would-gasp-some people try to keep it for themselves (even though it literally grows on trees), as has happened with everything else in history, period?
Oh, and what would we do about all that?
