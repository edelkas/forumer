I was trying to see what would happen if you watched a demo in the main menu, then hit the debug menu button after the ninja died during the replay. he was still in the death pose. But when I hit P to play the level, I pressed enter to bring him back to life. Then... oh my gosh! It's cheating death!

So how to do it.

1. Open N and watch a demo on the menu.
2. Wait for the ninja to die. When he does, hit the debug button.
3. Hit P to play the level.
4. Hit enter to bring the ninja back to life.
5. He's alive. But he cheated death like in userlevels and story.

NOTE: When you kill yourself by hitting enter again, it will go back to normal. But if you die by falling, then it won't go to normal.
