Everyone seems to be complaining about the amount of unrated maps these days.
So I was thinking... What's the cause of all of these unrated maps? This isn't directed at anyone, it's just my way of looking at the problem. Here's what I think:

New users submit crappy maps because they don't know what qualities a good map has. They don't know what qualities a good map has because they aren't playing/rating/commenting other people's maps. Who knows why they aren't, but this is the source of all of the unrated maps. If none of the new users these days rate other people's maps, then those maps never get publicly rated. At the same time, the unattractive/spammy maps that these new users are submitting not only don't get rated themselves, but they push other good maps off of the newest page!

I don't know about you, but if there were a flood of maps coming in on the newest page right now, wouldn't you think that at the same time there would be a flood of ratings? Wouldn't you think that all of those authors who just submitted a map would stick around to see if their maps get an initial vote? I know I do. And while I'm waiting, I make sure that I rate a few other new maps. If every author who sumbitted a map to numa also rated a map or two at the same time, the whole unrated maps problem would be more or less solved.

Hence my idea to increase the amount of maps on the newest page, and the pledge that I took. So, does this sound like a logical assesment of our situation?
