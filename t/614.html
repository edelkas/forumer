No keyboard-sharing. Too taxing on the processor, and only one person would get the benefit of using the space bar to jump. I'd prefer an online 2 player co-op, where you can team up with some guy over the 'net (or at least over the network) to help you on the level. It would be very nice to have someone helping to distract enemies, or fetching gold, whilst you go around hitting switches. In fact, there could even be an option to have one of the players being invincible, with the exception that they wouldn't be able to hit switches or get gold - only things to do with enmies, or clearing a mine-field. Online co-op could be implemented via the Flash Communication Server. If we shared the tasks between two different computers, instead of making one computer control both players, it would be a bit faster. However, Imagine a lag... just when you're about to hit the final switch in the level. Ouch. Ah, well. I still think it would be better than sharing a keyboard. Half the sweat, twice the fun!
