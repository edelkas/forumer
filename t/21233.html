Anybody may join at any time. When you join, the current judge will "deal" you 4 maps. He/she will do this by hitting Random on numa and PMing you links to the first 4 maps that come up.
Now that everbody has 4 maps, the judge chooses a category. It can be anything from "puzzle-ish" to "noob-ish" to "feels like going to the bathroom". Seriously, be as random as you like.
Each player submits one of their 4 maps to the judge. The judge chooses the map that he thinks fits the category best. The player who submitted that map gets one point and is the judge of the next round.
The new judge deals everybody seven new maps and a new round begins.
