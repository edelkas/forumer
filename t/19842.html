#373747 4 2007-10-22T19:54:02Z beginner2
  • Originality is a must.
  • Try to make your own tileset. I did that to try because people think that it means make it or else. No, try to make it. If you can't, then use one from NUMA, but make sure that you credit the author.
  • Don't make it for the super-fast people. Make it for a larger population, you know, those who go a little slower than others?
  • Try to include a few close calls, but give the person enough time to be able to actually pass the close calls. Don't make it a DDA-ish map.
There's my 2 cents.

#378404 8 2007-11-11T01:58:58Z Tapp
1.Try getting a tileset from mosiacism (I've made a few race ones)
2.Make sure that the ninja never stops
3.If you want to make a KRA then make the basic level (no enemies) then make a good demo for it. Then use that demo to test, and build it just like a DDA.
4.If you want to make it hard, put in speed traps to make the player go at a cetain speed otherwise something will go wrong.
5.To make it even harder, add in one or two tricks, like extra large perpendicular jumps, that you have to do.
6.Make the route well defined. There is nothing worse than a race with no obvious way to complete it.
