http://mapwiki.therealn.com/

Taking the wiki concept and applying it to N maps, the website lets users create, edit, add to or change N levels in a common arena. Anyone who visits the site can comment to offer criticism, and anyone who registers an account can edit the maps.

The intro on the home page should cover the basics of getting around, but let me know if you need help. Beyond that, go ahead and start editing. I know there's a lot of talent here; let's get some masterpieces out. Whether you go to paste the very start of a map, a map that's almost finished, a finished map that feels wrong, action, puzzle, DDA, tileset, anything at all. Map away.
