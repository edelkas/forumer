#104766 16 2005-10-01T15:34:02Z astro
I'm on ME, and it's nothing like the hellish thing people make it out to be. I use it very heavily, and the only time it ever crashes is when it runs out of memory (normally when I'm running Photoshop, 30 tabs of Firefox, music, and games on my 256mb of RAM), which doesn't happen that often at all.

People who say these things about ME have either been caught up in the myth or are running a hardware-software setup that really negatively influences the computer's stability (which can happen to _any_ operating system). ME is Microsoft's best DOS-based Windows, there's no doubt about it. Minor stability problems are worth it for the upgrades - visuals, bundled updates, Universal PnP (the best feature IMO), (limited) system restore. That said, I don't think it would've been worth upgrading from 98 SE.
