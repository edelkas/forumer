InsomNicon

The Contest:

The main objective is to stay awake for as long as possible, but there are a couple of conditions present:

Quote: Conditions!
You will need to be in the IRC for the duration, so that there's no cheating.

You will need to fulfil one task from the judges each hour, by the turn of the hour, or you will incur a penalty for the next hour. Some of these tasks will be N-related, others just plain stupid.

You may not sleep. Ever.

The last person awake wins!

Dave and I will be the judges, but if we feel we need a break, we might ask a respected fellow member to step in.

Information:

InsomNicon is not planned at the moment, but keep checking back. Players may join up to 4 hours after the start of the contest, however the hour-checkpoints missed will be considered when we determine the winner of the contest--therefore, if you showed up two hours late, you have to stick around for an extra two hours after other players have dropped out.

If you are at all interested in this, contact Dave or me ASAP, or leave a post in this thread.

Winners so far:

Thanks guys!
