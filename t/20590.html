Celebrities

I am just fed up with how celebrities are in the news, magazines and all over in general. The news and tabloids cover literally everything about the lives of celebrities. You might see this on the cover of a magazine "Look who had a baby." The question I have is: Why should I care? Estimating, millions of people have a baby each day, why should I care about this one person? Answer: Because their rich. Wow! I just think it's just so stupid that some people will be interested in this stuff. Also, odds are, you'll never even meet any celebrities so why does it matter. And don't say "There life is more interesting." It's not. The news and tabloids just word things so it sounds interesting. Yet I have not met a lot of people who understand this.

You have friends and family. The stuff that happens in their lives aren't much different then the stuff you see in magazines. The only way its different is because they have a lot of money and the magazines make it sound like a grand, amazing thing when it's something so ordinary. You have your own life. Live it.

#388360 10 2007-12-11T16:24:10Z Spawn of Yanni
Well, celebrities got where they are by dedicating themselves to doing what they do. They got there thanks to a degree of hard work and commitment, and to say they don't deserve the money... well, it'd be unfair to say that. You can't really know if they really deserve it or not.

You say that there's no reason we should care about their lives. Take a celebrity having a baby; of course, there are millions around the world who would've had a baby on the same day, but the difference is that these are, quite simply, people we are familiar with. We don't know who Parvita Krishnamuralitharan is, but we do know Halle Berry. We've seen her in movies, so she's a familiar face. Immediately, that makes any news about her relevant. In the same way I would care if that kid in Year 7 got into a fight, I would care if Britney Spears decided model her head as a bowling ball. I recognise the name, and that familiarity makes the news interesting/entertaining.

The other thing about celebrity news is that it lets us in to the part of their lives that we never get to see. We usually only see the screen personalities. While Jack Bauer is out stopping terrorists, Kiefer's gettin' wasted. I can understand how you might disagree, but I simply find that interesting to know - seeing the human faces behind the light and make-up.

Why do you see these people so often in the news? The public -wants- to know. The large majority, that is. You said it yourself, all your friends at school find the news interesting. The people who print these stories, they're the ones who benefit from them. If one kid out of every thousand says "Who gives a damn!?", they will really not care. They'll still be raking in the money. By -making- the stories sound large and grand, they make people want to read about them. More readers = more money. Tha's what it's all about, these days.
