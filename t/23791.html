Most talented newcomer of the year wouldn't really work as they whole point of the Dronies is to find maps/mappers of the highest calibre. As it is usually people with the most experience who are the best mappers, the average skill of potential winners would be inherently lower, so there would be far less competition for any new mappers with exceptional skill. It wouldn't be fair to the older candidates who have a far wider range of people to compete against. If any new mappers have the skill to compete with the veterans, then they would fully deserve to win, and probably could.
