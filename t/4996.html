Don't Stutter

The first half (right half), based on Charred, is open with a lot of gold and several ways to go, some of them optional, others you can take in any order. However, there are very, very nastily-placed gauss turrets pretty much everywhere. You need to move quickly and decisively, and don't have much time to think, although there are a few spots where you can stop (both switches and near the border of Zebra on the top half). There are also a few puzzle jumps here, either just to get to the switches (the jump a little below the center of this half) or to get to the extra gold (very top-left of this half) without dying.

The second half (left half), based on Zebra, is my way of showing you why you should be happy that Red_Reamer used gauss turrets . This is a maddeningly hard mine-jumper, which gets harder as you go lower except for the very bottom switch. This is why you get dedications for completing it

I don't expect the map to get rated very high due to difficulty, but it's rather fun until you get too angry at the second-from-bottom mine set
