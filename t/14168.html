Here! Have the first act of my hypothetical film script, written exclusively between the hours of 1am and 3am on 3 different occasions!

(Well, I don't have cable, so I have to make my own fun...)

For a properly formatted version (With margins, italics, boldness, and varying indents- oh my!), download the word document.

And keep in mind that while I have a vague idea what I want to do for the first three episodes, I've written this at godforsaken hours (at one point while intoxicated, and at one point loaded with panadeine after having just come back from getting stiches in the top of my head) and haven't proof read or edited it at all yet. I thought it could be more 'interesting' while it was still... raw, crap, and poorly thought out.

It's fairly terrible, but it's a starting point for a project I've been wanting to get off the ground for a while now. Give it a read if you've got some spare time. And please, don't spare the critique- but keep in mind I have no delusions about the quality of this stuff.

Also, please don't give me advice on the direction of the plot. I've got that down, though I probably should have worked out exactly what would happen in each act and scene before beginning to write...

Overall style of the show: Think the Fuck Forever music video introduction. Yup, something like that.



Teaser, Scene 1

A small apartment bedroom, charmless and cramped. The walls are mostly bare. Its late night, and the room is scarcely lit by streetlight.

[We see a younger BEN stirring from sleep, and a figure by his bed. As he opens his eyes, he starts as he sees THOMAS standing over the bed.]

Thomas: Hi.

Ben: Youre not here.

Thomas: I thought that too, you know. I was wrong.

Ben: Im dreaming.

Thomas: That makes sense.

Ben: Yes. It does.

[A pause, and we see the room revealed again, without Thomas in it.]

[The alarm clock sounds, and it is day. We see BEN rise from bed, enter the bathroom, and wash his face. There is nothing in the mirror, but as BEN turns around, he sees THOMAS immediately behind him. BEN jumps, shocked.]

Ben: Youre not here. So theres a rationalisation. Im dreaming. Or hallucinating. Or something.

Thomas: Try pinching yourself.

[A pause, and then BEN moves back to the bedroom, talking distractedly to THOMAS as he dresses.]

Ben: Or swallowing pills. Medication. For mental instability. That works.

Thomas: How do you know you need it?

Ben: Because Im talking to somebody who isnt here.

Thomas: How do you know Im not?

Ben: Because youre dead.

Thomas: How do you know Im dead?

[A pause. Ben is finished dressing.]

Ben: Because I killed you.

[Exit BEN]

Teaser, Scene 2
L.A, inner city- Morning

[BEN walks through the streets to work. A figure catches his eye, briefly, which seems otherworldly and insubstantial. He walks through a crowd (literally through), and BEN loses sight of him. Some stock footage and fast editing brings us through the rest of the day to the night.]

Teaser, Scene 3
Bens Apartment- Nighttime

[BEN is in bed, and stirs from sleep to see THOMAS standing above him]

Ben: The whole terror of the night thing?

Thomas: Yeah?

Ben: Its a bit of an exaggerated title, dont you think? Mild annoyance and sleep disturbance of the night might be slightly more accurate.

Thomas: I can do more than just irritate.

Ben: [Goadingly] So whos a big scary ghost?

[THOMASS face distorts into something horrible, slimy, and possibly maggoty. With arms outstretched, what remains of Thomas rushes- or, rather, floats- towards Ben and far, far into his personal space. A moment before it touches him, we cut to Ben truly waking up. For a moment, he appears relieved]

Thomas: Bad dream? [BEN starts]

Ben: Oh, its you. [Pause] How could you tell?

Thomas: For how many years did we sleep in the same bedroom when we were kids? You get to know these things.

Ben: How come I could never tell with you?

Thomas: I didnt dream. You, on the other hand, kept a diary of yours. Through your teenage years and everything. Some of them were pretty entertaining.

Ben: Oh. Good to know.

[BEN gets out of bed and pulls on a coat.]

Thomas: The ones containing Lucy were always the most entertaining. Remember the one with the whipped cream and the midgets?

Ben: I honestly dont.

[Still in his pajamas, under the coat, BEN heads out of the apartment.]

Teaser, Scene 4
[A nearby park, with the dawn just beginning to break]

[BEN is sitting a bench. Far across the park, a ghostly figure catches his eye, and he follows it with his eyes, until it disappears behind a clump of trees on the other side of the park. He is relieved that it- whom he believes to be Thomas- hasnt spotted him, but THOMAS is actually behind BEN.]

Thomas: Running isnt going to work.

Ben: Why not?

Thomas: I can walk through walls. It cuts off a lot of distance.

Ben: So how come you can reach floor 30 of my apartment?

Thomas: I can walk into walls, too.

[A pause]

Ben: You had no right.

Thomas: No. I didnt. But how did you?

Ben: Because we were engaged. Because we were planning on having many fat little children and moving into the country and breeding llamas for a living or something like that. Because we were in love.

Thomas: And we werent?

Ben: A happy life. A life with many more llamas and a lot fewer suicides than the life you gave her.

Thomas: Im sorry.

Ben: If I forgive you, will you go away?

Thomas: I wish I knew.

Ben: Does it suck, being a ghost?

Thomas: More than I can say.

Ben: Good.

[A few seconds of stock footage and footage from morning in the apartment plays, the sun goes up and back down again, and we are left in no doubt that a day has just passed.]

Teaser- Scene 5
Early the next night, in the same park

[BEN is sitting on the grass, looking at the sky, when THOMAS approaches. A FRUMPY WOMAN is walking her dog- something small and ridiculous- nearby.]

Thomas: You werent back in the apartment.

Ben: I know, what with being the person who wasnt there and all.

Thomas: You might not want to talk too loudly.

Ben: Why?

Thomas: Because that frumpy woman over there is looking at you as though youre a bit odd.

Ben: [Turning to look at FRUMPY WOMAN] That frumpy woman looks a bit odd, herself.

[The FRUMPY WOMAN overhears Ben and leaves in a huff]

Thomas: Tactful.

Ben: Youre a bastard.

Thomas: And youre a murderer.

Ben: But at least Im sorry. [A pause, and its repeated with far more gravity] Fuck, Im so sorry.

Thomas: So am I.

[The touching reunion is cut short by CGI effects as Thomas fades away in some sort of whirl of trippy, trippy colours.]

Ben: [To the now nonexistent Thomas] So its over now?

[For a few moments, Ben remains in the same position, reaching his hand into where Thomas stood a few moments ago. Eventually, he begins to return to the apartment, deep in thought. Partway there, he spots an INCONSEQUENTIAL GHOST as it walks through the walls of a building. He gives chase for a few steps, until another INCONSEQUENTIAL GHOST catches his eye, walking along the other side of the park. BEN spins around a few times, scanning for more ghosts or information.]

Thomas: [As a disembodied voice] No, Ben. Not for you.

OPENING TITLES
(Gomez- Chasing Ghosts with Alcohol?)


Act 1, Scene 1
City Of Lounge
Bloc Party- So Here We Are

[The room is blackened, and the camera navigates it in sweeping moves as the lights slowly come on. As time passes, we can make out the silhouettes of dancers, drinkers, and motion everywhere- except on the still shape of JACQUES BELAIR]

[The movement on set as well as the lighting matches the music.]

Jacques: Ive always liked to live on a large scale. Tried my best, since I was a kid, to be the top. The best there is. Some people say that its easier to aim low; dive off the low boards before the high ones. Less painful if you realise youre going to hit the water belly first halfway down. I never agreed. I always preferred to forget my reservations and just throw myself off.
It made sense at the time. Throw all of yourself against the universe, hoping that the universe would yield to you, rather than the other way around. And it worked, for a spell. Until the universe stopped yielding to me and I landed belly- first into the chlorinated cesspool of last year. If you aim high and fail, its a bigger fall.
A few days ago I ended up at the top of a rather large building. All I had left in my life was substantial eviction notices and insubstantial memories. Id always liked to live life on a large scale. It was a large drop.


Act 1, Scene 2
[The lighting becomes standard for the rest of the episode, and everything is visible from this point on. The place is alive with dance, drink, and people moving through life- oblivious and a stark contrast to JACQUES, stationary in death. GOD is the only other person not moving or infected in some way by the atmosphere, watching JACQUES.]

[Enter SIMON, HOLLY]

Simon: The place is busy tonight.

Holly: It is. So who reported one of them in here tonight?

Simon: They didnt leave a name.

[A pause]

Simon: Look, so I was thinking, while we wait, if youd maybe like to grab something to drink?

Holly: Probably not a good idea. We should stay alert, hope we see something.

Simon: Okay, sure. That works. Youre right. We should stay on task.

[Another pause]

Simon: Do you want to dance? [Sensing incomprehension] With me?

Holly: Okay. Suddenly I do feel the need for a drink.

[Another pause. Enter BENJAMIN, BLANE]

Blane: I get mystical stuff, yeah. You cant have this gig and not think so. But dragons they cant be anything but old wives tales.

Benjamin: Yes, but who said that old wives tales are doubtful in the first place?

Blane: Does it matter?

Benjamin: The old wives themselves. So who can you trust?

Blane: Explain it, then. How can dragons possibly exist in this world, undetected?

Benjamin: The same way life can exist. And ghosts. Just because you dont understand it, doesnt mean its not there.

[HOLLY and SIMON make their way to the group]

Holly: Hey! Guys! Whatre you going here?

Simon: Yes. What are you doing here?

Blane: Got a call. Somebody in here apparently started acting as though they were convinced there was a ghost in front of them.

Benjamin: Apparently it was a celebration party for a guy who got a promotion. One of the guys was convinced Charles, who used to hang with the group before he died, was sitting in his chair. He panicked, shouting and throwing handfuls of nachos at the empty place. His girlfriend was trying to calm him when he ran off.

Holly: Kind of like Macbeth.

Benjamin: Exactly like Macbeth. Except with some guy in the background complaining about having to clean up guacamole.

Holly: How did he die?

Benjamin: Who? Guacamole guy?

Holly: Charles.

Benjamin Drug overdose is the popular theory.

Holly: So they were druggies. And how do we know the guy who saw his friend wasnt just hallucinating?

Benjamin: We dont, but

Blane: I think I see him.

Holly: Whatever. You guys are here. Im going to go get a drink. Tell me if you need anything.

[HOLLY heads to the bar, not seeing JACQUES. BLANE moves towards JACQUES]

Ben: Howd you get the message before us?

Simon: Didnt. I was here with Holly on a. Umm. Date. With Holly.

Ben: Wait. So you two... You finally asked her out?

Simon: Would have. If you lot hadnt shown up.

Ben: We didnt know you guys were here. We got a call. We heard there was a ghost or a shade of something- thought we should check it out.

Simon: Youve got to be kidding. Ben, theres no ghost here. I just used it as an excuse to talk to Holly. Why did you guys come?

Ben: We got a call. Unless you hired a guy to pretend his friend had gone crazy, theres a shade here.

Simon: Bloody hell.

Ben: So, youre dating Holly, then? Congratulations. When were you planning on telling her?

Simon: Bloody hell.

[SIMON moves to leave]

Ben: Where are you going?

Simon: Out. Away. Someplace thats not here.

[Exit SIMON. BEN moves to rejoin with BLANE, who has reached JACQUES, and GOD, who is staring at JACQUES. Ben moves to talk to JACQUES.]

Blane: Wait. Look.

Ben: At what?

Blane: Him. [BLANE points to GOD]. He can see the ghost.

Ben: Are you sure? Wait, no. Of course.

Blane: So what do we do?

Ben: The same I did with you. Hes a spotter. We talk to him.

Blane: He can see the ghost. Hes a murderer. Could be dangerous. Could be insane. Are you sure you want to try this? [BEN moves to talk to GOD] Ben! Wait a moment!

[GOD turns away from JACQUES to look at Ben and Blane]

God: Hi! Im God! I could be smiting you right now. But Im not. Because Im merciful. And thats a good thing!

Blane: Remember that whole insane thing I was talking about?

Ben: Hi. Im Zeus. Its a pleasure meeting you.

God: No, youre not. Youre a twenty two year old guy trying to test if insane or not by seeing if I treat you like Zeus.

Ben: [To BLANE] He seems alright to me. [To GOD] True, sorry. Really, Im Ben. Its a pleasure meeting you. And Im not one for subtlety, so I was wondering what youd think of coming with us to help ghosts move from the end of this life into the next. Its kind of our calling. And the pay isnt too bad, either. Would you like to join us?

God: Yes.

Ben: See? Hes totally on the level. And he agreed to help us.

Blane: And the fact he just agreed to that by no means makes his claim to sanity seem at least slightly dubious?

Benjamin: No. [Moving towards the exit] Holly! Were leaving.

Holly: Whos the new guy?

Ben & Blane: [Together, Blane disdainfully and Ben enthusiastically.] God.

Holly: Oh. Cool.

[Exit Ben, Blane, and Holly. The lighting begins to dim, and everyone on set seems to eventually disappear, save JAQCUES BELAIR.]

Jacques:
They say that death is the end. The problem is that they dont really speak from experience. If only we were all that lucky.
We are those with deep regrets, those who have been murdered, and those whose unfinished business makes them think theyre not ready for the afterlife yet. Shades are people whose loathing of their killer binds them to this world even after their bodies dont. Ghosts are those who wish- who need- to haunt their killers.
So where does this leave those of us who have committed suicide?
I never told her I loved her. And Ive never hated anybody more than I hated myself for not telling her before I left.
I love her. And in all these years, I never told her.
So how do you haunt yourself? How do you come to peace with yourself? How can you manage to simply fade away?
Or maybe I was never meant to.
Its cold, being dead. Its colder than youd believe, when you dont have a body. And lonely, too. Very.


Act 1, Scene 3
Inside the Shades second- hand bookstore.

Daisy: So it didnt go very well.

Simon: Understatement. Might as well say Tiananmen Square didnt go very well.

Daisy: I went there a few months ago. Its actually pretty, these days.

Simon: How symbolic.

Daisy: Im sorry, Simon. [A pause] Want a coffee or something to drink?

Simon: Tea

Daisy: How English of you.

Simon: And I was trying to keep my nationality secret. Bollocks.


Act 1, Scene 4
A street in the inner city.

[BEN, BLANE, HOLLY and GOD are walking towards the Shades bookstore. BEN and BLANE take the lead, and GOD trails behind, constantly distracted by displays in the windows, cars, and empty space. HOLLY frequently returns to drag him forwards.]

Ben: Does anybody else feel like weve forgotten anything?

Blane: Like what?

Ben: I dont know. Its like that feeling when youre packing clothes and you think youve left behind your razor or something. But you push it to the back of your mind until you end up in Africa and realize you didnt pack any pants.

Blane: No, I think were good. We didnt come with anything. And were returning with an omnipotent being. Which cant be too bad.

Holly: Dont mock him. He might smite you.

Blane: What are we doing with him, anyway?

Ben: He can see them. Were recruiting him. Like we did you. Besides, how can it be a bad thing to have a God around?

Blane: Please. What would a God be doing in Los Angeles, of all places?

Holly: You do know what Los Angeles translates to, right?

[The camera tracks Holly, Blane, Ben and God as they enter the Shades bookstore.]

Daisy: So how did it go?

Ben: Good. We picked up a God.

Daisy: Oh. Is this one of those things that takes a long time to explain?

Ben: Kinda, yeah.

God: Hi. Im God.

Daisy: Pleased to meet you.

[Daisy offers her hand to God, who stares at it curiously]

Blane: Hes all powerful and has infinite wisdom. Can you tell?

Ben: Did Rachel call while we were away?

Blane: Isnt it usually the girl who stresses when the guy doesnt call?

Ben: Are you implying Im being effeminate?

Blane: Do you know how manly using the term effeminate isnt?

Daisy: So wheres the ghost?

[A pause]

Blane: About that

Ben: I told you guys!

Blane: When?

Ben: When we were coming here! I said I felt like wed forgotten something! Like that feeling when you leave the iron on.

Simon: You iron?

Ben: On occasion

Blane: Very manly.

Act 1, Scene 5
City Of Lounge
Vast- Touched

[After an establishing shot, we move in onto BLANE, who is talking with a distracted bartender- Moriarty]

Moriarty: Ah, my dear, unobservant Blane. They were easy deductions. Your tie is loosened, but not undone. Youre breathing heavily. So you came here quickly, and didnt have the time to pre- meditate coming here quickly or the foresight to think of taking your tie off. Judging by the fact you came so quickly, Ill guess you forgot something important. Judging by the fact youre talking to me, Ill assume its about a ghost. Judging by the positions of the scuff marks along the side of your shoes, which appear to be relatively new, Ill assume he was about five foot six, maybe 25 years old when he died, and had brown hair.

Blane: You saw him here, too.

Moriarty: Yeah, of course. I just figured you guys were here to pick him up. You noticed him, obviously. Then you left with some random. And now youre back. Thus, I can easily deduce that youre all either drunk or stupid, and seeing as your speech isnt slurred, Ill go for the second.

Blane: He still around?

Moriarty: Left about 5 minutes ago. Went out the back way. Or, actually, the wall just to the left of the back way. Hell be around someplace.

Blane: Thanks.

Act 1, scene 6
Inner city LA street

[BLANE walks idly through the inner city, looking for a sign of JACQUES. His mobile rings, and he takes the call as he continues to wander the streets.]

Blane: Hello?

Ben: Blane. Any luck?

Blane: No. Talked to Moriarty. The shade left just before we got in. He really needs to lay off the Arthur Conan Doyle.

Ben: Its late. Holly and I are heading off soon. Well call it a night, alright? Just come by to pick your stuff up.

Blane: Sure.

[BLANE hangs up, and turns to head back. ANNE is running in the other direction, carrying coffee and a handbag. She runs into Blane, and is sent sprawling on the sidewalk. Her coffee spills, as do the contents of her handbag- among them, a mobile phone, a cross, several cloves of garlic, and a gun.]

Blane: Sorry! Are you alright?

Anne: [Collecting the items and putting them back into her handbag.] Fine. And dandy. If you ignore the whole sitting on the sidewalk thing with hot coffee spilt on me and the last train back leaving in all of twenty seconds.

Blane: Sorry. You should look where youre going.

Anne: Wow. What perfect timing for your timely warning. [ANNE attempts to stand.] Ow. I think I might have sprained my ankle.

Blane: Are you okay?

Anne: What part of I think I might have sprained my ankle gives you the difficulty? [Begins to limp away] Ouch.

Blane: [Picking up a clove of garlic from the sidewalk] Wait! You forgot your... garlic.

Anne: Thanks.

Blane: Odd sort of a thing to be carrying around in a pink handbag.

Anne: Why? What do you usually carry in your pink handbag?

Blane: Look, its just-

Anne: Its 11.55. The last train leaves in the subway in exactly three minutes and maybe if I hobble fast enough and resist the urge to open about my life to the cause of the coffee spread across my chest, I might just be able catch the thing and return home to lick my wounds and maybe, just maybe, get a good nights sleep.

Blane: Sleep? Leave yourself that vulnerable to vampires?

Anne: Huh?

Blane: Garlic and crosses. People use them to keep vampires away.

Anne: Oh. Right. Umm. Are you sure?

Blane: Pretty.

Anne: Oh. What about silver bullets?

Blane: Thats for werewolves.

Anne: Damnit. Saying the persons name?

Blane: I think it might kill mummies. Or just help you relate to them on a more personal level. One or the other. You really dont watch many horror movies, do you?

Anne: Just none containing vampires. Or, umm, werewolves. Or mummies. [A pause] So what do you usually do for ghosts?

[ANNE suddenly looks apprehensive and vulnerable, and BLANE gets a knowing look. Hes about to say something when we cut.]

End of Act 1
