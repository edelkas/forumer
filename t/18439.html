#345020 2 2007-07-19T13:55:19Z gloomp
Once you've made your map, copy the data from the upper text box, then put the data in userlevels format which goes like this:

$Map Name#Author Name#Map Type#Data That You Copied From Upper Text Box#

After you've done that, go to the folder where you keep n and all the documents that came with it, and open "userlevels.txt" and paste the data in there beneath the clearly marked line.
Now just save the changes, and your done!

p.s. Please refrain from calling it "n game" in the future. Just call it "n"
