This is a very fun IRC map making game that is simmilar to TMM. Lord day came up with the idea and I wrote the rules. The first game was with Lord_day and Me vs. Palemoon and yahoozy vs. smtid and jtbandes. sythe33 timmed. It can be played with any # of people above 2. YOu can talk to any of us on IRC if you need more help playing.

Rules: Team members go into a seperate channel. Each team member switches off the map in 2 minute intervals. The other member times. There is a 1 minute break between each turn. It goes a total of 12 minutes so each team has 6 turns or 3 turns each player.

Talk to any of us if you have questions.

#252746 4 2006-11-10T23:12:07Z lord_day
Basically, we split six people into 3 teams (note any number of teams can be made). So the game starts. One person on your team spends two minutes (excatly) on a map. After two minutes they send the data to their team mate, who spends another two minutes (exactly) editing/adding to the map. The data is sent back to the first person, and its is added again. This happens until each person has edited the map 3 teams, giving a total of 12 minutes. That is a twelve minute team tag. I think that is clearer.
