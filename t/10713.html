#192492 2 2006-06-21T15:27:22Z Twistkill
When you downloaded N, a bunch of files came with it other than N itself. One of them is called userlevels.txt. What you do is Select All and Copy the level data you want from NUMA, then you open your userlevels.txt, and follow the instructions there. Then save your changes and open N again. You'll alos want to make sure that the original copies of N and userlevels.txt are in the same folder they came in. Just create a shortcut on your desktop for easy access.
