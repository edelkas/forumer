You can make fake one-way platforms, a thwump that zaps you on it's right side (but has the cyan part at the bottom so it's misleading), and I think you can even make glitchy doors that kill you. Pretty inane stuff.

The most useful thing is to NaN the last number for the code for a drone - that makes it stationary. Lazer and chaingun drones become turrets and zap drones become large mines.
