#285142 6 2007-01-13T09:32:06Z Kablizzy
I thought Expanse was a Hell of a step up. I now know what the niche is for the trio, and I think that our first one lacked a focus that we had for Expanse. I chalk it up to experience and just being able to reflect on what had already gone on seemed to help immensely. Plus, just on a personal note, my mapmaking has grown exponentially just between Origins and Expanse.

In fact, I just went back through my Legacy stuff, and I realized that the maps that we made for Expanse were so much more refined than those for Legacy, mostly because of the experience of us knowing, "Well, this didn't go over well, let's try this and this," and I think the map quality is better overall for it.

Beyond that, I counted up how many of my Legacy maps would have definitively made it into Expanse (I didn't count maps that I was iffy about), and I came up with around 25, one quarter of the maps I did for Legacy. Not that they were bad, but the level that we expected Expanse to be seemed to jump a few notches.

Just to look at things like Mother Must I? and Constellation versus Arrant and TMZ... I think I, for one, have bettered my mapmaking by a lot just taking part in this project. And, some of the maps that the fellas put out on the table were just mindblowing the first time I saw them. A lot of this stuff had so much more oomph to it, and thusfar, Zenith is shaping up to be much the same.
