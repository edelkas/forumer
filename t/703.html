Well, firstly... I'd like to express that, though only slightly flawed around the edges, N is a BRILLIANT game. Being a Flash game developer myself, I can barely believe that such a game is possible to make...

The main issue I'm posting to discuss is playing games in debug mode. I created my level, double-checked that I'd put in the Ninja and the exit (when I didn't do that, N didn't save the map properly), and hit the save key. Great, it saved properly this time. So imagine my surprise when I pressed the key to 'Continue playing the game'. My Ninja was frozen in the air at the start. At first, I thought the game had crashed, but, after a second glance, I noticed the numbers in the bottom-left corner were still ticking. So I decided to play around with the arrow keys. Nothin happened. This sometimes happens when playing games on the internet, so I clicked on the application to assure that Flash Player responded to the command... and just for one frame, the Ninja unfroze, and put his limbs out a bit.

So I carried on clicking, and the Ninja kept moving. It seemed as if the only way to use him was to continuously click, again and again and again. And this worked. However, I found myself incapable of playing my game, having to control the arrow keys, the space bar (my new jump key) and the mouse (to continue clicking). I was wondering... does anybody else experience this problem when playing a game from the debug mode? And has anybody got a cure for it? I've tried rebooting the game, but that doesn't work. Could you possibly fix this in the next version of N for Mac (if you figure out what's wrong, of course). Because personally, I play this game for fun, and it gets boring playing all the levels without enemies over and over again... mines and obstacles are hard enough, but I haven't even been able to get far enough to see how hard the missiles would be! Maybe you should make more levels comprised entirely of platforms, where the only limit is time and how far you can fall? Just wondering. I understand how hard you guys must've been working just to get this far. Other than that, good burrito!
