It's the newest SW Battlefront game, and PSP exclusive. It has been shown in the commercial for the Battlefront PSP Slim pack, but it also has Hero CTF, 5 new maps for the BF series, and you can enter the capital ships in space assault now on PSP. There's also a new Heroe and Villain, Kit Fisto and Asaj Ventress, previously only available with the Xbox 360 BF ll patch. There are also 2 new starfighters, the B-Wing and TIE Defender, and there are command posts in space battles that can actually be captured, but there's only one of those on two maps, and you have to be playing in the right era. There's also a campaign mode on this game, following Renegade Squadron, of course, which makes this game pretty good, once you get over the new control scheme (control conspiracy) and inability of Heroes on land battles.
