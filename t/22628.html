The All-Metanet Track Team

I've realized after reading this thread that we've got quite a few runners on this forum, so I thought it would be neat to have a thread where everyone can post their personal bests. Maybe it'll spark some friendly competition. We'll do this DyeStat style. Post your personal bests for the events that you run, and I'll update the lists. If you want any events added, just say so and I'll add 'em on.

Note: I realize that the 1600 poses a problem because many people run the 1500 or full mile. For the purposes of this thread, everyone will convert their times to the 1600. Use this calculator to convert times. Just select the distance, punch in your time, and check the 1600 result in the 'Purdy' column. Use that time here, and be sure to note which event you've converted your time from.

Edit: Added jumping events and pole vault. Also, Grassman 7z7 brings up a good point here about calculating your times. Please note that the calculator linked above is only for use in converting times between the 1500, 1600, and full mile because some places run things differently than others. In other words, you can't project a 400m time from your 100m or 200m time.
