#92058 15 2005-08-18T03:42:00Z nevermore
Right, example.

My new map, Doublethink.

I spent about half an hour on the original tileset (read description of the map on numa.)
I spent another half an hour trying to make a level out of that tileset.
I realized I couldn't, and spent another half hour adjusting the tileset to my needs.
I spent a half hour formulating how the level will play, messing around with items.
When I have my idea, I place my enemies and the ninja, spending about another half hour.
I playtest the level for another half hour, and find what objects need to be placed to help the ninja move about.
I playtest for a half hour, adjusting things as needed, changing the timing of the drones, moving little things around.
I playtest, playtest, and playtest more. Now is when I add things such as the floor guards in the bottom corners.
I playtest more.
Now I place the gold, and I spend a while making sure it's in the right place, and that the patterns fit the style of the map.
I playtest more.
I spend a while acting very paranoid that people won't like the map, trying to find every flaw and correcting it.
That amounts to quite a bit of time, eh?
