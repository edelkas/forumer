Well, first of all, thanks for your amazing (and sometimens frustating) game.
I'm obviously talking of N! And now I'm here to suggest you some things to improve this game (note that some tips here are already listed in some other thread).

1) Episode-saving option. From 1.2 to 1.3 I had to repeat all episodes I've already beaten. Maybe a oven system would be better than the autosave (or both).

2) New items and enemies. Special items like fast-running ability, invulnerability, invisibility, or even nasty surprises like slower, paralyzer, lifespan reduced... and more enemies: for example, a chaingun turret, cannon turret, lifespan zapper (something that reduces the lifespan)...

3) Multiplayer options: competitive and cooperative modes.
Cooperative: up to 4 players try to reach the exit in special designed levels (more difficult) and at least one must survive.
Competitive: up to 4 players try to reach the exit. The first wins. Special abilities like weapons for N?

4) HighScores improved. A general summary where players can see their total score (all episodes).

5) Support for the Map Archive. An option to grab the most downloaded maps

6) New Ned option to set the gravity (for example, from 0,1 to 1,9) for a scenario. This will affect all speeds (weapons included), N jumps, N fallings, and even the lifespan.

7) Interact with demos. Play the same level while viewing another N trying to beat the level. Something to say "I've beaten the level better than him!"

8) Some MIDI track. They occupy very cilantro space.

9) An option to listen my favorite MIDI, WAV or mp3 file.

10) A Ned option to enable\disable life timer for a scenario.

11) A Campaign editor.

this is all for now, i forgot many things but I'll continue to post...
