A brief overview of Australian politics, for anyone who cares:

The next largest party forms the Opposition, led by the Opposition Leader (duh) and the Shadow Cabinet (Shadow Treasurer, etc). The Queen's representative (Australia is a constitutional monarchy) is called the Governer General, and proceedings in Parliament are controlled by the Speaker. The same applies at state level, except that the leader of the Government is the Premier, and the Queen's rep is just the Governer.

The Coalition consists of the Liberal Party and National Party (formerly Country Liberal Party). The Nationals focus on rural seats. The Liberals are much more powerful and hold mostly urban and suburban seats. The coalition is moderate conservative, and their strengths are traditionally economic management and growth, fast and on-budget completion of major projects, and the encouragement of business, trade and free enterprise. It is traditionally supported by business owners, white-collar workers and the upper class. They usually get voted out because the public feels they are not supporting public services enough, or that they are not helping the lower classes enough.
