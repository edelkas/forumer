So, I like Rock Band. Enough to make an Excel sheet on the list of songs. So I did. Gimme some feedback (besides the fact that this was a big waste of time) and future ideas to improve it.

The Rock Band Excel Sheet - July 7th, 2008 Edition

Features:
  • A list of every song available to play (218 songs)
  • Difficulty ratings for every song, for individual instruments and band play
  • Includes the release date of all songs, and what songs are available to purchase in packs
  • A personal "Purchased DLC" column for checking off which songs you have yet to purchase
  • Color and automatic text formatting to make updating the list easy
Coming Soon:
  • Current price of songs/song packs
  • Scorehero.com's lowest confirmed 5-Star and 6-Star rating for every instrument on Expert
  • Personal high score columns for every instrument on Expert, formatted to show when you attain a 5-Star or 6-Star rating.
