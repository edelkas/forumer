Hey, that's a great idea! In fact, you can already do it! When you're in the highscore menu, click on an episode and look to the lieft. There you will see a table that says "personal bests." There, you can watch replays of you're best scores on each level.
#246358 3 2006-10-27T10:36:11Z Atilla
In fact, you can also do it for userlevels. Click on the time of your personal best, which is next to the entry for the associated level, and the replay will start.
#246435 4 2006-10-27T15:12:48Z Britannia
When you finish a userlevel, you replay data will be temporarily stored in the text box to the middle left of your screen. Once you complete another level, close the game, etc. you'll loose it. However, as Atilla says, you can still view your replay by clicking the Replay button to the right of the level.
