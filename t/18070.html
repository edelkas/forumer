How are you supposed to make demos? I know you can hit start and stop demo recording but what do you do after that?
#336120 2 2007-06-27T01:05:56Z Azjps
Begin recording the demo (1), then start playing the level (P). After you're done and you return to debug mode (\ ~), the box on the bottom right that states "Demo Data" will contain your demo data, usually a bunch of numbers with | in between. You can copy it out of the box and do whatever you like with it then (to use it, paste it into the demo box, then press 3, Q, P)

The editor_manual (section 7) that comes with N contains a more detailed explanation.
