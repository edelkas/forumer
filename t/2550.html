Would be neat to have a bounce block that acts like a 6 sided die. Every time you hit it, it turns up a number. That number changes all linked enemies to whichever enemy was designated for that number (can change so it isn't so predictable from level to level). So maybe they are set as follows:

1 - Chaingun drone (route W)
2 - Laser drone (route W)
3 - Homing drone (route A)
4 - Standard drone (route A)
5 - Homing drone (route D)
6 - Mine

Or any combination you choose (could be homing launchers, gauss turrets, thwumps, etc.) with any route and/or direction you choose.

So, basically, the robots would move as they currentlly do until the block is hit and then they would change form and/or route (all 6 could be the same kind of enemy with different routes, for instance).

Like I said, not exactly a 1.4 feature, but would be cool to design some levels with something like this.
