How to get a date
By: Obby

(Authors note: The intended reader age is in high school. Anything less is not dating. Its hanging out. Not the same thing. Anything older is in college. If you cant ask any single girl out of the thousands, then you have no hope.)

Getting a date with the opposite sex is an extremely difficult, risky, tricky, and --- Wait a minute. What am I saying? No its not. Having fun is an easy thing to do. If youre going into this looking only to be with the other person, then youre already going in the opposite direction. The goal should be to have fun with the person. If not, then something is wrong. The reason why getting a date is easy is because its completely up to you. You call the shots. Now, unless you think making your own decisions isnt fun, then getting a date is a breeze. So, assuming that youre setting out to have fun, then read on. Remember this: Getting a date is not difficult. The only reason it is seen as being hard is because of the mindset people take, and gossip.

So you may be thinking How is this easy? I cant get up the nerves to ask her, and she might say no. Then people will make fun of me. Let me just say this. The point of dating is to have fun with someone. You know what type of person you would like to hang out with. So use your judgment, and chose someone you know you will have fun with. Be realistic with this, and you just increased your chances hugely. Now, as for her saying no to you. (I might add, if you pick people with similar interests, then she probably wont say no as long as you follow my advice.) Lets take worse case scenario. She says no. She might even have an attitude with it. You know whats going to happen? You know what will happen, despite your worst fears? Nothing. Maybe things are awkward for a week. Maybe someone pokes fun at you a day later, if the girl decides to talk about it. (Lack of judgment on your part, really. But usually, women may say no but will still feel good about the interest taken in them.) But really, people dont care. No one will remember after a week, trust me. Your social status will not fall because someone didnt want to hang out with you. Now, another aspect of her saying no. If she says shes busy, you should believe her. If you think shes lying, so what? You just spend your time angry, and you still are dateless. Its best to just believe her, forget about it, and move on. So now, you may be wondering about how you will get the ovaries-on-the-outside to ask her. Look at it this way. You are being yourself, looking for someone like you. You just want to have fun. You are being yourself. If you cant honestly be yourself without being nervous, then you need to sort some things out before you start dating. Now take all of this and realize that you have nothing to be nervous about asking. After all, its just that. Asking. Its not like you are doing anything. You are asking weather or not you will do something. Being nervous would be like getting nervous thinking about going to bed. Doesnt make sense.

So now you know you will ask her out. Youve picked which gal you could see yourself having the most fun with, and you have nothing to worry about. Unless having fun is worrisome to you. Heres dating gets easy for you. The thing to remember is to be yourself. (Yeah, yeah. Clich. But its true.) If you are going to ask her out by dressing up and looking as cool, then you are setting yourself up for some serious non-fun events. Look at it this way. You will act differently now, and probably for a while. But after that, you will revert to your normal self. You get comfortable around her, and you let things go a bit. She then is with a different guy than she started dating. A guy that doesnt exist. Then, you break it off, and it was all in vain. So be yourself, guys. It all ties back to the girl most similar to you. Ive said it a lot, but its pretty important. So be yourself, and you should have no problems. The next step of this is to look good. This doesnt mean change your look into models you see on TV. No, this means to wear some classy casual clothes. Nothing major, but show that you can and do acknowledge you arent in your house anymore. Also, take a shower and smell nice. Youd think this is a given, but its not. Scary. Anyways, your look should say I can look nice while being myself, and you can be seen with me. Your dress needs to be comfortable, and shouldnt look like you just bought the Old Navy stock. Got the look down? Good. Moving on to the approach.

The goal of the approach is to ask her in a place that is private enough that people wont have the opportunity to butt in or to be part of your conversation, but public enough so that she wont feel that you followed her down some dark alley. A key to this part is to not burst in on her. Make sure she knows you are there before you start in. Making eye contact is best. However, say she is at her locker. You obviously cant make eye contact. If this happens, dont walk up and jump in to asking her. What you should do is from semi-afar, say her name. Say her name as you are walking towards her, so that she will know that someone is visiting her. Another main point is to smile. Smiling can change a conversation more than you know. If she sees you are happy to be talking to her, she will be happy too. Being nervous or serious can change her mood, and severely hamper your chances. So what have we covered? Look nice but natural, dont butt in but rather start slow, and be happy. Now you have acknowledged that you both are in for a conversation, and the mood is set for content and relaxed. Ready? Lets talk.

Now is the conversation. Now is the time that youve thought thoroughly about. Now is the time that you ask the girl that you could have the most fun with, if theyd like to have some fun. See? Easy. Now say hello or some variant. A Whats up? is a no-no. Weird huh? Heres why. Asking her how she is doing in an evasive way puts a lot of conscious and unconscious stress on her. Suddenly, she has to keep the conversation alive. And if she isnt in the best mood, she wont be inclined to start anything. You may wonder how you can start a conversation without a question. Its simple. All you need to do is incorporate what you are interested in. This works in two ways. First, you are handling the conversation without pressuring her. Second, it means that she already knows something about you. You may or may not have heard this before, but women size you up the moment they see you. Looking clean, happy, and interested in her will do wonders for you. Here is a good example of what to say. So I thought that test in History was easier than people said. I was expecting worse. Here, you just stated what goes on in your life, and asked her for her opinion without actually asking her. This thing should be somehow connectible to her. There must be a connection to you two, somehow. This example will work because there is no pressure on her to start a conversation, and she already knows you. Oh yeah, one thing. Dont lie. You absolutely must tell the truth about yourself. Ok, so now youve started a conversation. The pressure is off, and you are in a good mood. Let the conversation flow. Dont force it. If it dies out, and she doesnt want to talk or has to leave, then let it happen. If you think everything went well, you can talk to her again, and it will be easier. But well deal with a conversation that keeps flowing. Sometime, you have got to ask her out. It should be semi-spontaneous. Dont even think of memorizing what you will say. Doing that isnt yourself. Thats not what you want her to see, or even what she wants to see. Plus, conversations almost never go the way you plan. So if you spend time memorizing, youll be left in the dust should she say something different from what you are expecting. But it cant be so spontaneous that you blurt it out no matter who is talking. Some good examples of dates are movies, miniature golf, hanging out at a park or mall, or even after school at school. One thing to remember about going to movies is that of course, you pay. As for choosing a movie, dont be totally submissive. Put out what you think would be fun to see, but let her ultimately choose. Dont stand around saying I dont know, you chose.. Doesnt work pal. Remember: ovaries-on-the-outside. Anyways, just ask her if shed like to hang out. The Comebacks look funny. I think Ill see it this weekend. Would you want to come? Something along those lines, and youre set. Should she be in a good mood and similar interests, she will agree as long as she isn't busy.

So now you have your date. Go out, have fun, and incorporate what you just learned into the date. One last point is to not be too serious. Dont consider that you are dating until at least the third time you two were together alone. And for those that arent driving, heres a bit of advice. Dont have your mother take the both of you. Its only acceptable to be dropped off by your mother if there is no other possible way. What is better is to have your father take you, or a sibling. Really, anyone but your mother. This isnt because mothers hurt your image. Nah. If the girl is that shallow, you shouldnt have chosen her. Rather, its on a subconscious and social level. First, mothers normally dont handle this situation well. They will probably be nice and all, but they arent there to be nice. They are there to get you from one point to another with courtesy. Thats why fathers work well. They know that youre trying to have fun, and will probably stay out. Even when they are chatty, its another guy talking. The girl wont have to worry about her dates mother talking all the time.

So yeah, its pretty straight forward. If you do these things, I personally guarantee you will be successful.

'Aight, so here's a quick idea of what to do...

Kissing is generally, nay always, a private affair. That being said, you should be in a place where you are both having a good, relaxed, time. The idea of starting a kiss is that it should be somewhat spontaneous. You should know that it will happen, but not when. Now, jumping into it is a mistake. You must be somewhat close to start. Say you are hanging out at one of your houses, watching a movie. You're sitting together, but not really close. You know the evening is going well, and you can feel something special. (A big point is to know if she is interested in you too. I'll assume you know how to read subtle nuances in her behavior, but if you need help with that, just say so.) So what to do is lean over and say something funny, or along those lines. Just something to get you two together, but not right next to each other. Just a second or two. That's all. After that, assuming the chica feels the same for you, it's a breeze.

As a aside, girls have come up to me in the past and said that they liked me--and I didn't like them in return. I've systematically made them all feel bad, by not handling the situation properly; acting passively-aggressive or being unclear about what I mean.

It's actually more difficult to let someone down than to be let down yourself. In the end, the person doing the rejecting will feel more awkward than the one rejected.



What to do on a date? I think Obby covered it, if not pleonastically: Act naturally. He said that you should keep the focus away from yourself--I disagree. If you're naturally narcissistic, don't pretend otherwise. Sure, make certain that she's having as good a time as you are, but don't obscure your personality traits to give a good impression. If she doesn't like you for who you are, she'll find out eventually.

(This is assuming that you're dating and not bar trolling for a one-nighter. If you're trolling, make damn certain you pretend to be someone else; she's not going to know you long enough to care and you need to exemplify your most desirable traits within a small window.)

Do something you know that you'll both enjoy. Again, don't be false about yourself. Do something you know that you'll enjoy; do something you figure she'll enjoy. The only real difficulty here is figuring out which activity resides in the union of those two sets. If you do something that you don't enjoy, she'll know--try as you might, she'll find out.

Don't date to impress, date to connect and you'll do fine.
