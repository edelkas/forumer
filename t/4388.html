Well, you can skip levels somehow, in two ways.

The first way is called Practice mode. Go to Configure and switch practice mode to on. The bad thing, though, is that you can't submit your highscores you get on the levels you've unlocked while playing in that mode. I avoid practice mode.

There's a better way, anyway! Go to Highscores and select an episode that you've played. Click on one of the levels in that episoide and you can start playing that level! Without going through the entire episode!

There you go. I hope this helps!
