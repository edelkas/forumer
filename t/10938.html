As the other thread died, I felt like starting up a new questionswap thread, as I came across a really amazing featured question. It's not one of mine, but it is amazing.

Quote
Q. If you could do everything in your life over again and you were forced to change one decision, what would it be and why?


Canada


A. Oh man. If I could go back and time and change one thing about my life?

Pull up a chair.

I would tell the woman I met in Japan that I loved her. I wouldn't have just said "See you later. Take care." and smiled. I would have gotten her address and number. I would have told her that she was everything I wanted her to be, and that the week we spent together was the most magical week of my life. I wouldn't be sitting here in my apartment in New York, dredding going back to the office tomorrow and having to deal with idiots trading their lives away for the stocks I sell. I would be living in harmony with her in Kyoto.

We met in a bar, where I was drinking away my sorrow because a deal had fallen through the floor and caused me a good amount of money. I had thought my life was over, but she quickly showed me there was more to life than the money I was earning. She showed me the ocean, she showed me youth, she showed me love. And I forgot all that when I boarded that train and left her forever. When I damned myself to this "American Dream." All this money doesn't make me happy anymore. The only thing that makes me happy anymore is her. Jesus Christ, I'm crying now. I love her so much. Thank you, friend. Thank you for reminding me of why I live. So I can find her again. I need to go back to Japan. THank you so much, you don't know how much you've changed my life right now. You have no idea what you have done for me.


United States
