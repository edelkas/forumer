The 1337 vs. The Mob

By now, I've enough experience in hosting a contest to do a good job with it. I've done the Sig of the Year, Symmetry, and RBTT is going on. I think I know what I'm doing. (To a certain degree)

This is a 10 round elimination competition in which two teams compete against each other in a test of demoing. There will be a group called The 1337. The rest of the participants will be part of The Mob. At the beginning, The Mob will have 7+ people. The 1337 will always have 3 people. In each round, both The 1337 and The mob will be faced with demo challenges. But since The 1337 has fewer people than The Mob, they will have a handicap.

For each round, 5 maps will be presented to the two teams. The maps may be from NUMA or any map pack. If the 1337 have faster demos on ANY two maps, they win the round. If not, The Mob wins the round. The losing team loses a member as punishment. (All demos are frame demos)

At the conclusion of each round, the winning team will be announced as quickly as possible. Since this is a team-based tournament, no one person can be held responsible for a loss (usually). Therefore, we have decided to let the losing team decide which one of their team will leave them. A majority vote will be taken. Each member of the losing team will select 1 (one) person to eliminate. They must provide some reasons. The 2 people with the highest votes will be put on a poll. The public as well as the participants will vote out one of them. The survivor returns to The Mob. If The 1337 lose, then all three will be put on a poll. The one with the most votes will be out. The remaining 2 will join The Mob. The new 1337 members will be selected by The Mob when The 1337 lose. Only those who have winning scores will be eligible for 1337 Membership.

I have decided to have a round 0. In round zero, those who do not respond or do not place in the top 20 will be eliminated. So basically, I'm just telling you up front that there will be people who drop out. I'm prepared.
