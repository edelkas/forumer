#196210 3 2006-06-29T07:23:46Z Atilla
Ned is part of N v1.4. Just hit ~ or \ at the main menu, as it says in the readme. If you have an international keyboard, this may not work. Try @ or . If that fails, well... just try every key until you find it. Also, make sure you have capslock on when testing a level - it runs in frame-by-frame otherwise.

If you don't HAVE the readme, editor manual or userlevels (all of which are text files, and should come in the same folder as N), go download N from the "downloads" section of the Metanet Software homepage. Click on the link or the banner at the top of the forums to get there.
