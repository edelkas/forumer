As far as I know this hasn't been discovered.

dc443 showed me his demo of a triple side bounce block jump, shown here. After a few minutes I managed it myself. Then I started experimenting, and found that in frame by frame, quadruple side bounce block jumps were possible. Finally, I tried a standard triple bounce block jump and pulled it off after a little while, without frame by frame.

It's amazing that this hasn't occurred to anyone until now. Check out the demos for the quadruple side and the standard triple below.
