#1366 1 2004-11-15T10:37:18Z Arachnid
Hard on the heels of logins, editing and deleting, I've just added support for rating maps to NUMA.
New features of note:
  • You can now rate maps - select a rating and click 'rate' from the individual map page.
  • There's a 'Top Rated' item on the left menu, so you can view the top maps overall and in each category
  • Each map now has an indicator of its rating - 0 to 5 ninjas, in 0.5 ninja increments.
  • A map's rating only shows up if it has 5 or more votes. This is to prevent maps with only one or two high votes topping the 'charts'.
Feeback is appreciated.
