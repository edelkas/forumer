The Love Sessions may not be the best maps I've ever made, but they have a special place in my life. In many ways it marks a new beginning for me - not as a mapper, but as a person.

The maps were created on a purely stream-of-consciousness style. I wanted a slightly abrasive finish to the whole thing, and I tried not to delete anything at all unless it was blatantly unplayable or disappointing. You could say I was experimenting, playing with my sense of style a little.

A lot of the maps were also quickly produced, and in a semi-distracted mindset where my attention may not have been fully on the level. This was intentional and a desired part of the process. That said, on many levels forethought and planning was involved, and some ideas were thought up while not playing N.

Don't forget the Love Sessions competition - complete the 'easy'-mode levels as fast as you can in userlevels mode!
