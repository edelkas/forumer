In case you don't have an account, I'll tell how you get one. It's really easy.

Just go to the Configure section in N, and look in the bottom right corner. You should see some spaces where you can type things. That would be the place where you can make an account for N. With this account, not only can you submit maps to NUMA, but you can also get your name on a position in the highscores, if you get one.
