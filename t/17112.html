This is a common bug. The most likely cause are NaN's (glitches) in the level code. To fix, just go through the level code and replace all instances of 'NaN' with a number, eg. 276. Then, just delete all the items that appear in new spots, and replace them where they should go.

That's a complicated description, hopefully you'll be able to understand.
