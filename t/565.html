-Deadline is Tuesday, July 20th 2004 at 6PM GMT.

Rating:
-The maps will be rated on the following criteria:
-0-10 points for Looks&Design. The better the map looks and appeals, the more points you get.
-0-10 points for Difficulty. Was it too easy? Frustrating? Just right. This score will tell.
-0-10 points for Creativity. The more original and clever your ideas are, the better.
-0-15 points for Fun&Replayability. Is the map enjoyable? Is there any reason to play it again?
-0-5 points for Other. Is there anything we liked about the map, but that doesn't fit the other categories, you will get points here.
-There will be 3 Judges. Me, Brickman and MacrossDreams, that is if they agree to do the burrito. If not, I'll have to look for someone else. If any of the 3 judges decides to enter the contest, a support-judge will do the 3rd rating, I suggest Nexus for this burrito. The 3 Overall scores will be added for a possible total amount of 150 Points.
-Judgement will start as soon as the map is posted in this topic. We don't want to have to do all at the same time after the deadline.

Have fun and good luck.
