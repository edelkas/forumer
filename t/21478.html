#19181 1 2008-02-14T23:40:09Z usaswim
Every graphic designer has done something in their lives that really sparked their imagination and caused them to be interested in what they do.

I remember the first time I ever touched anything related to graphics. I was creating a timeline for a subject, and I wanted to make a circle. Naturally, I could've used Paint, but I found it very inconvenient. I had this software that came with my Dell called Corel Paint Shop Pro. I opened it up and quickly found the shortcut to make a circle. After that, I fooled around with it and I was awed at the things I could make.

It wasn't until I talked to xdude when I found out about Photoshop. I tried it out, and of course, I was confused and unknowing at that time. So, ever since then, I've been using Photoshop. (It was also the time when I learned about torrents and keygens )

So now, it's almost a year since that time, and I'm amazed at how far I've come. I am especially grateful at all the awesome graphic designers here that helped me be where I am today. If I had never been to the Metanet Forums, I would've probably never have pursued graphic designing much further and I would just a normal kid at my school. Since I have started graphic designing, all my teachers have been very impressed at my work on projects, and my friends are all jealous. I'm even getting into web design and CSS, and I've found sorta a "job" in which I can make money out of it.

I'm also erecting a plan to revamp my school's website, which is utter crap in my opinion. A year ago, I wouldn't care, because I had absolutely no taste back then. Now, I look at everything critically.

So, this is one last thanks to the Metanet community, and a special thanks to Harley, who is awesome at everything
