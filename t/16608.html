#311310 6 2007-04-08T16:53:18Z kkstrong0
To Watch Demos

1. Open the game
2. Press "\"
3. In the top box, enter the desired map data
4. Press "L"
5. In the bottom box, enter the demo replay data
6. Press "3"
7. Press "Q"
8. Press "P"
9. Make Sure Caps is ON

To Make Demos

1. Open the game
2. Press "\"
3. In the top box, enter the desired map data
4. Press "L"
5. Press "R"
6. Press "W"
7. Press "1"
8. Press "P"
9. Make Sure Caps is ON
10. Repeat until you have the desired demo
11. Submit to NUMA with a comment and a rating (hehe!)
