#254015 3 2006-11-13T17:33:55Z nevermore
No, no, the reason the thwump doesn't move here is because it's sandwiched inbetween two [5] tiles. The thwump treats the 5 tiles as filled tiles, because all thwump and drone collisions assume that an occupied tile is a full tile. Thus, the whole row which the thwumps are in is rendered to be filled in spaces, meaning it's just like putting the thwump in the middle of a wall.
