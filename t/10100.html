#182245 15 2006-05-30T12:18:38Z Atilla
Oh dear. Are my questions too difficult?

Anyway, since there appear to only be three people in this thread, I might as well post answers...


1. Ning the Uncompassionate, Discworld's resident Ming the Merciless wannabe ("I will return!"). He's only mentioned once in The Last Hero, so it's not suprising that you don't remember it. Still... "I will revert to this vicinity" sort of sticks in the mind.

2.Mightily-Praiseworthy-Are-Ye-Who-Exalteth-Om Oats and Visit-The-Unbeliever-With-Explanatory-Pamphlets. And his friend is indeed Smite-The-Unbeliever-With-Cunning-Arguments.

3. The Almighty Nuggan (for a given value of "Almighty", of course). He also likes paperclips, the right thing in the correct place in small desktop stationary sets, and the country of Borogravia. At least, he did until his recent disappearance.

4. The Great God Orm, who is cursed with a name which is unfortunately similar to the much more important Great God Om. It was in Pyramids, of course, when one the two remaining followers of Orm attempted to sacrifice a goat in the Assasins' Guild dormatory.

5. I was thinking more along the lines of his official UU titles, such as the Professor of Cruel and Unusual Geography, Lecturer in Slood Dynamics, and the Fretwork Teacher. But yes, he is also known as all those. Though I believe it's technically "The Hero With A Thousand Retreating Backs".
