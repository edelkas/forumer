You could probably use much of the code you already have for N with this idea, but I think it could be fun.

I'm thinking multiplayer mainly to reduce or eliminate the complexity of the AI. But basically each person would have a "ninja" (or whatever their toon would be called) and do all the stuff N can do. You can place drones of various types that kill any player they touch or fire on and hit. Very much like N.

But... Each player has a ball that they can shoot that will bounce around until the player shoots again (making the first disappear), or it hits a drone, or it hits the other player. A player can hit their own ball to send it off in another direction. The ball always fires in the direction the player is facing and at the velocity the player is moving (plus a little bit so they can't catch it).

If you are hit by another player's ball then that opposing player gains a point. If you get hit by a drone or die through any means except by another player's ball, then you lose a point. Kinda like scoring happens in Doom. In any case, once you "die" you restart in your safe zone. Once you leave the safe zone you are a free target. You cannot fire from within your safe zone. You cannot pass through other player's safe zones nor can your ball pass through them.

You set the game up to play to a certain number of points or a set amount of time.

Anyway, thoughts? I think it would be very fun.
