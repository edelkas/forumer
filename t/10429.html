#187202 2 2006-06-10T13:42:35Z Ngaged
To play the map, you must...

1. Go to debug mode.
2. Press L to load your map data.
3. Press P to Play!

Pretty easy, eh? Unless I misunderstood you and you actually don't know how to load and save and stuff...might as well tell you that, too.

To save:
1. Go to debug mode.
2. Copy all of the data in the level data box (the upper box).
3. Paste it somewhere in a file, like a .txt, or a word document.
4. Save the data!

To load:
1. Copy all the level data from your file.
2. Go to debug mode.
3. Get rid of everything in the upper box.
4. Replace it with your level data.
5. Press L to load the map.

Hope I've got it covered for ye.
