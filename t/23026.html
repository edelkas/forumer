The goal of this project is very similar to all previous Metanet Image Worms, except with one restriction. Every image must be an unaltered tileset created in N. Each tileset must be an original. As usual, do your best to allow each tileset to flow from the previous one.

Remember, the left and right borders of each tileset will be cut off in the final product. Please keep this in mind while you submit your tileset. Please make sure to attach the leveldata of your tileset onto your post instead of just using a hosting site like pastebin.com.

Tilesets will be done in order of signups on this thread. I will make the first image, and Evil_Bob will make the second (he will be assisting me throughout the process). I will allow a week or two for signups, so spread the word.
