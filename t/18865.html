[wayofftopic]Oh god! I can't take it anymore. I need the forum. I only made it a day, and I just can't stay away. I was just a bit mad in the Leaver's Thread. [/wayofftopic]

Anyway, Space Cowboy is a pretty good game. the game basically has you flying a ship and battling in the air. There is also a tank that can transform. It's your average MMORPG really, except the flying part...that...and you have to go to war. There are two nations, each with a Leader. The Leader is an actual guy, and you can elect people to be leaders. The leaders usually discuss with each other (There are 2 leaders. 1 for each nation) on when to declare war, and, or, when to make peace. In the wars, you have to fight to the death, when you die, you get teleported somewhere else. It's a great game.

Also, That Cabal Online game looks amazing. The problem...You have to be European to play the game, and I am not European, so that's a bummer.
