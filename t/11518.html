The password is undisclosed until you PM or IRC AzMiLion or I.

Come along to Metaville and create whatever you like using unlimited Lego bricks. If you play Return to Blockland, (thread here,) then there's no excuse to avoid this server. You must finish off your buildings on one base before moving on to a new base.
You cannot build anything obscene.

Otherwise, do what you like. It's really great fun! If you help to build this city, you can be part of something great.

If you don't have Blockland you can download it at the Return to Blockland website. It would be a good idea to look at this thread as well.

See you in Metaville!
