#235405 3 2006-09-30T05:31:45Z Atilla
1. Jump into the bit with 3 pieces of gold just left of the start.
2. There's a drone going clockwise around the next platform on the left. Follow close behind it to the switch above the thumps, then quickly turn around and head back to the bit with 3 gold. Jump over the drone coming from the right.
3. Wait for the drones just to your left to get out of the way. Run left to the next switch.
4. Wait for the drone going clockwise around the leftmost platform to go past. Follow it to the exit switch. As soon as you get it, jump back up to avoid the thwump and head right.
5. Walk to the right across the top of the level. Watch out for drones, though they shouldn't give you much trouble.
6. Wait for any drones in the way to move, then drop down the rightmost shaft and run to the exit.
