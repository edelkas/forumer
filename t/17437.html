The competitors will judge other competitors' episodes, and PM them to me (to avoid vengeful biases). I will decide when to end the competition, but I shall give at least 5 days notice.

Clear rules:

1-Each competitor shall make, and submit in this thread, a single episode of six levels, in any way they wish

2-Each competitor shall then will rate all other competitors' maps, on a sytem of N/15 for each map and N/10 for how well the maps work together as an episode, giving the total for each episode as a percentage. The average percentage given out must be 50%.

3-Each competitor shall then wait for me to end the competition, then submit their ratings for all other competitors' episodes to me via PMs

4-I shall post the overall results, ranked by greatest mean percentage in a comment on this thread. I will also attach the individual ratings

5-The Winners' Episodes, mine and also an episode of six collaberative maps will form the first map pack from the group.

I hope that something will take off from this
