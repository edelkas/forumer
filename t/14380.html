Tileset Usage Contest:

Goals:
The goal of this contest is to see who can make the best map while everyone is using the same tileset.

Rules:
-First five tilesets must be submitted
-Each tileset must be submitted to NUMA categorized under tileset with a link to this topic
-For each tileset the judges will decide whether or not it is good enough for the competition
-Only one tileset may be submitted per person

-For each tileset five maps must be made
-You may start submitting maps as soon as the first tileset is posted (though you may want to wait for the tileset you want)
-Each map must be submitted to NUMA with a link to this topic
-The Judges will judge all the maps based on the rubric later in this post
-Only one map may be submitted per person (so choose your tileset wisely)
-No tweaking of the tileset unless you are given permission by its author
-The map must be an action, race, or puzzle map (no DDA's)

Rubric:
_/10 Gameplay
_/10 Fun
_/10 Tileset Usage

Deadline:
January 6th (judging begins January 7th)

Prizes:
1st Place- ded and a signature tag
2nd Place- signature tag
3rd Place- signature tag
