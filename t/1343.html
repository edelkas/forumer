For completion, I generally do a couple things:

First, I seperate the demos that completed from those that didn't, (Duh, hehe)

Second, I go over the demos to see if they got all switches, all gold, all that fun stuff.

Third, I go over the demos again to see who completed it the fastest.

Fourth, I go over the demo to see how exciting things were, and how much the run impressed me.

Fifth, I go over the demos to see if one person was more creative than another in completing said demo.

Lastly, I take a look at some subleties, ranging from things such as how often a player hesitates throughout a map to how many missiles that player dodged, to how many sandwiches that player ate in a given sitting.

But more importantly than all that, I go on which demo I think did a better job of doing what needed to be done.
