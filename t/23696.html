L4byrinth (Area Trigger)
http://nmaps.net/136604

Restart NReality (a mod of N, download @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Area Trigger

Area Trigger:
Placed after the playerMod "|", i.e. the sixth "|"
||||||^x0.y0:x1.y1^startTriggersID(separated by "."),stopTriggersID(separated by "."),specialOnTriggersID(separated by "."),specialOffTriggersID(separated by ".")

(x0,y0) and (x1,y1) formed the rectangle area that trigger things

you can put multiple area triggers, separated by ";"

e.g. to make the ninja trigger the greedy drone when enter the top left area in this map and stop it when the ninja back to center cross:
!6^372,276,2,0,141,0^,,-10,1,5.6^,9,0,4||||||!5^250.150:384.286;!5^250.286:540.314^,5;!5^384.150:410.430^,6
