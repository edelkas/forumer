#13937 1 2007-02-08T14:28:43Z TornadoTK
Quadradius is a turn based board game similar to Checkers. You move your pieces around the board, one square a time, trying to jump on (not over) your opponents pieces. However, this is where the similarities end.

Power-ups spawn on the board, and completely devastate your pieces, your opponents pieces, and the game board. It's like Chess, if Chess had random items. Strategy is involved, and you must be prepared for any random event to happen.

Give it a shot, it's actually really fun.
