#251124 17 2006-11-07T08:32:28Z Atilla
It's fine the way it is. The whole point of test levels is that they don't count towards ratings. The test category is a flag which says "This map is experimental or something, and is not indicative of the author's true ability". It is also used to store contest maps, which often have wierd limitations which make comparison to normal maps inaccurate. Incomplete levels don't count because, well... they're incomplete. If people like your tilesets, you will be recognised through the maps they make using said tilesets. Or you can move them to N Art.
