Left & Right (OneWay On/Off + Rotate)
http://nmaps.net/133401

Restart NReality (a mod of N, download @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of one way platform on/off + rotation

To work with one way platform on/off & rotation, you need to use special triggers (ref: http://nmaps.net/132939 )

oneway special off : make the one way disappear

oneway special on:
case 1: if the one way has disappeared, it will reappear
case 2: if the one way is not disappeared, then it will rotate 90 degree

In this map you can see how it works, and when you touched the 2nd door trigger, the first oneway disappear, and when you touched the 3rd door trigger, the first oneway reappear again.
