#465109 4 2008-08-28T17:19:11Z 7!
To find out what hardware you have, go to Control Panel, System Properties, Hardware tab, Device Manager. Basically you'll need drivers for 3 things: Video card, network card, and sound card. Here's mine as an example. Download the drivers from the manufacturer's site, like smartalco said. You can download them from your computer manufacturer, (Dell, Compaq, etc.) site or from the component manufacturer's site. Ex. If I had to install video card drivers, I'd go to nVidia's site.

I highly, highly, highly recommend you download the driver installers somewhere (friend with internet / public library nearby?) and then transfer them to your computer and run them.
