#6396 1 2006-01-23T17:54:40Z sweep
Many websites, such as last.fm and del.icio.us use a lovely tagging system. I think NUMA should have the same. Basically, people should be able to apply tags to maps. These tags could be "loopy", "fast", "puzzling jumper", "innovative" or anything you thought applied to the map. This would have two benefits. Firstly, people could search for maps that have certain tags. If I wanted, for example, to see some keep rocket alive DDAs I could search for maps with KRADDA in the tags. This would turn up all the KRADDA. I could also search for something like "challenging" or "complex".

Finally, this could improve the suggestions so much. Maps could be compared by tags. If you tended to favourite lots of maps with lasers in, the tags of these maps would include the word "laser" and as a result more maps with the tag "laser" would turn up in your suggestions.

Any thoughts?
