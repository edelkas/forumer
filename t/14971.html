#280430 10 2007-01-03T01:59:49Z Kablizzy
Okay, it looks like your problem stems from simple timing issues. What I suggest you do to get better is to open a blank map, and put a single gauss in it, and dodge it for as long as you can. At some point, you'll figure out its pattern, and how the gauss fires, and the timing. Do the same with rockets, drones, and so on, and then try it with certain tiles that you're having a problem with.

Just open up a map, put in a smattering of tiles that you're having problems with, and try all sorts of jumps and button combinations, and such.

Also, I put a demo in the map thread for you to look at. It's a pretty crappy demo, but it shows a bit more of what you can do with the map. Take special note of how I was dodging the gauss.

Wolf, you just have to get a feel for the way N physics works. Don't feel bad or anything about your skill level - a fair number of people have been playing N for *years.* A lot of people probably have 100 thousand deaths. But don't let that intimidate you either, you can get fairly good pretty fast once you get the basics, like where N will stop sliding or how high he will jump.

when N is facing a wall, if he is just touching it, and not sliding on it, he jumps slightly higher.

you jump off perpendicular to a slanted surface, except when you hold a direction key towards the direction it's higher in. you will go higher if you're moving.

don't be afraid to pause between jumps. you can get faster later. and don't feel bad about dying. there's a reason there are unlimited lives. some people have taken a long time to beat a userlevels set. especially me.
