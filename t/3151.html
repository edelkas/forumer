#65648 4 2005-05-14T07:20:17Z LittleViking
We don't have hover-vehicles because of the cost-benefit ratio. Hovering 4-6 ft. off the ground offers almost no benefit over a traditional ground-based vehicle.

The problem, of course, is making it work. Your idea is huge thrusters. Tanks are rather heavy; the thrusters would have to be extremely powerful, wasting tons of power and fuel that could go to better causes, like traveling longer distances.

You also have to think about the recoil from a tank turret; there's a reason helicopter's don't fire tank shells (they would lose control and crash, if you don't know).

Ground-based battlecruisers are completely unneccessary. The whole point of boats is simulating land, on the water. We have artillery guns for land; we have guided missiles; we have infantry. Floating battlecruisers serve no purpose.

Also, hovertanks wouldn't be able to easily take down large ships. Ships are made to not sink; they take a lot before they'll go down. And either way, warships have stronger guns with more ammunition than a tank would anyway.

So there you go. Hovercraft just aren't practical. Name something a hovercraft could do well, and I'll tell you that there's something that already exists that'll do it better for less money.
