Solo Games, my non-existent virtual company consisting of just me , has produced three new titles for you to enjoy for free! Check them out and let me know what you think of them.

NOTE: Download speeds can be a little slow. I'm working on getting a faster download site like Sourceforge or something. You can download all the games at this link, but it is 7.0MB (Not bad for 3 games IMHO): All Games

=================TOGGLES===============
Summary: Toggles might be the only popular game here, considering it has an online high score table. Many people don't get the cool tricks you can do to get highscores, so make sure to take a look at the in-game help menu and read up on the scoring section. I challenge anyone to beat my current highscore.
Development: I developed Toggles from scratch using C++ (although one source file goes quite the C route) and is about 10,000 lines of code. The download comes with the source code.

Windows Binary: Toggles
Linux Source: (There are some known problems with the linux version) Toggles Linux source

=================Mymaze=================
Summary: Accidentally walking into a seemingly endless maze, our nameless hero has to surpass many challenges and puzzles to eventually regain his freedom. There are 50 challenging levels with 3 secret levels. The help includes a walkthrough for the first 20 levels.
Development: Mymaze was made using the Game Maker program. It happens to be my third GM project and there's quite a bit of content packed in. I imagine 5-6 hours of gameplay.


Windows Binary: Mymaze

=================1945====================
Summary: There are many clones of the classic vertical scroller
game, 1945, this is just my rendition. There are 8 challenging levels with a short storyline. You can use joysticks to control the ship and if you have two, you can play two player! Featuring music from Starfox.
Development: This was my second game made with the Game Maker program, thus, I wasn't as proficient in it as I was when making Mymaze. Still, I think game deserves a little play time. It's one that I still pick up and try to beat my previous high score.

Quote: JeCa, 2006-10-24T19:23:25Z
I would like a bit more info about what exactly you do in the different games.
Sure!

Toggles: The goal is to get all the toggles under the water, but to make it to the next level, you only have to get a certain percent based on the bar below. You are rewarded for speed AND consistency. For example, getting 10 Toggles under water without bringing one up in under three seconds is the first combo. Getting 60+ in under three seconds is the fourth and final combo. Combos give you X amount of points times whatever level you're in. The fourth combo gives you 1000 = X. Thus, getting the fourth combo in level 10 would be worth 10,000 points! Thus, maybe it's a good idea to add rows and columns as frequently as you can.

Mymaze: This a lot like the old Adventures of Lolo games. You have to solve some pretty challenging puzzles. Can't really think of much more to this game.

1945: A basic vertical scroller, arcade-like game. You shoot down enemies and try to make it through all 8 levels. From start to finish, it takes about 12 minutes to complete.

If you want more, let me know.
