#361093 24 2007-08-31T06:04:02Z tktktk
In placing gold, remember risk-reward ratio. (Ooh, alliteration combo x 4!)

That is, the amount of gold placed in a given area should generally be directly proportional to the amount of danger/challenge. In episodic maps, the amount of time consumed while attaining that gold is also a factor.
