#60022 2 2005-04-14T19:16:33Z nevermore
It's because of lag. Certain enemies and objects change the speed of other things in the level, and sometimes throw them off. I'd say place your enemies first, so you can anticipate those mistakes.
#60032 3 2005-04-14T20:50:19Z brickman
As a general rule, never use a chaingun drone in a DDA. It has a 95% chance of making your level sometimes fail. On the other hand, most others can be usually relied upon to perform the same way every time (but will affect each other when you place them), so put as many of them as you want. And if you use the "enemies first" philosophy you will be interested in learning that thwumps never affect anything or are affected by anything, so you don't have to do them first.
