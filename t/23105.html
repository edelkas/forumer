Conceptual Map Contest:Post-Final

Ok, so this is my first contest on metanet, and I had an idea to compile all the new concepts in a competition.

In case your wondering:


Conceptual
Quote
1. a general notion or idea; conception.
2. an idea of something formed by mentally combining all its characteristics or particulars; a construct.
3. a directly conceived or intuited object of thought.

Basically, make a map with:
  1. A concept that is new and never used before.
  2. A concept that has not been used much before.
  3. Or a concept that isn't new, but is different than the original concept in some way.
[/spoiler]

Examples of old concepts would be:
Door-Eerie's
Disentangle
Blackout

Spoiler: Standards
  • This contest is separated into rounds. A certain amount of people will be eliminated depending on how many people join.
  • Maps will have themes. So be ready. I am open for suggestions.
  • Maps may be submitted to NUMA or posted in this thread. If submitted on NUMA, post a link to it in this thread.
  • No NReality.
