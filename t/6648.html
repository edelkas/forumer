02-0: Rotate (118.600) - Loved it. I wish I could make maps like this.

02-1: Mousebot (81.000) - Very odd flavor and dynamic, reminds me of one of your older maps, namely, Artificial Intelligence, except compact. In either case, great stuff.

03-0: Rocketman (120.700) - Very Slow-paced map, but I like it. I usually don't like jumper maps, but this one stands out, akin to that Metanet map. You know. Yeah, you know the one. *clone*

03-2: Boop (87.375) - Now here is a map that I would love if it didn't look as empty as it did. If you hadn't already noticed, I have a fetish, of sorts, for filling empty maps. This suffers from that syndrome. Perhaps putting a mine on each of the four sides of the blocks, or perhaps some floor guards down at the bottom, to discourage side-running the terminal switch. perhaps a small design (5-tile, ended with quarter-slopes) at the top mimicking that on the bottom would help, aesthetically. Gameplay-wise, it's great.

04-0: Ominous (86.500) - It's on NUMA! I thoroughly enjoyed this level, your minefield placement, superb as usual.

04-2: Sharp (-----) - Didn't have time to beat it, but I got the gist. I absolutely loved how the drones got to the top at the same time as I did. It really made the level work. The concept is magnificent, too.

04-3: Warm! (77.350) - That's the way, uh-huh, uh-huh, I like it. Uh-huh. Uh-huh. My least favorite thusfar, but yet still better than 54 of Blizzy's maps.
Seriously, though, it's a bit odd to traverse, but that gives the map its charm. The rocket/floor guard combo really works to bring out the flavor in the roast Ninja.

06-0: Pomegranates in the Moonlight (81.050) - Brilliantly simple. I love how, as the chaingun moves, it gets more of a shot over the area, and poses more of a threat as the map goes along. It's maps like these that make me proud to be a part of Legacy.

06-1: barrier (81.975) - I was surprised at how easily this map was beaten. I usually have a hell of a time with chaingun maps, but this one really did it for me.

07-4: Squib (66.975) - Groovy stuff. One of your better maps.

08-0: Industry (86.150) - You know I love this map, that rocket is persistent, but the tileset is amazing.

08-1: Rune of Control (92.750) - Loved it. Completion was easy, all-gold was hard. Great stuff.

09-0: Treacherous (-----) - Reminds me of my 049, except this one's, y'know, good. Again, didn't have time to beat it, but I got most of the way.

09-3: Organized (-----) - Hard, hard level. And that one gold that's too close to the mine looks off. I'd consider moving the mine down in line with the rest of the gold, and standardizing that. Beyond that, it's an LV Minefield map, how can you hate it?

10-0: Simple (86.575) - Great map. You know how we do.

11-1: Take your Time (75.500) - Brilliant. Love it.

12-0: Eh (80.825) - While I was playing through this map, it hit me, "When am I going to get to the maps he hates?"

14-0: g'yarr! (82.425) - The drones flooding in was a great idea, and they were timed just right. Probably my least favorite of your levels thusfar, but still by no means anywhere in the realm of cuttable.

15-2: Shuffle (-----) - The first time I made it across the pond, so to speak, it was a fluke. Haven't been able to do so since. Great concept, it's just stumping the hell out of me.

15-4: Desolation (-----) - You stole my name, you name-thief! Anyway, I totally didn't get to record my awesome kick-flip off the bottom side of that top platform, but suffice to say that I didn't know that you could kick off of those, and it was badass. #130443 3 2006-01-07T09:36:51Z tktktk
Grr... I commented on all of these and then it was all deleted when my post didn't get through. Here we go. Fantastic stuff overall.

2-0: Excellent small map. Seems like it should be generic, but there's just something special about it. The launchpads are a nice quirky touch and add to the flow.

2-1: Obviously a remake of Artificial Intelligence, and it works surprisingly well in the limited space. The thwumps were very unthreatening though.

3-0: Odd name, I thought. It's a very simple jump puzzler, and there was just enough substance for me to enjoy it.

3-2: This is what I'd call a "Metanet cookie cutter map". Usually that would be a bad thing, but this happens to play very well.

4-0: This should be taken out since it's been submitted to Numa. Great mine/bounceblock field, but I was kind of weirded out by the stuff on the bottom.

4-2: The inside was interesting (I'm glad you made it asymmetrical), but I got a kick out of just timing the run up one side under the drone.

4-3: This one was just all right. The rocket/floorguard combo was nice, but I feel like I've played this type of thing before.

6-0: Great chaingun bunker map. There are plenty of safe places where you can duck down at any time. The open spaces are harder to traverse, but never overly so.

6-1: I didn't care all that much for the structure and pattern of it, but it was still pretty good. The chainguns did their job well.

7-4: Reminds me of a more minimalistic maximo map (woo alliteration). The drones applied plenty of pressure, but I always had a chance to slip away in time.

8-0: The way this is set up is brilliant. I love unlocking all the gold and then cruising right on through the bottom at the end.

8-1: The way you sorted the drones to move in two separate rows on the bottom was brilliant. Pretty decent overall.

9-0: Good tough map. Getting back up forced me to make some very difficult maneuvering. Kind of reminds me of my map Ka.

9-3: It was interesting working with the seeker drones on the way back from getting the exit switch. I don't like the way the minefield looks, but it plays well.

10-0: Ah. Two routes, one easy/boring, one harder/rewarding. I like it, and it works well as a column starter.

11-1: This was the only one I actually disliked. I think it would be a little better if you put some gold in between the mines or something.

12-0: Very open and fun. My favorite part of this map was getting to the top section using the side thwumps rather than the middle one.

14-0: I love the way that first drone is timed to be by the exit switch right about when you get there. It makes for some cool situations.

15-2: By far my favorite of this bunch. The concept of keeping the drones out of the way so you don't slam into them making that big jump is fantastic. The difficulty is tough but well-balanced. Great job.

15-4: You get some real style points for this one. The platforms were laid out nicely, and the bottom provided a cool alternate route. The top rooms were classic LV style.
