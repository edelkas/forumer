This gave me an idea. An idea so great that my head would explode if I even began to know what I was talking about.

Anyway, the concept is simple. For future versions (N2 and beyond), a piece of 'gold' (Red gold, blue gold, whatever) that grants temporary invincibility. I think that this would function on a number of levels, but most particularly, it would open new *types* of levels to consideration. For instance:

[image lost: post-16-1154372392.png]

In otherwise impossible circumstances, the Ninja could do things that he couldn't do. Of course, making it a power-up would likely open the door for other types of powerups, as well, but in this instance, I believe, it would open the door to more mapmaking ideas and more complex types of levels. Getting a switch can only be implemented in so many ways. =D

Of course, Invincibility powerups have been suggested before, but the idea stemmed from the image above. The invincibility image would be that of a 'force field,' of sorts. Thus the merit of the re-dredging.
