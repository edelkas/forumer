Judges:
Me
-open-

(anyone who wants to judge may, but they must have some kind of instant messenger service).

Rules:
All entrants must meet up with any one judge on the IRC or AIM, or any other type of instant messenger. The judge will let them know when to begin, the halfway mark (17:30 minutes), and the deadline.

The judge will specify exactly where to put the ninja, to ensure that no one cheats.

Contestants may choose a tileset to base their DDA off of ahead of time

35 minutes of mapmaking exactly. No more, no less.

3 entries per person.

Judges may not enter.

Judging Criteria:
x/15 Length
x/10 close calls
x/10 propulsion quality

Deadline
January 20, 2007.

Set up mapmaking dates with a PM. Any questions?
