Ok so here's the idea, there are switches that would be the same size as normal switches, maybe they would look different, maybe a red center, but when you hit them, mines appear. So maps could be made with mines that dont appear til you hit the switch, so turning some parts from safe areas to very difficult areas. Also, if you had the mine trigger switch, maybe it would be possible to have a switch that made some mines disappear. I know it sounds a little weird, but it might have some cool applications.

#132658 15 2006-01-15T18:45:35Z Aries_Griff
Niccus, I think that if you think that this has limited possibilities, then you have a limited imagination.

You could make levels with a few paths at the beginning with a different mine switch in each, yielding totally different levels depending on what path you take.

You can make levels that get progressively harder by putting these switches in.

You can make levels that are unpredictable and dynamic with switches like this.

You could make mazes with door switches and mine switches.

You could do like Solarion said and make N art that changes depending on switches (what comes to mind to me is making a flower in different stages of blossoming).

And you can do like Metanet and make it so when people want to go for all gold, you make it really hard for them, but now you can use mines to make the whole level harder. Imagine a level with a few caches of gold, and for each cache of gold you get, more mines appear in the level, making it an easy level if you get no gold and a very hard level if you go for all gold.

Just a few ideas.
