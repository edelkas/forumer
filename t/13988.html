(For Lostprophets, Go Down)

Muse:
IT CODGING RULED!

I'd never screamed so loud.

Anyway, from the beginning:

Thurs 23rd November '06 (Bare professional)
Met up with mates, bla bla, met some random 19 year old that made me feel like a giant. Shwoz 5foot1.
Tube Train chugs.
Exit Wembley Park Tube Station. Fall over Webley Stadium. It's around 200 metres away. Looks so cool.
Twaddle over to Wemblewy Arena, newly 100% LEDed. Got my ticket taken and got a pink flurescent wristband. Woo!
Enter, gasp at refuribish Wembley Arena, down a bottle of water, enter the floor.
Get forced to sit. Stand, go to piss. Twice. Curse my pathetic bladder control!
Buy gig top.
Over an hour and a half later, the support act, The Noisettes, appeared, who were actually pretty good. Though the music was repetitive, and the lead singer continued to squeal like a pig cut in half. But the most disturbing part of their 40 minute act was watching the insane drummers moobs almost hit him in the face several times over.

40 minutes.
Bet Khights will be starting act.

5 Minutes.
Crowd explodes. Curtain drops to reveal Matt in his orange tracksuit, Dom, mit freshly shaven head, and a giant upside down cone with a pixellated display on the outside, on stage right. Raised to reveal Dom on a podeum.

Cue Knights.
Crowd roars.
I'm instantly thrown back 5 metres, but still have a perfect view. Bouncing begins.
I've never heard so many people imitate a high pitched voice.
Moshing ensues.

Follows on Starlight. Everything displays stars. Chorus sends them into space travel screensaver

Butterflies & Hurricanes. Awesome. The piano is ace. Extended guitar solo live is always great.

Map Of The Problematique. Continual jumping has never been so fun. 'Lonliness be over' bubbled over in the bg.

Riff. I knew it, everyone stared at me whilst I imitated it. Not cool. All cos I'm too hardcore for them

City of Delusion. Whipped out an acoustic and played tremendously. Introduced us to Trumpet player Dan. Though trumpet failed to do the string parts (as it's a trumpet) and effects were appauling, it was still a great song live.

And then he grabbed his reflective Knights guitar, walked to stage right. Hit a note. Held it. Put it to giant amp. Screeched. Took it away. Pulled a funny face. Changed note. Did this 3 times, and on the fourth time, the correct 1st note was played. Wow. *Drools at thought* Who cares about the awesomeness of the song, the atmosphere of the crowd, that 30 seconds summed up the ghettoness of the night.

Gathered around Dom's lil stand facing him, played forced in. Was expecting a follow up of bliss, got Hysteria instead, which lived up to it's name.

Citizen Erased. Brilliant track, shows Beller's writing ability. And insane hopping and jumping across the stage whilst playing shows his athletic ability too.

Hoodoo. -gutar intro = /me . Buty piano variation we ace. Go me, the only person that knew the words! Had some fat guy starin at me for it...

Invincible. -chaos pad = /me all like 'WTF?!HAX' but he used the time with free hands to raise the audience's energy levels. Me? I didn't need it, I had energy tablets Go Dextrose!

Supermassive. So. Fudging. Kewl. RANDOM MARCHING NINJAS IN BG! Solo hade me crap me pants too.

Time is running out. Most energetic performance by rabble (bar Knights) which was hard to keep up with.

New Born. Holy schmokes. I lurved es. Just the piano performance, the bassline, it is all fab.

5 min flippin 'yeah we're gonna pretend it's ended' which wore me out the most.

Apocalypse Please. What can I say? The piano is beautiful.

Bliss. Random countdown timer on right. 5 down to 1, through the quiet beginning bit, then it went nuts. Images then shown in countdown timer really made you think.

Balloons! 6 giant balloons, about the size of 5 people, were released from nowhere, filled with silver paper, I gotta hit one! They refused to play until we popped the last one, which refused to for 3 minutes, frantic boos could be heard everytime the balloon was relaunched by people. But the popping was insane, crowd started a mosh pit with no music

Muscle Museum. I'd have preferred seeing Fillip or Sober, even Sunburn, but I knew that wouldn't happen. This one's too popular. Hell, twas still good.

'This one's dedicated to all you crazy people in the middle.' MEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE.
Stockholm Syndrone begins. Love the quietLOUDquiet even more live. The space invaders style display was trific. Been searching for it on the net. No success.

Take a bow. Great ending song. Chanting about government rocks socks.

And alas, 2 Museiful hours were over. Dang.
Wish they'd played Hyper Music or TSP. They are great crowd action songs.

Lostprophets - Reading Rivermead... Sports Hall?!

Friday 24th November
Meet up with my mate Chris. Head off to this place. Completely shocked by the size of the place. And what it was. A sports hall! Twas funny. But yeh, took so long to get throo the door I missed the name of the first support, and they were pretty good. But Bring me the Horizon were balls, I don't care if they were British, they were BALLS. Hell, it may have been good if they had lyrics. Even Slipknot has lyrics. This was just 100%:
HHHHHHUUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGHHGHHGHHHHHHHGAAARR.
Oh dear.

Was also surprised by the amount of diddy lil emo kids in there, that weren't over 12. And some random lil ten year olds! Was so confused. I didn't care, it meant I had a great view

Sooner or later, no wait, long time later, LP finally reared they Welsh backsides, and put on a hell of a performance. Started with A New Transmission, understandable. They've improved so much since I saw them supporting Linkin Park on their Meteora Tour ('03).

We got treated to a variety of class tracks, such as: (in no order, I've forgotten )

Can't Catch Tomorrow
4am Forever
Sway
Rooftops
Town Called Hypocracy
Last Summer
To Hell WeRide
We Still Kill The Old Way
Everyday Combat
Last Train Home

And then he asked us if anyone was at Reading '04 when they nearly killed 60000 people? Shinobi.

Instantly, a giant circle formed. I got to see everything, I was on the edge of it. About 50 people then went crazy whilst everyone watched on in awe.
But it wasn't over. Burn Burn began. The circle reopened to double, triple the size, and everyone went nuts. We reached the middle of the song, the band continued playing, but Ian gave us a 2 minute pep talk, saying how he wanted the fucking place to explode. And it did. The mosh pit was largened once again, and every one inside began lining the outside of it. As Ian began to sing the quiet Burn Burn chant style lyrics, everyone, including me, who was shovved in by some giant man who wanted to join in himself, started pacing round the inside. I was so lucky I got out before the screaming lyrics began. I'd have died. Twas completely nuts. Ian is a great ringleader.


I won't compare the two concoerts, it'd be pointless, just about everything that could possibly be different between the two concerts was different. But I will say one thing, when deciding between who to buy tickets for, Muse or Lostprophets, whatever choice you make is a stupid decision. Get both.

Amen.
