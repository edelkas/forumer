[Bracket]

Due Process of a Round
  1. The round's map will be posted and the player whose performance will be bid on will be announced. The MasterBracket's parameters will also be revealed at this time.
  2. Players will send me (Shark) their bids for the round via PM in the correct format.
  3. About 2 days after the start of the bidding phase, bidding will close. I will not accept any bids after the end of the bidding phase.
  4. Members will now work on their runs for the round in highscore mode. They will submit their runs to the Nreality server before the end of the highscoring phase. (highscoring phase = 7 days)
  5. I will post the final rankings of the map in the thread, and award points accordingly. All players' bids will be revealed at this time also. The points will be collected or awarded from each player as the win or loss dictates.
  6. Awards will be given out and twists for the next round will be discussed.
***I will explain each part in detail below.
Spoiler: The Betting SystemEach round, one player will be chosen to be bid o (Everyone bets on the same person). The remaining players will bid on the player's performance in this format: [BID:Shark, 50 points - 4,5,6,7,8] Which means that you are bidding 50 points that Shark will get 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, or 8th place. If you are correct, 50 points will be given to you. If you are wrong, 50 points will be deducted.

ONLY BIDS IN THE CORRECT FORMAT WILL BE ACCEPTED.

Rules for Bids
  • Brackets can only have 5 members in it! ONLY 5! NO MORE NO LESS!
  • your bracket has to only have consecutive numbers. You cannot bet [BID:Shark, 50 points - 1,2,5,7,9] because the numbers are not consecutive.
  • You cannot change your bet after you've PM'ed it to me.
  • You cannot bet twice in a round.
  • You cannot be more points than you have.
Spoiler: The MasterBracketI will post the MasterBracket each round. It will look like this: [MasterBracket - Round 1 - 5,6,7,8].
All members of the bracket will receive 100 points. Anyone that places higher than the bracket (0,1,2,3,4) will get 150 points. Anyone that places below will get 50 points.
Spoiler: AwardsAwards will be given given throughout the game, and please expect lotsa lotsa twists. The game will also unpredictably change between groups, individuals, pairs - and even unofficial alliances. The awards will vary from simply being point awards to disadvantages that you can give to other players or teams. If the round calls for a team discussion (for instance, if you had to select two members to kick out and deprive of certain powers), everything should be done within the thread within the time limit.
Spoiler: Miscellaneous
  • There will be 5 rounds.
  • I will accept as many players as there is interest.
  • you may join in the middle.
  • There is no elimination.
  • This will be done in highscore mode with NReality.
  • Please expect twists.
  • You do not have to be the best highscorer to win this competition. It's a combination of skill and brains.
