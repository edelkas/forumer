#203476 4 2006-07-16T18:12:29Z Sammage
Your ms/f is in the bottom left corner. The absolutely perfect ideal for this is 8ms/f, that means you'll see every single frame when playing. If it's between 9-15, you've got an excellent speed for highscoring. If it's around 30-70, you'll have some difficulties with timing but it's quite possible to get highscores, and 70+ will probably have severe difficulty with timing.

Timing is quite important with jumps. Also remember that most highscores have spent a long time getting the physics down, so they can abuse it for their own ends.

The one that always amazed me was walljumping, people always seemed to walljump way faster than it should be possible to. But with practice it's really easy to shoot up a wall quickly without losing momentum.

Chances are, if it seems people are slowing down in midair way too fast, it's just they're pressing backwards a lot earlier than it looks.
