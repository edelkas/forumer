Types

.....Right, the way I see it, there are four different types of DDAs: Multi-enemy, KRADDA, concept, and n00b. I'm going to take time and try and describe all of them, and how I make them.


N00b

*shudders*

.....OK, a n00b DDA would be something like this. They have lines of launchpads, random Z snapping, and enemies which don't do anything. DO NOT MAKE THEM! THEY ARE TERRIBLE! If you want to make a DDA, at least put some effort into it.


Multi-Enemy

.....A standard DDA. A ninja, a bunch of enemies, and some propulsion. Not too hard to make once you get the idea. A good example would be this. Of course, the thing that makes M.E. DDAs great is close calls, so make sure you get plenty of those.
.....The way I make them is actually pretty simple. First you place down your enemies. Try to use as many as you can, but no more than 30 in total, or it will lag. Next, make a rule based on how you want the DDA to turn out. For example, normally I would say something like, "I'm not going to use any launchpads in this DDA," or, "I will make this go for at least 2500 frames," or both. Follow your rule no matter what. While you are making it, if you run into an enemy, don't block that enemy straight away! Try and propel the ninja away to another enemy. If you can't do that after several attempts, then block it off. Once you have no more available space, end it.


KRADDA

.....In case you don't already know, a KRADDA is a DDA where a rocket follows you for the entire level, like this. The key to making good KRADDAs is not only creating close calls for the ninja, but also close calls for the rocket, as it passes through gaps in walls. This is hard, but there is a trick to it. This is why KRADDAs with wide open spaces, are rarely good.
.....When you start making a KRADDA, I find it easier to start with an empty map, not a full one. I then need to pick a spot to add the ninja and the rocket, usually near eachother. I then just make it like a M.E. DDA (make a rule etc.) except I also try my best to get the rocket to go off on a different course to the ninja. When that happens, I place tiles around the area to make the rocket go in an extremely small area, hopefully between two tiles. (This is quite hard to explain, just watch the example above, and you should see what I mean.)
.....Once you have had some decent close calls for the rocket, and you have filled up as much of the screen as possible, you can place the exit.


Concept

.....OK, this is a pretty wide category. This is the category for new concepts for DDAs. An example would be this. It is basically when a DDA does something which has never (or rarely) been done before.
.....Of course, there isn't a trick to making these. You just need to come up with an imaginative concept. Don't try to make these, you can't really. You'll just stumble across a trick or a glitch some day, and then you can use it in a DDA.


NOTE: Some DDAs are mixtures of these four types. For example, this would be a cross between concept and KRADDA.

Propulsion

.....There are many different types of propelling the ninja while making DDAs. Launchpads aren't the only way around you know...


Launchpads

.....Doesn't even need explaining. Just point it where you want the ninja to go, and you're done.


Trap-doors

.....Right, there are two different types of trap door propulsions really, the type that speeds you up, and the one that reverses the direction you are already going in. For the first type, turn on C snap, and place the key 3/4 of a tile away from the door, in the direction you want the ninja to go in. For the other type, place the key half a space away from the door, on the opposite side of the door. (also hard to explain, you can see both types here). This also works by placing a normal door, and then putting a locked door on top of it, placing the key in similar spots.


Bounce blocks

.....This is a very simple propulsion, simply place down any number of bounce blocks in the same place (the more, the stronger) and put some gold on top (5 or more pieces.) This also works with thwumps instead of bounce blocks.


Normal doors

.....This is also simple, place a normal door down, and put six pieces of gold on top. When the six pieces are collected, the ninja will bounce away, speed up, or sometimes explode. You can also put down twelve pieces to allow the door to be used twice, eighteen would be three times, etc.


Thwumps

.....Just go between two thwumps, and you will go flying away. Occasionally you will get crushed though, but if you are half a tile in the thwumps, it should work. Another type, which is incredibly useful, is if you hit the corner, you will fly off, much like corner kicking. Very helpful when you want to change direction without a launchpad.


Locked door

.....More complicated. Also very unpredictable, but useful sometimes.

1. Make a locked door
2. Go to the debug menu and have a look at the level data, the last part should look something like this: 9^210,588,0,0,7,21,1,0,-1
3. Ignore most of it, you only need the third number after the ^. (it should be a 0 or a 1.) Change that number to read NaN. (not a number). The code should now look something like this: 9^210,588,NaN,0,7,21,1,0,-1
4. Press 'L' to load the edited level data.
5. Try it out.


Other

.....There are other types of propulsion, for example glitched tile propulsion, but Jiggerjaw12358 probably doesn't want me to give away how they are done.

FOR A MORE EXTENSIVE PROPULSION GUIDE, CLICK HERE

Tips
  • ALWAYS place your enemies first. (except propulsion enemies eg. thwumps.) If you try to add enemies later, it may not only not work, but screw up earlier enemies as well.
  • Press 1 before you play. This prevents enemies like chaingun drones from screwing up.
  • Don't have long waits. No one likes those.
  • Don't use gold delay. I don't mind it actually, but it can lower your rating quite a bit.
  • Try to make long DDAs. (at least 1000 frames)
  • Don't EVER include teleporters. They are a cheap way to get out of a tough place.
  • A very common mistake: Make sure you reset your level data.
  • Use a wide range of propulsions.
  • Make sure you use as much of the screen as possible.
  • Cheating death is a pain, unless you are actually basing the level on it, like this.
  • Oh and of course, practice. You rarely get a masterpiece on your first try.
  • Change your boss mode key. Hitting tab+q right at the end...
  • SAVE FREQUENTLY.
I want to hear all feedback/suggestions.
