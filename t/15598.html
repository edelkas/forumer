Basically, this is a contest in which you build a map using one of the 3 tilesets I choose.

----------

Rules

1. You must use one of the 3 tilesets I provide.

2. You may only create a playable map; this means no N Art, Tilesets(of course), DDA's, ect.

3. You may submit up to 3 maps. You don't have to use all three tilesets; you may use one tileset all 3 times, if you like.

----------

Rewards[/b]

The 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners will each receive a dedication. I will make the 1st place dedication; the 2nd and 3rd place dedications will be made by the 2nd and 3rd judges.

If one person has more than one map place, his/her highest ranking map will be the contender for the dedications. His/her other map will not be included.

----------

Judging[/b]

Judges may be contenders in this competition. The extra judge will take their place in judging their map alongside the other judges. By volunteering to judge, you are promising a high-quality dedication map to a winner, and you will devote a reasonable amount of time to judging.

Judge 1:[/b] ConfusedCartman(me)

Judge 2:[/b] Topolord

Judge 3:[/b] McP3000

Extra:[/b] Open

----------

Judging Format[/b]

Your map will be judged out of 3 things: Aesthetics(how the map looks), Replayability, and Enemy Placement. For the Overall score, the Total score from each judge will be added up. This is how each map will be scored:

----------

Tilesets[/b]

You may change small parts of the tileset, but the tileset must keep the overall feel that the original version had. The judges will determine if you have not done so. If and when we do, we will let you know that your map has been disqualified. You may edit it and resubmit, but only once; if you do not manage to re-awaken the original feel of the map, you may not use that tileset again. The disqualified map will not count against you in terms of maps submitted to the contest.
