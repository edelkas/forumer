Over a private IRC chat a few nights ago, me and my new partner capt_weasle have begun a new series of maps. Level pack this aint. Those are starting to get old. No no, we've had a better idea. Welcome to our world.

This is partly inspired by levels packs such as The Legacy and Project 42, and the videogame Fable. You are a ninja. You have awaken in a moss forest, yet you have no idea where you are. You neither have an idea of what you are doing nor an identity. Set out on a quest to find out who you really are and what your purpose is, you find yourself in a world where your choices and actions affect averything that happens to you. Every door will take you to a different map, and each new map will have several doors to choose from. Whether your choices are based on personal gain or serving others, it's up to you. Welcome....to - Silence of Memory -

This thread is open for questions, that either I or capt_weasle shall answer. Capt. should begin this shortly...

Alright, listen up. Here's what it's all about. Actually, here's a link first - link!

Not really an awesome map, but hey, we're doing our best...sorta. Anyway, you are given a map (click the link). Each map has at least 2 doors. I will post a 'story' here that goes to each map, and explains the situation, and what each door leads to. Every door in the map will be either for good or evil. It's up to you to choose which one to go through. Some doors will be harder to get to, others will be easier, that all depends on what you are after. Sometimes there will be rewards for going through one of the doors. Sometimes there will be consequences. But we will get to that later.

In this first map neither door affects you. You just awoke, so now you need to decide where to go first. This is kind of like the Epic, only more involved.
