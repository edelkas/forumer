This one was tough for me too. The only advice I can give: Jump like crazy for the first two rooms, and on the curved one if your speed starts to go halfway through, you're probably not going to make it. Keep jumping on the curves for boosts, it's the only way this level was possible for me.

Also, at the start, you can get a small boost off the thwumps when they start going backwards, or wall jump off them (just make sure you land in a bit that doesn't instantly slow you down). This is VERY helpful.

The last room killed me the most, and to be honest I kinda think it was fluke I survived that one at all.
