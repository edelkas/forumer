You have the rest of this month to nominate, and the earlier the better. Any nominations at the end of the month will have to be very good to convince me to re-jig the nominations I'll have chosen out by then.

There are a few categories which need nominations more than others. Contribution would be greatly appreciated. These categories are:
Race map of the year

Tileset of the year
Most Innovative Use of Objects or Tiles
