#45019 3 2005-01-09T04:33:27Z Sammage
Patience and persistence, that's all you can really do with this level. Steal the start of the highscore routes, since they're helpful, but once you've hit all the switches the highscore routes become difficult, so just find the best route that suits you after that. Remember the drone routes after each death so you know when to tread and where.

I beat this level the same way I beat every other level - clambering over the bodies of a hundred dead ninjas.
