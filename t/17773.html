Hello there, Grey Dog.

You start an episode with 90 seconds, and it counts down from there. Each piece of gold gives you 2 extra seconds. The reason why you start with such little time is because your time carries over through an episode. Therefore, you must have not collected enough gold in the previous levels. You must restart the episode and try to have a good amount of gold before advancing through the levels.
