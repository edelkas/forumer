The most important part of curing insomnia is to not stay in bed if you can't sleep. If you're still awake after 20 minutes, you need to get out of bed and do something else. Something relaxing. If you get into the habit of lying awake in bed, your mind associates the bed with something other than sleep, which makes it harder to return to a normal sleep pattern.
