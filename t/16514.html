You should read up on these:

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBCode

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_signature#Internet_forums

Now here are the instructions for adding images to your signature:

1. Find the image you want to use and save it to your computer. Right click on the image and go to SAVE AS. Remember to save it someplace you can remember easily.
2. You now need to save the image on a website for it to work on the forum. Many of the people are using Photobucket or Imageshack. There are other sites out there you can use, but not all allow linking outside of their graphic site. Imageshack or Photobucket generate the bbcode needed to add to your signature.
3. After your image is saved on your website, copy the bbcode that is the direct link to the image.
4. Go to edit your profile.
5. Scroll down to the Edit Signature option.
6. A new window will pop up where you will be editing your signature at. This is where you add links, photos, and text.
7. Refer to the Wikipedia article for the bbcode used to add images. Plain text can be added as well, but you can format the way you want it.
8. Once you've added everything you want to your signature click on the update button located at the bottom of the editor.
