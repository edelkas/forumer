#72113 21 2005-06-04T03:33:06Z swankidelic
I voted "To give your mind an excercise", and, please, allow me to expound on that a bit...

One of my most favorite gaming experiences is with Ninja Gaiden for the Xbox. That game redefines "difficult", but not because of the level of difficulty, but rather the curve. There's something magical in that game, in that the next level, or the next scene, is just barely beyond your skill level. The game will beat the daylights out of you, but it's fun enough to try again, and each time you get a little bit further. Eventually, after a long session, you reach an amazing level of mental focus. It's like the nerves in your hands and wrists are on fire, electric. I'm sure there are some gaming veterans out there that know what I mean.
IMHO, the thing that makes n worthwhile is that it does the same thing with so much less. Think about it, n only uses three buttons. Three. And with those three buttons, so much is possible. n acheives something that I really admire: elegance. It's fun, it's *truly* challenging, and it's simple. n reaches the pinnacle of gaming, and embodies something that many console and pc games have lost in the past couple of decades.

OK, I'm done. I'll get off my soapbox now.
