Ive gotten quite good with making guides and images!

Your Background - Deviant Art type in to search box 'gimp brush sets':

----------------Change your brush opacity to 60
----------------Change color to black
----------------Click 3-5 random spots around your sig
----------------Go to the layers dialog
----------------Mode > overlay
----------------Create a transparent layer
----------------Change color to white
----------------Click 3-5 random spots around your sig
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Mode > overlay

-Repeat the above steps until you like how it looks, then continue-

----------------Go to the layers dialog
----------------Flatten your image



Coloring your background - 2 ways

-do one of the following-

-first way

----------------Create a white layer
----------------Choose two colors that go with your render
----------------Then use the gradient tool
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Mode > overlay



-second way

----------------Tools > color tools > colorize

-Hue - changes the color
-Saturation - brightness of your color
-lightness - the higher, the whiter



-Makeing your background standout - 2 ways

-you can do both-

-first way

----------------Tools > color tools > brightness and contrast

-secondway

----------------Create a transparent layer
----------------Place new layer below your render
----------------Paste your render on the new layer until it is completely covered by your render
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Mode > overlay



Your render:

-Placement-

If your render is on the right, have it face left.
If on the left, have it face right.
If in the middle, have it looking straight ahead.

-if your render is pointing the wronge way-

----------------Use the 'flip tool'

-makeing your render stand out-

----------------Tools > color tools > brightness and contrast
----------------Duplicate your original render
----------------Filters > blur > gaussian blur 10 x 10
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Mode > screen or overlay

-creating a shade around your render-

----------------Duplicate your render
----------------Go to filters > blur > gaussian blur 10 x 10.
----------------Filters > artistic > Photocopy
----------------Change "percent white" to the highest
----------------Then put duplicate layer, below your original render.

-if you want the shade to be bigger-

----------------Layer > Scale Layer

-if you want the shade to have color-

---------------- tools > color tools > colorize

-Hue - changes the color
-Saturation - brightness of your color
-lightness - the higher, the whiter



Your text - Dafont

-Placement-

Not covering your render
In a noticeable place
Space around it

-create a border around your text-

----------------Create a transparent layer
----------------Movenew layer below text layer
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Right click text layer 'alpha to selection'
----------------Choose your transparent layer
----------------select > grow 3 x 3
----------------'Bucket fill tool'
----------------Fill whole selection with color of choice



Your Border: - 3 ways

-first way

----------------Create a transparent layer
----------------Select all
----------------Change color to black
----------------Edit > stroke selection by 10
----------------Change color to white
----------------Edit > stroke selection by 8
----------------Change color to black
----------------Edit stroke selection by 5
----------------Go to layers dialog
----------------Mode then choose anything from multiply to overlay

-second way

----------------Select all
----------------Edit > stroke selection 2

-third way

IMPORTANT! Do not close your old image after if you do this border. You may not like how it turns out. I also suggest doing one of the first two before doing this one.

----------------Select all
----------------Flatten image
----------------Copy
----------------Ctrl+z
----------------File > new. 50 x 50 plus your original
----------------Create a transparent layer
----------------Paste your old image
----------------Select background layer
----------------Select a brush thats small and jets out
----------------Click along the edges of old image. If the brush is to big, then find a smaller one

Your Scanlines: - How to make scanlines:

----------------Choose your layer with the white shade
----------------'bucket fill tool'
----------------Change 'fill by' to 'Pattern fill'
----------------Choose your scanlines
----------------Change the 'affected area' to 'similiar colors'
----------------Change opacity between 10 and 30
----------------Then fill the areas around your shade



-----------------------------END!
