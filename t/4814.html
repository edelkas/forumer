If you are to MANUALLY EDIT N's location, and you set him 8 pixels into a tile, thwump, trap door, or locked door, he will automatically pop up. You can set it lower than 8, and he won't jump as high, but no higher than 8 UNLESS you put a launch pad in the ground, which would make it NON-USERLEVEL FRIENDLY, but provide a further toss.

I haven't seen anything like this, and I'd like to call it "V's Auto Jump"

The beginning of THIS level uses this effect, I made it just to show how it works. It works in EVERY direction, and you can do it in more than one direction (such as in the corner at the bottom left or right, and he'll shoot up and out).

I discovered this when I was playing around in NED, and noticed that if you put the cursor near a wall, and hit enter (to respawn) he'll get shot away from the wall.

I'd like to see a DDA with this, but I'm a bad DDA maker, so I'll let you guys try something if you want.
