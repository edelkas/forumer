That is a preview for the map pack I've been trying to organize and facilitate since before the beginning of Christmas break. There have been a few set backs, but it's still going strong and that is sort of why I'm doing this; to remind myself that I still have work to do, and work I'm still doing.

I like hype. I like buying into hype. And the best thing is when something is hyped, but not beaten to death, and exceeds expectations, so that is what I will attempt to do, hype this a bit to get the word spread and then, hopefully, everyone will be blown away. How will I do this blowing away you ask? Well, very simple, because this type of map pack has never been undertaken before and everyone loves new things.

Anyway, sorry if I sound conceited, but I'm just excited to start the word for the pack. Here are the basics of the pack:

Theme: The map pack is essentially a music pack encompassing many genre's, styles, and era's. It is episodic in nature with 5 levels per episode and each episode representing its specific band. Upon release the pack will contain 2 columns, or 100 levels, OR TWENTY BANDS (Sorry, couldn't resist the caps, it was staring at me). There will likely be revisions in the future, but that is about the size you can expect the pack to be unless anything drastic happens in the next month.

Participants: TIS A SECRET! But I can tell you that NO ONE will be dissapointed. You may not believe me, but it's true. Though this is the set-up, it's a collab pack. I will be making half of the maps, or my own column, while the other column will be made by 10 of the brightest map makers of the community. Each with their own episode and band to bring to life however they see fit.

Expectations of the pack: It is my desire to bring these bands to life through the game. It is also my desire to have a very diverse, fun, and top notch quality pack so I have asked people from very varying styles and mapping techniques to help out, and I promise you, no two episodes will be alike, and all 20 episodes will be awesome.

I was thinking about posting a few of the maps, but I think I'll hold off on that, I just kind of want to spread the word.

But here's the last thing I wanted to mention. As I mentioned before I plan on enlarging this pack after the initial release so if anyone is interested in future installments, or even a slim possibility of this one, of collab columns toss me a pm and I'll get back to you. While I'm putting forth what I think of my favorite bands, the point of this pack is diversity and how everyone views music and bands differently, therefore, I want many more people in future installments if they believe they can make great maps that exhibit what they believe to be the themes and ideas of their favorite bands.

Anyway, sorry if I'm a bit long winded, and I hope this doesn't come across badly, but expect the pack in about a month, give or take according to how school and work turns out during that month.
