Not too long ago, I put N on my schools' public server. Kids played it all the time, and it was fun. Unfortunatly, then they wanted to make maps.

The maps they created were spammish, laggy, and pointless. At first, they only added them to userlevels.txt, which was fine, but then some of them started creating usernames and posting their "maps" on NUMA. Once, I told one of them that his map wouldn't be rated very high. He said, "I don't care. I'm justing posting it for fun." I know NUMA already has a problem with spammy maps, so, in an effort to stop the pointlessness, I deleted as many copies on N I could find and replaced them with a text document. It read,

Q: Where has our beloved N gone?
A: I deleted it.

Q: But why?
A: I never should have introduced it. Recently, Some users of N have become members of NUMA. They are all n00bs. Every one of them. The levels they make are spammy, n00bish, and laggy.
As it turns out, NUMA already has a problem with n00bs like them clogging up the system. All of the GOOD level makers are leaving, because of these n00bish entities. It has to stop. I just feel like I should do my part for the community of N, and allowing people like these to ruin our expirience is the wrong path to take, even if they mean well. I can't let the pollution go on. This "map" making must stop.

Q: You can't stop me! I'll just download it again!
A: You're only hurting yourself and the community of the game you love. By posting maps like these, you're ruining the expirience for others.


There was a sort of uproar. It was talked about throughout the entire next day. People started downloading N and hiding it in folders with misleading names. People came to me and said, "Did you hear about the guy who tried to delete N? Well he didn't. I hid it in this folder..." Afterward, I accessed the public folder and deleted it. Soon after, I noticed that my file had been edited. Somebody was responding to the message. The message:

You are an idiot. You thought these places were the only ones. You failed to kill it, it has spread, and you are now out in the open (sort of), and we know you are there, so we will use extra precautions. You obviously know nothing, and pretending to be a big shot and deleting everyone's favorite game, which you didn't succeed, doesn't mean you win and you are really, really smart, you are, in fact, quite the opposite.
-The Guardian of N

Apparently, this person, whoever they are, failed to get the message (also, by the way, I have "succeed" at N, beating not only all 500 levels but also all 400 legacy levels. Unfortunatly, I couldn't give them my .sol file, 1. because it would reveal my identity, and 2. because they probably wouldn't know how to use it). This "Guardian" is quite the hypocrite, in my opinion, calling me a big shot.

Anyway, I am unsure of what to do now. I have a couple options:

1) Continue to attempt to delete ever copy of N I can find

2) Alert NUMA to watch out for maps created by them (I happened to discover some of their usernames)

3) Petition for a new feature in N: only allow certain people to use Ned (i.e. only those who complete column 8 something)

4) Attempt to show what these people are doing is wrong and bad for the community (probably won't work - spammers are very stubborn sometimes)

5) Ask myself: do I really care if NUMA gets another truckload of n00bs?

The thing is, I am at risk doing this now. If one of these people should happen to view this topic, I could be in danger. However, I think it's all for a good cause. What do you think should be done?
