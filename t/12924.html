How does everybody always avoid homing missiles? Since they move at the same speed as the ninja, once you jump or loose just a little speed you get caught.

Any advice?
#236133 2 2006-10-01T23:30:24Z Levinous
Okay, if you're running in a straight line, and the rocket is behind you, jump and slow down, and the rocket should turn around. But the rocket's turning angle isn't as good as yours, so it takes longer to turn around. That way you can get some extra time there.

Another way is similar, except that you jump high up, so that you land with the rocket directly above you, then you start to run, and the rocket should explode into the floor.
