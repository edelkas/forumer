Rules: There is only one rule. The rule is, there must be at least one of everything in the map. And not just enemies, either. There needs to be at least one of every single tile as well. It doesn't matter what way they face. As well as one of every tile, you also need at least one of every enemy, and everything else like bounce blocks etc. Also, they all must be visible otherwise the map will be disqualified.

Each person is allowed to submit up to 3 maps if they wish.

POST HERE TO JOIN! PM ME TO JOIN AND BE A JUDGE!

ALSO, Judges are allowed to enter, but they have to judge their own maps fairly.

NOTE: The contest finishes on the 20th Febuary so make sure all entries are in well before then!
Judging will then commence and go for approximately a week.


Here are the images you can add to your signatures if you wish.





If you're both a compedator and a jugde, feel free to animaring userbar from my signature.

EDIT: Oh, I forgot the judging criteria! Maps will be judged on:
How it looks /5
Use of enemies and other objects /5
Difficulty /5 NOTE: Difficulty is not ranged from easy to hard or anything like that. Judge it on how completable it is, but not too easy. Know what I mean?
Creativity /10
"Funness" /15
Gameplay /20

TOTAL: /60
The judges' marks will be averaged to form the end mark.


Prizes: 1st place: A dedication and a signature bar.
2nd place: A Signature.
3rd place: Another signature


Judges: Me (Weisslenny0) ....... Easy going. You'll be getting at least 40/60 from me, unless your level really really sucks...
