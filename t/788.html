One day, an evil villain by the name of Gorgonzola attacked some people on a mountain and a spirit. The spirit had always lived in harmony with the people on the mountain. The spirit would not give up in protecting the mountain people, no matter how hard he would be hurt. Gorgonzola realized he must retreat from the well-fought battle to seek assistance. After heading for shelter, he scavenged the household for a phone. Gorgonzola called up his friend, Erick, to help destroy the spirit. Completely by coincidence, Erick was going to try to do that all by himself. Erick thought. It was hopeless. He was afraid to even dare have the thought of it, but he knew that he needed help. Erick had already contemplated that Gorgonzola would need his assistance, and was embarassed to say so, even though the facade of emotions upon his face showed otherwise. He gave him a smug grin to let him know he would help him, despite the quarrel between them the day before. "I'll call up John," he said with a reassuring tone, "He'll help you." Gorgonzola smiled back at him, knowing everything was the way it should be. Erick called John on his supposedly "useless" cell phone, and gave it to Gorgonzola soon after John answered. Gorgonzola asked John, one of his closest friends, to hack into the Pentagon and steal the government's ultimate spirit-annihilating weapon: the Spirit-Zap. John connected to the Pentagon, and started up his bypassing software. He accessed the administrative section, and selected the elliptic curve encryption cypher. After nearly three hours of monotonous typing, coding, and mind work, John managed to crack the encryption cypher on the government database. "Only 19 more security systems to go," he thought. As John continued in his efforts to hack into the Pentagon, the spirit enclosed Gorgonzola in a small hallway, and electrified him. Gorgonzola rushed down the dimly lit hallway, just hoping for revenge on what has brought him so much pain. Gorgonzola turned the corner to see his archenemy, the spirit, waiting for him. The spirit opened it's mouth wide as Gorgonzola rushed at it. The Evil Gorgonzola was swallowed whole by the spirit, and all of the people on the top of the mountain lived happy lives.
