#122436 22 2005-12-12T22:25:45Z Sammage
I had a similar problem, different part of the body though (just in my hand). Any levels that require precision jumps or repetitive jumps, or working on a highscore for a while. Two things that have worked for me:

-Moderate your play, and that means taking regular breaks, which is the big issue for me while trying to get highscores. This is just something you've gotta force yourself to do (plus when you get very good it takes less time to make a highscore, so that's a bonus).

-I changed my jump button. This didn't do anything for my right hand which at one point clawed up so much I couldn't grip anything, but with my left hand, using two fingers at once to jump instead of just one changed the entire motion needed to jump, used more of the whole hand and I found I could play a lot longer without pain (plus I changed the jump key to z from the busted up shift key, didn't have to hammer the keys so hard). In your case, you might wanna change the way you're seating, your distances relative to the keyboard, that sort of thing.
