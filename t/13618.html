Every two weeks, I will present a set of five maps. You will attempt to kill yourself as quickly as possible in one or more of the five different maps and submit your demos . No using the enter key or fbf. You are allowed three demos per map. The fastest death on each map is put into the FDCRH (Fast death challenge record hall), soon to be made on this very topic.. After the fastest demo has been displayed in the FDCRH at the end of the two weeks, you are not allowed to post something which beats that record and expect to HAVE your demo in the FDCRH. If you are submitting a demo, be sure to type up the challenges you are doing in bold text so as to be very clear. Be sure to submit your entry in the
code
format, or as a file attachment (.txt extension). Enjoy!

Current Maps (challenge from November 5th to November 19th):
1:Hedge by Zorn
2:Cluster by tktktk
3:Crooked by walrus_of_death
4:Halftone by Lucidium
5:Cyclone by Jiggerjaw12358
