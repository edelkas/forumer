Quote: "The Contract"
I. The undersigned parties agree to make two (2) maps in the computer game N each day. One (1) of the maps will be submitted to the N User Map Archive (NUMA) and the other will be submitted to the Legacy Team for "Zenith" and future projects. If the situation occurs that the Legacy Team no longer needs or wants map submissions, the signed will only be responsible for one (1) map per day.

II. Should any of the signed parties fail to make two (2) maps in any given day, he/she should lose any elevated forum status (i.e. Admin, Moderator, MetaNember) and be permanently banned from the Metanet forums. Exceptions can be made for extended absences, such as vacations, if all parties agree in advance.

III. This contract takes effect on the date it is signed and will continue indefinitely.

IV. The contract may be terminated with an Intense Map-Off at any time if all parties agree to it. The Intense Map-Off will see ten maps made by each author, and the rules will be decided at the time of the map-off.

V. Each of the signed parties may participate in a collaboration map with one another during the alotted time period. Collaboration maps count as 1/2 (50%) of a map for quota and tallying purposes, and each party must participate in at least two (2) collaboration maps to account for one full map for the alotted time period. Pursuant to section IV - If both parties agree during an Intense Map-Off - collaboration tally rules will apply.

VI. Should an extreme unforseeable event (e.g. computer failure, surprise party, cholera) prevent any of the signed parties from completing their maps for the day, said party will not be required to map. All other parties are still expected to fill their quota.
