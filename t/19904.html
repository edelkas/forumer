#416859 21 2008-03-14T20:50:39Z Tapp
I got it! If you put a glitched trap door that lets you fall into the floor with a one-way on top of it, it glitches. I worked out that this is because the laser drone uses the same collision detection as tiles, and thus it doesn't know where you are and fires at where you were last seen. Look at the attached level, and try jumping around then returning a few times. I'm thinking of making a puzzle level using this.
