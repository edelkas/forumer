hey,
i guess the thing is: it's not just about the ninja's _speed_, it's a combination of the ninja's velocity (speed+falling direction) and the ground's direction (i.e how sloped it is).

in order to start playing an animation, you need to know that it's appropriate -- in this case, we need to know that falling will kill you. but, we can't know this without knowing (a) what direction you'll be moving when you hit the ground (B) what tile you'll hit.

but both (a) and (B) can change up to the instant you hit the ground.. so we couldn't play an animation before that.

sorry if this doesn't make sense ;)

#24048 10 2004-06-29T17:18:05Z Guest
hey,
you should really watch the "help" items on falling

death by falling has very cilantro to do with:
-height
-speed

it _only_ depends on your velocity (speed + direction) when you hit a surface, and the direction that surface points. when you hit the ground, your velocity is measured perpendicular to the surface -- if it's larger than a certain threshold amount, you die. this is more or less how it works in real life -- if you fall straight down onto flat ground, it's worse than if you fell onto a steep slope.

for instance, i'm guessing that in your jumping case, you're jumping, say, left to right, moving fast horizontally, and hitting a sloped surface that looks like this: /

which means your velocity is almost perfectly perpendicular to the slope's surface -- which is the worst possible case. if you want to survive, start pressing <- in midair so you're moving more slowly when you hit.

we realise that most platformers use vertical distance to determine jump-deaths, but we thought that given the weirdness of N, this more "realistic" calculation method would be better.

if you want to make sure you don't die from a jump, just make sure you don't hit the landing surface too hard. hit it at a glancing angle instead of straight into it..

For those of you who don't understand what perpendicular means...

If N falls onto the ground at a certain speed, he dies. However, you may have noticed that if N walljumps too hard, he dies in the cieling. It's as if you turned the game upside down. So basically, if N is moving too fast horizontally, and he hits a flat wall, he dies. If he is moving diagonally, and hits a surface that is flat (to his point of view), he dies.

However, I think the animation would be possible, because if N reaches a certain speed (regardless of direction), he will make the animation, no matter what he will hit. He will make the animation only because he is going too fast through midair, not because he will hit something.
