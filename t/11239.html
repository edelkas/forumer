#202951 19 2006-07-15T10:12:38Z LittleViking
Hey hey! Important development for you all. I found a better screen capture program than CamStudio. It hardly lags N at all and gives high quality video with extremely small file sizes. (i.e. 5 minutes of video with audio in 1 MB) Plus, to make things easier for us, it lets you select a window to capture. You won't have to box out an area like with CamStudio.

http://www.download.com/Windows-Media-Encoder/30...1788.html

And yes, I'm sure a lot of you hate Windows Media, but who cares. The program works wonders and YouTube will accept it. It's exactly what we need.

Now, to record the music that's playing, you might have to set up your recording device. So if your videos come out with no sound, do this: In Windows Media Encoder, go to Tools>Configure Devices..., double-click (default audio device), and in the Pin Line dropdown box, there should be an option called Stereo Mix. Select it and check the Enable box. And then you should be good to go.
