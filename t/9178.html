For my dark lady...

One gentlemen from Italy stepped aboard a ship, bound somewhere that was- in fact- a lot simpler to get to by simply travelling across the land, rather than travelling about twice the distance over land merely to get to the sea, and to take a boat from there. But it didn't really matter... Boats were pleasent, and he could have enough time to mull over the events of the last few days in his mind on the peaceful voyage.

Walking upon the deck of The Collosus, he had to say that he was quite impressed- it was a classy ship, and a huge one. The Collosus. Totally unsinkable, or so they said. It sounded like a bit of a challange, but oh well... let the worst happen. If he were to die, so be it- for what is life without love, and what is love when it lies with a maiden of whom you have only memories and a single ring?

And with a heavy heart, he went off to try to find his bunk.

* * *

As I walked onto the deck of The Collosus, I almost gave an ejaculation of surprise upon finding out just how large the deck was.

My longtime friend and companion behind me in his famous lodgings on the street that often smell ever so slightly of bread, I was off on an adventure of my own... yet I missed his company, for all of his faults. Even his cocaine addiction was a small price to pay to be constantly in the company of his brilliance.

And here I was, a humble doctor, travelling alone on such a large boat, bound for exotic locations currently unknown to even myself. This would, I thought to myself as the boat began to move, be a voyage to remember.

Little did I know just how correct that thought would prove to be.

* * *

And thus begins the epic voyage of The Collosus, and a journey which will end in thousands of small allusions to- well- stuff, many riddles and many tasks. A journey in which many, many members will perish and some will- hopefully- survive.

So, it's time to introduce the people whom shall soon, for the most part, quite likely be corpses. Each member of the forum is granted two characters, who may be whoever they like, within reason... No superheroes or special qualities, please! Allusions to people in plays, movies, or books are a plus.

Enjoy! This should be good, once it gets going. We'll have a few days of voyage in which people can introduce eachother, and then we'll get into the whole riddle related thing. I hope this works. The plans are still hugely incomplete, but I've got quite a bit to work from, and this should be fun.
