#442866 4 2008-06-08T02:03:54Z Pikman
It's NReality only. It mostly happens when you get annoyed that a level isn't loading, storm out of user levels and into the editor. The high scores board then finally loads while you're in the editor and draws over what you're doing. This is easily solvable. Just change the mode. If you're editing your level, press [Home] and [~]. If you're playing, just press [~]. If you're on the edit menu, press [E], [T], [P], or [M]. The screen has to reload in each of these situations, so the high scores box should disappear.
