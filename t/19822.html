*The Next Day*




Sally awoke with a start. She had just had that nightmare again. The journey in her car; the mysterious man in the passenger seat; "It's coming to an end," he had said (she could never forget those foreboding words); the sudden shower of...

Her mind snapped back to the present. Day dreaming wasn't going to get the house cleaned. For the first time that day, she looked around and took in what she saw, sparking memories of the events of the day before.

The remains of the rat were still splattered all over the walls. She expected nothing less; Chuck wasn't the type to clean up his mess.

"The poor thing could've never seen it coming", she said, to no one in particular. Wondering where Chuck and Dave were, she went to the kitchen to make herself some food before the daunting task of cleaning up the rat. On the table was a note.

"Tired of world. Leaving on rat's escape pod. Love you."

The note was signed "Chuck". The question that immediately cropped up in her head was answered when she turned the note over and saw "I'm off too. Dave." Annoyed, she scrunched up the note and through it in the bin, wondering why they didn't take her with them. She noticed that she didn't hear the satisfying sound of paper hitting the side of a bin as it dropped in. She looked around to see that her bin was gone.

"B*******. They took my bin." She was rather attatched to that bin. It provided her shelter for 7 months in 1982. Still, she had to keep moving. The house needed cleaning, and the bin needed replacing. She decided to start with the latter.

Relieved to find that Chuck and Dave hadn't somehow taken her car, she put her keys in the ignition and started up. She loved driving. It seemed to be as close as she could get to getting away from the rest of the world. Cruising at 60 miles per hour on the freeway, Sally noticed that on her right was a disturbingly long line of... what were they? She slowed down and moved into the right lane, wanting to find out more, and immediately regretted her decision.

Not 4 seconds after her action, Sally's car was surrounded by the mysterious walkers. Left, right, front, back, on top of - even a couple underneath, but then their faces were underneath the tires, so they posed no serious threat. Amongst the chaos came one man, who Sally noticed was actually fighting people off her car. Did he want her for himself? Or was he really trying to save her? His face was pressed against the windscreen now, and she could see him mouthing "DRIVE DRIVE DRIVE B****!" She pressed down hard on the pedal, and the swarm of attackers were helpless as they fell by the sides of the car. Only one remained - her apparent saviour. She stopped, and let him in - an action that she really didn't understand, only justified by the fact that she didn't understand anything that was going on anyway.

"Thank you, ma'am." Sally looked at him and wondered why she was being thanked. She opened her mouth to thank him, but he stopped her. "Please. Just... thank you."

A few minutes passed, and they were getting to know each other. Sally had just established that he was a huge fan of Lost - her favourite show.

"You love it too?" he asked Sally, as she nodded. "Oh, it's wonderful," he continued. "This season is easily the best. It's a pity, though," he said with a chuckle.

"A pity? What do you mean?" she asked.

"You know. It's coming to an end."

Sally's heart skipped about seven beats.

She looked to the sky.

"Oh my God."
