Well, I'm officially renouncing my claim here, if you can do better, go for it. I haven't pulled it off yet, but I'll let you in on how I've been so sure you can better your score. There's two ways: First, at the beginning, instead of grabbing the first switch, jump over it, and hit the third slope. Jump off the opposite slope as normal, and come back down as normal. Instead of jumping all the way over the bottom ridge, you can instead jump into the switch. If you get the momentum just right, it'll catapult you over the ridge and down into the bottom part just perfectly. This shaved about .5 off my time. The second trick is at the bottom. Instead of single jumping into the pile of gold off the slope, you can double jump, much like you would a triple jump off a wall. It requires perfect timing, but the times I've pulled it off, it's nearly doubled my speed going into the gold pile. Two problems with that: The top homing rocket comes in too high, and I usually get hit by it on my walljump, and second, if you double jump off the slope too hard, you cling to the wall above the gold. That *should*, in theory, give you around 159.225 or so. However, I haven't been able to pull it off in one run, or without dying. The one time I did pull it off in a single run, on my way to hit the jump pad towards the exit, I hit the bottom lip, and it shot me off to the left of the pad. I then hit the pad, and hit the overhang before being catapulted to the exit, and ended with a 157.450 or something. So, if you can do it, kudos, but I've got far too much going down this week to be trying again. I'll have to settle for a 1st place instead. Poor me.
