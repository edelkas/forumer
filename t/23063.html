#450650 34 2008-07-04T01:33:54Z LittleViking
I went into Wall-E expecting two solid hours of adorable robot action. Which is to say, not expecting much. If I want simple cuteness, I can search for kittens on YouTube. But everything I've read here compelled me to see it, so I did, and I was pleasantly surprised in the theater. Wall-E turned out to be a very intelligent and even slightly dark movie. For a movie that's apparently being marketed to young children and giddy girls, it actually hits on a lot of good dystopian sci-fi themes. And of course, the "Presto" short attached to the start of the film is a great addition. It's a brilliant throwback to the old slapstick-animation-set-to-music style of Looney Tunes and the Mickey Mouse cartoons of the 40's and 50's.

Despite the praise, I wouldn't call this my favorite movie of all time like some people here, but it's definitely still worth seeing. See it in theaters if possible - the movie has a few really stunning visual scenes that would be lost on the small screen, and Wall-E probably is actually a good date movie - but don't feel too bad if it passes you by. Some people will be put off by the overall environmentalist message of the film (me included, no matter how liberal I am), but it's never enough to ruin the experience.
