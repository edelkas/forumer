#22671 3 2004-04-29T14:56:27Z Guest
hi,
i don't know.. isn't the current version frustrating enough?

the problem with punishing deaths (by not resetting the score) is that we designed the game (and levels) so that death would be common/frequent, and no big deal -- sort of like how in tony hawk, falling is no big deal.

it's something we'll keep in mind, since we ARE trying to think of as many different highscore categories as possible, but i'm not sure it would really be fun -- for instance, one of our ideas was to have a "least # of deaths per episode" score, but.. can you imagine how frustrating/tense something like that would be? maybe too much.

anyway, thanks for the ideas; our current plan is to have the following categories:

-highest 10 "remaining lifespans" for each episode
-highest 10 "amount of gold collected" for each episode
-lowest 5 times for each level

if there are any ties (for instance, with gold or level times, ties might be fairly common), they'll be decided by the "other" stat; i.e if two people have the same # of gold for an episode, the one with the larger lifespan gets a higher rank. likewise for the level records -- the person who had collected more gold would get a higher rank if there was a time-tie (but, this is maybe less unlikely since the times have a granularity of 0.025s, so there will probably alwasy be a very slight difference).

we'll consider adding/changing categories based on how people seem to be using/liking the v1.3 setup..

I would expect to see a *very* high correlation between these categories. Meaning, that whoever collects the most gold the fastest will generally have the higher remaining lifespan. This is a direct result of the infinite retrys available to everyone.

Granted, on alot of the later levels, where basic survival is the huge concern, there will be a greater variety of scores.

So if you're not keen on the idea of losing all the time spent on a level that you die on, then you could add a death penalty, maybe even just 1 second per death. THAT would definitely add a ton of variety to the scores, because, while it's easy to get a near perfect run given an infinite amount of attempts, it won't be that easy when you're losing a second of time every time you retry.

For the puzzle solving aspect of the game, I like the infinite amount of retries, but in terms of highscores, death should have a penalty attached. And falling in tony Hawk DOES have a penalty attached. You lose the time it takes to get up and get going again, which could have been used for scoring more points. That's exactly what I'm suggesting should be implemented for this game.

Keep in mind that I'm suggesting an alternate high score list here. There would still be the regular one with no death penalty, so if you think a death penalty somehow makes the game too frustrating, you don't have to participate in that mode of play. It would be open to those who want the added challenge and enjoy it.

Once the high scores stagnate (and I'm almost positive they will at some point), there needs to be a way to renew the competition. This can be done with new levels, but it can also be done by adding a broiler highscore mode of play.
