Have you ever seen a melee attack in Halo? That's the best example of hitting someone with the butt of a weapon.

Try this if you can't picture how easy it is:
Put your hands up as if you were holding a rifle, with your left hand holding the barrel steady for aiming and your right hand holding the butt of the gun, with finger on the trigger. I think we can agree that it's easy to quickly remove your finger from the trigger and to drop the butt from your shoulder.
Do these two simultaneously, with a speed consistent with hitting someone:
-Arc your left hand either (1) up so that it is over your left shoulder or (2) left so that it's next to your left bicep.
-Arc your right hand either (1) underneath the gun, bringing the underside of the hilt into your enemy's face, or (2) right, making the butt hit your enemy in the side of his head.
Both examples use the point between your hands as a point of rotation, so that part of the gun pretty much remains in the same spot.
In both (1) and (2), the gun barrel never points at you, and only accidental discharge of the firearm is possible, since your finger needs to be off the trigger. It's also easy to put the gun right back up to aim.
