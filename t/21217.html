#403973 24 2008-01-25T06:15:02Z Keron Cyst
I just saw it today. Kind of surprised me, this thread already appearing

It was very... unique; I'll tell that much. Basically, to sum, think of an American, more alien-like Godzilla in New York with a monstrous twist (heh, and that one addition by its writers is probably the scariest feature of the movie)... with the ENTIRE movie being shown from the perspective of a camcorder, from the point-of-view of generic civilians caught in the event of the creature's uprising. It was really interesting! I was warned beforehand by one of the people in my group who went to watch it that quite some people who saw it earlier had to leave because of motion-sickness, but none of the people in my theater showing were affected.

It's rather unrealistic, even ignoring the presence of the super monster, but I enjoyed it better than the Tom Cruise version of War of the Worlds. (The computer graphics were brilliant, perhaps only topped by few movies other than even Superman Returns, in my opinion.) Actually, I'll tell this much: if you thought War of the Worlds sucked, but thought that a different director, perhaps producer, and actor(s) could have turned it around into an amazing movie, you'll probably like Cloverfield, since to me it was like an excellent remake vaguely resembling WotW's style, in a sense. The post-apocalyptic/horror atmosphere was superbly developed in an exponentially better way than WotW, although I found the ending to be rather abrupt... too abrupt, IMO. The entire plot is a little like Cave Story in that you might feel like piecing together events afterwards, to comprehend it better, which is perhaps what Dave was referring to. It would be best enjoyed with a small group of friends who were terrified by Aliens or other movies of that nature (unfortunately, the supernatural doesn't faze me, so I personally didn't get the best effect, ha). Perhaps Resident Evil fans might like it even.

I thought Beth's lover (one of the chars.) was Tom Cruise at first, but the credits showed him to be a "Stahlwart-David" or something like that (don't exactly remember). He looked (and even acted) very similarly like Cruise. All of the actors/actresses were great. Expect a lot of "sh--" and "Oh, ---"... naturally.
