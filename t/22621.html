Sounds about right. What it doesn't mention, however, is that over time countries will merge and the ideas behind each ones version of 'justice' will compete. At that time, the one with the 'better' sense of justice will prevail - the tyrannical government will lose it's populace, as will an overly anarchic one, to more evenhanded laws. It's memetics on a global scale,

While this doesn't imply that 'justice will prevail' - purely because there is no absolute 'justice', it does go to show that over time societies evolve to better suit their populace. Seeing as people everywhere are (fundamentally) the same, this implys that laws are somewhat justified in being upheld because they have evolved that way. If they weren't so good, then they would change (or are currently still refining).
