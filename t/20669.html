Yes, that's right - it's time to make a DDA with Quasi-Random Drones!

RULES
All participants must make a DDA that follows all the following restrictions:
- It must be at least 800 frames long
- There must be at least 2 total laser/chaingun drones (all quasi-random, of course)
- There must be at least 5 total seeker zaps (all quasi-random, of course)
- There must be at least 4 points at which a drone drone may turn in either of two directions
- In each possibility, N must survive the full 800 frames

JUDGING
It will be judged as follows:
- DDA Length: Out of 25
- Close Calls/Entertainment level: Out of 25
- Effect of the quasi-random turns on the map: Out of 50

I will be judging, but if anybody else wants to, feel free to PM me.

If you're interested in trying, post here to sign up. As soon as 5 DDA's have been submitted, there will be no new sign-ups.
I expect this to take a while, so take your time.

Have fun!
