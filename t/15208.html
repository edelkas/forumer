The Caero CompositioN - Issue four - The Obby/McP Fiasco



It is a known fact that we love our pranks here at The Metanet Forums. With masquerades like The Kablizzy Fiasco and The Pony Parade, and riddling mysteries like The Y&BB and The Legacy: Origins.

Just recently, a few members decided to have another go at it. The plan being that Obby post his friendship resignment towards McP, and have "hired actors" help fuel the fire by taking one side or the other. Of course, all hell was unleashes, and many members joined in the storm of arguments and flame wars. And while the acting was horrible, and it was nothing but a smaller "Kablizzy Fiasco", it still fooled many, many members. Myself included.

I guess the funny thing is how realistic it seemed. We truly believed that Obby and McP were that foolish. With their reputation, this probably ended up being easier than they thought. The first two posts here sum everything up nicely. Also, big ups to Tsukatu for calling it. Nothing gets past that guy. And Tanner rocked the hell out of his rants.

See you on the next,
Nate Sly.
