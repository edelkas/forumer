I Took some of my free time to make a icon for N Game 1.4, Thanks for downloading. You must have Winrar to open the rar.

Preview (ver2)


How to install:
-First, download it and extract it where you want.

-Now, right click N Folder (Or if you want the Exe ) and select "Properties".

-Hit the Customize Tab, and select "Change Icon".

-A new window will pop-up, hit browse at the top of it and search where you extracted it.

-Select it, and hit OK, and the same on the Properties window.

-Have fun with a nice looking N Folder/Exe.
