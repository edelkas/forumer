Right, let's say (hypothetically) that we all agreed that it was reasonable to have a maximum of two maps per page.

The current options too stop people posting more are:
1) Ask them politely not to in the comments
2) Put a warning in the NUMA system
3) Simply block submission of maps when the user has two on the page already

I am here with another suggestion. How about they can submit as many maps as they want, but they queue up? Then, once they have two on a page (or whatever we agree on), they have to wait for a new page then the map in their queue that's been waiting the longest gets submitted.

Possible problems:
1) They submit hundreds of maps in one crazy map-submitting bout and we're forced to enjoy their work for months
2) Arachnid can't implement such a system

Possible solutions:
1) a) Make it so the queue becomes full eventually at a certain number where no more can be submitted
1) Put a warning in the NUMA system
1) c) If they haven't logged onto NUMA, their maps aren't automatically submitted
2) He can ask me for help, as it would certainly be possible and I know people who would think it's a doddle. Of course, Arachnid probably would be able to do it on his own... but still.

What do you think?

#74896 26 2005-06-15T10:18:32Z Arachnid
Ok, I've implemented this, and credited Sweep with the idea on the news page. If you find bugs, please let me know.

To clarify and reassure:
- When a map is added to the "Newest" page from the queue, its creation date is updated. A little odd, I admit, but this at least ensures a map doesn't get popped out of the queue - straight into the second page of results.
- When multiple maps are popped off the queue at the same time, the map posted last will be shown at the top. This means that if you have nothing in the queue or on the "Newest" page and you post a map, your map will end up right at the top, and the maps that were popped from the queue will be below it.

Edit:
Quote: Teedle, 2005-06-15T06:35:02Z
Yeah.... but maybe they're all too lazy and/ or stuipid to change page numbers?
Perhaps I wasn't clear: Of all the people viewing the first page of a listing, only 8% click the 'next page' link. 10 per page seems to be fine.

A couple of other things I forgot to mention originally:
- Thanks for giving me something interesting, practical, but fairly simple to work on, sweep. For those that care, I was able to implement the system almost entirely within the database (using a trigger), which was interesting, and made it really simple.

- The 'avalanche effect' occurred to me, too, but I doubt it'll be an issue. In the worst case scenario, adding one map could cause one map per user who has one in the queue to be added to the what's new page, but not only is that unlikely, the main result is that those that add lots of maps at once don't get as long on the Newest page. Boo hoo.
Absolute worst case, there are more than 10 people with maps in the queue, and the avalanche could spit out multiple maps per user. Once again, not that much of a disaster and still a huge improvement on how it was previously.
Personally, I don't think this will be a problem. Since I don't track new NUMA maps too closely, I'd appreciate it if interested parties let me know if this sort of thing seems to be a problem.

- The queue becoming backlogged and huge. I don't think this will be a problem, either. I'll keep an eye on the queue and see what happens, though.

- Mods. I'm not _against_ mods - I believe I was the person to first mention them (as something I was considering) - but I've tried to debunk the 'mods are the ultimate power' sort of myth. Even with a good system, there's a limit to how much information I can practically give mods, and without enough information, it's going to be really hard to, for example, definitely determine if someone is cheating.
I believe the queue solves one of the biggest problems people had with NUMA and were calling for mods for, so we'll see how it goes.
