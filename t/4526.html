Alright, I've got a whole load of new maps that I want you guys to see. Keep in mind that this isn't an episode, just a bunch of episodes.

My Gorgeous VIP List: As I made it I considered NC7. If it ever happens, and if the theme is small maps, that'll be one of my submissions. I really like the tiles and the polish.

Dessert: I really, really like this one. There's a bunch of gold as an extra challenge. I particularly like the strategies involved in getting the gold in the rocket/mine area.

Groggy: A pluish map. It was originally fleshed out when I thought the theme for NC7 was going to be maximo maps. I like it a lot, even though it may not be as good as the original plue.

Bing Bong, Avon Calling: I love the tiles on this one. I also love how it plays. It's a puzzle/action map with lots of gold challenges. Slave to Lamp: I'm not quite sure what this was inspired by. It reminds me of tktktk's Heroic. I don't really know what to make of it. It's a race/action map that's very queer, but I think I like it.

Unrequited: A mine-field heavy map. It may be too long and hard, but I really like how each individual part works. I tried to keep everything somewhat open-ended, and I think I succeeded to some extent.

Vespa: A drone-heavy map. I don't like this one as much as I like the others, but I still think it's fairly fun. Mostly inspired by maximo's maps, but all in all it doesn't really play like his drone maps.

I've been working on these levels for a month or two now, and I've really poured my heart into them.
