#117059 3 2005-11-22T18:42:19Z Aries_Griff
This is a really tricky level. Both the jump onto the ledge after the thwumps and the jump up over the mines to get up to where the floor guard is require you to use incline jumping. You don't need to really do it for the jump onto the ledge after the thwumps, but the last jump you do have to do it as far as I know. Basically the idea is to get a good amount of momentum going up the slope, so if you let go of the arrows, youd be sliding uphill. Then hit jump without hitting left or right. If you are going fast enough, and not hitting any keys while jumping, that should rocket you off the slope at pretty much a right angle to the slope's incline. And you have to be fast coming off the ledge after the thwumps onto the incline and going up the incline or else you get shot. It's tough, so good luck.
