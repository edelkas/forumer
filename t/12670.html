#231260 18 2006-09-20T21:56:07Z Twistkill
Quote: xdude_rox, September 20, 2006 05:44 pm
I want to start over new, how do I delete my .sol file?
Read this thread for information on where it's located, and then just delete it. When you start up N again, it'll automatically create a blank .sol file.

Alternatively, you can right-click N's window, click on the settings tab, and drag the local storage slider all the way to the left. Close N, reopen it, and your game should be clear. Make sure to go back to the slider and place it all the way to the right again, or else it won't save your progress at all.
