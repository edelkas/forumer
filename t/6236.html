Since the minimum amount of rated maps required for the authors list has been augmented, I would like to suggest that NUMA's review system be configured so that you vote and give your review at the same time. For example, you write your review and underneath the review box there would be a scroll-down vote box that you had to vote with before submitting your review. I know plenty of people don't vote because you have to do it seperately from reviewing and it takes up time and internet credit.

What do you think?

Edit: If you don't like the way I suggested, we could take out the 'compulsory' part and just make it so that you could be able to submit a vote and a review at the same time. IE there could be a scroll down vote box as suggested above but it could be optional.
