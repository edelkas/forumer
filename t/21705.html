Are you a mapmaking highscorer? A highscoring mapmaker? A general jack-of-all-trades? This is the competition for you.

Round 6F - Concealed Lasers, 127952
Mapmaking and highscoring in progress.

Mapmaking
- Based on a theme, all participants will make a new map or submit an old map.
- Each participant (and the three judges) will be asked to select their two favorite and two least favorite maps of the round.
- The lowest-scoring mapmaker(s) will be eliminated.
- The highest-scoring mapmaker(s) will take a lotto ticket (see lotto tickets, below).

Highscoring
- After I resubmit the chosen map(s) from the mapmaking section under Nreality's privatecontest function, all participants will highscore on this map.
- The lowest-scoring highscorer(s) will be eliminated.
- The highest-scoring highscorer(s) will take a lotto ticket (see lotto tickets, below). The maker of the map cannot win on his own map.

Lotto Tickets
Every time a player wins a round, they take a lotto ticket. When a player is eliminated, if they have a lotto ticket, they still have a chance of winning the competition: at the time that there are three players left, one of the lottery tickets will be chosen at random and that player will make up the fourth player in the finals (see finals, below). As well as one lotto ticket from the eliminated players being chosen, one lotto ticket from the remaining three players will be chosen as well. That player will get an extra point for the final rounds.

Finals
When the final four players (three remaining + one lotto ticket) are chosen, the finals begin. The finals consist of six head on competitions between the pairs of two finalists. Every pair of contestants competitively highscore and mapmake against each other (judged by three judges). Each finalist will receive a point for each head-on mapmaking session they win, each head-on highscoring section they win and if their lotto ticket is chosen.

Prizes
First place receives a ded map and a sig-tag
Second place receives a sig-tag
