#167656 4 2006-04-22T03:06:14Z Tsukatu
Get Process Explorer. This nifty little widget will let you take a closer look at processes. Thing is, it might be a specific thread within the process that's causing trouble (as is not uncommon for me if I leave Azureus on overnight).

For example, when my explorer.exe starts going ballistic and eating all of my CPU and memory, it's not the fault of explorer.exe, but rather the thread ntdll.dll!RtAllocateHeap that is within explorer.exe.

So yeah, get Process Explorer and have it boot on startup, and then right-click on the problem process, go Properties -> Threads, and kill the specific thread that is causing the problems.
