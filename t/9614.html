Engrish.com

It's a collection of translation mishaps, mostly from Japan - like the "Satan coffee" shop and "Marxism tea" which is allegedly God's favourite. Many products include English words just to look stylish, you see, and many people don't really know or care what they mean. It's kinda similar to the quasi-random kanji you often see on Western clothing, tatoos, etc.
#174951 2 2006-05-12T11:31:18Z JeCa
An easy way to translate a swedish word into something really different is to use the expression "I took it for granted".
Directly translated into swedish you say that expression like this
"Jag tog det fr givet."
For granted becomes "fr givet" which sounds almost like for-given.
So if you're not really sure about what things mean, you could make agbig missunderstanding without being aware of it...
