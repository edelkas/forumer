Instead of the usual one-thread-one-map thing, I decided that a thread for a certain kind of level (the best kind!) would be good for the organization of this whole subforum. In order for this to work, though, a type of format for entry is necessary. So, post your levels like this for minimum clutter:

TITLE: "Erkle's day at the zoo". Self-explanatory.
DIFFICULTY: In order for some regularity, let's limit ourselves to grading the maps by words [Easy-Normal-Hard] or (if you absolutely have to) by numbers or turnips [always out of 5].
PLOT: Put as much or as cilantro explanation as you like here. Anywhere from one line describing what it is that's going on ["save the princess"] to a professional-sounding prologue ["It was cold that day, my friends; cold like the beating of an old man's heart, like the winters of blah blah blah..."]. Hopefully yours will be somewhere in between.
SYMBOLS: If an area or a thing on the map is supposed to be something, put it here. Common ones would probably be "Mines are dynamite" or "Zap Drones are security guards". I suppose no real stringent format is necessary here, I have faith in your grammar. ...bad idea, I know.
DEMO: Is a demo included in the text file? Yes or No. Not necessary, but handy and cool-looking for some. Make sure that there's an obvious space between the level code and the demo code iif you have one.
NOTES: This is where you put all person-specific bits in. Like, "this one took me 8 hours to make", or "block placement inspired by Monet". Also, this would be a good place to mention if your level has sequels planned, or if there's other junk in the text file for some oddball reason.

Remember, "N/A" or "None" is a good alternative to just leaving something blank. However, the more things you leave blank, the less interesting it's going to look, and the less people will want to try it.


For copy+pasting goodness, and to make the format as simple as possible, use this.
[B]TITLE: [/B]
[B]DIFFICULTY: [/B]
[B]PLOT: [/B]
[B]SYMBOLS: [/B]
[B]DEMO: [/B]
[B]NOTES: [/B]

Well? Get crackin'! Be one of the cool people to get theirs in on the first page, like me.

P.S.
Try to keep uninteresting "cool map make more plz" posts to a minimum.

EDIT: You can write as much uninteresting crap below the level's specs as you want, seeing as you've already made your post useful.
