#175084 4 2006-05-12T23:23:34Z Twistkill
Quote: Rebar, May 12, 2006 07:18 pm
But how do I put it in? And can I make it any smaller?

You have to host the image. Go to www.imageshack.us and upload the file there. Then it will give you some codes. Go the bottom one that says "Direct link to image", select all, and copy. Then go to your edit sig window, click the IMG tab, paste the URL there, and then it will automatically put the tags around the URL. Click Update Signature and it will show up in your sig.
