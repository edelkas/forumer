The Frog and the Turtle

The frog jumps,
with a leap and a bound!
he races past the lilies
and the ever-so poisonous snakes.
The turtle slowly swims,
and takes his time,
making progress slowly,
and always getting home.
The frog races around,
snatches all the files,
closely missing every pitfall,
a show of miraculous agility.
The turtle, seeing the frog, tries to go faster,
but the frog is always just one step ahead
of the turtle, who finishes just barely slower.
