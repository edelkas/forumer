Sig of the Week

Themes for Season 2
Spoiler: Week 1 - Fonts/TextThis week we are focusing on Fonts and Text. This is fairly open ended in that you have to make a sig that incorporates some kind of text, but trying to find the correct font for the type of sig you make is much harder. Pulling off the right font can be a challenge, but if you do it well I'm sure it will pay off in the end. Good luck!
Spoiler: Week 2 - EarthThis week is all about Earth. Don't jump to conclusions about what you have to make. There are so many different meanings and ideas that come when one thinks of the word Earth it's overwhelming. Use your imagination and don't create the very first thing that comes to your head. Good luck!
Spoiler: Week 3 - Comic Book CharactersWelcome to Week 3! We'll be focusing on Comic Book Characters this week and I hope to see quite a bit of talent coming out of this one. We all have our favourite comic book characters even if we have never read the comics that correspond with the character, or read a comic that not a lot of people of our generation have heard of but had a character you easily identified with. Though the theme itself isn't very interpretive the amount of things you can do with it are broad. That's what I hope to see this week. A broad spectrum of heroes or villains, with or without superpowers. Good luck and can't wait to see what you all come up with!
Spoiler: Week 4 - WaterThe theme this week is water and by that I mean all sorts of things. Water has many properties and hopefully one of them will spark your attention enough to create something new and different. For instance, water encompasses all 3 states of matter: solid, liquid and gas. It nourishes life and can destroy entire cities. It can hold an immense amount of heat or radiate freezing temperatures.
Whatever your theme is for Water, I hope you do the best you can. Good luck!
Spoiler: Week 5 - BiologyWelcome to Week 5. I was a little disappointed with the turnout from last week, as only 2 artists entered and they were both newcomers. But I hope this week gives a lot of potential turnout, as I think biology has many cool aspects and pictures I'm sure you could find to tweak and make them look cooler. For this week I don't care what kind of sig you give me as long as it relates to ONE OF THE MANY DIFFERENT KINDS OF BIOLOGY. Research biology a little bit to help give you an idea of what you would like to do. Good luck, and I hope to see a lot of entries (good or bad) from all of you this week!
Spoiler: Week 6 - Warhol-esqueWeek 6 brings the famous art renderings of Andy Warhol. He is, and continues to be, one of my favourite artists and it is because of this that I want to see what you all can do with his style, yet still add in a drop or two of your own. Please look up his work on Google or some other image search engine so that you can get the color style down correctly. Also, this week ONLY if I happen to receive 5 or more entries I will be doubling everybody's points received for the week. This means a big boost into the lead could be made by one of our imagers here, or it could mean no hope for the rest of us. Either way, good luck to you all and I hope to see many entries this week!
Spoiler: Week 7 - NINJAS!Week 7 brings the stealth, speed, agility and overall bad-assness of ninjas. Whether you're an innocent bystander or an evil, magic-wielding demon bent on taking over the world, ninja's are usually somewhere close by, making sure you don't step too far out of line.
Make an image with a ninja. That's all I ask.

Welcome to Season 2 of the Sig of the Week (SOTW) contest! Last season you guys exhibited great skill and a great amount of improvement in all your images. This season I'm proud to see a handful of new imagers and a lot of the old veterans hanging around. Hopefully you all take interest in this and make this season bigger and better than the last. Make sure to read all of the rules and have fun!

---

Point System
1st - 5 points
2nd - 4 points
3rd - 3 points
4th - 2 points
5th - 1 point
everyone else gets no points.


---

The Rules
1. PM your entries each week to southpaw with either a direct link to the image or the image itself. Only one entry per person.

2. You must have created the signature yourself. No stealing. If you think someone has stolen someone else's signature, PM me with your complaint, be sure to give me a link.

3. You must follow the theme for the week. No exceptions.

4. Your signature must not be any bigger than 600 pixels wide and 200 pixels tall.

5. You cannot switch or substitute your signatures after they have been submitted.

6. All tie-breakers will be decided by three random users on IRC. If they entered a signature and it just so happens to tie, someone else will be substituted in their place to vote.

7. Your signature must be in by Wednesday of that week. No exceptions.

8. Previously made signatures will not be allowed.

9. Advertising of any kind is not allowed. Period. The only exception is if the theme of the week has something to do with advertising.

10. You may not post the signature image you have made for the current week in your own signature that appears at the bottom of your posts. You may, however, post the signature image you have made for the current week within your own image thread. If you post the image in other people's image threads your signature will not be included for voting at the end of the week.

11. VIOLATION OF THESE RULES MAY RESULT IN A BAN FROM COMPETING FOR ONE OR MORE WEEKS AT SOUTHPAW's DISCRETION

---

I will be keeping a list of each persons scores for the past week and the month. Every month a person will be recognized as the winner of that month, as well as earn a free signature OR a dedication map. An overall score sheet will be compiled so that everybody can see how many points they have accumulated per week. The total scores at the end of the month will be posted as well. Good luck and have fun.
