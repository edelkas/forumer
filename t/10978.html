Read:
Ok. Now that i've got your attention, I will introduce to you the 'Continue' game.

Point:
The main point of this game is to continue on a story.

Rules
You cannot end the story in any sort of way.
You cannot write more than 10 words every turn.
You cannot have two or more consecutive posts.
You cannot have two or more posts in a row (same rule as above).
You have to respond to last poster's punctiation at the end (if at the end, it has a . the next poster has to start new sentence following the same story).

Example:
Poster 1: I had a cracker
Poster 2: and I gave it to a bird.
Poster 3: The bird flew away,
Etc.

Fun:
Have fun!

Starting:
The poster after me can start off the game.
