Bullet Time
When toggled with preferably the X key, everything (even your lifespan timer) gradually slows down to 25% speed, but N moves at 50% speed

- Sound effects become slower and deeper in pitch
- N can see each Chaingun bullet and the air resistance trailing them (to tell what direction each bullet is traveling at)
- Gauss projectiles travel at sniper rifle bullet speed; just not instantaneous and laser-like

The sacred Golden Ingot adds 1 second to the N Bar (located above lifespan timer); time in the N Bar is drained as normal time while bullet time is toggled, and when time runs out, N can't use bullet time.
