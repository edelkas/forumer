I'm not going to read through all the other topics so I'm just going to say if somethng I say was repeated on other threads then it just means they're really good ideas and should be used! - but several of these ideas shoudl be unique

continued mutilation - mentioned a few times and still good - if a player gets zapped by a bot and falls through another bot onto a mine his corpse should get zapped off the next bot and explode on the mine and flying limbs should act independently the same (goes for all bots, explosives, and bouncies/trampolines/springs - whatever you call them) - but maybe for the one-way platforms the bodyparts should bounce once then fall through

my take on weapons - rather than an all-or-nothing weapons system why not incorporate them as elements of N's crazy action-puzzle gameplay? for example make a couple of weapons like shurikens and smoke bombs.. and make it a one-off pickup like gold - but only able to carry one at a time.. now if you have one button for 'weapon' it uses the item in the slot and that item is gone for the rest of the level (for example shuriken flies horizontally from point thrown, and will destroy the first bot it hits.. smoke bombs might stop enemies tracking you for 10 seconds or so) so you could make puzzle elements like an extrance you can't get past without taking out 2 laser turrets but there are only 2 shurikens on the level and there are 2 other chasing zap bots

gameplay variations - ideas for toggle-able gametype variants
implement a scoring system based on amount of gold collected
timer on/off - playing without timer removes gold so you can't score (or perhaps greys out gold so you just walk through in front of it so it can still hide other buttons)
gamespeed - half (molasses mode), normal (mainstream mode) and double speed (monkey mode)

multiplayer modes - 2 players maybe more if possible
co-op - one timer, both must complete the level (players active until both in door for extra challenge)
race - each obstacle only reacts to the person who activated it (player colour-coded doors/buttons/gold/etc.) and the winner finishes the level first
hoard - same level objects, seperate timers, must complete level individually (if one player succeeds they end the level and wait for the other, in one dies they retry the level alone but timer doesn't go back up as usual - winner is who's timer runs out last
tag - players have separate timers. all obstacles (bots/mines/doors/etc.) are removed (keep trampoline/springs and gold though) and one player randomly starts as it and just needs to try and touch the other to tag them (with a delay of 1-2 seconds to prevent looping tags when overlapping). 'it's timer runs down so gold is still functional - the winner is the last one with time left on their timer

ability to select colour of your player and their blood

ability to pick colours on blocks in level editor

wacky gravity zones - make it possible to change gravity in some areas like in Serious Sam so some Escheresque levels have you running up walls and across the ceiling being careful not to jump out of the gravity zone and into another where you might suddenly be plummetting to your doom! (also don't forget controls might seem reversed from it )
