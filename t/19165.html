Level data, from start to "|"

This space is filled with digits,
the first digit represents the up-left-corner.
The second one represents the square below it.
And so on for the whole colomn, then we move a square to the right
untill the "|"...
The digits represent a type of block:

0 = Empty square
1 = Filled square
2 = 45 slope open on the top-right
3 = 45 slope open on the top-left
4 = 45 slope open on the down-left
5 = 45 slope open on the down-right
6 = Hollow slope open on the top-right
7 = Hollow slope open on the top-left
8 = Hollow slope open on the down-left
9 = Hollow slope open on the down-right
: = Globe slope open on the top-right
; = Globe slope open on the top-left
< = Globe slope open on the down-left
= = Globe slope open on the down-right
> = 30 open horizontal slope open on the top-right
? = 30 open horizontal slope open on the top-left
@ = 30 open horizontal slope open on the down-left
A = 30 open horizontal slope open on the down-right
B = 30 filled horizontal slope open on the top-right
C = 30 filled horizontal slope open on the top-left
D = 30 filled horizontal slope open on the down-left
E = 30 filled horizontal slope open on the down-right
F = 30 open vertical slope open on the top-right
G = 30 open vertical slope open on the top-left
H = 30 open vertical slope open on the down-left
I = 30 open vertical slope open on the down-right
J = 30 filled vertical slope open on the top-right
K = 30 filled vertical slope open on the top-left
L = 30 filled vertical slope open on the down-left
M = 30 filled vertical slope open on the down-right
N = Halfopen square open at the top
O = Halfopen square open at the left
P = Halfopen square open at the bottom
Q = Halfopen square open at the right
# = halfopen square open at the right.

Item data, from "|" to "!"

First comes the item number, a "^", then the X-cordinate, then the Y-cordinate, then eventual special things depending on 

the item.

Item name: Gold
Item number: 0
Special numbers: 0
Output: 0^X,Y
Comment: None

Item name: Bounce block
Item number: 1
Special numbers: 0
Output: 1^X,Y
Comment: None

Item name: Jump pad
Item number: 2
Special numbers: 2
Output: 2^X,Y,Xdir,Ydir
Comment: Xdir and Ydir are the speed numbers when you hit the jump pad

Item name: Gauss turret
Item number: 3
Special numbers: 0
Output: 3^X,Y
Comment: None

Item name: Floor guard
Item number: 4
Special numbers: 1
Output: 4^X,Y,1
Comment: The "1" cannot be changed for some reason

Item name: The ninja
Item number: 5
Special numbers: 0
Output: 5^X,Y
Comment: None

Item name: Zap drone/Lazer drone
Item number: 6
Special numbers: 4
Output: 6^X,Y,AI,folw,shoot,dir
Comment: AI:  0 = Surfacefollow CW
               1 = Surfacefollow CCW
               2 = Dumb CW
               3 = Dumb CCW
               4 = Alterrnating
               5 = Quasi-random
               # = Standing still
         dir:  0 = Facing right
               1 = Facing down
               2 = Facing left
               3 = Facing up
               # = Facing down
         folw: 0 = Doesn't follow you
               1 = Follows you
         shoot:0 = Doesn't shoot at you
               1 = Shoots at you with lazer
               2 = Shoots at you with machine gun
               # = HIDDEN ENEMY (This enemy disappears when you go into the editor)
         If folw = 1 and shoot = 1, folw will turn into 0

Item name: Normal door/Trapdoor/Locked door
Item number: 9
Special numbers: 7
Output: 9^X,Y,pos,trap,dX,dY,lock,Xadd,Yadd
Comment: pos: 0 = Vertical
               1 = Horizontal
               # = Vertical
         trap: 0 = Normal door
               1 = Trapdoor
               # = Trapdoor
         dX:   X in squares of the door
         dY:   Y in squares of the door
         lock: 0 = Normal door
               1 = Locked door
               # = Locked door
         Xadd: Additional X-movement of the door measured in squares
         Yadd: Additional Y-movement of the door measured in squares

         If trap = 1 and lock = 1, trap will turn into 0
         In case of trap- or locked door, the X and Y is from the switch,
         not the door.
