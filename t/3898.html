#3539 1 2005-07-02T06:54:59Z Arachnid
I've just introduced a couple of new features I'm quite proud of: See Also and Suggestions.

Each map now has a link 'See similar maps'. Clicking on this will take you to a listing of the maps the system has determined are most like this map.

When logged in, you will now have an additional link in your left menu, 'Suggestions'. Clicking on this displays a list of maps NUMA thinks you are most likely to enjoy. The accuracy of this list depends on your favorites list reflecting which maps you like, so ensure your favorites list is up to date and interesting.

Feedback on these features is really appreciated - I'm particularaly interested in knowing how accurate you think they are.
