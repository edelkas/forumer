N+ LSS Forum

Greetings to my fellow N+ players.

In the absence of a structured map-sharing organization like NUMA and the inability to freely upload and download maps to a common place, I propose that we form the N+ LSS - an N+ Level Sharing Society.


Within this group, we all add each other as friends over X-Box live and distribute maps that we have either made or collected from other people.

I'll try to keep this first post updated with a list of members and the maps that they currently have available to distribute. Members should periodically return and update their own posts to indicate any new maps they obtain and can distribute to others (so, for example, if I send someone a map, I'll update the list of maps they have available here if they're willing to distribute it further). Please note the author of the maps you are distributing and the type of map (survival, single, co-op, or race). For co-op maps I think it would be nice to note the minimum number of players that can complete the map or episode, so people are aware they shouldn't try some co-op maps with less than 3 or 4 people, and that some can be completed with just 1.

I'd like to refrain from using this thread from discussion of individual maps and keep it mostly administrative - though I definitely encourage discussing maps of other people in other threads if enough people join the LSS to warrant it.

You do not need to have made your own maps to join the LSS and get new maps, though a willingness to distribute them is appreciated (but again not required).

All that said, I'll begin by offering up a single episode and a couple of maps that I have made for the taking. I'll probably be online off and on this week, so anyone who sends me a friend request can be the first to get some new maps from the LSS. If there aren't too many people who are on at that time asking for maps, I'll even be happy to host a full co-op game for everyone and we can play together.

Zaphall: Normally avoiding a floorguard is easy, but a long electric fence that dictates when it is safe to jump complicates matters.
