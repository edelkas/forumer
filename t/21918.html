#419157 2 2008-03-20T16:10:23Z Pikman
Pressing [+] selects the launchpad for placing. To place it, you need to hold one of the following: W, A, S, D, W+A, W+D, D+A, or D+S. Each of those stands for a direction. While holding any of those keys, click in the spot where you want the launchpad, and it should appear.
