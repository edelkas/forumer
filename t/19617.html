#370907 8 2007-10-09T23:58:28Z Weisslenny0
Ah, this is the other topic that I haven't commented on. Wow, 5 songs to do. OK, here we go...

Force Majeure
Nice start; the accidentals are interesting. I really liked the piece when there was only about 1 minute to go, because it combines the original melody with awesome percussion. When it got to 20 secs left I was really feeling the beat etc., and I like the way that it can loop well.

Hexaplex
Interesting start. Much slower than Force Majeure, but I liked it. I liked the percussion when there was only 50 or so second left. It really added to the melody. Again, good ending; nice and loopable.

Red Telephone
Ah, a longer piece. Again an interesting start; it started off like a scale, but it wasn't. Interesting. I liked when the melody came in after about 30 secs and the chords really added to it. In general the piece just got better as it went along. I think that the faster notes could be a bit louder so they stand out more though. I liked when the faster still notes came in after 2 or so mins because they added to the melody, then it went back to the original and that sounded really nice. It got interesting at the end because it changed, but I just thought it would repeat. Not sure that the fade-out worked - I think it would have been better if it looped.

Vive Cathodique!
I really liked this piece, and the beginning especially . It just got really nice once the 2nd instrument came in with quavers; in one way it was a slow piece but another fast, and I really liked that. Then the guitar-like instrument came in, and it added to the melody nicely. Currently there's just over a minute left and there's the awesome melody with the guitar, and that's awesome. Then another instrument (glockenspiel?) comes in, and that's even more awesome . Hmm, interesting end. TGhe melody changes, but it's repeatable I think, and sounds really good. In short, I added it to my iTunes "awesome music" playlist .

Qubcois Folk Hero
Nice beat at the beginning. I was tapping the beat, and that was fun . I like how it starts of slowly melody-wise, but the actual piece is fast. The synth is awesome too; I liked it. Currently just over 2 minutes left and it's still sounding really good. I can imagine this being in a sort of space-like level or something - not evil boss music, but would work well for levels. Now just over a minute left and the guitar sounds really nice. I like how the melody works, and the slide in the chords also works, and more importantly they worked together. The fade-out at the end was good this time, but not for game purposes, I think. Still, good to have a loopable track that fades out.

And just a general comment for all the pieces, they all sound like awesome game music. And thanks for giving me an enjoyable time listening to them
Lenny
