#64876 4 2005-05-11T05:50:54Z TornadoTK
0 = Normal
1 = No Left
2 = No Right
4 = No Jump

One number, multiple combinations.

Example:
No Left, No Jump = 1 + 4 = 5
No Left, No Right = 1 + 2 = 3
Normal = 0
No Left, No Right, No Jump = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7
