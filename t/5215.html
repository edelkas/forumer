#108457 3 2005-10-15T11:23:23Z KinGAleX
Teh Review:

00-0 Desolace - Amazing tileset; vaguely reminded me of a few Metanet levels, but not in a bad way. Optimally placed rockets. The right amount of gold. Seriously, I worship you and your maps.

00-1 Wounded - Once again, amazing tileset: I don't even think I need to bother mentioning that any more. As a rule, I don't like chainguns, but somehow you pulled it off.

00-2 Patronage - Great map. Makes you think before you jump or whatever. A bit minimal, but still good.

00-3 Circumscribed - Almost certainly inspired by Toshosoft's map from his Episode. I can't remember the name. Wider than his was, and slightly easier. The floorguard added some annoyance though - once again, in a good way.

00-4 Return of the Native - This level screams "CLASSIC PITCH!" Very reminiscent of your earlier maps, and just as good. The exit switch was hard to get, and the gausses well placed.

The Package - Teh Rox0r - As close to perfect as you could get. There's just something about your maps that I love. Keep it up!
