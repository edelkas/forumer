How can I make drones which don't move?? I saw them in some maps and would like to put them into own maps.
#83282 2 2005-07-13T19:54:08Z HeartView
Put locked doors on all sides that do not contain a tile (wall) and then force N to trigger the switches for those doors (by putting them on a square the ninja has to cross or on the ninja directly at the start of the map).
#83303 3 2005-07-13T22:07:32Z Sendy
Or, much nicer IMO is to just put the last value of the object as a NaN. It doesn't do any harm and freezes the drone without all those clunky, noisy, and possibly laggy doors.

Of course some people think code modifying is something to be frowned upon, but I can't honestly see why if it's all fair on the player's end.
