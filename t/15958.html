#14108 1 2007-02-18T09:37:12Z astro
Based on a project of mine and a suggestion of Tanner's, I give you the online #N IRC Quotes Repository, a place to submit and rate quotes. It functions in a similar way to bash.org, except that:
  • ratings are given from 0 to 5
  • it's designed for use by this community
  • there's no moderation process
  • it doesn't suffer the bash.org problem of older quotes getting better ratings (indeed, it suffers the opposite to a small extent)
Try it out!
