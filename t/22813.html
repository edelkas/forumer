Yes, I know that everyone has to have a website devoted to their "company," but this isn't another web design sites. It will just be my own personal site for my every whim. I originally had this site for a school time line project for AP European History but it had been sitting unused for about a year. So I decided to make use of it, and here it is. Well, some of it. It is still in the works, but I plan on having it up and running soon.

Some notes: First, the words on the main page (on the hand) do not function as links because Yahoo SiteBuilder does not support that (at least to my knowledge). I was considering using HostMonster for the site but I already was using Yahoo for this one and I'm too lazy to change it (plus, this one is linked to my dad's account, so I don't have to pay for it... yet.) Also, for those who have running sites (I know blue_tetris does), how wonderful in Project Wonderful? I'm considering using that to help cover some of the costs, though I don't like the idea of ads on a more minimalistic site. Or perhaps I would put up a PayPal donate button. Who knows?

That said, I will be most likely moving all of my forum-run comics onto this site (acting like I update those anymore ), and some other random stuff no one else here will really understand (inside jokes ftw!). Without further adieu:

My Website!

Comments, criticism, etc. would be much appreciated.
