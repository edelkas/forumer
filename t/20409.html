The background graphics are not going to happen. It's against Metanet's design philosophy for N - they want the player to be able to interact with everything they can see. Also, this has been suggested frequently before. Please search and read the pinned threads before making suggestions in the future.

Different level size has also been suggested repeatedly and will not happen, unless you buy N+, which necessarily has different level sizes for each platform due to differences in screen size and shape.
