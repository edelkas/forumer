Well considering this was the first real peice Ive produced with 3DS-Max, when I create it! Since college Ive been meaning to get back into annimation (thought about trying to get into it proffesionally) but its such a hard nut to crack, especially with such little experience. The fact I live in lil' old Tassie (the island on the bottom of Australia) doesnt really help either .

Whats wrong with the gold anyway Keron? Its only in one of the shots so I just made them semi transperant yellow squares, but it just didnt look right. So I turned them into diamonds instead. Simple solution, as I said I ran out of time very fast. I wouldve made this into a full 2min if not longer trailer had I had the time but as I lost the core files when the college decided to delete them and considering my home PC has always been too poor to run the programs I havent been able to dabble, which has really begun to annoy me. Ive just grabbed myself a new PC though and am after a good quality graphics card so I can get back into it, so hopefully Ill be able to produce some more stuff and finish the darn trailer propperly!

As for rendering, blergh dont ask. We were working on pretty sub-par machines so each frame took about 20secs to render. Fine for single images such as above, but for full blown annimation it was an absolute nightmare, and in the end I had to choose between quality and quantity of shots. Obviously a lesser ammount of well done shots was the better option (it doesnt matter how many shots you have if theyre all crap!), but I didnt get to use anywhere near as many of the models I had created (missile launcher, those little floor scuttling things etc) and even worse I didnt get to do a dieing Ninja (Id done a couple, but as the deaths are one of the best parts of N I really didnt want to put in a sub-par death scene in mine so I cut it), but hey hopefully over the new year I should be able to find my way around the program better and learn some easier ways to get things done.

I should get the DVD uploaded within the month, Ill have to upload it at work too because Ill be buggered if Im going to upload something that big on 56k! lol. Sorry to keep you waiting guys, but Im really thrilled at the amount of enthusiasm thats been shown, its really great!

Squiddy
