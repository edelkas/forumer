Hmmm...you've basically got two moderately difficult jumps. The first on the left, direct your ninja to the right so you don't hit the ceiling, and then quickly turn left. The second jump is trickier. Jump onto the middle platform and then head to the west edge (at about the centre of the level). Get as far as you can before running up and completing a perpendicular jump. If you don't know what that is, just run up and jump holding the opposite direction to do it.

That's about the easiest way to do it. You can view my demo if you like. Just paste it into the lower left textbox in the userlevels menu.
