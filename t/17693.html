Are you ready to find out who the best sig tagger is on metanet?

Sig tags come in all shapes and sizes...

The Rules:


1. There are no particular rules besides the ones stated below. Create your sickest sig tag and then post it in the forum. Judging will take place from there.

2. There are three categories:

i. sigatar set
ii. small sig tag
iii. large sig tag (aka signature)

Users may enter 3 submissions for each category. Judging criteria down below.

3. Contestants may only submit 3 sigs total. You may not submit 3 sigs for each category, but you are welcome to submit two sigs to one category. Please include a title so that judges can differentiate your sigs easier.

4. Anybody may submit sig tags between June 10th and July 31st. All sig tags must be ones that have been created during this contest. Sig tags can be cropped, or just pictures with a cool background, but make them however you want.

Judging:
There will be 5 judging slots, plus a sixth so that judges can submit their own sig tags and not have to rate their own. I don't know how long it takes to make an INCREDIBLE sig tag, but I figure within the next 6 weeks is good.

Contestants are invited to bribe others to vote for them. BRIBES MUST BE DONE VIA PM. ANY BRIBES FOUND POSTED IN THIS FORUM WILL RESULT IN DISQUALIFICATION.

Non-contestants are invited to campaign for their favorite. CAMPAIGNING MAY BE DONE IN THE FORUMS AND WILL NOT RESULT IN DISQUALIFICATION OF YOUR FAVORITE.
