Q: "N+", a console game?!
A: Yep, multiplayer N, basically, for the Xbox Live Arcade, Nintendo DS, and Sony PSP. N+ for DS and PSP is now in stores, and the Xbox Live version is purchasable from the comfort of your home.

Q: "Robotology?!"
A: C++ & OpenGL.

Q: Wait, wait, wait... so what's the deal with N v1.5?
A: Between N+ and Robotology, N v1.5 may not be out for a while. Mare & Raigan are planning on making two game modes, each with their own sets of highscores; the current mode will be a sort of "arcade" version, and the new one will have slightly different (supposedly easier to manage) walljump programming, and different Chaingun Drone behavior (no random bullet bursts), along with many other minor tweaks. Check out the specific News article regarding it for more information. You can also get more up to date news from the new blog: metablog.
