#431730 2 2008-04-26T18:30:59Z PsychoSnail
To watch the replay of a map on NUMA, you take the 1.4+ data and paste it in the userlevels box, and then directly after that data you paste the demo data. The demo data only gives which keys were pressed at what times. Without the level data, it's meaningless.

In the box, you should see this:
$mapname#author##longstringofdata#<THISISWHATYOUCOPY>#
Without the number signs.
It should look like this, for any future reference. The #'s mean any combination of digit(s). The $ is how many frames the demo is.
$:#|#|#|...etc
