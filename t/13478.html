Alright. The rules for this are as follows:

One person posts a sentence, but leaves the ending open with a "..." The next poster then fills in three words to complete it, and posts a new open-ended sentence.

Example:
Poster 1
Today I shot...

Poster 2
...my own head.
What would happen if...

Poster 3
...the forums closed?
Who would ever think to...

And so on and so forth. Have fun!
