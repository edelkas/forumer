I came into the Legacy team, and participated in Zenith under the auspice that I was there for my creativity and artistry. It seems, in retrospect, this was a misconception.

In the end, I made fourteen maps for Zenith, five of which were accepted, and four of those five edited. I hold no beef with any of the Legacy leaders, compilers, and editors for any of this, because I know that they just have different mindsets to me. I am entirely anti-Creative-Control, and for the artistry. Some of the other Legacy guys prefer commerciality and a sense of a conglomerate, as opposed to the parts of a whole. I felt a little slighted by some of the edits, but I do realise that this is just their ideologies as opposed to mine.

And so, I present to you the unedited unadulterated maps I made for Zenith, as submitted to the Legacy team. I quite like them.
