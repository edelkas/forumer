#21274 1 2008-08-28T13:47:43Z Spawn of Yanni
The new post on the Numa home page got me thinking - Hot Maps is essentially now being officially recognised as a replacement for the old "Newest Maps" page. I still firmly believe that Active Authors would be a better replacement, as long as you allow the user to go beyond just the 10 newest authors (by saying "Next 10" at the bottom or something, you know what I mean). The Active Authors page, I thought, was pretty fantastic when it first came out, since it was a great way of showing loads of new maps, and there was no need to worry about getting the page flooded by one author.

In my opinion, Active Authors should be the first page you see when you click on the Maps tab. I think most people took Hot Maps as the new Newest Maps page because it's the first thing you see after clicking Maps. (I'm assuming that Hot Maps wasn't -meant- to be the Newest Maps replacement, higher-ups correct me if I'm wrong.)

At the same time, if Active Authors is going to be serve as the Newest Maps page, whatever formula currently makes the Hot Maps page look how it does needs to be reworked. I first thought it was based on Dave's Float and Sink idea, but either it isn't, or it is based on that and the idea isn't working. It should really be doing what I feel it was first made to do, and what its name would suggest - creating a "top-rated of the moment" sort of list. I'm not sure exactly how it would work, perhaps some kind of weekly top-rated list or something.

Currently, the Active Authors page is almost entirely useless. As far as I know, no one seems to be using it. Instead, what we have is a sort of screwy new maps page in Hot Maps.

1. Hot Maps is more of a temporary Top Rated system. It shows which maps people have enjoyed more than others and so shows which maps YOU might find more enjoyable over others.

By showing 6 of a single author's maps it makes the page look ugly. We can already see the last 7 maps submitted by an author when we click on their newest map so there should be no reason for Active Authors to show us that.
Also, Active Authors is created in ascending order, with the person who submitted a map most recently at the bottom and the 10th person before them to submit a map at the top. The order is not Float and Sink and I believe someone needs to rework the entire page to show JUST ONE MAP and order it in DESCENDING ORDER.

3. Top-Rated of the week? If we're going to do that we may as well have a Top Rated page that has options. If you want to see that last 24 hours, or the last 3 days, week, month, year, or all time we should be able to. That would allow Top Rated to become useful again. It wouldn't just show AfternoonDragonStep04 all the time anymore. You could see the Top Rated map since 1 year ago and enjoy it. Then if you want to favorite it you won't have the issue of having to go look for it.
Also, if Top Rated were to come back it would need a higher weight to the ratings. Instead of just making everything out of 5 votes, Top Rated should be weighted by 10.

There. I said it.
