Moving Walls

One very special usage of thwumps which many great DDA makers love to use is called the Moving Wall method. Its becoming more common, and was popularized by Lucidiums Pressed for Time. When placed in a specific area, half-way engulfed in a tile, a thwump is placed facing the inside of the wall. See this diagram.



In the diagram above, as long as there is any tile in the red area, the thwump will move in the direction of the arrow when touched. In the last two cases, a little more care is needed when placing the thwumps. They will not obey the special Moving Wall rules if they are placed using 1/2 snap. The third thwump in the diagram must be placed exactly one pixel above its 1/2 snap position to work. The last thwump must be placed exactly one pixel left of its 1/2 snap position to work. Both of these can be done by code-editing. Knowing that the 2 numbers directly after the 8^ represent the x- and y-coordinates of the object, you know how to manipulate its position to move it one pixel in the direction you want (youll be subtracting 1 to do this either way, from the x-coordinate for a leftward moving wall, or from the y-coordinate for an upward moving wall).
