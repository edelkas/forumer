#24527 13 2004-07-10T12:05:30Z tunah
The animation problem seems to trigger at the point when the last sound effect finishes (so we have silence). Apparently this is a problem with the OSS support in the linux flash player. (Doesn't support ALSA in this day and age? ;-)
Found this info .
The workaround is to use the esd support instead. First, make sure you've got ESD installed (comes with gnome, doesn't it?). Then run
"esd &" to start esd in the background. Then start N.
ESD will automatically quit after a period of inactivity.
The downside for me is quite a bit of delay for sounds, so I just turn the volume to zero. (If turning the volume to zero disabled sounds completely, then ESD would be unneccesary, that'd be a nice feature to have).
Hope this helps.
