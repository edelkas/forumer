Here's a thought. How about mines that won't blow up when N first touches them? Instead, they would have a delay of about 2 seconds and then blow up. I can see creating maps that have these mines all over the place where N has to make it through "on time" or face the bitter end. A race to stay ahead of the explosions, so to speak.

A related idea to this would be to have mines that don't kill N on contact, but instead kill him when they explode. So you can set up "traps" for N that have triggers to start a chain reaction of mines blowing up.

Just some thoughts.
