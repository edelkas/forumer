#199427 2 2006-07-06T01:45:55Z Twistkill
I guess I'll help you.

When you downloaded N, it came with a bunch of .txts in it's folder. One of them is called userlevels.txt. It already has a bunch of them in it, but you can add as many more as you want. To do this, first read the instructions in the userlevels.txt file. Then go to NUMA. On the left-hand side, you will see links. You can click Top-Rated to see the top-rated maps, or Newest to see newer maps that were recently submitted. Then you will see the names of levels, their public rating, (If any) etc. Click on the one you want. You can also go directly any map if you know the URL. When you see the box that says "v1.4+ data", right-click the text inside that box. Select All and Copy it. Then, open up your userlevels.txt file and follow the instructions to complete the process. Open N and go to Userlevels in the Main Menu. It will load the userlevels that are in your userlevels.txt. Select the one you want to play.

One more thing I'd like to tell you: Do not take the N .exe or the userlevels.txt file out of it's original folder that you downloaded. If you want to play N without going through all of your folders, just right-click the N .exe file and make a shortcut on your desktop.

I hope this helps you.
