#47700 10 2005-01-24T04:41:51Z Tsukatu
Well, I had finals at my school when I made that thing. I took a piece of graph paper, and then wrote out when I finished my final (but had to stay in the classroom) the rough path I wanted the ninja to take. Then I put in doors, placing the buttons in various places. I made sure that some doors would be in the way of getting the button you ultimately need to get to progress to the next little two-tile deep level of the maze. Then I decided which squares weren't necessary to be in, and put trap doors there that screw you over. 'Tis fun stuff indeed.
It took a helluva long time, and I got lost more than a couple times in the making of it.
