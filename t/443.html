hey,

we're finally done, and you can get 1.3 from
.


new features include:

-online highscores
-internal personal best system
-150 new levels (50 from the 1.3 beta)
-deathlog: who is your #1 nemesis?
-improved post-death ragdoll collision
-improved enemy AI refresh rate
-unlockable secret mode!!
-new credit screen art
-new episode completion images
-"boss mode" (lowers cpu usage)
-visual quality selection
-Ned: alternate key for object editing

the linux version isn't up yet, but it should be within the week.


thanks for helping us test the beta; we're really sorry your highscores have been lost.


due to the fact that previous versions had fully-enabled cheats, we've also had to ignore your progress in 1.2 and the beta. this won't happen again, as we overhauled the progress system so that now your "real" progress, as well as your cheating-enabled progress is tracked seperately. (see n_cheats.txt for more details)


we're going to take a short break from N so that we can work on our next game (which we're submitting to igf2005); we promise that there will be a v1.4 of N, and probably a v1.5, but you'll have to wait until september, which is the IGF deadline.


prospective 1.4 features:
-a revamped and much cooler Ned (see the Ned page for details)
-new levels
-contests
-custom levels playable in game mode
-more unlockable secrets

thanks again for all the support and feedback.

enjoy,
Mare and Raigan
p.s - we've released the first of our tutorials -- check out the of the website. it comes complete with source from N!
