I wish I had found this game sooner! This is a FREE shooting game where you can play against opponents online. You run around shooting people with over 17 weapons, and there are a variety of game modes, including:

Capture the Flag - 2 teams try to run to the other Team's base and collect their flag. If you get their flag, you have to run back to your base with it. If you make it successfully, you get a point. Your flag must also be in place for it to work, though. If your flag gets captured, and the person hasn't made it to their base yet, you can try to get it back. If you kill the guy who got your flag, it becomes "free". If anyone on your team gets it, it automatically gets transported back to your base.
Rambo Match - Whoever gets the Rambo bow gets super Rambo powers, and only then they get points for kills.
Deathmatch - Simplest mode. Run around and kill every person who's not you. Every man for himself.
Teammatch - Same as Deathmatch, except there are teams.

Among others. I know some of you have this game, and if you don't, you need to do so NOW. Here's teh link. What's your feedback on this game?
