SJD_BG: To me, ambient can encompass all genres, if you know what I mean. You can use any instruments or methods to create ambient, so it's not really about checking up to any particular rules. For instance, we have stuff like Stars of the Lid which is very drone-based and soft, or stuff like Fennesz which uses white noise and is aggressive, yes, but it blends together into a wall of sound. Either approach, or anything inbetween.
