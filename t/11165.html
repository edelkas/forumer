Quote: Toshosoft, July 13, 2006 06:55 am
The drones have to look pretty much like a sphere. What you're suggesting looks more like a cylinder .
Sorry, forgot to mention you need to change some of the values in that box. Specifically, the length, and check the "start" and "end" caps boxes. (Setting "Segments" to 1 will make editing easier.) That'll get you a sort of octagonal box.

I did that here, except I added the caps manually in Point-Edit mode (the big button with the three dots in the upper-left corner) by using the Add Edge tool to make a central square connected to each of the corners of the octagon. I then filled up the holes with the Edit > Fill Holes and moved the central square out a bit to make it appear as a regular octagon on all sides. Added the eye (made a geodesic sphere, then Build > Convert to Subdivided for smoothing) and a semitransparent material, and:
