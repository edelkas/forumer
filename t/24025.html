Story Line Additions Pack


So I'm gonna keep this short and sweet. Basically what will happen, is I will make a list of 15 contestants who will each provide the pack with a single episode that they design. The First Person (Who will be me unless somebody else really wants to be first) will begin a story line. The first person will then make an episode (00 - 04) which goes along with that piece of the story line.

Example: Lets say that Riobe incorporates a sail across the sea in his story piece, then one of his levels may be designed to give the feel of a sailing trip.

After The first story piece is written, contestant #2 can begin to write their piece of the story and begin to construct their episode. Make sure that the beginning of your story piece locks in with the ending of the last piece of the story.

For example: If Riobe ends with his voyage landing on a large island, the next piece should not begin with the ninja jumpin' around on the moon. HOWEVER, feel free to make this story as wacky as you'de like it to be. If you want, you can then start off saying that an alien came down and took your ninja up into space.

Rule statement: Unless you are the 20th contestant, you can not end your story piece with a showstopper, like "after his tiumph, bob fell off a cliff and hit his head and exploded". None of that can happen or else the contestant will be asked to edit it so it can be added on to.

Please do not begin to write your story and construct your episode until the last story piece has been posted and YOU HAVE READ IT! Please read ALL of at least the story piece before yours before writting your piece and constructing your levels.

WARNING: Make sure that your story piece is beefy enough to incorporate a whole episode worth of levels.

For Example: Don't make your story piece sound something like;
"Then bob dug his way through the ground and popped out into the enemy fortress". And thats the end.

I'm not saying make your story piece long and descriptive, but make enough material to support 5 distinct levels (Meaning that if the example above were a piece of the story, don't make 3 levels of your episode the ninja going through the underground tunnel, and the rest him in the fortress).

As far as levels go, do not feel that your levels need to be challenging. The episodes will not be released until the end of the contest in one big .txt document. It is your choice how hard it is, and also if you want to have your episode become progressively harder, or if you want one difficulty.

Please, PM a .txt document of all the levels for your episode and not all the individual codes.
