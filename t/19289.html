On the 12th of Septmeber, I submitted my very first map to NUMA. (Although I had made others but didn't know how to save them, lol).

Although I have been playing since 1.3 days in 2004. I clearly remember one ridiculously boring French class at which I sucked at Frence so it was boring! A friend put this game called N on his 64MB (OMG so much memory!!! I thought... seriously though a dollar per megabyte!?)

Anyway, I remember being so shit when I first played. 0-0 was like hey look at this I am cheating I am 'wall jumping' up the wall!!! Look at me go WOOOO!!! Then i learnt if you were in narrow columns you could tap shift (as it were) repeatedly to gather extra speed and height. I clocked 1.3 (much to my friends' amazement) then N v1.4 came out and after a couple of months chipping away slowly, but surely I was really popular with people who couldn't beat a perticular level. "Hey, Schultzy beat this level" "okay! "

The history teacher would be like, "Hey, Ben watchya doing?" I'd be like, "Just helping Brotchie download his work.."
"Okay" he said.

After a while I got hooked playing N. I bludged alot of schoolwork, made a few maps which I got my mates to play and got a few highscores.

Then you know the rest. My votes got resetted. I was banned. I made 'ska' and the rest is history...

I am only really starting to knuckle down on school work now and can't wait till athetics day . It's all coming along well. Thanks for the awesome memories

Ben Schultz
