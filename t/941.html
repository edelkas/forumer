#27502 5 2004-09-11T21:43:16Z Rahl
The file should have come with N, it will be called editor_manual.txt or editor_manual.doc

press ~ to bring up the debug menu, then paste the level code into the "level data" textbox, click out of the textbox, and then press "L" to load the level. when the level is done loading, press "P" to play, and turn on capslock or it will not play correctly.
