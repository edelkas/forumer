#6859 1 2006-02-14T20:43:46Z sweep
I saw a real old sort-of documentary film in Geography last week. They compared a tribe of bushmen in who were at perfect piece with their environment with the city of Johannesburg just 50 miles away. There were loads of really fascinating things in it that had an affect on me, but there's only one part I'd like to share with you guys. When talking about why Johannesburg was so built up, it said something along these lines:
Quote
To save time, time-saving devices have been invented, and created in their millions. Although they were created to save time, people have just ended up doing more and more as a result of these machines, as they have to maintain and look after them. Children are even sentenced to 10 years of education just so they can survive in this harsh world of "time-saving" devices.

At this point, you're probably wondering why the hell I'm posting this in the NUMA discussion section. Well, my point is that it bears great relevance to NUMA.

The Bushmen are at peace with their environment. They look at what they've got, they don't complain, they live as a peaceful and happy environment. The people of Johannesburg, on the other hand, are constantly trying to improve but only making life harder for themselves in the process. The Bushmen are very contented, whilst the people of Johannesburg are unhappy and stressed. We need to learn to be at peace with our environment, NUMA, and work well as a community in it. There's nothing wrong with innovation, but we should stop looking to new features to solve our problems, and instead should try and fix the problems within the community by changing the way we behave.

I hope this doesn't go over everyone's head.
