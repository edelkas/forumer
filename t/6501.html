#127791 2 2005-12-29T01:22:16Z Maniac
What you want to do is search your computer for a .sol file. It's a file that Flash uses to store data, such as your progress in this case.

To find the .sol file:

1. Boot up your Windows search (I'm assuming you're using a Windows. If not, reply and I can give you Mac directions)

2. Check "Search for Hidden Files"

3. Search for *.sol

If no files come up, your progress has been irrevocably lost.
