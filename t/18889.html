This is the Wikipedia game that I have created with me and a friend.

Objective
Try and get from one topic to the next in the least amount of clicks.

Rules

1. You can only use the links inside of Wikipedia to get to your destination. Clicking on a link to some other website like Google and connecting back to Wikipedia is not okay.
--
2. No clicking the back button (I can't really enforce this one but please use the honor system). You may only click the back button once you have come to your target destination, at which point you should record your steps.
--
3. Once you have made it to the target page record your progress backwards. For example...
If I were to record my progress from "Grass" to "Infusion" I would put - Infusion>Tea>lemongrass>Grass


And that is it. If you have any questions just post them here. I will try and set up a new challenge every week, but please bear with me. I will post the challenge and at the end of that week the person who has discovered the shortest route will win a... DED map. just for fun.

Do not PM me with any submissions. Only post them here. If someone copies someone else's route, the first person to post the route will get the recognition, so don't sweat posting it here. Thanks.

--------------------

Week 1 Challenge - Go from China to Tetrahedron in the least number of clicks.
