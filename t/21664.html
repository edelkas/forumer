This poll is about Alan Moore (and David Lloyd's) novel V for Vendetta. In 2006 it was taken in by the Wachowski Brothers (The Matrix) and made into a movie. They of course had to cut some things out, and as you know major characters such as Mrs. Heyer, Alistair Harper, Derek Almond, and Rose almond are completely removed from the storyline. Also, vents are moved around, such as the destruction og the Old Bailey and the BTN Tower. The St. Mary's virus did not even exist in the graphic novel. Most important of all, the character of Evey Hammond is drastically changed in the movie, as is V (not as drastic though, but he becomes more personable in the movie).
