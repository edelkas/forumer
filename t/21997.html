#19678 1 2008-03-25T00:36:30Z miyomiyo
Ratings are an unnecessary addition to the comment system. Ratings become an obsession, a meaningless obsession. I spent so long watching my name go up and up on the top rated authors list until I realized that I was slowly killing myself. I had gotten to the point where any rating lower than a four made me disappointed and even a four wasn't great. Don't let this happen to you!

A comment says ten times more than a rating and, unlike ratings, cannot be abused. If somebody says they are rating a map four, it subconsciously makes me more likely to rate the map a four. If somebody explains what they liked and disliked, I have an easier time disagreeing and freely stating my actual opinion. If ratings are disabled, people feel obliged to comment on maps, thus showing the author who it is that is playing they're maps.

There is nothing a comment can do that a rating can't. And that is why I am making this pledge. You can, too. All you have to do is disable ratings on all further maps you make. It's as simple as that.
