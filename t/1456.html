Couldn't find this anywhere in the manual and even did a search for "snap" on the forums and couldn't find it.

But, for those interested, pressing Z while editing a map changes it to "no snap" and X changes it to "1/2 snap". Basically, if you don't want something aligned to the grid then hit Z and place it (then hit X to return to "normal").

Just thought the folks that don't know this would find it useful.
