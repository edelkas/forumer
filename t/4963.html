What is a codemasher you ask? Well, it's what a call a level that you have made from smashing two levels together and fiddling with it. Anyone can make one quickly. Simply get the code for level #1, paste in N, get code for level #2, paste in middle of level #1 code, load, tinker so it works, and bang! you have a codemasher.
