#475025 2 2008-10-11T01:37:58Z PsychoSnail
Yes, it's been suggested quite a few times. Currently, you can search for a certain author in the search box, and when the search results load, you can subscribe to an RSS feed for the author by pressing the RSS feed button in your address bar (assuming your browser supports RSS feeds). You can also access RSS feeds by replacing the "browse" in the URL for search results with "browse.rss" (without the quotes). This also works for any search term.
Some people don't like RSS feeds, though, or don't know how to use them, so such a feature would be nice, though not the highest priority.
