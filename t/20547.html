OK, well I was listening to maestro's N Music, and thinking, "I wonder if it's possible to make a music DDA..."
So, my challenge to everyone is to try and make a music DDA! A music DDA is defined by me as a DDA (Don't Do Anything map) where you hear music/a tune.
Post here if you wish to enter or judge - any number of each is allowed.

DATES AND RULES
Each entrant can submit THREE (3) maps.
All entries are to be submitted to NUMA, and the link posted here. It'd also be nice if you put a link to this topic in your map description.
The contest will go from today until JANUARY 20, 2008.
Judges are allowed to submit maps, however they are not allowed to judge them.

JUDGES
Me
Condog

Entrants
maestro - Entry 1
fingersonthefrets - Entry 1

Good luck to everyone!

Lenny
