#136138 12 2006-01-26T19:03:53Z Raigan
as with many suggestions, this is techinically possible, however it would require fundamentally changing the way collision is handled, and thus recoding much of the game

we're still experimenting with different approaches to collision for the next game, one thing we're keeping in mind is that having a very complex system that can do many different things may not be the best idea, since (a) it will be harder to implement, and ( one reason why people like N seems to be that you can make a good map in under 30 minutes.

not only that, but we think that maps are BETTER because of how simple/fast they are to make, since you can quickly experiment/test very easily.

so we're trying to find a good compromise between a system that lets people be creative and do lots of different things, while still being basic/simple enough that they can do things fast.
