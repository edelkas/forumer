Hey guys, here's my way of beating the level. I'm sure you can get out of the drone pit no problem, right? And I'm sure you get in the bomb shelter easy, right?! And I'm sure you can get the exit button good, RIGHT?!?!
But then you have to dodge tons of missiles, Yayyy!! Okay, heres how you get the level finished. Run out of the pit, then wall jump onto the first steep slope with the jump pad on it, then jump over the jump pad onto the slope that is right above the gold. Right when you're just about to get hit by missles, hit the jump pad, and get on the top slope- this IS the hardest part, it might take three or four tries- then jump to dodge another missle, and then jump onto the last platform (while dodging the last missle) and you're home free from the missles. It seems like alot ... it's not.
Now, to dodge mines, drones, and chainguns.Wait until the chaingun is out of range, then jump onto the left-most platform in the secondn row and wait until the chaingun is out of range again, then jump to the middle-most platform and wait again, except this time wait until it's IN range. Now, here's another hard part, for some the hardest. Slowly fall of the edge of the platform, dodging the mines and the chaingun. Then you hit the the right-most slope on the left side-dodging the thwump- to get to the bottom. Oh, no! You hit the thwump. Now you have to do all that all over again. Don't worry, you'll get it. When you get in the pit, make sure you're timing is right so you don't hit the drone, too. Then carefully sneak to the exit. CONGRATULATIONS!!! You have just bet arguably the hardest level in the game. One more level before unlocking blue ninja!! - ? + = Blue ninja. Yaayyy!!!
