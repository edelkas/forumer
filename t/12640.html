Urban Dead is a web-based MMORPG about a city called Malton, which is infested with zombies. Over time, you accumulate Action Points, which you spend towards actions: moving, attacking, speaking, etc.

You can be either a survivor or a zombie in this game, and you can always switch in-game (by dying as a survivor or being revived as a zombie).

Zombies are pretty straight foward; just attack and kill. Survivors, however, can barricade buildings as protection from zombies, use weapons, heal each other, and revive zombies. I prefer being a zombie, they're easier.
