#250165 9 2006-11-05T01:39:11Z TornadoTK
For anyone who doesn't play DDR, here's what the modifiers mentioned mean:

Boost: Arrows start slower than usual, then speed up as they move up the screen.
Dark: Target arrows (gray arrow markers at the top) disappear.
Hidden: Arrows disappear halfway up the screen.
2x: Arrows move 2x as fast up the screen.
Reverse: Arrows move from top to bottom.

Now, as for your scores, all of them are basically the same as playing on Heavy. 2x, Boost, and Dark don't constitute skill, since they give little change to the song. Hidden and Reverse only take more reading skill, which can be demonstrated on a controller as well.
