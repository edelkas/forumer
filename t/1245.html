Dustman, here are a few suggestions...

1. Start out with a general idea of what your level is going to be like.

2. Don't hide objects (such as the exit switch in this level).

3. Make sure every object you place in your level has a specific purpose.

4. Play through your levels, and as you do, ask yourself what parts are fun and what parts are unnecessary or frustrating.
