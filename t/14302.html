#266561 6 2006-12-08T17:20:42Z Sendy
Time: I have more time than most people on this forum. I don't have a dayjob (it's impossible in my current situation and thankfully not needed). I am persuing a career in music so basically when I'm not writing tunes or doign housework and chores I make levels. Sometimes I stay up all night brainstorming ideas. I'd also love a job as a level designer so I justify this as something to put on my CV, even if the chances of getting a job in game design are a billion to one!

Inspiration: Mostly just the way the ninja moves and interacts with the objects provides infinite inspiration. I've also been influences by the great map packs such as Dave's, the Legacy, Uber Haxor's, TDL's packs, and various authors on NUMA such as ATOB, krusch, LV. The list is endless.

I still also use the Metanet columns as a model for a lot of maps.

Patience: As an artist I'm naturally attuned to the way of creativity. I realize that all patience is is a willingness to press on, try different things, and let the Infinite express it's-self through me.

Thanks to everybody's kind words. I really hope I don't let you down!

Sendy

EDIT: One more thing I wanted to mention. There are plenty of easy/fun levels this time, at least, I hope so! I think I've mastered the skill of being aware of the difficulty contour for my maps and/or their different routes, etc and manipulating that.
