Welcome to the one and only inverted jumper contest!
The Object of this is to make a jumping puzzle that uses only the upper half of the map!


[size=100]Rules:
1. You may only use the upper half of the map. The rest must be open space, except for precautions to stop poeple who fall from getting back up.
2. All tiles must be connected somehow by other tiles to the roof or walls, except 2 groups.
3. No enemys allowed. (enemies are the upper part of the object editor)
4. No bounceblocks or oneways


Example map:Reversed Gravity


[SIZE=3] Scoring:
1-10 points: followed rules
1-25 points: fun
1-15 points: difficulty
x/50= your score
