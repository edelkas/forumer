#474104 8 2008-09-29T01:02:04Z Tsukatu
I'm an atheist. I also don't believe life has any purpose whatsoever. I don't live for a god, and I freely accept that my life has no meaning whatsoever. I understand my place in the universe - none of my actions will have any meaningful consequences, and everyone I affect is just going to die anyway.

But given that, making a logical conclusion to suicide is just plain stupid.

For one thing, why exert the effort to end something that doesn't matter? You'll have to think of a means, you'll have to prepare it all, find a place to do it, ponder whether or not you want to leave a note, and finally, at the moment itself, it's not going to matter how callous you can pretend to be on an online forum - the event itself will be extremely emotionally traumatic. If life is meaningless, it certainly isn't worth all that effort to end it. If you really don't care, then just shrug and leave it alone, and let it play out.

On top of that, I don't even see why you're being so hasty. From what you've said about your life, it seems like it's going pretty well. That's pretty much my story at this point, also - I'm succeeding in life, and I'm having fun doing it. I don't derive any holy affirmation of purpose from life, because as far as I'm concerned that's completely inapplicable, but I am having fun.
Think about what else is fun but pointless - video games. Video games are completely frickin' pointless, but they're fun! I play video games, completely with the understanding that they will do absolutely nothing for me except entertain me. It's the same reason I watch sitcoms, or porn. None of it is meaningful, but it's obviously enjoyable.
If you were to apply the same logic you're using about life to these things, I should hope it's obvious why it would be absurd to declare that a huge avenue of entertainment is pointless and therefore must be destroyed; if it's pointless, then don't frickin' worry about it. Life, like so many other things, is pointless, but that doesn't mean it's not fun as all hell, and it's irrational beyond all else to conclude that it should be ended because it's pointless.

From my perspective, this life is what I have and it's enjoyable, so there's no reason to exert the effort to end it. In fact, there's all the more incentive to let it keep going, and even to make it a point to improve myself more so that I can maximize the enjoyment I can derive out of life.

If nothing else, I've always been curious as to how my perceptions of the world will change as I get older. When I was 13, I was fairly certain I pretty much knew it all. A few years later, I could only laugh as I looked back at myself and remembered how secure I was in my convictions, what I thought about the world, my place in the world, and my general outlook on life. Then I aged a few more years, and repeated the process.
I'm 20 now, and so far it's been one long amusement park ride. Even the things that weren't fun are at the very least interesting, and my mind only ever wandered into the same place your mind is now when I wasn't rational enough to realize how silly it is.

I, for one, can't wait to see where my life takes me. I enjoy watching movies and I enjoy playing games, and to me, my life is like some amalgamation of the most interesting movie and the most enjoyable game I've ever played, and the fact that it's pointless doesn't slow me down one bit.

I'm similarly confused about why religious people ask me why I simply don't kill myself if I believe life is pointless, because they - just as you, apparently - don't realize that that knowledge is empowering, and gives me all the more incentive to live my life and love every second of it, and not because it's some happy-go-lucky mindset I've tried to enforce on myself (vis most religions today) but because it's genuinely entertaining to me.

So stop being a retard and get back in the game.
