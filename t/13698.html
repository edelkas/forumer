I'm the youngest of five boys. Growing up, I always felt like was a blessing. My older brothers have always led by example, and I've always been mature beyond my years as a direct result (in my opinion) of the examples my brothers have set.

But, unfortunately, it's not that simple. And part of the problem is that I have excelled far to easily for my own good in some cases. This is an arrogant statement, but forced modesty is more arrogant, and I'm not going to lie just to make you guys think I don't have a high opinion of myself. I am academically and socially successful, I'm creative but analytical at the same time, I breeze past things in the way others can't. I'm not someone that works painstakingly to do well, I just do well. By all accounts, my life should be brilliant. But this ease has come back to haunt me. Where all my brothers have been successful academically, I have managed to follow - but where my brothers have been successful in certain areas, I have somehow pooled all those areas and been successful in pretty much every one.

I know what you're thinking: "fuck you. Your life sounds great. How is your self-proclaimed success a problem?" Well, that's what I might have said if I was you. But as I said, it's not that simple.

When you become so good at following the success of four older brothers, you feel like you have to follow what they've achieved. In many cases this isn't a problem, but when it comes to love, it is. Each of my brothers found someone they fell in love with around about my age. The sad truth is that not all those relationships have lasted - but they were still great relationships. The problem for me is that I've spent the last five years of my life building up to this moment in time, expecting to find someone and fall in love with them. I want to madly fall for someone, and I want them to madly fall for me.

But, heck, it hasn't happened. Probably because I'm only 17. But in my mind, I'm scared shitless. I have great friends, which I maintain great friendships with, but I feel lonely. Very lonely. Within myself I am possessed with following the path - leading the same life my brothers have led before me, but the truth is, it can't work like that. I'm a logical person. I'm aware of this. But no matter how much I tell myself that it doesn't matter about where I am, inside I will always be burning until I find someone.

And to make things more confusing, this deep desire to find that special person makes liking anyone difficult. In the past week there's someone - who will remain unnamed - who I like. She's a very touchy-feely person, and we get along well, but she gets along well with so many people. By all rights, I should just like her and see how things go from there. But in my mind it's nowhere near that simple. I know I only like her, but because I'm so desperate inside to love someone I spend all day thinking about her, and the smallest interaction to her can send me into an incredibly good mood for the rest of the day or - and these happen 50/50 btw - she can send me into a deep depression for the rest of the day.

Now I've hit a three-day weekend. Yesterday was a day of depression. I know this will carry over through today, Saturday and Sunday. Great. I won't be able to be productive because I'll spend all my time staring into space and feeling shit about someone that I do like - but not that much - and she's also pretty completely different from me. In my mind, if we were to start a relationship, I honestly don't see the dynamics. But for some reason, my instincts do. And it's fucking me up inside.

This is only one week in. I'm scared. This feels like it will control me indefinitely. I love the highs - but the rest is just awful. I feel like I know where I am logically, but my instincts can't follow this logic - and I am completely and utterly caught up with this person. Which just makes me feel so incredibly lonely.

If you've read all of the above, thank you. I appreciate it. That's a lot of self-absorbed crap you've just trawled through. Please, if you have any comments, I want to here them.

/edit: Here's something to add. Early this morning (about three AM) I stood still for two hours in my room listening to music that makes me feel... supernatural, I guess. I had the window open. It was freezing. I chose not to close it for the whole two hours. It felt like I was going insane at the time. But I'm not sure if I was just putting it on. Once the music stop I collapsed on my bed and fell asleep immediately.

Vent. A lot. If you're like me, it could be better to vent to people quite far removed from your own life rather than with anybody too close- it can help, to some extent. Space yourself, and try to make the feelings fade.
Or ask her out, and risk rejection, and risk feeling horrible for days afterwards, to the extent where you almost come close to consdering wearing nothing but black and buying a Fallout Boy CD.
Or ask her out, and hope that things will work. That this girl who you're fond of might be- or end up being- fond of you, too. And you'll still lose the ability to be properly intellectual, to have proper perspective, to be objective in many aspects of your life, but there's the off chance you'll wind up loving it more than anything else.

But try not to let it simmer too long- my first real crush simmered for upwards of two years as I refused to take any action at all, and it was- well, pretty unpleasant. I strongly recommend you try to do something. Anything, really. See what happens, and move on in some direction, in some way, onto something different than this.
