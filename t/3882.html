#80120 2 2005-06-30T23:13:54Z great_sea
1) Yes, there is a way to play unincluded levels. I am assuming you are talking about using levels from NUMA. In this case, go to the map's page and copy all of the data out of the "1.4+" data box. (CTRL+A will select all on a PC). Then go into the folder in which N is installed. Open userlevels.txt and paste the map data at the bottom. Now when you open N and go into the user levels, you should see your new map.

2) No, there are absolutely no weapons for N in the game.

3) When you are playing a level that you made (I assume in debug mode), nothing will happen when you finish it or die unless you hit the tilde button (~), that is if you are using a PC (I dunno about mac). This will take you back to the debug menu.

4) Boss mode exists for the purpose of hiding the game if you happen to be in your school or place of employment or wherever else you don't want people to know what you are doing. (i.e. - go into boss mode when the manager walks by so he doesn't know you are slacking).

Most/all of these questions could have been answered by browsing around the forum before posting and reading the readme.txt and userlevels.txt. I pardon you, however, because I know the world can be a cruel place, and you are new.
