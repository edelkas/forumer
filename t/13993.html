I'm not sure if this is right, but I think that there should be tutorials to let newbies know how to make decent maps. Therefore, I made a tutorial to show people how to make a map similar to my latest map, Lemongrand. Well, here it goes.


Lemongrand is a nice, metanet-esque level. It's simple to make a map like that.

1. I start with an empty map, then I make a jumble of E-tiles.

Simple? Good.

2. I patch up the E-tiles with 1 tiles. You can patch them up with 2's,6's,3's and 7's if you like.

Still easy? I hope so.

3.This is when it gets a little more complicated. I put stand-alone E-tiles in what we just created, a room.


4. I connect these tiles with mines. You don't have to connect all of them.

I hope you're following this.

5. To some, drones are hard to put in, mostly because of their paths. You can usually find a good path for drones. I put in zap drones here. All the drone's paths are CCW Dumb.

6. You find a good spot for the exit and it's switch. Put N near the exit.


7. Just put in gold wherever you want and feel free to put in extra things to make it more challenging.


And we're done! Sorry if this confuses you. I'm completely aware that the tutorial's vague. And I didn't save the level so there's no end result for you.

Well, I hope you can do good with this!
