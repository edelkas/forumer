#189007 2 2006-06-13T19:32:03Z Levinous
If the ninja is close, then he will get pwned.

If the ninja is far away (usually at the other side of the level) then he may survive.

It's all to do with the chaingun's bullet angles.

It's like a shotgun. You shoot close at something, all the bullets should hit the target. If you are far away from the target, the bullets spread outwards.

See?
