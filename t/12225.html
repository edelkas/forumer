The basic idea of this thread is to tell an epic tale of the life of a ninja. Each person that posts in this thread with the intentions of advancing the story must type 1 paragraph minimum. Make it interesting, detailed, and exciting, and above all, be creative. However, don't get crazy and go on about forks and aardvarks for twenty minutes. If need be, your post will be edited for silliness, swearing, suggested themes, you know the deal...

To tickle your tastebuds a bit, let it be known that there is a little extra...secret project in store for this when it's done, so look forward to it.





Quote: The Tale
          One day, long ago, in a galaxy not much different then ours (Except it had floating gold and lazer monsters), A young ninja walked through the forest. He noticed a small grey platform on the ground. Upon stepping on it, he was launched into the air. "I'm flying!" he exclaimed.
          While he was flying in the air, he saw something directly above him. It was only a shadow, but he knew it was something dangerous. As it came into view he could see its color. Red, with spikes on it. 'Now where have I heard about this before...' thought the ninja. Suddenly, he remembered a conversation he had with his grandfather upon leaving his house for the walk in the forest. "Now, when you go out into the forest, there are a couple of things you should watch out for." His grandfather had said. "First and foremost, there are mines. They float in the air. They are red, and have little spikes on them. Even with the slightest touch, they will kill you. It will blow you apart into pieces-" That's all he had to remember to know that he was about to hit a mine, and die! As his entire life flashed before his eyes, he saw a big white square. He remebered what this was right away. "It's a bouceblock!" The little ninja exclaimed. He lunged at it, and found himself sliding down its side wall. He was about to slide off it, and fall from an incredible height! Thinking fast, he jumped off it, gaining a little bit of height. Then, he attached himself right back to it. He repeated that motion 3 more times, until finally, He stood on top of it! It moved down a bit from his impact onto it, but it raised right back up. "I did it!" He exclaimed. Suddenly, it hit him. He was 125 feet off the ground, with practically no way to get back down to the ground. "I don't think my grandpa is going to like this..."
          But then something caught his eye. It was briiight yellow and squared. And he was oddly attracted to it. He thought for a moment what this could be. Then he remembered his grandfather's other story: "One day I had been walking in the woods. I saw something shiny and picked it up. I noticed that I felt much younger. I realised that this shiny thing was the "gold" they talked about in folklore. It could extend your lifespan but it can also hide switches that can trigger traps. So remember, gold will extend your lifespan but it can also be dangerous. So if you see some, DO NOT TOUCH IT!!!" 'Hmm...' the ninja thought. 'There is NO WAY this gold could be a trap' and jumped on it. Suddenly, walls sprang up from all sides! He was trapped. But wait...there were 2 paths out of this trap! But which should he choose?
          One trail led to a field full of mines, while the other lead to some tunnel. Unable to see the other side, and hating mines, N jumped into the tunnel, crawling for two minutes straight. On the other side, though, he found a long stretch that led to some sort of building. He heard multiple cranking sounds, and bullets starting hitting the ground around him.
