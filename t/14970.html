#280406 3 2007-01-03T00:56:25Z Twistkill
We appreciate it when people asking for help are coherent and tell us what their problem is clearly.

You must not drag the N.exe or the userlevels.txt out of the original folder it came in, and if you do, you have to make sure both of them are in the same directory, like the desktop, or another folder. If you want easy access to them, you can simply right-click and create a shortcut to the desktop for them, which what I have done.

If that fixes your problem, than read no further. If not:

At the top of your userlevels.txt file, underneath that line that is like this: ***DO NOT MODIFY THIS LINE***, if you do not have "&userdata=" just underneath that, than nothing will show up. Make sure that's there.

If this fixes your problem, then you're good to go.
