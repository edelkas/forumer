#68794 4 2005-05-23T00:40:48Z nevermore
Ok, place a launchpad into an empty level. There will be a couple of numbers in your level data at the end after the 0s and 1s.
Find the last two numbers in your level data. They are the x, y values for the power that the launchpad launches you.
If you make the first number higher, it will make the launch pad launch you more to the side.
If you make the second number higher, it will launch you more vertically.
Get it?
