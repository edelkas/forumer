It works like this: Each player takes the previous word, makes a phrase or sentence that uses that word, then replaces that word in the phrase/sentence with another word that makes it mean the same thing. An example is below, starting with 'up'.

He drank up the water.
He drank down the water.

He downed the water.
He swallowed the water.

The swallow flew across the sky.
The bird flew across the sky.

And so on.
