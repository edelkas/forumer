I am starting an Innovation Contest where you have to make maps that are entirely different from all other maps. Well, generally.

Rules:

1. Absolutely no collaboration, but you may ask questions
2. You must find a new idea for a certain tile, or certain item
3. No N-Arts or DDA's. Sorry about that.
4. You can use as much space as you want, just be sure to make the borders perceptible
5. You must submit your maps as links or in those user level things. You know what I mean
6. You may not submit more than two maps...yet
7. If I find a map that uses the same technique on Numa, your overall rating gets cut in half.

Guidelines/Judging:

Innovation (tiles/items) -/50
Funness (the obvious) -/25
Looks nice (you know) -/15
Use of space (read rules) -/10

Judges

Judges all get to grade each map. I will be one. There will be somewhere between three and five.

1. turtle
2. 0truths
3. chaos rider

Judge Rules:
1. You may submit maps (if at least ten people in total participate)
2. You must get your judging done by a certain deadline, or your judging does not count
3. You may not judge your own maps.
4. In the end, if there is a tie, I will decide who wins.
5. Submit your judging to me with a PM. DO NOT POST IT HERE. Otherwise, it will not count.

So, sounds good?
