Echoes. Often found in large, cavernous spaces, or rooms that are relatively empty. Why not?

When you've just made a big map, that is rather spacey, you could have a button to "put" an echo on. You could draw a space (a bit like the space filling tool in the tile section) and fill that with echo. Ninjas falling, running, zap drones speeding towards you etc. This idea originally came from playing the .swf versions (at school, can't download there) where the sound was really bad, and the zap drones sound slightly strange.

I'll admit that this is a pretty useless suggestion, and it'll probably get shot down more than I think it's going to be.

Plus, I'm guessing this would be very hard to implement. Sound isn't N's best aspect (M&R said it themselves somewhere in press reviews I think), but its still a grab at thin, (echoing) air.
