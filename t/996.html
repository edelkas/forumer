Okay, here are the comprehensive rules for our Numa Time Trials. The Numa Time Trial Society (NTTS) is designed to get more Forum members to play User Created Maps. There are some really neat maps out there, and the NTTS is made to bring them to everyone's attention.

Before we start, here is the NTTS Team of judges:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
Daddaluma,
LittleViking001, And
Myself

Here are the judges and players who are currently on my watch list:
There are currently No judges or players on my Watch List.

For those of you unfamiliar with the watch list, I put judges on the watch list to see if they continue judging and/or contact me during the week. If they do not for two consecutive weeks, I will relieve them from their duties as judges. I need reliable judges for the NTTS, and unfortunately, I can't have missing judges.

As far as players, any players on my watch list are deemed unable to play in NTTS tournaments. If a player is caught cheating, spamming, or doing other unsavory things that I deem unworthy, I will declare that any and all submissions from that player are null and void, and will not be judged.

The Time Trials are separated into two parts. The first part consists of the above judges nominating maps from the Numa archives. All maps selected must be found on , and Each Judge can nominate up to two maps per week. Once each Judge nominates their maps, the judges and/or contestants vote on which map the contestants play in the corresponding poll. The map that gets the majority of the votes becomes the map that contestants play through. If there is no clear winner in the poll, or if the judges cannot decide on a map for the week, and a map needs to be posted for the contest to begin, I will choose a map to post as that week's map.

The second part consists of the judging. Contestants must be registered forum members (This makes our burrito a whole lot easier, trust me). Forum members can play through the selected map and post their demo data in the NTTS contest thread. Submissions and judging will be done on a two-week basis. From the time of the Thread going up (Which is usually Sunday Nights), Contestants will have 10 days to submit their map demos, which is the following Wednesday at 23:59 PM PST. After which, the above judges will scrutinize each contestant's demo data. Once the Submission time has passed for the week, NO further demos will be considered. If you don't know what time PST is, PM me and I'll let you know.

As far as Demo Submissions go, each member is allowed No more than Two demo submissions per week. This allows contestants to watch other demos, and discover routes that they wouldn't have thought otherwise possible, or otherwise try to improve their time.

Once all submissions are recieved, the judges will begin scrutinizing the demos recieved. The judging will begin on Thursday mornings of the second week of submissions, and will end on Sunday Evenings that week. The judges will then vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a second runner-up in each of the four following categories:

- Time: Time is an important factor. The speedier you finish a map, the better off you'll be in this category. A competitor's Time score will be measured by the number of frames it took to complete the map. Players who complete maps will be given priority over those who do not complete maps, regardless of their Time score. As in the actual game, we will also take into account the amount of gold retrieved during a particular run. Each Gold piece will subtract 80 frames from your overall Time score.

- Style + Entertainment: Style is also an important factor in how we will judge. If you complete a map with more fluid motions than other contestants, you'll have a better chance at winning this category. If you backtrack or make a mistake, that will count in this area as well. We will also judge on how entertaining each contestant's data is. If a contestant bores the judges, he/she will get a considerably less score than a contestant whose moves wow and amaze.

- Creativity: Being creative is what this contest is all about. If you can do something that no one else can, like, say, jump in between two mines that no one else can get through, that will give you some brownie points in the Creativity area. Also, if you can use the map and the surroundings to your advantage better than the other contestants, you'll get a good score here. Also, how well you execute said creative moves will be taken into account for your Creativity ranking. If you can do something that no one else can, but it takes you a long time to do so, then we will take that into consideration.

- Completion: We will also be determining scores by checking to see how completely you finished the map. For instance, some maps can be completed without opening all doors and without getting all gold. We will judge this category based upon how complete a user's demo is (All Gold, all triggers, etc.).

There may also be map-specific goals, such as survival time in survival maps. These map-specific criteria will be included in our judging of the above categories. For instance, Least Shot At is now a map-specific goal, as opposed to a full-on category, and would be taken into account under Style, or Creativity. Survival time will be taken into consideration under Time, and so on.

I will list any map-specific goals within the Contest Thread, under each category that has a goal with it for the week.

Now, as far as scoring goes, Each judge will vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a 2nd runner-up for each category. First place in a category gets 5 points, second place gets 3 points, and third place gets 1 point. Fourth place and beyond earn no points. In the event of a tie, points will be awarded for each contestant to tie. We will declare 4 weekly winners each week, and each week's points will also carry over to the competitor's seasonal total. At the end of the Season, which we will determine the length of later, we will declare 4 Seasonal Champions, one in each category, based upon the number of points they recieved throughout the season. We will also be declaring an NTTS Season 1 champion at the end of the season.

In addition, Maniac will be using a 40-point system for his "Perfect Demo" award, given away weekly. The 40-point system uses the same four categories as the regualr judging, but has no other affect on the outcome of the competition.

Official judging begins on the second week of submissions, on Thursday mornings, and continues through Sunday night that week. Point totals, both weekly and cumulative, will be posted on Sunday nights, just before the next week's contest.

As far as weekly scoring goes, here is the breakdown:
Each week, I will post standings in four areas:
The Blue-colored standings are for the total votes that each player recieved this week.
The Red-colored standings are this week's point totals for each player that participated this week.
The Purple-colored standings are the cumulative point totals in each category for the season. This takes into account points from both this week, as well as past weeks.
The Green-colored standings are the overall point totals for the season for all players.

The seasonal point totals in each category, as well as the overall point totals will determine which player is our categorical champion, as well as determining our seasonal champion!
That's it for now, if anyone comes up with anything, I'll be glad to post it right here.
