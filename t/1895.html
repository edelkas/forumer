Anyway, my first contribution:

Doors versus One-ways

Many people know there are ways of cheating one-way doors. This method was new to me, so I shared. Some knew it, at least one person so far did not. Anyway, it opens up a huge opportunity when you consider that trap doors work with it as well. You could have a wall of one-way doors that can't be crossed until you trigger a one-way door that opens up the bug to allow you through. I think it has potential, at least.

Anyone else care to contribute to this thread? Mainly, I'm looking for bugs to avoid and bugs that turn themselves into features, like the one I just mentioned. Putting them all into one place (especially if stickied) would help the map writing community.
