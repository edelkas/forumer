If you look at the highscores, it's very easy, you can speed jump up, and then just hold left all the way down, and you'll grab all of the gold and all of the switches on your way back. When you reach the top, make sure that you don't pause, hit the very back wall, then push left until you run off the slope. When you run off the slope, make sure that you hit the first slanted part, and not the ledge. If you hit the ledge (The one with the top jump pad), the gauss turret will hit you every time. So, when you hit the slope, hold left, and pray that you get all the switches. I would say the best method is to speed jump up, and run as fast as you can down, without hitting that uppermost ledge.
