#3424 1 2005-06-23T01:35:46Z Arachnid
Ok, here's my idea for how to improve/'fix' NUMA:
  • Ratings will be removed. Bye-bye ratings.
  • A 'favorites list' will be introduced. Users can add maps to their favorites, view their (and other people's) favorites, and download all their favorites as a user_levels.txt file
  • Instead of ratings, maps will have an 'approval rating'. This will be the number of people who have added it to their favorites, divided by the number of people that have viewed it, as a percentage. This seems like a much more balanced system, to me - it's more or less "voting with your feet", as the approval rating directly reflects what percentage of people think the map is good enough to include in the list of maps they want in their user_levels file.
Opinions? Comments?

#77667 13 2005-06-23T14:02:18Z brickman
This system may work for the top-end maps, but it groups anything that isn't top quality into one big "0" group. Because very few people would put average or pretty good maps in their list (only excellent ones), so anyone who is currently getting a 3.9 average or lower would probably not get any and thus look the same ratings-wise as the maps that are 1s, 2s and 0s. Plus, just imagine how easy it would be to cheat this system--you don't even need multiple accounts, just a browser with tabs. And all maps ratings would decrease with time and with every time that someone with it as a favorite actually plays it. The first step if you even want this to be a moderately logical system is making the number of views only count each ip once (or accounts). And make a check box for people who like to search but won't be adding any maps to their list to not mess up the scores of any map they view.
