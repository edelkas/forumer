I did this story for a 7th grade journal entry based on a fantasy land.

One day in the beautiful, blue day of the earth, the Hubble Space Telescope found an earth-like planet recently after it had some major upgrades. Everyone was aghast of the sight of a new planet. We named it Ida. I got this name from when I was dreaming of going to a concert but on the way home a meteor shower sent us a warning that a giant planet called Ida is coming to crash into Earth. The planet was so big nothing could push it back or off-course. The dream felt very real. I woke up and realized that I was having a nightmare. I remembered the name Ida since. This dream is a true dream that I had once. Ok back to the story.

NASA sent some new astronauts and I went along for naming the planet. Ida is giant planet that about 2 1/2 Jupiters can fit in. Ida is also 33,550,720,139 Mi. (I made up the number. It had to be farther than Pluto.) from our sun. we took off in the new Columbia SP2, which is capable of cruising at mach 3.2 (Approximately 2435.2 mph).

The trip took about a week to complete. When we got there, we were dumbfounded to see that it was just like Earth. There were creatures like horn toads, microscopic orange fish called crawlers that can gather up into one large group to make one large super organism. They can sneak up on prey and climb on it then inject a paralyzing poison and eat the prey up with their pincers that can pierce.

It was paradise. Then a peculiar meteor burned through the night sky and crashed into Ida. Minimal damage happened because its a large planet. Then a head popped out of the meteor squeaking chronically. The meteor has zits? I said. Everyone laughed out loud. But not for long did they laugh. The thing said, You shouldnt have said zit.

Oh yeah?! I yelled, Zitty Zitty zit zits! I repeated the phrase 5 times. The thing said its species name is the zit. Then it squeaked and other zits came out and squeaked and they all chased us around. They were trying to hug us. I told everyone Get to the crawler pond quick.

Squeak! The zit knocked the janitor down and hugged him. I was thinking he was supposed to be back on the ship cleaning everything. We got to the crawler pond and jumped over it with great accuracy. Then the zits stopped with poor accuracy and all fell into the crawler pond. And you do not want to know what happened next after that.

One zit survived. I asked him, Where does your master live? It replied weakly, The planet Zitton. Then I told it, Well thats ironic. Ill spare you. But your people wont be spared. I knew there was no technology on Ida but Kazam! I found my portable radio wave cell phone. The technology of a cell phone and a radio telescope fused. I found a cell phone tower on Earth with a signal of 2/5 so I called the militaries of all nations to come up to Zitton, which is 5 times s big as Earth and left of the Milky Way.

Of course, when we entered the galaxy where Zitton is I somehow felt really weird. I gazed at the planets and one had salty seatbelts attacking intruders and one with wireless phones paralyzing prey with an attack called the hold button. I named the galaxy The Galaxy Of The Retards

We reached Zitton in 2 days. The land was red with technological buildings and shelters. It was a miracle. It was like life on mars but with unknown technology. All the nations militaries got out of the shuttles. Approximately over 65,000,000 people were our side. And the arm-hugging retards had 1.5% more soldiers than us. Then, what seemed like the battle of all time and space, the war of the worlds began. Swords clashed, fists and kicks collided, guns shot. There were too many zits to be defeated. I found out it was because there is one species of zit for every noun, adjective, and verb in the whole English dictionary.

My guys took a break in a dead zits home. The home was a dump. I had to rest in the hovel anyway for shelter against the zits. I had a salad for Linner (The time between Earths lunch and dinner on Zitton). Then I found the olive oil. I accidentally spilled it as zits were creeping behind me. It ran on the ground and ran over the zits feet.

No, The zits squeaked, The olive oil, its melting me! Ahhh! Nothing of the zits were left. My crew mused over what the heck happened. The smart cook said, I get it! The olive oil mustve been attracted to the zits skin! Which is made out of an unknown element. So their skin mustve heated up and melted everything of them!

I get it now. I said, The weakness of the zits is olive oil! We have at least a 6-month supply on board. This may not be enough to kill them all but it can slow down the zits from hugging our people and we can make an atom bomb of olive oil. Extra virgin olive oil! we all agreed and went to the shuttle; we slashed, punched and shot all zits in range.

We communicated the nations to go to the earth and modify the Manhattan Project to make an atom bomb of extra virgin olive oil. They agreed and went to Earth. We grabbed all the olive oil in boxes and grabbed bottles and dumped them in lines. We managed to make bombs out of the bottles with sodium which the ship was fraught with. We thickened the olive oil so water could slowly travel through the oil to the sodium and then put sodium in the edges on the inside of the bottle. The oil bombs were filled with 3 drops of water and sealed and thrown down. Seconds later the water would touch the sodium and explode killing tons of zits.

4 days later Japan called and said they came up with an alternate plan to destroy Zitton once and for all. We were told the plan and we agreed to the risk. We fought and destroyed many zits. But we ran out of oil. Our best hope was to find shelter away from the zits sights. We waited 1 week and then the nations landed on Zitton. They said they brought more oil for my crew. We ran to the big building where the master lay.

We saw him drinking the olive oil, building up immunity. We knew the oil plan was useless against him. He yelled in a deep voice, So the crew has come. Your oil plan is_

Yeah, we know. Its useless against you. The master said to call him the Omega Zit. And he let out a deep loud hum. He started running after us. We stole zit jetpacks that ran on Zittons air source and let out the air source for acceleration.

He chased us for hours. We slashed his hands, we shot his legs. He blocked every attack we used against him. I used the zits eyewear studier, which studies a targets stats. I figured out the Omega Zit was made of only skin and Zittons air. He was filled with olive oil and his body was sealed to keep the air in so now was my chance to use a dagger to pierce his skin. I threw the dagger; it slowly cut through the detonator on his skin, which then caused him to explode with olive oil spreading over Zitton.

The zits werent dead yet. Then was the time to use the bomb project. It was simply a bomb of extra virgin olive oil with an anti-matter atom of gold inside an artificial magnetic field (magnetic fields are sources of energy that repel anti-matter). When matter and anti-matter (which has an opposite charge of matter) touch, it creates an explosion equal to 100 atom bombs. I learned this from National Geographic. We dropped the bomb. When the detonator activated causing the artificial magnetic field to deactivate. Then the anti-matter atom hit the matter metal in the bomb. We left before this happened.

The bomb exploded and all of Zitton was covered in olive oil. Everyone cheered that the retarded huggers were gone for once.

YEEHAH! My crew said. Then I said to make a big yell to all nations.

WOOHOO!

We went back to earth after picking up a peculiar CD. After putting it into the computer at my house, I saw my crew heading towards Ida. I saw the part where we encounter the zits, when we went to Zitton and fought the zits. The fight had the gladiators battle hymn added too. It showed all we did as 5 separate DVDs Each 3 hours long.

I showed the nations the movies by e-mail. Then someone of my crew asked, I wonder, what happened to that zit you spared on Ida?
