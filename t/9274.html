#169685 5 2006-04-30T01:06:50Z Silent_witness
Not to diminish the effort that jtbandes put into being helpful or to say that his path isn't any good (it is a nice job, actually) but since I just finished making this map for you in Mspaint instead of writing a research paper, I may as well post it in case the above suggested path just doesn't work for you. The flight path should be simple enough to follow, and the color only changes when the flight path has to cross itself of if that part is of special significance, which, be that the case, I will point out below. The places where the path splits are places that one can either follow the white path for an easier jump (less gold) or the normal colored path (more gold). The start/fork and finish/reconnecting of the splits are marked by purple circles. As you progressively move up through the landings, there are two ways that you might want to get the gold, depending on the positioning of the zap drones. Jump method A. is jump over run then run back and jump method B. is run under jump back. On the left side, on the second from the top landing there are the words "wait here?" . I put those there because, depending on the postion of the zap drones, you may have to wait a little bit so that when you get on the highest landing on the left side and to the part that is signified by the bright green or bright green/white (depends how much gold you want and where the missile is) because you will want to make sure that you don't run into the back of the zap drone as you attempt to run off the edge and over to the other side. If you don't wait long enough below, you will probably get stuck waiting behind him above, which will then possibly affect how much speed you can get and whether you even get to the other side upon running off of the edge.

I hope that this helps (actually, I don't, because I hope that jtbandes' map was so useful that you are now past this level) and it isn't too complicated for anyone. Good luck!
