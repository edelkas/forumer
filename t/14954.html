http://www.adamdawes.com/windows/win_hpursuit.html

Not amazingly deep or varied, but kind of addictive and well worth the small download. It's sort of like a cross between OutRun and Death Rally, for those that have played them. You get points for 1. the distance you cover and 2. eliminating enemy vehicles, while avoiding civilian cars and staying on the road. Enemies may be destroyed by shooting them, forcing them off the road (or into the back of slow-moving vehicles...), or use of the 3 special weapons which you may acquire.
