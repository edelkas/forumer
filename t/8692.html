Gday, guys. I've got something sorta big to announce... But I'd rather waffle on and use as much ado as possible before the relatively straightforward announcement.

The Legacy, my friends, is the team of mods. A team that, as they put it, "became a force to be reckoned with." But what good is that, if there's no force that reckons with them?

So, myself and- well- I'll keep the details small, but I'm part of a rather small group. That calls itself "LA3^3", and the name is something of a hint at the actual members. Anyway- let's just call them... Atramentis.

So, we've put together a team... the idea, at first, was to partially challange and partially pay tribute to the Legacy team. Then, we started moving in different directions with different ideas... there's quite a bit going on, and much of it looks as though it's going to be rather interesting to watch develop.

But... how to introduce the LA3^3 team?

I thought a new column for N sounds like quite a nice idea, sorta to compliment the Legacy set but also with a slight twist that makes it recognisable as an individual project. So I made one. And here it is. 50 levels, about 80% of them never before seen, from yours truly. As in, me.

Some have been posted on NUMA before, but have been slightly modified (and/ or polished) for use in this column. And I am very, very proud of the result... some of my favourite levels made by myself of all time are in this collection.

Each episode runs in classic M&R difficulty, both within itself and in the entire episode set. The difficulty curve is slightly harder (and a bit more... haphazard) than would be idea, but hey- you can't have it all.

And each episode has a theme, which are as follows:
Episode 0: Prolouge
Episode 1: Lines
Episode 2: Towers
Episode 3: Symmetry
Episode 4: Pseudo- Survival
Episode 5: Augie March song titles
Episode 6: Floating objects
Episode 7: Race maps
Episode 8: Trees
Episode 9: Epilouge

Enjoy!

(A note- while this is all my own work, LA3^3 is- indeed- founded, and hopefully going to go places in the not too distant future. Stay tuned...)
