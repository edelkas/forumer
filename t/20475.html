Dronies Voting 2007 is Closed!

Welcome to Dronies Voting 2007. Voting will occur starting today, December 1st, 2007 and will end on December 31st, 2007. Results will be announced shortly thereafter. The Mare and Raigan Award for Contribution to the Community will be included in the results.

Here are the voting rules. You may only vote once for each category. Once you have submitted your vote form, you will not be allowed to submit again, so I suggest scanning the list, and choosiing wisely before making your selections. Save your choices in a .txt file if need be.

Importante! Just like last year, you may not vote for yourself. Vote for someone else. It makes for good sportsmanship.

Enclosed directly below are all of the nominees for this year. Nominees were chosen based upon raw nomination count to reach at least five nominees, and less than ten nominees. In categories where more than ten nominees were selected, tiebreaker votes were given to community members who had not already nominated for that category. In instances where less than five valid nominations were received, the remaining nominees were chosen. All nominees are listed in alphabetical order (I think - it's 5 AM).

In order to vote, we require your Username either on the forums or on NUMA as well as an E-mail address. The Legacy Team does not distribute your E-mail address in any way, and after the season is over, all votes will be deleted. If you don't feel comfortable giving out your E-mail address, you may input a random E-mail address, but in that instance, if there are problems with your votes, we will have no way to contact you about them.

If you notice any inconsistencies with the form, please let us know. If I left anything out, or if anyone has any questions, please feel free to bring them to our attention. Thanks to all of the people who nominated, and happy voting!

-The Legacy Team
