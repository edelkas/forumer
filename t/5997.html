The way I did this was to jump on a thwump after it comes at me and ride it back over as it moves slowly back, and jump to the next one as it comes back. The key to this is to trigger the next thwump as you're jumping onto the one coming towards you. It's tricky, but I got it to work, and I wasn't that great when I did it. Or you can try to wall jump most of the way up, then jump onto a thwump and out, like the guys on the high score table do. I think my way is easier if you're not really good.
