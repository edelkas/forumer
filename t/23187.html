BooBoo Cave (Custom Path)
http://nmaps.net/131461

Yeah Yeah Yeah Everyday we have a new Update!

Restart NReality (a mod of N, download @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Custom Path

CustomPath for Drone:
6^x,y,6,0,droneType,direction^^^customPath

where customPath is formed with U (up) D (down) L (left) R (right)

e.g. in my code:
!6^252,492,6,0,121,3^^^RRRLLLLLRR!
This drone will move right 3 tiles, then left 5 tiles, then right 2 tiles, and repeat and repeat and repeat
