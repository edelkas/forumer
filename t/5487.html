#111998 2 2005-11-03T01:58:54Z nevermore
I saw it. No pun intended.
I suppose I have negative influence, seeing as my wallet was stolen inside the theater where I watched the movie. My critique of Saw II is about the same as of Saw.

Firstly, I love the idea. I thought the concept was very interesting - not only is the victim physically challenged, it shows the extent to which a human mind will take itself. However, I thought it was executed very poorly. I haven't seen the first Saw in a while, but I remember that I really liked the whole scenario. While the writing was weak, the directing wasn't great and it sort of seemed like a wannabe Quentin Tarantino movie. Nonetheless, it's hard not to enjoy it, regardless of how technically bad it was.
Now for Saw II. I hated - I mean absolutely despised - the scenes outside of the deadly house, i.e. the scenes with Jigsaw and the cops. They were just mind-numbingly monotonous and dull, in my opinion. Because the deadly house was fairly action packed, it made for a jerky flow, and the strange pace made me feel uncomfortable. It's a strange feeling that's sort of hard to explain. I thought the traps were pretty cool, but that was about all I liked about this movie. The plot twists seemed forced, irrational and predictable, not to mention the plot holes that came with them. One of the cool things about the first Saw was that it really took a look into the human mind. With this one, the characters were very flat and stereotypical, and there was nothing interesting or surprising that the scenario made them do. The end came on very awkwardly too. Still, as with the first Saw, I still found myself enjoying it, even though it was pretty bad.

I must say, my favorite trap was the glass box with the razor-blade holes in which the woman gets her hands stuck.
