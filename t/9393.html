My contest, 'Don't Look Down!' is a contest in which there will be no enemies, the main danger is falling.

-Rules-
1. There must be NO enemies, that includes mines.

2. You may include these items, gold, one-way platforms and doors of any type, bounce blocks and launchpads (edited or otherwise).

3. You must post the map to NUMA and post a link to it in this forum.

4. The maximum amount of maps alowed to be submitted into this competion is 3. You may change your previous maps posted into the competition.

That's about it, if you have questions, ask me on this forum or email me.

For the Judging, I will judge, and I will need two other judges to help me. *The judges can't submit maps for this contest* The judging will go as follows...

-Judging-
The total is out of 25.
5 for aesthetics/how nice it looks.
10 for originality.
10 for playability (fun).
