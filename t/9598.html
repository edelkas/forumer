Okay, here is how I would like things to work, although, participation on your part is required for it to work properly. In an effort to promote NUMA mapmaking, each person will see who is above them, go to NUMA and rate 3 (or fewer if 3 are not available), of their maps, after which they will leave helpful (notice, helpful...not necessarily nice) comments that will be useful for helping the above person improve their mapmaking abilities. Postpostscript: Please post a link to your username NUMA screen for the next person's convenience...thank you.

Postpostpostscript: Please really and truthfully play all the chosen levels (unless they are art or something), rather than just rating and commenting on them after viewing them.
