V.D. Map Competition.
(Very Difficult Map Competition)

Rules:
  • The map must be to NUMAs standard of "Hard" or "Difficult"
  • The map MUST have a completion demo to prove it is possible by the end of the competition
  • Only ONE submission to the contest per person.
  • Include the tag VD in your submission to NUMA.
  • Map must be playable (No DDAs)
Judges: (Total of 3 Judges)
  • mrgy05
  • 3v!1
  • Mechtradevil
Scoring Guidelines: (Total of 100 Points)
  • 25 Points - Difficulty
  • 25 Points - Aesthetics (Overall map look)
  • 25 Points - Demo (Performance)
  • 15 Points - Object Placement (Flow)
  • 10 Points - Tileset (Flow)
Scoring Standards:

You have to consider what NUMAs norm of "difficult" is, your demo is one of the most crucial part of this contest. If your demo is slow, sloppy, just a completion (not AGD) you will get a lower score. Your demo is to impress the judges by showing close calls, flow, and different unique ideas that are incorporated into your map.

Prizes:
  • First Place - You choose a N-Art I (mrgy05) DED to you and a First Place VD sig tag
  • Second Place - I choose a N-Art I (mrgy05) DED to you and a Second Place VD sig tag
  • Third Place - Third Place VD sig tag
Contestants:
  • Goatman
  • darn hippie
This contest lasts 31 days
December 10th, 2007 - January 10th, 2007.
