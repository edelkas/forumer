Q: Right, I'm reading this now thinking "What the hell is this about?"
A: First off, if you don't remember the first, I'll explain. This is a competition which brings all the image makers of this forum together. People will be making images using Photoshop, Paint Shop Pro, GIMP, MS Paint, everything they can get their hands on to enter this competition. Some categories might be harder for certain people to complete than others, but the window is open for everyone of every skill level.

Q: How do I enter?
A: You make an image (Duh!) and send it via to me! Me and a special weekly guest will judge the results between ourselves, and post seperate scores. While we are judging, I will post the entries in this thread for the public to see, you can guess which image belongs to who if you wish.

Q: Soo...when does it start?
A: TODAY! Yes, the first week of the competition starts today, and the theme is Freestyle. The guest judge is the grand mod himself, Kashkin.

Q: How are the images scored?
A: The images are scored differently depending on the theme, the overall scoring system is three categories, each scoring ten points each, totalling 30. Adding mine and the guest's scores to create a final total of 60.

Q: Are there any prizes?
A: Yes.

Q: Can you tell us what they are?
A: No. And to save space, because it's a secret.

Q: Any rules?
A: Yes, there are. And I'll list them below:
  • 1 image per person
  • All work should be made on the spot, no past works.
  • If you wish to submit your work to DA, you can, but you'll give away which one is yours.
  • No cheating, my moderating friend Kashkin will check up on this.
  • All images must, as in, MUST be in .PNG format. If you have a problem with this, talk to me about it.
Q: Is that all?
A: Yes, get making!
