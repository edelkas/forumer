The N Factor![/color]

The Rules[/color]
The rules are similar to SCD, and a bit like Changing Enemies by max_ride.
Basically, you have 17 days to make a very effortfull map. I will choose the style of map. After with our 4 judges we mark the map like this:
n/5 for tileset.
n/5 for playbility. {Except for Quick DDAs}
n/5 for effort.
n/5 for length.
n/5 for how fun it looks {Just for quick DDAs}
Half marks are given. You may explain why you gave it the mark!
Total score should be n/20.
If you come last with the judges you get just the one point. If your score is the highest you get the number of participants as your point. The rounds are as follows:
1. Action
2. Survival
3. Puzzles
4. Tileset {This is just marked overall out of 20}
5. Prisoner
6. Race
7. Quick DDAs As short as you like. Have a go! If you can't do it I'll double your score on the Race round.
You can also join in the middle of the contest. You will get your points timesd by the round number. So if you start making a quick DDa and get 8 points, you get your score timesd by 6 which makes 48. You can also drop out. If you drop out on prisoner, you double the score of the last round.
You can only submit one map per round
You can submit old maps.
This week it is Action - get mapmaking!

Prizes[/b]
1st Prize = 2 sig tags + 2 dedication tileset
2nd prize = 2 tags + 1 dedication tileset
3rd prize = 1 tag + 1 ded tileset
4th prize = 1 tag
5th Prize = 1 tag
