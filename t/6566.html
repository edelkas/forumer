#129589 3 2006-01-04T02:36:26Z Aries_Griff
Try this: from that lower part where the terminal switch is, run hard right and jump once you hit that 45 degree incline. Wall jump a total of three times up, once to send you into that vertical piece of wall right above the rocket launcher, wall jump off that, then wall jump off the right side wall again right before you hit the incline-ceiling part because your momentum will send you hard left to a nice landing if you hit that incline with enough upward momentum. If you do it right, you should walljump three times in quick succession, off the right, left, right side in that order and land on the inclined part close to the exit of that area. So just jump off that before the rockets catch up and you should be fine.
