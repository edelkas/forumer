Hahahaha. I love it when people fail to use common sense.

When a highscore replay is recorded, even if you are playing the level normally and start with a different ammount of time, the replay is saved as if you started with 90 seconds. That way everyone is on level ground, and your place on the list depends ONLY on your performance on that level. To see how much time they took, subtract their record score from 90.

Thank you all for answering a question I didn't even ask. I solved my own problem within a few minutes, so I apologize for wasting your online time with something I could have simply helped myself with.

As for the true solution I was inquiring about, the amount of gold you collect per level does affect how much time you start with in each prior level. I went through the episode again, but purposely went for more gold that time around. And sure enough, I started with plenty of time to beat the level.
