I've been thinking of ways to enhance the game a little, as optional high-score-less modes, and I got to thinking... if the ninja is a ninja, he could use a clichd samurai sword.

Now, obviously, the ninja can't use his sword against the bad guys. They're solid chunks of metal. Besides, weapons are innately stupid in a game like N.

But, what the ninja can use his sword against...

Walls and Ceilings
Press the arrow key towards a wall and press and hold the Sword button. The ninja will stab his sword into the wall and dangle from it, coming to an immediate (if jarring) halt. Release the Sword button to pull the sword out and drop.
Uses: A temporary respite if you manage to find a little cubby to hide in.
Dangers: Homing droids love dangling meat.

Floors
Press the down arrow key while the ninja is running and press and hold the Sword button. The ninja will stab his sword into the ground and spin around it, reversing in direction nearly instantly and translating his momentum appropriately. If you let go of the Sword button while the ninja is swinging around it, he will instead translate all of the momentum and come to a dead stop.
Uses: A quick reversal into the opposite direction can save the ninja from a nasty enemy...
Dangers: ...and possibly throw him straight into another.

Bullets
Press and hold the sword button while N is not facing a wall, and N will draw his blade and use it N-style to deflect bullets that come towards him anywhere in the 180-degree frontal hemisphere -- even while he flies through the air! Note well, N-san, that even this almighty power cannot stop a gauss bullet or a guided missile, and doesn't do a thing against a droid either (except perhaps tick it off a little).
Uses: When the ninja draws his blade and deflects bullets, he saves his hide and can drench himself in gold.
Dangers: While concentrating on being so cool, the ninja can't change momentum, and fails to notice bullets that come at him from behind.

Thwumps
Stab a thwump in the brain by using one of the above techniques and it will explode, destroying the twhump (and the ninja) in an impressive display.
Uses: None.
Dangers: Innumerable.


Of course, giving the ninja a weapon of such unimaginable deadliness -- very cool though it may be -- scares away all of the bankers who are willing to cash in the ninja's gold. You will never enter the high score lists if you have a sword in your belt.

Deflecting bullets? That would ruin the physics of the game and make certain levels much easier. Plus, I'm not sure how that would be coded. It might be impossible.

It would make certain levels much easier, yes. It would also make levels designed to take advantage of the optional feature (by throwing a number of chaingun drones at you) actually possible to play.

In terms of implementation, it's very easy: if a bullet would hit you in the front, it doesn't (basic trigonometry to determine whether the angle of impact is less than 90 degrees or greater than -90 degrees). On the same frame as the bullet *would* hit, the ninja's sword arm and blade jumps in position to be perpendicular to the bullet impact and the bullet plays a "ding" noise instead of the classic ricochet.

Have you seen action movies and games where ninjas block bullets with swords? The effect is comically unrealistic (yet disturbingly cool), which I think would be great for an optional game mode designed for entertainment.
