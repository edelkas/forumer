Quote: zey_, May 26, 2006 07:39 pm
sorry for reviving this thread, but im changing my sig and now that doesnt work. and now i cant even put back my old pic! anyone know what to do?

Right-click the image you want to put into your sig and save it to your computer somewhere. Go here and upload the image. When you upload it, it will give you some codes. Go to the bottom one that says "Direct Link to Image", Select All and Copy. Go to your Edit Sig window, and click the IMG button on top. A box will pop-up and ask for the URL. Paste it into the box, and then click Ok. It will place the proper bbcode tags for you. Save your changes and you're done.
