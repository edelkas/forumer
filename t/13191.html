Don't worry, this isn't for my vocab words. This is much better in my opinion. This is for my english class, though. We had to integrate phrases (prepositional, appositives, ect) into a story of our choosing. Bear with me on this one, it's a lot better than my last series. It's a fun read. Short, too.

- - - - -

Sorry Mr. Turner, there aint nuthin wrong with yer sink, Donald looked up from his squatting position under the cupboard, sweat starting to roll down his pudgy face. Ill have to check the other pipes. Yknow, the toilets and showers n stuff like that. Standing up, the overweight plumber cracked his back, and then removed a pen and pad from his back pocket, jotting down notes.

Garrison Turner ran his hands through his thinning hair. Donald wasnt one to miss details, and if he couldnt fix the flooding from their sinks and toilets, no other plumber could.

Unfortunately, thisll have to wait till tomorrow. I got a job oer at the McKenzie residence down the street. Seems the sewage drains runnin underneath their house broke down, and it came up through their sinks. He grimaced. Now its everywhere, on the walls and ceilings, and it aint pretty. He jotted down some reminder on his planner, and handed Mr. Turner a receipt.

This should be the cost of the visit, today an tomorrow, assuming I can get it fixed. Sorry bout the flooding.

Water under the bridge! Mrs. Turner walked into the kitchen, smiling as always. Youre welcome back at any time, were very eager to get this problem done with.

Thank ye very much, maam, but I should be off. Have a nice day, now. He nodded towards both of them and went out to his truck.

Shame. I guess well have to put up with it until hes back. Garrison turned to retire to his office, before yelping suddenly. Dag-nabbit!

Whats wrong, Garry? Harriet walked briskely over to him, hand on his back.

These new dentures, is what! Third time I bit myself this morning. He rubbed his jaw fervently. My old ones fit better.

Well, that just goes to show you have to be careful where you place things like that. Youre always losing things. She glanced over at the calendar hanging on the wall. Oh, you do remember you have that doctors appointment?

Almost forgot, Im going to be late! Loss of teeth and memory, he retorted. Snatching up his coat, he ran out the door. Harriet Turner walked out to the lawn, waving to him as he left.



And how are you feeling today, Mr. Turner? Dr. Hutchinson, flipping through his files, sat across the room looking confident.

Garrison shifted uncomfortabely, breathing steadily. Strangest thing. You know those stomach aches Ive been having? They just vanished! I havent taken anything for it, either. I mean, one night I feel like Ive swallowed a baseball, and the next I feel great. The doctor looked up oddly from his chair.

About when did the pain go away? He shifted through his notes, eyeing them carefully.

Oh, Id say around two days ago. The ironic thing is, now that I wont have to buy medicine, the pipes back up and I have to pay for a plumber. You win some, you lose some. Dr. Hutchinson looked up from his report, eyes narrowing.

Now, you saw youve had some problems with canker sores?

He rubbed his jaw some more, wincing from the sharp yet subtle pain. Correct. Its these new dentures that Ive bought. Theyre so big, I keep biting myself. Couldnt seem to find my old ones I lost a few days ago.

Lost? He went back to his notes.

I went to bed a couple nights ago, woke up, and they were gone. I swear I put them away, but even Harriet doesnt believe me. He sighed. Well, I suppose I could have fallen asleep with them still in my mouth. But that doesnt explain why theyve gone missing. The doctors eyes suddenly widened.

Wait, when did you say youre stomach pains went away?



Back at the house, Donald was grinning triumphantly.

Thank you for coming so soon! Mrs. Turner walked into the master bathroom, just adjacent from her bedroom.

Not a problem, maam. I think I found what was backing your pipes up. An object was blockin the drains that lead from this toilet right here. Lucky to have found it before anything serious happened.

Harriet looked over at the mangled object the plumber now held in his greasy hands. What is it? She asked.

Looks likea pair of dentures.
