Direct copy paste from my word document for the first character.

Character Ideas:

Name: Chad Exoskeleton Kinghorn

Personality: Is not very social, feels at home with technology. Prefers machines to people (Yes I realise this is the same as what I just said). He is actually extremely intelligent.

Appearance: Medium length, dishevelled brown hair. Always wears jeans, a T-shirt, fingerless leather gloves, sneakers and a pair of glasses on his forehead which he uses when working.

Power(s): Robotic suit that he calls his Exoskeleton.

Starts out as Exoskeleton-Prototype which is simply just the most basic parts of his suit linked together with hydraulics to increase his strength, there is little or no armour plating. It is simply polished steel without any colouring apart from the suits name etched on to the backpack portion. The suit consists of a torso piece which serves as armour plating for his chest and backpack piece which serves to give him flight via Angel Wings (They look like 3 lengths of rectangular metal with a single booster on the first piece which is connected to the suit and 2 boosters on the next 2 pieces, the wings fold up in to the backpack piece when not in use. Named Angel Wings because the white flame of the boosters make them look like an Angels wings when In use), boots which have small boosters on the bottom for stability in flight among other uses (Jumping higher etc.), a pair of gauntlets which he mainly uses for protecting his hands, a headband like helmet with a visor on the front which he uses for scanning the area and getting feedback on his suit and has armour pieces at each joint to protect the machinery (Knees, shoulders, elbows, waist). With this he also uses a large railgun rifle which has a VS (Virtual Scope) which interacts with his helmet so he can aim as if hes looking through a scope without actually having to lift the weapon up to his head.

The second version is called Exoskeleton-beta It has a dark brown metallic colour to it, has the suits named etched on it and looks more professionally made in general. The entire suit is now armoured, the helmet now covers the entire head but the face is not armoured and is simply covered in the same material of the original visor (It now gives vitals, has improved scanning systems and is easier to make sense of), the flight systems on the back have been improved (As well as looking more well made there are now 4 wings instead of 2), the stabilising boosters on the boots have been improved and now allow him to seemingly hover for a short period of time. The gauntlets now have a low powered laser placed between the middle 2 knuckles and have a high voltage panel in the centre of the palm that can be turned on and off to give foes electric shocks varying from a small shock to stun or a large one to kill. The railgun has been improved to fire the metal longer and further, the metal slugs have been improved from a simple metal pole to an aero-dynamic spike for increased penetration and the VS has been improved to highlight known vital areas, night vision and thermal vision and to distinguish between friend and foe (Via small electrical devices on friends). As well as the improved railgun 2 small hand held firearms have been developed that magnetically attach to the armour so he can place them anywhere, the work like miniature versions of the original railgun except the VS now only highlights foes but has the night vision and thermal vision added to it.



The third version is reconfigurable so has 3 names for its 3 main modes, these are Exoskeleton-Delta, Exoskeleton-Epsilon and Exoskeleton-Gamma.

Delta is Black and Red in colour and has a demonic look to it and is configured for being in the middle of the action and brawling so it has more armour, boosters equipped to parts of the armour to increase the speed at which he can hit and a fully armoured helmet with a small visor which has VS built in due to having no firearms, the angel wings are now called Demon Wings there are still 4 but the booster colour is now blackish-red in colour and the position has been reconfigured to be in an X shape on his back. All boosters are now Blackish-red in colour and have a heavily increased amount of thrust for the sacrifice of being able to use them for more than a short period of time apart from the boosters on the bottom of the boots which have been configured to allow him to use a quick short bust or a longer boost used to hover which paired with the new Demon Wings allows him to fly in to battle at a very fast speed.

Epsilon is white and light blue in colour and has a smoother has an angelic look to the armour, the helmet sticks with the full face visor from beta but also gives vitals of anything that is in its sights, it is configured for ranged support, it has increased the amount of Angel Wings from 4 to 6 for improved flight, it forgoes all railgun based systems for energy based weaponry with a very large laser bombardment cannons mounted to the shoulders which fold over on to the back when not in use and can be used for quick speed boosts in flight. The lasers on the gauntlets have been improved and the boosters on the boots have been removed. The railgun has been replaced with a laser rifle which has an extremely large range and the VS has been improved to reflect this. The mini-railguns have been replaced with laser pistols which fire faster and are more accurate the VS has been improved on these to show places where the guns could cause an environmental attack (Melting the chain holding a girder up etc.). The boosters on Epsilon are all pure white and the lasers are all light blue. The suit is also equipped with medical technology.

Gamma has the same polished steel of the prototype and is configured for medium range combat with improved railgun technology. The only differences between this and beta slightly more armour (the armour on the helmet now covers the entire face apart from the headband shape across the eyes which makes it look like the prototype helmet with armour, the railguns can now change the metal slugs before firing (For example charging them with electricity, heating them up etc.) and have an improved firing mechanism for further increased range, speed and reload time, an auto-reloaded also increases the reload time allowing for fairly rapid fire, the VS on all 3 weapons is now equipped with thermal and sonic x-ray as well (Can see heat and sound signatures through objects). The lasers on the gauntlets have also been removed.

All versions of the suit after beta have respirators equipped so he can breath anywhere apart from a total vacuum. The next version of the armour will be called omega and essentially be a combination of Delta, Epsilon and Gamma. Major difference between lasers and railguns is the way they deal damage, lasers use energy which more burns things away on the surface and gradually cuts through things (Although when I say gradually it can be quite fast) where as railguns fire metal slugs at high speed which simply rips through objects allowing you to fire through objects.)
