#363314 2 2007-09-08T18:44:51Z SamuelDKelso
Are you sure your tuner can't handle it? I just did it with the built in tuner on my acoustic. Or do you just have a tuner that tunes to EADGBE?

If so, I'll try to explain it:

Play the low E on the sixth fret and tune it to sound like the A string. Voila D#. Now play the low E string on the seventh fret and tune the A string to it. PLay the A string on the fifth fret and tune the D to it. Leave the G the same. PLay the D string on the ninth fret and tune B to it. PLay the B string on the fifth fret and tune the high E to it.

Hope that helped!
