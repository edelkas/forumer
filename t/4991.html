Would be nice to "bookmark" levels and/or episodes we like to play in N. N has a LOT of levels and is very hard to remember where each of the "fun" levels are (or even just tracking which ones you have high scores on). So, any way you could add the ability to flag a given episode/level and have it visible on the Highscores screen for fast and easy selection at a later time?

Also, it would be VERY nice if it did a compare of our currently listed high scores for the selected episode/level and gave us some visual indication of where we stood at a glance (like a different color for being #0, for instance). Obviously, this would only work for high scores we have already downloaded (by selecting an episode).
