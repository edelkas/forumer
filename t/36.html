#22047 8 2004-04-07T15:17:27Z Guest
Multiplayer on a single PC would be possible but it might be problematic.

You're pretty much restricted to input from the keyboard and mouse. And, you'd obviously want to use the keyboard for both players. This would mean that at most 4 keys would need to be pressed at once.

Keyboards originally weren't intended to be used this way and some people may have older hardware that isn't capable of coping with the unusual amount of activity. They can expect keys to be unresponsive simply because too many keys are held down. This could mean you may not be able to move your character properly because your friend is jumping diagonally or vice versa.

It would probably be best if the devs left multiplayer out just to keep the source simple. With the source you'd be able to make the adjustments nessecary for a multiplayer mode to work over the net and the keyboard issue wouldn't be a problem.
