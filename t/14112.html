I was looking for an opinion on something I thought of.

After seeing Britannia's topic about how the forums weren't too lively anymore and reading your post, an idea popped out of the blue. Three months on the forums and eight hundred posts later, I figured I should do something for the community.

I am thinking of start a contest where people of all levels (map making and playing) can participate. The aim of the contest if for people to connect maps with map makers. My first thoughts at who would be suitable to make a map would be the Legacy team and a few very well known authors (krusch, astheoceanblue, KinGAleX).

The map makers would be asked to make a map in which they thought represented their style. It didn't have to be the best, and they wouldn't need to spend a lot of time on (ten minute map?) but just something they thought would be individual to them and typically them.

The contestants would be see the maps, and they would submit their guesses, who's maps belonged to who, all in a PM. A correct guess would score a point, an incorrect guess would score no points. I could hold many rounds and which people had the most points in the end would win.
