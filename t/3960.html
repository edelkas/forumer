Well, in case you couldn't see, the ninja breaks off the antenna of the Drone, stabs it to death with it, then uses it to break the door open.

About the bleeding: Well, what exactly do those drones consist of? A Anti-Gravity Mechanism, some kind of motor for their motions, and a sensor that seems to be nothing but a laser beam and some kind of basic data analyisis. Do you think that would take up an approximitly 2x2x2 meter Octawhatever shaped Drone? I don't think so. So I guess they are filled with some kind of material, something that helps them keep afloat. Of course, to do that, it'd have to be less dense than air and they'd bleed upwards, but for the sake of easy to understand comics, I decided to just treat them like some kind of primitive life form.
