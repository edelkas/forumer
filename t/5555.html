NaNs have been discussed extensively. They exist because something that's not a number snuck into your level data. Usually this is because when you push [L] to load the level, you still have the text box highlighted, meaning that [L] is read as part of your level code, thus creating an NaN and messing with your level. To fix this, click outside of the text box after pasting, before you press any buttons.

To register here, check this out. It's the top point.

To register on numa, open up N, go into configure, type in your desired username and password, along with your email adress, click create new user, go onto numa, type in the aforementioned username and password and your email adress, check your email to validate your account, and voila.
