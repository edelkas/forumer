Hey there. Here's a game that I released a while ago, just with changed rules. It's kind of a competition now.

'Don't Say a Letter' is a game where every week, I change the letter(s) that you can't say. The objective of this game is to say the longest sentence without saying a certain letter(s) that I've chosen.

Example: Without saying the letter 'E', I've written:
Spoiler: 65 Words.I am going to do this now, looking at all of you talk is boring, so I am posting this post in this thing that you will look at in a tiny bit, so I pray that this post has a lot of words in it so I can kick Trodgor001 out of first, so I can nab it away from all of you humans.

Rules: There are a number of rules in this game. Sorry, guys.
  • No 1337. For instance, pwn3d doesn't work.
  • Run on sentences work.
  • You can only have one sentence.
  • No copying from any resources. This is an example of something that wouldn't work.
  • No repetition. This wouldn't count.
  • The word "thy" is officially banned from this game. No buts, just don't say it.
  • You MUST post how many words you posted at the bottom of your post.
  • Numbers do not count as words.
  • No spamming. For instance, "uhh... uhh..." doesn't count.
  • Your sentence must make some sense. Blurting out random words will disqualify you.
  • Edited posts will not be counted.
  • Semicolons are also banned if used more than 5 times.
