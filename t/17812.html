--Made with VB .net--

Just a small application I made. My dad was a really good help.

So basically, enter in the map's information, and demo (optional), and out comes it in userlevels format! You can save it as a text file onto your computer as well. Sooo... that's it? I doubt it was really necessary, but I'm bored everyday anyway.

Something new on v1.0 is the multi map integration. It allows you to upload .txt files of maps you have already made and convert them into a userlevels format. You can upload as many maps as you want. All you do is enter the author name and pack name, and look for the .txt files. The file name will be the map name.

The new feature in v2.0 is that you have the ability to read your userlevels.txt file and browse through all of your maps! It doesn't matter if you have 10, or 10,000 maps in there! It will upload them in mind-blowing speeds! I uploaded the entire Vehemence x 2 (2200 maps!) in the blink of an eye. No, literally. It loads millions of times faster than N. And when you click the map, the data appears in the textbox.
