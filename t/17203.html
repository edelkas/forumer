#15092 1 2007-05-04T16:59:32Z sweep
My time is up.

When I first joined this community, I was very enthusiastic. I was full of ideas for how to improve this place - and heck, I like to think that I have, in my own small way, improved it. I was still pretty young, and this community was one where people didn't discriminate against age - Metanet was the perfect place for me at the perfect time.

I sit here as a global mod, a member of the Legacy Team and an admin of NUMA. It's no accident. Becoming NUMA admin feels like the culmination of my contribution to this community, it was always leading to that, and I always had plenty of ideas for how to make it work. I'm a creative mind, but the one thing that drives the creativity is enthusiasm. And the reality is, I've lost enthusiasm for this place.

The irony is that this happened just before I got the NUMA job. I was probably on the brink of withdrawing from this community back then, but I just couldn't turn NUMA adminship down. Things have changed within NUMA as a result of my participation, but not as much as they would have or could have at one point. I'm not sure whether I've grown out of this place, or maybe that I've lost enthusiasm because I've got all the social status/power I wanted.

Either way, I'm sorry. You guys must have expected great things from me, but when it came to the crunch I really couldn't be bothered. I'm not going to pretend to have enthusiasm, I want to spend my life doing things I'm passionate about. I have music to record, universities to apply to, girls to fall in love with (well, er, one at a time, but you know), gigs to play etc etc. My time here has run its course. I won't pretend otherwise.

I'm not going to write a long list of people I'd like to thank. You know who you are, you know what you've done.

Expect me to pop in every now and again I guess. But, for all intensive purposes, my time is up. I will continue to look after Bitesize applications, but apart from that consider me cut off.

x
