Metanet Two:

The Goal:

Take a metanet level, and recreate it. Use the feel, gameplay, enemy/item use, and recreate it into a level that while it's entirely different, plays exactly the same as your level of choice. Hopefully the result will be better than the original

Basic Rules:

Not really rules, but guidelines to use while recreating your levels of choice.
  • Use the same items and enemys.
  • Use the same tileset style.
  • Use the same strategies and ideas.
  • You can use any level you want, including the 80's collumn.
  • You cannot use anything that metanet wouldn't use(bugs, code editing, etc).
  • You are allowed three submissions.
Getting Started:

I hope this picks up, I think it's a great idea, and a fun new challenge. If you want to have the actual data for your selected levels, use Eeyore's program here:

N Level Data

When you are done, submit to NUMA, then post a link here.

The Judging:

It will be based off of 50 points.
  • 20, for the overall quality
  • 20, for the difficulty and quality of recreating your level of choice.
  • 10, for the fun-factor/playability of the map.
