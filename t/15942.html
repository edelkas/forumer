#298376 3 2007-02-17T23:08:02Z Twistkill
It isn't very hard at all.

Copy the data from the map you want on NUMA. The data is labeled 1.4+ data.) Now, go into your userlevels.txt. You see that line that says *****PASTE ALL DATA BELOW THIS LINE*****? Well, that's what you do; You paste the data anywhere underneath that line, (and also underneath the "&userdata=") but make sure it does not interfere with any of the other data you see there. You need to make sure they are all separated from each other.

Now, go to Userlevels in N. Look around for the map you just copied. If you found it, congratulations! If no maps show up at all, then this has happened: You have moved the N.exe (The game itself) out of it's original folder. The userlevels.txt and N must be in the same directory. If you want them easily accessible, make shortcuts to them onto your desktop, but do not drag the original copies out of that folder.
