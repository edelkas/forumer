I thought about putting this in the Other Games section, but seeing as this is in the conceptual stage, it is not quite a game yet. So far my ideas are:

5 layers in a level. Enemies can now rain death from above/below!

Multi-layered enemies. Now some enemies and all bosses have some depth!.

Various easter eggs. Some suprising things to be expected.

Somewhat of a storyline. Not much though.

New powerups. Of course, I haven't really decided on them, so I'm going to need help.

New uh.. those enemies that can capture your ship (don't know what they are called). See Powerups.

Radar. I'll have to explain this more thoroughly. When you are on a layer that an enemy is NOT on, it will be more transparant depending on how "far" the other layers are from you. The catch is, it auto-updates positions very slowly, and there is no distinquishing between layers.

You get more moving room. No longer are you limited to just one row. You now have four.

New bosses. Hey, something has to have a better chance at killing you than everything else...
