#50450 50 2005-02-15T04:50:25Z tktktk
Hey guys, not to give you an extra deadline, but Mare and Raigan said that we have to have our map suggestions for 1.4 user levels in by the last week of February. I think it'd be really awesome if we could get some of the best entrants into the game, since they would fit well with the other levels. This isn't the contest deadline or anything, but if you want your maps to have a shot at making it in you'd better submit them by then.

And here, I'll start us off:

Map: N Your Eyes
Author: Kablizzy

First off, I could absolutely see this in the main game. There isn't a single item that looks odd or out of place like so many other user maps have. The tileset is very nice, clean and simple. The gold placement was good too.

I sorta liked how the drones were placed as well. Metanet levels often seem to have everything symmetrical except for one or two key elements, and in this case the drones were it. They offered quite a different experience depending on which way you went up... but then again there was no real reason to go right since left is so much faster. The exit switch was an interesting challenge that could be undertaken in several different ways.

Overall the level was nice and easy. It kind of reminiscent of nine's Quick Round series, particularly Technology. Nothing too frustrating either, I really couldn't think of anything bad about it. It was a great way to kick off NC6.

Score: 42


Map: defenscerrator
Author: Eli

Another good attempt as far as Metanetness, just a few little things to pick on. You went slightly overboard on the gold in the upper right and the pieces between the doors in the lower left looked a little weird to me. The upper left looks messy and cluttered, and there wasn't much reason for having three rockets; one or two would have sufficed. On the other hand it does have style, and I liked how the gold in the exit room went alongside the tiles.

The upper right felt kind of monotonous going through that thrice, and those gausses were like attack dogs if you tried for the gold in the upper left (not necessarily a bad thing). The bounce blocks and mines seemed relatively well placed, not LV-caliber but good nonetheless. The last part was my favorite, the way the one-ways were set up with the rockets was unique and the gold was fun to try for.

Overall an above average map with three distinct parts, each with its own feel. The first part was boring, the second so-so, the last excellent.

Score: 35


Map: I Get Around
Author: HeartView

Well barring the slightly out of place circle of tiles in the beginning, this one is a beauty with its graceful, streamlined look.

However, the gameplay itself is where there are a few problems. The bottom part is nicely designed and fun to play. Then you come to the two up-facing thwump areas. The one on the left is simple enough, but the right one has that pesky floorguard that makes it just a little too difficult to try for. I did sorta like how you were forced to use the thwump to get up to the final level. The part that kills the level for me though is the last part with the exit switch. The lazer drone combined with the floorguard is a deadly combination. If you're going to make a part of your level that frustrating and difficult, put it near the beginning.

Overall, this is an average level that's partially ruined by an irritating ending.

Score: 29


Map: Up, up, and... run away!
Author: brickman

Hmm... sorta like Metanet infused with brickman. The sides and top are perfect, but then there's those double sided one-ways and such in the bottom and center that look a little clumsy.

It plays very nicely though. To start you can either just drop through or take the jump onto the first bounce block. Then it's a reasonably simple climb to the switch. After avoiding the rocket, you drop down and can either repeat the process on the other side or risk taking on the guarded exit switch with extra obstacles. If you went ahead with the other switch you can cruise on past the floorguard and, avoiding that annoying little mine, drop into the exit.

Overall, this is a strong level with a well-balanced difficulty, in spite of forcing two very different styles to collide rather awkwardly.

Score: 36

Map: Turret Arena
Author: Maniac

Designwise, the only thing that sticks out is the exit. The tiles that enclose it aren't connected which makes it look a little funny. The top half also looks a tad cluttered, but nothing terrible. The general design is kind of uninspired. It also kind of looks more like an extension of your style than Metanet's.

Now, this level is hard. And it's not just that it's hard, it's that it's not much fun to play either. The lower part is fine, the center switch was a nice little diversion, and the entire bottom half is actually kind of easy. But then you get into the top half and it all goes downhill. Two rockets and two gauss turrets in a sea of bounce blocks makes things too difficult. I think it would have been much more balanced if there was only one gauss.

Overall this is a hard and frustrating map with more bad qualities than good. This isn't to discourage difficult maps, but just remember they have to be fun as well.

Score: 24


Map: Moving away, but getting closer
Author: Echarin

A little too random-looking if you ask me. The double one-ways in the center are a no-no, and those chaingun pens are just plain weird. I don't like the way the gold is scattered either. Gold should be placed in hard to reach places, not the easiest path.

The only real threat while playing through the level is the chainguns, which happen to be my least favorite enemy. They tend to result in lots of cheap, unfun deaths, which unfortunately was the case in this level. In spite of all that I did manage to have fun with this level however. There was at least some skill and timing involved avoiding the chainguns, which was good since it stopped it from becoming a game of Russian roulette. The floorguard was useless, but the gauss turrets and most of the mines were used well.

Overall, an okay map made a little more interesting (but not necessarily better) by a couple of chainguns.

Score: 30

Map: Arrow'd!
Author: so_what/nevermore

Sigh... if I were to close my eyes and pick randomly out of the levels on Numa, this is exactly the kind of map I would expect to pick. It just doesn't look or play Metanet. At all. There are numerous little messy things that are characteristic of a regular 'ol user map. There's also that irritating exit/adjacent door glitch.

It plays okay, but again, not like a Metanet level would at all. The first section is your standard drivel. The lazer drones presented a considerable challenge, but the chaingun wasn't a threat at all. The switches behind the gauss turrets were pretty simple, as was the exit switch room. Dropping to the goal was very easy if you went about it the right way. The level was somewhat fun, but it didn't have anything I hadn't seen before.

Overall this was a pretty simple map with one or two nice moments and several rather dull ones, with virtually no Metanetness whatsoever.

Score: 25


Map: Tombs of Giza
Author: so_what/nevermore

This one has a nice design. The lower left could have used a one-way rather than two doors, and the exit shouldn't have been half covered like that, but otherwise good job. I really could see this as a main game level.

The first part of the map is very easy, and almost becomes a chore as you continue to play through. The bottom left section is good; the gauss turret is challenging to get past and the thwumps are easy with the right strategy. Then there's the rocket area, the most difficult part. That top switch is a real pain to get, and requires a lot of skill and strategy.

Overall this is a solid level with three sections, ranging from dully simple to satisfyingly difficult.

Score: 35

#52000 87 2005-02-28T01:37:35Z tktktk
All right, you asked for 'em:

Map: Fluidity
Author: Lancer

Okay, like you said in the description, this would definitely be in the Freestyle category if there was such a thing. It's very reminiscent of 0-0 in its design, and aside from those four pieces of gold that are too close to the tiles it could easily pass for an earlier level. It's a large sprawling level that has a good style to it.

The level is quite fun to play, and it's very open to different routes. It's really simple to complete, and even to all-gold. I don't know why, but it was very frustrating when I tried to though. In my opinion it could have used something to get up more easily on the right side; rockets and sheer walls tend not to mix well. Other than that the level flows very smoothly. The center part with the gauss turret is nice and seems to be the heart of the level. However, most of the top area feels bland and unexciting while at the same time somehow still fun.

Overall, a very simple map similar to some of the earliest levels, with a nice flow but some rather boring parts.

Score: 37


Map: Tension
Author: Lancer

Woah. Couldn't be more different from Fluidity, and that's not really a good thing. There are several things in here that, while creative, Metanet just wouldn't do. The biggest one is the bottom right. Not only does it seem rather cluttered, but there's no way I'd ever see something like this in the main game, with the one-way clusters and all that. The left looks ever-so-slightly mine happy, but overall okay.

Hard, and overly long too. The beginning is probably the best, especially the bottom bit with the drones. Getting back up while avoiding the gauss is annoying and I kept dying there for some reason. The lazer area is very complex, and the one-ways are like cages of death if you're not careful. The exit switch is one of the few areas that displays some elegant simple fun. Then there's the exit section, at first I didn't realize that the best way to get up there is to jump to the little slanted tile first.

Overall this is a lengthy, difficult and somewhat frustrating map that bears little resemblance to a Metanet level but still has some redeeming qualities.

Score: 29


Map: Angularity
Author: JeR(K)

You've got to be kidding. Why do you guys submit stuff like this that you've got to know has nothing to do with the theme? The "forcefield" of locked doors around the switch, the gold on top of the switch surrounded by the messy mine room, and the ridiculously cluttered bottom section make it obvious that this is a user map, and a subpar one at that.

This is one looooong level, and there is very little enjoyment to be had in playing it. The center drone part is very slow, and then you drop into the difficult and claustrophobic gauss room. You often have to sit waiting for the lazer drone to move out of the line of sight. Then there's the mine-laced room that's very easy and just seems like another obstacle to get through. By this time I've pretty much lost track of all the switches you have to waste your time getting before sliding into the exit.

Overall, this is a very long and tedious map that is not in any way enjoyable to play through.

Score: 19


Map: Inversion
Author: maximo

Wow. The only real thing I can pick on is that it actually feels too polished to be a main game level. Still, the elegant simplicity makes up for the overly refined style, and I can see Mare and Raigan making something like this. The layout sort of reminds me of 39-2 but it doesn't play anything like it.

I like how the difficulty ramps up slowly as you move higher up in the level. It starts with a simple floorguard, then goes to 2 floorguards and a gauss, then the floorguards and a rocket, and finally the two gausses and floorguards at the top. The drones are a nice touch and balance out the level. The action is fast paced and extremely fun. The difficulty is perfect, it's a great challenge for beginners and a quaint little romp for veterans. One of the few things I don't like is how the launchpads are placed so that you can't reach the second side platform at all, or the the second middle platform without difficulty.

Overall I think this is a favorite to win this contest, simple yet elaborate, exciting and fun.

Score: 43
