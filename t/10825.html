#194639 4 2006-06-26T04:20:08Z Kablizzy
1. A topic is something like this, where you simply talk with other forum members about things.

2. You don't, unless you have something mindblowing that absolutely requires one. Insofar as clovic's subforum, that's a running joke that we have.

3. There are a couple of ways. The first way is to click on the "Quote" button at the top of someone's post, that will do it automatically. To do it manually, just type in [ quote = name ] (Without the spaces), and it'll do it for you. Don't forget to add the [/quote] at the end.

4. Simply put, you have to make them, or someone has to make them for you. I had mine custom-made quite a while ago, and each person has their own way of doing so. You may want to visit Elaxion's thread, and see if he'll do one for ya.
