The title explains it all. Using Ngaged's Progressive Map, you need to post a demo in this topic, with the best close calls as possible.

Thanks to Ngaged for his map, and his permission.
Judges
ME
~3 more judges needed, please

Judging
40 points for originality
-Were the close calls initiated well? Was it dramatic to see the demo?
40 points for non-repetitive-ness
-Were the close calls different, and required different skills? Were close calls initiated with most of the enemies and not only one?
20 points for speed
-Was the demo quick, yet it still had many good close calls?
Note: close calls are more important than the speed.

Rules

1. Post the demo here, in this topic.
2. Maximum close calls with ANY enemy is 5.
3. One demo per person. Please do not edit your demos.
4. Contest ends on March 12th.
5. A close call is counted as an encouter with an enemy (mines NOT included) half the size of N. *this may be changed*
6. Demos must be completed!

Note: remember that you are submitting the BEST close calls, not the MOST close calls.

Have fun!
