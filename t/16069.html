Rules
  • Each week, participants will be randomly split into two teams.
  • On Sunday, I will give one randomly chosen team a theme for making thier map.
  • Each member of that team will have until Tuesday to make their map.
  • Each person on the other team will be randomly assigned to a map on Wednesday.
  • Using the same amount of the same items and tiles from that map, they must create a new map by Friday.
  • On Saturday, the judges will vote for a map from each team. The map from each team that gets the most votes will be eliminated
  • Anyone who does not make a map will be eliminated.
  • No DDAs, Survivals, Narts, Tilesets, or Incompletes are allowed.
  • E and D tiles do not count, nor does gold
  • Last man standing wins.
