This is really simple.

One poster posts that something is happening in 3 days to the next poster. The next poster post something funny that they would say and do as a result of knowing this.

Example:

Poster 1:The Apocalypse is happening in three days.

Poster 2: Oh, my gosh!!!! The Apocalypes!!!! Ahhhh!!!!!! (jumps off Mt. Everest)
The next poster will die in 3 days.

Poster 3:Uh, oh. Wait a minute. I'll go on a killing spree for 3 days!
The next posters birthday is in 3 days.
[and so one...]

You can get specific (like: 3 days, 7 hours, 43 minutes, and 12.6 seconds) but the "3 days" is mandatory.

Guys, the event can be either good or bad.
