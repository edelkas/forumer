#322310 3 2007-05-18T22:26:19Z Hendor
Yes, it would happen to us if we finished 12-2 with 0.35 seconds left. The idea when you are playing episodes is that the amount of time carries on from level to level. So, if you finished 12-2 with 87 seconds to go, you start 12-3 with 87 seconds. You only get 90 seconds at the beginning of each level, in your case, 12-0. To beat episode 12, you are going to have to restart the episode and either beat each level faster than you did before and/or get more pieces of gold, because they add 2 seconds to your total time.
