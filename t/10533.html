Theme: Episodes

This go around we are asking for a 5-map episode. The maps are to be episodic in nature and if you want a bench mark, go play Metanet maps and not the userlevel packs floating around. I'll explain the scoring shortly.

The rules are simple and are as follows:

-No bugs. If Metanet doesn't use it, you don't.
-All episodes must be submitted in .txt format attached to a post here or linked to a hosting service that has it (iHud is good place to host if you need a place to host a file [Also, keep in mind that ihud files *do* expire, so keep your hard-copy so we can reference it if it does go away]).
- The levels should be in userlevel format, which if you don't know goes as follows:
$name of map#your name#nedcon#map data#
-No editing of maps once they are judged. This is gonna go on an honor system, so please, if you are caught, you will suffer an undisclosed penalty, and trust me, you won't like it.
-All authors are allowed 1 submission, period. This is because we have to judge five maps per submission. So take your time and don't rush in your submission, because you can't submit then resubmit and just replace your old submission.
-Once you submit it is your final submission. You may edit a map until it is confirmed as submitted (as in I'll post and say you can't edit anymore because I'm going to put all these into one file for the judges).
-I've decided a deadline, see below.
-Seeing as all scoring is based on judges' discretion, there will be a zero tolerance for any arguing of scores. If you would like to discuss a score, carry it out via PMs with the judge in question (aka, don't complain to me about someone else's review).
-It is your responsibility to check all scoring for your maps, not ours (and please check our math).
-You can not use pre-existing maps in your episodes.
-If I forgot something, it's common sense. Don't be underhanded, and it should be fine.

Scoring is as follows:

Every episode will be compiled and placed in one master .txt file. Willing members of the Legacy elite alumni will play through the levels and vote for their favorite 3 episodes from all the entires. The ten episodes recieving the most votes will be our finalist episodes. These ten episodes (and only these ten) will be judged by Me, Lucidium, and Littleviking001 (and clovic if one of the finalist episodes is a judges entry) as such: 15 points per map, and 25 points of overall episodicness. The 15 per map is as if the map was standalone and the 25 points are for if the maps worked well as an episode, how they increased in dificulty, time in the episode, gold usage, etc. (episodic stuff).

The deadline for entries is August 11th, midnightish US central time.

::edit::

Oh yea, any and all questions please ask so we can clear them up, rules are hard to write comprehensively.

::edit 2::

Prizes are a secret but trust me they will be very very amazing and worthwhile.
