The contest is to make a level that combines an animal from the sea and the land, like what im sure we've all done in photoshop, hybrid style...for example, combine a sea turtle and a giraff for all i care...(thats a crazy idea)

the creature must be made of tiles at least 80%, objects such as mines or gold can be used to distinguish what part of the animal comes from what, but it needs to be mainly tiles, i dont wanna make this into an art contest, there arnt enough really good N artists out there to make that interesting...

the level must be an action level...dont come crying to me if the tileset doesnt make a great action level...either change it so it does, or just submit it anyway cuz the tileset is just too good not to submit...

levels must be submitted to numa...but thats kinda a given...

pretty much everything else goes...any items, edited or ortherwise, NaN's, edited pads, psycho canons(i wish you wouldnt), door glitches(keep those to a minimum please), and anything else you can think of

an example of an acceptable level is my very own
butterphish
keep in mind, you do not have to blend 2 things together like i did, a walrus with an elephant head is a perfect example of something that is acceptable...

if your having trouble coming up with ideas, feel free to ask...although im not so sure i should be helping ppl much, id much rather see alot of cool tilesets then a lot of shitty ideas gone wrong...maybe i could just take a few points off creativity or originality...

scoring:
40 points creativity --------must i explain this one?
30 points recognizable-----this is important to me...if all i see is a blob, theres something seriouesly wrong
20 points playability--------not as important to me, but worth the effort if you want to win
10 points originality--------*read below

it also needs a cool, hybrid name...not required of course, but id like to see what ppl can come up with...

*i know sites with tons of these things on them...if i start seeing submissions of things ive seen before, and if they look at all similar, that last 10 points is going way down...worth the effort if you wanna win...

i would like a total of 3 judges, incuding myself
