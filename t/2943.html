#2642 1 2005-04-24T04:31:57Z RokusaburoMichiba
N repeller/attractor
It attracts/repells N and the effect is stronger the closer you are to it, could be useful for DDA's

Sound Drone
It's a zap drone only that it only chases you if you move near it. As long as you're not moving when it's near you, it won't notice you.

Wailer
Works alongside the sound drone, if it sees you, it will attract sound drones by 'wailing'

No Jump/Walk Tile
When you're on that tile, you cannot jump/walk

Ghost property enemies
Enemies that can go through walls

Timed doors
Doors that open/close on certain intervals

Color switches
When a color switch is activated, all corresponding doors of the same colors are opened while a door of a different color would be closed until the corresponding colored switch is activated, which in turn the previous color would also be closed
(so if a blue switch is activated, only blue doors are opened and if a yellow switch is then activated, yellow doors open while other colored doors including blue doors would be closed)

Numbered switches
Switches that must be activiated in a certain sequence

Levitator
Thing that allows N to levitate when N is above it (N is not allowed to jump, however)

Uh... I had more...

#62808 15 2005-04-29T20:55:41Z brickman
I think the sound/wailer drones could be good if you changed it slightly. Here's my take:
Sound drones are not bound like the other drones, when you are within a certain range (lets say 2 or 3 inches) and either jump, run, land or wallslide, they will hear it and home in on the last spot they heard the sound from. Unless you're jumping or you let go of a wall, this means you're getting it. We'll say they move at up to the speed of missiles, but instead of turning they will just accelerate in your direction (not all at once, and they do have to slow down first if they're going away from you), and bounce off walls. I can picture it in my head but I'm not quite sure I can describe it right in words. The best way to deal with them is to leap over their heads, since they can't track you in the air, and then run at top speed as they hurriedly turn around to chase you. This is pretty nice, but the real fun comes with tracker drones there as well. These basically sit still until they see you and then move like the sights for a gauss turret, but without the restrictions of staying in its line of sight. Instead of stopping and shooting, however, these things will emit a loud noise whenever they're tracking you which overrides normal sound drone behavior and causes them to home in on the trackers. However, the sound drones will pass through the trackers, and if you keep moving they'll always be targeting the area behind you, so you're basically being chased by a crazed homing drone which is bouncing off the walls and nipping at your tail. This is the real cool part, and it may not even be worth the bother to have them function without trackers because the trackers are what makes it good. Maybe they don't do anything until the tracker is activated and you place them in the level paired with the tracker (like with doors).
