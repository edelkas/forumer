'Number of horns on a unicorn' is a unitless measure. 'Acre' is a squared distance unit. 'Teaspoon' is a unit of volume, a cubic distance unit. And 'lightyear' is simply a distance unit. Using feet as the unit, you end up with X(ft) = Y(ft / ft) or Y(ft). So they can be converted.
