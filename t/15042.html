I wouldn't recommend sendanonymousemail.net. it's a known logger, and several people in the anonymity community suspect it of having government sponsored (a.k.a. monitored) remailers in it's chain. it is, of course, still very popular...

if you want to be really secure, you have a couple of options.

Harder, but far more secure way: use Cypherpunk or Quicksilver nym (short for Pseudonym) servers. some instructions on this are found here:
http://email.about.com/cs/anonemailtips/qt/et041304.htm
(a good article, despite being from about.com)

Easier, but less secure: Use a web-interface remailer.
a couple good free ones:
http://anonymouse.org/anonemail.html
http://www.anonmail.de/ (requires registration, but more feature-heavy.)

[/paranoia]
