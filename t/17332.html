For basic Mafia rules, see http://www.mafiascum.net/cgi-bin/wiki.pl?Newbie_Guide.

Deathy Mafia is a variation of Mafia in which there are five players: four cops and a mafia. Each cop is of a different type. The four types of cops are:
- Sane: Always gets the correct result of investigations.
- Insane: Always gets the incorrect result.
- Nave: Always gets "innocent" as a result.
- Paranoid: Always gets "guilty."
If you are a cop, you are not told which type you are. You must deduce this by reasoning and logic, and decide who the mafia is with that information. During the day, players may say what results they got on their investigation. In the case of the mafia, they may lie and make up a result.

At the beginning of the game, the moderator tells everyone their roles, cop or mafia. All the cops get a head start, and may investigate someone immediately. The mafia must wait until the first night to make a kill.
