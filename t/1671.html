Okay, here's a short 'cap of a possible setting:

The dark skies overhead looked ominous. The clouds, black and foreboding, heralded the coming of the invaders. It seemed as though the skies were always overcast since the invasion had begun, and that the Sun only peeked through periodically. He couldn't remember any days when it was sunny and cheery anymore. The world was in turmoil, and it seemed these days that the very earth was revolting. Earthquakes were a regular occurence, and volcanic activity in the north was frequent. The waters rose and fell regularly, and the blackened skies above yielded often to Tornadoes and Hurricanes. There were few cities that hadn't been ravaged by the incoming hordes, and those that did still stand were rife with poverty and in danger of natural disasters.

From his vantage point atop the grassy knoll, he could see the entire kingdom below. The last of the industrialized townships in the south, Klaa D'iel was perhaps the largest city still standing. No news had come from the northern parts of the continent, nor from overseas either. Ships hadn't been spotted in weeks, as the seas were too rough to travel. He sat atop his trusty steed, gazing downward at the bustle below. The city was a large one, nearly a million residents, most poor and starving now. The shipments of food from out west still hadn't arrived, and the messengers that the Premiere had sent out had not returned. he feared that the western towns had fallen as well. He sighed lightly, and kicked his steed into motion, towards the city. As he approached, the steam billowed from the homes and businesses within, and joined with the darkness above. As he approached the large steel gates, the guards called out to him. He called back a password, and moments afterwards, the gates creaked open. He nudged his horse into motion again, and moved inside...

Now, I tried to keep the definitions as loose as possible, so that it can be built upon. But my initial idea was a world in which the planet as a whole is partially industrialized, steam power and moderate technology, yet many aspects still medieval with modern twists, such as certain forms of transportation (Horses, carts, trains, no cars, a kind of crude steam-powered plane, perhaps airships, but I don't usually like sinking to that level), and weaponry (Swords, perhaps crude firearms, rudimentary forms of explosives, perhaps), and even materials (Steel, telephones, maybe televisions and radio, although the prologue I came up with there didn't seem to make that likely), and technology (20th century science, modern medicine, no genetics or nanotechnology, but perhaps cybernetics, etc.), but the overall feel, I suppose, harkens relatively - And I absolutely hate using this reference, but it seems appropriate - to the Setting of Final Fantasy 7, in that the world is partially modernized, yet not quite "modern". If anyone wants to build on this, feel free, and if you don't like it at all, that's fine, too. It's just one of a multitude of ideas that we could go with. As far as the invaders thing, I thought that would provide some action, not for the RPers to go "crusade" against, 'cause that would be a bit too "heroic", but for some post-apocolyptic-feel. Also, this could only be the status in one part of the world. If the world is less modernized, the world could be huge, and communication between continents, and even civilizations could be limited to very little, or even nothing. The people could even have no notion of how large the world is, reminiscent of a pre-columbus era as far as exploration goes, but with more modernized technology. That would keep the RP from limiting itself too soon, and it could make for some neat stuff to do.

Either way, just an idea I concocted here on the spot, and just one of many ways this could go.
