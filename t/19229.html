RULES

1 - You may participate in any round(s) you like

2 - You may submit any amount of playable maps that adhere to the rules below (note that there are no object restrictions)

3 - I will submit a map that your map is reworked from evry round

4 - Each tile on your map must be a part of a specific formation (e.g. 3x4) on my map that I decide each round (this is the most complicated part - I will provide examples before the first round starts)

5 - You will submit your map(s) either by link(s) to NUMA, or by .txt file ALL IN THE SAME POST. That means edit new ones in as you like

6 - Maps will not be accepted after the deadline, unless a good reason (I will decide on this) is provided prior to the deadline.

7 - You may judge in any round you like

8 - If you judge, you must judge every map that round, except your own if you also participate. Judges have three days after the round ends to PM their judging to me. Incomplete or late judging will not be taken into account. I will submit the judging the first time I'm online after the judging deadline is passed. If no-one else has judged, I will be the judge.

9 - The judging criteria will be :
- n/40 for overall gameplay
- n/40 for use of tiles
- n/10 for use of objects
- n/10 for innovation
- n/x for aesthetics*

*aesthetics is a tie-breaker decided by me - in the unlikely event that multiple maps have the same overall score then I will differentiate between them with this criteria.

10 - The way that overall score is worked out is somewhat different to most competitions. Each player's highest-scoring map for each of the above criteria is taken, and then added together to make their overall score. This is to make submitting more maps give a slight advantage.

If you have any queries or suggestions, tell me before the first round starts!
