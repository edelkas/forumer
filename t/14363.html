While thinking about binary, and browsing through NUMA, I realized that we already have a binary system. The favorites! Favorites are binary. They are yes and no. I think this should be expanded on, with somewhat of a "Top Favorites" Section. Now an issue involving this has been brought up. Saying that in the binary system, top rated would be improbable because the rich just get richer, and will never make room for other maps. Which is why, if implemented, it should be broken down into three/four different subcategories. Which would most likely be the following.

Quote: Top Favorited
"Today's Most Favorited"
"This Week's Most Favorited"
"This Month's Most Favorited"
"All-time Most Favorited"

I think this would be a really sweet addition to NUMA, and all the new changes. It's binary, and we don't even have to overhaul our rating system for it.
