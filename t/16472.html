EDIT: Sorry, but this is only for Americans only. We apparently have different phone numbers in different countries. [/b]

Here's how it starts: Get out a calculator. You probably have one in your OS, so open the dang thing.

After this, type in the first 3 (count 'em, three) digits from your phone number, excluding a (1) if you have one. Also, leave out your area code. If it's (1) 555 - ..., don't count the 555.

So far, it should look like this:

(The first three numbers in your phone number)

After you enter this in, multiply it by 80. Add 1, and multiply the number by 250. After you get this number, add the last 4 numbers from your phone number. So, if your phone number was (1) 555-555-5555, you would use the fourth set of numbers, or the "5555". Add that number again, subtract 250, and divide that number by 2.

Here's how it works step by step if anyone cares. The instructions are in bold:

let x be the first three numbers of your number and y be the last four.

x
x 80
80x
Add 1
80x + 1
x 250
20 000x + 250
Add last 4 digits
20 000x + 250 + y
Add last 4 digits again
20 000x + 250 + 2y
Subtract 250
20 000x + 2y
Divide by 2
10 000x + y

Ta da!!

Sub your first three digits in for x and last four for y into that final answer.
