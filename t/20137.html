Alright. So I figured my map pack was just forgotten about, but today I made a map for the first time in a while and have gotten several comments about 1) Whether or not I am still working on it, 2) if so when is it coming out, and 3) Why has it taken so long?

Here's the deal. I've been extraordinarily busy with school, work, women, guitar hero, and life. I will be honest, I have not done anything with it in quite some time, but I now want to finish it, polish it, and release it with an even better product than it was originally going to be.

In answer to question 1, I am now, after tonight I will make at least 3 maps a day until I have sufficient product. It will come out when it's done, but with this 3 maps a day thing, this time it will actually have a time frame and WILL BE FINISHED!!

Since it has taken so long I want to do something new and in addition to the rest of the product. For anyone who either doesn't know or doesn't remember, the pack is a dedication of sorts to music as a whole. I have selected some of my favorite map makers and also I'd add, some of the communities best, to make episodes centered around a band. The plan was for me to make 10 episodes and 10 others to make one each. I didn't get all the maps from everyone who was going to do it, but I did get a lot.

So here's what is going to happen, while I am making the remaining maps to get my column finished I am opening a portion of the 2nd column to a sort of mini contest. I don't know if the demand will be high for participation, but if so it might be cool. If not I'll most likely resort to making the rest of the second column myself. Or I might think of something else, but hopefully people will like this idea and participate.

If you're interested I want you to make an episode. The episode should have 5 maps and should be at least tentatively similar to a traditional episode, i.e. levels go easiest to hardest, no races or DDA's, just use common sense, this is a traditional type map pack, you can be creative, but I don't want bogus levels.

IMPORTANT: Before you start mapping, if you're interested, send me a pm about what band you would like your episode to honor. If you want to do a band that has already been chosen, because several episodes are already done, you simple won't be able to, I'm sorry. Make sure you pm me or I won't be able to take your maps if you choose a band that's taken.

If you have any questions ask them here, otherwise pm me and this will get rolling.
