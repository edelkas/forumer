The re-making old maps contest.

Heres the deal:
i have three really old and really bad maps.
the prize goes to whoever can make the best re-makes.
i will show them once i have about 3 or 4 entrants.

this is how you score:
1/10 points for as much of the original map used.
1/10 points for gameplay.
1/5 points for gold placement.
1/5 points for enemies.

each re-make of a map i do this for, and then i add them all up.
then, i take an average of all the re-makes of a map by one person, and compare it to the others.

example:
KKstrong0

3 points for gold placement.
1 point for enemies.

map 2.

5 points for gameplay.
5 points for gold placemant.
5 points for enemies.
total: 20.

map 3.
10 points for original map
9 points for gameplay.
4 points for gold placemant.
2 points for enemies.
total: 25.
average for user kkstrong0: 20.
