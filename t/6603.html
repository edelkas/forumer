#129496 3 2006-01-03T18:23:06Z Jiggerjaw
Well, I'll try to help you out. Go to http://numa.notdot.net and find the level you want to play. Now, copy the level data from the bottom text box, and paste it into your userlevels.txt file with all of the other level data. Go into N and push userlevels. Then push refresh list, and it'll appear in the list.
Or...
Copy the level data from the top text box from numa and go into N, and then press ~ or \ This'll take you to debug mode, or Ned. Paste the data into the top text box in Ned, click outside the box, and push 'L', then 'P' to play the level. Be sure that CapsLock is on before starting it, or it'll play in frame-by-frame mode.

Ned is short for N editor. It's part of N - you don't have to download it seperately. As it says in the manual, just press ~ or \ at the main menu. This may not work if you live in a country that has a keyboard layout different to the US. It's @ on British keyboards, for example.
