Rules

There will be one round per month (or as quickly as we can manage), in which each participating member will emerge with a score. Rankings will be kept for the cumulative score. You may participate in as many or as few rounds as you like; if you enter the competition a few rounds in, your score will be equal to 2/3 of the last-place contender. After 10 rounds, the frontrunner will be crowned champion of MARS 2008!

A round goes as follows:

1 (optional)
1a. Make a map. This map must be unique for this tournament. Your goal is to make it difficult but beatable, and above all fun. You will not be competing on your own map, so biasing it towards insider knowledge will not help you.
1b. Make a benchmark demo.
Quote
Your benchmark demo should not be a speed run, but should not take unnecessary detours.  Simply beat the map in a leisurely but efficient manner. (This is admittedly open to interpretation.)  It's important that you don't go for an AGD, but rather, get only gold that is on your way to completing the level.
Repeat: the benchmark demo is not an agd, or even a fast run, but instead a simple, efficient, A to B to C (ninja to keypad to door). This tournament will be much funner for all if the benchmark demos are a reasonable average for competitors, with room for improvement. Remember, you aren't competing on your own map, you are setting up a field of play for others to compete on, and then you will be judged based on how fun it was to compete there.
1c. PM the map data to me, (or potentially another MARS official to be specified) along with the bencmark demo, which will be used as a average time for the ninjas competing on your map to shoot for.
NOTE: You may submit your map to NUMA after the round ends.

2. You will be assigned your first "set." (3 competitors + 1 map = 1 set) You compete in three sets in each round, each against two other ninjas. You will know your two competitors are, but not the creator of the map.

3. You and your two competitors will each be PMed the map, along with the benchmark score. Each should play the map, and may try as many times as they desire before deadline to achieve the highest-scoring demo possible. It is recommended that you play in userlevels mode, so that you may see your score (gold counts) as you play.

4. Results from the first set will be posted here, and you will be assigned your next set, against two other ninjas on a different map.

5. When you submit your demo for the third and final set, include one other item: A ranking of the three maps you played this round, from first to last, based on one criteria: how much you enjoyed the map.

Scoring:
If you submit a map:
  • 10 points if you submit a map for the competition.
  • 20 points for each 1st ranking of your map.
  • 5 points for each 2nd ranking of your map.
  • -10 point penalty for each 3rd ranking of your map.
Runs:
  • 40 points for the best run in your set.
  • 20 points for the second-best run in your set.
  • Based on all three of your sets, you will receive a "central score" that can be anywhere between about 50 points and about 200 points. The formula will be kept secret to help avoid skewing of the data by competitors and mapmakers, but rest assured it is completely objective.
How sets are determined:
The first set will be completely random. The next two will be "power-matched": If you do well against your competition in the first set, you'll be competing with two others who did as well. The third set will be power-matched based on the second.
Not all maps submitted can be used an equal amount. They will be evenly distributed as much as possible, and then randomly chosen for the remainder. Maps less used can't earn as many points, but also aren't in danger of losing points due to a 3rd place ranking.

Medals:
Round Winner
Cumulative Frontrunner
Cumulative Second Place
Cumulative Third Place
Participation/Support
Current State

The first set is up! Maps will be PMed to you.
Have your first runs done by the end of Sunday. I'll begin tabulating the results and setting up Set 2 whenever I have time after that. Even if I haven't tabulated the set yet, any entries after 3:00am monday (whatever time zone you're in) will not be counted.
