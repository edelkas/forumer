#99905 9 2005-09-12T18:44:51Z LittleViking
He's right, the slopes of the lines are different. The red triangle has a 3/8 slope (0.375) and the green one has 2/5 (0.4). Obviously, the total area of the shapes doesn't change.

The difference in slopes cuts a small triangle off of the top one, and adds a small triangle onto the bottom one. The difference should come to about one square in area, and thus make up for the square that was removed.
