Vertigo Games

In particular, take a look at Liquisity and Ore No Ryomi 2.

Anyway, Liquisity is a puzzle/action thing wherein one must guide the bouncing ball to the goal, avoiding and/or utilising obstacles such as spinning platforms, pinball-style bumpers, and bubble streams which push the ball around. It is not difficult, but it's still kind of fun. You'll probably finish it in a hour or two. It also includes a level editor.

ONR is an odd cooking/management sim, which requires you to put out fires, wash dishes, and empty the trash whilst a line of impatient customers waits. After closing for the day you may invest in such things as automatic dishwahers, new types of food, a tip jar, and advertising. The main part of the game is frantically trying to prepare food (usually by hitting the correct sequence of keys), which is fun although rather repetitive. If you play for a while, you may be invited to the "Iron Cook" show, which features food preperation challenges (such as serving 25 perfect beers in 60 seconds) and silly (but funny) voiceovers.
