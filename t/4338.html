#89294 11 2005-08-08T20:09:23Z Maniac
Oh, you're Musliman. Now, please don't take this constructive criticism personally, I give this to everyone, Islam or Christian, black or white, etc. etc.

Make sure every object in a level has a use. Make sure every enemy in the level has a potential to kill N. If it doesn't, you should probably delete it. For example, if you have a large block of mines, leave the borders and delete the entire inside of the block. Faster loading times can only help your score.

Second, if you're not sure exactly what to do, you can start out using MetaNet-style maps. Look at the main-game maps, and do your own versions of them. People won't mind if you do a map in the vein of Hunted or Artifact.

Third, make sure all of your maps are fun to play. If they aren't fun for you to play, do you really think they'll be fun for other people to play? Replayability is a major plus.

Follow these guidelines and I'm sure you'll produce quality maps. You try, and that's the first step to becoming a good mapmaker.
