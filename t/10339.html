I hate to say it, but I sorta disagree with Sweep and much of the mainstream opinions he's evoked.

Why? Here's what he is saying- "People comment, affecting the way people vote. Bad comments = bad votes. Pick + select your comments, and you'll be able to help your map's ranking." Well. Umm. So?

It won't be any more unfair in benefiting your map than a bad comment is unfair in hindering it. It might alter people's interpretations of THE SAME MAP, but so what? If you can make a map look better by editing comments- and if everybody is aware of the ease with which this is done- then people will know that the comments create a certain amount of bias, and that the comments posted might not always be representative of what the mainstream thinks. Therefore, people will be more likely to vote based on their perception of the quality of a map, rather than on the perceptions of other people.



... having said that, I don't like the idea of my criticism getting deleted and, as such, ignored- especially if they try to be helpful, constructive criticism. It might take away some of the motivation to improve, if you can just ignore, control and 'delete' part of what suggests you could. (By which I mean comments.)

Furthermore, while this might have some positive effects- as outlined above- it seems a rather stupid thing to do just because of the possible way people might react based on how I described. I sorta like the idea, and sorta agree with sweep, and sorta think that criticism should be respected enough so that it can't be idly deleted- so, I guess I'm against the idea. I just thought that this might be an interesting angle.
