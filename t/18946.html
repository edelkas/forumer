#16773 1 2007-08-16T23:03:41Z Mosh
I suggest separate ratings bars for different aspects of maps, instead of one big one for the entire map. I used one of my own maps (and used real ratings and comments I received to produce such statistics) to show how it would be displayed graphically:



It would split the map into five categories: "Action", "Difficulty", "Originality", "Flow", and "Intensity".
A little overwhelming? Nope. Anyone who plays any map would easily be able to sum up the map from those categories. The ratings would also be attached to the "Post a New Comment" box, so you could only rate if you posted a comment. The comment would display a letter and a percentage for each category at the end of the post (A,D,O,F,I - respectively). Instead of the 1, 1.5, 2 - etc rating system, there would be a three-part drop-down menu to select your percentage for each category. The first box would be the 10's column (up to 9), the second would be the single-digit column (up to 9), and the decimal menu would also stretch to 9. Any vote of 99.9 would be translated as being "100%" in that category.

Also, all of the votes would be summed up in an "Overall" rating, which users can not directly pick, to give an overall display of the map's ratings. This overall rating would now actually mean something, since it is derived from unique aspects of the map and a players rating on those aspects.

This entire system (which would be easy to fabricate, using the old rating system as a model) would give actual meaning to votes, as they deal with unique parts of the map, not just the map itself.

It is also notable that the "Add To Favorites" box has moved. I think it looks great under the thumbnail, and is easier to spot. This is entirely not the focus of the suggestion, though.

Note: This would only apply to playable maps.


Simplified Version
Add five rating panels, each with one aspect of the map receiving a rating. These five are automatically combined to present an "Overall" rating. This adds meaning to the votes in a simplified fashion.


I love the idea, and I hope it gets implemented. What do you guys think?

Cheers,
