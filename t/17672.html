#15545 1 2007-06-05T21:54:36Z Tsukatu
I've been thinking about a movie I've been wanting to make for a very long time now, and I recently settled down to write the cinematic trailer for it. After this, I don't actually think I have it in me to write an entire script, but here's what I have for the trailer so far:

(Fade in to a shot with the camera looking up from the ground to the top of a catholic cathedral. The environment is very dreary and somewhat gothic: the steps leading into the chapel are in very poor repair, the stone ground is cracked and covered in grime, the sky is filled with dark clouds with occassional lightning strikes in the background, and the dark facade of the cathedral stands domineering like the iron fortress of a tyrant. A crowd of miserable, wretched figures in simple dark, hooded robes are hunching their way slowly up the steps to the cathedral.)

NARRATOR: In a world...

(Cut to a shot of the altar inside the cathedral at a dramatic upward angle. The inside of the cathedral is as dark and tyrannical as the outside, only more fanciful, and behind the altar stands an ornate, menacing crucifix. The priest of the cathedral is wearing fancy robes that are black as charcoal with silver embroidery and a massive cross around his neck. He stands with his back turned to the camera, facing the altar, and turns around dramatically with his hands raised. The camera zooms in on his face as he turns around, and we can see that his ghostly pale face has a sinister, almost demonic, evil scowl.)

NARRATOR: ...where religion rules...

(Cut to a horizontal pan from the altar's point of view at the hundreds of wretched dark-robed figures sitting humbly in pews, praying. The figures are all bent, dirty, and miserable, and all are wearing wooden crosses on simple white string around their necks, on top of their robes. These are the CHRISTIANS. The inside of the cathedral is very majestic, though thoroughly demonic. As the camera pans across the pews filled with CHRISTIANS, very brief clips of violent scenes flash intermittently: gunfire and screaming in a chemical laboratory; men and women in white lab coats being shoved forcefully into line by CHRISTIANS with submachine guns; an enormous pile of books in flames surrounded by the CHRISTIANS, who are watching, limping about, and driving wheelbarrows full of books into the burning pile.)

NARRATOR: ...and science is punishable by death...

(Cut to a shot of a man in rags manacled to a wall by all four limbs, several meters above the ground, wrestling uselessly against his bonds and screaming. The camera flies back quickly to show an enormous, dank, underground torture chamber that features a stupendously large assortment of torture devices and rag-wearing people strapped to them. The devices are being operated by the CHRISTIANS, who are limping about all over. Some of the CHRISTIANS are standing guard at doorways and holding machine guns. Fade out when the camera backs out through the wooden double-doors leading into the torture complex, which slam shut in front of the camera. There is a CHRISTIAN on either side of the double-doors with a machine gun in hand. This shot shows us a glimpse of the face of a CHRISTIAN underneath the hood, which reveals a long, hooked nose, drooping flesh, and a permanent scowl, covered in warts. Like Emperor Palpatine from Star Wars, but uglier.
Fade back in to a shot of the inside of a tavern, which is dimly lit by oil lanterns and has simple wooden tables, and is generally very medieval-looking. The shot is focused on a group of three CHRISTIANS sitting at a wooden table, all sipping out of crudely-fashioned wooden mugs. Their hoods are pulled low so that none of their faces are visible.)

NARRATOR: The only hope for mankind...

(A CHRISTIAN hobbles quickly over to the table where the three are sitting. He slams his hands down on the table dramatically and then slowly raises an accusing finger at the CHRISTIAN sitting in the middle.)

CHRISTIAN (hissing): I know who you are... scientists!

NARRATOR: ...is applied reason.

(The three accused CHRISTIANS (#'s 1, 2, and 3) sitting at the table leap to their feet. CHRISTIAN #2 (middle) quickly pulls something out of her robe and tosses it at the CHRISTIAN (#4) who approached them. CHRISTIAN #4 catches it and looks at what it is - it's a bar of soap. CHRISTIAN #4 is confused.)

CHRISTIAN #2 (female): That bar of soap is made out of a base.

(CHRISTIAN #4 is still confused. CHRISTIAN #2 holds up her cup.)

CHRISTIAN #2: And this is lemonade, which contains citric acid.

(CHRISTIAN #4 is confused and worried. CHRISTIAN #2 pulls off her hood, revealing herself to be CHEMIST, a stunningly attractive woman in her late 20's. She stands confident.)

CHEMIST: Have you ever heard of "heat of neutralization?"

(CHEMIST splashes the lemonade onto CHRISTIAN #4. Upon contact with the lemonade, the bar of soap explodes like an incendiary grenade, completely engulfing CHRISTIAN #4 in flames. CHRISTIANS #1 and #3 pull off their hoods, revealing themselves to be PHYSICIST and BIOLOGIST. PHYSICIST looks like a stereotypical male engineer in his early 40's, complete with glasses, plaid shirt, and pocket protector. BIOLOGIST is a more or less bland asian woman in her 30's. CHEMIST, PHYSICIST, and BIOLOGIST all sprint for the door of the tavern, but the surrounding CHRISTIANS rush to block the way.)

NARRATOR: Three ex-professors of science...

(The three scientists stop suddenly, as they are facing a few dozen angry CHRISTIANS. The three are in an open area in the middle of the tavern, and a few dozen more CHRISTIANS are surrounding them in a wide circle. Cut to the three to show the worried but determined look on their faces. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: An evolutionary biologist...

(Cut to the three scientists standing at a simple pier at dusk, overlooking a lake. BIOLOGIST is explaining what she is about to do.)

BIOLOGIST: According to Darwin's Theory of Evolution, all organisms keep mutations that help them survive in the environment.

(BIOLOGIST squats down on the pier, leans toward the water, and fills her cupped hands with some of the lake water. She stands back up, turns around to face PHYSICIST and CHEMIST, and raises her cupped hands above her head and drinks dramatically. Because she is looking upward, the camera has a clear shot of her neck, which grows gills as she drinks. She then smiles at CHEMIST and PHYSICIST, and dives backwards off of the pier. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: A chemist...

(The three scientists, dressed in their modern clothes instead of the dark CHRISTIAN robes, have just stopped walking on a trail through a forest because they've spotted a half dozen CHRISTIANS charging at them farther on down the trail. CHEMIST steps forward and pushes the other two scientists back.)

CHEMIST: I got this, you guys. A gas' pressure, volume, and temperature are all related to each other. Down here on the surface, the pressure is going to be constant. (The charging CHRISTIANS are getting close.) So in order to increase the temperature, you have to increase the volume. (The charging CHRISTIANS are almost upon them.) And the best way to increase the volume of gas...

(CHEMIST inhales deeply and, just as the CHRISTIANS are closing in, breathes fire on the lot of them. All of the CHRISTIANS are disintegrated instantly by the heat. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: And a physicist...

(The three scientists are standing on a post-apocalyptic metropolitan street. The three were sneaking through the city and were spotted by a CHRISTIAN, who is screaming and charging at them from a distance. PHYSICIST pushes CHEMIST and BIOLOGIST behind him and explains what he is about to do aloud to them.)

PHYSICIST: According to the right hand rule... (he does a thumbs-up with the base of his palm on his stomach and his thumb pointing out of his stomach, straight in front of him) ...when you make a rotation... (he curls and uncurls his fingers to show that the rotation is clockwise) ...the direction of the torque is along the axis of rotation. In this case...

(The charging CHRISTIAN is now upon them. The scene goes into bullet-time as the PHYSICIST does a Matrix-like sideways cartwheel clockwise, supporting himself on one hand. His other hand punches the CHRISTIAN in the stomach when PHYSICIST is upside-down. The resulting punch tears the CHRISTIAN into ribbons: in slow motion, the rest of the CHRISTIAN's body folds inward as he is sent flying backward by his stomach, and his spine visibly snaps almost immediately. As he flies back, his flesh, muscle, and bone are twisted and torn in an excessively bloody manner that is entirely too gruesome even for a movie intended for mature audiences. The body of the CHRISTIAN is so mangled and mutilated after flying two meters through the air that he is no longer recognizable as something that was once human, but he continues flying across the street until the clump of flesh and sinew slams into a ruined building, splattering blood, shards of bone, and unidentifiable organic bits all over its wall. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: ...meet a student...

(Brief shot zooming in toward STUDENT's face. He is in his early 20's and has a clean punk/biker look: cleanshaven, short spiked hair, stylish leather jacket. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: ...of thermodynamics...

(Close-up shot looking over STUDENT's shoulder as he explains to whoever is behind him (he is looking through the camera) what he's going to do to the pack of CHRISTIANS charging at him like a horde of trolls in the distance.)

STUDENT: You know what entropy is? It's disorder, chaos. And it's proportional to heat. So if you increase the heat, you increase the chaos.

(STUDENT pulls out a zippo lighter, lights it, and holds it out in front of him. The camera pans as he moves his hand to show what he's looking at through the flame. Every CHRISTIAN he looks at through the flame as he sweeps his gaze over them is vaporized. Fade out.)

NARRATOR: ...and embark on a journey...

(Shot of the four dramatically coming to the top of a steep hill. Fade out slowly.)

NARRATOR: ...to find the ultimate weapon...

(STUDENT is sitting in front of a campfire and looking into it. Close-up on a portrait view of his face - he turns to his left and looks at the camera dramatically.)

STUDENT: ...the Logician!

(Brief shot of the inside of a humble log cabin. A gentle old man wearing a hooded pale gray robe (with the hood on) looks up mystically at the camera.)

NARRATOR: ...to fight their greatest foe.

(Very fast zoom in to the face of the POPE. POPE is sitting in an immense cathedral in the same style as the first cathedral shown, but much more ornate. Most everything about POPE and the cathedral he's in is pitch black with ash-grey and silver designs. POPE is wearing very ornate robes, as a Catholic Pope would wear, complete with ridiculous hat, but completely black and diabolical. His face is contorted in such a drippingly evil, ghoulish scowl that he hardly seems human. Zoom back out very quickly, out of the cathedral doors and over several miles of land to show that POPE is in the middle of a completely ruined, post-apocalyptic city, with streets completely filled with CHRISTIANS. Cut to a close-up of PHYSICIST.)

PHYSICIST: One does not simply walk into the Vatican.

(Cut to a shot of PHYSICIST standing on the roof of one skyscraper in the city and looking at a pack of CHRISTIANS standing on the top of another skyscraper about fifty or sixty meters away. PHYSICIST sprints and jumps off the edge of the skyscraper. Cut to a view of the front of the CHRISTIANS on the other skyscraper, who are unaware of PHYSICIST. PHYSICIST is visible leaping in the distant background. His jump takes him into the air out of view of the camera, but he definitely soars at least thirty meters high with his jump. After several seconds of uneventful silence, the PHYSICIST lands directly on the CHRISTIAN who is just in front of the camera, crushing him out of view. PHYSICIST lands as evenly as though he just performed a simple hop.

Rock music starts playing to accompany the following scenes:

BIOLOGIST, with the gills she grew earlier still visible, jumps off of a sea-side cliff and starts flapping her arms desperately. As she flaps, her arms morph into large feathery wings, and she is able to fly with them like a bird.

CHEMIST is holding her ground from the CHRISTIANS surrounding her in an abandoned old shack in the dead of night. She is spraying any CHRISTIAN who comes near with a supersoaker filled with a bluish fluid. Every CHRISTIAN who is hit by the spray is petrified instantly into a chalky white stone.

PHYSICIST is wielding an enormous industrial spring as a weapon. He is on the ruined city streets at night surrounded by CHRISTIANS, and he's doing clockwise hands-free cartwheels and hitting CHRISTIANS with the spring whenever he's upside-down. Every CHRISTIAN he hits explodes very messily.

BIOLOGIST, surrounded on an open field by CHRISTIANS, still with gills and the wings she grew earlier, now has two enormous mammoth tusks sticking out of her face that she is using to attack the CHRISTIANS coming at her.

An epic battle on a field leading into the city - the three scientists, STUDENT, and LOGICIAN are attacking an army of CHRISTIANS. The CHEMIST is doing flame-related nonsense to destroy the CHRISTIANS who come at her; the PHYSICIST is bopping CHRISTIANS with the spring (no cartwheels) and sending them flying; BIOLOGIST has gills, wings, mammoth tusks, and now has a pair of human-sized spider legs behind her own legs as well as a scorpion tail, and is attacking CHRISTIANS with her tusks, wings, and tail; STUDENT is using his vaporizing lighter trick, and the LOGICIAN is standing in the middle of the group not really doing anything.

End rock music montage.

Cut to a dramatic shot of POPE coming down the steps from the altar of the cathedral he was in. Cut to a shot behind him, showing that LOGICIAN is at the base of the steps. The POPE raises his arms mystically.)

POPE: Intelligent Design!

(Swirling violet light fills the space between POPE and LOGICIAN, and a very demonic-looking and over-muscled unicorn is assembled out of thin air, facing LOGICIAN and snarling. LOGICIAN raises a hand to it.)

LOGICIAN: God of the Gaps.

(The earth shakes and an enormous crack is opened in the ground directly underneath the unicorn, which fails to scramble out of it in time. The crack is wide enough to separate LOGICIAN and POPE by a few more meters with a gaping pit, and it splits the cathedral in two. POPE is concerned about the falling debris while LOGICIAN stands ominously. Fade out. The title of the movie fades in, then fades out. Fade in quickly to a shot of the three scientists traveling over land:
PHYSICIST, on the left, is in an enormous contraption made entirely of springs. He is very high above the ground, standing on the legs of whatever the hell the thing is he's in, which are a few dozen meters tall.
CHEMIST, in the middle, has a box of table salt and a jug of water. She is pouring the two in front of her, which is enabling her to somehow ride on top of the flames that are resulting in the water meeting the salt. If this movie trailer has so far had its desired effect, the audience should not even want to know the ridiculous excuse used to justify her riding flames through the air.
BIOLOGIST, on the right, is now a horrifying amalgamation of at least 15 different animals, and is completely asymmetrical and dilapidated. She's as big a beast as PHYSICIST's "vehicle," and features random body parts from different animals, including four spider legs (which are as big as she is), that enormous scorpion tail in addition to a whale fin, and enormous insectoid mandibles. Her torso and the right half of her head look like those of a seal, her left arm is a penguin flipper, her fifth leg is some kind of bird leg, her right arm is a pair of enormous octpus tentacles, and her left eye is an enormous compound eye (as from a housefly) that takes up the entirety of that side of her head. Her enormous lizard tongue is flicking in and out of her mouth as she's gimping along like the disgusting mutant that she is, and her second pair of honeybee wings (oh, did I not mention she has enormous honeybee wings?) is twitching uncontrollably.
The three race past the camera "heroically." Fade out.
Fade in on the release date of the movie, and fade out again.
END.)
