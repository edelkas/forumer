#71162 15 2005-05-31T03:11:28Z brickman
When you break a game you find something the author didn't intend you to be able to do that can basically be used to "cheat" in a sense. Often breaking involves skipping areas of a game entirely with something the author thought was impossible to do (see some 05-2 demos for a good example), or finding a shortcut or "easy route" through a level that wasn't intented to be usable, or finding a "sweet spot" while fighting a boss that makes you not get hurt by many or all of its attacks, and other such things. Breaking a survival map would mean finding some kind of "infinite loop" which allows you to survive indefinitely by doing the same thing over and over (and since survival maps are about seeing how long you can survive, a path that allows infinite survival is the worst thing that can happen to it.
