#422681 3 2008-03-28T08:41:01Z 29403
W - Everytime he touches a wall he goes in an alternate pattern.
E - He follows a surface clockwise.
D - He follows the outline of the map clockwise.
S - Every time he touches a wall he goes in a random direction (except the way he came from)
A - He follows the outline of the map anti-clockwise.
Q - He follows a surfice anti-clockwise
