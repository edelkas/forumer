I am a member of a photo website called flickr, and on it my main interest is group called 365 days (yes, days should be capitalized, it's my thing not to). 365 days is a project where you take one self portrait every day. The goal is to do it for one year (after that it's optional). Currently, there are 4913 members to the group, and while not all of them do the project, there is certainly more that one thousand people doing it. I, of course, am one of them. I have been doing the project since new years' day, or 222 days.
However, for the founder of the group, ChrisMaverick, and a few other people, today is day 365, so it seemed appropriate to create the topic today.

Feel you might find this at least mildly interesting, so discuss.
