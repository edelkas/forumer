How do you get different colors for your ninja?
#117199 2 2005-11-23T05:22:30Z KinGAleX
You unlock a different colour, or "flavour", with each column you defeat. With the first column you defeat (it can be any) you unlock pink, and so on and so forth. No matter what order you beat the columns in, you'll get the flavours in the same order.
