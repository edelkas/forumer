Well, friends, the nominations have been submitted. Fifty maps have been selected, the cream of the crop. Now it's time to see what maps all of you think are the true legends. Here are the rules for voting:

The Voting Rules:
1) You must PM your votes to either tktktk or sweep.
2) You may vote for up to five of the nominated maps, ranking them from 1st choice to 5th (see the scoring system below). If you want to vote for less you may, but we strongly encourage you to vote for five.
3) Your votes must be submitted no later than midnight on June 30th. The final results will be posted shortly afterwards.
4) You may not vote for your own maps.
5) Preferably, don't tell other people who you voted for.
6) The Legacy team reserves the right to decline your votes, and in this highly unlikely event we will inform you of why they were declined. We will only do it with very good reason. We may also tell people about it in this thread to get a second opinion.

The Scoring System:
Each person can nominate up to five maps, ranking them from 1st to 5th (1st being highest). Each rank will receive a point value as follows:

1st choice: 5 points
2nd choice: 4 points
3rd choice: 3 points
4th choice: 2 points
5th choice: 1 point

The number of points that each map receives will be totaled, and the 20 maps that receive the most points out of the 50 will be inducted into the Hall of Fame. The ten base maps referred to in the nominations thread were inducted automatically, and are not included in the voting list.
