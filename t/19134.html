False Tiles

Perhaps the most enigmatic of the many propulsion methods, false tiles are strange tiles that appear as 5 tiles with Q alignment (oddly, they take on the likeness of a 1 tile with Q alignment in thumbnails on Numa). However, they act nothing like this type of tile. Rather, when a ninja reacts with these tiles, he is either propelled out in a seemingly random direction and speed, or he passes directly through them, depending on the area of collision.
