I love N, I've played it for almost a year now (though through three hard drive crashes I've lost most of my progress, I'm back to Episode 12). And there's really only one improvement I can suggest: Why have N enemies stop pursuing the ninja after it's killed? The death animations are one of the most entertaining parts of the game, and I'm always disappointed when at last all the body parts have stopped moving, and the fun is over. Why not have the gauss cannons keep firing, the missile launchers keep launching, and the machine gun and laser drones keep doing their thing even after all life has left the little black fellow?

Why should the enemies be so furious and bloodthirsty, only to lose interest immediately after death?

I realize this might be a problem if the corpse hits a mine and fragments, perhaps have an option to turn off corpse fragmentation? It'd probably be a necessity if hostiles continued to attack the ninja after death, since if the corpse blows into bits there'd need to be a whole other set of rules to tell them which piece to attack. Better just to keep the whole thing together and let them blast away.
#71389 2 2005-06-01T03:07:36Z brickman
You are SOOO lucky this topic isn't about what it looked like from the title.

Anyways, they actually already DID improve the stuff in this regard. Until version 1.3 (a mere .1 ago, which is pretty recent) the dead ninja didn't interact with anything except doors and tiles (and maybe jumppads, don't remember). Now your body (and body parts) will set off mines, interact with bounceblocks and oneways, shoot off of jumppads, and be zapped by any electricity based enemies it happens to touch. Which is a lot, considering that the point is to NOT die.
