#15550 1 2007-06-06T03:07:32Z Mosh
Alright, here's two ideas that incorporate mirrors into N. And no, Realtime reflections of the Ninja is not one of them.

Mirror Tile
It simply has a thin colored strip on the last pixle of it's image to distinguish it as a Mirror tile. When a laser drone fires upon it, the laser bounces off, and follows through to a random coordinate - retaining normal laser principals and rules. Floorguards can travel on it normally.

Mirror Drone
A drone, with an undecided fictional speed, which has normal drone principals applied to it. When a laser hits it, lasers are emitted from all apothems (basically, the flat sides) of the drone. The original laser hits the drone and lasts normally, as if the drone was a solid object - but the Mirror Drone stops in place, taking in all of the laser energy from the original beam before firing, creating a sort of delay between Laser->Mirror, Mirror->Apothem Rays.

What do you think?

Collide with the drone? Since when is the drone involved?

I proposed to treat the Mirror Object like any other object that can be "hit" by a laser - An Activated Trapdoor, a Locked Key-Door, and a closed Normal Door.

I simply said that a code for having the Laser Drone "check" if it's firing on a normal Obstruction (tile, or the three door options), or a Mirro Obstruction. If it were the Mirror Obstruction, it would reflect a laser (or create a new laser with it's source as the point of contact between Laser1 and the Mirror -) point in the direction of a corresponding angle.

It's extremely easy to do, even in N 1.4's AS build.

=Analysis=

And yes, it does work. Collisions are detencted because of a solid obstruction between the laser drone and which is triggered by N traversing into the firing view of the Laser Drone, correct? The Laser Drone, once it has "seen" N, searches for that solid, and draws the Beam. The beam is fired, and is represented as a 2-Pixle (Give or take one Pixle) beam that stretches from the center of the drone to the obstruction. A number of objects are labeled as "solid" - such as Trapdoors that are deployed, Locked Doors that have yet to be unlocked, and Normal Doors that haven't yet been opened by N's general proximity to the door. By simply adding this "solid" property to the Mirror Tile, and setting it's placement rotation to that of a Launchpad, then placement and fuctionality have come full-circle. As for the interference of "placement" and other Drones and their paths, another simple check would be used. It would seek an obstruction in front of the drone, as all drone paths do when deciding when to "turn", but the Mirror object would prevent the traveling of the drone if it were inside the square at all. It would take up more room than a standard tile if placed between two tiles (or even a Half-Tile for that matter), but it would be very simple to code as, once again, it would retain "solid" properties such as the Door Examples.
