Cool map. It's fun for a first. I assume that you dont have an account on NUMA; otherwise submitting maps is really easy. Go to the configure menu in N, and make an account on that. Then go to NUMA and sign in using that account and you'll get a confirmation email. Blah blah blah, its all very standard.

Once you have an account, go to submit a map, and place your level data in there. The bit you submit is the data without the tags. So, remove the $name#author#comment# and such. Um, if that doesn't help just ask again, telling us what your problem was.
