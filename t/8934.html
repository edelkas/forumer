I think the best way I can think of to prevent sniping is for numa to keep a private count of how many zeroes a user has given out. If the user gives out a high percentage of zeroes (maybe around 35-40%) they are given a warning by the system (unless they have, say, less than 10 votes, in case they really dislike one of the first maps they play) Eventually, if the percentage zeroes out of their total votes becomes too high, they lose their ability to vote. I realize there are a lot of bugs in this idea, but I think with a bit of time and refinement it could work pretty well.
