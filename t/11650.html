What's happening:

N is addicting. We all know that, that's why we are here. It's a great game, with many great features. Though there is nothing that makes this game more addicting than map-making. I have spent days doing nothing but. Highscoring is also another aspect. I have played N countless hours, mastering every technique, and as with map-making, it too holds a huge amount of my time. I make maps that people enjoy, and I have many great highscores. Though both have cost me many hours that could've been spent on things that matter. I don't want to give you a sob story, so here are the facts. N is stunting my life. I am a person of many dreams, and N is not one of them. Therefore, I have decided to stop. Not completely, but enough to let me live my life, and pursue what I want. My serious map-making is over, and my serious highscoring is over.

Greatest Hits, Vol. 1:

I am not one to just leave, I don't find much in that. I wanted to leave something that you all would enjoy. It was originally going to be a level pack of all my greatest maps I've submitted, and that's it. A simple one column map-pack. But then as I kept making levels, and getting idea's, this has turned into something a bit more complex. There are two columns, and a little extra as well.

The columns:

Column one starts out easy and progresses in difficulty as you go. Same with column two. You will recognise some levels, and some you will not. I not only used my previous levels, but I improved them, and made them the best I could. I also worked very hard on the organization, so there shouldn't be any problems there either.

Column two is themed. Ten different themes, one for each episode.

The themes:
  • 1. Art. Each level is a sort of modern art, that is most resamblant to the art I make outside of N. Well, at least as much as I can make it with the tiles given.
  • 2. Food. Sound corny? Maybe it is, but I came out with some pretty good maps for this one. I'm pretty sure you'll be able to tell which is which.
  • 3. Map exploration. The goal here, was to use the same basic tileset, and make it into five different maps, each being as different as can be.
  • 4. Movies. This one is pretty understandable. Each map represents a movie.
  • 5. E tiles. Even more simple. Each tileset is composed of only "E" tiles.
  • 6. Randomness. That says it all, complete randomness. First two levels are IRC "vent" maps.
  • 7. Shapes. Well.. Shapes. Each map is based on a shape.
  • 8. Composers. These are all great pianists that I have much respect for. Their names are kind of hidden, but you should be able to know who's who if you know you're stuff.
  • 9. Enemy's Goodbye. Five maps based around the word "Bye." And even more important, each one uses a different main enemy.
  • 10. Style. These final five represent my style. Even though I create maps of all different styles. This has always been my main, and my best. I know many don't particularly like this style, but I feel that I pull it off exeptionally well. I worked very hard making these maps out to be as best as I can.
The Extra's:

There are two extra quirks to this map-pack. Something I added in for kicks, to make things a bit more interesting.

Hidden tracks:

One episode of a concept of mine. You'll see.

Uncut:

These are ten maps that I really enjoy, but didn't quite fit into the pack. I thought it absurd to leave them out, Which spawned the "uncut" section.

In closing:

A lot of time went into this pack, these maps are my best, and my greatest. I highly suggest you play them. As many as you can. I know for a fact that you will find at least one that you really love, so please look past the first few levels. Also, I would love to have any critisism. Any. Whether it be a map in particular, or the pack as a whole. I hope you all enjoy this. I'm kind of bummed about leaving. Though I'll still be on IRC a bunch, I'll post here when I can, and you'll see me on NUMA now and then(now as Cadmus).

Enjoy the pack.

~Cheese
