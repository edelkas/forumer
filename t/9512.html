This is an air combat simulator, and it's obviously the 5th in the series. You fly over 25 planes across 28+ missions, with several dificulty levels. Let me start with the graphics. They are excellent when it comes to the planes and mountains, etc. but up-close on the ground they are bleak. But, you hardly notice it when you are flying at over 900 MPH over 20,000 ft in the air. The game has a smooth 60fps, a framerate rarely seen in PS2 games because of the inferior hardware.

There many missions to go through, and a couple of hidden ones. They range from the standard "Shoot down everyone who isn't on your team" to the suppressing fire missions, to the more complex: one mission requires you to escort a weakened plane through an AA gun field by watching the circles on your radar. The circles represent where the AA guns can fire, and you must fly between them. The lower you fly, the smaller the circles are on the radar, which is an advantage. There is also a similiar mission where you must fly through a radar detection area, and circles on your radar represent where they detect planes. Once you navigate through the field, you will come across an airfield where you must take pictures of the planes BEFORE they take off, or else you will have to start all over again. One mission even has fake enemy readings on your HUD.

The gameplay is spectacular, as usual. You can fire regular missiles with a robust lock-on system, (all planes have them) and different planes have different secondary weapons, ranging from bombs to advanced missiles.

There are 4 difficulty levels when you start the game: Beginner, Novice, Medium, and Hard. There are several ranks every time you beat a mission: C, B, A, and S. When you beat EVERY mission with an "S" ranking on normal, you unlock 2 levels even harder than hard mode: Expert and Ace, the hardest difficulty in the game. On Ace difficulty, you can be shot down with one missile!

The planes are all licensed and do exist in real-life, in case you're wondering. <3 The game is noticably speedier than Ace Combat 4, and the ranges for missiles and guns are strangely different: On Ace Combat 4, the farthest range for you to fire a missile was about 800 ft. In Ace Combat 5, it's about 4000 ft. or something like that It's still good.

Has anyone else played any of the Ace Combat games? If so, which was your favorite and why?
