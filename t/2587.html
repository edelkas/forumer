To everyone here,

Due to an insugence of a combination of personal events and family problems, I am going to have to leave the forums. I can't give you any timeline on when I might return, or if I will return for that matter. If it where in my power or ability to stay here I would. I really don't know how to explain why I'm leaving other than I just can't stay. You can start haxxor theorying now and what not, and I only wish I could be polymatic laughing behind the scenes, but it wont happen. I really will miss all of you, even the ones of ya I don't like. And to those of you who don't like me. Well you all can dance a happy dance today.

To The fantastic two,

Mare, Raigan, please never stop what you two are doing. Your work is amazing beyond all means. Easily the most fun I've ever had on a computer my entire life, and for that I commend you both emensly. On a personal note, N and it's addictive nature saved me from a lot of personal sins. Had N not stolen my soul like so many of us here, I wouldn't have a soul today as I walk away from it. I can't really ellaborate on that more than your amazing game really got me out of a lot of very deep holes I had dug for myself in the months before I was graced with your game. Wether or not you know it or believe it, you two are the hands of God in my life.

To the two guys who will know who I'm talking about when they read this,

You two have been like brothers. And I don't mean brothers in the way black people use it Had I not found you two I would still be a crappy map maker and a crappy confused little teenage kid trying to be cool on the forums. I've vented at you two more than my closest freinds at home and you listened to every word as if I were a son. I just can't put into words how much it's going to kill me to not be able to talk to you guys any more. I just wish I could have it some other way, but I can't. The loss of you two as my constant freinds (who are always on line, heh) is bar none the hardest part about leaving. If it werent for some of those late nights and long talks I really wouldn't be here today with the knowledge I have. Staying up till 3 on school nights just wont be the same without you two anymore. And Matt, take my PM to heart, wether you act on it now or later. If I ever come back, you two will be the first to know, mainly because you're never offline.

Back to all you all,

I'm really sorry to those of you I've let down, and I'm really sorry about NC6 being unfinished. I guess I'll just leave it up to you'll to sort out, I don't really know. And just so you'll know, that's why my last couple of maps have sucked misserably

I got nothing else for ya'

Adios,

Brad
