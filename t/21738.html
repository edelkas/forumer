I've beaten the co-op levels as well. I believe you are referring to the level which has a central room with several drones (It's called 'the buddy system')? I've seen it beaten (and then beaten myself) by starting the level by jumping to the ledge on the right of the door. If you time your jumps right, you can stay on the left side of the right ledge indefinitely jumping straight up and down, giving your compatriots time to complete the level. I've also seen one person do something similar on the left ledge. Stay away from the walls - you basically want to be standing (jumping) on the inside corner of the ledge.
