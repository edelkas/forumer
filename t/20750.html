Ok, here's how it will go: I will name a book. Then, the next person names another book using at least one word from my book's name. Minor words like "the", "and", "or", etc... are not included.

Example:

First post - The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring by J.R.R. Tolkien
Second Post - Ringworld by Larry Niven

The above example illustrates another rule: the word may be found within another word.

Here's mine:

The Day of the Triffids by John Wyndham
