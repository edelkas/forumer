Both of the games were released one year apart, and by associated game developers NaughtyDog (Publishing Jak and Daxter), and Insomniac Games (publishing Ratchet and Clank).
They both feature a main character and a nonhuman sidekick traveling around planets and fighting off large, often egotistically devious bad guys, with slapstick humor along the way.
Both feature platforming and shooter gameplay styles.
Both feature various driven vehicles, upgradeable weapons, near-identical art styles, near-identical gunplay styles, and near-identical animations.
Both feature racing components.
Both have tons of sequels on different platforms.
Both have the sidekick "Saving The Day" at multiple points in the storylines.
NaughtyDog borrowed displacement mapping technology from Insomniac Games, in exchange for pixel-relation technology from NaughtyDog. Both games include these technologies, so both of the games include very nice, albeit very cloned versions of special effects.
Both include monetary systems that are earned by breaking the environments and/or killing enemies.
Both games include generic weapons, such as versions of a shotgun, versions of a machine gun, and melee attacks.
Both games have identical save/load systems.
Both games include at least one female character catching the eye of the male hero; which, in both games, causes the sidekick of the main hero to verbally antagonize or taunt the hero in multiple ways.

Now... which one of these games do you personally prefer?

I'd have to go with Ratchet and Clank - simply because I find it more fun to play.

~Mosh

Now here is where i get my gaming discussion hat on.

Now on which is better depends upon which game it is in the series.

Jak and Daxter vs Ratchet and Clank:

Now at the begining J&D was much more platformy than R&C (infact the entire game was based around it no weapons) while R&C and a bit of platforming but had quite a few weapons and required use of them. Now since i prefer shooting to platforming i prefer R&C.

Jak II vs Ratchet and Clank 2: Going Commando/Locked and Loaded

Now we get to the middle point, J2 had introduced guns and vehicles and the entire game seemed to flow very well story wise and gameplay wise and you could get about 2 upgrades for each weapon. In R&C2 weapons were made upgradeable and you could get armour and the gameplay stayed pretty much the same just expanded. Here i liked both games just as much as the other.

Jak 3 vs Ratchet and Clank 3: Up Your Arsenal

The last in the original series of games, J3 stayed pretty much the same as J2. R&C3 was expanded more in making more upgrades to your weapons etc. My opinion stays the same in that i like both games just as much.

Spin offs! (only ones i've played)

Jak X vs Ratchet: Deadlocked/Gladiator

Ah the first 2 spin off games. Jak X... well... Cart racing. Ratched: Deadlocked/Gladiator removes clank from your back and turns the story mode more in to a series of challenges, it expands the weapon upgrades even more adding mods (these may have been added in R&C3 i forget) and giving you 2 upgradeable robot buddys. overall a good game sticking to the spirit of ratchet and clank game. Needless to say i prefer Ratchet: Deadlocked/Gladiator.

So yeah i prefer Ratchet and Clank.
