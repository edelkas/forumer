#181468 2 2006-05-28T17:19:27Z wedgie
Easy solution? No. If you have completed all of the levels with practice mode on then the only way to correct this is just to go through and complete all of the levels again with practice mode off and then you will be able to unlock the flavours. Also make sure you complete the very last level in the end episodes (9-4,19-4, 29-4 etc..) as the episode will still show up as completed even if you havent completed the last level.
#181685 3 2006-05-29T02:34:54Z Atilla
You also have to finish them in the correct order. For example, if you haven't finished 0-4 without practice mode, you shouldn't be able to play 0-5 - so you're going to have to complete 0-4 before 0-5, and so on.
