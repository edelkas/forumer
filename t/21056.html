#18780 1 2008-01-09T23:22:18Z Wasted power
Okay, in this map, you're able to squeeze through '5 tiles. Yep. Normally it isn't possible, but yet when you think about it, it's common in real life. I assume you're sitting on a chair behind a table, and the space between the chair and the table can be represented by a '5 tile. Yet there's no normal way ninjas can fit into that space.

If he gets squeezed into a '5 tile, weird things happen.

Ascending the shaft: You can press left and right, nothing will happen. Jumping kills you.

Descending (falling down) the shaft: Trying to cling to the left wall works, but you can't cling to the right wall. If you try to slow down your fall by clinging to the left wall, you will die anyways when launched out of the shaft. If you don't press anything you will die by gravity. If you jump you die.

Now, the puzzle: Try to complete this level. It is doable. I still haven't figured out why jumping kills you.
