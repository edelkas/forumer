I was doing some experimenting in Ned (a lot of experimenting), and I found this.

When you have a flood guard on a floor, enclosed by a number 7-S tile on the left, and whatever on the other side, when you die, press enter next to where the floodguard stops. It has to be far enough away so that it doesn't zap you, and far enough out of the floor so that the ninja springs out of the floor. When you land, the zap drone won't notice you're there. It isn't that much, but it looks pretty funny!
