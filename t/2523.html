Quote: TornadoTK, Mar 20 2005, 03:36 AM
Can you believe that this Boss 16 looks less menacing than Boss 15 up there? It's actually because he's loaded with Cannons, Vulcans, and Front.

Yeah, the real trick to getting far in this game is to sculpt the baddie well by picking where you attack first carefully. I suggest that you waste a few extra seconds at the beginning to go for the back first in the first couple levels. That way, he will grow away from you, and won't end up quishing you for much longer. It also helps to pick his weapons carefully--missiles are the crappiest things he has, so I am much more willing to face a few dozen missile launchers than hundreds of cannon blasts at once. Whatever you do, never concentrate on the front.
