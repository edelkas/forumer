Twilight has arrived! I don't think I could describe this pack much better than epigone already has:

Quote: epigone, 2007-09-24T17:46:40Z
Many of you probably forgot all about the whisperings about this map pack. In fact, we deliberately kept it a secret to build more mystique. Little did we know how many problems, setbacks, and various other delays would arise. This project was started almost a year ago; originally meant to be 100 maps, four people each making five episodes, it has been reduced to 80, and only 3 members. They are epigone, Pheidippides, and Zres. The fourth was Hawkk, in case you didn't know.

In a way, playing this pack is going to be a look back into the past. Some of the maps are going to feel retro, especially with how our respective styles have grown and evolved since the majority of the maps have been made. There is some new stuff in addition to the older maps, and all of the maps have been edited at least once over the last couple of months to update them. Think of the older maps as a fine wine, getting better with age.

Getting better with age, indeed. We certainly have spent a lot of time working on this pack, and I must say it's been a lot of fun to work with epigone and Zres. They both deserve a big hand for their hard work. I feel confident saying that our hard work is greatly reflected in these maps, and it really has made us better mapmakers than we were when we started.

When you play these maps, think of them as an extended column instead of a column and 6 episodes. That's the way the difficulty curves (Or at least should curve.) In case you're curious, here's a list of who made which maps:

Here's where it gets interesting:

And there you have it! Do you know where you are? Because Twilight is upon us. Enjoy it!

Edit: Yeah, that's what it was. Assigning columns was what I was trying to comment on before. When you assign columns, you have to force maps into spots that they don't want to go. Setting out to make a 19-3 is so much more difficult than making a map and asking where in the pack it should go. Legacy learned this lesson early on - Expanse and Zenith were both done through freeform mapping, and then arranged later.
