Wow, level 85-0 is the most frustrating level I've ever come across... I was so furious at my failures, I took to biting a water bottle furiously to relieve the tension in my jaw so I could breathe normally... Anyways, I finally figured out the secret to those stupid STUPID stair things that go up the walls!!!

What you do is this: Instead of wall sliding on the little bits and trying to jump from that position (which can drive you insane) you jump to the top of each section and drop down along the wall. Once you reach the bottom, jump. Use your best judgement as to when to press back towards the wall again, but it's a hundred times easier to do!!!






Sorry if this has already been said or anything, but seeing how I nearly bit through my bottle (which is full of water) in anger, without a single thought as to what I would do if I did, I figured I couldn't be the only one to ever be so aggravated by this level, and everyone could benefit from my discovery!
