#126611 2 2005-12-24T12:54:14Z Villemancer
Well, its a common beginners problem;

It happens because of the lagtime that the map has, increases everytime you put an enemy (turret, drone) in to the map.
Thwumps, floorguards or anything else doesnt affect this.

So: Put the lagging enemies in a DDA FIRST, the add ninja and start moving it around with non-lag objects.


I hope that helped a little.
