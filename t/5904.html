I'm going to clarify on football terminology and culture in Australia, just to try and drag this back on topic. In the event that this fails, it's locking time.

Australian Rules Football is most popular in the state of Victoria, which has a disproportionately large number of teams. Many Victorian supporters follow their team with religious zeal. It is also popular in South Australia, and (I think) Western Australia and the Northern Territory, and is followed to a lesser extent in other states. In Queensland and New South Wales, the rugby codes (League and Union) are more popular than Australian rules, and are often referred to as football; in the other states, football is generally Australian rules football, except in ethnic groups in which soccer is popular. American football is NOT the same as rugby, and is referred to as gridiron. The AFL (Australian Football League) has recently been trying to gain support for the sport in other states, particularly Queensland and NSW. I live in Victoria, and support the Collingwood Magpies.
