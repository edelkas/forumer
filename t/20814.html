OVERVIEW

the general idea of this competition is to create two maps in one. however, these two maps must be possible to beat using one demo. the two places n starts at will be shown on the map bye a piece of gold with a mine on top of it. the two maps may touch (see below for explanation for what this does and does not allow). In general, the goal is to create 2 miniature maps that look fun on their own, and result in one demoed run through looking very different from the other (for example, while one map will have you ricocheing off of a bounce block, you will be in the air in the other). basicaly, create a demo, than build two maps around it.

Reward:
i have no idea. if anyone wants me to, i will try and make a little image saying they are the winner of this competition (i am okay at that), or if they want a ded i will try and make one. basicaly i would like suggestions on what i should make it.

Specifications:

working in groups of one or two, contestants will submit up to 2 maps and AT LEAST 1 corresponding demo per map.
the maps will have n starting off at a place specified by a piece of gold and a mine (when running the map, this will be replaced by N in edit mode).
there will be a maximum of 2 designated judges, and all the contestants may judge each other. i will not judge, however if one of the judgings is obviously unfair, i reserve the right to ignore it/ revise it.

Rules:

scores will be created as follows:

Map:

design x/20: does the tileset look good? do the 2 mini maps share design features? do the maps look different enough that it is not obvious it was made for this competition?
fun x/15: are the two maps fun to play by themselves? i understand this is hard to do, so this will be graded the most leniantly of all the sections. a 5 is a map that a mentally retarded snail could do in it's sleep, or a numa pro, using frame by frame, would take roughly 3 hours to do half of (not too hard not too easy, to summarise).
numa rating x/15: this is the numa rating for the map times 3. if you don't want to post it on numa, or do not have over 5 votes when the map is being rated, than we will double the fun score instead

Demo:

flow x/25: do the maps have you sticking N in a box to stall for a while so the other demo can finish (if used for good reason, this is ok, but it better be good)? does the demo run continuosly, and not do odd motions when there is no reason to?
uniqueness x/25: how different does the demo look when run on the top versus being run on the bottom (do both have continuous wall jumps, or does one have you running and jumping past laser drones while the other has you wall jumping around mines)?


Extras
  • • for the numa rating, if you believe your map has been sniped, you may request that we double your fun score, or take your current numa rating, multiply be 3, and add 4. your choice.• if you create more than one demo for a map, the second demo will be evaluated based on the same criteria as the first. however, uniqueness will also be based on how different the 2 demos look from each other. the resulting score, minus 40, will be bonus points (you may not lose points from this, unless you are abusing it)• if a chain gun is used in your map, a bonus 0-5 points will be added, depending on how many there were and how many close calls there were with them (they are the hardest to build around, so it is only fair to reward it)• if you cannot or will not submit to numa, just post it here and i will double your fun score like if you did not get 5 votes• if anyone has any suggestions on how to revise this, i am open to them.
