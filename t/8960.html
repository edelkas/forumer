#152998 4 2006-03-09T01:33:17Z tktktk
About Contintent. In this and several of your Legacy maps you based the timing of everything on how long it takes to get the gold. The problem with this is that it doesn't allow as much for different play styles, and makes getting the gold semi-mandatory (you could just wait around, but no one wants to do that). It's not a huge deal, just a little thing I wanted to point out.

For I in Team, you're right, that method generally works very well. For the record, though, I'm not totally against any chaingun map that doesn't have stuff to hide behind. I also enjoy maps like this one. With open space chaingun maps, for me it's important that you can get quickly to another part of the map as the chaingun fires, or be able to control how the it fires by how you move as it charges up. I in Team is somewhere in between, and it doesn't totally work as an open space map or as a "bunker" map. However, your way of doing it works well enough that I don't think it necessarily needs to be changed or switched out.
