#206681 4 2006-07-24T13:10:28Z Twistkill
It takes more than just saying 'I wanna make DDAs" out of the blue. It will take lots of practice and free time. And maybe a little bit of insanity. Here are some pointers:

1. Try not to use too many launchpads. Especially edited ones. Try to find other methods of propulsion; Thwumps, hidden doors, etc.

2. Not too much gold delay. Gold delay is when you place many pieces of gold on top of each other to stop the ninja from moving when he's on a launchpad, etc.

3. Close calls are a must. Try to make death seem almost impossible to avoid, and then save the ninja at the last second.

Remember, you can't simply jump into making a DDA unless you have patience and the will to learn. These pointers are helpful if you already have the jist for making a DDA. I hope this will help you.
