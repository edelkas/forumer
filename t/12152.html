Hey, everyone. It's 12 o clock on a Friday night and where better to be than around here?

Just thought I'd drop you all a line, try to give the impression I haven't forgotten you all. I mean, that... like... Kablunny guy, and the Cyst, and Blue Pong... they're all great guys and the source of many, many really vivid memories.

Life's been mixed. A bit odd, a bit painful, a bit blissful, and it's all more odd than I can describe. I mean- a mixture of still, from a while ago, and a couple of new things that've come up that I can't easily explain.

In exactly 11 hours I'm going to be with the girl I've lately began to give the consideration I might actually love- and never mind the week that's passed, never mind the times which are still to come between tommorow and when I'll see her again next, there's my world- right there. Being with her is such a stark, pleasent contrast with everything else, and I love it.

And... I'm sounding very vague and kinda disturbed as I'm typing this. Really- who needs coherence? Everything feels more from the heart when the brain ain't telling the grammar how it should be working.

Anyway... I was saying something profound, I think...

In the heady mixture of Snow Patrol, live music, writing, studying, occasional drinking, and giddying emotions my life has become it feels like I've lost some kind of sanity and clarity I swear I once had.

And the fact I'm here telling you all about that is... slightly odd.

But my thoughts have been feeling jumbled for a long time, jumping from one train to another and as the subtext becomes the actual text everything feels like I'm constantly emerging from one fevered hallucination into another, almost identical one. Though a large amount of that is probably due to sleep deprivation- speaking of which, I should probably be heading to bed some time soon.

The odd thing is that even in the moments of intense joy and indescribable (let's ignore the fact that 'indescribable' is in itself a description, shall we?) things are still extremely odd. But it doesn't really matter and I really don't care. Sometimes there will be some form of clear- headedness I'll otherwise be missing, and sometimes there won't.

After a teacher did some... well, that's far too long a tale to explain here, but I'll go on anyway. After she did some things we'll just glance over here to a friend of mine- causing her to weep, and shudder, and make anybody who saw her send their hearts out to her- I yelled at this teacher the next day for what she'd done to one of my closest friends and one of the greatest intellects I've ever known, and how she'd crushed her on both counts. I got rather passionate, and things are extremely strained on this respect, too.
Because- and I knew this- I wasn't helping this friend. I was making life far harder for her by showing how far the issue had gone.
And then there's having to walk into class every lesson and deal with this... idiotic literiture teacher ('teacher' meaning her job title, not what she actually does) and endure the hostility from a person of whom I have less respect than the vegetarian meals I've eaten... it's kinda strained.

I've started writing again- not just in my journals, but short storys and the like. If there's any popular demand, I could post them here and hope for some critique?

And now I must avaunt.
("Avaunt, faul beast!"
"Who will make me?"
"I will."
"You?"
"I'm the avaunt guard".)

Good night, everybody here. Rest assured you're not forgotten and it's kinda cool knowing that this place is still around.

Love you all.

- The Daily Llama
