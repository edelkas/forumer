Please post your submissions for the NUMA redesign contest here, as a link to a mockup image of the "newest maps" page. Up to 2 submissions per user are allowed. Please do not enter unless you are prepared to put the time and effort in to flesh your design out in a reasonable timeframe.

The winner will get:
- $75 in their paypal account.
- Some numa swag (to be determined - likely a t-shirt and maybe something else) with the NUMA logo from your design on it.
- Recognition on the site (and a link to your site at the bottom of each page).

Entries close this Sunday (the 20th of April), at which point I will post a poll. Metanet users will decide the design they like the best, with the entry with the most votes at the end of the following sunday winning. In the event of a tie, I reserve the right to break it by choosing the one I like best.

Please do not comment on entries here - save that for other threads. The only posts in here should be submissions. Thanks.
