Welcome to the KRA contest! DDA's and Races are allowed here. The objective is to create the best KRA map. Now, there are basically 4 categories. They are KRADDA and KRARACE. You can only take part in ONE category.(except for the HRTPDESRKRADDA category)

KRADDA

This category is for any type of KRADDA, regardless of HRDEKRADDA or 2WKRADDA or even HRTPDESRKRADDA(this is a seperate category). You will be judged on a few criteria.

Rocket and Ninja Interaction(Closeness) /5
Rocket Going Through Tiles /8
Propulsion Used(Type of KRADDA) /7

KRARACE

This category is for KRA Races. They are judged closely by many criteria.

Difficulty(10) - Speediness /5
- Spaces Required For Rocket To Travel /4
- Ninja Movement Difficulty /5
Complexion and Design /6

Overall: This is the category for the top 5 of each category above. All ten qualifiers will be given cred and ded on my next level pack DDA. The best 2 will qualify for the final round which comprises of both of them having to face off in a map creation in the opposite category that they took part in. The winner of this round will win a special treat.

HRTPDESRKRADDA Category

This category is going to be a simple one where those of you who aren't good at mapmaking can try out for. Just try guessing what that stands for. You can stand to get some cred and ded on one of my level pack maps.

I will need competitiors and judges for this(duh!!). Please just reply by telling me if you want to be a competitor or a judge and tell me which category you would like to take part in. For the HRTPDESRKRADDA category, just try your luck. Have Fun!!!
