Ok, I know this as been beaten to death more times than N when I play, but I think that if theres a multiplayer mode that will work its this:

Use a symmetrical map with some tough enemies and lots of gold. Every player starts in seperate area of the map. the game ends when:
1) Theres no more gold left. The player with the most wins.
2)Theres only 1 player left alive.

Notes: No weapons. No health. If two Ns were to collide, they just go right through eachother (like one was behind the other). I think that this perfectly implements a muliplayer game, yet keeps the basic idea/theme of the game. Thats my 2 cents.

One more note, with the enemies, I would use mostly the laser guys, mines, and the gauss guys, that way they're somewhat impartial as to the target (aka: Its not allways "going" for one player, as is the case with those blue chaser things. Those blue things would go pscyo with 4 people)
