I ran a search for this and didn't really find this, so I thought I'd throw out my idea for a possibel new twist for N. More aerial moves. I know this is a game based on physics, but if you ask me, the ninja's moves are pretty un-physical. So, why not take advantage of it and put in some more jump moves. Particularly I would want to see a move where the ninja jumps, then grabs a hanging corner, and throws himself straight horizontally under the corner with some momentum. Also, it would be cool if there was a wall move where N could bring his legs up onto the wall like a crouched jumper and then rocket off the wall directly sideways with more momentum than a regular wall jump, but none of the height. Also, if there was a way where N could be falling, then say if the player hits shift+a direction, N flips in that direction in mid-air, making him go much faster in that direction, but with little control. Kind of like a side-to-side speed burst in midair. I know that putting in any of these suggestions would change gameplay quite a bit, but I thought they were cool ideas.

If someone doesn't understand what I'm saying, I'll draw pictures on request.
