Kablizzy mentioned something about this, and as I've been donating a few of my weekends to charity recently I thought I'd share, and maybe encourage others (who can) to do the same.

At my university (and at many UK universities, I don't know about the US), there is an organisation/society called RAG. Officially it stands for Raising And Giving, but that's such a cheesy (and partly misleading) name I don't think it has ever been called that by anyone, ever.

RAG has two aims in life - charity and having fun. This week at uni is RAG week, which means we've been running about all week dressed as giant animals delivering pints, pizzas, roses, porn mags or whatever was requested to lectures, serenading random people and doing sponsered outdoor sleepovers (last night - and if was freezing. I should have stolen a second sleeping bag to go outside the first one). Also throughout the term we run Rag cloakroom at the Students Union, but that's a more minor thing, allthough it makes steady money. For all of these, all proceeds go to charity

The other main event RAG does os a RAG Raid - which take place about every other weekend. We go to a city on Friday, go out Friday night, sleep on a floor somewhere (normally a church floor, allthough last time we blagged a common room which was heated... lovely), collect money all day Saturday, go out again in the evening, then head home on the Sunday to collapse and sleep. It's awesome. So far this term I've been to Manchester and Liverpool, but there have been raids I haven't been able to go to. In the last 8 weeks we've collected over 10,000 for various charitys from raids alone, and had excellent fun while doing it (180 of which has been from me, but I'm not great at collecting - yet). In just under a fortnight we're doing a sponsered hitchike to Dublin for the weekend, which should be excellent - no collecting, but hitching instead, and the same fun nights out.

Other events later in the year include the Big Easy - a giant school fete, and the Rag Blag - start with a small item like a pencil and see if you can swop it for something better (which we then auction off). Last year someone got to a TV in about 10 swops.

My point? Charity is not only a good cause, but it can be fun. If your local school/college/university has a RAG equivalent, join it. If it doesn't, which is very likely for schools, get involved in whatever events you can, and if on the street you see a guy dressed as a giant kangaroo waving a bucket at you in a hopefull manner, give what you can - it might just be me.
