#16547 1 2007-08-03T21:01:15Z Skyline
Personally, I don't really like my maps too much at the moment. I feel as if I'm going through an awkward stage in mapping; I don't know my own style. Sure, I'll try out and experiment with some different styles, but none of them really feel right to me. As an overall statement, I think that I'm too concerned with the flow of my map to actually make my enemies completely effective. I'm too used to building race maps as much as I used to, and the strategy of placing enemies simply for close calls doesn't transfer over to action maps. I think that in order to be completely satisfied, I need to increase the general effectiveness of my enemies, and build up a certain niche of mapping style. I'm not saying that I would stop experimenting; it's just nice to have a good home style to come back to.
