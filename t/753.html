in the highscores menu, click on an episode to view scores for. (right side)

Then from the small menu in the middle, click on either 'episode' (to view replays for an episode score), or 'level 0', 'level 1' etc for individual level scores.

Then, from the largest table (just right of the kill count), click on the particular score you'd like to watch the replay(s) for.
