Okay, this is how this game is going to work. Every competitor gets $10 000 virtual, which they can use to gamble. Every week a random map from LV's generator will be put up. Prior to this, players will make bets on the outcomes. Bets can only be made saying that you will be able to complete the map, or that your opponent will not. Amongst yourselves you may also decide things such as the odds of an event happening, as long as the net money of every competitor maintains the same. Allow me to give an example. Player A and Player B agree that the odds of Player A completing the next week's make is a 1/6 chance. So Player B bets $18 that Player A will not be able to complete it and post a demo, while player A bets $3 that he/she can. If Player A manages to post a unique demo, then he/she gets $36 from Player B ($18 from Player B's bet, and $18($3x6) from Player A's bet), while if Player A fails, Player B gets $6 ($3 from Player A's bet, and $3($18/6) from Player B's bet) from Player A. If there's some part that you don't understand, please say so. Betting will start in Febuary.
