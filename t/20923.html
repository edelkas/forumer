The Weekly Gangwar
Spoiler: What you need to know- You can join up once the competition is underway.

- When you sign up, you will be placed in one of three gangs, by my choice, with the idea being that the teams are fairly even. These teams are: Cult, Faction and Sect. If I think they are becoming very unbalanced, I mightshift you about - sorry if you don't like that.

- Demos will only be accepted for a week after Tuesday/Wednesday midnight GMT, even if the map is released later. If you update to a faster personal best after that time, you count as 'no demo posted' (see below).

- We will be using the normal highscoring system, not speedrun. We will also be using Unreality's server for the highscores, which can be found here. If you have a good excuse why you can't do this explain to me via PM.

- The gangs' scores are worked out by the following system:
3 points for each highest average time
1 point for each highest individual time
-1 point for each lowest individual time
-1 point for each lowest average time
-1 point for each time a member didn't submit a demo

- The person who signs up first for each gang will be its boss for the first round. However, in further rounds, the boss is the person who has the most cumulative points by the following system:
3 points for each highest overall time
1 point for each highest time in their gang
1 point for second highest overall time
1 point for a close call (i.e. within a second of the fastest overall time, this could potentially apply to the whole field bar the top player, or no-one)
-1 point for each lowest time in their gang
-1 points for each lowest overall time
-2 points for each time the member didn't submit a demo
In the case of ties for boss, the one who signed up first wins.

-It is the boss' responsibility to make sure every member submits a demo. If someone doesn't, he/she can be kicked out if the boss wants them to leave. Rejects can join other gangs if they want to, but if they have been kicked from one, they cannot rejoin that one ever.

- Please be sporting and don't leave it untill a couple of minutes before the deadline to submit, if you can help it. I won't be on any team, but I'll be playing the map anyway and if I (or any other non-competitor, for that matter) gets the highest individual time, you don't
N.B. This doesn't apply to the lowest individual times.
