Alright. I'm assuming you all know what a Doppler Sound Effect is? When you're standing somewhere, you hear a car approaching, and as the car gets closer it gets louder. Then when its going away from you, it gets quieter. Thats basically what I'm talking about.

The closer a Gauss is to you when it shoots, the louder it gets. Each drone would have its own individual 'mechanical' whirr, hum, or buzz - which would get louder the closer it got to you. A Floor-Guard wouldn't have a static Doppler-Effect, but if you jump over one that is scooting under you, you'll hear its sound effect for movement.
Of course, mines wouldnt have a doppler Effect (Unless chain-reactions are implemented in the next version - though I'm not suggesting this), and neither would Gold.

[physics]Actually, that's incorrect. The Doppler effect means that you percieve a wave (in this case, sound) as being a higher frequency (which determines the pitch, for sound) when you are moving towards the source (or vice-versa), and lower when you/it are moving away.

Sounds are louder when you are near the source because the sound wave has only a so much energy. As it travels, it spreads out, so the energy being transferred from the wave in any given area (in this case, your ear) is less. This is unrelated to the Doppler effect.[/physics]
