i'm a DDAer too so i may be able to help. i have made a DDA just using bounceblocks and gold here. pile a few bounceblock on top of each other, usually the more there are the more powerfull the propulsion is. then pile gold on top, not necessarily in the middle though, just so that n will 'collect' the gold when he goes towards the bounceblock. when he starts collecting the pile of gold he will sort of move in front of the bounceblock, then after all of the gold is gone he will shoot from out of the block. this is quite tricky to do and can also kill n lol
