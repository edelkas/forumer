Hi everyone!

A couple months ago I decided to start building an engine for my own game. It's inspired in N! and some stuff from other games like soldat and line rider (I dont know why but it reminds me line rider...).

New version!!:

Stik 0.3

Instructions: Just uncompress the .rar and run Stik.exe

Major changes in this version (0.3):
-Completely redesigned collision engine, now uses ony 1 polygon!!! and can handle curves.
-Thanks to the new engine maps can be made with only a black & white bitmap (you guess right, that's paint my friends).

Changes in the future:
-Multiplayer (hot seat)
-Water engine
-Particle effects (Dust)
