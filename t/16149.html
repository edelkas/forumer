Weclome to the Changing Tiles Compitition

the rules:
1) There are 10 positions for the competition. If upon popular demand, more may be opened

2) The competitors with participate in 9 rounds. At the end of each round, the lowest scoring person will be removed from the competition

3) The 10 competitors have a week to create a map that follows the following conditions:
... A) May only use the specified tiles for that week, E and D tiles may always be used. This implies that all maps MUST contain the tiles for that week, and those tiles must influence game play
... B) May use any enemy type, but after each round, the most popular enemy with be removed from the next round. Mines are excluded
... C) All maps must have a ninja and exit. No race, DDA, survivals can be made. N art, as long as it is playable, (like such map) are allowed. To make sure you map is not against the rules PM me or any other judge to clarify.
... D) all maps must be submited to NUMA

*note: all maps that do not follow any part of the rules will be disqualified*

3) Maps will be scored as such:
> X/30 for use of tiles to create a coherent tileset
> X/30 for play (how hard/easy, how much fun it is)
> X/20 for use of enemy complimenting the tileset
> X/20 for use of objects complimenting the tileset
All told: X/100 or as a percentage

*note: because of the way this is scored the tileset should be the premises of the play. Therefore, maps that feature platforms and bounce blocks as an alternative (rather than a compliment) to the tiles will be penalized*

In the circumstance that a map is made that does not contain any enemies or objects, the score will be out of 60 or 80 and expressed as a percentage

Prizes:
First place: a user bar (if i can find someone to make one) and a ded. as well as bragging rights
Second place: a userbar
Third place: a userbar

Judges
