N+ lag over XBLA is horrible. It seems that this has been a known problem from the start and I wonder if it started when Metanet had to reprogram the game for Voice Chat.

For those interested in the development cycle, read this: http://www.gamedev.net/columns/events/gdc2...cle.asp?id=1131

Some relevant quotes from the article include:

Quote
Around comes June and the team is in the final stretch before reaching Alpha status, which is the point where all the features in the game must be complete. This meant general tweaking of the gameplay code as well as adding in voice communications, which the team had completely forgot about. Although setting up VoIP wasn't a huge deal, they have to spend some extra time tweaking the network code because they weren't on a peer-to-peer connection but rather a client-server connection and hadn't thought about that related to VoIP earlier on.

Quote
The network lag was a result of simply too much data being streamed between the client and server, they hadn't implemented any sort of priority system which they realized in hindsight, and so were forced to reduce the state send rate to a mere 5x/sec which is rather slow for a fast-paced action game.

Quote
By September the team is in full bug-fixing mode, mainly focusing on multiplayer issues due to about 20 different combinations of game modes and connection types, many of which were similar but slightly different in how they had to report to the Live service. Also numerous were issues involving disconnects, signing out and session interrupts.

Guys, you made a GREAT Single player and local coop game but how come it is "broken" over XBLive. I talked a friend of mine into buying the game because of the XBLive coop option and we had a horrible time with lag. Voice chat seems to be great, but playing the game was horrid. Here are some examples of the issues we had:

1) My friend was hosting the game. There is a coop mission where a missile chases you around. I was pretty good at dodging it, but the longer it was airborne the more its flightpath became erratic. I would be clearly ahead of the missile when all the sudden it would gain super lag speed and kill me. (I would even explode and die when the missile was still yards behind my char.)

2) My friend would hear me die before it actually happened. He knew I was dead before I did. Huh?

3) We were constantly "dying" on each other's screen. He would run ahead and blow up on a mine and then magically have made a jump I clearly saw him not make. Heck, he even blew up on my screen! There was blood and he was suddenly up and running again?

All of this to say, are XBLive games patchable? Will Metanet continue to troubleshoot the game or is the game considered finished? I just feel really guilty that I talked a friend into buying a game that we can't actually play together.

Thanks for listening and I would love to hear from the team. (Wishful thinking?)

#412915 2 2008-02-29T18:18:15Z Comassion
I certainly wont' deny that there is defnitely lag in co-op maps. Sometimes I've had it fairly severe, otherwise it's pretty mild, so it's variable.

I do disagree that the game is unplayable with the lag. More difficult, to be sure, as the missile and the timing of certain jumps needs to be precise and sometimes can screw up due to lag, but these are things I've been able to work with and still complete levels.

As for seeing your friends die and not die, it's something you get used to, as lag causes their avatar to run into a mine when in reality they stopped short of it. When they actually die on their screen, you'll know on yours.

These are problems that would be nice to have fixed, to be sure, but overall it hasn't affected my ability to complete levels successfully in co-op, and I have a blast playing online. Give it another shot, and I think you'll find that it's not so bad once you get used to it - and the game is certainly not unplayable.

One thing that I've heard you can do to mitigate how much lag you feel is to zoom in on your character. I'm not sure how much this actually helps, but some people seem to think it does.
