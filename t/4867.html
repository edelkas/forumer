#4437 1 2005-09-17T12:56:22Z sweep
Yesterday I was playing around with a keep rocket alive map for the "Imagination" contest when I came up with an idea... what if you completely try to eliminate N from the equation? Of course, you still have to control N, but it's not him you have to protect, it's JUST the rocket. Like a KRA map, except you're not controlling N in essence, you're controlling the rocket. It's hard to explain, but... well, I decided to make it. I soon decided it was very hard to make. I was picturing a simple gaming type console interface. Space in the middle for rocket, four "stations" on the outside, each station with three teleporters directing to each of the other stations. If you were at the station at the top, the rocket would go towards it. Station at the left, the rocket would go towards it. Etc.

I soon discovered it was very hard to make teleporters and stuff. I ended up making a "pipes" system instead, that doesn't look terribly great but satisfies the job. After many hours of working on this, the final step was to add the rocket.

Attached is the level. The rocket is very hard to control. Anyone that could supply a demo of it going the whole way round... well, I'd be impressed. This map isn't polished off, and it doesn't work... but I love the concept. Originally, in my mind, there could have been an obstacle course for the rocket to avoid, but as going round in circles is already VERY hard, I figured and obstacle course might be asking a little much.

But I love the concept. Maybe if there was someone really talented with teleporters they could make this really work.

But anyway, have a look at the level. Feel free to play around with it, do whatever you want. Make sure you credit me if you do anything on NUMA with idea... and, well, have fun.
