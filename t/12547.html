#228155 8 2006-09-13T20:29:09Z Zres
I have good advice: a characteristic of a good map is how the objects just seem to "grow" around the tileset. The gameplay of the map must be how the tileset is meant to be. For example: if you make an air-based map, put in objects that would seem good and fun in air. The bounceblock with one mine under it is a classic.
