Here's what I believe about street 'art.'

There are two different forms. Graffiti, and Urban art.

Graffiti is focused around tagging, writing nasty phrases, offending/going for a shock factor, and just plain defacing things. That type is despicable. It's not cool. Period.

Then, there's urban art. Urban art is ultra-cool, regardless of whether it's legal or not. An example of urban art is that staircase. Urban art can have a purpose (such as a political or symbolic statement), or it can just be pretty. If I have the money, I'd love to hire urban artists to paint a wall of my house.

Speaking of graffiti, my cousin was in a really interesting ballet production. On the back of the stage were ~five hired urban artists spray painting on a wall that was slowly rising. The dancers did their stuff, and at the end of the performance the wall was finished scrolling, and the artists had created a masterpiece. It was so cool.
