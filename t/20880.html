#18608 1 2007-12-29T21:54:42Z epigone
I believe we have witnessed the death of a genre. What has become of my beloved 'race' category? I used to love race maps, they were what I primarily made and primarily played. I've since lost the touch of making creative, fun, original races, but I still love to play them. Unfortunately, it seems that everyone else has lost the touch too. I haven't played a good race in months. Occasionally I see one that gets rated high and bitesized, and it might be fun - but its just more of the same. Lately all races seem to be extremely cookie-cutter. There is no originality, no creativity, no innovation. The standard for what is considered good in a race map has changed as well. I love a race that has a intriguing path winding through and around a creative tileset. Now, its just a bunch of loopy, linear paths where the main goal seems to be who can add the most close calls with drones and who can drive the players crazy with the insane timing required to beat them.

Has this genre truly died? Are we destined to a future of unoriginal races that are more of a nuisance to play than they are fun?

So I offer this challenge: make a race thats innovative, original and creative. Focus on the tiles and the pathing - make it an adventure, not an annoyance. Most of all though, make a race that is fun.

I have an idea as to how this has happened. Not sure if it's that accurate, but I'm just thinking. You know how we have all these levels or level packs created by some of the more well-known members of the community? A vast majority of those maps are more Action. These Action maps are then shown to potential beginners to the game who then start making millions of action maps.
