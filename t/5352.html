I downloaded it a few days ago and its great. Its a MMOFPS (Massively Multiplayer Online First Person Shooter or something like that) so you get to play online with real people. Theres a campaign mode where you play with other people online to complete the objectives for your team (the teams are Axis and Allies by the way. The main objectives are usually to retrieve something or defend something but theres sub-objectives that you can do, such as blowing up something with dynamite. Heres an example of one of the Campaign levels.

Axis Objectives
Main objective- Prevent the Allies from stealing the gold from the bank.
Important-ish objectives- Prevent Allies from stealing the tank (If they DO get the tank you can shoot the driver to stop it and if your an engineer you can build barriers to block the tank) and stop them from blowing the bank door up.
Other objectives- I cant exactly think of them right now....I only played this level twice.

Allies objectives
Basically the opposite of the Axis objectives. (Steal gold from the bank and get the tank)

The levels are quite big and its lots of fun working together to complete the objectives.

Now for another important feature. What type of soldier will you be? Theres 5 different jobs you can choose from.

Soldier- All rounder I guess. No special abilities. (I think)

Medic- Every team needs one of these. They carry health and ammo around with them and they can revive you if you die.

Engineer- This is what I am. Engineers carry tools around to repair broken turrets or set them up or if you need a bridge built to get to the enemies base then you need an engineer (In a snow level theres a part where you need to do this) And they have dynamite. You need dynamite to complete some objectives and to blow up tank barriers and stuff. Oh and engineers have landmines. They're great.

Covert Ops and field ops- I havent been covert or field ops before. All I know is that the covert ops have backpacks that they can blow up when they press a button.

Theres an experience points system too. If you play well you get upgraded a rank. This only lasts until the end of the campaign (the campaign is usually about 3 missions) Also theres little abilities kind of thing. Theres 3 bar type things (battle sense, light weapon ability and the other depends on your job) and if you do well in that certain catogory you get 1 square up on the bar (theres 4 squares) and you get rewarded. For example when you level up for the first time in light weapons, you get a bigger ammunition capacity. Do well in battle sense and you get yourself a pair of binoculars. And the other catogory depends on your job. In engineering the bar goes up if you repair things and set up lots of stuff. For the first square in engineering you get rewarded with better skill with explosives. You get the idea. The abilities also disappear at the end of the campaign. You can also earn medals by being skilled in a certain thing such as being skilled with heavy weaponry (turrets and stuff).

Oh and after each round theres little awards such as "highest accuracy" and "No friends award" These dont really do anything but they're a nice little addition.

Great game.
