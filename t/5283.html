The real problem with Cubish is the beginning. That thwump run is just tedious, especially when the drone part is fairly difficult as well. Doing the thwump bit again and again causes more stress later on in the level, meaning that you'll put more weight on your moves and jumps, meaning that you're more likely to:
1) Slip up because you're nervous
2) Be too cautious and run out of time
3) Focus on one drone while another comes around behind you

It just makes for a level that's all in all, just too long.
