OMIGOD 1000 POSTS AND BEEN HERE 2 YEARS BOTH ON SAME DAY FEST

Here lies a map pack, that is unusual, to say the least.
The contents are weird in several ways.
The maps be all races, not usual in shape or size, style or other. Abstract to say the least.
Maps range from 400 to 3000 frames in length, with the average being approximately 800 frames.
After all, you can't play 50 long maps without killing yourself.
Secondly, you'll notice that the pack is episodic. Difficulty increases, mayhem increases. 1 column in total.
Puzzley themes are common in the latter. Thought it'd make a change. Some re-occuring themes, like forts, and the sub500 possibilities of all levels in episode 4, are fun to spot.
Thirdly, you'll notice 9 out of 50 of the maps do not currently exist. Through no fault of my own, these find themselves trapped in a dodgy hard drive. Bah. The pack should be fully up and running by the weekendend though.

Maps in this pack have had a variety of time schedules spent on them, from 10 minutes to 10 hours.
You'll be surprised how long some took.
These are a variety of my race maps, tweaked versions of those submitted to NUMA, or lurking on my computer, or brand spanking new, or otherwise, that I have finally got off my fat arse and compiled.

There's bound to be one you enjoy, so enter with an open mind.

I'd like to dedicate this pack to Epigone. For a while, I've been on the case of making... different races, but Epi brought the sh!t mainstream, and now we're seeing a great selection of abstract and original races, getting off of the current loopy loopy drone busting mainstream, which is fantastic. Mwah.

Have fun!
