#2919 1 2005-05-19T20:39:06Z brickman
There's something about the highscores here that really baffles me. You people spend potentially hours perfecting a highscore run, repeating the same level over and over for time, and then go ballistic when it stands for all of 8 hours before being replaced by the guy who had it before you, and complain about how one person literally owns the boards. Yet nobody goes for the scores that are likely to stay yours for a while--the episode scores.
Think about it. Firstly, my opinion is that an episode score holds MORE value than a level one, because being able to do well 5 times in a row in different settings is a better display of skill than doing well once after not doing well the previous 99 times. And even if you don't, there's no denying that a score that requires a full episode of fast runs in one sitting to topple is going to stay yours a lot longer once you get it than any of the level ones. And it gives people who can't normally get on a chance, since the people who dominate the level runs are somewhat out of their element. If nothing else, someone who comes up with a new strategy for a level will find his improved score goes a lot longer if its used in an episode run that requires competitors who are in a hurry to just improve that one level to be bogged down with the other 4.

So why does noone really go for them? I obviously don't expect the episode scores to be maxed out, but there's really noone trying. I chose episode 34 at random, worked out how the level scores add up if you subtract 90 from all but the first, and compared to the episode score. The level sum is about 207 seconds, the actual best episode score is 198.550. Doesn't sound huge, but I know that one any (and I mean ANY) individual level 8.5 seconds of inefficiency won't last a second. Even if you divided it up, 1.9 seconds on any given level is a huge difference. The difference between the 0th and first places on the levels are .025, .400, .225, .300 and 1.250 seconds, while the episode difference is 15.825 seconds. Its worse on newer levels, where even though the lists have filled up I watched a 0th place demo last night where less than half the gold was snatched in gold-rich levels. Why do the episodes get shunned?
