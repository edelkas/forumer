#3434 1 2005-06-23T19:51:34Z LoD
At the moment, there is a heavy discussion about the new fav system on NUMA.
While some think the fav system is an improvement against map spammers, others fear that medium maps won't have a chance.
A third system has been purposed by unregistered "Jazzhands":

Quote: Jazzhands
How about this for an alternative: instead of the favorites list, just have a simple vote of confidence type system. A 'Vote for map' button where the 'Add to Favorites' is now. There's no such thing as a negative vote, no 0 ratings anymore, etc., if you don't like a map just don't give it a vote. Then the leaderboard would just be a ranking of which maps have the most votes. It's the same as new system, except that it separates map-evaluation from the favorites list, which people would probably be stingier with. People can use their own criteria for determining if a map is worth a vote, of course, but it's impossible to vote a map down. You could keep the pageview ratio as well to stop the most-viewed maps from clogging up the top ten through sheer inertia.

For further information on the systems and for the discussion, check this forum and NUMA itself.
