--- --- --- ---

There was a time when everything was peaceful. Not peace as we may know it, but a settle, relaxed tone about life. Everyone had their differences, always have and they always will. No force on heaven or earth could change that. But this was a time when there wasn't a race for power, money, or survival. No one was at war. However, plain common sense can point out that peace does not hold for very long. Hunger for gold, a hunger for death. There were some who did not agree with the way of N. They advocated for a new system. Unfortunately, this was a system of slavery. It did not settle well with the devout believers of N, for they were the ones who were suggested to be sold into slavery. Soon a schism broke out between races. Some went one way, others on their own. Everyone has their differences.

The Drones were never an aggressive race. They were not created to serve, nor created to destroy. Like everyone else, they had their free will. And it was their choice to follow the dark path. A choice that once followed, offered no way back. A one way platform, one might say. A leader arose some amount of time before the schism, declaring that a change in government be made. A law that should be passed, he explained, to contain Man's "oppression". Slavery the counselors called it. But this political leader would not accept the term. He thought it of a way to stop prejudice. Man's thirst for the gold was becoming too great. What he did not understand was that Man needed it for the survival of their race. Drones had not a need for this gold, the counselors pleaded. But he would not have it.

This was around two years before the break of government. During those years the Drone's political leader conducted speeches to gain his people's trust and confidence. He told his followers about his 'path of freedom'. He made every attempt, reasonable or not, to grasp their attention. And after two years, most had chosen his path. Yet some refused, joining by Man's side to stand against the leader. Man and Drone, side by side. Something rarely seen these days. One more year and war broke out.

The High counselors would not stand for it. They wreaked their powers on all who fought. New rules had been laid down. Drones were to be striped of their freedom. Some were granted the ability to seek, but it was highly unreliable. The others were programmed to a specific path, and were to stay there for as long as they lived. There were divisions among them as well. Some assigned to guard, with chain guns loaded at their sides. Others could dispatch lasers to attack oncoming threats. And the ones who chose not to cooperate were fully relieved of their abilities. They were to crawl on the floors, nothing more, and nothing less, for all eternity.

Man had his own privileges revoked as well. Life cut short, only their thirst of gold could keep them alive. The High counselors removed all but a few caches of gold. They created a system of mazes to hide the gold. For years they had not been found. Man's numbers drastically fell, almost to the point of their extinction. But two unlikely travelers stumbled upon them deep in a forest on the edge of the land. Soon everyone knew about them, and there was a mass exodus to these mazes. The counselors knew they would find the mazes someday, so they laced them with traps and mines.

The Drone leader soon got word of the mazes, and he ordered all of his followers to them. And from this day on, they guard the hoards of gold stowed away in the mazes. The surviving race of Man had been granted a special metabolism to help them get past the Drones, as the High counselors thought it fair, as long as the Drones were still a threat. This new metabolism was fueled by the gold, but could run out.

And to this present day, Man, and those who followed the way of N, were called Ninjas. Several Tribes of Ninjas were formed, and were all separate colors, yet all were banded together into one covenant, devoted to getting all the gold. They hope to seek a way to return the peace between themselves and their opponents. And this is their story...

Episode 1:

Dark clouds smeared the gray sky above. Fires streaked across the fields, no sign of plant life for miles around. The battle grounds were an entirely different scene than the mazes just east of the field. Here was different. Bodies lay on the ground, breath ripped out of their souls. Electrical parts, tossed about like a childs toys. You didnt see this in the mazes. There it was more orderly. Everything deceased was cleaned up by unknown forces, making an eerily clean haunted house of tunnels. Yet on the barren battle grounds far away from any cities or establishments, there was a feeling of decay in the air. Such a strong sense of death that you could almost taste it.

South of the grounds revealed camps. A couple hundred, at least. Women set about their daily chores, cooking and cleaning. Children played out in the open, laughing without any knowledge of what was really going on. Any disabled men that could not hunt or gather stayed behind, aiding in the development of homes or attending medical tents to treat the ill or injured.

Most of the warriors went off to fight if they could find it. Although they were unable to use any kind of force gathering gold in the labyrinths, while fighting they could muster every ounce of energy and weaponry to hold off any enemy that approached. But for now it was quiet, not a Drone in sight.

A low campfire lit up the darkening sky. Three figures huddled around it, attempting to warm their frigid bodies. Night was approaching, and night meant enemies. The small scout group could not withstand an ambush. They would have to head back to the camps soon, or risk being open targets. One of them shivered violently, a wisp of cold air disappearing into the air.

We should pack up. Jack looked up, eyes bloodshot.

Not yet, we have to wait until a half hour after sunset. Then we go. Jack looked to Russell, who had been pleading to go back for the past 45 minutes. Both of them knew that they were prohibited from going anywhere until their shift was over. Unless there was some sort of attack or injury, they were to stay put.

Im with Jack, we cant leave. We only have about an hour left, anyway. Sarah was the youngest in the group, and one of the scarce women that served their military. Young, but tough. The scout assembly before us didnt report anything, and were not expecting another wave of Drones for at least another few days.

Russell scowled, still advocating that they leave. I dont like this silence. Jack laid down on his pack and glanced at him, one eye open.

Come on, Russ. You know that its always like this at sunset. Always has, and its not bound to change. The trio was just recently assigned the shift at dusk, and it was only their second night out. Sarah stood, looking at the moon just coming out behind the deep cloud layer. A shaft of light spread out over the valley, shoving away the darkness. She looked back at them.

Full moon tonight, thats a relief. At least we can get some light on the way back home. Besides, we... but something made her stop. A worried look hit both Jack and Russell in unison. Something was wrong. Both of them got to their knees and then slowly advanced to their feet. A glimmer about a mile out into the field. Sarah stepped back.

Uh, guys, I think we might want to get out of here He knew in an instant what it was. Scout Drone. In a matter of minutes it would spot them if they didnt move. Now. He reached down and grabbed his backpack. He started down the hill, his internal compass telling him he was going north towards the camp.

Sarah was just about to start after him, but Russ grabbed her arm. What about the fire? We ought to put it out! But she was already pulling him down the hill. No time! was all she could manage before an alarm broke the silence that overshadowed the valley. This was not good. All three of them knew what was happening. The field for miles around would be flooded with Drones in a matter of hours.

They had to get back to camp.

Episode 2:

"Run. Run back to your camps, but deep down inside you know it's not worth it." Death lingered in the air. Fear and agony so stagnant you could reach out and touch it. "Fools such as you will be dealt with. You death will be a slow and excruciating one." The caverns lay dormant since they were created. No life, as none could be supported in the desolate place. Yet although nothing was present, it was there. Spirits drifting in the fog, faceless and emotionless. Screams of thousands of innocents echoed in the nights, rays of light breaching holes in the ceiling illuminating the dead. "The wager of sin is death. The guilty shall receive their rightful place in Hell, and you are guilty."

* * *

Sarah reached the camp first, closely followed by Jack and Russ. They rushed into the General's tent, breathing hard. General Bradley Saunders stood from his chair, gleaming into their eyes. "What is it, soldiers?" Jack drew in a deep breath and explained.

"Scout drone, 2 miles to the south. They'll be here shortly, we must act quickly!"

Sarah intervened, "Sir, if we don't assemble the rest of the troops now, we'll get caught unprepared." General Saunders crossed the tent to a table nearby and snatched up a radio. The megaphones outside screeched then reduced to a soft crackle.

"Attention! This is not a drill, I repeat, this is not a drill! All able-bodied soldiers report to the front of the camps." He set down the radio and flicked a switch. Sirens screamed out of the horns, signaling an attack.

Jack, Russ, and Sarah ran outside to the front field where every other soldier gathered. In their hands were spears, rocks, or anything else they could find for defense. Jack snatched up his own spear and trotted to the beginning of the formation. Russ and Sarah would be spread out among the other sections.

Suddenly everyone went quiet. A low humming noise broke out across the valley, slowly growing into an ear shattering cry. Lines of dark blue appeared over a hill just over a half mile away. Drones.

It was going to be a long night.
