Now, on to the rules and such. If you've played urban dead or hell rising then you will totally understand this game right away. If you haven't, maybe watch our first game and then sign up. The whole game is based on dice rolls (it uses a d6, two d20s, a d4, a d10, and a d8). There are 30 zombies in the city, and the goal of the game is to be the last player standing when all the zombies are killed. You have an infinate inventory in the game, so there's no point in dropping items. Only two lifeforms may be in a square at a time. You can go for other players right away (not recommended), or kill a bunch of zombies first. There are different suburbs and some of them add modifiers to the game. More on all of this later. All four player will take turns, and no one will talk between each players turn. Once someone makes a turn I will try to respond as quickly as possible (I have a life you know, wait, no I don't, but you get what I mean).

All players start with 80 hp, no items, no skills, and fist is the only attack they know (15% accuracy, 2-3 damage).

What you can do in a turn:

Search for items
Use an item
->Use a weapon on a zombie (if one is in the square you are in)
Move one square
Purchase a skill

All of these actions take one full turn

Searching for items

When it is your turn, say something like "I'd like to search please" and then I will tell you if you found something, and what it is. No matter what square you are in on the map, the chances of finding anything are always the same for every player. You can find any item no matter where you are.

Pretty much I'm going to roll a d8, if it lands on 1 or 2 you found something, if you found something I'll roll two d20's and whatever number comes up corresponds with an item on the list of items. Don't bother thinking about this sort of stuff though becuase I'm going to be calculating everything for you.

Use an item

You can only get weapons and items by searching, no players may give awayitems.

Here is the list of items:

Spray paint - Allows you to grafitti a message that other players will see when they go to the square you grafittied it in

Medikit - Heals 1-10 hp

Bike - randomly transports you to a different square

Energy Drink - Gives you plus 15% accuracy on all attacks for three turns after you use it

Here is the list of weapons:

Weapon Name/Damage/Accuracy

Fist(you always have this) 2-3 15%
BB Bat 5-6 20%
Chain 3-5 30%
Revolver 7-8 40%
Grenade (1 Use) 20 100%
Uzi 4-9 50%
Axe 10-12 10%


To use a weapon, say something like "I'm going to attack the zombie in my square with my baseball bat"

When you move to a square with a zombie in it, they won't attack you until you start attacking them. Once you start fighting a zombie, you can still do whatever you want with your turn, but be warned, the zombie will still try to attack you. All zombies start with 40HP, except outskirts zombies(more on that in the map section).

Half of the zombies use baseball bats, and the other half use bite (10% accuracy, 4-5 damage)

To use an item, say something like "I wanna use my enegry drink." When using spraypaint cans, PM me what you want to spraypaint. Later, when other players move to that square, I'll be like "kablizzy, you moved north, someone has spraypainted 'hate is the most powerful weapon' It is now sniperwhere's turn."

More of this will be explained when you read the map section.

Move one square

The city is divided into squares, image the city is a chess board and you are a king, that's how you move. To move say something like "I'd like to move North." You can move N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, and SW from the square you are on(unless you're on the edge of the board).

Purchase a skill

When purchasing skills, you need Kills(K). You get 1K for killing a zombie and 2K for killing another player. When a player loses all of thier 80HP they are out of the game. To purchase a skill, say something like "I wanna buy skill number 2 for 3K"

Skills:

skill 1:3K Plus 5% accuracy on all weapons
skill 2:5K Plus 10% accuracy on guns
skill 3:5K Increase damage range by 1 (example: 2-3 becomes 2-4)
skill 4:7K Increase damage range by 2 (example: 2-3 becomes 2-5)
skill 5:10K Plus 20% accuracy on all weapons
