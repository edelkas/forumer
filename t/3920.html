One-minute Drawing Contest

Use Paint and any of its sidebar tools (or any simple drawing app. if you're a non-Windows user) to quickly whip up an image in one minute or less; watch the corner computer clock, and stick it in an attachment to a reply in this thread!

Rules:
1. Don't cheat and go over the time. It might be worth the risk, but if too many people get suspicious and
2. Only a simple utility; e.g. not Photo Shop No special effects. If another person can't recreate a similar copy in less than 10 minutes, then it'll probably be frowned upon.
3. Draw it from scratch. No inspiration whatsoever (or an old image that you're adding to); just get a blank, white flat screen and start doodling.
4. Only one entry. You can keep scrapping and restarting a pic. as many times as you want, tho'
5. It can be about anything legal!! Draw a pic. of bullets ricocheting off of walls, your favorite car, a, uh, dgh... plane, yourself, whatever!

Judges! I'm not picking out the finalists (just two phases) on my own You cannot judge if you're competing.



Now get drawing!
