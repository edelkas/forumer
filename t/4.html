hey,
actually, it _is_ implemented, but we had to add a few rules to make it more "fun-feeling".

one of the rules is, if you hit something which accelerates you (like the jumppads), then any part of your velocity which is pointed AGAINST this acceleration is cancelled. so for instance, if you're moving right and hit a right-pointing jumppad, you'll still preserve your inertia. but if you run into it while moving left, your left-inertia will be cancelled.

without this rule things were a lot less fun; for instance when you were moving fast downward, jumps didn't move you up -- they only slowed your descent.

so, just think of the jumppads as being sort of like "cannons" in donkey kong country..

raigan
