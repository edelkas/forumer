#471031 2 2008-09-17T20:13:57Z Dead_N
You have no clue what a demo data is?
When you complete a level, you get some data that you can copy. Pasting this data in the correct area can make it possible for you to watch your 'replay' and see how you played, this is called a demo.

Ok, when you are in the N-editor, you can see two white boxes. The above one is for the level data (The one you paste to load the level) and the one below it is the box for demo data. You can record a demo and when you complete a level, you can copy the data into your clipboard. This data is called demo data. If anyone gets this data, he will be able to see your demo/replay if he has loaded the level.

If you don't understand me, don't worry, some mod will obviously come across this topic.
#471231 3 2008-09-18T08:00:28Z George
If the terms aren't simple enough, 'level data' basically implies the data that is read to display your map in N, and 'demo data' is a replay of the run you make on your level.

See the editor's manual included with the N game download for more information.
