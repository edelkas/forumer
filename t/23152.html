N+ Force Field Drone! Now available in NReality

http://nmaps.net/130843

Restart NReality (a mod of N, d/l @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Force Field Drone

Force Field Drone : 6^x,y,Path,0,16,direction

optional parameters: ^detectRadius,forcePower,forceDelayTimer,forceMaxCount,forceMaxCountDelay

Default:
detectRadius = 100
forcePower = 4
forceDelayTimer = 10 (The time gap between the pushes, if you are still in range)
forceMaxCount = 3 (The number of times the drone feel tired and need to rest for a bit)
forceMaxCountDelay = 200 (rest for 200 frames)

Some N+ players please kindly check if this force field drone behaves the same like the N+ one, many thanks.

Alert!!!!Please notice that this Force Field Drone is subjected to change. It is not final. If you make maps with it, the maps & demos may break.

#452583 13 2008-07-13T04:34:47Z The Kitchen Sink
You can make what is essentially one of those pinball knockers by adding

^20,7,2,999,1

After the end of each, and making sure they don't move. 20 is about the size of the drone itself, 7 you can change since it's just it's power (default 6), 2 is the interval between repulsions, and by then you should be out of there anyways, 999 is how many times it must be hit before it temporarily powers down, and 1 is the interval between power downs. Basically whenever you touch it, it repels you at the force specified. It's a lot like a jump pad, but it depends on which angle you touch it at.
