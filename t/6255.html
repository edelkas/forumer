Smaller schools typically don't have stereotyped crowds. In a large school, people have to join large communal movements of like-minded and like-dressed individuals. In small schools (or private schools or schools in certain neighborhoods with little diversity), it's not necessarily the same. In such small schools, people are usually people first and stereotypes second, because it's easier to get to know someone on a personal basis.
