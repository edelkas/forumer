Didn't Jesus already have countless miracles under his belt to prove that he was the Son of God? Not coming back wouldn't have said anything anyway - God sent His Son to die for our sins, and that's exactly what happened. Good Friday doesn't happen; Jesus doesn't die for our sins, but he does die. Then he comes back to life. People are Christians because Jesus died for our sins. If Christ hadn't died for our sins, Christianity wouldn't have become so explosively popular. If people still had to work hard to get into Heaven instead of just accepting Jesus Christ as their personal saviour... the faith would suck, and wouldn't be called Christianity.
