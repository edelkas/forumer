It is finally here. After 11 months of feverish nominating, and a lot of work from the Legacy Team (as well as KA and spect, who have been very generous in helping out) we finally have the full list of nominations for the Dronies 2006. This year voting will be so much easier - there's only five nominations to choose from in each category (apart from N Player and Map Maker where, as always, you can choose whoever you want) and the voting is done via a really simple form, it really couldn't be easier.
