Okay, here is the way I found when I got to that level (neither description nor picture will be as good as those made by Aries ). The points I talk about in detail should have dots in the picture. A dot also means that you need to wait there so as not to get killed. The route is in black until you get the switch, where it turns blue.


As soon as the level starts, jump over the waves. Bounce off of the last one into the air a bit, then fall back down on the convex side of it and use that momentum to propel you up the right wall. Land at the door, get the gold if you want, then wait for the area below to be clear and fall down to the lowermost ledge. Note that if you jump off of the door platform to get to the lower ledge, you WILL die if you don't slide down the wall to the left of it.

Once you get down to the ledge alive, wait for the drone in front of you (to your left) to go down, then slide slowly down the tiny wall to your left. You will most likely need to walljump once so you stay on that wall longer. Then, fall down the angled slope below you, and get into that small dent in the floor before the drone gets there (it will pass over you). Quickly jump to the switch.

After you've gotten the switch, walljump up the passage above you. Hold down the jump key at the end, because you'll need to stay up above the drone until it passes. Fall down behind the drone, then walljump up the long passage on the left. Quickly come out the top ledge, and jump to the door.

#142640 3 2006-02-12T16:46:10Z whaleshow527
It's not really as difficult as it looks.
Diagram: once you get the terminal, move to the left and stay there until a drone (red) comes down from above. Walljump up when it's out of the way and carefully avoid another drone while climbing up a slope (green) then quickly jump up and run out of through the chamber before another drone (pink) comes down and gets in. Then just walljump up to the exit. Probably not the easiest way, but that's how I did it. *shrugs*
