Ink And Paper is a webcomic project of mine. I've currently got over a year's worth of comics stockpiled for it, and there are new comics updated every Sunday and Thursday.

It goes live tonight at Midnight, the second the clock strikes 12.
No forums live for it yet, but there's a contact form live already that you can send us your feedback through.

The site design wasn't to be super flashy or super sexy. It's raw performance. The load times are minimal, the structure is easy to use and extremely user friendly, and the front-page numbered drop down archive of every comic will make catching up a breeze.

It's a comedy-based setting. No "storyline", but there are reoccurring characters with names and personalities. There are many reoccurring themes in some of the jokes as well. All the art is hand-drawn to begin with but in later episodes I might add computerized effects such as laser beams and fire and such.

I'm the main artist and writer of the project; I did about 90% of the work. My friend Kevin is a sub-artist just getting into the comic-creation world as a minor hobby, but his work is easily presentation worthy.

We launch the comic midnight, tonight.
If you happen to see it, toss us your thoughts with the contact form!

I'm sure a few dozen of you have seen my comic on these forums Sidestreet 210. I can bet you a pretty penny that Ink and Paper will top it, twofold. A lot of these comics are centered around a young-adult audience, a bit away from the young-to-mid teenager audience I wrote 210 for. I'm sure most of you will find it to be funnier because of this.

Cheers!

Any requests or suggestions off the bat?
