Mmm... in Victoria we have ENTERs (Equivalent National Tertiary Entrance Rank - someone wanted a good acronym...) which are, basically, your ranking comparitive to everyone else. The actual score to determine ENTERs are you three highest subject scores + your score for English + 10% of every other final-year subject you completed. The scores themselves are computed by complex statistical analysis. So, yeah, I suspect it's pretty much the same as the SA system. How it compares to other countries I don't know...
