Personally, I don't find it to be a bad game. I'm currently on Chapter 9, and it's turning out to be good storywise, and playable in gameplay. I do admit, the gameplay gets boring, and the graffiti helps nothing, but everything feeds the storyline, and at times, you can't help but play just to see what happens.

**SPOILERS, BIG SPOILERS**
Trane (the main character) starts with aspirations of being a big-time artist, but then is inspired to send a message to the police force of the future (think cops mixed with steroids and mech armor) by disobeying every graffiti-related law possible. Tagging untaggable trains, nailing spot after spot, and gaining street cred. Unfortunately, he's set up every which way, his dad was was killed for knowing trash about the mayor, and his brother (also being targeted) may be next, since Trane makes the mistake of telling someone where his hideout was. The police want Trane dead, and Trane's crew doesn't want to die, so right now, he stands alone. More story coming soon!

I still think it's worth a full playthrough rental.
