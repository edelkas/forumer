I wrote this story for English class. It's probably the longest story I've written, but it's still fairly short. It's kind of science fictiony I guess, but I didn't think of it that way. Anyway, enjoy!


Living in the Past

The roaring crowd drowned out the grunts and groans of the two fierce competitors. On the brilliantly lit stage, the wrestlers fought, tackling and dodging, each determined to find the others weakness and use it to gain the advantage that he needed to win. It was a close match, but then one of the fighters saw his chance. His opponent was standing with a slight limp. He deftly evaded the approaching adversary, who was attempting to put him in a headlock. The next moment he struck, forcefully pushing the opposing wrestler onto his weaker leg, and slamming him into the ground. He finished the poor, cowering soul off with a quick but devastating body slam. Rick Ralson, the defending champion for nine years running, had once again taken first in the Texas State Wrestling Championship.

I am undefeatable! crowed Rick triumphantly. I will take on anyone, anytime, any

But Ricks giddy, conceited babbling came to a halt, and suddenly he was nowhere to be seen.

* * *

There was no time to lose; the deadline was fast approaching. The blueprints had to be submitted with in a few short hours.

Oh, what am I going to do? whimpered the architect.

He was designing an underground compound for a government operation he wasnt important enough to know about. It had to be both vast and stable, which was something quite difficult given that it was to be two miles beneath the earths surface.

I cant think! said the sickly-looking man. I just cant work under this kind of pressure. They gave me this project because Im Jimmy Wilton, one of the greatest architects alive, but they wouldnt listen when I tried to tell them Im no good at designing structures to take immense pressure. What am I going to do? I cant

He went on like this for several minutes, then sullenly attempted to continue with his work. He scribbled some lines and symbols down on the paper. He stared at the blueprints through his thick glasses, then crumpled them up and began whining again.

Oh, its no use! Ill never be able to do this. Its impossible! He burst into tears, sobbing violently but a moment later all that remained was a small damp spot on the carpeted floor.

* * *

The wind struck at the assassins face with sandy needles, but she gave it no heed. There was only one thing to pay attention to: the target. Nothing else mattered to this cold, emotionless sniper, nothing else existed. Of course, that wasnt entirely true, she had to listen for the slightest noise from her surroundings. The guards were mostly either unconscious or asleep in the surrounding tents to avoid the blistering heat. Still, there was plenty of danger involved, at least enough that the target had to be neutralized within the next few minutes.

The assassin knew little about the unfortunate man who would be dead momentarily, only a few physical characteristics to identify him with. The flap covering the entrance to the tent had been removed. From her spot behind a few conveniently placed crates, she peered inside and instantly recognized her target, who was speaking rapidly in Arabic to a few companions. The killer knew that once the shot had been fired she would have literally seconds to escape from the camp. Annette Kavorsky took a deep breath and readied her rifle. She slowly aimed it at the targets forehead and the gun dropped silently to the sand, alone and useless without its mistress.

* * *

Oh, those memories, whispered the man in the black cloak. Those memories so painful.

He had been there kneeling on the ground for several hours or maybe an eternity recalling events from the distant past. They could not be changed, not without severe consequences, and not by him even if he wanted to. There was only one man who could do that, and he had disappeared long ago.

Why, muttered the man to himself, why cant I just move on? Theres nothing holding me back except

The black-cloaked man stopped. He got up and looked around cautiously. Something something wasnt right. Could it be? Could it possibly be that

But from here his thoughts, along with his existence, faded into nothingness.

* * *

When Jimmy Wilton opened his eyes, which were still red and bleary from his minor breakdown, he saw nothing. There was nothing to see but a pitch white void that seemed to engulf both his body and his mind. Understandably, this caused poor Jimmy to go into a state of panic. His first instinct was to scream, and scream he did, but it came out as a sorry little squeal that was instantly stifled by the immense space. He desperately tried to get ahold of himself, which he did best by using simple logic.

All right, Jimmy reasoned to himself in a voice he could barely hear. There has to be an explanation for this. Maybe Ive died a meteor fell on my office or something and this is where Ive ended up. But this isnt how I would have imagined heaven or hell. Maybe its some kind of purgatory. Or maybe

At this point he trailed off, because he noticed a tiny speck quite a distance away. When he saw it, he was simultaneously compelled to run away from and towards it. Then he realized that he was not floating, as he had previously assumed, but standing on a white surface (or transparent, it was impossible to tell), and he was perfectly capable of walking around.

Maybe Im dreaming, Jimmy said thoughtfully. Yes, that must be it. Well, I suppose I should go and see what that little dot is.

So off he went. The speck never seemed to get any closer, even after he had walked for what seemed like several hours. Finally it began to change shape, first widening, and then becoming three distinct shapes, which he finally decided must be people. They seemed to be coming towards him even as he continued tirelessly toward them. They became more and more detailed, until at last he could make them out easily.

First there was a tall, shirtless man, tanned with rippling muscles. He looked to be about in his mid-twenties, and strode towards him with a confident air. Next was a woman with long black hair dressed from head to toe in beige camouflage. She seemed perfectly calm, as though she was trained to keep her cool in any situation. Finally came what appeared to be a man in a ragged cloak as black as coal, with the rest of his features except for his shadowy face hidden from plain view.

They were approaching quickly now, until finally both Jimmy and the other three stopped, staring for an awkward moment. The shirtless man was the first to speak.

And who might you be? he inquired, almost condescendingly. When he got no immediate response, he said to his two companions, Looks like weve got ourselves a mute one here.

M-My names Jimmy. Jimmy Wilton.

Well, thats more like it. Im Rick, and this heres Annette and um well, he wont tell us his name, so I just call him Josh.

As Rick mentioned him, the black-cloaked man bristled, as though he did not much like his new nickname.

You wouldnt happen to know where we are or why were here, would you? asked Jimmy timidly.

This time it was Annette who spoke. Im sorry, but we dont know whats going on any better th

Actually, I have a good idea of why we have been sent here, interrupted Josh. We have been Moved from our dimension to this one. I believe the man who did this wanted me

WELCOME, the voice emanated from every direction, a deafening and hostile blast of sound. "I AM THE CREATOR OF THIS DIMENSION. I HAVE MOVED THE FOUR OF YOU BECAUSE I HAVE NEED OF ONE OF YOU.

This just gets weirder and weirder, whispered Rick.

IN ORDER TO DETERMINE WHICH OF YOU I NEED, I HAVE DEVISED A SERIES OF TESTS. THEY WILL BEGIN MOMENTARILY, AND WILL CONTINUE UNTIL ONLY ONE OF YOU REMAINS.

Until only one of us remains? cried Jimmy. He began slapping his face violently. Wake up! Wake up! Wake up!

Snap out of it, Jim, said Rick. Well all get through this.

Im not sure about that, replied Josh sadly. That man has complete control over this dimension. He could make a boulder appear over each of our heads and crush us if he wanted to.

How do you know all this? asked Annette. And why did he bring us here?

But before Josh could answer, their surroundings began to change. A leafy ground appeared beneath their feet, slowly becoming more detailed, more real. The whiteness above their heads morphed to a robins egg blue, with a few wispy clouds starting to take shape. Most noticeable, however, was the dense green shrubbery that formed all around them, and in the distance a monstrous volcano was being created, complete with spewing lava.

None of this is real, is it? Jimmy asked, shaken. I mean, we cant be hurt or anything, right?

Of course we can be hurt! snapped Josh. How else do you think he intends to get rid of us, you blasted fool?

Now they were stopped again, this time by an ominous rumbling. Suddenly black smoke and ash mingled with the air, and an instant later fiery liquid death followed, moving down the side of the volcano at an alarming rate. By that time the best course of action had become quite obvious, but Jimmy felt the need to instruct his companions in case they were unsure of what to do.

Run!

The other three had already started off, however, and Jimmy was soon following close behind them. As they looked back, they saw that the area they were standing in only seconds before had gone from a deep green to a bright orangy red. Branches and vines scraped against their legs and obstructed their path. Stones and roots caused stumbling and cost them precious seconds. The going was painfully slow, and each time they looked back the lava had oozed closer, obliterating the foliage they had only just struggled through. It was near enough that they could feel the lavas heat radiating as though already searing them with invisible tendrils. Twenty feet ten feet

Then without warning the four came to a cliff. Hundreds of feet straight down below them was a vast ocean, glistening beautifully, basking in the last rays of a pink and orange sunset. It would have been a lovely and tranquil sight had it not been for the dire situation they were in.

We have to jump, theres no other choice! yelled Annette.

With no time to spare, all four leaped off the edge and into the thin air. Icy wind blasted all around them, and had they looked up they would have glimpsed the molten rock drop off the cliff behind them. However, all they could see was the azure sky and the thick inky indigo of the water below.

ALL FOUR OF YOU HAVE PASSED THE FIRST TEST. THE SAME WILL NOT BE SAID FOR THE NEXT ONE.

And then all at once their surroundings began to change again. As they fell, they saw the ocean below them morph into dry land. The sun was rewound from twilight to early afternoon. The four realized with confusion that the sandy ground was being moved away from them, giving them the odd sensation of falling up, flying from the ground although still hurtling downwards. It finally stopped, and by this time they were a good five miles up and could barely see it at all. To make matters worse, ring-shaped metallic barriers formed before their eyes, each one armed with a deadly blue electric charge that jumped back and forth through their centers. They floated patiently in midair, waiting to zap into oblivion anyone who so much as touched them.

This is crazy! hollered Rick. No one can survive a fall like this. Even if we were to make it past those zappy things, wed still be toast!

Although they were in too much of a panic to realize it, communicating should have been impossible. The dimension keeper had forgotten that with the wind blasting in their ears it would be difficult to hear anything at all, let alone a complete sentence. It was this that saved them.

We can control how we fall, said Josh, if we lean in the direction we want to move in. We can also fall faster or slower depending on our aerodynamics. Steve may well be insane, but he knows what hes doing. There is a way to pass this test.

It was difficult to veer away from the death traps; they had to begin well before they neared one. The first one was easy to avoid; it was a blur as they sped by. However, what lay ahead (or rather, below) was essentially an aerial minefield. The four were split up, each leaning in a different direction to dodge one that they were coming at straight on. They could see the electrical surges coursing through each one of the metallic killers.

There were more and more of them, with narrower gaps between them. They floated, silent and emotionless, as the four flipped frontward and backward in a desperate fight for their lives. Then suddenly there were no more, there was only the ground below them, which was getting nearer all the time.

Well, thats great, we survived those electric barriers, but how the heck are we gonna survive hitting the ground?! asked Jimmy frantically.

It looks as thought well have to use that to slow ourselves down, replied Josh, and the others looked to where he was pointing.

It was a mesa with a sheer rock face on all sides. It curved gradually at the bottom and eventually flattened out and became the ground, basically making the entire thing a gigantic quarterpipe.

Everyone slow yourselves down as much as you can, and try to steer close to the side of the mesa, Josh instructed.

They had gotten used to maneuvering themselves by now, but it was still a difficult task to get near the rock wall. Closer, closer and in an instant the top of the mesa was above them, and they found themselves staring at smooth reddish brown stone, whizzing past about five feet away.

Get as close as you can to it, but dont touch it!

The other three did as they were told, but now the vertical rock became an extremely steep slope.

On my mark, put your feet to the rock and slide, making sure not to push off.

Are you nuts? said Jimmy. This cant possibly

Now! yelled Josh, and there was no time to argue.

The four put their shoes against the slope. Each could feel their rubber soles wearing down. The curve of the slope became less and less steep, but they were still going at a terrific speed.

Lean forward and start running now, or youll fall backwards!

They began running down the slope, which by this time was nearing a forty-five degree angle. Their bodies were strained with the immense pressure of gravity that was pulling them down. As the slope curved into the horizontal ground, it felt as though they were being pulled into the earth itself. They tumbled and rolled across the hot sandy ground, and lay where they had fallen for a good while.

Annette was the first to recover. Is everyone all right? she asked.

Ohhh, groaned Jimmy. We just fell thousands of feet and ran down the side of a cliff. How can we be all right?

Wheres Rick?

The response was a whimper of pain from several yards away. I think I broke my leg, winced Rick.

It could have been much worse for all of us, Josh muttered. That maniac has to be stopped somehow.

How do you know that guy? asked Jimmy. It seems you know exactly why were here, so would you mind explaining?

Hello? Im in pain over here, called Rick through gritted teeth.

Dont interrupt, replied Josh curtly.

But my leg!

It will have to wait, we dont have anything to treat it with anyway. Now, lets see it started back many years ago, in my junior high school. My name was Reginald, but I hated it with a passion and no longer go by it. I was a nerd, or at least I was labeled as such because I was too intelligent and unpopular to be cool. The other kids picked on me constantly. Get out of here, Reginald, theyd jeer, no one likes your style! They werent exactly good at giving insults, but they would practically beat me into the ground if I got in their way, the brutes. I was an outcast, and I hated my life.

Then I met Steve now Dr. Steven Cardgage and he became the only friend I ever had. Now we were outcasts together. He was always dreaming up crazy ideas, and one day he told me that he would to dedicate his life to finding a way to create his own world, where he could be free from social pressure. I went along with it for a little while, but I quickly began to lose interest in his scheme. Steve, on the other hand, became more and more obsessed. Eventually it resulted in the end of our friendship. He pursued his goal, adding on even more crazy notions along the way, like turning back time itself. Eventually he became an accomplished physicist, but a few years ago he disappeared without a trace. Now

SO ALL FOUR OF YOU HAVE SURVIVED ONCE AGAIN. IT DOES NOT MATTER. IN THE NEXT TEST

That is enough! bellowed Josh. You think youve figured out how to control our dimension from this one, eh, Steve? You cant even control things properly in this one. Even I could do better!

ARE YOU ASKING FOR A CHALLENGE?

You bet I am.

All at once the space around them was white again, and a man materialized. He was short, overweight, and generally unimpressive.

You think you can do better, Steve sneered, then go ahead and try.

And Josh was gone. For a few moments they stood in silence, then Steve disappeared as quickly as he had come and Josh was back once more.

Well, that was easy, he said. Steve always did have a problem with making rash decisions. Taking advantage of his pride was a cinch. In case you were wondering, I Moved him to a mental institution, where he belongs. We should be going back ourselves any moment now.

Theres just one thing I still cant figure out, said Jimmy. We still dont know why he needed us here in the first place.

That egomaniac needed someone here to see his triumph, when he would take revenge on society by messing with reality in our dimension. He meant to take only me so that he could rub it in my face that he had finally achieved his dream, but he still hadnt mastered control over time and space, so he took four people rather than just one. Apparently he couldnt actually see us until he materialized, so he tried to weed you three out with his tests, believing only I would be clever enough to get through them.

Well, I guess we wont be seeing each other again, said Annette.

Thats right.

The four had just enough time to say their goodbyes before fading once again out of existence.

* * *

Rick nearly collapsed on his broken leg as fans screamed all around him.

You really are the greatest! shouted one.

He sighed. No, Im not the greatest. Just a guy who happens to be good at wrestling.

* * *

Jimmy uncrumpled the blueprints, and took few deep breaths. He stared calmly at the sketches and diagrams for several minutes. Then he smiled.

Thats it, he said simply, and got to work.

* * *

Annette picked her rifle off the ground. She looked sadly through the tent opening at the man she was to kill. He was talking and laughing.

Maybe some were meant to kill, Annette murmured, but Im not one of them. She got up and slipped cautiously away, leaving her weapon behind.

* * *

Reginald sat in silence, thinking one last time over those painful memories of his past. He gave a deep, heaving sigh.

Those days are over. Time to focus on the ones ahead. With that, he silently walked away, towards the rising sun.
