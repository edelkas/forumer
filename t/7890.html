#149300 2 2006-02-27T19:20:52Z Notae
You may have to press and hold ctrl, shift, alt, or (if you have a laptop) the fn key while you press the print screen button. (I know I have to press fn). Try each of those keys and if it still doesn't work I can't help you.

OR

If your problem is the the key isn't actually making your computer physically print the screen, you have to press the key and then paste it into a drawing program. (Your printer won't print out a copy of your screen when you press it! )
#149442 3 2006-02-27T23:25:24Z Twistkill
Hit print screen when the window is in sight and then open paint. Go to edit and then click paste. The image should appear. Then save it to your computer somewhere. Now open your reply window and attah the image to your post and you're done.

OR...

Hit print screen when the window is in place and then open paint. Go to edit and then click paste. The image should appear. Then save it to your computer somewhere. Afterwards, go to http://www.imageshack.us and upload the image, then go down to "Direct Link to Image", copy that URL. Now open your reply window, hit the IMG button on top of the reply box and paste the URL you copied and hit OK. Now just reply and you're done.
