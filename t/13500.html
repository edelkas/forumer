Alright, with all these packs coming out I figured I should make my own. I was aiming for a complete column, but I quickly realized that:
a) I am incredibly slow at making maps.
b) I can never find any inspiration to start a map.
So, after 2 months of working on it, I've decided to stop at 40 maps.

The whole point of this experiment was to mess around with different styles and try to improve my mapmaking skills a bit. Comments on what you like/ don't like are appreciated. If you like it that's great, and if you completely hate it that's ok too. Just keep your criticism constructive. I'm also trying to figure out what to put on numa so post your favorite maps.

I've recycled a few of these maps, but for the most they are completely new.

Edits: Ok, I finished the last two episodes and I got a few things I'd like to say. First, I'd like to thank everyone who has supported this, whether through commenting on this topic or on numa, or by just downloading the pack. Secondly, a few notes on the levels. I've tried to get the difficulty right, but making fun, hard maps is, well, hard.

Thirdly, I'd like to announce a small contest for this pack. I know I should've started this with the orginal release, but the thought only came to me while I was making the second part of this pack.

Rules/Prizes:
-The first three people to send me a completion of the entire column will recieve 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place sig tags.
-Anyone who gives me an all-gold completion of the pack will receive a special GOLD sig tag, and the first person to do so will also recieve a dedication map.
-I don't care how you get the demos to me, but make sure they're in a mac compatible file or else I won't be able to read them.
-Edit2:I almost forgot. You also have to complete these as if you were in a normal episode, so your time can never drop below zero.

Finally, don't forget to comment on the rest of the pack.

More Edits:On Cyberjuda's request I'm clearing up enemy-spam in the last two episodes. This attachment will be updating more frequently.
-Took out excessive rockets in 08-4.
-Replaced drone guass combo with a single rocket on 08-2.
-09-3 -Made the beginning less frustrating and removed a mine and laser.
-Last edit for now: changed around 09-4 and made it shorter.

So, without futher ado, the pack.
