#369796 4 2007-10-05T07:01:50Z Spawn of Yanni
Indian soap operas are far more dramatic than any other soap operas I've seen. The camera work is ridiculous. To emphasise an event, the camera would, for example, zoom in on the main character's face, three times. It would then do a rapid pan of the room he's in, zooming into him some more during this, and then have the camera move so that his face comes in three times from the left, three times from the right, and then zoom in to his face three times again. In an especially dramatic scene, the camera would then proceed to do some twists, and all previous camera movements would have a bit of a trail on. If the scene in question deals with more than one person dealing with shock as a result of the dramatic event that has just taken place, this whole process is repeated for the next person. It looks like the director has just bought some video editing program and is playing with the features. As in, seriously.

But hey, that's what Indian drama is. Drama. To the max. They really don't hold back. And there's no reason for them not to - Indian cinema is Indian cinema, this is what they do. There's no reason for them to be like Hollywood or anything.
