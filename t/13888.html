#257184 9 2006-11-20T00:29:54Z Tempus_Fugit
I used to hate this level. Its actuallt one of the easiest for me now, but it'll probably take you a while.

Okay, This time you do the green bit first. Speed is of the essence here, so jump onto the first wall and do a quick wall-hop onto the ground. Run. When you go off the edge, the chaingun will see you. Without getting shot (duh) do a small jump onto the entrance wall. Get the first switch and come back down, then immediately jump towards the far wall. I don't think you should be able to get the switch in one jump, so get it as you start going back. Land as close to the mine near the entrance as possible, then go straight down. The gun should miss you.
You'll want to land on the slant to break your fall, and then run. Jump onto the left wall bit of the next entrance, and run to the right. This is the fastest way I know of. Jump up the wall, (should take one jump to get under the thwump, then a second to get to the switch.) and land on the block. Very quickly, hop up again, and go straight down next the block. It'll kill you if you hit the floor, so either glance off the corner of land at the bottom of the room.
If you did all of this quickly, the chaingun will still be in the right corridor. Run to the gold, and collect it, (doube duh) in blue. Then just glide up to the exit.
Remember, you have to do the first bits fast so the chaingun can't see you.

Phew! Hope this helped.

The idea with this level is timing and a little finesse. The best thing you can do is get both seeker drones behind you when you're running back towards the door; if you do this fast enough, the rest of the level becomes trivial.

The hard part in this animation is the frame where the ninja pushes aside the bounceblock, and the drone chases behind him. For that, you want to wait on the middle bounceblock until you can run and then slow down so the drone chases you, while still having enough speed to push the bounceblock away.

#265686 23 2006-12-06T23:11:17Z scythe33
Quote: Aries Easy Way
This is one of my all-time favorite levels, I think the map is well made and it shows. The key to this level is simple: you better have skill, and don't screw up. Seriously, this is a tough level to beat consistently, so keep trying, because you get better at doing each individual piece of the level the more you do it. Which brings me to how I'm going to attack this level. There are six groups of gold with a switch in it, and it doesn't really matter in what order you do them in, they may as well be separate mini-levels that you have to do consecutively. I'm going to list each seperately in the order I think is easiest in overall terms (get the tough stuff out of the way first is best in my opinion).

First section: The mines in the lower right.
Really, all this section is about is navigating the mines and jumping precisely. Pure skill once you know the way. My suggested route to get back up and out of this part sends you back to where you started, but you can do it the other way, you're just a bigger gauss target.

Second section: The drones and mines.
Well the drones follow a set pattern, so just drop in between them, or when one of them is in the lower right corner, drop to where he is (he'll move and you'll be set to grab the switch and wall jump out of there easily). Shouldn't be too bad if you see what's going on and realize the spacing of the mines is not bad at all.

Third section: The switch at the top, next to a wall, with mines above and below.
Note that when you're getting up here, you have to make a big wall jump across the main portion of the level that's easily screwed up if you're not experienced. Getting to the actual switch is no problem. Either jump from the lip of the pipe down and to the left of the switch, or wall jump off the lip of the pipe up to the switch. Then wall jump off to either the lip of the pipe down and to the left, or onto the curve in the pipe to the left.

Fourth Section: The switch with a floorguard under it.
Be cognizant of the floor guards on the way here. Remember this is the final level of the episode, so time is not an issue. This part itself is really not too bad of a reflex thing, just drop in the middle and jump right away and you can do it, or you can lure the floorguard to the side to buy yourself more time. Grab the switch, and don't step on the floorguard or mines. Just jump as soon as you hit the ground and you'll be fine.

Note - Getting down from there can be tricky, either wallslide the vertical mouth of the pipe or jump off the mouth to the vertical mouth down by the mines, or you could walljump from the upper left vertical mouth to the other side wall, the reverse of the way you got here.

Fifth Section: The switch guarded by thwumps.
The easy way to do this part is to drop from the lip by where you started and fall hard right, so you land on top of the thwumps. They'll move out of the way, then jump over them to the middle over top of them as they come back. As soon as they both trigger a second time, jump and get the switch. Then just jump out before the trigger a third time, which shouldn't be too hard, and you'll be fine. You can do this part faster, like having the thwumps trigger only once or twice (you can land on top of them and walk on top of them back out if you're good, like in my highscore). I'm just giving you the way that requires the least precision.

Sixth section: The terminal switch.
Get up to the lip of the pipe that is below this part, jump up to the wall, wall jump to the switch, and waltz to the door. Just don't get killed by the gauss turret.

For the diagram, the red line is the way to the switch(notice that the third switch has two routes to the switch I drew up because both work fine), the blue line is the way to relative safety, and the green lines are different ways out (which work just fine, they're just not the routes I use most often).
[/easy way]
