Part 2

Concerning gold as dangers

While on the hunt for gold, watch out for hidden switches. Would you choose to take it all or choose to take a little bit? Many highscorers choose all, but you should choose a little of gold.

Why? Because the traps in a one-cell walk way. A one-cell walk way is a place like a tunnel where one object can fit.

Another answer is gold blotches. when there's lots of gold many people immeadiatly take all of it for the life of N but there may be traps under it.
