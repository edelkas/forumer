#403402 5 2008-01-23T14:03:09Z astro
That's some very nice audio.

I don't like the cut from the piano to the strings either, mostly because it feels like the piano should just continue (and, after all, you do bring in the piano a little later). I think the harp's entry (the twinkly thing is a harp, right?) is also a bit abrupt because the piano stops right after the harp comes in--you could perhaps change it so there's just a little extra piano there. In fact, on closer inspection, I notice that both times the piano plays one final note when the other instrument(s) come in. I reckon it would sound better if the piano stops before.

Apart from those transitions I think it's as good as it can be.
