1. Fall warning. Could be used to tell when you could die. if you drop too fast, some kind of indicator (maybe the ninja flashes red, or you hear some beeping) will turn on to tell you that the floor impact will kill you. You can use this to know when to wallslide.

Mine dropper

4. Ninja headband. `nuff said.
