This is a new contest, with the idea of winning in different ways

CONGRATS APG,
see bottom

Grading and Rules
  • all the maps for this contest must have a way of winning besides just going to an exit door
  • maps will be graded out of 100 points
  • x/40 for originality of idea (most maps will get around a 25 for this, very very original is 40. for example, my demo map would be around 20 as it's not that fun of an idea
  • fun x/40 (do you want to keep playing this, what would people rate it multiplied by 8)
  • functionality x/20 (are there wasted spaces, does the map work, does it look pretty, are there any problems. my map, for example would get about a 5 as it is ugly and nearly impossible)
    ALL OF MY VOTES WILL BE IN ORANGE
Rules
  • old maps may not be submited
  • once one person has posted an idea for a way to win, no one else may use it
  • no KRA's or death map's of any sort
  • the map must be playable using user levels
  • the map may have no bugs
  • the map must leave demos (same as above)
  • under the maps description all rules must be posted, and that the map is for this competition
  • when you make a map, make a post saying the map's name
  • when you first come up with the map idea, post it
  • the map may not use bugs
  • you may submit 2 maps, with one edit allowed per map within two days of the map being posted
FAQ and Info (i'll answer all q's here)
  • • this contest does not have an official end date yet, but am aiming for about 2-3 weeks after we get 4+ competitors• there is currently no prize, but if anyone is willing to offer a ded that would be great, or i will make one with my VERY limited mapmaking experience• anyone who cheats in any way will be banned from the contest, and if any judges are obviously showing favoritism i will kick them off
