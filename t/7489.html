Getting the same experience with a 3D version of N would be virtually impossible. The main problem is that regardless of the camera angle, the entire map could never be seen at one time. It's often necessary to simultaneously track the location of enemies that are in two or three opposing directions, which can't very well be done in 3D space. The only feasible solutions would be multiple cameras or some kind of radar detection, but in all likelihood those would still be too confusing and unintuitive.

And yes, I believe work on that project has been discontinued.
