Ten-minute Photoshop Contest

Use Photoshop and any of its sidebar tools (or any complex drawing app. if you're a GIMP user) to quickly whip up an image in ten minutes or less; watch the corner computer clock, and stick it in an attachment to a reply in this thread!

Rules:
1. Don't cheat and go over the time. It might be worth the risk, but if too many people get suspicious and
2. Only one entry. You can keep scrapping and restarting a pic. as many times as you want, tho'
3. It can be about anything legal!! Draw a pic. of bullets ricocheting off of walls, your favorite car, a, uh, dgh... plane, yourself, whatever!

Judges! I'm not picking out the finalists (just two phases) on my own You cannot judge if you're competing.
I am the only judge so far. Just ask to become a judge.

Now get Photoshopping!
