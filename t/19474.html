The Academy
Sundays 12:00-1:00 Eastern Time every week.
This may sound more officious than I mean, so let me preclude this post with the disclaimer that this will be a low-key project. Acidity and nastiness will not, will never be tolerated.

The Academy is the embodiment of the interactive part of the mapmaking process. It exposes steps of a map in progress to the subjectivity of public opinion. And it's live-action! :Q

:-: Every week at noon (Eastern Time) on the dot we adjourn in #academy on the IRC. My apologies to those of you elsewhere in the world who can't make it. If popularity increases, we might work something out. Call me. ~_^
:-: There will be some sort of direction to start us off every week. Whether it be a theme to follow, an idea to keep in mind or a suggested style, we all embark on a new map.
:-: At 20-30 minutes into mapping we will begin with the first mapper (most likely alphabetically) and he or she posts what he has come up with so far using the wonderful www.pastebin.ca.
:-: After the first person we take ~ five or ten minutes to play the map, reflect, and write down our comments. Comments can be suggestions, appraisal or criticisms, but nastiness will not be accepted. The time limit is a loose boundary. When we're all finished we may proceed.
:-: We each send the mapper a link to the pastebin.ca post that contains our comments in a private PM on IRC.
:-: We have a brief public discussion about our comments and how we felt. Once the conversation is over, we move on to the next mapper and he or she posts his or her map.
:-: A mapmaker can choose to include or exclude any suggestions he or she wants. At least keeping an open mind is key.
:-: We end an hour after we begin, longer if necessary. At this point the finalized versions of the map are submitted to numa and links are pasted in the #academy.
:-: Every week the transcript of the class will be posted in this topic. For privacy's sake, the personal comments will not and cannot be included. However, the guest mapper's comments will, as they will be in the actual channel.

Every week we will have a new guest mapper in to help guide the class. He or she will post his or her comments publically about each map. Our first week's guest will be: Yahoozy.

Involve yourself. This will be really fun and really creative, especially for those people who are having creative difficulties. No sign-up is necessary. Show up at 12:00 Eastern Time on Sunday and stay for as long as you can. Preferrably the whole time.

Discuss!
