Well, even if I'm listening to happy music, all my poems end up really dark and depressing. For example, while doing a homework assignment, I attempted to turn my 55 Fiction into a poem. And I was listening to a very bright song. It turned out like this:

Escape
I returned home
Into the decaying tenement
Where life was rare and happiness rarer
Into the dreary hall with flickering lights scattered
Flashing night into day, dark into light, erratically
Longing to break glass and leave the decaying world
I struggled to breathe in the depths of depression
I turned the twisted key in the warped door
Not noticing the lurking presence behind
The blade slipped in and damaged
As blood seeped with ease
I fell to the floor
And thought,





Maybe Ill run away later.

I have no idea why. I think I just like using strong phrases.
