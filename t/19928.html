I was stumbling through Fmods today, when I found this. Now, I implemented it on a test forum and it actually works.

For those whom are wondering, it is a mini-imageshack hoster. It is implemented in the post area (where you post) and works a lot quicker, giving you space that you need. It's like Imageshack fused with the forum. And I speak for myself when I say, that it is a pain to go, window-to-window, getting the uploaded image and post it, when you can post it while you put your image. This follows the regular Imageshack guides (the appropraite image files) to be transfered into code. With the increase of image-makers and cmic makers, this could only benefit the forum.

Plus, it's very easy to implement. It took me 1 minute to put that in that test forum, so it's not much of a pain.

Post in the Test forum, and you'll see what I mean.

http://theawaiting.9.forumer.com/index.php?act=idx

On the bottom of the post, there will be an option to upload an image files as a code.

So, tell me what you think of this option. Should it be inserted in order to simplify images and comics everywhere, or is it bad? Discuss.
