Actually, yeah, I fully agree with this statement.

The most important thing to do to start a relationship is to let the object of your affection know you exist.

Even in college there are people who still take the middle-schooler approach to romance, which is basically:
"If you like someone, it is absolutely critical that you not let her know you exist. If she's in a class, avoid it; sit well out of earshot and out of sight in the classes you do have to take together; if she's going to a party, avoid it; if you see her in the street, cross the street and avoid eye contact; avoid at all costs getting into a conversation with her. You are a ghost, and will have most success if at the end of your school career someone can say your name to her and leave her drawing blanks. Taking these precautions will guarantee success."
Don't be like that. Talk, engage, make it obvious without directly saying it that you're into her.
(If you do state it plainly, though, that leads to a sudden, dramatic lurching in the amount of awkwardness. Just make it obvious so you don't have to say it.)
