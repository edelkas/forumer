#56761 17 2005-03-28T20:14:04Z Guest
hi,
well.. as i mentioned, the majority of user levels are incredibly hard.

they're simply not meant to be played 5 at a time, which means that when you DO stick 5 of them in a row, the game goes from quite challenging to ridiculously frustrating. we have included a few of these where appropriate (i.e as the last level in later episodes). can you really imagine playing an episode made of 5 mount-doom-type levels? it's too much.


also, maximo does have 2x as many levels as anyone else. we couldn't make an all-maximo episode, for the above reasons.. it was simply too hard and took far too long to beat.


using our one or two favorite maps from many different authors let us fix the difficulty problem. also, we just thought it would be nice if as many people as possible could see their name in N. we wish we could have included everyone who's in NUMA, however that wasn't possible.

no doubt there will be some complaints when the user maps are revealed. many maps were cut because they used too many expert-only tricks, exploited "bugs" (i.e teleporting), ran too slowly due to too many objects, or were simply way too hard.

at the same time, we did try to include the broadest possible selection of maps, especially those which did new and inventive things with the game.

one concern we have is that some people will find an early level unbeatable, and will thus be prevented from ever seeing all of the user-made maps.

anyway..
raigan
