#30649 2 2004-10-05T22:46:02Z Guest
Yeah, that got me when I first started, too. But just for future reference, this would belong more in the "N Help" forum.

Anyway, here's what ya gotta do... You gotta learn the double-wall jump. When you get back to the end of the hall, at the bottom right, there's a cilantro space that's only one space wide. jump into that space, and then hist shift (or your jump key), twice rapidly. it'll launch you upwards at a faster rate than a normal jump. Land on the block above the first door. from there, you have to jump above the mine on the wall above you. To do this, you have to jump more upward than to the right. you have to jump first, holding shift down, and then pressing right to move towards the point above the mine. It's not easy to do, especially when you're first starting, but it is doable. the part after that is easy, and you can obviously do the rest.

By wall mines, I'm assuming you mean the two mines on the far right wall. The easiest way to get back past them on the way back up is to keep your momentum and walljump all the way up. Look at the highscores to see what I mean.
