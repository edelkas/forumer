#183832 2 2006-06-03T00:56:50Z Mark
The main ingredients for a good dda are:
- Lots of close calls
- Interesting methods of propulsion
- Minimal gold delay (it adds lag and doesn't move the pace along most of the time)
- Some sort of originality (very hard to do - don't worry too much about it)

Still, a couple of good tips are to place your enemies first. Then you can work the close calls around them without causing extra lag of the enemie firing when you're not expecting. It's also good to have a rough plan on what you want to do before you start. You may have to adapt the tileset/object placement to get the close calls you need or go where you want to go.

Other than that, look at some of the best ddas on NUMA and see what they do to be considered so good. Try to mimic certain aspects of their design, whilst being creative and developing your own style. That's about it!

Good luck!
