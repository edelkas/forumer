#349302 10 2007-07-31T08:23:29Z Atilla
Symmetry does not mean "playing the same map twice", unless the areas are completely isolated. In most cases it means playing in an area which is twice as large. There is also the fact that your direction of approach may necessarily be different for one side of the level. If, for example, you were to traverse the level counter-clockwise, you would approach the left side from the bottom and climb up, whereas you would approach the right side from the top and fall down. This can be a significant difference as far as gameplay is concerned.
