  1. After you rate a map, you go to a ''Thank you for your rating'' page. I think it should simply refresh the map with your new rating.
  2. And that leads onto the suggestion that it should tell you what you rated on the map.
  3. An options page. This could have things such as Change password, "How many maps on Top Rated/Random/Newest/etc." , a comment to appear on your author page and stuff like that.
  4. A simple one. Make the rating out of 10, rather than out of 5 with .5 ratings.
  5. The idea of a histogram was suggested a while ago. I tried to take a jab at it and wondered what you thought. (the ratings of a imaginary map, where 40% of the rators gave a 4, 30% gave a 3, and so on.
  6. After you submit a map, you automaticly go to a random map with the lowest number of votes, and are asked to rate it.
