Just wanna tell you guys about Counter-Strike 2D, a 2D version of the most popular online shooter in the world. The game is top-down view, and it's kind of fun. The bots on that game aren't any smarter than its 3D counter-part, but there is online multiplayer support.

Counter-Strike 2D has a built-in Editor Mode where you can create your own maps and submit them to Banana, a site mainly known for its CS map archives.
