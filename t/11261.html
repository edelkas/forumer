Hi. I've been getting a couple of PMs lately, and e-mails, and well. I guess that brings me back to here more often than I would be otherwise.

I thought I might owe it to the lot of you to say, I dunno. Things are going. Life is moving, and it isn't all bad. Yesterday I came rather close to kissing a certain somebody who's meant a lot to me for a lot longer than I'd easily be able to describe here. I mean, and then Dad appears, and I jerk my head backwards and end up raising quite a lump on my head due to the impact of the brick wall.

My family will annoy me, often, with their petty arguments and alliances within the household, the whingeing, the lack of peace and intrusiveness of living in such close quarters with people so... I don't know. I won't say small, or small minded, but often so concerned with these little unnescessary grudges and miniture fights we really don't need and would be better off without.

There's been quite a bit of laughter, lately. A lot of hanging around with friends, planning stuff (preperation for next year's Pre- Or- Post Exposure Prophylaxis Day is already underway, and it will be a day to remember. This is amoungst many other, more local projects) and, finally, it looks as though I might be in a band that'll end up halfway serious. One Way Jesus, it's called. It has around 6 members.

My mapmaking has been... very haphazard. I have a lot archived, saved for. Well, I guess I shouldn't give it away.

And, I'm here again. Well, sorta. I'm not sure, really, but I guess... I'm potentially here, if you ever need to contact me. I haven't exactly left. I guess I don't really see much of a need of you guys in my life, no hole that you'll fill or... something like that. But I am here, and if you ever need me, I will never hesistate to reply to a PM or e-mail or offer my opinions wherever they're wanted. I don't want to selfishly leave. I want to do the right thing.

I've been listening to more music than ever, and have memorised more lyrics than I ever thought was possible. I have about 15gig on the computer, about half of which comes from actual CDs. I've been painting, too... some slightly surreal, impressionist stuff. It's quite bad, but I do kinda like it.

School has... been a huge mixture of ups and downs. Grades and sometimes going up, sometimes down. Some are higher than they've ever been, others- like Lit and German- have plummeted. Lately I've come very close to yelling at my Lit teacher, and storming out of the classroom, on more than one occasion. And she hit me, once, in class. Only on the hand, but it's still wrong.

I've started keeping a journal, too. Leatherbound, written with a fountain pen. It's... odd, what it contains, when you read over it. Overtures of lightheartedness and frivolity and... nothing substantial or groundbreaking underneath. No archived secrets. Just some records, some observations, some comedy, and some meditations on not much at all.

There's laughter. There's music. Lovers never having kissed, and empty space yet still unseen. Writing, dancing, singing, living, debating, playing, walking... There's life.

And I guess that's sort of what I want to say. I love you all, but I guess I've allready told you that. I'm here, and if you ever want to talk, I'm more than glad to listen.
