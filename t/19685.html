One of Our Aircraft is Missing is an ambient piece that I have composed, musicated on and edited for this project. It is made up of 2 layers, essentially. The guitar layer is drone-based but has exact playing, and a little western influence, which might remind you of the band Earth. In fact, Dylan Carlson once mused that all that matters is the notes, and how each individual note is arranged within the piece. I very much subscribe to that theory, and it's in evidence here. The guitar here is clean but powerful and bass-driven. The second layer is the ambient layer, containing ambient drones and glitches that contrast the guitar but do not override it. Enjoy.
