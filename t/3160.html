Here it is. Finally. The first round of the Numa Time Trial Society Season 2.

I dont like to override democracy usually, but I really want to start off season 2 with the right map, and Im just extremely confident that Cliffs is a better map for the NTTS than Debris.

So there you have it. Your map for round 1 is officially Heartviews Cliffs.

Saturday will be our starting point, so all contestants will have until Tuesday the 24th to submit their two demos.

Take a gander at the wonderful NTTS site created by none other than our own super mod Kablizzy. And then familiarize yourself with the new rules below.

Rules

- You may replace a previously submitted demo at any point up until the deadline. You may do this as many times as you like. Make sure you delete any demo youre replacing when you post a new one. If you accidentally leave 3 demos posted at the time of the deadline, the earliest two will be used. To avoid this make sure you delete old demos.

- IMPORTANT: The order submitted will very likely have a bearing on the judges rankings. To ensure that we know the order that each demo was submitted you may not edit any post in which you submit a demo for any reason. If we see a this post has been edited by . . . tag in any post that contains a demo submission, that demo will be disqualified.

- We are using the user levels option on the menu. So make sure your demos are in the proper format.

- There are two categories for scoring. "Time" and Best Demo. Best Demo has no set criteria, so make the most kickass demo you can and hope the judges like it. "Time" has three places worth 5, 3, 1 points, and "Best Demo" has five places worth 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 points.

- A single person can win two slots in the "Best Demo" category but only one in the "Time" category.

Your judges are: trib4lmaniac, nevermore and this week, jeffkillian filling in for me.

Thats all I got. Ready set go!
