#185406 13 2006-06-06T14:09:33Z JeCa
But, if you have Nvidia desktop manager, there's a better way to hide windows. First, you create two separate windows. Then, when the boss comes, press a pre-set key combination - I use ctrl+F12 - to throw the window to the other desktop. If you don't tuch anything, it will still be the active window, so when he walks away you can just press ctrl+F11 to get it back. If you mark another window, then you have to use another key combination to jump to the right desktop and start playing. When he comes, just go back to the first desktop and he wont see anything! Really usefull thing, that manager...
