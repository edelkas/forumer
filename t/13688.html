OK, heres a bit more information on how creating multiple objects at a time will work. Creating multiple instances at once is not supported in the current version. First, you will specify the region type (line, filled square, ellipse outline, ellipse fill). Then you choose what object to place. Then you choose how much space there is in-between the objects (1/4 snap, 1/2 snap, full snap, custom- Objects are not snapped if the region shape is elliptical) and then you use your mouse to place the objects.
