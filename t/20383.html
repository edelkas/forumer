The first two maps reminded me of something Dave made in one of his columns, which is a good thing. Manical was some good fun; it would be better if you could expand on that whole theme and turned it into extended level. Diabolical was felt a little too claustrophobic, and it was a very small map, too, making the enjoyment limited. I know you aimed to be 'as evil as you could', but it was too hard to be any fun. It was more of a trial and error map where you just kept testing out routes until they worked. The last map, Fiendish, had the same basis as Diabolical. It was really just a map where it was more finding the correct route, and not actually requiring too much skill. Try incorporating you 'evilness' into larger maps next time.
