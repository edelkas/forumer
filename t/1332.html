I think that what they were trying to get at is that water doesn't exactly treat things as simply slow-motion. Physics get distorted while under water, and behaviors of force exerted on something (like a ninja) are never constant under water. Certain water pressures have differing effects on things, depending on the depth, etc. For instance, if you're standing in a puddle, the water does next to nothing to you, same as in a kiddie pool. But if you're walking along the bottom of a swimming pool, or along the bottom of the ocean, the pressure affects movement.

Personally, I think water would be kinda neat, just not too terribly economical.
