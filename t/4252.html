hi,
you're correct, there's an ambiguity since you get an axis, but you could go in either direction (call them + and -) along that axis.

however, you want to push the objects apart. only one of the two directions will do this, the other will push them together. so you just need to figure out wich of the two directions is "right".

look at the vector which points from object A's center to object B's center (call this the delta vector); this vector points away form object A, and you want to push object B in the same direction, i.e away from object A.

only one of the two possible directions along the axis will point away from object A.. to figure out which, look at the sign of the dotproduct of the delta vector and one of the directions along the axis.

have you tried reading the slides from flashinthecan? (they're available on the tutorials page, underneath the two tutorials) there's more info and some source there; also, reading through the source that comes with the tutorials will definitely help provide more info.. it's very simple, but at the same time there are many tiny things going on.
