#15320 1 2007-05-22T03:15:02Z Street_Wolf
I know that lately, I've been really ranty and whiney about N evolving and growing and how hard it is, and how the community . . .etc etc etc. I think though, that everything changed last night and today.

I decided to go to NUMA again and see if there was anything I could dig up that isn't too terribly hard. I browsed around, and decided to try out some categories I didn't visit much. I went to race, and I think I found a winner there!

I tried Anyway the N Way, and of course I died pretty quickly into it . Meh, this would probably end up in the "can't beat, throw away" category, for me. But something about the map kept me going. I started to get the urge to beat it. I never really felt this excited about trying to beat a map before. This was new to me. I went at it again. And again. And again. I must've been on that map for more than an hour, when finally I took all four paths in a row, narrowly avoiding death at every corner, I hit over the last obstacle, swung down the starting shaft, and ran all the way across the map towards the exit in a glorious final run!

As soon as I hit that exit, I got so full of excitement, I let out a loud cheer! (Luckily there was no one home to here ) but I knew that that was a turning point. I checked out another couple race maps today, Two Paths (made by same author as Anyway) and The Crazy Miner. I've been at these for the past hour or so, and I actually managed to beat Two Paths! I've bee making progress on The Crazy Miner as well.

I still am nowhere near a great N player, but I really think I just caught onto the spark of excitment recently, and so I'm excited to really get deeper into the game and enjoy maps with everyone else. I also discovered I love the play style of race maps, so I think I've found my niche here in the game.

Just thought I'd like to share this with the community.
