Well, the way pokemaniac said would work, but it's kinda harder to understand...

You know the main Ned screen, right? Well you copy the level data and put it into the 'level data text' field in Ned, then you load the level with [L] (after clicking outside the text field to prevent NaNs), then you copy the demo you want to see and put it in the 'demo data' text field, and then you load the demo with [1] (again after clicking outside the field to prevent it from screwing up) and you play it with [Q]...

That should work
