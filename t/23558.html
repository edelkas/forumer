Three categories, music and lyrics. Music must be completely instrumental, and preferably streamable. And now an open category with both lyrics and music. Lyrics most be written only. Most people don't have the tech to record themselves at home so this is the fairest way to do it. 5 Judges, judging from 1/10. High and low scores will be dropped. You may only submit 2 songs in each category. The deadline is Sept. 29th.

Rules
- 2 Songs per category.
- "Lyric" songs must be written only and "Music" songs must be completely instrumental.
- Deadline is Sept. 29th.

Judging
- I hand selected the Judges in order to achieve a genre balanced panel.
- Entries will be judged from 1 to 10. (This is simple, I know.)
- The back up Judge will judge the judges entries.
- The high and low scores for each entry will be dropped and the remaining scores will be summed.

- Southpaw
