Well, here is my idea for a new competition... I hope ya enjoy it.

RULES
1. All DDAs must use provided tilest
2. No tiles may be changed- except for those blocked by a one way
3. The one ways may not be deleted- unless being replaced by a block
4. 1 entry per person. If you create a totally kickass one after submitting your first, you may post that as a replacement.
5. The ninja must end on the door (and complete the thingo)
5. The judges (aka my decision) is final.

SCORING

Part 1
{No. of frames (multiplied by) y} / deaths in 6 runs
Total + bonuses = grand total

So... X is relevant to the amount of enemies you place. Enemies must be activated and/ or used (with exception to, obviously, mines.) So, a level layerd with inactive thwumps around the edge or rockets, lasers and gauss turrets behind locked/ trapped doors shall not score high. Scoring for enemies is as folows:
1 point for gauss turrets
1 point for thwumps
1.5 points for non- homing zap drones
2 points for lasers
2 points for rockets
2.5 points for homing zap drones
4 points for Chain Guns
0.2 points for mines
0.2 points for bouncy blocks
Please do not spam mines, or layer any of the above items

I will test each level 6 times, and out of that I shall divide your score up to this point by the number of times, in those runs, N dies before reaching the exit. If he dies 5 out of 6 (or more) you are automatically disqualified.

And, for bonuses,
+ 400 for each instance (as in, groups) of using doors as propulsion
+ 1000 for each time N gets hit by an enemy, but survives. (Only applicable once per enemy.)
+ 2500 if N survives (permanently) after completing a level
+ (Up to my discretion) for using many alternate methods of propulsion
- (Up to my discretion) for EXCESSIVE (also my discretion) gold delay
- (Up to my discretion) for DDAs that, basically, are boring and/ or just generally suck. (See Brickmans post.)




THE END DECISION

A few of the highest rating courses will be judged for overall coolness and fun by a panel of volunteers, (if nobody joins in- just myself) and award a winner and runner up. These will not necessarilly be the two people with the highest points.

DEADLINE

Probably April 15... may be extened or cut short...




Yeah, well go knock yourselves out.
