Well, I figured out how THWUMPER'S numbers go...

!8^420,540,1

The ! tells N that's its something new, the 8^ tells N it's a thwumper and it's about to give location, the 420 is it's position on the X axis, the 540 is it's position on the Y axis, and the 1 is the direction the thwumper is facing.

If you change the 1 to anything other than 1-4, it'll shock from YOUR right, computer screen's left.
