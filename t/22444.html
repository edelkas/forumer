First of all, thank you to the designers, who submitted such excellent designs, and to all the users (330 in all) who voted on the designs. The response to this has been nothing short of amazing.

Voting is now officially closed, and I've totalled up the votes using my whiz-bang-vote-tabulating-program. The output is below. First, a word about it: The number of votes against each name is the number of votes a design had when it was eliminated from the competition (see previous posts for details of how voting works). Since everyone could choose up to 3 maps to vote on, the totals will not sum up to the total number of users, so don't be surprised.

So without further ado, the results:
In 12th place, "Commander's design #1" with 6 votes.
In 11th place, "DDRave's design #1" with 6 votes.
In 10th place, "Condog's design #2" with 7 votes.
In 9th place, "SkyRay's design #1" with 10 votes.
In 8th place, "Condog's design #1" with 11 votes.
In 7th place, "GForce20's design #1" with 13 votes.
In 6th place, "n0valyfe's design #1" with 21 votes.
In 5th place, "Red Reamer's design #1" with 32 votes.
In 4th place, "Harm-Less's design #1" with 46 votes.
In 3rd place, "T3chno's design #1" with 72 votes.
In 2nd place, "USASwim's design #1" with 89 votes.

And in first place, with 177 votes, "Harm-Less's design #2"!

Thank you again to everyone who participated, and huge congratulations to Harm-Less. I'll be working with him on implementing his design for NUMA over the coming weeks, but he recently informed me he will be away for 2 weeks, so there will be some delay before the design goes up. Keep an eye on NUMA's news page for more details closer to the time. And just as a teaser, there's a lot more than just a redesign in the works for NUMA.
