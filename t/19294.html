On a side note, ed101cool, if what you mean is more of "How do I get more userlevels?", simply:

1) Go to the folder that Nv1.4 is in.
2) Open the text file called "Userlevels".
3) Copy the map data you require (making sure its in format: "$MapName#Author#Type#Data#").
4) Scroll to the bottom of the Userlevels text file.
5) Paste the data at the end of the file.
6) Save.

Here is what you do:

1. Download Firefox if you haven't already.
2. Go to numa.notdot.net and find the link on the left side in the navbar that says "Firefox Extension"
3. Download and put the extension in your toolbar thing (where the URL box and the Home and Reload buttons are)
4. Click on the little arrow next to it and set your N executable position and your userlevels executable position.
5. From now on, if you want to play the userlevels you are going to have to open Firefox and use the extension to open N.

At least that's how it works for me.

Oh wait, I think I know what he's asking.

If you're asking how to play userlevels, you can:

Go to debug mode by pressing ~ or \, then paste the map data excluding the title, author, and #'s in the top-right box (There's a manual in the N folder for more information).

You could also save userlevels to enter the "userlevels" section by opening up the userlevels file. Then, copy and paste the whole data where it tells you to.

I hope this helps you.
