Welcome to The Monday Night Numa Map League!

Beginning today, January 1st, 2007, The Legacy Team presents a weekly activity for mapmakers of all kinds. the MNNM league is here to encourage mapmakers of all skill levels to engage in a ritual, of sorts, and to participate in something to call their own. Each week, participants will make a single map - The only stipulation? It has to be Monday. After each Monday ends, the thread will become locked, and all of that week's maps will be compiled in a file, and attached to this thread. The MNNM is an ongoing project, but will have mapping 'seasons,' of an as-yet determined timeframe.

To become a member, please post in the MNNM Sign-Up thread declaring your intentions to join the League. You will be assigned a member number, and be given a signature tag for use in your forum signature.

From there, simply open up NED, and begin mapping!

Each week, mapmakers will abide by a given theme for their maps, and post those maps to NUMA. This theme could be purely aesthetic, conceptual, rather specific, or very loose. Each Monday, a new theme will be posted in this thread, and league members will have until the end of that night to post their map. Once the map is posted to NUMA, each member will post a link in this thread, and at the end of each week, the maps will be compiled in this first post for everyone's enjoyment!

Each map posted to NUMA should be formatted with the Week Number and Theme. For example,
