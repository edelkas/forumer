Click ADD (to add new network)

/join #N

Here's a tutorial for anyone wanting to use gaim for IRC.

1. Go to Tools -> Accounts and then "Add."
2. Select the IRC protocol.
3. For screen name, just put whatever nickname you want.
4. For the server, put numa.notdot.net
5. The self-alias is optional. Save the account, check the "online" box for IRC in the list of accounts.
6. Once logged in, a new conversation will open saying "Spoofing your IP. congrats." You can either type "/join #N" here or go to Buddies->Join a Chat, select your IRC account, and type #N for the channel.

To make it easier, you can go to Buddies->Add Chat and put #N for the channel. Then, right click on the chatroom on your buddy list and select "Auto-Join."
