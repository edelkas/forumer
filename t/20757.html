By what definition do you claim God is male?

Do you mean He has a penis?

More likely you mean that he is "male" because he is our "Father," as in, he created us (as opposed to literally being our "father", as in donating sperm). But wouldn't our "Mother" have had a larger part in creating us? If God created us singlehandedly, then isn't he both our "Father" and our "Mother"?

So actually, he's male in the religious sense, which has nothing to do with being male or female. He's male, in that we use a male pronoun, and he has "masculine" qualities. But the male pronoun is used because most languages don't have a gender-neutral pronoun (other than "it", which is only for inanimate objects). And the fact that God acts "masculine" is only due to the fact that he's omnipotent, and power is culturally and historically attributed to males.

So, by what definition do you claim God is male?
