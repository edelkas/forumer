Okay, I really like the game and all, here are my suggestions, the first two, by the way, are purely aesthetic.

For the K button suicide, the explosion works when in midair, but I think on the ground the ninja should pull out a sword (grey triangle thing) and commit seppuku, because that would be a very cool way to go. Hand pulls out sword, sticks through back, blood spray and collapse. Nifty.

Secondly, to make deaths more exciting and interesting, the environment should continue to affect you. If you hit a mine and blow up and a limb hits a bounce pad jump thingy, the limb should go flying, possibly with blood trails. Gatling guns should fire their full round at your body, meaning that when they hit you, you're pinned to the wall with gunfire at first and then fall down again. And also, corpses activating mines, etc., falling onto instead of through bounce blocks. This would add to the realism and make dying even cooler.

Finally, an actually gameplay suggestion. In terms of killing enemies, if you maneuver a rocket launcher rocket without getting hit by it so that hit will hit the launcher itself or another enemy, (even a mine), the target should explode. Gatling gun, gauss gun, and laser gun fire should also be deadly to enemies, though for balance I'd imagine that the latter 3 should perhaps require a more solid and prolonged hit, since missiles to something are much harder to pull off. This would allow for generalized awesomeness by use of ninja skills.

My last suggestion would be a multiplayer or battle mode, with current gameplay as puzzle mode. In this mode, two ninja or possibly one ninja and robots would face off, the ninja having access to shuriken thrown in the direction of movement by another key. The goal here, I guess, would be to kill all opposing targets. I think battle mode with different maps having their own hazards, and with multiplayer (preferably online if somehow possible) would really open up the gameplay.
