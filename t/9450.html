Make sure you are connected to the internet and that, if you have a firewall, it will allow N to connect. If you have dial-up, it might take a little while. Also, I believe the server went down not too long ago, which may explain why it wasn't responding. Although you shouldn't have to create an account to watch other people's highscores. Just go to the highscore menu and click on an episode and level. N should automatically download the scores for that level/episode. Click on an entry in the highscores table, and it will show you the demo for that score.
