Well, it's quite simple, my friend. Seeing as you asked coherently and politely...

This is copied from the editor_manual.txt file that comes with N, which answers many questions you may have about Ned:

Quote: The editor_manual.txt
(Make sure you click somewhere outside the boxes when you load the level and demo data)

1. Press [L] to pull up the text boxes.  Paste level data into the upper text box, paste demo data into the lower text box.

2. Press [L] to load your level.
3. Press [3] to load your demo.

4. Press [Q] to initiate playback of the demo.

5. Press [P] to start viewing the demo.

6. Make sure caps lock is on!

#247798 4 2006-10-30T04:14:44Z LittleViking
To be perfectly clear.

When you want to record a demo, press 1, then P. Play the level. After you're finished, press ~ to go back to the editor and press 2. Now, your demo should be recorded and you can copy the data from the demo data box.

To playback a demo, paste the data into the demo box, press 3 to load it and Q to start playback. Then press P and the demo will play the level.
