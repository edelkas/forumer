#415432 2 2008-03-09T23:04:16Z Pepi
WWII was caused by neither science nor religion. The seeds of it were planted when the Treaty of Versailles was signed. This treaty came into effect after WWI when Germany had lost, and it forced Germany to not only pay war reparations to the Allies in full, but to accept all responsibility for the entirety of WWI. The broken German economy was obviously unable to pay the debt, and it caused a lot of hatred among the Germans towards the Allies. A political figure rose to power, vowing revenge. His name was Adolf Hitler. He supported eugenics, he wanted territory for Germany, and ultimately, world domination.

The Treaty of Versailles "fell between two stools" as the saying goes. There were two options available to the Allies: make a generous, forgiving armistice, or exact incredibly harsh punishment that completely destroyed the German military and occupied/governed Germany for a long time. Versailles did neither and both at the same time. It allowed Germany to keep a small army, didn't occupy Germany, and forced reparations. The combination of hatred and a military were the cause of World War Two.

I think those facts are right... I apologize if any are incorrect.
