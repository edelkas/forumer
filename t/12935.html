My story starts, like so many others, with a girl.

An actress, actually. And an extremely good one, who's starred in quite a few Australian films, had many guest roles in well- known Australian television series, and is currently filming for one of the main roles of an arthouse short film which will be shown in a lot of festivals.

I'll make this brief and to the point- I have never met a girl like this. She is extraodinary in every way... I cannot describe how many cliche's she fills my heart with. I want to say "I love her", but this is... too large a comment for a 17 year old to make. And yet I constantly find myself thinking that I might, or that I do.

And this girl, in the last two films she's been in- and in the one she's filming at the moment- is making out with a large variety of guys. 1 in the last film, as well as a sex scene (which didn't actually involve much physical closeness- very artsy stuff) and 2 in this one. One of which unzips her jacket and engages in some on- screen fondling of... well. You can figure out the specifics for yourself.

And I understand the distinction between acting and, in real life, 'feeling'. The difference between experiancing emotions in the context of the stage (think of Stavislavski here) and in real life. And I understand that acting is a large part of her life, and I truly don't want to make it awkward or unpleasent for her- or to make her feel guilty for doing something that's such a large part of her life. And I've talked about this with her, too- for hours, just tonight. We got rather deep and philosophical about the whole issue, and I realised again how great it is I can connect with this person in such a way, and on such a level, in which I have never connected with any other person.

But here's my problem.

We've discussed it. A lot. And... it shouldn't cheapen kissing. It's not the action which carries the romantic connotations, it's the intent behind it. It means nothing on the screen, when it's directed and you have to rotate slightly to stay in the light- I understand this. Intellectually. And yet I find myself not wanting to kiss her in the same way as I did before. It's the same act, but the act is meaningless. Only the intent and the feeling behind it counts for anything.

And I don't want to think and feel in this way. That nothing is real, nothing is meaningful, and- professionaly- a person can make out with another and it means nothing. I don't WANT lips, tounges, and saliva to mean nothing. I don't want holding her and kissing her and holding hands to mean- on a physical level- nothing. I don't want everything in my life to be reduced to the more abstract concepts of intent and motivation and feelings. And yet this is the mindframe in which I find I have to think. One which I hate.

I hate being stood up whenever her agent calls- turning up to the location of our planned dates only to recieve a delayed text message saying she can't make it. (Which, thankfully, has only happened once.) I hate planning to meet her and see her and then her being prevented from seeing me, after I've turned down invitations with friends and caused them to grudge my devotion to the few times in which I can see this certain girl. I hate barely seeing her- sometimes once a week, sometimes twice, sometimes going for longer when she's needed a lot for filming. I hate not being around her, and I hate being prevented from seeing her, and I hate that she's so busy and we can't just be normal dating adolescents, lost in eachother and the rush of hormones.

I hate the constant looming threat that she'll get a really great role that'll mean she'll have to move overseas or interstate. She came relatively close to one, and we decided that if she were to get it she would go, I would support her, and we would keep a long distance relationship for the 9 months she would be away. And, whenever I considered the possibility of her leaving (which is more or less a constant, low level threat,) I felt like death.

And I hate myself for hating all of this. I wish I were fine with it, and I wish I were more supportive and able to just continue on, fine and happy. It's not that I'm not jealous of the other guys, and I certainly don't feel threatened. And the girl in question has told me, several times, that she'd hate it if our roles were reversed and I were the guy forced to make out with a multitude of girls for my work.

It's not an issue which is festering inside me. We're open. We've talked about it, a lot, in discussions which keep on veering towards the deeply philosophical. We understand eachother- which really is amazing, considering the depths of the issue we've explored.

And yet.

As much as I... think I might love her, I do hate all of this. I'd think that she's not the right sort of person for me, but she's insert pseudonym here! And if you knew her, you would understand that there truly is no other person anything like her. She's so different from anybody else I've ever met- funny, deep, beautiful, and it sounds like I'm reeling off a list of major selling points. But it's not that she's great with the selling points and 'top of the range'. She's insert pseudonym here, which manages to make her the most important thing in my life in a way nobody else could ever be.

She's the most important thing in my life. And also the most painful.
