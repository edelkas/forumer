Welcome, friends, to the Hall of Fame! The HoF is a Legacy production, an annual awards show meant to be a supplement to the Dronies, so that one show is concluded every six months. It's not just a display of the best maps of the year, but a showcase of the greatest maps of all time. It can include any map ever made, including Metanet levels and others that haven't been submitted to Numa. Regardless of whether they're action, puzzle, survival, or race maps, those honored in the Hall of Fame will be only the very best, the ones that are truly deserving of legendary status.

The Nomination Rules
1) Any map from any place or date is eligible for nomination, even the in-game Metanet levels. They don't necessarily need to have been submitted to Numa.
2) You may nominate as many maps as you wish, within reason.
3) You should nominate maps that you consider to be the best and that you believe have stood (or will stand) the test of time and become true classics.
4) You must PM your nominations to tktktk or sweep. If you could include links to any nominations that have them, we would greatly appreciate it.
5) Nominations must be submitted no later than midnight on May 31st.
6) The Legacy Team records and thoroughly considers all nominations, but in all likelihood not all of them will make it into the final voting list. This is due to the large number of nominations we expect to receive.

The Base Maps
Several "base maps" have been picked out by the Legacy team to be inducted into the Hall of Fame automatically. We tried to include maps that everyone would consider legendary, as well as some of the winners from last year's Dronies. Since they're guaranteed entry into the HoF, you guys will not be nominating or voting for them. The base maps are as follows:

Convention by LittleViking001
Bing Bong, Avon Calling by nevermore
Compartment by Lucidium
Mount Doom by maximo
Mother Thumping Impossible by blue_tetris
Plue by maximo
Rue the Whirl by stepself
The Crazy Miner by formica
The Improbability Drive by formica
What Now?!?! (an N riddle) by Sendy

Remember kids, you've got a month to get your nominations in, but that doesn't mean you should wait until last minute. Have fun now!
