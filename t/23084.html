#450604 2 2008-07-03T23:04:57Z Strife
I'm not usually a fan of Indiana Jones (which isn't to say I dislike it, just that it's never really drawn me in), but I found the first half or so of the movie quite entertaining. That changed drastically toward the end, though. Overall, I pretty strongly dislike the movie.
As the end was getting near, I was trying desperately to remember whether or not previous Indiana Jones movies included supernatural things as opposed to explaining them away as something mundane... and then I got my answer. The end was a bloody joke.
You can usually tell whether a movie like this is good or bad by examining the motivations of the groups involved. In this case, let's focus on the aliens. Imagine you've finished teaching some rising alien culture how to properly irrigate crops, and you're back on your ship again. HQ calls in and says, "go ahead and build an enormous temple around your ship that's filled with mazes of sliding rock. Put a bunch of treasure that the humans will find valuable near the bottom of the UFO, put hiding places in the walls for the humans to hide in, and then line the entrance with spikes and stuff, and a staircase that slowly recedes into the walls (have it take, like, 20 seconds or something). Then what I want you to do is detach your head and hand it over to the natives, and encourage them to worship it and stuff. Then take a 2000 year nap. Once you're done with all that, feel free to head home." Now, I'd respond to that with a wtf and just leave, but the aliens decided to follow this convoluted plan for some reason.
(That or it was a "we can't go home now - Frank's head is detached! Let's all just sit completely frickin' motionless until someone comes along and puts his head back." "Even if it takes a few thousand years?" "As long as it takes!" "What do we do in the meantime?" "I dunno. Let's cover our ship with a mountain, and then carve a deathtrap in the inside." "Sounds good to me.")
#450634 3 2008-07-04T00:50:23Z Atilla
I saw it and didn't like it. I agree with Strife - it started off okay, but just got worse and worse as it went on.

I mean, you don't expect an India Jones movie to be incredibly realistic, but this one just seemed to get progressively more ridiculous and idiotic. Aside from the completely atrocious plot, the thing which irked me most was that the characters frequently avoided a completely obvious solution merely to extend a chase scene and/or prevent the "good guys" from shooting anyone - apparently only evil communists shoot people now. The point where it passed from "a bit silly" to "utterly stupid", for me, was the scene in which someone yelled "They're getting away! We have to stop them!", referring to the evil villain standing in the vehicle in front without any cover, while their hand was resting on the casing of a loaded machine gun pointed directly at the villain. Apparently the two other people who were sitting directly behind the gun when this occurred also failed to notice this possible solution.
