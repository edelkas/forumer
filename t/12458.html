#232299 44 2006-09-23T06:42:26Z tktktk
In terms of pure fun, I just want to highlight one of the few games that has torn me away from N long enough for me to beat it: Donkey Kong Jungle Beat for Gamecube. It's a platformer controlled by bongo drums. Yes, you read that right.

And yet as unlikely as it may seem, the game is basically a gigantic wad o' fun. The controls and the combo system take a few minutes to get the hang of but after that they're completely intuitive, and you'll be stringing combos across nearly entire levels in no time just by clapping and banging drums like a madman. If it sounds dumb, it's because words don't really do it justice. It really needs to be experienced to understand. Now, the game isn't perfect, and it's probably not even among my all-time favorites. It's not your typical platformer, nor is it an epic quest with tons of replay value. It's just simple fun, and that's all there is to it.

Plus, all that drumming adds up to a pretty decent workout. No joke.
