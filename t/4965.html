I was struggling with whether to put this in messageboard games or not, but I decided that the heart of the game is really custom levels.
I also had the idea of making this a contest/tournament, but it's so easy to cheat, I decided against it.

So, here goes.

This is a game. It starts when one person (me) names a custom-made map. This has to be a fairly popular map, so that enough people will be at least somewhat familiar with the map. I will not link to the map, because that will invite cheating
You have to recall the map in your memory, and try to reconstruct it tile by tile, object by object without looking at the map. Of course, you can just cheat, but where's the fun in that?
Everyone who recreates the map should post it here in a txt document. Don't submit it to numa, because it's really not an original map, considering the fact that you're copying another map.
Once I feel the submissions are dying down, I will declare a winner (being the person with the map most similar) based on how similar the gameplay is with the original, how similar they look, and if essentially all of the right objects are in there. In reality, the winner will be the one with the map that reminds us most of the original map.
The winner of the last game chooses the next map, and chooses the winner for the following round.
Once again, please don't cheat and ruin the game for us. This has the potential to be really fun, as long as people are honest.

I will start with maximo's map "Flight."
