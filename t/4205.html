hi,
an easier method is: while N is running, right-click on the window and go to "settings"; a small menu will appear.

the second tab at the bottom of the menu is "storage"; drag this all the way to the right (0k), then click "okay" and exit the menu.

the next time you start N, everything should be blank; if it doens't prompt you to increase the storage space, then right-click/go to the menu you were at before and drag the slider all the way to the right.
