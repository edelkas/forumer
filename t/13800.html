Boxers also have two sections, and in my experience people do not usually refer to pairs of underwear. For example, you would say "I need to buy new underwear." rather than "I need to buy a new pair of underwear." Underpants come in pairs, because the word ends in "pants" and therefore inherits the plural form.

Also, if going by the "number of sections" hypothesis, I think you'll find most bras have at least three different pieces and are therefore not pairs.
