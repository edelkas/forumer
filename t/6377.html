According to my psychology classes, the sensation known as "deja vu" occurs when there is an error in the transmission of sensory data to the brain. Partial or corrupted data is processed, the problem is found, and the same data is then transmitted again (hopefully correctly, this time). You then have a strong feeling you've been in this situation before - and you have, a fraction of a second ago.

#168037 37 2006-04-23T06:05:18Z Tsukatu
Bob is a carnival, watching a show. In the third act, a man lifts an elephant with his mind. Bob, upon seeing this, is surprised, for he remembers seeing that same man lift that same elephant earlier in the first act. Bob has experienced deja vu.

Let's make it a given that the man lifts the elephant only in the third act, and that no such feat was performed in the first act.

When Bob asks those around him whether they have already seen this, they tell him no, the man has only lifted the elephant in the third act.

Here is a log, from an objective viewpoint, of what Bob sees:
In the first act, a man juggles a variety of different objects.
The second act is "the Aristocrats" act.
In the third act, the man lifts the elephant.

Here is a log of what Bob has in his memory per act; new events are in bold:
First act: "I am at a show in a carnival."
Second act: "I am at a show in a carnival. In the first act, a man juggled things."
Third act: "I am at a show in a carnival. In the first act, a man juggled things. In the second act, there was a family who came on stage [...] and while they were still soaked with it and naked, they yelled, 'the Aristocrats!'"
End of third act: "I am at a show in a carnival. In the first act, a man juggled things, and then another man came on and lifted an elephant. In the second act, there was a family who came on stage [...] and while they were still soaked with it and naked, they yelled, 'the Aristocrats!' In the third act, that same man lifted that same elephant again.."

If Bob had left sometime before the third act, he never would have had deja vu, and he would not be able to describe the person or the elephant, and he could not say that there was an act involving a man lifting an elephant. However, because he leaves after the third act, he can remember the man lifting the elephant in the first act.

What happened?
Human memory is extremely malleable, and because of this is very unreliable. Every time you remember a memory, you remember it differently (whether the changes are slight or extreme does not matter; keep in mind that future changes can correct past changes). Bob's mind, upon seeing the man lift the elephant, for whatever reason associated it with the first act, and so, looking for a place to put the memory of the elephant, it copies it both into the third act and the first act.

Looking at it as sequential processing (which isn't entirely correct, as the brain uses parallel processing):
:first act viewed
:looking for place to put first act events
:place found for first act events - 7:00 pm
:second act viewed
:looking for place to put second act events
:place found for second act events - 7:30 pm
:woman left of me is asking me what happened in the first act while she was away
:woman was away at 7:10 pm
:memory retrieved from 7:10 pm - man juggled things
:concept sent to Wernicke's area for translation into speech, relayed to motor cortex to relay message to woman
:woman shows recognition, situation has been resolved; returning to task of lower importance - watching show
:third act viewed
:looking for place to put third act events
:place found for third act events - 8:00 pm
:place found for third act events - 7:20 pm
:duplicate memory detected - 7:20 pm and 8:00 pm; bringing this to the attention of prefrontal cortex

...

:wife is asking what happened in first act
:first act determined to be the period between 7:00 pm and 7:30 pm
:events stored in time range 7:00 pm and 7:30 pm are - man juggling; man lifting elephant
:wife is asking what happened in third act
:first act determined to be the period between 8:00 pm and 8:30 pm
:events stored in time range 8:00 pm and 8:30 pm are - man lifting elephant

...and so on.
