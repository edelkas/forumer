hey everyone,

N is in the "gameshadow" awards, in the Best Game category; this is pretty flattering since all of the other games we're up against are awesome! You can check out the competition here: http://www.gameshadow.com/awards/bestgame.asp?category=1

More importantly, you can vote for your favorite game (hopefully N ) here:
http://www.gameshadow.com/awards/feedback.asp


As for news from the front:

Sorry for the lack of forum participation on our part recently -- take it as a sign that we're getting things done instead of surfing the web all day!

The bad news is, September and October were/are non-Robotology months; mainly we've been working on a webgames contract that will pay for Robotology development to completion (yay!), but there have also been some other things such as having to get an application done for a grant (this sounds easy, but our final application was 80 pages!!) and lots of talking with Microsoft -- we've been officially greenlit/approved.. after about a year of paperwork!

The good news is, come the end of October we won't have anything to do except work on Robotology!

We don't have a solid idea of when it will be done; there's still too much to do for us to be able to estimate how long things will take. However, our friend Heather has been enlisted as the official Metanet manager/scheduler person (she's mostly worked with small multimedia companies, doing project-managing and other organize-y things) to help us stay focused and organized.


Thanks yet again for all of your support; we're sorry about the delay, however this should be the last break from "real" (read: Robotology) work we have to take.

Raigan and Mare
