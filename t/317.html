The left side of 29-3 is pretty easy on the way down, but I haven't been able to figure out a good way to get back up. Getting that gold stash is easy, though.

On the way down, just jump over the turrets to the left, fall down the wall and wall-hop all the way to the right when you get near the bottom. Fall down that wall and when you get to the bottom, run towards the section with the exit switch. This works basically 100% of the time for me.

#23757 8 2004-06-16T19:46:19Z Guest
hey,
when we were testing 29-3 we found 3 different ways to get up through the rockets "most of the time"; one way is to get the right-most rocket behind you, and then just jump up the right-hand wall. one is to use the bottom jumppad and leave up the left side, and the other is to go up the ramp in the middle (running from lower-right to upper-left)

HOWEVER

just running along those paths _will_ result in you being killed randomly. what you need to do in all cases is to get most of the rockets to shoot+follow you, so you can control/position them in your favour before you start the run. this is the key step -- you need the rocket of the launcher which is directly in your path to already be chasing you (and hopefully behind you/not in your way). that way that launcher won't just shoot straight at you, and by the time the rocket hits something and a new one is launcheed, you'll be safely past the launcher.
