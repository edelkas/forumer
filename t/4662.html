#95908 6 2005-08-31T15:00:36Z Raigan (guest)
it's possible to stretch the swf (as that website did), however we disabled this because of how awful it looks. unless you stretch by a multiple of 2x, there are cracks between the tiles, the text is fuzzy, etc... just terrible looking. god.. that's the worst thing about that web version, the people who put it up just don't care how it looks! it's horrible.

since N is 800x600, the only viable scaled version would have to be 1600x1200, which i don't think many people have yet

however, the other alternative is to change the resolution of your monitor; sadly we can't yet get N to do this for you, but with a batch file and a utility such as multires http://www.entechtaiwan.net/util/multires.shtm you can have your monitor automatically switch to 800x600 when you play N.

raigan
