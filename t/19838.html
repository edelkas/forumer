- 1 cheap spring airsoft gun*
- Water
- Red food coloring
- Clear tape

STEPS:

2. Load BBs into magazine.
3. Put magazine into gun.
4. Cock gun. Gun should be ready to fire.
5. Now, fill the barrel half way with water using a funnel.
6. Using same funnel, add a good amount of RED food coloring.
7. Place a piece of clear plastic tape over end of barrel.
8. Find a friend.
9. Shoot them.
10. Watch and laugh.

- Make sure your friend isn't bigger than you.
- I take no responsibility for any outcomes of this prank. This is only for comedic purposes. You, by implied consent, take all responsibility for anything that happens. Even. Unless it's cool. Then it's all me.

* Gun should be cheap, preferably plastic. Any expensive gun won't like water in it. And a spring gun in necessary because there isn't anything in it that can't handle some water. Now, the barrel is sealed by the loaded BB, but it's better to be safe than sorry. Also, remove orange tip for prank.
