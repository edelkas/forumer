It has been done before, and it rocked, but then it got messy and stopped many, many years ago

i think we should give it another go,

basically, the epic is a series of levels where they lead on from one to the other, creating a story (even if one somewhat similar to the hitchhikers guide to the galaxy) as N is led from area to area by anyone who wants to add to the adventure!

The rules are:

ninja MUST start in the far right column (first map is exception)
the exit MUST be in the far left column
the two rightermost columns MUST be identical to the two leftermost columns in the previous map
no indoor, or underground maps are allowed, these are based on outdoor landscapes
the map must have the tag "epic." (no speech marks)
no author can make two areas in a row

try to keep the levels from getting too difficult and chaotic. but difficult maps may still be included, if theyre deemed worthy ^^
