#377011 6 2007-11-05T01:03:44Z Kablizzy
I always make my maps how I envision them, and then just straight-up, across-the-board, make them easier. No matter what map, I scale the difficulty back, and my maps come out damn near perfect, difficulty-wise.

Whatever difficulty you make a map to be, make it easier. I used to think that there was this "perfect" difficulty for a map - There were X amount of enemies for an area this size or that size, and it was always too hard. As a general rule, NUMA maps are all too difficult to be as much fun as they should be - Thus, always make your maps easier than they are.
