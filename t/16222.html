Welcome to the Deadly Duo Extravaganza!

The contest scheme is that all interested forumers will sign up via this thread, and they will chose to either be in the mapmaking or highscoring pool. From there, i will *randomly* select one mapmaker and one highscorer to create a team.

When the first round begins, the mapmaker will have a week(GMT) to create a map ideal for highscoring, and it would be judged accordingly to that fashion.. From there, the maps will be judged by 3 people (McP3000, and two others i have yet to select). Next, a map will be selected by us to be used as this week's highscoring map. It must be ideal for highscoring, and not just fun (we promise it will be both though).

Also, here's the final catch, there will be 3-5 rounds of competition (depends on success, participation...laziness). And each week we will have a highscoring theme. Yes, it may sound strange, but i've already have gotten some nice ideas cooking up.
Also, people will receive points accordingly out of 10 on the maps and highscores to combine for 20, and after the 3-5 rounds we will have our winners.

Lastly, if you cannot participate, then pm/post in this thread and say that you would like to judge. I will need one *good* highscorer as a judge and one *good* mapmaker as a judge. I /will/ be selective so no-one should feel bad if i discard them as a judge. Once again i encourage everyone to enter the contest, but if you would rather judge instead, then just pm me or post...

Well, i thought of that too, but there's really four aspects to it. First off, it's different and fun to be paired up with someone randomly and introducing a team factor into the tournament. This whole teamwork and cooperation idea is something this community has really not done yet, successfully, and i feel its something we need. Next, is the fact you can...conspire of sorts...to have a highscorer play the map before hand to help playtest it, and possibly get an upper hand on the level...if its selected. Third, participation. I want many different types of people to become interested in this. Usually there's three types of contests: "good mapmaker's" contests, "n00b mapmaker's" contests, and "highscorer's" contests. By molding them together, not only is there a "purpose" to making a highscorer's ideal level, but its gratifying to see your own map demo'd by others in the community. I guess the two parts of the contest could be held separately, but indivualism isnt what i wanted to get at when creating this contest. Lastly, I WANT TO.
