#266753 2 2006-12-08T23:59:25Z Twistkill
First and foremost, welcome. By the way, it's just called N. Not N-game.

Boss mode is actually quite useful:

1. Whenever you're playtesting a map in Ned, and the rocket, laser, or N sliding down a slope sound effects starts, if you go back to the main menu, the sounds loop infinitely. Activating Boss mode stops the looping sounds.

2. N, by default, takes up lots of CPU power. Activating boss mode cuts down the amount of CPU power required to run N, so you can go off to do other things and still not lose your progress.

3. The reason why it's called Boss mode: (If you work with computers) Whenever your boss comes by, you can quickly hit the Boss mode button to show that blank screen so he won't suspect much. Also useful if you're slacking off when you're supposed to be doing your homework and your parents stroll by.

So, there ya go.
