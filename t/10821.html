#194546 2 2006-06-25T21:53:35Z Qix
This level is not that hard. All you have to do is trigger the thwumps and then wall jump on the side of one up to the top. Get the exit switch and then trigger the thwumps on your way back, so they don't hit you when you cross the pit.
#196275 3 2006-06-29T13:21:33Z scythe33
A trick that can help: Do not trigger the thwump on the right. Just walljump up the left one all the way to the ceiling, then jump across so it goes down. That way, when you're trying to get back to the door, the right thwump is well below you and is out of your way.
