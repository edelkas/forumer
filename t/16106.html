How to make a KRA

Try to make the race not overlap too much, otherwise there will be a huge empty spot without a tileset, such as in The Tax Man Naked. The reason this map was top rated is because it was the first good KRA and also the flow is stupendous. It could have been better there were more tiles, but that made the race quite longer

Step 1

I recommend you start from the bottom, but you can start anywhere on the map.

Ok, you start from scratch. Before you do anything, place N and the rocket on the page, preferably close together*. First, just make a *flowy* tileset. Don't worry about looks right now. Worry about flow. Jumps are very important. Try to make N go everywhere, and make a long tileset if possible.

*For an excellent KRA, place the N and rocket far apart, but much skill is needed to make a great race

TIP:Try to make the tileset not overlap too much, otherwise, there may be a huge empty spot in the middle.



Step 2

This is when we get serious. Make it so that the rocket will stay alive during the entire thing. Try to make the rocket hug the curves of the tileset. The tileset should be forming.

TIP: Make a demo of your completion. If you aren't a good N-player, ask somebody to demo it for you. Keep that demo everytime you play.



Step 3

By this time, you should be making narrow tunnels for the rocket to travel through. Don't make huge gaps for the rocket to go through. The tunnel where N plays should be wide, but not too wide. Don't make it so that N will have to squeeze through a tube. That is a n00b race. Don't do that. The tileset should be done. Make the tileset look neat and beautiful. Looks like it would make a great playable tileset, eh?



Step 4

This step is the most fun. In this step, you get to add mines, gold, enemies, ect. Please no lag. Nobody ever likes lag. Don't overload it with mines or gold. Stick with the same gold pattern throughout. Don't put random gold everywhere. Or too much of it. Don't put too much of mines either. You should do the enemies last. In this, I added drones for a few lose calls, but not too many, because that would create lag. Use the same demo to playtest your enemies/mines/gold. Make the gold easy to get along the way.
