#20330 1 2008-05-25T21:03:44Z Mardok
I made an account just to express my anger about this level.

Take a look at Episode 67 Level 4.

This is the perfect example of how to piss people off. I'm talking about the last half with all the bombs, the gauss turret, and the floorchaser.

First of all, it takes forever to get to that last part, so you don't have as much practice and are not prepared to quickly correct any blunders you might encounter.

Secondly, the slant in the hill prevents you from accurately jumping to any location that isn't infested with mines.

Most of the levels are challenging, but this one is challenging because it's unfair. It requires an enourmous amount of patience that was unseen up until this point. Your accuracy has to be even more precise when attempting to make a jump, and what's worse is if you do die, you have to wait another minute and a half at least in order to get back to exactly where you left off.

It's too annoying to go through all the stuff that was laid out before the last part. The previous area's are not even challenging, they're time consuming, annoying, and tedious.

I spent at least two hours on this part and have not beat it.

Don't make any more level's like this. If you want to make a level as challenging as this one, at least put it at the beginning so you can get more practice and won't be quite as annoying. There's a very fine line between challenging and unfairly tedious.

#439639 8 2008-05-27T10:08:32Z astro
It might just be an indication of how awesome I am at the game, but it took me five minutes to beat that level twice. The only two difficult bits are the jump (between the mines from the diagonal slope with the thwumps) onto the little platform and jumping from the other diagonal slope onto the horizontal with the floorchaser.

With the first bit, don't try to get really close to the gap between the mines and jump vertical. That's tricky. Jump diagonally from behind the gap--behind the mine/gold on the right hand side, even. You could also try jumping off the thwump. For the second jump, make a little jump to get some momentum before going for the horizontal platform.
