#7393 1 2006-03-12T09:08:39Z Tsukatu
Who here has seen that new release, UltraViolet?
*raises hand*

So whadya think?
Good action scenes, great soundtrack, excellent scenery... lack of continuity, poor acting (except for Milla Jovovich, who I am very surprised participated in this), incoherent script, and god-awful blood effects (the ones that existed, anyway).
Am I right, or am I right?

I love seeing Milla Jovovich in Resident Evil movies 'n junk, but this was far beneath her. It's almost depressing to see her in something this low of quality.

Plot-wise, the movie was acceptable through the first two-thirds, but after that everything went to Hell. There were a scattered moments that made me practically leap out of my chair (I was restrained by friends) and shout "What!? Where the fuck did that come from?" It was that same feeling when you fall asleep and then snap awake later on in a movie, but didn't realize you fell asleep, to see that in the movie something completely different and irrelevant is going on.

You remember when Violet uses the hologram not to distract whatsisface's security, but instead to test it, sees it's hopeless, and the next fucking thing she's on the playground with the kid? Why... why does this work? What makes the director think it works? "Oh fuck, can't raid the corporation, might as well dick around in the park before you die. Yeah, I'm pretty much giving up on you, kid." How the fuck did she even get to the park when security apparently owns the city and appears on every street corner, recognizing Violet immediately?

And what's with the bullshit lead-in to have a flaming sword fight, which in and of itself was fucking ridiculous. The director wanted it so badly that he sacrificed continuity. I mean, she's a hemophage, so she can see in the dark (as was demonstrated by her old friends when the villain somehow appears within their organization), so the villain can't exactly use that against her as he had intended. It is revealed later that the villain himself has actually been a hemophage for a long time and so can see in the dark, which doesn't explain how he went so long without being detected (as that what his corporation focuses on), why he wasn't dead when he touches the boy (whom he says will kill hemophages on contact), and why he gets so pissed about Violet getting hemophage blood on him. (Not to mention that pathetic sequence that followed - "You got hemophage blood on me." "Yeah, I did." (pause; takes off gloves) "It's on!" (pause about half a minute long) "Yeah - it is." (commence awkward fight sequence))

They had to have improv'd half of the fuckin' movie. The had to have. No script that shitty makes it to the big screen.

Oh, and can we talk about the complete lack of blood? Violet totally destroys dozens of people with bullets and assorted bladed objects, at some points even removing heads and torsos. But have you noticed that the final moments of the fight sequences, when they're showing everybody dead and Violet doing some pose in the center, that there is no blood anywhere? It couldn't be hidden anywhere - in one of the final battles, she's against guys in white radioactive suits in a steel bluish white room, so blood would stand out, and that's the scene where she cut most of the people up with a katana.
The only blood I can actually remember seeing is the blood in the tanks that the hemophage soldiers are trying to get in the very beginning of the movie (speaking of which, Violet appears to have absolutely no dependency on blood; only an ambiguous getting-sick scene in the very beginning of the movie wherein she cures herself by injecting herself with some mystery non-red-colored fluid) and the scene after the above-described fight against people in white radioactive suits. She grabs a katana by the blade several times, and so what they did to show her bloody hands was slash up her gloves and then color her palms red with a sharpie. There was no actual liquid involved. Oh wait, fine, there was that scene directly afterwards that includes Violet's ass for a glorious minute and a half, and while everyone's eyes are diverted to her latex-covered ass she squeezes a ketchup packet. No joke - she squeezes her hand, and a very thick viscous fluid much like ketchup oozes and drips out.

I like how every fight scene involved Violet calmly walking into the center of a bunch of armed men and then kicking all of their asses, posing at the end. This happens, what, six or seven times? I have to say, the scene where she makes the Chinese guys shoot themselves is pretty badass, but I do have serious problems with plot-based things before and after that segment (Vampirism comes up nowhere else but on that rooftop; degrees of vampirism do not come up anywhere; the Chinese guys on the rooftop are never explained; the base of operations for the hemophages is never explained, such as why it exists and why it hasn't been blown to smithereens yet; why didn't the kid get shot; why is the kid suicidal, and if he isn't suicidal he'd have to be a goddamned retard, which he proves wrong by acting and speaking intelligently). Let's also keep in mind that in just about every scene with guns in which Violet kills everybody, no matter the distance between Violet and her opponents, security never fires at her, instead insisting on charging right up to her face, gun likely on safety.

I could keep on going with other total stupidity, such as "Number of guns detected - many!" (Who the fuck would build that kind of sensor? "Durr, if someone comes in with two hundred fucking guns, clearly the main desk will see some humor in this situation!"), the nonsensical Christ/Nazi symbolism, the noseplugs, my God, the noseplugs, the villain turning around as though he was suddenly stricken with amnesia after seeing his entire bodyguard team be slaughtered by hemophages then choosing to explore a side room and conveniently finding some coffee, the demonstration by the villain that even though the hemophages can move fast enough to dodge point-blank bullets that he can pick up his gun and shoot three of them in the head before they move, Violet's admitting that she can't take on 300 soldiers and subsequent accomplishment of this very feat with uninhibited ease, the fucking gravity device on her belt, the nature and questionable intelligence of Garth, Violet's numerous ridiculously easy escapes, Violet knocking other hemophages out by pulling out their fucking hair, and Violet's sudden crippling depression over her condition.

There are plenty more that I'm not thinking of. There's just sooooo much wrong with that movie. So goddamned much.

I highly recommend you don't see it, or at least see it out of morbid curiosity.
Bring a notepad and see how many retarded things you can count. I bet you you won't be able to scribble fast enough.

I will say, though, that it deserves to be in a movie theater, even if it does belong at the bottom of the list of movie theater movies in terms of quality.
