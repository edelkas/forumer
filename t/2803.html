#60828 9 2005-04-20T02:41:35Z great_sea
I mostly agree.

Another good reason for deleting maps:

If everybody kept every single one of their maps they had ever submitted, NUMA would grow exponentially, and the good maps people make would be burried quickly in the rubble of rest (actually this is already happened to a degree, it seems). True, NUMA is already huge, but "cleaning up" ones maps makes it MUCH easier for people to find good, fun maps without having to sort through all the trash.

And that's what it's all about, right? Allowing people to easily find something they can have fun on. In the end it is not about ratings, it is about having an enjoyable experience.

Of course, there is no reason to go on a deleting rampage, but it is good to tidy things up once in a while- in my opinion.

But still, even if one of my maps has a low rating, like a 1-3, yet there are mostly (or even a couple) positive comments; I would keep it, because the people that cared enough to actually say something enjoyed it. (Which leads to another topic - how annoying it is when people give low ratings with no comments ).
