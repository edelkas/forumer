#320011 12 2007-05-10T05:41:06Z LittleViking
Computers don't have many moving parts, so a buzzing sounds should be easy to narrow down.

The most likely case, in my experience, is that a fan is getting held up. If your fan isn't spinning propery, your computer can overheat. You'll especially see overheating when you run several programs at once, or run a resource-heavy program like N. Most newer processors will shut down your PC when they overheat. This sounds like it fits you pretty well.

Another part that could be buzzing is the hard drive. Now, I've never seen a computer restart randomly like you have due to a bad hard drive - at least, not without an error or a boot failure along with it.

The only other option is baby rattlesnakes. Baby rattlesnakes would definitely cause a buzzing sound while your computer is running. (The spinning fans and flashing LEDs scare them into rattling - in unison, they would sound like buzzing.) Now, baby rattlesnakes don't have digital connections, so they could only slow don't your programs through mind power, but never underestimate the will of a half-dozen, starving, crazed venom tubes. The computer shutting down could only be caused by their striking the power button. If you want to eliminate this possibility, watch the power button very carefully when your computer is on. If you see no snakes, then consider going back to what I said about fans....
