I have talked with Arachnid about this and he has a plan like this in the works.

From what he told me it will be known as a 'token-bucket' system. What this means is that for every map a person posts a 'token' is taken away from their stock of tokens. At the most a person can accrue 3 token. You earn tokens back through time, so 1 token may be received every hour, or every 3 hours, or however long Arachnid would like (he was saying one hour but I think that was just an example).

It was suggested that this system could also be set manually for certain users who are abusing their privileges on NUMA, so by setting their token maximum to 0 they are essentially banned from submitting maps until a certain date. This would work much better than banning someone because they could still contribute to the NUMA community through constructive comments and ratings. If these people who have been suspended from submitting maps still continue to be arrogant assholes then I assume further action would be taken.

Anyway, that's what I know and yes, it is on the way. I just hope sooner rather than later, but Arachnid has been extremely busy lately and I wouldn't expect anything like this by next week. Until then just keep informing the admins of spammers and they'll be dealt with.
