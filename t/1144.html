Ok. As I was playing through the story mode, I got to a level where my laggy jumping made it almost impossible. When I say laggy jumping, I mean, when I jump in the air, the game would stop for less than a second, and then continue. But in that time, N would move a LOT.

A cilantro later, I was listening to iTunes, and I decided to play N again. I know normally, you would suggest that CLOSING all the other programs will help, I completely ignored this suggestion . I played N, and it worked flawlessly. I was stumped at the time. But I decided to close all the running programs, and see if it was still fine. As soon as I closed iTunes, LAG!!!. So now, whenever I want to play N, I just open iTunes, play a song, any song, and leave it playing. Now, the only lag I get, is when the song changes ( the song will stop playing ).

If you have this same problem, get iTunes, and try it out. You have to actually PLAY a song. Not just have it running. This may or may not work for everyone, and you may not need iTunes. I might try it with some other music players.

Good luck .
