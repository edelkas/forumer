Alright, some may say I'm wasting space adding another thread for the same argument, but I believe this point deserves it's own space.

Whenever someone tells everyone "don't rate" on a map this pisses me off. You know what I do? I play the map. And I rate it. Honestly. Let me go through a few maps that say "don't rate" in the description and then i'll tell you why I bloody well should rate them however I would like to!

Checkpoints by Nevermore.
This is map is very useful. He asked for no ratings, but in my opinion that makes no sense. I rated it based on how good it was. I thought it was extremely helpful (at the time of course, now the new ned has gridding it's relatively pointless, but still) and as it was helpful to that degree it got a 5. Just because a map sets out to do something different from most maps doesn't mean it'll be rated using the same merit system as others.

The Fall of Anakin by astheoceansblue.
This is a very similar example. Someone believing that because a map is different it will get rated badly. Well, people rate maps on what they set out to do. This is good N art - I gave it a 5. Please don't ask us not to rate something in cases like this. We will rate how good we think it is. There's a reason NUMA has a ratings system!

Tri Table by Maximo.
Here we go, a very different example. Not only that, but it proves that in my opinion not even the great Maximo is exempt. Here he gives the reason that he didn't put enough effort in and so it shouldn't be rated... what a load of utter rubbish! Likewise, when people think or know they've made an awful map and they ask you not to rate it... that's beside the point! If you're going to post something on NUMA put your effort into it, make it worthwhile! If you haven't put in enough effort, put in more! You can't ask people not to rate a map because it's rubbish! That's their choice! You're liable for posting a map that might get a bad rating, it's your fault - either live with the vote, put in more effort or just don't post. As it happens, this is possibly a bad example as it's a pretty good map (unsurprisingly for Maximo) but the point still stands.

Jump N Run by BuzZzty.
Ah, this is the map that started the argument in the first place. Well, as I said in the comments, if it's a bad map either try harder, don't post or just live with the rating you get. I understand Sendy's frustrations when he/she says that asking for votes just gets you more... well, that's because people like me just get pissed off by people saying stuff like that on a site built purposely to rate maps. If you want to share your maps without them being voted, it's possible. Try it.

Non-tower Jump by Pixleman.
Once again, a case of someone asking not to rate because their map is a little different. Voters on NUMA try to vote on the merits of the map in what it was trying to set out to do (well, the majority of us do) so you have to trust us to vote for what is right. If it's a good effect, it will be rated high. A bad effect, rated low. If you think it's a bad effect, don't post it, improve it or live with the rating you get.


On top of this, who cares if you get a low rating? That map will probably not be seen for a long time because of this, but as it's a 'bad map' it wouldn't matter. Alright, your overall rating will suffer - but that's your fault. You get rated on maps you make. And to be honest, I don't care about my overall rating. The only time you should ever ask for people not to rate is if you didn't make it. And if you didn't make it, you probably shouldn't be posting it anyway.

So PLEASE, people, if you post a bad map don't ask us not to vote. It's your fault if it gets a low rating.

So PLEASE, people, if you make a map that's a little different trust us to vote it on it's true merits. We're not stupid you know, well, most of us aren't.

So PLEASE, people, don't care so much about ratings anyway. Live with your lesser appreciated maps. MOVE ON WITH YOUR LIFE...

So, PLEASE, people, if you REALLY don't want it rated, you can still share it with your friends without NUMA - there's a thing called notepad, use it!

Another point: it wasn't long ago that someone posted a map and asked people not to vote so they could still edit it... well, this was because they could post the fastest times in the level description. Don't be stupid: the comments form can be used for that.

Stick a fork in me, I am done.
