#328003 3 2007-06-06T13:46:54Z usaswim
  1. PrtScr(Print Screen) button located at the upper right of your keyboard.
  2. Go to Start>All Programs>Accessories>Paint
  3. Go to Edit>Paste (or Ctrl-V)
  4. Crop down the image (optional)
  5. File>Save
  6. Upload on Tinypic or Photobucket
  7. Copy the image url that is between [img][/img] tags
