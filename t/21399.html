N Idol


First of all - Paul is the walrus. (apparently)
Second of all - This is a contest
Third of all - crappenpats

So basically the idea for this contest is like American Idol (I don't know what they have in other countries. Google it if you don't know.)
Everyone makes a map. You have one week. There will be three judges for this competition, but the "audience votes" and decides who will win.
A poll thread will be posted either here or in Test and Inquires with every contestant and their map, and the contestant with the least number of votes gets kicked off. In the event of a tie for least number of votes, the judges will also have rated the maps out of 10, just basic NUMA rating, and that will decide. The judges votes will be placed on the poll so everyone can see and so they see what "professionals" would rate it.
After X number of weeks, the top two contestants will each make two maps to be rated by the audience. Then all the maps entered will be made into a map pack for everyone to enjoy.

Each week will have a theme, any theme, not very restricting though so everyone can still make their own very different map.
Each week one person will get kicked off, but in case there are more than 8 contestants, (thats 2 months), there will be 2 voted off each week until only 6/5 are left. (odd or even start numbers)
