Using trap doors for propulsion isn't nothing like using launch pads. It is very hard to direct your self. A trap door can move you in many directions depending on your speed and the angle you hit it at. They can even sometimes kill you! My best way to get good at using trap doors for proportion is just to practice and my watch a few of DDAs on NUMA. It's not the easiest thing to do, but be patient and eventually you'll get good at it.
