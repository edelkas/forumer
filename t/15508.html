#13736 1 2007-01-28T01:55:34Z scythe33
Count the number of people who have voted a certain amount by it's square for the averaging.
I.E. a map is 5'd by three people and then sniped, so as far as the average is concerned, it got 5'd by nine people. That way, 3 5s and 1 0 would average to
(9*5 + 1*0)/(9+1) = 4.5
instead of
(3*5 + 1*0)/(3+1) = 3.9
That wouldn't eliminate a person's ability to rate a map what they think it deserves, but it should make a dent in the effectiveness of sniping.
Improvements?

You square the number of people who voted for something:

ie.

3 people vote 4
2 people vote 3.5
1 person votes 0

Now it would be like 9 (3*3) people voted 4, 4 people (2*2) voted 3.5, and 1 person (1*1) voted 0

That's what he means.
