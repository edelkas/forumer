I had trouble with this level too, but once you have a strategy it's easy. Here's how I do it:

Build up momentum jumping up each wall. Jump from crevice to crevice, allowing the rockets to hit the wall each time before continuing. If you have enough momentum, you can skip a crevice. Once you near the edge, the rocket to that side may see you as you're jumping. That's where the rocket-dodging skill comes in- stay close to the edge as it nears you, then jump away from the wall momentarily and the rocket will get confused and likely hit the wall when it tries to come back.
