Okay, first off, before I get too harsh this pack is amazing. 9/10 of the maps are just 5 star quality and very unique. Just overall this is a very well made pack with tons of quality in it. I really think you guys did some good stuff here.

That said, i'm going to talk about what I didn't like about the pack, because criticism is more helpful than appreciation if you want to get better.

First off, I didn't like the x-4 difficulty choices. x-0 through x-3 would be easy, some with no challenge at all and then x-4 I would spend 15 mintues on just figuring out what to do. You did that early in the pack and some later, I didn't enjoy that, it didn't fit. I found 9-3 easier than 0-4, which isn't good imo. The same can be said for a lot of your x-4's.

Secondly, your enemy combos in your hard maps were what I would judge as being too hard. Chain guns + about anything just aren't good and you did that a few times. Also you guys used rockets far too much throughout the pack. I love rockets, but some times you guys pushed it. Especially when you paired them with tight spaces, drones, thwumps, gauss. It became overwhellming at times since rockets are so relentless. And when they were in the open they were so hard to get rid of, then another enemy would come and you were screwed, I didn't find that fun.

Now for the 2 maps I can't fathom why they made the pack. 7-4 I'm a God and 8-4 Cue "rocky" soundtrack. 7-4 was long, awkward, tedious, the rocket should have been switched for a gauss for such a long map. This was the last map I beat in the pack in quiet honestly I ruined it for me. I don't remember the good maps anymore, I just remember hating this map. I got to the end, 3 times in a row just to fall into that pit before the exit door and die. That's not fun after a long map. It's just annoying and makes maps unenjoyable to beat. After I beat it I was still frustrated at it, it wasn't enjoyable for me at all. Too long, too hard, too many challenges and tricky spots.

8-4, that left most switch is just pointless. Why does that jump have to be so pixel perfect? There are infinite otherways to make that spot harder without requiring a jump that precise. Sure the map was short, but that jump was just absurd. Sure I can do it now, but it took me 30 minutes or so of just trying that jump over and over again to get it, not my idea of a good time. The rest of the map was bland as well, nothing really fun or interesting about it, it was just a hoop to jump through to beat the map after you accomplished the first jump for the left switch. Aggrivating at best.

Horrendix appendix (9-1) fell into the same annoyingly tricky catagory. All in all, when you guys stayed away from tricky jumps in awkward spaces you shined. I can't understand why you guys made maps like horrendix when more fast paced maps like Knots, Snags, and Entanglements were ten times as fun, and some of them still just as challenging.

All in all, good pack, but in future endevours I'm going to sugest you guys stay as far away from tricky maps and multi enemy combos encluding chainguns and rockets as possible. Or at least make sure they are breif encounters.
