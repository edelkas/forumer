#218252 2 2006-08-22T01:14:33Z Ngaged
'Tis a simple question.

1. Make sure your N application and Userlevels.txt are in the same directory, like your desktop.

2. When inserting levels into Userlevels, it must be put in a specific format:
$Map Name#Author#Level Type (Action, Puzzle, Race, etc.)#Level data#
Be sure not to forget that # at the end!

Hopefully I've covered this problem for you.
