Favorites files are a convenient way to download a fair number of fairly good maps, and they pre-segment userlevels files to reasonable sizes. If you have collected a fair number of favorites and put a fair amount of effort into collecting them, post a link to your favorites file (at the bottom of your favorites list page in NUMA). Or, if you've played someone's favorite file and thought it was quite good, post that.

http://numa.notdot.net/userlevels?count=1000&favorites=2298

Please post favorite files where less than half of the maps are yours.
