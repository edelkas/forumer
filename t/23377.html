How To Make An Origami Balloon

Please tell me if you find anything confusing or and grammatical errors.

Supplies:
One Sheet of paper
Scissors (Optional)

Quick Tip: Make you folds as good as you can to make the best balloon
Spoiler: Step 1O.K. lets get started whip out your sheet of paper. Before we start folding we need to make this piece of paper into a square. This is where youll need your scissors (If you dont have any than you will have to tear the paper). This is what it should like when youre done.
_____1_____1
Spoiler: Step 2
Step 2 is very simple. We are going to make it into a smaller square. Just fold it in half. Then fold it in half again.

_____1_____


_____2_____
Spoiler: Step 3
This step is a little complicated so I will try to explain to the best of my abilities. Lift of one of the flaps of the square, and open it up. Now fold it down (you should end of with a triangle kind of thing). Now take the triangular flap leaning on the square and fold it over to the other triangle flap.

_____1_____


_____2_____


_____3_____


_____4_____
Spoiler: Step 4Lift up the square and open it up then fold it into a triangle like you just did you should now have a triangle. Now lift one of the flaps and fold it over to the other side of the triangle. You should now have to flaps on each side.

_____1_____


_____2_____


_____3_____

Spoiler: Step 5
Take a flap from the right side of triangle and fold it to the top. Now take a flap from the left side of triangle and fold it to the top. Flip the figure over and repeat this you should end up with a diamond shaped figure.

_____1_____


_____2_____
Spoiler: Step 6
Now fold the the flap from the right side of the diamond to the center of the figure. Now fold the the flap from the left side of the diamond to the center of the figure. Turn the figure over and repeat this.

_____1_____


_____2_____
Spoiler: Step 7 For this step fold the flaps from the top left of the figure down so that they are parallel with the fold you made in Step 6. For this step fold the flaps from the top right of the figure down so that they are parallel with the fold you made in Step 6. Flip the figure over and do it again.

_____1_____
Spoiler: Step 8I will try to get a better picture of this later but you will just have to use this one for now. Take the right flap you made in Step six and open it up. Now take the the right flap you made in Step 7 and shove it into (NOT UNDER) The flap you made in Step 6. Repeat this for the left side

_____1_____
Spoiler: Step 9Flip the figure over repeat Step 8.
Spoiler: Step 10 Now pic up the figure and blow into the end of it. If the figure does not blow up than you are either, blowing into the wrong end or one of the flaps from Steps 8+9 have come out. NOW CONGRATZ YOUR DONE.
