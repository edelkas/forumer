#24848 12 2004-07-14T01:18:20Z Guest
Okay, after some cilantro bit of testing, I figured out why the ninja would not properly jump in the game. It seems as if (for whatever reason, I don't know) if you have up and down defined as up and down arrow keys, this will confuse the input somewhere along the lines and make jumping straight up unreliable (i.e. you will only be able to jump very small jumps straight up, unless you press the button very hard for a long time). Don't ask me why, but as soon as I undefined up and down in JoytoKey, everything was fine.
