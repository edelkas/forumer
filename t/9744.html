#179348 41 2006-05-23T05:00:32Z Calcy
When you go up a slope, and try to get back down, a glitch in N causes the ninja to do (what I call) a spasm, as it tries to slow down its upward momentum and come back down, which wastes time. It takes less time to jump perpendicularly up and come back down, despite it being a longer distance.
