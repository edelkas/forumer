Ok. Scenario 1:
Map gets high rating. User plays map, and believes it is a lower rating in their opinion than the rating it currently is. Say, someone who doesn't like DDAs. A DDA ends up as a 5, and our someone would rate it a 3.5. This someone decides that because they would only lower the rating, they'll just not vote at all.
This is where the voting system breaks down. I don't like jumper maps as much as other maps. This doesn't mean I shouldn't vote on them. The whole point of voting is to find everyone's opinions and average them out. If you exclude people voting it lower, then you're just getting the opinions of people that really like said map. Feel guilty being responsible for a map getting rated down? Don't. It's your opinion, and the voting is there to voice your opinion.

Scenario 2:
I only have only experienced this problem once, but it irritated me nonetheless. I rated a map 4.5 instead of 5 because all _14_ rockets made it lag really badly. Then someone complained about me doing this. They said that it didn't lag on their computer. Therefore, they claimed, I shouldn't have rated it down for lag. This pissed me off. Firstly, how am I supposed to know it doesn't lag on someone elses computer? Secondly, maps should be built to cater the widest range of computers possible without hampering the gameplay/visuals too much. My computer isn't incredibly laggy, but anything in the region of _14_ rockets will definitely lag it, and that's not my fault, or my computers fault. The map is a 4.5 ninja map from my point of view, therefore. When combined with non-laggy computers, this will result in a fair score. This again links to scenario 1.

Basically: rate it on the basis of how you enjoyed it/how it played on your computer, not how anyone else enjoyed it. The rating system is there for you to voice your opinion. Use it.

/edit: also, on another note, I don't think indimidation of people who might like a map less is acceptable either. For example, here where Ben Schultz says "If anyone rates this lower than 4.5..." He might not be saying a lot, but it's seriously gonna put people off rating anything less. This skewers the voting system. Therefore it's bad news. There's nothing wrong with saying anything about snipers, as that's different, but people should feel entitled to rating a map 2, 3.5, 4, whatever. As long as it is a truthful vote based on how they enjoyed the map.

/edit edit: Another thing... if you have a map that you like that gets rated down, don't delete it because it has been rated down. If you think the map is worthy of your collection, keep it there. Ignore the rating, it's irrelevant. If you don't think the map is up to standard, then you delete it.
