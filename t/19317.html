Aclausted: Nice, a good start. Symmetry and thwumps both work well.

Acousted: Great. I especially like the corner gold setup.

Anthill: Cool, but those zap drones are really merciless.

Beacon of Distress: Interesting concept.

Bigheaded: Brilliant. Everythings perfect.

Blackout: The setup is unusual, but I love it.

Boxet: Whoa. It works, but its not one of my favorites.

Bumbed to Business Class: Man, you really have a way of setting up drone paths that psyche me out.

Bungie Gulch: Tough map, but very cool. The mindfield in the middle is a bit frustrating.

Call it a Day: A bit on the long side, but I like the ideas that come into play here.

Common Denominator/Remake: Very flowy and a lot of fun to play. I like Remake better except for the thwumps, which dont really seem to add a lot to the map.

Confussion: I like this, except for the fact that the oneways on the right side are cheatable. I thought it would be tough to do, but I actually got it on my first try. Contrary To: way to easy ibeat it frst try 0/5. Seriously, though, another very nice one.

Crey: Its different and cool.

Debt: In the lower half of your maps, I think, but its nice for a change of pace.

Dont Wake Daddy: I had a map kinda like this in Expanse, but this ones so much cooler.

Down Scale: Cool ideas, but they dont really play like theyre supposed to be part of the same map.

Dronn: Like Metanet on steroids.

#365129 6 2007-09-15T07:18:23Z tktktk
Hostages: Both the style and the gameplay are cool. The optional switches make things interesting, although they might have been a little "too" optional - meaning, getting past the seeker drones was pretty easy even without them.

I Built This House: I love the diversity of the different routes you can take. But again, though, I think the switches are "too" optional, it was quite easy to just get into the exit switch area and get back out again. As a side note, I don't know if this was intentional or not (and it's fine if it was), but the right bounce block is a quarter snap higher than the left one. Just thought I'd mention it in case you were going for symmetry there.

Minutes For Memories: Great stuff. The gauss makes things a little frustrating, but you've got the tiles in there to block its line of sight, so it balances out the difficulty a bit.

Parts: What a cool style. The rocket setup did make things frustrating at times though.

Red Room: Wow. You managed to take the sort of map that would ordinarily feel very generic and make it fresh. The gold placement in the gauss room really spiced up the look of the map.

This Tower was built with Babel: Getting in and out of the exit switch area was kind of frustrating at times. I know moving the normal doors too far would ruin the challenge, but maybe one or two tiles down would do the trick.

Today has Wings: I didn't like the rocket part until I figured out that there's actually a specific way of doing it that's actually quite fun. The different parts of the map fit together a bit strangely, but they're pretty enjoyable overall.

While You Wait: Simply awesome. Love it. The only issue here is the zap drone. It looks like you've allowed an opportunity to get around it while it's still in the tube, but I can't do the map fast enough to get there in time.

In my tests, I've found that the best place for the zap drone at the start is in the same horizontal row, but directly above the leftmost gauss. It would probably work one tile to the right of that as well, but it would be cutting it pretty close. Unfortunately this isn't optimal for the all gold route, but it's the only way I can think of to make it so you can get past the drone before it goes up the chute. If you want to make sure the gold route doesn't come to a bottleneck where you're waiting around for five seconds for the drone to come out, the only thing I can think of is to put some more gold in somewhere so it'll take longer to get everything.
