#332893 10 2007-06-19T03:31:28Z nevermore
NEVER BINGE DIET.
If you are truly losing weight to become more healthy (which, sadly, most people aren't paying attention to these days) then never binge diet. It's extremely unhealthy, and there is no reason to lose weight other than to feel better and more healthy.

Eat more often. Five meals a day with small portions. A small breakfast, a fruit in between as your second meal, and so on. Your metabolism will speed up while the amount you eat will either stay the same or go down. That's an important part. Regular exercise in accordance is also important, but maintaining a healthy metabolism is a great start.

#332970 16 2007-06-19T09:33:42Z Ethrel
Of them all, I would have to say that Nevermore is the closest to what I would say.

Small snacks thorughout the day is actually healthier and less weight-attributing because you eat when you are hungry rather than when you think you should have a meal. You eat what your body says you need rather than sitting down to a large meal and eating as much as you can so you can store it for the time until your next meal. I am 18, weighing in at 140 (not overly light, nor heavy for my body type), and I have always done he snacks throughout the day and a large meal at night to tide me through the sleep process. I have spent up to weeks or months at a time sitting on my ass all day, doing nothing more than going to school and sitting on my ass more, and I never gained weight.

Try making sure that the snacks you eat have some form of starch in them (potatoes, bread, ect), as that will help reduce how much you eat, because it will cause the vitamins and minerals that your body needs to be used better and more efficiently by your body. That will cause you to be hungry less often and thus, providing you eat when you are hungry rather than eating because you have some free time, will cause less food consumption.

Keep up some exercise routine, whether it is jogging, martial arts, or something. You are on the right track. I have a feeling it is what you are eating at snacktime rather than how much you are eating.

Another thing to keep in mind, you may have a body type that does not allow you to lose weight like that. Some people (I actually know a few) can diet, can do whatever they want to their bodies, and they cannot lose weight. The one in particular that jumps to mind eats basically nothing because her stomach is not large, does exercise, and is all-in-all very healthy. She has no reason why she would be overweight, yet she is because of her body type and genetics. You may be one of those people who simply cannot lose weight.

Try the things that have been mentioned, if they do not work you can always go talk to a specialist of some sort (your doctor should be able to provide some accurate and healthy information, even if it is only where to go and who to talk to). If nothing works, then you may just have that body type and be stuck with it. I'm not saying this to discourage you, only to alert you to the possibility, because the last thing anyone wants or needs is another one who falls to the crash diets or binge diets to lose weight because the healthier methods didn't work (or didn't work FAST ENOUGH). The biggest thing is this: Don't give up until you have tried the healthy diets. That and try to stay off of metabolism pills and things like that. Vitamins and minerals are one thing, but the chemical simulations of things like a metabolism boost or hunger reducers or what have you are just bad, and will harm you in the long run.

Don't lose hope, and best of luck to you! I hope something I have said here helps
