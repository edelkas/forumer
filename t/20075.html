Transcend. (Tentative Title), by Pheidippides

The Idea: This is a level pack with a storyline but not just any storyline. Nope, this is in no way like the little creative blurb you might see in a maps description on NUMA. Were talking a story. That means heroes, villains, plot twists, conflict, everything. My goal here is to create a pack with something enjoyable besides the maps in it. I think its going to be a lot of fun, but I suppose thats ultimately for you to decide.

The Current Story: Story 01 // The Awakening
Spoiler: Stage 01 // Grandeur CityIt certainly had been a while since N had last seen Grandeur City. Still, as he began to glimpse the thriving metropolis on the horizon, he slowed his pace from a run to a casual stroll, taking in the countryside. After spending months atop Mount Doom in the hopes of achieving transcendence, he could identify with the isolation of this locale and wanted to enjoy his last few minutes in it before plunging back into life in the city.

As he walked, he felt a certain lightness in his step he hadnt noticed before, as if through achieving transcendence he had lifted a weight off his entire body. He felt more alert, more focused, and began to wonder if transcendence had physical as well as spiritual implications for the ninjas who took the time to achieve it. Its a shame so few ninjas achieve transcendence anymore. he thought as he approached the gleaming mass of industry ahead. Indeed, it seemed that ninjas simply couldnt be bothered with the difficulties of achieving transcendence anymore, and had given up on it altogether.

Drawing closer to the city line, N began to see drones lazily coasting down the streets, floorguards zipping along on the expressways, and ninjas hurriedly running and jumping to their various destinations, and he remembered how he had missed Grandeur City. His training on Mount Doom had been intense, but it lacked a regularity he had so long depended upon. Once again his daily life would be scheduled and predictable, or so he thought, because all at once the unscheduled and unpredictable had happened.

Chaingun drones slowly glided toward Grandeur City, opening fire and sweeping the streets with their bullets. N felt himself break out of his casual pace into a full sprint for the city, dodging round after round of chaingun fire as he went. Whats happening? N thought as he continued at breakneck speed. Suddenly he remembered, as if he had been there just yesterday, that deep within the city there was a shelter where he and the other city-goers would be safe.

Ive got to get to that shelter and figure out how to stop this. N reasoned. Shaken but determined, N ran as fast as he could for the heart of Grandeur City. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 02 // Underground EscapeAfter a heart-pounding race through the city, N found the shelter he had so frantically sought. As he closed in on it, he could see that he was not the only one who had remembered the sanctuary. A small crowd of ninjas was already gathered inside, and as N entered they greeted him, though with urgent undertones in their voices. Amongst the group was a purple ninja, apparently the leader, who introduced herself as Q. "Hey, blue, ya got a name?" she asked as N pushed his way through the crowd.

"N," he replied.

"Another one? It seems like youre all named N. Anytime somebody names a ninja, they always call him N. Not too many creative parents around these days, are there?"

"Not too many creative writers, more like."

More and more ninjas continued to swarm to the shelter, chaingun drones gliding along in their wake. Noticing that the drones were too close for comfort, Q called for a lockdown, and the shelters steel gate began to close. At seeing the descent of the gate many ninjas nearby quickened their pace, hoping to get to safety before it was too late. One of them, a young green ninja, tripped and fell in the mad dash. To N, it seemed to happen in slow motion, and he was suddenly aware of a flashback taking hold of his senses
***
It was ten years ago, the last time that Grandeur City had been invaded. N was sprinting alongside his little sister, M, trying to reach the same shelter where he now stood comfortably in safety. The steel gate had begun to close, and N felt his mind suddenly swimming in panic. A chaingun drone rounded a corner nearby, and while it wasnt between N and M and the steel safe haven ahead, it had a clear shot at the pair as they ran by. In dodging the hail of bullets, N felt Ms hand lip out of his grasp. Horror-stricken, he turned to watch his little sister trip and stumble to the ground. With the gate just a few feet from closing completely, N had a choice to make save M and forfeit their chance at shelter, or save himself.

The young N chose to save himself and dove beneath the closing gate. From inside the shelter, he heard a chaingun drone preparing to fire
***
N awoke from his trance to notice that no time had passed in the world currently around him. He found that young green ninja in the same mortal peril as he was before, in the same mortal peril as M had been in all those years ago. Though his mistake then could never be undone, N was determined not to make the same mistake this time. Pushing his way through the crowd he lunged into the open, where chaingun drones were sure to be arriving at any moment.

Once outside, the shelters steadily closing steel door seemed to move unnervingly quickly, but N gathered himself and ran as hard as he could to where the green ninja lay, trembling and awaiting the eerie whirr of a chaingun drone loading its next round. In a swift move N scooped up the green ninja and turned around, and suddenly something of a lifeless grey in the distance caught his eye and that fateful whirring noise reached his ear. N now ran faster than ever towards his ever-shrinking window of opportunity at the shelters door and slid underneath the mass of steel just in time, with the green ninja in tow. He heard another round of chaingun fire pounding against the other side of the door, a mere second or two from hitting its mark.

Inside the shelter, N was cheered by his fellow ninjas for his act of heroism. The green ninja, whose name was G, was in awe at his blue savior. Q quickly ended the congratulations by calling N over to her, and G followed in his wake.

"Impressive," Q said as she patted N on the back, "very impressive. Your little buddy here is lucky that you were around. Not a single other ninja in this shelter wouldve done what you just did, N. That makes me think Im pretty lucky to have you around here, too. You see, I could really use your help in settling the score with Doctor I."

"Dr. Who?" N questioned.

"Dr. I. Hes the one who was behind the attacks ten years ago, and Im sure he was behind the attacks today. That means hes out of hiding and hes got a plan, which means bad news. Id go after him again, but I lost my entire team in trying to chase him away the last time. Thats where you come in. I need skilled ninjas like you to help me."

"I just achieved transcendence, so I think Ive got the skill youre looking for. After Mother Thwumping Impossible, taking down this Dr. I should be a cakewalk," N replied confidently.

"Good. That makes two of us who have achieved transcendence," Q reasoned. "With a few more able-bodied ninjas, well be ready for Dr. I."

A small voice chimed in, "Hey! I may not be transcended, but I want in on this!" It was G. "Its the least I could do for N to lend you guys a hand. Please!

"You?" Q said skeptically. "Havent you had enough danger for a while, kid?"

"Aw, cmon! Ill be alright. Who knows? You might be thankful in the end," G claimed desperately.

"To be honest, with your nagging Im already not so sure Im thankful N saved you," Q joked. "Actually, it looks like youre a good fit for this group. To be volunteering for something like this just to repay N shows both guts and loyalty. Welcome aboard, G."

"Thank you, Q!" said a delighted G. "I wont let you down!"

Out of the crowd came a red ninja, who strode up to Q and said, "From what I just heard, youre looking for transcended ninjas to help you stop these attacks. Id like to get even with those drones myself. T, a transcended ninja, at your service."

Where were you when G was in danger out there? Q thought. To Ts offer, she replied, "Sure thing, T. The blue one here is N, and the little guy is G. Welcome."

"Okay, now there are four of us," N remarked. "That should be enough. How do we go about stopping Dr. I, now?" He asked.

"By following me," said Q, and she ducked into another room in the shelter. Moving a desk in the middle of the room, she revealed a hole in the floor with a ladder descending into it. She clambered down the hole, and the others followed. Upon reaching the bottom, the group stepped off into what looked like an underground aqueduct. Just then, the ground shook.

"Whats happening?" G asked, worried.

"That was probably from the attacks going on above ground," answered Q. "Now quit worrying and follow me; were getting out of this city." [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 03 // Fiery PlainsThe four ninjas were hurriedly scrambling through the aqueduct when the earth around them gave another quake. This tremor was much stronger, much closer to the group, than the first had been. Before anyone could say a word to question or explain it N, Q, G, and T felt their escape route flying upward from underground. The piece of aqueduct they had been running in had been blown into the air by a rockets blast, and out of the ends of the pipe the four could see debris all around them and a rocky plain below.

"Were not even in Grandeur City anymore!" Q shouted above the roar of the explosion. "What could possess Dr. Is army to attack a random location so far away from their intended target?"

"Who knows?" N yelled back. "All I know is that we cant stay here, or by the time we find out itll be too late. Look around. We can jump down using all that falling debris. Follow my lead."

With that, N led the escapees out into the midst of the blast and began to descend towards the jagged landscape below. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 04 // Ancient ChasmNearing the ground, N began to think about what would happen when he, Q, G, and T finally touched down in the middle of the rocky plain. There were rockets flying all around, and few places to hide. There were a few shallow craters here and there, but those would only provide very temporary shelter, if any at all, for the group. As he bounded around on the falling debris, he noticed the ancient Ruins of Nopolis.

They were the remains of an ancient ninja city, and had been preserved but left well alone. No one wanted to enter the Ruins, especially after hearing the widespread ghost stories of ancient curses and clever traps. The place had baffled historians for years. Needless to say, N wasnt so enthused about the idea of going there himself. Thankfully, there was a large chance he wouldnt have to hide there if the rocket launchers saw him enter, theyd turn the ancient buildings into dust without a seconds hesitation.

That still left N with the problem of finding somewhere where he and the other three could hide. He was hesitating on his descent now, peering around for shelter. Theyd hit the ground soon, and theyd have to hit it running. G, who for the only ninja in the group who wasnt transcended was doing a fantastic job of keeping pace with N, noticed the leaders uneasiness.

"Do we have a plan, N?" he asked.

"Nope, not a thing," N replied, frustrated.

Looking a ways off from the Ruins, G spotted a small opening in the ground. It was well protected by the surrounding rocks and looked pretty deep, deep enough at least to shelter the four ninjas from rocket fire until the army gave up the attack and left the plain for the city. As they got closer to the ground, G still couldnt see any bottom to the gap in the rock. Could it be a cave? He wondered. Then he called aloud, "Hey, N! See that hole over there?" He gestured to the opening.

"Yeah, I see it. Its probably just another crater," N answered. "We wont be able to stay there too long. Otherwise the rockets could call for help and theyd have us cornered. Keep looking for a hiding place, G."

"Keep looking? I think this deserves a second look, N. It actually looks like a cave."

After a moment of exasperation, N gave in to Gs request and took another look at the gap in the rocks. G was right it was a cave. That was the ideal place to hide. Even if the rocket launchers signaled for other drones to look for them inside the cave, there was probably a huge network of caves beneath the rocky ground. Finding the group would be impossible in such a vast place. His mind made up, N leapt to the ground and ran for the entrance to the cave. G, Q, and T followed.

Suddenly a rocket hit the ground just in front N, and the smoke and dust from the blast obscured his view of the mouth of the cave. The four came to a stop amidst the flurry of rocket fire, trying to glimpse their intended refuge. Another rocket whizzed past them, and they dodged it just in time. It sailed away into the sky, and the rush of wind it created as it flew by disturbed the smoky haze for just an instant. An instant was all N needed. Regaining sight of the cave he motioned to the others to follow him and leapt into its entrance, leaving the chaos of the plain behind him.

All four ninjas made it safely inside, and upon looking deeper into their sanctuary saw that it wasnt as safe a haven as they had hoped it would be. Jagged rocks jutted from the floor and ceiling of the cavern, impeding their progress into its depths. If they were to truly be safe, they needed to get farther inside, but N faltered. "I dont know how we can get through here, guys," he said. "This looks too dangerous to cross."

"I think I can help!" G interjected. "This is my kind of thing lots of tricks, like cornerkicks." He motioned to the tips of the rocks. "I could jump off those pretty easily. Want me to lead?"

"Well," N began, "I guess we have no choice. Im not much with cornerkicks and other gimmicks, so it looks like youre up, kid."

"I agree. Go ahead, G, get us started," Q added.

"Alright. Here goes nothing," G said, and with that he jumped out into the middle of the caves jagged floor below and began his trek across the dangerous and ancient chasm. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 05 // Vaulted ChambersThanks to Gs amazing agility, the group had found a safe route across the deadly chasm in the cave floor. Quickly the four ninjas pressed onward, hoping to disappear from the sensors of the drones they knew would eventually pursue them. The darkness of the cave descended upon them with every step, ushering them into its depths. A voice spoke up, "This may not be such a good idea, guys. In trying to lose the drones, we might lose ourselves. No sense hiding where even we dont know where we are."

"Dont worry, T," Q said. "Youre transcended, for crying out loud! Youve got the ability to handle a little walk in the dark."

"Yeah," N added. "Wherever theres an opening in the cave, light will filter in. The darkness is only temporary, and well be rid of it when we find a way out."

"Maybe sooner than that," G said. "Look over there."

Light was emanating from somewhere in the distance, although G was right that it wasnt a way out. If it were an opening in the cave, the light would be a steady glow from the sunshine above. But the light wasnt steady or from above. It was flickering and dancing about the cave, like fire. Nearing it, N realized that it was, in fact, fire a torch. He rushed ahead of the group, grabbed it, and, wheeling around to face the other three, noticed that something was wrong. "Guys, wheres T?"

"What!?" replied G and Q together, and looking around they too saw that he was nowhere to be found.

Unsettled, N peered around and called out, "Hey, T!" Q and G echoed him, but their cries aroused no response from the lifeless cave walls. Further efforts to call T back to them yielded the same result. T had vanished.

"Looks like there are just three of us now," Q reasoned.

Her eerie message reverberated around the cavern and within the troubled thoughts of her comrades, surrounding them. N felt betrayed by the torch, which instead of dispelling the darkness had increased its weight, created fear rather than destroying it. Q called him back to his senses. "N, we should probably keep going."

"Right," he replied. "Follow me."

Continuing through the cavern, N began to see why the torch had been there. It had appeared out of place at the time, but as he scanned the walls of the cave he noticed writing. It looked like it had been scrawled there ages ago, and although N didnt understand the language, it made sense to him someone must have been down here before. "Guys, look!" He gestured to the wall.

"What do you suppose it says?" G wondered.

"Its not important," Q answered. "This writing means that someone was down here before, and that they must have come down here from somewhere. Look, theres more up ahead! If we follow this, we might find a way out."

"Exactly," N stated.

Following the mysterious cave writings, the group turned a corner and saw a light ahead. This time, it certainly wasnt fire. Steady light streamed from a hole in the ceiling of the cavern. The three reached the entrance and ascended, to Ns horror, into the Ruins of Nopolis. "No!" N cried. "Why here!?"

"Better up here than down there," G pointed out.

"Many would argue you on that, G, but I guess you have a point," N admitted. "This city may be falling down, but its organized. It shouldnt be hard to get out of here. We should split up and search for a way out of the city walls. Ill go this way."

The three went in separate directions and began their search for an exit from the mysterious ruins. Quickly N came upon a large tower, and an idea struck him. If he climbed the ruined buildings, he might be able to go over the walls and escape without having to find a way through them. Hopeful, N ran into the building and began his ascent. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 06 // Sneak DistrictSplit up? Inside the Ruins of Nopolis? What was N thinking? Q thought to herself. After the group disbanded in search of a way out of the Ruins, she had begun to worry about G and now watched the young ninja from a distance, hoping to protect him from whatever dangers might be lurking in the ancient city. G was, after all, in the most danger because he was the least skilled and most reckless of the three ninjas, although N, in Qs opinion, was beginning to challenge him on the latter.

Q and her unsuspecting protg wandered the gloomy city streets, making their way towards the city walls. Their footsteps echoed as they walked. Q noticed that G had paused to look around, and so she did likewise, stopping just out of Gs sight. Immediately, Q realized something horrible the echo of their footsteps had not stopped. Despite the fact that both Q and G were standing still, step after step could be heard reverberating off the Ruins. They were not alone.

Now frantic, Q wheeled around and peered through the haze that hung over the streets, and the footsteps continued, growing louder. "G, over here!" she whispered urgently, and she came out of her hiding place. G looked around, appearing worried, and he spotted Q. He put his hand to his ear as if to ask Q if she heard the echoes, and Q nodded and motioned for him to be quiet.

The two were now both scanning their surroundings, trying to find the source of the echoing footsteps. The sounds pressed on methodically, frighteningly, growing louder with each passing minute. The haze severely limited their sight as they fruitlessly searched. Then Q heard something else amidst the horrible scene a second set of footsteps.

This set of footsteps was louder than the first, and their pace indicated that the culprit was clearly running. Q turned towards G, but she couldnt see him. The haze had become so thick that it appeared nearly solid, but just then a light wind had blown by, lifting the blinding fog. G was no longer where he had been standing, and Q suddenly knew who was behind the second set of footsteps. Whatever was making those noises must have gone after G! Q realized. Ive got to help him! Now in a worried frenzy, she ran off after the sound of Gs footsteps.

As she caught up to the echoes, she noticed that G was not in trouble. A shadowy figure ahead of him, however, was. G had spotted the owner of the footsteps and set off in pursuit. G looked over his shoulder and saw Q behind him. "Q!" He called back. "This guys heading for the walls! He must know a way out of the Ruins!"

Quickening her pace, Q chased after the shadowy figure and its young green pursuer. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 07 // Sandstorm BlockadeQ and G relentlessly hounded the mysterious figure through the streets of the dilapidated city of Nopolis, and soon they found themselves closing in on their mark. Their prey had wandered into a dead end, with high walls looming all around. The chase was over. Curious to unmask the reckless runaway, the two ninjas slowly approached their helpless captive.

At least, they thought he was helpless. Just then, the trapped ninja made a single incredible leap to the top of the sheer walls. Q and G paused for a moment in amazement before resuming their pursuit, walljumping upwards as quickly as possible. Upon reaching the top, the pair breathed a collective sigh of relief, for standing between the runaway ninja and his escape was a familiar face N had joined the hunt.

But again, just when the chase seemed to be over, the ninjas realized that it had merely taken another turn. The strange ninja, peering from the edge of the wall to his captors and back again to the edge on which he stood, refused to be cornered. Without hesitation, he dove from the high city wall to the craggy plain below.

"Not the friendly type, is he?" N quipped as his eyes followed the ninja's fall. "Must've had a really good reason to avoid us like that. I wonder what he was so desperate to keep from us?"

"More like what he is so desperate to keep from us," said G as he looked down towards the ground. "I don't know how, but he survived that fall." He pointed to a figure in the distance, fleeing from the ruins. There was no mistaking him it was their escapee.

"We should keep tailing him," Q suggested. "If he's got that good a reason to keep running, I think we ought to find out what it is."

The others agreed, and together they descended onto the rocky plain, though not with the agility of the bizarre figure they were tailing. As they clambered across the rough landscape, however, they began to close in on the ninja once again, and N and Q picked up the pace as they neared their goal. G was right on their heels, but a sound reached the young ninja's ears that caused him to falter.

Beep.

Before he could utter a warning, G saw the plain erupt into a chaotic whirlwind of rock and dust. The blast was so great in magnitude that N and Q, who had not heard the nearly inaudible trigger, were lifted off their feet and sent sailing backwards. They landed quite some distance away, but G could see them stirring and knew that they would be all right, though a bit shaken up. The problem of the three's elusive prey, however, still remained, and G had a decision to make. He could give up the chase to avoid deserting his friends, or he could go it alone.

G chose to continue the chase, and leapt into the swirling sandstorm. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 08 // Lost GrandeurG emerged from the sandstorm and stared out onto the vast rocky expanse that still lay before him. To his dismay, there was no sign of the ninja he had been pursuing, or even of any other living thing, for what looked like miles. Then, in the corner of his eye, G spotted a flash of color as it moved stealthily between two rocks. Seeing no reason to remain idle, he darted towards the source of the blur, and immediately regretted the decision.

In an instant G was seized by two rather large and strong ninjas who had also hidden amongst the rocks and craters that blanketed the plain. He struggled and squirmed as much as he could, but to no avail the strength of these brutes was unnatural. A voice emerged from the spot where G had seen the blur of movement moments before. "Always the reckless one, arent we?" it said smugly. "It appears the hunter has become the hunted."

The voice was cold, cruel, and eerily familiar. Horrified, G looked up to meet the gaze of his captor as he emerged from the shadows.
***
As the dust settled on the blast-ravaged plain, Q managed to clear her dizzy mind. A short distance away lay N, who was still in a stupor from the force of the explosion. Q gave him a shake to try to snap him out of it, but the blue ninja remained unconscious. "N!" she called, but no one answered except the echo of the lifeless rocks around her. Precious time was slipping away, widening the gap between N and Q and their comrade as he hurried on in pursuit of the mysterious ninja. Refusing to wait any longer, Q hoisted N off the ground and carried him onward.
***
The force of the blast had sent N careening into a boulder, and the world was vanishing in a whirl of color as he stumbled to the ground. Soon darkness overcame him, and he fell unconscious. From amidst the blackness that clouded his mind, N heard someone calling his name, and suddenly the darkness dissipated. N, however, did not awake from his stupor to the plain he knew he was lying on. Instead, he found himself in Grandeur City ten years ago. Standing amidst the chaos of the citys first invasion, N realized who had been calling him.

Looking back, N saw his little sister, M, lying frightened and helpless on the ground. Ahead was the shelter she had tried so desperately to reach, but the door was closing it was too late to rescue her and still reach safety. Nonetheless, N ignored his instinct of self-preservation and dashed out into the fray to redeem his grave mistake of all those years ago. Diving through a hail of chaingun fire, he reached M and lifted her to her feet. Her fearful expression became one of wonder and gratitude, but neither sister nor brother uttered a word. N was in awe at his sisters sweet, almost forgiving glance. With new hope, N and M sprinted off into the ruined city. [Map Link]
Spoiler: Stage 09 // Dizzy ForestN and M darted through the smoke and dust of the ruined city completely undisturbed. It was as if the chaingun drones didnt even see the pair as they fled to the rocky plain on the outskirts of the metropolis. M strangely seemed to know exactly where she was going, and even though N couldnt possibly imagine her destination, he followed her.

Together they crossed the plain and came towards a forest, the mighty trees of which loomed massively over them. They crept into the woods, and N noticed that there wasnt a sign of life anywhere. Confident that they were now safe, M turned to her older brother.

"Thanks for turning back," M said.

"I'm sorry," N replied. "I'm sorry I didnt the first time."

"What matters is that you did this time," she said reassuringly. "I forgive you, N. Theres no time for apologies now, anyway. Dr. Is planning something terrible, and his preparations are almost complete. Hes even captured G! You need to reach his hideout in the middle of this forest before its too late."

Her words echoed in Ns ears, and N began to lose focus. The world was once again disappearing in a blur of color, and in an instant M departed from him.
***
Hopelessly trapped in the ambush, G had had no choice but to surrender. From the plain, his captors had led him across the vast expanse of rocks and craters and into a forest, where after a while they came upon a small modern building hidden amongst the trees. There were drones on patrol.

When they came to the door, a voice prompted the group for identification. The leader stepped forward.

"T," he said coolly. The red ninja turned to his companions, and added, "And a special guest for Dr. I." The grunts laughed and gave G a hard jab in the back, shoving him through the doors.

Once inside, G realized that the building was not nearly as small as it had appeared. He stared down into a large laboratory, the floor of which was several yards below his ground-level vantage point. The whirr of computers and machinery filled the large room with a constant hum.

G saw a row of large tubes along the wall, and to his horror he saw ninjas inside them, suspended in some odd fluid and breathing through tubes. They appeared to be sleeping. A voice called from the floor.

"Well, T, have you done as you were ordered?" it said.

"Better, Doctor," T answered. "In fact, the green runt is here to see you. Hes a strong kid, but hasnt achieved transcendence yet a perfect candidate for testing your latest formula."

"Excellent," the doctor said as he ascended the stairs from the laboratory floor. He was a short, stocky, orange ninja wearing a pair of goggles on his forehead and a greedy smirk on his face. "We dont often find untranscended ninjas around here. Youre right, T, the Artificial Transcendence formula could turn him into a very useful protg."

"And if he would join us, hed be useful in perfecting more powerful formulas as well," T added. At this, G spoke up.

"Why would I join you?" he demanded. T turned to him with a look of mixed anger and exasperation.

"Why?" he replied. "Why? How do you think I pulled off those stunts out there, G? Sheer luck? No, those stunts were the work of Dr. I. Hes created a formula that alters a ninjas genetic code to push him beyond the limits even of transcendence. With this formula, Ive reached Protoscendence, and am twice as powerful as your friends N and Q. Your friends are doomed if they challenge us, and you will be too, G, if you defy me."

G paused. After a moment, he replied, "Then consider me doomed."

"You fool, G!" T shouted angrily. "Transcendence could have been yours! Unlimited power could have been yours! Now youll be lucky if you still have your life when our plans are complete."

"Take the runt to the cell blocks," Dr. I ordered. "Hell pay for his foolishness. Lock him up!"
***
Q reached the edge of the plain, still carrying N. She had reached a large forest, and ducked into the trees to hide herself and N from view. Tired, she set him down and stopped to rest awhile. In the silence, she heard a strange voice.

"Q," it called faintly. To Qs relief, she saw that it was N who had spoken, though he was still very weak.

"N, thank goodness youre awake," Q said. N seemed not to notice.

"Q, w-weve gotta k-keep going," the blue ninja stammered. "Gs in t-trouble. M-middle of the f-forest" His voice trailed off. After pausing for a moment, N stood up. He was still dizzy from the injury to his head, but nonetheless he staggered off into the forest. Unsure what had come over N but unable to let him wander into the forest alone, Q followed.
