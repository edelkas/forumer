Q. On windows 2000/xp, pressing the Shift key (used for jumping) 5 times consecutively, or holding Shift for 8 seconds, triggers an os level function and promts an alert to open.

A. The alert that pops up has something to do with 'Filter keys' -- for now, you'll have to disable filter keys. You can do so by clicking 'Settings' or something to that effect. Sorry about this, we'll have a fix in the next version.
-----



-----
Q. My level has a bug. It's not working properly.

A. This could be any number of things. First of all, look at the level data example in the editor_manual.txt or .doc you'll find in the zip. Your level data should look just like that, with no spaces or newline characters. It should end with a number, not an exclamation mark, caret (^) or comma.

When you're pasting level data into the top textbox, make sure you click outside of the textbox before pressing [L] to load the level -- otherwise the 'L' will be added to the end of the data you just pasted.

Errors in level data can result in disappearing ninjas, lack of collision, and many other weird things.

Q. How do I play my levels in the regular game? Can I package levels in episodes?


A. At this time, you can't play custom levels in the real game. Ned, the editor, is just a beta version, and we weren't able to implement that feature before our deadline. We're working out the bugs now though, and plan on releasing a more interesting version of Ned and a version of N which allows you to play custom levels (among other things).
