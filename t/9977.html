when you finsh making a level with Ned from your program, press the "home" key and you will get a new set of directions in the bottom right of the screen. Now press "~".
From here, andonce yourl evel loads, you will see two boxes on the right. The top box is for the level data, and the bottom one is for demo data. YOu click in the top box and Crtl +A to select it all, and then copy it. Now go to the form on Numa.notdot.net and put the data in the level data box. Fill out the rest of the form and click submit and then accept. Now yuve submitted a map! When you become more experienced, you can mess around with the data in the top box to make your level do cool things like have hte ninja go to a new part of the map without you doing anything.
