NBuddy v0.4
made with Game Maker 7
Last Update: 27/03/08

NBuddy is (or better said will be) something like a desktop buddy. This version is only a pre-beta, that means it's far away from the final release.

Download:
NBuddy v0.4 (2.2 MB) (hosted with box.net)

Please comment.

Screenshots:





Current features:
  • simple menu
  • in-game help
  • 1 level
  • moving left and right
  • jumping
  • wall-jumping (difficult)
  • normal blocks
  • gold
  • mines
  • exit key and door
  • zap drones (which can't zap)
  • [k] key
Features I'm currently working on: (NOT included yet)
  • realistic movement and dieing (hard to make)
  • one-way platforms
  • bounce blocks
  • thwumps
  • more enemys
  • sounds (easy)
Controls: (you can also show these in the game by pressing [F1])

Menu:

[s] start NBuddy

[F1] show help
[Esc] close help

While you're playing:

[left arrow] go left
[right arrow] go right
[space] jump

[m] menu
[r] reset
[k] kill N
[Esc] close NBuddy

[F1] show help
[Esc] close help

Wall Jumping:

You can jump up a wall, too. To do that, you have to jump against a wall and then press the [left/right arrow] key. While you hold it you can press [space] to jump up. When N begins to "perform his move" you may release the [space] key.

NOTE: You can do this with "real" walls and platforms. If you jump up a REAL wall, you have to hold the [left/right arrow] key the WHOLE TIME. While you're holding the key you can press [space] several times to jump up the wall.

Known bugs:
  • You can't jump off a wall.
  • Sometimes you jump exactly "beside the wall" (difficult to explain). In this case you can't move any more. You have to press the [r] key to play NBuddy again.
