First, you ask your GM how long you have before the campaign starts to make your own magical equipment. He, assuming you're gonna be playing a spellcaster, gives you a number. I love it when they're generous. It stops to matter at a certain point, though. You'll see what I mean.
There comes a time in an adventurer's career that he has enough money to buy a ring that lets him use a Wish once per day, with a certain number of charges.
[9 (spell level) * 17 (caster level) * 1800 (command word activated) + 25,000 (XP cost)]
/ [5 (once per day) * 2 (50 total charges)] = 30,040 gp, which a starting level 9 character can afford easily.
So the instant you get the ring, you use it to get 15,000 gp, as one of the uses of Wish lets you do. On the second day, you do the same thing, getting another 15,000 gp. You do that for about two more days, and use your new 60,000 gp along with your money you have left over from being lvl 9 (about 36,000 gp to start off with) to buy a ring that lets you Wish twice per day, still 50 charges and all, which costs you 60,080 (you just make the "/ 5" into a "/ (5/2)", and that gets you double the original amount). So the next step is to go for a 3/day Wish. The cost is 30,040 * 3 = 90,120, which takes you about 3 days with your new 2/day ring. So now you have your 3/day wish, and you need a 4/day item... that takes you about 3 days. Then take your 4/day item and get one that doesn't have a limit on number of times per day, which costs about 138,950 gp. Bah, you can get that in a little more than two days.
Ok, now that you have a 50-charge ring of Wish, ya know what you're gonna do with it? You're gonna use 'em all up, laddy! 50 Wishes for 15,000 gp each gives you about 750,000 gp to play with. Use that to get a ring of Wishing with no restrictions (no charges, no times/day), which costs about 300,400 gp. w00t.
You have about 450,000 gp left over... a little more due to profit in between buying x/day rings of Wish. Use that to buy a second ring, one of use-activated Time Stop, which costs about 9 * 17 * 2000 = 306,000 gp... no biggie.
So now you have an infinite amount of time to give yourself 15,000 gp whenever you want. 15,000 gp times infinity is just about equal to an infinite amount of gold, give or take a few hundred quadrabajilliondy.
So a 9th level character with 13 days to kill gets everything he damn well feels like getting.
