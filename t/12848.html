Hell of Falling Sand

This game is honestly great, It doesn't have a purpose none other than great craftyness with the falling elements. Its really not hard to learn but you can figure it out for yourself, below there are some secrets. There in the color white if you dont want to see them.

Quote: Secrets @ Highlight to See Them

Sand : Sand is one of the basic falling elements, its mass is light, it doesn't really do anything just a basic element that falls. Also it devours the spout solid.

Water : Water is the second falling element, it has the lightest mass and it has a lot of potential. Such as it will help the plant solid grow or help the snig grow. But if it becomes contaminated with the salt element then it won't make the plant grow, and it will shrink the snig.

Oil : Oil is the fourth falling element, it has a light mass but its main purpose is that it burns up insanely fast when it comes in contact with fire.

Salt : Salt is the third falling element, has a light mass and basically just stops growth from the snig and the plant.

Cera {Wax} : Wax is a great element and has a lot of potential because it burns incredibly slowly when contacted with fire. But everything that got burnt falls down in one pixel wax cubes, regeneration! Its also a solid.

Plant : The Plant grows when it comes in contact with water, and burns faster than wax but slower than oil when it comes in contact with fire. Its also a solid.

Spout : Spout is a solid that generates water very slowly, not much to say about it except it gets eaten by sand.

Fire : Burns anything that it comes in contact with, Has slow burn on oil plant and wax. Fire ruins the maze! Also its not a solid nor element.

???: Kills all elements on the screen. Leaves solids however.

Eraser: Eraser, nuff said.

Wall: The wall is the basic solid, it blocks everything that comes in contact with it nothing really eats it.

Zombies + Snig: I'll leave this to you, I don't feel like giving away all the secrets

+ There are also alot of rip off's of this game.

Edit3: In fact, follow these steps for an amazing series of visual effects. (Credit to "Username" from JayIsGames) I'd recommend doing it on the small version of the game.
Turn everything off, then cover the screen with Spout. Start a ??? at the top of the screen.
Afterward, you'll have lots of little specks of spout producing water. Block off the bottom of the screen with an extra line of Spout so the screen fills up with water.
After that, start some plants in random places and watch as they eat up your screen. Cool effect.
Once they're covering the entire screen, start a small fire in the center. The fire will eat the plants, then the spouts will grow new plants, then the fire will eat those, etc.
The whole sequence is really awesome to watch.
