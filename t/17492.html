Hoo, boy. Do I have a lot of stuff to explain.

Jakel and Broghan's Map Pack! started off one day in February when I wanted to make a map pack. I started off PM'ing random map makers that he thought were great. Some people accepted, some people declined. After this conclusion, my list had ended up to be VV33, Topolord, uber_haxor, Mr_Mongoose, Palemoon, Broghan, and I. Our plan was to each make 25 maps, which totals up to a whopping 125 maps. After I PM'ed everyone the final list, Broghan mentioned to me on IRC that 7 was too many people. It was a tough decision on which person to cut, since they all had accepted. Since VV33 and Topolord are buds, I decided that if one declined, another one would decline, too. I kindly told them that there was too many people, and that it was just too hard to work with 7 people. This left the fivesome: Uber_Haxor, Mr_Mongoose, Broghan, Palemoon, and I. After about a week of working, Mr_M and U_H had told me that they just couldn't make any more maps. So, it left the trio of Palemoon, Broghan, and I, hence PB and J. After we had all made our 20 maps, I PM'ed everyone to collect the maps, and Palemoon didn't respond. (He still hasn't.) This leaves Broghan and I.

Anyway, onto the maps. There is a .txt file below this post, which holds 40 maps. I'm planning of having an expansion, which will include Broghan, Jakel, and more random mappers. (Don't call us, we'll call you. )

Enjoy!

I've consulted a few people about these maps, and from their feedback, I think a little explanation is in order.

First off, I would like to say that this pack was a different experience for me, so I wanted to
change my map schemes for the occasion. So I searched for ideas- on NUMA, and on the forums- that could help me.
I thought to look for Kablizzy, Maximo, KinGAleX and Maestro, because I am a big fan of their
earlier work in the original userlevels of N v1.4.
I loved the way that the maps had a tough demeanour, but also carried a sense of fun and epic.
Things like mines, one-ways, gold placement, awkwardness, were my themes. Therefore, I attempted
to make my maps in this way, to try and capture what was a fresh and exciting time for map-making.
So, I hope that you keep an open mind for my maps in this pack, because I wanted to make it a
different format, and above all, an enjoyable experience for you.

Oh, and there are a few dedications as well:
The Mistake- This is for Dave, and his love of chainguns. He most likely won't like it.
WHOSAIDZENITH?- For Clovic, and the real Zenith.
Marty McFly and the Sparatics- For Alex. Just because.
The Taming of the Drone- Aju and his use of seeker drones...CONFINED!

And there are others, but I forget.
Enjoy.
