I sorta kinda got this idea from Trogdor007, but he got the idea from me, so it's really my idea. What is it, you ask? A digital art worm. For an example of what this is, look here. So, what are you here for? To help create this worm. What do I do? Here are the rules:

1. Start out with a template of 400x250. Plenty of room to make something.
2. You may contribute to this as much as you wish. Just add your name to the list. Just please do not go more than 1 time in a row.
3. The left edge of your section needs to match up with the previous entry.
4. No obscene images/text, please. Keep it safe.
5. Other than that, you can have anything on the section.
6. You can use any image program, even MS Paint.
7. Se7en is darker.
8. You can use either .png, .jpg, or .jpeg for your images.
9. If you wish to use an animation, 600kb is the limit.
10. Enjoy.

Once I get some people on the list, we can start. I don't really need many people to launch this, so we an get started fairly quickly.
