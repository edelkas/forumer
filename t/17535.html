#331055 4 2007-06-14T01:23:18Z Azjps
Just a note specific to 36:

36 is considered a pretty difficult episode. You will need to get the gold on level 0 neural net at the bottom if you hope to make it through level 3, since each piece of gold adds 2 seconds of time, and this time carries over from level to level. Also, be sure to get the gold at the top in the level 1 data base.

The other levels can be found on the N wiki. For 36-2, you can probably save some time by falling directly at the beginning and then wallhugging on the bottom wall or the bounceblock. Same for the thwump ending: align yourself right above but slightly to the left of the mines, then fall straight down. You will trigger each thwump and land on them in turn.

Hopefully you will have gathered enough time to defeat planding makes perfect (the last level is fairly simple).

By the way, this belongs in Level/Ned help.
