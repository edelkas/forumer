The Idea:

To make a map using an old Metanet tileset. You must make the level your own, or as different from the original level as possible.


The Rules:

1. 3 maps per participant.
2. No changes whatsoever to the original tileset are allowed.
3. Maps do not have to be submitted to NUMA, but it would be preferred. But a tag, "metaset" would be nice if you did submit to NUMA.
4. Try to erase all enemies and then make the level totally your own.
5. Link you entry to this thread.
6. Judges may submit maps, but cannot judge their own submissions (obviously).
7. Use this program for the tilesets, http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=4072
8. You may use the 80's column.
9. I would like to know the level you remade too, so post it somewhere.
10. The maps are due by May 15, maps after that will not be accepted.


Judging:

Aesthetics- ?/5
Remake difference- ?/10
Fun- ?/10
