#284357 2 2007-01-11T19:21:11Z 29403
Press ~/` to go in ned first of all, and press J to make a plain, or H to make a non-plain.
press E or T to edit. When you've finished, press [home] then ` to go back to the ned menu. Press P (with caps lock on) to play your level. Reset it once you've finished it, find your userlevels and post it with
$Level naem#Author#Type#Level Data#.
Press user levels in the main menu and find your map. Play.
