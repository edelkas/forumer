Revolution.

Okay, here's what I want to do. Now that Revolution has dropped, I think the Map-packs are gonna start coming hard and fast, and I want to make sure that we can keep on top of things. Really, the only pack that should come anywhere near the production quality to Expanse is GSP, and perhaps P42, or whatever they're renaming it.

So, here's the deal. I want us each to start analyzing each of Revolution's levels, and give input as to what we liked, disliked, what should have changed, stayed the same, and so on, so that we can all become better mapmakers, progressively. From there, we could release critiques upon their map-packs, and launch reviews, analyzing good and bad parts of their efforts.

First of all, I want to discuss a couple things before we get into individual level analysis. First of all, their organization. Their levels, do ya'll think they were in relative order? Did it feel like they had spent time figuring out where each level was going to go?

The second thing I wanted to discuss was their editing. Did anyone notice any numbering errors/spelling mistakes that should be immediately brought up?

I haven't noticed anything within the first few levels, so I can't comment yet on the first, but as for the second, I didn't notice any spelling errors/numbering erros. I'd like to see what ya'll noticed with 'em, if anything, and then we'll get into individual levels.

#196714 7 2006-06-30T05:08:20Z Kablizzy
That's a good point, although we wouldn't be doing tributes, I'm sure, we do need to be able to get more ideas down, though, and make sure that we have a veritable wellspring from which we can work.

So, with that in mind, let's start the good stuff! Episode 0 was pretty decent, overall, but let's look at individual levels. If ya'll would comment on each level, as I am, that'd be awesome.

00-0: Late Bloomers - I really loved this, it's a really neat conceptual map, and it plays pretty well. I didn't quite notice anything vital that they should have added/subtracted from this.

00-1: Rise and Shrine - Another simple, fun map. The only real problem I saw with it was the gold placement. It could have been done far better, along the sides of the pillar, and along the sides of the outer walls of each pit.

00-2: Central Star - I really enjoyed this map, and I loved the tileset structure on the outside, I rather thought the concept was neat, as well. I also loved the star structure, but I thought the bounce blocks could have been better placed along the outside so that maneuverability could be a bit better.

00-3: Sneech - I thought this, as well, was amazingly done. It has a great balance of enemies and gold placement, jumping, and tileset. I loved the design, and the additional tileset work on the right.

00-4: De Toren - I hated this one. The one-way platforms seemed randomly placed, and the entire map lends itself to two ways of making it up the tower - First, you can simply walljump the left side, and jump over, taking an unbelievably easy way back to the exit. The second way is to cheat the one-ways, or just button-mash randomly, which isn't terribly fun. I'd say this map is good on paper, but shouldn't have made it in.
