I was Nediting and when I loaded my map some NaNs appeared instead of some characters, what are they?
#251895 2 2006-11-08T23:03:52Z Twistkill
They are glitches in the N code, and they stand for "Not a Number". They can cause any number of strange effects to happen. Tiles that can be passed through, frozen drones and other enemies, a giant N can appear, and there are many more.

.:EDIT:. Ggg49's post below also explains how they can appear.
#251896 3 2006-11-08T23:05:08Z Greengooguy49
They are errors in the code. You may see them in your map such as a mine outside of the map area or a piece of gold out of the area.

If you have been editing the code to make something like a teleporter then the NaN will be the edited bit that you have done. Otherwise you could have made the NaN by accidently typing somehting in the map code area.
