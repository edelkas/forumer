Got a good idea, but don't want to do it?
Post it here.

Basically, this is a thread where you may request an idea recreated into a simple map.
You can receive it in 2 ways.

1. You may have it requested in a txt. file so you can post it on NUMA (credit to us.)
Or...
2. We post it on NUMA with a DED to you for thinking it up.

Just do this simple form saying the idea:
Type:(Action, race, etc.)
Idea:(gamplay, theme, etc.)
and that's it.

You can also sign up to join making maps with these ideas, too!
