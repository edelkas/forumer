#36378 19 2004-11-14T04:09:03Z LittleViking
Mine started as an inside joke, then became a gaming handle, and now it's an internet profile.

Eating lunch one day at school, one of my friends from the group I was with pulled out a brand-name snack cake thing. The package was labeled "Viking Size!", but we all thought it was too small for any real viking to enjoy. Someone suggested that maybe little vikings ate something that size. I replied that there were no little vikings, the big vikings ate the little vikings. I never really thought it was that funny, but they all thought it was hilarious, so it stuck.

Years later, I had to decide on a name to go by when I played LAN games at my school. After thinking about it for a while I had narrowed it down to a few choices. To make the final choice, I knew people would ask who the new player was and I decided that "Who's Viking?" sounded the best. And thus, I became known as Viking.

Now, obviously, when you try to register a name on the internet you have to assume that common names, such as Viking, are already in use. A name like LittleViking would be more likely to be unused, but to be absolutely sure, adding 001 to the end makes for a completely unique username (by the way, 001 has nothing to do with the year 2001; I hate when people put a year at the end of a username). If you see something along the lines of LittleViking elsewhere, chances are good that it's me.


So there you go.

On a side note, I was inspired by Suki's name anagram to see if there was anything interesting in my own name.
There are probably better anagrams, but this is one I found:
Thar me went, the viking throne.
I never even knew the word 'viking' was in there, let alone the pirate referrence.
