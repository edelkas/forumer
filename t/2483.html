#54538 22 2005-03-17T17:55:51Z nevermore
You mean right here?:

Curve kick:
Similar to the corner kick. You run up to an 8 curve, or one that bulges at you. Just like in the corner kick, jump so that you nick the edge of the curve. Right as you touch, jump again and hold it. This isn't as effective as the corner kick, but still makes you fly.

Bounceblock:
You need to at least somewhat understand how bounceblocks work before you attempt this.
Jump onto a bounceblock and let it go pretty low. It goes lower if you move to the side when you land on it, so if you can, alternate pushing the left and right keys rapidly. If you can't manage that, just try to stay on the block while slightly moving. After the bounceblock reaches its lowest point and starts to come up, press jump twice quickly, but not too fast.

Oneway wall jump: (I only am gonna do this, because the others really aren't used in the highscores)
This one is pretty easy. If you are on a wall that has a oneway facing down towards you, walljump up the wall. Try to time your jumps so that your last one gets you right under the oneway. If you manage to pull it off right, when you jump again, you should go through the oneway. It all depends on how close you are to the oneway.
