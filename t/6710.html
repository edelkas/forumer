#6123 1 2006-01-09T04:29:18Z great_sea
I think it would be really nice to know (just for your own maps - not publicly) how many people have added your map to their favorites. It could read something like,

"This map has been added to the favorites of 12 users."
or
"This map is a favorite of 4 users."

(or however else is best to phrase it)

I always wonder if anybody has added my maps to their favorites, and I'm sure many other people do too. Sometimes a map may have a poor rating, but if the author knows some people have put it in their favorites, they may be less discouraged and less inclined to delete it. Besides that, I think it would just be an overall cool bit of info to know about your maps. So Arachnid, I really hope you can implement this! Thanks.

At a glance, I think the little tidbit of info might fit best to the right of the link to "See Similar Maps," kind of like this:

See Similar Maps - (this map is a favorite of X users)
