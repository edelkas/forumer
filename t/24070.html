#471944 12 2008-09-20T21:20:51Z Hendor
Quote: T3chno, September 19, 2008 10:39 pm
Was it AB that was the universal donor and O the universal acceptor?

No, it's the other way around. Read on.


Your blood type refers to which type of antigens and antibodies are found in the blood. An antigen is simply an identifier, while it is an antibody's job to attack certain things it believes to be harmful. People with blood type A have A antigens and anti-B antibodies. If they are given blood from someone with blood type B, their anti-B antibodies will reject it. Basically what this means is that you can't get blood that has antibodies against your blood type. So... people with the blood type AB can get blood from anyone because their blood has both A and B antigens and NO antibodies, or else their blood would attack itself. Because it has no antibodies, it is impossible for it to attack anything. Conversely, type O is the universal donor, because it has NO antigens and both A and B antibodies. Therefore, no blood type will attack it, because it simply can't identify it.

A common question after hearing this is "Why don't the anti-A antibodies in type O blood attack type A blood when the type O is transfused into type A." This question makes sense because type A blood has A antigens, so why don't the anti-A antibodies in the O blood attack them? The answer is that blood antibodies are found in the blood plasma, as opposed to the antigens, which are on the surface of the red blood cells. The plasma is not included in a transfusion.

Sorry for the long winded answer, but I've just recently learned this and was testing myself. I'm type A, don't know the Rh factor.

Ad - You will have received an O allele from your mom. Your type depends 100% on what allele your dad gave you. If he gave an A allele, you're AO, which is type A. If he gave a B, you're BO, which is type B. If he gave an O, you're OO, which is type O. The only thing you can't be is AB. Also, seeing as you don't know your dad's blood type, you have basically a 33% chance for the above three types.
