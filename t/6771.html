This is actually an idea from a Kurt Vonnegut novel - The Sirens of Titan.
Basically, the religion is based around the idea that fate is non-existant, and that luck is unrelated to God, or His will. Other than that of course is the main point, which is that God has no influence upon our lives, nor does he appear to care about humanity as a whole, or in specific cases. The religion strives for equality and peace through spiritual guidance, but not through worship. It claims that God is apart from our lives, neither present nor omnipresent; detached and disinterested.

Information on the book is available through Wiki - here.
I would reccomend reading the book for a better understanding. Apart from the religion, it's a good read.

Anyway, I wanted to gauge people's opinions. Is this, do you suppose, a valid basis for a religion?
