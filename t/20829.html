Untitled (I like F Minor)
Interesting start. I liked the use of the piano and snare. It gave it the beat which I couldn't really feel at the beginning. Plus, nice relaxed feel. Not too fast, nor to slow... I like it. 2 mins left now; interesting use of Sax and stuff, then something which sounds vaguely like a harp. I like the... quietness of it. I think that's the right word for it. 1:15; ooh, nice. Really relaxed - me likes it. Continues nicely and relaxed, and I like the way it ends at the end (duh ) with the fading out of instruments.
Personal note: Never listen to maestro's music while Mum's around. She will start dancing. I'm not sure if that's a complement or not.

Hyperchords
Hum, shorter song. Nice though; just a good feel to it, and a nice melody. Woah, weird at 30 secs . This wasn't so... cool. I don't know, I just didn't really like some of the chords here. Nice transition back into the first melody though, I liked that. I liked the use of clapping and stuff here too, which I don't think was there at the beginning. (If it was, I still like it.) Interesting end, too. Fadey-outy was good, but I didn't like the note it ended on. Maybe that's just me.

Supermassive Black Hole (Remix)
Sorta left this one out, for two reasons. One, it didn't really sound like my sort of music... and two, I haven't listened to the original, so I can't really comment on how well it was remixed. I do trust that it's amazing, though

Shipping Bells
Hmm... *pause*... nice. I like the instrument, and the way it self-accompanies... itself. 1:30 left; nice chords. I like the way that it then returns to the original melody, but with added parts. It gave it more depth, which I really liked. Then the song progressed nicely and enjoyably, and it had quite a nice ending. A strange one, but nice nonetheless.
