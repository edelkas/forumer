I just made N run about 75% better on my old bacon laptop. I'm not exactly sure which of these two things did it, so I will pass them both along. (It can't hurt to do both anyway.)

1. Turn off audo hardware acceleration (Windows)
1. Go to "Start\Settings\Control Panel"
2. Double click on "Sounds and Multimedia"
3. Click on the "Audio" tab.
4. Click the "Advanced" button under your "Sound Playback Prefered Device".
5. Click on the "Performance" tab.
6. Move the "Hardware Acceleration" slider all the way over to "None".
7. Hit OK, OK, etc.

2. Install

3. Try the game. Let me know if this did anything for you or not. I am intersted to see if anyone gets similar results.
