#237737 2 2006-10-06T02:28:46Z Ngaged
Hm, I might have a suggestion.

Try to avoid going fast in this level, except when you're around a gauss turret.

Also, try to avoid getting the gold. If you concentrate too much on trying to snatch it, you won't get very far.

Also also, I suggest going with the tiles. Just follow them along. Um, I know that doesn't make very much sense, but, for example, in one area, there is a slope above a mine pit. Jumping towards the slope will let you land in the middle of the pit, safe from harm. Incidents like that will happen if you just follow the tiles.

Hope this helps somehow!
