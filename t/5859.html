I beat the game a while ago, and there were a number of levels in particular that were very, very difficult for me (88-4, 36-3, 89-4, etc...). There were also some that just took me a while because they required a few exact jumps (Sibilant S, The Many, etc...).
Recently, I looked back over the levels I had completed (all of them), and decided to rebeat every single one that was difficult for me. So I did.
I strongly recommend that all of you do this. It's a great exercise, will definitely improve your N skills, and is very fun. It's also quite interesting comparing your skill now with your skill when you first tried the level. For instance, I had a lot of difficulty with Sibilant S. When I went back, it took me about five minutes.
Oh, and by the way, Apoplexy was still really hard.
