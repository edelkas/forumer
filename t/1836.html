#42012 7 2004-12-21T07:19:15Z maximo
I strongly recomend not sucking, and try and get a foothold on one corner of the map or africa if your risky. then secure that very very strongly (like every turn it gets 2/3 of your reinforcments). Then move out from there/other strongholds you have on the map, making sure your original base is strong. I recomend S.A. or Austraila becuase of the low borders amount. Just slowly slowly move out from those bases and just keep expanding your borders, and ALWAY have strong boarders, don't spread your armies too thin, or you get counterattacked and die.

that was long... hope it helps!

and for you advanced players who know the risk map way to well like me. I recomned lord of the rings risk, but don't play by their rules. Use the board and the rivers/mountains/fortress/leader rules and play the rest like normal risk. That is a lot more challenging and mind provoking with rivers and what not involved. It's better than the original I think. it takes some getting used to though.
