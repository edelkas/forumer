As far as I can see, there will be two possible places in the actual game v1.5 for new userlevels to be added. There may be another user-created column, like the existing 80s column, and there will most probably be a new set of userlevels with the game (or an extended/revised edition of the current set).

So how are the levels for these section chosen to be immortalised in the history of N?

Obviously, Mare and Raigan will choose ones they consider appropriate. The problem is that there are sooo many userlevels now - and so many good ones - that it becomes impossible to choose just a fraction of them for the game. Also, there are enough users now to get a large variety of levels and styles, so the lists won't be dominated so much by the 'masters'.

So what I think/suggest is that people should showcase their maps that they consider to be the best or most appropriate and suited to the game. They would have to argue for their position, and demonstrate why their map is suited for an episode slot or userlevel position, taking into consideration as much as possible (easy enough for beginners? challenging gold for high scorers? etc). People would not be able to submit all their maps obviously, but it would still become a large selection of the 'best' maps. The purpose of this is to whittle down the numbers to maps most people like and agree should be in the game, which then helps M&R make their final decision and sort out the episodes, etc.

Now, what do you think of this idea? Should it be implemented? And will it work?
