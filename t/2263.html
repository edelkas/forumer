What is a thing?

Supposing you take an object. I'm sure most of you would agree that if you take another identical object, and swap one atom of each with an identical atom of the other, they'll still be the same objects.

But what if they have only one atom each? Then you've switched them. Or have you?

You see, unless you accept that swapping one-atom objects doesn't really swap them, what's keeping a human from becoming a totally different human by replacing all of his/her atoms, as is the natural consequence of eating, etc?

Another paradox: Suppose you have a grenade in an enclosed chamber. You set it off, then collect the components, melt them down, and recreate the grenade. If it's not the same grenade, how is a jigsaw puzzle the same puzzle if you take it apart and rebuild it?

And then there's the issue of 'sanctified matter', as I call it: Stuff which you're not supposed to desecrate, recycle, etc., like dead bodies. All the matter in a dead body was just going to be thrown away in that person's excrement, so why is it sanctified? And what does it mean for matter to be sanctified, if it can also be replaced?
