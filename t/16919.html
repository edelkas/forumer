I have a LOT of thoughts.

1 - Waking Up - Way too simple, back & forth is always boring, not a single enemy, just bland. 1/5

2 - Fault Line - That is a LOT of drones. I have no idea how to get that switch. The closest I've ever gotten to it was 5 pixels away. Too difficult for most. 2/5

3 - The Recipe - WTF. WAYYYY too many enemies, I die instantly when I attempt to get the first switch because either machine gun drone kills me or the seeker drone kills me first. Extremely difficult and not very entertaining. 1/5

4 - Cast it Out - Very good. The bottom part with all those mines is just infuriating. I cannot get past that floorguard despite all my attempts. The room right above it with the 3 rockets is also pretty difficult. The floorguard just makes everything hard because you are forced to jump and hit the rocket. I can't seem to get that switch on the top right no matter what. 3.5/5

5 - Wasteland - Not too easy nor too difficult. Fun. Much simpler. 4/5

6 - Seasons to Cycle - The rocket part was very entertaining but the mines weren't as fun. The thwump part really got on my nerves. The laser part was a lot more difficult than I expected. 4/5

7 - Half Life - The bottom part REALLY got me frustrated. The rest is ok. 3/5

8 - Through the Iris - The top part is really fun, but the bottom part is a lot less fun due to the difficulty. The gauss is just annoying as hell. The top part is great to practice corner jumping, and I made a pretty nice jump that I am damn proud of although highscorers do it all the time. 4/5
