As we all know, new mapmakers are how NUMA survives. As old mapmakers leave new ones must fill the spots that they leave behind.

This thread is meant to give attention to new mapmakers who show great potential. Hopefully you all know of some new mapmakers that are awesome beginners and rock your socks. Post a link to their profile (and please provide a reason why you think they are awesome) and I'll add them to the first post. Kinda like the Level Packs thread except for new NUMA authors.

This thread is not meant to advertise for yourself but rather bring attention to others. Therefore I will stop accepting posts from new authors who would like to be noticed and only accept posts from authors who I trust can make good decisions about great new authors.

For those new authors who would like to gain some attention, please use either:
The Map-Rating Thread
-OR-
The Map Commenting Thread
Then if you feel like you would like some notice, suggest to somebody who is well know if they would consider referring you to this page, at which point I'll take a look and decide if I agree and from there you will be added. It sounds like a difficult process, and because of that I'm increasing the maximum number of maps for new author consideration to 50 and the length of time here in the community to 2-3 months instead of 1 month or under.
Hopefully this doesn't kill the thread, but I feel it's necessary to prevent people from coming here and spamming the thread, plus it allows you newer members to get real feedback on your maps instead.

---
So that we are all on the same page, this thread is meant to encourage and provide constructive criticism to new map makers. There is nothing better than letting someone know how they could improve.
