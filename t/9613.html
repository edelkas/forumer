On my latest NUMA level, High Rise, the auto-locking zap drones will only notice me if I am moving. I thought that they always noticed you and locked on, irregardless of whether or not you were moving. Any advice/tips/help would be greatly appreciated...thank you.
#174940 2 2006-05-12T07:48:47Z LittleViking
In this case, the ninja is standing on the inner half of a 5-tile, which zap drones can't go through. They won't seek him if they can't pass through that tile.

Seeker drones are notorious for not tracking the ninja though. After a while, you'll even get used to when they seek you and when they don't, and you'll be able to use it to your advantage.
