I was curious to know how we all see ourselves. I know that this may seem weird at first but I think it is important to take a step back from our front that we put up and look at ourselves from a realistic point of view. Your role in your family; how you function in society; what you think or have heard of what your friends think of you, as well as what your first impressions of people are and what you think of your friends; how your beliefs and faith affect how you act and who you interact with; your ideas of how things should work, how family should work; what concerns you for the future, the past, the present; your deepest fears and your greatest triumphs, and so on and so forth. Just an analysis of who you are as a person. I would like each person to take their time as well and not just write down "I think my family loves me." Instead take the time to look at what part you play in the lives of others, including yourself.

RULES

- None of the following should happen in this topic:
  • Harassment of any kind
  • Joking about how others see themselves, because I'm willing to bet anybody who does that sees themselves the exact same way and they just aren't willing to admit it.
  • Making defamatory statements about people, even if there is proof in their post. Take it to a PM if you want to confront somebody on a certain issue.


- Things that should happen:
  • Open discussion of the lives and issues concerning you and others free from any attacks within this topic. Might I remind you of the rules...
