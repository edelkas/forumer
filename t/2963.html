#62478 16 2005-04-28T03:07:19Z brickman
Its a glitch so common that I suppose we assume people know it. Put a one way pointing down (so you can fall through but not jump through upwards), and put it next to a wall. Jump right next to the wall and when n is bumpping his head on the oneway walljump and you will go through it. Its hardish if you don't know what I mean, so for the sake of seeing how it works I suggest you make a blank map with a shaft with a oneway, since that makes it easier. It only works going up, but there's many other smaller, less important oneway bugs which work in all directions, but you shouldn't worry about them (except maybe the one involving slopes and oneways, if you are on a slope you can go through any).
