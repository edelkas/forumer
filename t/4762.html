Some would-be-nice variations:

Delayed doors - hit the switch and have a period of time before it closes or opens. Time could be zero to make it instant.

Vertical/Horizontal Auto Doors - The idea here is that if the ninja is lined up with the door either vertically or horizontally (depending on the door type) then it will open or close (chosen when the door is placed), otherwise it is in the opposite state.

Death Doors - If they close while the ninja is inside them then the ninja is cut in half (dead). They can be placed on a timer to automatically open and shut every few seconds.

Tile Doors - They look just like solid tiles whether closed or open. If closed they behave like solid tiles, if open they behave like open tiles.

Trampoline Doors - They act like jump pads when closed.

Multi-Door - Linked doors in several locations. When the switch is hit it opens one location and closes in all the rest, rotating through each location in the order placed.

Robot Teleport Door - A door that has two locations. When robots go through this door it teleports them to the other location and vice versa.

Black Hole Door - When the ninja gets too close, he is sucked through the door and it promptly closes. It has a switch to reopen it, presumably accessible from the other side.


That's all for now.
