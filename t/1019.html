Rated E for Everyone

Two men are walking down the street with their dogs; one with a Doberman pinscher, one with a Chihuahua. They notice a restaurant, and realise they are hungry. "Let's go inside", the guy with the Doberman said. "But we have dogs, we won't be allowed in!" the guy with the Chihuahua replied. "Follow my lead," the guy with the Doberman said. So the Doberman owner puts on a pair of dark glasses, and walks up to the doorman to enter the restaurant. The doorman told the guy with the Doberman, "Sorry, no pets allowed." "You don't understand, this is my Seeing Eye dog." "A Doberman pinscher?" "Yes, they're very agile, and help fend off burglars." Convinced, the doorman let the guy with the Doberman in. When the guy with the Chihuahua sees this, he puts on his pair of dark glasses, and walks up to the doorman. Again, the doorman greeted him with "Sorry, no pets allowed". "You don't understand, this is my Seeing Eye dog." "A Chihuahua?" "A Chihuahua? They gave me a Chihuahua?"
