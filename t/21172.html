The Buzz Bracket.

Goal and Purpose.
~To crown a map as champion of all playable maps.
~To have fun seeing maps go head to head.
~To be as voter friendly as possible.

What is it.
~A Bracket Tournament.
~Either 32 or 16 maps in the bracket, pending on quantity of nominations.

How's it gonna go down.
~From now until February 16, 2008; my inbox on the forums will be open to nominations.
~In other words, nominate your favorite playable maps of all-time.
~You can nominate as many maps as you want, but keep it somewhat reasonable (as in under 50 nominations per person please).
~Rankings for the bracket will be determined by the number of nominations each map received.
~Ties will be broken by Team Mirage.

What I want you to do.
~Get onto NUMA.
~Make a list of your favorite playable maps currently on NUMA.
~PM me the list (maximum of 50 maps and minimum of 1 please).
~And have some fun doing it.

Team Mirage.
Brttrx, DemonzLunchBreak, Infinitum, Cooby, Shortshift, MidnightGetaway.
