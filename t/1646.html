Not sure where to post NUMA suggestions and get feedback, so here goes. Please move this if necessary.

Anyway, can we also get an "average rating given" column under All Users? It seems like some folks get their jollies off of giving low/high ratings and this would help deter that. If someone's average rating for the 400 maps they've rated is 0.5 then you know something is wrong. Maybe this doesn't need to be public knowledge, but at least be on the lookout for that kind of behavior. I've seen it happen in every other rating system I've ever been a part of (and no, I wasn't the one doing it, either ).

There has to be a way to encourage people to rate the good maps AND the bad maps. Some people hate giving a bad rating and some hate giving a good rating ("No one deserves 5 ninjas" - stuff like that). Might also help the ratings a bit if you put a description of each rating number when people rate maps. Tell them what the 0 through 5 mean, in rough terms, so that the scoring is even and fair.

Another thing to consider... I try to make easy maps, hard maps, any every kind inbetween because I know people have different skill levels and a different view of what is fun or infuriating. Some people will rate a map poorly just because it is too easy or too hard for them to beat, which is not what I would think the rating system was supposed to be used for. I would think that the rating system was about QUALITY. Maybe I'm wrong, so please correct me if I am.

Anyway, that's enough rambling. Thank you for your time in reading this and for your comments.

I somehow don't think this rating system means anything. Not the way it is now. When you're given 3000 things you COULD rate, and not encouraged to rate any of them, then typically only the ones at the extreems will be rated, and any maps whose average score is between 1.5 and 4 is not likely to get enough votes to even have a rating. I currently have no rated maps, thus am automatically at the bottom of the top rated list. The limited 10-5 scale doesn't help either-- it has no degrees of goodness, just "best" "good" "average" "poor" "very bad"and "donkey shit." A ten point system is much more logical. Additionally, people can see that a map has a 4 after 5 votes, and say "I think it should be a three," but instead of of rating it 3 he rates it 0 to bring the aerage down to three all at once, by himself, thus making the entire thing a useless system. Also, there's not enough rated maps for "top rated" to mean anything: there's a guy with an average rating of 0 halfway up on the first page of authors, and there's good people (like me) who have no rated maps under HIM on the top rated list. And last time I checked, the first page of "top" rated maps dipped into the "poor" range, possibly even the "very bad" range.

Revamp the system or scrap it, its useless now.
