hi,
does your version have [highscores] in the menu?

if not, you should download the full version: http://www.harveycartel.org/metanet/downloads.html

if so (or, if you've now downloaded the full version), you can actually watch other people playing the levels by going into highscores and clicking on a score in the list on the left. it's like magic!

...however, since those are all super-expert players, it might not help you. personally, i used to beat it by going up the left, but the last time i played that level i forgot my route and ended up using the right-hand wall. the key is to keep trying different sequences of moves until you find one that seems like it would work, if you could pull it off.. then keep at it till you can pull it off without messing up

don't keep trying the same thing if it clearly doesn't work since this will only result in unbearable frustration -- there are at least 2 or 3 different paths out of the bottom, keep trying!

oh wait -- even better, the NEHS has a route for you: http://www.freshsurf.co.uk/n-vision/nehs/e...e29/level3.html

i just have to say -- this community rocks.. what a great resource!
