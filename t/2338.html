Just about any singleplayer genre, though most of what I play is freeware. I mean, after all, with hundreds of commercial-quality free games out there that you can play for longer than many retail games and with more fun, why would you pay for anything but the best commercial games? So every once in a while I buy one, but most of the time there's more than enough on the web.
I don't do too much multiplayer. I can't do shit in fps deathmatches, and most other things its hard as hell to do an online matchup. And I have noone to play with for the few games that can go multiplay on one computer. The only multiplayer game I've ever really gotten into is the ever-popular Soldat, and I haven't played that since my files got corrupted after installing an update.
I have no problem with MMOs either, except that any free ones are, to put it bluntly, crap. I played Runescape for a while but realised once I had all but two of the free quests completed and high level in almost every skill that I had been doing it for the sake f doing it and it wasn't fun. I then tried Tibia, but quit after the game showed me in several ways that it didn't want me to enjoy it, the biggest of which being my knight fresh out of the training island stumbling accidentally upon a fire dragon with no warning whatsoever and losing most his stuff (even the pack) in the time it took to run three feet. Then I tried another one which I didn't even play long enough to remember its name before realising it was pathetic. I would love to have a good one, but it isn't going to happen, so my rpg fix is going to have to come from those primitive single player games where you don't find people standing in your way while you try to flee a berserker or stealing your kill that you spent half your health and a strength potion earning.
