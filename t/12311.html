Sure, I'll give you a hint or two.

The most important thing to look for in a Race map is the flow. You should make the player feel like they're going through the map smoothly. No sudden jumps that go off the path or anything like that.

Also, make sure you don't make the course repetitive. Don't have the player do the same thing over and over again. It gets annoying and boring.
