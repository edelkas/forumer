#305700 12 2007-03-16T21:54:45Z usaswim
Well, there is an easier approach to the "hounds" level. Stand right next to switch, and you will be barely touching it. If you do it right, the lock won't open due to object overlapping delay.

I can't help much on 29-4 because I skipped it and got the flavor instead.

About 29-3:
Rocket dodging isn't very difficult if you know the right path. The high scores demonstrate that path, although mine is the slower version. After you get out of the pit, jump to the left most wall and when you almost reach the bottom, jump again and go to the gold area and get the gold by wall jumping once. Immediately go for the bottom. Slow down when you reach the thwump, and the rest is pretty easy. Go back up by wall jumping until you get to the very top and then leap onto that slanted platform. Immediately jump sideways at a 45 degree angle to the right. The rockets should crash into the ceiling. On the right side, I just jumped onto the very right platform and then jumped again dodging mines. Land on the slope, and make sure the chaingun doesn't see you. I didn't get the gold.
