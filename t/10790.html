#193766 2 2006-06-24T04:13:56Z Twistkill
1. The program used to make N is called Flash. It costs a lot of money. Get it here.

2. The source code for any game can be used to modify it to your liking. In this case, N.

3. If you want to clear your progress and you can't find your .sol file, then right-click anywhere in N and click Settings. Click on the Folder icon. Slide the bar all the way to the left and click close. Then close N. Reopen N and your progress should be gone. Make sure to go back to settings and slide the bar back to the right or else it won't save any of your progress again.

I hope this answers your questions.
