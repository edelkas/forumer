Quote: MapSwap
MapSwap is very simple - all you have to do is give a map that you are happy to lose ownership over (generally an incomplete one) and in return you get someone else's map, that they've been happy to pass on. Map for map. Pretty simple.

By submitting a map to MapSwap, you are giving the next author permission to do whatever they want with your work.
You can submit maps that originated from MapSwap from NUMA, but you should credit the author who submitted it to MapSwap in the first place.

Idea: Me
Implementation: Pembie

Enjoy
