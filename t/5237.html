And your probably thinking "Hmmm, but what if the map maker uses the checkpoints way too many times?" Well the answer is simple, what if you can only add a checkpoint 1 time? So you see, long maps no longer have to be annoying and irratating, when with checkpoints come along, the fun-o-meter will shoot sky high! Oh the possibilities. Leave your comments.
