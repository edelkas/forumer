#306031 7 2007-03-18T08:00:22Z George
From looking at your first three maps, you have the potential at becoming a very good map maker. But right now, you still have a long way to go. You've had a pretty good start though, I commend you on that.

With regards to your maps, I think aesthetics will be to be worked on. A good map must have good gameplay, but if it doesn't look good, it will have less appeal. There was some clutter of gold and mines in the first and last maps, so I encourage you to space them out and make them a little more even. What uNcoNditioNal said was true as well. You need to spend more time on your tilesets. They are very important and often what makes or breaks a map. Keep that in mind. One last thing: playtest. I can't stress that enough. If you haven't playtested a map properly, often it will turn out to be too difficult or too easy, so be sure to test each map with care. My favourite of your maps was Parallelogrammatical, the minimalism worked perfectly.

Also, welcome to the forums, I hope enjoy your stay here.
