#133194 5 2006-01-17T17:43:21Z Teh Dude
This is fairly interesting. It seems that mastering N is really just about mastering the ability to determine then perform a perfect sequence of key presses. I will call this predictive.

Other games, such as first person shooters (think CounterStrike, Day of Defeat), are far more reactive.

I think this probably has to do with the amount of determinism in the game. N is more or less completely deterministic, nothing unpredictable happens. In online FPSs, there are other humans playing. As such, the game is not deterministic.

Also having other humans (or intelligent adversaries of some sort) adds a dimension of tactics; you must outwit your opponents.

N teaches you an important lesson as death is always your fault. In other games you may have just been outskilled or been unlucky - in N there is no one to blame but yourself.
