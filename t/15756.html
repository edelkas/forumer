Ahem. Anyway...

Your personal best for the Metanet levels is recorded automatically, and may be viewed by clicking on your score for that level in the Highscores menu.

Demos are also recorded automatically for levels in the user levels menu. Upon completing a level, the level and demo data will appear in the box on the left, in the format $level name#author#category#level data#demo data#. If you want to post the demo on NUMA, you'll need to get just the demo data from the end. Note that you cannot record demos this way unless you actually complete the level.

If you wish to record a demo in debug mode (Ned), you should click outside the text boxes, reset the level, and press 1 to begin recording. When you finish the level/die/whatever, you should then return to the Ned menu, press 2, and copy the text in the "demo data" box.
