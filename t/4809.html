#4384 1 2005-09-12T07:10:03Z Adam Dobay
Although most levels in N have distinct and original designs that are (and have to be) well planned, I miss one element of designing that was present in the level editor of that nice old game Jetpack, and I think it would not be hard to implement.

Many of my favourite levels were created by the simple method of putting a few full/empty blocks down randomly and then creating a level starting out from that. Besides careful planning, I think basing levels on the random element is a very effective method at times and is similar to a number of methods of art.

So, I think it would be really nice if there would be a button that would set a random number of the selected blocks of terrain around the map in Ned.

I would not extend the feature for objects, though (at least not to enemies), because that would complicate things.

#103804 11 2005-09-26T10:43:41Z Adam Dobay
While completely random levels also sound interesting, I was mainly thinking along the lines of simply placing a random number of terrain pieces along the empty level, and based on that you could go on with your normal level-creating process. It would not replace level editing at all, it would just add a kind of a 'muse' feature I like to have when I'm making a level. To provide an example (and I'm not trying to pull off a cheap advertisement here), this level of mine was created by loading a full map in the editor, and then placing random empty blocks by moving my mouse around with closed eyes and then clicking at some places. And from a few bits of emptiness, filling up the void and creating a level from that was the next, necessary, step. In that sense, totally random levels without any user 'interference' may really, as it has been said, result in mass Numa spamming.
