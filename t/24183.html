I just got this PM from Katsumaru, one of the oldest and most influential members the Metanet community has ever known:

Quote: Katsumaru
Tsukatu,

Of all the lives I have touched, I believe most sincerely that yours has been most rewarding to have known, and to have conversed, played, and laughed with. You remind me daily how much I've enlightened your life, and I want to tell you now - it was mutual. For these reasons, I've decided that you'd be the first I'd open up to about a deep aching I have felt for the years I've been with this community.

I and lukeprog are the same person.

I know, I know - such a betrayal of trust will no doubt well a rage within you, and rage at me if you must, as I full well deserve it, but know that I never meant to carry on such a terrible deception, nor especially for so long. I feel terrible, and truly and godawfully nauseous at the thought of what this revelation might do to the community... and to us. I don't want this to tear us apart, though I doubt any bond of friendship we've made can withstand such a cruel treason. I ask, fully with the knowledge that I'm undeserving to ask anything of you... nay, I beg you - root up what shreds of sympathy you may yet have and understand my plight. I am truly and deeply sorry for what this news may do to you, and to the community that has embraced both I and lukeprog as disparate individuals, two different minds.

Yesterday, I saw a policeman in the street, and I walked up and punched him directly in the nose. I didn't know him, and he didn't know me, and he did nothing to provoke such an assault, but I continued my barrage regardless. That's when the officer yelled at me. He yelled at me. And to top it off, I think his tone was somewhat upset. For this completely irrelevant reason, I feel I can no longer contribute any more N maps or high scores, and I'll soon be closing my account permanently and leaving the forums for good.

Should you wish to repost this message to the rest of the community, please let them know that I thank them all that have ever been nice to me and lukeprog. Even while they bought into my clever charade, I appreciated every kind word, even as each was as a knife into my heart as it reminded me that I was a such a fraud and liar. Know that I mean more than the words can convey when I tell you that I love you, all of you. Each and every of you has been so very dear to me, and I know that I'll never be able to put any of you behind me no matter how much I may try.

I have such a want to write so much more, but the wells of tears in my eyes are growing so deep that concentrating on this message is so emotionally effortful that I fear I may break in its writing.

Tsukatu, please don't hate me. The last thing I want to do is leave you emotionally empty and afraid to trust another again. In all that you have confided in me / lukeprog, I believe you're a wonderful and genuine person, and I know you'll find your place in the world.

I'm sorry, and goodbye.
-Katsumaru / lukeprog

I have come to terms with this message, and with Katsumaru. I have decided that I will not let it affect me, at the very least in the name of the immeasurable impact his two accounts have had in our lives.

I understand that a great many of you will be outright livid at this revelation, but I ask that you not express it here. His betrayal to all of us is far outweighed by his greatness and his tireless work and devotion to the community which has led us to the greatness we now take for granted. It is truly upon the shoulders of giants that we at the Metanet forums stand.

I ask that we have a moment of silence in honor of his memory.

u_u
