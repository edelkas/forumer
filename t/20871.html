Not all laptops are the same. I've seen many laptops with a full numpad attached to it; while others usually have Home, End, PgUp, PgDwn, Delete, NumLock, PrtScn/SysRq, and Pause/Break on shared keys. Try the Fn key, or look at the subscripted text on keys to find the Home key.
