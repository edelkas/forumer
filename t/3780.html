If indeed you do base a map's rating on how many times it has ever been added to someone's favorites, what is to prevent people from simply adding maps to their favorites and then deleting them immediately? People who don't really like a map enough to have it in their userlevels.txt would just add it to their favorites and then delete it as a way of "voting" for it. If this started becoming commonplace, is it really much improvement from the rating system? Possibly, but it seems like a degeneration from what the favorites system is really intended for.

Just bringing up my thoughts.
