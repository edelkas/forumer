Very easy, 3rd try .

A couple of things to remember:

1. Always play test your maps thoroughly, if you did that, you would have found the locked door glitch I used.
2. Don't overuse objects. The gold on the level is very overused, and it just slows down the reload time. Same with some of the bounce pads.
3. Plan out your level before actually designing it. The way this is designed, it looks like you just added random objects and called it a map. If you plan out a tileset and think about it before hand, the level will look a lot better, and flow better too.

Keep working on it, and remember, look at the whole map and how it works together, not just small pieces.

Read this for more info on making levels:
http://jeffkillian.com/NUMA.htm
