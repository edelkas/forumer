The theme is: "I hate Tsukatu!"
To clear some stuff up: this contest was not set up because of any depression-induced self-deprecating desires. I just felt that there should be a light-hearted contest, and I thought of an accompanying theme.
Note: You don't have to hate me in order to participate.
If anyone actually does hate me, then you can take it up with me privately.
In this circumstance, though, I'm just giving you guys a theme that has no relation to what you actually think of me.
So fire away!

This contest shows more focus on the artistic aspect of the map, so the gameplay categories aren't as significant.
You guys make maps, and they'll be judged according to the following categories:

Design: 10; How well does the map as a whole show your hate?
Creativity: 10; What kinds of new ideas are implemented?
[Judge Specified]: 10; That's right, it can be anything from "Effectiveness as a survival map" to "Is it a duck." This is for judges who feel that the map isn't being properly scored on the categories given.
Difficulty: 5; How well is the map scaled for beginners/veterans? Can the veterans expect a higher challenge if they want to all-gold the map?
Fun/Excitement: 5; I always thought this should be one category.
Demo: 5; Is there a demo? How good is it?
Other: 5; Anything else you want to add but couldn't in [Judge Specified].

The judges can also hand out 5 points to their favorite map, when their judging is finished.
Other than that, the rules for this contest will use the same ones as Maximo's string of Nedcons.

Any nominations for judges? I'll take two besides me, and one backup.
