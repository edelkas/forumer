#461824 2 2008-08-18T21:19:51Z PsychoSnail
[ spoiler]TEXT[/ spoiler]
In the Add Reply menu, just click the Spoiler button (top-right one) where you type in the reply, or just type that out.
Also,
[ spoiler=Text]TEXT[/ spoiler]
gives you a spoiler where the "Text" after "spoiler=" is the text (or image, if you put image code there) is the thing you click to reveal the "TEXT" (otherwise, if you use the first set of BBCode, it just uses the default "Click here to show/hide spoiler").

Remove the space in front of "spoiler."
