#206903 12 2006-07-25T00:19:49Z jtbandes
Yeah, blargh, I remember. So, here are the movements.

Edit: first post edited to include wedgie.

Note: All clockwise/counterclockwise movements are to be used as if you were looking straight at the face in question. References to the faces are used as if the front (F) face were towards you.

Faces
  • U - The top face of the cube.
  • D - The bottom face.
  • L - The left face.
  • R - The right face.
  • F - The front face.
  • B - The back face.
Movements
  • Movements are shown by the face to be rotated. A letter on its own (such as D) is a clockwise turn of the face, and a letter with an apostrophe after it is a counterclockwise turn (such as D'). A turn of 180 is shown by a 2 before the face (such as 2B).
  • For example: 2F R' D' R means: Rotate the front face 180, the right face counterclockwise, the bottom face counterclockwise, and the right face clockwise.
  • If you need to undo a move, change clockwises to counterclockwises and vice versa, then go through the sequence of moves backwards.

There are some slight variations of how these moves are notated too, depending on the source.

For example a counterclockwise move is sometime also known as a an 'inverted' move, and is written as Fi for a counter-clockwise turn of the front face.
Also, i personally refer to a 180 turn as F.
