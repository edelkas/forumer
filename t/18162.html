#348752 127 2007-07-29T23:21:39Z Raigan
we had this same problem with the user-maps column in N v1.4.. we tried to include as many people as possible by only choosing one map from a given author. the problem was that we had to reject many maps that were great, and still some people were upset that we didn't choose one of their maps. this time, we decided that we would vote for the maps blindly (without author info) so that the "best" maps (the ones we thought people would like the most) would get in.

mechtradevil, reviewing your maps, the problem with them is that they're (a) too difficult and (b) too long for what we thought would work. This is a common feature of most NUMA maps since they tend to be played (a) by expert players and (b) individually, which mitigates these features as "problems".. sadly in an episode those two things lead to frustration. We really liked the horse-shaped one, and "ham" was unanimously voted funniest map name!

We have the problem of making levels too difficult, we've been reviewing all of our levels and trying to make the learning curve more gradual, since we keep getting feedback from testers that the game is too hard. This is the #1 comment we hear from people we meet btw, "it's a fun game but i can't make it past the 3rd level".. for instance in the XBLA maps we made a specifically simple "intro" episode, but it was STILL unbeatable by new players since it required walljumping.. which apparently takes some time to figure out. As a result we've had to revise a lot of early levels to make sure they're not impossible.. so we also have the problem of making levels that are too hard and/or long.

Personally I like harder levels, and there will be some of the incredibly difficult levels in later episodes (possibly not quite as difficult as "motherthwumping" though ). However the number of slots open for such "expert" levels is very limited, and since most user-made maps tend to be of this type, competition for these "hard" slots was a lot more fierce than for easier maps -- we needed ot select an even distribution of difficulties, while the entries were quite skewed towards "hard".

Maybe we should have mentioned this, however we didn't think to. Anyway, i'm sorry to everyone who didn't make it in since all of the maps submitted were fun to play.. unfortunately there were many other factors which informed our choices. After we did the 80s column we swore "never again" to this sort of thing since it's not a nice feeling to know that no matter what you decide, your decision will make someone sad. But this was too good an opportunity to pass up.. and we think it'll turn out pretty well in th end.
