This is a contest I was working on. The level is attached. It is also on Numa - Here

Instructions:
The goal is basicaly to get all of the drones in the "pockets" without dieing before the time runs out. You want to get the lowest score possible. The level is attached. I will prabably submit it to numa.

For those who still don't understand here is a picture. This picture shows the level. You (N) must run back and forth (as depicted by the red arows) and get the drones (circled in green) into the pokets (circled in blue). Pretty simple. You prabably won't understand how to do this until you play it.



There are three competitions. They each have diferent goals.

Note:
Columns are pockets directly across from each other.
Rows are pockets next to each other.

Freestyle:
Your score is the same as your demo time. The shorter the better.
These factors are subtracted from your score:

10 points for every drone pocketed.
50 extra points for getting all drones pocketed.
100 points if you get all drones in only 2 Columns.
200 points if you get all drones in only 3 pockets.
300 points if you get all drones in only 2 pockets.
400 points if you get all drones in only 1 column.
500 points if you get all drones in only 1 row.
600 points if you get all drones in only one pocket.

Speed:
This one is easy. fastest demo getting all drones pocketed wins.

Checklist:
Your goal here is to demo as many of these "checklist" gaols as possible. You get one point per goal completed. Note that each one is a Seperate demo.
Here is the list:

For these Get one drone in the specified pockets.
3 in a row on the top level.
3 in a row on the lower level.
All pockets.
Right most column.
Middle column.
Left most column.
Left and Right Columns.
Left and middle columns.
Right and middle columns

For these Get two drone in the specified pockets.
3 in a row on the top level.
3 in a row on the lower level.
All pockets.
Right most column.
Middle column.
Left most column.
Left and Right Columns.
Left and middle columns.
Right and middle columns.

Additional Goals:
Kill yourself in a way other than a drone.
(Feel free to suggest more)

The score will be presented "Checklist goals + Additional goals = Total score"

How and when to submit your demos
.I think that the best way to do this is to PM me the demos or on Numa. I will post the current standings. At the end I will show all of the demos (I will put them on the web so you don't have to sort through lots of demos).

Awards:
At the end of febuary I will anounce the winners.
1st place in each section will get a dedication map and something to put in your sig. 2nd place might get something too (haven't decided yet). I will try to get some well know authors to make some of the deds (Thats your cue!). If so, the second placers can get deds too.
