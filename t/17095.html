Okay, I've got a slew of things to try if you want to do them all.

First, a RAM upgrade couldn't hurt. The only question is whether or not the RAM is compatible and still functional. If your 256 MB stick has a label on it, look to see if it's DDR SDRAM. Next, I would recommend running scans with Spybot - Search & Destroy, and Ad-Aware. They're a good way to get rid of most spyware and malware on your PC. You can get Spybot here and Ad-Aware here. They both do pretty fast scans, so it shouldn't be horribly time-consuming.

And finally, the most thorough part of this, a HijackThis scan. HijackThis scans your running processes and your Windows registry for unusual activity. If you run this and PM me a log file of the scan, I can tell you whether or not you have spyware and viruses, as well as how hard it's going to be to remove them.
