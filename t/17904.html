#334026 19 2007-06-21T16:08:31Z apg
You're having the same problem as me.

The comment values are determined by using the information between two even apostrophes:

Quote: mrgy05, June 21, 2007 06:53 pm
...VALUES (93522, 10252, 'Hmph', 'Well now I can comment, I was having problems... The background: I'm somewhat in flux at the moment, but I think that it will turn out to be a gradient from a very dark black, to mines, back to a dark black (From top to bottom, of course)')...
The one in "I'm" adds a fifth, odd closing tag which confuses the page. It's just like having extra tags when using BBCode.

Simply put, remove the extra apostrophes and everything will be fine.
