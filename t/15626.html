#297554 40 2007-02-15T07:06:24Z Tsukatu
So it's been a little more than a week since I bought a pack of Marlboro Reds and started puffing.
I didn't really get a good nicotine high until the third or fourth day, which was pretty trippy. I stayed rooted to the same spot as I finished half of a cigarette outside of a subway terminal, and as soon as I started moving I was like, "I wonder when the nicotine's gonna kick in OMG I'M AN ASTRONAUT." It was pretty wacky the first time, and only so because it was very different. I had trouble staying balanced and I was just generally happy.
At one point I had three cigarettes within an hour, and that left me feeling like shit. I had this dull headache that I would constantly forget about (don't ask) and this irresistible urge to be active and to sleep simultaneously. That Quadradius game I played with kai was actually played because I wanted to stay up so I wouldn't wake up in a pool of vomit - I wanted to either be sure that the nausea cleared or I voided my stomach while I was conscious.

Ever since then, every time I've smoked it was more or less because I've forced myself to. I really don't want to, but I must press on in the interests of science. Or something like that. I'm starting to dread the highs because I end up constantly off-balance and unable to concentrate, and that's starting to annoy the hell out of me.

Oh, and for kicks I wanted to see what would happen if I went to sleep with a fresh dose of nicotine in my brain. I don't remember any of the dreams I had, but I did sleep through my deafeningly loud alarm. My roommate got up to turn it off and woke me up, but then I just fell back asleep again. I had a perfectly normal night of sleep before that, so I can't find anything else to blame. I ended up missing all of my classes that day, which I actually wasn't terribly happy about.

There remains no good reason for me to smoke at this moment. I don't like the psychological effects the nicotine is having on me (and I'm pretty sure I'm not addicted... then again, those are famous last words), and I know that every other aspect of smoking is detrimental to the health of my body and the health of my wallet.

But I bought this whole pack, and goddamnit I'm gonna smoke the fucker.
(Jesus Christ, how heavy a smoker do you have to be to smoke an entire pack a day?)

#299476 57 2007-02-21T04:50:50Z Tsukatu
Oh, wow, I haven't made the finale post yet?

I finished off my pack two days ago. I had three cigarettes left in the pack, two of which were crumbled enough that they only had half of the normal amount of tobacco in them, so I smoked them all in one last go.

As I progressed through the pack, I developed a stronger desire to just throw the pack away and call it quits, but I forced myself to go through the whole pack... for the experience, goddamnit! (Consequently, I did level up.) The more I did it, the more I hated the thought, the action, and the feeling of sucking that carcinogen-laden carbon monoxide down my esophagus. Sometime in the middle of the pack, I was forgetting entirely that I was doing irreparable harm to my lungs and heart, and my mind was focused on whether or not I thought I could get used to the habit and how the hell people managed to smoke an entire pack a day.
On the day I smoked the last few, I realized I hadn't smoked at all for the last few days and that I should probably finish off. I was staring up at the isolated fire escape stairs I would climb to have a smoke and I was saying to myself, "I don't want to do this. I really, really don't want to do this." I eventually figured that I might as well sprint to the finish and get it all over with.

It just wasn't rewarding. The first nicotine high was pretty fun, I guess, but it was very short-lived and made me feel uncoordinated and dizzy besides. The con's were innumerable. I hated the thought, I hated the feeling of breathing in that thick smoke, and of exhaling it, I hated the taste it left in my mouth, I hated the strong smell it burned into my clothing, I hated having to avoid people who knew me on the way back to my dorm... combine all of those with a generally nauseous feeling every time I lit up, regardless of how many I ended up smoking, and a restless/unfocused mental state I couldn't help after I came back to my dorm.

There were way too many downsides to smoking that would convince me that it's hella not worth it, even if I found any pleasure from the "benefits" it gives.

Though I'm now mildly interested in trying a menthol cigarette, chain smoking, and smoking a cigar, there is no fucking way I'm buying another entire pack or smoking an entire cigar. If I do end up trying either, it'll probably be at a party where someone already has either. And after I know what that's like, I sincerely doubt I'll put that stuff in my body ever again.

So all in all, for me, smoking has been very much like punching myself in the face: intentionally harming myself for absolutely no good reason.
If you don't smoke, don't start.
If you do smoke, well, I just don't get you.
