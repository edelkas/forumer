The year is 2034. You are living in a post-apocalyptic world. Advanced robots created by humans decided that they were not satisfied with the world and wanted a "2nd try." They want to re-create the world and make it better. One of the aspects of the world they are displeased with are humans...

---GAME---

This game is going to work like this. I (General McSlave) am sort of like the storyteller/master of the story. You are the players. I guess you could say it works sort of like Dungeons and Dragons (although I've never played the game so I'm not exactly sure). Anyway, you will give me a description of your character and I will give your character statistics. Once all player slots are filled I will create your situations and the game will begin. Keep in mind that in "2nd Try" you will encounter many obstacles and terrifying beasts. Be sure you are ready. More of the rules (dealing with statistics and such) will be discussed after player slots are filled.
