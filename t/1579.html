Look at game you're talking about. It includes a whopping 8 different enemies (including mines), three types of door (none of which are reusable), tiles, two non-tile or door objects that you can touch without dying, and one collectible. And thats it. Nothing else. Its little wonder that people found new uses for old items. they had to. You can only go so long with that limited an object set. So you start finding new tricks, undocumented uses for the existing items. You can make a moving wall, you can make a stronger launchpad, you can teleport (Not that I want teleports in, they're an example), you can make a level that plays itself. You can screw with a one-way (no, I don't liek that either), you can make a small space that requires momentum to get through, you can make delayed mines and buttons (sadly, they may not work too well what with the timer), you can give n momentum by almost squishing him and thus shooting him out. Most of the useful tricks are well-known enough that the majority of players know about them already, and you can figure them out anyways (there's always the help thing if its too hard). And it adds variety.
I cannot think of another good game like n. I don't mean one that plays like n, I can think of two very similar ones off the top of my head which are very good. I mean a game which limits itself to only the objects found within the first few levels. Where over the course of the entire game you never see something new. No powerups (or downs), no conveyor belts, no water, no bosses, no scrolling levels, no moving laser beams or huge banks of switches, no tnt, no drills. No moving platforms, no gears, no pressure plates, no secret walls. And it has 6 times the number of levels in the two other games I was thinking of combined! At that point you have to ask yourself two questions: How did it work, and how much longer can it work? And if you don't allow creative uses of the current items (not even necessarily new ones, but new uses of oldies) then the answer to the second question is not much longer. You have to let the game evolve.
