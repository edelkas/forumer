#229146 14 2006-09-16T01:20:15Z Clifty
OK...
  • Launch pads: Do I really have to explain it?
  • Trap Doors: Turn on 1/4 snap, place the trap door key 3/4 of a space away from the door.
  • Bounce block: Put any number of bounce blocks down in the same place (normally about 1-15.) and then put a bit of gold on the corners.
  • Thwump: Use thwumps to push you around. If you can half squish the ninja between two thwumps, then he will go flying away.
  • Normal Doors: Put down 6 pieces of gold on a normal door. Sometimes you will die, sometimes you will bounce off, sometimes you will be pushed fowards. It all depends on the speed.
  • Glitch: Too hard to explain, because it contains code editing.
Did that help?
