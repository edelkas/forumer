#78025 9 2005-06-24T14:47:12Z Maniac
Make sure it presents a challenge, but it's not overly difficult. If you can beat it 1 in every 10-20 times, that's probably the best range to go for.

Also, don't use back-and-forth maps or maps that are overly long. They get boring quickly.

One last hint: Don't ever make a space where nothing's happening. Make sure every single millimeter of the map can get you killed if you don't keep moving.
