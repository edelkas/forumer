#291487 6 2007-01-29T22:08:40Z Zakiller2000
I actually liked 80-4, and I completed it after a few runs. I'm progressing way faster through the last episodes than the middle episodes... Timing is definitely the key, and patience is the hand that turns it. (YAY SOMEWHAT DEEP MESSAGE.)

One of my solutions. (spoiler tag'd for those who'd rather find out themselves.)
SpoilerGo counter-clockwise, starting from the mine switch (finish the most difficult one first in my opinion), then kick up the wall to the droids. Wait till the top droid turns, then carefully fall to the switch. Get back up as fast as possible while avoidng the mines and droids. To get to the puppy switches, wait until you're at a certain part of the pipe (PRACTICE) and kick off, flying to the left pipe. Carefully hit all the switches, avoiding the puppies. The thwomp switch just requires timing, and the switch above that just requires wall-climbing. I think those are the only switches, and I might have explained it a litte wrong, but I hope you get most of it.
