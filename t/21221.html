"N+(Ninjas for the nonviolent)

From the look of it, N+ doesn't seem like the kind of game you'd spend your hard-earned points on. It's gray, it's 2-D, and it seems to involve a tiny stick figure as the central character. Pass, right?

You might want to rethink such an easy dismissal. N+ is the Xbox Live Arcade version of a wildly popular flash-based freeware game called simply "N". That's short for "Ninja", which is what your little stick figure guy is supposed to be. But not the shuriken-hurling type, apparently.

There's no killing in N+ unless you count all the times you kill yourself by falling too high or landing on a mine. Blowing yourself to bits is actually fun at times.

But there's a ton of jumping in N+. That's all have to do really - jump around the level until you to the switch that opens the exit door onto the next stage. But nothing is ever that easy. There's a ton of stuff that can kill you, including the aforementioned bombs and a host of other turrets, and security bots that haven't a whit of love for you or your ninja-kind.

There's also of course, a times ticking down, but you can replenish it by collecting gold coins scattered around every level. Does this sound like some classic video game or what?

What makes N+ really new and fresh through is its customizability. You can design your own levels with an in-game editor and upload them for others to play. The vibrant community of N players on the Internet are already doing this. Let's hope N+ on the Xbox Live Arcade find the same kind of support.

Rating: 7.0/10"

-Tom Price
