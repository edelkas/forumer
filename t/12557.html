Couple of problems with it:

1. It isn't good to put many locked and/or trap doors like that. There are better ways to make a level challenging.

2. Quite difficult. Using the thwump to get up to the upper-right platform isn't a bad idea, but there is a floorguard and 3 gauss turrets in the way. 1 well-placed gauss turret is enough.

3. Going back to the beginning to get the door switch. Especially with those 3 gauss turrets.

So, it's not bad, but it has a few flaws in it. If it were on NUMA, I would rate it a 2.5/5.
