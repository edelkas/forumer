no, this is not a contest, but is about contests in general (if there is a better thread for this, move this topic please).

Alright, now for those who are staring a contest please go to this topic.

but what this PSA is really about is the 8th question in Sweep's topic:

Quote: Sweep, 2006-02-24T14:29:40Z
8) Will you have enough time to maintain the entries list, the scores and the judging? Remember that it can be surprisingly time consuming.

Pay close attention to the bold letter words. They are the reason im posting this.

I have noticed that quite a few contests have had to move the judging dates back and/or the judgings never come in until much later. It's stupid that alot of you can make a contest and then not finish it. In one case (Cowgomoo's "to jump or not to jump") judging has not been fully completed and the contest has been going on for five months, way past the "scheduled" end. Another example would be Whykickamoocow's 11x11 competition, where the judgings should have been completed a week or two ago (only one judge has finished).

So, in other words, if you always find yourself busy with "something", dont start a contest. It will just be harder on you if you make a contest a dont find time to finish it, and then people get really pissed and then the whole thing just dies. Then you would have to ask yourself, "what was the point?"
well, you should have been smarter to begin with, because everyone who i've PM'ed, asking when they would finish the contest said they were, "busy with other things."

So, remember, think alot before you leap into making a contest.

(if any mods feel this is in the wrong place or that it is pointless, im sorry, i just really wanted to get this off my chest)

thanks for reading.
