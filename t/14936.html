I had a similar problem a while ago, and I wasn't able to use my CD-drive because of that.

Here's the sentence: Your power supply is underpowered. In other words, it can't provide your master HDD with the power it needs to run properly. Buy a new power supply, or do as I did, mount a second supply in your computer case.
(I think you should insulate the second power supply so that it doesn't have direct contact with the computer chassis, just to be on the safe side)
