The movie Most is a sad tale about a father and his son. I have not seen the movie, but I'll try to retell it as best I can. It's about a man who works a bridge, letting it up so that boats can pass under. One day, he takes his young son to work with him. As the day goes by, work passes slowly. Eventually, a boat comes to pass under the bridge. The man throws the switch, and the bridge rises. But he soon realizes that a train is coming, so he knows he must lower the bridge in order for it to pass over. As he goes to grab the lever, he notices his son is missing. After looking frantically, he finds that his son is stuck in the gears of the bridge. Now he must come to a decision. He must either pull the switch, letting the train pass by, saving a few hundreds of lives, or save his son, and let the train crash, possibly killing all of the passengers. Here is where you come in.

Which choice would you make? Would your save you son, or would you save the lives of the passengers? (You cannot save both)

For me, it would be a tough decision. Of course, I am not a father, so I couldn't really understand the scenario to its fullest. But if I place someone elses there, such as my sister, or even my parents (odd thought, though) it would be extremely difficult to bring myslef to let them die. I realize that I would be saving others, despite the fact that they are total stangers, but I couldn't live with myself if I knew I was killing someone I loved. Yet, based on my family beliefs, the person stuck in the gears would want me to save the people on the train, but of course I have no way of knowing that for sure. So, after thinking about it for a while, I would have to save the lives of the people on the train. I would never feel the same again, though.
