#46079 10 2005-01-14T03:44:13Z Tsukatu
Not really a magic trick, and not really "squeezing" between anything, it's simply how the game copes with movement and massive velocities. When the ninja moves, instead of having the pixels he's made of physically haul bacon over to the new location, his previous position's pixels disappear, and then they reappear in the new position. He doesn't exist anywhere in between. The game does this so fast that you see it as fluid movement, but really, he's making like a Bohr electron and doing that funny cilantro trick where he can move from one place to another without ever actually being in between those two places!
So because the ninja is moving so damn fast, the space he travels in that split second is enough that the ninja exists in the open spaces on the way up. As far as the game is concerned, there are no tiles blocking the way, since it doesn't detect any solid tiles directly in the way of where the ninja is.
