Exact same thing happened to me.

For no discernable reason , this remedy works, however.

First, open up your C drive, then documents and settings, then your user name, then desktop, then n_v1pc-1, (or something like that.)

Finally, open the userlevels.txt file, scroll down to the bottom, then close it. Absolute finally, simply open the N application in the n_v1pc-1 folder!

It works every time for me, but you have to do it every time you want to use userlevels. I know this problem very well. I figured it out after people kept telling me to "check" that the userlevels is actually in the folder. It was, I opened it, then boom.
