::: FAQ :::

Q: What is a doppelgnger?
A: A doppelgnger is the ghostly in some cases, the physical double of a living person.

Q: What do doppelgngers have to do with N?
A: If doppelgngers didn't have anything to do with N, why would I start a Doppelgnger Contest? You'll find out soon enough... Let's lay down the rules.

::: Rules :::

1. Mapmakers will work in pairs to create two maps, which will comprise one entry.
2. To make these two maps, the mapmakers will make a tileset. The first mapmaker will make a map using that tileset, and the second mapmaker will make a map using the same tileset, only inverted (All tiles become open space, all open space becomes tiles).
3. Entries will be submitted in one .txt file with both levels in userlevels format ($name#author#Doppleganger#code).
4. Each map will be judged separately out of 20 according to the rubric.
5. The scores for each map will be added together for a total score out of 40.
6. Although this would be an unlikely occurence, mapmakers may not use old maps.

::: Scoring Rubric :::

*/10 ~ Enjoyment
*/10 ~ Aesthetics

::: Prizes :::

First Place, Second Place, and Third Place ~ Signature Tag
Fourth Place and on ~ A pat on the back.

The final results are attached to this post. Thank you for competing!
