Think of some of the main aspects of a good map
-Flow (kinda falls under gameplay)
-Looks
-Object Placement
-Gameplay
-Originality

I think the most important is flow. Im not talking perfect flow like a race map, but just enough so you can move around a fairly good amount.

That map you posted has ideas and gameplay that are all becoming overused. So try coming up with the next big thing. Theres been bounceblock bridges that became popular, and the circle gold pattern around rockets is getting boring.

Basically, just come up with a new idea, and take your time to use it the best you can.
