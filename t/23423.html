#458518 3 2008-08-05T17:16:32Z Raigan
This is definitely nothing specific to N+, my guess is that it's a problem with the XBLA DRM scheme, which apparently is getting fixed in the new dashboard update.

When you buy a game, it gets registered to (1) your physical xbox via its serial number, (2) your gamertag.

To play the full version of a game, it needs to verify that you've bought it via (1) _OR_ (2). (1) requires that you're running it on the xbox you bought it on (not just the HD/memory card AFAIK), (2) requires that you have an internet connection.

Are you sure you didn't have an internet connection the previous times? And/or possibly you need to have your gamertag info on the HD.

Basically, I don't know

But the stupid DRM stuff is definitely annoying, NONE of our games work if our internet goes out, because we've had RRoD twice and thus verification method (1) no longer works..
