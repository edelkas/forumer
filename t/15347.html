Welcome to the userbar and signature competition!

Basically this is a competition where anyone who likes creating userbars or signatures can create two per week according to the weekly specifications and submit them here. NOTE: You are allowed to change your entries during the week.
The top 3 entries each week will win a userbar that they can put into their signature. They will get to choose the colors of the userbar and how they change from one to the other.
The winner of each week will also get a level dedicated to them.

JUDGING: There will be four criteria for judging:
1: Use of color(s) n/10
2: Creativity n/10
3: Use of the week's theme n/10
4: "Just looking at it" n/10 (basically this is for how good it looks when you just look at it. Hence the name.

Judges' marks will be averaged and the winning 3 entries will be posted here.

Judges[/u] will rate each and every picture in the week after it closes. All judgings must be emailed to me within 5 days of the week's entries closing.

Specifications for userbars and signatures:
All Userbars MUST be no bigger than 400X20 pixels.
All Signatures MUST be no bigger than 400X200 pixels.
EDIT: I changed the size. Signatures can now be 10 times taller than userbars. You can go over this size, but I (and possibly other judges) will penalize you.

If you wish to join this competition, post here saying so!

TOPIC FOR WEEK 4:
Team battles. This is for the whole game of N. Basically, get two (or more) teams of enemies from N and have them battling in some way. Any use of weapons allowed, like maybe having N shooting a chaingun or something. Each team must have at least 2 "characters" and you're allowed to use each character more than once.

---Previous weeks---

WEEK 3:
Good Ol' Fashioned Fighting. This can be the whole game of N or just the little character N. You choose.
This has to have something to do with N (either the game or the Ninja) and also something to do with fighting, and all melee fighting at that. You're allowed to use things like knives, swords, hand-to-hand combat, maybe even a matrix sort of thing, with no guns or weapons developed after 1800.
You may, however, have laser drones and chaingun drones, as long as they aren't shooting.
Feel free to use N poser to get some nice poses of hand-to-hand combat if you're doing that.

A reminder that entries will be back to 2 per week from now on, unless I say otherwise.

This week will end on Sunday the 11th of Feb, at midnight GMT.


WEEK TWO:
N Sports! Yes, the whole game of N!
Make your picture something to do with N, and something to do with sport. For example, you could have two Ns with little tennis rackets and the ball could be a floor guard. Or, If you're good at 3D, you could have a picture of N wolding a gold club which looks 3D!
Experiment with the topic, and remember: You are allowed to submt 3 (YES, THREE!!!) entries this week because it's so long.
Feel free to use whatever you want to get pictures of N and enemies etc. Please have things that are form the game though. No made-up drones.

This week ends on the 4th of February.



WEEK ONE:
N. Not the whole game though, just N. Try and cram as much about N as you can into your userbar or signature!
NOTE: It's fine if you have N getting shot or his blood or something like that in there, as long as you DON'T have the enemy that shot him as well!
NOTE 2 (too, get it? It's a bad pun...): You are allowed to use any programs to get N. You can draw him in an N art if you want, then put it in your signature or userbar. Whatever you think is good, as long as it has N.
ALSO, your entry will NOT be disqualified if you have other things in the image, but it is likely to recieve less marks in the "weeks theme" criteria.

Week one ends on Sunday the 28th at Midnight GMT.
