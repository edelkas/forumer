Quote: macaddict_17, Jan 14 2006, 11:35 PM
Sweep, you do know that by listening to full length previews, you could record them using WireTap and have the song without buying, right?
You could do that with music on digital radio, or you could do that with music on myspace. That's past the point. You can get any music for free if you want to break the law. It's easy. Peer-to-peer, filesharing and all that.

We could load tracks with DRM to fix it, but the truth is that just stops people from playing music when and where they want to. We could not let people preview tracks, but the truth is that if you're buying from and independant music site then you'd like to hear the music before you buy.

We believe in something called trust. We could put as many chains and locks around the music as we want but people will always be able to cheat the system. DRM has no effect except causing general consumer annoyance. We want people to share music, we want people to hear music, and the moral issues are up to them. There will always be idiots who lack morality, but there is no point fighting against them.

We'd rather people got to hear what they were about to buy before they bought. It's a common courtesy that Amazon and iTunes lack FULL commitment on.
