a minute ago i found out how to prevent objects appearing ouside the level

first, when you save level data do the "userlevels.txt" do NOT forget to set a "#" at the end of the level-data.
then there will no problems playing it in "User Levels"

second, when you load a level to NED you have to copy it to the textbox (withOUT "#") and then CLICK SOMEWHERE ELSE... so that the blinking "|" is not in the textbox.

then press [L] to load the level, this should work... no more objects outside
