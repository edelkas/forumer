#72295 19 2005-06-04T18:24:54Z brickman
You know what? I think that the talk about the bounceblock has made me realise (or think I've realized) just WHY this happens: the bounceblocks and walls have mass, while the oneways and doors are only one pixel thick in terms of how the engine regards them (regardless of how they look). When you walljump on a bounceblock or tile it works normally, but something about having just a single-pixel thick line makes it screw up when trying to walljump, so he can only do it if the game counts him as clinging to the wall. But fortunately, you can work it so that he counts as being pressed against the wall without actually grabbing it and losing momentum by just holding onto the arrow key, so it doesn't look like it.

Ah well. I'm pretty sure I've got the first half right, anyways.
