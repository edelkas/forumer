#1132 1 2004-10-18T00:24:11Z brickman
I think that the timer being a requirement may be causing the player to be far more hasty than they should, and this takes away from the experience. Sure, it shows skill to quickly get through a level, but evading enemies is also important. But with a mandatory 90 second timer, and no way of knowing whether the next 4 levels will be gold filled cakewalks or long grueling ordeals, the message to the player is that stopping/wasting time is not allowed.
A perfect example of this is the level bore hunting (I forget the number). If you just wing it, the level is 97.9% impossible, because the laser drones will be in a position so that when you get onto a certain platform they already have the top of it covered in laser by the time you land. I spent forever trying it to no avail. Then, just for fun, I set the game to "practice mode" to play through the previous episodes again, and when I got to bore hunting I realised that if you just let the drones move for about 7 extra seconds, they will be on the wrong side of their platform when you get to the crucial part. It took me about 5 minutes to beat it then, and another 5 to win in normal mode. But under the pressure of a timer, the mere thought of wasting a good 7 seconds of your precious time is unthinkable. So you just muscle through it, or try to, and end up using less skill but more effort.
I think we need to change n to a points system. There is a "par time" for any given episode, and beating it grants you points for the time left. Getting gold also gives you both points and extra bonus time. Running out of time means no points for this level. There would still be a hard mode, which would be the origional n with slightly higher points as a reward.
Or at the very least, give each level its own time limit.
