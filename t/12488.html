This could be a contest that has never been done, despite the fact it is very simple.

RULES
  • You must submit your maps to NUMA, and post a link here.
  • All maps must be KRADDAs. (Keep the Rocket Alive, Don't Do Anything)
  • You can only submit 2 maps in the contest.
  • Judges can submit maps, but cannot vote for their own map.
JUDGING

The judging is very simple. Basically each of the judges picks any number of maps that they like, and they are all awarded one point. The map with the most points at the end is the winner. If there is a tie, all the judges revote for the tied maps. A judge cannot vote for their own map.
If you want to be a judge, post here.
