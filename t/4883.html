Requirements: Windows 98/Me/2000/2003/XP

You ever wanted to change whom the PC is registered to and change to manufacter logo? Here's a neat way.
First, create a new text document anywhere. Rename it to OEMINFO.INI.
Add this:
[General]
Manufacturer=Your Manufacturer
Model=Model #

Save the .INI and in XP/2000, move it to
%windows directory%/system32
In 98/me, it is
%windows directory%/system
If you want to add a picture, get your picture/make one. It has to be no larger than Height-114 pixels and Width-180 pixels.
Save it as OEMLOGO.bmp, (IT MUST BE IN A BMP FORMAT). Move it to the same directory where we moved OEMINFO.ini, (depending on your OS).

How to edit your name and organization.
Go to start, hit run, type "REGEDIT," and hit enter.
Go to:
HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion
Now on the right panel, you'll see many objects. Look for the objects
"RegisteredOrganization." Right click it, hit modify. FIll in anything you want for that and hit "OK."
If you want to change your name, go to
HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion
and right click the object "Registered Owner."
Change it to whatever you like, and hit "OK."
Exit out of regedit. No reboot required. Go to control panel, hit "system." You should have something like this:
