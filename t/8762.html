#161971 2 2006-04-05T01:56:23Z Twistkill
Right-click and save the image to your computer. Then go to the reply window here, at the bottom of the window you'll see a little box that says "Attach Image" Browse for the picture, then click ok, and then hit reply. You're done! You can do this with any file type, but you can only do one at a time.

If you want to upload multiple images, it's slightly longer:

Save image to your computer. Go to http://www.imageshack.us and host the image. When it's done, at the bottom, you'll see a URL code that says "Direct Link to image" Copy the whole URL, then go to the reply window. At the top, you'll see a button that says IMG. Hit that, paste the URL in the box that pops up, hit ok, and then click reply. The image will show up in your post.

Attaching the image is a little faster, but with imageshack you can post several pictures at once, and you don't have that little "Attached Image" text above your picture.
