#55979 12 2005-03-25T18:51:23Z nevermore
Ok, if you die, there are two things to do. Either move the mouse where you want to come back alive, and push [enter] or push [`] and press [R].
For demos, paste them into the demo box, push [3] and then push [Q]. After the level finishes resetting push [P] and turn capslock on again.
