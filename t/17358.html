#321383 2 2007-05-15T12:06:01Z George
Firstly, make sure that everything in the boxes 'username', 'password' and 'email' are empty. Then select a username and password and type them into their respective boxes. Then click 'Create new user'.

The method above should work without fail. If you still get 'error', I suggest you check your Internet connection and firewall. Often security software may block N from accessing the Internet.
