A 5 year old can play N? I better get my little sister to start playing N then

actually, we got an email from someone who had three generations of their family playing! i think the youngest was their 7-year-old daughter.

i think my favorite email of all time is this one:
"
And so today, as I saw the exit screen and read the message "this game is dedicated to the brave ninjas everywhere...", it occured to me that the game could be viewed as a very interesting social satire.

In fact, one of the basic premises that we impose upon ourselves as we begin playing is that the robots are evil - when, actually, they are so faceless and mechanized that it hardly makes sense to judge them at all, or at least any more than we would an elevator.

Further, our hero's motivation is unclear, and yet we assume him to be a hero although the only thing he seems to do is steal. Everyone loves a non-violent crook, so is he 'the good guy' because he is fighting against the odds or simply because he is the only thing in the whole game world that seems to posess a soul?

This brings me to the sorroundings - the aspect of the game I find possibly the most meaningful. Each uniformly grey level can indeed be seen as an allegory - either of bureaucracy itself or a dehumanized, bureaucratic society as a whole. Each level is intricately unique on countless levels and yet it all boils down to the same thing: getting enough gold to survive and moving on.

It takes some passion, skilled dodging and serious effort, but by the time you realize it, the grey machinery has already consumed you - you are, after all, a gold-craving puppet in a world populated by soulless robots that is much too large for you to understand.
"
