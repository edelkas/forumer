#349337 2 2007-07-31T13:37:31Z LittleViking
The easiest way to do this is just to have a mailto link.
<a href="mailto:vinos@email.com">E-mail the administrator</a>
If you want to automatically add a subject line, do this:
<a href="mailto:vinos@email.com?subject=Message from your website">E-mail the administrator</a>
Anything more complicated than that would require some javascript to process it, but I think this should be enough for almost anything.
