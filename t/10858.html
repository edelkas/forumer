#9730 1 2006-06-27T16:15:29Z LittleViking
So here's the idea. We had a small group of people talking to each other through Ventrilo's voice chat. Each of us opened Ned and started telling each other what gameplay bits to add to a level. All of us follow the same instructions as we're giving them to each other. Of course, the explanations are naturally kinda vague (i.e. "Put some E tiles around up in the air" "Now put bounce blocks near the E tiles") so the maps all come out looking differently. After about 10 or 15 minutes, we each have a finished map (albiet, maybe a horrible map, but that's not the point). When you play the maps, you should see some similarites, but they're still very different. And here are the results:

Anyone is free to join in, as long as we don't get more than about seven people (the max load for the server). You need a microphone and the Ventrilo software, then PM me for the server info.
