#124198 3 2005-12-18T06:47:28Z Killfest2
The key with this level is being smooth with your jumps and landings, and a bit of luck at the bottom. You can't afford to land on corners, or be too slow running to the next jump etc.

And at the top, jump up, draw the two chain drones into firing, and just as they finish, go and get the switch, capatalising on their recharge time; repeat to the bottom.
