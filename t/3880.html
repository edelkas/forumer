I just had a great idea. At least, I think it's great.

A secret unlockable that makes N invulnerable to self-inflicted deaths. You could survive the biggest of drops. Imagine the replay value this would add to the game, going back through all your favorite levels and leaping from the highest point down to the very bottom, not having to worry about dying from gravity. Obviously you wouldn't be able to unlock new levels or get highscores using this mode, but it would still be fun to toy around with.

I don't think this has been suggested before. When searching, I found some similar stuff (slider to adjust gravity level, etc.), but not this. I thought it was kind of a neat idea for a simple unlockable, just so there is more of a reward for beating the game... (or some other accomplishment if applicable). I don't know whether it would be difficult to code, but I am just throwing this out there.

So what do you think?
