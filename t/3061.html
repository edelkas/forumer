I know this has come up before, but I'm not sure it's ever had its own topic (ooh, go go grammar). So here are some ideas I've had about an overall highscore list.

When you hit the submit button, your score is uploaded regardless of if you're in the top 20 or not. Then on the highscores website, there's a list of everyone with their ranks all summed together (a 0th place is 0 points, a 50th place is 50 points). So everyone would have somewhere between 0 and some odd thousand points. For examples see some video game high score sites.

In addition, there could be a place to do head to head comparisons with the people around you, so you can see what levels you have to improve to increase your rank. Another example.

Please add your comments about how you think this would work.
