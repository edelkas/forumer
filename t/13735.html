So when replying to a topic or starting your own one. Can you see the button that says IMG above the area where you type your message. Well when you click on that button it comes up with a new dialogue window which says to post the URL for that image. To get a URL for your image you have to go to an image hosting site. eg: http://photobucket.com/ There they will let you upload your pictures to their servers nd they will give you a URl forthat image you then post that URL with the tags [IMG]*insert Url of image here*[/URL]. But by clicking the button I mentioned befor it automaticly makes the [IMG] tags for you.

Another way to post an image is to click the link below the area where you post your writing which says File Attachments and browse your computer till you find the image.



Hope that helped. If you have any other troubles then post again.
