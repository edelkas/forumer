Artrage 2

Here is something some of you may be interested in, a paint program called Artrage 2. I found it on the school computers, and fiddled around with it. I liked it, and went home and looked it up. Voila! Artrage 2 apearred on Google. There is a free version, and a purchasable version with a couple of extra features. It focuses on realism, trying to make it seem as though you are actually painting something. For instance, if you keep holding the brush down, the paint on it starts to thin and run out. Or, if you brush on top of another colour, they will blend. I like this feature a lot, and this program is overall great fun to toy around with. And, it is a painting program, and you never see many of these around anymore, especially of this quality. Nowadays companies see larger profit out of image editing programs, and never give image creating programs a chance.

Artrage is brilliant. IF you're really good, you can make something that looks amazing. But, the best feature is the trace one - you can trace over images, with paint or whatever you want, and whenever you click to start a new brush stroke, it automatically changes the colour of your stroke to the point from which you're stroking.

It's brilliant, and so easy to use.
