Ok, much like in the map rating thread, in this thread you must demo on at least one map for every one you post.
I'm starting this because demos mean a lot to me, it shows where the map is flowy, where it is too complex to be well understood, and what parts would need difficulty adjustments.
I don't say it would prevent you from playtesting your maps, it's only to see how others understand the gameplay of the map, and enjoy it.

Rules :

* Along with your demo you must rate, but you need not declare what you rated.
* You may only post one map per demo.
* Death demos allowed, of course.
* You may post a worthwhile demo, like more than 10-15 seconds.
* Nice moves are welcome (close calls, combos, etc...) ! Keep in mind the aim of the thread !
* You may only post a map if you post a demo on the map directly above yours, so if you have already demoed the most recent map, you can't post another map.


What do you think about it ?
