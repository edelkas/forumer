The Completed Column

The Completed Column is the culmination of my work for the past few weeks. It involves 51 levels, of all difficulty, ranging from so easy you could do it blindfolded, to nigh impossible. Every level after 08-0 (inclusive) is very hard, so you can pat yourself on the back everytime you complete one. I'd love to see demos from any level, and I will compete with anyone for fastest times if they want to. (I never said I'd win!) Just to let you know, I've completed every level, so you should all be able to do so to, with a bit of patience.

The two people who have helped me most on this pack are rocket_thumped, and Wedgie. For that they each get a dedicated level, as well as the whole pack being dedicated to them:

rocket_thumped

He play tested everylevel, from 00-0 to 07-4. Without him, this pack would only be half the quality it is now. For that he is awarded 02-0: What have we left for our rockets?

I love you man, keep on rocking.

Wedgie

Wedgie played every level in the pack to make sure it wasn't cheatable, and also play tested 08-0 to 09-5 for me. He has insane skills, but even he was put to the test on some of the levels . Without him, many of these levels would be cheatable, so for his help he is awarded 07-3: He's a pirate

Wedgie, you are teh OMG! when it comes to playing N. Keep going dude.




And without further ado, the pack. Enjoy.
