Spoiler: [bAfrican Chicken Peanut Soup[/b]]
===========================

INGREDIENTS:
---
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 clove of garlic, crushed
1/2 yellow onion, finely diced
1 large carrot, grated
1 sweet red pepper, thinly sliced
1/3 cup tomato paste
2/3 cup creamy peanut butter (NOT natural or 'old-fashoined')
1/2 teaspoon cayenne
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
4 cups chicken broth
1 1/2 - 2 cups shredded cooked chicken

===========================

PROCEDURE:
---
1. Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan and add garlic, onion, carrot and red pepper. Simmer until onion is transparent.
2. Stir in tomato paste until it is evenly absorbed. Once it is absorbed, stir in the peanut butter and stir until the peanut butter is absorbed into the vegetable mixture.
3. Add the cayenne, salt, and black pepper, then gradually stir in the chicken broth, 1 cup at a time.
4. When the broth has emulsified with the vegetable-peanut butter mixture, add shredded chicken.
5. Simmer for 15 minutes (I simmer it uncovered). More salt or cayenne may be added for taste.

===========================

RECOMMENDATIONS:
---
1. Before heating any pans or starting any cooking, get all of your ingredients chopped, diced, sliced, grated, shredded, etc. It's difficult to cook this meal if you don't have everything prepared as stated.

2. I like to use crunchy Jif peanut butter. The little peanut kernels are great for texture and Jif tastes delicious. If you don't have crunchy Jif peanut butter then use the best tasting peanut butter you know of that fits the requirements.

3. If you can, buy a pre-cooked chicken. It's less work because you don't have to cook the chicken yourself, and pre-cooked chickens from the store usually taste pretty good. Don't use the skin though, I just eat that as I shred the chicken.

4. Although the recipe says to do everything in a saucepan, I like to cook the vegetable mixture in the saucepan only. When you get to the part where you have to add tomato paste I transfer the vegetable mixture to a heated soup pot, then add the tomato paste once I have the vegetables in the pot. From there it's really easy because you don't have to worry about overflowing the saucepan once you start pouring the chicken broth in.

5. Make a double batch! This soup tastes 4 times better after 24 hours, and making a double batch allows a lot of leftovers.

===========================
Spoiler: [bsouthpaw's Cinnamon Milkshake[/b]]
===========================

INGREDIENTS:
Vanilla Ice Cream
Milk
Cinnamon
Almond flavoring (NOT organic, the kind that has alcohol in it)

===========================

PROCEDURE:
This is my own creation, so I haven't taken exact measurements, but I will approximate what I have used.

1. Make 2 cups of a regular milkshake with the vanilla ice cream and milk. If you can make this in a large mug with a fairly wide mouth that would be perfect.

2. Coat the top of the milkshake with cinnamon (about 2 teaspoons). Stir until the cinnamon starts clumping together in little specks in the milkshake.

3. 1/8 ounce Almond flavoring. Stir until you feel it has been mixed in thoroughly. Enjoy!

===========================

RECOMMENDATIONS:

1. If there is too much almond flavor, or not enough cinnamon just experiment with measurements until you get it just right! This may take anywhere from the second try or the 7th try, but it's worth it once you have figured out how much of each you like in your milkshake.

===========================

scythe33's rum pie!
A classic, I tell ya.

9-inch pie shell (already baked)
1/2 cup sugar
2 tablespoons cornstarfch
1/2 teaspoon salt
2 cups milk
2 eggs, separated
2 teaspoons unflavored gelatin
3 tablespoons cold water
2 tablespoons rum
1 ounce melted unsweetened chocolate (cool)
1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar.
Stir together 1/2 cup sugar, the cornstarch, and salt in saucepan. Blend milk and egg yolks; stir into sugar mixture. Cook over medium heat, stirring consantly, just until mixture boils. Reserve 1 cup of the custard mixture; set aside.
Soften gelatin in cold water, stir into remaining custard mixture (not the 1 cup reserved, the other part). Stir in rum. Place pan in bowl of ice and water or chill in refrigerator, stirring occasionally, until mixture mounds slightly when dropped from spoon. Combine chocolate and the reserved custard mixture. Pour into baked pie shell.
Beat egg whites and cream of tartar until foamy. Beat in 1/3 cup sugar, 1 tablespoon at a time; continue beating until stiff and glossy. Do not underbeat. Fold remaining custard mixture into meringue. Spread on chocolate mixture. Chill at least 3 hours or until set (overnight is good). If desired, garnish with whipped cream; sprinkle with shaved chocolate.

Szechuan Noodles

Ingredients

* 6 garlic cloves, chopped
* 1/4 cup fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
* 1/2 cup vegetable oil
* 1/2 cup tahini (sesame paste)
* 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter
* 1/2 cup good soy sauce
* 1/4 cup dry sherry
* 1/4 cup sherry wine vinegar (or try 1/8 balsamic vinegar,1/8 red wine vinegar)
* 1/4 cup honey
* 1/2 teaspoon hot chili oil
* 2 tablespoons dark sesame oil
* 1/2 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper
* 1/8 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
* 1 lb spaghetti
* 1 red bell pepper, julienned
* 1 yellow bell pepper, julienned
* 5 scallions, sliced diagonally (white and green parts)

Directions

1.Place the garlic and ginger in a food processor fitted with a steel blade.

2.Add the vegetable oil, tahini, peanut butter, soy sauce, sherry, sherry vinegar, honey, chili oil, sesame oil, and ground peppers.

3.Puree the sauce.

4.Add a splash of oil to a large pot of boiling salted water and cook the spaghetti until just done, not soft.

5.Drain the pasta in a colander, place it in a large bowl, and while still warm, toss with 3/4 of the sauce.

6.Add the red and yellow bell peppers and scallions; toss well.

7.Serve warm or at room temperature.

8.The remaining sauce may be added, as needed, to moisten the pasta.

9.Enjoy!

Here's a classic

Lobster Thermidor(for two):


Ingredients:

1 lobster, preferably live
3-4 spines celery, depending on size, medium to small dice
1 medium onion, medium to small dice
1-3 cloves garlic, depending on personal taste, minced
2 T(ablespoon) shallot, minced
1 t(easpoon) fresh lemon juice
1/4#(pound) butter
1/4# flour
1 1/2 C(up) milk
1/2 t cayenne pepper
S+P (salt and Pepper)
1 T parmesan cheese

Method:

Steam or boil lobster, 7-8 minutes if steaming; 10-12 if boiling. Cool in ice water. After lobster is cool, split in half lengthwise. Remove meat from tail and claws, keeping half shell of body whole. The claws don't really matter, you can do whatever you have to to get meat out of them*. Discard all undesirable innards.

Saute onions, garlic, celery and shallots in all of butter, until transparent. Add all of flour, whisking constantly until smooth. Add milk. This is where it gets tricky. Depending on the temperature of the milk, you may need more, maybe not so much. What you'll be looking for is a semi firm mixture, stiffer than pancake batter but not as stiff as, say, cookie dough.

Turn off flame.

Dice lobster meat, add to mixture, add lemon juice, cayenne, s+p. Mix thoroughly.

Scoop mixture into halves of shells, mound it up nice and full, sprinkle with parmesan and bake for about 10-15 minutes at 425f-475f.


Bon appetit!



*For a truly stunning presentation, while claws are still attached to body, carefully cut the very tip of the pincers off. Use shears to cut down the top and bottom of the claw and then around the base off the claw, where the claw meets the knuckle. If done correctly, the sides of the shell of the claw will fold away like a book, and you can carefully pry them away and you should be left with just the meat at the end.
This, however, is a very complicated procedure; one that I am just mastering.



Edit: I forgot the tarragon. How could I forget the tarragon? Add 1 T minced tarragon(fresh, 1 t if dry) immediately after the milk.

My all time favorite: Heavenly Egg Drop Soup

6 cups Vegetable Stock
1/2 matchstick-cut bamboo shoots
4 dried forest mushrooms, soaked to soften & cut in matchsticks
2 free-run eggs, beaten
2 tbsp. chives, chopped
1/2 tsp. Oriental sesame oil

Heat Stock and add Bamboo and mushrooms. Simmer for 15 minutes, then bring to a boil. Reduce to a simmer and gradually add beaten eggs in a thin stream. Stir constantly.
Remove from heat, add chives and sesame oil.
