Character Bio:

Name: Sargvaas
Race: Human
Age: 27
Region: Haven't decided yet
Profession: Berserker/Mercenary
Bio:
Sargvaas grew up on the streets of [metropolitan human city I haven't decided upon yet] as an orphan. He was raised by a group of street thugs, whom he had witnessed steal and murder for the first six years of his life. When his gang was finally caught by city officials, he managed to hide away, and wasn't caught. From then on, he had to resort to taking action himself in order to get food and such. As a boy, he slept high up on a rooftop of a wealthy family's house, who never found out about it. He would rise early in the morning and head straight into the slums of the city, where he eventually founded a small gang of orphans. Because of their young age and their nature, he had to frequently discipline his gang, and so became a quite capable fighter. Soon, other neighborhood boys got the same idea, and by age 13, he was looking at gang warfare across the city. He had moved into an abandoned block of houses in the slums, and his gang used this as a sort of base of operations. At this point in his life, he still did not have a name; everyone just referred to him as "the boss."
Children yet so young did not have the stomach for fighting with weapons, so the main form of fighting was with one's own fists. Sargvaas enjoyed fighting, he loved it. His gang made a name for itself as the most militant gang in his part of town. During a confrontation with another gang, he would charge ahead of his gang (and became pretty athletic) and immediately, at the start of the fight, be within striking range of as many enemies as could fit, and somehow last through the initial part of the fight and continue to take down the opposition with incredible alacrity. His favorite battle tactic has always been, and will most likely always be, fear.
Back at home, he would spend his time lifting crude weights. He believed that if he were to be the strongest, the gods would favor him and pay special attention to him, especially his patron deity, the god of strength and luck, with a heavy lean toward recklessness in combat. So far, his faith has not let him down, and he does seem to be favored by his god, as he escapes unscathed out of impossible situations, in which he places himself, routinely. Over the years, he has also become by far the strongest of the gang leaders physically due to his habit of lifting weights when he thinks, eats, is nervous, etc.
When he turned 16, things took a nasty turn for his gang. A new member joined his ranks, and quickly displaces him as gang leader. When he challenged the newcomer for the top position, he was deceived and run out of town. He wandered alone for a month or so, barely finding enough food to survive, usually from passing merchants and messengers (whom he ambushed, which sometimes got him in a bit of trouble). While wandering particularly far from civilization one evening, he found himself surrounded by lizardmen (near the desert... try and tell me a snake is more adapted for the desert than a lizard), who knocked him out and took him home out of curiosity. It turns out that he was the first human they had seen, and so they began playing with him. When they realized he could work, they offered to include him in their tribe. He was very much like them, not surprisingly, and enjoyed their company. He was slowly taught their language, and in return he attempted to teach them his. The result was his fluency in their language (he was surrounded by them, and that was the only language he could speak with them in), and their loose understanding of a heavy street dialect of his language. He was also taught to read and write their language, and he regretted never having picked up a book (to read, anyway; he had probably bashed in some skulls with dictionaries) and never learning to read.
When he was 19, his tribe met another lizard tribe, and war broke out. He fit right in, fighting exceptionally for a human, yet on par with a lizardman. After a few more wars, involving usage of weapons, blood, and death, he became a more adept fighter than even the lizardmen in his tribe, rivaling their chieftan. After seeing him in combat, the lizardmen decided to give him an appropriate name: Sargvaas, meaning "the bloody-handed" in their tongue (they had previously referred to him as "Human," as in, "hey Human, pass the goat").
One fateful day, their tribe moved too close to a town and began receiving harassment from city guards and mercenaries... so they decided to do what any rational tribe would do: they sacked and burned the town. Sargvaas noticed that he felt no tie to humans, since they had shown him no compassion or honesty, while he felt right at home with his lizardman tribe. They gained plenty of loot from the town, and Sargvaas became interested in human culture again. The lizardmen had made an attempt to speak in his human tongue to keep his fluency in it, and it paid off during their assault on the town, causing confusion and panic among the city guard.
The tribe was also very interested in humans, and so they began preying on small villages and hamlets. In doing this, Sargvaas obtained a few suits of armor of exceptional quality (from captains of the city guard, mainly, which only fit him in his tribe) and human weapons. He realized that he was actually far better using human weapons, since he had been using unweildly (for him) lizardman weapons, and his massive strength and speed (do you know how hard it is to swing a greatclub that's a size category too large for you about as fast as a drawn katana? that's how this guy learned) made him an awesome force to reckon with in melee combat.
At about the age of 24, his tribe had migrated to his home city, and Sargvaas became consumed with a need for revenge. He convinced his tribe to make a full-scale attack on his home city, in his old slums district. He went in by himself to a different part of town and bought (with spoils from other towns) several large cloaks, which he gave to his tribe. His tribe, however, was having doubts, and so those who believed in him separated from those who didn't want the attack (Sargvaas' invading force included 7 of the fiercest warriors of his tribe and their chieftan, the rest stayed back).
Sargvaas and his crew walked deep into the heart of his old slum district during the night, all wearing heavy cloaks that concealed their appearances. He quickly found his old base of operations and entered silently. Taking his old gang (whom he had forsaken) and their new boss (the same one that displaced Sargvaas) by surprised, it was an incredibly easy victory, and all that was his ex-gang lay in bloody chunks on the ground. He had gotten personal revenge against the gang leader, by the way, and it was a very short fight: he swung three times, missing, shattering the leader's weapon, and finally cutting him in half across his torso, the whole fight lasting less than four seconds. However, when another gang crashed the party, as their idea of a secret midnight attack, the lizardmen got carried away and began killing any human they saw. Sargvaas went with them, and they managed to bring down a few gangs, some of them neither entirely involved nor directly hostile to them. The city guard finally rushed in, and after a long fight involving the deaths of countless gangmembers and city officials (kill:death ratio was about 26:1 for Sargvaas and his buddies... they caused quite some havoc), the lizardmen lay dead, and Sargvaas returned to hiding.
He escaped the city and moved to far-away lands, where he built up a reputation as a hired mercenary. Within a month of moving away, he received news that his old town had fallen to lizardmen... apparently the more peaceful members of his original tribe saw that the city became extremely vulnerable after Sargvaas' suicidal assault, and they gathered allies and took the city by storm. Newspapers explained that they came in two waves: one expert group to serve as a first wave, and then the second wave, ten times the size, came in for a crushing victory over the city. Lizardmen then flooded into the city and settled there, becoming the first major city of lizardmen in history, its population soaring to rival the old human population. In a way, it triggered a new cultural revolution for the lizardmen. Only few papers mentioned a rumor of a human leading their first charge, and these rumors quickly disappeared and were deemed irrelevant, with suggestions that the human had died during the attack.
For three years, Sargvaas has lived as a mercenary. He has been a town guard, a member of the militia, and an extremely reliable messenger, but the biggest demand for him has been in the position of bodyguard. His strength and fighting instinct in conjunction with his strong faith family have made him a loyal and fierce warrior, and his fighting style has given him the title of "berserker" by those who have seen him fight. If not for his attempts to keep his profile low, Sargvaas would become a legend in his region, especially given the stories his men-at-arms in his guard and militia days are more than willing to retell.
Sargvaas is currently unemployed, but is hoping to find a new employer in the town in which the campaign will start.
His preferred attire is a very finely crafted set of full-plate, which he got custom-fitted as a favor by the mayor of one particular town for outstanding work as a mercenary, painted a deep red, with black and gold etchings, which would be very recognizable if not for the very heavy cloak he wears to conceal his entire body. He keeps his hood up most of the time and his cloak open only enough to barely see his deep red plate, when looking at the right angle. His brooch was another gift from one of his female employers, and is a design of two lizards biting each others' tails. On his back, he carries his preffered weapon (a greatsword, more below), a crude maul (from his tribal days, more of a good luck charm... that works, apparently), and four javelins. Inside his cloak, on his belt, he has a pair of bastard swords, and a shattered longsword without a sheath, dangling from a chain (the property of the gang leader who displaced him, also a good luck charm).
His preffered weapon is a finely crafted serrated greatsword with a crimson blade, matching his armor, which he ordered to be crafted by the most reputable blacksmith in the country. The hilt is pretty much featureless and is specially designed for his massive grip. The sword is built especially for him... a person of his phenomenal strength and approximate height.
As for his physical appearance, he has dark brown eyes, and the same color hair that he keeps a few inches long to stay out of the way. He otherwise doesn't maintain his hair. He has a crooked nose and a slightly displaced jaw from fights in his earlier days. Surprisingly, he has no scars or deformations on his face aside from the broken nose and unnoticable jaw displacement. Covering his face is stubble he maintains at a very short length.Sargvaas stands six and a half feet tall, but tends to slouch and lumber, putting him at a usual 5'11".
Sargvaas prefers to charge almost blindly into battle, putting himself specifically in the most dangerous position available, from which he can reach the most enemies.
When he enters combat, he isn't irrationally angry, but is instead more like a tactician. He commonly barks out orders (or "suggestions," as he calls them after the battle if he can't pull rank on his comrades), which change over to the lizard language when he becomes very involved. Sargvaas otherwise wishes to defer attention from his association with lizardmen.
