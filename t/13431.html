#246421 2 2006-10-27T14:35:38Z The_Juggler
The only way that I know how to do it is to just type the quote text in manually.

This is in the same format as the quotes on this forum. You would just add the brackets.

QUOTE=The_Juggler,October 27, 2006 10:32 pm /QUOTE
or
Quote: The_Juggler, October 27, 2006 10:32 pm
Insert quote here

edit:
The comma between the user and month automatically changes @ sign.

You can also write anything in the QUOTE= area.
like QUOTE=SHDOO The Juggler's signature is better than mine /QUOTE
Quote: SHDOO
The Juggler's signature is better than mine
It does not need to have the fully formatted date and time with it.
