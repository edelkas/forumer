#5802 1 2005-12-20T15:14:06Z sweep
There are two types of maps. Playable, and non-playable. Instead of non-playable counting for nothing, as great N-Art and great DDAs do take plenty of skill, there should be two seperate charts. Both in terms of authors and maps.

Authors Chart (playable)
Authors are ordered by who has the highest average rating achieved from 10 or more rated maps from the action, puzzle, race and survival categories.

Authors Chart (non-playable)
Authors are ordered by who has the highest average rating achieved from 10 or more rated maps from the n-art and DDA categories.

Map Chart (playable)
A chart of maps in order of rating from the action, puzzle, race and survival categories.

Map Chart (non-playable)
A chart of maps in order of rating from the n-art and DDA categories.

I fail to see any argument against this being implemented. It's logical, and will stop people complaining about n-art ending up at the top of the overall charts. They're two seperate types of map, it just makes sense to do this if you ask me.
