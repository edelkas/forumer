#17371 1 2007-09-30T18:16:10Z max_ride
Some of you have probably seen my newest map and already know, but this is for those who don't or want to know the reasons.

I have been on NUMA for roughly a year and a half. All of my time spent here or in other N related website has been a learning experience that was made fun by all of the people on here. There have been people who have been helping me since my first map that I ever made, and they have stayed with me up until this day. I am really sorry to all of the people who enjoyed my maps or who I have grown close too, but now is the time for me to move on. I first found N while I was at frogs' house and he was playing. I started playing at his house, and little by little I gradually got more and more interested. Finally I went home and downloaded it onto my own computer for easier acess. Once I started playing N I was instantly hooked right in. Most of my freetime was spent sitting, couped-up in front of the computer screen playing this amazing game. After about six months I found out about NUMA and decided I would try to make maps. When I started making maps I became more enthralled with the game and wanted to spend an increasing amount of time playing. Eventually frogs also showwed me the forums which did nothing but suck up more of my time. I was amazed by how many people and how much activity there was on the forums. After a few days of browsing I made my own forum account and was now involved in anything I could find time for. After making about roughly 100-150 maps lord_day informed me about IRC. Once I started that, two special things came with it. IRC maps and collaboration maps. Once I started making these types of maps, all it did was suck up more of my time that should've been spent on school or sports. On IRC I made a bunch of friends who became closer as time went on. By this time I was making about an average of 1 map a day which is quite a bit when I was now in 8th grade-9th grade which takes up a lot of my time. Many people claimed to like my maps, but all of the maps I really liked were ingnored or disliked. I know that this isn't something I can change, but it just made me mad that I spent all of that time making the map just to see it fail. Now I'm at 284 maps and 213 of those maps have been rated, but about 100-125 shouldn't have been, but were due to my multi-accounting. Now I am at 284 maps and have lost complete interest in N. I continually try to make maps, but nothing is successful. I think I get something and just plain lose it due to lack of interest/inspiration. I plan to visit as often as I can, but I don't think I will be active when it comes to map making/map playing. Expect to see me around the forums finishing up my competition and you may also see me around IRC. I plan to keep in touch with my friends here on NUMA, and I will try to help improve NUMA as much I can while I'm around. My stay here has been fun, but now it is time for me to move on.

Thanks,
Eric
