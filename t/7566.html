Ok! Basically this contest was thought up over the past couple days. Im not sure if people will incredibly like it, but oh well.

The rules are:
-You must use a minimum of three enemies that shoot projectiles to a maximum of six that shoot projectiles
-All projectile shooting enemies must be able to shoot the Ninja at a minimum of two different places
-Projectile enemies are: Gauss, rockets, chain-guns, and lasers.
-All other enemies may be placed in any quantity in any place, except thumps, thumps also have be able to attack the Ninja at a minimum of two different places
-It can be any kind of map except N Art and DDAs
-Two maps per person

Thats basically it.

Criteria are going to be:

Use of Multi-use Threats - 70
Basically what the name entails.
Originality - 30
Have you seen this design often or not?

If anyone would like to suggest a different or change to criteria, I will consider it.

Anyone one wishes to be a judge can apply. I know that some people may already be bogged down with other contests. I was kind of weary of adding another contest since all the other contests are still going.

Judging begins as soon as possible and the deadline for map entries is March 31st, 2006. If there turns out to be too many contests already going on and because of this it affects this contest, I will temporarily shut this one down until there is less other contests.

Anyways, have fun with it.
