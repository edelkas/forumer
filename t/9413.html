Well, they're not great, but they're not bad. It's clear you had an idea for each level and put thought into the tileset and the gameplay. That counts for a huge amount. What you seem to be lacking is the experience to avoid unnecessary objects, use things to their full potential, account for techniques advanced players use, and deal with or take advantages of the oddities of the N physics engine. That comes with practice playing and making levels. (Presumably. My maps still suck.)

A good start:
Don't use enough enemies to cause lag.
Don't hide switches behind gold. Make doors as transparent as possible, not confusing.
Don't add enemies that have no effect on the gameplay.
