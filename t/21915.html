Just throwing this out there; 'cause this was a favorite game of mine for awhile. Lugaru is a 3rd person fighting game, oriented around context-based controls, meaning that are only 3 controls seperate from moving (default: W,S,A,D). Besides its simple control scheme, it was a *OK* map editor buitl in, it's just rather hard to use and get used to.

The game has motion blur:



Wolf tackling the main character:


^Notice the real-time blood that the characters get all over their chests, faces, arms, etc.
You can die from blood loss in this game.


Even if you think it's ridiculous (and I did when I first tried it), download the demo. The game has very low system requirements, and is a great game for when you only have, say, 5 minutes and you want to play something.
