Right, here's what you do.

If you've got a good idea for a map that would only work on N+, but don't have a beloved Xbox to build it on, then make it in the special version of NED below and submit the NED code as a .TXT, along with a full screenshot of the map and a description of what to do, in a post. Due to the new enemies and tiles, note any and all enemy substitutions and tile placements in your post somewhere.

If an N+er likes what they see, they can nab the map and build it, and then play on it forever.
Plus, they will have inadvertently signed a contract stating that everything they own now belongs to you. But don't tell them that.

Basically, it's a chance to blow off some map-making steam, and be creative in the process.

Have fun!
