#395603 10 2008-01-02T01:45:09Z LittleViking
I wouldn't worry about bandwidth as long as you can keep it under 300-500kb a page. 300 is the usual rule of thumb, but you have some leeway being an art and design site.

What I would focus on is making the site scale in size. You could easily get it scaling vertically with the trim you have. Just cut it somewhere along the non-decorated and split it into a top background and a bottom background. You could even split the top and bottom for horizontal scaling if you're creative with it. Here's how I would do the top. Basically cut it into four corners. You could have the top left (with your logo) always stay where it is, and have the top right included all the stylistic detail across the top. It would slide back and forth but always get covered up at the logo. I can mock up some CSS if that doesn't make sense.
