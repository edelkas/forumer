Okay, let's see... using my professional unknown-language (usually Japanese ) game-playing skeelz, I have deciphered the following from this game

It is very vaguely similar to Mario, in which it's 2D and you control some... thing ( )... which can jump about. There's water and you can only stay in it for a limited amount of time before you start losing health. At the beginning, there are two objects to the right. The one to the right does something I have no clue what, but the one closer to your starting point heals you.

By default, if you didn't toy around with the configurations:
Arrow keys = move around. Up looks up, and down looks down and uses/picks up shtuff.
Z = Jump
X = Shoot
A = Switch to Previous Weapon
S = Switch to next weapon
W = Brings up map (once you have it, of course )
Q = Brings up inventory

Kill an enemy and it releases either a heart (to heal you +1; you have 6/6 HP) or a triangular piece of gold. Accumulate gold, and your level increases. From level 1 to level 2, for example, your gun shoots two projectiles instead of one, but injury can also deduct level as well as health, so watch out!

More later. *gits back t' playtesting*

#46794 11 2005-01-19T00:14:44Z Keron Cyst

I found out where to go, so I'll post a small guide here on what I've been able to do so far.
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
At the beginning, just skip through all the unintelligible babble by tapping Z & X (both pass through the text, so using both rather than one will go faster). As I said before, the floppy disk to the right is a save point, and the heart box restores your health. Go through the door by pushing down. Go to the left, taking care to thwart the currently fatal spikes. Avoid the bats, and grab the heart container (+3). Continue downwards, avoiding enemies. You'll come to water; it's not lethal, but you can only be underwater for so long, and it also slows movement, so jump over the pool and come to the end. You'll find a room. Enter it and there's a sleeping old man and a gun Grab the weapon (down) and leave, and now you can blast your way through the bats and those... weird jumping animals

Items left behind by enemies disappear after a short while (this is quite similar to Super Metroid ), so make sure you grab them! The floating hearts restore health by +1, and the triangular gold pieces add a bit to your Level (makes your weapon stronger). You can stay here and level up (going into a different area and going back has the enemies respawn), but there'll be plenty o' gold in the later levels
Now go to the original start point & save if you want. Heal if you were injured in the journey to get the weapon and back. Now go right and blast the bricks, killing the enemies. Watch out for the door; just like The Legend of Zelda: The Ocarina of Time, it's a trap monster. Blast it to bits and go in.

Yadda-yadda-yadda. Z & X Okay, now go right. The tower (which will be the way of gaining height in this area) carries another save point and health restoration device. Within the tower, drop down to the level of the creature next to the two pots, and go right. Keep jumping and you'll reach a treasure chest. Push down, and voila! you have a device that shows the area you're in (may be handy for detecting hidden areas) and your location (a blinking white dot) toggled by pressing W (deactivate by pressing Z or X).

Go back to the tower, and go all the way up. Now jump right, and you'll find another heart container past some... weird particle fountain

Back to the tower it is again! Save if you want. Now go left of where you fell into this area, and jump on the suspended blocks to where the fisherman's at. Fall into the pool and push down on the stars, and you'll obtain some kind of fishing rod Shoot the fish and get back. Cutscene!
Now, unless you want to save again, fall down to the very bottom. Don't bother with the house right beneath you; its door's locked. Go to the house on the right and prepare yourself for a small boss battle

Saw the area? It leads nowhere! Tried the door? It's locked! Move around to trigger a cutscene; the rabbit thing will attack you, proba'ly not realizing you were a friend Then he's taken away by some fairy creature after the door is slammed down by a large monster; the boss. If you have a really fast trigger finger, you don't even have to move; just face it and keep shooting, and you'll lose only a bit of health from touching it, and you'll be able to defeat it easily

Git back to the tower and heal & save. Now go right this time, above where you found the map-device, but underneath where you found the heart container. Ignore the house, and go to the bunny with the military suit blocking the way. He'll move and go to the house (nothing important there, but you can go there anyways if you want). This frees up the doorway, which leads to a small-medium room with lotsa enemies

Blast your way through until you find a key and go back (don't bother saving nor healing). Remember the house with the locked door in the bottom-left? Make your way to it. Now you can open it, and you'll find a place similar to the beginning screen. Save at the floppy disk above (you can rest at the beds in the other room, methinks) and activate the teleporter by using the computer. Now use the teleporter, and go right, blasting your way through any enemies which come your way. There will be some flying creatures which allows you to practice shooting upwards (although you can just jump & shoot as well ).

Jump over the high wall, and you'll come into another cutscene (Z & X, as usual). Apparently the bunny's taken away yet again Continue eastwards, and drop down to the room below with the floppy disk above it (you'll find three sprites that look like spikes; forget 'em. They're just part of the background wall). Save, and blast the two bricks upwards. The door it leads to takes you to the heart container you couldn't reach before. Jump right at the end of the block and you should be able to land on the middle one (took me a few tries, so don't fret if you can't get it on the first try yourself ). Continue along eastwards, blasting apart anything in your way.

If ever you wanna die an 'orrible smokin' death, all ya gotta do is go down to where that white, invincible, flame-resembling creature is speeding and touch it (-100 health)

Later on you'll stumble upon another room with a floppy disk, an unusable bed (don't know why; per'aps Daddaluma does ), and an elevator. Go to the top and open the chest; it contains a rocket launcher with ten shots (switch weapons with A or S). Why don't I type "ten rockets" instead? Well, you launch more rockets with Level, eventually like a barrage once you reach the max. of Level 3 The health under the elevator and the ammo in the bottom-right always regenerates when you re-enter the room.

Continue along moving right. Now you'll notice that the enemies drop ammo as well as the standard heart & gold pieces. Don't forget to level up your rocket launcher!

Eventually you'll come upon giant versions of the flying creatures which occasionally launch off green plasma orbs... unfortunately in your direction They sometimes give off 3 rockets or 3 hearts in one power-up (new sprites)

Then you'll eventually come upon a seemingly impenetrable force field That's where I'm at; if you decide to commit suicide and go down the path of where that speeding white creature is to see what's ahead, you'll observe a boss and a door, but I don't know how to get past the force field

And I have no clue what those gigantic, numbered eggs below are for
