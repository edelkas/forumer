Eyes closed, one palm facing the grey clouds above and one facing the gently blowing grass below Nijin meditated intently upon his training.  He reflected upon the last five years of his life here at the Doki Gardens.  Gardens is misleading, it was more of a shrine, temple even, located next to the Doki River.  The grounds were roughly five square miles of thick bamboo forest, five small white pagodas marking the corners of the gardens.  At the center was the Shrine of Doki, which had existed for many centuries as the center of Way. 

Nijin recounted his first impressions of the Shrines elaborate spires and floating rooms.  Parts of the Shrine sat directly above the Doki River below, levitating over the currents below, held in place by the monks who channeled their minds through the Shrine. 

A group of these monks were sitting on one of the concentration platforms in a traditional Way meditation stance, using their combined efforts to keep the platform stable in the air.  When the wind picked up the platform started to tilt and wobble but the monks did not break meditation and held the platform aloft.  As the winds continued to increase however the let the platform slowly drop back into its place and walked inside after ceremonially coming out of meditation.

Nijin remembered coming here as a teen, much older than most that arrive as infants, and he was looked at as an outcast by his peers.  He was however quick to learn the grounds and adapt to his surroundings.  He remembered skipping sleep to walk the grounds and memorize the gardens he now called home.  Within a few months he knew the gardens as well, if not better, as any other student his age. 

Nijin focused upon his teachers over the years; most notable of course was his Way Father, Kokije.  Every student had a Way Father or Mother, and they were the students personal instructors into Way.  Kokije had chosen Nijin shortly after he arrived in Doki.  It was Kokije who instilled the drive into Nijin to excel beyond his peers who had been here for many years more than he. 

Kokije was a member of the Higher Council at the time and was seated above all but two other monks.  Being selected by the third highest monk in Doki was an honor Nijin could barely understand in his ignorant youth.  Kokije had taught all but one of the grand monks of the current era, a feat unmatched in ages past.  Kokije was arguably the greatest teacher at the time of Nijins arrival, and Nijin would be his first student in nine years, a fact that did not escape the eyes of the Higher Council.

It was also Kokije who had given Nijin his name.  Nijin means fated one and many at the time speculated that Kokije saw Nijin as a special boy.  Nijins birth name was Hon-Burein, Burein being his hometown.  Burein is located about a thousand miles east of Doki in the province of Sorreina.  Hon-Burein had made the journey alone after escaping his home to follow Way.  Way was outlawed in Sorreina and practicing it by punishable with death.  Hon-Burein had heard about the Doki Gardens and strived for them since youth.  When he was old enough to make the journey he left under the cover of darkness and traveled west for six months until reaching the Doki River and a group of monks who pointed him upstream towards the Shrine.

The soft rain drops on his arms broke Nijins trance as he returned to this plane of thought.  He thought of his first feeble training sessions with Kokije as he walked back to the Shrine and his cot, he smirked at his past failures.  The rains came quickly and forcefully in Doki.

Nijin spoke while standing, But Father, why has this fallen upon me?

It is your fate, son.

Later that evening, just after dusk, Nijin was walking through the gardens.  He took his favorite path, one he had found himself some years prior.  It wasnt a main path and as such was much more private than the main paths that led to and from the Shrine.  Nijin walked carefully along the unbeaten path, making sure he didnt disturb anything as he contemplated the last ten hours of his life.

Sure Ive been an exceptional student, being able to surpass my peers after only five years of studying at the Gardens, but that doesnt enable me to transcend in life.

He turned his left palm up towards the star filled sky and his right faced the soft cold dirt below him.  The moonlight gave the grove a blue glow until a lonely cloud drifted in front of it.  As the clearing was under the shadow of the cloud Nijin felt a cool hand upon his shoulder.

My chosen son, why are you here? A voice came from the form though its lips did not move, its face covered with compassion.  The voice was deep and resonated in Nijins head and he was forced to think over what he heard multiple times before he could comprehend.
