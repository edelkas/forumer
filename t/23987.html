Greetings, fellow Metanetians!

Tonight, you may have noticed the forum prank that we pulled, and I have some pertinent things to say to those of you who weren't around for the live announcement. First and foremost, I wanted to thank everyone here in the community for all of your support and good spirit through things like this. It's you guys that make a place like this amazing, and I really appreciate all of you who joined in the fun!

This prank was bigger than just a prank for kicks - We've got a purpose to this one. First of all, if you guys notice, the staff has changed a bit. As of right now, we've got ten staff members - Keron, blue_tetris, and LittleViking as active admins. Mare and Raigan as site admins. And as of right now, six moderators; maestro, Tsukatu, Atilla, George, wedgie, and our first new addition: Calcy!

Now, for those of you who are no longer staff, this is particularly important: For right now, we're trying to take staff down to a bare minimum, and we'll soon be doing some hiring of new staffers - or old staffers that become active once again. We're still in discussions over this aspect of the community, but we'll see how things go over the coming days and weeks.

Secondly, we've got some delicious forum changes coming up! Nothing in this way is set yet, but we've been discussing over the past nine months about some large changes coming to a forum near you - We're going to be unveiling some small and maybe some large changes with the forums, depending upon what happens this coming handful of weeks.

Thirdly, and perhaps most importantly, those of you who have never been a part of one of our pranks, consider yourselves initiated, and welcome to the Metanet community. You guys have just been a part of one of the most fantastic parts about this place - And this night, in particular, was the most fantastic night we've had in the history of the community. For those of you who missed it, I apologize, and wish you guys could have stuck around for the good parts, but nearly nine months of sporadic planning came together tonight in fantastic fashion, and I hope those of you who *were* here enjoyed it.

For those of you who weren't around for the IRC and Ventrilo festivities tonight, I suggest that you guys download an IRC client or join us on Ventrilo some night - And have some fun with us! Of course, none of this is set in stone, so until then, keep your eyes out for some future awesomeness from the Metanet Forum Staff!
