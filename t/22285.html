Okay, here's my opinion in order of appearance:
0.Two floorguards fused. Too easy at start, took so long as well that I couldn't be bothered to work out the last part. It's like putting a 60 second respawn delay on.

1.Very cheatable, NaN'd rocket got very distracting and excessive use of gold delay.

2.Pointless object spammage, once again, cheatable.

3.Much better map this time, still it consists of many different conflicting aspects, parts are too easy or constricted, and not to mention the n00b trap. Remember, wide open spaces improve gameplay.

4.Interesting, not much pressure and interesting thwump placement. Bottom left corner and thwump direction suggest a specific path, though it is not enforced any further.

5.A lot better once again, though cheatable. Hiding switches is strongly unrecommended, and the last part, though with okay gold placement, is very frustrating for an ending, and involves waiting.

6.Ah, I've seen this before. Once again it is very constrictive, and the map is based solely on getting there fast, with only one real route and very little freedom or difficulty.

7.Much better map, though I hated the waiting aspect, and the hiding of switches. It was also frustrating trying to remove the thwumps, even to get a look.

8.Interesting route back, I found it mostly involved holding one button, thus having the same problem as the map 0.

9.Good freedom of movement, good map but too quick and simple.

10.Geez, a chamber map. Try to avoid these. The first row took away from the whole map, I advise you to try it without the rocket to see what I mean.

11.Interesting, I liked the use of timing, though it is still essentially two maps jammed together.

12.The gold placement is completely out of whack, and the teleporter is both unnecessary and unreliable.

13.I liked how the laser drone was near impossible unless you were completely accustomed to the game's drone timing.

14.? Please delete.

15.Yes! Now this map is definately your best so far, since it is actually playable. Infortunately, it does force you in to the very uninteresting higher route in the second half, taking away from the map.

16.Pretty good claustrophobic map.

17.If my computer is correct, this is 16.

18.Ah, the greed level. Why is the no gold route so easy? And why is the gold route so ludicrously hard? Try putting it in perspective.

19.Terrible. See 0., the ending is also very annoyingly long (it requires waiting). Also, I've noticed the thwumps on the edges. Try not to do that in future.

20.Getting better, gameplay's too concentrated though.

21.Decent, the lasers do push you away though.

22."And Mare and Raigan said upon the techno-prophets: Though shalt not mix art with play."

23.Same as 18(to a lesser extent).

24.Interesting, same art problem but I like the gameplay and gold placement.

25.Grr... This art is everywhere!
