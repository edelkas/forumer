No, I'm sorry, it's "$level name#author#type#level data#". And MetaNinja's 'level data' was outside the quotation marks, you'll notice - it was not part of the format.

I think he wishes to watch the demos for userlevels. This can be achieved in two ways. Firstly, you could open the level in debug mode, paste the demo data in the appropriate box, click outside the textbox, press '3', press 'Q', and, finally, press 'P'. Oh, and make sure caps lock is on. The second option is to copy the v 1.4 level data, go to userlevels from the main menu, paste the data into the demo data box on the left, type a #, then paste the demo data after it, followed by another # (so the format should be $level#author#type#level data#replay data#), and click the watch replay button. Oh, yes, and you can watch your personal bests again by clicking on the box that displays your best time, just to the right of the associated level. This will also put the PB's demo data in the appropriate box in the same format as you would paste it in, so you can grab it if you want to post it.
