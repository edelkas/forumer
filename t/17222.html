Alright, I guess you could call me a noob, but whatever. I have decided to make an entire episode of N levels, but since I have work that needs to be done I am not going to get the levels finished. This is where you come in.

Anybody who wants to join can go ahead and make a map.

Anybody who wants to judge PM me. 5 judges max including me, first come first serve.

Here are the rules and procedures:

1. Think of your favorite show. Imagine your favorite episode inside your head. If the show is funny then start laughing. If its sad, then start crying, unless you are in a public setting. If your emo, then go ahead and cry.

2. Now, take your favorite show and incorporate it into a N map. It can be from any show, although more popular shows would get more attention from the general public than not popular shows. The first map in this episode is from "Dr. Who," which is the longest running TV series.

3. I'm looking for 4 maps. The 4 maps with the highest scores by the end of the contest will be included.

4. Each contestant may submit only three maps each and each map must be playable. DDA's allowed, and KRADDA's are encouraged. If you can create a playable, difficult level out of N Art or tileset, then be my guest.

5. Judges may submit their own maps, but they cannot judge their own maps. The judging will be out of 40 points as follows:
a.) Gameplay - 15 points total
b.) Relevance to chosen show - 10 points
c.) Difficulty - 10 points
d.) Style - 5 points
If there happens to be a tie anywhere between 1st place and 3rd place, the judges will vote on which map is the weakest link.


To get full points on difficulty, make a map that isn't too hard and isn't too easy. I figure if someone dies about 10 to 15 times before they figure out and beat the map, then that is probably good for difficulty.

Style is pretty much free points, so long as you choose one or two styles, such as DDA, Puzzle, Action, etc.


This contest ends on May 30th, so get started! I basically just joined Metanet today, although I've been playing N for a while. Submit maps to NUMA (obviously) and put in the description, "For the Favorite Show episode contest." PM a link to your map to me and I will send it to the judges for judging.

That is pretty much it. If you have any questions then go ahead and ask. Yes, this is a nerd contest. No, there are no prizes, but you may generate a somewhat larger fan base. Plus, this is an entire episode, which makes it more meaningful I think.

Looking forward to seeing your creations,
