<font> is reduntant, use it and I will smack you. That is why we have css. It's also redundant because you can apply to same attributes to the wrapping <p> tags. But seriously, only use attribute styling directly into tags. css is the way to go.

giving <hr /> a width attribute of 100% is also redundant because it is automatically 100% anyway.

You need to know about doctypes too... you only need a / on self closing tags in xhtml, i.e. 
<img src="image.png" alt="title of image!" />
where as html is simply 
<img src="image.png" alt="title of image!">

....get the basics down first.
