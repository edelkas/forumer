Ahh, this has happened to many people before.

At the top of your screen, there's a timer. You have 90 seconds at the beginning of an episode to beat it. Every piece of gold you collect gives you 2 extra seconds. The reason why you have so little time is because time carries over through the episode. You took too long to complete the previous level, so you don't have enough time to beat this level. You must quit N and restart the episode to continue because of this. Sorry, man.
