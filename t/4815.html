Quote: Ngaged, 2005-10-30T14:12:20Z
I'm not interested in the Urban Dead stuff, either. Eating brains sounds extremely boring...
Urban dead is a game sort of attached to the brain-eating site. Basically its a low-tech MMO where you play either zombies or humans in a quarantined city of undead. Both sides are composed entirely of players, and the humans have a wide variety of paths they can go down for experience (healing, axe or gun training, running DNA scans on zombies and reviving the ones who look like they're slain human players) or utility (barricades, searching improvement, free-running, and so on), while the zombies are limited to just combat but still have a variety of skills for improving themselves in various ways (faster movement, stand up faster after being shot, increased claw and bite effectiveness, etc). The game sort of favors humans in many ways, but there are a few advantages for the zombies (like being able to select targets and not having to search for ammo), and more importantly the zombies have recently become very well organized near the center of the map and are putting up a huge fight, with a cluster of suburbs ranging from dangerous to suicidal for humans (sadly, the rest of the map is almost all human territory). The only thing that might annoy some (aside from being primarily text-based instead of graphics-based--although its not obnoxiously text-based like those really old games and it has buttons and a visual map) is that you're only allowed 50 actions each day (and for low level zombies movement and standing up count as multiple actions), and that forces you to budget your moves and doesn't let you play all day (although you can have several characters and there's a very active forum), but this really increases the strategy and is part of the core of the game (not just something tagged on at the end to save his server or get little kids to bed on time). The community is very strong and I've recently been spending more time on their forum than this one, and it actually makes the game twice as much fun.
I think you should all try it, and those that tried it a year ago and couldn't find anyone else on should try it again.
