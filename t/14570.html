So, in the same vein as the Vehemence map-pack was, I've got the brilliant idea, of a similar, but pretty different project.

Here's the basic idea:

10 (Or maybe 15, depending on the quantity of responses) people will make 10 maps over a period of 3 nights. I don't want people to get exhausted or feel as if they have to make all their maps in one session. Take your time if you choose to be a part of this.

Here is the proposed catch. Each map you make has to be at least relatively different in theme and/or style from all the rest because we're making 2 (Maybe 3) columns together. Here's the deal, after I see how many people are interested I will organize you guys into 2 seperate groups of 5 (Or once again, possibly 3 groups) and each of the groups will represent a column. When you make your maps they have to be different because each of your maps is going in a different episode.

Here's the layout: You have 5 people paired together for the first column.

The first episode will be

0-0 - Person A
0-1 - Person B
0-2 - Person C
0-3 - Person D
0-4 - Person E

And so on and so forth.

SO! The first person makes their 10 UNRELATED maps, seriously, I really want you guys to concentrate on this part. One of the main focuses of this pack is to have as much variety as we can come up with. The second person then makes maps /similar/ to the first 10, but puts their own style and twists into it.

The goal is to get the 10 episodes of each column to be episodic yet very diverse and unique. Not to be overly tyrannic, but after I see how many sign up I'll order the days for people to go. To speed things up we will only be taking 15 days, that is the columns will be worked on simultaneously so that it doesn't take a month or more.

One thing to keep in mind, while I want you all to use what you are good at and the idea is diversity, remember that these are still levels within episodes. Try and keep that in mind when making your maps, while I want your own stuff in it, I want them to look like episodes.

Anyway, I think that sums it up pretty nicely. If you have any questions or would like to volunteer do so and let's get this started.
