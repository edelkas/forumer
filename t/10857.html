Demo Derby

Demo Derby is a competition where fast demos earn you points on maps provided.

The fastest demo earns you 10 points.
the next fastest demo earns you 9 pts.
The next fastest demo earns you 8 pts.
etc.

demos are judged in N-editor format.

rules:
well, do whatever you can to get the fastest demo possible to the map given.

procedure:
there will be 5 rounds. in each round, You will have a different, possibly harder map.

judges:
me (sharc)
open-

level testers for playability:
open- (will playtest 1 level or so per week)
