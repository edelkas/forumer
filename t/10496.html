1.5 has been revealed. It is the coming of N Online.
I have played it, and it is quite fun. There are as of now 3 modes: Co-op, Race, and Gold.

Co-op will sometimes have a map in which you must rely on your online ally to get that switch to open your door, and viceversa. It takes teamwork most of the time, or just clearing a map with speed.

Race is a battle between you and the other player, to complete the map with the fastest time. Gold can be taken by any of the 2 players, and can be the turning point when the race ends.

Gold is like Race, but instead its about getting more gold than your opponent. Usually the largest cache of gold will be heavily guarded, but getting it can put you into the winners circle.

You will use your NUMA username and password to logon, and right now its connecting to someones IP number directly, as there is no server for the game right now. It is quite fun, and I can't wait until they release the game, some time by the end of June.
