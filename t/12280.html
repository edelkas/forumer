#221750 2 2006-08-30T21:07:08Z Twistkill
Flaming: Basically, calling someone names, arguing, swearing at someone, using derogatory terms, etc.

Trolls are people who flame and annoy other members unpurpose, purely for attention, and feeding a troll is basically adding onto the inflammatory messages, ie. flaming them back, etc.

A PM stands for Personal Message. It is used to send a message to someone that only they can see, instead of asking a random question in the middle of a thread. To send a PM, click the person's username to display their profile. Then, you'll see a button somewhere near the middle to send them a PM. Alternatively, you can just look below the member's posts and click the button.

Hope that helps ya.
