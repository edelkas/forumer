Metal Arms: Glitch in the System is one of the hardest console games I've ever played. I'm only on I think level 16 or 17 (right after you work with Krunk and his Control Tether) out of 42. And this is just on Normal. I simply must try Nuts of Steel afterwards (last difficulty, after Hard)

It steals so much from Halo; the tanks are Scorpions (they both have m. guns and turrets), the gun turrets are Shades, the enemies consist of specific classes with types distinguished by color, Grunts are Grunts (both panic if in small numbers), RATs are like Warthogs (driver, and independent gunner in the back)... xD Plus you have a Primary and Secondary weapon, which you scroll through columns of boxes on either side of the screen by holding a key and using L & R, with which you can also view data on a weapon (MGS! ).

But it also has 1337 physics, and the AI is one of the best I've seen. The enemies actually throw 'nades, take cover, and jump from ledge to ledge! The graphics are also beautiful, if you've seen screenies of it. IMO best game of '03.
