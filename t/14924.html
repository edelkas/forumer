Level 1 Riddle:

The thieves spent 6 hours in the home of Billiard Bill. At first they searched the house, trying to find where he hid his huge diamond, valued at half a million dollars.
Then they tried beating the information out of him. They fled at dawn, fearing detection.
Fatally hurt, Bill crawled to his desk and typed a note to his partner, John Madden. In a desperate race to type it before he bled to death, he near blindly typed his death note.

It read:

John -- four men tried to make me tell where I had hidden the diamond. At first they looked through the house, raving like madmen. Then, in desperation, the barbarians split open the cat! When all failed, they beat me, but I did not tell. I'm dying. The diamond The diamond is hidden in the vane."

"Bill died this morning," Inspector Winters told Dr. Boris. "We have his murderers, but not the diamond."
The inspector handed Boris a copy of the death note. "We took down the vane, the weather vane, a cock, but there was nothing inside it," the inspector said, "We're still searching the house."
Boris read the note and said, "You also failed to find the body of the cat, but you did find a broken barrel of liquor."
"Why, yes," said the inspector. "The thieves were thorough. They broke the barrel and every bottle in Bill's little wine cellar."
"How many walking sticks did Bill own?"
The inspector looked puzzled. "One."
"It must be hollow," said Boris. "You'll find the diamond inside it."

Question:The inspector found the diamond, but how did Boris know?
