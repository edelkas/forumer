You guys can finally have this. I've been making a concerted effort for a good four months to see this pack through to the end.
I wanted to create something people will remember me for, something that will satisfy everyone, and something that was, in it's own way, beautiful.
It all started one day when I made a map entitled "The Gala Event" (the title being a reference to the Beastie Boys). Then I thought that it would be wonderful to make a large amount of maps, and spend time crafting each one, to make something that could stand on it's own. 100 maps was my original goal. But, after a while, I started to lose steam, and my goal seemed to be too far away to ever work. The project was dead in the water. But, after a while, I realised that I could do equally as much with less, if you know what I mean. So, I chose 50 maps as a reasonable goal, with a bonus addition to that. The 50 maps I grouped as The Gala Event. I finished the last one today. And the bonus maps were to be an addition to both The Gala Event and my Quick series (where I attempt to make each map in 5 minutes or less). There are 10 maps in Sir Swift.
Please enjoy every one of these maps, and find beauty and happiness in them.

I would like to extend a deep, and heartfelt thank-you to all of my friends and aquaintances in this community, who have been a constant source of happiness and inspiration. It's refreshing to find people who love what I love, and do what I do.

Please leave comments, inform me of any bugs or problems, and enjoy my legacy to you.
