Vent Maps are when 2+ peopl- Eh. Here's a quote from LV, it explains it much better:
Quote: LV
Each of us opened Ned and started telling each other what gameplay bits to add to a level. All of us follow the same instructions as we're giving them to each other. Of course, the explanations are naturally kinda vague (i.e. "Put some E tiles around up in the air" "Now put bounce blocks near the E tiles") so the maps all come out looking differently. After about 10 or 15 minutes, we each have a finished map (albeit, maybe a horrible map, but that's not the point). When you play the maps, you should see some similarities, but they're still very different.

So I was thinking we could have each person interested PM me with 3-4 lines of instructions, and then I'll compile them and PM you a copy. You could follow the instructions and make the map. Then, submit to NUMA and link here!

I realize that people might have conflicting instructions so here are some rules you have to follow:

  1. Make your instructions as general as possible. For example, "Add a couple mines near the middle of the map" would be acceptable, while "Add 14 E tiles around 7 mines organized in the shape of a heart, and then add 2 rocket launchers on each of the 4 corners and surround them with Laser Drones" wouldn't. One or two specific orders would be fine, but 35 specific orders would ruin a map. Keep your orders general.

  2. PM me 3-4 instructions. I'll remove those that contradict/interfere with someone else's or pick the most suitable one.

  3. Please post in this thread if you want to be PMed the complete list of orders.
For Round 1, only the first 12 people who sign-up will be allowed to participate. Any extra will be put on Round 2, which will start as soon as Round 1 is over.
