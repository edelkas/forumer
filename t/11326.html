OK... here's why it actually won't work. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction, yes? So when millions of people jump upwards, the earth will move downwards a little bit, so that momentum is conserved. Then after the high point of the collective jump, not only will the people fall down but the planet will fall back up a tiny amount - the same amount that it moved down by when the people were rising. Then the people meet the earth and all momentum is cancelled out and we are back where we started. No change.
