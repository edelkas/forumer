#108154 4 2005-10-14T02:15:25Z brickman
No, its not the game. If you press any key even .025 seconds later the demo can potentially turn out differently, and if its something like .150 seconds (a more realistic number, though you still wouldn't notice it) its not at all unlikely that the outcome changes. If you pressed everything at the exact same moment two times in a row, the results would be identical. Thats how demos work.
