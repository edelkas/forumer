*sigh* We get this question so often, it ought to be in the FAQ...

If you only have 11 seconds to complete 5-4, you need to play Episode 5 again and do either

a) beat the levels faster
b) get more gold

Time is cumulative over levels, and you have 90 seconds to beat the episode. This means if you finish 5-3 with more time then you'll have more time on 5-4.
