Well, as I used to be a frequent browser during GameHippo's prosperous times (it's fallen due to staff inactivity and constant hacker attacks; there are less than 10 people who regularly visit its forums. E.g., a member recently made a thread about leaving because a hacker had changed his password), I stumbled upon N v1.2 ~Marchish and got hooked right away.

I checked around harveycartel.org/metanet and saw that MetaNet Software had no way of bonding the community, so I e-mailed Mare about possibly creating a forum. Of course, being an undying fan of the Stick Soldiers series, I decided on Forumer (since WSU's - WhiteSpace Unlimited, designer group for SS & SS2 - last two forums were hosted by Forumer) and sent her a small .TXT layout of the forum's groups & such. The blueprint appealed to Mare, and it was born! A link to the forums was provided on the MetaNet homepage, and we were off to a nice, late March 2004 start

Raigan seemed nonexistent about it (and still does, posting a few times every month)

NUMA? Go ask Arachnid I've absolutely no clue, except that it's probably been around for half a year or so.
