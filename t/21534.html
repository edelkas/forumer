Me in 10 years.

I had to do a comparison between an alcohol promoting ad, and an ad that 'informs' about bad binge drinking. I could write forever about the Corona ad, but only a paragraph about the non-drinking. Is this evidence enough to show my young mind is already corrupt with the lust for a Beer lifestyle?

------------------------------------------------------

The corona beer ad, personally is the absolute most effective lifestyle commercial I have every seen. I am in love with the lifestyle the couple ha￼s, and makes me think of a Corona every time I see it. Corona Beer have refined their target/niche market to fit perfectly what every young person (and old in some cases) wants. A slab of Corona, with a surfboard, awesome surf on a beach and a stunning looking girl bringing the lime to put down the neck at sunset. I think that a hell of a lot of kids as well as 20 somethings would look at this ad and immediately crave the lifestyle to be their own.

It has an effect that makes the audience think Corona is synonymous with this perfect life, and therefore if you drink Corona, you will have the lifestyle of this couple. Even the way I have described it above is enough evidence to show that it has had an effect on me personally, as I myself think this is a perfect lifestyle.

Corona Beer would have done a lot of research into the perfect lifestyle, and have used beautiful clean imagery to display it and slapped their product on it. Not that this is a bad thing, as Corona actually tastes better than all other beers (I think so anyway).
￼
The music used, in itself is also a massive emotion starter, as it makes you think of tropical beaches. The song Run River by John Swift is a song that inspires a longing for beaches, as in this day and age acoustic music seems to reflect chilling out on a beach in the hot sun and enjoying yourself. An example of the lyrics are Carry me, to my home in the ocean, carry me away. This song was made before the ad was, but it, in one sentence sums up what the audience feels like after watching the ad.

On top of this all, the mere fact that I could find this ad and the song on the internet but not of the non-alcohol ads is evidence that other people want to see this ad as well, and desire towards this, but not safe drinking.

------------------------------------------------------

The ad Im doing that promotes against alcohol is the ad where the main guy goes to the bar and he and his mates heads are cut off to show their brain. As he buys more and more drinks, his brain starts to lose its electricity, he is unable to perform simple tasks, and then decides to drive home, and crashes. This ad is very accurate if the target market was middle aged guys who go to the bar and play pool with their mates, but it is however a rather boring ad, and doesnt appeal to many people. It is an infomercial, rather than a lifestyle commercial. I couldnt find an example on youtube, and the fact that I could find the corona ad and not this one is proof enough that it isnt nearly as popular, watched, or takes the interest of people. The mere fact that I have gotten to the end of this paragraph and have nothing more to write is also evidence that drinking alcohol is much more interesting and exciting than drinking in moderation and not driving. The corona ad is different, as it doesnt promote binge drinking but social drinking.

------------------------------------------------------

...Gotta love that lifestyle ad.
