Alright, here's the deal. This text file includes two versions of a level: one with a missile turret, a guass gun (those two are easy), three thwumps and a buncha mines. It also has a few buttons.
The other has none of the above, with gold placed at regular intervals to reestablish the grid, and a coin where the missile turret and thwumps are.
Your goal is to, after viewing and probably playing the first level, go to the one without enemies and try to record a demo that works for the first level while playing the second. You must remember where the safe spots in the mine areas are, and where the buttons are and how to avoid the enemies; but you're doing it blind! Its pretty nervewracking by the time you get to the mines, and I was SOOO nervous when I got to the thwumps because I had no idea if I would/had made the jump at the right time. You'll see what I mean. The fact that you won't know about a mistake until you're done sure helps this atmosphere.

Of course, taken at face value, the level is unbelievably easy. But doing it blind is another story.

PS I included a demo made in the empty version. You can see I was pretty nervous in that top part.
