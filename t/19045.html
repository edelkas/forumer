#358132 4 2007-08-22T02:48:02Z usaswim
People will play your maps if they look good, that's the most important thing. Also, make them fun. A bunch of random bounce blocks and gold is not considered fun. I suggest you work on your tileset, which is usually the most important part as it is the foundation of the map. A fun tileset will make a better map in the end. You'll have to spend some time on the tileset, they don't take a few minutes. Never make your maps repetitive all the way, as people will get bored really easily. Don't make your maps insanely long. Just by looking at The Great Labyrinth, I wouldn't play it, because judging by the tiles and amount of clutter, it's going to be a really long and tediously boring map. You'll gain more experience over time, and find your own style. Take other people's criticism and try to fix what they don't like.
