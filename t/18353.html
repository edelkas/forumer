#342134 2 2007-07-12T15:36:27Z Rincewind SW
Mine used to do that. I'm not sure if it's exactly the same problem, though. I had a poor signal strength when the wireless router was in one position, but I moved it to a higher position and my signal improved. After some experimentation with objects in the path between the receiver and the router, I found that mirrors and electronics are will probably halt the signal. There are some full length mirrors on a wall between the two points, which slide on a rail, and if a mirror is slid across to block the path, the signal usually cuts out. A few normal objects like walls and cupboards shouldn't stop the signal, so if none of this applies to you, there's probably a far more complicated solution.
