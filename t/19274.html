#362460 6 2007-09-05T18:50:37Z southpaw
when you are finished playtesting and all do the following:

1. Go to the main Ned menu
2. press "R" to reset just in case you haven't
3. click in the level data box (the top box) and press Ctrl+A and then Ctrl+C
4. Open up Notepad and press Ctrl+V. Save the document.

If you want to put it into userlevels then add the little thing that Django said at the beginning, and don't forget the "#" sign after the leveldata. I would suggest saving your levels seperately though because then you can access them easily for quick changes.
