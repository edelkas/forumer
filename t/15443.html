#289662 3 2007-01-24T23:06:55Z Twistkill
Hello!

It's not very hard to do this...

Quote: The editor_manual.txt file
1. Press [L] to pull up the textboxes.  Paste level data into the upper text box, paste demo data into the lower text box.

2. Press [L] to load your level.

3. Press [3] to load your demo.

4. Press [Q] to initiate playback of the demo.

5. Press [P] to start viewing the demo.

6. Make sure caps lock is on!

There are also other resources you can use to find out any other question you have regarding N:

N Wiki
The editor_manual.txt and readme.txt files that come with your N download.
FAQs (They also have juicy information regarding this forum, Metanet, and the next game Metanet are creating, Robotology.)
