#327089 5 2007-06-03T21:27:14Z LittleViking
Good taste is the only thing that really matters, but it's hard to say if someone has good taste or not because it's so subjective. You mentioned Rue the Whirl, so you obviously enjoyed that, but maybe someone else didn't. That doesn't mean they have bad taste, it just means you have different tastes.

I think having personal standards is what really matters for bitesizing. When you bitesize a map, it means you think the rest of the world should see it. It's like adding it to the "Favorites pool" - the public Favorites, the community favorites. If you're putting a map out there for the world to see, it should have at least something special about it. If you're bitesizing every map you see (or even 10% the maps you see), your standards for what people "need to see" are probably too low.

My favorites list used to be famous around here for having an eclectic mix of maps. Some were masterpieces of Numa, some just had one little quirk that I thought was interesting. It wasn't just 5-ninja maps either; there were plenty of times I'd give a map a 3 and then favorite it. There were maps of all styles and quality levels, but most importantly, they were all in one way or another worth playing. The maps that made it on had nothing to do with my forum posting, the quality of my maps, how many votes I'd placed - these were just maps that I wanted to save and look at again later.

So like I said, bitesize members need to have some level of standards just so they don't bitesize every other map on Numa, and they should be able to recognize special traits that make a level worth showing to the world. Beyond that, everyone is welcome as far as I'm concerned.
