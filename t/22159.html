#425634 3 2008-04-06T13:07:27Z Ignate
Like Commander said, look at your hands, but make them chunkier. The fingers (excluding the thumb) are roughly the same length as the palm.
The thumb is divided into two segments and the others into three, and make sure you don't mix up between the left and right hands.

I hope I've helped. You can go to some bookshops and find some comic-making books.
