#24464 4 2004-07-09T03:08:42Z Vlad
Quote: bearwolf, Jul 9 2004, 02:51 AM
Ok I'v tried tappin jump when I make contact, but that just makes me jump up again. Which makes the box fall... Idunno what aint I getting here? Sounds simple..

That's the idea for that level. There's no way to really soften your landings. That level requires you to either leave a block as soon as you land on it (it is called "step lightly" or something along those lines...). Another alternative is doing smaller jumps once you hit it, if you really want to stay on the block. If you do a smaller jump once you're on the block, you won't fall as fast on it, and be less likely to hit a mine.

#24693 9 2004-07-12T05:34:55Z kyle
If you catch the side of the block and wallslide on it, and jump up from there, the block won't move down far enough to hit the mine. This is the easiest way if you want to actually land and let the block stablize before moving on to the next block, and your jumps are always too high. The jump immediately method is only good if your reflexes are up to never standing still on a block.
