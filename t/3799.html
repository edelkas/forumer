#78239 12 2005-06-25T00:42:49Z Arachnid
The fatal flaw in counting reccommendations has already been pointed out. The whole reason for dividng the number of favorites by the number of views was that this would even out ratings between new maps and old maps.

Lurkers who view and never vote or favorite don't matter: they decrease the average rating of all maps equally, so the rankings are the same.

I really don't see the problem with viewing a map affecting it, either. If you're viewing it even though it's crap, it _should_ get a lower rating. If nobody wants to add it to their favorites, then it doesn't deserve a rating. Yes, that gives no feedback to those who are still learning, but a rating system alone is poor feedback. Trying to improve your mapmaking skills on ratings alone would be like playing a game of "hot/cold" across an entire city. Far more useful for learning is the constructive criticism of other mapmakers.
