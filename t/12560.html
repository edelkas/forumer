Inspired by the Flat Earth Society forums, I thought it would be fun to do what they do. To try to scientifically explain a flat Earth, as opposed to a round one. I mean, when you think about it, how many of you have real proof that the Earth is round? Have you gone into space and looked at it? Have you ever tunneled through the Earth and come out on the other side? Of course not. So what makes us believe everything we've been told about the shape of our world? Maybe the Earth is flat.

The objective of the game is simple enough. People will post ideas that should point to a round Earth, and others will try to disprove them. Use as much science as you want to. Be as technical as needed. Above all, be creative. (Not crazy and outlandish, though. Please don't bring space aliens into this. Government conspiracies are okay though.) Also note, this isn't a 'disprove the post above you' thread; You can argue about any topic brought up, from the very beginning. And if you can't think of a way to disprove anything that's up now, you can just post a new argument for others to discuss.

If I missed anything or you have any questions, ask away. Otherwise I'll start it up.
