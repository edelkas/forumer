"Don't look a gift horse in the mouth" refers to the fact that you can get a good idea of the condition and quality of a horse by looking at it's mouth (if you know what you're looking for), it's something that prospective buyers might do to decide if the horse is worth it. Not something you should do when someone is giving they're horse to you.
