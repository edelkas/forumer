Action. Race. DDA. Survival. Art. Where are all the puzzle maps?

No, no. Not "mine-jumper" puzzles, or "find switch A underneath bounce block #67" puzzles. The ones that make you go:

Sh t.
I f cked up.
Big time.
(Followed by a necessary suicide.)

The puzzles I'm looking for give you enough time to think as you play it, or at least give you time to think before you begin. For example, Lucidium's "Rocks and Hard Places" acts somewhat like a puzzle, but it's more action as you have to adjust to the thwump's patterns on the go, or try different patterns with each go.

So I wanna dedicate a thread for maps, old and new, that follow this concept. It could be one where you also need skills (Sendy's "What Now?!?!"), or even some studying with trial and error (TornadoTK's "Lockbuster").

If you have a map, please put it here and I'll add it to the main list. If you don't, try making one. It's a nice break from traditional levels.
