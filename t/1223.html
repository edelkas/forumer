There's a new kind of map on NUMA, and it's ready to make its debut as an entirely new map type: Control Rooms!

In each level, you are trapped in a room, and in an identical room, is placed a zap drone. Using the switches and trapdoor triggers in your room, guide the zap drone into a square goal to beat the level.

So far, only three have been made, two by me:





And one by the now-famous Captain Donut:



It is a very good, and entertaining type of level, that I estimate shall soon explode in popularity. I'd like to see more of these very soon!

Also, Arachnid, if you're reading this, could you make Control Rooms a certain category of its own? Since it will be popular, we will need to be able to quickly find Control Room puzzles, and not have to sort through the other puzzles to find them.

I'd really like to see more Control Rooms in the future!
