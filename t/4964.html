#105644 26 2005-10-05T02:57:37Z nevermore
I used to be very caught up with ratings. I cared a lot that people liked my maps. Then I got hit by a huge blow. About ten of my maps in a row were rated something around a 3/5 with no bad comments, and all of them were maps that I absolutely loved. I went through a short period of absolute frustration, and I couldn't believe that all of these maps that I really didn't like were very highly rated, while what I believed was my best map had a 2.5/5.
So I quit numa. Literally. And I didn't just silently fade away, I decided I'd make a big deal about it, and post on the forums how upset I was. Looking back, I think I was a total idiot. Then I started to realize that ratings don't matter. Arachnid, Kablizzy and LittleViking gave me some very good advice.

Now when I make a map, I keep in mind how people will like it, rather than focus on other people liking it. Nowadays I focus the most on how _I_ like the map, and I can honestly say that since I had my epiphany, my maps have increased in quality.
