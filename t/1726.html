Sphix, forgive me for the criticism, man, but that was not that great. I'm glad I'm gettin' to ya before some of the others, 'cause, well, I'll be nicer about it. I hated the mines, the one-ways at the beginning weren't placed well, the thwumps didn't add much to the level beyond killing N at inopportune times, the jump timing is way off, and the only way I got through some of those was through sheer luck. The map seemed terribly cluttered, and you could have spaced the mines much further apart, and still achieved the same effect.

But, it's an interesting level, to say the least. I definitely haven't seen anything like this, ever.

So, enough with the nitpicking. Here's what I'd do:

Head on over to Numa and take a look at some of the maps there particularly the top-rated action maps, since that's what you seem to be doing here. Those will give you some ideas hopefully, and perhaps some insight into making more in the future.

Secondly, get yourself an account on Numa, which is the same as your account in the N Configure Menu. Upload your maps there, and people will see them, and rate them, and give you advice. If you have any other questions, send me a PM, and I'll get back to you.
