#227456 8 2006-09-12T01:50:13Z krusch
I think mikey210's advice (about drawing inspiration from all those Metanet maps you have at your disposal) is the best you've been given. When I make episodes, (virtually) the only maps I play between mapping sessions are LittleViking's Chronicles, Barabajagal's Episodium and Metanet's episodes. I find them very inspirational.

Another thing to consider is the levels' atmosphere/feel. I prefer a range of different styles and atmoshperes in my episodes, but rocket_thumped likes each episode to have a distinct theme, as demonstrated in the Echo pack. Different people like different themes (or different combinations of themes), so find what works for you.

Remember that your levels don't have to be epic in length, and often the simpler ones will be mos effective. Once you have all your levels made, a good thing to do is to go through them, and sort out which ones belong on Numa rather than in your episodes. Do this based on map length/difficulty/jumperness. Jumpers aren't fun.

Hope that can help you at least a little, and good luck with your episodes.
