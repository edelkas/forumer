#138461 3 2006-02-02T01:26:32Z Ngaged
For naming and sving...

1. Select all the level data in the upper box and copy it out.
2. Paste that into a txt file or a word document, or something like that.
3. Save the file. Save it as the name of your level.
4. Voila! Now you can later select the data from your file, copy that out, put it into the upper box of debug mode, click out of the box, and press L!

(To play, just read BSBen's post.)
