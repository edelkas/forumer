#125327 2 2005-12-20T22:13:33Z Guest
right first get the key at the botom right. That opens the left side of the room above you. if you wall jump off the middle octogon into that small room you get the key for the left door above the main key.

Next go all the way round the map anti clockwise to avoid the rocket. Drop in through the door you just opened and get the key without getting hit by the rocket, that's hard. Then run like crazy to the bottom left of the map and simply run to the door.

It's a tough level becasue there are several hard bits that you might screw up on. It might comfort you to know that the next level is quite simple.
