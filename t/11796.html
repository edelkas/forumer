#10544 1 2006-08-09T09:44:11Z shoftee
Well, quite some time has passed since the map-pack idea came to me, so I think it's safe to say that this map pack wasn't meant to be a "legacy clone". It is true, however, that Legacy had an incredible influence on my map-making ability. I'd like to start an endless rant about what's the difference between a column and a bunch of episodes (and also the difference between an episode and a bunch of maps), but in the end I decided to make the map-pack speak for itself.
If I had to say the truth, I'm really proud of this map pack, and I feel I found my own style of map-making while I made the maps in it.
Now, onto the more important part. While I was making this map pack, I suddenly realised that I've never made a single dedication map. And since I'm such a nice guy, I decided to make, not one, not two, not THREE, but f*cking thirteen of 'em. Yes, thirteen. And, another idea that came to my mind while I was at it. Since these deds are for a few really special people, I decided to make a pretty interesting experiment. I decided, to carefully examine each of the map-making styles of twelve of those thirteen people, merge 'em with my own, and see what comes out. I should say, the results were astounding.

tktktk - Great, great, GREAT maps in the legacy. You absolutely deserve this one.

Kablizzy - Your maps were incredible too, man. Simply awesome. Keep up the awesomeness.

maximo - I think this one doesn't need to be explained. It's like... maximo. It'd be hard to find a reason NOT to make him a dedication.

nevermore - One of the best, what more is there to say?

LittleViking - As our most active admin, an NTTS first place winner, a good OP in the IRC channel, and also a great guy with great map-making ability, this one is totally justified.

Sweep - Thank you, Sweep, for bitesizeNUMA, the Dronies, and all of the other little (and big) things you've done for this community.

(after listing the legacy members, and no, I haven't forgotten about Ian and Lucidium, but I'd rather see their maps in Expanse too before doing them dedications)

KinGAleX - For the helpful comments and advice when I was in a bad mood, and the great judging in the NTTS, you totally deserve this.

spect - A good friend, a great highscorer, even after retiring from it all, and has huge potential for map-making. Also, he ran the NTTS superbly. Again, totally justified.

krusch - An astounding map-maker, a good judge, and overall a really friendly guy.

blue_tetris - A great guy, with lots of heart and talent, great story-teller, great map-maker (yes, I'm talking about MTI and stuff), and his dedication map is the easiest to recognize.

Barabajagal - Again, a great guy, an awesome map-maker, and most likely the future first place winner on NedCon 7.

Mare and Raigan - Last, but absolutely not least (don't you even dare think about it), I made a dedication to the creators of this game we all know and love. Great job guys, simply awesome. Keep it up
  • Dave - The One (guessed by thecalcgenius).
    Like I said, it's the most obvious of them all. You seem to have been thinking too much about this one. The tileset is made out of... like... tetris pieces. It's hella obvious!
  • Mare and Raigan - m.mirror.r (again, by thecalcgenius).
    Apart from the obvious title, nobody recognised the '&' sign in the tileset. In some fonts it looks like a handwritten capital E, but with two lines going up and down from it. Nevermind, this is the second most easiest, the next are gonna be harder... I hope...
  • LittleViking - Quicksort (guessed by spect).
    I really liked how this map turned out. I've always seen the LV map as a map with platforms, seeker drones and mines. Don't ask me why, I wouldn't know . Anyways, nice guess spect, really nice. Also, the way the drones go in different direction when the hit the opposite wall is so... quicksort-ish. (programmer reference, don't try understanding it).
  • spect - Vertical is for n00bs. (OMFG! spect guessed his own ded! Awesome!)
    This map, I think, is pretty metanetty. But spect's style (at least from the maps I've seen) is also pretty metanetty. So there .
  • krusch - "MUST. EAT. BRAINS." said the fluffy bunny (Again, by spect)
    Here I left the style of the map to speak for itself. Most people thought this map would be Blizzy's because of the name, but no, it's not. It's good ol' krusch's map, and a good one at that, too. The gold and mine placement are mine, at least I think so . Also, if you look at it closely, the bottom-center part of the map, that's a bunny with flappy ears, viewed from the back. The top-center part is the same bunny, but upside down, and faced from the front. And that half-tile is it's face expression. A cold, half-tile face expression... . Nice guess again, spect!
  • nevermore - "'Tis the wind < 3 > nothing < 4 >." (by bobodahobo)
    Back into the chamber turning, all my soul within me burning,
    Soon again I heard a tapping, something louder than before,
    "Surely," said I, "surely, that is something at my window lattice.
    Let me see, then, what thereat is, and this mystery explore.
    Let my heart be still a moment, and this mystery explore.
    " 'Tis the wind, and nothing more."
    Obvious enough?
  • KinGAleX - The desert ain't THAT scarce. This is. (by bobodahobo, again)
    A reference to a KinGAleX map. Yep. And cute mine-patterns too. Totally. Wewt.
  • tktktk - Modern (by spect. Nice work spect!)
    I really like the tileset and the action in this map... I don't know about you, but it seemed tktktk-ish enough
  • Kablizzy - Grablic (OMG! spect again!)
    Inspired by Legacy 49-0 - "Flatline". The name is a hint that comes from Blizzy's nedcon entry. For some reason the... uhhmmm empty... space... things... whatever, in the tileset reminded me of grabling hooks... I'm weird like that, nevermind. The way the drones block your path in this map is really interesting.
  • Barabajagal - "Sorrow more distant than a star." (spect?! Again?!)
    This one really made me think about map-making styles. From the maps Baraba has on NUMA now, I could say that he loves using floorguards, and his mine pattern is quite artsy... It came out as a pretty cool map, and it's probably the hardest of the deds. Most of you probably don't know, but "Barabajagal" is actually a name of a song. The name of this map is a line from the lyrics and somehow it really suited the map... No idea how that happened, really.
  • maximo - Destruction Art (OMG CYBY!)
    Heh. I like this map. A bit disappointed that maximo didn't like it as much though. It was inspired by 66-2 of Legacy, named "I don't wanna go to town". I've said it before, I'll say it again, me and maximo both agreed that episode 66 was hell, and I think all of the Legacy Elite would agree with that, too. And the name, the tileset reminded me of one of the "Destruction Arts" in Bleach. Too bad only a few would get the idea. Right...
  • Sweep - Ponies can be decieving. (OMG CYBY AGAINZOR!)
    Now this, is a bit illogical. When I looked through Sweep's maps on NUMA, I realised that he had no defined style... which was a kind of a shock to me, seeing as he has made maps for a long long while. Still, I remember one of his old maps called "Sun" (nice map, btw), so I decided to make something with drones going around the level... in the end I decided to make it all square-y, and make the drones go around the squares... It turned out nicely, I should say. One thing that I realised afterwards is this: "OMG! I forgot to put squares in the lower left corner! I screwed up!" and then I look up: "Phew, I forgot to put squares in the top right corner, too.. all is well...".
Right... so after a long while, you finally managed to guess all of em. Nice job, to all of you. And since TCG and bobodahobo helped out, I'ma make them deds too, I guess. And to the rest of you: That's what you get for not wanting to play a game as simple as this .

And the thirteenth is:
  • cyby (yes, I'm writing it as cyby on purpose) - ** x 4 (HA!)
    So... why cyby? Well, why not, goddammit! This guy, you guys have no idea how much he helped me with this thing. I still remember how we both double- and triple-checked the maps for fun-ness and stuff in the early hours of the morning. As my totally-worthy-of-being arch-nemesis, cyby totally deserves this ded! Right... so what's with the name? It's a bit subtle, but it's still not impossible to see. There are the letters C and J in the map. C made by the bounce blocks, and J from the gold and bounce blocks. Four times each, so there you have it: CJ x 4. Right...
SO... is that all I can make? Certainly not. In fact, you guys should actually expect another bunch o' levels a bit after Expanse (I'm about half way there already ). Again, I'd like to thank The Legacy Team for making such a great pack, KA for the helpful advice, spect for being such and nice guy, same for krusch, Baraba for helping me with the maps, Dave for all of the great MTI clones he made, cyby for the help with the all-golding and overall playtesting, and last, but not least at all, Mare and Raigan for making this wonderful game. Keep on rocking guys, you're good at it.
