Here is where you find out how to install new custom fonts to Windows XP. Any program which loads fonts, be it Microsoft Word, Adobe Photoshop or even Microsoft Paint will automatically update. Gimp, if open at the time, will require a click of the refresh button.

Step 1: Finding your font
Websites such as DaFont provide free fonts, but be wary, as some fonts cost to download, so read before you click! Once you find a nice font, click the download button for PC and you're away.

Step 2: Finding your font
This is the easy way of installing your font. Once you've unzipped your file containing the font, open the folder, there should be a file with a O, A or TT on it, letters coloured red, green and blue respectively. Highlight these and select move file from the left pane.

Step 3: Finding the Windows XP Font Directory
A little browse window will pop up. From this click all the - buttons you can see to reduce all open lists to nothing, then click the plus signs next to the following icons, in the following order.
  • C:\ Drive
  • WINDOWS
  • Fonts
Step 4: The finishing touches
Once you select the fonts directory, by highlighting it, click ok, a little window with a progress bar will show up, this is installing your font. Once that's disappeared, you're all done! Just open a program such as Photoshop and experiment!
