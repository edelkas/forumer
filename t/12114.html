Actually, no you can't. The demo system is completely deterministic - meaning that it only records key presses, and nothing else.

#219240 15 2006-08-24T18:46:25Z shoftee
Let's just say it might not be as easy as you think. (As I have already said in the thread mascote_ponto linked to).

[RANT]

Quote: thecalcgenius in that thread
Yeah, it seems (to my unworthy eyes, compared to M&R) that it would be easy to show. Since numbers are used to correspond to keypresses, perhaps we could signal -1 to be the suicide command.

Ex:
50:35791394|35791394|243|0|0|-1
would make the ninja move a ways to the right, stop, and kill himself.

Okay then, I may as well explain it...
First off, for 50 frames, it would look more like...
50:35791394|35791394|243|0|0|0|0|0
Why? Because... the numbers between the seperators are 7 frames of demo data. And since the number there is 50 (which means 51 frames, since we start the count from 0), that means the first 7 numbers are for the first 49 frames, and there's one more for the other two frames. After clearing that up, let's see what those numbers actually mean... And I'll also explain why what tcg suggested wouldn't work.

Note: if you don't understand binary, you might as well give up on reading this.

As of now, each frame can be represented as a number from 0 to 7 (inclusive). So that's from 000 to 111 in binary. Those digits describe what keys are pressed. The way the current demo data is, the right digit means you're pressing the [left] key, the middle is the [right] key, and the left is the [jump] key. For example, if we have the frame [101] it means we're pressing the [jump] key and the [left] key. Simple enough?
Okay, so how do we have one number for seven frames? Well, we just have seven bunches of 3 digits (in binary), for example [010], [010], [010], [010], [010], [010], [010] (which is pressing the [right] key for seven frames), we just make one big binary number from these.
Which would be: [010010010010010010010010010] (Note that the first of those frames is the rightmost three digits)
Converted to decimal, this is the number 35791394.
After explaining that, I'll now explain why it's gonna be troublesome to implement the [kill] key thing.
Since we have to put another key in each frame, that means we now have bunches of FOUR digits, for example [1000] means you're pressing just the [kill] key. Yes, but what do we do with existing demos then? All of the demos up until now are made for three keys, so if we add another binary digit to each frame, it's obvious why they wouldn't work.
The number above, [010010010010010010010010010] would mean that we go to the right on the first frame (the [0010] in the right) and then pressing left and committing suicide on the second (the [1001] on the left of the [0010] frame). Thus, as far as we know, implementing this would break all of the demos we have until now.
And that thing TCG suggested: for one, no negative numbers allowed, the way negative numbers are described in binary makes it impossible for this to work in all cases.

[/RANT]

Still it bothers me because it's a good suggestion that I'd like to see implemented, too... Ah well...
