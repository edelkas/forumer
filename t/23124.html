You. Hurt. My. Brain.

Chrono Trigger was one of the best and most popular RPGs on the Super Nintendo. (And that's saying a lot when we're talking abuot the system that housed such RPG giants as Final Fantasy 2 and 3, Earthbound, Secret of Mana and Mario RPG, to name a few.)

The combat has an active battle system - the kind where each player has a bar that fills up over time, and they can take an action when it's full. Faster characters fill their bars faster. It's not pure turn-based fighting, but it's far from the real-time action of Kingdom Hearts.

If you've never played Chrono Trigger, you're missing out on a great gaming experience. Actually, based on the way you asked about it, I'm guessing you haven't played a lot of Super Nintendo games. So here's what I'm going to recommend: Look around at some garage sales, swap meets and independent game stores until you find a Super Nintendo system (I can't imagine one costing more than $20 these days), buy it, and come back to us for the list of games you should buy. Again, they'll be cheap. Maybe $3 each, $7 for the expensive ones. For starters, Zelda: A Link to the Past, Super Metroid, Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy III, Super Mario World. You'll get the console and half a dozen five-star games for the price of one new DS title.
