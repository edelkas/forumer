29-3

This is held by most players to be one of the most frustrating levels in the game, and after playing it for a while you will undoubtedly agree.  The difficulty spikes midway through, but don't give up--you'll have to redo the first three.
First, evade the drones by running left and wallclimbing.  While instinct tells us to make three hops and get onto the flat ground to jump out, I have found that if you instead follow this wall for FOUR jumps, using the fourth to cross to the right side so that all it takes is one more tap to get you out, you will succeed 100% of the time (once you know what you're doing), whether the drones chase you or not.  Doing this part efficiently means you can spend more time in the hard part and finish sooner.  Once at the top, descending into the pit is a simple matter of falling down to the uppermost slope and then running right.  Don't take any chances on the thwump, he's not worth it.
Now, here's the hard part.  I can imagine everybody having their own opinion on the most efficient way of getting up, and which is best probably varies from person to person.  I'll just give my way, in case you can't figure out one of your own.  When coming out of the tunnel, jump on the slope and then wait a half a second before jmping off the wall.  When you do, head for the second jumppad from the bottom.  It takes some expirementing to get the missile where you won't hit it coming up, but its very possible.  The pad will shoot you up and then the slope above you will send you to the left.  Get up against the left wall and allow yourself to fall almost but not wuite all the way to the jumppad below you, then make a quick walljump (landing on the slope right next to you) to crash the missiles above you.  Hit the pad, walljump, and get yourself over to the middle area.  It sounds a lot longer than it is, but its still not one of the fastest routes.
Now, for the last area.  It doesn't really matter whether the chaingun drone is up top or on the bottom because its the same technique either way.  Jump so that you will fall between the middle ground and the closest mines to either side (you can do the right if you walljump from the right wall), falling as straight as you can and landing on one of the sloped tiles.  Then wuickly run towards the exit to avoid the potentially most infuriating death in history and fall onto the exit.

Congratulations!  You're almost through this nightmare.

29-4

Almost there!  First, start by running right and quickly climbing the wall.  When you reach the top, you're gonna want to grab the switch and then leap across the chasm to the other side, and as long as you leap in time you should be safe from the chaingun's first volley.  Now, here's where I differ from Maniac.  While his technique may not waste any time, I'm yet to get into the center with it without getting shot.  So if you just wanna get the episode over with, wait on the upper-felt ledge until the chaingun on the right starts charging, then drop down to the left just before he does so that he can't hit you.  Don't drop too far, because the manouver is pointless if you can't walljump back out as soon as his spray has passed you, and you'll probably just geth it by his buddy.  But if you timed it right, youwon't have to worry about bullets as you leap across the chasm grabbing the exit switch and the left inner one.  How you handle the remaining two switches is up to you (personally I used the wall to stop my fall, then dropped to the ground and ran around to use the jumppad to get me high enough for both switches), but remember not to trap yourself with lasers above the jumppad and hurry up because things get really messy if the other chaingun comes around before you're done.  You may have to run to the left wall before going into the exit tunnel if he's made it to the bottom of his cycle by the time you're going for the exit, so as to throw off his aim.  Just remember: keep in mind the chaingun AND the lasers before doing anything, and make sure they're pointed away.
