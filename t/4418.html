#4026 1 2005-08-13T18:54:04Z tktktk
You guys may have heard some say that eventually we're going to run out of ideas for making maps, since there are only so many working combinations of enemies, objects and tiles. I started this project to prove them wrong.

How am I doing this? By making a series of unique maps using only mines, gold, tiles, ninja, and exit. This will show that if I can make fun, challenging, and radically differing maps with such limited resources, we can make exponentially more with all the options available to us. You may have seen the episode I posted on Numa and in the Episodes Thread. That was the first episode in this series. For those of you that didn't see those maps, I encourage you to check them out as well.

My Simple Challenges were limiting in that they each pinpointed one specific aspect of playing N. With these episodes I'll be able to go beyond that limitation and do anything that's conceivably possible with what I have to work with. Also, note that while the Simple Challenges didn't really take advantage of my mapmaking ability, these maps will. Rather than churning these out in large numbers, I'm spending as much time and effort on these as I would any other map.

Here are all the Minimalist Series episodes. I really want feedback on these, so please leave comments, suggestions, etc.
