OK, after having seen all three next-gen consoles in action, it's time to release my verdict on which is most likely to win the war. This will be an unbiased opinion.

Nintendo Wii
Lets start with the one I own, the Wii is an exceptional console, which lacks power, but has many other capabilities to make up for it. The most important of this being the Wii's remote, which shocked most people upon revelation. Control systems using this feel much more realistic, compared to beefed up graphics using a typical games controller. The downsides are the fact that bluetooth can really mess up the remote and the sensor bar, also most of the main features are only accessible with a wireless internet connection, or having a Nintendo Wi-Fi USB Connector; costing around 25. The games available for this console are truly superb; The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess is the main title for the Wii's release, and it won over most people from the PS2's generation. Red Steel is the main FPS for the console, with Call of Duty III following close behind. My final opinion of the console is great, it rocks, it gives an exhilirating experience that cannot be matched.

Microsoft Xbox 360
The release of the 360 one year ago tempted me a lot, this made me think wether or not to buy this now to get my fix of Nex-Gen gaming, or wait until the 'Revolution' came out. I chose the latter, and the name change didn't put me off. Back to the 360; the main appeal is Xbox Live, this is the best online gaming system for consoles at the moment, even surpassing Nintendo's Wi-Fi system; it has a downside though, cost. You have to pay to use Xbox Live; it's worth it though, with games like Halo 3 on the horizon, this looks to be a promising year for Microsoft. Consoles bought recently will have had the overheating problem solved (I hope), but early consoles shut down because of the temperature rise. Another downside is the Premium/Core packages, you may think that you get a better deal getting the Core package and a Hard Drive; this is a bad idea, get the Premium package so you also get the chrome disk tray. The nicest idea was the face plates, they can make your Xbox unique to you, so I would advise getting one.

Sony Playstation 3
At the time of writing, this console has yet to be released in the UK, so I have had the priviledge of seeing one in action. This console has a few downsides, the Blu-Ray disks may never be popular; making this a risk. Also the price is astonishing, 500-600 is a lot of money that some people may not be able to afford. But this console is worth the price, the most expensive console with the 60Gb hard drive is about 600, but looking at the graphics and power it can churn out, it looks truly amazing. If you want your fix of proper next-gen games, this is where it's at. The price and lack of immense launch titles may put buyers off, but in the future this may seem like a purchase worth its cost. The style of the console looks really groovy too, which I like, the size and weight (5kg) mean moving it about is hard work, but they are not really downsides to the console.

After looking in depth at all three consoles, my verdict is that the Wii will come out victorious if Nintendo keeps bringing out good unique games for it. It still has games for the die hard gamer, while new, unique games will attract new buyers. DS compatability is also a high point for the console, and Pokmon Battle Revolution seems to be it's Joker. People think it will be bad, as it's Pokmon, but it is leading the way with DS Connectivity and Wi-Fi. The Xbox 360 will be it's main rival, but unfortunately the PS3 will lack with sales because of it's price. Wait a while and the PS3's price will crash, like the PSP's did, it looks brilliant, and I love it, but I just wouldn't buy one.
