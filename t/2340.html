It's not teleporters specifically, but bugs in general. But here's why I'm definitely against teleporters:

1. There's no way to distinguish between them and regular launchpads.

2. Virtually any map that has one lacks the polish we're looking for anyway.

3. It's inconsistent with the other levels. It might be cool, but if you start out with your basic items for the first 300 maps and then add these just out of the blue, you're going to leave people very confused.

I don't mind things like squeezing through tight spaces or trapped drones quite as much, and I think Lucidium's Easy Squeezy and LV's One-Way are some rare examples of bugs used in great maps with the right kind of feel to them.
