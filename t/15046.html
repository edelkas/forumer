#282062 12 2007-01-06T18:56:32Z Spawn of Yanni
Clicking every frame? Not sure what you mean by that (maybe something I don't know about) but what I do know is that, in debug mode, you have to have CAPS LOCK on to play the levels. So, when you're at the debug menu, you can press [P] to get the level ready, then turn on caps lock which allows you to actually play through it. You could also switch caps lock on first, and then press [P] to play the level.

When playing a level in debug mode, you can do some extra stuff, too. If you die, you can press [enter] and the ninja will respawn where your cursor is. Also, when you die, you can press [spacebar] to toggle between having your ninja in one piece and exploded, and can hold [shift] and move your mouse around to move the ninja around after he's dead (this also works when he's exploded). Have fun!
