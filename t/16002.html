Okay, so it's not entirely free. But it's still fun nonetheless. In the free game, you're limited to a few places. (Unless you get a travel pass in the game.)

If you want to play more of the game, you can sign up for Guardianship. (For a one time payment of $20.00 USD) If you want some more upgrades to your character with Guardianship, you can get them for only $5.00 USD more.

There are some rare items in the game, including the "Blade of Awe" Which is a hard weapon to get (Since it's separated into pieces) and holiday rares which sometimes can be only available once in the game.
