Edit: Wait, I have a feeling that you're talking about recording a death replay in user levels or something. In that case, no, not possible. However, you can do it in Ned.

To record replay, press R, then 1, and play.
To watch replay, press 3, then Q, then play.

Use the lower textbox in the debug menu to get your data.
