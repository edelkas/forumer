I don't know what you mean by online/offline images...

but here is a rundown of the other things:

smilies
Are you talking about how the smilies always appear at the bottom of a post when you click on them? That happens for everyone. If it is annoying you, then when you click on the "Show all smilies" button then just add in the code for the smily you want. That is usually what I do.

spoiler
If you want to know how a spoiler works, there are some things that become frustrating when you first try to use them (believe me I spent over an hour figuring it out).
When you make spoilers in your forum post and then click "Preview post" it doesn't show the actual spoilers. Instead it gives the code for the spoilers, which can really throw some people off. Don't worry about that. Some things that may help are:
Spoiler: When you make a spoiler with a name you have to type "spoiler=" and then type the text you wantNow I have made a spoiler. I can put whatever I want in here.
to end the spoiler I put "/spoiler" (replace the quotations with brackets). That is pretty much it. Don't worry if the Post Preview doesn't show you the spoiler, they do that so that you can see all the text or images or whatever you have put in.


Hope that helps! Let me know or someone else know if you are still having trouble.
