Oh. My. God.

There's a free version-thingy of a Trackmania game! Right Here

For those of you who have never played this fantastic, fantastic game, it's basically to Gran Turismo what N is to Super Mario Brothers. In fact, there are many similarities between trackmania and N.
Both have fantastic physics engines, both are tile-based. The original trackmania had a puzzle element in it - you had to set up the tiles so the track was as fast as possible. Or, you could just race. And you had to use the physics to race well - breaking before a down slope so you get a perfect landing off it at the end, etc...

Really, Trackmania is one of my favouritest games ever. And they've released a free stand-alone addon! Excellent!
