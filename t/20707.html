#390687 4 2007-12-18T16:28:24Z iangb
As soon as you land on the end of the arrow, hit jump (and left, a little). This takes you to the other end of the five gold pieces: if you are quick you should have time to hit jump again and go over the horizontal thwump. From there, as your screenshot shows, you've left yourself with two options: either 2 or 3 precise jumps to avoid the mines and get to the start of the final vertical part, or wait until the horizontal thwump returns and ride it (walljumping against it's back) over the mines. An easier way is to get the trap door switch that is below and to the left of the ninja in your screenshot: this makes the going much easier.
Getting to the top is just precision, then to complete the final straight you need precision and speed. As soon as you hit the slope under the second gold jump up to the third piece: the thwump should pass below you. Land in the gap between the mines and then you have one final jump to make to the exit.

Good luck.
