Now for zaka, there are two ways you can play levels (which you'll find at http://numa.notdot.net ). There's debug mode (in Ned) and userlevels mode.
To play in debug mode, start up N and press ~ to go into debug mode. Copy the "Map Data" from Numa (not the "1.4+ Map Data") and paste it in the top box. Click outside the box and press L to load the level, then press P to being playing. To play at normal speed, turn caps lock on.
To play in Userlevels mode, you need to enter the data into your userlevels.txt file. For that, simply open the userlevels.txt, scroll to the bottom, and paste the "1.4+ Map Data" from Numa. For the Userlevels mode to work, you need to have the N executable and the userlevels.txt file in the same folder.

Hope that helps.
