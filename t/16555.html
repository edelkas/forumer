Quote: Jakel
How to put images in your signature (By Jakel)

I have this tutorial for two different web browsers: Firefox and Internet Explorer.

If you use Firefox:[/b]
1. Host an image at Photobucket or Imageshack.
2. Right-click on the uploaded image, and click on "Copy Image Location". This will now send the image link to your clipboard.
3. Go to the "My Controls" link at the top of the page, and click "Edit my Signature".
4. Press the "[IMG]"[/img] button at the top of the text box. After that, a pop-up should, well, pop-up. Press "Ctrl+V" (For Windows), or right-click and click on "Paste" in the text line, and press "OK". If you look back into the signature text box, it should say something like: 
[IMG]http://someimageuploadingsite.com/somenumberandlettercombination/thenameofthefilethatyouuploaded.somefilettype
5. Press "Update My Signature". It should now show an image above the signature text box.

You have now just put an image into your signature! Congratulations!

If you use Internet Explorer:[/b]
1. Host an image at Photobucket or Imageshack.
2. Right-click on the uploaded image, and click on "Properties", and a box should pop up.
3. Highlight the long image URL, and copy it (Ctrl+C or right click and press "Copy"). This will now send the image link to your clipboard.
4. Go to the "My Controls" link at the top of the page, and click "Edit my Signature".
5. Press the "[IMG]"[/img] button at the top of the text box. After that, a pop-up should, well, pop-up. Press "Ctrl+V" (For Windows), or right-click and click on "Paste" in the text line, and press "OK". If you look back into the signature text box, it should say something like: 
[IMG]http://someimageuploadingsite.com/somenumberandlettercombination/thenameofthefilethatyouuploaded.somefilettype
6. Press "Update My Signature". It should now show an image above the signature text box.

You have now just put an image into your signature! Congratulations!
