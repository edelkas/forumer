1.5 will be just the same as 1.4, free, etc..

about the zoom: the right-stick pans and clicking it adjusts the zoom. there is a "regular" static, non-scrolling fully zoomed out setting which makes it just like the original, and then two levels of zoomed-in-ness. this was necessary in order to support tiny TVs where fully zoomed-out is too small to see anything and/or people who sit very far from the TV. if like us you really prefer the original non-scrolling view, you may need to sit closer depending on the size of the TV -- with a 20" TV, sitting 4 feet away is fine, but 10 feet is probably going to require some zooming!

we're sorry to those we've disappointed with the graphical changes, we didn't do them ourselves but we did direct the artist and approved everything, they weren't just made without our consent! i suppose it's inevitable that changes will always result in mixed reactions.

the good/bad news is that the DS/PSP graphics are even more of a departure _BUT_ there is also a "pure mode" option which lets you have the original graphics.

#389450 84 2007-12-14T22:05:54Z Raigan
i don't think anyone knows the actual release date because the game is still being programmed as we speak.

we still haven't seen the multiplayer maps for the handhelds, we did all the maps for the XBLA version, but we only had time to make the singleplayer levels on DS/PSP.

the XBLA version is done and is just waiting to be released, it's exactly what we wanted to make, just like the original. the editor isn't as nice since gamepad just doesn't work as well as mouse, but the multiplayer makes up for that.

for the handhelds, they're still not done -- there are still lots of small problems with things not feeling or behaving correctly, and we're still working with the developer to try to get these all fixed so that the game plays identically to N. but, since it's not done we don't know yet how well it will turn out.

so right now we can only really endorse the XBLA version, the DS/PSP is definitely getting there but is still not good enough to release. the various dates are most likely estimates.
