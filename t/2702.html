"Banannakid" you're just in luck, ive got the exact same laptop (5150 3ghz) and i had the exact same problem, this occured to me about a year ago, i was playing CS, everything was fine, all of a sudden, things shifted to a bullet-time like situation, i ignored it, and noticed it was the reason of me getting completely owned...

Anyway, i fiddled around with the BIOS, to get there, as its starting up (when you see the big blue dell) press F2 and you'll be in the BIOS config and noticed it wasnt the sound card, hard drive, drivers, anything, i came to a conclusion, its the processor, you see, dell put 2 processor's inside of the machine, as a 3ghz processor on its own would cost a insane amount (at that time) and these two processors are configured by a little thing called "Intel Speedstep", this has made me hate intel for the rest of my life.

What Speedstep does is adjust the speed if the processor by judging whats going on, in our case, it jumps from 3ghz, to 1.6ghz, i decided to disable speedstep, the processor now runs at 1.6ghz, but the battery now lasts up to 5 hours, it doesnt run ANY different and N still runs at a cool 10-15ms. (around the 20's in a busy situation), not only that, the slow-mo (which changed into a slideshow when i didnt install the dell P4 drivers for the battery) was gone!, problem solved!.

Now, in order to do this, ill explain how to get into the BIOS again...

Start up the laptop, as the bar is filling up and you can see the big blue dell press F2, you'll end up in the BIOS config, you will know when you notice that all the text is green, press ALT+P a few times untill you see two options...

Intel Speedstep - Enabled (Change to Disabled)
Hyperthreading - Enabled (DO NOT TOUCH)

now reboot it and play N for about 30/40 minutes, you'll notice that the problem is gone (hopefully).

And i'd like to point out, DO NOT ASK DELL, i tried the same last year, the only intelligent responce they gave me was "maybe its your processor" after i said that it probably was 500 times.

And if you completely loose sound one day, dont worry, its not the drivers, its the hardware, you can either, get charged about $800 (Australian) for a motherboard replacement, or do what im doing, get a specialist to replace the soundcard for around $300 (Australian), this friday a shiny new Creative sound card will be in this =)

i hope my solution solves your problem
