How do you put your own user levels on the gmae?
#129813 2 2006-01-05T02:46:34Z Ngaged
When you downloaded N, it should've come with a Userlevels.txt. That is the place where you can put your N levels.

However, you must put them in in a certain way. Here:
$Title Of Your Map#Author (Or just your name would be fine.)#Level Type (Action, Race, Puzzle, etc.)#Level Data#
And it's VERY important that you don't forget that last # in it!

Then the final thing you have to do is to make sure N and the Userlevels.txt are in the same directory. For example, I put both N and my Userlevels.txt on my desktop!

Once you do all this, you should have your maps ready to go!
I hope I made this all clear to you!
