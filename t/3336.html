This is happening, guys. No more procrastination.
Here goes: A series of collaboration maps.
The next person to post gets to continue on the map I've made, and I'll PM it to them.
You are allowed to do a section 23 to 92 tiles in area. 23 tiles is one full column, from top to bottom, one tile wide. 92 is a column from top to bottom 4 tiles wide.
Use these tiles wisely, and make sure you don't go over the limit. This doesn't have to be a room map, and I'd like it not to be. I suggest you first decide what section you want to create, and then add appropriate items and tiles. So, here we go!
When you post and you don't want to be a part of this, say that in your post please.
And please be prepared to wait a little while to get the map PMed to you.
Once we fill up a full map, I will create a username; you guys can choose what it's called. We will submit our collaboration maps with that username.

So let's start working! By the way, I haven't finished my section, so the next person will have to wait a half hour or so...

If you wish to edit or veto a section or addition, you may do so, but only after asking the person who added that. If that person says no, and you still strongly believe that this correction should be made, post the map on this page.
Yay!
