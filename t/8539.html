Final Fantasy 12 came on on the 16th here. I resisted buying it for a whole week, but then I couldn't take it anymore, and I caved in and bought it last Friday. It was just getting so much praise heaped on it that I figured I'd be able to get into it even though it's in Japanese.

I was really wary, because I've bought several other RPGs here, only to give up within the first few hours (or first few minutes with Suikoden 3 ), simply because the language barrier made it nearly impossible to enjoy the game.

I had no idea how much my enjoyment of a game comes from being able to understand it effortlesssly. Even action games like God of War and Ratchet and Clank are drastically less fun than their english counterparts.

However, like I said, I could resist and I bought it. I've played it for about 20 hours or so (I've logged 40 hours cause I forgot to turn it off overnight), and I was a bit skeptical at first, but I'm really starting to get into this game. The battle system is a quite radical departure from past Final Fantasy conventions, but it's a most welcome change. No more random battles at all. In fact, no more battle screen at all. It's not even like Crono Trigger where you can see the enemies and when you get into a fight the battle menus come up and it plays out like a normal RPG battle. This is pretty much real time fighting here.

I haven't played many MMORPGs, but I'm told that the fighting is very similar to them. You see an enemy on the screen, you walk up to it and your character will begin to attack it. There's an action bar which fills up, and when it's full your character will attack, and the bar will reset and begin filling up again. You're free to move about and run away (literally run away), you can interupt your character at any time and give him new orders, like item or magic. You only control one character at a time, but you can have 3 members active in battle at one time. The two you don't control will attack continuously, and you can customize their AI through the "gambit" system (sort of like a rudimentary programming language actually) After 20 hours, I am really digging this game's battle system.

And also, after 20 hours, the story is beginning to pick up and I'm getting intrigued. It definitely starts out slow, but there are a ton of hints that everything's going to get alot more complex and involving. In terms of plot, and this is the highest possible praise I can levy upon a game, FF12 is similar to FF Tactics. It's obstensibly about a war, but the war is only the front for one side to gain these immensley powerful god stones.

So that's all I've got at the moment. If you want more in depth reviews and pictures and movies and stuff check out the major game sites. All of them have got some good preview coverage.
