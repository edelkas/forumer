Spoiler: Part 1||Chapter 1N bit his lip as the bullet bit the ground. He could feel the heat from the rocket behind him.
He landed like a cat on a sloped tile, ducked and cursed as a laser beam shot past him, then leaped off the tile, clearing a distance equal to four times his height. He grabbed the edge of a bounceblock and pulled himself up.
As he felt a slight pressure under his foot, a ring of trapdoors closed around the exit. N cursed again, loudly, and slipped off the bounceblock, landing on the laser drone below him. The drone hummed loudly in protest. Seconds later, N felt it heat up.
He had seconds.
N soared off the drone, executed a fluid double backflip, and dug his hands and feet into one of the trapdoors. He could feel, rather than see, a laser dot on his back. He walljumped upward and snatched onto the edge of a one-way platform, just as the drone opened fire.
The 3500˚C+ temperature of the laser was enough to melt through the trapdoor. N whirled as he heard the thump-thump-thump of chaingun bullets slamming into the ground. With superhuman reflexes, he snatched one of the bullets out of the air, dodged the rest, then plummeted, smashing the bullet through the laser drone's hard outer shell.
It crackled and whined. N could feel it heat up, and he positioned the gaping hole in the direction of the chaingun.
A wave of heat, a loud zap, and the chaingun hissed, buzzed, and crashed to the tiles below it.
N leaped onto the bounceblock, then double-jumped off of it, grabbing the edge of the cooling gap in the trapdoor. He pulled himself through, ready to depress the exit switch, leave this level, and finally achieve transcendence.
He saw a flash of color in the corner of his eye. N whirled, just as the thing disappeared below a tile. Exit forgotten, he jogged over and looked down. His heart stopped.
There, on a locked door, sat a blue ninja in the lotus position. N could tell it was a female-and exceptionally beautiful.
She looked up, saw him, and was gone.
Spoiler: Part 1||Chapter 2He woke up.
It was 4 o'clock in the morning. N lay quietly in bed. Ever since he had taken up a life of chaos and death, he had had recurring bad dreams, and last night was no different. He remembered the hundreds of chainguns, thwumps raining from the sky, and, of course, N's nemesis Espada777777. N and Espada were twins, separated at birth. And while N fought for justice, Espada fought for chaos. Neither had ever met the other, but both had heard the legends of their brother.

N got out of bed, poured himself a coffee, turned on the computer, and opened his message service. "You have one new message," mumbled the computer in blank voice. N opened the message. "Assignment Number 231," he read, and sighed at the thought.
"For twelve years now, a ninja known as Espada777777 has caused destruction and despair." Shocked, N opened his eyes wide. "We have found his secret lair, and need you to infiltrate it. You must execute Espada and save a close friend of ours who he has kidnapped."
N was curious to who this person was. He read on.
"Her name is Lepidoptera. She is a highly skilled blue ninja as well as an acclaimed master of sabotage."
"Who is this blue ninja?" N thought to himself. The message went on.
"We believe that Lepidoptera has been brainwashed, and we now have evidence to believe that she is hidden in Espada's lair."
For a moment, his mind was blank.
Then with a shock, N remembered that fateful evening five long years ago. The blue ninja that disappeared leapt unbidden to his mind. He recalled his endless searches for her. A red surge of hatred for his treacherous twin boiled in his chest, but he held it in.
"You must do this, N," commanded the message, "for the good of the country, the world, the whole of ninja kind and Lepidoptera. Do you accept?"
The email ended. N sat for a few seconds, pondering.
He hit the reply button. "Yes."

Spoiler: Part 1||Chapter 3N sat in the helicopter, downdraft from the blades whipping his nonexistent hair around his nonexistent face. He leaned out the open side and looked down at the cement below him. Then he looked up at the pilot, who was watching him, headphones and microphone positioned oddly on his triangular head.
N gave him a thumbs-up silently, and they lifted off as the door hatch slid into place.
N donned his own headset and adjusted the mike, adjusting the dial on the side for frequency. He found the pilot's and sat back in the seat, thinking.
"Kid," crackled the pilot's voice through the ancient headphones. "We'll be there in about four hours. Espada's lair is one of the most dangerous places on earth, so I'm gonna give you cover fire for about 300 yards. Then you're on your own."
N sighed and tilted his head back. 'Kid'? Some kind of official operation this was. He looked back at the pilot.
"What do you know about Lepidoptera and Espada?" he asked.
"Not much. All I know is, I was ferrying Lepidoptera back to the HQ. She said she had some incriminating pictures, I dunno what that meant. Then, suddenly, there was some kinda electric pulse, and I lost control of the chopper. We were about eighty feet above the helipad, and we both jumped as the copter fell. I came out fine, but I think Lepidoptera was hit with some debris and got knocked out. Next thing I know, there's someone pressing a gun to my back. I see Lepidoptera picked up by a ninja in black and get carried away, and the gun guy goes after them. I climb down to HQ and tell everyone what happened, and naturally they called you, N."
"You didn't even try to rescue her yourself?"
The pilot turned around in his seat and looked at N for a long time. Presently he said, "Kid, I've learned a lot of things in my life as a soldier. First thing I learned was, if you can't do it, don't try. Works marvelously."
N felt fury building in his chest. "You chose your own skin over another ninja's. What kind of soldier are you?"
The pilot turned back and looked out the front window. "Kid, you don't antagonize a pilot. I'm liable to drop you off right here if you don't stop trash-talk..."
"What's wrong?" asked N.
A beam of heat sheared through the window, blasting pieces off the rotor and melting into the engine. The helicopter died as the pilot wrestled furiously with the controls. They began to plummet.
"Laser drone," screamed the pilot. "Gotta be a damn dozen of 'em. Help me!"
"I can't fly a copter!"
"You're supposed to be a ninja! Get me outta here! I don't wanna die!" The pilot's voice rose to a shrill scream.
N stared at him. "Save you? After what you said? No thanks, bub. I'm out."
He strapped on his parachute and turned to leap from the broken window.
He couldn't do it.
He had to save the pilot.
N turned back and grabbed the pilot with one strong arm. "Hold on tight, I don't know if this thing can hold both of us."
Another laser flashed through N's field of vision, and he leapt from the helicopter's cockpit as it exploded.
They fell toward the desert.
Spoiler: Part 1||Chapter 4A laser flashed inches above N's head and he heard his parachute rip as it caught fire. He grabbed the pilot to help slow their descent.
They smashed into a sand dune. N let out a grunt of pain as his leg twisted under him. He reached out and felt the pilot's pulse--stable, but faint, and growing fainter.
He gritted his teeth against the pain and lifted the pilot, limping through the dunes until he tripped on a protruding stone. Lasers exploding around him, N fell into the dank darkness of an underground cave.
He regained his footing as he landed, looking around, eyes opened as wide as he could open them in vain search for light. He felt around for the pilot and located the unconscious ninja, hoisting him again over his shoulders.
Which way to go? he thought, swiveling his head and sniffing the air.
Then he saw it--a faint, almost nonexistent flicker in the opposite direction of the cave mouth.
Light.
With only a moment's hesitation, he looked back, then limped off toward the flicker, carrying the injured pilot on his back.
