I've explored lots of them and I believe GIMP to be the best. The major fault of most of these programs is that they try to hard to resemble everything that Photoshop is. They end up being mini-versions of Photoshop with tons of missing options. GIMP has most of Photoshop's options and if you use Gimpshop, a mod which makes GIMP look like Photoshop, it's nearly up to par.
