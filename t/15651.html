Okay, as everyone would have notices there is a lot of forums popping up all over the place, now if you follow some of the things here, it would help to have you forum become more succesful and mabey stop so many dissapointments.
  • Have an idea that is new - No point creating another image forum, do something new and different.
  • Members are the most important things - No point asking for staff members if they have no-one to moderate.
  • Don't over-rate yourself or the forum - Such as making your self stand out, just act like the members and don;t ubuse your powers.
  • Have a skin that's light and inviting, not black and dull. I just visited Elements forum and I'm staying due mostl to the skin.
  • Staff members are not important till you have members that you can trust.
  • Don't rush into things, lets things happen slowly.
  • Don't put so much effort into the forum until you know it'll pay off.
These are just some pointers that I have come across from the many forums I've made. Also, don't rip of other poeples ideas. Such as I've seen many people use the forum rules from here and forum descriptions, it's your forum do your own work.
