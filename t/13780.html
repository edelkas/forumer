Strength: 8. I'm too lightly built to have much strength even if I worked out, and I haven't been doing much excercise lately.

Constitution: 12, and Great Fortitude feat. I'm a bit unfit, so I can't run that far, but I rarely get ill or badly injured. The only time I've needed to see a doctor in the last five years has been to have my wisdom teeth out, and everyone commented on how well I healed up afterwards.

Intelligence: 18. I think we all saw that coming.

Wisdom: 12. I'm a bit absentminded, but not really naive. I can also be annoyingly stubborn at times.

Charisma: 8. I'm very introverted, and my eccentric nature often annoys others.

Feats:
Agile
Great Fortitude
Combat Expertise (Take penalty to attack roll to increase defence - I tend to be cautious and defensive rather than aggresive)
Dodge
Improved Counterspell ("That's physically impossible, because...")
Weapon Finesse (Use dexterity rather than strength)
Spell Focus: Evocation (Physicist)
Silent Spell
Persuasive (I may not be charismatic, but I am good with language)
Negotiator (Ditto)
Skill Focus: Concentration (Augh! Exams!)
Stealthy (+2 to hide/move silently)
Fade into Violence (Take advantage of confusion of battle to slip away from danger)

Class: Wizard, obviously. Although I have quite a few cross-class skills thanks to my intelligence. I'm definately not a barbarian, since I'm well-educated, restrained, and not very strong. I don't have the charisma to be a bard or sorcerer, or the faith to be a cleric. I don't have enough strength to be a fighter or ranger (and I detest manual labour), and I prefer civilised comforts to the outdoors, so I'm certainly not a druid. I could be a rogue, but wizard suits me better because it has greater emphasis on intelligence and study, and less on lurking in dark alleys and such.

Alignment: Mostly neutral good, occasionally true neutral.
