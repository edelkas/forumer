#165560 3 2006-04-16T23:43:24Z tktktk
Okay, I made some changes based on your comments. I also added one new level, Garden Heart, which has the same thorny vine type thing as Hanging Garden but plays much differently. See the updated pack at the bottom of this post.

Earl of Tastyburger: Changed the center gold placement and the launchpads. Now you won't be smashed back down if you jump by the exit, the exit switch, or either end.

Pinned Down: Changed name to Scramble, as that's basically the feel you get when playing this one. Removed aesthetic enemies. Moved the entire level over to the right a bit. Put two blank boxes "shooting out" at the end of most of the lasers. Lined up the gold placement with the lasers' lines of sight.

Spherical Playground: Changed the gold placement to wrap around the edges of the circles. Added some additional gold.

Flagship Dynasty: I originally had more gold at the top, but it ended up looking kinda cluttered. Also, I wanted to keep the amount of gold proportional to the risk of getting it. The bottom gold is a lot riskier to get in my opinion.

Blind Spot: That's an interesting idea, but the disorientation of looking from the ninja to the drone is supposed to be part of the challenge. I suggest focusing on the ninja while keeping the drone in your peripheral vision. Then when you see the drone get closer to the horizontal shaft, you can shift your focus to it so you can time your movements accordingly.

Hanging Garden: Got rid of the four launchpads on the right and the rightmost trapdoor. Added a trapdoor on the left and moved one of them a bit to the right, plus a floorguard and a "3" tile on the ground. Should be somewhat easier now.
