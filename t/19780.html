#372466 3 2007-10-16T23:03:25Z shoftee
Oh, I agree with LV.
Moving N wouldn't help at all. Firstly, you should get a program like CCleaner and use that to get rid of temporary files. (Make sure you back up your N data before you do that, the program has a history of wiping the shared objects that N uses.)
You'll also want to defragment your C drive, at the least.
Generally, it's good to not keep useless stuff on it.
