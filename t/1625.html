#37295 2 2004-11-21T17:14:33Z tktktk
Very fun, but a bit too easy. On the one hand, jumping and bouncing around while throwing beads all over the place is a blast. Once you get master the controls you can just go crazy. On the other, death is trivially simple to avoid, since you can just sit in a corner and wait for your health to regenerate. Those berry-shooting enemies were very irritating had me doing a lot of that. Plus, it's too easy to reach any area by just holding the up button. I do like the Yoshi's Island style graphics though.

Overall, it's a great little diversion and I definitely recommend it if you want a break from the frustrations of N.

Score: 7/10

Edit: After getting farther into the game, I realized it actually ramps up in difficulty very quickly. It can be frustrating after all. My score still stands though.
