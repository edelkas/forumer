Ok. This is called the letter game. This is how it works.


Example:
player 1: AHHHH! He's coming to get me!!!!!!
player 2: Being silly again? please...
player 3: Can we lower the volume? I'm trying to study

All you have to do is make a word sentence or series of sentences. Whatever letter the first word in the last post started with, you start the first letter of the first word in your post with the next letter.

There are 4 rules:
1. They do have to make sense. This is not acceptable: "This stupid ping-pong TV book!"
2.They can only have 2-18 words, but there can be as many sentences as you want.
3. They do have to at least have some continuation of the sentence before. This is also not acceptable.
player 1: Help! I lost my cell phone!
player 2: I like paper
4. Don't copy sentences or partially use one from someone else. You have to make up your own.

If and once we get to Z, we'll just go back to A.

I'll start at A: And here we are, enjoying life! Well, at least for now...

Have fun!
