#119540 3 2005-12-01T14:24:35Z Guiseppi
Go to a map, and copy the level data from the box thingy, and in the game, press \ or ~, and then select all and paste the data into the top box, click outside of the box, and press "L" to load it. Then press "p" to play the level, and make sure caps lock is ON. to reset the level, go back to the debug menu with \ or ~, and press R, also, you can reincarnate yourself where the mouse is by pressing ENTER. Hope I was helpful!

No, no, no, I think what he means is how to get the level into userlvls...What you do:

You can't just paste that level data into userlevels.txt, you have to do it in this format:

$Name of map#Author#Category#Map data#

Then you can play it from your user levels menu!
