#147662 5 2006-02-23T23:47:09Z Maniac
Alrighty then. Here's what to do:

1. Make two seperate areas with 1-wide dead-end passageways somewhere in them. Put launchpads in the end of these. Place the pad the ninja will exit out of first, then the one the ninja will hit.

2. Delete all walls in between the two areas.

3. Go to the text box with your level code in it. Go to the end of the code. You should see something like the following:
2^xcoordinate,ycoordinate,xacceleration,yacceleration

The 2^ means it's a launchpad, and the first two variables represent its location. The last two represent x and y acceleration.

4. Positive numbers launch the ninja up and to the right, negative numbers launch the ninja down and to the left. Type in different numbers into the xacceleration and yacceleration fields, then load the level.

5. Go down the narrow corridor, then enter frame-by-frame mode. Hit the launchpad, then note where the first and second frames of the ninja are.

6. Mess with the x and y acceleration until the ninja hits the other launchpad in the second frame.

7. Congrats! You now have a fully functional one-way teleporter.
