#233380 2 2006-09-25T22:18:51Z stepself
You need to wash with antibacterial soap every day for a few weeks at least. When I got mine done, I was told to use ointment like Polysporin on the ear, every night for a few weeks as well. My ear took forever to heal, so I suggest shortening that time down to one week maybe (apparently Polysporin reduces risk of infection, but increases the healing time). During that time, you need to "floss the hole", so to speak. Put ointment on the pierced area, and slide the piercing back and forth to spread it through.

Also, if you have a piercing that can be removed, don't take it out for a few months. It'll heal over pretty quickly otherwise.
