get featured.
But, I believe that featured maps should be chosen from bitesized maps, because bitesized maps are maps that generally fit the liking of the community -- featured maps, although a good idea, depend upon the liking of the reviewer only. Picking featured maps from bitesized maps (and returning the bitesizing function) will, for the most part, make sure the community agrees with the feature.

Hopefully, we can get 100 people to vote here, and then someone can get Arachid or whoever's running Numa to come and make changes geared towards the majority.

southpaw's take on the issue:

I think that there are some things that need to be cleared up. Not all of the current Reviewers on NUMA are the best with words. Some even write at a grade 5 reading level, which just doesn't cut it for me.

If Bitesizing were to come back there are two things I would hope to make clear:
  • Bitesizer's should NOT be Reviewer's, and vice versa.
  • Featured maps should not be able to be bitesized, but bitesized maps should be allowed to be featured.
Bitesizer's and reviewers should be separate because there are people who have a keen sense of what a great map is that should have the ability to show everyone else, even though their choices of words may not be stellar. This is where Reviewer's come in. They should only be able to show the maps that they think are the cream of the crop and be able to eloquently explain why they think so. This is also why I think the Reviewer's section needs to be cleaned up. Not all the reviewer's have a great choice of words and would be much better suited having essentially the same position but without having to pen the reasons for their decision.

As for Featured maps not able to be bitesized, I think that this is fair. If Featured Maps are supposed to be the best of the best, and bitesized maps are supposed to be a map that is implemented amazingly well (even if it does have 1 or 2 flaws) then there should be no reason why Featured maps should HAVE to be bitesized. Featured maps are already amazing. Bitesized maps just have to be maps that play well.

Also, while typing this post I came across the idea that possiby reviewer's should have to post a review every 2 months or have to reapply for Reviewership. This way only those who really have something to say should be given the status, otherwise it's wasted on individuals. Why have something if you're not going to use it?
