There is no way it can happen other than hacking. Any scores with stupid times are all hacked. Mare and Raigan do clean them up every now and then, and there is a thread for reporting any hacked runs in kicking around somewhere too. Although you will probably find that all of the hacked runs have already been reported.

You can usually tell from the actual time in the replay, if the replay is complete and generally anything else that isn't normal in the replay.
