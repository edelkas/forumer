Quote: spoon, August 18, 2007 12:14 pm
1-3 are "meh" 6 is awesome though. Also, how do you use gimp? I downloaded it, but I have no idea how to use it

1. First off, you might need to get some good brushes:
GIMP Brushes - DeviantART
2. Download and extract brushes.
3. Go to My computer->C:// (or you main hard drive)->Program File->Gimp->Share->gimp->2.0->brushes
4. Now place the brushes you extracted in that folder.
5. Open up GIMP and then click "New."
6. Set the dimensions to around 450x150.
7. Press brush and open up the menu on the bottom and find any one of the brushes that you just got.
8. Now place the brush and click once where you want it.
9. Now repeat steps 7. and 8. until the sig is halway full.
10. Now pres Shift+F. ---What this does it that it flips the sig horizontally. Now you can use the same brush but it will have a different look.
11. Now after putting burshes in, make a new white layer.
12. To simply give it color, go to the GIMP interface window. There should be 2 boxes one black and one white double click each and set a color for them. They can be different, but try to match colors (which I suck at ).
13. Then select the gradient tool
14. Leave opacity at 100, and in the list, the selection should be FG to BG (RBG).
15. You should experiment with the gradients tool now. Click on the sig and drag a line in any direction and see what the result it. If you don't like it, Ctrl+Z to undo and do a different one. Find one you like.
16. Press Ctrl+L. There should be 2 Layers. Click the New Layer and there should be a list in this small window. Open the list and find overlay. Select overlay.
17. Now you should see your sig in color now.
18. In the window with the sig, go to the userbar on top and click Image then Flatten Image.
19. To add text, click the "T" icon on the GIMP interface window. On the bottom, select your font and click somewhere in th sig and type it in. You can always change the color with the button near where you selected size and font.
20. Flatten Image again and save it. And you're done! Mess around with this as it is just a guide to see where to go and where the buttons are.

~Any questions just ask me. But don't ask me ANYTHING about font. You're better off asking someone else about that.

Also, heres another way to colorize and make your image a lil more sharper:

1) Create new image
2) Do your brush thing
3) then duplicate the layer
4) filters > Blur > garssian blur
5) put both boxes to 13 then press OK
6) go the the layers dialoug then press overlay
7) Merge Down
8) then go to tools > colorize
9) Move the bars back and forth to you get it to what you like

Try not to bring the light bar lower than 0. Bring the hue bar up to make the color brighter. The top bar is just for picking your color.

If you want to colorize a new layer, create a new one and make sure its WHITE. Then go to the layers dialoug and press multiply. It will show the last layer and you are still able to colorize it. If you dont make the background white, it wont colorize well.
