Ah XIII love that game.

It's one of my favorite games on the gamecube. I love the atmosphere, the style & the story. Especially the story, even if they didn't make it themselves. I often play it with my friends. That's another thing I like with the game; the multiplayer action. Overall, I'd give the game a 9/10.

Maybe maybe in about ten years and the games gets a retro-status maybe then Unisoft will make a sequel. One can only hope.
