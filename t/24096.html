Reversion

Hosted By: Riobe & TKS

There have been a lot of maps on NUMA as of late. There have been NReality maps, and regular maps. I've seen many of those, all over the place. What I haven't seen is maps that can be played in both. So this is TKS's as long as my plan. You guys will make maps that can both be played in NReality and Nv1.4.
Spoiler: Rules & Standards
  1. First off, all maps have to be possible to beat in both N v1.4 and NReality v6c2. No exceptions.
  2. Maps have to be different. There has to be a unique distinction between the normal map and the NReality map.
  3. Maps have to be possible to beat, as in playable and beatable. No survivals, N-Arts, and DDA's (Obviously) allowed, and will not be scored if submitted.
  4. Maps will have two different themes. One theme will be a tileset theme. The tiles will have to represent something. The other theme will be the NReality Theme. This theme will represent a certain type of NReality map.
  5. This contest will be separated into rounds. In each round, a certain amount of peoplle will be eliminated based on how many people are in that current round.
  6. Maps may be submitted to NUMA or posted in this thread. If submitted on NUMA, post a link to it in this thread.
  7. I am open to suggestions for tileset themes.
  8. Any profanity, racism, sexism, etcetera will result in a ban from the contest, and possibly more drastic measures from a moderator.
  9. All maps must have the tag reversion.
  10. All maps must have a completion demo attached to the level, whether posted to NUMA or in a text file.
  11. I will not accept submissions via PM at the moment.
  12. As there may be some competition, remember the main idea of this is to have fun.
Spoiler: JudgingJudging will be as follows:
  • Fun-How fun is the map. ?/20
  • Aesthetics-How good does it look. ?/20
  • NReality Theme-How well does it follow this theme. ?/15
  • Tileset Theme-How well does it follow this theme. ?/15
  • Re-playability-Is it addicting, or after one try, does it make you not want to play it again. ?/15
  • Difference-How much of a difference is there in the two versions. ?/15
I'm definitely a judge. TKS might be a judge. I might need another judge anyways.
Spoiler: Prizes As of the moment, undecided. I'm open for suggestions on this too.
Spoiler: Contestants
Spoiler: MapsNone yet.

That's pretty much all of it. I'll need to fill the spots in for contestants, so please join ASAP.
