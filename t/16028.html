#299779 6 2007-02-22T03:50:08Z Kablizzy
Everyone gets there at some point or another, and, honestly, the only way I ever figure out if my block is over is when I sit down and start mapping. I don't start with an idea, I just go for it. In fact Vehemence operated on the very principle that not a lot of thought needs to go into maps for them to be great. Yes, sometimes a quick map is just a poorly-made map, but most of my maps for Legacy and Expanse and now Zenith were made in this fashion.

Not too keen on AMLT's simplicity, but if you spend enough time reticulating your splines, you'll have a good finished product.
