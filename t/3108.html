#65327 30 2005-05-13T02:36:46Z Daddaluma
Ok, here is a copy of the rules I just wrote up. If you've got major complaints, or there are typos, let me know.

Numa Time Trial Society (NTTS) Rules

For a complete overview of all things NTTS go to Kablizzys NTTS site. I will list any and all changes from those rules here.

This is basically a replay contest. We will have a new map to compete on every 2 weeks. Players will have a week and a half to come up with the best replays they can. We will be using the user levels option on the menu, so make sure you know how to properly play a level and save a replay. Because this function only records completed replays, we will only be accepting fully complete replays. No more replays where you die in cool ways or anything like that.

Each person will be allowed to submit 2 replays. If you choose to submit your two replays early in the week, you may still submit replays later on to take the place of the earlier ones.

If you do decide to submit a new replay in place of an old one, please make a new post with the new replay and delete the old replay from your old post. The order of submitted replays will make a difference in the judging. There is likely going to be a great deal of strategy involved in submitting your replays. So take a moment to think about whether its a good idea to submit your killer replay right away or to hold off until later in the week.

So, once the week and a half is up, and we have all the replays in, the three judges will begin to watch them and rank them. Previously we had the judges rank the best three replays in 4 separate categories. This season were going to try something different. There will still be a Time category with 3 places receiving 5, 3 and 1 points. This will not require judging. One person can win only one slot even if both their demos are faster than anyone elses.

Then the judges will rank the top 5 best replays. The judges may use whatever criteria they like to rank them. 1st place could be the fastest and most stylish replay whereas 2nd place could be a replay where someone pulled off a really cool stunt that no one else did. Its entirely up to the judges discretion. The ranks will be averaged and the winner will be determined from that. There will be 5 places receiving 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 points.

A single person can win more than one spot if both of their demos are different enough and still spectacular. It will be up to the judges to decide.

Your judges will be:

trib4lmaniac
Nevermore
Daddaluma

If for some reason a judge cannot fulfill his duties then our alternate, jeffkillian will take over for the week.
