There might be memorials for them, but I really want to honor them at metanet; the
place that I, and many others frequently visit to relieve stress, to just have fun,
or otherwise.


7 years ago, September 11th, 2001, a series of terrorist attacks happened on the US.
Why? Not too many know. All we do know is that the twin towers and the pentagon were hit,
and that there were some very bad people behind the plot.

I'm pretty sure that most of you know about the attacks, and I really put up this topic
to honor the victims, the survivors, and the heroes of the attacks. The people who fought
the terrorists, the people who died on the planes and in the buildings, and those who lived
to tell the story.

I know that many users aren't in the US, but I please ask that you help pay tribute to those people.
Who knows? Someone who you know could have been connected to one of the victims.

I feel that the best way to honor them on metanet would not be only with words, but with other things.
The people at metanet are talented in music, arts, writing, but also map-making.

For this memorial, please PM me whatever you want in it. I'll include everything from the worst things
to the best, as long as they're sincere. I'll probably put the maps and writing tributes
in a .txt file, and the rest in a zip. On the actual day.

I'm not sure that many people will participate in this, but whether it's one piece, or several,
please try to put something into this. I'm putting this up here this early, because I want some time
for the pieces to come in, as not to rush people. I'll stop accepting about 5 days before the date.

Cheers.
