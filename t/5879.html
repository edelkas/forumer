THE FUN CONTEST

It's incredibly simple. All you folks have to do is create a map which is FUN! It can be any style of map, other than DDA, as long as it's FUN. (It doesn't actually have to be fun, but you'll get a better score that way...)

We're only going to judge you on three categories.
Funness of Tileset: This basically means how much fun your tileset allows us to have. One with a Playpark-style tileset will probably score alot of points in this section.
Funness of Objects: This means how much fun the objects allow us to have. Say, for instance, you have mines underneath each bounceblock. That's not very fun, and will get you rated down.
Funness as a Whole: This means the overall feel and funness of the map. Obviously.

All categories are out of 30. This gives a total out of 90 from each judge, and out of 270 overall.

The judges are:
blazeraid
KinGAleX
Templex
maestro (Backup)

If any judge enters a map, maestro will take that judge's place for the map.

All maps must be posted to Numa and linked directly to in this thread. I will then make the necessary adjustments to this post.

There is a limit of four maps per entrant, and you can bring a map up from any time. However, for each old map you enter, you must also enter a new map; but they don't have to be in any order, as long as they're both there.

You may not edit or replace any of your entries once they have been judged, or even partially judged.

All entries must be in by 11:59:59 pm (GMT+0) on the 17th of December.

Here are some examples for the contest:
Really pretty quite sorta fast by Formica
Seriously, just play that map. Flying around a circuit really quickly? Diagnosis = Fun

Shrapnel by tktktk
This one maintains a good flow, and it's hard to believe how awesomely it turns out. Diagnosis = Fun

Escalator To Heck by Niccus
However nice a map design this is, it's just not that fun. It's so frustrating! Diagnosis = Unfun

I'm not saying that you have to have easy racey levels to win this; those are just a couple which quickly come to mind.
