Some of my ideas on this subject:

a) I do go through most of the maps on NUMA (if people spam the front page, I use those links at the bottom, that let you look at less recent maps ).

However, to stop mods having to review all the maps, there could be a button on every map page which alerts a mod to the map. After this is pressed by one or more persons the mods get an alert to look at the map and if 75% of the mods percieve it as spam, it goes. Abuse of this button, if it becomes an issue, should result in warning and/or blockage.

c) Ratings should DEFINATELY need to be backed up with a comment/opinion. If this is garbage or totally unreasonable (i.e. OOh, ROCKETS! 5/5 or UR GHEIGH WITH CHILDRAN!!!! 0/5), a mod should be able to remove said comment/rating (again there should be a majority vote for this, to prevent favouritism). This, however, opens up issues. Who is it who rates 0/5 for most maps with blind trapdoor traps? This kind of thing is borderline - I can see the logic behind the vote, but by the same token, a map isn't worthless just because it contains unneccecary trial and error. Maybe votes are too complicated an issue to moderate?

d) Maybe all this is too much hastle for Arachnid? It does sound kinda complicated

e) I agree with the other nominations for mods in this thread. I'd also nominate TDL/formica, at the risk of seeming favoriteist. I just think these are good authors/NUMA users and are on a lot of the time.

f) The more mods the better, within reason. Maybe 6 or so?

Just some initial thoughts for debate...
