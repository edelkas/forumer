NTTS Contest Week 8!

Welcome to week eight of the Numa Time Trial Society! Now, with color! Our main purpose is to encourage Forum Members to play more User-made maps through Numa.
If you're new to the NTTS, comprehensive rules can be found here.

Okay, first order of business this week... this week is actually TWO weeks. We've decided that players and judges alike don't have enough time to complete their participation, so here's what we're gonna do:

First off, we're giving you guys 10 days to submit demos, instead of 5. This means that submissions begin right now, and will continue through Next Wednesday, December 1st.

Secondly, Judges have double the time now to judge the demos for each week. Juding will now begin on Thursday Mornings, and will continue through Sunday evenings. This means that Thursday, December 2nd is the first day for judging this week.

So, we're down to three weeks before we declare a winner! You guys better ramp up the demos before you're out of range!

We are (still) currently accepting submissions for becoming an NTTS judge! PM me or any other member of the NTTS team for more info.

Here are your NTTS judges for Week 8:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
LittleViking001, And
Myself

And as always, here are the rules for Week 8:
  • This week's map is Meteor Shower, by Lucidium.
  • Any Forum member can post their demo data for this week's map in this thread. The above judges will review each member's demo data and at the end of the two-week time period, we will declare a winner.
  • Each contestant is allowed to post Up to two demos for the weeks. Competitors are allowed to post any time from the beginning of this thread until 23:59 PST (GMT -8) next Wednesday, December 1st, but it may be to your advantage to post later in the week.
  • Submissions begin now, and will end at 23:59 PST (GMT -8) on Wednesday, December 1st. If you don't know when PST is, PM me, and I'll let you know.
  • Judging will begin Thursday morning on December 2nd, and the points will be posted Sunday Evening, just prior to next week's contest.
Good luck, and happy playing!
