Ok, this is very fun and cool. I will start saying a sentence, like: I saw a mushroom. And then for example, Duke says a sentence: But it was poisoned. And so on! It can be a very long bizzare story. Of course you can say more sentences in a post. Remember to put a "..." at the end, if you want some OTHER to complete YOUR sentence, or simply put a "." at the end if someone else should complete YOUR sentence. Remember to make your text in the bold style so everyone can see that it is you that have written it. A good example can be found here.
Have fun! (And I really hope that nobody has did this before)

ShadowEyes: Blah, blah

Maniac: Blah, Blah
