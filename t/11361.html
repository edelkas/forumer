and for invader...photoshop is all about messing around...and sometimes what you create is good enough to remember how to do...its also about combining effects...jsut cuz a couple effects dont look great on their own doesnt mean they cant have a place in a sequence of effects that work towards what you want...the matrix thing combines 5 filters, some duplicated layers and some blending options...most of which i would never use...

for example, on my current siggy, the background was made by doing the following...

filter>render>difference clouds (x10 or so)
image>adjustments>levels (take the center triangle and move it almost all the way to the right)

repeat...lol...alot of times...seroiusly...and if the levels thing is gonna make it all one color, exit out of it and invert the colors, theny ou can go back to it and it should work just fine...


and heres a lightning tutorial in a few steps...(like my avatar)...difference clouds, a couple of times no more then 3 or it will look strange, invert, levels(do same thing as the above)...thats your lightning, so if you dont like it, start over...once you get lightning design you like, go to image>adjustments>hue/saturation, set it to colorize, and play witht he bars until you like it...
