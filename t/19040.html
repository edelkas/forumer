Challenges

There should be a button on the main menu that says challenges. When you press it bring you to this list of categories of certain challenges you can choose from. Some come be survival, complete a map in a certain ammount of time, complete this map without jumping. Could be anything.

Posting demos

After every time you beat a level or die, it will ask in that box to press "s" for save. Then you can go to the main menu and view your saved replays. You can show funny or stupid deaths or maybe something so unbelievabley lucky happened to you, you have to show some one. You can also press the pause button and then "s" save, to show people what happened to you until that point of saving. You can also press "p" again while dead/won or again after you pause, to post right away.

Posting Challenges

When you beat a level or die, you can press the "c" button for challenge. You can post it on the net for people to try to beat. Like make it through the level with more gold, or no jumping, and see who can do it faster.

This isnt necessarily my idea, i just thought it would be cool on the N games.
