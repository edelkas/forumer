#23516 11 2004-06-05T05:39:44Z Tsukatu
In the main menu of N, where it says stuff like "Play Game" and "Credits" and such, press the ~ (tilde) key, it's right above Tab. This will take you to the debug menu. Highlight the top text box (it should be filled with 0s and 1s and such) and delete everything there. Fill it with the Level Code provided. Then click back anywhere else on the game screen and press L. To play the level, press P, and make sure Caps Lock is ON. If you die and want to restart, press the ~ key again and press R, wait for the level to finish loading, then press P again, making sure Caps Lock is still ON.

To watch a demo, replace the stuff in the lower text box with the demo code. Make sure the level is loaded first. Press 3 to load the demo, then press Q to cue demo playback, and press P to watch the demo, making sure Caps Lock is ON. You can stop the demo at any time by pressing ~. To stop the demo playback, press W. Then if you want to play yourself, press R to refresh the level, and press P to play it, with Caps Lock still ON.

I hope that's clear.
