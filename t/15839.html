#296579 2 2007-02-12T10:21:55Z apg
There may be several reasons causing this. Here's a list, from the most likely to most unlikely reason:
  • Userlevels.txt is missing a "&userdata=" tag. Add &userdata= before the maps.
  • You have renamed your userlevels.txt.
  • Userlevels.txt is in a different folder than the application.
  • Maps are formatted incorrectly, or you are using raw map data. The maps should be formatted like this:
    $Level name#Author#Category#Map data#
  • You don't have any maps in userlevels.txt.
  • You haven't unzipped N yet.
  • Either the application or userlevels.txt is corrupted.

but if i do any of those things I'll lose my progress, right? meanwhile, how do i play maps from the user forums?

Hey! Welcome, thereisnospoon! Just to let you know, doing so will not delete your N progress. Just try it; nothing bad will happen!

If you want to play maps from NUMA - the N User Map Archive, just click on a map that you like by clicking on the Newest Page or the Top Rated Page. There, you can find userlevels created by members here! Just look to the left side of the screen on NUMA, and you'll find all sorts of options.

To play these userlevels, you have to choose a special map such as Dragon_Moon's Afternoon Dragon Step 04. If you want to play a map, you look on that page, and you should see a small square with a thumbnail of the map. (Note: Do NOT try to play any N-Arts unless the description says so. It will lag the program, and cause N to close.) Below the thumbnail, you should see two text boxes that have numbers and some letters in them. Copy the upper one's data, and go into N. From the main page, press ~. That will bring you to Ned, the N level editor! Now, you should see two text boxes again. Highlight all of the data in the upper box, delete it, and paste the data that you copied into the box. Now, press L, and it will load the map. Press P to play the map, and you're ready!
