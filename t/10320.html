Quote: Trogdor007, June 06, 2006 11:16 am
I'm sorry... What is malware?

Malware is an abbreviation for "malicious software", and includes adware (generates pop-ups constantly, changes your homepage, etc), spyware (reports your browsing habits, personal information to someone via the net - which slows down you browsing because it eats bandwidth), viruses, and various others. Adware and spyware are more common than viruses, but usually also less harmful to your computer (although perhaps not your privacy). Unfortunately, there's a distinct lack of security software for Macs, so if you do get infected annoyingly difficult to fix. However, before you try anything drastic, I'd get more info on this Viewpoint software.

Oh, yeah, and Limewire is not noted as being the most secure of software. A lot of malware is propagated via it and other similar programs.
