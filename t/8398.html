#156348 2 2006-03-21T03:30:11Z Twistkill
This belongs in N Help...

You've made the level impossible. You have to collect enough gold through every level so you have enough time to beat the next one. You can't just ignore gold if it's out of your way, each piece of gold gives you 2 extra seconds, which can really help. You start with only 1 second because you eithier didn't collect enough gold from the previous level, or you took too long to complete the previous level. Eithier way, it's now impossible and you must start the episode over. Sorry for the bad news, but it's the only way.
