#318230 2 2007-05-04T21:36:11Z LittleViking
Quote: fingersonthefrets, May 04, 2007 05:05 am
(ps. can someone tell me how advanced you can make things look with elements?)

Elements is really a lot like Photoshop, except for certain features. I'm not sure which features exactly are different, but it's usually the less-used ones (like CS2's new perspective tools). To make sigs, a program only needs the most basic advanced-image-editing tools. Layers, brightness/contrast, hue/saturation, layers and blending options, dodge and burn, transparency, effects like render clouds and generate noise (although you could also find pictures of noise and clouds on Google, so that's not even necessary).

The short answer is yes, you can make good sigs with Elements. Lots of people here use The GIMP, which probably has even less features than Elements does. Heck, Brickman made an entire N comic series with nothing but MS Paint. Making sigs depends more on your knowledge of making sigs than on the tools you have. Search for some Photoshop tutorials on sigs, and you should be good to go.
