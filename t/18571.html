#347658 3 2007-07-26T16:26:29Z Ethrel
Go with the second one.

In my personal experience, long distance relationships rarely work, doubly so if you have never met the person/rarely seen te person. After a few years of seeing them constantly you can handle being apart for long periods, but not at the beginning.

Now, the second one you can see a lot more often. Even if there are no feelings or she doesn't want to be with you, at least give it a shot. Things have a way of working out for the best, but only if you actually put the initiative to start things. If you two get to the point me and my current girlfriend did, then so be it (starting off dating because there was something there, but not really anything more than friendship and opportunity, and we fell in love). If not, then you two will break up. If you two were meant to just be friends, you will remain friends (whether you date first or not). If not, then you won't speak to each other again after a short while. Just go do it and trust that whether through some divine guidance or random chance, things will turn out the way they are supposed to.

EDIT: Oh, and if in doubt about her feelings towards you, just ask. It's that simple. If she is anything like a 'typical' female, she will love the fact that you are at least open and willing to 'talk about feelings'.
