EXPLANATION ON OONTZ (If you aren't musically ebonic):
"Oontz" (Also known as "Oonts" and "Oom-sss") is what I call Heavy Bass. The bass is the Oon, and the hi-hat is the Tz.

Say this with a basic 1-2-3-4 beat: Boo - Che - Boo - Che (Silent 'E' in Che)
Then double beat it: BooCheBooCheBooCheBooChe

Sounds techno-ish, right?
