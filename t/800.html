I was sitting in bed last night, thinking of all kinds of things that would be cool features to add to N. (I was also thinking of a console version of N, which is why most of these probably won't happen. )

1. Multiplayer. Four ninjas. One exit. And gold. Each ninja has a different color (i.e. cook, blue, red, green Ninjas) The multiplayer should probably be limited to 4, and the score would be determined by gold score and finish time.

2. Human guards. They have a cilantro more brains than your average drone. They are limited to the ground, but they have a better searching ability, such as the ability to search a whole room by looking around. If thye see something, they will go over here, survey the area for a while, and then go back to his post. If N is spotted, then the soldier opens fire with whatever weapon he wields. The soldier can be killed by a drone's fire, however (i.e. a gatling gun misses, hits the guards, guard dies) and the guards could have ragdoll physics. The guards could also get killed by the next feature.

3. Ninja sword. It would add an all new element to the game; Combat. A simple button stroke can slash up guards, but drones would still be unkillable. There can also be enemies with swords, and you can swordfight them by rapidly pressing the strike button, the faster one winning. This feature could be implemented as a pickup weapon, and it could possibly be implemented in multiplayer (For taking out opponents).

4. A soid storyline. I came up with this: N was a product of a series of experiments to create perfect ninjas from a extremely deadly ninja named One. The problem with N's batch is it's dependence on an element to sustain it's metabolism, in N's case, gold. As N watched his clone siblings die off from the unnourished weakness, he decided not to die so easily. He broke out of his prison and replenished himself on the base's god stash. He knew what he must do; Run away from the evil scientists who created him and keep himself alive by breaking into various high-security gold vaults and taking the stash. You could even have one of the enemies be One himself, and have N fight and run from various other ninjas throughout the game. Like I said, none of this will probably happen.

Oh well, there's my 30 cents or so.
