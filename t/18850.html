I was just messing around with NaN'd launch pads a few minutes ago, and found that if you NaN the number before the last one (ie: '2^360,480,NaN,-1'), and kill your ninja by pressing Enter, if you respawn the ninja and make him hit the NaN'd launch pad, he will be frozen in place where he was just before he was respawned.

Eg: Try this level. Kill the ninja, respawn, then hit the launch pad.

http://www.pastebin.ca/raw/653518

Sorry if this is already known. Just thought I'd show it.

Oo. And another thing I've just noticed, if you NaN both numbers, (ie: '2^360,480,NaN,NaN'), the ninja just freezes.
