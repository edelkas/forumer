#169441 11 2006-04-29T05:56:28Z tktktk
All right, guys. It's taken me a while, but I've managed to scrounge up the basic framework for this thing. If there's anything major that I've missed, that needs to be expanded upon, or that you don't agree with, please let me know. Feedback on the part that's being posted on May 1st would be particularly helpful; it's looking a little on the short side at the moment.

The following will be posted (hopefully) on May 1st:
Quote
Welcome, friends, to the Hall of Fame! The HoF is a Legacy production, an annual awards show meant to be a supplement to the Dronies, so that one show is concluded every six months. It's not just a display of the best maps of the year, but a showcase of the greatest maps of all time. It will include all playable maps, including Metanet levels and others that haven't been submitted to Numa. Regardless of whether they're action, puzzle, survival, or race maps, those honored in the Hall of Fame will be only the very best, the ones that are truly deserving of legendary status.

Here are the rules for nominations:
1) Any playable map from any place or date will be eligible for nomination, even the in-game levels. That means they don't necessarily need to have been submitted to Numa, but maps such as DDAs cannot be nominated.
2) You may nominate as many maps as you wish, within reason.
3) You should nominate maps that you consider to be the best and that you believe have stood (or will stand) the test of time and become true classics.
4) You must PM your nominations to me or one of the other Legacy members.
5) Nominations must be submitted no later than midnight on May 31st.
6) The Legacy team reserves the right to decline your nominations, and in this unlikely event we will inform you of why they were declined.

All right, kids, off we go. Get nominating!

The following will be posted on June 1st:
Quote
Well, friends, the nominations have been submitted, and now it's time to vote. Here are the nominees:

[Insert nominees here]

Here are the rules for voting:
1) You may not vote for your own maps.
2) You may vote for up to five of the nominated maps, ranking them from 1st choice to 5th. If you want to vote for less you may, but we strongly encourage you to vote for five.
3) The Legacy team reserves the right to decline your votes, and in this highly unlikely event we will inform you of why they were declined. We will only do it with very good reason. We may also tell people about it in this thread to get a second opinion.
4) You must PM your votes to me or one of the other Legacy members.
5) Your votes must be submitted no later than midnight on June 31st. The final results will be posted shortly afterwards.
6) Preferably, don't tell other people who you voted for.

Scoring System:
Each person can nominate up to five maps, ranking them from 1st to 5th. Each rank will receive a point value as follows:

1st choice: 5 points
2nd choice: 4 points
3rd choice: 3 points
4th choice: 2 points
5th choice: 1 point

Now as this is an awards show with multiple winners, the voting results will be accessible to the public in as unbiased a manner as possible. That means we'll show the number of votes each maps got and all that, but without glorifying the maps that got the most votes over the others.

A small number of truly legendary base maps will be selected by the Legacy team, and will be put into the Hall of Fame regardless of the number of votes they receive. The base maps will be kept a secret until the public-chosen additions are revealed. The nominations list that the public will be voting on come June 1st will include nominations from other people along with the base maps.

Here are the maps I'm nominating as base maps. I tried to base my picks not on the ones I personally like the most, but rather the ones I think all knowledgeable players would agree are "classics":
