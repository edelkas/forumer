Ok, so, I've been playing a game called Threadspace: Hyperbol. Basically, you are a ship in a battlefield, (look in the picture) and you have to destroy all of your enemies. Sounds like your average game, right? Here's where it gets interesting, Your ship can't move all over the place. Meaning, you have to move a few feet then wait for your engines to recharge. This means that you have to use strategic thinking to make your kills. You can "spin" you weapons to attack people, or, shoot your bullets around corners. You have to fire off your command center, deploy it, then produce weapons for yourself. Not every weapon is offensive, there are also defensive weapons. Two, in fact, that I use all the time. The first is called the Singularity, it's basically a black hole. Every bullet that it sucks in causes it to stay open longer (If you upgrade it, it grows for every shot it swallows). Next is the Repulsor. It does the exact opposite of the Singularity, or, it blasts the projectiles away from it. The Repulsor's upgrade allows it to repel the projectiles at a greater velocity which will A. make the target easier to hit, and B. do more damage. At the moment (from now till the 21st) the game is only $10. Normally it's $20. Anyway, if you were to download the Demo, you'd have to have Steam. Anyway, the demo let's you play all the levels in Objective mode off line forever, and play online for 4 hours. In the full game, you can create your own levels.
