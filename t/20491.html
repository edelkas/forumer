I realize I have to get to Europe, which will give me a few extra weeks to figure out how to survive this. At this point, suddenly I have full control of my actions in the dream. It's almost like a puzzle challenge: get to Europe without being converted to purple goo. I've had this dream 4 times over the past six years, and I've never beaten it.

--1st time I had the dream, I tried to stay in my bathtub surrounded by water. When the goo made it to my area, the walls of the bathtub disintegrated, and everything went purple. (duh.)

--The next time I had the dream, I caught a flight on a small local airline that was flying to a Canadian airport, where I could connect to a flight to Europe. It was one of those 15 to 20 person planes, and was luckily leaving right as I managed to make it to the local airport. About halfway through, one passenger realized he was on the wrong flight, and begged the captain to do something. We happened to be flying over his destination, and the pilot takes us down. (this is a dream, I should have expected something stupid like this.) I sit there in some tiny town in a deep valley, watching as an ocean of purple goo billows over the horizon.

--Almost two years later, I had the dream again. I managed to catch the same flight, and the same guy wanted to get a special stop. I begged and begged the pilot to not take us down; I explained the goo situation. She didn't believe me, so I ended up tying her up and hijacking the plane. Of course, I don't know how to fly, but I figured out how to keep the plane steady. I ended up going way off course, and flying over the ocean of purple goo. I ran out of gas, and crashed into the stuff.

--The last time I had the dream, I instead got in my car and headed straight for the nearest port where a cruise ship was taking off. Unfortunately, I was woken up before I got there.

I've found myself actually thinking of this while awake, and trying to come up with strategies to beat it. I've explained it to friends and relatives, asking for their best advice. It's quite amusing.
