#71379 2 2005-06-01T01:59:18Z brickman
Yes, teleporters an be a bit annoying if you don't know the secret to keeping him alive. But its your lucky day: I'm gonna tell you the secret *FOR FREE*!

Once you place the jumppad, enter frame-by-frame mode and hit the teleporter. On the second frame of n shooting away from the teleporter, put your mouse cursor where n is because thats the exact spot where you need the destination pad. It only works if you come out on exactly the second frame, no more no less. Not quite sure where line is drawn by the game between powerful jumppads that will just be stronger and telepads that will only work on the second frame, but thats how it works.
#71384 3 2005-06-01T02:28:50Z great_sea
Also, the number for the launch pad power will need to be very precise. Many times I find myself going into the decimals to get it just right. Like brickman said, go into fbf and find out where he is going, then keep narrowing your number range until you can get him to hit the (exiting) launchpad just right. And if it is too hard to get him to hit one launchpad precisely, then put two or three next to eachother to give some room for error.
