#410708 3 2008-02-19T21:53:35Z George
You will be able to view your personal best on 69-2 by going to the High Scores menu, and clicking level 2 at the top left in episode 69.

When you're making your way back up, try jump as high as you can from the far right. It should carry you past most of the middle section. Your other option is to try lure the drones into a more favourable position for you.
