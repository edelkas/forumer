This level was put here because it was easily cheatable on NUMA by looking at the full image. This is basically a map that uses a circle protected by a launchpad repelling feild. On the bottom are two access panels that open either the top or the bottom of the circle. Inside of it is a gauss turret protecting lots of bounce blocks each hiding an access panel to the exit. Most of them are fakes. But only four of these panels have a trapdoor that will seal off part of the turret to help you. One of these will unlock your exit with the flaw of a full force attack if you ever manage to get out of the circle which has sealed itself.

Personally, I think it's easier to go into the top with the least risk of being shot.

Edit: I have a demo as proof it can be beat.
