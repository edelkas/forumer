#460704 2 2008-08-15T05:43:02Z LittleViking
The "fourth button" is the first frame you press jump in. When you press and hold jump, it records the third button (jump) and fourth for one frame, and then the third for every frame you hold it after. It's recorded so the game knows when to walljump, for example.
