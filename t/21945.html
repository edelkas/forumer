Unrated Maps

I, <insert username here>, pledge to rate as many unrated maps as I can, no matter how boring or badly designed they may be.

Right now, most of the maps that aren't bitesized or created by famous authors never get rated. Because of this, hundreds of wonderful maps are overlooked. Not only are people missing out on great maps, it discourages newbie map makers when they spend hours on a map and it doesn't get rated. So please, to make NUMA a better place, do your part by rating unrated maps.

Quote
Do we have to play them?
Some have clusters of mines that take up half of the map, etc...
Yes, no matter how horrible a map looks, you can't be sure until you play it.

Some people are talking about hard it is to keep the promise. I never intended this to be "YOU MUST RATE EVERY MAP" sort of thing, I just want people to make an effort to rate more maps than they might otherwise. To be honest, I only rate some of the unrated maps I see, but I do make an effort to.

Oh, by the way, I came up with another idea to encourage voting! Whenever someone rates one of your maps, rate one of theirs. I know this may be impossible for people with popular maps, but its just a cool idea you might want to try.
