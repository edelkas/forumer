#266563 5 2006-12-08T17:25:24Z stepself
They're unrelated, historically. pi is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, and e is defined as the limit (as x goes to infinity) of [1+(1/x)]^x. It has various other definitions too (it can be expressed as a sum, as the base of an exponential it's the only function that's its own derivative...). So yeah. Totally unrelated... well, except through Euler's formula: e^(i*pi) + 1 = 0. But that relation was discovered well after both pi and e were defined. Kinda neat that it works out so nicely, eh?
