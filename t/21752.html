The Basics
Quote: Wikipedia; with some edits
Like Mario Kart DS, Mario Kart Wii is based around single driver gameplay, unlike the approach by Mario Kart: Double Dash!! on the Nintendo GameCube, which featured two players on one kart.

A total of four different control choices have been confirmed. Players can choose to play the game using the Wii Wheel peripheral that comes packaged with the game, with which you tilt the control as if it were a steering wheel (although this also works without prepriheral), the remote and nunchuk, a Nintendo GameCube controller, or a Wii Classic Controller. Powersliding, another main game element, can also be set to happen automatically, to help newer drivers, but no boost will be gained.

This game has been announced as supporting the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection service. It will support an online racing mode where players will be able to go against each other in a single race racing simultaneously and an online battle mode. It has been confirmed that it will also feature 12 player online races, and 8 player online battles. It has also been confirmed that there will be online leagues and rankings along with local rankings. Most of the online ranking will be done from a whole separate channel. You will also be able to download and send ghost data using the Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection.

Nintendo's UK site confirms that the European release of game will feature online pre-determined text chat between players before races. It will also feature an online spectator option which allows you to view your friends races and join in at the start of the next course. There will also special worldwide online events organized by Nintendo.

The game will feature many of the old weapons and items that have been used in past Mario Kart games, and that a number of new items are also being added.

In addition to expanded gameplay, motorbikes were added. In 50cc mode, karts are used; in 100cc mode, bikes are used; and in 150cc mode, players can choose between the two. In addition, you can now perform tricks in the air by shaking motion sensitive controls, or via a button on the Classic and GCN controllers. The game will also have 16 brand new tracks, and 16 remakes of older tracks, which was also incorporated into Mario Kart DS. 10 battle stages are also being added for a revamped battle mode. Miis (which can wear 3 different costumes) will also be usable during the game, along with 24 other playable characters.
