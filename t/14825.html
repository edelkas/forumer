I dicided to make a review of this game after I beat it.

Secret of Mana II



Storyline: 9/10

This game probably has one of the best storylines of any game I have ever played, however it can be nonsensical at times. The Tree of Mana is withering for no apparent reason!!! Four fairies go out to find help, 3 of them wimp out, one finds you and makes you the chosen one, you must go take out the sword of mana from the tree of mana to save it. How that would help, I got no idea. 3 different storylines depending on the main character you select sure do create some variety, but i'll talk about that in Content. You succeed and fail at the same time at the end though. You may kill that final boss and save the world, but the tree's screwed anywho. You always seem to get there, like, 15 seconds too late to save Mana. Quoting the ending sequence, "Remember...make sure your children remember...because in 1000 years, Mana will return to your world." Part 3? You betcha. (On a side note, EVERY Secret of Mana game has someone who's, like, 10 years old. Aside from Carlie, she looks 6, but that's just because she's half elf. She's 15.)

Graphics: 6/10

The graphics were a bit dissapointing. They're between old-style pixels and good graphics, and that is the worst in my book. You have to admit though, some of the spell animations and sprites are still pretty good.

Gameplay: 10/10

This is exactly the kind of gameplay I seek in an RPG. Live action fighting, especially as a party, makes fighting all the more fun, and tends to be easier to deal with then going into a fight menu and taking turns. The easy-to-deal-with menu makes playing a lot easier, besides the equip menu. The equip menu isn't that great, but I ain't using decimals in my ratings. Overall, it's very smooth gameplay, and it shouldn't give you any trouble.

Content: 10/10

This game has a hell of a lot of stuff in it. 3 different storylines with seperate final bosses/areas, a secret boss, tons of weapons, armors, accessories, spells, lands, ect. The most amazing this is there are 6 main characters, which you choose 1 main and 2 allies. The main character determines your storyline, even though you can choose any of the 3 characters of your party to control. The characters even have class advancements, which they choose either a path of light or darkness. There are two class changes, one at level 18 and the other at 38. Technically, there are 42 classes, 7 to each character. Special item seeds can be used to create the ultimate weapons and armors for the final class advancements, and create seal items to help players advance. Every character has a different storyline, but the endings to their storyline are specific to two characters. For example, Carlie and Kevin have seperate storylines at the beginning, but their goals are the same by the end, and both of them have the same final area/boss which the other 4 do not have. And, to top it off...the secret boss...The Black Rabite. (Rabbits FTW.)

Sound: 9/10

The soundtrack of this game is amazing. I tried finding a copy online, but I haven't been able to find a free copy, unlike most games. Why? People respect it too much. It's too popular. If you do manage to get a copy, you'll listen to it for quite a while. Probably even while playing other games. It ain't perfect, but it is positively stunning, in any case.

Overall: 9/10

Final Comments:
This game is wonderful. Besides the graphics, it is a stunning spectacle of how great classic RPG's can be. I suggest playing this sometime, maybe even going through all three storylines. This game is for the SNES, but it may be nearly impossible to get a hold of an english copy. If you plan on playing it, an emulator works just as well, that's how I played it.
