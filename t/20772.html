I think that the biggest problem N has is the lack of good introductory maps. I have seen several people decide to play something else because the first few maps are either uninteresting or too hard. I personally would have gotten interested in N a few years earlier if I hadn't gotten stuck early on in the game.

What I would like to do here is create a set of easy maps that we can (hopefully) have people play to get them interested in N.
The difficulty should be about that of the first few episodes, or harder for short maps. The maps should not be incredibly long or slow, as that will just bore people (including yourself).
Essentially, make an very easy map based around something you think makes N fun.

Please submit any maps you make for this to NUMA, with the tag "introductory." This will allow anyone to download the complete set by going to http://numa.notdot.net/userlevels?q=introductory&count=100.


I would also like some feedback: is this a good/bad idea? Should I do it differently?
