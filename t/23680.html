This is not an FAQ, just a guide for newbie's to follow so they can get used to NUMA quicker.
Spoiler: GuideWelcome to NUMA!!!

Hello! Welcome to NUMA. This is like a mini info comment to help you get to know the place. Just a few things you should know:
  • Be sure to check out the home page to see all the recent news, if any.
  • The hot maps page is where your submitted maps go temporarily. A respected limit is 2 maps on the hot maps page at once, 5 maps per day. If you go over these limits, you may be suspended, banned, and or some of those maps most likely will be delisted.
  • Positive attitude is influenced on this site. Respect others, others will respect you (At least most the time), and vice versa.
  • Feel free to customize your profile and avatar. Profiles are allowed to be big. You don't even have to use it. Feel free to whenever though.
  • Be sure to check out the forums, N Wikia, and join the IRC, if you want to that is.
  • For more info, feel free to check out the FAQ's It is strongly suggested that you read through these extensively.
  • Spamming is prohibited. Demo spamming is another thing, but refrain from doing it as much as possible.
  • Other noobish behavior will usually result in suspension or ban. Check the homepage or the FAQ for more info.
  • Read the manuals that come with the game instead of asking around. Everything you need to know about the game is in those manuals.
  • To favorite maps, click the silver ninja next to the title of the map. It's a frequently asked question here.
  • All NUMA acronyms explained here.
  • Last but not least, if you would like help making an N level, feel free to ask on the forums, or more suggested, check out this tutorial to help you.
And that is it! Welcome to NUMA, and enjoy your stay.
