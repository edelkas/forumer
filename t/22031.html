#423486 7 2008-03-30T07:24:31Z Atilla
FLail has already been mentioned, but Helix Games also produced Jumper Redux and Jumper 2, which also have editors. Ninjah is another platformer in the vein of N, FLail and Jumper. Pekka Kana 2 is a different style of platformer, somewhat reminiscent of New Zealand Story (aka Kiwi Kraze), which has an editor and level packs available for download.

In other offerings, Zep's Dreamland is a puzzle game which looks like a platformer. Typhoon 2001 is a Tempest 2000 remake (arcade-y shooter) with level editor. Neverball is an action/puzzle game in the vein of Super Monkey Ball. Custom map-packs are available, and ytou can make your own maps if you've experience with GtkRadiant. Battle for Wesnoth is a turn-based tactical game with a map/campaign editor and downloadable content. Battleships Forever is a real-time tactical game with (rather limited) mission editor and ship editor.
