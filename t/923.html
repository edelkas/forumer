Yeah, DDA2 N's revenge was probably the map that inspired me to start making DDAs. It also influenced my style, in terms of close calls and lots of enemies.

LV's level is pretty good, I always admire good timing with thwumps "happening" to reach a position at the right second to set n straight, and its nice to see my thwump riding technique used (where you have wo twumps going backwards, one to push n and one for him to stand on). Its exactly what I said about DDA's always improving off of each other (although that wasn't discovered by luck, it was an idea).

And now, I present to you my seventh level, along with the first six again (I'm done giving each a seperate txt file). This level would have not been possible without the technique of using gold to delay a launchpad. I started with a shaft that worked perfect with 5 turrets and no golds, but then I tried to add the left side, and the thwump was enough to throw it off (the missile came after the thwump, although I had it planned already). So I started messing with oneway down walls, doors, and moving the pads. I even tried removing a turret, but it just wasn't the same.
Then I remembered the gold delay trick, and had it up and running in 5 minutes. Viva les golds!
