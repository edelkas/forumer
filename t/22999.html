You buy N+ with Microsoft Points in the Xbox Live Marketplace.

So first, you need an Xbox 360 and Xbox Live. If you have those, you can probably find a computer or department store that sells Microsoft Point cards. (They look like this.) N+ costs 800 points, so get that many points in cards and go to the points website to enter them in.

After that, just log in to Xbox Live to download N+.
