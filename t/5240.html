Sin City
Plot: Three seperate stories from Sin City, that ever so slightly interweave. General theme of humans being bastards. The movie version of the comic Sin City.
Comments: This movie looks amazing. They've gone to real efforts to make it look simply like the comic has come to life (they shot the entire movie on blue screen, you know) and it looks stunning and spot on. There is not a single movie that looks like this, and certainly not one that does it so well. I do sorta feel that I don't get emotionally envolved quite enough though, mainly because the stories are too short to really become involved... that said, this movie is a work of art and very entertaining. A masterpiece, infact. Just not enough emotionally involvement to make it an all-time great.
Rating: 9/10
