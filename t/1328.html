As I mentioned I might earlier, I've now added RSS support to NUMA. For those of you who aren't familiar with what RSS is, it's basically a way to keep up-to-date on new items.

If you're using Firefox, you'll now get an RSS icon in the bottom-right of your browser when viewing the browse pages (newest, maps by a particular author, categories, etc). Clicking it will allow you to subscribe to the RSS feed for that page. This will add it as a 'live bookmark' - a folder in your bookmarks that will always contain the latest items for the page you subscribed to - it'll automatically update itself when new maps arrive. Other RSS readers, both browser plugins and standalone are available, which will display tickers, pop up messages, create messages in your email, etcetera.

People not using Firefox can still take advantage of RSS - a small RSS icon appears at the bottom of the browse pages in all browsers, and clicking on it will send it to your RSS reader (if you have one).

If anyone'd like to post what RSS reader they use as a reccomendation to others that aren't familiar with the technology, feel delicious.

And yes, logins are still in progress.
