Jhonathan
~~~~~~~~
"So, are you sure you've got everything you need for the trip? I really would be devastated if you weren't to make it back in one piece," said Bernie as Jhonathan gathered up the potions he would need and his bag.
"Don't worry, I've made this one before. I've got speed, strength and healing potions in here, and I doubt I'll even need to use any. I mean, they did recently increase the number of guards between here and Fort Avernum," he replied as he added trail rations to the bag.
"Yes, but they're only guarding the paths. We both know you're not going to be following the same route they do, and there are plenty of brigands out there."
"Forget about them," he said, pulling on his heavy cloak and sticking his shortblade in its hidden slot. "I can handle anything I find. Next time you see me, I'll be bringing you two potions worth of mandrake." And with that, he set outto the south, towards Fort Avernum--or more specifically, towards the patch of extremely herbs located near it, his thoughts drifting. It isn't too common, in truth, to have such a valuable patch so close to such an important town without more people knowing about it, but mandrake was so rare that very few people even knew what it looked like in its unused form--it was so rare, the chances of ever seeing one weren't worth the time to train it when you could be learning to find, say, some energetic herbs. Some people regarded it as a myth entirely. He knew that there were few things some people wouldn't do to know the location of this patch, but thats why you have to cover your tracks out here. By now he was already off the beaten path, partly to reduce the chance of someone following him and partly because he knew that nothing useful ever grows near the roads--many times he had found herb patches purely by luck while on his way to a different patch, entirely because he took a slightly different route each time. He would already be passing two lesser herb patches on the trip there which he would sample, and one other was due to bear some fresh herbs at the same time he would pass it on the way back. Oh, what another alchemist wouldn't give to have this boy collecting his herbs. . . but nothing would have been able to convince him to leave his brother and to abandon the city of Formello with no potions. Who needs a group of guards anyways? They'd just make the job harder. . .
Suddenly, the snapping of a branch nearby jolted him into full awareness. He turned quickly, but saw nothing. Curiously, he went over and examined the spot. He couldn't find whatever had made the noise, but he did notice three distinct sets of footprints--only one of which could have been his. "So I am being followed" he thought to himself. "Looks like I'm going to have to pass on those minor herb patches until the return trip, or at least until I can throw them off. . ."

As he was nearing Fort Avernum, Jhonathan desperately wracked his mind for some way to throw off the people following him. He had already tried stopping in for a meal at the inn in Fort Duvno and exiting the fort from the other side, but they had apparently split up with one person watching each exit and so continued to follow him without actually leaving the underbrush. He decided on a plan. He detoured towards the swamps behind the fort, and once there he stooped down and pretended to be picking some herbs. Jhonathan saw the two walk in and gestured over to them to join him. They did, and he ordered them a light meal. "I suppose the food here takes everybody some getting used to, but its better than starving. Or going your whole life without eating like in the game. Anyways, I'm ready to go now, if you are. A bit of a trip back to Formello, although I know the way well. I'm sure they'll be grateful to you, though, so it'll be more than worth it. Just follow me. I've actually got a minor herb patch or two just off the trail, but I'm not too worried about them--they're pretty common herbs that I can afford to lose if necessary, and I doubt you'd recognize them anyways. I've alread got the important one."
After the three finished their meals, they set off to the north, along the path that would eventually get them to Formello.
