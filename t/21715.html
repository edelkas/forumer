Okay, well I had this idea (and yes, max_ride, an innovative one) about a contest. I thought I would share it with y'all and see what you guys thought. And just to let you guys know up front, I don't plan on starting it now or any time in the near future; This is planned for the summer.

Here's how things will be run each round:
  1. I will release the map to be highscored for the round. Anyone and everyone will have access to play around with it. However, no one will submit demos. I will select the individual(s) on which you may bid on. (hang on, I'll explain.)
  2. Two days later, the bidding will close. I'll announce the start of the round. Anyone who is signed up for the tournament may submit demos for the map at this point.
  3. Five days from then, entries will no longer be accepted. The round will end. I will post the final rankings in the first post and award points as needed. Next, I will post all of the bids in the first post, separated into two groups (Winning Bids and Losing Bids). The points will be collected or awarded as the win or loss dictates. (See below for specifics) I will grant special powers or awards to the winning team or individual.
I have some ideas on how the bidding system could work but I'm not exactly sure it'll work. Getting this sorted out is the only reason I even made this thread in the first place.
  • First of all, when one bids on a player, you select a general bracket of space that you think the player will place at the end of the game. For example, for player X, I might say, {BID 35, [5-9]}, which here means that I am bidding 35 pts that player X gets 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, or 9th place.
  • Obviously, if player X does not rank in the above-mentioned bracket, he loses 35 points. If he does, then I award him 35 pts.
  • Players can only have brackets that include either 3, 5, or 7 consecutive numbers. For brackets that have 3 members, prize money is multiplied by 1.5x. for brackets that have 7 members, Losing bids will result in a 1.5x loss.
  • No player can remove, nullify, or in any way tamper with their bet after they've placed it. All players must PM me their bets. All bets placed on the thread will cost the person -20 pts per bet.
  • I will post a MasterBracket each turn. This bracket will vary in size and shape. All members of the bracket will receive 50 points. All those below the bracket will receive 25 points. Those above the bracket will receive...you guessed it! 75 points! Those that don't submit demos don't get anything. NOTE: You can still BET even if you don't submit a demo =]
Awards will be given given throughout the game, and please expect lotsa lotsa twists. The game will also unpredictably change between groups, individuals, pairs - and even unofficial alliances. The awards will vary from simply being point awards to disadvantages that you can give to other players or teams. If the round calls for a team discussion (for instance, if you had to select two members to kick out and deprive of certain powers), everything should be done within the thread within the time limit.




There won't be any elimination for this competition.


Thanks for reading through this, guys, and if you have any suggestions or objections to this, please say it here. If you have any questions for me, then by all means feel free to post them here. I would really appreciate if you guys could think of things that I haven't answered in this post about how the thing works, and whether or not anything I have posted up here has any vulnerabilities, (aka the bidding system).
