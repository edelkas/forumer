Well, first of all, before you even think about playing a userlevel, follow these small instructions:

1. Make sure your userdata is in the correct format. The format is: $Author#Level name#Level type (Action, Puzzle, Race, etc)#Level data#
Make sure you don't forget that last #!
When you have all of your levels in this format, save it.

2. Make sure your N application and your Userlevels.txt are in the same directory, or in the same area. For example, they could both be in your N folder. Or they could both be on your dektop. Anywhere you choose, but they must be together.

Now, you may think about playing your userlevels! Just go into N, and go to Userlevels. If you followed the above instructions, your userlevels should show up.
