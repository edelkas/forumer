For those of you who have and use Steam, get The Specialists. When I first saw it, I said to myself, "wow, this looks like everything I've ever wanted in a FPS."

Then I actually played it. After joining the first server, I couldn't pick my jaw up off of the floor. You can bet the next four words out of my mouth were "oh em f cking gee."

I suggest you get the game, don't read up on anything or view any demos... but instead head straight into a deathmatch game with lots of players, go spectator, and just watch. I absolutely guarantee you you'll be completely blown away.

The very first thing that happened with me:
I read the manual and saw all the really cool stuff you could do. So I set up my own server where I could practice this awesome stuff. After I got a loose grip on the controls and the nifty matrix-ey moves, I joined a deathmatch server.
The very first thing I saw when I walked through the doors of dm_corporate was some black dude in a trench coat and sunglasses doing a sideways no-hands cartwheel past the doorway... time slowed down (bullet-time), and he opened fire on me with two uzis. I got shot a whole helluva bunch, then ran after him with my shotgun. Some other guy decides to party, too, but uzi-man does a flip over me (jumping completely out of the way of my shotgun blast) and kicks the other dude in the face. I whip around and try to shoot him again, but he jumps away again to the side, and I miss him by miles. As for my third (and final) shot, he was running toward a wall, and I led, but he did a backflip thingy off the wall and shot me again.
So I died, but it was so friggin' awesome. I had to pause to get a shovel and a wheelbarrow to unload the steaming piles of awesome from my room, disconnecting from the server to stop the merciless flood of red-hot awesome.

As the title says, I have never been so impressed with a FPS. This game is cooler than three times Halo and Halo 2 combined, after mixing in a pinch of Half-Life 2. I am not kidding you.

Why the hell are you still reading this? Download it, sucka!
