#415338 3 2008-03-09T18:43:05Z taaveti
The line maestro was looking for is "using namespace std;". Also, there's a "cout >>" which should be "cout <<" on the 5th line of main (which also needs a semicolon), and the 10th line of main needs a semicolon too.
In terms of style, you'll probably want some indentation to make scope clearer, and to make it it clear what's going on in the cout statements which cross multiple lines. Of course, it might be perfectly indented and that just got eaten by the forum. In that case, try putting it int a [ code ] tag.

[edit]P.S. Not related to coding per se, but the "Perimeter is" output should probably have a space at the end (and it should probably be calculated based on length+width, rather than length*width).[/edit]
