#319492 2 2007-05-08T15:02:13Z Spawn of Yanni
Framerate. It stands for milliseconds per frame - the number of milliseconds that each frame exists for. Each frame put together makes a moving image - you know, like how if you draw loads of images of a man walking in slightly different postions in the corner of each page of a book and flip through the pages, it looks like the man is moving? Each page would equal a frame, and this refers to how much time each 'page' is visible to the eye.

You would normally like to keep that number small - the faster the framerate, the better the gameplay seems to be, generally. I think it's usually 8 ms/f, for me at least.
