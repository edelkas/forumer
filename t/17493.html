http://www.konjak.org/chalk.htm

It's an arcade shooty thing. Well, sort of... instead of conventional weapons, you are armed with your fearsome stick of chalk, which is wielded via the mouse. Most enemies can be destroyed via a "connect-the-dots" mechanism, but there's all kind of other stuff you can mess with as well (slingshots and giant fish, for instance). Hugely fun, and it's a nice change from run-of-the-mill scrolling shooters. The author should extend it a bit and try to sell it on the Wii...

Oh, and check out Tripline while you're there. It's a rather difficult puzzle game, in which you have to connect like symbols without crossing your own line.
