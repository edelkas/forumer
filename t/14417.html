#268745 2 2006-12-12T09:40:14Z Winjet
First off, this should probably be in Custom Levels.
If the walls are 1 space apart, then you just press you jump button. the faster you hit it, the faster the ninja will go up. however, if it's one ways or doors, or something not a wall, then you have to use arrow keys.
If you need more help, just click the "Help" option.
#268746 3 2006-12-12T09:41:39Z George
Chimney jumping is generally considered jumping in between one space. To chimney jump fast, just press jump very quickly. There's no need to press any direction button.
#268748 4 2006-12-12T09:50:05Z Winjet

You can also use fbf, which George here does. You turn CAPS LOCK off, then tap your jump key, click the mouse, let go of you jump key, click the mouse and just keep repeating. The ninja eventually goes hella fast and will get killed if he hits a ceiling.
