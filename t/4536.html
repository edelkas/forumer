Here is a very small suggestion I have not found elsewhere in the forum.

I would find it interesting if the game submitted the kill count of the Highscore menu (the one on the left) which would add up to show the total number of kills by specific types of robots, as well as the total number of deaths (ie. the total number of tries) in all N games.
This would on the one hand show the type of monsters people mostly die from (though this of course changes with the types of levels and the number of the monsters in question in them), and also how many times people have tried a level around the world.

Of course this suggestion would not provide anything especially useful, but it would perhaps be fun to look.
