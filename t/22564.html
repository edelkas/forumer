Just so all of you know, a majority of these bands would suck - despite the telented musicianship.

Making a band is not about sticking the best of the best in there and hoping they work. It's about placing musicains in there that complement each others styles. For instance - one of the reasons Guns and Roses' guitar players (Slash & Izzy) were so notable is because of the way Izzy's rythym and atmosphere suited Slash's searing leads perfectly.


You can't just stick a bunch of people in there - think about it. If you wanted to make the best cookie - you would probably add the best ingredients, right? Think, I'll add chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, sugar, otmeal rasin, peanut butter and all the best flavors. They're good on their own - mixed with complementing things such as flour, or butter - but together - they are a disaster. Put some thinking into it.
