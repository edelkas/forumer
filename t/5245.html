Voting rules:

1) No voting for yourself.
2) No voting more than once per category choice.
3) If you wish to decline to vote in a category you may, and if you wish to decline to vote in a category choice.
4) If I wish to decline your votes, I will tell you so. I reserve the right to do this. I will only do it with very good reason, and it's unlikely it will have to happen. I might well tell people about it on this thread, to get a second opinion.
5) Please don't tell other people what you voted for. It ruins the fun. Of course, it's your secret, tell whoever you wish to, or hint at it, whatever, but it does ruin the fun; try to avoid it.
6) Don't throw pointed objects, you could have someone's eye out.

Vote counting system:

The choices are each worth thus:
1st choice: 5 points.
2nd choice: 3 points.
3rd choice: 1 point.

If it's a draw, then the same process will be undertaken except with only 1st and 2nd choice counting.

If it is still a draw: the winner of any category is the nomination with the most 1st choice votes. If two or more maps have the same amount of votes, then whichever of these maps has more second choice votes wins. The same goes for third choice if the category has a third choice vote allowance.
If they have the same amount of second choice or third choice (depending on the amount of choices for the category) votes, then a yet to be decided group of independant ejudicators will vote on who they think deserves to win.

If you didn't understand any of that, or have any questions, please ask. But don't worry about the voting system to much, if you don't understand it it doesn't really matter, only I really need to know the mechanics of it all. I just believed it was better to let everyone know how it will work.
