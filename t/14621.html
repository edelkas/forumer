We've all played Prisoner maps before. Anyone that has beaten the game has gone through the utter claustrophobic hell that was 80-1. And after that map, there were many more. The map inspired an entirely new genre of maps. They weren't really puzzle, or action, or race. They were Prisoner maps.

Today is the beginning of a contest that commemorates these bold, fresh, and new maps. Your objective? Make a prisoner map.

THE RULES
-2 maps per person
-Each map has to be a prisoner map
-No old entries. (Like you actually have any.. )

Now, what exactly is a prisoner map you may ask if you are a complete retard that was completely lucky to muster up the ability to stumble upon this forum and read this post? It's simple. At one point or another, the ninja is placed between two drones, and forced around a linear path at the designated speed of the drones. I'm going to be flexible with the definition, because I would love to have some very creative entries. Don't push it, though. I can, and will, disqualify you if your entry simply isn't a prisoner map.

JUDGING
Judging is simple. It will be divided into two categories.

CREATIVITY
10 Points

MAP QUALITY
20 Points

THE JUDGES

Aju- Yours truly. A brilliant mapmaker, and a tough critic. It's hard to please this guy.

Orange- Some random guy that I like on IRC. He makes good maps. Really easy on the ratings. You're sure to get a good rating from this guy.

Sevarus- A dark, twisted gothic guy that will probably commit suicide before this contest finishes. I don't even think he plays N..

THE PRIZES

1st Place: You get an auto-graph from the wonderful Aju. He's really cool. It's totally worth it.

2nd Place: Some random digital camera I have laying around. Oddly enough, I don't obsessively take pictures of myself, so I don't really need it.

3rd Place: An iPod Nano! It's Apple's pride and joy, and it comes in like, 5 colors. You'll get black if you come in third. No other choices.

And that's it! Let the contest begin...
...Now!

#300865 89 2007-02-25T03:40:19Z Aju
Scissor Heist by Evil Bob

Creativity Very creative, in my opinion. It really adds many popular puzzle elements smoothly without detracting from the fact that it's a prisoner map. It totally pushes the envelope. 9/10

Gameplay Very fun, but a bit too difficult at some points. A lot of the time I found myself doing a little trial and error because of an unnecessarily frustrating part. At the same time, there are certain parts that really shine out. The beginning works beautifully. It's smooth, yet still very easy to get messed up on. In general, the map keeps you on your feet. 18/20

Total 27/30

Asylum Route 66 by Evil Bob

Creativity Really great. It feels like an action map in the beginning, but that title is quickly shed in the middle for a satisfying puzzley floorguard part. The optional ending was totally trippy awesome as well, with the rocket melding perfectly. The map uses many elements from different things to create a unique experience. 10/10

Gameplay Simply superb. The laser in the beginning works almost perfectly, and the rocket at the end is aggravating but still awesome. The maps many elements work together almost perfectly. Yet at the same time, I can't help but feel indifferent at some parts. The thwumps only seemed to annoy me, and the constant use of one ways made me feel uneasy. 17/20

Total 27/30

The Rugged Road to Freedom by qlexx

Creativity Not that creative. The beginning just feels like it's been done too much. The only real notable creative thing you did was the laser center, but it was far too frustrating to count for anything. The end was just like the beginning, so already spoken for. 6/10

Gameplay Good. Even though the beginning is overdone, I couldn't help but love it anyway. The mines weren't everywhere, so they weren't overwhelming. The optional gold was placed very well too. My only gripe is the laser center. It simply didn't work. The lasers were annoying, and seemed to always kill you cheaply. The awkward tileset didn't allow much mobility either. The ending was satisfying, however. It used the same killer formula as the beginning, making such win. 16/20

Total 22/30

Drilling for the Fix by rocket thumped

Creativity Creative, yet inspired. There were tidbits that were especially creative and cool, while others were clearly taken from other maps. One creative part I really liked was the chaingun part. It worked well for a prisoner map. Pulling a chaingun off in a prisoner map was very unexpected. The jumpad was also a cool little thing too. The bottom right corner really disturbed me, however. I've seen that exact thing in a formica map before. It wasn't your proudest moment, I must say. 7/10

Gameplay Very fun, I must say. It wasn't overly frustrating, but it provided a nice addicting challenge. Each part was quite fun, and extremely well done. My favorite part had to be the chaingun section. I was extremely impressed to find how well you pulled it off. I also really liked that thwump in the beginning. The extra drone was also pretty cool as well. 19/20

Total 26/30
