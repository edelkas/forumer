IVAN is an awesome game once you figure out how to survive. The graphics aren't very pretty but there are a lot of effects like flies, lightning, fire, and blood to spice things up. Also the character's sprite changes for each piece of equipment that you are wearing and various other things like being covered with blood or water, which is an important element for me. I just can't really get into a game when my character looks exactly the same no matter whether I'm wearing platemail or leather.

You can find lots of tips at the forum or the wiki.

Psst... If you are a cheater like me, you can just backup the stuff in your save file if you don't want to lose your character when you die.

Yeah, I downloaded it yesterday, probably from the same place as you (gamehippo), and to say it stacks up well against the only other roguelike I've played would be like saying halflife 2 stacks up well against the origional doom. And nethack was hailed as a classic!
Better in every way than nethack if you ask me. You can tell when food is rotten (usually), its got a sleeker interface (not to mention graphics), the body mutilation stuff is interesting (although not really a big deal yet for me, since all I've gotten yet is bleeding or stuck in webs), there seems to be a lot less up to chance (I remember in nethack there was no way of telling whether a scroll would heal you, enchant your weapon, shoot a bolt of lightning or blow you up), and I actually knew what I was doing. Only thing missing was that you could start with a horse in nethack if you picked the right person, and even that may be taken care of later.
