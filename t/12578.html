I made an N boot screen to replace your boring Windows XP boot screen.
Limitations: Must be in your WINDOWS directory and be a 16 color BMP...
Here's a video on YouTube: N Boot Screen

And the install instructions:
Save the image in the attachment. Copy and paste it in your /WINDOWS/ directory. Now, go to control panel, folder options, View tab, uncheck "Hide protected OS files" and hit yes, then okay. Go to your C:/ drive, (or whereever your BOOT.ini is), open up BOOT.ini in your favorite text editor. Add the line to your /WINDOWS /FASTDETECT, (/NOEXECUTE might be there too, dw about it), "/bootlogo /noguiboot"

Save the boot.ini, (may be read only, right click the file, properties, uncheck read only, hit ok), then try adding the "/bootlogo /noguiboot" part. Yours should look like this:
multi(0)disk(0)rdisk(1)partition(1)\WINDOWS="Microsoft Windows XP Professional" /FASTDETECT /bootlogo /noguiboot

Don't worry about my boot.ini's rdisk and partition info being different from yours, I have XP load off the second hard drive. DO NOT TOUCH ANY PART OF THE BOOT.INI OTHER THAN JUST ADDING " /bootlogo /noguiboot."

Download the boot.bmp here:

http://www.ihud.com/file.php?file=files/05...224563/boot.bmp
