I just bought Myst IV, because it was cheap.

It annoys me.

Not the puzzles, but the movement. When you have to click 6 times and go through a montage of still images which you've seen fifty times before just to walk across a room, you have a serious flaw in game design. Yeah, I know it's supposed to be so they can have detailed environments and a simple mouse-only interface, but it's still driving me insane. If I click on door on the opposite side of the room, it should move all the way there, not stop at the bookshelf, the cupboard, the other bookshelf, the fireplace, and the table. If I wanted to move to the table, I'd click on it, not the door!

I'm not very far in, so hopefully it will improve. It's not so bad in the more open areas, but cluttered rooms... ergh!
