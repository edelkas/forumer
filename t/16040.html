First off, I would like to say that this is probably the best song ever written. Everything in this song I love, the length, the mood, the sound, everything is perfect. But one thing has always bothered me, I never knew what the lyrics really meant. So now, I'm stripping the lyrics down and analyzing every line. I'm not completly done, but if anyone wants to add something, just tell me.

The entire song can be listened to here. Please, for my sake, do not illegally download it.

So now lets rip it apart.

Quote
There's a lady who's sure all that glitters is gold
Glitter may symbolize life. Millions of tiny peicies of foil of different colors and shades. And the color gold, could represent happiness or wealth. So basically there is a girl who wants/has her life in happiness and wealth.

"Or maybe this line means that she grew up with a rich family and thinks that everything can be bought. So she thinks she can buy her way to heaven " - Impulse

Quote
And she's buying the stairway to heaven.
This is the big question of these lyrics. The "Stairway to Heaven" could represent a lot of things. This could be saying that the girl is trying to get to heaven without doing a lot of work...just buying her way up.

"I'm thinking the stairway might represent happiness, so she's trying to buy happiness. And we all know that doesn't work as well." - Solarion

Quote
When she gets there she knows, if the stores are all closed
I'm not totally sure what this statement means. It could mean, going with the idea that she is trying to buy her way into heaven, that when she knows how to end her life...the stores are all closed. Which could possibly mean that the ideas and opinions are no longer there.

Quote
With a word she can get what she came for.
Again, several things. "word" could represent, the "word of prayor" or maybe a "password".

Quote
Ooh, ooh, and she's buying the stairway to heaven.
With the "word" from the above statement, she is now doing what she came to do in the first place.

Quote
There's a sign on the wall but she wants to be sure
I'm not quite sure what this means. Maybe there's something helping her along with her decision but she wants to make sure that she makes the right choice before she does anything.

Quote
'Cause you know sometimes words have two meanings.
I believe this represents the fact that all situations we are faced with, there are at least two choices we can make in them. Or this can also stand for people, everyone is a human being but a lot of us have different opinions.

"Also, this line applies to the message pretty well too." - Solarion

Quote
In a tree by the brook, there's a songbird who sings,
Okay, so there's a sudden change in the story being told. This sentence brings in the country, forest feeling presented in the rest of the song. The songbird could stand for a person with a different idea trying to find their way.

Quote
Sometimes all of our thoughts are misgiven.
Someone with the wrong idea or perspective at looking at something, can change their opinion on it.

Quote
Ooh, it makes me wonder, Ooh, it makes me wonder.
The story shifts from a third person outlook, to a first person, which is held throughout the rest of the song until the final words.

Quote
There's a feeling I get when I look to the west,
Wow, this one really baffles me. Could be multiple things. Could refer to getting high, or maybe a certain outlook on something at a certain place or time.

Quote
And my spirit is crying for leaving.
An addiction, maybe? A broken heart? "Spirit" could be a state of mind or thoughts that he wants to get out of his head, that he wants to leave. Or this could mean suicide. His Spririt, wants to leave.

Quote
In my thoughts I have seen rings of smoke through the trees,
This is another great one. "Smoke" which comes from fire, fire is death. The "trees" could represent someone's ideas, opinions, or thoughts on something. So all this basically means, in his mind the consiquences of death are in everyone's minds and affecting the way they think. Maybe the suicide from the above statement?

Quote
And the voices of those who stand looking.
Oxymoron? Usually when someone stands and looks, they don't say anything. But this could also mean what the people who think before they speak have to say.

Quote
Ooh, it makes me wonder, Ooh, it really makes me wonder.
The "Wonder" again, which is said over and over throughout the song. He could be thinking about the choice that needs, or has, to be made.

Quote
And it's whispered that soon if we all call the tune
A "whisper" is something that we don't want someone to hear. "Call the tune" could mean to agree on a statement or decision. So something will be said after an agreement is made.

Quote
Then the piper will lead us to reason.
So after the statement is made from the above analesys, the "piper" (maybe someone low in authority) will lead them to "reason", which could be why the decision needed to be made in the first place.

Quote
And a new day will dawn for those who stand long
A "new day" could represent a second chance, or another choice to add to the decision. But, the second chance is given to only those who "stand long" which could represent being able to withstand temptation, or being able to endure to the end...or not commiting suicide.

Quote
And the forests will echo with laughter.
The ones who do receive the "new day" are laughed at for it, maybe out of jealousy. But it says "echo", so it comes back again and again. Also, the last three quotes all have the word "will" in them. This could be saying that the consiquences are already known for the decision, or maybe that the decision has already been made or story is being told from a point of view in the past.

Quote
If there's a bustle in your hedgerow, don't be alarmed now,
It's just a spring clean for the May queen.
Yes, there are two paths you can go by, but in the long run
There's still time to change the road you're on.
And it makes me wonder.
A LOT of controversy over this. Played backwards, many people get the impression that the lyrics say "Oh here's to my sweet Satan. The one whose little path would make me sad, whose power is Satan. He'll give you give you 666. There was a little toolshed where he made us suffer, sad Satan." Very convincing, those words are very crisp comparred to other songs played backwards with so called "hidden messages". Personally, I'm not sure if I should beleive this or not. The lyrics themselves do not make a whole lot of sence. But lets rip it apart anyway.
The reverse version of this set of lyrics can be found here and here. Thank-you to Twistkill for those links.

Quote
If there's a bustle in your hedgerow, don't be alarmed now,
"Bustle" dictionary definition is "to move or act with a great show of energy." So basically he's saying, if theres a row of bushes that are moving with great energy, don't be scared. BUT, be thankful for hidden symbolism. The "bustle" could represent doing something with pride and being boastful. A "Hedgerow", which is a row of bushes or trees forming a hedge, or maybe a wall, a stopping point. What he could be saying is if there is pride in where you end up and stop in your life, do not be suprised.

Quote
It's just a spring clean for the May queen.
A "May Queen" dictionary definition is "a girl or young woman crowned with flowers and honored as queen in the festivities of May Day.". So basically, it's someone important. A "spring clean" is the time of the year that we reorganize and clean out old things, which usualy brings back good memories of the past year. So this could be saying that a young girl, possibly the one from the beginning of the song, and is probably trying to clean out some memories of the past.

Quote
Yes, there are two paths you can go by, but in the long run, There's still time to change the road you're on.
"yes", why would he say "yes"? He is saying it either in an agreement or an answer to a question. I think it's an agreement because in the last set of lyrics, there was no question. The "two paths" that he is refering to could be the choices of the decision, or it could be the good and evil. BUT, "in the long run," could say that the path chosen may not have major consiquences for the distant future. "There's still time to change the road your on", well, simply you can change your ways, if you're have a bad time of your life, you can have your sins forgiven and continue on. I have a feeling there is a deeper meaning to this, but I'll leave that for you guys to find.

Quote
And it makes me wonder.
Woohoo, again with the wondering. The last statements he made he is unsure about...maybe? Or the fact that he is still thinking about what to do about something.

Quote
Your head is humming and it won't go, in case you don't know,
"Humming" can mean multiple things in this, but I have a feeling that it means "to be in a state of busy activity.". So he is saying that your head is in a busy state, maybe in thought, but then he says "and it won't go", maybe not being able to find the right answer, or maybe lost in thought. "In case you don't know" this statement is usually said in two different forms: in a sense of trying to help someone, or in a sense of trying to mock someone. He could be saying, "you're thinking to hard, just to let you know." OR "Ha, you don't know.". I'm not sure really, I want to know what you guys think.

Quote
The piper's calling you to join him,
The "piper", mentioned earlier in the song, was going to "lead us to reason", now the piper is calling you to join him. Just to point it out, in the first statement about the piper, the word "...will lead us to..." now in this context, he is saying "...calling you to...". He is no longer talking about himself, he is now talking to someone else. Maybe so he can lead us to reason? Also the word "calling" was used, not "asking" or "wishing". Calling means he could be asking you from a distance or that he is demanding that you join him. What do you guys think?
