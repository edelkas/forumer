Now, keep in mind that this is NOT a thread where you ask for dedications.
No. This thread is for those people that just don't know what type of map to make/need inspiration/have mapper's block. In this thread, people say what type of map they'd like to see. This can be anywhere from a fast paced action map with a cool flow, to something really specific, such as a close-quarters minefield map.

Map-makers that are having trouble thinking up ideas for their maps can come here, scroll down the list, and see if they'd like to create any of the suggestions posted here. If the idea is finally created into a map by someone, kindly post the link to the map here. Oh, and please credit someone for an idea. It's common courtesy. And don't forget to link to this thread in the map.

For this thread to work, there should be a variety of ideas, meaning many peopel should post their various ideas. You can post as many as you want, just make sure that you bold it to ensure easy reading for us map-makers on the go.

I shall begin with this:

A close-quarters chaingun map that relies totally on skill and not at all on having the chaingun's bullets luckily pass right over you

#112244 9 2005-11-04T03:09:44Z nevermore
I'd really like to see a very fast race map that's _very_ open-ended. The concept is slightly hard to explain. It's somewhat of a "branch" concept.

Let's say you start off in the middle of the map. You will have four choices - Go all the way to the right, go all the way to the left, fall to the right, and fall to the left. Each of those will give you an opportunity to break off and join another route, or stick with the same route or a slight variation of it. It'd be really cool if this were done with just one exit, and it would require a lot of work on the mechanics of the level. Still, if someone gets this to work, I'll be very impressed.
Note that I don't mean for it to be claustrophobic and physically branchey. I mean that you should be able to get to another section of the map very easily and quickly.
