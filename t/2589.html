N can get killed by fatal plummets, bullets to the head, landmines, electrocution, homing missiles, and laser beams, and he'll just get up and try the level again. But if the TIME runs out, then you have to start over from stage 0. It's the only form of death that doesn't give you another chance. Why? What makes timing out so different? It's far more likely to happen on later stages in the episode, where the arbitrary loss of progress is more of a slap in the face.

So why not let timing out be treated the same way as every other possible form of death in the game? Or at least have some kind of auditory warning that time is running low. Something.
