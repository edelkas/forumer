#113334 3 2005-11-09T01:59:21Z Atilla
Not quite right, Ngaged.

Pressing 'S' saves the level data TO THE CLIPBOARD. You must then paste it into a text file (of course, copying normally works just as well). To play the level again, you should paste it into the top text box, where it says level data, click outside the textbox, press L to load the level, then press P to play it. You can also put it into your userlevels.txt and play it through the "Userlevels" menu in N. The intructions for doing this are in userlevels.txt and also in the manual, which I suggest you read if you haven't already done so.
