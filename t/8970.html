Legoland: Very simple, quite easy, fairly fun. In a column this would be an early level.
Noir: Weird, and strangely fun. I started with a totally different concept in mind.
Remorse: Too difficult? Maybe I should delete the mines at the bottom and the thwumps on the side.
Poor Orphans: Totally incomplete. I just gave myself vague placeholders for items and enemies.
Fritz: Just tiles and the ninja. I'll finish this later.
Simple Switch of Syringe: This isn't quite complete. I'm still toying around with the drones. I'd like to delete a couple of the drones and make it so that if a drone sees you, it blocks you from winning, but I'm not sure how to do that quite yet.
Lotus: Too hard? I may just keep two drones in the first block, the rest simple with one.
White Widow: I have no idea.
Foyer: Simple and fun, a little challenging, but not really.
Aluminum Sun Overcast: I love the visuals (turn your head to the left) but the map itself could use some retouching. Advice?
Chalet: Fairly easy. Pretty fun. Don't let the chainguns see you.
War-Torn: Incomplete. I'm using chainguns quite a bit in this pack.
Despot Chalet: I love this concept, and I think the implementation is alright. Maybe it's too similar to Chalet.

I think this will be my last update. I have a great surplus of maps already, and I'll be able to narrow that down to 42(?) easily.
Uh, so, yes, new maps.

Keel Haul: I like this one a lot, especially the route to the exit switch. The laser and gauss work well together, I think.

Hare Krishna Henna Hookah: Eh. I like the bottom tile structure. The level itself isn't too interesting, although it's fairly fun.

Hip-Hop Alleyoop Saffron City: Jump through the hoops! I like jumping through the hoops. In fact, I think this is one of my better maps. I like how if the laser is in the way of the hoops, it'll almost always finish firing before you hit it. Cool timing.

Pratchet: Elementary little puzzle. I think it's adorable.

Pull: This might be a bit gimmicky, but I still enjoy it. To say the least it's fun to zoom through the... star things...

Unconditional Suspense: Kind of cool, but altogether not that fun. Especially the rocket section. If anything, I'd redo that. Also, generally I die against the wall on the left after getting through the wall of bounceblocks, as the thwumps squish me. I should fix that.

Styleless: Okay, this one kind of sucks. Oh well. It'll be cut, no doubt.

Sanctuary: This is one of my favorites, actually. The chaingun works perfectly, I think, and the drones are pretty cool, though maybe I should open more possibilities for the drone section. Also, the bottom left feels empty.

Federali: Really quirky, really neat. Not much more to say. Love this one to death.

Downpour: I spent a long time tweaking this and making sure that it wasn't just a cool concept, and that it was actually fun to play. I think I succeeded.

Danglemouse: Quite abstract, I'm not sure what to think of this. I like the top left. It's very unorthodox.

Ineptitude: Another crappy one. I'm slipping. This'll be trashed.

Hype: I feel the same way as Kablizzy with this one. The thump mechanism's neat, and the tiles are cool, but it's altogether not particlarly fun. Probably because it's too tense. Maybe just making the switches more accessible would improve it.

Terremoto: Very gimmick oriented, and it requires very accurate jumps, but the fact that there's hardly anything to kill you makes up for that, I think. You have to hit the launchpads at the right angle so it shoots you into the tiles next to it, and then you have to jump quickly, so it sends you flying in the air. Maybe this is better suited for numa.

Pus: NaNed drones!? Yeah. I like this, but we might not allow NaNed drones into any Legacy project. I find I don't have much to say about many of these maps, just because they're generally not as interesting as my earlier expanse maps. Oh well. At the very least, I think this one's enjoyable.

Nazareth's Baby: Novel concept, I think, and it's quite fun to figure out how to get past the rocket. That's all.

Vauxhall: I reckoned I needed a thwump map in the mix. So I made one. It didn't turn out fantastically, but I do believe it's entertaining. I'd like suggestions or edits for this one.

Fulham or Hastings?: Very fun, methinks. I like the laser, and I like how the bottom switch becomes a lot easier to get if you lure the zap drones out. The rocket works well with the drones.

Daddykins: Very Barabajagal inspired, this is one of my best maps in this pack, I think. It might be a bit too hard to get the switch directly above the rocket, though.

Writhe: I had this conceptual map lying around, and decided to shove it into my expanse pack. It's got an interesting concept, but it's probably not special enough to go into Expanse.

Antwerp: This is pretty awful, actually. It can be played as a race on the way to the switch, which at first I thought was cool, but then realized the map just sucks. I'll axe this.
