#230341 3 2006-09-18T20:08:09Z Twistkill
A regular Moderator handles a few sections of the forum. Whichever sections they moderate, they can lock, move, delete, merge/split topics, and they can warn or suspend members.

A Global Moderator handles all sections of the forum. They are not restricted by section.

An Administrator handles all sections of the forum, and they have access to the Admin Control Panel. There they can change almost anything about the forum's looks itself, and not just the members. They can add smilies, change whether or not which sections accumulate posts, change forum titles and/or descriptions, and much more.

Anything else you want to know?
