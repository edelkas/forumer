#11260 1 2006-09-16T04:45:20Z stepself
If you don't know already SE, or Summer's End, was to be a Legacy-type levelpack, but kind of fell apart over the summer (see details here). Anyway, this was my contribution, one column of levels.

This took me way longer than expected, but no matter. It's done, for better or worse. I hope you guys like it. I've added some extras too, maps that didn't make the cut, but that I still enjoyed (your levels of enjoyment may vary ).

Some notes on the column: difficulty ramps up pretty quickly, and then stays more or less constant till the end. Gold is sparse throughout, so if you want the true episode experience, go for all (or some) gold for each level. The last episode in particular contains only optional gold, so you'd have to get at least some in order to beat it in time. Alright, I'm through justifying this, enjoy!
