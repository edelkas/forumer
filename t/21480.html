Pandemic is a pretty fun time waster, i which you play a new strain of a virus. Your goal is to take out the entire human race, by distributing your points to the correct things. However, you only have 200 days before the Humans develop a cure.

There are three main "stats" to amp up. For example, In an RPG, there would be Strength, Vitality, etc. Well, these ones are: Visibility, Lethality, and Infectivity. As you can guess, visibility is bad, Infectiviy and Lethality are good. And, there are certain things that increase Visibility, but increase something else as well., i.e. Sneezing: + Visibility, + Infectivity.

The in-game instructions give a better idea of the more complex things.
