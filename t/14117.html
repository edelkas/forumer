Each coming month, as N's popularity grows and grows and new members are attracted to the community, we receive what plenty of folks refer to disparagingly as "noobs". And why not? With anticipation for the next version of N and TNG just around the corner, we can expect to receive dozens of new members in the near future. Most of these new members will invariably be noobs, clawing their way to the top by assuming superior post-counts and using too many N-based initialisms will skyrocket them to stardom.

Other newcomers, however, are of the cuter variety.

This thread has been started in an attempt to discern and highlight the acheivements of the cutest new member! The cutest new member must have joined within the last month or two and proven his or her cuteness. So what define cuteness you may ask? Here are a few factors:
  • The member doesn't try really hard for acceptance.
  • The member is genuinely enthusiastic about N and helping the community, not just to acheive some sort of status.
  • The member has a very likable personality.
  • The member seems youthful.
  • The member has something in his or her posts, avatar, or sig which seems super kawaii.


So, without further ado, I present the cutest new member:

apg

apg just recently joined us and, since his arrival, he's participated heavily on NUMA and in discussions about N. His zeal for the game and willingness to participate in community activities would make you wonder how long he's been on the forums. So he's a hella good member, sure. But what makes him the cutest?

To get to the bottom of this I decided to catalogue his forum activities and dissect them on the basis of cuteness:



Quote

Moreover, it's how he uses them that denotes his cuteness. He doesn't lather them on for attention. He uses them sparingly and succinctly to present an emotion. Emoticons, when used this way, actually save space. They are both economical and cute.

He's also very enthusiastic about getting things done around here. Check this thread. apg may be new, but that doesn't mean he's any less eager than the vets to assist in manging things. He's got the kind of vivacity that this community needs to propel it into its next generation of games. Notice how colorful the post is.
