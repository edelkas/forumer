#188537 6 2006-06-12T19:43:41Z Kablizzy
What you have to do is this:

Before you start playing the map, press [1]. You should see, in the upper left, "demo data recording stopped." followed by "demo data recording started.

When you go into the level, it should be recording your run. If you go back out to the Ned menu, you'll have to press [1] again in order for it to record again.

It seems like your problem is the recording part, as when this happens to me, it's only because I didn't press [1] (Record) before playing the map.
