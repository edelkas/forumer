STill, I created friendships with LittleViking and Kablizzy, (he registered shortly after I did originally, just 10 members after me). Good ol' days where I was 654321. Things got better as I tried to establish myself as a veteran in the community. I would go through a few more nick changes, 7654321, Pavel, and my current one, Vyacheslav. I have installed many OS'es countless times since I joined. In conclusion, I honestly never thought I'd be here for 3 years. I am honored to be a part of this ever-growing community and hope my future contributions to it will be beneficial.
