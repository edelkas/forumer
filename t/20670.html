I have an idea for a contest, however i do not want to put too much effort into organising it if nobody is willing to do it. the idea is this:
you cut a map in two parts(they do not have to be equal), any direction you like (horizontaly, verticly, however)
than you make 2 maps, one in each area, and place one piece of gold with a mine on top in each. then submit a demo. this demo must work for both parts of the map, with n starting on top of the gold/mine (which you delete of course). courses will be graded based on the usual things:
quality: x/10 do the courses look good?
fun: x/10 are they fun to play? (duh)

as well as some unusual:
asymmetry: x/20 the more different the 2 courses are the better. bonus points wil be given for completely different looking courses, as well as having n start in different places in the 2 areas.
demo skill: x/40 are there chaingun drones/ lasers/ rockets? do the demos flow? points will be deducted for "cheating" (sticking n inside of a box, beating the top, than beating the bottom) and courses that are not action based(at the judges discretion, puzzles that are played quickly will not be penalised).
length: are the courses long? the longer the better, so long as it is not obviously stalling.
and the last 20 points are going to be the score of the map on numa after 1 week x4. if you don't want to submit it there/ have less than 5 votes after a week, your quality and fun scores will be doubled to make up these points.

if anyone is willing to judge, please post a note, also if anyone has suggestions to make this better/ more fair or has suggestions for a reward (i'm not a great mapmaker, but i would be willing to do a dedication if the winner would like?) that would be great. thanks for looking and pls tell me what you think!
