I know the topic of celebratory fireworks had been raised one or twice, but that's not what this is.

Basically, a drone or turret would shoot a large bullet, or thing, that would, on impact explode into several mines (that gradually dissapear) or mini drones/missiles that home on you (then gradually dissapear).

That way, even though you've evaded the first shot you have to either keep running from a wave of missiles, or not be so careless about getting shot as you'll make more mines.

I think it's clear why I've called it a firework drone for any smart ass who wants to quote me on "but that's not what this is" and ask questions like 'so why did you call it that?'.
