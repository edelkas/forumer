Swiss Highscoring Tournament

Tired of signing up for highscoring tournaments and getting unfairly eliminated in the first round? The SHT is here for you! Everyone plays ALL rounds!

If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask me.


What exactly is a Swiss Highscoring Tournament?

[color=GRAY]The principle of a Swiss tournament is that each player will be pitted against another player who has done as well (or as poorly ) as him or herself. No player will be eliminated.
The first round is either drawn at random or seeded according to rating. Players who win receive a point and losers receive no points. Win or lose, all players proceed to the next round where winners are pitted against winners, losers are pitted against losers and so on. In subsequent rounds, players face opponents with the same (or almost the same) score. No player is paired up against the same opponent twice.

Players are arranged in order of skill. The top half is paired with the bottom half. For instance, if there are eight players in a group, number 1 is paired with number 5, number 2 is paired with number 6 and so on.

Three rounds can handle eight players; four rounds can handle sixteen players and so on. It is not uncommon to have more players than this, and, if fewer than the ideal number of rounds are played, it can happen that two or more players finish the tournament with a perfect score, having won all their games but never faced each other.

A player can enter such a tournament knowing that he will be able to play in all rounds, regardless of how well he does. The worst that can happen is having a player left over when there are an odd number of players. The leftover player receives a bye, meaning he does not play that particular round but receives a full point as if he had won a game. He is reintroduced in the next round and will not receive another bye.


Miscellaneous Information:

We will have as many rounds as needed to determine a clear winner. If 8 or less players are interested, then we will have 3 rounds. If 16 or less players are interested, then we will have 4 rounds. If 64 people or less are interested, we will have 5 rounds.

I will need judges to watch and referee matches. I will need the judges to help pick out maps for each round. Judges should be ready to get PMs from users attached with demos. Each pair of people will have different maps. All maps are to be demoed in userlevels format. If anyone has any trouble playing/retrieving maps in userlevels, do not hesitate to ask a judge for help. Judges may participate!

To help determine skill levels of the players, you will need to rate yourself from 1 to 5. (5 being the best) Be truthful, as it may have a huge effect on the fairness of the pairings in the first round.

We will spend exactly 7 days on a round. Players may PM their judge as many times as they want during the time frame of 1 week. Gold counts.


Tiebreak rules:

The tiebreaks used in this tournament are as follows:

1) Head to Head. If the two players played each other, the person who won wins the tie-break.

2) We add up the scores of the opponents you won against. The person with more points wins the tie. For example: Bill has 3 points. Bob has 3 points too. Bill won against Ben (1 point) , Bartemius (0 points), and Bradley (2 points). Bob won against Bon (0 points), Britney (2 points) and Betty (2 points).
So Bob wins as his opponents have 4 tbps (tiebreak points) while Bill only has 3 tbps.

3) In many cases, the people are still tied. If they are, then we will carry on to level 3 of tiebreaks. We this time add up the scores of the people they lost to. The person with the most points wins.

4) coin toss! (We won't need this, trust me.) ...If it is a tie for 1st, 2nd, or 3rd after considering the first 3 tiebreaks, will have an extra round.


Rules regarding byes:

Byes can either be given or requested. If requested, requesters will receive 0.5 points for the round. Players may want to request a bye if they are unable to play the on the computer for more than 6 days at a time. For instance, I might be on a cruise to Mexico and therefore unable to get on a computer.

If the Bye has been awarded to you, then you will receive a full point. Those that wish to join late may join anytime before the end of the second round. They will auto-receive a bye for the first round and wil not be rated.

No player can receive more than one bye, whether it was requested or awarded.
