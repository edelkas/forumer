Sig of the Week

Basically, I will give a theme and maybe some special requirements, then you will enter the sig, that you yourself made, and Ill make a new topic for the members of our wonderful forum to vote on. The winner will then receive a tag stating they won that weeks SOTW (sig of the week). Basically, you will have from Monday to Friday to make a sig and send it to me through PM along with the link to it. Then voting will commence throughout the weekend.

Ill be using a points system also, to see who finish most consecutively and best overall.

1st - 5 points
2nd - 4 points
3rd - 3 points
4th - 2 points
5th - 1 point

The rest of you will get zilch points.
Note: The person with the highest amount of points at the end of the month will get the Sig Maker of the Month Award.

The Rules
1. You must have created the sig your self. No ripping. If you think someone has ripped someone elses sig, PM me with your complaint, be sure to give me a link.

2. You must follow the theme for the week. No exceptions. This is at my discretion.

3. Your sig must not be any bigger than 500 pixels wide and 200 pixels tall.

4. All tie-breakers will be decided by a set panel of 3 people, if they entered a sig and it just so happens to tie, someone else will be substituted in their place.

5. Your sig must be in by Friday of that week. No exceptions.

6. Previously made sigs will not be allowed. This will rely on the honor system, don't be a douche bag.

7. If one sig doesn't get more than half the votes, the best two sigs from the poll will go to a panel of judges.
