In this game, you create your own planet. You can be good or bad. Or both. You will interact with the other planets (trade, declare war, etc.) Create the info for your planet like this:

Name:
Size:
Desription of planet:
Main life form:
Description of life form:
Supplies:
Currency:
Color of planet/sun:
Climate:
Attitude of people:
Army stuff:
Good/bad place to live?:
Position in space:
Length of day:

you can include more stuff if you like.

Here's mine:

Name: Corolost
Size: 68,000 square miles
Desription of planet: Corolost is in the far reaches of the universe. It's a lonely planet.
Main life form: Corolostians
Description of life form: They are a 6 feet tall, have four big feet, blue fur, and a trunk.
Supplies: Dry ice, advanced space ships (capable of traveling the entire universe), minerals, and plants
Currency: floobagom
Color of planet/sun: planet-different shades of blue sun-orange
Climate: warm, cold... it changes
Attitude of people: they are friendly!
Army stuff: 5000 battleships, lasers, mines, and other explosive stuff
Good/bad place to live?: kinda good...
Position in space: The edge of the universe
Length of day: 37 earth hours

Now, let the trading, chatting, killing, and fun begin!
