This was the brief given at the start of the contest:

In the modern world, humans who skate have skateparks to show off in.

In the modern world of N, N is seriously lacking in such a thing.

And so, I bestow upon you such a challenge. You must design a level, not for the purpose of completion, but for the purpose of showing off N's tricks. Basically a skatepark, except for N. Things like bounceblocks to show his gargantuan jumps on, and thwumps positioned so N can use them catapult himself. Anything that shows off what N can do. You should also be aware that there may be things that I can't do but you can - always provide a demo with your level. If you don't, I may not discover cool stuff that you can do in it.

The rules:
You can have as many objects as you want.
Thwumps should only be used in a level as a tool to help N to cool stuff, and not to kill N. If it is possible for them to do so, it isn't a problem, but they should only be there if they can be used to do tricks.
No exits.
The thwump thing also goes for all other enemies - if it looks cool, it's good.
Final date for entry: not yet set. Will be once I get an idea of how things are turning out.
Maximum amount of entries: do as many as you want. As long as you've spent (a bit of) time on it.

The point scheme:
Appearance - 5 points
Loading time/lag (the less there is, the higher the points) - 5 points
Ease of use (if really cool tricks are easier to do it's a huge bonus) - 10 points
Fun factor (how fun is it to play?) - 20 points
Quality & Quantity of cool stuff N can do on the map - 30 points
Ninja rating (how would I rate this map on NUMA if I didn't know it was linked to this competition?) - 5 points
Originality (pretty self explanatory) - 15 points
Overall thoughts on map - 10 points
TOTAL - 100 points.
