Silent evening- Venomium

Your words causing my delirium.
Good and Evil no longer in equilibrium.
Were alone, were not together,
Without that, what does is matter?
The guitar tunes echoed in my head, while the words sprang from my lips. We are a good team, John and me. His play, my text. It sounded even better then; in the shadows of evening, shadows which were being driven by the campfire. Our friends listened to us; Mirande, with her eyes closed, a smile on her lips, her head resting on my shoulder. Olivia on Mikes lap, silently mumbling the words of the song, spilling beer on Mikes knee, and Mike, slightly drunk, stroking Olivia, while staring in the swirling flames.
Allow me to get through to you,
Down in the gloom,
Love lurking, our bringer of doom.
I love you, you love me,
Why can't we see that this is simply meant to be?
John smiled, and looked at Jennifer, his hands delivering the sounds caged within the guitar, while the melody caressed our ears. He partly turned and looked straight at me. I could see the question he wanted to ask, burning in his eyes: "When will you make your move? She's alone, and cold. Youre singing, but not acting. Do something, do something now!"
I looked at Jennifer and was amazed again that she had no boyfriend; greenish, sparkling eyes, long blonde hair, softer than silk, red carveless lips, never touched, never desacrated.
Innocent as one could be, unfortunately, just as naive.
I saw Olivia watching me, mumbling the song as I sung the last words, and she saw me observing Jennifer. Her expression was like a book; "Come on, take your chance, give her something to drink, make her drunk."

Olivia is Jennifers best friend, yet not that innocent.

The flames increased, their heat embraced us all and we gladly accepted it. Time began to pass, darkness tightened, and the shadows multiplied. Our fire, a beacon in the cold, chilling grasp of blackness. Our fire, representing our friendship, through the darkest of times.
Time flies when youre having fun. Or when youre drunk. I smiled and closed my eyes while taking another nip of my Vodka.
A few moments later I had to stand up to take a piss, knowing that when I got back, I could go and sit next to Jennifer... Why not? We were a little drunk, both of us and something might happen. It just might...
I went and when I got back, I tried to make it look as casual as possible, but John grinned, and could barely contain the joke he wanted to make. Giving him an angry look, I thought by myself: I'm here, now start talking, idiot!
She rested her head on my shoulder, the soft, long hair covered the entire left side of my body. It smelled like the image of a blue, little waterfall, stroking in the midst of a beautiful spring forest. A white rose amongst red, an oasis in the desert.
I put my arm around her, gave her a sip of my drink and smiled. Her soft body curled up to me, her mysterious eyes closed and her mouth uttered soft words, barely recognizable: "Could you sing for me?" Her eyes slowly opened, carrying a soft sparkle. Of hope? Of love? I didn't know, but what I did know was that I had to do what she asked of me.
"Sure, what song would you like to hear?"
She placed her hand on my chest and lay her legs partly over mine. "About that girl, you know, the second you sang this night."
Angels in the sky,
Never free without their price,
their perfection.

The purest crystal,
The flawless ruby,
The light in darkness,
You.
I started to improvise, I was so close to her ear, I got dazed by her smell, her softness, her touch...
Ocean eyes, staring into the void,
waiting for something, waiting for someone,
perhaps even waiting for me.
Know that I'll be here for you,
Always and ever.

Your blonde hair,
Waiting to be touched,
Your soft lips,
Waiting to be kissed...
She turned to me, her eyes wide open and then, she kissed me.
Her right hand in my hair, her left stroking my back. Her lips against mine, her tongue melting with mine.
I was exalted; her lips, her nose, her face, so close, so real, with me, for me!
I couldnt believe this, where we finally together?
Our kiss slowly ended, we were both smiling, in her arms I felt free.



I awoke, another school day began. Another day, without her. Her death, two years ago, still touches me.
