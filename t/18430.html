Turn Based:
[b]Heroes of Might and Magic 3[b] - Pretty sweet game once you get the hang of it, but sometimes you'll get shafted by the randomly generated maps. Once, a friend of mine found the Holy Grail (an item that gives your base really nice powers) in the first month of play (23 turns). Made me weep.

#345625 14 2007-07-20T23:42:50Z Tsukatu
CounterStrike is always a classic, as is Half-Life Deathmatch.
If your group is 4-6 people big, you could try out the Splinter Cell 3 multiplayer, which is designed specifically for that number of players (one group is 2 or 3 spies, the other is 2 or 3 soldiers).

If you're playing with one other person, I highly, highly recommend Splinter Cell 3 cooperative mode. I'm really into that concept, though, so I may be kind of biased.
Actually, so much am I in love with that idea that I'll hijack the topic slightly - does anyone know of any excellent games you can play cooperatively (preferrably 2-3 people)?

As for turn-based strategy...
Any 'Total War' game is excellent if you want to play a relaxed multiplayer game, since any given turn can last a bit (especially if you want to command the battles yourself). One of the best LAN parties I've had with a group of friends was spread out across a few rooms playing Shogun: Total War. We had a movie going on in one room, music in another, and on any given person's turn, the rest of us were just chilling out and talking. Our idea for future games (which we sadly didn't have) was to set up two additional monitors so that the rest of us could watch personally-commanded battles from the perspectives of both people involved. I started playing a game of chess with someone else on the side, too.
Anyway, the point is that it was very good times. I don't know if you'd enjoy that sort of an evening or whether you're more focused on an action-packed shooter, but I found it most excellent.
