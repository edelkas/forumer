This is a five day, five map, Level pack. It's for my favorite author, Sendy. You can find all the maps on NUMA.

I had a special reason I had in mind for making each map. I put it in each map's description and structured it like a letter. Here are they, if you want to read them. It's all true.
Spoiler: Her MailboxDear Sendy:


I saw a girl.
I saw her sitting with her back to a mailbox, watching the traffic.
Watching me.
I wanted to ask the girl.
I wanted to ask her why she was sitting, if she was alone.
Alone like me.

I walked around the girl and her mailbox instead.

At least she has something.



Smort.
Spoiler: Because They Don'tDear Sendy:


A friend once asked me if Trees feel cold and alone when they loose their leaves.
The answer, of course, is that Trees go through a natural cycle that is much like sleeping every winter, and it does not mean that they are losing anything. The ground gives them sustenance, and they return something to it. It is natural. It is their purpose.
I only noticed while I was out on my street today how unlike trees are to humans.
Is it so strange for me to wish I were a tree?



Smort.
Spoiler: ThisDear Sendy:


What is the accumulation of all we have done?
What is, currently, the peak of man's existence?
What have we done it all for?
It seems to me that the only thing we do flawlessly is exist, and that is a miracle.



Smort.
Spoiler: The Same TodayDear Sendy:


Yesterday, I cried for the one I love. I wish it was for war. Humans are so self centered.


Smort.
Spoiler: LifeDear Sendy:


I had a love, but she left me.
I had a friend, but he left me for love.
I have a family, but they do not understand.

The world has given me nothing, and I have taken nothing from it.


Smort.



The end.

I woke up this morning feeling utterly alone. I remember my ex boyfriend and how he would comfort me but at the end of the day it was just words - I often felt truly alone in his arms. I learned to read his body language too well. That's why I left him, because I learned to see more clearly.

Sometimes, I want to be alone. Sadness never eats me up, in fact, my own suffering seems poignant and almost beautiful. I look back on it with a sense of wonder and awe, grateful to be able to feel such delicate emotions.

Nobody chooses to be alone, but I don't want to pretend to be who I'm not in order to gain acceptance. I'd rather be lonely and have a small number of good friends who know the real me, warts and all, rather than loads of friends who have come to enjoy the act I project. I am selfish, and sometimes, I DO want to be left alone. Of course, I'm also my own worst enemy.

We want the profound meaning of life to be made clear, and I think we're all in for a big disappointment. Trees make good furniture.

I have still been unable to truly love someone other than myself. I suppose that makes me a 'bad person' but all I can say is - I Tried. Of course, I give common respect to everyone, or try to, but that's not the same as love. I wanted to love somebody else, desperately, to make me feel human. But all I truly have is a love for myself, my close family and friends (and I only love them because they are close to me, and our relationships are mutually beneficial). The most important relationship you have in your whole life is your relationship with yourself, so why not start the love from there? I'm attracted to selfless people. Some of them admire the way I am. The grass is always greener... I wonder if they know that secretly, I admire them, too.
