#122277 4 2005-12-12T03:48:11Z GamingWolf
Here are my thoughts on this situation. My favorite is Action Maps. As bland as that might seem I have a actual reasoning behind this. I'll go through the types you listed except action as I am comparing it to the other categories.

DDAs: I like video games. I don't like video games that are purely videos. I want to actually PLAY and interact with them. Sure there's the occasional move left or move right but I'm hardly interacting. I should make an essay on why movies make me feel empty.

Race: I can race in any Action map I want to. Surely maps are put in here because of an intended speed. Then again action maps can still involve speed.

Puzzle: This one is worth noting. Let's guess this straight you are trying to tell me these are puzzles. Well to me most of time they are not. I know exactly how to do puzzle maps. Actually doing what I have planned is what makes it difficult. Considering that I am also finding people who don't test their puzzle maps.. it becomes even more unfavorable to me. Oh yeah and I'm using action to get through these maps too.

N Art: Oh no Piccasso is rolling in his grave now. People have been telling me that they like N Art because they respect the challenge that it took the author to make it look nice. Well that's not the point at all. The point is I like maps that I actually move around in. How it looks is of no concern to me unless it interferes with gameplay. Of course when it comes to good maps. Making a good tileset that doesn't really change the functionality of a map is better than the original good map you started with. That does not distract me from favoring action maps though. They are the core of the game man.

Incomplete: I must admit it can be interesting to what maps could have ended up through the power of what I sometimes like to call "beta maps". However that means that there is purposely something wrong with it and we're asking people how to fix it. Not my cup of tea. Oh and a few times this category has been used for maps from authors that don't want people to vote on. That's a negative.

Test Level: Whoops I think I merged the category a bit there. So Incomplete says.. hey I'm not done yet but you can see it anyway. I still don't want to see that. Test levels are saying that I should check out a few features or make sure that the author knows what they are doing. An action map implies I don't need your help. That takes guts... and I respect that although we know some gutsy people turn out to be not-so-good mapmakers.

Survival: Oh no. I have no goal in this level except an unlimited time limit in which I am to avoid many hazards. Action maps have a goal. Survival maps insure me of death. The high scores can only get higher in this one when it comes to time. What if I have to use the bathroom? Okay, people might pee in their pants just to keep playing. What if I have to sleep? Someone is doing better than me because they are not sleeping. You wouldn't want someone to be better just because they miraculously found the time now would you? Of course there's pausing but eventually the power will go out whether you like it or not. Someone would do better because they never have had bad weather attack their homes. With action maps when someone does better than you... pure skills man. And he's encouraged to go faster to beat your score. (See Race for Race/Action map discrepancy) Oh sure there's a limit on how high the score can go but I figure that's a small price to pay for constant frustration from making your survival map score as large as possible.

Uncategorized: There are maps like that? If you mean from merged styles, forget that I already went over the previous categories. Now how you would make a category completely different is beyond me. Unless you decide that the number of frames has nothing to do with the goal of the level, then I could understand. Chances are they will not be to favorable. You see I hate people who think just because I beat them at video games that I cheated. (I don't use cheats. It' more obvious when the game doesn't even have recognizable cheats) They say stuff like, "Oh you're not allowed to use that attack" or, "No sneaking up behind me with a rocket launcher". Unless it's in the options menu, that isn't going to happen anytime soon. With making up rules that don't actually exist in the game, the game itself won't actually congratulate me on this specifically if I won that particualr challenge. For instance try one of Templex's JUMP-ONLY maps. Nothing in the option menu that has a jump-off. Whoops... failed there.The game (whether by the orignal or a hack and there aren't any hacks for this game that I'm aware of.) doesn't say, "You defeated a jump-only map" or something like that. Did I mention I REALLY Hate people who try to enforce limits of elements of a game that were progeammed that way? Therefore I find it unfavorable.

Other: Other? Well let's go through the instances of clicking "other". First of all I did not click other for the reason that there's another type of map. That's what Uncategorized is for. Secondly, no I don't like all the types of maps. Someone is bound to have a category they personally dislike without telling anyone about it. If you really do like all of them most likely it's because you want anything that has to do with N. If you saw that ninja on a coffee mug you would get it. I don't like those kinds of people. Get away from me. A few of those were DDA and N-Art. I explained why I don't quite fond them. If you clicked because of a combination of the categories.. well whatever. I explained hybrids in my Uncategorized section. Now if you don't like any of these types there's a few reasons to not like you right now. You wouldn't like any category right? Don't you see that Uncategorized button? The other reason would be that you don't like to play N and you probably get your rear end out of here right now.

I hope I have given you plenty of insight as to why I like Action maps. It's the standard of N and NUMA of course.
