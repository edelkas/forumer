#380760 2 2007-11-19T04:31:37Z George
There are two ways of completing this level: the short way which requires a bit of skill, and the long way which requires a little bit of patience.

To complete the level the short way, when hitting the exit switch, only touch it for a frame or so. That way, it won't trigger the trapdoors that force the zap drones in. The long way is as it is, and requires collecting the keys to exit from the top.

I've attached some demos to help. Just paste either one into the lower left textbox in the userlevels menu to watch.

#380990 4 2007-11-19T23:02:53Z George
You might not want to bother with the gold, so you may want to follow the route in my demo. The only difficult places seem to be collecting the exit switch and reaching the exit itself. When you go to collect the exit switch, make sure you're as further left as you can be, and as soon as you touch the floor, jump immediately to avoid the floor guard. And when you make your way back up, do you best to avoid the gauss, by jumping quickly, or by luring it's aim to somewhere else.
