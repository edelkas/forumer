#19058 1 2008-02-02T23:52:50Z Animator
Let me explain. I've hit the golden mark of 100 drones (fan-made) and I want to celebrate it with a contest. I'm stuck with 2 ideas.
Spoiler: Drones at Random comic competition:
-You are allowed to make 1 comic based on exactly 20 drones from the last edition of Drones At random (the last 100 drones.)
-You can also make up to 4 comics, but the drones cannot be repeated (thus forcing the player to use all 100.)The best one will be considered.
-Fan drones are allowed (i.e. Master drone, Kenny, any drone non-D.A.R. related), but they won't count
with the total of 20 drones in a comic.
-It's pretty much freestyle, but do comply with the forum rules.
-Can be as many panels as you please/require.
-Judging is as follows:
=Well made: x/5.
=Has 20 drones (exact): x/5.
=Has humor: x/10.
=Overall: x/20.
-Score will be determined in percents. (i.e. 65%.)


Pros of the contest:
-You can make some awesome comics, with the variety of drones.
-Judging is simple.
-The drones will be found easily.( When I post the spritesheet up, of course.)

Cons:
-Way too many drones can equal not enough ingenuity for a comic.

or...
Spoiler: Drones at Random Drone Contest:
-Make a drone, only a drone. Floor guards may be allowed.
-Can be any drone you wish, this contest is freestyle. Comply with the forum rules.
-It must be .png and the limit is 100px. by 100px. Enough space for a drone.
-Allowed to submit up to 3 drones. The best one will be considered.
-It cannot be a drone stolen from my Drones thread, Ignate's spritesheet, the Screen shots of N+, or any drone from N, as well as any other drone thread here. If this is found out, you will be immediately kicked from the competition.
-Your drone must be made from scratch, or you may get an inspiration and edit the regular N drone, but it must be completely and totally different.
-Judging is as follows:
=Aesthetics: x/10.
=Detail: x/5
=Innovation: x/5
=Overall: x/20
-Score will be as a percent. (i.e. 98%).

Pros:
-Very simple and fun.
-Don't have to be an image expert to do this.

Cons:
-This could be a very easily cheated contest (unless you have every single link of a fan drone.)

So... decide.
