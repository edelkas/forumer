Sodaconstructor is a really cool physics simulator in which you can create moving virtual machines. It can take some getting used to, but if you get good enough you can get your creations to walk, crawl, or roll across the screen. It's also the thing I was doing the most before I got hooked on N.

Be sure to check out the sodazoo, because they have some really neat models from people around the world. In fact, the reason I mention this site at all is because in the last update they included one of mine in the zoo. It's listed as freaky_cubivore, under tktktk.

Keep in mind, you probably won't be making great models right away. It took me several tries before I was able to make even a simple model. But just try it out, and try to find some of the really great models. In the links section they have the sites from a few incredible constructors that are definitely worth visiting.
