Haunted House 2 (Ghost Drone Type 2)

http://nmaps.net/130949

Must play the original Haunted House first:
http://nmaps.net/130910

Restart NReality (a mod of N, d/l @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Roboboo Ghost Drone Type 2

Ghost Drone 2 : 6^x,y,Path,0,102,direction

optional parameters: ^acceleration,maxvelocity,minAlpha

Default:
acceleration = 0.02
maxvelocity = 2
minAlpha = 0(default zero, set it higher to make it not totally invisible when you face them)

The original Ghost Drone will follow you when you are not looking at them. They are always harmful.

The Ghost Drone Type 2 will become invisible and harmless when you are looking at them, that means you can run through them. They are only harmful when you are not looking at them.
