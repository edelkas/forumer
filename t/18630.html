How could she.
The thought crosses my mind. She betrayed me. I throw my head down and grit my teeth in thought. She said she'd never do this to me. I lift my head up and look out the window leading to the shed in my backyard. Maybe this is the only way to make things right. I open the door to my backyard, and slowly, I walk to the shed, with deep thought in every step. Just don't think about it. I take a deep breath. I go into the shed, and I look around. In the corner, I see an axe. Yes, yes that's it. I grab it, and I now realize my hand is covered in sweat. Just don't think about it. Finally, I think over everything she did. Everything she said. Something snaps. I can feel the rage and the hatred building up inside of me. Yes, this was the only way. With a new passion, I throw open the shed door and go back inside the house. I walk into the kitchen. There she is, standing there. Just looking at me. Grinning. Does she not think I'm going to do it? Is this just what she was planning, what she wants? Is this her idea of revenge? I grit my teeth again and take up the axe in both my hands. Taking a step forward, I lift the axe high in the air. She's still smiling. Just don't think about it. With all my strength, I chop down with the axe, over and over and over, using my hatred to fuel my strength, and every blow is stronger than the last. Why did you do this to me? WHY. WHY. WHY. WHY. I strike again and again. I hope she's happy. Finally I give up. That d*** pipe won't come out of the sink.
"D*** it, I can't do this without my tools. Why you got to throw away all my ****ing tools then expect me to fix the sink a week later?"

So there I was, sitting in my helicopter flying it everywhere and anywhere I wanted to go. I can see clouds that are like "Hey, dont bring the helicopter through me" but I soooooo do it anyway. I finally get to my destination. My home.

Wal-mart has been my home for years now. I steal things like food and toilet paper about once a week to keep them on their toes. Really doubt theyve noticed yet. During the nice days, I sit outside in front of the store daydreaming things like... a rich guy giving me money because I'm awesome...maybe finding an unlocked door to a steak factory...the typical dreams everyone has.

People who I've taken the time to tell my story too always ask why I don't sell my helicopter for cash so I dont have to steal from walmart and sleep on top of the shelves by sneaking up before closing. I tell them "because helecopters are freaking awesome". Sad to know they're never going to understand the joy of flying it.

One day I decided to change my ways and move onto a better store because the greeter in front of the store is starting to give me dirty looks. Walgreens. It's like the same thing, but less mart-ier and more green-ier. One day I scraped up enoug money to buy something, so I got a twix bar and made small talk with the cashier. I asked the question "Why do I smell piss everywhere?" She said it was because I was in a walgreens. I stole some useless crap on the way out.

The next day, I bought the same candy bar at the same register. Cashier told me they were having a special on helicopters in isle 5, but I already have one of course. She asked to go for a ride in it with me one day. It didnt matter where we went she said.

I eventually followed through with this and we took a trip over to nowhere all night. She thought I was crazy at first, but it turns out I'm a sane man in a crazy situation. I came through the store every other day to say hi if I saw her.

Later that year, she quit her job and was going out of town for college. Last time I saw her. Pretty much my first good friend in a while. I decide to take this all like a man, by treating it like it wasnt a special memory to make it seem less depressing. Starting up my helicopter to go anywhere reminds me of the times we spent flying around speaking our minds like open books. We didnt have to talk about anything too intresting...just anything...

I saw her working as a bank teller about 4 days ago. I was amazed to see her through the window, and went inside to see her reation. I stood in line like the rest of the people waiting to see her. And when it was my turn and I stpped forward, she saw me as just another guy. No need to remind her of who I am. If she cant remember then I dont want her to.

Now I go to the library's computers to post my lifes story on the internet.
