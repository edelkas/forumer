#470748 6 2008-09-16T23:42:09Z PsychoSnail
Either you accidentally deleted it, or the .sol file corrupted itself. The N .sol file seems to be so complex compared to many other .sol files that it sometimes gets corrupted, and the next time you launch it, it resets the .sol file. So, there's nothing you can do to reverse the effect, unless you have a backup of the .sol file anywhere (which is why you should keep a backup of the .sol file).
You had really horrible luck to have the .sol file corrupted right after you completed the game. Cleaning the registry or reinstalling the game won't help, because N doesn't even write to the registry, and reinstalling the game doesn't change the .sol file, which is stored in a separate folder with all other Flash games' .sol files. More info on .sol files is here.

Unfortunately, that doesn't explain why your highscores aren't working. Do you mean that your personal demos aren't working, or that the highscores top-20 lists aren't working? Either way, I'm not sure why it's like that for you, though the latter can be caused by Flash not being able to connect to the Internet. Also, is it like that for NReality also, or just N v1.4?

Edit: Though if you still want to quit playing N, now is the best time.
