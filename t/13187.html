The Equivilante Contestador.

The Equivilante Contestador is a contest where you have to make a map that uses the same amount of tiles and objects.

Example: http://numa.notdot.net/map/63457[/color]

Rules:

  • You have to have to have the same amount of tiles as you do items.
  • The amount of tiles and items can't be over 50.
  • Judges can submit.
  • Each user can only submit 3 entries.
  • The border tiles don't count as tiles.
  • All doors and door switches together count as one item.
  • After judging starts, no withdrawing allowed.


Important Dates:
  • January 9 - Prizes are given.


Prizes:
  • 1st place - Dedication and signature tag.
  • 2nd place - Signature tag.
  • 3rd place - Signature tag.
