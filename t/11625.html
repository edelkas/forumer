#209980 2 2006-08-01T18:47:05Z Kablizzy
I agree wholeheartedly. Their 0 and 10, 1 and 11 columns were blow-me-away, but as I went on, it seemed that they got too intricate for their own good. One thing that I was shocked about was how much I disliked Baraba's maps - I usually love his stuff, but this time around, he had nothing that wowed me. Take 06-0, for instance. It's a puzzle jumper, it took me 20 minutes just to figure out how to get into the middle area (Which is entirely my fault, but I digress).

All in all, they did a killer job, but a number of the levels, primarily Baraba's and Pariah's, aren't my style at all. Which is rather just a personal dislike of mine than anything that was wrong with the maps. Just didn't quite fit my tastes.

Mostly, the things that bugged me were aesthethic quirks - For instance, KA's maps were great fun, but aesthethic messes; Doors in half-positions (Not covering full areas) always bugged me, but beyond that pet peeve (As I know most people don't care), these were great.

krusch did a killer job on his maps, his 05 was a bit lacking for me, but I rather chalk that up to my playing those maps before, when they were in earlier, less-refined stages.

I noticed that they seemed to have a difficult time ramping up difficulty, and pulling all of the episodes into a line that would integrate them smoothly. Things like 04-0, I noted, should have been earlier, while things like 04-1 should have been later. I attribute this almost entirely to their rigid structure with which they assigned episodes. As individual levels, these were amazing, save for a map or two that would rather belong to NUMA; so, beyond a couple personal dislikes, this thing blew me away.

All in all, this is the best levelpack we've seen to date. So, publically, I'm going to rave over the pack, so as to generate further interest (And, as my little experiment yesterday showed, if I like something, the community likes something), and to get more people into playing the maps.
