#426302 2 2008-04-08T15:42:50Z Spawn of Yanni
You have to go around to every 'dark' door, defeating the evil versions of your friends. You know how there are various save points? There are four main ones, they allow you to teleport to each other. Look at the map a lot - after a while, it'll show you where each remaining fight is.

Also, I -think- you can find unopened doors on the map, if you look carefully. You see the lines connecting each section? You might be able to see a small dot in a particular section with no line coming out of it - that's an unopened door. I'm not sure if that happens before you're allowed to see all the remaining fights, though.

Basically, go around opening doors, and look for ones which have got funky blackness around them in particular.
