#136261 2 2006-01-27T02:23:38Z Ngaged
Actually, it should be map#author#type#level data# (for Userlevels). Don't forget that final #!

When you get to the debug screen while making a level, there are two boxes. The upper one contains the level data, aka the looooong numbers. Right click and click select all, right click again and click copy. You can now do two things, maybe.
-For Userlevels, follow the thing above, putting in the numbers as the level data in your userlevels.txt. Save the file.
-If you just play on debug mode, then just paste the level data in a word document or a txt. file. This makes it handier. Save the file.

I'm not sure if it's what you're looking for, but tell me if you need more info. Hope I helped!
