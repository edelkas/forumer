The Alternative Love Poem

This is, basically, an English assignment from a while ago. I earnt myself a tidy A+, and thought you might want to take a look at this poem. Enjoy!




Her eyes were the deepest shade of toilet water blue

And her hugs were as warm as freshly spewed spew.

Her hair was as dark as the lid of my pen-

She truly was beautiful- and her name was Sphen.



My heart torn out, my love thrown away,

And shoved onto a metaphorical dump truck on Valentines Day.
It hurt so excruciatingly, a pain above all,

Like the pain you feel when you accidentally staple gun your tongue to the wall.



My love for you is forever a red, red rose,

That in a most desolate garden grows.

Flowering softly, where the air is still,

Grows my love- which looks vaguely like a daffodil. *



I dont even think you know my name,

Day after day, its all the same.

Looking on sadly as you give your love away,

Day after day, day after day.



Each day feels as sad as the last-

And yet, whenever you walk past,

My heart stops



And now Im dead.



*(Authors note: My love is also needs to be watered frequently with water, otherwise known as di-hydrogen oxide, and can be used by florists in flower arranging.)
