Privileged moderators' abilities are restricted to only one or a few certain forums given when promoted to the new class (who monitors what forum should show underneath the forum's name).

Q: "Member Titles?" What's a Member Title?

Q: Cool. How can I get one?
A: We don't just hand Member Titles out like free passes to an amusement park (I used to, but that was when I was a moron, back in the day ). One of these two must occur, two of them unfortunately on your part:
  1. You must have done something very drastic in the community that affected numerous members (this can be a good or bad thing), like piss off a lot of people without actually breaking any forum rules, win a NTTS round or some other major contest, etc.
  2. You must be ber-1337 in some field, like working wonders with Paint, being a math genius, or helping Keron Cyst with his homework in the IRC room!
  3. (this counts as a third but it's so rare that I didn't label it above) An admin can give one at random to a member, whether he wants it or not. Heheh, that's always fun.
  4. You must have been around awhile. Members who have been here for years and have been contributive to the community are often rewarded with a Member Title, even if they haven't done anything revolutionary.
But at any given time you can lose your title or it can be replaced with something else, so don't take it for granted!

Q: You said something about warning above - how does the Warn System work?
A: A mod can warn you + or - 20% depending on something bad you've done; the mod can send you a PM or e-mail about why you were warned. If you made one, warn logs hamper your staff application severely when we look through your forum history to determine moderatorship, and basically tell you when to stop acting like a moron 100% gets you a forum suspension, so you don't want to go there!

Q: If you're suspended because of warns, how do you regress?
A: According to the Forum Rules, we'll keep the warn level at 100% for a month. Then we drop the warn % down by 20. One wrong move can get you back in Suspension Land, and can last from twice as long as that to a permanent ban, depending on the severity of the forum violation

This means that the topic is dead and no longer a point of interest.[/color] Reviving is rarely a problem when you've got something meaningful to contribute to the topic at hand.
