Some person specific advice for tsuki on getting into soldat

Alright, tsuki, based on your forum personality, I think you would probably like to demolish your opponents with the M79 the most, out of the weapons, with chainsaw probably as a secondary. However, since the m79 is the hardest to learn to aim, I think you should start with an auto first. You wouldn't like the minimi because of its slowness or gun #2 (I forget the name) because you have to take a lot of pauses to reload, so you are left to choose between the very fast steyr and the more powerful but slower firing ak. I personally like the ak best, and liked #2 for a while because its small cooking wine unloaded very fast. I think you will dislike both the barret (sniper rifle) and those who use it, although you will hopefully opt for blowing their brains ouot instead of ragging on them. I can't imagine you camping.

I suggest you play a dm against bots with an auto weapon first to get used to the controlls, interface and aiming, then switch to the m79 and get used to it. Then jump right into an online ctf, and follow the first guy to leave your base using the upper route.
