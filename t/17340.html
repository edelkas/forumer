#329134 7 2007-06-09T11:16:19Z Spawn of Yanni
Phase 1: It begins. [Yes this summary is going to have weird-ass phase names. Deal with it.]


The sigs of nevermore, wedgie, Kablizzy, LV, Trogdor, and thecalcgenius had links leading to some parole numeri page. From the corresponding numbers, we got COFFEE - the name of our target.

Trogdor posted another clue identifying the prize again - pointing out the forum we have to get to is #54.




Phase 2: Cerebrum, or Tumbling Down The Rabbit Hole.


"It may go through more than just space."

Trog keeps hinting at time and the future. Look at the last day in the calendar (without editing the address) and you get an entry called Cerebrum.

Searching cerebrum gives you a post by Trogdor that mentions the Noetic Realm, which fits in the asterisks in the calendar entry.

Forum searching Noetic Realm gave us the clue to search for the man of the mind (enter stupidity by me here).

The man of the mind is a member called Cerebrum.

Cerebrum's signature has links to various members, do some funky shit here with the third character of each name and you get CHITS. CHIT is a word that is found in another link in his sig.

The first letter is found here: S, from the difference mentioned above.




Phase 3: Enter the Mafia.


Forum searching CHIT gives you a nevermore post."I am surrounded by noise. It's time to enlist in La Cosa Nostra. Are you ready?"

"I am surrounded by noise" gives you the second letter: I - This is the tricky one. Trog already confirmed that this is the right letter, but said we haven't found it the right way. My theory is that it refers to the "I" itself being surrounded by letters in the word "noise". I am probably wrong.

La Cosa Nostra is the Mafia. So we must join the mafia, i.e. check out the mafia joining thread.

At the end of the first post in this thread is the edit "3/1/1 - LETTER MAFIOSO MAFIA" with the slashes going to http://ntoysmmosef/ and http://of.the.18th.server.forumer/

This gives us 3rd Letter, 1st Mafioso, 1st Mafia: In the first game of Mafia on the forums, IangB was the first mafioso: so the letter must be N.

In that same thread, there is also a clue at the bottom of the first post giving us the letter E, after some cool maths shit.




Phase 4: Shit, It's Brit.


Shift the letters ntoysmmosef one space to the left and you get BritanniaWD. With the clue "of the 18th forumer", we are sent to britanniawd.18.forumer.com.

There, a thread by Brit is found. It tells us the next letter is N, and leads us to the 75th thing that you can't post in suggestions in another thread here on metanet.

The 75th thing is a samurai, with a reference to the letter S. This is where we hit the bottom of the rabbit hole. We have the letters SINENS.



Phase 5: Into the Dragon's Den.


Brit's sig on the other forum completes the set of sigs from all the players. Taking the underlined letters from each of their sigs gives us NAAGGOY, or, rearranged, ANAGOGY.

Forum searching ANAGOGY gives us a Brit post: "Welcome to the Dragon's Den. ANAGOGY."

Forum searching Dragon's Den (or doing some exceedingly cool but useless "Hell" shit) gives you another Brit post.

A link at the end of the Brit post gives us the letter A, seemingly the first of this new word.

"123: "this is the first ever map ive made, im still not totally sure how to use teleports or how to make the \\\'smoke\\\' type explosion thingy. anyone care to comment?" <-This refers to a map description by wedgie123 (wedgie on NUMA).

Search this on Google, and you get a cached result of his map. The description has since been changed, to "Dragon's Den. My first, it goes upside down." Take wedgie's first letter, turn it upside down, you get the second letter: M.



Phase 6: SeXy.


The most recent comment on the map is by nevermore, telling us to find what wedgie has to say about the X, on Friday 13th 2005.

On that date, wedgie made a post in a nevermore thread referring to a map called "Unlocking the X". At the end of his post is "Mhm. Another clue pending."

The "e" in that clue is in italics, suggesting that the next letter is E.

A while later, the "Another clue pending" changes to "#Nottherabbithole", a clear reference to an IRC channel.



Phase 7: You said there was no IRC! ;_;


Going to the channel #DragonDen gets you immediately kicked from it. However, it gives the message - "Hey, I didn't invite you into this! Chanserv attack! By the by, the next two letters are L. Sic!"

So, we have the next two letters, L and L.

Forum searching "Sic" gives you a thecalcgenius post with this clue: "Chanserv sic! Retrospect, and look at the beginning of the Dragon's Den. See it. Listen, too. See it."

"See" is underlined. This is a clue referring to the letter C. However, it's not as simple as that. TCG is telling us to go back to the beginning of the Dragon's Den - GASP! That means C is actually the FIRST letter of this new word.



Phase 8: Fuck, how many phases are there?


Just when it seems there are no leads to follow, the very first post of the thread has been edited. "Welcome to the end. Dragon's Den: IA. Rabbit Hole: IS."

This means that we should add these letters to the ends of the words they refer to. For the first phase, the Rabbit Hole, we had SINENS. Add IS to the end, and we come up with SINENSIS.

For the Dragon's Den, we have CAMELL. Add IA to the end, and we come up with CAMELLIA.

Trog previously mentioned that the words don't necessarily go in the order we found them. So it's not SINENSIS CAMELLIA, it's CAMELLIA SINENSIS.

Camellia Sinensis is some sort of Green Tea - Kamikaze, when he was accepted into your ranks, made references of Green Tea, confirming the significance of Camellia Sinensis.

And that's it. The password. Here I am. It's been fun.
