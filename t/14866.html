Welcome to NedCon8!

Happy New Year!! This year, The Legacy Team is starting things off right with the eighth season of the Neditor Contest! This season, we have a brand-new theme for participants to work on; ReMixes!

For those of you unfamiliar with the idea of ReMix maps, a ReMix map is, in essence, an interpretation of another person's map. There are two types of ReMix maps; Conceptual and Literal.

A Conceptual ReMix map is where you keep the essence and the idea of the original, but change the tileset or playstyle of the map, with the same general idea and feel. For instance, if one were to ReMix Mount Doom, one could keep the tileset, and ReMix the map's context, as seen in this map, where the map's tileset was changed, but Mount Doom's concept stayed intact.

A Literal ReMix map is where you keep the structure and form of the map, but change the theme or essence of play. A good example of a Literal ReMix would be this map, where Mount Doom was turned into a DDA, using the same tileset.

This season's theme being ReMixes, participants can ReMix any map that has been posted to NUMA as of prior to this post. Any NUMA maps that were posted after this post may not be used.

Each contestant is allowed 2 submissions TOTAL for the contest. You may ReMix the same map twice. If the total number of submissions received reaches 25, each contestant may submit an additional map, for a total of 3 maps each.

All entries must be submitted to NUMA under the same name as you use here on the forums. If your NUMA account name and Forum account name are different you must send me a Personal Message so we can verify your identity. Each submission must be your own unique work, any map plagiarized from another member, or any map submitted from a non-member will be thrown out accordingly.

Each submission must be posted in this thread, along with a clearly-labeled link to your ReMix map, as well as a clearly-labeled link to the original map. The link to the map that you are ReMixing must be in your post, and it would be preferable if the link to the original map were listed in your NUMA submission, as well.

Here are the stipulations for this season;
  • All maps must be submitted to NUMA. (With the exception of those who don't have access to NUMA)
  • A link to all submissions must be provided in this thread. Along with that, a link to the original map must be provided in this thread.
  • Manual code editing of any kind may not be used.
    If the map you are choosing to ReMix has manual code editing in it, it is a good idea to choose another map.
  • All objects must be completely or partially visible. Completely hidden objects may not be used.
    If the map you are choosing to ReMix has hidden objects in it, it is a good idea to choose another map.
  • There may not be any purposeful use of bugs, glitches, or other unsavory map making tricks. If there is any Grey area, send a personal message to one of the judges, and we will pass down a decision on whether the trick is allowed.
    If the map you are choosing to ReMix uses bugs and/or glitches in it, it is a good idea to choose another map.
  • Any map violating any of these requirements will be disqualified from the contest, with no exceptions.
  • If you wish to protest a score, send a personal message to one of the judges, and we will pass down a decision on whether the score is acceptable.
    Posting in this thread to contest a score will result in immediate disqualification from the contest.
  • It is your responsibility to double-check scores on your maps, not ours.
  • There will be no editing of maps once they are submitted to this thread. We use an honour system for this, so if you are caught editing your maps after they are submitted here, you will be immediately disqualified from the contest.
  • You may not use pre-existing maps for your submissions.
There will be three judges (Myself, LittleViking001, and KinGAleX). The cumulative total of the three judges' scores will be the participant's Total Score.

Each submission will be scored out of 50 points based on these catagories:

Fun, Difficulty, Creativity, Execution, Other.
  • (0-10) Fun: How fun is the map to play? Would a person who plays through the map do so again and again? Is the map enjoyable and exciting?
  • (0-10) Difficulty: Is the map too difficult, or too easy? Is the map a bowl of porridge? Are there any points in the map where the difficulty is too much for the idea of the map?
  • (0-10) Creativity: What sets your map apart from the others, and from the original? Why is this a memorable map? Did you do anything new here that people haven't seen before? What changes did you make to the original map that made the map unique?
  • (0-10) Execution: How well did you ReMix the map? Were the base elements of the original map still contained within?
  • (0-10) Other: This category is used for criterion that don't fit into any other category, but the judge feels needs to be mentioned.
Our Submission Deadline is going to be February 14th, 2007 - Valentine's Day. at 12:01 AM.

With all that said, good luck to all of our Neditors, and you may begin!
