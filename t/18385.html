Well, this pack was actually supposed to be my farewell. It was supposed to have more maps, too. Instead SUBLiME kept me here, and I regained my passion for mapmaking and my interest in the goings-on of this community. So, having realized I wasnt about to walk away from it all, I continued the pack with a different idea in mind. I just made maps not as a way of saying goodbye, but just for the sake of mapmaking.

Because I kept my focus for these maps away from NUMA (as I did with Monologue, this packs predecessor), I did not just make your average maps I made fantastic maps. Indeed, many maps in this pack are among the best Ive ever made. Just as with Monologue, I came away from this pack feeling like a far better mapmaker than I was when I started. Let me tell you, its a great feeling. And to think all I had to do was stop submitting my maps to NUMA and just enjoy mapmaking.

What else brought about such a change? I took my time. In fact, I started this pack in April or May. Aside from the odd NUMA map for the MNNM League or something, Ive spent the last 3 or 4 months on these 20 maps. Each map took me a good majority of the day to finish, mostly because I didnt stop playing and tweaking a map until I could all-gold it (Sometimes I didnt even stop there.). I made maybe 2 maps a week at the very most; some weeks I made no maps. It was taking a very long time by just about any standard, which is mainly why I cut back on the number of maps in the pack. Still, I give you a respectable 20 maps, each of which was crafted delicately and playtested to perfection. Ladies and gentlemen, Dialogue.
