#193122 28 2006-06-22T19:05:00Z Jiggerjaw
The demo is in the upper text box underneath the description of the map. This text box should be labelled "Map Data". Copy and paste that into the upper text box in debug mode (you get there by pressing '~'), click out of the text box (that step is very important), and press 'L'. This loads the map. Then, to record a demo, press '1', and press 'P' to play. Be sure to have caps lock on so you don't have it in frame by frame (necessary only for tough jumps and immense speed). To watch a demo, copy the demo data from the text box (on a map) or from an attached file (on the forums) and paste it into the lower text box in debug mode. Click out of that text box (again - very important), and press '3', followed by 'Q'. Wait for the map to reset (automatically happens by pressing 'Q'), and press 'P', and watch the demo. Then go boil some water and throw in a box of Ramen Noodles and have a good ol' time.
