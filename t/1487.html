I wrote this essay for english class. The assignment was to write a 250-word essay including a flashback. As you read it, keep in mind that I purposely made it very vague. You have to use a little imagination to fill in the gaps in the story. Here it is:

The man in the rocking chair had no future. After sitting and thinking for a long time, he came to this conclusion. It was not because of anything he had done in the past at least, not on purpose. But after endlessly thinking over what had brought him to this point, he realized that it was all because of one fateful day, many years ago.

It had been bright and cheery that morning, but he had been in no mood to perform his duties. He convinced himself that the classified units wouldnt come to any harm. After all, what could happen? He was only one of several dozen keeping watch over the units. If there was a break-in, which was quite unlikely to begin with, surely there would be some warning. With this thought in mind, he drifted off into a light slumber. Suddenly he awoke with a start, immediately realizing something was amiss. He ran through the halls, shouting for someone, anyone but the complex was deserted. After a few moments of trying to steady himself, he worked up the courage to check on the units. He had never actually seen them before; no one but the highest-ranking officials were allowed in the room. He found the door unlocked. He opened it.

As the man in the rocking chair recalled that day, which had forever altered both his life and the lives of countless others, he knew what he had to do. He got up, and slowly set to work. Soon the future he once had would be restored.

This isn't my usual writing style, I'm trying something new. Any feedback would be greatly appreciated. It's okay if you just hate it, I would still like to know.
