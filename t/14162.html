I know other (perhaps similar) advice has been given already, but I'm going to give my two cents, the way I see it.

Firstly, just be friendly to her. Say hi. If there's something you have in common, try and talk about it.

Secondly, screw holding back. Take her aside, and tell her you like her. That's all she needs to know. If she knows that, she may make particular effort to get to know you. If she doesn't seem to do anything about it, then hell, it doesn't matter, you don't even know her, so what if she knows you like her?

I disagree with what Obby is suggesting. It's similar to my approach, but I think the whole "you are really pretty" thing can backfire on you. Just tell her you like her. Compliments are good, but you can come across as kind of slimy if you're not careful.
