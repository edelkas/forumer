"Stories From the BK Lounge"

3 people enter a New Jersey Burger King at different times on the same rainy night. One is raped in the bathroom, one is beat up under a table because he didn't have any money to buy his drugs, and one attempts to commit suicide after finding out his girlfriend just had an abortion. He never knew she was pregnant.

At three in the morning, they all wake up dazed, and find themselves all walking out at the same time, each visibly shaken. They awkwardly avoid eachother, and walk out into the parking lot.
