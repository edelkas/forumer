#20890 1 2008-07-26T12:47:05Z Browzer
First, I love N+, which is why I want to express my feedback and hopefully get improvements in the next DLC pak. I beat the original game, and have beaten the DLC. Here are my thoughts on Map Pak #0:

WHAT I LIKED
----------------
- The humor. I actually looked forward to reading the titles for each level.
- The quantity. Good number of levels.
- Focus on coop levels. (The first and only time I played the competitive multiplayer levels was to get the achievements).

WHAT I DIDN'T LIKE
-----------------------
- The levels were way too simple. And I don't want people telling me, "Well, what do you want? Everyone complained the original was too hard!" Clearly, there is a difficulty spectrum, and the maps here fall far too close to the "easy" side. When players can simply walk or make a few simple jumps to the end of the level, that is just a waste of time.

The people who are buying this map pak probably beat Master Control (which isn't even in the Top 10 difficult levels, IMHO). I think the developers can assume a certain level of competence here. Please make the next map pak more challenging.

- The bugs. Why are these things still not fixed? The save system is still messed up. The "previous best" times are always blank. And most of all....the LAG.

- BROKEN coop level on "Life Support (for real this time)". Please, PLEASE play-test your levels online before releasing them. "Life Support" was unplayable on the original game, and it's unplayable now. The only way people are beating it online is with unconventional methods (which really aren't fun to rehearse OVER and OVER).

Original level: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A94EDQicPms

New Level: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7W--5EdykJo

SUMMARY
=========
I like the amount of content here, and the highlight is the coop levels (that work). But I hope the net map pak is more challenging and has some bugs fixed.
