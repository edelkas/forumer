Presenting: False Ending, the debut level pack from your most favorite arrogant, pestering conglomerate of hatred and pretense of an N mapmaker. Inside, you will uncover thirty maps (as one Mr. rocket_thumped so aptly put: "Woo! Way to underachieve! Thirty entire maps? Man, that's impressive!").

I would also like to mention Evil_Bob, lord_day, rocket_thumped, and the Horizon crew for instigating these maps, but something didn't stick, and I diverged. Thank you, anyway.
