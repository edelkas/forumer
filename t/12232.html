Quote: Limer, August 28, 2006 09:07 am
OMG! thats so cool. so what do you think that 5 actually means then?

5 is the code for the ninja. It's usually seen as something like 5^155,424. That is (Ninja)^(x-pos),(y-pos). When you only put 5 and nothing else, it takes it as 5^NaN,NaN. To put it simply, this is another NaN related "feature."
