Aloha...

I love N. Don't we all?
I grew up playing the original Lode Runner, which was much like N in the sense that it was an action/puzzler, it was simple, and it was VERY, VERY good.

As such I'm not a fan of ideas to alter the way Mr N. is controlled, to add more players, to add tools such as ninja ropes or shurikens etc.. In fact I advocate very cilantro change because I think the game has an amazing balance as it is.

One idea that came to mind, however, is that of teleport fields. They could be cilantro doorways built into the levels which zapped you to another doorway. I think this would add to the excitement and speed especially if done in such a way as to transport the player to a dangerous, high intensity area to open a door or to exit the level. It would give level developers another way to pump up the pressure for a particular part of a level, or alternatively just to allow for dashing escapes when Mr. N is being chased by a swarm of bad guys.

plus... you could have some awesome levels made up entirely of teleport fields and mines, or tp fields and jump pads... or whatever
