#270217 2 2006-12-14T23:51:03Z LittleViking
When a plural possessive ends in s, you don't add an extra s after the apostrophe. Example: The dogs' toys. The toys belong to more than one dog. When the possessive is not plural, an extra s is added. Example: The gas's volume. This includes proper nouns like James.
