So, here's how this whole thing started...
I was trying desperately to make a simple map. I started with these little nail looking things, staggered in such a way that I was happy with, and I started adding gold. then came the mines, the chaingun, you know the story...but my dad came in at the time of its (almost) completion and said, "What's this supposed to be, railroad spikes?" And so I named it "Driving the Last Spike", after a Genesis (my favorite band) song, and posted some of the lyrics to the song as my description. After doing this, I said, "I should make a series out of this." Out of this came my new series, the Musical Series, an idea which I don't believe has been done before...well, one could argue that Formica's "Tribute" is the same idea, but whatever. On some maps (like Follow You Follow Me), I decided on a song and built the map around that concept, and on other maps (like Once in a Lifetime), I started with an idea, and chose a song to sum up the concept used in the map. Many of these are simple, fun, and fast paced, and I enjoyed the hell out of making them. So, I'll update this post with links to each of the maps as I make them. Use this thread to tell me what you think of the maps! Hope you like playing them as much as I liked making them!
