#247895 5 2006-10-30T16:48:45Z wedgie
Haha, first of all, I like how only 2 of the levels you named were ones that didnt have a 0th by kry-x-orange, a known, or at least a highly suspected cheater.

From looking at those levels you posted, a lot of the jumps that i can see that you were referring to were all jumps that all play on the physics of the game. Lets take 71-1 for example:

I have noted some of the different types of jumps that johnny_faneca does on his 0th run:
  1. This corner 'push' is something commonly used by highscorers. If you get the ninja to touch the wall and the ceiling at the exact same time, so that is exactly in the corner, and you jump, then the jumps momentum pushes you downwards instead of up. Meaning you can get out of a corner faster.
  2. This jump gets you extra height because you are changing momentum as you jump. Basically what you have to do is run at the slope, run up the slope, then release any direction buttons that you are pressing and then press jump. This will make the ninja jump perpendicular to the surface you are running up.
  3. With 3, this jump gets extra height because you are jumping twice in just a few frames. You have to jump just a few frames before you get to the slope, then, just a few frames later when you touch the slope, you jump again. The 2 jumps being very close together is what gives you this 'combined' jump height if you will. This is the same technique used on a double bounce block jump.
  4. To get the height needed on this jump, you just have to hit the jump just right so you get that sweet spot of the jump. The way to do this is to hit the jump button at the exact frame that you touch the wall, also, if you are not holding any direction buttons then this usually helps.
  5. This last one is probably the one you were referring to most, but this jump works in the exact same way as jump 3. All that is happening is that you are jumping just before the slope, then jumping again as you touch the slope, giving you this combined jump height needed to get the gold.
To briefly go over the others:
71-4: This just uses the same 2 quick jumps going from a slope to a vertical wall.
92-2: As some of the others have already mentioned, the height on this is gained from a 'double jump' off a bounce block. If you land on a bounce block and then keep moving about while your momentum pushes it down (moving around makes it push the block down more than standing still), then when it reaches its lowest point before moving back upwards, tap jump twice and you will jump off the bounce block twice as it moves upwards, giving you the combined height of 2 jumps. You can experiment with the gap between the 2 jumps too for varied heights and from varied bounce block compression heights i.e. landing on a bounce block from a small height and still doing the double jump.
Finally, 31-1. The first jump on this level is what's known as a triple jump. This happens by jumping off the floor just before you reach the slope and then jumping off the slope a few frames later (just like in jumps 3 and 5 on 71-1) but then you jump off the wall too. So you are making three jumps in a very short space of time, equalling a higher jump that normal.

I hope this has been some help to you, I don't think i could have gone into any more detail lol. But as the others said, just practice. I've been playing this game for about 2 years now. Check my 96-0 score, do you think i could have done that when i started playing this 2 years ago? I think not.
