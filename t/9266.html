Let me clarify. The licence agreement allows N to be distributed in altered form without and kind of profit being made, including that from advertising on a website. It is legal to link to the Metanet homepage so that others can dowload it, or to host the UNALTERED zip file which may be downloaded from Metanet. It is NOT legal to host the .exe without the documentation, or to convert it to .swf format so that it can be played over the web. It may also be illegal to host on your site if you sell advertising.
