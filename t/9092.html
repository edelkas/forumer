#166466 5 2006-04-19T04:04:28Z Teedle
You can't reinstall N. You can just reinstall the .sol file, which stores all of N's data. Thus, deleting your .sol file will prompt N to create a new, blank one and- vola- you've effectively reinstalled it, or done the closest possible thing.

And you probably just screwed up the formatting of the userlevels file a bit, with a character or a space that isn't supposed to be there.

And this belongs in N Help.
