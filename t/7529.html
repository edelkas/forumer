Best Use of a Tileset Contest[/b]

This is a contest I have wanted to start for a while now. I know I do not have a high post count, I am not well known, ect., but I am respected at other websites(HighImpactHalo, for example). I hope that this contest is not overlooked because I am new, but hey, you take what you can get. Anyway, this is a contest that will test your creativity. I will supply you with a random tileset, and you make a map out of it. I am also looking for judges, so anyone who is willing to help out would be greatly appreciated. I will take only 1 map per person per tileset, so you have one chance until I start with a new one. Click here for the tileset.

Tileset made by schmidty563

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Judging Scores

Your map will get a total score out of fifty from each of the judges. The breakdown of the points looks like this:

20 points for creativity(Are the techniques I am using to make the map new?)

20 points for playability(If someone beat the map, would they want to play it again?)

10 points for map design(Is my map eye pleasing?)

I hope you have fun with this contest!

Judges

Currently, I(Phyre) and
Hydron will be judging. There are still two spots open, because the last to sign up for judging will be the backup.
