Again?!?
Yes, but this time is isn't going to be forgotten, this contest will be run similar to NTTS, you have 10 weeks, during each week you will have a set theme to make an image to. The theme may be userbars, the theme may be abstract, it could be anything.

So...
I will be doing all the judging with three helpers, who will be chosen by me, each image will be scored like so...
Relevance ?/5 This is how relevant the image is to the theme.
Effort ?/5 How much time and effort was put into this image.
Colour ?/5 How the colours work with each other, do they clash, or do they blend smoothly.
Style ?/5 How the content works with itself

Judges
-Chasethebase

Rules
  • You submit your images to Chasethebase using the forum's integrated Personal Message function. Please do not use CODE or QUOTE to separate the image data. Just copy & paste it!
  • You may enter ONE image per week, so perfection is necessary.
  • You cannot use previous works!
  • Once the image has been submitted, that is it, it cannot be returned or edited.
  • Each week will end at 7pm GMT on the Monday. Judging will commence and last until 4:30pm GMT on the Tuesday, when the next week will start!
  • Judges are allowed to enter images!!!
  • Also save your image under your username please!
At the end of each week the results will be posted and I will tally up the final scores.

Best: 5P
1st Runner-up: 4P
2nd Runner-up: 3P
3rd Runner-up: 2P
4th Runner-up: 1P
