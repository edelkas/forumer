#2204 1 2005-03-14T00:46:26Z Daddaluma
They are both awesome Strategy RPGs.

I would very highly recomend both games, with the slight edge going to FFT. Although, Disgaea will be easier to get into and is probably more engrossing in the short term.

And as I mentioned in the Game Naming Game thread, Disgaea is the ultimate game for munchkins. It's possible to get all the best equipment and the highest stats before you even begin the storyline. It's probably even faster to just skip the storybattles if you're just going for as much power as possible in the quickest time possible.

I have 2 copies of Disgaea now. I couldn't suppress the urge to play it again, and now I'm in Japan with a Japanese PS2, so I had to pick up the game for a second time. It's so worth it.

As for FFT, I'm having a great time playing it on an emulator on my computer. It's a tad slow in this day and age, so the ability to ramp up the framerate and to save states is priceless. Also, playing it on a computer gives you easy access to your memory cards and savegames, so you can cheat like crazy, or try all those cool things that you wanted to before but couldn't (like play through the game with just Ramza as an Assassin). You can also skip all the boring parts (like doing random battles to gain experience and JP).

So there you go. Play them both. They'll both offer you literally hundreds of hours of gameplay (and if you're insane, possibly thousands of hours).
