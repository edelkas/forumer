#299802 17 2007-02-22T04:56:55Z Hendor
To get the renders from Planet Renders, become a member (takes 30 seconds). Then, find a render you like when browsing the gallery. Click on it, and it should pop up in a new window in the full resolution (it won't do that if you're not a member.) Then right-click and Save As somewhere one your computer. Make sure you save it as a .PNG. Then you can open it in Photoshop or GIMP or whatever you want. Just put it on top of your image and resize it however you want.

Hope that helps!
