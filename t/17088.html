"I wonder what it will look like?"
"I wonder what it will look like."

From what I've known, the second one has always been the grammatically correct term for questioning statements like these, but over the past few years I've been increasingly seeing them ending in question marks instead What's the rule on this?
#315948 2 2007-04-26T05:55:29Z blue_tetris
No question mark. That's the grammatically correct way. The sentence is a statement. You are telling someone what you are wondering. "I wonder about the future of America." isn't a question. it demands no answer. In the same token, what you posited is a statement. It tells a fact. It doesn't ask for an answer.

Pronounce the sentence as you would if you were asking a question. The rise in intonation at the end should also contradict the phrasing of that statement. Even though the sentence itself seems to stoke conversation, in general, it's no query.
