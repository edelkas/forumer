The gold that you collect over the episode adds up and ties over to the next level. So you probably didn't get much gold in the rest of the episode and ended up finishing 9-3 with 0.247 seconds on the clock. You will have to go back and complete the whole episode again, making sure that you collect enough gold to beat the later levels of the episode.
