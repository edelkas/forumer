Troll riddle:
Row outwards in a spiral so that the troll, running to stay on the same side of the shore as you, is opposite of you through the center of the lake when you're 1/4 of the way out from the center. Then beeline to the shore.
You need to move (3/4)r before the troll runs (pi)r.
d = r * t
(3/4)r = x * t --> time it takes to get to the shore is 3(r/4x)
(pi)r = 4x * t --> time it takes to get to you before you get to the shore is (pi)(r/4x).
If you stick those in an inequality: (pi)(r/4x) > 3(r/4x)
The troll takes longer to get to the shore than you do; you can escape.

Now turn your hand clockwise - you can easily turn it 180 degrees. Basically, with your right hand, it's easier to rotate a key clockwise than it is to turn it counter-clockwise.
