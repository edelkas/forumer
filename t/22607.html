Well, I was pretty sick of the word mini, and the letter n, but I've finished it. My minimalism map pack. This is quite possibly my best works so far, taking advantage of my map-making strengths. Each one of these maps has a certain theme or trick, but what they all have in common is a very high difficulty level. If anybody can get so far as 18, I will be impressed (particularly as two of the maps are not humanely possible, unless you want to spend the next 15 years of your life in front of a computer screen). All maps can be commented on NUMA at this section of NUMA, while an up to date download is available here Ratings have been disabled for most of them, so if you particularly enjoyed on or two, saying so on this thread will be the easiest way. And above all, see it as a challenge, hit by a shrink ray! Oh, and I'd like to thank condog for playtesting extraction distraction, and turtle for inspiring yurtle the turtle:the cause of all this misery. And to answer your question, I did not intend claustrophobicism to be suggesting phallic imagery, that was purely the flow and shape of the map. I am extending the series now, so far it's at 34 last count.
