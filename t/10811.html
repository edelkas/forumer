DESCRIPTION
This is a search for the best DDAs that use no launchpads. There will be four categories: trap door, bounce block, normal door, thwump, medley, and glitch. Here is an example of a normal door map.

RULES
  • All maps must be submitted to NUMA and posted here, with a link to the map.
  • Maps will be accepted until there are 3 (three) entries in each category, in which case entries will be allowed until 24 (twenty-four) hours after the 3rd (third) entry in the last category to have 3 (three) entries.
  • Each mapmaker may only enter 1 (one) map in each category. The mapmaker may change that map up until the final acceptance date (24 (twenty-four) hours after the submission of the 3rd (third) map in the last category to have 3 (three) entries).
  • Each map must only use the propulsion for the category it is enterd in (trap door, bounce block, thwump, or normal door). Maps entered in the medley category must use all 4 (four) kinds of propulsion listed above at least once, but do not have to use glitch propulsion. Maps in the Glitch category must use only glitches for propulsion.
  • A judge may submit maps for the comp, but must not vote for their own map (see judging, below)

SCORING
Maps will be scored by the following criteria (in order of importance, starting with the most important):
  • Use of propulsion
  • Close calls
  • Awsomeness

JUDGING
This comp will be judged differently. Each judge will pick the map they think is the best in each category and the map they think is the worst in each category. The judges will have up to 48 (forty-eight) hours after the entry of the last map in that category to PM me (bufar) their votes. A judge may not vote for their map as the best map or the worst map. The map that is voted the best by the most judges will win in that category. A vote for worst map cancels out a vote for best map. In case of a tie for first place, I will ask all judges to re-vote for one of the maps that is tied for first place. The map recieves the most votes in the re-vote will be declared the winner.
