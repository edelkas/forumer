The Basics
Every week, I will give everybody an image to edit. They may do anything they want to it, the only goal is to make it look good or humorous, whichever they think will capture the voters. The voters will be the forumers. At the end of each week (Friday), there will be a poll with all of the entries that week, and it will run throughout the weekend. After 10 weeks, the person with the most points will be declared Metanet Photo Manipulation Champion.

The Rules
  • You can do anything you want with it, like add renders, brushes, and so on.
  • You can only submit 1 entry per round.
  • Entries can not be pornographic in any manner. There are some exceptions.
  • The entry you submit must include this round's image in it.
  • Entries can be of any size.
  • You can PM me your entries, or post them here, whichever way you feel safer.
  • Since a poll can only have up to 10 choices, only the first 10 people to submit their entries will qualify for that round.
  • If less than 3 people enter in a round, I will extend the round 2 more days. If still less than 3 members enter, then the round will be skipped and a new image will be brought in.
  • If your entry receives no votes, you will not get any points for that round.
Judging
The judging system works like this, 1st being most votes -
1st - 5 points
2nd - 4 points
3rd - 3 points
4th - 2 points
5th - 1 point
Everybody else - try harder

In case of a tie for first, a 24 hour poll will be put up. The entry with the most votes will win.
The prize will be an exclusive signature. The second and third place finishers will also recieve an exclusive sig-tag. Everybody else will recieve a sig-tag.
