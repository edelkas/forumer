#127567 4 2005-12-28T07:28:24Z Jiggerjaw
And to add items to the userlevels.txt file, found in the folder that N is in, use this format:
$Map Name#Author#Category#Map Data#
Just place that in there, then go into userlevels and press 'refresh list'.
Then you're on your way.
