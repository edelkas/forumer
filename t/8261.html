Anyone playing N with a slow computer and feeling frustrated?

I play N with a slow computer.... N lags seriously especially on tense moments, maybe there are too many objects to test-hit. Skipping a few frames per few seconds happened a lot to me.

With a slow computer, I can hardly do any precise moves. Fortunately in most levels, there are always a safe (but slow) route for you to go. So my approach to N, is to find the route that minimize the skill required

With this in mind I'm able to pass most of the episode.. and I have around 20 episodes left to go.

Highscores? Never think about it. But finally one day when I reached 97-1, I found that it is one of the level that requires extremely few amounts of precise direction changing.... (on a slow computer you aren't likely to able to change your direction at the exact frame you want), so I go for it and luckily enough to get a 9th highscore.

And then I found that most of the scores in 9th column are "under-scored". Look at 97-3, many of the 97-3 highscores haven't cleared all gold...
(maybe this is due to some of the N players quit N before reaching the bottom half of the 9th column)

I love most part of N, but I have something to whine about.
1. I would like to have an option to switch RagDoll off..... the ragdoll made a lag computer more laggy, the ragdoll seems to consume your computer resources every times you die, and when you have been killed more, your computer became more laggy, and more lag leads to more dead.

2. Anyone find the bouncing box annoying?

Firstly, to jump at the desired height, you need to jump at the correct frame after landing on the bouncing box, which is very hard to do on a frame-skipping computer.

Secondly, the bouncing box is quite "inPhysics. Say, to suit the law of physics, "when the bouncing box is moving upward, the Ninja standing on it should always jump higher". But the result is "no, not always, sometimes you will jump much much lower when the bouncing box is bouncing you upwards", and I call this "inPhysics".
Another example, when you land on the bouncing box, then at the moment before it bounces back upward (i.e. the moment which the bouncing box is stationary), the ninja should able to jump at a normal height. But again the result is "no".... and I think that this is "inPhysics" too!

Although this is just a game, realistic is not required, and no one ever officially stated that the bouncing box suits the law of Physics, but I still feel quite annoying when the bouncing box contradict to my basic instinct.
