Bontago is a strategy game where you build towers using blocks. You get item so you can blow the other peoples towers down. The higher the tower, the more space it controls. You can get items such as rockets, fans, volcanos, bombs, earthquakes, jumping blocks, and even anvils. Be careful about what you throw, they could destroy your tower if you didn't build it well enough. The physics are pretty nice but are rarely bad. Try it out for yourself! You can also play this networked with friends or siblings!
