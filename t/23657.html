Looking around on Numa, Ive found that a lot of people have had avatars of their ninjas doing interesting things (such as The Kitchen Sink, Pepi on here [who wont know me [ts:smiley=2] ] and Radial_Intergers and Radium Falcon) I thought that these were really cool, and recently Ive made my own avatar based on this concept.

Then i had an idea for this contest-per-se, so here's the idea.

> People take a screenshot from an n level (this could be existing or made solely for this purpose)
> They try and get the ninja doing the most interesting thing possible.
> They send the pic here, and after judging, the best pic here wins a ded from the judges.

I thought that "N-Art" was a bit of a misnomer, after all in most cases its just spamming masses of objects into crude shapes (at least when compared to regular art, admittedly, the Thwump Jaguar is an exception) Hopefully, this contest can help redefine it into something funner, and requiring less button clicking

Rules

> The screenshot can be as big as this site permits, or small enough to form an avatar, your choice.
> Spamming one enemy over and over again is not allowed, this shouldnt look anything like a conventional N-art, but look more like an actual playable level.
> Any objects and tiles can be used in the screenshot. However, colour and background mods are not allowed, otherwise people would just post insanely garish images. Any modded objects (ie alarm drones, rotating laser drones are allowed.
> Only one screenshot per post, and only 10 are allowed from the same poster throughout, otherwise it would be spamming (this may be repealed depending on circumstances)
> Each N-art must have a title, and most likely youll want to add a caption to add it it. try to make it short and sweet tho, we dont want massive reams of text overshadowing the picture

The N-arts wont be judged for a while, just fill this thread with any (good ) screenshots for now. its not really meant to be a formal contest with deadlines, just an opportunity for you guys to show off your creativity.
