Rules
These are the rules for adding a map to 'The Epic'...
  • Each map must play right- to- left, with the ninja starting on the far right, and the exit being on the far left.
  • Each map MUST contain, on the far right, the two columns that were on the far- left of the previous map. Thus, the series will play like a single, very long level.
  • Every level must bear some resemblance to something real- ish. No pretty patterns, or metanet style levels. Try to incorporate gravitiy in most, if not all, of the level design.
  • All level descriptions must link back to this thread.
  • You are not allowed to create 2 consecutive 'epic' maps.
  • The description must link back to this thread, and you must link to the map in this thread. The description should always involve- usually further- the vague plot of the thing. Nothing gory or too serious.
  • Nothing too insanely hard.
  • If I judge a level 'not worthy' of The Epic, it won't be included in the semi- official list. But this will only apply to levels that broke any of the above rules, or just suck to a Yanu- like degree.
Hey guys- just thought I'd fill you all in on The Epic. You should get the hang of it soon.... join, all willing people! This should end up great. I hope. Please join in.

New rules
(these rules won't affect existing levels, but will affect new ones.)
  • No more 'inside a building' type levels; the idea of The Epic is realistic landscapes, and the enterior of a bulding- type levels never seem to fit that. A cityscape or something might be allright, but no office blocks.
  • You must post your map within 5 days of it becoming your turn. If not, it'll go to the next person.
Please DO TRY to follow the rules- they've been bent and outright broken a lot lately, and, well, that's allright, but they ARE there for a reason. And I'd really appreciate it.

Oh- and maps in This general style are exactly what I'm after...
