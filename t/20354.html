Well, it's very simple. Person A posts a word or phrase, Person B defines Person A's word/phrase and posts one for Person C to define, etc.

Confused? Example:
Person A - Mouse
Person B - 1) small rodent
2) device for manipulating the cursor on a computer screen
eat
Person C - The intake of food
........



Rules:
1) DON'T copy a dictionary definition. Looking at dictionaries are fine.
2) Be civilized. This isn't Bash & Trash, so behave yourself in the choice and definitions of words.
3) Only one word/phrase per post allowed. If you really want to post more words, wait for someone to define yours first.
4) Choose existing words. Don't make up your own.
5) Avoid dull, easy-to-define words like in the example

2. (used to mark a proper noun, natural phenomenon, ship, building, time, point of the compass, branch of endeavor, or field of study as something well-known or unique): the sun; the Alps; the Queen Elizabeth; the past; the West.
3. (used with or as part of a title): the Duke of Wellington; the Reverend John Smith.
4. (used to mark a noun as indicating the best-known, most approved, most important, most satisfying, etc.): the skiing center of the U.S.; If you're going to work hard, now is the time.
5. (used to mark a noun as being used generically): The dog is a quadruped.
6. (used in place of a possessive pronoun, to note a part of the body or a personal belonging): He won't be able to play football until the leg mends.
7. (used before adjectives that are used substantively, to note an individual, a class or number of individuals, or an abstract idea): to visit the sick; from the sublime to the ridiculous.
8. (used before a modifying adjective to specify or limit its modifying effect): He took the wrong road and drove miles out of his way.
9. (used to indicate one particular decade of a lifetime or of a century): the sixties; the gay nineties.
10. (one of many of a class or type, as of a manufactured item, as opposed to an individual one): Did you listen to the radio last night?
11. enough: He saved until he had the money for a new car. She didn't have the courage to leave.
