If you take a look at my map, "Mountains come out of the Sky..." and the title goes on, you'll see that I tried to incorporate "beating the laser drone". Basically, you have to move faster than the laser in order to survive. To my dismay, however, all you have to do is go even slower in order to avoid death. In order for it to have worked, there would have to be some way for the laser drone to make it so that you could not win. Like a trap or locked door only pressable by a drone. This could open all kinds of new possibilities in the world of traps. At the end of "Mountains come out...", you notice that if you go fast enough, the laser will end up trapped, but if you go too slow, the drone will end up in the narrow shaft on the left. This would be good without the magic of really fast wall jumping, as the drone would then be impassible, but you can just jump past the thing before it fired. However, if there were a drone-only trap door switch in that shaft that blocked you off from finishing the level, the intensity of the "beat the laser drone" technique is heightened considerably. I really like this idea, however, I just don't know how hard it would be for Mare & Raigan to add this in to the programming for Nv1.5. Please tell me what you think of the idea, and tell me if you think it would be easy or hard to incorporate it into the game.

Thanks.
