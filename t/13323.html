#244070 3 2006-10-21T18:16:55Z Ngaged
1. How to Play 'em.

It's really easy. Just reset your map (press R) and then press P to Play it. Be sure that caps lock is on.

2. Userlevels.

This might be a bit more complicated.

In your N folder that you got when you downloaded N, you should have something called Userlevels.txt. That is the file where you can put custom-made levels in there and play them almost as if you were playing them in the real game. To put your own levels in there, you must put them in in a specific format:

$Author#Name of level#Type of Level#Level data#

Fill in those spaces, and just put that all in into your Userlevels.txt.

Hope I helped! If you need any more help, feel free to ask.
