Disclaimer: For the sake of this argument i will be assuming that the 20-billion-year heartbeat theory is correct; that every big bang is followed by a big crunch and vica versa.



At the end of a universe, after the big crunch, all the energy in the universe is compressed into one point. Because it is so compressed it is not unreasonalbe to suggest that the energy could take the same form as it did in the previous life/s of the universe; that after each compression the energy ends up in the same position. If this is so then we could also presume that the energy would expand in exactly the same way in every one of its infinite expansions, and that the universe is therefore on a loop.

Which brings me to my main point; if the universe is indeed on a loop, as i have hypothesised above, then the life each of us leads has happened before an infinite number of times, and will continue to happen for an infinite amout of time.

So basically our lives last for an infinite amount of time, since they are repeated exactly for all of eternity, which makes us immortal.


Of course, all this depends on a number of unprovable assumptions and physics that i have no idea about, but it is still nice to think that every beautiful sunset, and every wonderfull moment actually lasts for eternity, rather than the fleeting portion of your life that it seems to.


And with that i bid you have a pleasant argument.
