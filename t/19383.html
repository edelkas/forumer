Map Rater\'s Organization

Tired of your maps being consistently ignored on NUMA? Of having it kicked off the Newest page in less than 10 minutes? Of getting little or no feedback on your maps? The MRO is here for you!

The MRO is a non-profit organization that ensures that your maps get rated, and more importantly, that you get comments and feedback on your maps. To be a part of it, you need to:
  1. Join a team. There are 3 teams. More will be added if there is enough interest.
  2. Make 1 map and submit it to NUMA. limit ONE (1) map per team. You can join up to TWO (2) teams.
  3. Post the link in this thread. IMPORTANT!! If you are in Team Blue, post your link in BLUE. If you are in Team Green, your link must be GREEN. If you are in Team Purple, then yours must be in...can you guess? (PURPLE) This is to avoid confusion.
  4. Vote AND comment on all your team members\' maps by the end of the week. If you feel that you cannot do this, then please DO NOT sign up for this. Failure to vote/comment may result in suspension.
  5. Repeat for the next week.
