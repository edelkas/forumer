Aside from the triple force field bug and the minor bugs from N1.4 involving 1-ways, the physics is flawless and smooth, as you would expect. And the new additions to the drone army create even more possibilites in map-making. (btw, are the 2 new drones even in the campaign at all?)

5+/5
