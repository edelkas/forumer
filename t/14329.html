This map pack was swift (made in just 24 hours) and I think it breaks many of my normal conventions. It's fun at times, super simple at times, kinda tricky at times, and quite a few of the maps are puzzles. In fact, I was initially going to make 50 tiny puzzle maps. Instead, though, I made this.

A lot of the challenges require near frame-perfection and non-linear thinking. Most, however, involve screwing around and having fun. See how quickly you can beat the whole pack!!!

Enjoy.
