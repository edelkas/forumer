I play N on and off. One thing that pushes it from 'fun and challenging' into 'frustration' is having to do a finicky part of a level over and over and over again, only to be killed by something semi-random in a second/third/etc part of it.

I keep on wishing N's designers had played "The Killing Game Show" (aka "Fatal Rewind") and stolen its Unique Feature. Like N, the level completely reset when you died. Like N, it had a 'replay' function. But here's the big difference: KGS would automatically go into the replay when you died, and at any time you could start moving the joystick to take over. Or you could not touch anything and just watch yourself die in exactly the same way, if you liked.

Once you did that tricky jumping bit in the middle of a level, though, it was DONE, unless you decided to do it again.

It'd save me a hell of a lot of swearing!
