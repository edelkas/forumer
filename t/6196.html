#122630 12 2005-12-13T18:40:49Z Sammage
There were some strange ones in the highscores nominations, but highscores this past year have gone CRAZY. At the beginning of the year, we had Unsane's 33-0 and pootify's 56-3 as the best runs around, and a bunch of runs from Afterthought that were sloppy but had so many decent innovations they'd still end up half a dozen seconds ahead of everyone else.

Then along came kryx, trib4l and seifer both became amazing, toad, and glupi and the highscores started to get locked down. Then JF started going for scores and with just a few simple innovations on most levels (double jumps on bounceblocks and JUMPING before going down ledges were his two big ones) started taking highscores, to the point where he could now probably improve just about any highscore around in a few minutes. Melchior and blobglob have added a bunch of extra tricks to speed up runs. Clux just came completely out of nowhere and on levels that people had been working on perfecting forever, just blew these scores out the water with innovations. And Spect, I'm sure the guy is a robot, his recent scores have been so precise it's insane.

So from where we were before, not knowing many tricks and not seeing many highscores really worked on to perfection, to today where we have half a dozen maniacs, each with a giant list of tricks and innovations at their fingertips. Trying to choose the best couple of highscores out of these is hard because by the time you've finished saying 'I like blahblah-blah' there are four new highscores up there. Very few of the innovating highscores are still around today, and a casual audience may not get why they're so brilliant.

The nomination list is weird because it's made up of some older highscores that people might have voted for at the beginning of the year, and some of the newer highscores that lose context without knowing the older route, and a couple of newer highscores that aren't that impressive once you've seen x y and z done before.

IMO, there are three categories of jaw dropping highscores:
Innovative highscores: Scores that employ a new trick, or a trick that no-one has ever thought of using in that way
Examples: Melchior's 0-4
Pootify's 56-3
Afterthought's anything ever. (in particular his 76-2)
Clux's anything ever. (30-2 is definitely one of his best)

Technically difficult highscores: These are the scores which don't necessarily add a major manouever, but are still so much faster than their counterparts on skill alone.
Examples: Blobglob's 2-4 (also an innovation for the crazy use of walls)
My 83-3 *duck*
Unsane's 33-0
Trib4l's 58-4 (great example of taking an innovation and perfecting it)
Melchior's 11-0 (the original corner jump, but the current highscore is just very slick)

Amusing highscores: These ones are the ones where something unexpected happens. Hilarity ensues.
Examples: Spect's 11-4
JF's 59-1 (may have changed now?), my 62-1

There probably aren't enough amusing highscores that last to make an entire category for them, but there are plenty of innovative or technically insane highscores.
