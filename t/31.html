You've got levels which are similar to mazes with the added bonus of enemies and switches. You don't have any means of killing the enemies so they really are just obsticles to be avoided or bypassed. You have to open the exit, clear the path to it and all of this within a given timelimit (the amount of gold you have).

The pause prior to playing the level is there to give you a chance to orient yourself and determine the best plan of action.

Sure you're character jumps around and clings to walls and in that sense the game is an action game but you'll see in later levels, (specifically ones with one-way walls) that the choices you make often determine whether you can complete the level.

Either way I think you'll agree that it takes more brains to play than Mario.
