As I run down the forest path, I scoop up mushrooms from the undergrowth and eat them to gain more Action Points and Mana. I generally evade low level monsters and make good time back into town. When I arrive, I find that almost everyone in the town has been turned into zombies. "My extra pants must have saved me from the brain rot," I say to myself, "Now, if only I had a towel. If I really had it together, I'd know where my towel is."

Resolving to find a towel, I head toward the nearest Water-Sports Recreation Facility or WSRF for short. Having discovered the shortcut, I promptly appear within the locker room of the seemingly abandoned edifice. I search the lockers but find no towels. I do, however, add a pair of dirty socks, three pieces of steroid-laced chewing gum and an Orcish Battle Axe. I use a piece of gum with the socks and they become powerfully dirty socks. I gain 3.14 xp.

I enter sneaking mode (which basically involves crouching down and saying "Shhh!" every so often) in order to explore the rest of the building. In one room, I find a variety of wind-surfing equipment piled against the wall. Actually, it's not so much piled against as creating the wall. That's a lot of sails, is basically my impression.

Suddenly, the wall explodes and from the flaming wreckage emerges Brian Dickenson, Zombie Dude of the Waves. Hanging from his dead but still taught forearms are all the towels that a normal WSRF can be expected to contain. "Never steal my wind!" he incantes as the pile of rubble behind him is picked up and hurled at me by a tornado of magical rage.

For a moment, all I can think about is how much I would like to have one of those over-sized swords from Final Fantasy. But then I remember that I have something much better: Powerfully Dirty Socks. I pull the socks from my backpack and hurl them at Brian. They are sucked into the gale and disappear. I wait for a minute, you know, hoping that something happens. Maybe it's a time delay. Maybe I'm catching a bit of lag. Eventually, though, I have to face the fact that the damned socks didn't do anything and that I'm right back at square one. Square one minus socks, that is.

Suddenly, I have a flashback to that time in my childhood when that chick I was strangely intimate with despite only having a pre-teen's emotional abilities told me that, if I ever came across a situation like this, I should just believe in myself and she would show me the way. While I'm in the semi-comatose state of having a flashback, I'm picked up by the winds and tossed, feet-first, into the head of my nemesis. Awakened by my impact onto the towels that he left behind as he dissolved into a pile of dust, I decide that zoning out instead of making important decisions is probably a pretty good idea. I gain 172 xp and more towels than I could possibly need.
