#138465 9 2006-02-02T01:39:12Z Atilla
Yes, that's because of the way the collision detection works. You can go a little way inside a wall or object if you hit it while moving. You can go far enough to hit things on the other side if you're moving quickly.
#138470 10 2006-02-02T01:48:22Z Maniac
Also, trapdoors (and one-way-walls for that matter) have about 0 width as far as the collision detection system is concerned. That is, when you wallslide on the left side of a trapdoor wall, you're wallsliding on exactly the same location as you would be if you did the right side. Which makes them easy to penetrate in this way.

My suggestion: just don't run into the zap drone. The trapdoor is metal, you know
