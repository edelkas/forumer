This is all preparation for the actual game. Everyone is allowed in, but make sure you will have time to participate in this. It will still take a while to put together, so it may not begin until summer. But to get things set up, here is the first assignment...

- You are an eager politician from Earth, wanting to get your hands on land in order to start your own government.

First Assignment Land Grab![/b]
- Using pictures, highlight which area of Colossi you would like to receive. The limit is one country.
- To support your claims, you must write a one paragraph essay explaining why you would like to receive what you are asking for. This is a formal letter to the founder, Jacob Colossi, and he will take into account spelling, grammar (it doesnt have to be perfect), and most importantly, valid reasons to support your argument.
- If someone else has already asked for something you want, go ahead and cut up the country to your personal desires (you still have to leave some portion to the other person). Remember, you are competing for territory! If you choose this path, your essay will be graded more severely, seeing how you are taking away someone elses land! Another reason to make sure your essay is well thought-out: after the other player has made a counter-argument (see point below), you do not get a chance for rebuttal!
- If you have chosen territory, and someone else has submitted a request to take that land away from you (see the previous point), you may write one counter-argument to explain why that person is being unfair or idiotic, and why you want your land back. If you have a valid, decent, and supported basis, the country may be divided more equally among the two, but the final decision will be made by Colossi and his fellow delegates.
- All of this is important! Even if you do not get what you want, save your anger. There will be time in the game to settle these disputes via some kind of treaty between the two countries, or even open battle, with the victor deciding the outcome of the borders (more will be explained later).
- After the deadline has passed, a final map will be drawn out to determine the final countries and their borders.


Map of Colossi. Countries are numbered.

Secondary Rules:
- So, if you notice, things can get crowded. If you want some land in a certain country, but it is full, please consider going elsewhere.
- Don't be too greedy.
- The total limit of players is 20-30 (pending)

Some Advice...
- Choose realistic names for countries and people. Try out a name website or something to come up with unique yet stylish names.
- Think about what government you set up. This will have a major affect on later game portions. Just as an example, if you set up a democracy, your country will be eligible to elect new leaders every now and then, and that means you will not be able to play as leader unless you are re-elected! Have caution. This will all be explained later.
- - - - - - - -

So, if there are any questions, PM me. Post your 'letters' and and pictures depicting your desired land here. Good luck and have fun. Deadline is ONE MONTH.
