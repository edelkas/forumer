#128675 7 2005-12-31T07:25:59Z Jiggerjaw
I have the current (as of yesterday) top 1000 maps in my userlevels.
I don't know if there's a limit, but there is a limit to one's patience when waiting for the damn thing to load. If you have more levels in your userlevels.txt file than in your N game, click File -> Find or Ctrl+F (windows) and search for '&'. Barabajagal's map's Tumble & Tipsy and The New New Testament have always given me trouble. Just find the & and delete all of that junk (like &amp= or something) and refresh list. I hope that works for you.
