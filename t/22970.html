My opinion is that marijuana usage - both medical and recreational - should not be restricted by the government, and that there is nothing immoral about it.

Medical usage is the easiest case to make. Cannabinoids (the active ingredients in marijuana) have many useful and beneficial properties, especially for treating nausea or insomnia. Studies have shown that marijuana can help treat the symptoms of Cancer, AIDS, MS, Muscular dystrophy, OCD, Tourette Syndrome, and certain types of epilepsy (I can dig up the sources if you guys want). The fact that the government often prevents people from obtaining this beneficial medicine is appalling.

Recreational use: I've... heard... that it's enjoyable. It also doesn't cause physical addiction, it is impossible to overdose, and all major risks come only with chronic use. As long as a person uses marijuana in moderation, there's absolutely nothing wrong with it.
