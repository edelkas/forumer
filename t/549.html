#24456 2 2004-07-09T00:36:41Z Oneiromancer
The Submit button is right above the list of your personal scores at the bottom center of the highschore screen (between the score lists and the episode selector at the bottom). If there are no scores recorded for you for an episode, the button will not show up.

It does happen for me quite often that the game won't "sync" with the server if I just start playing when I open up N. And even when I go to the highscores screen it takes forever to download scores. I found that it goes a LOT faster if I go to the Configure screen from the main menu, let it sign me in, then go to the highscores screen. Works like a charm.
