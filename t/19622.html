Most engraver programs can output MIDI (.MID) files. If you can export into MIDI, you could download the trial version of Fruityloops Studio (unlimited use, limited features) or something similar and export it into MP3 with basic synthesizer sounds. But exporting only into MIDI would allow you to hear it play in Windows Media Player, even if it wouldn't sound top-quality.
