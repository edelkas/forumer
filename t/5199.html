DISCLAIMER: This topic will have nothing to do with actual piracy, or the act of pirating games, movies, or any media. It will only have to do with the ethics and economical effects of piracy. You have been warned.

Most people don't give two cents about the piracy issue, yet sometimes people overlook certain details. Piracy doesn't always lose money, and here is my case. I have pirated time and time again, though I cause no harm to the economy of the gaming industry, music industry, or movie industry. Why? Because 99.99999% of the time, I cannot buy that product anyway. I'm stuck out of buying anything online, and I have almost no budget to buy anything with. I'd never steal, as that takes away a physical object from another person, leaving them no choice but to not buy it, while piracy is making a copy that wouldn't make a difference if it existed or not. Now, if a company was to make no gain or loss from my act of piracy, aside from the gain of a user/fan, is it okay?
