#48625 5 2005-01-30T03:53:59Z Tsukatu
Or, the easy way:
As that first thwump moves back, jump on it and move with it. As that second one is moving back, jump onto it, and barely high enough to trigger the third one, and stand and move along the top of the second one. When the third one starts moving back, jump on top of the third thwump, getting enough air to trigger the fourth... etc, etc.
