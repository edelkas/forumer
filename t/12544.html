Map Mongers' Maps - If this isn't updating for you, it may be cached, and you'll have to alt-F5 it.


This is not a contest, a competition, a compilation, or a conflageration. There is no judging, boasting, rating, whining, or jousting. Just make a flippin' map if you want to.

If you'd like to be a map mongering mongler and join our merry menagerie of map making, just follow the rules.

The rules are straight forward, but I'll change them from day to day, or just from time to time. Submit a link to your map (make sure it's titled) in this thread to have it incorporated. It's a bit more than some sort of vent map or ten minute map, or maybe a bit less. It's merely a good way to make some maps in a communal way, a most satisfying way.

If you've got an idea for the framework for a map that people could make, PM me, LouDog004.
