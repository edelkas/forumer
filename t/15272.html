An eclectic mix of twenty-five exotic herbs, spices, and dancers.

Foreword:
First off, I'm not an exceptional mapmaker, and this isn't an exceptional mappack. That said, I put plenty of
effort into this and by my standards it turned out well.

I'm glad I've made this pack. Firstly, it's liberating to just make a map, set it aside, and move onto the next. It's also liberating to not be at all concerned about how it will be accepted on NUMA, being considered as a single, stand-alone product. Finally, I do think my mapmaking ability has improved. There some maps in here that I think are very nice, and I'd like to keep making maps of that quality in the future.

Anyway, that's the foreword. Here are the levels. By the way, most of them are quite (very?) easy. Go for all-gold.

My rating: / (4/5).

It's a very different pack, as the name implies. These are above your average maps in my opinion.

But there's something wrong. The first that comes to my mind is object placing.

The gold is not "naturally" placed, by which I mean that the gold is just scattered around, and it's hard to find a route for getting all them. Most of the time, I could just get two/three at a time and had to return back. And some of the gold are very easy to miss (all-golding 2-0 is pain ).

Don't get me wrong, though. This definetely wasn't a bad level pack. It's a very good one, to be honest. Enjoyable, challenging and original in many ways. It might just have needed some more fine-tuning in some levels.
