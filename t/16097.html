#305683 8 2007-03-16T21:28:42Z usaswim
Yes, I hated that level to death but it wasn't my least favorite. It wasn't hard after I did it a few times. So, you can get up. Good. Now all you need to do is go down. You wait until all the drones are at the bottom, and then climb onto the right wall. Make the drone chase you. After a while hanging there, jump to the left wall and make the drone notice you. You should be about 1/2 down by now. There is probably one drone left. Keep next the the wall (don't hang onto it!) that drone is on, and jump off when you are close to the drone. By now, the other two drones have probably turned back, so you go must go to the bottom. Grab hold of the wall at the very bottom right so you won't die. On the other side, collect ALL of the gold. If you go halfway, the gauss will kill you as you leap off. The rocket isn't too hard to dodge.
