I found a couple of fun freeware games the other day, so I thought I'd share them.

N-Ball is an odd little physics-based platformer, not a noobish "sport" map. Well, actually, it's probably both, but I'm discussing the former. Basically, you have to guide a bouncing ball to the end of a level filled with various physics-orientated (including ragdoll) objects, preferably as quickly as possible. There are no enemies - only holes, and red platforms which must be avoided. Graphics are simple, but effective. Watch it spawn a thousand suggestions along the lines of "We should have physics object X in N" . As for the name, I have no idea whether it's coincidental or not. The game IS a little similar to N...

Pogo Sticker is another game where you must guide a bouncy thing to the goal as quickly as possible, this time a vaugely humanoid one. It uses only the mouse - your character leans in the direction of the pointer, and the direction they bouce (and, to a lesser extent, their movement in midair) is determined by the angle they lean at. Ragdoll mechanics are involved, so it can often be a challenge to stop the #*^&$% thing swinging back and forth like pendulum and actually go where you want. The mouse button can be clicked to increase the bounce power. Waiting a little while gradually restores the power to its normal level. Once again, no enemies, just an obstacle course - you have to avoid bashing your head too much or getting stuck, bounceless, on the scenery, though.
