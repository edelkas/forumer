ZZ9, that's a weird coincidence. I just recently downloaded Dofus. Got bored with it though.

Anarchy Online (AO for short) is a previously pay-to-play-MMORPG, but now it's completely free to download and play. The add-ons still cost you though.

Also, Guild Wars is something I like to call a "Massively Multiplayer Single Player Role Playing Game", because it has no monthly fees, and plays very differently as opposed to, say, WoW or AO. They just released their first stand-alone add-on, too! The two games are separate, you don't need the other to play the other better or anything! This is a really good game.
Although if you are a fan of grinding and farming and leveling, this game is not about those things. It's about teamwork and strategy, much more than WoW etc. are.
