Make a map that is scary, horrific, terrifying, etc. Making a map in N that is scary will take a big chunk of experimenting with enemies, items, and other such things, so really, don't think of this as a foolish contest. Now, I'm not trying to be funny or weird. Make it scary, like the way a horror film, or a horror game would be. Judging will be done after the deadline.


Deadline:
October 1st.


Rules:
Don't joke around, be creative, be clever.
You can submit old maps if you'd like.
Must be playable in some way, cannot be N-art.
You can only submit up to two maps to the competition.
You must submit the map to NUMA and link it.

Judging Criteria:
Horror -- 50/50 -- Quality of scares.
Experience -- 50/50 -- Overall how good the map is.
Total -- 100/100


Judges:
Sept
uber_haxor
Jig
jtbandes - Backup judge.


Prizes:
1st Place: Sig, avatar, a ded, and a mystery prize.
2nd Place: A ded, Mystery prize.
3rd Place: A ded, Mystery prize.

Note: All prizes are optional.
