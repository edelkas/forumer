#110785 7 2005-10-27T09:15:54Z Arachnid
There's a transparent proxy somewhere between you and NUMA. When you click on pages that've been loaded through it before, it's returning the version it cached before you logged in. There's not much I can do about it from my end that I'm not already doing. To get it to load the right page, though, you can hold Control while pressing F5 to force it to reload from NUMA.
