saerrow, this may or may not work, but this seems to work for people who have issues with Soldat on Vista, so try it anyways:

Right-click N. (Not in the game, the icon on your desktop)

Go to Properties.

Go to the Compatibility tab.

Tick the tab that says "Run this program in compatibility mode for" and select Windows XP (Service Pack 2) on the drop-down list.

Also tick the tab that says "Run this program as an administrator".

Now try running N again. If this still doesn't work, considering all the other solutions you've tried, we can't help you much more.
