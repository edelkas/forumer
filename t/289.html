I was playing N the other day, getting blown up by mines, and an idea popped into my head. What if the mines only blew off a limb, rather than making the whole guy explode? It would be a bit more realistic, and I know we all want as much realism as we can get in our stealing-gold-from-deadly-robots simulations.

Yeah, it would be kinda nasty. But what you have to consider is, how nasty would it be if the stump of the recently departed limb spurted blood? Now that would be nasty. A cilantro explosive amputation doesn't seem so bad now, does it? Just keep it clean. For the kids.

I'm not sure how it would best be implemented, though. If you could get location detection would work, you could get cool stuff, like blowing the guy's head off. On the other hand, it would easier and almost as cool if you just picked a random limb or two and sent it flying in a different direction than the rest of the ninja.

After I thought about this for a minute, I realized it would be completely awesome to do the same thing for laser hits.
