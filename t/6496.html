Well, since some people don't like posting in my thread for help, I'll come to them.

(Aries's Easy Way)
36-2, np complete

This level is pretty easy except for the left side, so that's what I'll go over.

To beat the left side, all you have to do is realize that thwumps trigger when you are a set distance away from them, and that you can exploit this. The mines next to the wall make it so you can't just fall all the way down the sides, but that's ok. All you have to do is fall down the right side of that part of the level, so you are to the left of the mines that hug the right wall. If you are close enough, the thwumps on the right will trigger before you get to them, and you should actually land right on top of them, and the thwumps on the left aren't fast enough to get over to kill you. So just align your fall to the left of the mines on the right, and just let yourself fall. The top of the thwumps on the right will periodically break your fall and the left ones will never get to you. Really, the only part that you might have trouble on would be getting past the first thwump, if you do it my way, and all you have to do to avoid getting killed by the first thwump is to NOT just walk off the edge where it is. You can lure it over and get out of its way and then fall if you're slick, but I recommend just jumping from that little horizontal piece of floor up there, so you can line yourself up nicely, and you'll fall right past that first thwump, because your downward momentum will make you go too fast for it.
[/easy way]
