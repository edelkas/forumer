Rules
Choose a map on NUMA.
Play the map, get used to it.
Open a blank screen.
Replicate the map from memory.
Post the map data and a link to the original map in this thread.

Judging
There are no judges in this competition - instead, after all maps are submitted, everybody PM's me what they think is the best, second best, and third best replication.
If you think somebody cheated, PM me saying so. Anybody who has three people accusing him/her of cheating is disqualified.
