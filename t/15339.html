#290123 5 2007-01-26T03:03:59Z repeat2341
You're talking about double- and triple- jumping. Search for threads about that. Search for a thread by me called "Jump School." There's also a series of levels that are designed to teach you to pull that kind of stuff off. I'm not where I can get the links right now.

Basically, you tap jump just before hitting a surface, and then jump again as soon as you do. A triple jump is the same, but bouncing off a flat onto an incline, off of a wall, and into the air.

Bounce block double- and triple- jumping are also required skills, as are corner kicking and watching high scores.
