#397710 2 2008-01-06T17:10:01Z astro
"We long for justice and it is God alone who can offer that justice."
"There is no hope for humanity from within the human race. Our only hope is the God who breaks in from the outside."
This article seems to conveniently skirt around the whole issue that, far from bringing justice, God is letting these genocides and other evil happen. God is omnipotent, and so he could just stop it all. He gave his son Jesus--but he hasn't stopped suffering.

"The God who gives a second Adam."
This hasn't happened yet, right? Why do we need to wait? What possible benefits of waiting could outweight the suffering that currently happens constantly?

"If there is evil in the world how can there not be a God?"
The linked article argues that, without God, evil and suffering would be normal and so wouldn't seem out of the ordinary. I don't follow. "Evil" is just a label for things we consider to be morally despicable. As a label it doesn't require the existence of God. Suffering is the condition of people being in pain or distress. If we are not arguing about creation--and so are not arguing that only God could have created us and made the unpleasant sensation of pain--then I do not see how this argument works. We are not always in serious pain (or suffering greatly), so it is only logical (and not an argument for the existence of God) that we would "cry out for justice" when we do experience, or see/hear people experiencing it.
