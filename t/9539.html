I have seen topics like this strewn across the N help forum so many times, it isn't amusing anymore. Here are the ways to delete or resolve the problem of cheated levels:

  1. Finish all the cheated levels again, in the correct order. Basically, if you pretend you haven't cheated at all and can't access the cheated levels, they will be unlocked normally.
  2. Delete your .sol file. This can be done by following the instructions in .sol files for dummies.
  3. Click on the opened flash window and go into settings, then slide the storage bar to far left and OK anything it says about deleting files. Then once you have done this, slide the bar back to where it was. This should then delete all progress, allowing you to go for it all legitimately.
[/list]You should NOT:
  1. Start a new topic to ask this question again.
  2. PM me or any other member of the forum about this.
Please, tell me if there are ways I could add to the list.

The very first post in this thread has the 3 main ways to reset your progress. Deleting your .sol is one of them, which is what you have done.

Go the highscores. Select the episode(s) you cheated on, and look in the upper left hand corner. Do you have a personal best for all the levels in the episode(s)?
