For about a month, I've been putting together thoughts- a lot better arranged and thought out than my thoughts for the Meteor thread- and scribbled comments for a new, hopefully rather spectacular RPG I've been working on.

I've got about 10 'riddles' (Though much more than that) and a further 3 'steps' fully drafted, with about 5 more riddles and 10 more 'steps' existing in little notes to myself to be more fully explored later. There's going to be about 25 riddles and 16 steps to get through before finishing, but this may change, depending on if I can get away with doing less or if you all die or not.

This roleplaying game is hopefully going to be rather different to anything done before, dealing with influences as varied as Gary Kilworths satire, the television show 'lost' and Agatha Christies '10 little niggers'. Not to mention some very Joss Whedon style situations, provided any of you know him and his work well enough. An a ton of small in- references. All set in a survival- type situation, desperately stumbling towards the exits as they rest of you are picked off, one by one.

None of the graphics, sound clips, or animations have even STARTED to be converted to a format more readily shown on the forum than sketches on loose leaf paper, but they'll get there. I'm going for a very lush look for this, in order to give it a very different feel than, for instance, Kablizzy's forum event. Not to mention I have some interesting, relatively short prose allready drafted for the plotline, which should also help here. Trust me, this should be big, if it works out.

(And, for the record, the riddles are going to be- for the most part- very different to the type done by Kablizzy, though there may be some influnces from what he's done...)

And, hopefully it will. You'll all control 2 characters- seeing as it's so easy to get killed, it's nice to have somebody to fall back on, rather than to die on the very first 'event' and be forced to stick around only as a ghost, with no real motivation to get through this.

So. Hopefully this'll be big, and I have a lot allready planned- riddles, graphics, music, sounds, even a nice little excursion to another- very simple and basic- forum involving no signup, created exclusively for this event, with a certain 'cameo' primed to make a guest appearance that'll hopefully turn out fairly interesting.

Anyway. The problem is, if I only get very few people interested and sign up, this whole thing will probably just flop. So this thread words twofold.
First of all, as an advertisment for something being planned that'll probably come out in a month or two, and second of all, a way to let me know if this will just be a waste of time- if this generates little intrest. It just won't work as well without a relatively large 'cast'.

Keep all of this in mind, and that this is looking to be a LOT more special than Meteor, who here is interested?

A quick update, just to avoid any confusion-
This idea is currently been pushed aside as something else takes priority. It will go ahead, though under very slightly different circumstances, before too long.

A brief overview of what this thing is going to be about is as follows, just to clear up any confusion-

A large number of strangers step aboard a ship. Before long, the lot of them end up in... some rather unusual circumstances, trapped on an island fraught with mystery, peril, and large amounts of coconut rum.
Mysterious mysteries and perilous perils surround the place, and stress begins to kick in as people begin to die- sometimes for no discernable reason, sometimes when making grave mistakes in an attempt to unlock the mysteries surrounding them at every turn.

Danger lurks everywhere, like a lurking shadow of dangerious lurkiness- outside of the struggling group, as well as within it.
Plots twist, superficial mysteries are solved, as well as greater ones regarding the whole nature of their situation and, to some extent, their reason for being there. All the while, the group on the island gradually becomes smaller...

Ghosts, hauntings, mysterious messages, supernatural creatures and laws imposed upon a bunch of varied people, each mistrusting most of the others... And cryptic crypts and, you know, some hardcore alliteration is evident at every turn.

Still... on the bright side... at least there's free rum?
