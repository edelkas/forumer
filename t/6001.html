#119496 2 2005-12-01T06:18:56Z LittleViking
There are basically two ways to do it. One is to have the ninja standing on a tile in the first frame after hitting the teleporter. See first example. The second way involves standing on a one-way platform when you hit the teleporter - second example. This actually creates the somke puffs in mid-air with no tiles needed inbetween. I suspect bounce blocks would work too, but I wouldn't recommend them because you have less control over how the player hits the teleport then.
