#292025 2 2007-01-31T02:45:48Z Twistkill
Ah...

Open N and go into the Configure menu. In the bottom right hand corner, make sure that highscore submission is on. After that, you should fill in the 3 fields of information below that, (Self-explanatory) and click "Create new user". From now on, all highscores should be recorded properly. This will also activate a NUMA account for you, (The N User Map Archive) so go check out your e-mail for an activation link. Be sure to check your spam boxes.

Also, here's some great links regarding N, NUMA, Ned, and miscellaneous stuff about Metanet:

N Wiki
FAQs
Forum Rules
The editor_manual.txt and readme.txt that came with your N download.

Have a nice day.
