Welcome to the MetaNet Forum, home of the popular game N!

The main rules here are:
- No dissing or excessive swearing
- PLEASE, no flame wars! Take it out in e-mails/PMs. If such post battles are executed here, they will be edited/deleted with any participants warned.
- No posting in PURE CAPS. Such posts will be edited/deleted with the posts' authors warned.
- Please make an effort to post with proper grammar. Bad spelling, punctuation, etc. or anything to purposely make people stare at posts longer will be edited/deleted with the posts' authors warned. Posts submitted in the Geek-Talk language are only allowed if a direct translation lies below the text.

- No trolls allowed. Find another forum if you feel like trolling.

Violation of these rules may result in a brief inability to post or temporary, if not occasional permanent, bans.
