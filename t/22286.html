Every time I put together a level and 'Save' it, the data gets copied into the text box.. but if I paste that data into a TXT file and re-copy it into the level data box and load it, several (not all) of the tiles and objects have moved around. Is there some way to prevent this?

#428740 2 2008-04-15T22:15:01Z LittleViking
If you're using notepad, try turning off Word Wrap. (Format>uncheck Word Wrap) I've had problems with word wrap mixing up level data.
#428810 3 2008-04-15T23:22:32Z Izzy
One of the most common problems is that you don't click outside of the text box before pressing "L"
#428818 4 2008-04-15T23:31:14Z Pikman
Also, if you recopy it with blank lines in the data, it might mess up the replacing too.
#429017 5 2008-04-16T13:10:52Z lordbug
Wow, 3 different responses! Thanks for the quick answers folks! I tried copying my data in with all three of those suggestions in mind and it worked beautifully.. Thanks again!
  • Word Wrap
  • Think outside the box! (aka Don't type 'L' into your data and then load it)
  • Blank lines do not a NUMA map make. <== (select)
