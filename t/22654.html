#438641 3 2008-05-23T20:03:05Z Msyjsm
Now I feel guilty...I'll edit this post with comments a bit later. Look for it!

THE EDIT TO END ALL EDITS:

01: A bit off-putting at first with all the one-ways, I kept getting stuck on them, but after I got the hang of it the map was pretty fun.

02: Pretty good, I liked jumping down in the beginning, but after that it was a pretty average map.

03: Excellent. Short, slightly challenging, plus an awesome tileset to boot.

04: Well, I liked the way it looked a lot, but the launch pads kinda cramped my style, and getting some of the gold was a bit irritating. I think I'd like this level a lot more if you just deleted all the launch pads...

05: Didn't like this one. Getting the switches felt tedious, as did getting the gold, and there was nothing pretty in the tileset (which is odd, since most of your other maps have awesome tiles) to distract me from the gameplay.

06: AWESOME. I really liked the look of the level, and the launch pads + bounce blocks worked really well with the gauss.

07: Average. I liked the gold placement, but the level was a bit too easy and occasionally getting stuck on the two normal doors was annoying.

08: Cool level. I liked how it started out as a prisoner map and then you just kinda had to run out before the drones did.

09: Good, simple fun. I'm a sucker for arcs of gold.

10: Another cool concept; I've always been a fan of KRAs, and this is a cute twist on the formula.

11: Exciting stuff. The gausses kept me on my toes the whole time, and exiting the "vest" only to jump back in felt very...satisfying.

12: Awesome tileset, awesome gameplay. My only complaints are that getting the exit switch is kinda tough (for me, at least) and the laser drone sometimes shoots through the tiles (nothing you can do about that, though, since it's a bug).

13: Erm, I dunno. Nothing about this one clicked with me. The whole level just felt sort of empty.

14: Decent. I liked the tileset, but getting the switches was a bit too easy. If you got rid of the normal doors so that the drones were a bigger threat in those areas it would be cooler. I'm not sure what to make of the one-ways; sometimes I like them, sometimes not. Who knows what my problem is.

15: More frustrating than fun. Again, getting stuck on the normal doors is bothersome, and adding the randomness of the floorguard on the bottom seeing you makes the level very not fun. Also there wasn't really anything else besides that mechanic, and therefore nothing to mitigate the pain.

16: A mixed bag. I liked the chainguns being able to shoot me through the multiple one-way walls, but the thwumps rushing me along didn't let me enjoy that mechanic. It was exciting watching the thwumps just barely miss me, though.

17: I loved this. Good old-fashioned jumping, plus some fun optional gold. The gausses were well placed too.

18: When I first saw this I thought it would be impossible. Fortunately it wasn't. One of the more enjoyable seeker-drones-in-close-quarters maps I've played.

19: Yup, everything about this level was great. Getting all the gold was great, getting the switches was great, evading the lasers was great. It was a blast.

20: Another amazing level. Incredible gameplay, with a sweet name to boot

21: Well, I guess you kinda know my feelings about this map (i.e., ) already, but I can expand on my NUMA comment a bit. What made this so fun for me was that even though there was a bit of backtracking, the one-ways made the trip interesting and fun both ways. You really used the objects to their fullest potential here.

22: Jeez, you're on a roll. Unrevolutionary, but exquisite fun nonetheless. Awesome tiles, and lots of different routes for highscoring.

23: A solid level. A nice variety of action, and some nice gold placement. I especially liked the laser drone peeking in near the end of the map.

24: Stylistically unified. I would've hated the thwump bits in most other maps, but it fit with the tileset extremely well. I guess it was the old "the whole is greater than the some of its parts" effect.

25: Some nice puzzle elements here. I also really liked the tileset. A lot.

26: Great rocket dodging opportunities here. Somehow dodging rockets is just that much more fun with a kickass tileset.

27: Woo tileset. The gameplay was quite good as well, but man that tileset just blew me away. (Sorry for mentioning your tilesets so much, but they're just way too good.)

28: Perhaps my favorite tileset in the pack. The gameplay was incredible too, although I found navigating around the one-ways with lasers shooting at me to be sort of tricky. The one-ways add a lot to the aesthetics of the level, though.

29: Hm. Pretty fun overall, but the launch pads were kind of annoying to avoid. Also, there wasn't really a point to having the ninja start inside a tile.

30: A good solid end to the pack. Nice flowy tiles.

Favorites: 03, 06, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 26.

The pack definitely got better as it went along. Good job.

Also, even though I said I'd be too lazy to compile my AGDs, I went back and watched them all when commenting on the levels, so I figured I might as well throw them all into a text file.
