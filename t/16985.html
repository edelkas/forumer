The Day of Silence is tomorrow. Basically, it's an event to raise awareness of abuse against gay/lesbian/bisexual folks, and it gets attention by having its participants go the day without saying anything (it reminds me of fasting). The idea is that the verbally abused can't speak up about it, so this is a poetic tribute, in a way. I participated last year, and I plan to do it again. The only challenge I really faced was getting somebody to explain what i was doing 30+ times, so I planned ahead and printed out a sheet of paper explaining what I'm doing.
I guess the reason I do it is because I find the event sickening because of its very existence. The simple fact that harassment to these folks for something as minor as their sexuality is still a major problem makes me sad. We shouldn't be having events like this but, hey, there ya go.
I'm curious if anyone else has heard about this, or is planning to play along, or disapproves altogether.
