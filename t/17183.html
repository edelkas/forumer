It's a proven fact (ie, countless experiments and case studies have been performed) that children who watch violent televesion and play violent video games tend to be more violent in nature and more desensitized to violence. I do agree that it is impossible to place the blame for violence in the world on the shoulders of video entertainment though. Correlation is not the same thing as cause and effect. While the correlation exists, it's not fair to say all violence has a root in violent video games / tv. Sure some violence may be a result of this real-life violence, but not nearly enough to warrant a ban of these products. Every party has a pooper, Congress: stop pooping on ours.
