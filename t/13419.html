This level is a very nice end to the 10's column. It is fun, hard, and entertaining. The two rockets provide good protection to the very open level, and there is plenty of room for playing around. From the start, run to the right, jump over the launchpad, then jump and walljump into the first crosshair. Dodge the rocket if it got in, then jump in, flip the switch, and drop out. Launch yourself up, walljump, and land in the top crosshair. Jump across, flip the switch, then drop through the top of the center. Activate the terminal, then drop on the bounceblock on the left, and continue down and to the left. Jump and walljump, and flip the third switch, to drop down. Launch and walljump, then jump across and flip the final switch, then jump up to the ledge, and run to the exit, beating the column.
