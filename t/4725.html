Just last night I downloaded a new game called Icy Tower. I've been out of the house almost all day today, and I've also spent time getting a collection of characters as well. Yet I can still say I'm already addicted to this thing. I won't bother explaining it, its pretty simple: just ascend the tower as fast as possible, and more important, with as many combos as possible. You can try for either the highest floor, score or combo you can get. And it features full modding ability: you can make custom characters with their own sprites, sound effects, music, and even filters/palettes applied to text and/or the whole game. Some are crap, talking so much that even for the slowest players will have them overlapping to unintelligableness, and some are supremely excellent (and the two default ones aren't bad either).

I know digit boy actually posted about this earlier, before there was an other games forum (I ran a search because I remembered he had made a few characters), but the only person to respond to any was Kablizzy and so I figure it can't hurt to talk about it again. Besides, now that its in this forum and posted by a member who isn't a complete noob at the time of posting you can OFFICIALLY ignore it like all the other topics I've made in this forum. But thats your loss, you guys would love it.

I just realized that after a certain point (lets say floor 500) highscores really become a matter of attrition and luck alone, not skill. While watching the 1016 combo replay out of curiosity (which I don't suggest doing, its not as cool as you think) I discovered that after a certain point (no idea when) the timer stops increasing your speed and starts just spinning around randomly (and faster, to show that its done). Additionally, after the first few hundred floors it reaches the point where the average floor size stops shrinking because they're all the minimum size. Thus, after a certain point the game stops incrementing its difficulty in any form for the rest of the infinite tower. Now, I'm not denouncing John Beak's skill, because it far surpasses any of ours, but the only thing seperating him from the ten people behind him except the fact that he kept it up for more cycles in a row.
