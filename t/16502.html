Recently I have come across a few varied opinions about how the rating system should work. Im attempting to set this out in this best way possible, so we can sort this problem out. The question I am asking is:

If a person has an opinion on a map, is it wrong for them to vote against that opinion?

I think you should only vote what you think a map is worth, while others that I have talked to think you should be allowed to vote whatever you want, regardless of your opinion. I know this question is hard to answer, as there are no such rules implying this on NUMA, and even if there were, they would be hard to enforce, however, here is my opinion on the subject:

I think we have the rating system as a way to signal our opinions of a map to others, without going through the effort of posting a comment, which we dont always want to have to do. Rating against your opinion in my view, is almost like rating against the system. When rating maps, we should all try and rate on the same standard, so that ratings can be more meaningful. By filling in the gaps of the written indicators S&M supplied to us, I come to this:

0 = Perfectly Awful
0.5 = Amazingly Bad
1 = Very Bad
1.5 = Bad
2 = Below Average / Not Bad
2.5 = Average
3 = Above Average / Not Good
3.5 = Good
4 = Very Good
4.5 = Amazingly Good
5 = Perfect

I view the rating system in a symmetrical way, and believe that S&M gave us these written indicators as a means of unifying or ratings.

Also, a point to note out is that nowhere in this did I say peoples opinions have to be the same on all maps. That would be pointless, and ruin the voting system as well. All I am saying is that your rating should reflect your opinion on the map. I understand that I may be looking into this far too much, and that I have become to philosophical with my ideas, but its an issue that irks me.

Also, while I am here, I would like to ask people who give out average ratings of 4s and 4.5s to think about what they are rating. Is that map /really/ flawless? Is it /really/ very good? Remember, you are aiming to give out an average rating of 2.5, as 2.5 is the average rating.

So what are everyone elses opinions on the subject?
