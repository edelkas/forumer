#5191 1 2005-11-14T17:09:38Z sweep
Some people may remember a time on Ned where you could remove the border tiles. Well, I propose this is reinstated. With two modifications.

Firstly, the level code includes info that the border no longer exists. Just something like a b1 for a border, a b2 for no border. This could be at a convienient place in the code where it could be removed easily or could be ignored by an old version of Ned. Those of you that were around when borders could be taken off will remember that although you could remove the border tiles, the level code would not store info about the border tiles and so when someone else loaded it up the tiles would remain.

Secondly, so it's not just N falling into an abyss forever, when he crosses the bottom of the screen (where the bottom of the border is) he would automatically die. He could get flung up above the top of the screen, as long as he came back down and landed (etc. etc.) Of course, it would just be an y value to cross that would kill him, so he could be a million miles wide of the screen, but if he crossed that y value he'd die.

Basically, this would not only save us from lining the edges with mines, but would give the game many more possibilities (long range laser drones, blind jumping, MASSIVE JUMPS etc.) due to the larger space possible to inhabit, and ultimately shouldn't be too hard to code.

Any thoughts?
