Well, you know what this means. The game is basically the same as last time, with a new and improved set of Judges - me, Atilla, Sweep and Red Reamer. There are small changes - see below - but the main idea remains the same. (Incidentally, the last scavenger hunt took place here)

The Rules -
We (iangb, Sweep, Red Reamer and Atilla) will continue to post as normal throughout the forum and NUMA. However, in some of our posts we will include referances. Each judge will post a maximum of 25 referances, 5 from each category: Literature, Film/TV, Games, Music and Misc. Misc can be anything that doesn't fall into any of the other categories, or it can be and extra referance from a 'full' category (like a sixth 'Music' referance, or similar).

The aim of the game for you, the players, is to find as many referances as possible. Ideally, we will have made 100 referances between us, allthough this isn't guarenteed to happen. When you find a referance, PM the author with the referance and where it is from to get a point.

Surprisingly enough, the person with the most points wins.

The judges can start posting referances whenever they wish, but they will post here to let you know when they are going to start. This slightly more staggered start will (hopefully) keep your interest without making it impossible to start playing a bit later on (like, in a few days time if you miss the topic first time around).

So there we go. Post here if you have any questions/comments, and keep an eye out for referances! A 'standings' topic will also be made, once some people have got points.

In case anyone else is actually reading this thread, here are my references:

Literature:

Quote: The "Most Likely To..." Thread
^ Most likely to be abducted and shipped to a distant institution beacuse he's a Misfit.

A reference to the Obernewtyn Chronicles, a popular series by Isobelle Carmody, in which the main character (Elspeth) suffers just that fate. The odd capitalisation should have been a giveaway.

Quote: mini games
There should be a Brockian Ultra Cricket minigame. It would work well.

Brockian Ultra Cricket is a fictional sport mentioned many times in the Hitchhiker's Guide series, by Douglas Adams.

Quote: Nickname Game
^ Chief Bunburyist

"Bunburying" forms a substantial part of the plot in The Importance of Being Ernest, by Oscar Wilde.

Quote: The archnemesis hood of Blue Tetris and I
A pox on your house, iangb!

Paraphrase of Mercutio's line from Romeo & Juliet. Most versions have "a plauge" rather than "a pox".

Quote: Your top three suggestions
By Quar!

Not, despite what people kept telling me, an obscure medievil card game. That would make no sense. Quar is a god in the Rose of the Prophet trilogy, by Margaret Weiss and Tracy Hickman - hence "By Quar!". After all, who swears by a card game?

Games:

Quote: The "Most Likely To..." Thread
^ Most likely to attack flying foxes

Directed at Gamingwolf, this was a rather oblique reference to Star Fox (and Fox's rival, Star Wolf).

Quote: The archnemesis hood of Blue Tetris and I
Has someone been feeding b_t moon sugar again?

Moon sugar is an illegal narcotic in the Elder Scrolls series (and, believe it or not, has an entry in Wikipedia). Speaking of which, Oblivion is being released shortly. It looks really good, but I'm not sure if my computer will be able to handle it...

Quote: Subvert an Acronym
GLA (Global Liberation Army)

The terrorist/freedom fighter (well, it depends on which side you're on) faction in Command and Conquer: Generals and its expansion, Zero Hour.

Quote: Subvert an Ancronym
WWTAO (We Work Together As One)

One of the favourite quotes of your ally Double H, in Beyond Good & Evil (er, the game, not the Neitzsche book, obviously). The other was DBUTT (Don't Break Up The Team); both are from Carlson and Peeters, a military manual.

Quote: Just a queer thought
If something were to happen to me, all the world's women would grieve!

Edgar, King of Figaro, from FFVI. I can't believe Daddaluma was the only one to get it - do you really think I'm such a philanderer?

Movies/TV

Quote: What finger do you use?
I just wave my hand at the shift key and it presses itself.

A reference to Jedis' habit of using switches and buttons by waving their hand at them - lazy sods.

Quote: like dart guns
I'm sure N needs a collection of rooms of flying knives

The movie House of Flying Daggers, obviously.

Quote: It's Coming
Resistance is futile!

You will be assimilated! The Borg, from Star Trek, of course.

Quote: Macintoshes Vulnerable
There can be only one.

Tagline from the cult favourite, Highlander, which had numerous incarnations on TV and movies.

Quote: Subvert an Acronym
CONTROL (Shh! It's a secret!)

The agency Max works for in Get Smart. What it actually stands for is never made clear, but everyone knows that secret agencies always have acronyms for a name. Their enemies were KAOS, which is rumoured to stand for Killing And Other Stuff.

Music:

Quote: The Member Above You
^ Is a wild one

The song, Wild One (Real Wild Child), which was a hit for the Australian rock'n'roll singer, Johnny O'Keefe (known as "The Wild One"). A popular cover, Real Wild Child (Wild One), was performed by Iggy Pop.

Quote: The "Most Likely To..." Thread
^ Most likely to start a weird religion that worships oysters and the colour blue

The name of a band - Blue Oyster Cult.

Quote: I have to apologise
Obviously when they said "We don't need no education" he took it far too seriously.

"We don't need no education" is a line from Another Brick In the Wall, by Pink Floyd.

Quote: Post the strangest thing you can
We shall overcome, for we have lovely hats which will abjure the divine gaze of frogs.

We Shall Overcome is a popular protest song.

Quote: NUMA is blue.
Why is it blue (da ba dee)? Is it lonely? Did its server leave it for another site?

Blue (Da Ba Dee) is a song by Eiffel 65.

Miscellaneous:

Quote: Homework Help
Because it symbolically reveals though deeply significant symbollic symbolism and metaphorically allegorical analogies that Macbeth is the claw of the Ur dragon, scattering seeds of devastation. Or something.

"Rith is the claw of the Ur dragon, scattering seeds of devastation" is the flavour text from the Magic: the Gathering card Rith's Attendant.

Quote: Homework Help
I had a really annoying English teacher in year 11. All she did was go on about the deep significance of trivial phrases that noone sane would even notice. You can only listen to so many explanations that the fact that Passerby X, who is only mentioned in one sentence on page 435, is wearing a blue shirt, which symbolises the main character's inner struggle to weigh the torment of existence against the horror of nonbeing.

"Weighing the Torment of Existence Against the Horror of Nonbeing" is the name of one of Calvin's snow sculptures, from Calvin and Hobbes.
