Actually kkstrong0, the definition of a "head rhyme" is a rhyming sequence with constant alliteration. Alliteration needs more than one word, like "Jack picked a peck of pickled peppers". Many of those words have the "p" sound in them, so therefore, it qualifies as alliteration.

So basically, a head rhyme is another word for alliteration.
