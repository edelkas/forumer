Apathy of political matters is perfectly acceptable of the average citizen (I don't expect you to keep up with the circus our politic has become), and is even an option to legislators, who don't have to vote on every bill or amendment. Here, Obama would come face-to-face with many issues he's dodged in the past, the most prominent of which is gun control. It's a very real possibility that over the next presidential term a Supreme Court justice may resign, die, or otherwise need to be replaced. That's two faced, and that won't pass if he's in the Oval office.

The point I'm making here is the same as the one in my previous post.
