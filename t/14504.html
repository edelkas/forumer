Non-playable maps are maps of the following actegory:
Tilesets: These are generally non-playable since they only have tiles and maybe a ninja.
DDA: DDA (Don't Do Anything) Well, according to the description, we shouldn't do anything. They generally do the whole level by themselves.

Impossible Maps Are non-playable since they cannot be copleted. They cannot be beaten. Hence, they are non-playable.
