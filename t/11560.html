#10348 1 2006-07-30T15:22:02Z LoD
This is a project that had been on my mind since the NAM 1.1. After having realized that it's impossible to implement into the N Art Maker, I made a stand-alone application of it. The N Tile Art Maker converts images to N arts, but it does not use objects but tiles.

Update: In version 1.1, you can deselect types of tiles you don't want in your NTAM art (e.g. curves).

Update: NTAM is now available here.
