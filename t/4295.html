Just found this nice new game yesterday, and its good enough that I'm sharing it with the community here. Its basically a space shooter where enemies teleport in and you have to kill them. Sounds generic, right? Well, the things that set this apart are the nice way they set up your two attacks so that you really do have a choice of how to handle any situation (not like most where there's a primary attack and a "press this when you're overwhelmed and you'll clear the screen but it has to recharge for 30 seconds" joke of two attack modes), and the great AI. Seriously, this AI is good. It probably uses the cover better than I do, and when its done hiding behind it they rush at you and then stop to shoot at just within the radius of the death explosion while constantly moving to thwart your efforts to kill them. Really, just seeing how well he did AI is probably worth a download.
The only real gripe I have is that the "boss" ships (which take numerous hits to kill but could be mistaken for a regular enemy until they open up with their space machinegun) are completely random (I've gotten one as early as "level" 2 and other times I've reached 3000 before seeing any, and occasionally two pop up at once).

BTW, my highscore is 33800, but I'm sure I'll topple it soon.
