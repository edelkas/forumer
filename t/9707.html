Name:

Colour:

Attributes will all start at zero as they will be gained during gameplay:

Strength.

Charm.

Intelligence.

Weight. In Kilos.

Rules:

Each player will select their first house and job from a list, only one job for each person, houses will have to be bought with money earned. Which means you can only buy a house that you can afford.

Okay here are available jobs so far:

Fast food restaurant order taker person ( forgot name ).

Restaurant waiter/waitress.

Builder.

Assistant teacher.

Cardboard box factory worker.

When people join they can PM me or ask on this thread for any other available jobs, but not until the ones listed are taken.

Here is a list of available houses, you dont really need them but fi you dont you will live on the street so you will look trampy and may not be able to get a job.

Dusty garage - 100$
1 bedroom apartment - 500$
2 bedroom apartment - 1000$
2 bedroom house - 3,000$
3 bedroom house - 5,000$
4 bedroom house - 7,000$
5 bedroom bungalow - 10,000$
5 bedroom villa with outside pool - 20,000$
6 bedroom mansion, front and back garden and indoor pool - 40,000$
6 bed mansion, front & back Gdns, inside and outside pool, tennis and basketball court - 100,000$


More on gameplay: Buying a house means you can meet girls or boys and build relationships, they will be able to move in with you and become working partners also. Also you will be able to buy a car which will increase your charm, increased charm means you can get promotions quicker, girls/boys will be more attracted to you. Also having a better house increases charm.

You can go to the gym to gain strength, this helps depending on what job you have, but if you have a job that uses the brain more its best to go to the library and increase intelligence.

There will be costs for admission to the library as well as the gym, which are listed below:

Gym - 1000$ a session (i know it seems alot but youll know why), increases strength by two points.

Library & Schooling - 1500$ a session, increases intelligence by two.
Each players attributes will be tallyed by me.

Food - You will need to eat everyday so you dont die obviously.
Tiny mac - 100$
Medium mac - 300$
Big mac - 500$

A day in the game will finish once everyone has requested what they would like to do for the day, post something to tell me what youd like to do like this:

Apply for job as (the job you want)
Eat (mac meal)
Go to gym
Buy house (type of house)
Go to library
Chat up girl/boy
Buy car (from list)
Set up own business of your choice (i will tell you how much it will cost, you can only do this once all first jobs have been taken).

Note: You may only do five things each day

If you would like to buy a car tell me which one and price will be given.

Commitment will be raised when you get promotions or better jobs and when you get a girl/boyfriend, raising commitment means you will get promotions quicker and after a while you will be able to have a baby. Having a baby doesnt really do much, but if you do you can use child benefit money to live on

Each individual player will also be given scenarios which they will have to deal with, by doing the right things they could win or lose money or attributes.
