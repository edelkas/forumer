I'm thinking about pitching an idea for a racing game. No idea for a name, story, or anything; this is strictly my idea for gameplay mechanics so far.

The game plays like a cross between Midnight Club 2, and Need for Speed: Most Wanted. The game is divided into two parts, which are Free Roam and Driving. In Free Roam, you're free to drive around without a goal. You can drive to upgrade shops (upgrade your car), detail shops (make it look pretty), car dealerships (lost your car?), raceways (make some side-cash) and garages (your own, or your team's). The new use for free-roam, however, is to discover the city.

Your map will begin blank from the beginning of the game. With every street you drive on (or near, but that's only a few feet), it will uncover it for you on the map. As you drive around, you uncover more and more of the map. Secret paths such as alleyways, boarded/gated areas, and general paths that you can drive once you see them are unlocked only through driving them, and are a darker shade on your map. Stunt paths, buildings, and other major shortcut areas that are not meant to be driven on cannot be automapped. Instead, you can drop down Point of Interest (POI) markers on your map.

POI markers can be simple shapes, like dots or lines. You can also shape circles, squares, and freeform triangles over the area you want. Then you can name your area, and take a snapshot of the area from the point you marked. See a parking garage you can jump out of? POI it with a dot. Does driving through the stadium shave seconds off of your time? POI it with a line. Does the broken freeway wall lead to a straightaway below? POI it with a triangle arrow.

But why bother with POI's when you have a map? During the Driving section, you'll be given one of three racing situations: A-to-B gives you a start, and an end. This is the majority of what you'll be racing, so knowing the city gives you the advantage. Circuit will be used from time to time, and hitting shortcut after shortcut is a key to success. Evasion is an event that can happen in or out of a racing event. Police will be around, and driving off-road, hitting cars, buildings, or any object for that matter will get them onto you. Running a red light is enough for you to get tailed by a cop. You want them off of your tail, and there's only one way to do it - you have to shake 'em. Alleyways, jumps, buildings, and anything you can drive into and drive out of will be your only hope, because driving on city streets with the cops behind you is like running from a train in a tunnel. Use your POI's to ditch the 5-0.

TACK 1 - I'm definitely not throwing in any collector missions (ex. 100 secret packages, 30 stunt jumps, etc.) because it's pointless to drive around and try to collect things for a 100% rating.

TACK 2 - For stunts, there will be a button to start a recording of a demo, and when you finish it, it saves a rough copy of what you made for a showcase edit mode where you edit the clip you created with fancy camera angles.

TACK 3 - Thanks to Zres for the inspiration for a storyline idea.

He's beaten a few of the lower tier, but you challenged him anyway, and put your garage on the line. So much that did for you, because he smoked you with a run you've never seen before. With that, you lose your garage, your fame, and everything you worked for.

But now is your time to strike back. Your boy Redd heard that Edgar is bringing his crew in to take your cars back to Riverman's Falls, and he's bringing the title of Graystown's champ with him. You're going to his city to return the favor and win back what's yours..

..and his."
--------------------------------

TACK 4 - The racing section of the game will include a checkpoint system created entirely by you. Before each race, you set down your own checkpoints that show you your own driving run. They are not necessary to succeed, but if you want to be reminded of which turns you wanted to make, you can drop them down in order. An arrow will point to your next checkpoint, and if you miss a checkpoint, there will be a button to skip to the next one.


That's just the main part of the game, I got too lazy to type the rest. How does it sound? I always thought finding shortcuts was overlooked in racing games, so I wanted the player to learn the shortcuts beforehand.

Throw in ideas, suggestions, pro's/con's, or even storylines, or a title for the game itself. I may just submit it to Rockstar. God forbid I let EA get their hands on it.
