Using the forums Search engine, I was quite astounded to find no more than one post about this. Though I guess some of you probably have heard of a guy called Ben "Yahtzee" Croshaw, I figured he was worth a topic of his own.

Basically, Zero Punctuation is a series of game reviews ( ) thrown at you at approx 72wpm (words per minute)( ) accompanied by a completely hilarious set of simple animations ( ) by a guy who hasn't been close to touching a Halo game until he was asked to review the third one ( ) and contain some of the funniest pitch-black humor ever to be seen on the net ( ) where everything basically is a 3:30m long sentence ( ) spoken in such a way that you either don't understand a thing or laugh hysterically or don't understand a thing but laugh hysterically anyway.

You don't have to take the reviews themselves too seriously, but these are definitely worth checking up.

Zero Punctuation at The Escapist

My favorites are:
MOH: Airborne (some of the worst game bashing I've ever seen)
Orange Box (Portals: The only game he doesn't have any criticism for)
and: Fable (Where I really agree on everything he says)

Now just sit back, relax, enjoy... And get ready to watch again.
Also, if you like the existing vids there'll be a new one out each wednesday. Maybe something to rss...
