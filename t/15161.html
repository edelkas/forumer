Changing Objects DDA Comp

Yes, it is a bit like max_ride's Changing Enemies comp, but with DDA's!

What it is:

Every week (starting today, or when we get enough competitors, probably 10 min.) I will choose a method of propulsion to feature in your DDA's. The judges will judge the DDA's based on the following:

Scoring:

Close-calls: X/10
Tileset: X/5
How effective method used to evade enemies: X/5
Length: X/10 (the longer the better)
Total: X/30

What it is (cont'd):

Then, after judging, the judges will send competitors home. Each week, that number of people sent home will decrease by one. How many rounds will decide on how many competitors who wish to partake in this competition. How many people sent home first week will depend on the number of rounds.
