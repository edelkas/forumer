Hi, Howdy, Hey to all you Neditors out there. This is the next season of The N map editing contest, neditor for short.

This one is based on unfinished maps. There a three tile sets I'll link to below that can be used for this contest. Only these tile sets can be used for the contest. Each contestant is allowed 2 submissions TOTAL for the contest. You may use the same tileset twice, or any combination of the two for your to entries.

If we reach 25 total submissions, I will bump up the maximum to 3 maps allowed... this is to allow you all to borrow ideas, plus it tempts you to post more maps... two birds one stone... Just a heads up

All entries must be on numa under your name on the forums (I'll exclude the trinity from this rule for obvious reasons) This just helps everything go smoother. Also, no submitting two versions of the same map. You must make sure each submission is your own unique work

No contestant map tamper with the tilesets in anyway, they must be used as is and only items placed into the map.

All maps must have a ninja and a finish, no survival maps. I only put this because the 3rd tile set is very open to a survial map, and the survival catagory on numa.

NO DDA'S will be accepted, posting a DDA will count as one of your submissions, but will not be judged, no exceptions.

There will be three judges (Me, Littleviking 001, and Kablizzy, and if any of them do not accept the position, Suki again. I tried to mix it up this season with new judges...) Also, judges may submit maps, and Suki will be the judge of those.

Each map will be scored out of 50 points based on these catagories:

(0-10) Use of tile set: how well did you utilize the entire tileset? Did you use all of it, was it all flowing and nicely put together. What makes yours different from others using the same set?
(0-10) Creativty: What sets your map apart from the others? Why is this a memorable map? Did you do anything new here I haven't seen before?
(0-10) Fun: was it fun to play, and Would I like to come back for more?
(0-10) Difficutly: was it too hard, or just right? Too easy maybe? Try and find a ballance here
(0-5) Excitement: new catagory, did this level get my going, was it a thril to play? This is the make neditors challenge us, and keep the game fun and fastpaced, the way it was designed to be played...
(0-5) Other: This is things that don't fit into another catagory, but the judge feels needs to be mentioned

Thats it for the judging...
