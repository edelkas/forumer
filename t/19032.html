Idk were that slogan came from, but here's my idea.

Look at those poor, moronic enemies. Does the AI deserve a little sympathy? Do you hate the ninja because "he" landed on a mine? Do you want more total play time? OR do you just want a different way to play n? All of these desires are answered by...

CHASE MODE!
(dun dum DAA!)

The basic idea is that, instead of playing the ninja, you play through several levels as a humble drone. You start out as a normal zap drone, but you can obtain in some stages, additional weapons (deployed with the Jump key). You have free movement, so jumping is not needed. You can also, instead of wall-climbing, latch onto a wall and become a "surface-follow" drone. To do this, simply press the direction of the tile(s) you wish to circle.

You have the limitations of any drone: partial tiles spell doom, but you can float freely around open areas. As I mentioned earlier, there are several weapons. You can get the powers of almost any enemy. You normally need to touch the ninja (now computer-controlled), but why bother if you can deploy a bullet barrage, homing rocket, laser, or gauss turret attack to take him down? You can gain, by picking up a certain power up, the abilities of any enemy except: floor guard (you CAN be a seeker drone) and mine. Press Jump to automatically target the ninja.

Along with these power-ups, there is another factor: Honor. If you kill the ninja right away, before he takes the gold, you will get the maximum points. Each point of gold remaining when he dies is one Honor Point. However, you must also compete with the other enemies in the area. The ninja is an avid gold fan, so he will take risks. Stop him before another foe does, or you will lose every Honor Point from the level, as well as ten from your total. If the ninja successfully takes all the gold and reaches the door, your Honor Points will be set to zero.

Honor Points affect the abilities you gain throughout the game, and are needed to unlock new episodes within Chase Mode. Completing an episode is not enough, you need a set number of points to continue. It's tough to be a lackey. Getting 100% Honor on EVERY level leads too... rewards. I have no ideas, I'm sure metanet and/or the community could think of something.

It adds no keys to play, and has nothing against the rules. The "kill" key does nothing, as you cannot destroy a drone. Please post responses to my idea.
