Welcome.

It seems the day has finally come, and the Project 42 subforum has been unmasked: a new attraction for our precious Metanet Forums' front page.

In March this year, soon after the release of the astounding Legacy Team's first project, a few humble IRC-goers were chatting about making levelpacks. The topic mainly focused on hype, and the amount of hype those authors would generate in comparison to Legacy, if they were to work on a levelpack together.

Soon after, Aju began asking around on IRC, looking for keen mapmakers to work together and form a little mapmaking alliance, with the end product being an episodic levelpack, much like the Legacy.

And so, the team was formed.

9400
Aju
Bigblargh
Evil_bob
KinGAleX
krusch
sept
and spect

Working together, the above mapmakers strove to create something with flair, something new, and something for you all to enjoy.

With great pleasure, I announce the release of the Project 42 levelpack!
The levelpack can be found in the 'Collection' thread, or right here.
I hope you all have a wonderful time playing.


Edit: Another thing, feel free to post in any of the threads in this subforum. That's what they're here for.
