#142792 2 2006-02-12T23:06:46Z Teh Dude
What kind of Ethernet cable are you using between the laptop and PS2? It needs to be a twisted pair one so that traffic can flow both ways (as you are not using a hub or switch). You will also have to bridge the Airport connection and the ethernet connection (I am not sure how to do this on Macs).

Otherwise, you will have to get a device that can receive Airport signals and has an ethernet port for your PS2. Apple have Airport Extreme base stations but these cost $$$.
