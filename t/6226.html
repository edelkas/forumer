Welcome to the world of bitesizeNUMA.

What is bitesizeNUMA?
It is a website containing links to maps on NUMA that community members with good judgement have deemed to be of a good quality.

How do I use bitesizeNUMA?
Click on a map thumbnail to open the map. The rest is pretty self-explanatory when it comes to general use.

How often will bitesizeNUMA be updated?
As soon as someone submits a map, it updates the website. Hopefully, people will be updating the website often, with mostly recent maps, but also older maps.

How do I add maps to bitesizeNUMA?
Only I can give you an account to do this. If you want one, just PM me about it, or email me at chimneysweepATgmailDOTcom. Whether you get an account or not is at my discrection, and if you don't get an account I will tell you why. You are perfectly at rights to appeal this decision, but I always have the final choice.

Final notes:
Please feel free to suggest features for the site, and please report any bugs you find. And if you see any abuse of the system, tell me and I'll sort it out.

I hope you enjoy it!
