Welcome to the "Create the Tileset Competition"


Rules
  • You will be given five incomplete maps to choose from, each of which have objects, but no tiles. Your job is to create a tileset around the objects and make a map.
  • You may only create maps from the ones given.
  • You may NOT add additional objects.
  • Five submissions per person. You may only use the same objects once!
  • Judges may submit maps, but of course, can not judge their own.
  • Of course, no old maps.
  • Replacing a map that is already submitted is not allowed.
  • No N Arts, Incomplete, Tilesets, Survivals, DDAs or Test Levels allowed for submission.
  • Final date for submissions will be Friday, February 2, 2007.
  • All the gold in the map must be possible to get.
  • No glitched tiles.
  • PM me your maps or you can post them in this thread.
Judging

Each judge will give the map a score out of 100 possible points.

x/20 Gameplay: How fun is it?
x/20 Creativity: How well did the author place tiles?
x/20 Objects: How well did the author use the objects in the map?
x/20 Unique: How unique is the map as a whole?
x/20 Looks: How good does the map look?

Judging will begin after the deadline.

Judges

The maps chosen are Metanet maps, but without the tiles. So credit goes to them.
  • Myself
  • Obby
  • Pembie
  • Fallen
Prizes

First Place: Signature Tag and Dedication
Second Place: Signature Tag
Third Place: Signature Tag
