Quote: LittleViking001, October 15, 2007 06:02 pm
Instead of copying and darkening, could you just create a new layer and paintbucket it with black?
It would have less precision, and the idea is to have a working copy to send to other pieces or areas on the fly. For all of my silhouette works, I save a copy of just the silhouette. Those things come in handy - I've used each of mine at least thrice in a multitude of endeavors.
I tried that method first, and it didn't work as well as one might think.

Also, one of the upsides is that the darken tool also shades the pixels one-pixel outside the selection box ever-so-slightly, creating an auto-fade effect so it's not as jagged as paintbucketing it.

Even moreso, just creating a new layer doesn't give you the vector-esque ability to create odd and disproportional lines and selection marks of transparency; which really looks great in the end.
