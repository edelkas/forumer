McP3000
=======
McP3000 was banned for making a death threat towards Arona Daal, on top of several other infractions which had previously resulted in him being suspended. Seeing as he only just returned from a suspension, the staff decided that this was it for old McP.

Arona Daal made the thread I hate in Bash & Thrash, intended to let people tell others what they hate in the world. While it may not have been the most intelligent topic to grace these forums, McP's post was unwarranted and unnecessary - as a death threat always is, no matter what the context. This, on top of McP's existing warn log - which was "one of the most entertaining things I've read in a while" (according to Tsukatu) - proved to be too much.

Examples of other offences include posting a picture in Serious Discussion who's subject was "Buttsecks". He then did it again after it was taken down. He was suspended for this because he obviously knew better. Not long after the suspension was lifted, he made this death threat - after a suspension, you pretty much have to be perfect. Death threats aren't perfect.

Pros: He wasn't really an idiot as such; to some extent, he was a respected member of the forums.
Cons: Huge repeat offender. Bobby Brown ain't got nothin' on him.

Morals:
  • No death threats. Ever. Anywhere. It is very possible you will get banned.
  • Lots of warns =/= good thing. Try staying away from them; I hear it helps.
