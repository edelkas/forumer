#316847 6 2007-04-29T20:11:49Z Twistkill
(You may not understand what I'm saying about the weapons if you're unfamiliar with Soldat weapon statistics)

New features I like:

No more reloading dropped weapons. (You keep whatever ammo was in there when it was dropped. Yay.)

No more "if you pick up a dropped weapon while you're still in the switching weapons animation the gun is frozen" glitch. (Yay)

Fatter, more visible bullets. (Yay)

Color-coded pings (Green for good, yellow for average, red for very laggy) and flags next to server names in the server list. (Cool)

BattleEye anti-cheat protection. (I don't know how well this works against cheaters yet, but yay.)

However...

Spas and AUG seem to be overpowered. Seriously, the Spas has +2 speed with -1 fire interval, the AUG has a self-bink reduction with +1 movementacc.

Minigun can actually kill stuff decently now. (It's still not very strong though)

The Minimi is totally ruined. +2 movementacc on top of it's 2, the highest out of the autos, making it a whopping 4, and -1 damage, with only less self-bink?! What the hell, man...

There's a supposed hit-registration system fix, but I seem to still get away with M79 hits sometimes...

Also, I seem to be doing a lot worse than I used to on 1.3.1... I got a K:D of like 4:10 on a server with decent people, I couldn't seem to hit anything. Maybe I'm just not used to the weapons yet...
