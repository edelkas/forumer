The game was inspired by N and it unique use of physics. I read the tutorials a while ago to learn and I made some basic physics demos. But now im using what I learned to make this game.

I'm hoping for this game to be a fast paced 2D side scrolling shooter survival game, but it depends on how the programming goes as to what the final product will be. Enemies will be comming at you fast attacking, shooting, jumping and you will have to survive.

1. The brown tiles are the basic ground, and the blue tiles are ice, so they're slippery.

2. You get out of the water by pressing the jump button repeatedly (mario style).

3. Right now its just a demo of the physics and guns, very early stage. But I don't mind showing what im currently working on.

The graphics are nothing right now, I got tired of taking twice as long to make a game becuase I had to do my own graphics. So I am just making the game, working on the fun factor. And if anyone decides to help and make graphics it will end up looking ten times better.

I plan on updating the game tonight so check back later.
