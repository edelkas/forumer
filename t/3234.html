Quote: glenno14, May 19 2005, 03:19 PM
mgs 4!? why havent I heard about this yet!

Because E3 is where the people you expect to hear these things from first hear about it first. E3 (which stands for electronic entertainment expo, for those who care) is where all the major companies making or publishing games show off what they're making at the time, often with the first glimpses (or mention) of a game in hopes that their unveiling will attract more attention than the others and always with new demos and screenshots. Its basically an industry-wide publicity competition, where everyone competes to make the most gaming magazine editors say "whoa, I'm devoting a page to that one for sure." They may show off a demo from a partially made game, or a new graphics card, or a new physics demo, or even systems. Anything that'll get them some mention.

Its a wonder that most gaming magazines don't make the issue right after E3 twice as large as normal.
