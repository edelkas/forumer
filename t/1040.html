However, in hindsight, I think that Least Shot at is a bit too narrow, and I'll tell ya why. Initially, I had wanted broader categories, and I think that Least Shot at is kind of the proverbial "Odd Man Out", in that, we have categories such as "Speed", and "Style", and then this narrow category tacked on to the end. The way I see it, It just seems a bit out of place.

So at the end of each week, say during the weekend (our judging time) we vote for our favorite demo in each catagory, most votes win."

I think that this has the merit of reducing the confusion in multiple demo posts like we're having now, as it would also reduce the level of confusion when someone wants to resubmit demo data 11 times because everyone keeps doing better than him/her, especially when they don't follow the guidelines of posting in one place, which no one really has yet. In addition, if we only get a handful of submissions for the week, so long as we have three, we can declare winners, as well as award points in all categories, instead of having gaps in the scoring system.

And for those who don't wish to read all of my word-vomit above, here's my two cents:

Taking into account that I think we're doing away with the points from here on out, I do agree with Brickman that this is nigh-impossible. However, I like the idea that a person can do a fast, reckless run, get shot at 50 times, and get to the exit without getting all gold, and at the same time, a person can do a slow, methodical run, never get spotted, and finish with all gold, and both players can still win a first place ranking in two of the categories.
