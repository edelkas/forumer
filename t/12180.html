Half-a-Map-Each Comp


The Half-a-Map-Each Competition is here. I believe this is a totally new concept in Contests.

HAM comp will consist of 3 rounds. In each round, participants will make a map that occupies all of the top half/right half of the map. Those maps will not have an exit. The second part of the round is when the maps submitted are distributed among the participants. You will not get your own map. The distributed maps are to be finished and will be judged (judging is explained further below).

Judges

There will be only 2 judges. I will need two quick, experienced judges. You will be expected to have your judgings in quite quickly. (5-8 days)

Uber-Simplified Procedure

Will be updated soon.

Judging

Judging for the maps is quite simple, really. the average of the 3 judges' score is the score out of 30.

Each map will be judged on the following:

*/10 tileset
*/10 playability
*/10 individual contribution (your half of the map) this score may be different from your partner for that round. it is based on how playable and how good the tileset is on your side of the map.

your score will be added together every round.
