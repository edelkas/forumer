The Map Naming Competition
[/u]

The idea of this contest is not to make a MAP, but think of a map NAME for an already made map.
I will post a screen shot of a map, and the person who comes up with the *best* name within a week wins that week. This is a lot like the Caption Contest, with an endless amount of weeks, and a new winner each week. The prizes have yet to be announced, but it may include either a ded map or sigtag for 1st place.

There is no contestant list, you can just PM me the name anytime you want.
Judges: notkitt, Release

The week one image will be posted up soon, this week begins today and ends the 11th of March, 11:59 GMT.

Have fun!
