#1 Isn't a very good response, you know. Although you were trying to help him, I believe you're doing it the wrong way.

To save levels in Ned, Pate_Rake, get the data in Ned from the lower box. It's shown how to get the data in the Ned menu, it's really self-explanatory. Select all and copy the data. Then, open Notepad, (I'm only assuming you're running Windows) and paste the data there, and then save the file. When you want to load the data again, simply select all and copy the data in the file, then open ned, and paste it into the lower box. Press L to load the data, and you have your level ready for editing/playtesting. Make sure you click outside of the box before loading the data with L, or else you will get a NaN, which is a glitch in N level code.
