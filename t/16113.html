This thread is to share maps that you think are earth-shatteringly awesome that didn't get enough attention their first time around.

The main goal of this thread is to maybe find a gem that you never saw before that you might add to your favorite maps.

Don't link to a map that everyone and their mother has already seen and played. Yes, I know Flight and Domo-kun FTW! are awesome maps, but everyone already knows that.

Please, DO not do not do NOT do not do not DO NOT just link to a map using the newest cool looking tileset trend. This thread is about maps that are your most favorite ever, that will probably never ever leave your top ten favorite maps no matter what ever gets made.
