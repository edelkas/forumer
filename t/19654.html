#370167 3 2007-10-07T01:42:20Z player03
Glitched tiles: there are actually two types of these- tiles that appear to be there but don't stop you, and tiles which can propel you in unpredictable directions.

Type 1: These can be formed by putting almost any unrecognized character into the level data. They have the appearance of whatever tile was in that place last, but they do nothing. See Surrealism for an example of this.

Type 2: These can be formed by putting in a ^ into N. They have the appearance of a vertical half-tile, but they interact with N in strange ways (i.e., stopping him, propelling him, and/or killing him). See any Glitchtastic level for examples.
