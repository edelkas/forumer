My dad comes home in his Jeep, and I freak out. I leave the screen up(thankfully with the board index up, no communication), and dash to our pool table and hide behind it. He opens the door to the computer room, then shuts it and heads to the kitchen.

So, I figure, He's going to be coming back any minute! Now's my only chance! So I sprint to the door to his smoking lounge, and through a door, and go outside, knocking a fan over by accident on the way.

Now, I think he might be after me. So, outside, I unlock a little door leading to the crawl space underneath the house. Very uncomfortable down there. I lay there for, what, fifteen minutes maybe? Then I hear him leave the kitchen and go to the computer room, and sit on his recliner and watch the T.V. I slip through a narrow opening and down into our basement. I head for the foot of the stairs by the back door, and accidentally step in a basket, bending a piece of scrap metal and making audible noise.

So, I rush to one of the two rooms in the basement, shut the door, and lay by a desk, with my feet squeezed in a discarded wooden chair, with a lawn chair in front of my head. If Dad found me, I'd be finished.

Every so often I check upstairs to see if he's still watching the TV, or if he's asleep.

At 4 in the morning I successfully slink upstairs and fall asleep.
