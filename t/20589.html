OK...
So, I was just making my own test level with bounceblocks and a homing turret, when the turret approached me. I waited so that I can jump to freedom at the last moment, but hit right instead. I was at the edge of the bounceblock. I thought I was dead, but the turret went right past me instead of killing me. I waited there for a few seconds more, and watched as I saw the turret orbit 3 times before killing me.

So. The main goal of this competition is to get the rocket (the turret is hidden underneath the bounceblock) to orbit around you as many times as possible while you stay still, only moving left and right.

RULES:
GENERAL
1.1. You must report the number of times you jump off the top of the bounceblock, but you are allowed to walljump as many times as needed.
1.2. Land safely on the bounceblock. You can then only move left and right, so that the rocket orbits around you.
WHILE ORBITING
2.1. If the rocket hits you, it's game over.
2.2. If you fall off the bounceblock, game over.
2.3. If the rocket explodes, it's obvious. The next one will hit you, so game over.
AFTER PLAYING
3.1. Make sure you have the demo recorded.
3.2. PM me the demo, along with the number of times you jumped off the top of the bounceblock.
3.3. Please have the txt file in this format: $###leveldata#demodata
3.4. In my reply, I will tell you your current position and your score.

Level data attached.
