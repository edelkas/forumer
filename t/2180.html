#48995 12 2005-02-01T22:02:44Z brickman
Warning: slightly off topic

Am I the only one who thinks that, graphics aside, exile was a bit better than Avernum? Well, sure, avernum had features exile didn't, like people joining the party and more quests, but I think there were more areas in which I preferred exile than Avernum (notice, however, that I have only played exile 3, but have played all 4 Avernums and gone through everything in the free version of all but #3, and the one I spent the most time with of the Avernums was 1 so some of this may not be accurate for the others). Like the magic system--what were they THINKING? I mean, exile's magic system was PERFECT, forcing you to travel and find multitudes of people and perform multitudes of quests if you wanted every one of the numerous and varied (somewhat) spells, but then in Avernum, not only can you get half the spells in the game from the same person (or just by starting with high spell skill), but they only have one spell per level (on average, some have none) and no way to directly buy spell points. The system of spell levels ended up getting warped so badly that if you didn't buy a spell first and you got an improved version as a quest reward or from a spellbook you literally couldn't learn it ("you mean that you can't teach me the level 3 spell I just earned by saving your life because I haven't been taught level 1? Can't you teach me level one first then?"). Plus, the area of effect spells were replaced by "multitarget spells" which completely ruin the balance (especially in the first Avernum), because you don't even have to use any strategy and can zap spellcasters on opposite ends of the battlefield. And what happened to scrying in the third game? It was one of my favorite things in exile 3, but they didn't even bother in any of the avernums. One of the biggest other things they did wrong was making maps and food both unnecessary (seriously, who told them that only needing to eat one unit of food when you do completely unnecessary and useless "rests" made more sense than having the whole party eat every day?) and less desirable to collect. I don't care if holding 500 rations is unrealistic if the alternative is having a week's worth of food take up enough space to reduce your carrying capacity by about 50 gold worth of stuff per trip, and there's NO REASON FOR MAPS TO NOT COUNT AS SPECIAL ITEMS. And while having different shops offer different prices for your things does add a nice element, having shops only accept certain items in exile is even better, because there were only a few places to unload certain loot types and every town offered something different. And of course there's the issue of identification, which is a joke in avernum because you automatically have item lore of 20 without raising a single point of stats that increase it there, while in exile it only went up if you paid for it and thus the sages were very important.

Seriously, if it would let me I would have exile installed on this computer, even though I can just as easily take avernum, simply because they took three steps forward and 17 steps back when they did avernum.
