I'm gonna give you some tips:

First off though, Your first map was decent. Wasn't as noobish as I expected it to be.
The second map though. Displayed a huge problem with your gold. You want your gold to be in challenging places, but not that clumped together. It should be spread out throughout the map, and should help make the map more pleasing to the eye. Not only the gold though, but also all the other enemies, and more (kinda) importantly, the tiles.

No spamming objects. Like the gold. There shouldn't be too many, or too little of things in your map, although you might want to not use some type of enemy or object, which is ok. Just go search on nmaps.net, where you are able to sign up using the N executable file. Just go to configure, type your desired username, pass, and email, then click "new user" where you will then have a brand new account on nmaps.net where you can post your maps.

I hope That was enough...
