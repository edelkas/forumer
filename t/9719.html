#176524 3 2006-05-16T03:39:35Z LittleViking
There are exactly two things that could be happening here.

1) You're submitting it with all the userlevels data. i.e. $Title#Author#Category#[data]# If you are, you only need to submit the data part. Exactly what you copy from Ned, you submit to Numa.

2) Somehow, your tile data is incorrect. This is less common, but it will give the "corrupt data" message as well. There have to be exactly 713 characters before the | in the data. If you want to, PM me your leveldata and I'll tell you whether or not it's okay.
