http://www.rasterwerks.com/

Phosphor is a first-person shooter developed in Macromedia Director. This means it runs in a Shockwave Player, right in a browser, and it should be multiplatform. The game looks just as good as some of the popular older shooters like Unreal, Quake 3, Half-Life and Counter-Strike, and I was getting good framerates (anywhere from 30-90 fps, usually about 60). You can choose to play against bots or set up a server and go against against other people online. Since it's still in beta, there aren't many levels or game modes to pick from (only Deathmatch on one level, I think), but it's still cool to check out what can be done all in a browser.
