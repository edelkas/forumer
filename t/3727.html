#80119 9 2005-06-30T22:56:45Z Arachnid
Math.Pi is a member variable, not a function, so it's no slower than having a local variable or constant. Pi is only specified to that precision, because that's the limit of precision for the floating point numbers used.

Calling sin() 100 times a second isn't exactly going to stress anything. You'd need to be calling it far more often than that for it to be much of an issue.
