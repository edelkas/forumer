Here's a step by step guide to create an account on NUMA:

1. Launch N.
2. In the Configure menu, create your account at the bottom right.
3. Then go to NUMA and attempt to log in.
4. You will then be sent an activation email to your chosen account.
5. Open up the activation email. (Email services such as Hotmail and Yahoo sometimes identifies the activation email as junk, so be sure to check your junk folder as well.)
6. After activating your account, you should be ready to go!
