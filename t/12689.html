#231097 2 2006-09-20T10:47:00Z L3X
Load the level in neditor (press "~" in the main menu), and after loading the level, paste the demo data into the box, press the button for beginning demo playback, press caps lock, and continue playing level.
Or paste the level in the userlevels box, put a "#" after it, and paste the demo data afterwards
