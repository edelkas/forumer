  • When one of your maps have 4 ratings, your number of rated maps goes up one, but the map in question isn't nor is supposed to be listed.

  • A tag is required to submit a new map
  • People with fewer than 10 maps have result truncation[/color]
  • In Opera 9.5, the "hover over the image for a full-size version" doesn't work right - the image expands to the left and goes off the page.
  • Thumbnails do not register.
  • Submitting a map onto another NUMA Users profile.
  • Rating twice on maps submitted before the change
