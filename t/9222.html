This exact same thing happened to me last night but it turned out that I had left the "#" both on the beginning and the end of the level data, which is why my level wouldn't load because they aren't a part of the level but rather just serve as separators between information in your userlevel.txt, or whatever it is called, file. You might want to check and make sure that your "#" things are absent from both the start and the end of your data.
