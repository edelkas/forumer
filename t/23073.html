So I've been around on numa since 2004 (my earliest map is number 844 on the numa database) but haven't posted regularly for a long time.
I've got quite a back-log of maps but unfortunately for a number of reasons they often don't get played. I think I may post at unusual times for the rest of the numa community, being from Australia, which means they're often overlooked and then bumped back a few pages. Now I love feedback even if I can be known to take negative feedback poorly, but I do come across a fair few voters who will give me a poor rating for the obvious reason that some of my maps can be difficult. Any way I ramble..

Please, browse my maps in the archive (though not too far back, there's alot of junk in there too ). I did make them to be played by more than just myself and over the years I've been my biggest fan... Here are some I'm particularly proud of:

Out Hud
A fairly minimalist jumper puzzle. It's not too difficult to complete, but what little there is does still effectively add to the challenge. Don't fall off!

Gardenia
Not too sure entirely how to describe this one. Mostly an action map, but it's not frantic.

Perception
Another jumper puzzle. This one is more difficult but definately slower paced.

Arch Carrier
An action/puzzle map. This one I felt particularly overlooked, but I atleast enjoy it.

Lowride
A drone puzzle map. Jump between the drones at the right time to advance.

Parallel
A race map. Race through the tight course to beat the drone to the exit!

Magmoor
An enclosed mine-jumper puzzle. It's pretty rough, though each individual bit doesn't take long to master.
