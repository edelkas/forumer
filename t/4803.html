#4378 1 2005-09-11T23:47:10Z Maniac
Here are four more things to add to the "things to potentially add to user mods" pile.
  1. A switch that turns all one-way walls on and off. A switch that turns one one-way wall on and off would be nice too.
  2. A switch that reverses the direction of all one-way walls. Again, a switch that reverses the direction of one one-way wall would also be nice.
  3. Ditto, except toggling one-way walls (turning on walls off, turning off walls on)
  4. Obviously, seeing the previous three suggestions, an option for a one-way wall that has it off at the start of the map.
Just four things which I don't think would be _too_ hard, and would greatly contribute to mapmaking.
