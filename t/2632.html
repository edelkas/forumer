Did you read the interviews with us linked from our press page? they've certainly got some answers.


we originally made a NinjaEngine, which you can still find around the internet -- basically we played around with a number of different parts, like the physics, the collision, etc., until we decided it was time to just put everything together and get a game started.

We didn't really have a cohesive idea of what N would be, initially. We had a good idea of what the gameplay would be like and feel like though, and a good idea of what type of game it would be.

And then: inspiration struck us.

As one of those interviews mentions, it's actually a true story, based on the life and experiences of one of our friends. It was perfectly suited to a game, really.
