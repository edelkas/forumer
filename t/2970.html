we used mostly flash mx; really there isn't much of a difference between that and mx2004 other than mx2004 has the _very_ useful "alias font" option.

if you know how to program, flash/actionscript is very easy to learn, just download the demo from www.macromedia.com and read through the "actionscript dictionary"; it's 99% identical to javascript.

if you don't know any programming, then learning that will be your first challenge. highschool or university intro-to-programming classes are probably the best bet... then again, the internet is getting better and better, i'm sure if you google "intro to programming" you'll get some nice tutorials, and/or university course webpages.
