NTTS Contest Week 7!

Welcome to week seven of the Numa Time Trial Society! Now, with color! Our main purpose is to encourage Forum Members to play more User-made maps through Numa.
If you're new to the NTTS, comprehensive rules can be found here.

First off, you guys need to step it up a notch or two to catch up with LV, especially Knu, wherever he's flown off to. He's taken the lead with last week's scores, and we're heading into the second half of the season, so get it up!

Here are your NTTS judges for Week 7:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
LittleViking001, And
Myself

And as always, here are the rules for Week 7:
  • This week's map is Up Down, by Maximo.
  • Any Forum member can post their demo data for this week's map in this forum. The above judges will review each member's demo data and at the end of the week, we will declare a winner.
  • Each contestant is allowed to post Up to two demos for the week. Competitors are allowed to post any time from the beginning of this thread until 23:59 PST (GMT -8) on Friday evenings, but it may be to your advantage to post later in the week.
  • Submissions begin now, and will end at 23:59 PST (GMT -8) on Friday evenings. If you don't know when PST is, PM me, and I'll let you know.
  • Judging will begin Saturday Morning, and the points will be posted Sunday Evening, just prior to next week's contest.
Good luck, and happy playing!
