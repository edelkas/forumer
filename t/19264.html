Basically, this is an experience point game. Yes, I know that these are overused, and yet I am still using them. Basically, you will be choosing a character type to play as, and, if you want, you can form allies. That is where it gets interesting.

Rules:

1. Before you join, you must PM me
2. You can join any time you like, but you must start with the lowest stats possible.
3. WHEN YOU FORM ALLIES, BOTH YOU AND THE ALLY LEADER MUST PM ME TELLING ME. BOTH OF YOU.
4. The team leader is chosen, at first, by the two or so people who start the ally, and then will be chosen later by the person with the highest stats. If you die, then you will start all over with the same stats you had in the beginning, unless if you want to change your character.
5. We will not start until I get at least two groups (or a group and a person), so whoever starts off will have a better chance at surviving if you start from the beginning.
6. ABSOULUTELY NO DOUBLE POSTING unless if your name is turtle.
7. Every time you post, you must attack, or buy something from me, or at least do something that is not spam, or you will lose five points. This is because of the long-lasting pain that will reach people. See below for more details. Backstabbing your team is allowed. However, your team captain will likely kick you off if you do. I realise you can ruin the entire game by doing this.

How does it work?
Basically, you have two different attacks, and a certain amount of defense. I will be playing, but I am the medicine man/the marketer. So if you get poisoned and need help, or if you are just cut and need a bandaid, you need to come to me and give me a certain amount of your 'experience' in order for you to not die. If you end up not coming to me within a certain amount of posts, you will find that you lose one or more health for every post, or every other post, or so, depending on your health problem.

What are things that will cause me to go to the medicine man?

Easy to cure, not hard to fix

Small cut: you need a bandaid. Every five posts that you dont get one, you lose one health point.

Broken bone: You need a cast. Every two posts that you dont get one, you lose a point.

Stubbed toe: You need an ice pack. Every four posts that you dont get one, you lose one health point

Temporary blindness: You need shades. You can keep shades, but you may not share them. Every two posts that you dont get one, you lose a point, and your team captain loses a point. If you are the team captain, you lose two points.

Temporary deafness: You need to either stay out of this thing for a WEEK, or you need to pay me.

Itchiness: You need skin cream. You can use skin cream five times in total, or share it, so long as the total is five. You lose a point every three posts if you fail to apply the cream. And if you want to use someone else's, you must first ask me.

Medium, can be dangerous
(You ought to PM me to insure that I dont forget about this if you want to stay alive with these)

Open cut: You lose a point for every post until you get a bandaid.

Bad breath: Everyone on your ally loses a point for every five posts until you get a pack of breathmints. Or you buy mint gum, but that is more expensive.

Stuffy nose: May turn into a cold if you don't buy enough liquids.

Bad hair day: Will cause temporary blindness to somebody in your group every five posts until you buy a comb, which you can keep, but you cannot share.

Sliver: you lose five points for every four posts unless if you buy tweezers which you can keep, but cannot share.

Confusion: every other attack that you throw will hurt someone on your own team, so you should buy a TV, which will be useful for your ENTIRE group because you can all use it.

Deadly Diseases:
(you are probably going to be dead if you get these early on in the game)

Common cold: You need cough sirrup. It only works once, and only you can use it. Every post that you dont have it, you lose a point. Every other post, you lose a point, AND one of your team members gets it. Just imagine how this thing can multiply, so you need to buy cough sirrup quickly.

Open wound (large): You need a BIG bandaid. Otherwise, you lose one post after two turns, and it doubles for every other turn until you die.

Spinal/cranial damage: You are out for a week unless if you buy surgery, which takes a day. And if you are out for a week, imagine what your opponents can do to you.

Poison: You need an antidote, or you lose three points every turn. If you have an antidote bought already, you dont need to say that you are using it.
