#169183 7 2006-04-28T10:05:32Z Atilla
Yes. "Algorithm" means a predetermined set of rules/instuctions for completing a task or solving a problem. In this case, the algorithms for pathfinding are "Go straight until you hit a non-empty tile, then turn clockwise" and so forth. The algorithm for baking a cake would be turn the oven on, mix the ingredients, etc. But I digress.

Anyway... 96-4 has plenty of zap drones. Note how they go in different paths around the tiles, and treat the doors as a solid barrier. 86-1 has a nice display of drones constrained to a rectangular path. 80-1 shows a prisoner-type level. 85-4 is another one with lots of drones, syncronised this time. 95-4 also has many drones. There are plenty of others, but those should be enough to get started. Er, although I realise they're mostly a bit late in the game. They're just the ones I remember because I've played them more recently.
