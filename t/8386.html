Parsec 47 is another Kenta Cho scrolling shoot-em-up. The controls are rather familiar, in that you shoot lots of weak bullets with Z and a charged strong shot with X. But here, the graphics and music are even more retro than usual, the gameplay is divided into Parsecs (which allow you to save and start again at every ten Parsecs), and you encounter retro bosses with the usual huge barrage of bullets. It's fun
