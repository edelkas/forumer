#241115 12 2006-10-14T21:11:45Z Tsukatu
I don't think that most people who have anything better than a TI-82 fully deserve them. I have a TI-83+, and I've learned just about every facet of its functionality, with the exception of some boring finance/statistical commands that I never bothered to learn. I've made Yahtzee and Battleship with full GUI's in BASIC. I can do more with my TI-83+ than any of my classmates who have a better model. It just doesn't benefit you, and you may as well go with a more basic model if you're not using it for something as advanced.
I cringe when I hear some freshmen brag about having TI-89's for their geometry classes. Even a TI-82 will have more than they'll ever use throughout their high school years, and probably a year or two into college (and it could be all they'd ever need if they're not majoring or minoring in math or a technical science).
If you're using a TI-83+, a TI-84, TI-86, or TI-89 as a frickin' scientific calculator, you've blown a ridiculous amount of money. The TI-82 is a very, very powerful device and likely has more than you'll ever need, but people love big numbers. Higher model numbers are just insane new features for math majors. How many of you people with TI-89's actually use their 3D graphing functions? Do you even know what a derivative or an integral is (which the TI-82 handles just dandily)?

If you're interested in games, save money by getting a TI-82 and a gameboy. Don't disgrace a TI-89 Titanium by using it for trigonometry and games.

"I am UltraBot 73000. I can conquer galaxies. Kindly point to the planet you wish me to obliterate."
"Hey, yeah, can you do the dishes? Thanks."
"Er... yes, I am capable of something as simple as dish-washing. There, dishes done. Can I enslave some ancient races, or what? If you let me think for 20 seconds, I can draw up plans for a fully functional cold fusion reactor. Would you like me to?"
"Sing me a song!"
"*sigh* I love you, you love me, let's conquer a galaxy..."
"Dance!"
*plods around*
"Bring me lemonade!"
"I can design a delicious smoothie perfectly suited to your body's nutritional needs. Should I?"
"Lemonade!"

I want to see TI make an artificially intelligent calculator that can insult the user if he doesn't use its advanced features frequently.
