As you all know, I was in Florida for a week about a few weeks ago. During said time, I had access to a Windows computer.

Warning Forever

Download it here

Awesome, awesome, awesome to the power of twelve. This game boils down the entire genre of "your ship shoots other things" to the most potentially awesome part, the boss fights, dueling the huge ship with you the puny one. In each phase, you take on a boss ship with multiple destroyable parts, weapons and a core. After defeating it, you get points and bonuses, and then you move on to the next one.

Oh, but it's not that simple. Each boss gets harder than the last, obviously, but they evolve in response to whatever you focus on. Spending a lot of time getting rid of the lasers so you won't die so often? The next one has more. Going for the core quickly? The connecting points by it are gonna get much stronger. The bosses can also get a bunch of arms to swat at you with, several deadly weapons, and even boosters to just slam into you and destroy you. It's an awesome game. Get it now.

Jumper Two

Download it here!

This game is a real gem. You're this hideous block-shaped abomination with only one goal - to escape. According to forum legend Brickman, it's "similar to what N might look like if all of its levels were designed by Killisk." To get around from stage to stage, you've just got to do one thing. Jump.

Each stage poses several puzzles to impede you from your goal, and as you solve the stages, the environment changes and a story is laid out. The graphics, while not amazingly super-duper-Halotastic, get the job done and still look pretty good. The music is to die for. And what with sixty included levels, and a level editor for importing your own sagas, you'll never get tired of it.

=======

Well, to be honest, those two games were so good I didn't have time to check out a third. Play them.
