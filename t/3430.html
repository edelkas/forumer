#70801 4 2005-05-30T15:50:18Z Maniac
This is a known bug.

Just make an empty level and add the following code:

6^276,324,2,0,NaN,0

The 6^ designates that this is a drone. The first two numbers are the coordinates. I forget what the next two are.

The second-to-last number says whether it's a chaingun, laser, or zap drone. Putting a NaN(Not a Number) in there causes your eye to appear.

EDIT:

Darn, too late. At least I have the explanation.

By the way, the drone won't actually do anything other than move around. Moving N into it has no effect.
