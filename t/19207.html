Would anyone be interested in playing a game of Rumble? I would be willing to mod it.

The complete rules can be found here http://kevan.org/rumble.cgi?genre=hero&mode=rules

It is kind of long, so I'll summarize it quickly. There are around 4-8 players, who all start out with 100 energy. You want to be the last one standing. Each turn, you can split up your energy attacking and defending. If you are attacked for 40, and you defend with 25, then you would lose 15 energy permanently. There are also powers which help with these kinds of things, that everyone bids on at the beginning of the game. The powers up for auction are chosen by the players, and then all the powers are bid on, using energy. The winner of a power gets it, but loses energy based on how much they bid. A full list of powers can be found here http://kevan.org/rumble.cgi?genre=hero&mode=viewall

If you are interested, please post in the thread.
