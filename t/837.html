#724 1 2004-08-21T01:14:25Z sALTEDfISH
Three ideas:

1) Some sort of defensive posture, like crouching, that will allow you to be immune to one attack, like say, a Zap Drone. Ex: if you are being chased by two homing missiles, crouching will allow you to withstand the blow of one, but unless you get up and crouch again, the second missile will kill you. In order to make things fair and balanced, this posture would not work against a Laser Drone or a Chaingun Drone. Mines, well, of course there is no defense against them...

2) Some sort of weapon, not to destroy enemies, but to, perhaps, temperarily(sp) stun them so that you can make an escape, once per level. This would not work against projectiles, obviously. (side note: maybe some sort of mine disabling action that works once a level?)

3) Lastly, some kind of teleporting device. I mean, we've got Zap Drones, Laser Drones, Launch pads and other sorts of somewhat furturistic stuff, why not telepads? It would certainly make levels more interesting...

please post thoughtful replies, not "oh that's stupid."
