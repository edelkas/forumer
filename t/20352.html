#384247 13 2007-11-29T02:00:00Z Atilla
This has been suggested several times before, and it's not going to happen.

Reasons:
- Anything other than MIDI would drastically increase the download size.
- N works fine with a media player in the background.
- Most people would prefer their own music to anything M&R would compose anyway. Especially if it's MIDI.
- The library of music would be very small. Consider the number of hours you've played N. Imagine a two-minute track repeating. Over. And over. And over.
- M&R's time is better spent on other things.
