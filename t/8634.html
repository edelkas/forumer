#160460 17 2006-04-01T08:42:38Z ZZ9
I think the current system should go. Although we could have an alternate system based on fractions and percentages. Whne you comment on a map you can press one of three buttons, marked "Good", "bad" and "No opinion." All the maps have a fraction showing the amount of people who have an opinion and the amount of people thinking it's good. You can then turn it into a percentage.

An example: Map A uses this system and has been rated "good" by nine people. 9/9=100%. However a n00b comes along and rates Map A a "Bad" it reduces the fraction to 9/10 which is equal to 90% effectivly a 4.5. The difference comes when you have huge numbers of people rating a map. Map B has been rated good by 500 people. The 501st rates bad. The rating is now 99.8% Normaly this is reduced to 4/5 which is 90% however with this system we would get more accurate ratings since we would have 100 point values rather than 10.

The no opinion button is there so that you can comment without affecting ratings.
