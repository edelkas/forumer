More Questions That Haven't Been Asked Frequently But Undoubtably Will Be (MQTHBAFBUWB):

Q: Uh, what?
A: A large cottage made of teapots.

Q: What is the Answer to Life, the Universe, and Everything?
A: Eight less than the number of levels in this pack multiplied by the number of episodes in the pack and divided by twice the number of levels in each episode.

Q: Aaah! How am I supposed to work that out? My mathematical ability is so low it's, er... what do you call those numbers below zero again?
A: "Make an abacus out of second-hand toothbrushes" and "negative", respectively. Also, I hope you don't manage your own finances.

Q: Why isn't blue more red?
A: Because that would imply that the specific gravity of cheese is less than the finite interval of a mollusc expansion in the X-Y plane, which is clearly not the case.

Q: How difficult is this level pack?
A: Average, on average. The earlier episodes are easier and the later ones harder, of course. The later levels of each episode are also substantially harder.

Q: Is this why you haven't submitted anything to NUMA for a while?
A: Yes, mostly. Although I had a break from mapmaking for a bit.

Q: My house is infested with beavers. What should I do?
A: Contact your lawyer and send them a notice of eviction. If they fail to comply, spread rumors that they have WMDs and wait for the military to take care of it.

Q: Why isn't your first level pack in the "Levels packs" section?
A: I blame trout-flavoured icecream.

Q: Is it really necessary to write such silly descriptions?
A: Yes. Always.

Q: I still think you're a total lunatic.
A: You still can't tell the difference between a question and a statement.
