#291061 3 2007-01-28T19:39:28Z whykickamoocow
Or you could right click on the N window, then go to settings. Click the file tab at the bottom and it says how much is N allowed to store on your computer, It should say unlimited now, if you move the bar to nothing all your progress will be lost, then move the bar back to full and you will be able to play again.

Just make sure you move the bar back again.
