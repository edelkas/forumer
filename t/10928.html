#196787 5 2006-06-30T09:12:28Z sweep
Descriptions not yet put up (I'll do that once inductees are confirmed)

Coalition by LittleViking001 (written by KA)
If Convention is the epitome of littleviking001's map-making, Coalition is its sequel, the pinnacle of this great author's illustrious career. M&R included it in N 1.4 for its incredible likeness to a Metanet-authored venture, with its amazing use of tileset versatility, object placement, and multiple enemies, sans clash; coalition is a true extravaganza, and will be cemented in history as one of the community's favourite maps.
"Excellent! Another masterpiece!" (Kablizzy)

Desperado by jeffkililan
The beauty of Jeffkillian's Deseperado is that without playing it would be easy to dismiss as another run-of-the-mill bounceblock/rocket map. However, once you begin bounding about with your ninja this level comes to life - some say Desperado is the perfect action map, and it is very, very hard to find a good reason to disagree with that opinion.
"Great map... the epitome of rocket/bounceblock action" (krusch)

Fossil by Zorn
You would be hard pressed to find a better map asthesically than Zorn's eyepopping masterpiece Fossil. The Hall of Fame could never be complete without a classic Zorn map, and this is about as good as it gets. Visually stunning, fun to play, and stylish beyond belief, Fossil is a simply a classic.
"Great tileset, fun gameplay, perfect difficulty." (Red Reamer)

Twisted by Killisk
Twisted by Killisk is proof that a great map doesn't need any enemies - gravity does the job just as well. Based on a perplexing labrynth of unusual tile arrangements that make navigation harder than you expect, Twisted is a puzzle stripped down to the bare bones - quite simply superb.
"Best map ever... I heart this soooooo much" (joem)

Advanced Robotics by tktktk
Advanced Robotics by tktktk is a prisoner style map that runs on a vast, traffic-like network of drones. Level 76-4 of the Legacy Project, Advanced Robotics is yet another reason why tktktk is seen as such a talented author - style, with substance.
"I love this... one of my faves" (krusch)

Edgeplay by Sendy
It looks like a playpark, but, in reality Sendy's Edgeplay is a pure action map based on the premise of navigating around the edge of the map. The entire map only has one enemy: the rocket in the center. So simple, so effective, so very brilliant.
"This is really fantastic. The varied styles, the 'high flying' aspect... this is the type of map I'd love to make, but I can never quite get down." (stepself)

Valves by Lucidium
Valves by Lucidium is stylish, innovative, elegent and altogether puzzling. Taking advantage of both a floorguard bug and the way a floorguard interacts with the ninja, Lucidium created a simple but very effective puzzle - and ended up with a faultless, intruiging map.
"Brilliant idea, perfectly executed. 5 ninjas." (tktktk)

domo-kun ftw!! by krusch
Australian map-maker krusch's domo-kun FTW!! is an undeniably brilliant slice of minimalistic action, fused with krusch's downright sexy style. Each and every enemy is used to great effect; everything is just so damn perfect.
"This is gold. Let me put it to you this way: one of my favorite maps ever. No. Kidding." (Baraba)

Flight by maximo
When a map is one of an author's best, it is generally a good sign. When a map is one of maximo's best, it's an absolutely fanfrickingtastic sign. Flight is addictive and great fun, even by maximo's standards; full of open space, Flight is a quintessential action map, a level of legend.
"By far one of the best maps I have ever seen. I'm 100% in love with it." (tktktk)

Bubble Point by stepself
Bubble Point is yet another visually stunning map from the community treasure that is stepself (voted author the year in 2005). Simple but effective drone paths decorate the mine-ridden landscape - Bubble Point is a map to remember.
"Brilliantly crafted" (Baraba)
