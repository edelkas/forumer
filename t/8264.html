#154527 2 2006-03-14T03:51:12Z ImpotentBoy2
try to make as few launch pads as possible. instead try using thwumps and trap doors that pop up right behind you.
try making it so that areas are reused with different outcomes each time, maybe from doors and traps.
try to put as many enemies on there that almost kill you.
try being more creative with your tile sets the your objects
and start off with hold right or hold left only DDA, so that is easier to get him moving.
