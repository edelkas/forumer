Quote: Sucker, Jan 23 2006, 05:06 AM
Sprites are game characters you can use for movies, images, et cetera.

They don't have to be game characters... it's just certain type of animated, 2D object, generally a small bitmap-esq image with a transparent background. In programming, sprites are seperate and handled differently to scenery, backgrounds and suchlike.

Can you import them into N? Not really. N users vector graphics (Obects are a group of points with lines drawn between them and sometimes colours in enclosed places, rather than a bitmap-type thing like sprites). I think there *is* a mario skin around somewhere, but it only works with one level and requires you to run a seperate program.
