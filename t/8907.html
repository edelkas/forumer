#8009 1 2006-04-12T09:45:34Z sweep
Jumper puzzles. They're just not fun. I'm sorry... They're frustrating and boring. And yet so many people seem to make them. Why? Because it seems like everyone loves them.

I was just talking to Atramentis about his new jumper puzzle, Tip. I gave this map 3/5 because I simply didn't find it fun enough to warrant anything higher. Talking to Atramentis about this, I discovered that he doesn't like jumper puzzles either - he didn't make this map for himself, he made it for others. My vote being the 5th vote, it revealed an average of 4.5/5.

I don't believe that NUMA members honestly like jumper puzzles that much. Although I'm not that good at N, I've been around for long enough to become pretty damn good in comparison to the majority of NUMA users. If I find most jumper puzzles hard and unenjoyable, I'd expect them to think the same.

I think it's a case of a mystical air surrounding these maps, a belief that they are some kind of fine art, a popular belief that everyone seems to share. As a result, anyone who doesn't enjoy playing them is too worried to give an honest rating, and just slaps a 4.5/5 on it.

People are no longer giving honest ratings. If you don't enjoy playing a map, then you shouldn't really be giving it a 4.5/5. The sheep of NUMA are following the crowd, and although fun maps get high ratings, the less enjoyable maps often seem to get high ratings on the basis of the myth surrounding them.

I felt compelled to post this; now I would like your opinions.

Ok, I should probably clarify on my points. Maps should be judged on what is applicable. In N Arts, you judge it on how good looks. People who say "meh, I look at it for 10 seconds and then get bored" and therefore give it a 2 - that's not valid. You're treated an N Art in comparison to an Action map, and they're just not the same thing.

However, puzzle maps are designed to be fun. That's the idea. I will rate one higher if it shows a greater degree of mapmaking skill, but ultimately I don't find them enjoyable. Puzzle maps belong to the genre of maps that is attempting to make you have fun, and therefore they should be rated as such. I appreciate some people enjoy them - then they can go ahead and rate them as such. But some people don't. They should rate honestly.

There's a reason the ratings system works on averages. This is something people seem to miss out. The idea is that you get the average opinion of the community, and if the community who don't like something are either muted or are pretending to like it then that's ruining the system. I know of several authors now that are creating jumper puzzle maps despite the fact that they don't enjoy playing them... I mean, that's completely ridiculous.

Also, in my original post I implied all jumpers are bad. That's not true, to be fair. There are a small number of jumpers that I love, and I do appreciate the worksmanship on others, but my point is that all of them seem to get 4.5/5 regardless of the fact that half the community doesn't find the majority of them fun at all. It doesn't make sense.

Let's attach ratings to these people. Those who love jumper puzzles give 5/5 on average. Those who like some jumper puzzles give 3/5 on average. Those who don't like any give 1/5 on average. Now, let's figure out what this would give the average jumper puzzle:

5 +5
10 +5
15 +5
20 +5
25 +3
28 +3
31 +3
34 +3
37 +1
= 38
38/10 = 3.8 ninjas out of 5.

See my point? Things even out. There's a reason the ratings system works on averages. The ratings system is there to sift out the maps you will enjoy from the maps you don't enjoy.
