N, I wish i knew
----------------

N, I wish i knew
Why i played you out of the blue.
It's not that your annoying
But why?

Metanet, I wonder how
You thought of such an amazing game
Metanet please take a bow
But why or am i just lame?

Numa, I wonder why
You go to all the bother
Because no one buys
I wonder why, tell me my brothers.

I just cannot see
for the life of me
why i continue to play this game
I think it's the fear of being lame.

I play this game,
I thinks it always the same.
But somehow
The makers must take a bow.

The thing is
I do truly know
Though i keep writing but
............

This game is an addiction
Even more so that you must continue to play
This is my prediction
Most of the people that read this have played N half a day.

But still we continue to play
and create.
We talk to those with the same addiction
I am one of those in my prediction.

Raigan and Mare i thank you
for making such an impact.
I thank you all through
The good and the bad. In fact,

I can't think of one of those who read this
Who are not nice.
The N community is probably the best one
We stick together and fight for what is right.


Thank you N community,
BumBumMilkToffee
