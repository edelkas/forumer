You are going to split up into two different groups chosen by you, and play the survivor game, somewhat like the TV show.

Okay, there must be between 10-50 people total. I would like an even number, but we can live without it. Okay, you can choose your groups, as I mentioned earlier, and you should choose your best friends on the forum. I will not be playing, so I dont mind that you wont choose me.

Just like in the TV show, you will have certain tasks you need to complete (such as demoing a certain map with a completion), and the group that has the most amount of people who dont complete this task will be sent to tribal council.

Before then, I will give you guys a day or two do lie to each other with PMs, making up fake plans with each other, such as pretending to want to vote off one person, but actually wanting to vote off another, etc. Then, during tribal council, you will all (in your groups) PM me your votes. Whoever gets the most votes gets voted off.

I'm not sure what to do in case of a tie, so if someone could give me an idea for that, I would greatly appreciate it.

As soon as there are only ten people left in total, the groups will merge. As soon as there are only three people left, the previous people who got out will come back and will be able to ask you guys questions WHICH MUST BE SENT BY REPLYING HERE, NOT BY PM'S, and then they will choose who to vote to be the winner.

For this, I am still not sure what to do in case of a tie.

I want to make sure that a few people are interested before I go on, so reply!
