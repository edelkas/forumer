#359007 15 2007-08-24T14:16:48Z Ethrel
People do change constantly, however that is also what we call 'growing up'. However in regards to actions and personailities that are different from the natural course taken by each individual during the course of said 'growing up', very few do actually change. I know from personal experience that people CAN change, though it takes a lot of effort and usually has a meaningful reason behind it. The biggest part of that is the person needs to fully and truly see whatever aspect of them they want to change and be completely honest with at least themselves and then have a driving want to change it, as well as the willpower to carry out whatever may be necessary to effect the change they want to see in themselves. And then the willpower to force themselves to act and behave against whatever they are trying to change, which in itself is the hardest part of changing.

However, that said, people cannot change other people, but having strong support while changing does help it along a lot, and without the support of other people, changing yourself is very hard. Other people play a large part in someone changing. Though one person may not be able to change another person, that person could be the key, if you will, to 'unlock' the change, thus making them essential to the process, and thus in a convoluted way they did change the other person, because without them, the change would not have happened.
