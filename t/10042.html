Hello, friend! I will help you get your Userlevels up!

First, make sure all the data in your userlevels.txt is correct. Make sure the levels are in this format:
$(Level name)#(Author of level)#(type of level)#(Level data)#

Once you've got that, make sure Userlevels.txt is in the same place as N.exe. For example, they could both be on your desktop, but not in seperate locations.

Hope this helps!
