I had a really crazy day today. It started out alright - My friends and I went to a friend's house, we saw a movie. We went outside for a walk and found a shopping cart in front of my friend's house, and decided to mess around with it.
My friend jumped in and I was pushing him around. We were going down a small downhill section when it hit a bump and I accidentally let go of the shopping cart. It's going relatively quickly, and turns towards a street sign, and ends up whacking into it, my friend still inside. Dazed, he gets out of the shopping cart. He's fine. A fairly obese woman near us has a dog. She just locked up her shop a couple of yards from the street sign.
"You assholes!" She yells at us. She seems to like the word asshole. She approaches my friend - the girl whose house we were at in the first place.
"You asshole. You guys are all assholes. You know, you could have broken your head open. You could have hurt someone."
My friend retaliates. "I'm sorry for what happened, but I had nothing to do with it. I wasn't pushing the cart or anything, just walking with my friends."
She says, "But they're your friends, right? So you're an asshole too."
The three other people with us leave towards a school playground we like messing around in.
I approach the woman. "This was totally my fault, and I take full responsibility. It was an accident, and I just dropped the cart. I'm really sorry, but it seems no damage was done, right?"
"You guys are such assholes!" Gee. She's persistent, eh?
My friend says, "Alright, fine, we're complete and total assholes because we made an honest mistake, right?" She gets called an asshole again. "Right, I'll just get it tattooed on my forehead, alright, 'I'm an asshole!'"
She retaliates. "Why are you giving me this sarcasm? I don't need this. What's your mother's name?"
"I'm not going to tell you her name."
"Yeah, that's because you know you're an asshole."
"Look, I'm sorry for what happ--" she gets interrupted.
"I don't want an apology."
"Well... What do you want us to say?"
"Admit that you're assholes."
I cut in. "Fine, we're assholes. Whatever, you can say whatever you like about us, but it's not true. Nobody and nothing was harmed, right? But that's great, we're total assholes."
We walk away from each other, she's still yelling at us, calling us assholes.
We screw around in the school yard, and get bored, so start walking back towards our friend's house. We still have the cart. We pass assholelady. She approaches the same friend again from behind.
"You know if you hit a car or anything you're going to be in serious trouble. That cart doesn't belong to you, it's illegal. I might call the cops."
I jump in again, saying, "Yeah, the grocery store is right up there. We're returning it now."
"Shut up you fucking liars!"
At this point another friend walks up, and says, "what right do you have to call us liars? We didn't do anything other than a simple mistake."
"I can call you whatever the fuck I want to, thank you very much!" She lunges at him, and he runs across the street. It's only a couple feet wide. He just wants to get away from the assholelady. She falls down onto her dog because she missed him when she lunged, and it looks very, very feigned. She gets up, yelling and cursing at the kid. She runs across the street towards him. "You fucking tripped me, you fucking asshole!" She grabs him around the collar, screaming at him.
"No, I didn't fucking do anything." He gets out of her grasp and gets safely a couple of feet away from her. We start going down the street, and she's following us, calling us punk-ass kids, saying she's going to call the cops. By this time we've basically forgotten about the cart. I don't even remember where we left it. She's still following us. I manage to escape, and walk down another street with another friend that escaped. We make it to the end of the block, and call them. The lady isn't with them any more. We turn the corner, and she's coming right towards us. We run away.
We get back to the rest of the group. Apparently, the whole time, she was yelling at them about something or other. These two young women walk up and tell us, "Oh my god, that woman was crazy. We were sticking around to make sure she didn't kill you or something."
We say thanks, and walk away. We realize that she commited third degree assault. We decide to report her to the cops. Apparently, you need to meet the cops in person to report something, so we called them and asked them to meet us on a street corner. It takes them 35 minutes to arrive. They were undoubtedly stopping for donuts.
When they arrive, we tell the story. "We'll keep our eyes out for her," is what they say. They drive off. That's it. That's all the fucking remorse we get for being attacked by some crazy lady.

I was afraid she was going to eat me. *shivers*
