The Metanet Coliseum!

( If you can't be stuffed reading all of this, all you really need to do is read the bits in the quote boxes.)

This game needs players who come to Metanet at least once every 24 hours.

Quote
Each player starts off by choosing a character and an Element.


Quote
You can pick any character at all, as long as they exist (well, as long as they have a wiki article.)

The Elements work as follows:

Earth > Lightning > Water > Fire > Grass > Earth 

You may pick one Element.

You then pick either Speed, Technique or Power.

Speed means you will be able to use your Power/Super Power more often.

Technique is interesting. Isn't as easy to use as Speed.

Power is the hard hitting choice. It takes a lot of Anger to get the Power/Super Power going, but once you do, insane damage will follow.

You start off with a regular sword, an anger meter with 0 Anger, 30 LP, 0 Gold and the ablility to attack and heal.

Your objective is to kill all of the other players by reducing their LP (Life Points) to 0.

When a player is killed, they become dead and have -10 LP. If a dead player is healed to positive LP, they become the undead and instantly have 10 LP.

If you kill an undead, they are out of the game permanently.


Powers/Super Powers:

The powers (after we choose them for you depending on your character) work like this:

Example:

aaaAAAA

The first three a's represent your power. When you have 3 Anger, you may use the Power.

To use your Super Power, you need to have 7 Anger (in this example) aaaAAAA. Using the Super Power would cost you 7 A.

However, you could use your Power twice, instead and have 1 A over.


Rules/Conditions/Regulations:

Attacking and healing:

You must attack and heal every turn.
-A normal attack does 2 LP of damage.
Healing heals 1 LP of LP.
-Each turn each player may attack one other player. If your element is effective against another element (a water player attacks a fire player etc), you do an extra 1 damage.
-If you heal someone with an Element superior to yours, they are healed by 1 extra LP (applied after the actual heal has resolved).
-If you don't have a weapon (bare fist), you can only every do 1 damage from an attack, regardless of Element.
-You cannot attack a dead player. A dead player may not take any actions.


Anger and Powers:

-The A limit is 20.
-You may use your power and/super power as many times as you want in a turn, provided you have enough A.
-Every turn/round, you are given the ability to attack and heal, buy powerups, use your Power and or Super Power etc.
-You get 1 A per attack against you.

Gold:

-Gold is your currency. You buy things with them.
-Killing a player (not the undead) earns you 30 Gold.
-Killing a player (not the undead) earns you 30 Gold.
-You get 5 gold per round, provided you make an action that round.
-AT THE END of everyturn, the amount of hits you recieve * 5 is the amount of Gold you get that round from the attacks.
-You can sell your items and weapons for some Gold.
-Do 5 damage in a turn, get 5 extra Gold. Do 10 damage, get an extra 10 Gold. The amount of damage you deal in a round (over 5) = the amount of bonus Gold your recieve. The maximum you can recieve through this bonus is 30 Gold. You recieve this bonus the moment you do the damage.

Other:

-If you upgrade a weapon, you lose the previous effects of the weapon.
-The Undead cannot revive players to the state Undead.
-All items can be used on other players (unless specified otherwise.)
-Inactiveness is an extreme sin in this game, you take double the damage from any attack if you are inactive for more than 1 round. (Consecutively.)
-Editing your posts is a no-no. I will go so far as to completely disregard your action if any suspicious editing happens.
-Rules will and can be edited at any time.

Buying and Selling:

Armoury:
The Armoury is only avaliable every 3 turns for 1 turn.

-You can upgrade your sword here.
-Selling your weapon earns you half the amount of Gold that you bought the sword for.
-The sword you start with is worth 10 Gold.

Upgrades:

Mug Shot: For the next 2 turns the normal attack damage you do is also converted into Gold. You get this upgrade onto your weapon by giving in one of weapons (the one being upgraded) for a turn and by paying 10 Gold.
-Ki Power. Sell a weapon for half price to get this upgrade. You Bare Fist attacks do double damage.
-Elemental Sword Upgrades. If your Element is fire, you get a fire sword. If your Element is earth, you get an earth sword. Having this upgrade allows you to do +2 extra damage (instead of 1) against people with an inferior Element. This upgrade also allows you to have 2 Elements in your possession, if you buy an Elemental Force Orb. (You cannot double up an Element.) 20 Gold.
-Twin Swords: This upgrade gives you two swords, and you can attack twice in a turn. (If you upgrade this you'll lose the ability to hold two swords.) 40 Gold.
-Tornado Spear: If the player equipped with this upgrade does more than 3 damage with a normal attack, then that player hit by over 3 damage cannot attack and heal this turn. (If the player has already attacked this turn, then they skip their attack and heal next turn.) 60 Gold.
-Blood Sword: You recover a quarter of the damage you do. (Not just including attacks.) 70 Gold.
-Meta Sword: You gain an Anger point for every attack made when having this sword equipped. 100 Gold.


Power ups/Items:

Elemental Force Items:
-Fire Orb: Changes your element to Fire and adds 1 damage to all of your attacks for 2 turns , it then reverts back to your original element afterwards.
-Water Orb: Changes your element to Water and minuses 1 damage off all attacks against you for 2 turns, it then reverts back to your original element afterwards.
-Lightining Orb: Changes your element to Lightning and you do +3 damage on your normal attack for 1 turn , it then reverts back to your original element afterwards.
-Earth Orb: Changes your element to Earth and everytime you do less than 10 damage (per turn), you gain 2 HP this effect lasts for 2 turns, it then reverts back to your original element afterwards.
-Grass Orb: Changes your element to grass, and take 1HP off any damage done to you, and add it to damage you inflict, +1. For one turn.
All Orbs cost 20 Gold.

Stealing Items: -Drain: Drain an amount of MP from a player equal to the amount of damage you have done from a normal attack this turn. 60 Gold
- Soul stealer: Steal an amount of HP from a player equal to the amount of damage you did to that player from a normal attack this turn. 40 Gold.
-Catharsis: Steal 6 A from a player fro 80 Gold.

Undead Revival:
(All of these items can only be used by the Undead.)
-Zombie Force: Adds 1 attack to your normal attack: 10 Gold.
-Kamikaze Zombie: An Undead sacrifices all of its HP to inflict damage onto 1 enemy. (HP sacrificed - 3 *2 = amount of damage done.) 35 Gold.
-Undead Regeneration: Recover 1 HP. Cost 10 Gold.
-Darkness Claw: The Undead normal attack adds 3 damage onto your normal attack. Cost: 40 Gold.

Healing Items:

- Potion: Recover 2 HP. 10 Gold.
- Super Potion: Recover 10 HP 40 Gold.
-Regen Potion: Recover 2 HP at the start of each turn for 3 turns: 20 Gold.
-Resurrection: Revive a player to the Undead state. 40 Gold.

Anger Increasing Items:

-Anger Pill: Increase your Anger by 2. 30 Gold.
-Rage Tablet: Increase your Anger by 7 for 70 Gold.


Damaging Items:

Interdimensional Rocket: 2 LP damage to 1 player. 30 Gold.
Kamehameha: 6 LP of damage to 1 player. 60 Gold.
Dye-know-might: 1 LP damage to 10 players. 40 Gold.

Support Items:

Indignation Howl: Increase your attack by 1 for 3 turns. 40 Gold.
Healing Staff: Increase your healing ablility by 2 for 3 turns. 40 Gold.
