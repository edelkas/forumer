Ok, for those of you who do not know what San Pasqual Academy is, it is a boarding school for foster kids. I came here because I was told it was good, but it isn't that great. In the course of 8 months, I have had 4 different illnesses (I usually get only 1 each year), become isolated from everyone (even cell phones don't get service, only home phones and internet), and the school is strange. Well, it's like any other high school academic wise, but the teachers are strange. My english teacher treats us like fourth graders, with a group point system, she never calls us by our names, because she assigns numbers, and we have classroom jobs. My spanish teacher is cool, but if we start talking about a different subject, usually he'll start talking about it too. Heck, sometimes he's the one talking about it when we come in. And the classrooms themselves are small, only holding about twelve students at one time, with the exception of the technology center. I mean, give me some elbow room people! The whole campus, residential and school, attracts a ton of bees, and it doesn't help that I have a phobia of them. We have a huge pool, but we can only use it in the summer, If there's a lifeguard around. We only got to swim for two weeks out of the three and a half months of break. I've become depressed, and before I was one of the happiest and kindest guys you would ever meet. Sometimes I wish I was never here, but there are some good things too, although scarce.[/rant]
