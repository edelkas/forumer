RTS = Command & Conquer, Total Annihilation,
StarCraft, etc.; build bases, gather resources, and fight the
enemy
RTT = something like Ground Control or Mech Commander,
where you assume control of an elite small squadron of units (mobility
of troops is a key factor to success) and do random junk; destroy
other enemies, hold a siege, escort a convoy, etc.

Okay. Now that we've got that cleared...

Developer: Stormregion

On August 3, under the orders of General Iron Tusk, the NATIONAL
ARMY OF PIGS crossed the border without warning and penetrated deep
into Carrotland territory. Within only two weeks, they'd burned
everything to the ground. The capital city is under blockade. As a
result of the bitter fighting, the rabbit army is decimated. Towns and
villages are deserted. The defense's determined efforts have not been

subterfuge. From the remains of their reserves, the rabbits create a
powerful fighting force. Without suffering too many losses, this force
must harass and sneak attack the enemy until they lose all their
supply lines and military objectives. Now, all that remains is to find
a capable officer who can lead this operation...
