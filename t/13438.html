[b]Mission 2.04.

Question: What is Mission 2.04?
Answer: Well, Mission 2.04 is where a group of mapmakers get together and add on to one map base.

Question: Uhh... I didn't understand that. Can you explain it differently?
Answer: The idea of this is that a bunch of different mapmakers sign up for Mission 2.04. After there are five + people on a team, the first person who signed up for the team (the captain) makes a map base. It can be just items, only tiles, whatever. After that, the captain PMs the next person the data, and that second person adds on to the map base. After he finishes, he PMs the map data to the third person, and it goes on.

Question: Can I delete anything that anyone before me added on to the map?
Answer: No. Just leave everyone elses object/tile placements alone.

Question: Can I show anyone else the map data, or submit it to NUMA?
Answer: No. Only after everyone is finished adding on, the team captain gets to submit it to NUMA, if they want to. If the team captain doesn't want to show anyone, and someone else on the team does, they need the team captain's permission.

Important dates:
November 3, 2006: Sign-ups close. If needed, I will extend the deadline.
