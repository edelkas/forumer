Hello, shivers21! Your question is a really, really simple one that is easily answered in the FAQs. Have a look:

Quote: The N FAQ
Q: How do I access the N level editor / Ned?
A: At the main menu of the game, before any sounds are played, push tilde (it's ~, under Esc)

Q: Erm, my keyboard doesn't have that button
A: Oogh... well, here are some others M&R worked out. Hope you can find at least one of 'em: , | (that's not an I or small 'l'; that's a vertical slash which on most keyboards is used by pressing Backslash while holding Shift), ^, , @, and '

And, when you're done making your level using the in-game instructions, check out your editor_manual.txt and readme.txt that come with your N download for info on how to use NED and save your levels, making/playing demos, etc. The userlevels.txt also has instructions and the means for you to add userlevels to your game.

So... that's pretty much it. Before you forget though, you should really check out these links pertaining to N, Metanet, etc.
