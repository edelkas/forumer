His corneas? The cornea is the clear bit on the front of your eye, and is supposed to be transparent. I believe you mean the retinas.

Anyway, you'd still be near-blind. And the light wouldn't be focused at all because your lenses and corneas would still be invisible. "But aren't they transparent anyway?" you may ask. Yes, but you can still see them, in the same way you can see glass objects - because they refract the light passing through them. To be invisible they would have to cause no rerfraction. (Incidentally, baby oil has about the same refrective index as a lot of glass. So, if you get a jug, put a glass in it, then fill the jug with baby oil, the glass becomes virtually invisible. It looks particularly weird when you use a beaker with markings on the side - it seems like they're floating in the middle of the jug.)
