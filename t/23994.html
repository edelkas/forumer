#470409 6 2008-09-16T01:56:00Z PsychoSnail
So when you create a level in debug mode, there's that top textbox with a bunch of random-seeming numbers and letters, right? That's your level data. Copy that, add
$<title>#<author>#<catagory>#
and then post your level data right after that last #. Take all of that, and paste it at the end of userlevels.txt, on a new line.

By the way, in case you don't know, you open .txt files in Notepad (All Programs, Accessories, Notepad) if you use Windows. I'm not sure what program is used for Macs.
