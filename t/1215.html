#31769 12 2004-10-12T03:58:00Z Guest
hey,
one thing to notice: for each of the three sets of tiles proposed by foxhunter, draw a "plus" in each of the squares (so that each square becomes a 2x2 grid).

now, the right set has become type "1" in Ned, the middle has become type 2/3, and the left set is full/empty tiles.

so, you _can_ make these shapes, sort of -- you just need to double the size of all the other shapes in the level.

these shapes could even be implemented in N as-is, by doubling the size of the grid and halving the size of each grid square. but.. sadly flash can't handle that many tiles. also, making things smaller by 2x means that you need to increase the rate of collision detection by 2x or objects will start to fly through things..

basically, it would be nice but the current engine is pretty much right on the limit of what the flashplayer can handle. we spent a lot of time fiddling with the grid-size/etc. to get the largest level size that still ran fast.

as maximo said, our next games won't be tile-based because we've come up with a system that will let us do the same shapes, plus a lot more. our new engine is actually super-cool and a LOT better than N, in terms of both speed and capabilities... it's exciting

raigan
