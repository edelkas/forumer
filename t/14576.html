#12944 1 2006-12-17T16:23:29Z sweep
In the past 24 hours, as I'm sure anyone that has spent any time browsing the forums is probably aware, a huge number of multi-accounters have come out of the woodwork, confessing to what they've done.

Whilst some people have found this pretty shocking, I'm not really surprised. We all knew that there were multi-accounters amongst us, we just didn't really know the extent. Earlier today maestro spent quite some time analyzing the votes in the NUMA database and has since identified four more well-known members that he suspects of partaking in multi-accounting fraud. We are following this up, and we will continue to see if we can expose any more cheats from the system.

Whilst it is fantastic that so many people are coming clean, this has made something clear: the system is inherently flawed. The way NUMA is constructed has allowed this to happen.

Here are some of the essential problems with the system:
  • The focus of the website is placed too strongly on ratings. If you want to browse maps and want to avoid the poor stuff, you'll head straight for the top rated charts. We want browsing NUMA to be different - you should be able to find maps by difficulty, tags, favorites and bitesizing. Being able to search by ratings is brilliant, but it sends out the wrong message - it makes people think that ratings are the be all and end all. Well, they shouldn't be the be all. Or the end all.
  • People aren't scared of being caught. Whilst Arachnid was in charge, he wasn't actively searching out multi-accounters. That's fair enough, he's a busy man, but it meant that anyone who wasn't a bit low in the brain cell department could easily cheat the system to their benefit. People need to know that they will be caught. Systems need to be in place to detect vote fraud.
  • People who commit vote fraud and get away with it find swaying the average rating of maps very easy to do. A single 0 on a map with four existing ratings of 5 has the effect of dropping the average rating by 4. There are ways round this: namely histograms and mechanisms to discard outliers automatically.
NUMA needs work. It needs restructuring. This is all very possible. We have ideas, we have plans, maestro has smarts and I have more solutions than we could ever need.

But something bugs me particularly. Discovering that so many people have been multi-accounting (pending even more likely additions to the list in the near future) has spelt it out loud and clear: all voting that has been made since the beginning of NUMA is corrupted. Every single map that has seen the top rated page very probably has some kind of suspicious activity in its records. I hate this. I hate the idea that there are traces of these crimes throughout every nook and cranny of NUMA, and I am keen to start over.

What I want to suggest is by no means certain. It's just something I wanted to throw out there, and something that maestro is keen to hear the community's response to. I know a lot of people may react badly to it. I am open to hearing what you guys think. My suggestion is drastic, and at the moment just a potential road that we've pondered.

I want to wipe the slate clean. I want to reset all the voting on NUMA, removing every vote from the system. No exceptions. See it as a detox. Washing all the crap out of the system. Starting again.

If we were to do this, we wouldn't completely discard the old ratings - we would save all the old average ratings to the database, so people could browse via that and all the voting of the past isn't completely wasted. Equally, the resetting of the votes would be done alongside introducing measures to solve the flaws in the system mentioned above. We would change the focus of the website, and start over again.

If you hate the idea, we want to hear it. We want to hear what you think we should do instead. If you love the idea, we want to hear your response too.
