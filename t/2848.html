#2556 1 2005-04-16T03:15:05Z swankidelic
This would be another way to show off n prowess, in addition to high scores. Every level (in single player only, not in NUMA maps or such) has a unique "medal" or gold pickup that would be extremely difficult to reach. A player can only get these medals once, and don't add to time or anything. What they do get is a listing in the high score boards.

For example, when you scan the high scores, and you pull up, say, Trench Run (I forget the digits) you see a list of players that have gotten that medal, or pull up player name and see which medals he has.

I also thought about calling this pickup "master" gold, or "seifu" gold, or "screw dat" gold (when the level is loaded you see where the medal is and say "screw dat"). The idea also occurred to me to split the medal up into parts; in order to obtain the medal a player would have to gather all the pieces, but this can be accomplished with locked doors.
