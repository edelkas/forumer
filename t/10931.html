Here is a little experiment that is easy. I remember it from when I was young.

(needed: peice of paper, string, washer or ring that has some weight to it)

Take a piece of paper and draw a big square on it. Then make a line down the middle vertically and one horizontally. So now you should have 4 squares.

number them 1-4. Next get a piece of string and a washer. Tie the string to the washer. Rest our elbow on the table and hold the string so the washer is hanging just above the cross where the line intersects. Hold the string in between your thumb and pointer or middle finger.

Now think to yourself that you want to move the washer back and forth from box 1 to 4 as to make a swing motion. Anything happen? Try 2 and 3. Try 1,2,3,4 to make a circle motion. then make it stop and reverse direction.

Do all of this while trying to hold the string and your hand absolutely still. Apparently we have tiny muscles in our fingers that will make it move without our knowledge.
