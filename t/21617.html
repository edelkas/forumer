yeah, i had to copy them over manually by looking at the level editor on my computer

BTW, ive made ports of Twisted (88-1) and MTI (yeah, that one) for those of us who are overly sadistic

the process for getting the layout on one of those maps is as follows:

1. enter the level you want to copy in highscores mode.
2. quit out of the level and back into the highscores mode (this will render the level as your background)
3. click main menu and immediately hit ~
4. congratulations, you should now be in the level editor with the layout of that level loaded

after getting the layout right, then i re-enter the level in game mode to position the objects.
