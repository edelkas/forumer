#195020 2 2006-06-26T21:26:55Z Qix
It's not impossible, but it's rather hard if you don't know what to do. Trigger the first thwump and then run across it and beat it to the wall. The next thwump should trigger and once it starts going back jump around the mine and land on the next thwump. Fall down and trigger the thwump and repeat the jump over the mine. Then you can just fall onto the diagonal tile and run to the exit. You can also try to slide by the thwumps down the middle, like in the highscores, but I find that a bit harder to pull off.
