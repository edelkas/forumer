I'm usually okay with route copying. When it's someone like trib4l who takes your route because, well, it can be improved - that's fine. The route isn't being copied more as refined. But the people who just blatantly use a route because they've seen a highscore use it, that's just rediculous.

The one that's really annoyed me, my 83-3 highscore. It gave me so much more respect for people - no-one tried to ape it for the longest time. Almost all the routes that I saw were unique, there was no copying. And that really added to the mystique of the score, because it allowed it to be so much higher than any other. But then came people with these insanely aping runs. They've completely spoiled the coolness of the score, it's now only 14 seconds higher than the next one. And they can't use the excuse that they're trying to improve the run - it's aping, to the core. There's one classic point in AAUdude2447's run near the top, where he does a jump that in my run, gets a bit of gold and incidentally passes a switch. The switch passing bit isn't needed, because you pass through it later in the run. But this person, so determined to copy the run to the core, when he misses this switch runs back and wastes a few seconds going back for the switch.

As far as I can see the point for highscores is to show off fast, inventive, cool runs. What it shows off in reality is the huge lack of fast, inventive, cool runs. The other day I got a 0th on 9-0. It took me five minutes, and I got it quite sloppily on the first run that I actually survived on. How? Because every other run on that level followed exactly the same route, the same route that I saw the first time I looked at the highscore for that level about a year ago or so.
