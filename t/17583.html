N Sig of the Year 07

NSY07 is here!

Since we might not remember the last NSY, I'm going to give a brief description of the contest. Basically, we are trying to find the best N-themed sig in the forums. That sig:
  • Has to have a direct relevance to N. (Some things that we usually associate with N are the Metanet logo, the ninja, the enemies, and the objects.)• Has to be no bigger than 500x125 and no smaller than 400x100.• Shouldn't be an animation. Self explanatory.• Must be made after May 31st, 2007.
I really don't know how many contestants to expect this time, but I hope it's a lot. Each person can submit a maximum of 3 sigs. (This may change if we have a lot of entries.)

I am hoping that our fine judges luminaflare and Jakel from last year are judging again this year along with me. They were great. If they are not interested, I will take volunteers. Our judges will score the sigs on the following criteria:
  • Creativity - */30
  • Appealing to the Eye - */20
  • Relevance -yes/no (All 'no's are eliminated)
  • effort - */10
Winners get cool stuff that is TBD.

Accepting all entries and questions till June 20th. I have summer vacation now; I'll be on hours at a time.
