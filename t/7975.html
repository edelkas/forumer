I'm sure this has been suggested before, and with good reason, but what if N were a sidescroller, like mario or kirby and the likes? Rather than make episodes seperate single blocks, connect them into 'worlds'.

I'm not saying to eliminate the boxed-in levels, because that is part of what defines N, but adding these sidescrolling episodes in as 'bonusses' would definitely work, and I for one would have a great time playing it.
