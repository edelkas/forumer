http://cybernations.net

Been playing it since June of last year. It's possibly the most addicting thing ever to happen to me.

It's a geopolitical simulator that revolves around the many alliances working either together or against each other to be of the highest political stance among approximately 35,000 players. The game itself is simple and requires an average of 3 minutes of work per day, unless you're interested in getting into the politics side of the game. In this case, it'll take as much time as you're willing to dedicate to it.

But here's a fair warning: It's more addicting than World of Warcraft. I've been playing it for almost a year now, and the hold it's having on me isn't, hasn't, and probably won't soon be breaking. So if you're interested in a simple game, sign up and join an alliance (I recommend the Grand Global Alliance )

Anyway, I was wondering if anyone else played it. There've been over 100,000 total nations created, and it's been on the news, so someone else it bound to have heard of it.

Anyway, go ahead and sign up, you can find me here: Dementual's Nation, \m/

But remember, if you don't want to get addicted, be very, very careful.
