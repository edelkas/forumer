#283144 19 2007-01-08T22:37:55Z George
Yes. In that txt file 'userlevels', just open the document, select the level you want, in a format something like: $Level name#Author#Level Type#Bunch of code#. Paste this in the top box of Ned, click outside the box and press 'L' to load the map. The press 'P' to play the map. Also, make sure that Caps Lock is on to play it in real time mode.
