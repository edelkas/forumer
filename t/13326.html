Welcome to the 'Limit My Resources' contest!

What is this said contest?
Basically, you will have to make a map...but theres a twist! (Believe it or not)

You will only be allowed to use a maximum of one of every enemy and two of every tile (except block tile).

So thats:

Enemies
Gauss Turret x1
Homing Launcher x1
Mine x1
Floor Guard x1
Thwump x1
Zap Drone (seeker) x1
Zap Drone (Normal) x1
Laser Drone x1
Chaingun Drone x1

Tiles
No. 1 tiles x2
No. 2 tiles x2
No. 3 tiles x2
No. 4 tiles x2
No. 5 tiles x2
No. 6 tiles x2
No. 7 tiles x2
No. 8 tiles x2
E tile - unlimited

Things to note
These are all maximums, you don't have to use them all.
Maps must be playable ie. not DDA or N-Art.
Deadline for entries is 17th November.
All entries must submitted on Numa and link posted on this thread.
All maps must of been made and submitted to Numa on or after 21 October.
Other objects like gold, bounce blocks and doors are unlimited.
Three maps per person maximum.


Judging
I need three volunteers for Judges.
Judges will judge all the maps and an average will be taken for map's final score.
Judges can enter maps and i will take there place to judge them.

Judges are :
Smitty
Jiggerjaw
Mc_george

Maps will be judged out of 30:

Use of Tiles - /10
Use of Objects - /10
Overall Playability and Fun - /10



Prizes
First Place - Sig tag, dedication map and bragging rights.
Second Place - Sig tag and slightly less bragging rights.
Third Place - Sig Tag and slightly less again bragging rights.
