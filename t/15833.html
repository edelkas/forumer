I had this thought, and it's only a rough idea right now, but I think that if custom highscores were to be implemented, it should be done in the following way. Each week, some 'judges' would select 5 levels that would have to be highscored. Then everyone would have a week to highscore it. There would be an online table that, perhaps, would show what times people where getting, but not let you watch their replay. At the end of the week, depending on the scoring system, the person who got the best times, would be the winner.

This idea, as I said is very rough, would keep highscoring fresh, and hopefully, like NTTS, focus more on innovation, rather than perfect runs. I've spoken to a few highscores, and some of them are finding highscoring boring, as there is only so much you can do among 500 levels. I can understand that this will be hard to implement, but I feel that it has benefits, such as hackers can be easily identified, and will have a less lasting affect.

So tell me what you think, and any potential problems you can see, and we will try and iron them out!
