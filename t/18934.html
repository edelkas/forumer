Welcome to the Cramped Competition.

If you remember when zey_ was active, his contest, The Original Cramped Comp, was pretty popular and had some amazing maps submitted. I had three judges to judge the maps, but one of them suddenly left, and I was left judgeless. Since then, the competition has been left, stranded in the depths of Contests & Tournaments.

I've decided to bring it back to life.


Objective:


In this competition, the way to win is by making an N map which is completely made out of [E] tiles, except for 75 (or less) empty spaces.

Sample map: Stuffed.


Rules:


  • If you do not have 75 or less empty spaces in your map, you will be disqualified.
  • If you want to have extra tiles around the playing area to add to the aesthetics, you may
NOT delete extra tiles. You must use some of your 75.• If you decide to use [1] - [8] tiles, you have to complete the square. For instance, if you use a [6] tile, you must use a [2] tile to complete a square.• Each person may not have submitted more than 3 entries.*• The maps must be action or puzzle maps.• To prevent multiple tags, you can only have the tags "Action/Puzzle", and "TCC".*• Map deadline: September 14th.• Judging deadline: September 30th.[/list]

There has been some controversy about if I copied this after the 9x9 competition. I did not.


Scoring:


  • - / 10 - Aesthetics
  • - / 10 - Object placement
  • - / 5 - Good or bad usage of space



Judges:


  • Jakel
  • 321southpaw
  • ZeroTruths
  • Open (Back-up judge)



Prizes:


  • A dedication map.
  • A custom signature/avatar set made by Jakel.
