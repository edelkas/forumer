00-00 ~ Wreckage
Tie straight. Suit buttoned. Wrinkle in the left corner. Not anymore, smoothed now. Good. Damien Parker walked out of the bathroom, a smug drin drawn out over his lips. Today was going to be a good day. Job promo, date saturday, everything was planned out for the week, life was swell. He walked out of his 2 story surburban home, locked it up, and set off to his Cadillac XLR-V. Key turned in ignition. The car hummed to life. He slammed on the pedal and with a squeal the car jolted onto the road. No point in going slow today. His promotion awaited.
Traffic was slow. He hated back-ups. He lowered the roof and stuck his head out over the window. Nothing seemed wrong, no accidents. This was strange. Wait, what was that? A whisper in the air drifted across the freeway, quickly growing into a loud hum. What? Suddenly a loud bang, and a car was in the air. In the air? That couldn't be right. The car slammed into the ground, crushing another smaller vehicle. The driver of the smaller car was racing to get away from the wreck. Two more cars sprang up, landing farther away, one in a ditch, the other on top of a tow truck. Screams of panic rose in the street. Most were already out of their cars and running for cover. No way was he going to -- a large truck obscured his vision, and he instintively jumped out of the car, just as the truck slammed into his car. For once not wearing a seatbelt actually saved his life. That car was brand new. He cursed under his breath and jogged across to the edge of the freeway to see what was going on. A large....large....stop sign!? What the... Another soft hum, and suddenly a beam shot out of the "stop sign", searing a semi-truck in half. What in the world was going on? He had to get out of here. He ran for the nearest building. But it wasn't a building. It was rubble with a door. His eyes widened. He looked out towards downtown. There were dozens more of the octagonal shaped objects shooting bright red beams, melting anything in their path. He looked back at the ruined building. Another hum, this time directly behind him. He reached for the doorknob and entered what was left of the building as fast as he could. This would have to do. Little shelter from those aliens, or whatever they were, was better than no shelter.
