#407967 5 2008-02-08T23:31:19Z LittleViking
There's really no reason not to put ads on your site, unless you want to give your users an ad-free experience. If you go to Google Adsense or Project Wonderful, you can sign up for free and put their ads on your site right away. The money you make from ads will probably be just enough to cover hosting costs, but still, it's better than nothing. (Again, unless you want to go ad-free.)

For hosting, I'd recommend one of these:
  • Nearly Free Speech has you pay for only the bandwidth you use. If you end up not getting much traffic, then you don't pay much to use it. If you use a lot, then you pay for it.
  • 110mb. Free host. I haven't used it, so I can't fully endorse it, but it offers tons of space and tons of bandwidth at no cost. They don't give you MySQL databases for free, but you said you have a static site, so it probably doesn't matter to you.
  • A Small Orange offers the cheapest hosting I found while searching, other than free. $25 a year get you decent storage space and bandwidth for a small site. If your site is very image-heavy or you expect a lot of visitors, though, I'd recommend something else.
  • Blue Host is the most expensive host I'm listing here. $7 a month gets you most storage and bandwidth than you could ever need. (TheRealN.com has more server space than I have hard drive space. blue_tetris, Kablizzy and I could upload everything we have to that place and not fill it halfway.)
Or, you know, if all else fails, PM me. Maybe we can work something out to host your files and point your domain to our server.
