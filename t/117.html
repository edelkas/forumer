i am really attracted to the idea of using the robot's attacks against them (like guiding a homing rocket back at an enemy to kill it). problem is, it would make some levels too easy. so how about something else.

imagine a force field blocking your path. it acts like a locked door (maybe it kills you, too). you open it, not by touching a switch, but but destroying the generator. only three ways would work. guiding missles to it, getting guass to hit it, or getting an ion beam to hit it (machine gun and zap drone would be too easy, probably). the generator could be the force field itself or it could be a seperate unit on the other side of the map. but i'll leave that to the game designers.

this solution would satisfy peoples urge to kill things and introduce another layer of skill and difficulty. how does it sound?
