Hey Everyone,

Firstly, I'll tell you the point of this important announcement. On the 16th of December I'm going on holidays to New Zealand for 5 or 6 weeks, and I won't have internet access. So, I wanted to make sure that everyone has something to do (even if small) while I'm away.

So, my question is, Do you have anything to do?
If you don't, and are unsure on what you're meant to be doing, check the Roster. If you are still unsure on what you're doing, post something here telling me or one of the other mods. And if the roster information is wrong, please post something there telling me.
If you know what you're meant to be doing, but don't have a composer/performer/editor, post something in the Request Topic so we can get it sorted out ASAP.
If there is anything you're unsure about with Metastra in general, check the General Discussion to see if there's already a similar question. If there's not, please ask it there.

Note for everyone: Please Check the Request Topic regularly, and please volunteer if you can do what someone is asking! Also, if you're on the forums, just drop by this sub-forum regularly. I know it's not easy to find nowadays, but it really helps if everyone knows what's happening. You could also get up to date with the news by going onto the IRC Channel (#Metastra), where there should be someone who can help you. And if you've got some free time, please listen to others' compositions or recordings to see what you think.
Notes for composers: If you've thought of a great song, why not compose it for instruments in Metastra! That way you can request people to play it for you too!
Notes for performers: If you've got any time, improvise. See if you can come up with anything that could be useful, then record it and ask a composer to do something interesting with it.
Notes for editors: You guys are really important. It's your job to make sure that all the pieces have been recorded properly and fit together nicely. If one part is out of time or tune, post something in that topic telling people, so it can be fixed.

Now, I think I've covered everything. If there's something you think I've missed out on in this post, please tell me ASAP so I can add it in.

Thanks, and I hope to see everyone organized before I leave.

Lenny
