#22342 5 2004-04-16T19:45:57Z Daddaluma
The book I used, "Japanese: The Spoken Language" is very good for learning grammar correctly, but I'm not sure how useful it will be without all the supplementary materials that go with it.

There are taped recordings of every conversation and drill in the book, so without them, it probably won't be useful except for learning grammar points. But in terms of grammar, it's probably the best book out there.

Other than that book, I haven't encountered any I think were very good, so you might ask around to see what other people say.
