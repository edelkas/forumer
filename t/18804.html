You dig underground and find minerals and bring them back to the plant to get money for them. You have a certain amount of fuel and hull so you need to go up when it starts to get low. You can upgrade your fuel, hull, drill, and other things by making money. The graphics are OK for 2D.

MOTHERLOAD
Spoiler: My Strategy - only if you realy need it
Dig a straight line into the ground, only go off your trail if you see somthing valuable. You do a straight line so you dont have to zig zag your way back to the surface. The first thing you should buy is a fuel upgrade. Then a drill upgrade. Then fuel again. Make sure you be safe and have atleast half health when going back down. When you start to run out of fuel, wright down your depth and fly back up. When your done, free fall it back to your last spot. Its smart to save ever time you resurface. It will restart your score, but its better than loseing everything if you run into a radiation pocket or lose fuel. After about 3,000 ft or so, buy atleast 10 dynamite pack and blow away any rocks in your path. By 6,000 ft you should buy 2 matter transmitters and nanobots for repair. After that depth, its prety hard to fly up when you get rarer things. when you get to the bottom telly back up and load up on atleast 50 C4, 35 dynamite, tellies, and ALOT of nanobots. Ull see why.
