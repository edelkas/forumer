Well, the recent Trend for maps seems to be to be very hard, and include at least a few Gauss Turrets and Missile Launchers. So well, I'd like to see more "basic" maps, along the style of the MetaNet levels.

Rules:

-No Gauss Turrets, Missile Launchers; maximum of: 1 Laser Drone, 1 Machine Gun Drone, 10 Zap/Seeker Drones, 5 Thwumps, 35 Mines
-The level should be between 20 and 50 seconds to beat, possibly a bit more for all gold
-Try to keep it simple. The goal is a level that can be picked up my any advanced N player, is fun to play, and not hard or too challenging, something that is relieving yet a unique experience.
-Rating:
Difficulty 5 Points: Is it too hard? Was it far too easy?
Concept 10 Points: Are these objects seemingly random? Is there a bigger Image behind it? Is there a specific theme to the map?
Fun 15 Points: How fun was it to play? Will I play it again? Did I enjoy it?
Looks 5 Points: Was the space used well? Does it look to stuffed and cramped? Many open spaces that are totally unused?
Other 5 Points: Anything else that should be noted positivly?

Total: 40 Points

Each Juror will rate every map, fina lrankings are based on the sum of the 3 ratings.
-2 Maps maximum per player
-Send In your maps by Sun, Jul 17th, 4PM GMT (Deadline might be extended, but don't count on it!)

I'm still looking for 2 more Jurors (obviously, they can't enter maps)
