F5 F5 F5 F5 F5....

Counting unique IPs would work better.

no, not really. counting unique IP's would just tell you how many IP addresses have ever viewed your user page, not how many times your userpage has actually been viewed. I'm pretty sure epigone meant the latter. If IP(A) visits your page 20 times in a week, your page views have only gone up by 1, if and only if this week is the first time they have viewed your page. It kinda goes against the point of having a counter such as the one epigone is suggesting.

Also, just to throw this out there, chances are that a unique IP that isn't registered on NUMA and has no idea what the game N is will probably view your page only once, whereas someone who is registered on NUMA is more likely to view your page multiple times, and I think that is what we are getting at: how many times a user comes back to your page, rather than how many IP addresses they have visited it from.
