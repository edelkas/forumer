Quote: Winstons, 2006-12-15T09:01:08Z
Is there a way of sending my highscores for a level to a friend, so he can watch the movie of me performing the level?
No, I'm sorry but there isn't. You can watch a quick replay, though, by pressing "R" after completing a level. The only way to show the completion to your friend is to invite him to your house. Or, use a separate game video record program (like FRAPS).

But you can record demos in user-made levels.

Editor manual tells you how to do that, step-by-step.

#270626 5 2006-12-15T15:31:12Z repeat2341
http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?sho...2&hl=level+data

If you want to do this, as George says, you need to do it in debug mode (read the editor manual for details on recording demos and using debug mode). To play the level, you need its level data, and that means you need Eeyore's N Level Data program. So get it, by clicking above, natch.

Oh, and you won't be able to get your existing score this way; you'll have to play and rule the level again. Getting your existing score involves .sol editing and sounds too hard to me; I'd just send your friend my .sol. That sounds bad.
