How often has it been said at the Suggestions board, that the discussed idea "has been suggested before". So, I think we should make a template like at Wikipedia.

At Wikipedia, you write e.g. {{wiktionary}} into the article and the Wiki engine creates a correct link to the Wiktionary on itself.

So, for example, there should be a template {{sugg_before}} at this forum. When you write this into your post, it is replaced with

Quote: template manager
This has been suggested before.

Or what about a poll-voting template? When you post in a thread with poll, you write only {{poll=4}} and the template manager votes "4" for you.

Yes, I know, {{stupid_idea=this}}, but {{sugg_before=false}}.

1. You think this is a stupid idea.
2. You think this is a good idea.
3. You would like to suggest other templates.
