#155234 2 2006-03-16T15:35:20Z Ngaged
Well, where do you want them? Here are your possiblilities.

1. Play in debug mode.
a. Copy the "Map data" from the NUMA map.
b. Clear the digits that are in the upper box of debug mode.
c. Paste the map data from the NUMA map into that box.
d. Click out of the box. Failing to do so will result in a messed up map.
e. Load the level by pressing L.
f. Turn on caps lock.
g. Press P to play.

2. Put them in Userlevels.
a. Copy the "1.4 Map data" from the NUMA map.
b. Paste the data into your Userlevels.txt.
c. Save the file.
d. Make sure the Userlevels are in the same directory as N. If it isn't, move it there.
e. Open N, and go to Userlevels.
f. It should be ready to play.

There ya go. I hope this solves your problem.
