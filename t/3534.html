I dunno, I guess it may have a "fun" period between the "too weak to do anything" period and the "ran out of things you can do for free so now you just sit around catching and cooking fish and watching the bars slowly rise so that by next week you can cook *gasp* another type of fish which heals 12 damage instead of 11. But that period is even SHORTER than it is in normal rpgs, due to the fact that there's only a dozen or so free quests and you're limited to about a fifth of the map. Wilderness might extend the fun a little, but there's a lot of risk and by now I bet that people have already ripped a gaping hole in the "improved" combat system's "balance" between the three "classes". In my opinion, seeing the person on their feedback messageboard (and you OBVIOUSLY need to pay to give them feedback or suggestions, both there and in polls) complaining that he now needs THREE ITEMS AND A PRAYER (or was it two items and a prayer? I forgot) in order to train on greater dragons without taking any damage at all. Oh, the horror! But then again, that problem seems to be embedded into the entire genre of MMORPGs.

Yes, I used to play if you couldn't tell, and left a little before the update to the new system, then played some more after it and left again because my character STILL had nothing to do. But its mostly the same as when I played, I think. And even if not, they mostly just adjusted stuff, not adding much, so you'll run out just as fast. If you must play, my advice is to never trust any player who you don't know in real life, whether they're teaming up with you in the wilderness or trying to sell you something or offering you "protection" to get to a quest item safely. And if you go for cooking skill, you might want a bit of fishing and should definitely get your crafting up enough to make the pans and bowls you'll need, and stockpile anything you use to cook inside the bank. And sell your good food items near the wilderness.
