Lately I've worked a bit on my 'single song challange' tileset series. I've done about 10 or so, these are my favourites.

Basically, I've listened to a song- sometimes once, sometimes one- and- a half times, but at absolute maximum twice, then build a tileset in N while listening to it. And I can't have any previous ideas for the tileset- it needs to be influenced almost entirely by the music.

And it can't take longer than the song twice. The design needs to be done on the first hearing, and you just do some minor polishing during the second.

Here are my 5 best so far, forming an 'episode' so far. Go on and join me making these- they only take 3 or 4 minutes, which is the entire idea.

Most of you won't have heard many of these songs, but even for those of you who have, it'll be hard to tell how the tileset was influenced by them- but it WAS, and that was the whole idea. Enjoy!
