#174686 5 2006-05-11T21:36:18Z brocerius
Any of you heard of the Demographic Transition Model? It shows the birth rates and death rates of countrys during various stages of developement.

Stage 1 High birth rates and high death rates. Few countrys are still here.

Stage 2 Medicine drops the death rate, but the birth rate is still high. This creates a population boom. This is happening in most 3rd world countrys.

Stage 3 Death rates stay low and birth rates drop dramatically. The population levels out. Many 3rd world countrys are moving into this stage.

Stage 4 Birth and death rates are low and stable. Population remains stable. Most 1st world countrys are here.

Stage 5 Birth rates slowly drop below death rates, and population goes into decline. Countrys like Britain are starting to enter this stage.


So basically when the whole wolrd is in stage 5 the problem will be underpopulation, not overpopulation. And the world is moving in that direction. All the countrys that are in stage 4 or 5 have been through all the other stages, and 3rd world countrys are catching up all the time.

Also the EU estimates that with progresses in technology we are now capable of feeding at least 20 billion people. Obviously this will increase with time.

This is why i dont worry about over population.


However, medicine is a problem since antibiotics will soon be inneffective against most bacteria.
