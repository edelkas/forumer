I'm not sure if it's been reported here or not, but I found a cool glitch that turns the ninja invincible! It also renders all objects as untouchable, so you can't beat the level, but oh well.

How to do it:
On 12-0, make the floorchaser go to the left of the jump pad. Then, fall down and hit the jumppad. If timed, correctly, it will elecrocute the ninja, but he will still jump off the pad. You will then be invincible, and will clip through enemies, bounce blocks, and gold. You cannot win the level, however, since you also will clip through the switch and the exit. You can still die from fallin or time out.
