I don't have a tutorial, but here are some pieces of advice:

1. Don't make too short DDAs. Nobody likes them.
2. Make sure it has many close calls. At least I think they're cool smile.gif
3. Put in some more objects than just launch pads. A good DDS has at least some enemies, tiles, trap doors, normal doors, gold. AND the some oh-so-famous launch pads.
4. An impressive DDS also uses some game glitches and bugs, for example - you can use gold delay and/or thwump launches.
5. As much as people hate too simple DDAs, it shouldn't have too many objects. If it has, prepare to see some serious lag.
Take a good look at #s 2, 3 and 5.
