#238410 9 2006-10-08T13:43:50Z jtbandes
I have conducted a test, and concluded that the drone's distance from the door has nothing to do with whether this happens or not. If the drone sees you when the door is open, it will go through, regardless of which side of the door you were on. If the drone sees you when the door is closed, it will only go through if you are "holding" the door open for it (i.e. you are standing on the other side). In this case, it will charge through the open door and rearrange your limbs a bit. If the door was closed when it saw you and it remains closed, the drone will not go through.
