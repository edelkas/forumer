Even on the same keyboard, some keys seem to respond later than others. The Shift key which is normally used for jumping, for instance, seems to respond later than letter keys ("Z", "T", and so on). But it's also a thing of experience and routine: If you always used Shift, you are slower when using Z or even the Up button.

Another thought: Laptop keyboards are thinner, which means the keys have to cover a smaller distance. A smaller distance means a shorter time to press the key, with the conclusion of a faster response.
