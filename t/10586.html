I'm in production of a map series called The Way of the Ninja, or TWotN for short.
It's based around the "Shadow Fighter" maps, in which you hide in shadows to avoid being spotted.

Here's the first map and the first area's storyline.

Awakening one night in your bed, you find a large blue orb of sorts in front of you. After a few seconds, it goes away.
You look out your window, and realise that the neighborhood was obliterated. Whats left are the shells of the buildings that used to be. You realise, that if you want to live, you must get to the building nearby, which holds a secret passageway to the next town.
