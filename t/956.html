Okay, I just started playing N today, but I'm already hooked, and have a few suggestions.

1. The ability to save replays manually, to separate files, so you can post them online or send them to your friends. I know your best replays are saved automatically, but some near misses can be pretty hysterical.

2. I know all sorts of extra moves have been suggested, but of all the ones I can think of, I would like to be able to duck the most. Would come in handy sometimes on those 45-degree slopes when you're about to get electrified by an enemy approaching from the rear.

3. I'm not suggesting that the game come with background music, but if possible, I think an option to play your own mp3/wav files in the game should be implemented. Anyone who's played the Balloon Battle flash game should know what I mean. (I'm aware it's possible to just play something like Winamp in the background, but that takes up even more processing power.)

4. I know this one's been suggested before, but the levels could really use a bit of colour.
