Here is the path that worked for me, although it took me awhile and the amount of gold gotten is rather minimal. The colors only change when the suggested flight path crosses itself and there are only three things that I need to point out. First of all, unless I mention otherwise, or if it appears to be an extremely stupid thing to do, you should always be moving. Secondly, at point A. you need to wait until the three thwumps get pretty close and then try to pull off the tight little evasive jump shown that should keep you alive (might take a few tries to get). Thirdly, once you get to point B. you need to let the thwump coming from the left side pass overhead and still stay there until the two thwumps from the right get close, after which you will do the shown jump. Continue normally from there.

I hope that this helps and wasn't too complicated. Good luck and fun times!
