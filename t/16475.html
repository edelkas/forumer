#370775 7 2007-10-09T08:20:19Z tktktk
Overall, it looks very good, but there are a couple of general problems I want to address.

First, the difficulty is generally pretty high on these, so a lot of the time I got the impression that I was playing through one of your Super Hard Columns, and that's not the vibe I should be getting from something in a Legacy pack. So, try to vary your maps a bit more in difficulty, and make a few easier ones to balance out the ones you have now.

Second, you have a lot of fantastic ideas, but a lot of them are used in ways that feel kind of unrefined or test level-ish. A lot of what you have now is a great fit for Ampersand, but not for the main Zenith pack. Some of the concepts can be reworked into something more suitable. In the future, don't be afraid to try out new ideas, but focus a lot of your efforts on integrating them into a normal action level format.

Again, what you have is great, it's just that in its current form, some of it doesn't fit with the style of maps that we're aiming for with Legacy.
