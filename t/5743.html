Go to numa, sign up, and post maps.

Or are you having trouble signing up? If so, in N, go into your configure menu. That's where you sign up. Type your desired username and password, and create new user. Then go to numa, type in that username and password, and give it your email. Confirm the email, and you'll be on your way.
