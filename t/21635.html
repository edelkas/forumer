The force drone pushes your Ninja away from itself with periodic pulses if you approach. You can run past them after about three pulses if you just keep pushing towards them. I have yet to see how they work in levels, as I have yet to play a level in single player or co-op that uses them, so I suspect that they won't be used until we get some new or user-built levels.

In the N+ changes thread it's noted that multiple forcefield drones can cause buggy behavior, like pushing you through walls.
