Oh, just thought I'd mention that you can completely disregard the whole 163 dpi thing as that means absolutely nothing unless you're printing an image. Simply make a 320 x 480 image and save it as a PNG as I did above. Harley, here's where you were confused:

The tech specs for the iPhone and iPod Touch say the resolutions are 320 x 480 @ 163 dpi. What this means is that the screen is 320 x 480 pixels, just like your monitor may be 1024 x 768. The 163 dpi simply refers to the distance between pixels. In one square inch of an iPhone screen there are 163 x 163 (26569) pixels. Similarily, your computer monitor is 72 dpi. One inch of the monitor has 72 x 72 (5184) pixels. You never have to take into account the dpi if you're only working with images on screens. If we were going to print these images for some reason, to achieve a resolution comparable to the iPhone, we woud need a image with a dpi of 163.
