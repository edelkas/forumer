So, I guess that means i put the switch JUST after the door then?
Not immediately after the door, but a little bit after the door, yes. Read my above post. Another factor is how fast N is traveling. If he's traveling very quickly towards the switch, like if you place the switch like a half snap away from the door, it won't affect him because he would be traveling too quickly for the door to propel him.

I know, it doesn't make a lot of sense with words, so just play around with it in Ned until you get it.
