#399423 9 2008-01-10T15:57:16Z Twistkill
Wedgie: What levels are your characters at? In one of my save games I'm a Guardian at level 30, with Veradux and Gligus as my partners. I have another Guardian saveslot around level 21 or so, a Destroyer save slot in the high 10s, and an Assasin save slot in the low 20s. Gunslinger sucks, I don't use it.

Replay the Paladin battle over and over again to get all of his clothing, and put all of it on Veradux. The stats are all excellent for his character and the poison piercing is at 600 for each piece. When Veradux does a poison attack, it will do a huge amount of damage... the subsequent damage for the 4 turns is only based on his strength, however.

The Judgement rifle is the best weapon in the game, especially Guardians, for every character except for Destroyers and Gunslingers. 50 magic, 30 strength, 30 vitality, 25 lightning piercing and 25 physical piercing.

The diadvantage of starting with your character as an Assassin is that you cannot use Shadow Blend; it's an NPC only move, like Amber... it increases speed by 500% for 4 turns, which ridiculously increases the Assassin's piercing damage. Amber can do like 1200 damage in one move at level 15 when this is enabled.

Here's a guide for the partners you should use when you unlock all of the characters:

If you start as the Guardian class, in my opinion the easiest class to use because their only weakness is a little bit of speed, so upgrade that above everything else: Veradux and Amber/Amber and Gligus/Veradux and Gligus

if you start as the Destroyer class, very high strength and good vitality, but somewhat slow, and pretty much zip in the way of magic: Veradux and Amber/Amber and Teco/Veradux and Teco

Is you start as the Assassin class... difficult to stay alive but with such a high speed rating, and the armor in the fourth area drastically increasing it, you can dodge hits more often than the other classes, and have lots more piercing damage, especially if you upgrade Smash and Master Strike: Veradux and Gligus/Veradux and Teco/Teco and Gligus

Don't even bother with the Gunslinger class. Although the magic moves have lots of special effects and can be deadly, you have low vitality and will be slaughtered in only a few hits unless you upgrade Intervention and Heroic Motivation...

Tip: The electro bolt is an absolutely essential move for Guardians. The armor they get throughout the game, especially in the last area, all has high lightning piercing ratings... combine this with Sunder, Wound, and the Magic Bolt attack, as well as the amount of damage being directly correlated to your strength and magic rating, you can do a ridiculous amount of damage and it will debuff enemies accoridng to its level ie. level 1 Electro Bolt will remove 1 buff, level 2 will remove 2, etc.

The same applies to Heroic Motivation - health recovered is 170% of your strength and magic at level 1 plus it removes one negative effect, 230% for level 2 and removes 2 neg. effects, 290% at level and removes 3 negative effects.

Anyone else got some strategies or tips for everyone?
