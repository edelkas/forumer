#465218 19 2008-08-28T22:15:36Z Pheidippides
I've had the album for a few weeks now, and I must say, it's /very/ good. Life in Technicolor provided a last bit of hype before I got to the meat of the album; the escalating music really got me excited to hear the other 9 songs. Cemeteries of London is a great, entertainingly dark-ish song with a great beat. Lost! and 42 were pretty good, but the album really gets good beginning with Lovers in Japan. Love that song. Yes is another good one, followed by Viva La Vida which, as good as it is, actually doesn't stand out because the supporting songs are all so great. Violet Hill is fantastic, a little harder rock that finishes with a somber little piano bit that provides a good contrast to bring it home. Strawberry Swing is a great little number, and Death and All His Friends ties it up very nicely just as the album began. All in all, I'm very glad I bought this. 9.9/10.

Pretty. Odd. by Panic at the Disco was good as well. Bought it on the same day as Viva La Vida, no less. I was in good-song overload for a bit over a week.
