rules of mapmaking:
  • No bugs or glitches (if Metanet doesnt use it, you dont use it.)

  • ??/10 fun&playability-left side(thrill for DDAs)
  • ??/10 creativity-left side
  • ??/10 fun&playability-right side (thrill for DDAs)
  • ??/10 creativity-right side
submit all maps to NUMA and post a link down here. all submissions will be accepted until the 23rd of july. you may submit old maps, just resubmit them to NUMA.
