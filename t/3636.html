#74499 3 2005-06-13T20:07:05Z brickman
Click OUTSIDE the data box before hitting "l", or for that matter any other keys. Just paste in the data, click outside, and load it, and like magic you'll no longer have problems. I'm assuming the problem is nly from the loading end--if you somehow saved it with a NaN in it, you're going to have to manually search the level data box for it and delete the whole string of code between the nearest !s before and after it to delete the object. And next time, search.
