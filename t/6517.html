#128220 2 2005-12-30T04:01:41Z Aries_Griff
I jump from the starting point to the middle blocks and walljump up to the third block, which I wallslide, then let the thwump aligned right above that third block go over my head (it's on the right side), then I get on top of that block. From there, I hop to the side of each of the middle blocks that is opposite from the lower of the two thwumps (so from that third block I jump to the right side, and then to the left side) and that triggers the lower thwump while I either wallslide that edge of the middle block or drop back down to the middle block below it and jump up to it once the thwump is gone. The top thwumps should go over your head once you're on the blocks, and if you practice it, you can evade the lower thwumps pretty easily. It just takes practice. And make sure on the way down that you don't get killed by that top right thwump if you're falling all the way down, try jumping high and then falling to the exit so you have more momentum.
