#341011 24 2007-07-09T19:53:19Z Frogs
Quote
What does that mean??
There was a recent contest, by the name of SUBLiME. N is being released on three different consoles (XBLA, PSP, and DS), so this competition was to see who could make a map that was worthy of being put on the different versions of this game. We were given three versions of NED (one for each console), and were allowed to submit up to five maps. This thread is for maps that we did not end up submitting to this contest. For regular maps, most everyone uses NUMA, or the N User Map Archive. Just create an account and submit your maps there. I hope this has been somewhat of a help.
