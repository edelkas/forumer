Welcome to the "Demo DDA Contest"


RULES
  • You will be given three 2,000 frame demos. Your job is to create a DDA with those demos.
  • Maximum of three submissions per person. Once you use a certain demo for a DDA, you can not use it again for another entry.
  • No Death DDAs, but KRADDAs are okay.
  • Judges may enter.
  • No old maps, because it's impossible.
  • All entries are due by Saturday, March 10, 2007 Saturday, March 31, 2007.
  • Any glitched object or tile is okay to use.
  • Post your maps in this thread or PM them to me.
  • Please post your map on NUMA link it to this page.
JUDGING CRITERIA

Each judge will give the map a score out of 40 possible points. No half marks may be given.

x/10: Excitement
x/10: Close Calls
x/10: Use of the Demo
x/10: Looks
