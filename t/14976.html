The Smart PackThanks to Pembie for the name
Ok, so here is the idea.

A pack consisting of 100 levels, but not by one person, or two, or three, but by 100 people, a pack comprised of the best map of each author arranged (hopefully) into episodic fashion (although not necessary, thats up to you people).

To help create the episode feel it would be helpful if you would try to post something complimentary to a map already submitted, but it is not necassry.

Group episodes (5 maps) may be submitted by a group of 5 people, but each person that participated in making that episode will not be allowed to submit another map.

To participate individually post with something along the lines of "I'm in.", the name of your map, and a link to your map. For groups post the name of your group (optional), the participants, and name and the links to the 5 maps.

In order to start things off I am going to submit a very simple map i made awhile back, but allows a lot of expansion possibilities to comlete an episode.

Edit: since I have not visited the level packs sub-forum in a few months I did not know of BuddyLee's thread, excuse the similarities, if one of the higher ups here thinks this is too similar you can remove it.
