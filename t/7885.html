How can I take screenshots for N-game?
#149267 2 2006-02-27T16:34:53Z iangb
Look on your keyboard for a key, part of which is marked 'print screen' or 'PrtSc'. It should be somewhere above your arrow keys, on most keyboards.

Hitting that button will save an image of your entire screen on Clipboard. Simply Paste the image somewhere (like Paint, for example) to have your scrrenshot.
Holding Alt while hitting the button will take a screenshot of the currently active window Usefull if you don't want to waste time cropping your image later.
