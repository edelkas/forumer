I tend to dislike maps that don't end properly with a win in the game engine. By these I mean kill-type puzzles (telling someone to killthemselves on a certain mine - why not just make the mine the exit? Admittedly a drone is different since it moves but I still don't like it), KRA levels where you can go into the door without manipulating the rocket, and 'herd the drone' levels where the 'ending' is all the drones being in a certain enclosure. I view these levels more as experiments than playable levels since you can't get a score in the game from beating them.

That's not to say I discourage experimentation, I've just never kept one since I prefer to play levels in game mode apart from some DDA's.
