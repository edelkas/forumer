#225046 13 2006-09-07T04:31:23Z The_Juggler
Are you using Dolby Digital Live or DTS-Connect? Are you using a DIAMOND XtremeSound XS71 sound card? Are you using Service Pack 2?

Give us your computer's stats (Clock Speed, VRAM and Videocard, RAM, Sound Card, etc). You cannot expect help with such little information given. Also it is like they say, Google is your friend. All I am doing is searching Google for this problem.

From what I can find this problem is caused by problems with the drivers of certain sound cards, particularly cards that support Dolby Digital surround sound. You may also experience this problem if you have problems with a lack of memory or are experiencing High Memory Address problems (which is less likely).

I am assuming that you have re-installed the game to make sure the install was not bad. You may try disabling your sound card in the device manager. If that does not work, remove the drivers completely. If that does not work, remove the sound card itsself and remove the drivers. If that does not work, try removing your RAM chips then re-inserting them to make sure they have not worked their way out of the socket due to chip creep.
If all of that does not work, post another message here and I will think of something.

This is not caused by Gametap, this is not caused by the game itsself. This may be caused by insufficient memory, this may be caused by the sound card, drivers, Service Pack 2, or another program that you have installed that messes with memory, drivers, or the operating system.
