This thread is for our editors. If you can edit music (Which means being able to use music-editing programs, combine tracks, add drumbeats, and so on), then sign in here. If you do more than one (Edit and Compose, for instance), please sign in to both threads. In this thread, make sure you list what programs you can use, and a short sampling of what you can do (For instance, being able to mesh tracks together would be foremost, having the ability to convert sound files would be another) with the editing programs.

Please, in this thread, only post once. If you have questions, pose them in general discussion.

Now, for you guys, what you'll be doing for the project is finalizing everything. For all of the individual projects, and for all of the group projects, you guys will need to be able to make everything sound professional. If volumes are too high on a track, or if a drumbeat is too prominent, you guys will need to be able to equalize things, and make the tracks sound great. From my experience with editors and editing software, if you're good at it, you can make anything sound good.

The first thing you should do, as an editor, is sign in here! Once we know how many composers, musicians, and editors we have, we'll divide you guys into groups of at least one composer, one musician, and one editor each.

The second thing you'll want to do, once we get groups assigned, is get in touch with your composer in their composer thread, and start thinking about what kinds of tunes we're going to be composing in the groups, and then once that gets done, we'll start recording individual parts, and you guys will mesh them together.

Thirdly, you guys will need to dictate to your groups and to the individual projectiers whether or not they're on-track. If something sounds horrible (If the track has too much static), let them know that you'll have to edit the track to minimize that.

Fourthly, you guys will need to be able to make the tracks sound good. For instance, if we have a jazz piece with a Trumpet, a Guitar, and a Trombone, you'll have to overlay the tracks in a way that makes them sound fantastic and finished.

And finally, it's your job to keep in touch with the other members of the group, and to make sure things are moving along nicely.

Beyond that, keep active, keep in touch with me, and welcome!
