How are you limited in what you download? Here are over 1400 sets of GIMP brushes. Have fun.

Also, I really don't like the look of the text. The pattern overlay just makes it hard to read as you can't distinguish the black parts of the text from the background. Perhaps you could add a white drop shadow or something to make it stand out a bit. To make a drop shadow: duplicate the text layer, alpha to selection on the bottom-most text layer, fill it white, deselect and then gaussian blur at about 4 or 5.
