According to Nintendo Power, no more copies of Chibi-Robo are going to be produced. This isn't an uncommon occurence by any stretch of the word, but those of you who haven't checked it out should definitely actively seek it out now since it's only going to become harder to come across from this point on. Several stores have discounted it, along with Battalion Wars and Fire Emblem: PoR, to the very agreeable price of 15 dollars. Call around and see if any of your local stores have it in stock, it's well worth the effort.

Hm. I played a demo of Chibi-robo at Target

It's pretty cool; you basically control a robot a little less than half a foot in height (hence "chibi") who performs tasks within a certain time limit throughout a house by cleaning up messes (with a toothbrush!), picking up trash (where the trashcan is its head, which pops up), and zapping equally robotic spidery... things (with some sort of laser gun). There are also other devices such as a head-propeller, I think, and your chibi-robo, who's also accompanied by another hovering, owl-like drone, is constantly using up its battery; even as you stand idly, and much more when you're running and performing actions, a number-laiden bar on the HUD slowly depletes. Your cord dangles loosely behind you as you run, and every now and then you have to hold it over your head and plug it into a wall socket or other such source of power to rejuvenate. You can stand on chairs, tables, pull out drawers from desks, etc.

I felt it would be nice for a rental but that the repetition of action would make it a bit boring after a while; I'm not quite surprised that it's no longer being produced
