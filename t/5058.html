This is the third time I'm writing the introduction for this topic. TWICE the window with the introduction has closed down, and every time it has got shorter and shorter. It was originally bloody long. Anyway, I'm gonna make this quick before someone shuts down my computer just before I press submit:

This thread is what it says on the tin - my NUMA commentary, basically NUMA in tiny bitesized chunks. I'll give my opinions on what's going on, and tell you about the good, the bad, and the ugly. Basically, it'll be a summary of what's going on at NUMA. Perfect if you only want to see a few maps, and also great for starting off conversation based on NUMA. Feel free to add your opinions, or anything you think I may have missed out. I won't be updating this every day, and I won't be looking at every map, so join in: write your own accounts. Go for it.

4th October
Weather: N00b heavy as usual, but quite a lot of great maps considering the time of year.
Firstly, I'll start off with my favourite map from the day.
Rose is N Art at it's best - simple and effective. I also like the way it's a rose made of mines, as that's perhaps symbolic. Sure, they were chosen because they're red, but I like the accidental hidden message nonetheless. It's gone in my favourites.
Random Shapes was another high quality map. Perhaps not perfect, but it showcases BenMiff's growing style and map-making talent.
The worst map of the day, in my opinion, is the abomination that is Simple Challenge? Putting shame to the good simple challenge name, this is simply awful. Needless overuse of enemies: check. Unoriginal use of enemies: check. Tileset that's been given no thought whatsoever: check. Load of bollocks: er, check.
Whilst My little Tribute to formica is a little awkward at times, and clusters enemies, it isn't too bad. But this isn't why I'm highlighting this map: look at the bottom of the page. Yes, tremble and fear, for local little bastard craftymerchant is back on NUMA. As many of you know, he's evading the ban under the name of craftymerchantR. How idiotically crafty of him. Anyhoo, I thought I'd point out that he's already trying to cause trouble again with a needless "boo" comment. Sure, it isn't that exciting, but he needs to be watched with an eager eye.
I can't help but feel Jumping Up The Schrappnel is needlessly overrated. It's a nice tileset, sure, and an easy map is always nice, but... well, you can bypass half the map by just jumping up the left wall. Doesn't that defeat the point completely? It just feels a tad unexciting and not very well made. Don't get me wrong: I am enjoying Macaddict's maps, but this is overrated. It is now 4/5 after I rated it, but it was 4.5/5. Overrated. 'Nuff said.
To finish with, Heart Broken is a rather nice map.

6th October (includes maps from day before too)
Weather: It has to be said, the n00b hurricane of the past few weeks seems to have cleared up. The past two days have been pretty good... but there are still plenty of n00bish maps and moments around good ol' NUMA.
Here we go...
The Magnetic Fields is a great ambassador for simplicity everywhere. Such a simple concept, and it works really nicely, and it feels really polished.
Aju proved his growing map talent with the lovely looking, lovely playing Delerious. The asymetric tileset works great with N moving about on it. Excellent work.
Meanwhile, the award for most suspiciously overrated goes to the plain awful Rat Race. God knows how this, for a start, was given a vote of 5 from someone. Furthermore, God knows how after 6 votes this map remains on 3 ninjas. This, quite frankly, is taking the piss. It's an awful map.
Of course, maybe Rat Race wouldn't have been given this award if it wasn't for
Speed Is The Key not reaching the five votes needed to reveal its average rating. How did Speed Is The Key get a 4.5 from wellsj? I'd like to find out, but until now I'm just going to go over to a corner and cry.
It is always notable when tktktk returns with another of his fantastic Simple Challenge series. #16 is nothing more than you'd expect from what has gone before.
Also notable is the return of the irreplaceable Sendy to the map-making fray. Dave Cut The Record Down To The Bone is a vintage Sendy piece of work.
The worst description of the past 24 hours has to be given to the odd beach+mine collaboration of Mine jumping in water. The title sounds slightly Japanese and the description lives up to it - poor grammar, and frankly it is just a bit odd.
Meanwhile, somewhere else on Earth, Have A Slope was simply just quite fun. It was unexpected, sure, and after a while this loses its replay value, but it's loveably simple and fun. It doesn't look great, but it doesn't matter! It is also completely unoriginal, but I don't care.
Something is not only a crap name for a DDA, but a crap DDA.
However, Ironmuffins is a great name for a DDA, and a great DDA. Really excellent work. Enjoy the show.


That's me done.
