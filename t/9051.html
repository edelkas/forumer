#166579 5 2006-04-19T10:56:46Z LoD
How to submit levels to NUMA

1a. Buy a new keyboard. (All-caps is awful.)
1b. Visit a course about punctuation. (Can be very useful in today's world.)
2. Launch N and click "Configure".
3. Fill in the three text boxes and click "Create new user".
4. Open your browser and open http://numa.notdot.net.
5. Log in (at the lower left) using the username and password you typed in in N.
6. After having logged in, choose "Submit a map" from the menu at the left.
7. Fill in the form. Open the file (or whatever) where you stored your level data, copy the data of the level which you want to submit (only the part which you got from the text box in Ned) and paste it into the suitable text box in the NUMA web form.
8. Click the submit button (or however it is called).
9. On the following page, check the thumbnail to match your level. If it doesn't, check your map data.
10. Click the submit button (or however it is called). Your map will be submitted to NUMA.
