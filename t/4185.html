There's this one.

If the server's being fussy, just read this one, which is an exact copy of the one in the link, minus the images:

This level is a very difficult level, but with practice it can be beaten. This level introduces 2x2 one-way squares, which, if you land in them, will trap you. Dodgem! From the start, jump and wallclimb up, then walljump to the exit to avoid the gauss. Jump and walljump again to the top of the figure, then jump onto the bounceblock, minding the rocket. Jump to the ledge and flip the switch. Now, drop down through the squares, onto the left slope of the diamond, and flip the switch there. Jump to the unlocked room. Avoid the rocket, which probably followed you there, then activate the terminal. After that, jump and walljump into the golden area, and snatch the gold from the zap drone. Jump up onto the slope, and use the launchpad to get onto the bounceblock's side. Wallclimb, then leap away from the rocket. Drop down the right wall, then walljump and run to the exit.

I despised this level. It's one of those hurdle levels. I found Curvaceous was annoying too, anyways.

Most people are saying to do the high road. I didn't do it that way. I ran and hopped over the jump pad, wall climbed up, grabbed the gold. By this time the rocket is dead. Then use the jumppad and jump off the bouncy block to hit the first switch. You have to time your bounceback or you'll head facefirst into the rocket. Hit the switch, jump off and hit the next switch on the way down. Get rid of the rocket (should be easy) then just use the new opened passageway to get the button. Sliding down the wall a lil will do it. Then it's just a case of wallclimbing up the far right wall and into the exit.
