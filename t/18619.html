Here's a poem. I'll have a song parody up in a minute.


Upon a Wall in Rome

I stood short and small

At Romes dark gate,

Shooting stars fall;

The walls a blank slate.


Staring the moon,

Blinking the stars,

Looking the sky,

Gazing the galaxy.


Outlined clouds,

Bursting with light,

A brilliant night;

I had to take flight.


I drifted like driftwood

Upon the open waters of my mind,

Passing darkly through

Stars and fires

Into a state of

Consciousness that was

Far more than anything

That I had found before.


Close the Eyes, Enjoy the Mind.


This is what I wrote upon the wall;

I felt in an instant the deepest truth.

Also knowing I could fall,

I rose, entranced, my mind aloof.


Only vandalism, graffiti paint,

A cold reminder of the death of art,

Sprayed with care on the face of a saint,

Erasing the memory of his heart.


Yet it spoke to me.

I spoke the writing.

I felt the wall.

I closed my eyes and enjoyed the night.
