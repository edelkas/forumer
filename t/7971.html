I've got a quick little example of an easy way to beat 97-0 I found a few seconds ago. I played it for about five minutes (looong time for me) and the second I discovered this, I beat it! Instead of dropping down fom your ledge like I was inclined to, hold left and hop as soon as the level starts. If you get it right, you should jump over the ascending drone and land on the wall across from you. Then, if you keep holding left (this works 9/10 times) you should let go of the ledge and just BARELY miss the two mines on your way to the floor. From there, take a quick running jump to the opposite wall, avoiding the floor chaser, and you should have a drone-free path right up the column using the chimney technique! (my biggest problem was drones getting in my way)

Quote: elaxion, April 06, 2006 09:17 pm
any one know how to beat 83-2, im stuck on it for know

If gold is not an issue, the easiest way for me was to jump on the first protrusion/vertical passage (I call them that for lack of better wording, with a protrusion/vertical passage being both walls that go either up or down, are linked by a directional access plate, and the switch/sliding doors in between as well), from there jump straight to the top of the third protrusion/vertical passage, after which you do a smaller jump which should make it so that you are falling down directly between the two walls of the last protrusion/vertical passage with missiles after you, which the slidey gate thingies will detonate (hopefully). Upon landing on the farthest left white bouncy block, wait for it to depress as far as it possibly will from the amount of your impact and then jump (watch for the overhang from the bottom of the third protrusion/vertical passage as it can often impeded/ruin the outcome of your jump/landing) to the third (from the right) bouncy block, after which you jump up the protrusion/vertical passage, which should now be unlocked due to your traveling through the farthest left protrusion/vertical passage. From there jump down the second, wait for the bouncy block, upon impact, to be where you need it, and then jump to the final/farthest right protrusion/vertical passage's bouncy block which should then enable you to jump through the above passage and over to the door on your right.
