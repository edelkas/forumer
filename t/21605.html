This is an image worm much like our other one, but inspired entirely by an album of music. Each image should follow after the other seamlessly to, in the end, form a single long image.

For the music part of it, images you make should be somehow inspired by a song (or part of a song) on the current album we're worming through. If you're the kind of person who can associate colors with music, perfect, use 'em. If you want your image to be something actually talked about in the song, go ahead. Want to copy the lyrics word for word and use those? Why not.

For each worm, I want to take an album and make images for it start to finish. To start, it'll be one 640x480 image for each song. OK Computer has 12 songs, so 12 images should cover it. If we get more demand than 12 artists per album, we can make it two images per track later. And in some cases, certain songs will go on 10 minutes, 20, 30, even more. Those could easily be stretched over several images. But for now, one image per track.

The first album I have is OK Computer by Radiohead. I picked it because it's extremely popular, well-known, and generally well-liked. It also has a fair amount of variation, so the worm can have more style changes.

Here's the tracklist to choose from. You can post to reserve the next spot in line or a particular track. (But try to be flexible. If someone really wants Fitter Happier, consider taking The Tourist instead.) Feel free to get to work as soon as your song's up. If we get more than one person who won't budge off of certain songs, we can allow more than one image for that song. But please, no more than about three for the same song. The other tracks need love too.
