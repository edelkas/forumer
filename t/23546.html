WEEK ONE THEME: DRONE MODS


-With this weeks theme you must make a map showing the wonderful things that drones can do. You are allowed to make action, puzzle, or race maps in order to prove your knowledge of what a drone can do.
-You may use any drone mods and you may use any other mod you see fit as long as the map fits the theme of Drone mods to the best of your ability. Drones mods can accomplish any number of things, and of course using a mod never before seen will score you bonus points.

On a side note, if possible, when you put your link here, which you must do to enter the map in the comp. , please post at least verbally what mods you used or the actual code of it. This is not required, but it will grealy ease the judging process.

This week's round ends Sunday, 11:59pm Pacific standard time (PST = GMT -8).
  • *PST = 11:59 pm sunday, August 10th
    *GMT = 6:59 am monday, August 11th
    * Hong Kong = 2:20 pm monday, August 11th
Any maps submitted after this deadline will not be accepted, no exceptions.
