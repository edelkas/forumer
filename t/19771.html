#372285 2 2007-10-16T00:00:25Z George
Here's how to do it.

1. Press 1 to begin recording your demo.
2. Press P to begin playing, Caps Lock to switch between frame by frame and real time.
3. When you're finished, press 2.
4. If you die, you can reset everything by pressing R, and repeat.

Please refer to the Editor's Manual or the pinned topics for help next time; it's all covered there.
