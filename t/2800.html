I was just looking at some of the photoshopped avatars and thinking how nifty it would be if you could do some of those things in-game... and then, for whatever reason, the idea of a posable ragdoll struck me. Basically all I see it as is a scaled-up version of the ninja suspended perfectly still just above a floor, and you can click and drag the various body parts around to put him in whatever pose you want... and possibly an "activate gravity" key or button that will then drop him to the floor in a potentially uncomfortable position. Maybe you could also fling him around like in debug mode. Pointless, but I think it'd be kind of fun to fiddle with if it wouldn't require monumental effort to implement, and it might make fashioning a humorous photoshopped image a bit easier.

If this is really just a stupid idea, don't take it too seriously, I'm simply rambling off whatever comes to mind as the new version approaches.
