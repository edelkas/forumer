EDIT I'm gonna kill the quote that the below passage should normally have; I've edited too much already for it to be truly authentic anymore.


=-=-=-=-=-=


A weird Californian, Internet alias being "Keron Cyst," found N on GameHippo and out of the blue from seemingly instant addiction suggested in May 2004 to Mare Sheppard that a messageboard ought to be created to bind a potential community that it may have gathered.

Plans were set, a .TXT blueprint of the generic categories, forums, and child forums of the messageboard was concocted, and an IPB board hosted by Forumer under the name "metanet" on server 2 was created purely because Cyst's previous obsession with the WhiteSpace Unlimited community (creators of the Stick Soldiers series) bore forums under Forumer, which was quite new at the time.

[ Yet somehow MN was able to establish a forum on server 2 (hence metanet.2.forumer.com) while the old SS forums were located on server 4 ]

(The newest SS forum is now hosted by Andrew Russell Studiosthe people behind SS3and is powered by SMF, not IPB.)

Ironically, Cyst was too busy with the basic forum setup at the time to worry about new staff (the forums were very small back then, anyways), and didn't help M&R with handpicking any potential moderators; in fact, he didn't even know any of their personal contributions and personalities, but seeing how the duo were smart enough to make N ( ) he went forward with the new staff member selections (Kablizzy, maximo, and LittleViking001) without hesitation as minor Moderators of the forum. Two are now Administrators and one is a Global Moderator.

Tsukatu came along later, a bit shortly after the Bash & Thrash forum was created, and what with his blatant swearing and criticisms of people's maps was unanimously elected for B&T Tyrant. He is now retired and iangb has taken his place as Sukian in his popular pinned thread at the top of the B&T.

Mojo's banner was thrown at the top of the boards (I think you can still find my test thread about it on one of the last pages of Tests & Inquiries somewhere) after showing off his Photoshop expertise and making sig. images for numerous people.

Some time between Kablizzy joining the forums and December 2004, The Wunder Bunny conspired with Cyst for a 2005 forum surprise, which was specifically the revamp in the Forumer skin (that is, making many images N-friendly). He was supposedly suspended (until Jan. 15, I recall ) because Cyst had told him of what he was going to do to the forum skin and Kablizzy wanted to tell some others (I actually seriously thought of kicking him out of the boards temporarily because of that possibility, which was where the conspiracy sparked from), and he supposedly went on a mass swearing spree, PMing Cyst rather vulgar remarks about his belief in strict constitution (I actually favor loose, but that was the way I was thinking at the time ), which gave him public evidence to boot him. The event was dubbed the Kablizzy Fiasco alongside the 2005 Forum Surprise.

[ Ironically, Cyst actually did suspend Kablizzy for a day, just so he could see what the board looked like when one was rejected by its system ]

I think a bit after the beginning of the year 2005, Arachnid made an IRC room from irc.mware.ca for the community and "moderates" NUMA Discussion (being its webmaster).

The Youth & Beauty Brigade, a large trivia search by the staff, also occurred. This made use of the password-protected forum icon, a Floorchaser. A bit after the Y&BB completed (or collapsed? hm), a password-protected forum was created for Trogdor007 and some others in a futile attempt to revive another puzzler for the community. It died before it was born.

nevermore, Maniac, and tktktk (I think iangb was a bit earlier) all were promoted simultaneously as a result of the need for more staff members of the growing messageboard, which then had ~1500 users.

Cyst then made the FAQs, finally finding a use for his read-only forum icon.

sweep becoming a mod was one of the more recent actions, after the post-Thanksgiving November hacking attacks (you can find synopses on those in the N Wikicities, which have a link at the bottom of the board index).

blue_tetris has also become a regular Moderator, away from RPG Moderator status like Daddaluma. sweep currently vies in competition against blue_tetris for control over forum subsections.
