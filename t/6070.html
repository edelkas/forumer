#120388 3 2005-12-04T17:18:44Z LittleViking
Frame by frame is only availible in debug mode. Press \ or ~ at the main title screen to access debug mode. To test at normal playing speed, turn caps lock on, and to test in frame by frame, leave it off. Clicking the mouse will advance to the next frame.

As a side note, I think this has got to be the first time I've ever told a new person to leave caps lock off.
