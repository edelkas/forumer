#12917 1 2006-12-16T21:49:18Z sweep
I've never been a horribly meticulous person in terms of planning ahead, as a whole. But I do enjoy a good plan.

Currently, I'm living in plan limbo. It's horrible. It's all as a result of me getting used to living with a situation by planning every day ahead. This does, of course, refer to her*... for several weeks now, each night I've planned ahead for the next day, how I'd act towards her and stuff like that. This isn't something I did purposefully, I just got into the habit, when you think about someone all the time it's only natural that you start looking ahead and making decisions. However, you can't really plan if you don't feel certain about your position. Whilst my opinion of her has been all over the shop, it has always been certain at any one moment. Until now.

This is plan limbo. For the first time since I've met her, I will not see the girl for five days. Then I will see her at a Christmas party. There will be alcohol. There will be music. There will be a very distinct chance of things happening. Fantastic opportunity? Well, yes. Especially because I know the feelings are pretty mutual, and this isn't just some random girl I kinda like a bit. There's *something* there. But the problem is, she completely betrayed me when I last saw her. I won't go into it, but she knew she was killing me inside. And she knew what she was doing.

I want to hate her. I also know that this party was set to be... well, it was very likely to be the night when we moved up a level, as it were. I don't know whether to go into this occasion with the intention of making her feel guilty and unwanted of her or with the intention of making things happen. Those are two pretty polarized ways I could go about it. I don't think there's really a middle-ground. It confuses me so much that I have no idea which way to go. I'm stuck in plan limbo. And worst of all, it's a five day long plan limbo. I will not stop thinking about this for many, many hours.

The irony, of course, is that whenever I've laid plans, they've always turned out to be irrelevant when it's come to the situation. She has far too much control for me to really plan anything. But the uneasiness I'm feeling for not having a true plan really scares me.

Are plans really a curse? Is it better to go into everything with eyes closed? Or should you always think ahead?

*aka "that girl I like"
