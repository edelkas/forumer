At first, I was planning on posting something big. Something marvelous. Something that had a lot of effort put into it. Instead of what I'm about to tell you.

This is the story.

===
Ngaged was feeling bored and kind of sad one day. He didn't have much inspiration to make maps. No ideas came to mind. (That explains the lack of maps on NUMA.) But Ngaged still wanted to do good things for the community and make some more maps. But he didn't know how he could do it...until...

Suddenly, Ngaged thought of an idea. Sure, it had been done before. But Ngaged was thinking of creating...a level pack.

Ngaged didn't want to do it alone. So he called some of his greatest inspirations of mapmaking. He called upon TDL (formica), Stepself, Pariah (astheoceansblue), great_sea, wellsj, and blue_tetris. Soon he got responses. TDL said he would like to participate. So did stepself. So did Pariah. So did great_sea. So did wellsj. B_T didn't want to (which I fully understand, man).

There was hope. Perhaps Ngaged and all of these brilliant people could combine forces and do something extremely awesome for the community. Because they all deserved it.

We planned to be ready by the end of summer. But then we wondered "What should our name be?" After a while, we all decided on a simple name, that fit with the situation. We called it Summer's End.

And so, everyone got straight to work. Everyone was out at work making maps.

TDL came up with some ideas for the project which everyone agreed upon. He even created a little subforum for us in his own forums so we could easily communicate. And great_sea announced that he couldn't work on maps anymore. However, to make up for it, he said he would make something a bit more interesting. And he got to work on that. Everyone else on the team was busy making their wonderful maps.

It was a great time. TDL was finished with all of his maps, Ngaged was, and stepself was nearly finished.

...

But then, when summer was drawing to a close, unfortunate things started happening. Wellsj announced that he couldn't participate anymore because he was preparing for school exams. (Which is the right decision, my friend. ) A bit later, great_sea announced that he was moving to a new home. But his new home didn't have internet. He said that he couldn't participate anymore.

With that, two members were gone. And it was almost at the end of summer, when we planned to release our level pack. Ngaged contacted the remaining members, but only got a real response and update from TDL. Summer's End wasn't going too well.

So I had no choice.

TDL had already submitted his portion to the forums as something else. And I then decided made my maps the second addition to my episodes.

Summer's End...was no more.

And that's the story. Sorry we couldn't do this for you guys.

===
For the Summer's End team:

I owe a big apology to teveryone, so I'll say it here. I'm sorry that we weren't able to do this. I thought we would, but before we could make some real progress, uncontrollable things happened to halt our progress. I don't know how or why this ended up the way it did...

Guys...Honestly tell me, please...Was I a bad leader?

Were you a bad leader? Not necessarily, but you could certainly have been better. It's extremely important to keep in close contact with your fellow collaborators from the beginning, and keep it up throughout the project. That way when things come up and impede your progress, you'll know exactly what's going on and take whatever steps are necessary to remedy the problem. It also helps to look ahead as far as possible so that everyone knows what problems you might run into in the future (for example, I announced my leaving for a month to the rest of the Legacy team several weeks beforehand).
