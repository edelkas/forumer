This level has been covered several times in the forum (for example, here) due to its relative difficulty at the beginning of the game. Just remember that at the beginning, there is no need to go into the bottom right area with the mine (the switches there are useless). At the floorguard part, jump in the leftmost hole first to lure it, then go in the second entrance and run as quickly as possible to get out. The rest of the level is actually pretty simple then.

Also, the N wikia has an useful walkthrough.
