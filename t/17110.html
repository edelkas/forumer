Plasma Pong! Same basic idea of pong, but with the addition of plasma shooting paddles.
Although the game starts out slow, and since you can deflect the ball simply by firing plasma at it, easy, but as it progresses, you'll find that the fluid dynamics become extremely complex, so it's hard to predict where the ball will be carried by the waves and eddies of plasma. There's also a multi-player, which gives the game a more lasting quality.

#318747 21 2007-05-06T04:44:57Z Gforce20
Hey, you can do a REALLY cool thing in sandbox mode. It looks like white noise with gray and black shades, and it forms into strange shapes. Here's how to do it:

1. Enter Sandbox mode and press Clear.
2. Set the shading to Plasma Shader.
3. No lines.
4. Use the Blackbody color-set-thingy.
5. Turn on 3-D lighting.
6. Switch cursor to Particle Mode.
7. Switch Viscosity and Jello on.
8. Set the color to black dye.
9. Click the left button on the mouse and hold. Move it around just a little while you hold.
10. Switch cursor to Sink Mode.
11. Quickly click and let go, then let the show begin!
