A couple of nights ago I was lying in bed reading.

"Kriiiiii... Kriiiii... Kriiiii..."

I look up. The noise stops. I go back to reading again.

"Kriiii.... Kriiiiiiiii..."

Again, the eerie chirping. It can't be crickets, surely? I've never heard them around college before. It's in the city centre, after all, and I can't help but think crickets have trouble digging tunnels in concrete. I get up to investigate - it sounds as though it's coming from the room next door - but the noise stops again. I wait a while, then return to my book.

"Kriii... Kriiii... Kriiii..."

This time the noise continues after I get up. When I walk across the room, I notice that the noise seems to be emanating from the small bin in my room.

The noise stops.

I look at the garbage bin.

It looks at me.

I prod it with my foot.

"Kriiiii....."

I look at it again. There's not even anything edible in there, just some plastic packaging. Surely it couldn't be insects? I prod it a few more times, and every time it makes a noise, but whatever is actually generating the chirping doesn't seem inclined to show itself.

There can be only one course of action at this point. I must remove the bag of garbage from the bin in an attempt to find the offender. I wish to go to bed shortly, and I'm sure as hell not putting up with that noise all night.

So, I gingerly hook the bag with one finger and lift it our, fully expecting some hideous bug-eyed thing with skittering legs to rush out of the bag, antennae flailing wildly, leaving a swathe of filth and decay in its wake.

Nothing.

I place the bag onto the floor, whilst keeping an eye on both the bag and bin.

I peer into the bin. Nothing there.

I look at the bag. It's your generic, semi-transparent, cheap plastic supermarket bag. I can't see anything that looks remotely biological inside.

I kick the bag.

Nothing.

Steeling myself, I remove a couple of things from the bag.

Still nothing. The bag is empty but for a few lonesome bits of plastic. But the noise was definately coming from the bin! And nothing has left either bin or bag! It must be coming from the bin itself! Ganesha preserve me, it's ALIVE!

I reunited the loving bin-bag couple, and the noise did not return.

I can only assume that two bits of plastic were rubbing together as they settled (tsk, tsk... packaging has no sense of decency these days) and that lifting the bag sperated them. And that's the tale of my bizzarre, but rather anticlimactic encounter with the Living Trash Can.
