It doesn't seem to have a proper website, so I'll direct you to Gamehippo instead.

Anyway, Poyo is a puzzle-y platformer made in Game Maker. If you really can't stand mine jumpers (or archaic graphics), you'll probably want to give it a miss. Otherwise, most people who like N should enjoy it. A warning, though: if you're using high resolution, the game will be tiny and difficult to see, which is rather annoying. Still, I've almost finished it despite running on 1680x1050, and I'm not renowned for my love of mine-jumping. The review on Gamehippo says it's very difficult, but I disagree - it's certainly much easier than N.

In conclusion, it's not going to win any awards, but it's still a decent game and worth the (quite small) download.
