In this game, each participant answers the previous riddle, and poses a new riddle.

Riddles, as defined for use in this game, are semi-poetic lists of clues. Play passes to the next participant when they gives an answer which fits all the clues, or the answer the person who posed the riddle intended.

I haven't a muscle nor nerve;
I'm not all too easy to swerve;
I use blade to kill blade,
I never get paid,
And I exist only to serve.
What am I?
