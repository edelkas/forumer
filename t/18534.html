Ohh, a common problem that is bothering you.

You start with 152 userlevels, and you can add more, which is what you are trying to accomplish. However, you have this issue. See, the original copies of your userlevels.txt and your main N.exe must be in the same place for them to be in sync and work properly. You must have physically moved either one of them somewhere else, yes? Whether they are in a folder, on your desktop, or anywhere on your computer, they must be in the same place. So, place those 2 things in any folder together, and make shortcuts to them on your desktop to gain easy accessibility.
