#330242 2 2007-06-12T01:10:03Z usaswim
The taskbar, on the bottom on the screen? You can't install or uninstall that And you most certainly can't end the process, because there is none!

Just move your mouse over it, and when it pops up, right-click an empty stop in it. Go to Properties. On the Taskbar tab, uncheck "Auto-hide the taskbar". Press Apply, then OK.

That should be the solution. If not, there may be other softwares affecting it. ObjectDock is one program that can control the visibility of the taskbar. If you've uninstalled it previously, re-install it. Once it's there, press the icon that says "Configure Dock". On the General tab, make sure "Hide the Windows Taskbar" is unchecked. Then uninstall it, or keep it (I recommend keeping it )
