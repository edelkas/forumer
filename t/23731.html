WEEK FIVE THEME: ANYTHING GOES


-With this weeks theme you must make a map showing the wonderful things that NReality mods can do. You are allowed to make action, puzzle, or race maps in order to prove your knowledge of what any mod can do.
-You may use any mods as long as the map fits the theme of object mods to the best of your ability. Mods can accomplish any number of things, and of course using a mod never before seen will score you bonus points.
-Since there is such a wide spectrum of possibilities for NReality mods, we are limiting everyone to 3 maps per person maximum

On a side note, if possible, when you put your link here, which you must do to enter the map in the comp. , please post at least verbally what mods you used or the actual code of it. This is not required, but it will grealy ease the judging process.

Also a demo would be helpful if included on NReality. This is optional

This week's round ends Sunday, 11:59pm Pacific standard time (PST = GMT -8).
  • *PST = 11:59 pm sunday, September 7th
    *GMT = 6:59 am monday, September 8th
    * Hong Kong = 2:20 pm monday, September 8th
Any maps submitted after this deadline will not be accepted, no exceptions.
