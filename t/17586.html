I will edit this later when I have more time, but although I will be busy with the Sig Tag contest I thought it would be really cool to create you own contest for N. I think there are a lot of good ideas out there that don't get put into action and if Metanet created their own contest I think that would be something really interesting.

It's sort of like a contest within a contest.


Please make your contest relating to N considering it's already in the N forum and I dont want to request a move...

The way this will work is by nominating an idea. I think 2 ideas per person would be good, and from there a poll will be taken for users to vote on their favorite idea. There will most likely be multiple polls if the number of ideas goes over 10, since you can only have 10 answers per poll.

If there are multiple polls then the winners from each poll will be polled against each other. From there the rules are formulated.


The first round will start out by nominating a certain type of contest - ie a prisoner contest, a swiss contest, etc etc (please be more original than these). PM me your nomination idea with a brief explanation of why you think your idea would be different.

The second round will consist of the rules of the contest, allowing users to really make their own contest here. We will start off with rule 1, with each person submitting two ideas for rule one. Then we move on to rule 2, etc, until we decide there are enough rules. You may submit your same submissions from Rule 1 to Rule 2 and any subsequent rounds, so long as they are not already part of the rules.

Once the rules have been decided on you will submit judging criteria, ie what should be judged and what shouldn't. To start I would ask that each person submit only one choice, but if need be I will grant two submissions.

Then we will nominate prizes, although I may just ask you to come up with the prizes you want.


And although people may say that this should be in messageboard games... well, it is a contest to create an N contest so I think there is some merit of it being here. Plus this post was up for grabs.

tell me what you think.
