SURVIVOR N Island


INSTRUCTIONS:
Congratulations, all participants will be instantly swept away to an island full of survival challenges! These maps may vary from custom-built maps to previously submitted ones by other authors. As in the real show Survivor, there will be outcasts, special challenges, the occasional puzzle, and of course people getting voted off. However, while the strongest mambers of a team may pose a threat to the others winning, they're also crucial to getting higher scores on the team challenges, which can greatly help your score. Meanwhile, the lower-ranked members of your team may be a weak link, but pose no threat to the average player. This challenge is about both skill-testing and social rankings.
THE COURSE:
Depending on the number of players, there will be a varied number of challenges throughout the competition, but there will be about 1/3 Survivals, 1/4 Puzzles, 1/5 Races, and the remainder a mixture of these. The challenges will be scored upon multiple ideas, often adding all the members' scores together or averaging them. Gold may or may not affect your team's score, depending on the type of challenge. After two challenges have been played out, each member will send me a Private Message saying who they vote to be cast off the island. The member with the most votes will be disqualified.
You will have 5 3 days to post your demo. In the event of a tie, you will have 2 days to decide who will play the tiebreaking third map and 2 days for the two players to submit their demos.
BONUSES:
There will be a number of surprises throughout the challenge. For example, players who get higher scores on some races and survivals may receive the Immunity Idol. For those of you who don't know, that means that that person cannot be voted off for the next voting. Also, the team members may also have to send one to Exile Island, where the two members (one from each team) will be sent away for the next challenge.
NOTE:
There will be no judges, as this competition is not based upon opinion, but purely skill and time.
