Well, you can go to your Userlevels.txt and put in the following:
$Map Name#Author#Type (Action, DDA, Puzzle, etc.)#Map data#
So yeah, put in the right stuff, and your Userlevels.txt should have your level in it. Just make sure N and the txt file are in the same place, like your desktop. That's what I do.
