#295977 4 2007-02-10T21:40:09Z Tsukatu
Quote: Bigblargh, February 10, 2007 04:02 pm
I believe evolution exists and stuff. That said, the egg came first, because it was the first thing with a gene mutation that separated a chicken form a non-chicken, whatever the criteria may be. The mutation does not happen AFTER the egg has hatched.
Exactly.

The best definition we can get of the chicken we know and love today is one that fits into a particular genetic mold, which resulted in generations of mutations. The parents of the world's first chicken are both whatever the chicken mutated from - the chicken would have those exact characteristics, except that it has a particular mutation that qualifies it as a chicken in terms of genetics.
The very first instance of a chicken that is going to fit that genetic mold is going to start off as an egg.
If the first "chicken" as we think of it started out as an egg, the egg came first.
