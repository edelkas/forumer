#380922 2 2007-11-19T20:17:35Z Twistkill
Ah yes, the dreaded sticky keys dilemma... I have 2 solutions for you:

1) If you want to simply change the jump key to something else, then you can change all of the keys to anything you wish in the Configure menu of N, on the upper right hand side. This is obviously a simple solution.

2) If you still want to keep shift as your jump key,then you can deactivate the Stickykeys shortcut on Windows XP. This will take a little more time, but it will also get rid of it for any other programs that require the usage of the shift key, such as Microsoft Word. Go to Control Panel -> Accessibility Options. In the Keyboard tab, make sure that the "Use Stickykeys" box is unticked. In addition, you should also click on the Settings button and untick the box that says "Use shortcut" option. This will completely stop the "pressing shift 5 times" problem that you're experiencing.

Good luck with N.
