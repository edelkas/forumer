#215078 4 2006-08-13T14:58:07Z iangb
Hrmph....

The search for unification is a major one. Simplified, the main difference between general relativity and quantum mechanics is that according to relativity, spacetime is smooth - be it flat or curved. However quantum mechanics describes spacetime on a tiny scale as a 'quantum froth' - totally unpredictable. Normally this doesnt matter because on the tiny scale quantum can apply and on every other scale relativity can apply, but for specific cases such as black holes - massive objects operating on tiny scales - there is a problem.

String theory essentially solves this problem by describing fundamental particles not as infinitely small point-particles, but as loops of energy with a finite width. Because that width is greater than the scale on which the 'quantum froth' operates, it can be ignored completely as it is too small to ever affect matter - by defenition, nothing can ever be smaller than a string-particle.
