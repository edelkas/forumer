This one is about my stance on drugs, alcohol, and narcotics.

Quote: Nothing But Common Sense
I think positively of myself. Some may call it an ego, some may go as far as calling it narcissism, but the majority calls it elitism. Im ambitious and very optimistic about my future. I have high standards for myself and have unwavering confidence in my intellectual abilities.
Because I see such value in the integrity of my mind, I have sworn... eternal hostility against every form of tyranny over the mind of man (Thomas Jefferson). My mental acuity defines me. Without it, I am nothing. For this reason, I see absolutely nothing beneficial coming from anything that causes damage to my mind, be it in the form of impairment of judgment, obsession/addiction, or physical damage.
This vendetta upon such forms of tyranny really began with my dislike of religion. Through all of my experience with religion, I have realized the importance of soundness of mind. I saw religion as retarding perception and retarding objective analytical ability. I refused to adopt religion because I felt it would do this to me, limiting my cognitive ability. I wouldnt stand for it.
But this lesson learned applied to much more than religion. When I was very young, I would see my parents drinking alcohol casually and wanted to do the same, to be like them. They would never let me have a drink (understandably), and I thought it was unfair. But after my series of revelations about the impact of religion on the mind and the importance mental integrity, as well as the discovery of what alcohol does to the mind, my perspective changed drastically. When my mother offered me a glass of wine on my 16th birthday, I responded with a cold stare and an explanation that offering me a glass of anything alcoholic was going to be received as an offer to remove one of my limbs.
People tell me I overreact to these things, but I disagree. Alcohol, for example, gives you an addiction, helps you lead a false life (the loosening-up effect of alcohol), associates you with a statistically more violent and ignorant crowd, destroys your intellect, and drastically ups your chances of dying on any given night. What are the benefits? It tastes good? I am utterly astounded that bars exist, but clubs in which patrons serve knives with which customers can stab themselves in the leg do not. The same goes for any kind of narcotic and for tobacco.
But what about the benefits of, for example, wine? It helps prevent heart disease, right? Two words: grape juice. I especially despise wine, mostly because it comes with all of the negatives of any other kind of alcoholic drink, except that its also ridiculously expensive and that its had far more hype than it deserves (which would be none). Wine connoisseurs just tick me off. Grape juice tastes better, is far cheaper, easier to get, legal for minors (teaching by example is a good thing), and better for your heart than wine.
Its really nothing but common sense. You can either pay more and risk your life, well-being, social happiness, intelligence, and success in life with some minor benefits, or you can have those same benefits, but greater, and without any of the downsides. It really comes down to, do you want to be stupid, or do you not want to be stupid?
My dislike of these things, I believe, came directly from my experiences with religion, which is simply further evidence for me that my religious beliefs are the most influential forces on my life.

I can explain the cheesiness of that last paragraph. The essay was really about a value I hold and how I got that value, and I wanted to show how much influence my antitheism has had on me. I realized at the end that the paper had little to nothing to do with religion, so I stuck in a corny concluding paragraph that I was considering revising.
