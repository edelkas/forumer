Shmoggys Big Adventure

Once upon a time there was this Viking name Shmoggy, and he found a dragon named Chris and loved the dragon, so naturally he rode the dragon hard. One day, while riding his dragon hard, he fell off and of course the dragon caught him, as they were flying in space, where there was no gravity. After Chris realized that he had fallen off Shmoggy, he was pissed off, and killed Chris with a razor edged blunt war axe. He decapitated Chris out of anger, but then realized there was no way out of space for him now, so he began eating the raw meat of his dragon to survive as long as possible and about 2 months later, the meat had molded because of all the hydrocarbons gathered on its electrons.

Eventually, he saw a planet and started using what was left of Chris, a tail as an ore to paddle himself through the washes of space. He did not know this but the planets name was Saturn, so when he began to get closer to the planet he kept getting hit with ice chunks. Then eventually one came that was the size of a smartcar and hit him on the head. He had tried to dodge it, but it was just too huge for him.

So when he woke up, he was inside of Saturn being a gaseous planet and all. Inside the planet he found tunnels. Shmoggy then began to traverse these tunnels wondering what had made them. "What or who" he began to think. He was still living off of Chris because this dragon was damn big, then he realized that there must have been someone who made these because these tunnels were made of rock and had lanterns inside them for light. These lanterns had a reddish green glow to them, so he took one of these lanterns off the wall and began to cook the last of his old buddy, Chris. He was bummed because 4 months had gone by, by now, and he was going to die from starvation. Just then he heard a thwump, thwump, thwumping on the walls, "Thwump, Thwump, and a Thwump" He realized it was coming from inside the walls, the walls of these tunnels, and he was like lawls, wtf are this effing n00bs, yo. They were actually Saturians.

Saturians were actually as civilized as humans are today, but mind you, this story takes place 2000 years ago, when Vikings roamed the earth. So they showed him how to use a computer, which they call smorgs. So he begins using these "smorgs" as if he had grown up with them and the Saturians were like, Holy Shit bro, this Viking pwnzorz Caligula. So the Saturians hatched a beautiful plan, they would make him a god among gods. They had this power, which they had torrented from oops, can't put this in here.

Just then, the Saturians equivalent to the FBI came in, only these people were called the S-E-X. These foundations, this orgy, this group of elitists, these gangbangers, were like, "Stop, in the name of love, before I rip out your heart and guts. They had been searching for years, decades even for this cult of evildoing Saturians. But the S-E-X knew exactly what was up, little did Shmoggy know it, but the rock that had hit Shmoggy earlier was driven by the S-E-X. It was in fact a smartcar and when Shmoggy had been knocked out, they had put a transmitter on him.

So when the transmitter had gone hog-wild and was ovulating, sensing the danger of becoming a god, Shmoggy was in they infiltrated the evil lair. The evil lair was shot up the block by the S-E-X, but they were trained sharp shooters with there training in the S-E-X training facility, protecting the funeral games. So every one of those Saturians died, but Shmoggy survived the onslaught, the S-E-X had shot em all up, and there saffron blood was every where. Little did they know, the Saturians were able to respawn just like in Halo 3, but the S-E-X were trained for this as well. One of the S-E-X saw them respawn and was promptly killed with the Saturians overwhelming blast of silent intelligence. It was the first S-E-X casualty of the battle.

Well, long story short, the S-E-X was all eliminated, and they're neutralizers eliminated. Those that were not eliminated were in the custody of the Saturians, but Shmoggy survived the wave of neutralizing blasts once again, and so the Saturians made Shmoggy a deity, though Shmoggy was distraught at all the death, despite being a Viking, and so once he became all powerful and had read them the story::"the tortoise and the hare," he wiped them out using mind overloading binary codes. The Saturians were sad, because they were dead. Unable to respawn, having lost all there health, in the battle of S-E-X.and Shmoggy was happy.

The End.
