This is my first time, so don't go hard on me.

This contest is a mapmaking/judging/contest.
but with a twist.

I think of a theme (or suggest it if you want.) of a type of map you make.
The judges will play the game and based on the gamplay, aesthtetics(flow), and fun, with the scoring chart below.

gameplay-n/10
aesthetics-n/10
flow-n/5

When all the judges have . . . judged, I will calculate the mean score of all the judges, and that will be their score, adding each mean of each submitted map. Wow.

Rules!

@ No using maps already made on NUMA. You have to start fresh.
@ Judges may submit, but under the supreme rule of the head judge.
@ Anyone can join, but only 5 can be judges (including me), due to the fact that I won't be going up and down for 50 judges.
@ Any one can join at any point, but they will have to start with 0.
@ Each round lasts 6 days, so no worries.
@ (to judges) no harsh judging, constructive critism needed.
@ (to mapmakers) you can only submit 3 maps per round.
@ The Suprme Judge (me) will have to multiply the score by 4 with the judges that submit their maps and continue with the same process. A bit complicated, but I have a calculator in my hand.
