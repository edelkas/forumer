#294569 3 2007-02-07T01:38:16Z Twistkill
You most likely have a NaN in your data, which stands for "Not a number", which is a glitch in N code. Some people actually use these NaNs purposely sotp drones from moving, etc. Their effects vary, from having a giant N in your screen, to the aforementioned stopping drone movement, to various glitches that can be exploited, and etc.

Can you put the map data into a .txt file and attach it here, please? We can help you identify where the NaN is and how to get rid of it.

Or it could quite possibly be what epigone mentioned...
