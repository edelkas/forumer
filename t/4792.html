#99399 4 2005-09-11T01:19:24Z nevermore
Except for this one.
This has been suggested before many times, and the answer is a definite no. Firstly, it would mess with many, many levels in the real game. These levels weren't designed for that, and if drones could blow up mines it would make some levels easier, and the highscores would have to be reset.

Secondly, this can't be implemented in many different situations. I can think of a couple of puzzle levels that can use it, but it couldn't be used in a variety of ways.

Thirdly, it's just not a very good suggestion. Just in general. It's not that interesting, and most people probably wouldn't use it in most of their levels. And what if someone didn't want the drones to blow up the mines in a level? That would be rendered impossible.
