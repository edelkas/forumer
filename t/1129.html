Level Story:
----------------------------------------------------
N has been sent on a special mission for the government. Unfortunately, they messed up the teleporter codes, and N's new world has been quickly jumbled. Enemies seem almost lifeless, and debris litters the rooms. Blocks also appear to spell out Tsukatu, however, given his current situation, N doesn't have time to check. Can N escape safely? Or will this day prove to be the end of N's sanity?
