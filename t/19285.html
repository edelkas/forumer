#362635 2 2007-09-06T05:24:41Z tktktk
Okay, I don't know if you've looked in the other threads, but generally what we do is have everyone play everyone else's maps and write just a line or two commenting about each, what we like and dislike and so forth. Keep in mind that we tend to review based on a higher standard than we would regular maps, although yours tend to be great so I probably won't have much criticism anyway.

Boole and You - The drone paths make for some interesting gameplay. I completely ignored the launchpads during my first runthrough, but they definitely add to the flow of getting the gold if used correctly.

Carbon Marriage - Awesome tileset, and a very unique map. I wasn't sure how to get the exit switch at first; I like the puzzle element that gives it. The rocket placement was great as well; it made things kind of awkward, but in a challenging rather than a cumbersome way.

In Absentia - I liked the way the drones were set up. Getting back up after taking the exit switch was interesting as well.

Sloapes - Best of the five. The simplicity of the gameplay is great. Getting both bunches of gold as well as maneuvering around the final couple of floorguards at the end was a lot of fun.

The Big Eat - Very nice. The tileset is simultaneously terrifying and adorable, and the final jump to get to the exit is quite exhilarating.
