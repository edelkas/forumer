INTRODUCTION

First off, BT inspired me to do this RPG because of the way he did it, he has SKILLZZS. Anyway, in this game, we will start of with 1 planet, 1 infintry group, 1 light vec, 1 air, 1 heavy, and 1 fleet and 100 funds. Your can name your home planet anything you like. Each planet, character and item has there own uninqe ability. We will bid on new items defences for your planets, ground, space unites, and be able to take over other peoples planets. The first start of the game we bid on 6 planets, that means there will be 6 players. The first 6 posters will be in the game. Once thats happened I will make another thread that will start the game.

RULES

1)You can only bid on 1 item, person, vehicle, defence, or planet at a time.
2)When a person attacks you, you cannot do anything else except defend.
3)No alliance, no contracts, but you are able to do a last wishes before you are obliterated.
4)Cannot turn back a bid, they are final.
5)Each face lasts for 2 days, any post after will be cancled.

I may add more later, I am the game host, so dont try and cheat cus I didnt add somthing important.

PHASES

TENDING TO HOME PLANET

You can Either make get resources, bid on item, vehicle, or defence, Do somthing with units.

CAPTUREING

You can iether buy a planet, capture by force, there is also special event item.

BATTLE

You can Capture planet by force by attacking player, put one of your planets up for bid for other players, buy players planet.
