I've been thinking lately, with the Dronies coming up, and all, that in addition to the current highscores (Which I'll refer to from here on as overall highscores), that the idea of Highscore seasons come into play. Each, say, March 1st (In homage to the original release date), the seasonal highscore boards wipe clean, in order to keep them from stagnating. The overall highscore boards would include all runs (Both those from versions prior to 1.5 and those from the temporary highscores), but the temporary ones would be self-contained, so that those who don't have a chance in improving overall runs can still place in the temporary system - runs on the temporary highscore list that are good enough to place on the overall highscore boards would stay up there, but the temporary ones would go for a year, be tallied and archived, and then wiped for a new season.

I may not be explaining this well, but any of you who are familiar with the Diablo Ladder seasons know what I'm talking about.

Yes, it would take more maintenance, and more space than the current system, but I think that it would add a dimension to gameplay and competition, as well as warranting a more solid place in the Dronies, as well as making highscores a bit more of a big deal than they currently are.
