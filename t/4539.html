i sort of doubt it -- for instance the xbox has been out for a long time and has an active hacking community, but there's still no flashplayer for that.

the problem is that the flashplayer is _very_ complicated and writing your own is just not feasible unless you can hire a team.

jsut getting some vector graphics loading/etc. is easy, but supporting actionscript and proper rendering (which is _very_ hard to do unless you write your own software renderer.. which is a huge job) is a lot of work. making it 100% compatible with the actual flashplayer is probably incredibly hard to do, especially since the real flashplayer uses double-precision floats which i doubt any consoles support without extra work.

..but i'd _love_ to be proven wrong of course
