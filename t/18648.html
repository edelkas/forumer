#349290 8 2007-07-31T07:29:03Z writetyper
yeah i seen your maps, and they rock compared to my firsties.
twist kill told me this (wondering wether it will help you):
Quote: Twistkill
Okay, for your maps:

Your main problem is that they're too cluttered and spaced out. What you need to do is create a map where all enemies have the potential to kill you somehow. Nothing should be wasted. Also, don't always think that a lot of enemies = difficult, because one well-placed rocket turret controlling an important area can be more devastating than 3 gauss turrets in other places.

Secondly, the gold. Try putting gold pieces in groups in places where enemies can guard them (Or not)... just make sure they're not spaced out everywhere, okay?

Thirdly, your tilesets. I hate people who judge how good my maps look, but yours... no offense, but they need some work. What you need to do is to come up with a cool design, or maybe just make the map very playable, like me. My tilesets aren't very pretty, but my maps are fun because I focus on the flow. Now, don't copy me, however - you should keep practicing around with Ned until you find your own style. Also, you don't have to submit all the maps you make to NUMA...

Oh, and one more tip: PLAYTESTING. This is important. Please play your maps through to see if they feel right or are just throwaways. That's what every good mapmaker does.
