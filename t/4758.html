Hello dear friends. Perhaps some of you have noticed my recent absence on the forums. Perhaps all of you have not. In the case of the latter, then this post may not be very relevant to you. In the case of the former, it will be only slightly relevant.

Either way, I am sure you will all be pleased to know that I have not randomly died and/or fallen off the edge of the planet. No, I am still very much alive and/or kicking, thank you. I might die eventually, but maybe not. The details are still to be worked out. Until that date, or lack thereof, you can expect about the same frequency of posting as you have seen over the past several weeks. This applies to NUMA as well as the forums. The reasons for this decampment are multifarious and diaphanous, to put it plainly. I really do like virtually hanging around with you guys, but affairs in "real life" have really become life-like. I may still drop by occasionally to see how things are, but you can take this post as my formal good-bye.

I could reminisce of the time when I first arrived on the forums and NUMA, make a few witty jokes about the unspoken but obvious "forum hierarchy," or write heartfelt and loving goodbyes to a few people here of whom I have grown especially fond; but I presently have neither the time nor the energy to do these things.

All I can say for now is that I hope all of you search for truth in your own lives. If there is one thing that matters in the whole scheme of things, it is truth. If you don't believe that truth exists, figure out whether that's true or not. If you do believe in truth, then search for it with all of your heart. Really, though, I will miss all of you. Thanks for all the good times, and even for the bad times. Goodbye for now, and like I said, I will definately drop in after a while to see how things are.

Much love,

great_sea

(by the way, if any of you is interested in keeping in touch via email in the future, just drop me a PM. i would be happy to know you outside of the forums)
