#93583 12 2005-08-23T18:19:21Z nevermore
Ok, I just beat the level again. I'll walk you through it.
I suggest you do things clockwise, starting with the top right. So, jump to the right, wall jump up the wall, and jump of so you're holding onto the wall on the left. Wall jump up, and jump off, then quickly walljump again off of the thin tile. Land on the curve next to the floor guard, and jump over the guard. Jump quickly off of the platform. Jump down and land so the guard follows you, then jump up again. Walljump off so that you land in between the two mines, then jump again and get the switch. Jump out, wallclimb up, and jump to the right and get the gold and the switch. Jump off, and slide on the sideways pipe.

Jump over to the right side, wallclimb up, and stand over the drones. Wait until the drone is about to pass in front of you, and drop down. You'll get the switch, and you can climb up the left wall, and swiftly jump out. Slide down, and stand next to the mines.

You just have to keep your cool here. I can't describe this part. You just need to jump through the mines, then jump out.

Here's the thwump part. Jump down and trigger the top thwump. Slide down and trigger the second thwump. Jump to the left, so that you're against the left wall. Get the switch, land on the floor, and quickly jump over the thwump. Land on the wall, slide, and jump up and land on the thin tile. Jump up, get the exit switch, and fall into the exit.

Good luck again.
