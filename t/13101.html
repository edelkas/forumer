Okay, when you get to the last episode in a column, (Episodes 0-9 is a column, 20-29 is a column, etc.) you only have to reach the last level of that episode to unlock the flavor for beating that column. For example, if you have reached 29-4, you don't have to complete it for you to unlock the flavor for that column. You can close N, reopen it, and go to the configure menu. Your unlocked flavor will be there. A weird bug, but it's completely true.
