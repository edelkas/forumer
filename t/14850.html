All of you know that the Wii uses Wi-Fi, and it uses friend codes like the DS does, so this is the thread for exchanging friend codes.

Find this by clicking the letter icon on the bottom corner of the Wii Menu, and then clicking on the notepad icon (bottom left), then the address book. Your Wii address is shown on the cover

Format your info as so...

Name: (Your forum profile name will do, or your real name if you prefer)
What I want to use it for: (Will you use it for talking/future online play/mii channel?)
Wii Address Number: (Obvious really...)
