What if the ninja not only had to dodge the robot droids, but had another foe on the map? What if he had to not only dodge him, but truly defeat him using only his wits and the level around him? What if the ninja...had to fight ANOTHER NINJA???


The evil ninja, made evil by some long and tragic backstory that I don't want to write at this moment, would chase you across the level. He would have a sword, or shuriken, depening on what the deevelopers want, and would have all the same abilities that N has. He would follow you across the level, jumping across gaps, flying up walls, and dodging lasers. Of course, this is a drawback to him, because he can be injured by the enemies on the map. Lasers will kill him, gauss cannons will blow his head off, and droids will zap him, and even chase him if they see him. This forces the ninja to be smart. he will not blindly pursue you, and as you make some crazy flying leap, he will decide if it is safe. When a laser is charging, he will be wary of the danger. Rockets do home in on him. When a droid locks on to him, he will evade first. He is a ninja, after all.


Please add on with ideas or criticisms.
SpoilerOne thing I would like to try, although not necessary, is for the ninja to be able to slow him down. Not by any extra buttons, mind you, but just simple things. If you were to run straight at the evil ninja, then jump at the last second, could you knock him down? Could running straight at him, then cutting the opposite direction be used as a way to disarm his sword for only a moment? These are technically against the spirit of N, but they would be sweet.

#291576 36 2007-01-30T01:18:46Z Keron Cyst
I think having proto-N being able to actually recognize enemies and dodge them would be extremely difficult to program; take a generic FPS for example, which themselves are quite complicated: if it's low-quality the enemies won't even defend themselves and will try to simply rush you, and if it's a better FPS they'll simply take cover. Proto-N would have to not only be told to jump over an incoming Zap Drone or Floorguard, but also how high in case there is a ceiling of mines. What if there were two incoming Zap Drones with chase algorithms, one behind the other? How early should proto-N jump if there is a strip of one-ways above pointing up which leads to Thwumps or Chaingun Drones in another narrow alley just above it?

Proto-N would also have to be programmed to manage BounceBlocks, and I think its required use of them would take forever to code if it was to utilize them in such a way as to complete my No Grip for YOU, Young Ninja!. Same with momentum-ganing of climbing ramps.

In short, I'm not criticizing this idea; I think it is possible to make proto-N, but it'd take forever, and M&R probably don't have the time for it as I presume they'd move on to bigger and better things
