#284951 2 2007-01-12T23:10:42Z wedgie
Erm, yeah.

The time starts at 90 and each gold piece adds 2 seconds to the timer. When playing through episodes though, the time you have from the previous level carries over to the next level, so you would be starting at a time other than 90.
#284954 3 2007-01-12T23:13:39Z George
Yeah, it's how much time you have at the end.

You can view high scores from the main menu. Just click on the episode and/or level you want and watch one of the top 20 scores.

Also, if you want to submit a high score, there is a submit function on the top left next to your personal bests.

It's basically a measure of time.

A score of 95 would be 95 reguardless of what time you started at. It scores based on what your END time would be if you Started at 89.99999(90).
