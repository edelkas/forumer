Waterstorm is a 2D underwater shooter with alot of different weapons and submarines. It is written in Java so everyone (Linux, OSX and Windows users) can play.

The game is for free but you can get a supporter account.
A Supporter Account is like a "Premium Account" in most other Games with the only difference that you decide how much you pay for the extra features.

Give it a try, its really fun.
