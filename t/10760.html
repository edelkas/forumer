O.K., so I was going to keep my 0ths a secret so that no one could steal them, but it seems L3X and KryX each found one independently, so the game is up. Anyway, I'm not that great of an N player. The two 0ths I currently have (L3X: I retook 38-1 ) were obtained purely through innovation; my 52-2 descent is a good 2 seconds faster (and quite a bit sexier, if I may say so myself) than the traditional ones, and my 38-1 route is about 2 seconds more efficient than trib4l's. But I know that some of you crazy N fiends out there will beat my scores, like KryX, who would've taken 52-2 if he hadn't missed two golds (my post-descent run is kinda crappy, as opposed to KryX's and alimbach's, which are beautiful).

So, in an attempt to immortalize myself in the annals of N, I propose an Innovators' List, which keeps track of who innovated which levels, regardless of who currently has the high score. Of course, there are several problems with this idea, namely how to determine what is a significant innovation and what isn't. I want to say that to be considered a major innovation the run has to be a 0th. (Before better people copy and streamline it, that is.) But that brings into play the skill of the player, which I'm kinda trying to take out of the equation. Also, I don't know how to handle hypothetical innovations that are then employed by others. And I don't even know if you veterans remember who innovated all the levels that are "perfect" (e.g., 5-2). Basically, it's a very slippery slope, and I don't know if it's even worth it trying to quantify innovations. But at the same time, I am really curious about who formulated these high score runs, and think an Innovators' List would be an interesting resource to have. Anyway, you guys tell me what you think.
