N+ LSS, huh what's that? Well let me tell you.

N+ Level Sharing Society, LSS for short, is a website created to share N+ levels with the world. Since N+ currently doesn't have public sharing this is a great website to get your maps seen by the N+ community.

At the N+ LSS we let you post your gamertag along side with your episode/level name, description, and picture. From here members of LSS may get together with you one day to play and download your episode/level. Then, members go back to rate and comment on your episode/level.

There is also other things to do at LSS. You could chat about N+ or the original N. Get some help on a certain episode or coop episode. As well as the common off topic discussion.

So if you are looking for a way to share your N+ episodes with the world or you need some help. Be sure to check out N+ LSS. N+ Level Sharing Society
