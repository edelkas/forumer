#31616 6 2004-10-11T00:52:13Z Maniac
Yes, that's the effect. You're squeezed by a thwump into another thwump or a wall, and instead of dying, you're sent flying.

The reason that happens is because of N's collision detection script. Yes, I read the tutorial on that.

I'm going to use the level attached at the bottom for my example.

Go ride the first thwump up to the ceiling. N collides with the thwump as he lands on it, so the script repells him away. Result: N is standing on the thwump.

As the thwump moves upwards, N repeatedly collides with it, so the script keeps him moving up, thus N rides the thwump. Once N reaches the ceiling, however, here's the problem: The script needs to move N out of the way of the thwump, but it can't move up because of the ceiling. Thus N will die on the first example, because N can't move left or right either.

With the third example, the script finds it can move you up and to the left after all, so that happens, with no violent launching whatsoever.

Next, after you reset, ride the second thwump. Move into the center of the thwump. You'll die at the top again. Next, move to the right or left side of the thwump. This time, instead of killing you, the optimistic script finds it can launch you to the right to avoid killing you, and thus you are not killed.

Go to the final example, and drop on the bounceblock. The thwumps will move outwards, then as they move in, they squeeze against you. Even though there's a one-way above you, another bug renders it "weaker" than the bounceblock or thwumps. Thus the thwumps launch you through the one-way up.

That should clear up some ambiguity!
