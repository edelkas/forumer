#43177 8 2004-12-30T18:31:18Z Keron Cyst
Warning Forever, a Windows top-down shoot-em-up, rocks You control a small green, crystal-like craft which is armed with a manipulative turret to destroy a boss which continually increases in size, weaponry, & armor. Also, its tactics change (sometimes it uses its boosters to try to ram you; touch it, you die, as usual ) with the equipment it has.

The boss sports normal, slow-moving cannons, purple shotgun-like barrage weapons, homing missiles (they don't track forever, tho', so you can outmaneuver them), pulse lasers, laser-machine guns, and more devastating weapons

But your ship has the advantage of a purely controllable turret, in which you can change where it fires (you can shoot from behind, to the left... everywhere! ) and how large you want its firing spread to be (make it more concentrated or widened, depending on whether you want to blow apart specific cannons of the boss that're making your life harder, or if you just want to get rid of it as fast as possible and head for the core). Plus, you can slow down your ship to help avoid some projectile barrages more efficiently.

#43754 33 2005-01-01T19:49:32Z brickman
I've figured out one big facet of the system. Specifically, I've determined that the reason I kept getting frustrated in later levels was becasue the bottom of the boss was growing so fast that by the 8th boss I would have about 1.5 cubic centimeters to work with most of the time due to his bulk, while being attacked with lasers, mass ammounts of cannonballs--the works. Dodging is one thing, but dodging in a space roughly twice the size of your ship is not fun. So I figured out that since the order in which you attack sides of the ship determines the growth, attacking the front first is suicide. You can only hope to survive by always going for the pieces in the upper corners (upper left and upper right) first, aggressively stomping them out first. That way, he grows outwards instead of downwards, and by level ten you still have enough room to dodge all the crap he's throwing at you. Even with a false start, I was able to get farther doing that than I had doing it normally.
