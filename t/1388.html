Third Parties Have the Right to be Heard

I would like to commend The Torch on the article in the October 15th about third parties called, "Do You Recognize All the Names On Your Ballot?" Jackie Fuller has done an excellent job in recognizing that there are other options out there besides The Big Two. However, unlike the Torch, most media organizations fail to recognize that other people are running for president. Third party bias runs deep.

Jokes have been flying about the presidential debates and how predictable they have become. They are run by a bi-partisan organization call the Commission on Presidential Debates (CPD) to get Americans a perspective on the candidates. This replaced the non-partisan organization The League of Women Voters, who originally ran the debates. Non-Partisan differs from bi-partisan in that now the big boys make the rules rather than everyone. By excluding third party candidates from these debates, the CPD inherently unfair, even though poll after poll shows that a majority of Americans would like to see more candidates at the debates.

Do you know a country where two of the top five candidates are arrested and the media fails to cover the event? Surprise! It's America. Green Party candidate David Cobb and Libertarian candidate Michael Badnarik were arrested outside the debates in St. Louis on Friday, October 8th. They were attempting to enter the debate peacefully to show voters more options. Badnarik was also attempting to deliver a court order to a representative of the CPD to prove that the Arizona debate on October 13th did not give special treatment for Democrats and Republicans since it was sponsored by tax payer money (source www.lp.org).

In our own state of Indiana, Kenn Gividen, a Libertarian was slated to be excluded from the gubernatorial debates until the other candidates began accusing their opponents of being exclusive.

Even if the opinions of candidates vary from your own, don't they deserve to be heard? Swing voters prove that not everyone is one party or another, and still more only vote the donkey or elephant because they feel their voice will not be heard by voting third party. We all deserve to be heard, so let's give everyone a chance. It's the American way.

Jonathan Hunter (my real name)
