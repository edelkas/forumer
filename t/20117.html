#379516 2 2007-11-15T01:49:29Z bonzai
1) Banners can be replaced in "HTML Templates>Global HTML elements>Board Header". Scroll down to where it says "<!--IBF.BANNER-->" and slightly below that you should see
Quote
<img src='{ibf.vars.img_url}/logo4.gif'
Replace what's in blue with another image you uploaded somewhere like imageshack or photobucket.

2) Icons can be changed if you follow the directions here.

3) Forumer won't ever let you get a backup or your forum, and won't let you transfer the database to a new server. So yeah, we'd be stuck with them if we decided to move unless we were willing to lose all the old posts and such.

For future reference, not many people here would know a whole lot about forum coding and all that good stuff, so you'd be better off asking on the main support site.

1) You could add a background image to the #logostrip that will repeat without any obvious transitions (like a gradient). In other words, something like this:
Quote
#logostrip { border:1px solid #FFF;background-color: #FFF;background-image:url(put url in here); padding:0px;margin:0px; }
From there, the banner can be adjusted to fit. For something like what metanet has, you'll have to ask Pembie; he is, after all, the one who coded the skin for this forum, and right now I don't know how to do that.
