#52145 8 2005-03-01T22:25:04Z Tsukatu
Here's one that's completely crystal clear... with no "fraction of a second" business. This took me twenty or so minutes to get consistently:

-Make sure that as you start the level, you're holding down the "move left" key.
-As you approach the end of the first ledge, keep holding down left, and press and hold jump, too, so that your jump is redirected in the top left corner to go downward faster. You should land on the first thwump.
-As the thwump leaves and you're standing on air, hold down "right," so that you end up standing on top of the second thwump (and it's passing by very quickly).
-As you fall from that thwump (you should be right up against the right wall), do a wall-jump just above the niche below, and end up standing on top of the thwump as it recedes back to its hiding spot. Make sure to go back to the right in mid-wall jump, so that you end up next to the same bit of wall from which you launched.
-You should at this point be right up against the right wall, standing on top of the last thwump as it moves slowly backwards. At this point, don't do anything. Just let yourself fall from the thwump, slide down the ramp thing, and you'll fly past the drone and land on the ground below.
-As soon as you hit the ground, start running right, to continue with the level.

That's me totally overexplaining it, anyway. Any chance to fail this should be eliminated with the standing on the thwumps, which are more or less an occurance independent of your actions
