#255530 2 2006-11-16T18:07:33Z wedgie
One thing you can try is instead of pushing the opposite direction when you hit the slope, just release any direction buttons and then jump. So basically run at the slope, jump at it, and then just as you hit the slope release any direction buttons that you are holding and then press jump. Doing this should help you slope jumping consistency a bit.
