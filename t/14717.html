Firstly id like to say sorry for starting another religious thread, but i was bored in the mandatory school church service today and thought of this. This thread is not to argue about the existance of god, so please dont do that. I was just wondering why we exist. Let me elaborate;

If you believe, like i do, that there is no 'free will'; that our thoughts are dependant solely upon our upbringing, surroundings and our genes, then you must agree that there is no such thing as a 'bad' person. 'Bad' people are merely the conglomeration of a number of the mentioned factors, not because they are simpley evil - basically im trying to say that no one is born evil.

And if no-one is born evil, they are merely made so by there surroundings, then surely any actions they make on earth do not reflect on them, but on the person or situation that made them 'bad'. Of course this creates a chain, every bad person being made that way by someone or something before him, and so on, in which case surely it is unfair to blaim enyone for their 'sins'.

Basically im saying that yes, people do bad things, but ill bet that they had no choice (of course they had choice in the normal sense of the word, but the way there minds had been developed would ensure that they made a particular one of the possible choices, therefore negating their ability to choose), and if they had no choice i dont believe they should be punished for it.

Hopefully this will stand up against any arguments that life is a 'test', because life is actually more of a factory; we do not battle our way through it, doing this and that, we actually are pushed through it, having things done to us which makes us the people to do things to others.

And if this is correct, if life isnt really a test, and if every person is actually 'good' at 'heart' then surely life is pointless?

That brings me to my main point; what is the role of life? Why are we here?

Well, i have a theory about that, two;
1 Maybe life is like a factory, and every 'soul' lives through it over and over again, until life finally treats them good enough for them to become good enough to enter heaven, which is why heaven is so awsome; it is full of only the best people from life.
2 Maybe 'life' is actually heaven; maybe each person, when they die, is placed into a world like this and life through it joyfully over and over again. Everyone knows life isnt perfect, but to be honest with you im pretty happy with mine, and for all i know this could be my heaven. After all, there is no evidence that anyone other than me actually exists. Maybe each other member of this suppposed 'world' dosnt actually exist; they are merely here for my eternal enjoyment.




Anyway, that is my thought of the week. Hope you liked it.
