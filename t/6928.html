#134521 21 2006-01-22T02:27:44Z Mare
so, the end of the first day:

-we met everyone but the kinko's people and the cloud people.. and the "joboo's gems" guys -- they were there, but apparently people who make "casual" games also have bcom degrees and are way cooler than the rest of us

-a lot of the other games look awesome: narbacular drop is a really cool concept, cloud looks really cool, facade has crazy AI, and of course rumble box is super fun

-we were hanging out with the rumble box guys, who might be making a 2D physics-y platformer.. it could be the second coming of the 2D platformer genre, which is great since for so many years there were only re-releases of SNES games.

-the space is great: the computers are on low tables, with superhuge beanbag cushions and stuff, it's way more comfy and laid-back than the IGF.

-if anyone gets "the dish network", they have a camera permanently in the room and are doing interviews and stuff (we'll try to get more info on what the show's called tomorrow, i think it's satellite-only)

-each developer has to do a short presentation (3 per day, we're up tomorrow), so far they've been really great, it's really interesting to hear how other people do things.

-there is unlimited free red bull, which can be dangerous since my hands are now shaking like crazy (raigan)

-some people who tried N didn't seem to get it, but there were at least two who came back several times throughout the day, vowing to beat a level that they were stuck on

anyway.. we have to prepare our presentation for tomorrow... thanks for the encouragement!

mare and raigan
p.s - the two things that people are always super-impressed with are numa/user-maps and the incredible skills in the highscore replays; many people prefer to gawk at highscores while muttering "that's impossible!" under their breath to actually playing! anyway, thanks for making N so cool!!
