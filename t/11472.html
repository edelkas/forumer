#207568 6 2006-07-26T21:19:03Z Kablizzy
It is definitely a virus, most notably, a worm that has named itself svchost.exe. It is highly dangerous. I'd call some sort of Tech support immediately, disable your System Restore, and you may even have to resort to wiping your hard-drive, but I would try some things first, like uploading your important files to an online database.

Also, you can go to http://www.geeksquad.com for a free virus scan, you can also do a google search on AVG Antivirus, that should pick it up. You can also download Ewido anti-spyware, that sometimes picks things up that others wouldn't.

I'd call Geek Squad, or someone similar immediately. It costs money, but at this point, you have little choice. Also, if you have something like Spyware Quake on your box, you should definitely get a professional.

Edit: If you search google for 'svchost.exe', you'll get a few results telling you how to search for the svchost.exe process, and where it should be located if it isn't a virus.

However, since the dialog box is popping up, I'm almost certain that it is. And, if I remember correctly, it's a hard one to pick up.
