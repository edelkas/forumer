I wasn't here yesterday, and I won't be here tomorrow, but I'll take the time to point out that those of you who say nothing can be done are WRONG.

Wrong, wrong, wrong.


Here's what to do:

Navigate Start>Control Panel>Sound.

On the playback tab, right click all entries and select "Disable".

Click OK.



Sound cards are now disabled, and any and all sound-related glitches and lag are beautifully gone.


You won't hear any sound while playing the game, though.


Reverse when done.

#377389 24 2007-11-06T22:51:54Z OrigamiGuy
Problem solved.

The problem comes from Vista trying to meld your system sounds together and add audio enhancements, which is why moving N to a lower place in the sound queue sometimes works. Maratanos's solution of disabling the sound output will also work.

But I needed sound effects for running my ninja around; sometimes I just want to hear the little guy explode. Here's what I did:

1.) Go to Start => Control Panel => Sound.
2.) On the Playback tab, right-click your speakers on the list there and select "Properties".
3.) Depending on your speaker setup and your version of Vista, there should be a tab there called "Effects" or "Enhancements" or some such. Check the box marked "Disable System Effects" or "Disable All Enhancements" or whatever.
4.) Play N lag-free!

I hope this works for you as well as it did for me. Enjoy!
