#249397 30 2006-11-03T03:56:49Z Tsukatu
I had a GameShark, back when my N64 was undamaged by flooding of my garage. There were these series of codes called "Destroy [level]," such as "Destroy Dam" and "Destroy Facility." These codes would take whichever weapon you had in your off-hand and fired a round from it twenty times a second in the center of your field of view. It didn't have an ammo cap or a need to reload. This line of fire could blow up objects that weren't part of the terrain but that were indestructible otherwise. If you didn't have a weapon in your off-hand, you'd be doing a scary amount of unarmed attacks.

So for examle, if you had "Destroy Dam" on and you weren't using any extra weapons cheats, you'd notice that you automatically make an unarmed attack on whoever comes close to you (cuz you're swinging at the air in front of you constantly with your off-hand). If you were using All Guns or something and you whipped out another silenced PP7 in your off-hand, you'd see that you're firing insanely quickly at a point perfectly in the center of your field of view. Your ammo for your all of your weapons would be unaffected, and you could use your primary weapon normally. So anyway, it's really easy to kill a lot of your enemies because you don't have to fire a shot and all you have to do is look at them.
But then let's say the first thing you do when you start in Dam is pull out another silenced PP7 and then look at that truck to your right. If your line of fire from your off-hand hits that truck, it explodes. Seriously. Then you run around the level looking at people, and they get those deep crimson colors on their chests that you'd normally get if you fired an entire RCP-90 clip into their carcasses, because they're being shot 20 times a second in the same spot on their chest. Anyway, you blew up the truck, so you can't get past those doors, right? Well technically, they're objects. As soon as your line of fire from your left hand hits those doors, they're going to explode. So will all of those "indestructible" metal crates people are hiding behind and all of those fence gates and even every door you look at.

But anyway, I didn't use this to destroy levels and kill people quickly as much as I used it to entertain myself. If you put a rocket launcher in your left hand, it was friggin' armageddon. Grenades, same story. I'd make a whole mess of a room by setting my left weapon to be throwing knives, cuz those things bounce off of walls and stick around so you can pick 'em up again. I used to go to Surface with two hunting knives and run into people. There was this sickening chopping noise as the enemy was carved into pieces. You could just run right through them like they weren't there.

Heh, and there were these "Play Full [level]" cheats that let you play the unlockable levels in multiplayer fully. Normally when you play Facility in multiplayer, you can't go through that door to the rest of the labs or the big room with the gas tanks, right? Well, if you had "Play Full Facility" on and took Player 1 to that door that won't open in a multiplayer game and press the GameShark button, it explodes. The rest of the level doesn't have windows or doors. It's just terrain, but it's still hella fun to have sniper battles in that last room. There were also codes for each of the single-player levels that let you play that level in a multiplayer game.

Otherwise, my favorite thing to do was to make a battle epic by putting on Enemy Rockets, All Guns, Infininte Ammo, and Turbo Bond, and then running around with two AR-33's or two Phantoms. Those were the days.

[/reminiscing]
