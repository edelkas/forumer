The Rules

[SIZE=8] 1. I will put a selection of random tiles on a map. The contestants have a week to make a good tileset out of it WITHOUT REMOVING OR EDITING THE ORIGINAL TILES. Then the tilesets will be judged

2. I will put a random selection of objects around the tileset and the contestants have to use them to their advantage but not remove them (as in step 1). all of the objects have to be usable or reachable. These will then be judged.

3. Finially I will put a selection of tiles and objects on a empty map and the contestants will make a good tileset and good object placement on the map without altering any of the originals. the rules in steps 1 and 2 apply.

Prizes:

1st place: 4 deds, bragging rights
2nd place: 2 deds, bragging rights
3rd place: 1 ded, bragging rights

How it's rated

tileset/10
enjoyability/10
hardness/10 (10 being just right)

things like no jump, jump only or kra maps will get an extra 5 points. races will get an extra 2 points
