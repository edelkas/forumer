If you haven't already heard, the iPod touch has recieved some much needed software updates, including mail, maps, and notes, even customizing the icons on the dock and home screen. The original release of the touch fell very short of this. It also had many problems, like the "negative black" effect and the issues with Safari crashing.

It seems as though the week 38 touches dont have the problem with the negative black effect. At least not on the one that I have had. The issue that did come up is the problem with safari crashing. For me, that was too much. I had just bought a 16GB iPod touch, ignoring all of the shortcomings of the software, only to find out that I can't use Safari without it crashing every 10 minutes? I guess that's what you get for buying something a month after it hits the shelves.

So now, Apple releases this big software update including all these great features that the original software was lacking. And now I really want a new touch. But did they fix the bugs that Safari had? Can I use Safari without it closing on me unexpectedly?

Your confirmations/repudiations/speculations would be appreciated.
