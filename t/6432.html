(Aries's Easy Way)
On the topic of this level, the hard part which you are referring to is not bad if you do it correctly. The easiest way that I've found is to lure the floor guard by the terminal switch(the blue switch) to the right side of it's floorspace, then stand on the part directly right and down of where the floor guard will sit. I'll try to diagram it for you.

=
----^______/
_________|
_____(N)_/

where ^ is the floor guard,= is the terminal switch, and you are (N).
From that spot, just hop to the wall directly to your right and walljump right off of it hard to your left. You should be able to land right on the switch, and then jump out to the right as soon as you hit the ground, and you should be able to avoid the floor guard pretty easily. If you are really slick, you can land and jump from the curved floor by the switch and never even trigger the floorguard to come left again.
[/easy way]

And guys, if someone asks for help on a level, telling them to watch the highscores is usually not enough, because trib4l and johnnyf(and most other highscorers) usually don't do things the easy way.
