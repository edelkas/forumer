Spoiler: Deadly Trios
There are so many great players out there, but how do you set them apart from each other, how do you decide who is the best?


Perhaps this tournament will help answer that question.

The Trios
Teams will be in trios. Once everyone has signed up, trios will be randonly assigned. The leader of each trio will initially be the person in the trio who signed up first. However, after each map the leader will be the player who has scored the most respect points within the trio. This leader will be responsible for making sure the other members of their trio submit their scores and, if necessary, share routes.

Format
The ideal number of trios is 5 which would make the tournament 6 rounds in length. Each round will last a week, in which time everyone will play a map on which their points will be decided. The first two rounds will be for trios to accumulate points and for leaders to be made clear judging on how many respect points they have accumulated. There will be a map for each round and in the later rounds they will increase in difficulty. Late-joiners will either replace someone who drops out or if there are enough late-joiners within the first two rounds (not knockout rounds) they will become a new trio. In the event that a trio member drops out and there are no late-joiners at the current time, the trio can ask someone to fill the place from outside the contest. The player chosen must be approved by me and I will judge them on whether they are of a similar standard to the previous member of the group to keep teams even. If a player does not submit a demo the other members of the trio can vote to kick that player out of the trio. Their place will then be filled as if they had dropped out. Trio names will be chosen by each trio after the first round. The leader of each trio at that time must organise this.

Points
What are these respect points I have been talking about? Within each trio, judging on their demos, members will receive respect points in the same way that each trio receives points (will be explained further down). Submitting no demo is -1 to your individual score and your trio's score as it will show that your trio is not working as a team. As I mentioned earlier, leaders will be selected judging on their respect points. Also, within the two trios that make it to the final round, the player who has the most respect points out of everyone in the tournament will win the "individual best player" award. There will also be a "current best player" each round starting from the knockout rounds.

Bonus points will be awarded for innovations and highest times. To gain a bonus point for an innovation you must be the first to use a route. For example, if the player with the highest time gets 130 seconds and you only have 129.5 yet the player with 130 used your route and you submitted your demo first (this proves you actually made the route), you will gain an extra point. However, if the player with 130 has made several big innovations to your route, it will count as theirs. We will get more into the specifics after each round because I will explain why bonus points went to each player.

In short, submit fast demos and you'll be alright.

Trio Scoring
  • Trio with highest total time = 5 points
  • 2nd highest time = 4 points
  • 3rd highest time = 3 points
  • 4th highest time = 2 point
  • 5th highest time = 1 points
Individual Scoring
  • Individual highest time = 3 points
  • 2nd highest time = 2 points
  • 3rd highest time = 1 points
  • No demo = -1 points
Note individual scoring is within your own trio.

Rules
- Demos must be completed in Unreality's program and submitted to his NUMA map leaderboards. You can find his program here.
- You will be given a week to complete each map and that time will be extended if there is a valid reason (e.g. a player is away for a few days)
- All maps will be chosen from NUMA. Please do not submit your demos on the map's page over at NUMA until after the round using that map has finished, this is so that nobody copies your route.
- Maps will be private contests on Unreality's program so please do not PM me your demos unless you have a valid reason for not using Unreality's program.
- Please post in this thread for all questions and to discuss the maps and your times. You may bluff if you wish

NReality
I will be resubmitting the maps used in this tournament to NUMA in order to enable the private contest feature that lets only me see the submitted demos. Therefore, you can submit as many demos as you want. Each one will be timestamped, so if I say a demo is due 5 o'clock Sunday afternoon and I live in a different timezone to you, the timestamp on your demo should allow you to see my time.

- Download the program using the above link and unzip the contents.
- Open the program from the folder you extract it to.
- Go on the "userlevels" screen which can be accessed from the main menu.
- Enter the map number for the current round into the NUMA web address at the top and then click "Load Map".
- Click on the downloaded map to play it.
- All personal best will be recorded to the right of the map title. Ctrl-click your personal best to submit it.
- Don't be worried when you only see your demo on the leaderboard, you don't have access to the other's demos.

Well I think that about wraps it up. Like I said, if you have any questions please post them here.
