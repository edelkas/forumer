#321159 7 2007-05-14T19:36:49Z Guest
1. Don't read Barron's review book and hope it will get you through the test. That is the worst thing you could do. What you need to do is have a full understanding of the material and use the book to make sure you know the stuff.

2. READ the textbook from the class. This is the best thing you can do for the test. It's not as much of a matter from what you learned in your class as much as what you need to know from the book. Take about a week and a half and read the entire textbook.

3. Make sure you have a general knowledge of EVERYTHING and a great knowledge of anything BIG.

I took the test a few years ago, back in freshman year. I averaged a 90 percent in the class, and never really strained for a better grade. Come time for the test, I made sure I knew the book. I got a 4 on the test. I followed my 3 steps.
