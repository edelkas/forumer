#285547 6 2007-01-14T14:53:46Z Sammage
Agreed on the overuse of drones, but that for me just means 'there were maps with drones in them'.

59-4 T-Minus Zero was excellent, very metanetish with a hint of MTI.
12-4, Something Something, I'd never seen that concept used before
18-3 Prima Donna. Bouncing off those springs is so fun!
26-0 Equidistant. Really cool idea, 51-0 had a similar one but the added fun of boosting off the corners in this one makes it something special.
36-0 - Do not annotate. I thought it worked well, I'd like to see a cool demo of this one.
39-1 Expand yourself. This level shouldn't be hard but it was! The strange pattern of the drones is really cool.
40-4 Space station, cool race map.
41-4 de_dance, so much fun
43-3, Walkin' On Air, really well made level, I've enjoyed NTTSing it.
45-3, Kablizzy's 8th Symphony, everything is really well placed here, I died a surprising number of times.
46-1, A hero will rise. I enjoy doing badass jumps off thwumps in midair. 56-1 vauxhall for the same reason.
52-0 Tile High Club. Add in a gauss and remove the exit and you'd have the second greatest survival map of all time (the greatest being the level that 53-2 likely got its inspiration from)
48-4 Echarin, someone make a demo of keeping the rocket alive all the way to the exit switch, I'm sure it's possible.





Least favorite levels - 22-3, Pratchet. It's really not up to the quality of the others.
13-3, Shuri Shuri Kin. Getting out of there alive just seemed to be chance. Also, there's drones in it.
17-3, Too Metanet? Not Metanet Enough!. God, drones. This plays like a remake of one of my least favorite metanet levels.
47-3 Nazareth's baby. The other thing that's a abused too much in these packs other than drones are these multiple oneways with a gauss or rocket underneath. This level was my least favorite of them, the one where all the platforms were all messed up (one step forward, two steps back I think) was my favorite.
