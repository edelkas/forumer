#69480 4 2005-05-25T20:54:04Z brickman
Yeah, it took me a while to be able to do bounceblock jumps consistantly. You really only need to be able to do a single high jump, that double-jump thing is only for highscore freaks and maybe a handful of userlevels made for the aforementioned elites. All you really need to be able to do is exploit the extra momentum from a single jump while the block moves up. Basically, fall on it from a decent height, and make sure that you're always holding left or right as you push it down, which usually means alternating or slowing down from the jump that landed you there (you get nothing if you fall off), and then while the block is moving upwards hit the jump button full force to make a jump high enough to suit any realistic needs you may have.
As for the enemies, there's no cut and dry formula of "do this, this and this and they'll hurtle into a wall every time," that really is practice.
