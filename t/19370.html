epigone, the transition to college is the worst time to cling to a long-distance relationship. As much as you guys think you love each other now, there will be someone else in your life of whom you can say the same, or more. Your transition to college should be about growth, and progression in your life. Take the next step forward.

If not, I can guarantee you something like this will happen:
You ride the train, sitting in silence and staring at the floor, making a huge time commitment to see her. You're thinking about whether or not it's worth it, and you think of all the girls in your class who can make you just as happy. The only difference between one of them is that they're not her. But they are each unique people in and of themselves.
You arrive. You have a shitty time.
You get back on the train bitter and disappointed. You've just wasted a whole day. You stew and brood for the entire train ride, come back to your dorm, and pass out.
The next morning, you don't call her. She doesn't call you.
The same thing the next day, and then the next.
You wake up one day to find that you have voicemail from her. You listen through it, and then delete it. You don't call her back.
And gradually, you never speak to each other again.

...
End it, epigone.
