#311847 3 2007-04-10T12:17:09Z George
Welcome to the forums, Kratos123.

To answer your question, that's because there is no 'create user' page. NUMA's user system is incoporated with the game.

In N, go to 'Configure'. On the bottom right of the screen, you will see some some stuff regarding usernames and password. It's straightforward. Enter your desired username, and your desired password. Then click on 'create user'.

Once you are successful, go to the NUMA website, login with your new user, and an email will be sent to your inbox. From there, click the link to activate your account.

Enjoy your stay here. If you have any other troubles, just PM me (on the bottom left of my post).
