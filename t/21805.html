#416572 3 2008-03-13T19:40:57Z _destiny^-
My strengths :

Races
Simple yet enjoyable action maps
I can sit down and just make a map

My weaknesses:

To make a really good race, i NEED inspiration
I occasionally over-dose the gold in a big way
I am sometimes considered 'generic' in my action levels

When i want to make a map, i kind of look at a level i have played recently and liked, and try to reflect that style in my own level. I often use other peoples level openings to levels rather than make my own; one example would be my race 'The New Dimension' has the exact same opening as b3njamins map 'Mechanical Monster 3'. I can make fun maps very quickly by drawing a generally guided tileset, adding some extra routes to mix up the gameplay, placing some enemies in a strategic position and then using one-way doors to mix up the direction a player has to do the level; this is how i created my latest map 'Liar'. I have noticed that my races are rather popular, and since incluye asked for some advice on races, heres mine:

1. Be inspired, play a bunniesandsheep or a calamity7 race before you start, get yourself in the mood.

2. Sketch out the flow of the map, don't do anything with the tileset apart from the tiles you use for flow. Try to make an innovative, and creative route for your race. Try not to include loops if it's possible.

3. once you have the flow of your race set out, make the tileset REALLY cool, and try to keep a constant theme throughout the level.

4. Add some enemies, try to mix it up a bit, rockets, thwumps, lasers, chainguns. Don't be repetative and just have 12 rockets and a guass.

5. Make the level uncheatable, locked doors to block off the key and mines to stop you taking a shortcut.

6. Finally add some gold - but don't just scatter it everywhere, decide on a good-looking pattern or theme, and place it a maximum of 7 times around the map

shazam! You got yourself a race. The rest is down to how innovative and smooth you were with the flow.
