Sweep made this for the Contests and Tournaments thread but, It is probably necessary anyway.

1) Are you doing this game just because you want to make a game, or is it because you believe you have a really great idea that would make a really enjoyable game?
Only continue to 2) if you believe the answer is because you have a really great idea that would make a really enjoyable game.
--------------------------
2) Is your the aim for your game clear and simple?
Only continue to 3) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
3) Has your game been done before in the same or a very similar way?
Only continue to 4) if you believe the answer is no.
--------------------------
4) Have you drawn up a set of rules that are easy to follow and remember?
Only continue to 5) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
5) Are your game rules logical and as fun as possible?
Only continue to 6) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------6) Have you planned out your game and know what is going to happen. Only continue to 7) if you believe the answer is yes.--------------------------
7) Do you really think people will enjoy playing this game?
Only continue to 8) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
8) Will you have enough time to maintain the game? Remember that it can be surprisingly time consuming.
Only continue to 9) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
9) Have you asked someone if they have any suggestions or thoughts for the game?
Only continue to 10) if you have. If you haven't, feel free to ask in this thread so you can get a quality response.
--------------------------
10) Do you have any questions to ask before you start?
If the answer is no, start your contest. If the answer is yes, ask the question(s) in this thread.

Thanks to Sweep for this, I believe this was needed to stem the flow of games that will flop (I have learnt my lesson).

Please pin this.

Ask questions if you need to, Daddaluma or Blue_Tetris will be happy to answer your questions.
