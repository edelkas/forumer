This suggestion really wouldn't affect gameplay, but it seems that n's designers put a lot of effort in the game's death sequences (ie, ragdoll physics, et al) and I think this would complement that well.
n is a difficult game, and players end up retrying the same level over and over and over and over. How about keeping the dead bodies and severed body parts from previous attempts onscreen?
It would have an interesting "statistics of death" flair to it - you can tell it's that one corner of the board that gives you the most trouble because of the pile of deceased n's below it.
Of course it would make sense to implement a "expiration" limit on body parts to keep system resource demand low - adjustable through the preferences menu.

I also thought about a minigame that would rate players on the most "stylistic" death - kinda like the crash sequences in Burnout 3. "Style" could include the height, distance and spread of body parts; landing body parts in a target area, like basketball; number of bounces from mines and drones, et cetera. In the end, though, it might not be as fun as the main game and detract from it.
