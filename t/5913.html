#118215 2 2005-11-26T14:06:51Z Maniac
Oh yeah. I must say, this one's a tricky one.

Basically, in this level you're going to start having to get familiar with rockets, as well as multidodging. Multidodging is my affectionate name for the technique of actively avoiding multiple elements at once. In this case, the rocket and the various mines in the arena. You want to sort of pay attention to the rocket, and dodge it, while also making sure you land on bounceblocks so you don't hit mines.

If all of the above didn't make sense, trial and error works. Don't worry, the 20's gets fairly rough quickly.

EDIT:

As in, don't worry, it's not your fault, everyone has trouble on these levels.
