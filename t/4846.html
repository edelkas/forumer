Hi,
As some of you may know, we're trying to get accepted into a government program which helps people start their own businesses.

Next week we have to do a presentation, which is the most important step in terms of getting into the program; for part of this presentation we're required to conduct surveys with potential customers to get statistics about what people want.

It's supposed to be conducted via phone, so we have to ask for your phone number, but if you really don't want to give it to us, then please don't (either way we won't be calling you, they just expect us to have a phone number for each person "called").

We'll keep this confidential of course.


We were only given a week, so we _really_ need these in ASAP, by sunday at the latest.

Also, it's very important that we get a wide range of responses -- if you have a sibling or friend who _has_ played N, but not very much and/or they didn't like it, please ask them to do this survey. If we only interview fans then we might get in trouble

Again, thank you _very_ much for your help, it's really appreciated. If we get into this program it's going to be very exciting!!

Mare and Raigan
