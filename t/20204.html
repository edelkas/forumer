#380530 2 2007-11-18T12:49:24Z TheWigginator
DDA - Don't do anything. A level where you just watch the ninja go from start to finish.
KRADDA - Keep rocket alive don't do anything. Same as above but a rocket chases you and never explodes.
Those and other acronyms can be found in this thread.
Teleporters are just edited launchpads. A tutorial to make them can be found here.
Propulsion is ways of moving the ninja (generally in DDA's) without using launchpads. A list of them, along with other DDA tips, can be found here.

You can place your levels either before or after the DDA line. In fact the DDA line needn't be there at all, it is just showing you a way you can organise your levels.
Level data is arranged as follows:
$Level Name#Author#Type of level(eg DDA)#Level Data(top text box)#Demo data(Bottom text box)#
The demo data is not needed in the text file, but if you want to watch demo data on the user levels screen you must paste the whole lot in.

I hope that answers your questions.

And don't worry about being a noob, everybody was a noob once. (Some more than others...)

#380577 4 2007-11-18T16:32:37Z scythe33
A DDA is a map where the only way to win is not to do anything at all-usually, the main attraction comes from watching the ninja dodge death repeatedly. A KRADDA is a DDA where a single rocket follows the ninja through the whole map, sometimes through walls.
A teleporter is a modified launchpad that teleports the ninja to another location. Here's a guide for how to make them.
Propulsion is anything that moves the ninja, other than the player. Using locked doors for propulsion-well, that's exactly what it sounds like. Bugged locked doors, though, are a tad different. The above guide should tell you how to make them.
