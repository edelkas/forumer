Basically, my idea is that for different deaths you take (Falling, zapped, las0rd etc.), you get a different death animation rather than just flopping to the ground or exploding. My ideas are this:

Enemies
Zapped (Zap drone, thwump, floor guard): N jumps backwards from where he was hit, then convulses on the ground with blue zappy bits showing all over his body. Continues for a few seconds before he falls still.
Sploded (Mine, Rocket): N blasts into pieces with a more satisfying "BOOM" - Better explosion animation?
Las0rd: N floats upwards and slowly disintegrates
Chain gun: N falls to the ground slowly - clutching heart perhaps? collapses to knees, then flops forwards - Blood effects?
Worldspawn
Fall: Actually hear the "CRACK" as he hits the ground. Flops lifelessly to the ground - No actual blood though.
Spike - Use your imagination. That is, if you really want to. Blood and gore.
Crushed - All that's left is lifeless black heap. Lovely.
Launched into wall - First part to hit wall imbeds itself in it.

Post your ideas and suggestions for deaths, and, of course, comment on what you think.
