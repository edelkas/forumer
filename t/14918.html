Maybe it's just me, but don't you get pitiably fragged by the laser drone halfway through the level in that demo?

Watch the high scores. The secrets to this level for me (and I spent a loooong time on it) were:

1 - learning to hit the ground running and make a hard vertical jump at the appropriate time to outrun the floor guard in the upper section - releasing arrow keys when you jump makes you jump perpendicular to the surface

2 - keeping both zap drones in the upper section makes the level much easier

3 - you really only NEED one of the three switches, the one in the guarded box with the laser drone. of course, if you can't get all three now, wait until things actually get hard...

good luck!
