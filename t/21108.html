Rules
Your mission is to make a map for me.
But, there are a few catches!
  • It must consist of 2 or more levels in one map. For example, you could have a 'Choose Your Own', where you can choose between multiple levels e.g http://numa.notdot.net/map/77491, have a customized survival, or you could have two seperate levels, a multiway DDA, and to play each one you should respawn to each level in Ned, e.g. http://numa.notdot.net/map/75552
  • It must be a survival, puzzle, DDA, race an action map.
  • You cannot submit old maps.
  • It should not be stupid hard!
You have until 6th February to make your maps! Good luck! I can take unlimited submissions.

2nd prize --> 1 sig tags, 1 dedication tileset
3rd prize --> 1 sig tag

Judging Criteria
Tileset /10 --> How did the tileset fit with the map?
Gameplay /15 --> How much did you enjoy each part?
Difficulty /10 --> Was it the right difficulty?
Positioning of Objects --> /10 How good was the positioning of the objects?
How would you rate each map section on NUMA? --> /5 -- e.g if there are three sections, one was 2/5, one was 4.5/5, and one was 3.5/5, you take the average, so it would be 10/15, or 3.5/5. (round the scores.)
