Welcome to The N League II

I haven't hosted a contest for longer than I haven't played N, and that is a hell of a long time. But still doesn't mean to say that this won't be a bad one.

The Rules
  • You need to form a team with another player of the game 'N'. One player must be a mapmaker on the website 'NUMA', the other must be a Highscorer who's name is present on the 'N Highscores Database'.
  • Each team will face another in a series of 'heats' leading to a Quarter Final, Semi Final, and the 'Grande Finale'.
  • If the challenge set isn't completed by one of the teams within the 72hr time limit. The other team will win by Default. If both teams do not enter, they will both be eliminated.
  • The Mapmakers will create to a set criteria each week, as specified by the two judges. The highscorers will try to beat a map from 'NUMA' or randomly from a mappack. Each Highscorer gets 2 attempts, while the Mapmakers get 1 submission.
  • Don't worry, even through there are 8 places on the Tree, more can be added easily.
