#23596 2 2004-06-08T20:33:56Z Guest
hey,
yeah, we kind of like the way it is right now -- not cause it's the _best_, but because it works well and is "systematic" (i.e all combinations of tiles work without any special effort). whereas having custom-type art for each level would take a lot of time since the graphics couldn't be auto-generated by computer based on the world's collision shape.

but, for our next game (not tile-based) we ARE going to try to find another visual style which also works well in flash, and small "decorations" (like the text you mentioned) seem like a good idea. but we're also going to try to make the graphics as "automatic" as possible, since that lets us spend more time on other things. plus, that way you know that each graphical element you see has a "meaning" in terms of the gameplay, and isn't useless/merely visual.
