#105182 2 2005-10-03T10:11:40Z Maniac
This relies on a trick that's been used elsewhere, but not quite in the same way.

If you want to have floorguards that go over doors (locked or not) then you simply have to place the doors on the same plane as the floorguard. The part that will probably catch you is that, for some reason, you need to place the doors before the floorguard. And that's it.
