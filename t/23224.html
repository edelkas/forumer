Welcome!
Hey everyone. You now all have access to the Metanet Orchestra sub-forum, and ('cause you're so nice) you can all come into the #Metastra IRC channel. This post contains all there is to know about Metastra, so if you want to get involved our help out, read below. If you want to just check out some of the songs, feel free to browse the topics and post things in them.

About us and projects
Well, the Metanet Orchestra was originally set up by the great Kablizzy, before he left. It's currently moderated mainly by me, but also by Borealis and Sendy, with help from maestro, Yanni, and others.
Our aim was to create music for a game called Knytt Stories. The music was going to be eventually compiled into a ZIP file, so authors could use it on their levels and so on. Also it would just be an opportunity to get some musicians together and see what they could come up with. We're still working on the Knytt Stories music - hopefully it will be done shortly.
Another, more recent projects have come up for us. Animator's game and GamingWolf's game are both in need of music. Currently I am the only on helping out in this area, but other help would be much appreciated.

What you can do to help
If you're a performer, composer, or editor and with to help, please sign in with the respective topic. Please list what you play/use (instrument or program to edit/compose), and I'll hopefully get on to you shortly. You'll be added to the roster, and then assigned things which you can do.
Alternatively, just reply to this if you're unsure on what's happening, or anything like that. If you're really confused and want "face-to-face" talking, you can catch me on the IRC channel (#Metastra).

I think this is all. If there's anything major that I've left out please tell me. Also, if you've got any questions, please ask.

Lenny
