Now, when you see the description, you may think I'm stealing the game N. However I am not. This game is played by using N. The game is Running Ninja. Two players turn on practice mode and choose an episode to compete in. Then, both players load the level and start at the same time. The object is to be the first to beat the level. Time does not matter. Death becomes important. All lies in the fastest way from one point to another.

#182886 2 2006-06-01T00:19:29Z snowballinhell7001
Running Ninja has been revamped! A new scoring system has been discovered. The easy way requires practice mode OFF. However, the hard way requires counting every gold piece in any level you play, which is not fun.

The scoring system works like this. A grand total of 3 points can be earned for each level. The person with the most points wins the level and wins that level. The three points can be earned as follows:

Real Time: The actual real world time it takes to beat the level.
Gold Time: The amount of time remaining on your clock, including the time lost or gained in previous levels.
Ninja Time: The amount of time the ninja takes to defeat the level. This is measured by replaying the two replays SIMULTANEOUSLY. The other option is to count the number golds in the level at the start, and how many left at the end, and do the math to figure how long it took you. Or to have two referees with stopwatches. If the replay is close, let the replays run another few times until it becomes obvious which player won the point.

If one player wins all the categories, he receives a one life headstart on the next level. If he completes the level on this headstart, he automatically wins that level by a score of 3-0, but does NOT get a headstart next level. The first player to win three levels wins the episode, or in an alternate form, after the completion of all 5 levels, the player with the most points out of 15 wins.
