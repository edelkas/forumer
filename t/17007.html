#314652 6 2007-04-21T02:19:29Z Atilla
Episodic levels play as a coherent unit. They should generally progress in difficulty as they go along, although there are exceptions (for example, putting a short level with lots of gold after a long level is a nice change of pace, and also says "This level is here so you can make up for the time you lost last level, because this is an episode"). They should have enough to gold to be finished as an episode. Each episode should be harder than the last.

Aside from that, there are two ways to make the episodic nature more clear. The first is to group levels with similar gameplay or similar style together (you might have, say, five curvy maps, followed by five mine jumpers). The second, my preferred option, is to put a selection of different level types in each episode, so that it forms a whole which challenges all the player's skills rather than a subset of them.

Making a pack feel "episodic" is as much about organising levels as making them.
