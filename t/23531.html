MotherLode Guarded (Chase AI 2)
http://nmaps.net/135355

Restart NReality (a mod of N, download @ n.infunity.com) to retrieve this new update of Chasing Pathing AI 2

Chasing Pathing 10 (Path ID 10) : append the following to the drones/objects
^^^,10,direction,speed,chaseWhenSighted

The drone/object will first set its goal position as ninja current position, then once the object reached that goal position, it will reset its goal position again.

Such as the zap drone in this map:
6^588,492,10,0,0,1^^^,10,0,1

chaseWhenSighted: default undefined. Set it to 1 when you want it to chase the ninja only when the it 'sees' the ninja, such as the alarm drone in this map
6^300,84,0,0,132,0^,,,,,,,1^^,10,0,4,1

I personally think that this chase AI combined with alarm drone, makes the ninja act like more an assassin
