#364855 7 2007-09-14T04:55:47Z DemonzLunchBreak
My advice?

Relax.

Yes, it is good to try hard to be a great mapper. What's bad, though, is when you worry too much about not living up to some standard that you set for yourself. Don't worry about becoming "great." If you stick with it, you'll find your own style - and that style may be "great."

"Greatness" is sort of a subjective quality, though. What one person considers "great" could be garbage from another person's point of view. Making a map that you are happy with is far more important than attempting to please as many people as possible and/or receive a high rating. A rating tells you almost nothing about a map. People rate differently, people snipe, people over-rate - the rating system is not a good way to determine how much fun a map is.

Don't care too much about not being "great." Don't care too much about ratings. What you should care about is how you feel about your own map, and how much fun that map is to play.
