#356262 3 2007-08-17T03:48:15Z Atilla
It's not bad, but it could have been much better.

It's highly linear. Sure, there are some optional quests, but most areas are just a path from A to B, and are arbitrarily unlocked when complete part X of the main quest. Virtually all of them will be visited in the course of the main storyline - from the top of my head, I can only recall one area which wasn't.

The combat is rather shallow and frequently repetitive. You'll run into the same kinds of enemies over and over, and dispatch them in exactly the same way. Furthermore, the magic is highly unbalanced. Slow time is the most powerful spell in the game by far, and will allow you to defeat virtually any foe by mindlessly hacking away. It's particularly foul when used in conjunction with berserk, allowing you to demolish many bosses in a matter of seconds.

The game is also rather short. I completed it in under 20 hours the first time through, and I was hardly in a rush - I collected almost everything and spent quite a while doing silly things like trying to herd a dozen chickens into the local tavern.

On the other hand, the story and dialogue are quite good. Yes, the story is completely cliched, but not through laziness. It's presented in a way which uses the unoriginal plot as a strength, rather than a weakness. The dialogue is often amusing, and the voice acting is good. The graphics are also quite nice, in most places.
