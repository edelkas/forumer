Yeah, I just got back from seeing "Spamalot," and it was really fun. There was a woman star, and she was amazing - possibly better than Tim Curry was. Unfortunately, Hank Azaria wasn't there, so there was an understudy, but it's all good.

For those of you that don't know what Spamalot is, it's a Broadway show based on the movie "Monty Python and the Holy Grail." It's written by Eric Idle, and has a voice cameo from John Cleese. It's spontaneously ridiculous.

You know the part with the guy that has a cart in the movie, walking down the streets and saying "Bring outchyour dead!" They turned that into a musical number. It was great.

If you're in the NY area, I suggest you go see it. It's probably more appealing to die-hard Monty Python fans, but I'm sure people who don't love the show would enjoy the musical.
