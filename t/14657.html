#273765 3 2006-12-20T06:50:10Z 34x
also, you might get a NaN (something floating above the map) if you just paste it back in to load. After pasting, at the very end put a ! to solve this. then press load.

like this :

data: ...20!0^444,444!0^420,444

THEN PUT

20!0^444,444!0^420,444!

because the game will see the end of the code, instead of thinking the coordinates are 420,444L.

i hope that makes sense.

#273823 5 2006-12-20T12:24:45Z lord_day
Interesting idea there but I'm afraid not very good. Let me give you some tips:

1)Try to reduce the number of objects used. It massively increases loading time and makes the game slower to play. Not fun. (This includes the mass of gold used at the beginning)
2)Don't hide objects behind bounce blocks, or anything for that matter. It turns N into a guessing game. Also not fun.
3) Really think about your game play. The way to think is not: 'They will love this map because it's really hard' but more 'They will love this map because its really fun.'
4) Try to make more interesting tile sets. Instead of just making walls to restrict the ninja's movement, make tiles that aid it.
5) Kind of been covered, but don't place more enemies than required. It reduces the fun of the map in multiple ways.
6) Finally, check out NUMA for some map ideas. There you can find some amazing maps to help show you what a good map plays like.

So hopefully that should help. I look forward to your future maps
