#332090 3 2007-06-17T08:32:03Z Tsukatu
Strictly speaking, water, air, and space aren't dimensions, but physical mediums; they're physical things that fill the distance between us and the places we want to go. When traveling through water, we physically push off of the water we're moving on top of or through. When traveling through air, we use properties of the air itself (air pressure / Bernoulli's Principle) to physically push ourselves through the air. When traveling through space, we expel mass to give us a momentum in the opposite direction.

Time is not quite so physical, so it's not really a good idea to think about traveling through time as we travel through water, air, and space. The way we interact with water, air, and space is not the same way we interact with time. Namely, because time isn't physical, we can't play around with it and figure out a way to control it.

I don't believe time travel is possible other than at the velocity vector we are traveling through it right now, with some insignificant modulation thanks to Relativity.
