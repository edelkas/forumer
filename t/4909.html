1. Ned already has a move/paste function. Just click and drag to select a group of tiles, hold the mouse over the location you want to paste (the area is shown by a grey outline), then hit x to move or c to copy. Or did you mean for objects?

Reset without having to hit three keys would be helpful.

NUMA:

1. I see no real need for this, and it has been suggested before (or at least, something very similar). It would let you identify snipers, sure, but many people would use it to revenge-vote against anyone who rated their maps poorly.

2. You can skip pages (although not using a link). When you're browsing NUMA, the address will be http://numa.notdot.net/ followed by something like
browse?sort=votes&order=asc&start=20&count=10

Change the number after start= to view later pages. The one above, for example, shows the maps with the 20th-30th least votes.

browse?sort=votes&order=asc&start=80&count=10

would show the 80th-90th.
