#99039 11 2005-09-10T02:58:31Z Atilla
Um... I hate to burst everyone's bubble, but things like this are predicted by physics, and involve gravitational resonance. The probablility of it happening is actually very high. Similar effects are visible in the rings of the Jovian planets - the gravity of their moons disrupts those sections of the rings which orbit at an integer multiple of the frequency of any given moon, thus producing a nice pattern in the remaining rings. In the solar system, such frequencies are based on the orbit of Jupiter because it's so much larger than all the other planets.
