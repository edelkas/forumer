#18800 1 2008-01-11T20:15:38Z Wasted power
Okay, in a level, you can have more than one ninja. Unfortunately, by having more than one ninja, there would only be one that you could control. The rest don't even move. I call these the un-ninjas. If a zap drone hits an un-ninja, it passes through it. Anything can pass through it. It exists, but doesn't do anything. That's really unrealistic.

My idea would be to make the player control ALL ninjas when pressing the keys. Meaning, when you press jump, you can have four ninjas jumping simoutaneously. You could make interesting puzzles out of that idea. To win, you need to get all ninjas into an exit.

This wouldn't affect normal levels at all, but it would make interesting maps. Also notice that nothing happens between different parts of the ninja once he blows up. They could fly through each other in mid-air.

If a live ninja touches another live ninja, why not make them converge into a single ninja with the ability to survive a mine? I'm meaning that say you have three ninjas in one place. These ninjas become a superninja which can hit three drones before dying. In this case, you could create a level where you have to "collect" seven ninjas to traverse a normally-impossible minefield.

As well, think of the awesome DDAs you can make if you have ninjas flying around in four directions! Survival maps as well!

As far as I know, this hasn't been suggested before. Feel free to comment.
