Quick idea with a quick fix (I think...) for ya, sweepstro. I don't think it's come up before; at least the search reveals nothing, allthough it's not the easiest thing to search for.
Anyway...

Currently, you can view the rating of a map in terms of how many ninjas, in steps of 0.5. This is fine, and provides a quick and easy way to see a maps rating. However, if you hover your mouse over the ratings pic you get a numerical version of the score, still rounded to the nearest 0.5. What I'm suggesting is that, when hovering over the ninja pics, the actual score is shown - before rounding, when it's still stored as a single, or double, or whichever. It would just mean that users could see exactly what rating they got on their map - something I allways wonder when I'm looking at my own.

Any thoughts? Apologies if it's come up before.
