Tilesets should be easy to manuevre on, (or not, but only when the tileset is designed to be an enemy) and provide good visual effect. Shape is the first and foremost important, then you alter the shape so that it looks good. In this level the bad side is the objects - you could make a perfectly good level from this set with just a couple rockets and some bounceblocks.

Less is more. Think about that.
