I just have to reply here.

Electricity doesn't cease to exist if it can't find a ground. It simply stays at its source in some form of potential energy. If electricity dissapeared without a current, things like batteries couldn't exist.

Also, light never ceases to exist. Ever. Being electromagnetic radiation, light is not affected by friction so it never loses its energy as heat. Rubber in your shoes transfers electricity to the ground, rubber in tires does the same - I don't have a clear-cut answer on the subject; conclude what you will.
