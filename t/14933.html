Well, well, isn't it me again, posting more maps.
What can I say... it's been a while since I last did this, and frankly, it's only my second pack. Yet... it's probably my last one. Yes, 2 columns is enough work for me.

This past year... was my best, and at the same time, my worst year yet. Where did it all go, I ask myself... I can't really answer that. I had my first girlfriend, my first kiss... my first break-up... and, to tell you the truth, it was quite the experience.
And, no, this time I'm not going to skip the rant.

I will, however, make it tiny.
[RANT]

Where did that last year go...
As I'm typing this right now, it's 00:07 on January 1st 2007. And, frankly, I only feel older. I feel like another year of my life passed. It's all over. I had her love, and I lost it. Ahh... the good times we can't repeat, is it?

[The Offspring - Can't Repeat]

I love this song. It's one of the few songs that describe my feelings so well.

I met her on 3rd of March. She confessed to me around May. My first kiss was on 14th of July. And our break-up was around the beginning of September.

I'd like to share a few things I learnt in this past year.

Don't make women wait for love. They suck at being patient.

I learnt that I can be alive, that I can be happy.
I learnt that I'm right here, right now, to trust not only what I think, but also what I feel.
I learnt that life isn't so bad if you have someone to spend it with.
I learnt that even someone like me, a geeky computer game player, INTP, can find that special someone in this vast world of endless possibilities.
I learnt that even I could be optimistic if I had the support.
I learnt a lot of things, yes.
I learnt what love is, at the same time, I learnt what real pain is.
I also found out that my tolerance against certain substances isn't that developed.
And by "certain" substances I don't mean just alcohol. (In fact, I have yet to get drunk. I just realized that I get a bit excited even after a little drink.)
I learnt that I would hardly be able to stop myself from doing certain things when I'm in the mood. I'm sure most of you should be able to understand that.


A thought:
"All I have is this one moment. What I'm feeling right now is my only truth, my only proof. Everything else might be just my mind playing tricks on me.
Yes, this one moment. Everything before it is the past which only bothers me. Everything after it is the unknown future, which only frightens me."

There are quite a few things I'd like to mention. For example, one of Blizzy's posts in The Real N!... http://therealn.com/index.php?comic=10

Indeed, some days, I feel happy, other days - sad.
Some days, I feel like I still have a chance, like there's still hope, other days - like everything's gone, like everything's over.
Some days, I feel like going out in the world and yelling "You can't control me!", other days I admit total defeat to the unbreakable logic that binds us.
It's sad how I have to go through this, knowing it's going to be over in a few years. It's really sad that I know these things.


A quote:
"Ignorance is bliss."

Do you hate that quote? You do? Do you wanna know why you hate it? Because it's the effin' truth. Ignorance is such bliss! How I wish, how I really wish... that I never knew the stuff I do. I wish I didn't know how simple of a thing love really is, because it's so simple we don't have control over it at all. The feeling of knowing, and not being able to do anything about it, it's enough to make you go insane. It's like watching your life on a screen - all the good moments pass you by, and you have no hands with which to grab onto them. All the bad moments pass you by, and you have no hands to make em move faster. It's ironic.


"Live your life to the fullest, for you have only one shot at it."

Yes, I think I've finally managed to let go. Of course, it might be just one of my "high" phases again, but I feel like I want to be alive. After what happened, I now have a new view of the world. I can see that all I really need is my life, because you can be happy as long as you have that.
To all of you who are still in doubt, stop thinking about it, and actually do something. It worked for me.

[/RANT]
If you're feeling suicidal enough to actually read it, copy the stuff in the RANT tags and paste it into Notepad, or something.

And.. an endless rant it was, no?

...

Apart from the number of dedications I've done this time, I dedicate this whole pack to...
Time and change. Yeah, those two things might be able to describe the pack.

Now... onto the important stuff...
I will divide this pack in... four parts:
1. The Column.
2. Scrapped levels.
3. Uncut levels.
4. Special levels.

1. The Column.
I hardly give random map names to my maps. Almost all of them are references to different things, some not even related to the map itself... but still!
List of references
A lot of the deds are in the column... the map names make it obvious, in fact.

2. Scrapped levels.
Generally levels which I wasn't really satisfied with. I still left 'em in there, just in case I found 'em good for something along the way. Apparently, I didn't.

3. Uncut levels.
Levels which I couldn't find a place somewhere else in the pack go here. Yeah...

4. Special levels.
I still find the Special levels to be the best ones in the pack.
  • "Goddamn it!"
    A reference to the number 777... duh...
  • "Pushover"
    My first and only joint map. Tileset by BuddyLee.
  • "No time to platformulate"
    A dedication to Platformula (86-4) by Sendy.
  • "Transfer"
    A dedication to Valves by Lucidium.
  • "Total Ripoff"
    This map is special. It's an obvious total ripoff of a LouDog004 map, more specifically, "Longest", 82-2 in the game. I talked with LouDog and he gave me permission to release it, but I still feel as if my intention would be totally misunderstood.
    I admit it, I ripped off a map and I'm releasing it. And I'm doing it exactly because I've already done it with a Sendy map. Most of you don't even know about that thing, and that's why this is aimed at the ones that DO know.
    I've grown up a bit since then, y'hear? You probably don't even care about that anymore, but it so happens that I do.
    A long time ago when I started making maps, I couldn't help but play other people's maps and kinda follow the same style as the top-rated authors. I sucked at it, so nobody even noticed. All of the mini-maps in my last pack were actually inspired either by the other mini-maps in the pack or MasterJmd's maps (who later turned out to be AMLT). After some time, a few things happened (refer to the RANT if you're really interested), and everything changed. I started making maps for fun, and all of em were awesome. I started to feel something new, something different coming...
    Oh damn, I started ranting again, didn't I?... I better stop... (In fact, I think what I already said made my point obvious.)
