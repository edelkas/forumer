Round 2 Information:
This next round will be of the theme "abstract". I think we all know what abstract maps are. Entries due by Jan 25th. Get started!!!

___________________________
A Brief Introduction
This is a competition in which the participants will make a playable tileset of a certain theme. The Competition will either be 3 rounds(finaliez), depending on the amount interest there is in this. Inspired in part by the fall weather, my friends, the Tileset Madness Competition, and Kablizzy.
_______________________________________________________________________
Detailed Information
 The tilesets must have a ninja in plain view. Omit this for entries already on NUMA.
 They must be playable. The ninja should be able to easily move around the map. In other words, you should be able to make a level out of the map.
 They should be relevant to the theme. Please post a short note on how this map is relevant to the theme. Maps can and will be eliminated if judges are in unanimous decision that the map has no relevance whatsoever to the theme.
 The tileset must be at least covering of the map.
_______________________________________________________________________

Judging
Judging is going to be different from other competitions. The judges will scan through the maps and find the map in their opinion is the best map (based on playability and Looks). That map will get 0 points. The second best map will get 1, the third best 2, and so on and so forth

Quote: Shark
And also, stuff has changed. The best score is 0. Follwed by 1, then 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7...etc. If a map is chosen as #0 map by all three judges, then that map will get -1 points.  It is, then, possible for someone to get a -3. 0 is still the perfect score, while -1, -2, and -3 are better than perfect scores. Something to brag about, eh?

NEW!
The best two of the three rounds will be counted for each person. That way, you can still do well even if you don't participate in the first round. But to have the most chance of winning, you will need to submit entries to all 3 rounds.

Judging Criteria
 Playability
 Looks
