The Goal:
The object of this contest is to make a tileset in 30 minutes. No more than 30 minutes, no less than 20 minutes, no exceptions. Then once you've made the tileset, you have 300 seocnds (5 minutes) to fill it in with objects, again no exceptions. It must be fun, and look good. It must be playable in userlevels as well. Old maps are allowed. Up to 3 submissions allowed. You must put a link to this contest in your submission(s). Judges may partake in this contest if they wish, although I personally wont!
