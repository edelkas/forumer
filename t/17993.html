I was lying in bed last night, and I can't remember why, but I came up with this idea. The idea runs thusly: a group of people, in the form of a book club, discussing an album each week, from the progressive rock or metal genres. And then I thought it'd be good over IRC. And then I thought there might be some interest from other people to either join in or read it. Therefore: The Prog Log.

The discussion itself will take place among me, Kashkin, krusch, and a guest each week. These discussions will take place in #proglog on the IRC once a week. Each week is defined as the time from Saturday to Friday, since we're starting the first one on a Saturday. So, for instance, it could so happen that we do one on a Friday night, and then one the next night. Suffice to say, there will be one each week, where the week is between Saturday and Friday. Anyone is welcome to spectate, but will not be able to contribute.

After each week's discussion is over, I'll be posting a transcript at The Prog Log Blog. You can go there to read the first one now!

Well, there you go! That's all I've got to say, so here's the upcoming topic list:
