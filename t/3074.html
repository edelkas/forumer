20 questions

From that page, just click 'play' and get on with it.

Basically, it is an online version of '20 questions' - you think of something, and it asks you yes/no questions about the thing. (allthough the first one is 'animal/vegtable/mineral). It normally guesses the what you are thinking of within 30 guesses. When I tried it, it got a Rubik's Cube in 20 goes, music in 17 goes, and apple juice in 25 goes.

Wow.
