#316867 2 2007-04-29T21:52:38Z Twistkill
1) Select all the of the level data in Ned (From the top box) and copy it.

You will need a NUMA account. To get one, simply fire up N, click on 'configure', enter a username and password in the boxes provided, and click 'create new user'. Congratulations, you are now a member of NUMA.

2) Now, log onto NUMA using your the username and password you used. (A link to NUMA is at the top of this forum.)

3) After that's done, click the "Submit a map" option on the left-hand side.

4) Fill in the required/desired information, such as the name of the map, description, etc. Paste the data you copied into the box that says "Map Data".

5) Click submit, and you're done! Be sure to double check your map before submitting it, because you don't want any unwanted stuff. If you submit more than 2 maps in one day, they will not show up on the newest page until those 2 have been bumped off by other maps.

If you need some map-making advice, be sure to check out Jeff Killian's guide.

If you need any other N-related questions answered, check out the N Wiki, the FAQs, and usaswim's thread. You should also read up on the Forum Rules, as well as the editor_manual and readme .txt files that came packaged with your N download.

Oh yes, and welcome to our home. (Well, for some of us, at least.) We wouldn't want you to feel unwanted, now.
