#90114 2 2005-08-12T07:02:25Z JeR(K)
Ok I'll give you a quick lesson on the language learning system in finland.

Elementary (6 years long):

-English starting from the first year. (English is the "A1"-language)
-Fourth grade you have an optional language, options vary in schools, you don't *HAVE* to take anything ("A2")

High School (3/4 years long)

-English continues through high school
-New "B1" language: Swedish, throughout high school
-"A2" continues
-At the first year, you choose some subjects (we got to choose 2 or 3, 2 if you had A2, the maximum of choises is normally 12 subjects (if you take an hour subjs.), headmaster doesn't want to get all the timetables straight, takes too much of her precious time... )
-If you take a language as a subject, it will be "B2"



My languages:

A1: English

A2: German

B1: Swedish

B2: -

I'll prolly take another language in college if I get there. (it's not the college you have, it's a whole different system here). Could be latin.
