Ermacrules29
=========

Ermacrules appeared, at first, to be one of the average denizens of the Internet. He made his debut posting in the Favorite Curse Phrase topic in the Bash and Thrash. This would be one of his few intelligent posts.

After that, his second post was quoting the massive amount of text in this test thread without contributing anything at all.

This was where his destructive path began. He devoted the next few posts of his existance solely to advertise his own site without first establishing himself in the community. Then, he started promoting Mortal Kombat (the game where he got his avatar and nick from Ermac, one of the characters).

After this, he started one of his legendary tacky webcomics, Ermacs adventure comics. Soon after that, he made his legendary What is N? topic.

It all went downhill from there. Members were generally agitated at Ermac's inability to accept criticism and change, and ideas, though he did manage to implement him, generally were not worked on for more than about two minutes.

Eventually, he did attempt to change a little bit, making a small animation request topic. But by that time the damage was done, and the entire forum was against Ermac. He was banned by majority vote in a ban poll, and established the first "'Blizz ban" by getting Kablizzy to personally make a sarcastic remark about him being banned.

Morals:
  • Wait a bit before trying to advertise websites.
  • Read a bit before asking on the forums. We generally like to donate our time to things that haven't already been answered.
  • Don't diss N, especially seeing as this is the N forum
  • And for goodness sake, spend time on such touchy works as webcomics.
  • Finally, listen to criticism and change based on it, or you will generally get the forum on your bad side.
