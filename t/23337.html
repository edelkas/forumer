Well. I saw this on the news yesterday and decided to try it. I mean, three times the search base than Google? Wow.

So I go to Cuil.com. The homepage is dark. Something that I don't like. But I carry on. I search Metanet.
I search the same on Google. There is no noticeable difference in the content.

The biggest difference I see here is the UI. Each search result has an image- something I see that is unnecessary. However, I did find one thing quite interesting: Cuil does not collect any information on what you search, while Google does. Google has never bothered me with that though.

My final thoughts: Cuil is a fresh, new search engine, but it's far from competition with Google.
