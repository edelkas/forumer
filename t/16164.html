When I feel that the world is horrible, it's because of the things that go on and the attitudes of the people in it. You read, hear and see things like, "Man rapes woman," or "Guerrilla warfare breaks out in some country," or "Local man shot 9 times, reason unknown, suspect identified." Things like this happen everyday, all over the world. And I realize now that in the real world, not our glamorous life which I'll go into later, that no one will really care about anyone else and you gotta get independent since no one will do your shit for you...There are so many people, including politicians, that try to bribe for their own benefit, use mass corruption, and just too many people are dirtbags.

At the other end of the spectrum, life can be great. When you're with your friends, and having a good time you don't care about anything and just have fun with them. When you listen to music, most of the time you enjoy it, most of us have good days in schools almost everyday since we socialize with one another and I think that many of us get along well and I clearly know that some do not get along at all...I think that everyone really should just sit down for a moment and think how lucky we are to be in good countries and cities/towns and live a wealthy lifestyle, not having to worry where we'll sleep tonight, (unless you're into sleepovers a LOT), or not worrying about where our next food is coming from, but rather we have choices...most of the world is still stuck in poverty, so keep that in mind. With all that, well, welcome to this world.
