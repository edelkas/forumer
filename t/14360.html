Just for your and everyone else's edification, mars cannot be sufficiently terraformed.

Mars lacks a magnetic field. If Earth lacked a magnetic field, there would be no protection from solar winds. Without protection from solar winds, the atmosphere would literally be blown away over time.

Most astronomers think that Mars was hot and wet many millions/billions of years ago, just like earth. Mars' core likely cooled and so stopped producing a magnetic field at which point mars began its slow descent into the dry and desolate planet it is today.
