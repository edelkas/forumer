Camisade, n. - A surprise night attack by soldiers wearing the camisado.

The wait is over. Say hello to the newest (and greatest) addition to the ever-growing swarm of user-made map packs: The Camisade Collection.

One hundred levels of non-stop goodness, brought you you by four of the most accoladed authors on NUMA. Each author has spewn forth five original and amazing episodes, solely for your enjoyment.

astheoceansblue: 00, 04, 08, 12, 16.
krusch: 01, 05, 09, 13, 17.
Barabajagal: 02, 06, 10, 14, 18.
KinGAleX: 03, 07, 11, 15, 19.

We (astheoceansblue, krusch, Barabajagal, and I) would like to dedicate this pack to the wonderful fellows over at Legacy; you know who you are; for their continuous support and general attractiveness. Thanks, guys.

Make sure to watch out in the following days for special appendices to The Camisade Collection. More information will be given on these later; now, without further ado, dig in, champs!

#209825 8 2006-08-01T09:49:00Z krusch
Quote: Monkey_Of_Doom, August 01, 2006 07:33 pm
Anyways, a question for krusch. In level 1-3, what was the purpose of the thwump and making N ride all the way up, therefore wasting valuable time and boring the player for a couple of seconds?

Oh, I'm glad you asked. That level was based on the film Apocalypse Now, and the thwump was my attempt to make it more atmospheric. Like, the thwump is the boat. For me, it's as though you're going up a river, into an insane, strange enemy-filled ... place?
Something bizarre. That's also part of the title, Heart of Darkness being the novel which Apocalypse Now was based on.

Oh, and making the player waste time? It doesn't matter. The thwump isn't even needed, anyway.
