Trying to traceroute to your server shows lots of packet loss. If you don't know traceroute, it tells you about each node you pass through when getting from your IP to another IP. It tells you the name of the node, its IP address, and the ping time between you and it. If it can't connect to the server, packets are lost. (If you want to see it yourself, in Windows, go to the command prompt and enter: tracert 97.86.127.115 )

In your case, everything generally goes fine for a while, then it passes through a server at 12.118.186.70 and packets are lost until it times out.

I don't know how to fix a problem like this, but I'm sure someone does. If all else fails, call your ISP and tell them about it.
