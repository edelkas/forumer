"Vexx is a video game by Acclaim for PlayStation 2, Xbox and GameCube consoles released in 2003. It's a basic 3D platform game, like Super Mario 64 and starring the character with same name. The objective consists of collecting magical hearts and unlocking more worlds to play, defeating enemies and jumping across platforms. Vexx's goal in the game is to avenge the death of his grandfather, Vargas, by destroying the game's villain, Dark Yabu. Vexx is armed only with the legendary Astani war talons, which give him the power defeat his enemies, as well as fly and swim under water"
