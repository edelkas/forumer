Quote: AMomentLikeThis, July 17, 2007 04:55 pm
I could never imagine falling 'in love' with one of the characters. Falling 'in love' with a cartoon character is lust more than anything.
I really don't think so.

Humans fall in love because they want to be in a certain person's company, including in her romantic company. Meeting in person is not actually a requirement. In many cases, that can be an unhealthy sort of love, but it could just as easily be extended to someone you meet through business in which neither of you physically appear before one another. It's also easily possible to fall in love with someone whom you've not only ever met but probably never will meet, such as a celebrity. As long as you know enough about the person's life, behavior, character, etc., you can't really call that love unhealthy.
When you're talking about anime characters, you're simply guaranteed to never personally interact with her. But an anime can show you a great deal about that fictional character's personality, and so falling in love with an anime character is little different from falling in love with someone you'll never meet - it's still love in the truest sense of the word.

Hell, you don't even have to see someone to fall in love. Many people don't care in the slightest about looks, or are satisfied with a description. I bet you plenty of people have fallen in love with characters they've read about in a book, of whom any sort of illustrations have never been made.

But what bothers me a little more is that you say that anime characters can't present any traits other than their appearance. Unless you're looking at static images and not seeing that character interact with her world in the slightest, then yeah, it'd be lust (yet also in the truest sense of the word). But when you're looking at, as you say it, a "cartoon character," you actually get less from the appearance, and your knowledge of that character's behavior and personality is much larger in proportion, compared to what you would know if the character's appearance was more realistic. It is far more shallow to be in lust with an anime character than it is to be in lust with a human, because you know even less.

Then again, that doesn't make falling in love with an anime character very functional. If you are in love with a fictional character, or broader still, someone you'll never meet, that could very well impact your realistic romantic life. You could subconsciously turn down romantic prospects, for example, because you subconsciously compared her to an object of your love whom you'll never meet and found the more realistically feasible prospect inferior.

Being in love with an anime character, just like being in love with any fictional character, and just like being in love with someone you will never interact with (such as a celebrity), is just as much love as love for someone you could know in person, albeit guaranteed to be unrequited.

That said, I'd totally sex Haruhi any day of the week.
