Anyone played it? I freakin love it. It is by far the best console game I have ever played.

If you haven't, the name gives away the main point of the game. Just take Guitar Hero, and turn it into a band. It comes with the guitar, drumset, and microphone. All of them are very fun.

The drums are very hard for me, and is my favorite instrument in the game. The foot pedal totally kills me when I'm playing expert, and some hard.

The guitar is quite awesome, too. I love the solos and how you can finger tap them on the solo frets.

And the vocals are pretty fun, but I usually don't like singing in front of anyone, yet I do really good in terms of score. Low notes are very hard for me though.

My favorite songs on it are Enter Sandman by Metallica, The Reaper by Blue Oyster Cult, and the Foreplay part of Foreplay/Long Time by Boston (I think).
