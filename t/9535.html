Anyone have special things that really help them do something? I would like to know. And before I forget, don't post things like : Get gold, or wall jump.

Can you "double jump", or whatever the technical term is? It is a very handy technique that involves falling/jumping on a bounce block, and right when it is depressed to its fullest due to your weight, you jump just a little, which then allows the bounce block freedom to return to its original position with no resistance. This makes the bounce block come back up with greater speed/elasticity than one would otherwise get when jumping on bounce blocks. So, having made that little jump, which allows the bounce block the freedom to gain speed, the bounce block will come up and hit you guy at a faster rate than your original jump was carrying you at. As soon as the block hits you, hit the jump key and watch as your guys sails to new heights...that is, if you can get the timing down. Don't worry, it actually isn't a hard "trick" to learn/master, and it is most useful. If you wish, watching the top replay of Episode 77 level 1 should have a few examples for you to view, although they don't even show off the full capabilities. Good luck and I hope this helps you.
