#355601 2 2007-08-15T08:11:02Z George
It's called a 'launchpad', not a 'jumper'.

Those NaN's in the data you have stand for 'Not a Number'. It often happens when you add a character to a part of data while loading the map, incurring an NaN. Since you've NaNed the launchpad, you'll see some weird effects. It has different effects with different objects.

You can find a comprehensive listof bugs and glitches here.
