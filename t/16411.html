Less Incomplete Contest
Here's the deal. You are going to find an incomplete map and turn it into an incomplete map. You are going to add tiles and items, making it more complete, yet not complete enought to classify it as a playable map.
Rules
  • You must use an incomplete map from NUMA.
  • It must be classified as incomplete.
  • It must not be an incomplete DDA, nart, or survival.
  • You must add at least one item and one tile.
  • The map must not be complete enough to be classified as a map. The judges and I will determine this. Our decisions are final.
  • You must provide a link to the map you started with. You may post your entry on NUMA, NUPB, or PM it to me.
  • The map you start with must not be made by you.
  • All maps must be sent to me by April 17, 2007.
