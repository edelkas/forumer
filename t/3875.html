#79859 3 2005-06-30T06:31:58Z Eldrial
NaN doesn't turn up in Search, so i'll briefly explain it here:

Say you make a level. You finished, and you copied the data into a notepad to save it, blah blah blah. Later, you return to it to play it. You paste the level data into the Level Data box [the top box in the debug menu], and you press [L] to load the level. But when you pressed [L], you didn't click out of the Level Data box. Therefore, your 'typing cursor' is still in the box. So, when you pressed [L], you typed that letter into the data box, adding a letter to the data. Because letters don't exist in level datas, it was invalid. So you added a letter and loaded the level at the same time, but since the letter was added.... yeah. It didn't make sense to the game.

Overall, that just makes the level a little messed up, causing objects misplaced. Misplaced onto the border. I'm not quite sure how this is fixable... So you have to be careful.
