NTTS Contest Final Week!

Welcome to our final week of the Numa Time Trial Society, Season 1! Now, with color! Our main purpose is to encourage Forum Members to play more User-made maps through Numa.
If you're new to the NTTS, comprehensive rules can be found here.

Okay, guys, here we are, the last NTTS for the season. Overall, it went over fairly well, but if we're going to do a season 2, there are some things that need to be worked out. This is a special week, we'll have our first winner this week.

I would like to thank all of the judges for doing a great job during the past 16 week period, and I would really like to thank everyone who has participated in the contest, because, obviously, if it weren't for you guys, this thing wouldn't go off at all.

Again, Judges have double the time now to judge the demos for each week. Juding will now begin on Thursday Mornings, and will continue through Sunday evenings. This means that Thursday, January 6th is the first day for judging this week.

With that in mind, get out there and kick some royal ass!

Here are your NTTS judges for Week 10:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
Daddaluma,
LittleViking001, And
Myself

And as always, here are the rules for Week 10:
  • This week's map is Mitosis, by Sharpie-Love.
  • Any Forum member can post their demo data for this week's map in this thread. The above judges will review each member's demo data and at the end of the two-week time period, we will declare a winner.
  • Each contestant is allowed to post Up to two demos for the weeks. Competitors are allowed to post any time from the beginning of this thread until 23:59 PST (GMT -8) next Wednesday, January 5th, but it may be to your advantage to post later in the week.
  • Submissions begin now, and will end at 23:59 PST (GMT -8) on Wednesday, January 5th. If you don't know when PST is, PM me, and I'll let you know.
  • Judging will begin Thursday morning on January 6th, and the points will be posted Sunday Evening, and the winner will be declared that night.
Good luck, thanks for participating, and I'll see you during season 2!
