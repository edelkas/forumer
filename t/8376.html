As it is now, these rules are OBSOLETE. The new rules for season 2 will be up shortly, as well as the new judges, as well!

Here are the comprehensive rules for our Numa Time Trial Society. The Numa Time Trial Society (NTTS) is designed to get more N players to play user-made maps through Numa.

Here is the NTTS Team of judges:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
Daddaluma,
LittleViking001, and
Kablizzy

Here are the judges and players who are currently on my watch list:
There are currently no judges or players on my Watch List.

For those of you unfamiliar with the watch list, if a judge does not perform his/her duties during a given week, I put judges on the watch list to see if they continue judging and/or contact me during the week. If they do not for two consecutive weeks, I will relieve them from their duties as judges. The NTTS judges need to be reliable, as the contest can not succeed without a sturdy judging team.

As far as players, any players on my watch list are deemed unable to play in NTTS tournaments. If a player is caught cheating, spamming, or doing other unsavory things that I - or any other judge - thinks unworthy, I will declare that any and all submissions from that player are null and void, and will not be judged.

The Time Trials are separated into two parts. The first part consists of the above judges nominating maps from the Numa archives. All maps selected must be found on Numa, and Each Judge can nominate up to two maps per week. Once each Judge nominates their maps, the judges and/or contestants vote on which map the contestants play in the corresponding poll. The map that gets the majority of the votes becomes the map that contestants play through. If there is no clear winner in the poll, or if the judges cannot decide on a map for the week, and a map needs to be posted for the contest to begin, I will choose a map to post as that week's map.

The second part consists of the judging. Contestants must be registered forum members (This makes our job a whole lot easier, trust me). Forum members can play through the selected map and post their demo data in the NTTS contest thread. Judging will be explained later in the rules.

As far as Demo Submissions go, each member is allowed No more than Two demo submissions per week. This allows contestants to watch other demos, and discover routes that they wouldn't have thought otherwise possible, or otherwise try to improve their time.

Once all submissions are recieved, the judges will begin scrutinizing the demos recieved. The judging will begin on Thursday mornings of the second week of submissions, and will end on Sunday Evenings that week. The judges will then vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a second runner-up in each of the four following categories:

- Time: Time is an important factor. The speedier you finish a map, the better off you'll be in this category. A competitor's Time score will be measured by the number of frames it took to complete the map. Players who complete maps will be given priority over those who do not complete maps, regardless of their Time score. As in the actual game, we will also take into account the amount of gold retrieved during a particular run. Each Gold piece will subtract 80 frames from your overall Time score. With the implement of N v1.4, the scoring system for Time may change, depending upon the implement of the ability to play Custom Maps in the actual game itself. If so, then the Time category will be discussed between the judges, and we will decide upon a new scoring system.

- Style + Entertainment: Style is also an important factor in how we will judge. If you complete a map with more fluid motions than other contestants, you'll have a better chance at winning this category. If you backtrack or make a mistake, that will count in this area as well. We will also judge on how entertaining each contestant's data is. If a contestant bores the judges, he/she will get a considerably less score than a contestant whose moves wow and amaze. This category is primarily the judges' discretion, and there is no set scale for judging how "stylish" or "entertaining" a given demo is.

- Creativity: Being creative is what this contest is all about. If you can do something that no one else can, like, say, jump in between two mines that no one else can get through, that will give you some brownie points in the Creativity area. Also, if you can use the map and the surroundings to your advantage better than the other contestants, you'll get a good score here. Also, how well you execute said creative moves will be taken into account for your Creativity ranking. Creativity isn't simply limited to the path you choose for a certain map, or how well you performed within the map, it also takes into account things that no other competitor thought of, yet could easily do.

- Completion: We will also be determining scores by checking to see how completely you finished the map. For instance, some maps can be completed without opening all doors and without getting all gold. We will judge this category based upon how complete a user's demo is (All Gold, all triggers, etc.). Depending upon the goals of the given map, There may also be map-specific goals, such as survival time in survival maps. These map-specific criteria will be included in our judging of the above categories. For instance, Least Shot At is now a map-specific goal, as opposed to a full-on category, and would be taken into account under Style, or Creativity. Survival time will be taken into consideration under Time, and so on.

Now, as far as scoring goes, Each judge will vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a 2nd runner-up for each category. First place in a category gets 5 points, second place gets 3 points, and third place gets 1 point. Fourth place and beyond earn no points. In the event of a tie, points will be awarded for each contestant to tie. We will declare 4 weekly winners each week, and each week's points will also carry over to the competitor's seasonal total. At the end of the Season, which we will determine the length of later, we will declare 4 Seasonal Champions, one in each category, based upon the number of points they recieved throughout the season. We will also be declaring an NTTS Season 1 champion at the end of the season. this means:

First Place in a category:......5 Points
Second Place in a category: 3 Points
Third Place in a category:.... 1 Point

And assuming that there is a tie in votes, each player recieves the number of points for the place in which they tie.

Submissions and judging will be done on a two-week basis. From the time of the Thread going up (Which is usually Sunday Nights), Contestants will have 10 days to submit their map demos, which is usually going to be the following Wednesday at 23:59 PM PST (GMT - 8). After which, the above judges will scrutinize each contestant's demo data. Once the Submission time has passed for the week, NO further demos will be considered. Official judging begins on the second week of submissions, on Thursday mornings, and continues through Sunday night that week. Point totals, both weekly and cumulative, will be posted on Sunday nights, just before the next week's contest.

The seasonal point totals in each category, as well as the overall point totals will determine which player is our categorical champion, as well as determining our seasonal champion!
That's it for now, if I come up with anything else, I'll be glad to post it right here.

Welcome to the NTTS Season 3!

The NTTS - The N Time Trial Society - is a highscoring/demo-contest and the ultimate challange for everyone who likes playing N!

Judges:
- Spect
- Kablizzy
-

The rules should be quite obvious:
- All demos will be made in Userlevels, using the map for that week.
- No cheating will be allowed. Any cheating from any player will automatically disqualify that participant from the remainder of the contest.
- Once you post a demo, you are not allowed to edit that post for any reason. Thus, make sure the demo that you post is the one you want to submit.
- Only one demo per week will be allowed. Any demos beyond the first will be disqualified from that week's contest.
- Anyone can join, and participation every week is entirely voluntary (Although, participating every week gives you a far better chance of winning).
- In your post, please include the time that you finished the level with, for ease of reference.
- It is in your best interest to post your demo late in the week.

The contest is quite simple. The NTTS lasts for ten weeks. For this season, during each of these ten weeks, there will be chosen a pool of ten maps from Legacy, one map for each of the contest weeks. Each participant plays through the map for that week, and submits a completed demo to the contest. Everyone is allowed to submit One demo for the week, and each demo will be judged in both categories.

Demo submissions last for seven days from the start of the week, after which, the week is over and no more demos are allowed. At that moment, the judges begin deliberation, and rate the demos (see below for more infos about rating).


There are two categories in which your demos can be rated:
- Time
- Style


Time will be the Time which is displayed in Userlevels. The top five fastest demos will take the following points for the week.

Best Time: 5P
1st Runner-up: 4P
2nd Runner-up: 3P
3rd Runner-up: 2P
4th Runner-up: 1P

The judges will then rank the demos by Style. This is entirely judges' discretion, but this includes such things as tricks, route-finding, execution, close calls, and so on.

Most Stylish: 5P
1st Runner-up: 4P
2nd Runner-up: 3P
3rd Runner-up: 2P
4th Runner-up: 1P

At the end of the ten-week period, the person who has the most points will be named the winner of the contest.

This Week begins with this post, and starts with 5-4: Rawkstar Central. All demos must be in within one week from this post. Good luck and have fun!
