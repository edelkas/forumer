#92837 4 2005-08-21T01:30:54Z brickman
Just pick a gun and learn it. I suggest not picking to learn the m79 or barret because relying on them pretty much dooms you to remain a noob forever (occasional use is ok, but not if its the only gun you can use), and I suggest not picking the ruger because its the hardest to use. You should either go for the dual desert eagles, the shotgun, or one of the first three autos, because they're good guns and not too hard to learn. The bottom half of the list is all either difficult or of limited use. Also get used to occasional use of grenades, even if just for hitting someone trying to jetpack up a hole next to you from the previous floor. Movement isn't a problem once you understand the fact that you will always run faster the more often you jump while running, no matter what the terrain. Once you've got those basics down (using low intelligence bots may help), just jump into one of the flag modes--dm, pm, rm and tm are all based directly on killing and not being killed and thus aren't as good for new players as ctf, inf and maybe htf (that last was added recently, long after my period being new to the game, so I can't vouch for its noob friendliness), people aren't completely focused on killing because there's something else entirely constituting the victory goals and thus you're more likely to do ok, or at least not screw your team up and end up in last place.
