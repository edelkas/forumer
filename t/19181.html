#360344 2 2007-08-28T15:52:39Z Killock
I find it much easier to jump from the second highest bounceblock straight to the one above. The jump should be fairly easy. If you can't do it for some reason, you could try jumping to the right and then walljumping from between the launchpads. That route may take some luck if you're not good at precise jumping, but with a little practice you should be able to make it most of the time. If you really want to do the route you drew in that image, I suggest you try and gather as much speed as you can before jumping. Stand on the very edge of the bounceblock, run left and jump just at the left edge. Make sure you time your jump so you don't jump while the bounceblock is going down, otherwise it might hit you on the way up and slow you down. Demo included on all the paths described above. Also, 1 in 10 tries to get up there? You're obviously doing something wrong, it shouldn't take anywhere near as many tries.

#364039 4 2007-09-10T23:59:20Z scythe33
A skill you should have by now is being able to walljump without having to start sliding down the wall-i.e. walljump "from the air", which gives you more momentum than does jumping from a wallslide. If you can, try jumping off the side of the second-highest bounceblock on the right, then onto the wall between the launchpads, then onto the side of the highest bounceblock on the right. It should be quite easy to get between the launchpads (as compared with a normal jump off that block).
