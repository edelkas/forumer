#10395 1 2006-08-01T14:06:05Z sweep
I've been thinking about this idea for a while, just in the context of games in general, and, personally, I think it could make things really exciting. Furthermore, I realised it could apply to N - and I don't think it would really ruin any of the "classic" feel of N, but merely create a new, exciting gameplay element. Also, it would provide an alternative to one-ways and trapdoors.

Anyway, the actual idea. I've made a set of 5 images below to demonstrate the idea.

Basically, the idea is that you can connect any pair parrallel walls (that directly face each other) with a green laser. This laser does not harm the ninja, it is more along the lines of a switch. When the ninja crosses the laser, the laser turns blue and the walls close in on each other. Any enemies of objects "consumed" by the wall would be crushed too.

The main point of it is to create the excitement of having to leave rooms before they basically vanish with you in them. Imagine the excitement

Here is what I'm demonstrating in the images below:

Fig. 1A: The basic setup before the laser has been crossed.
Fig. 1B: The laser has just been crossed. It has turned blue to show this, and the walls have started closing in - note that the wall is doing more of a growing action, so it's not moving as a whole, it's just closing in from the inside.
Fig. 1C: Our sorry ninja has made a dash for the exit, but the corridor is closing in on him, and he's about to be crushed just before he makes it to freedom. Once again, note that the wall is doing more of a growing action, so it's not moving as a whole (all tiles connected stay in the same position), it's just closing in from the inside.

Fig. 2: Another example where the laser could be used.

Fig. 3: Another example where the laser could be used.
