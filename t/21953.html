#420150 4 2008-03-22T20:06:26Z Spawn of Yanni
I'm sure you can use Excel for this. Make headers for everything you need in one row, highlight the cells you've written in, and select "Create List", checking "My List has Headers" in the box that comes up. Then just enter all the information you need under the headers, and you can sort it into ascending/descending order by any of the headers, or search for certain things within the list, or whatever.
