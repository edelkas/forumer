#415355 8 2008-03-09T19:36:54Z southpaw
I gave the whole thing a three because I love the avatar, but I don't like the siggy. Here's a few suggestions:

1. Don't put "Click Here" on the actual sig. It distracts from the rest of the image greatly. The only exception to this rule is Harley's christmas box sig which really says "Pull the lever." If you want someone to click your sig try putting the text somewhere besides your actual image.

2. Get a new font. One that fits with the image better at least. I'm not sure what to say about figuring that out except to just test all your fonts out. If you already did that and the font there was the best one then I would suggest finding new fonts, such as DaFont.com or deviantArt.com for fonts. Then, of course there's always 22 Pixels Flock which has a lot of fonts that come with the download.


Other than that I like it. Though I do have to agree about the quality. Next time try and save the image as *.png instead of as a JPEG. The quality of a *.png on the web is much nicer.
