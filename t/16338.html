Good morning! Welcome to week ten.

The theme this week will be Unfinished Tilesets. I always adored NedCon #3's theme of manipulating existing tilesets, and I wanted to bring that to life here in the season's last installment. Each tileset was purposefully constructed to be unfinished, and there are a couple of rules for the maps, listed below. We will be taking a two-week break after this week, to discuss the second season and to compile all of the current week's maps in a single format.

The Monday Night Numa Map League is here to encourage mapmakers of all skill levels to engage in a ritual, of sorts, and to participate in something to call their own. Each week, participants will make a single map - The only stipulation? It has to be Monday. After each Monday ends, all of that week's maps will be compiled in a file, and attached to this thread. The MNNM is an ongoing project, but will have mapping 'seasons,' of an as-yet determined time frame.

To become a member, please post in the MNNM Sign-Up thread declaring your intentions to join the League. You will be assigned a member number, and be given a signature tag for use in your forum signature.

From there, simply open up NED, and begin mapping!

Each week, mapmakers will abide by a given theme for their maps, and post those maps to NUMA. This theme could be purely aesthetic, conceptual, rather specific, or very loose. Each Monday, a new theme will be posted, and league members will have until the end of that night to post their map. Once the map is posted to NUMA, each member must post a link in this thread, and at the end of each week, the maps will be compiled in the next week's thread for everyone's enjoyment! Maps from previous weeks are attached to this thread.

Each map posted to NUMA should be formatted with the Week Number and Theme. For example,

I absolutely adore the maps this week, guys, you all did some freakishly awesome stuff with those tilesets. You guys did stuff with those that I didn't even think possible, and that's the beauty of the theme, really. I know why NedCon 3 was so successful, because those tilesets weren't really anything terribly special, but you guys did some magnificent stuff with 'em.

Now, on towards the future, yeah? I have a couple extra things planned for the MNNM, like compiling the pack, taking a vote on everyone's favorite map per week, and then having a show-down at the end of that, and for next season, I want you guys to be a lot more involved in the process, because after all, this is for you guys. this is everyone's project, and I hope to hit 100 participants during the next season.

So, with that said, I'd like for everyone to head over to the MNNM Suggestions thread, and give a bit of feedback so we can discuss how this first season went, what you guys would like to see for next season, and any special stuff ya'll would like to see for the end of this season. I've figured that ten weeks per season should work out nicely, amounting to four seasons a year, with a couple weeks in between each, which is a lofty but attainable goal.

In either case, you guys freakin' rocked with these maps, I'm glad to have been able to map with ya'll, and I can only hope that things get bigger and better in future seasons!
