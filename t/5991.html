It was just yesteryear when I was whaling in Southern Europe, albeit unsuccessfully. I set to sea with one dream - to catch the Powder Whale, a largish whale common near areas like Spain and famous for it's powder-like look. Little was I to know that my boat was riddled with rats. Four days in, I noticed my food supplies slowly starting to diminish at an unlikely rate. After another four days, I was out of food altogether, and clinging to the top of the mast to avoid the scurrious rats. It was pure hell, and the sea didn't help matters; the water became incredibly ferocious, with huge climbing tidal waves engulfing everything in sight. My life was in danger. In such peril, I decided that as I had my wi-fi laptop with me, and there was an oil rig with a wireless internet connection nearby, I'd have a quick web session. It was then that I discovered a photo-shopping community named b3ta. The site thrilled me so much that I had to register. I registered as my usual, somewhat odd internet username of dogbuttocks. However, there was already a b3ta member named dogsbollocks so they suggested I changed my name for clarity. In a fit of confusion, I spontaneously mashed my keyboard, and the result that came up was "chimneysweep". Then, when I came to these forums several months later, the registration form wouldn't accept the username "chimneysweep", so I tried "Sweep" and it worked! Several days later, I died at sea returned to England to spend my days writing music and surfing the web.
