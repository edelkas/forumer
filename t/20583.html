------------------------Magnum

Air Time: Fast
Weight: Small

Dual Wield: Yes

Uses

------------------------Plasma Pistol

Stats

Rate of Fire: Medium -- Semi-auto
Accuracy: Medium
Fire Power: Low
Air Time: Medium
Weight: Small
Clip Size: Varies -- Battery
Dual Wield: Yes

Uses

Used for taking out peoples sheilds quickly. Then either charging, shooting, then punching them. You could also shoot them then pull out another weapon and shoot them in the head(noob move). My Opinion

I always get caught off guard with it. If i charge it up and wait/walk for some one, no one shows up. So usually shoot it into the wall. I hardly ever find it any way.

Pros: Can take out vehicles, drains peoples sheilds
Cons: Weak, easy to dodge, overheating

------------------------Mauler

Stats

Rate of Fire: Slow -- Semi-auto
Accuracy: Low
Fire power: High
Air Time: Fast
Weight: Small
Clip Size: Small -- Magazine
Dual Wield: Yes

Uses

Do not use it at meduim range, it is useless at that distance. Instead, just wait arround in a close quarters area. Im not encourageing camping, im just saying its smart to stay in a close quaters area. Running into a long range area with a short range weapon isnt smart, unless you know no one is arround. When you see a enemy, run at him(make sure hes remotely close to you) and shoot then hit him right after you shoot. Just do that to each guy you see. Most people panic if they're that close to a mauler, so they will mostly try and step back or run at you and try to beat you down. Remember, they guy has to be close to you.

My Opinion

This is the gun(other than than the shotty) that i rack up most of my kills with. Some of my friends call it the "Cowboy Shotty" because ot looks like a futuristic version of a old smith and wessen that shoots shotty shells.

Pros: Very powerful at close range, knife on it
Cons: Very weak at far distances, small clip, slow rate of fire
