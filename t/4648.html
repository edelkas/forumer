Arrogance.

It's a tricky thing.

I'm quite good at a lot of stuff. I'm very intelligent, confident, witty, entreprenuerial, musical and creative. A large chunk of people my age are complete idiots. I'm not one of them.

Is it arrogant that I believe this, or is it just confident? I know what I'm shit at. I can't swim for toffee, and for some reason I have the abilty to screw up all the time when playing most sports. Physical prowess... well, I don't really have it. I can do the high jump... pretty damn well actually, second best in my school... and I'm really bloody tall... and I'm alright at long distance running... and, I used to be really good at football and tennis. And I'm damn good at ultimate frisbee.

There you go, I went off on one again. But the thing is, all that I've just said was true. I try not to go on about it, but if someone asks me about it, why should I pretend I'm bloody good at some stuff? I, always, without a doubt come across as arrogant. But I'm not. I'm just confident. Honest. I know what I'm good at. If I'm shit at something, I'll admit it.

What's wrong with that? Perhaps it is the definition of arrogance, but even then, what's wrong with that? What isn't good is being arrogant and also ignorant. Arignorant.


So, where do you draw the line? What's arrogant? What isn't? What's wrong with being arrogant? Have you ever found you can't say something that's true without sounding arrogant?
