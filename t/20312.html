Sigur Ros awesomely puts up a lot of their music online for free. Just recently, I watched a concert video they put up from the Takk tour. Go here: http://www.sigur-ros.co.uk/band/disco/takk-live.php and scroll to the "video clips from concerts" section. You can watch indivudual songs or stream the full concert. I highly recommend the full concert. There's just such an incredible atmosphere in the video; I could only imagine what it would be like to see these guys live.

They play an awesome unreleased song starting at timestamp 95:50 of the full stream. So if you're a fan at all of the band, at least check that out.
