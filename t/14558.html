My N career began lets say about a year ago. I was frogs. I made maps. Then, I made accounts.

It all started with someone sniping all of my maps. Back then, I cared only about ratings. I thought the point of NUMA was to try to get the highest rating. So I got carried away. I made numerous accounts (you might recognize one of them, fluffysheep). My friend (dont worry man, i still love you and i dont blame any of this on you), who I won't mention, made more accounts, and told me the passwords/usernames. Most of the accounts made on my computer were not my doing, but it's still completely my fault. I could have stopped him. I could have had no part in it. So this is my sincere apology, to all NUMA users. I made a bad decision, and now I feel that it is my duty to inform everyone. Feel free to ban me completely from NUMA. It has taken away countless hours of my life. Hours that should have been spent doing something productive. I am thirteen. All summer I sat cooped up in front of the computer screen, while my social life was dwindling. So feel free to despise me, ban me, whatever. I just hope I feel a lot better after this confession. None of the high ratings or "top rated maps" ever made me feel successful. I knew, deep down, that my skill had almost no part in it. After using my accounts on maps 1-50, I realized that it wasn't about ratings. I realized that it was about becoming a better map maker. I then stopped using these accounts, and I have not used them since.

The other thing with this game is that its addicting. It's like a drug. Once I started, I couldnt stop. I tried to go, but I just kept finding my way back. So after leaving, I decided I couldn't stop making maps. I then became fluffysheep. I did not rate any of frog's maps with this account, or any of fluffysheep's with the others, but I might as well have. I am terribly sorry. I have let everyone down.

Also, I owe a huge apology to sweep, maestro, and arachnid. I am sorry for all the commotion I have caused.

Thank you Deja and Jakel for giving me the courage I needed to write this. I wouldn't have done this without either of you.

To some of those brighter members out there, this isn't a suprise. I'm just sorry it turned out like this.

I encourage others to 'fess up. I'd really like to see NUMA be a cleaner and much better place that isn't full of frogs' and fluffysheep's.

Jonathan Hawkins
