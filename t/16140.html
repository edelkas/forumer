#302024 2 2007-03-01T13:51:18Z lord_day
This level pack was a mixed bag. Some of the levels, were frankly awful, like 02-0 The Broken Ladder. But on the other hand levels like 08-0 A Hail of Gunfire, were really amazing levels. Also, the fact that the 09-4 of the pack was really easy to complete was a let down. So my future suggestions are:

1) Take more time over your pack. The good levels in this pack were completely comprimised by the awful levels here. Never settle for an average level or a 'filler' in your pack if you can.

2) Work on the difficulty spacing of your pack. At first the difficulty spacing seemed pretty good, but it then started to fray as the pack progressed. Some of the 03-s were harder than the 06-s for example.

However, overall, it was pretty good for a first pack.
