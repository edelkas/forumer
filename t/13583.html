How do I put my maps in the NUMA archive for everyone to play?
#249811 2 2006-11-04T04:23:41Z Ngaged
Well, first you need to get an account.

You can create your own account by going into the Configure Menu of N, and looking at the bottom right corner, where you'll find a place to fill in things about your account.

Once it is made, you can use your username and password to log onto numa.notdot.net. Then you can simply click "Submit a Map" and fill in the required areas for your creation.

Hope I helped! If you have any more questions, feel free to ask.

#249820 4 2006-11-04T04:49:59Z Twistkill
Lucky I was playing Soldat just as this thread was made, Ngaged... You helped out with NUMA itself, now lemme give him advice on map-making.

Don't go too overboard submitting as many maps as you possibly can. (Aka "ZOMG LOOK I MAID 13 MAPZ IN 1 DAI!111!1zero!1one") Testing them out, making sure they feel smooth, making sure there aren't too many unnecessary objects, not too much gold or overabundance of mines, etc. is called playtesting. Many new or ignorant (And I use that term lightly) map-makers do not playtest their maps, which results in sloppiness. In short, make sure the map feels right before you submit it.

And... if you do submit more than 2 maps in one day, they will not appear on the newest page right away. They will appear after your other 2 maps have been bumped off the newest page. This is called the queuing system, and it is needed to prevent too many of any one person's maps from flooding the newest page.

Just a heads-up.
