You don't have to.

It's called an "NaN" which stands for "Not a Number." This means that something happened to your code, causing there to be an NaN. The most common cause is typing letters accidentally inside of the code. You probably did this. To avoid this, make sure that before you press [L] or [P] or any other key you might press, make sure that you are clicked OUT of all of the boxes. If you are still selecting one of the boxes, the letter will be typed into that box, ruining a piece of the code.
