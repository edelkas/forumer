Conspiracy droid

- Instead of having a line of sight directly in front of droid (like the homing zap droid), the conspiracy droid can sense N in a 90 degree range for maybe around 5 squares in front of it. If N passes though the range of vision, the conspiracy droid starts to chase him. As long as N stays in the line of sight, the conspiracy droid will automatically correct its path to keep following. If N manages to run out of the line of sight, the conspiracy droid will go to the last place it saw N and do a 360 sweep of the area.
