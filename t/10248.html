im to lazy to read the articles but ray tracing in general refers to when you render a 3d model the computer sends out "rays" of light from all light sources in the scene, these rays then bounce off the objects and using algorithms the color/intensity/angle of the reflected/refracted ray is calculated and continues on its path until it either ends up completely leaving the scene or makes its way into the camera position of the scene... hope that helps
