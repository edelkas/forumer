This morning, in the shower, I realised how fantastic the One Life Challenge really was. I think we should revive it under the Legacy banner (which is cool because it was Tim's project).

Now, the records for that thing are incredible, so I believe a new "metagame" if you will, is absolutely necessary for this to get off the ground. I think the Legacy Origins pack would be perfect for this, due to it's arrangement by column, just like in-game play. I can't see any problems with play being ported over to userlevels mode, either. However, the use of Origins to revive NTTS takes some of the lustre out of the idea. Do you you guys think this will be a problem?

I'd be happy to manage this one, so long as I have some sure backup if it's needed. A partner would be very welcome, too.



This is a rough idea. If you guys think it poor, or you'd like to add a twist/innovation/idea/problem/concern, please do so. It's why I posted a discussion thread.
