Title case is where you capitalise all or most words: 20 Killed in Pancake Disaster, whereas sentence case is where you capitalise like a normal sentence (first letter, proper nouns, etc).

My question is more to do with headers (newspapers, websites, reference books) than artistic things like music. I don't mind how you name your songs but I prefer sentence case for headers and headlines.
