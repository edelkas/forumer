#430808 11 2008-04-22T14:22:40Z Pikman
Good idea. Mastery of the hard parts would help you to complete the level. I don't see what the trouble is. Practise mode scores are already ineligible for highscores, so you can't just skip part of it, get an impossible-looking demo, and get it on the boards. With debug features in practise mode, you could just skip to the part you wanted. But one main concern. In most occasions of this, wouldn't N be falling as soon as he respawned, which would usually be the incorrect velocity and/or direction? You would need angle and velocity adjustable variables or go back to one of the easier parts and get the desired angle and velocity.

Playing the level in debug mode would be hard, I think, because wouldn't you have to wait for it to turn up on the main screen and then reset it? By implementing it into practise mode, you can get to the episode and level you want easily, rather than having to wait for it to show up out of 500 or 1000 possible levels.

You can use it if you want to. You don't have to use it. The decision is entirely in your power, and even though it would be helpful IMO, you have a choice. Practise mode is there, and highscores are there for extra help, but feel free to go it alone. Don't attack this because you "wouldn't use it." Not everyone is as good an N player as you.
