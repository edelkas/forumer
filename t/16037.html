I fell in love with this game for the specific reason that I love war games, and besides the extreme violence and odd glitches, it's a great "RPG."

It's set in Vietnam, in 1967. The story is based aroung a new soldier in South Vietnam, who rides out the year going on patrols, rescuing soldiers, infiltrating bases, etc.
I won't rant about the story.

This is one of only two Vietnam war games made in modern time (And maybe ever,) and this does a fairly good job of reality.
But that's my opinion. If you've played it, share your thoughts. If you haven't, then play it.
