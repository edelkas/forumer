Well, it's over now, but any maps uploaded during the period when no thumbnails were showing up will still have no thumbnail for that map.

I have a solution, however.

All you need to do is get the data to be updated, and NUMA will update the thumbnail.

The easiest/most effective way of doing this is to go to the edit page, make a very minuscule edit, and save. Then if you want, go back and edit it back again and save. Ta-da! It's all better!

Unfortunately, rated maps cannot have their data edited, which throws a wrench in this. Those peoples' options are limited to delisting and relisting the map, or just trying to get in contact with the admins.

For all others, though, you can feel free to have your thumbnails updated now
