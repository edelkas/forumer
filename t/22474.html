#20125 1 2008-04-30T12:36:28Z Arachnid
I've noticed from looking at NUMA's logs that people tend to use search in a very limited capacity - mostly, it's just people looking for bitesized maps, maps by a particular author, or favorites. A fair few people search on a single tag - "fun", "playable", "action", etc, which is great, but there are very few searches for more than one criteria - eg, "bitesized action" or "hard author:lucidium". Refining searches like this can yield exactly the sort of maps you're looking for, very quickly.

So I have a few questions for you, NUMA users:
- Do you find search useful? Why? Why not?
- Do you find the suggested search terms at the top of the search page useful?
- What would encourage you or make it easier to refine your search with more terms?
- How else can I improve search to make it easier for you to find the maps you want?
