Post-Rock is the most gorgeous genre of music on the planet. From the early progressive mumbles of bands like Talk-Talk and Slint, also Godspeed You! Black Emperor to Red Sparowes, Explosions In The Sky, and Russian Circles, and through post-metal like Jesu, Isis, Cult of Luna and Pelican, It simply rocks the socks off of so few people, bar a few bands who have become popular. So, discuss this wonderful form of progressive music here. If you haven't heard it, go listen!
