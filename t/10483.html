Country Descriptions

New Colossi (1):

Right below the equator, this country has a very pleasant climate. Lake Bisanari provides an adequate supply of fresh water to towns and cities. Mostly flat land, the nutrition-rich soil is perfect for farming. However, during the late winter months storms that approach the east coast are disastrous. The low land is easily flooded, which proves to be a major problem in developing any establishments.

Country 2 (Unnamed):

This is a country of opposites. The south end is very hot and humid. This dry climate makes for poor farming, except around the north portion of Lake Bisanari. The north end of the country, however, is extremely cold and icy. Life is hardly sustainable up there, and only the people with the knowledge and skills to live in such a place live here.

Cistercia (3):

Cold in the winter, hot in the summer, this island country is a very desirable place. Fishing is an important source of income here, but Cistercia could not survive without it's attraction of foreigners. Long, smooth beaches and large waves make tourism a valuable resource. Yet during rainy months, unprotected land is at risk of hurricanes.

Exendergarsen (4) and Alagaësia (16):

The Gerimbaudi (Gare-im-body) desert covers most of the north, dry and merciless sand dunes dominating most of the land. Fortunately, Lake Adomi provides a rich oasis for which to grow crops and collect fresh water. In the middle region, the Meritian (Mare-ish-in) Highlands, the Sade (Sa-day) river flows in at almost 18 miles wide and 134 miles long, this is the largest river in Colossi. Scientists classify it as a long lake, even though it narrows into a 3 mile wide river. Off to the south, the Aranian Peninsula creates a small bay for incoming ships. There the climate is rather warm, generally humid. Small, natural beaches laden the coastline near the tip of the peninsula, making it a welcome site in the eyes of weary travelers. But they stay warm for only a short couple months, as they make way to the cold winters ahead.

Austra-Flaica (10) and countries Xarosia (5), Dawnduskii (7), 8, and 9 (Unnamed):

This mountainous region is location to 5 separate countries. The Remington Mountains are the center of all the political boundaries, also known as the 4 corners (which, coincidentally, also has a place in the United States on Earth). The largest east country holds the majority of the fresh water available to the rest of the countries in the continent. This is a big source of income for that country, as the others need to either buy from them, import fresh water from neighboring continents (very expensive), or convert the salt water from the Ricarde Ocean into drinkable fresh water (even more expensive).

Nordaugh (11), Barcelonia (12), and country 13 (Unnamed):

With the help of satellite photos, geologists were able to conclude that these 3 countries used to be one, large one. Together, the three countries create what used to be known as Angosnia. The middle country is covered in a tropical rainforest. This forest provides many materials, such as medicines and food. The northern country is somewhat similar to Europe, a foggy and rainy climate. Harsh, cold winters yield to humid and rainy summers. The southernmost country is cold and dry. A barren tundra, life is hard to maintain, but isn't impossible to support a few cities.


Arelius (6) and country 17 (Unnamed):

Made of all flat land, these two countries contain all of the perfect ingredients to farm. Rich soils make demand for the freshest crops extremely high. These two countries alone provide over 35% of all crops produced in Colossi. The rainy seasons are short yet hard, and extra care and money are put in into making sure the fields don't drown.

Raskousia (14) and country 17 (Unnamed):

Two very similar countries, they bear extreme resemblance to our own north and south poles here on Earth. Long days in the summer and long nights in the winter (North and South poles vary), the rugged landscapes are home to a frozen wasteland. The largest icebergs anyone has ever seen, shadowing even the massive Superberg off the coast of Antarctica. But people find a way to live here; doing whatever needs to be done in order to stay warm. You wouldn't want to get stuck in a blizzard here.

How to Play[/u]

- To start off, you need to develop your capital city. Build hospitals, banks, whatever you think will help you start off. A lot of these buildings will be important later on in the game (IE - Hospitals to provide health care in battles)
- Houses are a must. Peeps need a pad to crash, dawg.
- You may build a shipyard if you wish. These are definitely important, especially if you have recourses to export, or import goods from other countries, as well as being able to send out soldiers in war.
- Airports can develop as the game progresses. These are for bringing in people and transportation to/from war zones (again).
- Some items you buy can provide an income. Casinos, hotels, and other type of tourist-related activities can help you earn some cash.
- Taxes will be taken every now and then. I'll total up how much you've spent and decide how much more you will owe.
- Prices on buildings will go up as the game progresses.
- Please DO NOT buy all of the large buildings as of now.
- Every country has natural resources. Whether it be gold or oil, every place has something, so don't complain.
- As moderator god of this game, I reserve the right to manipulate you an any way I feel needed (but I'm pretty nice). If I feel as though you aren't keeping your community happy, or aren't paying up on anything, I will allow your citizens to riot. This will deduct your budget for damage costs.
- If you have natural resources such as gold or iron, you need to buy MINES and MINERAL REFINERIES before you can export your goods. If you have natural resources such as oil, you need to buy OIL RIGS (either land or sea) and OIL REFINERIES before you can export those goods.
