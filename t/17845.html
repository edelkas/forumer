I'm been playing this for several months now, and I find it quite fun. It's really simple, and sorta addicting. At the beginning, it's rather boring, but it gets really fun as you progress. Currently I can defeat a Level 9 robot 24/25 times and I can defeat a Level 10 robot 9/10 times and a Level 11 robot 1/2 times.

I'm ranked #85 and creeping up... slowly.
Once you get into the double digits, it's extremely hard to win, because most of these people are insanely good. Sadly, there are quite a few cheaters in this game, but that doesn't mean they have more experience points. You can upgrade, train, and battle your robot. It's pretty fun, I guess. You can spend $5 for more training... sessions. If you want to battle another opponent, you can "hack" onto their account. It's basically seeing what weapons and shields they have, so you can estimate their level of difficulty.
