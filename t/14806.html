Well, I thought it was pretty interesting, and other Soldat players might like to check it out (although the two definitely can't be compared, heh; a programmer at the Soldat forums checked out the sourceit's open-sourceand concluded that it's basically a bunch of if-then statements).

Basically, you control a janitor from Goodco. Dog Food by the name of Joseph who has an umbrella, and is sent off to chase a bunny which escaped from the factory, presumably who was to be made into dog food.

Joseph can jump and walljump two or the same wall, not unlike N, and can pull out the umbrella for a slow drift, or to bat on the ground to solve various puzzles. The janitor can't attack enemies, but can try to lead them into lava pits, etc. or just directly avoid or run away from them.

The puzzles are pretty unique although not too complex. Some of the more action-filled levels are quite fun, and there's even a bit of stealth in the game as you try to get around very Predator-like aliens (we all know Marcinkowski's tangent for Predator ) called Zorths in later levels.

The graphics were all created in Paint, I think. Pretty cool stuff Also decent sound and music. The levels are nice, but the real fun lies in the craziness of the plot, and weird cutscenes.


Yes, there is a minimap. Holding it will allow you to look around your surroundings (the janitor in the above pic. is below the screen; that's a Zorth).



It's a very inefficient memory hog, tho'. Try closing unnecessary background programs before running; it's been known to crash a comp. or two at the Soldat forums.
