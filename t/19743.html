So waaaaay back there was a game called "The Make a Map Game."
I think it was a cool idea, but it sort of degraded, so I'm starting it up again, but with a few changes.

Original Outline:
Start with a blank map. First person adds two objects, second person adds two objects, etc.

New Outline
Start with a blank map. First person adds FIVE objects OR tiles, second person adds FIVE objects OR tiles, etc.

Original rules:
You may not add tiles.
You may move some objects around.
We'll decide when the map is finished as a group.

Updated rules:
You may add five tiles, five objects, or any combination of each.
You may not move any objects around.
We'll decide when the map is finished as a group.

Thats the basic idea.
Have fun!

Oh, and PLEASE don't turn the map into a DDA!
