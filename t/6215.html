Also available in attached file.

See, what goes here is that each keystroke is recorded in a series of numbers starting after the penultimate # character. 714 is the number of frames (which is really slow for this level), and 17895697 means left. Now, you are not going to find 714 copies of the | character. Multiple frames are recorded in one large chunk of numbers (e.g. 35790848). So it's not exactly a frame-by-frame recording, leading to sometime inaccuracies.
