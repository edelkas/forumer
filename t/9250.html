#8317 1 2006-04-27T18:48:26Z Mosh
I put alot of thought into this after seeing gold delay used in race maps and DDA's.

Lets say you're making a map. You're stacking tons of things ontop of eachother. (switches under gold. Gold delay in DDA's. Etcetera.) What if you want an entire spot deleted, without having to click thirty times? (Such as the 20+ pieces of gold atop multiple jumpads in some DDA's)
I suggest a key+mouse-click combination (holding A and clicking in edit mode, perhaps?) that will delete all objects under the mouse in one click. Everyone might not find this useful, though A is ALOT closer to the ASWQ used in Block-Placement mode than Delete is. If people werent using it to delete stacks of items, they could just use the 'delete all' button/mouse combo and delete like normal (because they arent stacking), and then quickly shift between Block Edit Mode and Object Edit Mode.

What do you think?
