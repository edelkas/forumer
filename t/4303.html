#89117 13 2005-08-07T22:26:59Z jetcombo15
It's just an online encyclopedia. It gives you information on whatever subject you want to know about. For example, the N wiki on Wikipedia gives information on N: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N_game

You can also have individual wikis that are not on Wikipedia. They're basically a knowledge base dedicated to an entire subject. For example, take a look at the Homestar Runner wiki: http://www.hrwiki.org/index.php/Main_Page

What LoD wants to do is a wiki similar to the Homestar Runner one, basically an N encyclopedia.
