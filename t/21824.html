China is also the single largest reason the dollar is so weak. It's not that the GNP of the US hasn't grown as fast as that of Europe (it has) or Japan (it's even outdone Japan). However, we buy a lot from China, but we aren't one of the major sellers to China; with the exception of GM, European cars sell better in China than do American cars, and European products sell better in China than American products. China's currency is, however, "pegged" to the dollar, so to speak, so as they buy things from Europe, it's as if we're buying things from Europe, driving the price of the euro and pound up, whilst the dollar falls.

(That's all IIRC information. Don't take it as definitive).
