Barbed Wire - Decent. Nothing too flashy.

Beware of the Puppies - Neat concept, didn't quite like the close calls that many times in a row.

Fillin' in Time - Again, pet peeve of the exit door being untraditional, and I'm not sure if the floorguard is neccesary, but I could definitely see this as a later map. Borderline keeper.

Finglonger - One of the few maps that I can say wouldn't benefit from anything else. Great map. Keeper

Good Things Come in Pairs - Um... okay, yeah, you've got me on this one. It'd be fun if it weren't counter-intuitive.

Held up by Magic - Genius, I love this thing. Keeper

Hurdles -Ahhhh, this thing. I'd put this into {4}, this is exactly that type of map. Although, it would make for some neat variety in the pack. Borderline keeper.

My Pillow is all Spiky - I'd Numa-tize this one. Too drawn-out, quite a bit too complex.

Oops upside your head - Not sure on this one. It made me vomit up an Al Sharpton-shaped ball of clay, but I still had some fun. When in doubt, I say Numa it.

Peekaboo - I love this for {4}, which is looking to be an amazing set, if we go by some of this.
