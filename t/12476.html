#226555 4 2006-09-10T01:48:32Z Atilla
Change the "browse" in a NUMA address to "userlevels" and it will download all the levels that would have been on that page as a text file.

http://numa.notdot.net/userlevels?count=1000&favorites=168, for example, will download nevermore's favourites as a text file. http://numa.notdot.net/userlevels?count=50...c&type=playable should download the 500 most recent playable maps.
