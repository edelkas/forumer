#236365 52 2006-10-02T17:36:06Z sweep
Talk about missunderstanding/misrepresenting an idea.

The idea of a waiting period before you could start submitting and voting has two benefits. Firstly, it means snipers, who will often be sniping in the spur of the moment, are less likely to be active as they have to be patient to be able to snipe. It eliminates the hot-headed snipers from the equation, reducing the total to only the premeditated ones.

The second point is that it encourages them to spend the 10 days observing other mapmakers. A lot of mapmakers are bad because they don't play other people's maps and so they just don't understand what the point of mapmaking really is.

Mind you, I'm not sure the idea I suggested was a ten day period of not being able to submit maps, I think it was ten days of not being able to vote. Either way, it has potential.
