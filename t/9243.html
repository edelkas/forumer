Quick Theme Contest

A simple contest that, if done right, has quite a bit of potential. The name says everything. I will give a theme, and you have untill a certain deadline to make a map from that theme. Read on.

Rules:
  • The map submitted MUST follow the current theme.
  • Two entries per author.
  • You may not use previously submitted maps. Although you may use the same concepts.
  • The winner will post the next thread, with a theme that he/she has decided on.
  • The theme should be well thought out, fun, and clearly stated.
  • If the winner decides to not do so, it will be passed down to the runner up, and so on.
  • No N arts, DDA's, or Survivals.
  • Each contest will run for two weeks.
Scoring:[/u]

It will be out of 40:

10: How closely and clearly it follows the theme.
10: The overall quality of the map.
10: Originality
10: Fun Factor

Theme:
