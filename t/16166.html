If you know what MetaSecret is, skip to below the line. If you don't know, here's a quick explanation:
MetaSecret is a community project where people sent in secrets of theirs, anonymously. That pretty much describes it. It's that simple. It is based on the brilliant Post Secret, of course.
_____________________________________________________________________________

Firstly, thanks to everyone who participated. This project completely, 100%, relied on the community to make it great. You guys did a great job.

The way I have presented the secrets was an attempt to give all the secrets their own personality, and make it feel like they were coming from different people. I think that if they were all the same font etc then it would have seemed a bit odd. Because I chose a bigger font for a secret does not mean I think it's more important - in fact, some of the ones I made really small were my favorites. It was just an effective way of doing things, and you should read every secret as carefully as the last.

The secrets are not in any order, chronological or anything. They were pretty randomly ordered.

I did edit selected secrets for grammar. I also made minor edits where appropriate. There was an instance where someone submitted an image which just wouldn't fit in so I turned it to text. And there were a few secrets that I have not included, for a variety of reasons - a few were clearly just jokes, whilst one mentioned hatred towards forum members; this was clearly not appropriate for this project.

There was one instance where someone, by choice included their name at the end of their secret so people could contact them if they felt they were going through a similar situation. I decided to remove it, as it isn't within the spirit of the project presentation, but if they wish to say which secret is theirs in this thread then that's perfectly fine.

Please discuss this project. Obviously, we don't want speculation about who submitted what. But apart from that, discuss away... in this thread, or even start a new one if there's a secret which really hit you hard.

I hope you guys find this as moving to read as I found to compile it.

MetaSecret
