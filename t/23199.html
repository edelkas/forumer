#20817 1 2008-07-17T08:56:15Z max_ride
As many of you may know, I'm a younger member of this community at the age of about 15 1/2 years old.

A few nights back I was skating around with two friends because that is what we enjoy doing on a late summer night. We had stopped at the local Longs Drugstore to pick up a snack and head on our way. Well on our way out, one of my friends found a Sharpie on the ground near the store and decided to pick it up and put it in his pocket. Why did he do this? My other friend and I had no idea the reason, but just continued to skate as it wasn't that big of a deal. We were riding down the Boulevard on the isle in the middle of the street because there was no cars in sight and it seemed like the most fun thing to do, as it was still safe. We turned left onto a side street about a half block down. One of my friends and I continued riding down the street for about five seconds until we didn't hear the rugged noise of the third skateboard behind us. We stopped and turned around to see our friend tagging on a Pastry Shoppe sign using the Sharpie he has just recently found on the ground. We turned around to get him to stop writing as we didn't feel like getting caught as it was well past curfew for us young ens. We quickly took off up the street only noticing the one Suburban that had driven by the whole time he was tagging. As we got up the street, sure enough the giant spotlight and the notorious red and blue lights we right behind us. We hopped off our boards knowing what was too come. We were hoping that the pigs didn't just see our friend tagging but maybe just pulling us over for curfew.

"Who was the one with that Sharpie that was just tagging that sign back there," the officer questioned.
"Me," our friend J stepped up and confessed to his wrong doings.

The Police continued to ask us the basic questions, where we lived, our ages, our phone numbers, and what we were doing out so late. We all called our parents and too J's luck the police got another phone call that was more important than this one. The only reason I was mad is because if J would have never done what he decided to do, we never would have gotten stopped. Or at least that is what the police had told us... Not only are my parents more uptight now, but now we have a record for something stupid that my friend did!? I think that's an unfair law.






Now onto last night:


Our friend L was driving C (one of the friends I was with the night before) around with our girlfriends because we were not yet old enough to drive, and we wanted to hang out for a little while longer. We were about to drop the two girls off until we decided to sit in the car for a little while. A few minutes past, no one was around. And finally the first person the drives by... a cop! They drive by and quickly make a U-Turn right back to where we were parked on the side of the road. They asked the two girls how old they were and told them to go home since they were right across the street. They then proceeded to call for backup and told L to drive away, leaving C and I there alone with the now 2 cops. My friend C was carrying a Sharpie around with him for no apparent reason and we were afraid that they were going to bust us for that since we had just gotten involved in a tagging accident. They searched our pants pockets, but luckily C was keeping the marker in his jacket pocket. They told us that since it was our second night getting picked up for curfew in one week, that they were going to take us to Juvenile Hall for one night until our parents could come get us the next day. When we got to the station they made us empty our pockets and they found the Sharpie.

I don't understand the law that you can't be in a car after 10 o' clock PM if you're not with someone over 25!

When they found the Sharpie, they charged us with attempt of vandalism and hiding evidence since we didn't give it do them the first time.

Has anyone on here been to Juvi? If you have, it's a pretty creepy place, don't you agree?
If you haven't, don't make plans too. It's not a nice place.


Well it was just a little vent, so if anyone has comments/questions, feel free to ask anything. I'm open to answer every kind of question that has to do with this story here.
