I actually found this out one day:

Start with your demo, and save it. Save it again, and you'll notice the demo has one less frame in the frame count meter, although otherwise it is unchanged.

#31102 35 2004-10-08T20:36:35Z Guest
Yeah, it's not too late at all, you still have until Midnight tonight PST (-8 GMT). As far as frame count goes, The frame count only rounds once, you can't save it 130 times to lower your framerate that much, it'll only take it into account once. Also, the framerate time isn't just a time-telling factor. It tells the game how long to playback the demo. So, if you were to go in and manually edit the framerate, N would simply stop moving after that many frames. So, manually editing the demo data won't work.
