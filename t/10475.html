#188047 3 2006-06-11T22:31:36Z Levinous
To show an image in your sig, you first have to host it somewhere. I'd recommend here. Once it is hosted, you click on the link "Direct link to image", highlight it, and copy it. You then go into 'My Controls', edit signature, and click on the IMG button. Paste the link you copied into the pop-up that appears, and press OK.
