I remember when watching a DDA was amazing to me. I used to be awestruck by the amazing close calls, and all the improbably things that the ninja was made to do. Nowadays, I think, 'Oh, another DDA'. Usually I just don't bother looking at them, mainly because I know it will be exactly the same as the last one I watched. I look through all the comments of them and see all these people pretending to be amazed by the DDA. Surely, after seeing them so many times, they start to get a little less interesting? Though I still appreciate the effort authours put into their DDA's, they just do not appeal to me, no matter how many close calls there are or how close they are.

The problem is, DDA's lack the capacity to be innovated upon. Sure, every once in a while someone finds a new interesting way to propel the ninja, but what can be innovated on apart from that? In all truthfullness, I wouldn't be surprised if the DDA category eventually dies out, and I wouldn't really care if it did.

Discuss.

Also, let me clear up something;
Yes, I may come across as a blundering hipocritical oaf during this, considering that I would never be able to make a good DDA, let alone an innovative one, but I don't care. I just wanted to bring this issue up.
