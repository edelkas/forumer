hi,
you could just use the rotation itself.
for instance, say that an unrotated triangle looks like this: |\
(call that 0 degress)

the normal of the hypotenuse is pointing /, which is +/-45deg (flash considers it -45deg, but it _should_ be 45deg).

so.. to get the angle describing the hypotenuse's normal, just take the rotation of the triangle and add/subtract 45deg. then convert the result into a unit vector using cos/sin.
