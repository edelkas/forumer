#159032 2 2006-03-28T22:12:27Z Mosh
I, personally, do both. I begin building N's path, but then shape his path around the enemies and their attack patterns. Never make N's path, and then put enemies in - because you'll have to make the entire level over again if they hit you (or just place the enemies over, but that might detract from the close-calls.).
