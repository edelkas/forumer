#327348 8 2007-06-04T15:26:34Z Raigan
Our contracts with Microsoft/Atari state that we have to own all of the stuff in the game -- I'm guessing that this is so we're responsible for any lawsuits regarding the content of the game.

This means that when you submit the level to us, you have to be able to transfer ownership to us before we can include it in the game.

Unfortunately, you can only legally transfer ownership of something if you're 18+; we realize that a lot of people are younger, so a simple solution in this case is to give the level to a parent/etc so that they can submit it. If the level gets in we'll be crediting whoever you put down on the entry form, so they can put you down and everyone wins.

sorry about that.. you wouldn't believe how complicated this sort of thing is!
