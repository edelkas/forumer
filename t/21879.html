#19567 1 2008-03-18T09:47:24Z southpaw
If any of you have ever been on deviantArt.com and have an account there then you will know what I am talking about straight off the bat.

For those of you who do not, I will explain what "Watching" is. When you create a deviantArt account and start to find artists that you like and think are really cool you have the option to "watch" them. Basically what Watching does is send you a notice that there are submissions you have not viewed that were posted by the people you have decided to Watch. This allows you to be up-to-date with what your favorite artists have done recently. It's a nice tool to have and I find it very handy because I don't have to check an artists page often to see if they've posted anything new. Instead I'll get a message telling me when they have posted something new.


I know there are a lot of authors out there that are well known. I know that the search function is very capable of getting you to the author you would like fairly quickly. I know some people have gone far enough to add their favorite authors to their webpage browser's Favorites section.

I believe this would make things a little bit easier. You just click a button on the page and BAM! you can view any submissions made by that author since the time at which you decided to add them to your Watch list.

Here's a preview of what it might look like:


The button would alternatively say "Remove Author" and that would remove the author from your Watch list.
----------


Underneath "My Maps" would go "My Authors" or "Watching..." or something, and this would be where all the submission of your favorite authors would be posted for you to view easily.


I would love to hear anyone's thoughts on this idea.
