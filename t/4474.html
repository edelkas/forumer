#91791 3 2005-08-17T01:54:14Z LittleViking
Find a map you like on Numa. Click in the Map Data box. Press Ctrl+A to Select All. Ctrl+C to Copy. Go into N. Press ~ to go into debug mode. Click in the top box (labeled "level data"). Ctrl+A, Ctrl+V. Click outside the box. Press L to load the level. Press P to begin playing. Make sure Caps Lock is on.

There ya go. Step by step.
