#8429 1 2006-05-02T23:42:00Z bhauth
OK, here's the deal. You take the last map, and choose a half of it. (Top, bottom, left, or right.) You can't choose the same half as the last person. Delete everything on the half you chose up to the center line.

Now, re-do that half. You can move the exit and control panel whichever half they're on, you can edit the stuff in the line of middle tiles, and you can place doors and switches on the half you're not doing as long as either the door or the switch is on your half.

I'll start. I chose the bottom half of http://numa.notdot.net/map/1035 and made http://numa.notdot.net/map/40929

If you really need to, you can make other minor changes, but they should keep reflect the spirit of the level.
