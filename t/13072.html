I've actually never really been much of a fan of Super Metroid, or even MegaMan. I also didn't much like the top-down RPG's like Final Fantasy and Zelda. Granted, I love those games when they're three-dimensional (such as Final Fantasy VII and Zelda: Ocarina of Time), and I do fully acknowledge that the mechanics are more or less identical, just graphically enhanced. But I get bored very easily by the square-by-square movement. My attention needs to be grabbed with flashiness, so that I feel like my characters are awesome, and not a stack of numbers interacting with other stacks of numbers. The suspension of disbelief is far easier with three-dimensional games.
That and the cut scenes. I need to be reminded that my characters are totally badass and that the things they're doing are insane. No cut scene on an SNES will be able to sate my desire for the intense and spectacular.
If the original Zelda series was converted, plot and all, to the interface we see in Zelda: Ocarina of Time, I'd be all over them. Likewise with the Final Fantasy series as FFVII, VIII, IX, or X. And if Super Metroid were built like Splinter Cell, I'd be all over that, too. Less so if it was like Half-Life.
