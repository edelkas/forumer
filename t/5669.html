Morrowind is pretty good. But it has many, many flaws. The combat is particularly bad. Every enemy in the game can easily be defeated by standing still and just chopping away. And magic is pretty much useless, because
1. You can do more damage with a good weapon than with offensive magic
2. You recieve a miserable number of MP which doesn't increase with level (Although I actually created a mod to fix this).
3. Many spells are so expensive they're almost impossible (or just wildly impractical) to cast, and
4. Enchanted items cast much faster and recharge by themselves. And rings might as well weigh nothing, so you can just carry a huge enchanted armoury.
