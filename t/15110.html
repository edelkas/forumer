The need to submit a map to NUMA is not so frustrating that you should react like this. You should also use less smileys, as they can become annoying.

If you do not have a NUMA account, read this part. If you already do, (I'm assuming you don't) then skip ahead to after the ~.

Open N, go into the Customize menu. In the lower right hand corner, enter the required information into those fields, and then click Create New User. Go to your e-mail inbox and check for an activation e-mail. After you've done that, go onto NUMA, and log in on the left-hand side.

~

After that's done, select all and copy the level data generated by Ned. Click on Submit a Map on the left-hand side, and fill in the required fields. When you paste the level into the "Map Data" field, make sure it is just the raw data and not the $Name of map#Author#Type#Data# format. NUMA automatically creates that for you.

For more help regarding N and NUMA, as well as miscellaneous N-related stuff, refer to these links:

N Wiki
FAQs
the editor_manual and readme .txt files that come with your N download.

You should also read the Forum Rules. They lay out the guidelines you should follow to become a respectable member of the community.

Enjoy your stay here.
