I've used it in a few specific places, such as the quadruple jump at the end of 90-3 and 8-1. Those could also be done with one shift, but I find it easier with two.

What I do is keep my right pinky and ring finger on the right/left arrow keys and move my index finger to the shfit key. The problem is that you have to make sure you let go of one shift key before pressing the other one, or it will count as only one jump.

#169484 15 2006-04-29T12:28:40Z Sammage
One thing you could try is mapping one key to another. For instance I use the 'z' key to jump, and I hit it with my middle finger. With a quick internet search (and probably a long install time, it looks pretty complicated to get working) you can turn your 'x' key into another 'z', then just hammer zxzxzxzxzxzxz with one hand and direct with the other.

That way your hand won't be in a really uncomfortable position and you'd hopefully not strain your hand doing double shifting, and you can still direct the ninja while double-shifting.
