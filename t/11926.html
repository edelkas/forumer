#215669 3 2006-08-15T04:15:59Z Twistkill
That's what the debug mode is for, besides editing levels.

1. Copy the level data you want. (If you just want to test a level you've just made, you can skip this) and paste it into the top box in Ned.

2. Then, make sure to click somewhere outside the box and press P.

Make sure caps lock is on, or else you'll be in fbf mode, aka frame-by-frame. You'll have to click the mouse button to advance one frame in fbf mode.

There ya go. Any other problems you need answered, just ask away.
