#198767 2 2006-07-04T16:49:32Z Twistkill
I think this is your problem: In the level data you posted out of the level box, there are several NaNs.

NaNs are glitches in the N level data that cause weird things to happen. Sometimes people use them unpurpose to stop drones from moving, false tiles, a giant N, etc. I am pretty sure you didn't use them unpurpose, as you are complaining of the tileset being wrecked. Remove the NaNs in the level data and try again.
#198788 3 2006-07-04T17:15:59Z jtbandes
