#29847 3 2004-09-30T02:47:32Z Tsukatu
DDA - Don't Do Anything
General Description: Don't do anything in these maps, you're not supposed to. The only person this challenges is the creator, because he has to make it work, and it's hard to do that if he wants it to look cool. They're entirely pointless, but sometimes fun to watch.
Tsukatu's Description:: A waste of time, ignore at all costs.

Puzzle
General Description: A map in which you have to actually think as opposed to mindlessly run around. Beating these maps takes focused work if they're made correctly. Sometimes they're fun, some times they're too hard and not fun.
Tsukatu's Description: Too tedious, against what N is all about (the action).

Rescue Mission:
General Description: Start out in a relatively safe spot, work your way deep into enemy-rich grounds and flip the switch... then get back, past all of the enemies you thought you'd successfully avoided.
Tsukatu's Description: Usually fun, but some people screw it up. It's hard to screw it up, but there are people...

Race:
General Description: Two things could be meant here. It's either that they want you to do the map as fast as you can and put down your fastest time, or that it's in a racetrack form, and you'll have to do a few laps, each time pressing new buttons, to pass the level. Basically, be prepared to move around a lot and go through the same places a lot.
Tsukatu's Description: Quite fun, I haven't seen one butchered too badly yet. Look for these when you want to relax and have fun.

Action:
General Description: No thinking involved for the most part, it's all about how agile and fast you are on your feet. This genre is all about enemy dodging and avoiding mines.
Tsukatu's Description: My favorite kind of map, because this is what N is suited toward, and is thus the best to play. The category is pretty broad, which gives way to an awful lot of variety.

Survival:
General Description: You're stuck in a room (typically) with lots of enemies trying to kill you. Eventually they will succeed, but your burrito is to stay alive as long as possible!
Tsukatu's Description: A complete waste of your time. This shows that the creator didn't bother making a level with any coherent flow. So, in order to make their map a hit, they rely on you, because they have no ideas.

Hope that helps!

Well, there's some additions:

Puzzle does not have to be a slow paced tedious trick, it could just be figuring out a way to get through drones with set paths (soon I'll post a great example of this, expect a link tomorrow).

Race also includes time trials, a type I pioneered where there's a sentry acting like a timer and if you don't finish the level before he reaches the exit its all over.

Survival is often just a level with not enough actual "action" to go into that category, or its all broken up. Many of these focus on mines (aka "jump" levels would go here) and I don't think there's many that are actually just a room full of baddies and no exit.
