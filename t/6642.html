If you want an example of UseLess, here you are. The code below gets one character from the command-line input, and prints "Yes." if the character was "t", otherwise "No." (I have worked on this one for weeks.)

out A
out 10
set Y A

sub Z 1
jmp 19 Z
sub Y 1
jmp 31 Y

:19
set Y A
set Z #t
:20
sub Y 1
jmp 30 Y
sub Z 1
jmp 31 Z
jmp 20
:30
out #Y
out #e
out #s
out #.
jmp 40
:31
out #N
out #o
out #.
