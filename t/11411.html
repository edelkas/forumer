Q: What is NUMA?
A: NUMA (N User Map Archive) is a place for all the N users to submit there maps and to play others.

Q: How do I start using NUMA?
A: First open N --> Click Configue --> Enter a desired username and password --> Click create new user --> Go to NUMA and log in using your username and passowrd.

Q: How do I submit a map?
A: After Logging into NUMA click on 'Submit a Map' and paste the level data into the correct box and fill out the rest of the form.

Q: How do I play a map?
A: Click on Newest or Top Rated or one of the links under that, in the side bar on NUMA, then click on the map title that you want to play, copy the userlevel data from the text box and into the Ned editor textbox in N.

Q: I can't log in/ Create user/ other issue, what do I do?
A: If you can't creat a user, chances are that the username has already been taken or is banned, try using a different one. If you can't log in try clearing your cookies and chache, if the problem persists then e-mail Arachnid. For other issues, creat a thread in NUMA Disscusion in this forum, BUT search for the problem fist using the search function.
