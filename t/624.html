#25120 6 2004-07-19T16:00:00Z Guest
We thought that episodes was the way to go -- it adds a certain element to the gameplay that we like.


However, since many people mentioned that having to beat 5 levels in order to save was too frustrating, we added time trial and practice mode. The idea is, you can practice those tricky levels until you get good enough to perform under pressure (ie, playing in episode mode).


While it's true that we have specific ideas about where we want N (and future games) to go, we often come across suggestions that make us reconsider decisions we made. I think it's great.
