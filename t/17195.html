#15085 1 2007-05-03T20:55:19Z Navick

I don't believe that this has been found out before. It involves a line of z-snapped thwumps, a trampoline, a thwump to run on, and frames of multiple collisions. It's a pretty cool bug; it could be the new kind of trampoline. The line of thwump propulsion can be used in three known ways:

1. Hold right during the level. If you watch, you will get propelled so fast that you hit the ceiling and die. If you want to take some thwumps off of the top of the stack to reduce lag, it'll still work, but you won't get propelled as high.

2. See seven_two's demo. If you jump above the line of thwumps while they're coming towards you, jump onto the other thwump and ride it to the wall, then jump and wallslide against the line of thwumps, you will be propelled to the ceiling, and again, die.

3. If you jump and hold right before you hit the line of thwumps (using the original hold-right technique), you will be thrust upwards and hit the ceiling. Guess what happens?

I hope that you enjoy this. I recommend to play around with it, if you want to.

~Jakel

Actually, I was trying to make a hold-right DDA, and wanted to use the one-thwump version. I couldn't find the right location, so I started stacking them on top of each other, pixel by pixel. I found a spot that I thought that I liked, so I deleted all of the thwumps under it. I died, so I figured out that multiple thwumps stacked on top of each other propel the ninja. I just kept stacking until the thwumps wouldn't detect me.
