If you're looking for a challenge, here it is.

You are presented with a webpage, a picture, and text sometimes. Use any means neccessary to get to the next screen. This might include anything from changing the URL or looking at the page source, to searching google for a line from a popular German song. Sometimes you need to use external sound or image editors to find a clue.

You do have to use Internet Explorer, like it says. Some of the screens play important sounds that you won't hear in Firefox, etc. Also, you might stumble onto walkthroughs in your searching; please don't use them, that's cheating.

According to the homepage, only 0.09% of people that begin this riddle reach the final, 30th screen. If you think you're part of that very small group, follow the link and begin.

Begin!
