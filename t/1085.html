Good morning!

Welcome to week two of the Numa Time Trial Society (NTTS). Our main purpose is to encourage Forum Members to play more User-made maps through . We have some new rules for week 2's judging, as well as submission data, so I suggest reading over the rules prior to posting.
If you're new to the NTTS, comprehensive rules can be found here.

Here are your NTTS judges for Week 2:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Maximo,
Khane/Moogleboy, And
Myself

And here are the rules for Week 2:
  • This week's map is , by rrrobyp.
  • There are no map-specific goals for this week's contest.
  • Any Forum member can post their demo data for this week's map in this forum. The above judges will review each member's demo data and at the end of the week, we will declare a winner.
  • Each contestant is allowed to post Up to TWO demos for the week. Competitors are allowed to post any time from the beginning of this thread until 23:59 PST on Friday evenings, but it may be to your advantage to post later in the week.
  • The judges will vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a 2nd runner-up in each of 4 main categories: Speed, Style, Creativity, and Completion. For explanations on each category, visit this thread.
  • The player with the most votes in a specific category will earn a 1st place in that category. The player with the next most votes in a specific category will earn a 2nd place in that category. The player with the next most votes in a specific category will earn a 3rd place in that category.
  • Each 1st place in the four categories earns you 5 points towards your total points for the week, and for the season. Each 2nd place vote earns you 3 points. Each 3rd place vote earns you 1 point. 4th place and after earn no points.
  • In addition, Maniac will be using a 40-point system for his "Perfect Demo" award, given away weekly. The 40-point system uses the same four categories as the regualr judging, but has no other affect on the outcome of the competition.
  • Submissions begin now, and will end at 23:59 PST on Friday evenings. If you don't know when PST is, PM me, and I'll let you know.
  • Judging will begin Saturday Morning, and the points will be posted Sunday Evening, just prior to next week's contest.
  • Once judging is complete, we will declare four weekly category winners, as well as a weekly winner if one person's demo is overwhleming enough to warrant such a vote.
  • And finally, weekly scores will be cumulative. Your scores for each map will carry over until the end of the season.
Good luck, and happy playing!
