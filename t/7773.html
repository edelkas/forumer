Please go through every step before starting your contest.


1) Are you doing this contest just because you want to do a contest, or is it because you believe you have a really great idea that would make a really enjoyable contest?
Only continue to 2) if you believe the answer is because you have a really great idea that would make a really enjoyable contest.
--------------------------
2) Is your the aim for your contest clear and simple?
Only continue to 3) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
3) Has your idea been done before in the same or a similar way?
Only continue to 4) if you believe the answer is no.
--------------------------
4) Have you drawn up a set of rules that are easy to follow and remember?
Only continue to 5) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
5) Is your judging system logical and as simple as possible?
Only continue to 6) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
6) Have you created a map for contest to test out the rules and judging scheme?
Only continue to 7) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
7) Do you really think people will enjoy making and playing maps for your contest?
Only continue to 8) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
8) Will you have enough time to maintain the entries list, the scores and the judging? Remember that it can be surprisingly time consuming.
Only continue to 9) if you believe the answer is yes.
--------------------------
9) Have you asked someone if they have any suggestions or thoughts for the contest?
Only continue to 10) if you have. If you haven't, feel free to ask in this thread so you can get a quality response.
--------------------------
10) Do you have any questions to ask before you start?
If the answer is no, start your contest. If the answer is yes, ask the question(s) in this thread.


This is just a provisional guide, there may be exceptions. Don't be afraid to ask questions, and please suggest any changes you think should be made to the above guide.

Quote
The N Swiss Tournament
To-Be-Hosted By: Shark

The principle of a Swiss tournament is that each player will be pitted against another player who has done as well (or as poorly ) as him or herself. No player will be eliminated.

The first round is either drawn at random or seeded according to rating. Players who win receive a point and losers receive no points. Win or lose, all players proceed to the next round where winners are pitted against winners, losers are pitted against losers and so on. In subsequent rounds, players face opponents with the same (or almost the same) score. No player is paired up against the same opponent twice.

Players are arranged in order of skill. The top half is paired with the bottom half. For instance, if there are eight players in a group, number 1 is paired with number 5, number 2 is paired with number 6 and so on.

Three rounds can handle eight players; four rounds can handle sixteen players and so on. It is not uncommon to have more players than this, and, if fewer than the ideal number of rounds are played, it can happen that two or more players finish the tournament with a perfect score, having won all their games but never faced each other.

A player can enter such a tournament knowing that he will be able to play in all rounds, regardless of how well he does. The worst that can happen is having a player left over when there are an odd number of players. The leftover player receives a bye, meaning he does not play that particular round but receives a full point as if he had won a game. He is reintroduced in the next round and will not receive another bye.

---


We will have as many rounds as needed to determine a clear winner. If 8 or less players are interested, then we will have 3 rounds. If 16 or less players are interested, then we will have 4 rounds. If 64 people or less are interested, we will have 5 rounds.

I will need judges to watch and referee matches. I will need the judges to help pick out maps for each round. Each pair of people will have different maps. All maps are to be demoed in userlevels format. If anyone has any trouble playing/retrieving maps in userlevels, do not hesitate to ask a judge for help. Judges may participate!

#431977 156 2008-04-27T15:37:48Z mam
Here's my ideas for the second Weekly Gangwar, suggestions and stuff welcome:

The Weekly Gangwar II

A Highscoring Contest

Hey guys, thanks for reading this! Firstly, I'd like to point out that this is going to be significantly different from the original 'Weekly Gangwar', in more ways than one. There's still going to be the three old gangs, Cult, Faction and Sect, but I'm also going to introduce The Mob, who are not competing, which I'll explain further on. You can't join up without showing your commitment, you have to pass an initiation test, as set by the Boss of that Gang. More on this later, first we have to find our Bosses! The first week therefore, is going to be highscoring between all potential applicants for these positions.
Then we'll allow another week for the induction of a few member to each gang and the proceding weeks will be team highscoring, as in the original. However, there is also going to be an elimination aspect. Read on for more details...
Spoiler: RulesEvery week will end, and the next start, at midnight between Saturday and Sunday. I have a life, so this time won't always be available for me and then you'll get slightly less than a week to highscore. As we'll be using Nreality, your runs will be timestamped and if you overstep the deadline, you will be penalised.

The First Week:
Anyone can attempt to adopt the mantle of Boss-hood. The aim is to be within the fastest three on the given map, preferably the fastest. The 0th highscorer can choose one of two bonuses, seeing the map one day earlier or having an extra hour after the deadline, for his Gang in the third week. He also may choose which of the three original gangs to lead. The 1st placed runner wins the other bonus and can choose to be the Boss of either of the remaining two Gangs. The 2nd player wins command of the third Gang. All three earn the right to call their members 'Grunts' within this contest!

The Second Week:
This week will be an opportunity for the bosses to attract members to their Gangs before the real competition starts. However, you may collect Grunts as the competition procedes. The last day will also be available for highscoring by the Gang whose Boss won them this bonus.
The Bosses may set any criteria for joining their gang, as long as it's relevant to N playing ability. If a member passes your test, you must accept him, and if he fails you can't. Bosses won't be allowed to change their criteria for joining unless I see it fit (for example, if no-one can pass at all).

The Mob:
Anyone may join The Mob at any time without having to pass any tests. You may also join if you have been dropped from one of the three main gangs. I will preside over this Gang. The pupose of The Mob is to allow members who lack the skill to join any gang, or have been kicked out, to participate. If you Have the highest score in The Mob for tweo consecuitive weeks, you may rejoin your Gang.

The Gangwar:
The average score for every Gang will be found, and the slowest Gang (not including The Mob) will be forced to drop their slowest Grunt. All Grunts failing to submit a run without a reasonable excuse will also be dropped. At the start of any week, players may attempt to usurp their Boss. The boss will then choose a map and any challenger that gets the fastest run that week will become Boss. There is no penalty to any players for losing this, except less time to highscore the main map.
Gangs are disbanded when they drop to two members (including the Boss). When only one Gang reamains, the war is over.

I retain the right to alter the contest at any time and may add narrative twists as I feel fit.
