Of course, you'll have to obey the 4 rules of drone creation.

- No drone is created to insult, flame, and hurt anyone.
- Your drone may be animated, and if you made it in paint, shrink the edges to the point where it can't be shruken no more.
- All drones must have a name, and a reason for creation. We take any reason what-so-ever. Even boredom!
-Your drone must be made from your hands. Not the hands of others. We take any quality, so don't be shy that you may be embarassed of your creation.
