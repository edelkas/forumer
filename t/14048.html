#370977 7 2007-10-10T03:55:09Z George
If you look at my previous post, the demo there might help you.

To watch the demo:
1. Press ` (above tab) to enter debug mode. / works as well.
2. Copy the level data to the top textbox.
3. Click outside the box, so there won't be any NaN's.
4. Press 'L' to load the level data.
5. Copy the demo data to the bottom textbox.
6. Press '3' to load the demo.
7. Press 'Q' to begin demo playback.
8. Press 'P' to begin playing the demo.
9. Make sure 'Caps Lock' is on to play the demo in real time.
