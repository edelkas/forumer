#111115 2 2005-10-28T15:48:39Z Guest
you should very carefully reread the story

for each "episode" (set of 5 levels, i.e 2-0, 2-1, 2-2, 2-3, 2-4 is episode 2), you have 90 seconds. each piece of gold you collect adds 2 seconds.

so, if you start a level with only 5 seconds, it means that you have to collect more gold in the previous levels, and/or complete them more quickly.
