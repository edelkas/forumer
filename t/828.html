#26975 4 2004-09-06T23:09:56Z Killisk
in the main screen, press the tilde (~) key on your keyboard. Copy the map text that you want to upload, and paste it into the top text box, then press L (load) and when it's finished, press P to play it.

To reload the map, simply press tilde, press r (to reload) and p (to play).
