Argh. No. You're missing the point. Metroid Prime is not an FPS for much the same reason as Legend of Zelda is not a fencing simulator.

Raven Shield is the kind of FPS you describe. Yes, dodging is an important part of the game, yes, you can't just charge in and blast away. However, the actual shooting still takes skill, and combat is the primary focus of the game.

In contrast, Metroid Prime doesn't even require you to aim. Declaring it an FPS is akin to declaring a roller coaster simulator a racing game. Furthermore, there's a difference between exploration and reconnaissance. In Metroid, you're not trying to flank an enemy, or find the best angle of attack; exploration is an end in itself.

Also, the combat sections of Metroid Prime are very much run-and-gun. Damage is avoided by dashing around like a lunatic. I also find your claim that it is in any way a realistic representation of actual combat quite ludicrous.
