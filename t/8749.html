#161889 2 2006-04-04T20:33:44Z CDotLeach
let me try and explain to you in the simplest way possible.
1. Click on the level on NUMA you want to play.
2. Under the description of the level there should be two boxes full of text, copy the text from the top one.
3. open N, on the main menu press the button next to shift, (\) or (|).
4. a screen should come up with two boxes, select all of the text in the top box.
5. paste the text you copied from NUMA, into the top box.
6. click anywhere in the N screen except for the text boxes (very important), and then press L.
7. the map should load up on the N screen, then press P to play and make sure CAPS lock is ON!

Now how to download userlevels:
If you want to put them on the userlevels list, then follow my instructions. Otherwise, follow Gangsta-Skorp's instructions.

First of all, go to NUMA.
Second, click a level.
Third, copy the 1.4+ data (Bottom box)
Fourth, open up userlevels.txt and go to the bottom.
Fifth, skip two lines, and paste the data.
Finally, open up N and click userlevels. Please remember that the levels are sorted alphabetically by type. Then kepp scrolling until you find the level.
