#228922 29 2006-09-15T15:58:11Z Sammage
Jumping off blocks at speed - do 2 quick jumps on the block, one at a suitably low point on the block and another one very quickly afterwards. You jump, then the block comes up so you can jump again. Timing is everything, with practise it's pretty easy.

Unless by blocks you mean walls and the speed people jump up them, in which case: Open up nhigh (if you don't have nhigh, get it from the fan apps section, an awesome program), and check out the demo analyser. The optimum rate would be jump-space-jump-space-jump-space with 1 frame inbetween actions. Other than kryx who admitted he was cheating, there aren't many demos that reach that kind of speed.
