#13707 1 2007-01-26T04:29:10Z Red Reamer


It's here. A little later than I promised, but we can all take solace in blaming Clovic. The mappers involved, and in fact, many that were not, know that the organisation of this pack was ... nightmarish. I cannot even remember when this enterprise began. However, we got here in the end. Thanks to all for sticking by me.

The title of this pack, auri sacra fames (ASF for short), is Latin, and translates as "accursed hunger for gold." I thought it appropriate - and Latin, thus, sexy.

Aju, astheoceanblue, Barabajagal, Clovic, spect and I are the main contributers; spect may only have made five maps, but I enjoyed them every bit as much as the others. He was also one of the first people I asked to work on this with me. Incidentally, this is the second level pack he has been involved in, a detail I was incorrect about during my Nterview.

There are also ten guest maps, made by maestro, rennaT, LittleViking001 and Sweep.

As mentioned, this pack is non-episodic, so I'd recommend playing them alphabetically. Feedback and demos (especially an all-gold for Crotch Pheasant) would be much appreciated. Enjoy.
