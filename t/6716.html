#131148 3 2006-01-09T18:57:41Z wedgie
if your not playing from the userlevels and your playing a level that you have made then reset [r] the level. press [1] to start recording your demo and then play the level. after playing the level your demo data will be in the bottom box of the 2 boxes that are on screen. just click in it then [ctrl+a] then [ctrl+c] to select all and copy it to your clipboard.
to load a demo just select that bottom box and paste it into it [ctrl+v] and then press [3] then [q] then play the demo [p].
hope that helps?
