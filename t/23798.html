#468380 17 2008-09-09T05:10:23Z southpaw
So I am back from my weekend trip. I asked Frets to set up the maps as he wanted them to be set up and told him when I got back I'd check it out and see how he did. The comments and feedback are just... .well I don't really know what to say about them. They are much appreciated.

All in all this map pack says one thing to me: growth. Every level that Frets and I created started from when I was just budding as an author and when he was wanting to branch out from his normal styles and such. We've been great IRC buddies since early October or November of last year and we have been working on this since last May or so. The maps in here to me show a couple of authors who are discovering their style, making mistakes and then making those mistakes work in some way or another. Our inspiration came from all over the place. One such example comes from the star tileset which I modeled after a star that a girl I knew drew on my hand one night.

Of course, not every map in here is perfect. But what you guys have all said so far really helps bring home the fact that not every map (or map pack for that matter) needs to be perfect to be fun.
