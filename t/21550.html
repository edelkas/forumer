JACK-OF-ALL-TRADES

Are you a high-scoring mapmaker? Are you a mapmaking high-scorer? This is the contest for you.

Here's how it works:
Mapmaking
Each participant makes a map or submits a map that they already made.
This map must be by you and nobody else (sorry, collab-ers).
Pick any map you think is good for highscoring.
PM me a link to the map. I will post all the links in this thread at the same time.
Everybody must choose their two favorite maps and PM me their votes.
The mapmaker who got the least votes is eliminated from this competition.
The mapmaker who got the most votes wins immunity in the highscoring section.

Highscoring
The participants will all play the chosen map from the first section.
Each participant must PM me an agd before the time limit is up.
The highscorer with the slowest agd is eliminated from this competition.
The highscorer with the fastest agd wins immunity in the next mapmaking section.

We will continue to go between these two stages until one "Jack-of-all-Trades" remains.


Prizes
Undecided. Maybe some ded maps or sig tags (if somebody is willing to make sig tags for this competition).
The winner and two runner-ups will definitely recieve a prize of some sort.
