The week is over and you guys are doing an awesome job! There are 17 entries for this week.

Time was a very interesting aspect in this week.
As most of you noticed, it was possible to get through the closed door without getting the switch. Most of you did that. What only few people noticed was that it is possible to skip the complete left section and get up the right side using reverse-corner jumps, combined with walljumping and similar. You skipped a few gold with this route though and it was therefore hard to know which route was faster. JF ended up being the fastest with the "normal" route. ska followed him, with a very spectacular run indeed. The third spot though, is held by cyberjuda, who did a very very nice reverse corner jump to skip the left side.
