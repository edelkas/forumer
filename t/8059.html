#151799 3 2006-03-05T05:19:15Z Ngaged
'Tis quite simple, my friend!

Make the laser drone the last thing you put in for an object.
Go to the debug page and delete the last number in your level data.
Replace it with a Z (though it could be other things, but I use Z for safety).
Reload the level, and your laser drone should stay in place.

Or...

Put locked doors all around the laser drone. This way works, as well, but i personally think it looks ugly.

Hope I helped ye.
