hmmmm... does this belong in messageboard games?

So, the idea behind this goes thus:

1. Describe your map-making style, go into detail or be detailed in a more general sense of what you do and what the final results are.

2. Make a new map of your own that you think demonstrates your style as you have explained it.

3. I'll start off by making a map in my style, but the idea is to then make a map based on the style of the user above you, as described by them and seen in the map of theirs they make. So you make a map based on the above user's post, then describe your own style and make a map in it. (Get it? You submit two maps for each post; one imitating the style of the above author as best you can, and one of your own style.)

Any questions?

I'll start.

I usually use a lot of three and two tiles, as well as seven and six, to create a rocky, craggy, or atmospheric setting, and sometimes use real objects like a llama or a toilet as a basis for tile shapes. I often make 'Cave' style maps, and my gold placement is artistic. My mines are usually placed on tiles along the edges, and I tend to make lots of use of rockets, bounceblocks, and launchpads. I've gone through chaingun and floorguard phases, and mostly my levels are simply created, good-looking, and make effective use of enemies (I don't use too many.) There's usually lots of flow to discover in my maps because of the three, two, seven, six, and one tiles that I use. I usually mix clusters of gold in places where they are easy to get and become part of a completion, and some small pockets of gold that are more challenging and difficult to get too. AGD is always harder on my levels.

Finally, if you already have a map that you think really represents your style, you can post that instead of making a new one. But unless you're replying in this thread to a question or something, try and make a map in the above user's style for every post. Multiple people could try and make maps in one user's style, but that probably won't happen much.
