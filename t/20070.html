OK, here goes then...

I've organised the following like this:
NAME (in bold)
Listening through (what I felt)
Summary (if I like it or not, a rough rating/10, or something similar)

Oh, and any unrelated commantary is in blue.

Technically Frozen
Nice start. Which, although it has rests, gave a good sense of the beat. 20 secs in: Nice melody line. I liked how it went from action-y to relaxed, and back again. Nice ending, also.
Basically, I like this song. If this wasn't already in iTunes, I'd put it in now. Somewhere around a 9.5/10, if not higher.

Synth Stager
Interesting start. I like the drone, with the added notes above it. Then when the percussion comes in, it starts sounding really good. I would have possibly made slight variotions of the percussion melody, but that's just me. 1 min in; This sounds good. The lower instrument sounds a but funny to me, but the higher one works well. Good fade-out ending.
Hmm.. interesting. I don't think I liked it as much as Technically Frozen, but it was still pretty cool. Maybe 7/10?

Quartz Boulder
Nice chords, added to be the drum beat. I liked the little synth that came in, too. 30 secs in, nice use of the cymbals, and then again at the end with the drum, too. Good fade-out, too. It worked well.
Pretty cool, yeah. Maybe 7 or 8.

Progressive Norse, Of Course
Nice violin melody, and nice implementation of the electric guitar and drums. 30 secs in; nice use of the bongos, while geeping the electric guitar chords. I liked that. 55 secs in; oh, bongos and melody, nice! Then good use of rests, while keeping the bongos on. And good fade-out of the electric guitar.
I liked it. Probably hovering around an 8 somewhere.

Mining For Ghosts
Nice start. The beat can be felt well, and good implementation of the cymbals. They work really well just being soft. 35 secs in; nice piano usage, then on to steel (?) guitar. I liked that, and then with the piano chords later. Not sure if the piano chord worked at the end though.
Mmm... good. 7 or 8, again.

~~~ Phew, half way through. Let's take a break...
~~~ OK, break time's over. Let's get back to this!

Machinery
Nice, rhythmical start. I can see why it's called "Machinery". Nice low synth. This gave the music a more electronic feel. The higher synth sounds a bit funny though, though maybe it's just me. 55 secs in; ooh, this is nice. Vaguely underwater feel. I like that. 1:20 in; Interesting synth here, which fits the melody well. Both of them do, in fact. I liked how the ending went back to the beginning, sorta. That was good.
Again, 8ish.

Headache
Nice guitar melody, which is added to nicely by the bass. Interesting percussion though. It works, but I think it's too loud, possibly. For me, it blocks out too much of the melody. 45 secs in; Nice lower instrument. Well, it's not that low, actually, but it's nice. Again, I liked the way the piece went back to the beginning, with only guitar.
Hmm... maybe... 7ish. Bit higher. Sorta 7.5/10.

Ground Wire
Nice drums at the beginning, and I liked the guitar playing soon after. It's funny; although the beats occasionally conflict, you can still feel the beat really well. 50 secs in; I like the use of the percussion and the melody here. The two went together well, and the drums help the slight change there. The ending was good, though I think it cut back to one instrument to quickly.
Perhaps around 8 somewhere, yet again.

Exoticoustic
Yay, guitar again. I liked the melody that it played, and the percussion and the other instrument really added to this melody. I love the bass, too, 30 secs in. It sorta feels like it gets drowned out by the guitar strumming though, but that fades out nicely to end.
I'd go about 9, I think. I liked it.

Blue Ice
sweet synth action here. The percussion really works, and nice tango feel with the piano, followed by the synths some more. I really liked how you could feel the beat the whole way through. That made me happy. Interesting fade-out at the end, though I think it worked well.
Slightly higher than 9. Maybe.. 9.2ish/10. Could have been a bit longer though, because it's the only song under a minute
