#72892 5 2005-06-07T00:25:06Z nevermore
Last sentence - there might be your problem. Don't just put crap down. I suggest you lay down some tiles, and work with them, seeing which make you do what. Then, use one of them in a certain way, and build off of that.
Then, do the same thing with objects. Figure out how you can make a certain tile fit with a oneway, or something like that.
I think the problem with doubleback was the enemy timing. Chainguns are pretty accurate, so it's much better to use them in wide open spaces. Also, sometimes when you went with the flow, a rocket would hit you. Sometimes that happens with me, and I delete the rocket and replace it, and it can fix itself because of AI.
Also, I suggest starting a level with a certain angle in mind. Such as... A laser/oneway level. Y'know?
