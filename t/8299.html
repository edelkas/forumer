If you do not have a solid subject you will be wasting your time unless it is just a test forum to check out board mods or to see what the Admin CP, etc. look like.

There are literally thousands (no exaggeration; in fact that may even be an understatement if you look at Forumer.com's directories) of random, pointless off-topic forums. Less than 5% of them have more than 5 regulars. If you don't have a good foundation no one will find any reason to join.

And don't attempt to revolve it around your interests (in response to "... I listen to 3 bands, I surf, skate..."). No two people are alike.

Otherwise, good luck and happy topic-hunting
