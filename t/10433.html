#187316 2 2006-06-10T18:17:33Z Ngaged
Don't feel stupid. Evryone's confused and puzzled at first.

1. Load your map and stuff (you know how to do that, I assume).
2. Press 2.
3. Press 1.
4. Play the map. You are now recording while you're playing this run.

To make sure you've successfully recorded, do this:
1. Go back to debug mode.
2. Press 3.
3. Press Q.
4. Press P and see if it starts playing the demo.

Hope I've covered it for ya.
