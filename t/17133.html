#316744 2 2007-04-29T10:55:28Z lord_day
It's pretty simple. I assume you are talking about the glitch which makes some floorguards be able to travel over some doors, while others can't? Well if you place the door before you place the floorguard, then the floorguard will travel on it. If you place the door after you place the floorguard, then the floorguard won't travel on it. It's just a matter of placing floorguards and doors in the right order.
