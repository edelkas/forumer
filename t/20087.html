#377900 5 2007-11-09T07:21:52Z Ditocoaf
Life is never worthless or sad... It sounds corny, but it's full of endless possibilities. This isn't to say that anything is possible, but there are an infinite number of possibilities. Even if you don't end up becoming the leader of a country or curing cancer, that's not necessarily what life's about. Let me explain why this is a happy thing--

Think of this: The probability that you would exist right now is so small, it's basically nil. Yet somehow, against all odds, you find yourself with the power to exist, to experience. You can do whatever you want with your life, and the whole world just happens to exist. No matter what, this is an opportunity for you to do something, anything, that can make you happy.
If you have somehow come to the conclusion that life is worthless, then you are the freest person in the world. You could do anything you want, because you wouldn't see yourself as capable of losing anything of value. So even then, you could squeeze some enjoyment out of whatever amount of life you choose to continue for. But chances are you do value your life, which is a good thing. Take advantage of this thing that you have, this power...
You are not an audience member at a film of your life. You aren't the director either, but the point is it isn't a movie. You have the power to affect your life more so than does anybody else, and you also have the power to affect the lives of those around you.
If you go out into your life with the perspective of feeling lucky to be alive, and having power over your own life, you will feel better. Don't "wait for something exiting to happen" to you... stir things up a little. If you end up embarrassing yourself at school, so what? The number people in your school that would ostracize you in an embarrassing is much, much smaller than the tiny group of people you said would care about if you die. There are, for all intents and purposes, an infinite number of people you can meet, and an infinite number of things you can do.
Life may seem set for you right now, because you're stuck working your way through the path of school, but you can look forwards to the time in life where things open up, and you have many, many more choices. The decisions you make socially will affect you instantly, but only temporarily. The decisions you're beginning to make, will affect your life years from now. But even if you end up finding yourself in a bad situation then, it is always possible to change your life.
