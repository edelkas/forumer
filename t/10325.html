Colossus[/u]

Story: It's the year 3010. A new planet has discovered lingering on the outside of our solar system. Named after its founder Jacob Colossi, the planet has recently been colonized by eager immigrants hoping to escape the crowded Earth.

RULES:

- Only one country per person, unless you have attacked and conquered another country.
- You may not create your country with any more than what is given to you at the start. IE: You may NOT start off with more than the allowed number of citizens.
- For now you may only play ONE leader. No making two leaders to gain more territory.
- You are allowed to name your country and choose where you want your Capital within your own country.

Starting a Country:

- Once I have 10 people interested, the game will begin.
- AFTER the game begins, you will choose a number 2-17 (The numbers will be chosen randomly so there are no arguments over who gets the bigger land).
- First come, first serve when choosing numbers.
- Once you have been assigned a country, you will need to fill out this form:

Country name: Try to be somewhat realistic here, no really stupid names (IE: Noobl4nd).
Leader Name: Be creative. Something other than your user name would be appreciated.
Flag: Your flag will consist of a field (background) and design/symbol. (Ex: Black field with skull).
Leadership: Democracy, Communism, Dictatorship, etc. No made up leaderships please.
Religion: Choose from the religions that already exist. If your country has a mixed religion, then just put N/A.
Main Race: No aliens. Anything that we see here on earth is acceptable.
Population: EVERYONE will start out with 500 citizens.
Military Force: You may develop a militia after you have 1,000 citizens. You budget will affect how many soldiers you are given.
Budget: Everyone starts with an amount of $1,000,000.
