Okay, I don't know how popular this will be, but I think it's a fun idea. It's another "member above you" thread, and the goal is to make an acronym out of the username of the person above you. In the off chance that you don't know what an acronym is, it is a word in which each of the letters stand for another word. For example, "laser" is an acronym which stands for "light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation." "NASA" stands for "National Aeronautics and Space Administration." You get the idea.
Underscores and other symbols such as periods, ampersands, etc should be skipped ("Monkey_of_Doom" should be treated as "monkeyofdoom"). If there are numbers at the end of the username, they can either be dropped, or the numbers can be turned into their first letter ("Jiggerjaw12358" can either be treated as "jiggerjaw" or "jiggerjawottfe". "7654321" can be treated as "ssfftto", etc...). Prepositions and/or conjuctions may be added in addition to the letters in the username (just as is done in "laser" and "NASA").
Try to make the acronym funny, or make it have something to do with that person's personality, something s/he has done, etc... If you don't know that person well, you can just think up something you feel is funny. As this does require quite a bit of thinking, putting a group of words together that remotely makes sense would suffice, as I know it's not too easy. Some examples would be:

iangb - I am never going back
Guiseppi - Guy undertaking impious spamming escapade proves pitifully irksome. (Wow, I just thought that one up. I'm impressed with myself)

Anyways, you get the idea. I hope you enjoy this.
