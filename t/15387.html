I'm just wondering, has anyone here ever heard of John Titor?

If you haven't here's a little summary:

Basically he was a guy that left from the year 2036 and arrived on our timeline (assuming the string theory and inifinite universes) and arrived on November 2000. He went online until March 2001 when he had to leave. Now, this isn't like the hundreds of fake people that try to make up time travel stories. This guy posted about 10 pictures of the time machine he used, one picture being how he was able to bend a laser beam with the gravitational sphere that the machine generated, and the other pictures were documents from the Military in Florida from 2036.

Now, this guy would not give us any specific dates or locations for his predictions of our timeline because he does not want to alter it. However, he did make many great predictions that could affect us all. Some of these have already happened such as Mad Cow Disease. One of his biggest predictions is that the US will be in a civil war in approximately 2008-2010 (he said it will start in 2005 but we will not realize it until 2009). He also made the biggest prediction of a World War 3 in 2015 in which 1 Billion people will die world wide. Now, these to him were not predictions, they were events that occurred in his timeline. Our timeline can be different because of the divergence. For example, on New Years in 2000 he was very surprised that we weren't affected by the Y2k Bug because he said it was a disaster on his timeline.

Anyways, this guy was probably one of the most believed people that said they were time travelers. He said the reason he came here was because he was on a mission for the government to go to 1967 to retrieve a certain IBM computer which contained a special code that they needed for a big mission in 2036. He said he stopped by in 2000 to visit his young self and give advice to his mother before completing the mission.


Anyways, nothing has been heard from him since he left in March of 2001. I just thought we could start a discussion on this.
