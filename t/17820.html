Datarealms Development Log
Allright, I know Keron's allready posted about build13, but that was so long ago and this build almost deserves a new topic anyway! The game's still under development, and I've waited for this build for like four months now (the main developer got married during the time, which might've slowed the development).

For those of you who don't know about the game, it's a little similar to soldat, that is, you're a small guy in a 2Dworld that tries to shoot other guys. Except for not being a multiplayergame the two major differences are:

CC is also a strategy game, which means you have to mine materials, buy clones and such at the same time.
CC relies on a powerfull (for an indie game) physic engine, which allows Any material to be destroyed, deformed, or stockpiled. When a clone dies, he'll not vanish. Heck, when a robot "bleeds" oil, the oil will become a part of the scenery! The same thing goes for ships, where you can destroy one engine and see the dropship spin around like mad before hitting the ground. The game's also very detailed in many other aspects, and if you defend a position long enough you'll soon see a small heap of empty magazines and shells covering the ground.
However, the thing that makes the game stand out from any other game I've played is it's moddability. You can mod how much each empty shell of the gun will weight, you can mod the friction of the helmet that flies away when a clone is splattered by a rocket. You can make a dropship where the right engine is weaker than the left one. You can completely re-skin the maps, menues, clones. Almost everything's moddable! And things only seems to improve.

To those of you who allready have Build13, the most major changes are:

Improved Physics that'll prevent clones from getting stuck under falling debris, now slow movement will also crush your guys.
Stabilised physics, you FPS won't effect how the game acts.
NO TUNNELING!
New AI controlls for you own guys. Now you can mass up an army and order them to attack the enemy base or mine without having to controll every goddamn skeleton.
New Ortdering of Characters, switch characters from left to right instead of order of arrival.
Easier map-making.
No enemy ships landing directly over your base.
No more wallpoking with long guns
Improved loading-logics.

Hopefully you guys will enjoy this new build of an allready awesome game. I would, but I can't seem to download the new version...

Now lets just hope nobody posted this while I was typing...

Allright, I finaly got it working. And althuogh I like the game, don't be surprised if it's still a tad... Unbalanced. The last build (version of the game) game came with a major physic overhaul. You will probably notice how the enemy tends to "dance" over your brain. The AI will (hopefully) be improved for later builds, but the old Physics allowed the enemy to "tunnel" through any material simply by attempting to walk through it. This'd soon cause a big hole over the brain in the maps Grasslands and Prom Grounds (the one's we had in the last build). Now sadly, you can't really walk through anything, so a skeleton can sometimes be stopped by a single pixel or two hanging in the air. Also the AImode GoldDigger doesn't really work that well...

Still the game's an improvement from the last build, and the mods seems to work about the same way (soon there'll be a great load of build14-compatible mods at the forums. Guess we'll just have to wait for the next build for the tweeks, I wouldn't be surprised for a small "tweak-build" to come out soon.
