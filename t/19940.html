#376646 21 2007-11-03T19:25:41Z Raigan
just so it's clear: there are 250 singleplayer levels on XBLA, and 200 on DS and PSP, and all of these levels are 100% new!!

we're really excited about the XBLA version, it definitely is the game we wanted to make -- the actual gameplay is identical to N, but it runs smoother, the graphics are a bit "enhanced", it supports all sorts of different map sizes, and the multiplayer is really great. we had some friends over yesterday and were playing it, if you're good at N then you should be able to clean up on the leaderboards

it's been finished for a while and it's just waiting final testing and release.. they don't tell us the actual release date until a couple days before though


the handheld versions aren't as far along -- they've been sort of playable since september but due many factors (different screen size, handheld processors are limited compared to PC/360, etc) they're still not right.. there are many little problems which we're still working with the developer to fix. hopefully we'll be able to make them as good and faithful to the original as the XBLA version, it's still too early to tell..
