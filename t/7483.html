Parameters and such:
Read them, please.

I was thinking about tktktk's Minimalist Series and an astheoceansblue map by the name of daNse macabre and I felt inspired to start this contest. The theme is gold, mines, tiles. No other enemies or objects (bar the ninja and the exit/exits, of course) are permitted.

Judges are: pariah, Barabajagal and KinGAleX.
Backup judge is: Me, krusch!

Rules are:
- Maps may not contain any objects other than the ninja, the exit/exits, gold, and mines.

- A maximum of two entries per author.

- Maps must be submitted to NUMA and linked in this thread by March 15th.

- Any type of map can be submitted except for N-Art.

- Maps must have been created on, or after, February 12th 2006.

- Keep it friendly; don't be spiteful about anything or anyone.

Judging is based on 2 categories:
- Originality: I don't want to see copies of Bonzai; or any other map, for that matter. (mark out of 30)

- Overall Quality: This includes efficiency of item usage, playability/fun factor, atmosphere and so on. (mark out of 70)


I strongly encourage you to be creative; don't just make generic mine jumpers or races.
Think up some new concepts.
I also encourage you to be careful with your submissions; you only have 2 so use them wisely.

I'll try to keep this post updated and hopefully we'll have a productive and enjoyable contest.
Get crackin'.

Judging parameters changed with help from Sweep, thanks mate.

End!
