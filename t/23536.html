I have to agree, a tileset does really help in making a good level.
However, to really make a good level you just have to keep trying new things. Search the map database, look at some good maps and then just wait until you get a good idea. Don't rush yourself! And also, when making maps, just go with the flow!
hardly any new author gets great maps on his first few, and most of those that say they have a good first level have just delisted all the ones before it. so it just takes time, but you'll get there!
