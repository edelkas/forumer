The article doesn't try to disprove God or intelligent design in any way. In fact, look at the title. "Some More of God's Greatest Mistakes." It actually assumes there is a god, even if it's just to make his point (in a biting, sarcastic way).

The article is trying to give support to evolution. It doesn't neccessarily take a stance on religion that I saw. If you saw it as an attack on your religion, it's only because you went in expecting an attack on your religion. It just shows weird traits in certain animals that could easily be explained with evolution, but would be hard to do with intelligent design theory.

Taking, for example, the species with malformed eyes which don't work (because the animal doesn't need them), why does it have those eyes? Did God give that animal useless eyes on purpose? Or are they leftovers from evolution?

A person who knew of evolution would say that millions of years ago, a certain species of fish lived, with fully functioning eyes. Some or all of those fish moved into a dark cave where their eyes were useless. Over time, their eyes grew weak and deteriorated because they weren't used. It's the same way a human's "claws" (i.e. fingernails and toenails) are weak and brittle because we never have to use them. We haven't for a long time. With intelligent design theory, however, you can't explain the fish's nonfunctional eyes. There's no logical reason for them to be there if they're not used.

Again, I doubt the author expects to change anyone's views on Creation. Instead, it presents some interesting facts and implicitly supports evolution.
