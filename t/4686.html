Midnight Mansion is a great little mansion explorer game I found about half a year ago. Basically, the story goes like this:

Quote: Midnight Mansion
Inside a mansion, say legends of old,
Is hidden great treasure, rubies and gold.

But few dare enter, and none have returned,
For an early grave is all they have earned.

Traps and puzzles abound inside,
And also within the undead reside.

But great treasure is hard to resist,
so it's off to the mansion, you insist!

So, anyway, this game is rather fun. You dodge enemies by jumping over them. Like in N, most of the focus is on avoiding the enemies rather than destroying them, though you can destroy one monster if you happen to find a shield somewhere around the mansion. Various other things inhabit these places, so I suggest you download it!

With three difficulty modes, it's sure to excite anyone who plays, whether expert or new. The catch? It's shareware However, it's a $20 I'm very glad I spent. Buy it today! Or pester your parents to until they give in!
