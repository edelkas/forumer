Has anyone else played this game? The game Black, made by Criterion, is underated. Every review claims it's too short, the destructible environments have seams so they're not what they were thought to be, and the AI can be blind sometimes.

The AI is sometimes deaf or blind, yes. Sometimes you can creep up on somoene with a silenced weapon and shoot him in the head from behind, and if there's an enemy right next to him, he won't notice AT ALL. HOWEVER, when the action heats up, the AI isn't as dumb... They can sneak up on you and take away 1/4 of your health or more before you'll know what's happened.

Destructible environments do have seams, but most of the time you won't see them or they'll be destroyed before you can get a chance to see them... and they get destroyed quite realisticly.

Explosions are awesome. Explosions from grenades, rpgs, barrels, etc. all look quite nice when you're trying to stay alive in the middle of a huge firefight... it's atmospheric.

My biggest complaint: the enemies all seem to wear amazing body armor! There are 4 types of enemies: standard, (about 5 or 6 hits... and they rarely appear after level 1... ) armored, (about 15 hits on the average) heavily armored (aka shotgunners... about 28 hits or more for them to die!) and there are 2 types of shielded enemies. And they all come in groups of 2-3 or more!

Well, there's a review for ya. Does anyone else play this game, and what are your opinions on it?
