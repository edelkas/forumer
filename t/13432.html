(probably not any more helpful, but just in case, slightly more about anti-aliasing:)

It applies to more than just text--shapes, lines, and complex drawings also, as well as when you resize a large picture down to a smaller one. Without anti-alias any lines that aren't straight will appear block/jaggy, so anti-alias smooths that out by adding extra pixels of intermediate shades.
