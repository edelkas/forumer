Hmm, standalone player...

You can make one yourself:


If you haven't already, download N, and make a shortcut to it somewhere. Go to shortcut properties, find the field with the shortcut patch in it, and add " -x" (x being a random letter) in it. Like this:
Quote
[your N directory]n_v14.exe -x
.
If it has quotes, leave it outside the quotes. Notice the space. Click OK.

Now, when you start N, the player opens, but N won't load. There you have a standalone player

-OR-

Just open the .swf in a browser.
