1) Bottomless levels. Well you've got a lot of tips of the hat to Mario what with the bounceblocks, thwomps and... erm... time limits . So why not have an option in the level format that makes the bottom border into a hole? You could have a cool falling sound effect and screen shake like in road runner when the player falls down.

2) Colour schemes for each level. Maybe just to have different coloured air and ground. That would really lend atmosphere to each level and make it unique.

3) Reaction times of some enemies specified in the room format. I mean like the speed and turning time of the rockets, accuracy of gauss turrets, speed of the floor sweepers, etc, could be specified in the room format, with a 'default' value used if none is specified (i.e. how it is right now), to allow backwards compatability with existing levels.
