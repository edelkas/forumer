i don't know, we don't have a master plan about this

it's different from all the other games, which is nice since we like to be different. plus it's kind of geeky.

plus, starting at 0 makes the numbers more meaningful: the left digit of the episode tells you which "set" it's in, and the right digit tells you which specific episode in that set. when you start with 1, the last episode has a different left digit -- blech!

and yes, when dealing with arrays i suppose it's simpler, but.. all you have to do is add "+1" so it's not THAT much simpler.
