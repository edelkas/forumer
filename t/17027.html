#314924 6 2007-04-22T07:23:34Z apg
Quote: thermoman, April 22, 2007 05:30 am
i beat 0-9, 10-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 60-69. and 70-79 what flavors should i have?
You don't unlock flavours by the number of the column you beat, but by the amount of columns you've beaten. There are no specific flavours for a certain column. For example, the second column you beat in line gives the same flavour everytime regardless of its number. If you want spoilers, the flavours are listed somewhere in the N Wikia, but we're not allowed to give that information in the forums.

Quote: thermoman, April 22, 2007 05:30 am
I have beeten some collums but i did not get a new flavor for one of them.
Did you have Practice Mode on, or did you cheat an episode?
Check the high scores board to see if there's time in every level in the column which you didn't get a flavour from. If there are levels without time, beat the column properly starting from those levels.

Did you 'lazy beat' a level?
'Lazy beating' means that you used more than 90 seconds to finish the level. The above solution should work for this one as well.

If nothing works, try rebeating the last episode in that column. It might just be a simple data-saving problem.
