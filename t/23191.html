#453094 2 2008-07-15T22:16:43Z Spawn of Yanni
You start off with 90 seconds at the beginning of an episode, and you have to use that to get through all 5 levels in that episode. You must've finished level 3 on that episode with only 1.824 seconds to spare, so that's how much you start the next level with. You're gonna have to restart the whole episode and try to take less time and collect as much gold as you can this time around.

Or try to get yourself to gold from where you spawn in that level within the next two seconds. But I doubt it's possible, I haven't checked where you spawn from.
