This thread is meant to be a discussion for a future thread. Similar to the Map Rating Thread, people would post maps and other people would run demos for those maps. The thing I noticed about the map rating thread is that, even though a rating is posted for each map, little feedback is included. Most maps on Numa don't get demos for them, even if the map is painfully easy for the veterans that visit here.

So my idea is a thread with rules for people to post maps and get demos for them. Here are my ideas for the rules (probably what most of the discussion here will be about):
1. Every map gets a demo.
2. Only three maps can be up at one time. The small number should help avoid confusion and losing maps in the thread.
3. To avoid death of the thread if three impossible-to-beat maps are up, there will be a time limit before they're eliminated. After one week with no demo, a map is taken off the list of three. (This takes precedence over rule one, obviously.)
4. Anyone can post maps. This won't be like the rating thread where you'd have to demo a map before you could post one. I'm sure the new players want demos for their maps too.

I was also thinking there should be a system that gives frequent demoers priority in the list of three over new posters. People who contribute a lot to the thread should be rewarded. The problem is, I don't know what kind of system would be good for this, or what would ever work. You tell me.

So, if people like this idea, let's finalize the details and make the thread official. Gimme your opinions.
