All right, so the point of this contest is to make a very hard map. There aren't a lot of restrictions, and the judging is fairly simple. So have some fun with it.

Rules

No mines. "But, but, but" But nothing, no mines.
No squeezes. Don't force the ninja through less than ninja-sized spaces.
Make it an action map. I want action maps, not puzzles. Don't submit your pixel perfect jumper here.
Beatable in under 30 seconds. Difficulty, not tedium. Also, 15 seconds of absolute hell can be just as good as 30.

Scoring
0-10 points for fun
0-10 points for difficulty

Deadline - July 1st

To submit a map, simply post a link to your map on NUMA in this thread. The map must have been created between June 14th and July 1st. The map must also contain an all gold demo that is under 1200 frames. The all gold demo does not have to be by the author, it's just proof about the length.

Submissions

I'll drop these in after the deadline.

Judges

-LouDog004
-cyberjuda
-9_4_0_0_
-spect
If you would like to judge in this competition, please pm me. Please don't volunteer to judge unless you're confident you will be able to beat the maps that will be submitted.

Also, judges, if you want to participate, that'll fly right by me. We'll just figure out a way to judge your map without you.
