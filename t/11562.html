Ok. Firstly, thanks to LOD for making the N Tile Art Maker which makes this game possible.

Here's the way this works people: I use the N Tile Art Maker to generate tiles from famous album covers. I post them up as images here (yeah, I could give you the tile data, but it's easier to play if it's all infront of you, the image filesize is tiny anyhoo) and whoever guesses a cover correctly gets a point (obviously this only applies to the first person that states the correct cover, silly).

I'll start by putting up three cover tilesets, and I'll add more every now and again. I'm also noting next to each image the darkness I used in N Tile Art Maker for it, which you may or not find useful.
