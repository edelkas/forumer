Percentages? We're not machines, heh.

Most of the time, it's pretty clear at who should be a mod and who shouldn't. Oftentimes, there are certain members who just stand out so much that they almost force you to pick them as a mod. Folk in this vein are those like LV and maximo, whose contribution to the community was just so massive at the time that the choice was obvious.

But there are times when the decision is less clear, where there are many fantastic members of a given community to choose from. At that point, a multitude of factors come into play; Everything from availability to age to moderating experience all influence our decisions.
