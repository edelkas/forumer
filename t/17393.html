Dronies Nominations 2007 are CLOSED!

Dronies nominations for December 1, 2006 - November 30, 2007 are now open! All we need is your NUMA name (If you don't have a NUMA name for some reason, your username here is fine), and your list of Nominations - It would be preferable if you nominated something for each category. You most certainly don't have to - but the more nominations we get for each of these, the better the results will be at the end of the year. Results will be tallied through raw data, and will be reviewed by the Legacy Team for fraudulent entries. Now, we have a couple changes this season, so I'd like to take a moment to explain those -

N Highscorer of the Year - This has replaced N Player of the Year, since people got confused last year and were simply nominating their favourite person. For this, you should nominate the name of the person.

Comic of the Year - We're deciding to revive this one, but there will be one stipulation - You may not vote for The Real N. The Real N is pretty much a Legacy-run comic now, and from the number of pseudo-votes we've had for it in my casual discussion, I think it would be unfair to the other comic makers to include it. Any other comic is up for nomination.

N Community Recognition Award - This is a new award category for this year, and for all years to come. This is the community's equivalent of the Mare and Raigan Award for Contribution to the Community. It allows you guys to pay respects to the deserved of the community. Nominate here just like you would any other category - For a person in the community.

With those out of the way, Nominations are open, and please, discuss as much as you wish about them in this thread. I would like for everyone to encourage nominations throughout the second half of this year, and not do things last-minute.

Categories

Action Map of the Year
DDA of the Year
Fan Programme of the Year
Map Maker of the Year
Map Series of the Year
Most Innovative Use of Objects or Tiles
N Art of the Year
N Highscorer of the Year
Comic of the Year
Puzzle Map of the Year
Race Map of the Year
Run of the Year
Survival Map of the Year
Tileset of the Year
N Community Recognition Award

And with that in mind, Vote Away!
