So I was on a long car ride this weekend, listening to some of the tunes on my MP3 player, and I thought that mapmakers are kind of like musical artists. Don't ask me how, I couldn't tell you. So that got me thinking, "Musicians have concerts. Mapmakers don't really have much that's remotely 'live'. Can that be changed?"

I thought so, and I remembered the IRC. I don't use the IRC myself, but I know it's pretty big in this community because people can communicate instantly. So then I had this idea. What if some mapmakers got on the IRC and put on a "concert", I suppose, for the others gathered on the channel? They would make maps for the crowd, providing the night's entertainment for the rest of the IRC-goers. These would be brand new maps, prepared possibly in advance, but maybe not - who knows?

So basically, a few mapmakers organize a time, inform the rest of the community, and presto! We have a somewhat novel way of releasing maps to the public. I'm not sure this is very different from what might go on on the IRC already. As I said, I don't use it. This is just some idea I had on a boring car ride, and I thought I'd share. What do you think?
