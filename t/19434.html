Hey all

It's time for me to stop playing N game, to stop actively highscoring. I've reassessed my priorities, and N has simply taken up too much time of my life. I'm approaching my final year of school, and I simply do not have the time to continue playing. Nor do I really want to.

N used to be fun, used to be something I could fall back on to for enjoyment. Now, it's become more of a chore, playing just to maintain my top position or to achieve 'goals' of 200 0ths, 71100 seconds total time, all highscores in top 10, blah blah. And for what? To impress random people on the internet whom I have never met?

I'll not visit the forums anymore either, it will make it difficult for me to abstain from playing, but I might pop in once in a while. It'll be interesting to see how many scores I will lose over time

I've been playing N since sometime last June, about 450 days ago, and I can honestly say, I've easily spent over 500 hours on this game. That's about 21 days out of the 450, spent purely on N. Looking back now, it seems such a waste of life.

Perhaps I'll come back for a while when v1.5 comes out. But for now, goodbye
