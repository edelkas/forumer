What you are about to hear is a collection of ideals. I do not think that these ideals will ever realistically happen within human society. I do think that small incremental steps in this direction are possible. These are based upon my personal values, and so I would think it natural that people disagree with this to a certain extent. I'm not trying to convert you, or whatever. I think this would be an improvement, overall.

Laws
My idea for a perfect society is based upon the golden rule, "do unto others as you would have them do unto you." Everyone follows this rule. There are, however, no laws aside from this. There is one time this rule can be broken. See "Lawbreakers." Society will recognize that in order to maintain general happiness and order, no one should steal, no one should murder, no one should rape, no one should commit arson, etc. People recognize that others appreciate respect, and that respect begets respect. Respect for the possessions of others is essential. Property exists, and although much is shared throughout this society, there are some things that should not be shared. People are seen as mature enough to handle these issues, and to sort them out on their own. Compromise is extremely important when settling ethical disputes. If people disagree, and begin to violate the golden rule, they will be treated as lawbreakers.

Economy
Money has little value aside from little pieces of metal. Instead of exchange of money for useful services or objects, useful services and objects are traded for each other. Every job in society contributes its specific desirable object or service to society, while receiving objects and services in proportional amounts to those they contribute. Therefore, the people who have worked the hardest will have the most material possessions and services at their disposal. It is seen as their duty in society to disperse these resources to people who aren't able to provide for themselves, but the materially rich person has some degree of discretion, i.e. they can choose from a list of acceptable people.

Lawbreakers
These are the people who think it's a good idea to steal, rape, murder, and burn. Etc. They are discourteous. They mess with other people's stuff, basically, and in a severe way in which they do not want to be treated. Society does not contribute anything to these people's lives. These people are basically cut off from society, until they start to contribute. If they don't start to contribute after a predetermined amount of time, they are met with resistance. This society would be set in some distant futuristic age, and so really, people don't need to perform law enforcement, or anything. Robots just take these people, and drop them off on a jail planet. A jail planet is an inhabitable planet with no technology, no infrastructure, etc. Basically, society just leaves them there, and monitors their technological level.

Government and stuff
Everyone rules and has absolute power over one person. Their own self. That's it. No dominion. This society is designed to produce absolute freedom, as long as the people in question don't mess with the rights of other people. People are free to do as they choose until those freedoms clash.

Pro stuff
This system allows freedom. Freedom and equality, plus it operates under the assumption that everyone is mature, rational, and loves each other.

Con stuff
For this to work, everyone has to be mature, rational, and love each other.

Did I forget to cover anything?

I think that this would be a step up, but probably not perfect. What the hell is perfect anyways? Meh. Discuss.
