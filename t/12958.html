#237052 12 2006-10-04T03:42:30Z Kablizzy
What I did with Vehemence (Plug) is while I was creating the levels, I would watch TV, and I would pick out a word or two from whatever program I was watching.

For instance, the Colbert Report's "The Word" just had "Stephen Thinks it's a documentary" on the screen.

He just said "Science and Faith," "In Theory," "Separating Fact From Policy," and "11-Dimensional Multiverse."

All of which are decent names. Beyond that, think indie. Vehemence stuff like "Versa" (Named after Vice Versa, without the first part) makes for a good name. Also, a play on words such as "Logical Phallus-y." has always done well for me in the past.

Another good way to take a name is to pick out map features, and name it like that. Another of my Vehemence maps is called "Double Scepter" because it looks like a scepter, with two heads.

Finally, things that make fun of map names, such as "This is not a map name." make for fantastic names.

Hope that helps.
