I'm tired of all these half assed games floating around, so I decided I would try to organize a game of Diplomacy for the forum.

You'll want to read the complete rules if you're going to play, but they're long, so I'll give you a quick synopsis of everything first so you can decide if this sounds interesting before you devote a couple of hours to reading the rules.

1. This game is like risk, except nothing is based on chance.
2. The object is to take over every country on the map.
3. The entire game revolves around negotiating with other players. You will be given considerable time to discuss strategy and make deals with the other players. However, no deal is binding, and up until each player's orders are submitted, nothing is final.

4. Once the orders for a turn are all in, all moves are resolved simultaneously. The game board will be updated and then the next round of negotiation can begin.

If you're interested sign up here and begin to read the full rules so everything makes more sense. After you've read the rules, feel free to ask any questions you have.
