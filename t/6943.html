#134269 2 2006-01-21T08:49:40Z it0hg2
Press "S" to save the map to your clipboard, then paste that into your userlevels.txt file, making sure to follow the guidelines for naming your map and such outlined at the head of the file. Save the file, and you should then be able to go to the main menu, select "user levels" and choose your map from that list.

Hope I helped, and good luck.
#139198 3 2006-02-04T00:27:36Z Ravenclaw
When you finish making your level, save the top box code in a text document or something, then, when you want to get it back to play or edit, copy it and then paste it into the top box. Click out of the box and press L. The level should have loaded. You can mess around with it from there. I think that should help.
