#20085 1 2008-04-26T21:06:27Z PsychoSnail
I tried searching for this before, but didn't come up with a thread similar to this.

Right now on NUMA, you can only see the rating as an approximation, in the form of an image showing whether the map has a rating of 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2,...,5. As we all know, though, most maps don't have ratings that are an exact multiple of 0.5. As a result of this, what we see as the rating can sometimes be quite a bit off what the real rating of the map is.

It would be nice if next to the image with the ninjas showing the map's rating, we could see the exact rating, or at least a more precise number, such as 4.235, 3.495. Of course, ratings aren't everything, but being able to see a more precise rating would give a better impression of how good a map is, and it would be interesting to see just how highly rated the top-rated maps are, as opposed to seeing a whole bunch of 4.5s.

What do you think?

Edit: Actually, I just found a thread about this same suggestion. Here it is: http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=14063
