#471665 5 2008-09-19T19:06:39Z Jean-Luc
Yeah, I'd recommend VideoLAN VLC. It takes a bit of learning to use to it's full potential (which is WAY beyond the simple media player that most people us it as), but it's capable of exactly what you want. Additionally, it's web interface (or Telnet interface, if you're feeling nostalgic) will let you control it from all over the network.

http://www.videolan.org/

Another option for music control over the network is WinAmp + WWWinAmp. WinAmp is a popular media player, and wwWinAmp lets you browse the library and queue up songs over the network (Note: WWWinAmp is no longer under development, but it still works fine).

http://www.winamp.com/
http://www.nullsoft.com/free/wwwinamp/
