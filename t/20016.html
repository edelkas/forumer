I realize that this is an old post, but I felt it needed a response.
Quote: mattk210, November 03, 2007 06:34 pm
I would have to say illustrator is far superior in terms of vector drawing. For my flash game I import designs from illustrator (which works really well with flash cs3)
I don't agree. Flash can do everything Illustrator can do and then some.
At least, the version of Illustrator I tried couldn't break lines apart where they intersect; it could only edit the vector handles. Breaking apart lines in this way allows you to make better drawings than you could otherwise faster than you would otherwise be able to make them.

Essentially, if I was using both Flash and Illustrator, I would make my vector drawings in Flash and import them to Illustrator. Of course, I generally use Fireworks and not Illustrator.



Regarding this thread's main topic, I do make animations and games in flash, but I haven't completed anything recently.
(In other words, I made things like my avatar a while ago and I'm currently working on something big.)
