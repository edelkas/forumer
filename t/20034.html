Lately I have felt as though I have been at a loss for map quality.
I don't have an incredibly large amount of time to spend on N (life exists outside the spectrum of flash code), so my maps have, let's say, suffered.
After reading through the forums for a while, and looking at APG's ambience (awesome maps, I endorse all of them), I decided to make a map pack. This one includes 5 maps, just like the standard N episodes, all based on repeating patterns of various shapes. They should scale in difficulty, but all are relatively easy. I have made it a credo to never submit a map I can't beat, and so the difficulty of my maps suffers (if you catch my drift). Still, that shouldn't take away from the fun.

My questions are thus:
1. Are the maps arranged so that they scale from least to most difficult?
2. Does the level difficulty seem to scale inside the levels themselves?
3. Does it seem repetetive?

In essence, I am trying to get some feedback before the maps are submitted to NUMA.
I've kicked myself too much in the behind for submitting maps that aren't ready.

Enjoy.
