#168448 5 2006-04-26T00:51:32Z whaleshow527
Boy, I love this level! Watch my HS run!

For this third part, I suggest jumping into the tunnel asap after leaving the thwumps at the far left. Once in, it definitely helps to right-jump along every upward-sloping 4 curve, as TCG described. The first of the two bends is pretty easy to pass through, but it's important to curve upward and walljump off the right wall quickly to save time later. As long as you don't make any major slip-ups from here, you can probably clear the second bend and finish off the section *relatively* easily.
