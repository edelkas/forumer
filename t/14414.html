#271835 5 2006-12-17T04:16:45Z scythe33
Don't bother with highscores on this level. They're pretty crazy.
Also, maps arent of much help either. A few things to keep in mind are:
* Go quickly. The drones aren't as fast as you
* Move diagonally. It keeps them from seeing you.
* Go up the left wall. Well, it worked for me
* Get down quick (but don't run into anything)
* the "triple jump thingy": When between a wall and one of the bounceblocks, tap jump three times fast. It's a good way of getting up, and should keep you out of the way of mines and drones.
* Don't piss yourself off: it sucks! and your playing decreases too. If you're getting angry, go do something else.
* Watch the mines-to me, it didn't take much to get a feel for the drones, but the mines killed me alot.

Oh, and I can't believe you people. "Watch the highscores" isn't very helpful
