#25767 2 2004-08-11T02:17:33Z LittleViking
The number in the corner isn't frames per second. It's milliseconds per frame. So when it says 50, you're getting (1000/50= ) 20 fps. When it's over 100, you get less than 10 fps. Unfortunately, everyone has the same problem. The best you can do is close as many programs in the background as you can (or upgrade your computer).
