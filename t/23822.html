The internet is full of abbreviations that we use all of the time, yet rarely say out loud to other people. Here's my list; add or remove anything if you want.

JPG - "jay-pegg"

GIF - "jiff" (it really should be "gif" with a hard g because the g stand for graphics.)

PNG - "p.n.g."

ZIP - "zip"

RAR - "rar"

ISO - "ice-oh"

AVI - "a.v.i."

DivX - "div-ex" (not "divicks", which is also pretty popular.)

EXE - "ex" (a lot of people say "exey", which surprised me. I say "ex", but think executable in my head.)

MPG - "em-peg"

TXT - "text"

DOC - "dawk"

WAV - "wave"

OS X - "oh-ess-ex" (nearly everyone else in the world says "oh-ess-ten")

WMA/WMV - "windows-media-audio/video" (I don't pronounce the abbreviation at all.)

Torrent - "you-torrent" (should be "mew-torrent" I guess.)

nVidia - "en-vid-ee-ah"

DMG - "damage"


There are probably more, but I can't think of them at the moment.
