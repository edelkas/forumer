#386429 17 2007-12-05T02:31:15Z Hendor
Starting a level or episode with less time would make no difference at all to highscorers. If you check the boards, there are very, very few episodes that end with scores under 200 seconds. If you were to start with 45 or 60 seconds left as people have been saying, it still wouldn't be any harder than it was originally.

What would be a good idea is to make an option to allow time to pass faster. You could set time to pass x times faster. That way, even if you got the total gold count up to 300 seconds after the first level of an episode, it would take far less time to come down. You could set some of the harder episodes to just 1.5x or 2x, while some of the really easy episodes to 3, 4 or even 5x. You could even have competitions for each episode to see who could beat it at the highest time factor (This isn't the same as the kind of episode scoring we have now because if time was passing 10x faster than normal, it probably wouldn't make sense to go into a room for 5 pieces of gold.)
