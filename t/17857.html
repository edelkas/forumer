Guys, he said he doesn't have practice mode on.

The gold you collect does add to your remaining time for the episode, but if you still don't beat each level in the episode in a fixed and invisible 90 second limit, then it isn't recorded as a personal best. So, you could take more than 90 seconds to beat a level but still have time left on the clock, because they're seperate.

I hope I explained that well enough...

ksg: Go to the Highscores menu and select the first episode in the first column, which would be 00-0. Look in the upper-left hand corner of the screen and you'll see the personal bests for each level in the episode. If you have a time recored for each level there, then select the next episode, check if there are personal bests, and rinse and repeat until you come across levels that do not have recorded times. You must redo the episodes that have missing times, and the levels that do not have personal bests within those episodes must be completed within 90 seconds individually; Not what's counting down at the top of your screen.

You might have 'lazy beat' some levels in those columns, meaning that you've used more than the default time of 90 seconds to complete them.

Go to the High Scores menu and look at the upper left corner. You'll see a box with your personal bests of all levels in the episode you've selected. Check every episode in those columns for levels with "--:--" as your personal bests. Such an unknown time means that you've used more than 90 seconds to complete the level, and you'll have to pass it again, but faster.

If you still have problems, post again.
