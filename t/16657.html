#308868 10 2007-03-30T18:14:18Z Kablizzy
Yeah, it's not a matter of "how much," it's a matter of each map's worth. You can bitesize as many maps as you want, but I think people's beef with you isn't the number of maps you're bitesizing, it's the quality of maps that you're bitesizing - Bitesizing should be what you consider to be the best of the best - Something wowing to you. If you're bitesizing that many maps, I'd be willing to put money on the thought that a majority of them aren't "wowing" you. This is the difference between Favourites and Bitesize. Bitesize maps should be the absolute best of the best of the best of the best of the best - Favourites should be ones that you really like and below. If 12 maps that week wow you, then by all means, do it up. If 40 maps do so, go for it.

Here's my criterion:

If there is a map that wows me SO much that I would give it MORE than a 5, that would be a bitesizable map for me.
