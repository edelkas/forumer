#59412 9 2005-04-10T11:42:48Z Lucidium
We do have this Maths Challenge thing. The prize for doing well in one round is that you get to do another one.

They were less straight maths questions, and more puzzles that you needed maths skills to solve. Things like:

If you start with this string:

"MI"

And you have these rules:

You can add "U" to any string that ends in "I"
You can change "Mx" to "Mxx" (you can double anything that comes after M)
You can change "III" to "U"
You can change "UU" to nothing.

Prove whether you can or cannot produce the string "MU"

Or something like that, anyway. As I remember, they were fairly open ended questions like that. Proving things about prime numbers, that kinda stuff. Not done one for years though, too much bother.

[edit] That puzzle above is one I read in a book recently. Not one I actually remember being on a maths challenge paper. But the puzzles on the papers were in that style, though usually a bit more complex.

#59442 14 2005-04-10T16:53:59Z Maniac
No, you can only double things after M. Doubling 2 doesn't give you 3!

EDIT:

Also, yeah for me, only here, we have a team of 5 people, plus however many alternates. We all answer 10 Level I or Level II individual questions, then we do team questions, but then, here's the interesting part:

Relays.

The 5 people sit in a column of desks. Each person gets one problem. The person sitting up front does his or her math problem, then passes back a number. The second person receives that number, called TNYWR(The Number You Will Receive), then does a harder problem with that number. Pass back to #3, who does a harder problem, and so on.

You're not allowed to talk, but if you suspect your TNYWR is incorrect, you can kick the chair of the person behind you.

Once finished with everything, the points from all 3 events are totalled up, and the highest point total wins!

My team won last season. *hint hint to researchers*
