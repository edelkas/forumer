If any of you played the original free Scudden Death, it was very pixelated. Had a super-vague resemblance to Liero in particle function. I haven't tried out SD2 yet, but it looks good from what I can tell

EDIT Ah, that's right... a description ya want, eh?

It's 2D. Very, very pixelated. Some vehicles are less than 10 pixels highin fact most are. Vehicles, aye! You control an army. It is 2D, side-scrolling RTS. In the original SD (as I don't want to make assumptions about SD2, which I haven't played yet), you switched from one unit to another in individual control. They ranged from Bomb Cars to Helicopters to digging vehicles (yep, all terrain is destructible). You can also control buildings: turrets, radar, etc.

So it's like Mortar Mayhem, or Scorched Earth, or any of those 2D randomly generated landscapes, but instead of turn-based action with one tank, you control a whole army in real-time. Against another person on the same comp., and it appears in SD2 that you can fight against bots as well. Perhaps it has multi.; I'll try it out soon.
