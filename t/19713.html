Which version?
Photoshop 8 and 9 have generally easy-to-learn interfaces, while I can't stand the interface of CS3.
It generally provides you with more than you'd ever need to start off with. Paintshop, on the other hand, has a Top Bar with two rows (that are only half-full by default), and two sidebars that are easy to become accustomed to. The labels of all of the bars are in plain terms that say directly what the function does, and not the "Industry Standard" jargon version of their function.

Generally, I believe it's more curved towards non-industrial image editing, though it has the capabilities of doing such.

Either way, both programs are powerful, powerful tools.
