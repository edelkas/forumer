I just had this crazy idea. What if you could make some tiles together that looked cool, and you might want to use that particular bit of tiles in a future map? Prefabs are the answer. Just select that bunch of tiles and press P to 'Save As Prefab.'

Now, it's the future. You want to use that prefab. Choose the tile editing, then press 9 to go to the prefab menu. Choose that prefab you want, and just place it somewhere.

Now, one of the crazy parts is that maybe prefabs could be coded somehow, and maybe a prefab section of NUMA could be made, so people can post the ones they've made. I already know one I have!

So, please consider this, and don't shoot it down right away.
