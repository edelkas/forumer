And what really sucks about it is that every single appliance in my house is Kenmore. Except the microwave.

Okay, so here's the story. Just under 6 months ago, we went to Sears and got a refrigerator and a washing machine/dryer to move into our new house. That's when we made the biggest mistake of our lives. Now, present day. Some time ago, when I opened the refrigerator (a side-by-side), there were icicles hanging down from the top. It was like 15F in there! And what next? The (manual) temperature controls are frozen in place. We called Sears repair center, and they sent a guy over. He replaced the water dispenser, God knows why. Nothing was fixed.
In the meantime, our washing machine malfunctioned. We have a Kenmore Oasis, btw. It got to the point where Start became the shutdown button and Power cycled through the wash settings. The main control board screwed up, and a repairman had to replace the entire control panel and the main computer. But guess what? They shipped the wrong part. So we have to wait a few more days for the part to arrive. Also, on the dryer, the computer pretty much screwed up everywhere. I hate the "estimated time remaining" and all of the extra sensors. When we do bedding, it constantly stays at 27 minutes for no particular reason. Anyway, they didn't bother to fix it because they didn't find anything wrong with it.
Back to the refrigerator. A second guy came, and he said that we have to clear out the refrigerator and turn it off overnight, so all the ice can melt. The wall that seperates the freezer from the refrigerator was a big chunk of ice, and prevented any cold air from the freezer to go into the refrigerator. So, the refrigerator stayed at a toasty 65F for a while. Oh, this has happened 2 times since then. Repairing costs $100 everytime.

Lessons learned -
  • Never buy Kenmore. A very unreliable brand.
  • If you buy Kenmore, buy at least 5 years of warranty
