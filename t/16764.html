Are you trying to do this inside a .swf, or just with HTML?

Edit: If you just want HTML, it should look like this:
<form method='POST' name='colorform' action='http://website.com/pageToGoTo.html'>

    <option value='0'>Please select your favorite color</option>
    <option value='1'>Blue</option>
    <option value='2'>Red</option>
    <option value='3'>Green</option>
  </select>
   
  <input type='submit' value='Go' />
</form>
