#357260 3 2007-08-19T22:46:09Z Keron Cyst
I have no idea how to run a blog aside from consistency of posts so people can expect new entries periodically and thus know how frequently to stop by. I myself couldn't think of a particular topic to blog about, so I just blog about whatever random banter comes my way.

And a major factorI think unless you are ambitiously striving for anything commercial or something huge with your workis that the blog should be treated as something you should not rely on for popularity. People rarely comment on blogs, so don't take lack of comments as discouragement, and think of it more as a public journal for yourself in which you consider any views as simply a bonus but not the main drive behind the thing.

('Least, that's how I treat mine, and it's kept it alive so far :Q)
