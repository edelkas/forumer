The Rules (Rambling Version)

Atilla tells you. You are no longer a mewling kitten and have learned to keep secrets from Atilla, and so Atilla tells you.

In the beginning, there shall be Nine, and each shall fashion from the aether a tileset, which shall be sent to Atilla via the arcane mysteries of the PM button. And after the first week, Atilla shall engrave these tilesets upon a text file of granite, and place them in the forum square that all may see. And the people shall judge the maps, voting for their favourite; and the map which is most loved by the people shall be great in Atilla's eyes. If an author should fail to craft a worthy map, and receive not a single vote, then they shall be cast from the sphere of Heaven and a new god shall take their place.

Atilla will then say unto the Nine, take this tileset which was made for you, and create from it a level worthy of the vault of Heaven. And they shall each craft a fine level, and once more present it to Atilla. Again, Atilla shall show the levels to the people, saying "Which among these is the greatest?" And that which is deemed most worthy shall be taken, and the others cast aside, and any of the Nine who are unworthy shall again be banished, and another shall take their place.

Once more Atilla will speak unto the Nine, saying: "Take this, all of you, and playtest it. This is the map of your colleagues, which has been given to you." And the Nine shall take the map, and shall make small modifications. Once more Atilla shall place their maps before the people and ask which is greatest; and after their judgment, shall we know the One, True Map.

There are no turtles anywhere.
...it's so depressing.

The Rules (Clear Version)

Stage 1
I shall take the first nine authors to post here saying they're in (telling me on IRC works, too). Others may be taken later, so post if you want to participate even if more than 9 people are already in.
They shall each make a tileset, and PM it to me. Text, pastebin or NUPB is fine.
I shall put the tilesets into a single text file and post a voting thread.
Anyone who is not currently one of the Nine will vote on their favourite by means of a poll.
The map with the most votes will be used as the basis for the next stage.
If a map doesn't get a single vote, the author will drop out, making room for the next on the list.

Stage 2
Each author shall create a level based on the tileset which was chosen. Small modifications to the tiles are allowed - just treat it as if you were using a tileset from NUMA.
The authors shall PM their levels to me, and I shall post them for voting, just like the last stage.
The map with the most votes shall be used as the basis for the next stage.
If any author fails to get at least one vote, they will drop out and another will be drafted.

Stage 3
Each author shall play the level from the last stage. They shall then make some small alterations to the map - tiles, objects, whatever, as long as it's still more or less the same map.
Each map shall again be judged against the others - and this time, against the original, too.
The most popular will be declared the One Map, and shall be posted and advertised blatantly by me, bringing its contributors worship and praise.

Miscellaneous Stuff (Yes, this is important!)

Since we can't really rank people, there won't be any prizes - other than the huge bragging rights afforded to those who have their maps chosen. Hopefully, the end result will be so spectacularly glorious that merely being associated with it will be enough to attract hordes of screaming fans.

I encourage as many people as possible to participate, whether by voting or signing up as a potential mapmaker. It doesn't require a huge investment of time - just make a map and submit it once a week, or play a bunch of maps and vote for your favourite. Even if you're not in the first nine, you can still get drafted later on, and remember too that if you don't vote for your beloved, they may get kicked out! Also, if this goes well, I might run another one, so if you're interested make sure I know it!

At this point, I intend to give the Nine a week to make a map in each stage. If you don't meet the deadline, you taste the end of my boot and the next person on the list takes your place. I will then give people some time to vote - I'll set the timeframe for this later, depending on how fast votes are coming in on the first round, but it won't be longer than a week. In total, this competition should run no longer than 6 weeks.
