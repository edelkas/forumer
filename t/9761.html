This is my idea for a competition. Its simple. You take any tileset from any level, and replace the tiles. Where there are tiles, you take the tiles out. Where there are no tiles, you put tiles in. This is an example of a 'tile exchange', and its an inverted version of the first windows logo, which was submitted but i cant find it now.

Rules:
3 Submissions fer person.
No old submissions; must be new.
In the level you submit, you must put a link to the original level (the normal one, that you, the contestant, exchanged tilesets from.)
Judges are allowed to submit submissions for the contest.
Submissions not accepted after June 17, 2006.
Submissions MUST be submitted to numa.
Submissions can be submitted for any game type.

Judges:
Me-I will be the only judge to be also judging other judges submissions.
I need 2 more judges, so post if you'd like to be one.
Score:
The total score will be out of 30.
15 For thumbnail on numa.
15 for full view on numa.

Enjoy, have fun, and good luck.

If you don't understand something about the competition, post the question ,and i'll be only too glad to answer it.
