I'm finally summoning up the courage to submit this pack.

It contains 100 maps. 75 of them form the main pack, the Gallery. It's a diverse range - a few KRA maps, a smattering of gold-collection challenges, a good helping of adventure maps, and even a promotional exit jumper. Some will be easy, some will be hard.

It's never going to be perfect, and some of the maps are probably awful by your standards. So I want you to play through it, enjoy the successful maps, and rip in to the bad maps. List the maps you despise, and why. Don't hold back on your criticism. Do your best to make me cry.

The remaining 25 maps are unreleased maps, real oddities - the Hall of Freaks. You're not supposed to seriously play these maps - they're just for curiousity. The Hall includes a lost Catbert map (if you don't know what I mean by that, so much the better), a few tilesets (feel free to use them, by the way), one or two companion maps or prototypes of a few Gallery levels, and two prisoner maps that didn't quite work out. And there's madder where they came from.

My intention is to release a Version 2 of this pack. The maps voted bad by popular opinion will be improved or replaced, and a few new maps may be introduced to both parts of the pack. I might even do an artist commentary for the pack if you want (I had been doing one for this version, but gave up - there's nothing much to say about many of them).

So try to enjoy the maps, and please tell me where I'm going horribly wrong. Have fun!

#414359 2 2008-03-06T14:15:04Z gloomp
I have no idea why, but I've taken it upon myself to review each and every one of these:


There's no law against putting pennies in my ears, SUPERSTOOP!

If the gold here was any less interesting, this would be a failure. As is, it's a good map. Because you go for the gold, that gives the drones time to fall into their pattern.

Knights of Cydonia

Starts off nice, but after you get the switch, it all goes to Hell in a handbasket. Either I was following the totally wrong path, or the flow really was that stupid. I kept not collecting gold, redoing jumps, and never actually feeling like I was in a race. Plus, the seekers don't sense you.

Three Laps

So, you need to go around three times, right? Really, that's all? Sounds fun! Huh, wuzzat? It's a drone race?! That's stupid!!!

Temple to Frustraton

Ignoring the typo, the name's fairly accurate. The biggest problem is how tacked on the bottom is; it feels totally different than the rest of the map. The best part is definitely the gold design, so I'm gonna have to steal it.

Gigachip

This would be a lot better if you removed the outside thwumps, closed off all the side openings, and added an entrance at the top. THEN it would be fun.

Ticker Tape

Now this is fun. Everything works together wonderfully, as though engaged in some fantastical dance.

Potatoheart

I have mixed feelings about this one. I liked how you used one-ways and I liked the cache of gold at the top, but I didn't like the start. Overall, it's decent, but no classic.
