Is that a good thing? See, 1024x768 (assuming that's what you meant, or even if it was 760) is a ratio of 1.3 (width divided by height), whereas 1280x1024 is a ratio of 1.25--the appropriate 1.3 resolution is 1280x960. Most CRT monitors have an aspect ratio of 1.3, whereas most (or many) LCD monitors have a ratio of 1.25. The point is, those two resolutions have different aspect ratios, and so assuming you're using the same monitor one of them is going to look somewhat compressed and off.
