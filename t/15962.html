http://www.ufoot.org/liquidwar/v5

The graphics are simple and so are the controls, but it's still kind of interesting. You control a mass of pixels, which aren't very intelligent and try to rush towards your cursor via the most direct route. You enemies likewise control a bunch of pixels. Your objective is to capture all the enemy pixels by making yours rush them. This is slightly more strategic than it sounds - if you both just charge each other head on, it results in a perfectly even shoving match. Victory must instead be assured by flanking or surrounding a section of the opposing forces. LW supports multiplayer (on one computer or via network/internet), AI bots (though they aren't very good) and also custom maps.
