#385566 8 2007-12-03T01:26:15Z Pheidippides
I think Atilla really puts this question in perspective. The only possible answer is that style is not exclusive, and while krusch, NIGHTMARE, yahoozy, atob, and others have very distinctive styles, the styles are not specifically theirs. Still, you should consider the disadvantages to "trying on" another author's style, such as the fact that most often you can't pull it off as well as they can. Plagiarism should never be a question if you're simply incorporating another author's style into your own maps because, as everyone's said, they're still your maps. Unoriginality? Maybe, if utilizing another author's style becomes a highly recurring theme in someone's maps. But never plagiarism.

Look at maps by atob, yahoozy, and other well-known authors. There are recurring elements in their maps. These recurring elements, aside from being part of what makes their maps so well-liked, define their style. Basically, an author's style is the collection of all their mapmaking habits.

Edit: And basically, to answer your other question, this is a one-sided debate, because when you think about it there's no way style can be as exclusive as max_ride is implying that it is. A style defines an author, so using another author's style excessively can be seen as trying to be that author. This does make style a bit exclusive, but max_ride is really making a point that trying on different styles every once in a while isn't against any rule.
