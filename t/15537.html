Well, i'd say take your time (there's gold aplenty), and practice walljumping. You don't have to be sliding on a wall to be able to jump off of it, just close enough for the ninja to reach-this is a common misconception. Another thing is that you don't simply hold the button towards the wall and press the jump button randomly. I'm guessing you have trouble going up and down, so with a little practice on that, this level should be cake.
Also, don't be so cautious around drones. Be bold and make some moves that seem idiotic, it's the only way to learn.
Plus, if you stop holding a directional key while you're in the air, he'll keep going in that direction without accelerating. If you're holding it, that could be your problem.
