#360227 7 2007-08-28T04:23:20Z Atilla
Show, rather than tell. Describe the scene and the people in it. For example, rather than just saying someone was impatient, you could refer to an action which expresses impatience, such as sighing or rolling your eyes.

"Also," said Atilla, "It's not necessary to specify that I am speaking twice when you split my dialogue like this."

"You should also note that you don't need to specify the speaker every time in a conversation between two people." Atilla looked speculative for a moment. "You can also refer to other actions or gestures to indicate who is speaking. This helps the reader imagine a conversation because that's how people speak in real life. It can also be used to show us how someone feels or what type of person they are. A politician might make grand, sweeping gestures as she speaks, while someone who is shy might run his hands through his hair and look away from his audience."
