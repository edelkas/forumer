Map Contest - Stealth Series

After seeing the maps that are called "Stealth Fighter" maps, I decided
to make a contest based on such. You should create a map in which you
must avoid being detected by seeker drones and reaching the exit.

Rules
Map must be a stealth map, a map in which you use "shadows" to hide
from seeking drones. Being spotted by a seeking drone causes you to
fail the map, along with dying or not reaching the door in time.

Map must be possible (following the above rule)

Map can use any object and tile.

Map must be cool, and origional.

Map must have some sort of mission briefing as the description.

And finally, map must be posted in NUMA.

Scoring
Scoring is out of 100, and Judges should take into consideration for
scoring on maps the following:

Toughness - Not too easy, but not impossible.
Neatness - Obiviously, spamming objects and random tiles = Failure.
Mission Briefing - Should be a good idea.
Origionality - Should not be based on another map's placement
of objects and tiles. It should be your own work.

Judges (3)
RedKlonoa
