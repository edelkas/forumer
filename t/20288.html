Keeping your teeth clean is quite important. So, you must brush your teeth.

Before we get started, make sure you've got the appropriate equipment:
* Toothbrush
* Toothpaste

Get your toothbrush and squeeze some toothpaste onto it. The ideal amount varies depending on the size of the brush and the strength of the toothpaste.

You can wet the toothbrush before and/or after this process for enhanced effect.

Then, put the toothbrush inside your mouth. Try to make sure your mouth is open wide enough so that you don't accidentally get toothpaste all over your face.

This next step varies, depending on personal choice and whether you're cool and have an electric toothbrush. The basic process of moving the brush around your mouth always applies. If you don't have an electric toothbrush you'll need to manually oscillate the brush at the same time.

Once you feel that you have properly cleaned every part of your mouth, stop brushing and and remove the brush from your mouth.

Now, just spit into a sink and et cetera and you're done!
