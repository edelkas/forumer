The basic idea is to scour the seven seas (and four oceans.)

You get to be pirates! Yaaaay!
You gotta talk tough! Like, "you scallywags!"
And you get to go to places and attack people!

I am your captain, One-Eyed One-Legged Zacko! But you can just call me Captain Zacko. I will share the treasure we find with all of you at a reasonable amount. We use doubloons as money. When we land somewhere, you might want to spend your doubloons to get better stuff for your pirate, like swords, cool hats, and smoke bombs! There will also be a Ship Shop so you can buy some stuff there.
When you buy something from the Ship Shop, your money goes into the Ship Bank, and we store our doubloons there. You must ask me permission before getting money from the Ship Bank. If you die, you lose all your doubloons, but then reappear on the Ship and have to wait until we come back. You can then ask me to take money out of the bank. Understand? Good.

You can choose a nickname for your pirate, and it can be funny, serious, or anything.

Use your money wisely!

NO POSITIONS. PM me if you want to join and I might make another position.

WARNING! If you board this ship, you will obey me ALWAYS! Or you'll walk the plank! But you can give me suggestions on what to do! We will face enemy ships and almost complete and utter death! Now, the abilities of the crew:

Captain (Me): Gives orders, professional swordsman. Steers the ship.
Assistant Captain: Advises me, gives some orders, also professional swordsman.
Crow's Nest Spotters: They yell LAND HO! When they see land. I may reward them for finding good land or something. They can also observe things better than most people, like a weak spot in a wall or a hidden lever. They get pistols.
Jail Guards: Make sure captives don't escape the brig. They get paid a little bit more for guarding. They can use axes, scythes, and stuff like that.
Crew People: Basically keep the ship in shape. Clean, activate sails, fix leaks. Moderate sword skills. The more you fight, the better you get at swordplay. I'll tell you when you get better. Also, activate cannons when an enemy ship is attacking and lower life boats (Don't worry, No one is getting left behind!)
Dudes with Explosives: They can blow up weak walls with grenades and stuff. They have grenades and dynamite, but can purchase more explosives. You will have a limited number of explosives when you buy them from a store.
Really Strong People: Self-Explanatory. They can take down like a bunch of people with their bare hands, and their skin is more resistant to swords and daggers.

My Ship Shop is in the next post! I will add new stuff as we steal from different places! You can sell things at half the price you bought it at different stores we visit.
When you visit other stores, the items you buy have weight. Too much weight and our ship will sink. At the start, we have a ship that can hold 1000kg. We can purchase new ships throughout the game, if we combine our money. The new ships may have a higher weight limit and be more impenetrable to cannons and stuff. (Thanks to spiker 5 or should I say Eel Skin George for the suggestion about the weight and the ships.)

We all start out with 50 doubloons, an average pirate sword, and cool hats. Oh yeah, clothes too. Don't wanna forget those. I also have a compass and a map, and the Dudes with Explosives have unlimited dynamite and grenades. Crow's Nest Spotters have pistols with unlimited ammo, and can buy different bullets that are limited in ammo.

You MUST ask me permission before doing stuff like explosives or searching the area. We will all enter a shop at the same, 'cept for the ship shop.

Only one rule: HAVE FUN OR WALK THE PLANK.

SUPER EDIT:

Levels!
Thanks to Pierce West (Trogdor007) For coming up with this idea entirely. I had nothing to do with it!

there are six fighting categories: Brawling (Fist fighting, Martial Arts, Wrestling), Shooting, Swords, Demolitions (Explosives), and Specialty (Whips and Whatever).

At the start:
Strong Guys get +5 for Brawling.
Crew Guys get +4 for their choice of Shooting or Specialty, and +1 for Swords.
Exploders get +5 for Demolitions.
Jail Guards get +3 for Brawling and +2 for specialty.
Crows Nest get +5 for shooting.
1st Mate gets +5 for sword.
Cook gets +5 for specialty (Pots, Pans, Barrels!)

Each player gets a discount for weapons and items that apply to his or her category. I would think 50% would suffice.

Every week on Friday, the players get to choose one category to Level Up in. The amount of points they go up is directly related to how much work they did in that area. If none was done, they will get 0. If it was a category they had no experience in, then they can go to members of the party who do have experience in say... Demolitions and train with them. This will result in +1 for each member involved.

Special Abilities (Also thought up by Pierce West!)

Each Player either gets +15, +10 (if you're a cook, look out, or medic), or +5 if you're a strong guy (In addition to whatever is automatic to their "type.") to choose where it goes.

Suggested Skills:
Climbing (+2 for Crow's Nest)
Cooking (+10 for cook)
Ropework
Repair
Medicine (+5 for medic)
Sealegs (Without at least 1, may vomit and fall over)
Strength (+ 10 for Strong Guy)
Speed (+3 for Crow's Nest)

Figure it out yourself. Keep all your stats and items in a text file so you don't forget anything!
