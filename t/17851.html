I'm in love with this show. It's beautiful.

The entire show is based around the ancient art of bending, referring to bending an element (Fire, Water, Earth, and Air). Each art of bending is living in a different part of the world. The Air Tribe is known as the peace keepers, living in the high mountains, as monks. The Water Tribe lives on the North and South poles of the world. The Earth kingdom is in the mainland, along with the Fire empire. There is always an Avatar, who is born following the death of the prior Avatar. The Avatar is in control of all four, but must learn them all. The Fire tribe, following the death of the last Avatar, invaded the Air Tribe, and is thought to have killed them all. The Air Tribe was said to be the next to have an Avatar, but he (Aang) got away, and froze in a block of ice.

That's just a bit of this show. Each "bending" style is actually based on a form of martial arts, making the motions realistic.

I was watching a marathon today (actually, in the process of watching it now), and saw something interesting:
Fire can create fire and lightning from themselves.
Water can move water when it's already present, but can freeze it in any weather (ICE SPEAR!), and can move plants.
Earth is just "Gragh! Rock in face!" and pretty much any rock moving. Which is friggin' great.
Air is air. Yeah.

So what's up with this clear advantage in Fire and Water? Earth and Air are just those. If I'm wrong, please tell me. I'm trying to figure this all out.

I've watched a bit of the show, but from what I've seen, no type of bending has an advantage over everything.

Air and Earth seem more defensive than offensive, as a giant wall of rock will stop most things, and Aang seems to be able to block most attacks with a combination of his staff and air bending. Earth and Air are both capable of being powerful offensive weapons, but more often than not a good water of fire bender could keep them on the defensive.
