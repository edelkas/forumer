hey,

First of all, we're absolutely flattered by the idea behind N: Legacy. That you guys love something we made this much is inspiring. The name is cool too.

After _much_ deliberation, we're going to have to say that we can't
make a special custom version of N. here's our reasoning on the topic:

we learned when adding user levels to N1.4 that it's very difficult
to please most people and be as fair and unbiased as possible. By far
the biggest complaints referenced either our having not included
certain maps or authors, or our having included too many maps by
one author.

In light of that, we feel that by releasing a special edition of N
devoted to only a specific group's levels (regardless of the fact
that you guys are very talented level makers) we would be:

1. showing favouritism

2. opening the doors for tons of similar requests -- i.e everyone
will want us to make a special version of N with just their levels.

We think it's a _very_ good idea, and we hope you do decide to collect a
group of levels and release them together (for now, you could use the "type" field to get them in a particular order).

v1.5 will hopefully have a better userlevels system, which would allow
for multiple "userlevel.txt"-like files at once (we'll consider the possibility of using the user levels to replace the built-in levels), and/or episodes or
something similar.

I hope you guys understand the reasoning behind this decision -- we
want to support you, but we don't think we can do this specifically.
Please let us know if there's another way we can help though!

It makes us really excited and happy that people are getting so
creative and inspired, and doing such cool things -- which of course makes
having to say no so much harder.

Again, we're really sorry,
Mare and Raigan
