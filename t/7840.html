#148397 2 2006-02-25T14:30:42Z Teh Dude
It is a good idea. I think N could benefit from more timed actions. Currently there are ways of forcing timing (eg drones, thwump walls) but timed doors where you press a button then have X seconds to reach the exit would be good. This would mean that even if you fail to reach the exit, you can go back and try again.

Also, as you suggested, doors that open only after a certain amount of time. So you could have survival maps as part of the game.

The gold counter doors are a good idea too. Currently, there is not that much of a need to get gold. If you had to get at least Y pieces of gold to open the exit then this would force people to get more gold. Also you could distribute gold in different places so people would have a choice of which challenges to overcome. For example, one difficult area may contain lots of gold while there are multiple easier areas each with less gold. You could kind of achieve this by having doors, and people have to press Y buttons before they can reach the exit but this is messy.

In conclusion, all of these would make interesting additions as they add to gameplay/level design.
