#293439 36 2007-02-04T02:35:16Z Teedle
So it's been awhile since I've gone onto NUMA, but I decided to give it a shot. It's been said several, several times before, but just to restate it once more with feeling:

The problem with NUMA is the emphasis on ratings and the competetive nature of it. With the author rankings page, the top rated maps page, and all the rest of it, it becomes about beating other people rather than sharing maps.

Comments become in the vein of "its alrght 3/5", talking about the ratings and little else. Maps become rated down without explanation or advice (ala sniping), maps become resubmitted when they're lacking in ratings, and people get pissed off. A lot.
People also just become apathetic about ratings (on account of them not meaning anything anymore), like I did, and hence also become apathetic about NUMA in general. For me, NUMA has mostly been about the ratings- after all, what else is there, really?- and when you're fed up with how little they mean to the population of NUMA in general, you kinda lose the motivation.

It's also been pointed out to me that I only have a ratio of maps made to ratings of about 1:4.6. The reason for this is as follows: I enjoy making maps more than playing them, I prefer giving comments to ratings, I give bad ratings. They vary from day to day and mood; I tend to see the flaws in popular maps and recognise greatness in far less popular maps.

I understand why this is a 'problem', mostly- but I still don't think it should be. If you prefer to make maps, that should be fine. If you prefer to rate them, that should be fine.

The thing is that this shouldn't be a call to abolish the ratings system: It's good, it's useful, and it's nice to see that other people have appreciated your map or get to learning what sorts of maps other people like.

THE THRILLING CONCLUSION

What we DO need is a way to... add... something else. I'm not sure what, but we need more features to make NUMA more about making and playing maps than rating them or makings the ones which get the best ratings.

It's not a competition, and that it is sours the community. I dislike the NUMA- going community while I like the metanet community, despite how similar they mostly are, for this exact reason.
