I've played 2 and 3.

I hadn't really payed attention to the series until I tried out the second one at a friends house. I liked it a lot so I bought it. It was so fantastically fun. Playing ratchet and clank 2 for the first tim is definitely an experience that everyone should have. You'll be filled with an irrespresible euphoria that will last for at least a week.

3 was also tons of fun, but I got the Japanese version, so I couldn't enjoy the quirky storyline and characters nearly as much. Surprisingly this took away a large degree of enjoyment for me. The gameplay was as fun as ever, but it was also more of same, so there was no way it could match the level of fun that 2 gave me.

For people that haven't played any of them, I'd say start with 2 or 3, it probably doesn't matter which. I hear 4 is too short and 1 was lacking a ton of functionality (like weapons that level up) that makes the later games so fun.
