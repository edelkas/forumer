#386073 2 2007-12-04T05:42:06Z ToeFacekiller
There's a switch behind the door opener that activates the trap doors. If you just barely hit the door opener then you wont hit the switch, leaving the exits open. If you can't touch it lightly enough, you'll just have to do it the long way by getting all the switches and exiting out the top.
