You've seen one-way doors. Why not one-way switches?

-It can only be used if N is going the right direction.
-It can have more than one usable direction
-If wrong direction, one of two things happens (which is the only thing making this different from a switch enclosed by one-way platforms:

-N will die
-N will go through the switch but nothing happens.

-There will be an "angle of leeway" since N can hit the switch from almost any direction, so there will be "relatively" right, "relatively" down, etc.



OR


The switch can be made to resemble one-way platforms. This way N can only hit it from the right direction.


Either way, it's something new.
