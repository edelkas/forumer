That hazard perception test... just before I went for my P's, they changed it slightly, because in the previous version you always got about 90% just by counting to ten, then pressing the button.*

*Note for non-Victorians: The so-called 'hazard-perception test' involves watching a pixellated, low-quality video on an old piece of junk which is allegedly a computer, and pressing a button to indicate that you would brake/overtake/turn at that time. It is notoriously inaccurate, due to the poor definition of "brake", the abyssmal quality of the video (which makes it impossible to tell what some things - especially road surfaces and signposts - are supposed to be/say), the fact that the practice questions often form part of the actual test, and the fact that you can STILL get a reasonable score by waiting a few seconds and pressing the button, regardless of what the situation is.
