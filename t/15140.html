#283833 5 2007-01-10T13:47:36Z Sendy
At first you will swear (literally) that the rocket (a) moves too fast and (b) turns too quickly and © tracks you way to efficiently and that it's some kinda design flaw in N, but after your mental 'n-playing' substructure grows and gets pruned for a certain amount of time you find some surprising reflex actions kicking in. In particular I think the art to avoiding rockets is thinking about three seconds into the future whenever a rocket is near you - this is mainly because your best bet for avoiding a rocket is to manipulate it with your movements whilst still striving to attain the sub-goal of getting where you want to go.
