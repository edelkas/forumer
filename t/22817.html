The Linear Map Competition Two


I believe that linear maps are vastly under appreciated due to their being used most by n00bs.

Linear maps can be great in the right hands (i.e. Domination, spuzalot), but too few talented authors use them often enough.

Therefore, I'm starting this competition.

Yup, it's back, and it's better than ever! Now, to paste a large amount of the first thread, with some changes.
Quote
Linear Maps, by this Competition's Standards:
  • ONLY one path, not including for gold. Gold may be placed slightly off-path.
  • Path is always clear
  • Slight doubling back is acceptable.
Hm. Thought there was more
Rules not Regarding Map Style:
  • Competitors may submit one map per round. They may withdraw and submit a new map if the first has not yet been judged.
  • Maps may be submitted as a .txt in this thread or on NUMA.
  • An APG (All Possible Gold) demo must be submitted with the map.
  • If submitted to NUMA, include tags "Linear" and "LMC"
Judging:
I will need two more judges (assuming this takes off) as well as myself plus a back-up to judge on these factors:
  • Fun. /50
  • Aesthetics (sorry Twistkill!). /25
  • Use of Theme. /25
For a total out of one hundred. Judges should submit their judging at the end of the week, in a chunk. It is recommended to compile it throughout the week, though.
THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE:
  • Have fun!

Noticed the differences yet? I'll explain.

LMC2 is going to be split into rounds, and each round will have a theme. Be forewarned, these will be weird themes, and I encourage you to be creative with them.
After the deadline (a week) is up, judging will be tallied, and if you have the lowest score, you're out.
Wondering about prizes? So am I. I'm open to any weird and wacky suggestions you have.

linear [lin-ee-er]
adjective
1. of, consisting of, or using lines: linear design.
2. pertaining to or represented by lines: linear dimensions.
3. extended or arranged in a line: a linear series.
4. involving measurement in one dimension only; pertaining to length: linear measure.
5. of or pertaining to the characteristics of a work of art in which forms and rhythms are defined chiefly in terms of line.
6. having the form of or resembling a line: linear nebulae.
7. Mathematics.
a. consisting of, involving, or describable by terms of the first degree.
b. having the same effect on a sum as on each of the summands: a linear operation.
8. Electronics. delivering an output that is directly proportional to the input: a linear circuit; a linear amplifier.
9. threadlike; narrow and elongated: a linear leaf.
