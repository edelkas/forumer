#25016 4 2004-07-16T15:13:01Z Guest
at the beginning of the level you're playing, N checks what the current highscores are. Then, when you beat the level, code compares your score with the current lowest highest score, and with your current personal best for the level.

So here's what can happen:

-new personal best: you beat your old record for this level
-new highscore: you beat the online current lowest high score for this level
-both of the above
-neither of the above


If you got a highscore, it should say 'uploading', but as with any send/receive of data over the internet, sometimes packets get lost -- so on the highscores page (off N's main menu), we added a 'personal best' section with a submit button.

Note though, the submit button will only be visible if one of your bests is better than the worst highscore.
