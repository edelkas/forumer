Good morning.

Welcome to week one of the Numa Time Trial Society (NTTS). Our main purpose is to encourage Forum Members to play more User-made maps through . If You're new to the NTTS, comprehensive rules can be found here.

Here are your NTTS judges for Week 1:

Rahl,
Maniac,
Brickman,
Maximo,
Khane, And
Myself

And here are the rules for Week 1:
  • This week's map is .
  • Any Forum member can post their demo data for this week's map in this forum. The above judges will review each member's demo data and at the end of the week, we will declare a winner.
  • The judges will vote for a winner, a runner-up, and a 2nd runner-up in each of 5 main categories: Speed, Style, Creativity, Completion, and Least Shot At. For explanations on each category, visit this thread.
  • This week's map has no map-specific categories.
  • Each 1st place vote in the five categories earns you 5 points towards your total points for the week, and for the season. Each 2nd place vote earns you 3 points. Each 3rd place vote earns you 1 point. 4th place and after earn no points.
  • Submissions begin now, and will end at 12:00 Midnight PST on Friday evenings. If you don't know when PST is, PM me, and I'll let you know.
  • Judging will begin Saturday Morning, and the points will be posted Sunday Evening, just prior to next week's contest.
  • For now, each contestant may post demo data any number of times. However, Do not double post. If you wish to update your demo data score, please edit your original post, as to not clog up the thread. If you do happen to double post, Be clear which data you want us to score.
  • And finally, weekly scores will be cumulative. Your scores for each map will carry over until the end of the season.
Good luck, and happy playing!
