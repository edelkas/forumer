No. You should not be allowed to smack your child. It teaches children that when someone doesn't do what you want, you respond with violence and aggression; research generally shows that people who were smacked are more likely to use violence themselves (incidentally, has anyone noticed that most of those who support smacking people have mentioned that they were/are smacked? And, before anyone asks, I was not smacked). There should be small positive incentives for behaving, and non-violent penalties for misbehaviour (lectures, restriction of priveleges, etc).

And I wouldn't count a tap on the wrist to stop them touching something hot as "smacking".
