Wow. You don't know how to make or submit maps, yet you posted in the correct subforum

To create maps, just press `(which is above your Tab key) or \ (which is above your Enter or Return key).

To submit maps, you first need to create an account. Go to the Configure menu in N, and create an account.

Then go to NUMA, and enter your account information into the login. Then you can activate your NUMA account.

Happy map making!

to create a map, first have an idea, then go into N, press "\", then press insert or delete depending on if you want to make tiles or objects and press number dependingf on which tile or object you want to make, and make your map, then playtest, then submit the data that you get from the upper righthand box (i.e. copy and paste) in to the submit a map section in NUMA, then fill in a title and description,a nd press submit, and thats all

the process can take anywhere between 5 minutes and 100 hours
