#389548 2 2007-12-15T05:19:40Z Hendor
When playing episodes, you start the first level with 90 seconds. This is all the time you get for all 5 levels. This is rarely enough to complete them all, so each piece of gold you get adds 2 seconds to your time. Episode 59 does not have very much gold. The gold that it does have is also quite difficult and time consuming to get. So, I'd advise just getting through the first 4 levels as quick as you can and then using the gold at the beginning of level 5 to bolster your time. If you want to see the advanced routes that get all of the gold, check out the level and episode highscores.
