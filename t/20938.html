CAVE MAPS.
Okay, folks. The goal for this competition is very self-explanitory. There will be one round for all of the contestants, and a bonus round for the top three cave-mappers (this might involve a twist). See if you can handle the darkness, the pointiness, the drippiness, and the sheer awesomeness of cave maps! There aren't too many strict rules or regulations, so have fun, and make a quality map!

All submissions must include the following:
  • 1 Cave
  • 1 Map (You may submit up to 3)
All submissions may include the following:
  • Stalactites!
  • Stalagmites!
  • Dripping acid water!
  • Etc.
Scoring

Gameplay: 50 points
Aesthetics: 25 points
Atmosphere (Caviness): 25 points

Oh, yeah, and don't be lazy. Make a new map- don't pick an existing one from your collection.

Judges
  • Skyline will be judging!
  • Sept will be judging!
  • George will be judging!
