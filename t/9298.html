MetaNet toolbar





This toolbar should allow you to browse MetaNet and its forums quicker, as well as let you serch the internet using google and wikipedia. Implement the cookie cleaner, the history cleaner, and the cache cleaner and get notified when you receive new emails to your POP3, Yahoo!, Gmail, or Hotmail accounts.

Downloading is free, with no spyware or viruses, does not open pop-ups or hijack your searches, and no personal information is required. After clicking on the download link, a box will pop-up prompting you for installation. For the most expedient installation process click on "Run". Upon clicking run, the installation file will install itself to your computer. Once finished, click on the button "Run" in the new window that pops up.

The MetaNet toolbar requires Microsoft Windows 2000/XP, Microsoft Internet Explorer 5.0+, 1Mb of hard-drive space. FireFox 1.0.1+ is also supported by the toolbar.
