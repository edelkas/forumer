It's the year 2256. A machine that can send someone onto the internet in virtual reality has been invented. It has been released to the public three years ago. Knowing how this could benefit mankind, this machine was released with a price that allowed it to be affordable to the average family could afford it. But, technicians missed a bug in the final version of this machine. It basically allowed people to live a realistic, virtual life. Test subjects were only sent into the internet to test out basic functions. But, the bug made the enviornment extremely realistic, so realistic, it fools your mind into thinking it's the real thing. Get hurt in the internet, your body would take the actual injury in real life, just not visibly. Nobody got into the internet very seriously yet, but two months sgo, an online forum called Metanet Forums, broke out into civil war for reasons yet to be discovered.

Now, onto the RPG. you're basically on either the Meta or Net side. Your rank determines your position of authority and your member title determines your (physical) power. The higher it is, the higher your stats. Staff members get unlimited power, and whether you're an admin or a mod determines the limit of how you use your powers (someone tell me which is higher, admins or mods, so I can tell them how they use there powers). If you're wondering what the weapons are, they're basically guns that fire massive amounts of posts in one shot, because lag will hurt you. Higher ranking means a wider variety of weapons. If you know of any (real) viruses, and how to use them, you can upgrade your weapons to fire the viruse in the shots. They just can't be too bad, because overdoing it is a little unfair (yes, I know that's a loophole, but both sides can use viruses, and if your side is infected with a viruse, it'll take five posts for the viruse to settle down, along with giving the affected side a chance to use the viruse once [It can only be used twice though]).
