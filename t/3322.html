#70059 28 2005-05-27T21:35:12Z brickman
Castle of the winds is too easy if you ask me. Its got some nice features, but it feels like its a "roguelike for the easily frustrated" more than a game for avid gamers. A lot of that is because of the save-anywhere-and-in-as-many-files-and-as-many-times-as-you-want feature which made many things, such as identification, either useless or easily exploitable and made the strategy of anyone with detect objects be "Save. Enter new floor. Cast detect objects. Check maps. If there are fewer than 5 extreemely valuable magic items and three spellbooks, reload and repeat." And it is also in part due to the dungeons which don't redraw upon reentry and the magic system which let you become a powerful mage without compromising your other abilities (physical combat, defense, running) at all because you automatically get new spells every new level regardless of whether you use them or want them and can easily buy spellbooks at general stores to boot. Yeah, that and the lack of any really strong enemies (the fact that you inevitably reach the point of training on elder dragons and greater demons the same way you had one bears and wolves earlier in the game is one of my pet peeves with roguelikes and rpgs in general, but you reach that point really quickly in CotW).
And I think I remember playing skyroads. . . pretty good, but I remember it being pretty hard too. . . and too many special tiles.
