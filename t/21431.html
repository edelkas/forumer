Strange that no one's posted about it already. For those of you who don't already know about Project Chanology, Anonymous' ongoing offensive against Church of Scientology, the following links should be useful:

Anonymous' First Message
Project Chanology (Wikipedia)

Anonymous' Latest Press Release

Anonymous is, put simply, a collective of internet users that plans to take down the Church of Scientology. They are not fighting against Scientology's religious doctrines, but rather the terrible crimes and immoral acts committed by the establishment of the Church itself, particularly its policy of charging hundreds of thousands of dollars for auditing courses and its "fair game" policy of destroying the lives of those it perceives to be a threat. This video is useful if you want to learn more about the crimes they have committed (just some of many). These two sites have a great deal of additional information.

By far the most ambitious phase of Project Chanology thus far is the planning of dozens of real-life protests at Scientology churches around the world at 11:00 am local time on February 10th, 2008. They've already begun as of this post, and the mainstream media has taken notice, as seen in this article regarding the protest in Sydney, Australia.

It's quite an amazing sight to behold, seeing a good portion of the internet uniting quickly and efficiently around a single cause. And the plans don't stop at February 10th; there is an elaborate long-term strategy in place, with much of the focus being placed on getting rid of Scientology's tax-exempt status, which would deal a massive blow to what is essentially a money-making scheme disguised as a religion.

While I personally am not able to attend my local protest, I certainly support them and will be helping out their cause in other ways, both today and in the future. If you're interested in getting involved, or just want to see how things are going, the Enturbulation Forums are a great resource.

Good luck, supporters of Anon, and Xenuspeed!
