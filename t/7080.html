#135975 6 2006-01-26T01:48:54Z Atilla
Teh Dude is entirely correct. This apparent paradox, similar to Zeno's paradox (that's the one with Achilles and the tortoise, if you don't know), springs from a logical error. By choosing to only look at the half-way points, you are deliberately ignoring the point where you actually reach the end, and claiming this point cannot exist because you refuse to look at it.
