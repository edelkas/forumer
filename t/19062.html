The Simple Action Competition!

Welcome everyone. As you may already have guessed, this will be a map-making competition based only on how simple you can make an action map while making it as fun as possible. It sounds rather easy, doesn't it? Well, get to it, then!
Rules and Guidelines:

The maps must be in the action category. No races, DDAs, or puzzles... just the type that started it all.
You must create your maps from scratch. You are not allowed to use any old maps on NUMA.
The map must not contain more than 35 tiles, 25 objects (includes gold), and 10 enemies. The ninja and exits do not count as objects.
You will submit the map to NUMA, and provide a link to this thread in the description. Also, please post the NUMA link here as well.
You have up to 4 submissions, but only your best one counts.

There will be 3 judges and 20 competitors. You may ask to be a judge, so this is first come, first serve. However, if you are a judge, then you cannot take part in the competition itself. If more people wish to compete, I may consider adding you to the roster. I am participating in this competition myself, so I am one of the competitors.

The deadline to submit maps is October 17th.
Grading System

/5 Points for how well the map flows. Is there a path to follow that provides high-speed routes, or is it cumbersome to move around on the map?
/15 Points for how fun the map is. Does it make you want to play it again?
/10 Points for difficulty/enemy placement. Is the map overbearingly difficult or is it too easy?

The grades will be added together, thus the total will be out of 90. Whoever has the highest score wins!
