#206702 26 2006-07-24T14:42:24Z iangb
The way that fake highscores are going to be caught is a 'demo verifyer' - a bot that checks highscores by running the map/demo data and comparing the actual final time for the demo with the 'official' time that the demo claims. If it's false, then the highscore is automatically deleted.

The only thing that isn't caught by that is genuine demos that have been made by cheating - fbf or similar. And there ain't a lot that can be done about that, short of encrypting demos as they are made or similar.
