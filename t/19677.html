So, our verdict on the Origins and Expanse packs seems to be that they were generally of lower quality than our Zenith stuff. Matt and I both thought it would be a good idea to replace some of the worst offenders when we release the full 1000-map pack.

We're also getting feedback from the rest of the forums here. For right now we're just saying we want to know what we can improve on for Zenith, and keeping the replacements part of it a secret.

This thread is for deciding which maps should be replaced, and replacing them. Feedback from the public thread will be found here in the first post.

So, please list any maps that you don't feel are up to Legacy standards, along with a bit of explanation. Those of you who weren't here to map for Origins and Expanse, by all means, give your opinions as well.

Thanks guys.
