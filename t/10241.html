#185679 38 2006-06-07T02:24:32Z Twistkill
Quote: slain_wraith, June 05, 2006 10:43 pm
wat about me .....i need help

EDIT: i really need help....i cant find my .sol file and i want to start over....and i already searched all my files on my comp for the .sol and nothing is showing up...can someoen please help me....

If you can't find your .sol file, then do this: Right-click the window that N is opened in. Click on settings and go to the folder icon. Slide the meter all the way to the left; this will stop the flash from saving information on your computer. Then click OK and close N. Reopen it and all of your progress should be erased. Make sure to slide the meter back to the right when you're finished clearing your progress or else it will never save your progress every time you close it.
