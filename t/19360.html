The Story.
~I'll keep it short.
~March 19, 2007: Brttrx, DemonzLunchBreak, Cooby, Shortshift, and myself were just fooling around in an AIM chatroom. We submitted about 10 maps to Numa that night in a 30 minute period. This was what originally sparked the idea of The Mirage Explosion. From that moment on, I compiled every map that was made by any of Team Mirage. Our goal was to have 500 when all was said and done.
~April 30, 2007: DemonzLunchBreak retires from mapmaking. Some of his maps that he worked on before announcing his retirement are in the pack.
~June 24, 2007: Infinitum accepts the position as the Sixth Member of Mirage. He helped immensely.
~July 28, 2007: We decrease the initial goal of 500 maps down to 400 maps.
~August 26, 2007: We reach the plateau of 400 maps.
~September 11, 2007: Head for shelter. The Mirage Explosion has begun.

The Dedications.
~I asked each member of Team Mirage who they would like to dedicate the entire map-pack to.
~Brttrx replied, "To weed, for helping me get through the pack."
~Demonz replied, "To abstract!"
~Cooby replied, "To brttrx, shortshift, demonz, and midnightgetaway."

~And I decided to dedicate the pack to xdude. Wherever he may be now, I wish him the best in life. He was so helpful to me in my earlier days, and I thank him gratefully for that. Keep on rockin' on xdude.

The Pack.
~Okay, so here it is.
~8 columns. 80 episodes. 400 maps. 400 collaboration maps.
~394 playable maps. 6 non-playable maps.

The Competitions. The Prizes. The Accolades.
~First person to send in all-gold* completion demos for all 394 playable maps will receive:
~Four collaboration maps from Team Mirage dedicated to him/her on Numa.
~Signature tag customized by Team Mirage.
~Mystery Prize: Announced when the winner is confirmed.
~*Note: All-gold is possible on 59-3: Iron Curtain, even if it may not seem so at first glance.
~First person to send in completion demos for all 394 playable maps will receive:
~Three collaboration maps from Team Mirage dedicated to him/her on Numa.
~Signature tag customized by Team Mirage.
~Second person to send in completion demos for all 394 playable maps will receive:
~Three collaboration maps from Team Mirage dedicated to him/her on Numa.
~First person to correctly guess (via PM to Dejablue) which map lord_day helped make will receive:
~One collaboration map from Team Mirage dedicated to him/her on Numa.
~P.S. You are allowed one guess per PM. And nobody better spam up my PM box. Wait until I reply, then you can take another guess. Ignoring this rule will result in disqualification.

The Format.
~00-0 to 66-1: Actions, Puzzles, and anything in between (332 maps).
~66-2 to 69-4: Races and Race hybrids (18 maps).
~70-0 to 78-3: The Finale. Saved the best for last (44 maps).
~78-4 to 79-4: The Credits. Courtesy of uNcOnDiTiOnAl (6 maps).

Extra Tid-Bits.
~Some of the puzzles are quite tough to figure out. I will submit most of them to Numa within the next couple of months with their respective spoiler demos. Just in case you get stuck.
~Team Mirage is currently working on a new project. For now, it will remain a mystery as to what this project is. The only thing I'll say is that it is not a map-pack.
~However, Team Mirage is also planning to create a follow-up map-pack to The Mirage Explosion. It will not be released until after n1.5 though.

And without further ado, I present to you The Mirage Explosion. Enjoy it. Don't hurt yourself. And until next time, think of the world.

Sweet . I know I got all the visible gold, but I'm kinda worried about gold hidden behind objects (thwumps especially); I missed some gold in Qix's map pack because of those buggers and had to go back and redo a demo.

Some notes on the pack: on the whole, these were extremely good and a blast to play. I especially enjoyed the races section; there are some really awesome non-race-looking races in there, if you know what I mean. Overall the difficulty ramped up perfectly, but on a smaller scale there were some balancing issues. There were a smattering of episodes where I felt the 5th map was much easier than some (or all) of the others; in one case the easy episode ender was additionally a column ender, and if there is one thing I demand from episodic map packs, it is bone-crushingly hard column enders (or something). Also I noticed there were maybe a few too many maps that were themed around the whole "using angled tiles to glitch through one-ways" trick. Don't get me wrong, some of those maps were brilliant, but it did get a little bit repetitive at times. Minor issues aside, this pack was flawless, and is probably my second favorite of all time (sorry, but Expanse is just too good). The fact that these were all collabs makes the accomplishment even more incredible.

Oh, and Iron Curtain was a bitch to AGD.
