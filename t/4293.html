Here's a really cool trick that may help you. If you're falling from a higher height, try falling on a slanted tile. If you don't hold any arrow keys during your fall, chances are that you'll survive! Really useful in a few N levels. I hope I helped you out.

#88021 2 2005-08-03T19:30:45Z stepself
I think that this is mentioned in the readme. At the very least, it's in the player help section of the game, under "falling". I'd be surprised if there was anyone on this forum that doesn't know this trick.

For the record, impact from a fall is measured perpendicular to the surface you hit. That means you have a better chance of surviving falling onto a steep slope (if you're falling straight down). It also means that you increase the chance of dying if you fall towards a slope (ie. in the perpendicular direction)
