I agree with the slide move, but there would be exceptions. If you slide and you're still in the tunnel (or for a better example, under MINES), you should be able to crawl. Examples below.

Let's say crouch is down.
o If you're standing still, you can duck/move into crawl mode by pressing and holding down. You can also crawl at a sluggish pace by holding left or right.
o If you're moving (nomatter how fast) and you press down to crouch, you will slide (head-first would make the most sense in this case) and friction will kick in at 2x (or 1.5x) as much as standing motion. You cannot gain speed from crawling until you have reached crawling speed, which afterwards you can continue crawling.
o If you slide (or crawl) into a half-square crawlspace, you cannot get up until there is room, and you let go of down. If you slide under mines, you better hold down.
o If you let go of down in the middle of a slide, N will flip out of the slide in a roll and continue running with the same momentum. If you press jump while sliding, N will flip out of the slide into a jump, still holding the same momentum.
o For vertical crawlspaces, N should also be able to tilt into one of these. Holding crouch, he can drop down one. Letting go of down while inside one will make him stick to the walls in an attempt to slow down. He cannot enter one vertically, but jumping makes him climb (not jump) slowly upwards.
