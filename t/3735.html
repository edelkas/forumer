[Quote equals Kablizzy.]

Well, kids, here we are.

There are some things on my mind that I'd like to get off my chest here. First off, the attitudes around here. These need to stop. Now. I, and the other staff members, have been more than lenient with all of you on this stuff, and the flaming, whining, and bitching about menial things has gone way too far. We aren't here to baby-sit you guys; we're here to have fun, just like everyone. And when that fun degenerates into what it has, it's no longer fun.

I understand that you guys are young, and not as mature as I probably expect yall to be, since, after my initial experience here with all of you, I had some pretty high expectations of you all. But with the attitudes that have been shown around the forums lately, I can see that those expectations were indeed far too high. When I signed up as a mod here, I did it because this was an awesome place to be, the community was nurturing and welcoming, and things were going well. But the flaming and conspiracy theories and spamming have ruined that thoroughly, as has the influx of new people to Numa, and the aftermath.

Which brings me to my next point. Yes, we were planning another fiasco. LV and I have had it in the works for months now, and with NC7 so close to opening, it was the prime chance to step things up. But that, too, has been thoroughly ruined, due to the sour and bitter attitudes of some of our forum members. With that said, I feel you all deserve an explanation here, to clarify what exactly these fiascos meant to our community.

When the Kablizzy fiasco went off, it wasnt to confuse and anger you. It wasnt meant to create a rift in the forum, nor was it intended to frustrate you all. And it went off without a hitch. Everyone had fun, everyone had a laugh, all was groovy. And then came the Y&BB. The Y&BB was intended to be fun and interesting; something for yall to participate in, enthrall yourselves in, and have fun with. But most of you whined and moaned that we were conspiring against you all, and that we were abusing our moderator powers in order to conspire against all you little people. Where that came from, and what purpose it served, I still have no idea, but I didnt appreciate the sentiment then, and I certainly dont appreciate it now.

We had a discussion back in January, I believe, about forum activity waning. And it was decided that the Y&BB was an excellent chance to get you guys more involved in the forums, and it did so. The Y&BB was a smashing success. Beyond the handful of bumps in the road that our more scurrilous members provided to it, the entire Y&BB went over quite well, and ended more famously than I had hoped it to end. When forum activity waned a while back, we had another discussion, and it was decided to implement an IRC channel, which seemed to revitalize interest in the forums, as well.

So, back to the new fiasco we were planning. We had Arachnid set up a fake Numa for us, which can be found here. We dubbed this map archive Supernuma. We were going to put Supernuma everywhere, and come up with fake news, a fake about system, all that good stuff. We had gone through many versions of this prank before this particular idea fell into our laps. Weve been considering an elites lounge for quite a while now, but we hadnt actually thought about implementing it until about a week ago, or so. So, shortly afterwards, you guys happened to come up with the very same idea, which fit quite nicely into our plan. So, our idea was to make NC7 be maps exclusively from Supernuma, the elite lounge of maps, thus creating a bit of an uproar on the forums as to why certain people werent invited into this elite club, thus creating the backdrop for the prank. However, with all the discussion recently over actually implementing such a system, our goals rather fell apart for the prank actually succeeding, as a particular member has decided for us.

So, part two of this was going to be, eventually, to give people access to the only actual page on Supernuma. Within that page, well, well get into that in a minute.

So, my next beef here. As most of you know, Im suspended until the 24th. And now, for whatever reason, Polymatic and Kablukatu have been dealt with in a similar fashion. Which is the very reason why this isnt being posted on one of my own accounts. This wouldnt bother me as much if it werent in conjunction with all the other stuff that has gone on, which Ill go into shortly.

Now, going back to the maturity thing here, I believe that we, as a community, began as incredibly mature, and massively fruitful. But I have seen that maturity go to hell over the past months. And I do have a beef with Keron here. Throughout my time here, Keron has overtly asserted his maturity level as greater than the remainder of us, and, for the most part, he has lived up to that assertion. However, I feel as though he has been quite overprotective of the staff positions here on the forums. I feel as though, even in our most dire times, he has refused to provide the forums with a working administrative staff, particularly when we most needed it, even though he wasnt going to be there to fill the position himself. There were many, many times when simply adding one of us to temporary Administrative staff would have alleviated much heartache and much trouble for all of us, but due to Kerons severe unwillingness to do so (Whose motives for refusing to promote any of us are still unknown to me, beyond speculation), our forums suffered for it. Even the decision to promote one of the mods to a global moderator was an excruciating task on his part, when it was obvious to the remainder of the staff that it was the bare minimum that was required for the boards to remain functional, and much more action, such as one of the mods gaining temporary Admin status, was ideal. So, now, the time has come when Keron is out of school. Thus, since my Global Mod position was a temporary one, so I could watch over the forums while he wasn't available as admin, we apparently don't need a Global Moderator anymore. And Im sure all of this will sound pretty self-righteous and pretentious, but I assure you, this is a view shared by the entire moderator staff. Not only have LV and I proven ourselves more than worthy to take care of the forums, but were Maximo around, he would be in the exact same position, as well. By that same token, when we were lacking an Admin, Kerons only answer was to take his chances instead of appointing a temporary Admin in his stead. Which, in the eyes of the moderating staff, is a horrible judgment call. Particularly after my appointments around my other forums to Administrator and Moderator, Keron still hesitated. Im not sure what harm it could do to have an extra Global Moderator around, nor do I understand what kind of complex Keron must have to make him believe that he needs to keep his Admin position sacred from us filthy mods, in spite of what good it may have done for the forum. Overall, I think Kerons lack of maturity overcomes his better judgment at times, and cant see the benefits that certain actions would hold due to this seeming lack of common sense. This isnt meant as an Attack on Keron, as he has done a wonderful job on the boards, and has done many, many things to make these boards a wonderful place, but to inwardly assume that we arent worthy of Administrative duties and abilities - based, I assume, upon a fear that one of us would turn malicious - is unwarranted, and I feel that it has hurt the forums as a whole.

So, between all of this, Ive butted heads with a lot of you. And from that, Im sure Ive been seen as an elitist bastard, and of that, at least, I am guilty. But between the .sol sharing from trib4l, Brickmans flaming, the debates, the spamming, and all of the crap that goes on here just to push my buttons and see how far things can be pushed, it has broken my spirit. The last thing I want to do when I come home at night is to babysit and make sure that no ones flinging poo at each other. So, armed with this, we all made the decision to go ahead with our final prank.

So, back to that page I was referencing on Supernuma. Once the users got access to that page on Supernuma, it would hold my resignation from Metanet staff. With the stresses of my Staff positions on the sims, as well as the ridiculous crap that has been allowed to go on here - only some of which Ive covered - as well as the plethora of real-world issues that need to be dealt with, Its best for me just to step down from Metanet Staff before Keron goes through with my demotion. I feel as though Keron wishes to keep the moderators to the absolute bare minimum amount of functionality required to keep the boards running, and no more. This, on top of the suspension of all three of my accounts, on top of TDLs saucy little outburst(s) over something that was meant to be fun for you guys, on top of the remainder of the forum hijinks, was more than enough to make my decision.

In addition, for the good of the community, particularly after this, Ill be leaving the community on a more-or-less permanent basis. Being here as much as I have been has proven to be quite detrimental to my life, and my relationship with my fiance, my job, and my future in school have all suffered heavily due to the amount of dedication Ive put into staff here, and I think its time to concentrate more upon my family than upon this, when it has become so unfulfilling. It was a good run, and I can revel in being such a large part of the Metanet community for so long, and I will always carry the memories that weve all created. For the vast majority of the time that I was here, I enjoyed my time here immensely, getting to know all of you on a more personal level than I have been allowed to know almost anyone. For the past year or so, you all have been my family, the people whom I come home to every day, the people I associate with on a daily basis, and those who have been closer to me than my own family has been.

So, a couple final thoughts. First off, Im going to be relinquishing the NTTS site to Daddaluma. If he, or anyone for that matter, wishes to take it up, ask Daddaluma to talk to me, he has contact with me over at HoI, and I will send him the html files needed to reconstruct the site elsewhere.

Secondly, TTK, I havent forgotten about you. I cannot get into my PMs right now, due to my suspensions, but just as soon as I can, Ill be resolving things with you, as well.

Thirdly, and perhaps most importantly, the Kablizzy Fiasco, NTTS, Nedcons, and the Y&BB were, without a doubt, the pinnacles of my time here, and they will not be soon forgotten. Speaking of which, get to judging NC6 so we can move on to the actual NC7, the theme of which, I will announce here. The theme for Nedcon 7 will be Maximo-style maps, to herald Maximos return. I hope that provides for some extra reason to finish judging.

With all that said, it is time to say farewell. If ever you wish to chat, or just to say hi, you all know where I can be reached. Goodbye, Metanet. And good luck to you all.

***Marked***
