#75511 48 2005-06-16T23:53:02Z maximo
What I have so far... only one list but it's a start.

Top Ten Ways

Not to make a map harder:
10.) Length. Long maps get tedious and old really fast. Hard maps that are long become frustrating because dieing at the end means you have do a very long part of the level over again. A well known example is MTI. Which I know is considered good, but it is still very frustrating to die at the last room and have to do the whole thing over 50 times. Also Bricklayer is a good example. While not long in the length of the tile set it has far too many switches all over the map that it becomes one of the longest maps in the game. Do not put tons of switches on your maps or they quickly become too long for tastes.
9.) Cluster enemies. Large groups of things like turrets are excessive and pointless. One of two things happen, they all act like one enemy and you just extend the load time or it becomes frustratingly luck based. When there are large swarms of enemies or lots of turrets the game because mostly chance. While this can work in some instances, 9 times out of 10 it is very bad to group and cluster enemies. One or two well placed drones are always better than 10 seeking drones coming at you right away.
8.) Gauss Turrets in the middle of open maps or in the corners of small or narrow spaces. This while not always bad can very quickly become very very very frustrating. Without places to hide or at least get its fire off of you the map quickly becomes luck because Gauss are widely renowned as most random of all enemies. You can do everything right, go fast and keep going back and forth and the gauss will still get you some of the time, which in my experience is one of the most frustrating things ever. Instead of the middle of open spaces, try in a small crevice so the player has a chance to get behind or besides it and be safe.
7.) Grouping certain enemies in the same space. This is open for debate, but from my point of view there are 2 groups of enemies, smart and dumb. Smart enemies include things like rockets, gauss, and seeker drones. They actively pursue you at all times and react to you. Dumb enemies are ones like laser drones, chain gun drones, zap drones, and thwumps. Once they see you they dont stop their attack and start looking for you again until it is over, giving you ample opportunity to get away. Ive always found grouping any dumb enemy with smart enemies is a no no and just makes the map insanely hard. The lasers or chain guns trap you in place while you wait for them to stop attacking while the seeking enemies come and get you while you are pinned down. Instead put like grouped things together and youll end up with more beatable, yet still challenging maps.
6.) Awkward or trick jumps. Things like this take time and some level of luck to pull off. Having to jump from a steep slope, and wall jump off of a one way wall then land on the edge of a half wall just to turn and see the rocket on your tail is just too hard. Instead focus on smooth transitions from the different places on your map and places that are easy to get around in. Nothing is worse than having to make split second jumps one after another. Things that require exacting precision from start to finish are very frustrating. Instead try to make maps with many open paths and ways for the player to recover after a slip. I guess a way to sum this is up is dont make unforgiving maps and try not to make maps with tricky terrain to walk on (especially when you do that with smart enemies).
5.) Segment your levels. A long group of medium difficulty rooms does not make a medium difficulty map. It makes a near impossible map. Having to do room after room or section after section of tedious challenges is a drag. While each room is beatable as a whole the chances of doing it all in one clean run becomes very minute. Just because you can spawn at each room and do them individually it gets really old having to do the first four rooms 10 times in order to get to the fifth room and die until you get finally get it. Then repeat for room six and so on. Not fun and very time consuming. Instead try to make maps were there a few enemies and section and the few enemies carry on and are used through out the map.
4.) Guess and check. Do not under any circumstances make a map that requires the player to guess in order to beat the level. This is a cardinal sin in my opinion. It is no fun at all for anyone to have to pick between 3 switches with all but one leading to certain doom. Always make sure the player knows what does what in a map. If a switch opens drones and there is no way for the player to avoid the switch or they have to guess which one is the one that opens the door to the exit it is annoying. It doesnt make maps hard, it makes them boring. Instead of a good challenge the player guesses wrong, dies and then next time its the easiest map in the world. Easy ways out of this are simply no to do it. But if you just have to make it so the player doesnt die right away, just make it so it is harder later on. Never make it so guessing wrong makes the level impossible.
3.) Hidden traps. Nothing is fun about going for gold and then being stuck behind a trap door. It is worse than guess and check because you are supposed to go for gold as an extra challenge for those players who are up to it. Its so freaking frustrating to finally get that really hard to reach piece of gold only to find it kills you or traps you. Provide a way out at least. Getting harder for getting gold is a good thing, but getting impossible is a very bad thing that should be avoided 100% of the time.
2.) Small spaces and certain enemies. While some enemies work well in small spaces, some do not. Enemies that can be predicted like zap drones, thwumps, and in some instances gauss (emphasis on some) work well in small rooms where maneuverability is low things like rockets and lasers are just man slayers in small rooms. It makes the map no fun to play if the player knows it will take a very lucky run to get through certain rooms and putting something like a chain gun drone in the middle of a 3 by 3 room is just no way to do it. This is one of the more flexible things however because all of these enemies can work in certain situation, just some ore more apt to being a pain in the butt than a good time.
1.) There and Back missions. Oh my gosh, these are the absolute worst maps ever. Do not make maps that make you go through a drawn out corridor only to have to return the same way. Metanet did this a lot in the 70s column and it my least favorite column in the game. Having to traverse a series of tough challenges only to have to do the same thing coming back is annoying, repetitive, and takes a certain level of luck. Do not make the player play the same places on the map more than once. I dont care if you added enemies and it got harder (which is worse actually) it still sucks. Just dont do it, ever. Make maps where you go in a proverbial loop and go all over the map and end up back where you were if you want a long map.

As a note, all of these rules can be broken. In moderation of course but there is nothing saying you can do anyone of these things. Rules are made to be broken and these are no exception. If you do break these do so in moderation and do so only for a short period of time and do not break more than one or two at a time. Maps on NUMA these days are progressively reaching impossible heights of difficulty and its a shame to see some many good ideas fall to the wayside because they are too hard. Just play test a lot because this is the only way to tell if you have a good map and what needs to be changed. If you cant do it, neither can we.
