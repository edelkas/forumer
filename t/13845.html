#255962 3 2006-11-17T13:55:53Z JeCa
Twumps and floorguards are programmed in such a way that the path they will follow when detecting a ninja is pre-set when the level is loaded. It doesn't change during the level and is only affected by the tiles. If this was to be implemented then they would've to create a new pathway everytime they saw a ninja plus every frame they're moving (alt everytime they cross one cell), and that would cause much more lagg which probably would ruin some DDA's by making them impossible to watch. It's almost the same way with seekerdrones, which's path is pre-determined at the moment it spots a ninja. If the ninja's standing in a door that at the moment is open the drone will still believe it's open - and run through it - if the ninja moves.
