Basically he's a lawyer on a crusade against violence in video games. That doesn't seem so bad in itself, but the catch is that he's completely and undeniably insane. What you see here isn't all, not by a long shot. The amazing thing is he does something moronic virtually every day.

Exhibit A
Summary: Scott, the author of the video game webcomic VG Cats, receives an email from Jack Thompson claiming that one of Scott's comics is based on false information. Scott informs Jack of his little friend satire. After this point Jack decides to resort to name-calling and threats, and later denies that he even initiated the conversation. Then, like any self-respecting crazed lawyer, Jack tells Scott that if he doesn't stop emailing him he will take legal action.

Exhibit B
Summary: This'll be a little hard to follow, but here goes. Jack, for reasons beyond human comprehension, announces he will donate $10,000 to charity if a game developer will make a game in which a child goes on a mass murder rampage (or something of to that effect) as a result of playing video games. Several of these games pop up, but then Jack withdraws his proposal, announcing he was "only kidding". Apparently he was also kidding about giving money to needy families. The good folks over at Penny Arcade, another video game webcomic, decide they'll be so kind as to donate the $10,000 dollars for him. Jack then proceeds to try and get them arrested.

A quote from one of the guys at Penny Arcade: "Jack actually just called and screamed at me for a couple minutes," Krahulik said. "He said if I email him again I will 'regret it'. What a violent man." Ironic, isn't it?

Quote: Gabe
Tycho and I remain free men. The fact is that Jack actually sent the fax to everyone but the intended recipient at the Seattle PD. There were a lot of names to remember I don’t blame him. I’ve received literally thousands of emails in the past couple days. They are all commending us for standing up to Jack the way we did. You can certainly mail the networks he appears on. You can even send letters to their advertisers. You can contact the senators he speaks with and demand they ignore him. Jack is not special. He is not a unique snow flake as they say. He is just the latest vocal opponent of whatever is "corrupting" our youth at the moment. If we were to succeed in getting Jack blacklisted from the major news outlets someone else would simply take his place. Imagine him as an actor playing a part in a play. The point is that Jack Thompson is not important. If he were to be fired a new actor would simply take up the role.
