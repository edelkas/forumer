The World Cup of N[/color]

I have another highscoring tournament for 36 eager N players! Anyone in MPT2 would love this!

The Way This Works
1. There will be two divisions: North and South. There are six countries in each division. There are three players per country.
2. We will start off with pool play. I will write up a schedule so that everybody plays five qualifying matches within their divisions.
3. After pool play, the top team in each division will receive a bye to the semifinals, and the 2nd and 3rd team from both divisions will square off in the quarterfinals.
4. Each round will take one full week. There will be five qualifying weeks, one quarterfinal week, two to three semifinal weeks (best 2 out of 3), and three to five final weeks (best 3 out of 5).
5. We will be using Nreality and various user-created maps chosen by me.
6. Every match will consist of two countries facing each other. All three players in each country will need to submit a demo by the deadline. At the end of the week, there should be six demos on every map. Failure for a player to submit a demo will result in that team's forfeit. The scores of both team will be averaged out. The best average score wins the match.
7. If a player has an adequate reason to miss a week, the match will be postponed.
8. This competition will take a while and there will be quite a few breaks due to exams, camp, and possible dropouts.
