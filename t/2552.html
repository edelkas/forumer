So, I was unable to make a new topic for about 10 minutes there.

After some research on the adblock website and looking at the blocked elements on this page, I figured out that the filter *ads* blocks everything that contains those letters in that order.

That includes things like http://2.forumer.com/uploads/metanet/post-12-1105770822.gif

But adblock doesn't just not let you see the stuff. It actually gets rid of it. So I couldn't even make a new topic to ask about how to get around all this crap.

So I went digging on the adblock forum and found a little bit of adblock code that I don't understand but worked: /[^a-zA-Z]ads*/

That apparently restricts the filter to only things that exactly match "ads".

Solved my problem.

Anyway. Just wanted to share my little adventure.

Quote: Daddaluma, Mar 23 2005, 04:21 AM
So I went digging on the adblock forum and found a little bit of adblock code that I don't understand but worked: /[^a-zA-Z]ads*/

That's a regular expression. The two slashes ( / ) are caled delimeters, which mark the start and end of the expression. The [^a-zA-Z] means not any letter of the alphabet (the caret means not). "ads" is a literal block of text. And the aterix means anything (a wildcard).

/[^a-zA-Z]ads*/ altogether will block anything that matches that pattern.

.adsgalore - blocked
badslaughter - not blocked
ads - not blocked
%ads% - blocked

You get the idea
