Also called Power Electronics, Noise is often one of the most harsh subgenres of music. Classic examples of this type are Merzbow, Masonna, K2 and the Nihilist Spasm Band. Although, noise can be created in other forms, such as the music of Boredoms, or the very ambient walls of white noise utilized by Fennesz. Gotta love it.
