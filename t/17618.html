What about it?
The Action-Packed Competition/Tournament is where users compete making maps and highscoring. The map that scores highest will be placed in front of the players. The user that achieves the highest time will win, as well as the user that made the map that scored the highest based on the criteria. Unlike a tournament, however, players will not be eliminated every round, because this isn't a ladder tournament. The player and map-maker with the most points by the end of the competition wins. This is a 10-week competition. Every week is a new round. While map-makers are making their maps for the next round, players will be submitting their demos from the previous round, so we can save time. For example, while map-makers are making their maps for Round 2, players are submitting their demos for the winning map on Round 1. You may enter in both positions (map-maker and player).

The Rules
Map-Makers
  • All maps must be action.
  • Maps cannot be maps already submitted to NUMA. They must be new maps made after a round has started.
  • All maps must have an exit.
  • Collabs are not allowed.
  • Maps must be appropriate for high-scoring (meaning that there may NOT be any race elements in the map)
  • Maps will be judged based on the categories.
  • Mapmakers can join at any point of the competition. The mapmaker with the most points by the end of the competition will be considered the winner.
  • Map-makers have 1 week to submit their entries.
  • The highest and lowest judge scores for each map will be excluded (similar to the Olympics).
  • Judges may enter.
  • Map-makers may make as many as 2 maps.
  • If 2 or more maps get the same score, the winning map will be decided based on a poll.
Players
  • Players may join even after the competition has begun. The player with the most points by the end of the competition is considered the winner.
  • Absolutely NO FBF.
  • AGD's are not required.
  • Demos will be judged just like the highscoring system; the player with the most time wins.
  • Players have 1 week to submit their demoes.
  • Judges may enter.
  • You may only submit 1 demo per map per week
  • PM me the demoes
  • If 2 or more people get the same time, then the map that scored second will be the map that those people will compete on. This pattern continues.

Map-Makers and Players
1st - 5 points
2nd - 4 points
3rd - 3 points
4th - 2 points
5th - 1 point
Everyone else - 0
