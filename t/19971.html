Guitar Hero 3. Includes Muse, The Who, AFI, The Killers, Dragonforce, Guns & Roses, Cream, Beastie Boys, Metallica, Tenacious D, and many more. Check it out on iTunes.

Anyway, if you don't know the basic rules of Guitar hero, there are five different frets, a whammy bar, and a strumming... thing. You basically press down the frets that show up on the TV screen, and strum when it comes.

New additions to GH3:
-Boss fights
-New, higher point notes
-New songs
