Quote: N_Master3_0, November 16, 2006 10:12 pm
How do i make it my sig?
Do you have an account on PhotoBucket or another image hosting website?

If you do, then save the sig to your computer, and go to your account on the website. There should be a button that that says browse, click on it and find your sig. Then click upload it. Then, go to the forum and go to My Controls, and click on edit signature. Click on IMG, and it should ask you for a URL. Go to your image hosting website, and below the sig that you uploaded, there's a little text window that says URL above it. Copy the text, and paste it into the window that opens up when you click on the IMG button and click OK, and you should have your sig.
