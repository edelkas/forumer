#209324 6 2006-07-31T01:26:04Z Mr_Mongoose
I think he want's to know how to put it in his animation. If that is the case, follow these instructions.

1 Open Macromedia Flash
2 File>Import>Import to Library
3 Browse for the songs directory and put it in the Library
4 create a new layer
5 click on your new layer and look ad properties
6 Click sound and select the song

Edit- You are also going to have to put some keyframes in and use actionscript to determine when the music stops/starts.
