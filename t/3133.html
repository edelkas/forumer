hey,
it depends what you do last each frame: apply the stick constrains, or project the particles out of the world.

if you push them out of the world after applying the sticks, you won't have this problem.. but, the stick constraints will be slightly violated.


it also sounds like you need a better collision system; even if a particle is pushed into the ground by a stick constrain, you should be able to correctly push it back into the world.. in the jakobsen paper there is a simple "clamping" collision vs. the world, this guarantees that particles won't leave, you could try that.

The reason the particles in the triangle "pushed" away strangly is because you only applied the distance constraint in one iteration... just iterate through it more times and it should be more "stiff"

Do it like:

for(i = 0; i < NUM_ITERATIONS; i++){
Constraint();
}


what this does, is that it keeps the particles constrained that distance multiple times... because just once and Verlet integration will pull them apart
