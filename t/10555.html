          My Compulsive Day

	August 31, 2006. A day to remember. No questions asked. It was that morning that the most entertaining day of my life began. As I shuffled down the stairs that muggy, day-after-a-thunderstorm type of morning, I was pricked on the side by a curious-looking mosquito. It had exceeded the size range of a mosquito by far, resembling a bee. I killed the mosquito as it leeched away my blood. I felt jolts of pain, and overwhelming itching. 
I commenced out of the kitchen with blueberry muffins stuffed in my mouth, a book bag on my shoulder and 3 bucks for lunch. 
	At school, the first symptom of my bug bite had diluted; I no longer had to scratch incessantly. As I was making my way to Mrs. Colliers room, a boy, 7th grader by the look of him, was struggling to carry his seven-foot band instrument, his book bag, and several textbooks. Dont fall! I thought desperately, as his clumsy form awaited a stray foot to trip him. He suddenly gained poise and shuffled past. That was odd. How could he possibly-WHEEE! The warning bell reconfigured my thoughts. I had to get to class-and fast!
	I visited my locker and headed toward class. Please dont ring! I dont want detention! Please! I wasnt late. In fact, the bell never rang all. Even at 9:45, when we left 1st block, the bell hadnt rung. During Mrs. Colliers Freshman Composition class, many odd things happened. 
	We cant eat in class! Put away your food or give me some! said a purple faced Jake. I thought, yeah, we can!
	And I repeated my thoughts exactly. You can eat in class. Said Mrs. Collier in a robotic voice. 
	Really? inquired the class, but Jake much more loudly than others. She tried to say something, but no sound came out of her mouth. 
	OK. Cool! I revealed my foodstuff to the masses. This was when I was beginning to put things together. The boy regaining poise, the bell not ringing, and us being able to eat in class. Idiot! I felt really dumb. It was obviously a coincidence. But just in case, I thought, Jake! Do cartwheels! NOW! And at that instant, he sprang out of his seat and commenced in doing cartwheels. This is awesome! I..I can do anything! Mrs. Collier, Sing me a song! NOW! I thought. She did. It happened to be some Classical Music, so I think, ok, Stop! Sing Mrs. New Booty! Of course, she did. It was exactly like the song. 
	WHAARGHHH! yelled Jake, who was still involuntarily doing Cartwheels all over the place, and I was kind enough to make him stop. The rest of the class was puzzled. Very puzzled. They just had their Teacher sing them a rap song inappropriate for school audiences and some crazy guy doing cartwheels all over the place. I thought for a while. Then I decided that I didnt need to stay in school to spend my time practicing my newfound skill. Bell, ring now! And it did. Students leave the building and go home! They left, and I Telekinetically transferred my body to the desired location so that I could spend the rest of the day dong things that I could now do, with my newfound skill.
	The sad part is that All my powers vanished along with the bug bite at exactly midnight. I know you will not believe my story, but hey, I dont I believe it myslef!

I detest Hercules, Enough Said.
