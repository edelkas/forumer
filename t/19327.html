I work for Boston University's Personal Computing Support Center as part of their ResNet team, which is a group of people devoted to getting all 10,000 of the students at BU connected to the BU network and to the internet. This job is supposed to be a two-week stint, but the students this year were especially keen on getting their computers up early, so we're left now to the last few days of the ResNet program with 97% of the student body already registered. Most of us just sit around playing flash games.
I have become a connoisseur of defense-themed games.
My favorites are:
Vector TD
Shock Defence
Bloons Tower Defense
...about in that order. Vector TD is by far my favorite, Shock Defense is good for playing once or twice through, and Bloons is just amusing when you're bored out of your mind.

There's also a game I found called Anti-TD, in which you're the computer and you send waves against computer-placed defense. That one isn't as fun, though. I've attached a screenshot of the last level.

Anyone else know of any good flash games centered around the Tower Defense theme?
C'mon, help a brother out...
