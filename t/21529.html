Is there a way that I can save maps that I have created so I can play them whenever I want?
#410507 2 2008-02-19T00:04:25Z Pepi
Go here.

Basically, press "s" at the debug screen when you're finished to copy the data into your computer's clipboard. Then just press "control+v" in a notepad document to paste it. Then save the data.

Or, use NUMA.

#410514 4 2008-02-19T00:17:42Z Pepi
Sure. Use NUMA, and back up your maps to a notepad document. NUMA stands for N Users Map Archive - specifically made for your purpose.

By the way - in your notepad document, make sure word wrap is OFF.

If you have any further questions, feel free to drop me or any of the moderators a PM.

#410565 6 2008-02-19T01:43:59Z Pepi
In notepad, at the top, click "format" and make sure that word wrap is NOT checked. If it is on, then your data can and will be corrupted. I lost at /least/ half a dozen really nice maps before I figured out to turn word wrap off.
