#363712 2 2007-09-09T20:47:55Z Raigan
it's been a long time, but one error we made a lot was that the reference point on the tile _must_ lie on the line that contains the edge you're testing against.

for your tile, you could use a reference point that's at the top-center or right-center of the tile (i.e tile center + _one_ of the halfwidth vectors of the tile.. horizontal or vertical, but NOT both). that will give you a point that's one end of your little 45deg edge.

it looks like you're using a point that's in top-right corner of the tile, which would be wrong since that point doesn't lie on the 45deg edge. that's only a guess though!
