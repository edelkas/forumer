I searched for a topic about it already, and surprisingly, no one has made a topic about this game. It's a racing game where you get to tune cars with tricked out parts and run away from the cops when necessary.

This game is better than NFS: UG 2 because cops have returned and the graphics have been improved even more, somehow. EA always manages to amaze me with their games, and again they have made another amazing game. Graphics are outstanding on the cars. Even though the PS2 sometimes shows pixelated lines on curved surfaces, they show less on this game, and I can't believe that EA managed these kinds of graphics on the PS2.

The AI is challenging and competent. If you don't get a perfect start off the line or if you hit traffic too much, just restart the race, because that's how good the AI is. They almost never hit traffic unless you "accidentally" hit them into it.

The game is improved also because cops have returned from when they were on Hot Pursuit 2. However, they are actual challenges because the cops in Hot Pursuit 2 always appeared, no matter what, and it was linear in execution. But in Most Wanted, because it adds an open environment like GTA, they appear when you start to drive recklessly, and they can be dodged and taken out unlike the ones in Hot Pursuit 2. There are a certain number of cars that are originally set out for you at each heat level, and when it gets down to 2 cars, they call for reinforcements that arrive in 2 minutes. You get bounty from destroying cop cars and from how long the pursuit goes, and it will fill up faster depending on your heat level. The longer the pursuit level goes, the higher your heat goes. When the bar reaches the top, it resets and goes to the next heat level, and I'm going to explain them to you in detail here:

Level 1: The local fuzz. Not very intelligent and can be destroyed quite easily. Only 5 cars set out for you.

Level 2: Undercover local cops. Slightly smarter and take a little more effort to destroy. Originally 10 cars set out for you. Roadblocks occasionally set.

Level 3: Gets kinda tough. State authorities. Even smarter than the undercover local cops and take slightly longer to destroy. Roadblocks become more frequent, and there are 15 cars set out for you.

Level 4: You don't want to get to this point. Undercover state authorities. They're very smart and take a long time to destroy. Roadblocks are even more frequent, and they become upgraded to spike strips. If you hit one, you're done. A helicopter will chase you as well, so even if you get away from the ground units, the chopper will still be chasing you, so you can't get away that easily. AND, Rhinos will come after you. No, not the rhino tanks in GTA, rhinos are police SUVs that ram you head on, and during a high-speed chase they can get you real good. 20 CARS SET OUT FOR YOU. OMG

Level 5: Don't get me started. Federal authorities, they drive CORVETTES. Very smart and they utilize all techniques mentioned in level 4, but harder. 25 CARS SET OUT FOR YOU. ZOMFG

Level 6: Only available after beating Career mode, these guys are the ultimate cops. UNDERCOVER FEDERAL AUTHORITIES. And they sometimes use Rhinos during the chase, not just as rams. 35 CARS, OMGWTF. They utilize all techniques previously mentioned and the helicopter has more fuel so it can chase you longer... if you manage to get away from these cops you are indeed a master of this game.

There's a little chase meter when you get into a pursuit. As you dodge and evade the cops, the meter will change to "evade", or if you start to drive too slowly, it will go to "busted" and if it stays in busted too long... well you get busted. You get infractions as you dodge the cops, and everytime you get an infraction you get fines against your car. If you get busted, you will get an impound strike marker against your car and you'll have to pay all fines against your car at that point. If you get 3 impound strikes, your car gets impounded and you will never see it again. If you lose sight from the cops long enough, the chase meter will be replaced with a cooldown meter, and it will fill up as you stay out of sight. If you go to certain parts of the map, (hiding spots), your cooldown meter will fill up 2x as fast. When it fills up, you have successfully evaded the pursuit and it will give you a detailed listing of all bounty and infractions recorded. If they find you during cooldown mode, you have to dodge them all over again.

When you have a high heat level, take your car to the one-stop shop and change the look of your car to lower it. Paint changes lower it teh most, so choose a color, buy it, then go back in and change it again to lower your heat level really fast. Another way that is slower but it doens't cost you money is to simply park your car and use another one for a while. As you win races with that car, the heat on the other car will slowly but surely go down.

The down side to the game: stripped down customization. There are significantly less parts, and less categories to edit entirely. Some of them were actually kinda good, like removing custom headlights/tailights, spinning rims, sound systems in the trunk, spinning rims, and side mirrors. I mean really, who wants custom side mirrors? The only good thing they removed was neon. Neon added more color and style to your ride, and when matched to the right color paint, it looked awesome! But there's no neon! >:( And tuning your car has been downgraded as well. It's just a shoddy little menu on the pause screen, there are less categories, and way less detail to it.

This is by far my longest post! I hope you have read my review of the game.

Has anyone else played this game? And if so, what do you like about it?
