The Linear Map Competition


I believe that linear maps are vastly under appreciated due to their being used most by n00bs.

Linear maps can be great in the right hands (i.e. Cerberus,) but too few talented authors use them often enough.

Therefore, I'm starting this competition.


Linear Maps, by this Competition's Standards:
  • ONLY one path, not including for gold. Gold may be placed slightly off-path.
  • Path is always clear
  • Slight doubling back is acceptable, but try to keep it minimal, unless it's a there-and-back
Hm. Thought there was more
Rules not Regarding Map Style:
  • Competitors may submit up to five maps
  • Maps may be submitted as a .txt in this thread or on NUMA
  • An APG (All Possible Gold) Demo must be Submitted with the map
  • Map(s) must be submitted by February 1st
  • If submitted to NUMA, include tags "Linear" and "LMC"
Judging:
I will need four more judges (assuming this takes off) as well as myself to judge on these factors:
  • Fun. /50
  • Aesthetics (sorry Twistkill!). /25
  • Creativity. /25
For a total out of one hundred.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT RULE:
  • Have fun!
