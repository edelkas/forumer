Quote: pembie, May 29, 2006 04:52 pm
come to astralia and you'll the know what real pie is. Steak pie, beef pie, stake bacon cheese pie, (anything) and meat pie.

Speaking from experience, 'real pie' is about 30% pastry, 50% gravy and 19% miscellaneous gristle. I recommend the beef or steak pies. Unless it actually specifies which animal it comes from, meat pies can and do contain pretty much any kind of meat, including but not limited to camel, goat, buffalo, soy protein and human flesh.* Of course, they still taste OK because they're mostly gravy, but many people would find eating soy protein morally distasteful. At least with beef and steak you get decent sized lumps of real meat instead of unspecified gristle.

And nobody has mentioned the savoury goodness of that all-time classic, egg and bacon pie! So delicious, yet so nourishing! So easy to make! What can compare to the delicate blending of perfectly cooked bacon with the finest of pastry and the very best eggs?


*This is true. Well, except for the human flesh. Ask the Australian Consumer's Association. The legal definition of meat also includes pretty much everything that isn't classified as 'offal' (brain, heart, kidneys etc.), meaning snouts, tendons and blood vessels can be used to fill a meat pie. If your religion forbids eating animal X, or it just makes you feel squeamish, eating a meat pie is probably not a good idea.
