#63801 4 2005-05-05T02:45:33Z Guest
actually the some-DDAs-don't-work problem isn't related to the simultaneous-collision-non-death thing..

the problem with DDAs is that in debug mode, when you die, enemies react differently (i.e they continue shooting at you/etc.) than in "real" mode, where they stop shooting.

the reason for this is that it's very useful, when testing levels, to be able to re-spawn the ninja without having to reset the level.. so we just let the debug-mode enemies continue carrying on as if you were alive. whereas in the real game, once you die, they "idle"...

we actually were very confused for a couple hours when we first saw this problem, until we finally figured out what was going on. sadly, there's really no way to fix this, other than for users to run DDAs in debug mode.

sorry!
