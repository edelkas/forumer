I don't know what th/\t is about, but I'll answer the rest of it. In the highscores menu, there is a submit button right above the 'personal bests' section. If you don't see it, wait until the online highscores appear. If you still don't see it, then you don't have a high score in that episode which means you suck and I personally dislike you just a cilantro bit more. ...just kidding (about disliking you, the rest is all true).

EDIT: Oh right, forgot about boss mode. It's there in case you're playing at work and your boss walks by. You just press ctrl and nobody has to know that you're playing a game. It also cuts down the amount of the your processor being taken up by N. If you never use it (like me), go to the configure menu and change it to something else, like 'b'.
