Ok, so I've had this idea for about a year now, and I tried presenting it to the higher powers (e.g. Blizz) a long time ago, but it never got anywhere. It's a massive contest and sort of an RPG at the same time, but has the potential (I think) to be really fun, considering it can be run as long as people are still interested, has no limits on participation, and can even be joined at any time. Wanna hear it? Ok, here it goes.

You are a Neditor. You have recently begun your quest to become the most skilled of all Neditors, and have journeyed to Neditor Nation. Here, you compete against other Neditors by making maps. To assert your supremacy in Neditor Nation, you must out-map some of the best Neditors around. It wont be easy, either. The road to becoming Champion is a long and rigorous one are you ready?

So, think Pokmon. The Neditors you have to out-map are the eight Gym Leaders, plus the Elite Four. In defeating the Elite Four, a Neditor becomes Champion, and defends his title against those who defeat the Elite Four after him. If he loses to such a challenger, the challenger succeeds him as Champion and the process continues. Thats the great thing about the contest. You can jump in anytime you like, take as long as you want (if youre not in the race to become the first Champion), and the contest can be ended whenever it is necessary. Now Ill go into the rules:

Rules:
All Neditors would have to sign up in a specific thread. This is so I could add them to an Excel Worksheet, which I would use to monitor the progress of the entire Neditor Nation. Its an easy way to prevent Neditors from cheating by trying to skip Gyms and whatnot. Speaking of which, a Gym challenge would work like this:
  • A Gym has been set up with its Leader before the start of the contest (In its own thread? I dont know.). Each of the eight Gyms represents a different N enemy: gauss, rockets, mines, etc. The Gym Leader will make a map prior to the start of the contest that fits two specific guidelines:
    -- The map must only use the enemy specified by the Gym, and
    -- Another specification chosen by the gym-leader, i.e. jump-only, e-tiles only, etc.
  • A Judge would score this map before the start of the contest. The map and score then serve as a standard that Neditors will be trying to exceed in the contest. To do so, a Neditor must make a map that meets the two requirements of the Gym (the enemy choice and the other, unique specification used in the Gym Leaders map) and outscore the Leaders map.
  • If the challenger is not successful, they can try again. However, a challenger cannot post a new map until his last one has been scored by the Judge.
If the challenger is successful, he has cleared the Gym and is able to move on to the next one. After clearing all eight Gyms, a Neditor will challenge the Elite Four. In this challenge, the members of the Elite Four agree upon any two specifications (There need not be an enemy choice, but there could be.), and together they make a map using these two specifications to serve as the standard for their challenge. A Judge would then score it, and Neditors would challenge the Elite Four in the same way as they would a Gym Leader, by making a map to exceed the standard set by the challenge. This leads me to scoring
  • A rubric for ALL maps would need to be agreed upon. It should probably be something simple, like an overall scale of 0 to 20.
  • We would need many judges for this to work to cover up for absences. Before the start of the contest, all judges would need to score all of the Gym Leaders maps and the Elite Four map. That way, any Judge can score a challenger in any Gym. This helps keep the ball rolling.
  • Each time a new Champion is crowned, all the Judges would have to judge the Champions map. This leads me to the job of the Champion
The Champion is an ordinary Neditor who has successfully completed all eight Gyms and has surpassed the Elite Four. The first Champion attains his position with no further Challenges after the Elite Four. After the first Champion is crowned, he must defend his title against other prospective Champions (other Neditors who have defeated the Elite Four). To do so, he makes a map with two specifications, like the Elite Four does prior to the start of the competition. The Judges all score this map, and other Neditors can then challenge the Champion by making a map that meets his specifications. If, when the Judge scores the challengers map, it scores higher than the map of the Champion, the challenger becomes the new Champion, and the old Champion is eliminated from the contest permanently.

Heres an example to explain the game, as I understand its very complicated
  • Neditor challenges Gym Leader. Neditor makes a map encompassing the two specifications set by the Gym Leader. Judge then scores Neditors map, compares that score with HIS OWN score for the Gym Leaders map, and determines the winner. If Neditor succeeds, he advances to the next Gym.
  • If Neditor surpasses all eight Gyms in such a manner, Neditor challenges Elite Four. Neditor makes a map encompassing the Elite Fours two specifications. Judge then scores Neditors map, compares that score with the score HE GAVE the Elite Fours map, and determines the winner. If Neditor succeeds and is the first to surpass the Elite Four, he becomes Champion uncontested.
  • If Neditor is not the first Champion, Neditor must challenge the reigning Champion for his position. Upon becoming Champion, the Champion set two specifications, made a map encompassing those specifications, and had it scored, setting the standard. Neditor, upon challenging the Champion, makes a map encompassing the Champions specifications and has his map scored against the standard. If Neditor is successful, he is the new Champion, and the old Champion may no longer participate in the League (having achieved everything already). If unsuccessful, Neditor may try again.
  • Again, a challenger may not post a map to retry a Gym or other challenge until his first map is scored by a Judge. This keeps the Judges from becoming hopelessly backed up with challengers maps.
  • While this whole proceeding is going on, I will keep tabs on every Neditor using an Excel Worksheet. This prevents cheating and makes it easy to hand out prizes when the contest is ended.
Did somebody say Prizes? (Need a go-ahead from the global mods for some and help from imagers for others)
  • For the First Champion: Custom Title and a Champion Sig Tag
  • For the Longest-Reigning Champion (Champion with the most wins against challengers before being defeated): Custom Title and a Champion Sig Tag
  • For the other Champions: Champion Sig Tags
  • For everyone else: A sig tag showing how many Gyms they cleared while competing in the contest.
  • The Gym Leaders, Elite Four, and Judges should get a little something too, methinks.
Other Ideas:
  • Scrimmages? Neditors would challenge each other for fun, setting a specification each. The two specifications would then have to be encompassed in both Neditors maps, and a third Neditor would judge the maps. Records could be compiled, and it would add another dimension, such as player rankings, to the contest.
  • Gym Leaders, the Elite Four, Judges, and myself could swap the judging roles around to allow for everyone to join in on the game
A few notes:
  • In my opinion, Gym Leaders should represent the best mappers out there. Therefore, they will be selected and not volunteered. It's a lot more epic if you topple mappers like atob, blue_tetris, and the other greats to become Champion, isn't it? Note that all the Gym Leaders do is have to make one map, so hopefully we can still see participation from the veterans who are a bit less active than the rest of us. Judges and I will handle the bulk of the work.
  • Speaking of which, we need a lot of judges to make this work. That's where the rest of the community comes in. If we get enough, the Judges should be able to rotate duties to challenge the Gyms themselves.
  • I'm going to get some concept art up on Imageshack to give any helpful imagers a place to start. My work is mostly done in MS Paint and is therefore rather subpar.
Any questions? I tried my best to put this rather complex idea into words, but there are probably places where I fail to get my point across and details I forgot to mention. Feel free to post with any questions or ideas. Feedback is really what I'm looking for here, not sign-ups or anything. I'd love to get this going because it'd be something that could last all summer and beyond, but I'll need your feedback and your help.

#445143 60 2008-06-16T01:21:29Z Pheidippides
Wow, a lot of questions. I'll try to answer as best as I can. Here we go:

The Gym Leaders keep the same maps the whole time and are in a set order. Palemoon's example there with Charmander is spot on.

When a Neditor challenges a Gym, the Judge compares the score he gave the Neditor with the score he gave the leader. For example, if the Judge gave the Leader a 17/20, and gave the Neditor a 15/20, the Neditor loses.

The Elite Four depends on how many leaders we get. If we get enough, it would be a four-way collab map. If not, it could be a sort of ninth Gym Leader, with one mapper. Yes, the four-way would be tough to beat, but the +2 AGD bonus (great idea, palemoon) will help alleviate the difficulty to a beatable level.

A map is judged on overall quality on a scale of 1 to 20.

About scrimmaging, I was thinking of an "overall score" sort of feature to rank the top 10 or 20 or however many players. Scrimmage wins and losses would help/hurt your overall score, but wouldn't impede your progress in the game. I was thinking of using an equation. I'm just spitballing here, but maybe something like: (Gyms completed x 100) + (Scrimmage wins x 10) - (Scrimmage losses x 10) + (Gym AGDs x 10). That could use refining, but I like the idea.
