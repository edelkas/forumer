Hello, everyone, and welcome to Portfolio, an image contest that has been drafted and will be maintained by southpaw.

You might be thinking how this contest differs from other image contests from the past year or so. In this contest you are not making only signatures. There will be much more of an emphasis on design and quality of multiple image styles rather than focusing on making sig images or editing pictures. Here you will be making a collection of images ranging from sigbars to advertisements. The idea of Portfolio is to develop your imaging skills by making a large set of images with some guidelines and rules.
Spoiler: Week 1Image for Week 1 is due by 11:59:59 PM (PST), Monday, April 7th, 2008

This week we will start out with something small to get us all warmed up. Not all of us have been constantly making images *glances at T3chno* so we'll take this week to get our feet wet.

Your first assignment is to create a signature that represents something personal and important. For example, if you love martial arts, or anime or Sharpies, then make a sig about it. If your dog has always been a close friend of yours then by all means incorporate him/her in there. If you love alcohol by all means, make a sig focused around alcohol. There are no limits.*

Requirements for Week 1:
  • Your sig must incorporate no more than 1 (one) thing that is personal and meaningful to you.
  • Sig size requirements must not exceed 600 pixels wide by 200 pixels tall and shall not be smaller than 100 pixels wide by 100 pixels tall.
Please PM me the final results, so as not to clutter up this thread. I'll be saving all of your images in your own seperate folders on my computer for your final Portfolio.

Good luck,
-southpaw-

Welcome to Week 2! Glad to see the lot of you get in your images. Last week was great and I hope that this week turns out the same level, if not better, of quality.

This week we'll be focusing on music and album art. Your challenge for Week 2 is to create an album cover for your favorite band. For example, if you liked Hybrid Theory but didn't like the album art, redesign it. Or if you like Linkin Park in general you can come up with an original album art and put on a CD name that you think is fitting.

Requirements for Week 2:
  • Your album art must have the name of the artist and the name of the CD somewhere in a font that is legible. This means it can be fairly small but I must be able to read what the album name is and the artist name.
  • Your image cannot be larger than 12 centimeters (cm) wide by 12 cm tall.
  • You can create art for a fictional album and/or a fictional band, or you can recreate the album art of an album you like or enjoy.
Good luck to you all,

Welcome to Week 3 of Portfolio. For those of you that managed to complete your images for Week 2, congratulations. There was a wide range of style and design that I enjoyed looking at each and every one of the images that were PM'd or posted here in the thread.

Moving toward a more design aspect of the competition, this week we will be designing images using a stock image, which I have linked at the end of the post.

Logotypes (otherwise known as a logo) are designs that are intended to target a specific audience and/or get your attention toward a certain product. This week we will be designing an advertisement using a stock image.

Requirements for Week 3:

Spoiler: Everything you wanted to know about the contest------------
A quick thanks to incluye for helping me clean up the description of the rules
Rules and Procedures of Portfolio:
  • • Each image must be ORIGINAL. This means that you CANNOT submit an image that you have already made.• Try and push your comfort zone when making the images but also feel that you did your best job.• Each image MUST meet its requirements. If I ask you to make a wallpaper with an orange bicycle in it, it has to have an orange bicycle in it. If I give size requirements it is expected that you follow those as well.• If I think that you haven't fulfilled the requirements, you have one extra day to finish the image. This does not mean that you can just turn your image in a day late. If you don't turn in your image in time, it will not be considered.• Nobody will be eliminated. This is not an elimination contest, rather it is a challenge amongst everyone.• The contest will run for 8 weeks. Each week there will be something due, so by the end of the contest you will hopefully have made at least 8 images fulfilling all the requirements of each week.• Each week will end on a Monday allowing time over the weekend to complete anything that one may be missing.• I will post the requirements for the following few weeks to give each person enough time to complete every image required. I would like each image and each week to become progressively harder to complete, meaning more time would be required to complete the image. The due dates will be set, but if you finish your image a week ahead of the due date I will accept it.• You may post your work elsewhere before submission so as to get feedback on it, but once you have officially submitted it there can be no other changes unless I feel it has not met the requirements.• Once all the images have been completed and the contest portion is over I will compile each persons images into a single portfolio, one for each contestant. From here there will be a public vote on which contestant's Portfolio is the best of the bunch.• The voting will be held for 1 single week. The person with the most votes at the end of that week will be the Portfolio Champion and will earn their spot within the community as a prestigious image maker.• If you managed to have missed a week without notifying me and getting permission to finish it later then I will be removing 1 vote per image that is not turned in.
Note that since there is no judging criteria your images will have to appeal to the majority of people rather than a few judges. This takes the power of a few and puts it into the hands of many. Plus it involves the community rather than just those who sign up.

***Prizes:
  • 1st Place - A DED mappack by Frets and southpaw themselves, a sigabar (includes signature image, matching sigtag and matching avatar), and a custom title, courtesy of LittleViking!
  • 2nd Place - An episode DED in the same mappack and a sigatar (signature image and matching avatar)
  • 3rd Place - A level DED in the mappack and a sig
