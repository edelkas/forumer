For those of you running Leopard, (maybe earlier OS versions too... I don't know) you can use the webclip widget in the Dashboard to easily view the current featured map. Simply go to NUMA, Click: File>Open in Dashboard... then move your mouse on top of the name of the featured map and click on it. All you have to do then is press "Add" up in the upper right hand corner of the purple drop down toolbar. The Dashboard will open, the webclip will load, and you will have a very simple way of checking up on NUMA.
