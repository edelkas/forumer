#20582 1 2008-06-20T18:23:17Z Skyline
What if you ignored all the rules and restrictions of map making? What if you ignored all of the conventional rules of aesthetics and gameplay, or just ignored aesthetics and gameplay altogether? What if you just put down tiles and objects however they came into your mind, not worrying about anything else? So, yeah. Try to do that here. Just make whatever is on your mind.

Is this horridly and absurdly pretentious? Oh god, yes. But try to enjoy the freedom involved. These maps aren't to be judged or criticized. They're just meant to be made and played for the hell of it.

Post your own maps.
