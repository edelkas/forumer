Rule of Law
Follow the Laws and you won't lose.

Current task: Vote by 7/19 on the final round!

This may seem like any ordinary mapmaking contest, but it's not. Each round, the loser of the previous round posts a Law, or mapmaking rule to follow. There are two types of Laws: type Laws and number Laws. Type Laws restrict a certain kind of object or tile, such as "no left-facing thwumps" or "no 5 tiles." Number laws limit the total number of an object you can use, such as "must have exactly eight mines." Non-enemies in number Laws can only go up to 20, and enemies (mines not included) in number laws may only go up to 5. Just by following the round's Law, you get points.
This is an elimination type contest. The loser of the round will be determined by both judging and voting. This will continue until we have two mapmakers left.
In the final round, the third-place person chooses a Law, then the remaining two competitors give each other a Law to follow. The winner will once again be chosen by judging and vote.

Sounds interesting, but what if the loser of a round posts a lame law? And much more importantly, what's to prevent the two finalists from giving each other terrible laws to follow?

The deal with the Laws is that you can only modify an object. Example Laws:

- Must have 6 bounceblocks on map
- Cannot use right-facing thumps

For the first one, if it's an object limit, you can only go to 10.
For the second, you can't restrict an object all together, but you can restrict forms of it.
Plus, they only have one Law to follow (two for final round, and they aren't picked by the same person), so there's no huge restrictions. Also, if the Laws conflict, then I'll just pick a new one instead of waiting for the person to respond.
