I have an idea that could serve multiple purposes. It would be elevator tiles.

Wait! Don't go.
These would be half full tiles (like type 5) with ropes leading to the ceiling to designate them as not normal floors. When you hit a switch it moves upwards until some part of it touches another tile (note: several of these next to each other count as one elevator). Once there it goes down until it hits something. While moving the switch does nothing (this is, as far as I can tell, the only thing we can do that would make a multiple use switch work, because if it was a door the game would open/close it every frame while n is on the switch. Also would work with drones flipping switches). Note that this does not necessarily squish n on the part of the elevator where tiles meet, since you could do two half iles that produce a full N height between. It moves at the same speed as a sentry drone, and its momentum is added to n's if he jumps on a moving platform.

These do produce several benifits. First, you can have creative puzzles where you must get the right combination of elevators. Or actually work a drone herding or "controll room" map into the real game (if they can flip switches). Or make interesting situations where you need the added height of an elevator in the "down" position to reach a platform, or perhaps even have to walljump off of one (still or moving), or climb onto a moving one. And the momentum thing leads to interesting timing levels, where you have to flip the switch and get across the map to land on the elevator before it meets you at the top so you can use its momentum for a jump. Etc.
