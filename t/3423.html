So I guess what I'm seeing is correct.

From what I have seen, the behavior for surface follow ccw is like this:

If you chose starting to the right, it goes up, if you chose up it goes left, if left, down, if down, right.
In other words, if you do a list of all directions (up, left, down, right), it does the next one of what you chose.

Once started, its motion will be moving one tile in the current direction, then change to the next one, in CCW fashion.

And that's it, it moves in an area of 4 tiles (2x2)

But...

If any of those 4 tiles is occupied by any kind of tile, then it starts to follow around the surface, in CCW fashion.

If any of you having problems with this follows these rules, I think you could make your maps more predictable. And if you can confirm this theory, let me know

Anyway, I just wanted to see if this behavior was normal and I guess it is.
