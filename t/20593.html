#387910 4 2007-12-10T03:40:55Z Atilla
You need to have Caps Lock on to play in debug mode.

To play the level outside debug mode, you copy the level data and paste it into "userlevels.txt" in the format $Level Name#Author#Category#Level Data#. The data must be after the line "&userdata=", and userlevels.txt must be in the same folder as N.

The clipboard is the space where Windows temporarily stores things you've cut or copied, ready for pasting.
