#185938 2 2006-06-07T19:56:30Z Xév
To make a drone stand still, you need to delete the drone, and put it back in (To make sure it is at the end of the level code.) Then, backspace the last number in the code and change it to NaN. Click outside the box and load the level. When you press play, the drone will be motionless.
