Hi, Dusty here. I made a coupla levels, and I'd like some reviews before submitting them to NUMA. I'm working on another. Here's the basics of them:

The Golden Age: First N-level I made. Basically, playable N-art; lots of cool shapes. Kinda pointless but there is something satisfying about getting all that gold.

Namesake: Again, same thing, less gold but with a tileset saying "Dusty96" and a smilie face out of gold.

Far-Flung: A level with a bunch of cloud shaped tiles and a coupla birds/balloons made out of gold. As the title and content implies, you're up in the sky. Not very long to complete, but kinda fun.

Tributary: A text tileset I made for the person that referred me to N (Greg from Kongregate.) Text says "Thanks to Greg for telling me about N." Lots of gold, so don't play this if you've got a slow computer.

Supersize: I think this is one of my greatest accomplishments. It's a hollowed-out picture of the Ninja, maybe 200 times as big as the real Ninja is, with 3 hollowed-out mines that you can't touch (200 times as big as the real mines) and 2 pieces of gold (one you can collect, one you can't) and an exit switch (the blue part is a lot of zap drones, I wish I could figure out how to make them stay put). Play it to see what I mean.

Optical Delusion: A sort of checkerboard level. 2 thwumps, some launchpads you should NOT touch from the side, and some gold. 4 doors surrounding the exit switch, too. Look at the level as a whole, and figure out why it's called Optical Delusion..

Dodge 'em: This one is an edited version of a friend's (Hypronelite) level. The tileset is the same, except for the maze part with the door. And, the enemies and gold are all mine; it had too many enemies so I stripped the whole thing of all its objects. I have the old version if you want it, just ask me.

Cross Country: A little level where you have to dodge the mines and get the gold if you can. I should have put some more mines on the lower level; once you get down there, if your jump key is working you can beat it. Expect an updated level soon.

Madness Interactive: I named this level "Madness Interactive" as both a pun on the game and a literal depiction of the level. Madness, indeed.. It's so hard to decide; do you get the scattered gold, get one switch and go for the room of gold, get all the switches and go for the room of gold, strip the whole thing of its gold, or just trigger the switch and get the heck outta there? You decide. It's harder than it looks..


Anyway, those are all my levels. Code is enclosed in the document (don't worry, it's not a computer worm) Put it into userlevels.txt and enjoy!

#317196 6 2007-05-01T00:29:12Z Mr__X
The ones I tried were actually okay, if they were on NUMA, I'd give them a 3.

Just some advice.

- Don't cluster gold. Some gold=more time=good. Too much gold=lag=bad.
- I'm not sure if you did or not, but make the tileset before you put objects in, and spend as much time on the tileset as you do on the objects.
- Make sure everything has a use. You should be able to get every gold piece, and every enemy should be able to kill you.

There are some threads where experts talk about how to make good levels, but I don't know the links to them. Hopefully someone below me can post them.

Pretty good, but there's always room for improvement!

#317269 8 2007-05-01T02:40:56Z Kablizzy
Mmkay. I'm going to critique these like I would a Legacy level, hopefully this helps -

The Golden Age: Way, way too much gold. And no enemies makes this a tad tedious. With that said, jumping around is fun as Hell. Neat tileset to toy with, but yeah, adding some challenges and lessening the gold a lot would help immensely. Also, be careful to click out of the loading textbox in NED. You NaN'd a bounceblock (Which means the code bugs and it appears outside of the playable area).

Namesake: Nice lettering, but again, more enemies, less ambient objects. Try and go for a strategic placement of things - As things are with this, things look randomly placed.

Far-Flung: Again, very nice concept with the playable N-Art. This one is the best of the playable N-Arts.

Tributary: Nice play on words for the title. As with the first three, I'd say they're more meant for looking at than for playing - these first four, at least, would go better on NUMA than here on the forums. As for the gold halo, neat effect, but for a playable map, s'just way, way too much.

Supersize: I think these would all be better as non-playable N-arts, for play purposes. There are just way too many objects in these maps for them to be considered playable. I think they'd be better suited as plain eye-candy.

Optical Delusion: Ah, here we go. For a strictly playable map, way too many objects, mostly the gold. The gameplay gets tedious because it's such a large map, so I think this would do well as a smaller map (Perhaps even half as big). Great theme to it, and pretty good for an action level.

Dodge 'em: Nice little action map here, but again, way way too much gold. Enemy placement was good.

Cross Country: Neat idea, but it seems like you wanted to make three distinct maps out of this.

Madness Interactive: Very cool map - You have some things to learn about placement and aesthetics, but you're well on your way to making some good maps. Again, watch the textbox when you're loading your maps, you NaN'd two objects - Gold and a Mine, so be sure to click outside of the textbox before you load. Beyond the room full o' gold, this was quite fun. Just keep at it - And if you need further clarification, don't be afraid to ask, I'll be glad to help.
