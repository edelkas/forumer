It's usually not that it's faster to not get the gold, but that no-one has currently found a way of incorporating the gold into their route.

Like, if there is a left route and a right route. On one route it would take 13 seconds to get 12 seconds of gold, the other route might be much slower without the gold but only take 11.5 seconds with the gold.
