All levels are saved as text files. To play open Ned, put the text in the top box then click in the other text box and press L then P. (and make sure Caps lock is on.)
#129495 3 2006-01-03T18:18:43Z Jiggerjaw
Also, you can copy the level data into the file userlevels.txt in this format:
$Map Name#Author#Category#Map Data#
Then go into N, press userlevels, and press refresh list to play the map you've made.
