The task that was set...

The MiniMaps Contest

Judges:
Sweep
Lucidium
astheoceansblue (backup judge (judges any maps that judges submit))

At the beginning of time, man started to make stuff. Robots. Small cakes. Small robot cakes. Soon, man became bored with this process. He needed a change... and so, he just made everything smaller. Mini-robots. Mini small cakes. Mini small robot cakes. The process was an exciting one, and man found such a fascination in these mini creations that he continued making things smaller and smaller until he discovered that those tiny buttons on mobile phones are just not made for the fat human digits.

Anyhoo, that's neither here nor there. To the point.

There have been many classic maps for the game N over the past few years. Mount Doom springs to mind, as does Mother Thwumping Impossible and, naturally, the entirety of Yanu's collection. They may be classic, but they have one major flaw. They are not mini.

And so, this is your task: to recreate a map of your choice that we all know from NUMA, or the actual game itself, inside a map arena half the size of the one you're familiar to. This isn't simply about copying the map, it's about transferring and translating the gameplay and style to a smaller arena. Points are awarded for the difficulty of the challenge you take on, the extent to which you translated the gameplay and style to the smaller area, and generally how well the map plays.

Rules[/b]
- Most importantly, the minimap must be made within an area of 16 tiles wide by 12 tiles high.
- You can recreate any map that you want, but it must be either very widely known or on NUMA. If possible, you should provide a link to the map which you are mimicking.
- All entries must be submitted to NUMA, between the dates of 22nd of February and 21th of March
- It is your responsibilty to put "dibs" on doing a certain map. More than one of a particular map is allowed, the first submission may get more credit, depending on how similar the maps are.
- The map you mimic must not be your own.
- Maximum of 3 entries per person.
- The map should be entered as a Test Level unless the author of the original authorises you to do otherwise.

Judging[/b]
Each minimap is judged three times, each by a different judge. The criteria are as follows:
Translation quality/50 - the extent/quality of the similarity to the original map in terms of gameplay, feel, looks etc. Nothing more than half marks can be achieved if the map only looks like its predecessor.
Overall quality/25 - how good the minimap is, regardless of what it was trying to mimic.
Difficulty/25 - higher ratings are awarded for maps that the judge feels are harder to translate over to the smaller space.
Total/100
Overall Total/300
