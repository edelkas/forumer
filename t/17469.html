I found this on accident. But it is the coolest thing I found. I tried to translate some foreign text earlier today. So I copied it into the translator. But I held the mouse button down on the translator widget and then pressed the hotkey to return to the Desktop. Then it happened. The widget was dragged onto my Desktop. I dropped my mouth at the result.

For those who want to do this: Go into Dashboard (F12 is the default hotkey), click and hold onto the widget you want, move it around a bit, and then press the Dashboard hotkey again. Now let go of the mouse.

Here's picture proof. A calculator widget on the Desktop.
