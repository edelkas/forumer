This is something me and my friend Delgadude studied for awhile. Through strange glitches and manual code editing, you can create all the effects seen on the demo in the attached file.
In the same order as the demo, here are the glitches:

1. Offset placement of one-way (horizontal)
Created by: Manual code editing
How-to: First, you should know that it works best to use this glitch with a whole row of one-ways. So first, you should make your row of one-ways, while placing the wall that you want to be glitched slightly off. This is what you should have:

Next, exit the object editor by pressing Home. (Before you continue, make sure that your row of walls was placed last and your offset wall was placed last of all.) Go to the bottom of the code and look for repeating numbers:
Quote
7^156,324,3!7^180,324,3!7^204,324,3!7^252,324,3!7^276,324,3!7^300,324,3!7^228,318,3
Highlighted in red is your offset variable. Change this number to something even closer to the repeating digits, such as 324.01 or 323.99 (I'd suggest putting it within .01 in order to make it appear connected). Now your walls should look like they're going in a straight line. However, when N walks over the edited wall, he's sure to fall unless he decides to jump!

2. Wall-jump through the ceiling
Created by: In-game glitch
How-to: All you really need to do in this glitch is put one-way walls facing down with a wall you can wall-jump from next to it. Example:

To activate this glitch, jump into the corner where the one-way ceiling and your wall-jump surface meet. You must be as close to this corner as possible. Simply wall-jump next to get through.

3. Offset placement of one-way (vertical)
Created by: Manual code editing
How-to: Creating this glitch is done the same way as glitch 1. Activating it is different, however: First, your glitched one-way must not be at the very top (it must have a wall you can wall-slide on above it). In order to go through it, get as close as possible to the wall above it. Wall-sliding is the most reliable way to do this. Make sure you're pushing the arrow key against the wall as you slide. When you hit the glitched wall, you'll slip right through!

4. Gold delay through the wall
Created by: Gold delay
How-to: Just place a gold piece 1/4 below the one-way wall. Example:
-Activated simply by walking or wall-sliding on the one-way.

5. Walk through one-way on a slope
Created by: Non-natural angle
How-to: Make a slope that touches the one-way wall. Example:
-Activated by walking under the ceiling while on the slope.

6. Thin Invisible Surface
Created by: Excess surface on one-way
How-to: This is a very useful glitch for getting out of a tight squeeze where walljumping isn't available. Make a real wall (not a one-way) that goes up. Put a one-way wall just inside it, so that you can't see any of it, but it's touching the edge where wall and air meet. If you're using other Ned tiles besides E, you might be able to see it. Put one of these one-ways every 1-3 spaces. When using this, hold the arrow key against the wall the entire time; jump to find the next invisible surface. This also works on the floor, except here you'll just be stopped from walking a certain direction (you'll need to jump just a little).
Also, you can connect one-ways with up to 3/4 grid space between each one, and still have a completely flat surface due to this.

7. Slip through one-way via connected slope
Created by: Non-natural angle
Partial credit to exuberance for pointing out the fact that I missed this glitch.
How-to: Place a slope next to a one-way wall floor. Walk very slowly up the slope, and when you reach the one-way where the slope tiles end and there is space behind it, you'll tip off the edge (or if you're using this to go through a wall, you'll just fall through.) This is possibly due to the extra surface past the visible one-way wall.

8. Thwump through a floor/ceiling
Created by: In-game glitch
Partial credit to exuberance (ditto #7)
How-to: Simply make a thwump that goes through your one-way surface upon falling. Get onto the thwump, then stay on it until you pass through the wall. If you're going through a floor rather than a ceiling, just stay below the thwump (which should be facing up). IMPORTANT: If you're going through a ceiling, make sure to JUMP as soon as you're getting squeezed between the thwump and the one-way, or else it might get messy...

9. Usage of door through a surface
Created by: Non-natural angle
Full credit to exuberance for discovering this.
How-to: Use 1/2-snap to place your surface so that it's not on a full snap, but rather halfway. Then put a trapdoor, locked door, or normal door the same way as your one-way. Example:


10. Bounce block through a surface
Created by: In-game glitch
How-to: Place a bounce block one tile away from an opposing one-way surface. Make the bounce block move around so that you can touch the surface of the one-way and have the bounce block hit you. When that happens, you'll slip through.

11. Cliff-hanging through oneway
Created by: Non-natural angle
Note: This glitch is very similar to number 9.
When you place your one-way, make note of its position. If you put a slope or sharp edge less than 1 grid-snap away from it, you can fall through by touching it. Examples:
<Any of the ninjas can get through those one-ways unconventionally.

12. One-way teleportation brake
Created by: Lack of pulverization
Ordinarily a teleporter can kill you by slamming you hard into the solid wall or ceiling, as well as the floor. However, if you put a one-way far away enough from the wall, it will stop the ninja in his tracks without killing him.

13. Specially placed one-ways
Created by: Unknown
The strangest one-way glitch I've ever seen. Place two one-ways: one with a y-coordinate of 264, and the other 132, both facing up. Fall straight off of the higher one onto the lower- you'll fall through with one frame where you're on it.

14. NaN'd position
Created by: NaN
Replace the last number in the code with a non-numeric value (NaN it). It will appear to be facing up, but it really acts as if it's pointing down.

15. Launchpad through one-way
Created by: Invincibility frame
Upon hitting a launchpad while next to a one-way, you will be able to go through the one-way. This is because one-ways are infinitely thin; the slightest propulsion beyond them will go unnoticed if you're invincible (as you are when you hit a launchpad).
Note: This glitch isn't yet in the demo.

13. Specially placed one-ways

The strangest one-way glitch I've ever seen. Place two one-ways: one with a y-coordinate of 264, and the other 132, both facing up. Fall straight off of the higher one onto the lower- you'll fall through with one frame where you're on it.
I can't tell you what causes this, but I can tell you that it must be at 156, not 132.
For the record, any one-ways arranged 108 pixels apart will do this.

EDIT: Also, N stays on there for two frames, not one.

EDIT: Apparently it isn't always 108. If the top one-way is above a coordinate of 160, you may have to add only 107.9, and even less as its coordinate increases.

EDIT: There is actually a range of values that work for each position of the top one-way, though N stays on for the shortest time for the value closest to the center of the range. (I hope that made sense...)

EDIT: Ignore my third edit; it may be irrelevant. Also note that if N lands on an upwards-facing one-way panel with a y-coordinate at 267.9, he will fall through.
