#241718 15 2006-10-15T22:10:06Z elaxion
Hey, your sigs look very nice, but one thing that could improve them is if you add effects over the render. Try placing your render in a new layer, then dup. it, do a motion blur to the left and right, another render layer, then do a cubism at 4.5, new render layer, brush some with colors in your render, set it to screen at 50 percent opacity, new render layer, dup., set to overlay. done! Just try it, it makes the render blend better.
#241743 16 2006-10-15T23:09:21Z catspride

#241769 17 2006-10-16T00:15:04Z elaxion
Ok, sure. Once you are about to anchor your reder to a new layer copy it. Ok, now you can anchor the render. Place a copy of the render on a new layer and do a motion blur filter--->blur---->motion blur, facing to the left. New render, then to the right. New render, cubisim filter at 4.5, new layer, brush with 2 colors from your render or background, set to screen, change opacity to your liking, repeat 3-10 times, place a new render, place a new render, set it to overlay. That should blend pretty well, but I can help you even more if you need it, theres a ton of other stuff you can do that makes it even better.
