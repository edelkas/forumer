#51748 16 2005-02-26T22:15:03Z LittleViking
Even easier to read.

Quote: "PC Gamer"
Quote: "Floorplans of death"
N is the physics-powered, minimalist Flash platform game in which a ten-pixel-tall ninja, N, jumps and flips around while evading murderous drones. It comes with Ned, the level-editor, and there's a huge community of hardcore N players (well, more hardcore) producing amazing new levels all the time.
Levels range from the quite literally imppossible Survival maps to the Newtonian spectable of 'DDA's. It stands for Don't Do Anything, and levels of this kind are Machiavellian Rube Goldberd devices of near-death, shoving and catapulting your ninja into deadly traps from which he is rescued at the last split-second by a Deus Ex Machina platform or jump-pad.
N itself is completely free and on the discs, along with these five brilliant levels and the next five best. Thousands more are to be had at numa.notdot.net.
Quote: "IT'S MY NIGHTMARE"
Every good DDA features some close shaves, but this one virtually skins N's chin. Author Sendy openly teases you by flinging N into situations you realise are fatal at the exact moment you're saved from them, riding an emotional rollercoaster at breakneck speeds.
Quote: "LIGHT FANTASTIC"
The Light Fantastic is a more stylised DDA. N is dropped into an arena dotted with no fewer than nine laser drones, and is shoved around by the friendly end of 'thwumps' while beautiful patterns of laser light converge angrily wherever he was a thirtieth of a second ago.
Quote: "MISSILE RACE"
Race levels are essentially regular levels with superior flow, meaning you can work up enormous velocity as you hurtle round them, bouncing off walls and using the game's physics to accelerate. As an added incentive, this one also has a swarm of homing missiles chasing you.
Quote: "CHALLENGE #9"
A series of ultra-simplistic levels by N-expert TKTKTK, the Simple Challenges are more like tutorials than conventional levels. Each of them seems utterly impossible at first, but once you grasp the principle he's trying to teach, they're merely very difficult. Think fast.
Quote: MOUNT DOOM
Hard beyond words, but one of the most satisfying levels. The red mines that make up the 'lava' are so dense and devilishly placed only the nimblest of ninjas will find a path through to the top. Once inside, you've got a Gatling Drone and Thwumping stalactites to face.
