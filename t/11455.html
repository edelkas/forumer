Questions That Haven't Been Asked Frequently But Undoubtably Will Be:

Q: WTF?!

A: A small cottage made of teapots.

Q: How do I distinguish a level pack from other packs?

A: Thrust your hand vigourously towards it. If your arm suddenly goes AWOL, it's a pack of wolves. If your fingers are kissed, you're talking to a politician, and it is therefore a pack of lies. If it ends up spread over the table, the floor, the bench, and the person sitting opposite you, it's a pack of cards. If you discover that you've just destroyed your $700 computer monitor, it's a level pack.

Q: Is this actually any good?

A: Play it and see. Some levels are good. Some are not so good. Many are debatable, depending on your tastes. This level pack is somewhat quick and experimental - if there are a significant number of levels that people like, I will consider making another pack with more exacting standards.

Q: How come you made a level pack?

A: Because I felt like it. Also, it lets you distribute levels which would not be appreciated on a "stand-alone" basis - those which are too short, too odd, too difficult or too easy to be submitted to NUMA.

Q: You just wanted to write this silly description, didn't you?

A: Lies! All lies!

Q: A couple of these maps look familiar.

A: That's a statement, O Foolish One, not a question.

Q: Why do a couple of these maps look familiar?

A: There are three I posted in the Neddis thread. The rest are all new, though.

Q: What's with the name?

A: Well, it's five episodes consisting of five levels each. A quintilogy of five, you see. I wanted to make it three episodes just so I could legitimately call it a trilogy of five, but I ended up with too many levels.

Q: So, are you a total lunatic or what?

A: I prefer to think of myself as eccentric. In truth, I am either a minor genius or mildly insane depending on your point of view. But then, what man could live without being a little insane?
