I've come up with an idea, a vision if you will. A website that links to the non-awful maps on NUMA. Here's the concept:

- To add links to maps on NUMA through the website, you have to register and be accepted. The result? A cleaner NUMA. NUMA without the crap. bitesizeNUMA. Any possible detrimental affects? Possibly new users not being introduced into the community?
