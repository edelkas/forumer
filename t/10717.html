Emo = Texas is the Reason, Far, Mineral, At the drive-in, Piebald, Jawbox, Rainer Maria, Commander Venus, The Get up Kids, Jimmy eat world...

Depends. Half the time I'm likely to go on a "It's a branch from Indie Pop, retitled emo, a title which was then applied to a subculture in society, which was then reapplied to music more suitable to this subculture. At its most pure, 'emo' music is essentially independant pop which contains certain, hard- to- describe characteristics- but NOT the heavy, punk- ish manner and insanely depressing lyrics of most commonly applied to this genre of music."

The other half of the time I'll be like, "Yeah, it's the stuff with lyrics about razorblades." Both definitions are fine with me.

Emo bands I like? Some of Dashboard Confessional's stuff is nice. Panic! At the Disco is catchy as hell, but- I admit it- not particularly. You know. Good. And I still love the accoustic version of Taking Back Sunday's "Cute without the e". Great fun to play on guitar, too.
Heh. Overall, though, emo music isn't my style. Give me mellow, accoustic pop any time.
