#362580 4 2007-09-06T02:03:17Z George
You'll have use Eeyore's N Level Data program to obtain the level data.

If you already know how to make maps, then you can ignore my instructions below.

1. Press the ~ key (Above the Tab key) to enter Debug mode.
2. Copy the data into the top textbox and then click outside the box.
3. Press L to load the data.
4. Press 1 to begin recording your demo.
5. Press P to begin playing, Caps Lock to switch between frame by frame and real time.
6. When you're finished, press 2.
7. If you die, you can reset everything by pressing R, then repeating the steps from 4.
8. When you're finished, copy the data from the lower textbox.

The format you should keep the data in is
$Level Name#Author#Map Type#Demo Data#

You can refer to the Editor's manual for more information.
