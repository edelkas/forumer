Im trying to keep away from making symmetrical maps as far as possible...why? I dunno, maybe cause people call them repetitive because its like almost the same thing on both sides.

Well, you can have maps which are symmetrical except for one or two objects - such as putting that switch you need to get on the bottom on one side and up the top on the other, which makes them play differently. Anyway, in most symmetrical maps, you interact with both sides at once, so you're not "repeating" anything.

Anyway, I make maps by messing around in Ned until I get an idea. Then I make a map, or a section of a map, followed by testing and altering it repeatedly until I like it.
