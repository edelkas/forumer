#8442 1 2006-05-03T09:49:03Z bhauth
I do not like Radiohead. I do not see their appeal. Please explain it to me.

I like The Heat of the Day by Pat Metheny because every instrument there has a beautiful sound and it is chock-ful of quality rapid-fire melody that every instrument present contributes to expertly at every opportunity, and every difficult phrase is performed with perfect timing.

I like Seresta, by Howard Levy and Manfredo Fest, performed by Bela Fleck, because it one of the most brilliant core counterpoint melodies I have ever heard, and the even nicer sound than the above of the instruments comes through perfectly. And again, flawless timing.

I like Brace Yourself (Reprise) by Mike Paradinas because it has complex, textured drums that are still rhythmic, a decent melody on top, and a warm sound.

I like Manze's performance of Geminiani's composition of Corelli's version of La Follia (on The Grand Tour among other discs) because in it Manze uses his great ability to explore all the best flourishes and variations of a timeless theme, with quality counterpoint backing.

Radiohead (as I see it) has simple looping drums that don't counterpoint the melody, annoying vocals and instrument sounds, little technical ability, simple overused guitar riffs, and a very small amount of melody stretched too thin. They are more creative in a "hey let's try this it'll be cool" way than an average pop band, and far far less produced, but otherwise pretty similar.

So explain to me why you find them good.
