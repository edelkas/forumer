What about it?
The Action-Packed Competition/Tournament is where users compete making maps and highscoring. The map that scores highest will be placed in front of the players. The user that achieves the highest time will win, as well as the user that made the map that scored the highest based on the criteria. Unlike a tournament, however, players will not be eliminated every round, because this isn't a ladder tournament. The player and map-maker with the most points by the end of the competition wins. This is a 9-week competition. Every week is a new round. While map-makers are making their maps for the next round, players will be submitting their demos from the previous round, so we can save time. For example, while map-makers are making their maps for Round 2, players are submitting their demos for the winning map on Round 1. You may enter in both positions (map-maker and player).

The Rules
Map-Makers
  • All maps must be submitted to NUMA.
  • All maps must be action. NO races, N-arts, tilesets, DDAs, survivals. Maps without an obvious flow can be accepted.
  • Maps cannot be maps already submitted to NUMA. They must be new maps made after a round has started.
  • Collabs are not allowed.
  • Maps must be appropriate for high-scoring (meaning that there may NOT be any obvious race elements in the map)
  • Maps will be judged based on the categories.
  • Map-makers have 1 week to submit their entries.
  • The highest and lowest judge scores for each map will be excluded (similar to the Olympics).
  • Judges may enter.
  • Map-makers may make as many as 2 maps.
  • If 2 or more maps get the same score, the winning map will be decided based on a poll.
Players
  • All demoes are to be completed in NReality. Further instructions can be found in the post below.
  • Absolutely NO FBF. Players caught offending will be automatically eliminated.
  • AGD's are not required.
  • Demos will be judged just like the highscoring system; the player with the most time wins. NO SPEEDRUNS!
  • Players have 1 week to submit their demoes.
  • Judges may enter.
  • If 2 or more people get the same time, then the map that scored second will be the map that those people will compete on. This pattern continues.

Instructions for Map-Makers
  1. Make your map. Using NReality mods are allowed.
  2. Test your map. Do not submit impossible maps or maps without an exit.
  3. Submit your map to NUMA under your own alias. Please put APCTR(round #) in the tags to easily identify the maps. ie. APCT1 for Round 1
  4. Either PM me or post in this thread the link to your map on NUMA.
Instructions for Players
  1. I will announce the map once judging is complete.
  2. Open NReality, go to Configure, and make sure you are in Highscoring mode.
  3. You will load the map in NReality by clicking on User Levels in the main screen and entering the NUMA map number (the last 6 digits of the url) and clicking Load NUMA Map.
  4. Click on the first bar that's listed, and play the map.
  5. Keep on improving your score until the deadline.

    Map Name
    I/T: n/5
    O: n/5
    A: n/10
    G: n/15
    Comments
  3. Put all that in a .txt file or just PM it to me. Do NOT post it in this thread.
  4. Your deadline is 2 days after the maps are due.
