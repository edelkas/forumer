#291837 3 2007-01-30T20:55:15Z jtbandes
Most likely the cause of your problem is that you haven't copied N and its files out of the disk image. Your best option is to copy all the files out of that folder onto your hard drive. Personally, I made a folder called "N v1.4" in my Applications folder, and put all the files in there. Once you've copied the files, you can eject the disk image by dragging it to the trash, and you can also get rid of the n_v1mac.dmg file. At that point, you can just play N right off your hard drive!
