#7531 1 2006-03-20T20:23:23Z Raigan
hey,
We're off to the GDC tomorrow until saturday.. it's going to be really exciting to meet the IGFers and hook up with developers we've met last year and at slamdance.

We haven't been very active on the forums lately, because we're trying to stay focused and not procrastinate; so far this is going well, we've been spending most days working on the new game


We had hoped to have something playable finished for the GDC, however it doesn't look like that's going to happen.. there's just so much left to do! So far we've got the collision system working, the physics system working (though it will probably be redone at some point), and an editor started (right now it's just used to make scenes to test collision/physics). We've also made several graphics mockups and tests..

It looks like it will be another month or so before all of the prototyping and research is finished (there are one or two other systems which we need to get working, like the animation system.. we miss flash!), and another couple months or so to put all the pieces together into a game, do all the graphics, sound, etc. But it's hard to say -- it might not take very long to assemble all the pieces, or it might take a lot longer to get the graphics working.. who knows?


We still haven't decided on the platform we're going to use; right now it looks like we'll either go with C/C++ and some free libraries/engines, or Blitzmax. There are about 12 different things in our "stuff to try" folder, so once all of the technology is built and working we'll begin experimenting to see what combination of tools will be the best fit. We're still trying to release on as many platforms as possible, and hopefully things will work out well.


Thanks for your continued support and patience -- it's going to be a while yet before we have something to show you, but we know how you feel -- we're really excited about this new game, and quite impatient to play it!!


Raigan and Mare
