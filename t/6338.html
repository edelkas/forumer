To add more fuel to the fire, the author of the books "Snowcrash" and "Cryptonomicon" (I don't remember his name) wrote an essay once comparing the available OS's.

Paraphrased:
We can look at different operating systems as cars.
A Windows car is a pile of many, many pieces that all build and work off of each other. The result is very shaky, but it somehow manages to work. However, when there is a problem, it's very easy to locate the one problem part and replace it with a very small (if non-existent) fee.
A Mac car is a hermitically-sealed bubble. It's difficult to go on more "freestyle" roads with it, because it was built for efficient work. If something does go wrong with the car, because it's one big piece, the whole sonofabitch has to be replaced.
A Linux car is a tank. I don't mean one of those crappy soviet tanks, I mean something along the lines of a perfectly built (Swiss or German?) hovertank. It's extremely difficult to get it to break down. But what happens when it does break down? Eat three more metaphors:
Getting help with Windows isn't usually that big of a problem. The official Microsoft auto parts dealer is very nice about providing any replacement parts you need, usually at no charge. Sometimes you need to talk to a specialist, who are usually well-trained.
Getting help with a Mac isn't really getting help fixing as much as it is buying a new one.
Now, imagine a world where everybody loves tanks. The guy who invented a tank shared his design openly with the world, and since then an enormous community of tank-lovers and experts emerged, many of them even building their own tanks by fundamentally changing the design of the tank. There are millions of people in the world who know pretty much everything there is to know about a tank. Getting help with Linux is simply going online and finding one of the countless tech help forums that have a "Linux" subforum and asking. Usually anyone there will be able to tell you how to fix it and how to avoid having it happen again, and it's absolutely no cost to you!
We've compared design and repair. Now we'll cover economics.
100 people walk into a massive lot that contains Windows, Mac, and Linux vehicles. 90 of these people are attracted to the shininess and sleek facade of Windows, so they immediately get the impression that Windows is the best designed system. They pay the hefty price of a Windows vehicle, and buy many more Windows applications. 5 of these people are more attracted to Macs, for whatever retarded reason, and they decide to buy a Mac. But only 5 of the initial 100 people decide to walk over to the lot with the massive sign that says "FREE TANKS! GET YOUR TANK HERE! ANY DESIGN YOU WANT, WITH 100% CUSTOMIZABILITY! FREE TECH SUPPORT UNTIL THE END OF TIME! EFFECTIVELY AN UNLIMITED TIME WARRANTY!"

Fuck Macs.
Go Linux.
