If something like this happens (i.e. the ninja hits the launchpad but dies anyway), then you're probably overshooting it just a tiny bit and embedding your ninja into the tile. Lower the number a small amount - 0.1 or even 0.01 sometimes. In this case, setting it to -4.8 worked for me.
