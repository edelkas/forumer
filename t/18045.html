Spoiler: [bWho Are The Characters?[/b]]
Spoiler: BLACKThe ninja that sees most things as we do. He can be witty, and he can also be brutally cold. He's often the most violent ninja.
Spoiler: GREENWe're not sure if she's witty or just plain dumb, Green Ninja often calls everyone "Jim". Maybe it had something to do with her ex?
Spoiler: DARK BLUEDark Blue Ninja is the oldest of all the ninjas. He's a Chef, and makes excellent Pasta (Ep.1). He also hates MTI as much as we do (Ep.9). He is also confused (or rather, ridiculed) about his sexuality; or at least his security of such (Ep's.28 and 29). Some suspect his real name to be "Dave".
Spoiler: REDRed Ninja is the "boss" ninja. He's got his head up his rump most of the time, and loves to crack jokes at other ninjas. He's a meanie. Red Ninja is also a little slow on the uptake of conversation. He's also money-hungry (Ep.15), and he gets bored on the weekends (Ep.17). Red also smokes (Ep.27).
Spoiler: TEALThe Lackey Ninja, as his boss Red Ninja calls him. He's friends with Violet Ninja, a fellow pencil-pusher under the employment of Red.
Spoiler: PURPLEHe's the inquisitor of the ninjas. He ponders a lot, and thinks things through WAY too much. He is obsessed with his hair (who would have thunk' it?), and often dyes it. This is why he appears in multiple shades of purple.
Spoiler: BROWNThe general smart-ass of the ninjas, Brown Ninja is in constant competition with his ex, Green Ninja. He hasn't been the same since Green spotted some gold lying around after he completed a level (Ep.6).
Spoiler: LIGHT BLUELight Blue, of "Lieutenant Blue" as his friends call him, is a constant storyteller. She's always mucking about how strong she is, thusly the nickname, "Lt.Blue".
Spoiler: FUTUREFuture Ninja appeared once (Ep.12), and was never seen again. I guess the whole crotch-kicking incident warded him off.
Spoiler: SANDThe only time we've ever seen a sand-colored Ninja around here is when one corrected Green by proclaiming that their name was not "Jim". Instead, they proclaimed their name to be "Animator".
Spoiler: ANTSBelieve it or not, small bugs have began training in the art of the Ninjitsu. They also seem to have an obsession with gold (Ep.23).
Spoiler: OrangeOrange Ninja has an ego problem. Simply put, he likes things to be BIG. I wonder what he's hiding...? (Ep.25)
Spoiler: BROWN-ISHThe joker of the Ninjas, Brown Ninja simply likes talking. People try to shut up his nonsense whenever possible by telling her jokes, as her laugh is slightly less annoying.
Spoiler: GOLDGold Ninja, the God of all Ninjas, makes few appearances (Ep.26). He is, of course, the creator of Gold.
Spoiler: ZAPPYThe floorguard pet of Black Ninja. Zappy enjoys playing fetch, and plotting the death of all other Ninjas.
Spoiler: DRONETICUS III, SR.While his name is never actually revealed, Droneticus, or "Droc" as he prefers to be called, nearly assassinated Black Ninja in the past (Ep.13). He likes his tea promptly at noon-thirty, and nibbles ever-so-slightly upon a scone while drinking such a brew. He's got a PhD in Neurozappologistical Application. He is also the author of three children's books.
Spoiler: LUCThe only laser drone to have allergies (Ep.24), Luc hates all things with pollen. Sadly, Gauss Turrets emit pollen after every shot.
Spoiler: KILLERKiller, or "Turtle Killer" as he is known on the street, is a notorious pet-murderer. He often disguises himself as Pink Ninja to try to give the cops the slip, and even says his murders are "accidental", by proclaiming "Oops! I stepped on -Insert Pet Here-". Be wary of him.
Spoiler: PINKPink left Red after he had called her a "Homie-G Dizzle Slice Dawg" (Ep.20). What the hell is that, anyways? Nonetheless, Pink is best friends with Green.
Spoiler: MOSHAlthough he never appears in a comic - Mosh appeared in Episode 38 - "Awake". Mosh is indeed a ninja. What color is he? Thats for him to know, and you to never find out. Mind you, he doesn't look like any conventional ninja...

I can see where plot similarities can be seen between Sidestreet 210 and LV's Another Day. A significant difference, however, is the way the plots are presented. His plotlines, which seem to rely on the "random WOW!" factor, have a constant crew of characters which seem to operate on a satirical sense of pseudo-highbrow humor. My comics lack an instant "WOW!" factor, but achieve close to the same with very involving dialogue.

Also, look at the speech between his comic and my comic. I have a set-size of pixels I conform myself to for each strip, which is 400x700; just under a 1:2 ratio. LV's comic stretches and crops itself to meet any size requirements of the strip. This is relevant to the speech of our comics because I present much more dialogue in less of a space, while he presents less dialogue in more space. Though, what we both are doing isn't necessarily a bad thing. We both possess quite humorous comic strips, regardless of how we achieved such a title.

All-in-all, I can see similarities between the comics. I can also see, however, a vast sea of differences.

And with that being said, Episode 15 is being drawn. Paintshop Pro, here I come.
