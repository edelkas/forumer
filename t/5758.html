#115907 2 2005-11-18T23:37:46Z nevermore
NaN stands for Not a Number. It appears in level data when something that isn't a number is in your level data. It usually messes up something in your level. This is usually because people still have the text box where the level data goes highlighted when they push [L] to load a level. To avoid this, click outside of the text box before you load something.
#116146 3 2005-11-19T14:42:11Z brickman
NaNs do all sorts of things, a few useful but most destructive. It places objects outside the border if its in their coordinates. It makes drones into disembodied eyes if there's one where the drone type goes. It makes the drones stationary without any doors if placed in their movement type selector. It makes lauchpads screw you up (causing the "flying bug"). Generally, though, NaN's screw up your level.
