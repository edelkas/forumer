NOOB MAPS!!!!!!11!11one

As the title suggests, this contest deals with N00B maps. Here is your mission: Scour NUMA to find the N00Biest maps you can get your hands on, then change it so that it's at least a decent map. Here are a few quick rules:

1. The map must not have a rating higher than 2 (if it's not rated, you'll have to use your own judgement skills).
2. Your revised map must not change the map's type (for example, don't turn a N00B Survival map into a decent Race map).
3. Your map must resemble the N00B map in some manner (if you give both pictures to somebody, they should be able to tell that they're similar).
4. Revising a DDA is okay, but other non-playable maps (Incomplete, N Art, Tilesets) are not allowed.
5. All submissions need to be submitted as an external file or in Numa, NOT IN YOUR POST!
6. All maps NEED an original. And it can't be yours.

I'll need some judges... if anybody's willing to judge. I'll take as many as I can get.
Unlimited entrants, unless there are extenuating circumstances (such as too much bandwidth being used... not that that will happen... )

No concrete deadline picked yet. I'll decide on that when there are a good number of people willing to play.
