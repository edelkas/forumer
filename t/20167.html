There are a few alternative ways to get the switches; the diagram shows the ones that were-quite frankly-the easiest to show, because others are more complicated. You might find a different way easier. What's important is the last jump in the mines. It's really not as hard, or dangerous, as it looks, and it is the most consistent way (for me) out of the mines. The other thing is the long green jump across the course, which looks harder than it is; it shouldn't cause you any trouble.
