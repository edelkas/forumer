Click the names of buildings on that map-thing to move to different buildings. Click "Enter *Building Name*" to enter that building if it hasn't been barricaded to a point of no-entry. Click the 'Search' button to look through the building to try to find something. When on the same 'building' (Outside or inside, either.), and you see someone under the drop-down menu next to 'Attack', named 'zombie', thats a Zombie, and you should repeateadly click the 'Attack' button (after choosing a weapon from a subsequent drop-down menu) until they die, OR, until you're around 15 AP. Once around 15 AP, go find a building with some form of Barricade (Moderately Barricaded or heavily barricaded would be good. Also - in an area with very few zombies.) and click 'Log Out'. You recieve 1AP (Action Point) every half-hour. So, 24 hours from when you last played, you'll be good-to-go.
Also - if you have no clue what to do, I'd start with the 'Firefighter' class, since its weapon (Fireaxe) required no ammo, and has a good hit-percentage. Just dont get two(or more) of your characters close to eachother on the map, or they'll be deleted automatically by the system (or suffer combat/AP penalties).
