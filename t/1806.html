Other (0-5): anything a judge felt like you deserved more points for, maybe less for, this is the place for that.
Bonus (0 or 5): It's back... and here it is. Foxhunter wil give 3 out for his score, all other judges 5. the points go to judges favorite map

Map: Rooms 7
Author: Lartsa
Despite a clear lack of playtesting, and little focus on symmetry, this map is playable. Various problems, such as drones not doing what they're supposed to do (such as the drone that ends up doing stupid circles in the upper right chamber) and enemies that are bypassed way too easily (laser drones, chaingun drone), and a smattering of enemies that are nearly impossible to get past (the rocket launcher in the lower left), reared their ugly heads far too many times. As a map to play, it's quite entertaining. As a work of art, it isn't worth the load time.
Symmetry: 5; While the tileset and the room ideas were symmetrical, the general look of it didn't suggest much symmetry at all. I didn't feel I was doing anything similar in the two symmetrical portions, nor did I make any correlation between the symmetrical portions while playing.
Uniqueness: 7; Surprisingly, this kind of map is a rarity on NUMA, which is quite unfortunate. Seeing a level like this was a refreshment.
Fun: 8; I can't say it wasn't entertaining. Well, except for the lower-left rocket launcher.
Difficulty: 4; It tends toward being pretty damn easy, with a nice sudden spin into insane in the lower-left.
Excitement: 2; The general mood is relaxed. I didn't feel pressured by anything as I was playing the map.
Other: 1; Unoriginal name. If you want to be taken seriously in a competition, make up a better name.
Total: 27

Map: Circle of Death
Author: tktktk
More of artwork than a fun map to play, this map resembles more of a somewhat laidback test of coordination. Accessing the various switches can get tedious, however, and so it's much more appealing as a work of art.
Symmetry: 10; The thwumps at the top (that don't do anything) are enough like the floor guards at the bottom to truly make this map nearly perfectly symmetrical.
Uniqueness: 8; Maps like this have shown their faces on NUMA, and in one particular episode in the main game itself (the number escapes me), typically including the center of the map as a main focus, such as a "FL4M1NG SUN OF DEAHT!!1" or "TEH BIG FRICKIN EYE!1!"
Fun: 5; It doesn't seem like it, but this map is kind of boring to play. There is nothing to preoccupy you in your moving from one area to the next. It seems like you're moving freely to rooms that each contain their own separate challenge.
Difficulty: 7; Getting the four buttons on the sides can be frustrating, depending upon your method. The rest is pretty easy, up until you get to the exit, where it turns to dependency on luck.
Excitement: 1; Very laidback.
Other: 5; Working completion demo.
Total: 36

Map: A War At Tarawa
Author: JeR(K)
As exciting as it looks, and far more convoluted than it looks, this map presents a challenge to just about anyone bored enough to try it. The difficulty picks up toward the finale, containing surprisingly difficult situations. The end is rather lax, however, and nothing short of tedious.
Symmetry: 10; Aside from the doors, this map is perfectly symmetrical.
Uniqueness: 8; Gotta admit you don't see many of the "dick around below to access the top" maps.
Fun: 7; The bottom was pretty entertaining, but the top was just plain annoying in its difficulty.
Difficulty: 4; This was definitely unbalanced. The bottom was a snap - you could practically ignore the existence of the drones, and you'd do fine. The two side rooms on the top were crazy hard, and the top central room was pathetically easy.
Excitement: 3; The bottom can be exciting if you want to play around with the drones, but the top can never become exciting - you die too quickly, at a high frequency.
Other: 1; No demos suggest that there's a possibility you didn't playtest it, which is hinted at by the difficulty of the top corner rooms.
Total: 33

Map: Tentacles
Author: Igi
The radiation sign dubbed "tentacles" has a misleading appearance: the chaingun drones that make the map look like a goofy face belie the map's sadistic challenges.
Symmetry: 6; Symmetrical in two different ways that clash a bit. The sides would have been better filled in completely, and the opposite drone movements make the map look disorganized.
Uniqueness: 9; This is pretty unique. These maps aren't rare, but good maps like these are.
Fun: 7; Once you look past all of the death and agony, this is a fun map to play. Once.
Difficulty: 6; At times, it seems too difficult, but that's really just moderately challenging situations strung together.
Excitement: 5; All the way. You're never completely safe, and you have to keep moving to avoid death.
Other: 1; No... demos...
Total: 34
