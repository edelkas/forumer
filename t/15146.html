The 11x11 competition,
"Taking the 9x9 competition to the next level"


------------------Rules for this contest are as follows--------------------
  • You're only allowed to use the template when making this map, you may add tiles outside the border of the 11x11 square for asthetics, but no enemies or objects, if you do, you will be automatically disqualified from the competition. You may add in more tiles but stay within the square for gameplay
  • Each user may only submit 2 maps each, although entries may be changed at any time before the closing date, but once your map has been judged that's it.
  • No old maps sorry, although how many maps would you really have that are 11x11, really?
  • Please no N arts, incompletes, testlevels or tile sets, all other types of maps are acceptable; DDA, action, puzzle, survival and race.
  • Deadline for entries, Thursday, February 1, at 12:00 AM GMT. Anything submitted after that date will be ignored.
  • There is no limitations on what items and enemies you can and can't use, just keep them to the inside of the box or your map will be automatically disqualified.
PM me or post in this thread with any questions or problems you have with the rules of this contest.


----------------Judges and judging--------------------

Judges will be judging on a few things:
Best use of space (duh) X/10 - is the space used wisely and efficiently?
Funness X/10 - how much did you enjoy it?
Asthetics X/10 - Does it look good?
Game play X/10 - Does it all work well?
Giving a total of X/40, then each map is averaged by yours truly then a final score is posted when judging is finished (not for a long time yet...).

For the judges: Your description of why you gave this rating and what they could have done to improve doesn't have to be too long.
And yes the judges can submit entries.
Judges please start judging after February 1, at 12:00 AM GMT, the closing date for entries. And please don't post your results in this thread, put them into a .txt document naming it: 11x11 competition judge: *insert your name here*.txt and pm that to me. I will then average out the scores given by each judge adn post the final results i this thread for further discussion.
If any of the judges have ideas to change to the criteria then feel free to PM about it
