#306464 2 2007-03-20T01:11:42Z Twistkill
Um...

On the highscores menu, the individual levels always start out at 90 seconds, unlike in normal mode explained below. The highscorers play the levels on the Highscores menu. Go check them out. Click on an episode you've unlocked and select one of the levels, and look to the right. You'll see "Play Level *level # here*". That's where the highscorers do their runs, not in normal mode.

The reason you start out with less time is because while you're playing the game in the normal mode, the time starts at 90 seconds at the beginning, but that time carries over through the episode.

That doesn't make any sense.

Like I said, the time carries over through an episode. If you progressed from 36-0 to 36-2 with only 20 seconds remaining, then you start 36-3 with that amount of time. In normal mode. That is, playing from the Play Game menu, selecting your episode.

The highscores are different, you can run whichever level you want without going through the episode, and you start the level with 90 seconds, but of course, you must have already unlocked that level. Levels played in the Highscores menu do not give you progress.
