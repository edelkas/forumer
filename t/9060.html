#166692 11 2006-04-19T17:45:54Z Keron Cyst
Ghosts are all very fine, but how would they work? Racing games just have static terrain. N has action-performing objects.

What if you had to ride a Thwump sideways to get to another area? If the ghost doesn't trigger the Thwump it'd step on a mine or keep running into a wall or whatnot. If the Thwump recognizes the ghost as another ninja to annihilate, the timing could screw up the actual player.

If you wanted the ghost to just simulate movement so that (regarding the above scenario) it looks like it's riding an invisible Thwump, the game would have to be reprogrammed to record an entirely different type of demo data, aside from the current, even if the ghost-run is only for one play.

Plus, throw lag in there, player confusion, and all the other lesser points thrown in that I missed.

In short, it won't happen. I easily can see why anyone would want ghost movement, but it would be impossible to work in a game like N. Sorry.
