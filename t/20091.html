The key, I've found, to getting along with people is just to have a balance in ego. Don't feel inferior to people, because then you end up pushing yourself away from people. Neither of these things will really affect how people see you as a person, or what they think your personality is, these will affect instead how they act towards you. Surprisingly enough, I've found that those two things are to a large degree unrelated, especially on the net.
