#235228 9 2006-09-29T20:29:07Z Twistkill
Quote: cabamacadaf, September 29, 2006 03:54 pm
What is a Nan?

NaN stands for "not a number". It is a glitch in N level data that causes weird things to happen, a different one according to how the NaN got there. Some examples are drones not moving, a giant ninja, objects outside the level border, tiles that you can pass through, among several other things. They are sometimes used on purpose, however.

As for your problem, did you try using LV's revision?
