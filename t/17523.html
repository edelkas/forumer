Deceptively simple. What you have to do is reach the exit by using your limited block-summoning (and banishing) ability. This starts off being incredibly easy ("Oh no, a hole! Should I bridge it or just walk to my death?"), but after about level 20 you need to plan ahead carefully. There are also extra map-packs, though I haven't tried any yet, and a level editor.

EDIT: Oh, yeah, it works on Mac and Linux as well as Windows, if that's the way you roll.
