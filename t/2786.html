Had this idea soon after I posted my previous one. Add Veteran Mode as kind of a "Hard Mode."
The background goes like this: choose to play as an older "veteran" n who has been around the block a few times; he's older and wiser but his body ain't what it used to be. In addition to the normal puzzles, the player also has to deal with a stamina bar. The stamina bar depletes when n exerts himself, such as running, jumping, changing direction in mid-stride, etc. The stamina bar fills up while n is resting, like standing still or clinging to walls. When the stamina bar is depleted, n has to stand there, vulnerable, and wait to catch his breath (ie, stamina bar fills up to a nominal level). Here's the real kicker: the stamina bar is as long as your remaining time. The less time you have left, the less n can do before exhausting himself. Didn't bother to snag all that gold in the previous board? You're going to pay for it.
No other resitrictions, though. The point behind this is to help players hone their skills, become more efficient in completing levels (even the real easy ones). Hopefully this can help players become good enough to complete the later levels and add a bit more replay value.
