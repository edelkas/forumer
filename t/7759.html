No-Enemy Competition

This is my second attempt at a competition. Basically, you make a map(any kind) that has no enemies. No enemies meaning nothing can kill you except for a fall(No jump pads facing toward a wall). Now, the best choice for this would be a puzzle, and since we have some great puzzlers out there, this will give them a challenge.

Rules

1) No map that is submitted can be made before February 23(today).

2) Absolutely no enemies.

3) You may submit up to three(3) maps per person, and you may replace any map submitted with a new one up to March 23(final results will be announced then).

4) If you are replacing a map that was already submitted, specify the name of the map you are replacing, and supply a link to the new one.

5) You must submit your map no later than March 15. Any late submissions will have 20 points deducted from their overall score.

Judges

Judge #1: Me(Phyre)

Judge #2: bufar

Judge #3: Jiggerjaw12358

Backup #1: Greengooguy49

Backup #2: Guiseppi

*Backup judges will judge maps submitted by a judge ONLY, and they cannot judge their own maps.


Map Scoring

Judges will rate each map out of a total score of 100. The breakdown looks like this:

50 points for originality(Does my map use any new ideas?)

50 points for playability(Will others play my map over and over again?)

100 total points

Submission Setup

Copy this code, replacing everything within the asterisks with what it says to place there:

[url=*map url goes here*][color=red][u]*map name*[/color][/u][/url]

[color=orange]*author name as veiwed on NUMA goes here*[/color]

[color=blue]*date submitted goes here*[/color]

Have fun, and get to making maps!
