#10414 1 2006-08-02T02:05:05Z rennaT
I just finished watching V for Vendetta and was, as expected, totally blown away. The Wachowski brothers have out-done themselves. However, this is not a discussion as to whether or not the movie was a good one. If you can imagine for an instant that this film is not excellent, then that is your prerogative.

What I'd like to discuss is which, in your opinion, is better: The Matrix or V for Vendetta.

For me, at first, V for Vendetta was a clean win. It's real and prophetic. The demons from the past have demons from the past. The hero is compromised but uncompromising. V was superbly crafted.

But then I remembered that the Wachowski brothers didn't actually create V. They adapted it with supreme skill but it is nevertheless an adaptation. I still consider V for Vendetta to be a better film but I can't say as to which is the better creation.
