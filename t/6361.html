The Untouchables competition is a test to make a fun map with hardly any solid surfaces to hold on to. Here are the rules for the map:

1: There should be only a maximum 10 solid surfaces (conjoined surfaces do not count as 1 surface).

2: If a solid surface is blocked by a mine so that it is completely untouchable, it does not count as a solid surface.

3:Mines are not counted as solid surfaces.

4:The map must be artistic (you will be rated on this).

5:The map (of course) has to be fun.

6:The map will be rated on a scale of 0 to 20 on fun, use of surfaces, creativity and art. The score combined should be 0 to 80, the complete score.

7: There are no rewards, it's all in good fun.

8: Posting of maps ends at Decamber 21, 8:00 PM.

Those are all the rules. First 10 people to reply will be judges, judges post instantly and can even give out their own maps (you can't rate your own) , all judges per map (average score will count).

Good Luck!
