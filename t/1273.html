Ok, so you have a jump pad facing up. Once you jump off of it, it changes direction. The ultimately cool thing would be allow the map designer to pick the next direction with logic, but I understand that would be too much. So, how about just allowing us to pick two or more directions and have it just rotate through them in order? Or just have multiple ones that switch in set orders (like up to down to up, or left to right to left, etc.).

-- OR --

A jump pad that can change direction based on a trigger. So you place a trigger that forces the jump pad to face up and another that makes it face down. You get the idea. This one would be especially neat (and cruel) in multiplayer, competitive mode.


The second option would probably be a lot easier to implement.
