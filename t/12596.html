This is a story i'm writing in my free time. My friend is going to draw a manga out of it when I finish, so that should be pretty cool.

~The Curse of the Maple Staff~

Main Characters:

Blizzard ~ The main character of the story. Blizzard is the younger brother of Dreamscythe, who is an amazing cleric. Blizzard has followed in his footsteps, but is not nearly as good at holy magic than his older brother. He has been friends with Deviona, Dark, and Coli since he was a kid.

Dreamscythe ~ Blizzards older brother. Dreamscythe has practiced the holy magics for years in hopes of becoming a Priest one day. His quest for knowledge sometimes gets him into trouble, but he always comes out fine. Until he ventures a little too far

Deviona ~ A childhood friend of Blizzard. She tried to practice magic as well, but she decided it was much too difficult for her. She eventually became a warrior under the mentoring of Yalani; however she is still a novice. Her spiritual powers have yet to be revealed, despite this.

Dark ~ Dark is Blizzards best friend, and has a special bond with Coli. Dark is an excellent bowman, and spars with Blizzard for practice and fun every once in a while. He usually acts before thinking, which gets him into dangerous situations, but his friends are always there to help him out.

Coli ~ A skilled assassin who has a crush on Dark. She will usually hurt Dark when she is embarrassed, for reasons no one knows. Dreamscythe gave her a legendary weapon that he found on an adventure as a birthday present. It is called Avenger, and she keeps it with her wherever she goes.

Yalani ~ Yalani is the legendary warrior who wielded the legendary spear Fairfrozen in the battle against Zakum. He led a group to defeat Zakum once and for all, and emerged victorious. He was very injured after the journey, and disappeared after leaving the hospital. No one has seen him since that day.

Chapter 1: Prequel

Dreamscythe sat in the basement of his house, studying about magic. Blizzard was upstairs, preparing dinner. He sure must be hungry with all the work hes been doing recently.. Blizzard thought to himself. Hopefully hell like this. He poured the pasta he had made into two bowls, one for himself, and one for Dreamscythe. He sat them upon the table, and went downstairs. Dreamscythe? Dinners ready. Dreamscythe stood up. Come over here, Blizzard. Dreamscythe beckoned. Blizzard hurried towards him. Look at this. Blizzard looked at the article Dreamscythe had pointed to in his book. The Maple Staff? Dreamscytheyou cant be thinking of actually looking for this? Indeed I am, Blizzard. With your help, Im sure we can find it. My magic will become much more focused with the staff, and Ill be able to protect us better, if need be. Blizzard stared at him. Lets eat dinner for now. We can focus on this tomorrow. The two brothers went upstairs, enjoyed the meal, and went to sleep. They would start their journey in the morning.

Blizzard, wake up. Dreamscythe was standing over him. Its time to go. Blizzard put on his cleric robes, and grabbed his staff. Dreamscythe and Blizzard rushed out the door. So, where are we going exactly? Blizzard inquired. Ellinia woods. I hear the staff is on a pedestal hidden deep in the forest. And the chances of us finding it? Dreamscythe gave a cold hard stare, and Blizzard took back the remark. Suddenly, a group of Slimes came out of a bush. Oh great said Blizzard. Dreamscythe said to Blizzard with a smirk, Lets see what you can do. Go ahead. Blizzard raised his wand at the group. Magic Claw! Blizzards attack came smashing down upon two of the slimes. The 4 other ones charged him. Holy Arrow! Blizzards arrow shot through 3 of the slimes. The last one caught him off guard, knocking the breath out of him. Heal! Dreamscythe shouted as he prepared another spell. Holy Arrow! The last slime was obliterated by Dreamscythes arrow. Damn Blizzard said, getting off the ground. I should have been expecting that last one. Everyone makes mistakes, Dreamscythe said, chuckling. Come on, lets go. Blizzard stopped a second. Blizzard? Dreamscythe turned around. Blizzard walked towards a suspicious looking tree. He pressed the trunk of it, and suddenly, it opened, and he fell forward. Blizzard plunged into the darkness.
