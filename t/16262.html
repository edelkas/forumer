Debate Teams
  1. Intro
    I think VV33 had set this up a couple of months ago, but it didn't get anywhere. So I want to try it again, but a bit differently. Of course, I should have consulted VV33 before I did this, and I tried, but his inbox was full. We will form 2 teams that will debate a topic, one team taking the 'pro' side and the other taking the 'con' side.

  2. Rules
    1. Each team will have at least 6 people.
    2. Each member will have to make a post in the argument.
    3. A debate will last 1 week. Both teams can make as many posts to support their argument as they wish.
    4. In the debate thread, team members will be required to post in either brown or gray, depending on what team they are in.. Comments in the thread will be made in black, and Judge's posts will be made in red. My posts are in blue when I make annoucements.
    5. INTELLIGENT DISCUSSION ONLY! NO FLAMING, TROLLING, ETC.
  3. Judging
    Judges will decide the winning team. We will need NEUTRAL judges. Judges will grade the teams for each of the following: Teamwork (How well the members of your team support each other's ideas, etc.), Organization (How logically your posts were ordered, etc.), Manner (Courtesy, appropriateness of language, etc.) Matter (The actual information that your post conveys. .:.Counts Double!.:.)

    I will not judge this. (Well, I might. Depends on how things turn out, really...) I need volunteers to be judges. If you sign to be a judge in the first debate, then you will only be required to judge for Debate 1. You can back out from judging from Debate 2 if you want. I will accept all people who want to judge. (A few judges will definetly back out from judging at the last moment, so we need as many back-ups as possible. )

    Judges:
    1. Tsukatu! Our very own satanic moderator!
    2. blue_tetris! A heavily opinionated guy. Has his own fan club and is a moderator here at Metanet Forums.
    3. macaddict_17! A charming young forumer with a sexy avatar.
    Teams
    Sign up for a team here. You may be moved around if I (or another judge, or a participant) feels that the teams are unfair.
