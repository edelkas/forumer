Dear friends...I'm afraid I have to leave this wondrous place.

My mom is accusing me of spending too much time on the computer, when in reality, I'm not. She knows that I have played N constantly; she thinks it's all I ever do. She thinks I'm behind on my homework. And my leaving is the only way to solve this.

I really love this place, guys. I'm really going to miss it. Thinking about leaving just put some tears into my eyes. I've learned a lot while I was here, which is slightly surprising, seeing as it's just a video game forum. I believe I've gotten more mature during my time here, which has effected the way I act outside of my computer wonderland. Because of sniping on NUMA, I learned to just get over it and keep on going with it.
NUMA leads me to another thing. I'm out of map-making juice. I can't think of anything great to make anymore. And besides, I guess the ending of a column in my episodes is a good end to my little career.

I was never planning to leave this place, until now. To please my parents, I guess I'll have to leave. Which really sucks.

But I will definately not forget about this place. I may appear somewhere around here when you least expect it. And maybe my juices will get flowing and I'll submit another map to NUMA.

Do anything to my maps that you want. Just give me credit if you do so, please. If possible, it would be great to have all my maps rated, so I could see my total average rating when I buzz by. Doesn't have to happen, but it sure would be cool.

And on a little lighter note, don't forget my birthday, which will come in summer.

Take care, you guys. I'll always remember you.

-Mare and Raigan, for N. The greatest game I've ever played. Thank you guys so much for creating it. I'll try to be around for your future games.
-All of you, for being there to support me in any way. For being kind and friendly to me when I was feeling down or whenever. All you guys are my friends.

Goodbye.
