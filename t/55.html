Welcome to the Metanet Forum, home of the popular game N! [ Bold-faced, red text indicates a recently added rule ]

Main Rules:
- No dissing or excessive swearing (outside of the Bash & Thrash forum)
- Don't flame; it's lame. No flame wars or heated arguments are allowed outside of the Bash & Thrash forums. Take it out in e-mails/PMs if you need to. If such post battles are executed here, they will be edited/deleted with all participants warned.
- No posting in PURE CAPS. Such posts will be edited with the posts' authors warned.*
- Please make an effort to post with proper grammar. Bad spelling, punctuation, etc. or anything to purposely make people stare at posts longer will be edited/deleted with the posts' authors warned. Posts submitted in Geek-Talk / 1337 language are only allowed if a direct translation is also included.*
- If you're going to post in another language, please translate your posts into English (like with FreeTranslation.com, or altavista.BabelFish.com).*
- Creating threads or posts purely to increase Post Count aren't a good idea to do. This includes one-liner/emoticon-only posts.*
- No trolls or trollish behavior are allowed. Find another forum if you feel like trolling.
--> Regular members, please do not feed trolls! Ignore them and continue on with the topic's original subject (or, if they made the thread themselves, wait for a staff member to lock it, or better yet, inform one to speed up the process).
- Abandonware (games that once costed but now no longer since their creators no longer exist, thus becoming a debated branch of semi-interesting freeware) will be allowed for conversation but will be strictly monitored.
- Please don't discourage new members of the forum (usually those with the Member Title "Newbie") if they post something that has already been pointed out in other threads. Here's how I avoid frustration, due to my own massive forum attendance: Important Topics (pinned or stickied threads) are not for them to notice before posting, but to easily be found after they post their problem. This definitely includes flaming.
- Please do not embed logout- or any other form of maliciously distractive links within signatures.
- Absurdly large signatures shall be edited (or removed in unusual circumstances). (This usually just pertains to humongous images).
- Excessive spamming after unheeding multiple warns will result into a Post Count drop to 1. This includes posting "I suck at making highscores" in the Highscores forum section.*
- Constant literal correction is a concealed variant of trolling. If you are not part of the staff, don't backseat-mod and post "This topic should be moved to this section of the forum" or "Hey, you misspelled 'grammar,'" "*requests lock*," "You resurrected a year-old topic," etc. Even staff members don't need to publicly rebuke people; they can silently move/lock/do whatever to topics as needed, and manually edit posts themselves if grammar is horrendous, so continuous violators shall be warned.* If someone continuously harasses you, don't retaliate; PM a staff member and let the proper authority manage it.
- Members who resurrect an extremely old topic with nothing significant to add may be penalized for the action.*
- No userdata.sol files shall be attached; they will be removed, with the posters possibly warned.
- Signature size limits are listed below. Images should be no larger than 600x200 pixels. Including all images, quotes and text, the sig can be 250 pixels high. With all spoilers expanded, it can be 330 pixels. All images combined should be no more than 500 kb.


* - Newbies are flexible around these rules and won't be warned (except in unusual circumstances).

The below rules apply throughout the entire forum and to every type of community member.

Anyone who posts death threats and/or others' home addresses, telephone #s, and/or any other private matters without their consent, even if they're fake and/or were just joking, will be IP-banned immediately and the appropriate posts deleted You are allowed to post about your own privacies as long as you are above the age of 12, though such an action is not recommended.

Nothing implicitly or explicitly interesting shall be posted; any such topics include but are not limited to:
  • alcohol, drug usage, or other amply recognized addictive substances (you may refer to prescription medicine, but be aware that posts will be monitored nonetheless, unless discussing the societal control of such materials);
  • interesting P2P file-sharing;
  • kitchen sink or excess obscenity/vulgarity/indecency;
  • anything harmfully deceptive, continuously harassing (as in, towards a particular member of the board), defamatory or libelous (maliciously harming a person's reputation), or abusive;
  • anything sexually, racially, or ethnically objectionable or unlawful (which basically covers all of the above...);
  • ROMs (a.k.a. interesting and unnecessary copies of commercial products made without permission, regardless of whether a ROM owner has the original copy of a game that a ROM in question is based off of);
  • proxies;
  • and other topics as deemed necessary by a majority of the staff or regular members. If the discussion stirs a sense of doubt from the start, then that alone is already your signal to cease.
Anyone who purposely releases to the public a virus, worm, trojan horse, spyware, adware, or any other malicious software of the sort intended to damage computer software shall be permanently banned immediately and necessary actions taken.


- - - - -
Violation of these rules may result in a brief inability to post or temporary, if not occasional permanent, bans.
- You can take up to five warnings (100%, in increments of 20) before a forum-automatic suspension. When you're back, one wrong move will get you banned. Continue to support the community instead of burden it and your Warn Level should gradually lower. It may seem harsh, but then again we want a clean messageboard and wish to avoid penalties from Forumer...!

- Keep in mind that you can receive multiple Warns on one violation, depending on how harshly you broke the rule(s).

- The accounts of members with 0 counted posts who have been registered for over 1 year are subject to sporadic deletion at any time, to keep the database accurate with regards to board activity. After all, that's what the statistics are for!

Member Rights:
If you strongly believe that a staff member has penalized you for an invalid reason, you may put up a poll with at least two poll choices resembling Justified and Unjustified and action will be taken accordingly to results & discussion. If you request any form of penalization for a particular member, you may put up a poll with at least two poll choices resembling Yes and No and administrative action may be taken accordingly to the public results, but also list reasons why the person should be penalized.
