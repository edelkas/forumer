The Impossible Quiz

The Impossible Quiz, made by Splapp-Me-Do, can be very frustrating at times. The questions/answers can be very random at times, and the correct answer is not always the right answer. I personally haven't beaten the game yet, but I have gotten to question 91 or so. Thanks to nevermore and jtbandes for the help on 56 and such.

I have a guide up to question 50+ for you, which I will update frequently. There are going to be different parts in different spoilers for each 40 questions.
Spoiler: TIQ Guide #1-40.Question 1: How many holes in a polo? Click "Four". Don't ask me why.
Question 2: Can a match box? Click "No, but a tin can". It seriously can.
Question 3: .sdrawkcab noitseuq siht rewsnA Click "K.O". It's O.K. backwards, which is what the question asks for.
Question 4: Click the answer. Well, click the "The answer" in the question.
Question 5: Well, bring the mouse off of the browser, move it over to the left, and click "Next Question" without touching the blue.
Question 6: What's the square root of onion? "Shallots". I don't know why.
Question 7: The answer is really big. Click "An elephant". An elephant is big, right?
Question 8: Search! Move your mouse about 70 pixels below the E, and click "Yes!".
Question 9: What was the answer to question #2? Lies for the first time. Click the upper-right hand "That one".
Question 10: Choose food. Click the dentures.
Question 11: What follows December 2nd? It lies! It's "n".
Question 12: Click the smallest. Click the dot in the "i" in the word "click".
Question 13: What sound does a bell make? It makes a "f'taaang" noise.
Question 14: What can you put in a bucket to make it lighter?" Well, a torch lights up a bucket, doesn't it?
Question 15: Neigh! Whinny! OH ITS A HORSE
Question 16: What is the 7th letter of the alphabet? Lies! Click "H".
Question 17: What is 24-7? Hm. Is it 18? No. Where do you see a 17? Now click it.
Question 18: Stop... Hammertime!
Question 19: Color in the correct order. It's "Blue, orange, green, green, yellow". BOGGY, get it?
Question 20: Deal or no deal? It's "seal".
Question 21: Watch carefully! Click the upper left box.
Question 22: The choice is yours! That +1 skip looks pretty good, doesn't it?
Question 23: Save changes to 'Untitled'? BRAN!
Question 24: If you move your mouse back and forth on the colors, it says to "Click the 'V' in 'Lives'". Now, click the "V"!
Question 25: How do you kill a werewolf? The answers are very random. I'd choose "Shoe polish".
Question 26: Which of these place names don't exist? Arsefacey doesn't exist.
Question 27: I hope that you've been paying attention to the question numbers!" Well, what question is next? *Cough*28*Cough*
Question 28: What is this? A bun dance!
Question 29: What flavor is cardboard? I think that it tastes like egg mayonnaise.
Question 30: You know what to do.
Question 31: [Insert image of dog] Click "Woof woof woof".
Question 32: What are the main ingredients of shampoo? "Babycham and human faeces."
Question 33: How many letters are in his hand? Lies! There's seven letters.
Question 34: Elephants don't like mice! Move your mouse off of the browser, and wait until the elephants have entered the hole.
Question 35: Click this button for game over. Just wait until the button is gone, and click the green one instead.
Question 36: What do you call a wingless fly? I was going to say "Jason", but it's actually a "walk".
Question 37: Can you get this question wrong? Of course you can't.
Question 38: Mary Rose sat on a pin. MARY ROSE (LMAO)
Question 39: [Insert image of walking tube] Randomness again. It's "Cylindrical adventures".
Question 40: Put mouse ...here. Ooh, I love this one. put your mouse on the first button, right-click, and move your mouse over the "Adobe Flash" bar into the other circle. Click it.

I love this game. If you have any questions, post them here. I might not know them, but it doesn't hurt to ask. Post your status!

BTW, if anyone could help me with #91, that would be great.
