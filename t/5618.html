http://numa.notdot.net/map/18008

See this map. Seems, if an exit switch has a locked door over it, you can hit the switch without openning the door first. There's nothing special about the switch - it's just half-snapped into place like normal - you just have to fall from a good height to get it. I also tried other objects, like gold and trap switches, and it looks like this also works for gold but not anything else.
