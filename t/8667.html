#162498 5 2006-04-06T13:13:33Z rocketmaster
What you have to do is: to get down, simply go to the edge, drop, and slide down, pretty simple... After you get the switch, run like heck towards the wall, then jump up it really fast, and don't stop for anything :-) When you get to the top, there's a ledge that will propel you to the left, where you land on the top ledge. Simply jump to the right and lose the rockets. Worked for me, first try. On the right part of the map, simply drop all the way down and hit one of the angled parts (while the machine gun isn't looking) If you don't want the gold, there you go!
