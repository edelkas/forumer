#471520 41 2008-09-19T05:41:51Z The Kitchen Sink
Quote: mattk210, September 19, 2008 05:16 am
I realize you like NReality, and I mostly agree with you, but can you stop repeating that point please? It is possible for an optional addition to be a bad thing

* disinterest towards traditional elements in mapmaking due to increased interest in the new stuff
* cluttering and less overall design quality of the product

So your point is "people who would be making maps with N are making it with the new stuff in NReality, which could possibly lead to a worse map?"

If the latter point is true, then the mapmaker is simply not very good. That's hardly a fault of NReality. It just supplies the tools. To say that the N map would be better, as a general rule, from a mapmaker is silly. If a good mapmaker is using it, he will make good maps (see Evil_Bob's, for example). If a bad mapmaker is making them, then it'll probably be a bad map, that simple. In an alternate timeline where NReality didn't exist, and that was the only difference, I'm sure the good mapmaker's NReality maps would instead be their N maps, which could only take detriment from the lack of options. A good mapmaker can use these new tools effectively and not clutter the map. Just because the potential to clutter the map with new variety doesn't mean that it will happen, or that it is the fault of the program itself. The blame rests solely in the hands of the mapmaker. Not to mention the coding itself provides a a barrier for entry for most really, really bad mapmakers, as they generally can't get a hang of it.

If the former point is true, that's again a fault at human nature. NReality itself is hardly to blame. If it's optional then none of this need happen; there's no reason that NReality isn't completely ignored other than it's neat-o.

Essentially, variety != detrimental to design quality as a general rule of thumb. Sure, it has the potential to break up the design flow of the map, but this would only happen if it's a bad map itself. It has just as much potential to be a *better* map due to fresh content. Taking the former situation and applying it to everything is a silly way of thinking about it.
