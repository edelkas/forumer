OK, I think I figured it out.
If you click on a spoiler, It opens all spoilers under it in posts (and sigs) made by that poster. (only that poster)

Example:
Spoiler: Spoiler OneOpen
Spoiler: Spoiler TwoOpen

EDIT: Wait a minute... It's not doing it anymore!! Maybe it doesn't do it if you name the spoiler?
SpoilerOpen
SpoilerOpen

EDIT (again): Yep. That's it. It does it for unnamed spoilers.
