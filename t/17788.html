#330407 16 2007-06-12T14:49:43Z usaswim
Here are my opinions about that map:

1) Tileset. Too simple, too basic, but not aesthetically pleasing. Seems random with no theme. Unbeneficial.

2) Object placement. I recommend not attaching gold onto tiles. Don't line them up side by side like that. It's not aesthetically pleasing. Mine placement seems out of place, awkward, and ugly.

3) Locked doors. You overused them. Going back and forth everywhere to collect those isn't fun. At all. Moreover, surrounding objects and enemies with locked doors is horrendously ugly and just destroys looks. It's terribly ugly when you do that and it's inconvenient. I recommend changing the tileset so you only have to place a locked door on 1 side. It looks so much better that way.

4) Enemy placement. Place enemies where you can take full advantage of it. Don't overuse enemies just so you can cover up the entire map. "Recycle" enemies. Put them in frequently visited places. Don't stack enemies next to each other unless it's aesthetically pleasing.

5) One ways. Don't just put them wherever you want. Put them in places where they might come in handy. Placing them is large quantities side by side looks ugly, in my opinion, and is definitely not aesthetically pleasing.

#330921 25 2007-06-13T18:56:11Z usaswim
I could give you advice on races and DDAs.

DDAs
  • No clustering ANYTHING. EVER.
  • Rarely, if ever, use launchpads
  • Make it a good length
  • Don't EVER use gausses unless you use lots of them. They are seemingly pointless, and makes the map more difficult to make than it should. (Though I plan to make a gauss-only DDA sometime...)
  • Use an enemy as efficiently as possible. Don't just use it once, and then throw it away (as in, use a trap door to block a rocket)
  • If there's lots of rockets, don't just make them all die at once, because it's so unprofessional. Just find a way to escape them.
  • No teleporters. If you teleport all the way across the map, it's going to be really hard to follow, and is just an excuse to escape an enemy
  • Try to at least make the tileset somewhat attracting
  • Use a variety of propulsion
  • Use as much space in the map as possible. Empty spaces are just... no.
  • Making close calls is the most important thing. As long as there are uber close calls, everything else won't matter
  • It has got to be fun to watch
  • Don't make it take years to load. Short loading times often tend to make people watch it again.
  • Never use gold delay
  • No lag. Lagging just confuses people
Races
  • Tileset. Make it sexy.
  • Flow is very important
  • The entire map has to have a central theme. Nothing can be random, unless random is the theme of the map, but it has to be pretty obvious
  • Gold placement and amount is important
  • A few mines wouldn't hurt
  • Occupy lots of space
  • Don't make it follow a same path more than twice. It's just an excuse to make it longer.
