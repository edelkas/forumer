C is a tileset-based competition for those who know how to integrate a given tileset into the map's aspects.

You may choose ONE of the 6 given crosses and must make a map out of it. You may only edit the cross by a maximum of one tile. The rest of the tileset may be edited. However, the cross may not be moved around, it may not be connected to the sides either. The map must have at least two types of enemies, mines are considered enemies. The map must not be of extreme difficulty or extreme easiness. Gold must be included and DDAs or Survivals are not allowed.

The judging is based on how you manage to integrate it into the map to create a different style of map. You should avoid adding too many enemies as that ruins the concept of the map. Sign-ups are open until I close them. The deadline for the competition will be announced on the end date for signups.
