Ok, so anyway, these are icon files, that can be used to make your PC shortcuts, to ANY file, look like N Things.


HOW TO USE

You must first decompress the file, then go to the shortcut you want to change. Rightclick on it, and say properties, make sure you're on the shortcut tab, and then you'll see three buttons, one should say "Change Icon" Click on it. Browse through your files, until you see the files you decompressed, and hit apply. Congrats, you changed the icon. BTW, it helps to note the original icon location, just in case.

I made these because macaddict17 ( I think it was) made some Mac icons of this type, and anything on macs thats not on PCs (or better on PCs) annoys me, so..... one thing lead to another.....
