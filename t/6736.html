#132318 8 2006-01-14T13:46:58Z pokemaniac1342
As a Chinese who has a bit of study in Buddhism (my granny is one), the concept there is that your actions before death directly influence what you reincarnate as.

Every action begets some sort of consequence, so if now, in this life, you are mute, you might have been a liar in your previous life. If you are say blind, you might have looked at too much porn in your previous life (no joke here). Etc.

In fact, it extends beyond that. The buddhist "wheel of reincarnation" has 6 portions, mainly Heaven, man, animal, hell, hungry ghost, and another one I don't know how to name. If you did a crapload of evil deeds for example, you may not even get to reincarnate as a man. You might instead, become an animal in your previous life (this applies to people who slaughter animals for a living, for example), or even go to hell.

Therefore, this is one of the ways buddhism encourages one to do good deeds. Since every action begets some sort of consequence in the next life, if you want to live a comfortable next life, you need to do lots of good stuff. It's an interesting concept, and there are plenty of people who claim that they can see into their previous and next lives. I don't believe in it though.
