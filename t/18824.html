Mare and Raigan specifically said that multiplayer should not be suggested for the next version of N for the PC. It's not just about a disliking of the idea, it's just that it's not entirely possible for them to implement it using Flash, as well as the fact that they'd have to change a lot of the gameplay to suit multiplayer. Now with N+, which is coming out on platforms which do support multiplayer, they have decided to include it because essentially they're starting from scratch, meaning that they can make the game with the idea of multiplayer in their heads. No doubt multiplayer will be something to look forward to, which is why everyone is "drooling over it". The message at the top of the suggestions forum has been put there at M&R's request, I believe, because those are elements they simply do not want in the PC N. N+ is going to be something completely different.
