I was fortunate enough to talk to Philadelphia Flyers goalie (#30) Antero Niittymaki on AIM...here are the questions and answers.

Interview with Philadelphia Flyers netminder, Antero Niittymaki

Me: if the flyers played a better defensive game, i'm sure you'd be up there
Antero Niittymaki: yea we're struggling a lot this year.

Me: when you were young who were your idols
Me: hockey idols that is
Antero Niittymaki: just finnish goaltenders, i dont think theyre well known in the us

Me: what's the hardest team to face, in your opinion
Antero Niittymaki: i dont think there's a hardest team to face. some teams are bad because we haven't won in so long or have such a bad record against, like tampa bay, but every game is a new try and some teams might be easy at one point in the season and really hard at others it all depends

Me: did you really sneeze when the camera focused on you during a game
Antero Niittymaki: its just the cold gets to me. i also move my head around to keep it stretched out a lot too.

Me: could you tell me about the overtime goal you scored for the phantoms in the AHL?
Antero Niittymaki: that was hilarious
Antero Niittymaki: so it was the end of the season
Antero Niittymaki: we were playing the bears
Antero Niittymaki: they're the other pennsylvania team so we have a big rivalry
Antero Niittymaki: anyways it was tied going into overtime
Antero Niittymaki: and the bears needed to win the game to make the playoffs
Antero Niittymaki: so they pulled their goalie. in overtime. not too smart. but they did it.
Antero Niittymaki: anyways it was 6 on 4 cause they had a powerplay
Antero Niittymaki: and pulled the \goalie
Antero Niittymaki: and so theytook a shot and i blocked it
Antero Niittymaki: and it managed to bounce off the boards just right and go into the empty net at the other end
Antero Niittymaki: and sincei was the last one to touch it
Antero Niittymaki: i got the goal
Antero Niittymaki: how great is that
Me: that's sick
Antero Niittymaki: i mean i wasnt trying to get it in, i was just making saves but thats how it happened
Antero Niittymaki: it was pretty funny none of us knew what had happened for a second

Me: i don't think there's that many good rookie tenders in the nhl this year
Me: last year, there were many good ones
Me: lundqvist, miller, you, emery, the list goes on
Antero Niittymaki: that's cause of the lockout
Me: true.
Antero Niittymaki: i mean we're all talented but that year off definitely gave us a leg up just like ovechkin got a leg up on crosby
Antero Niittymaki: man i hate that kid
Antero Niittymaki: always scoring goals on me
Me: i was wondering what if there was ever a crosby-lemieux-jagr line
Antero Niittymaki: that would be sick
Me: yeah
Antero Niittymaki: i wouldnt wanna play against that
Antero Niittymaki: haha
Me: lol
Antero Niittymaki: just kidding
Antero Niittymaki: im always up for a challenge
Antero Niittymaki: but id need some good defense
Me: in shootouts do you get excited or nervous?
Antero Niittymaki: well at first i was nervous who wouldnt be and i think at the beginning every goaltender is nervous. i mean its such a rough position to be in. especially in the ahl with the playoff game situation. but you definitely need to just stay calm and treat it like any other breakway.

Me: it was a pleasure to meet you mr. niittymaki
Antero Niittymaki: same here, glad to have such good fans
Antero Niittymaki: keep watching
Me: i will
Me: see you at the next rangers/flyers game
Antero Niittymaki: definitely
Me: yup
Antero Niittymaki: later
Me: peace
