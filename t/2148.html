I've been tossing around an idea for collection maps and I even posted a suggestion that would make them a LOT more friendly to play (a gold counter). Basically, the idea is to design the map not to be ran through quickly, but to puzzled out and try to get as many gold bars as you can. No time limits, no rush, no other factors to determine your score. The trick to making this fun, of course, will be in the tileset and gold placement.

But, and probably most importantly, will any of you find a map of this kind fun? An example would be:

The Collector

No moving enemies and no need to do it fast. Lucidium is the only person that posted a score on this map as of this writing, but I'm not even sure if he found it "fun". So I'm asking all of you.

Fun? Worth making? Worth playing?

This kind of map will take quite a while to develop fully so I won't be making them quickly. Also, without a gold counter, it will be very difficult to make a map that has a lot of gold in it. If it is too hard to count then people won't bother.

So again, I ask for your thoughts.
