The N Hospital Fund
An inventive NEW competition!

This is possibally the coolest competition I've ever thought up.

Objective
The N Hospital Fund needs money for those in need. So, it is my duty to get together map-makers, good or bad, and make DDA's to rase money. But, how does this work, I hear you ask? Well, the hospital came up with a new way of rasing money. For every time you fake die in a map, money is rased according to which enemy was used. Here is a guideline:
  • Mines: $10
  • Floorguards: $20
  • Drones: $50
  • Seeker Drones: $100
  • Thwumps: $500
  • Rocket turrets: $1000
  • Unspecified: $5000
Every time you fake die in the DDA, the enemy you use is added to the total prize pool. The enemy(s) you use in your DDA can be used over and over again. Every week or so, a money target is set. If, as a group, you make this deadline by the end of the week, you go to the next round. The person who raised the lowest money is out of the competition. Until there is 5 or 3 people left (according to how many people join) the amount will be set individually and the person who rases the lowest money will be out. People can also get out of the competition by:
  • Failing to rase enough money
  • Forfeit
  • Not submitting a map
Every competition deadline will be the day prior to start. I am hoping to have at least 10 people or more until I start. PM's will be sent out with sig tags and odd ones to notify people of the coming deadline.
I shall use an example of this 'judging' on me recently posted map, NUMA Confessions (And Concussions)
Mine use: 36 = $360
Rocket Use: 1 = $1000
Thwump use: 1 = $500
Door use: 35 = $35
TOTAL = $1895 raised

Specifications
All DDA's must have an end and at least two different enemies to raise money for. Maps with launchpads in will risk a $10 dollar fine for eack one, because it is just too easy with launchpads as you can direct the ninja around easily. The only way to have launchpads in your map is to have them as a fake death maker. You can also use exit doors as people have discovered. The use of these doors will add a $1 per door to your total amount raised. THERE ARE NO INFINITE DEATHS AS IT IS TOO EASY TO WIN.

Competitors:
  1. darn_hippie
  2. DDRave
  3. The_apprentice - Thwump Hospital
Prizes
1st prize: Dedicated map (By numa_ninja), signature set (Courtesy condog_111), sig bar
2nd prize: Signature set (Courtesy condog_111), sig bar
3rd prize: sig bar

Target Money
Weekly prize pool:
$10,000
This amount is set per individual

Well, I guess that's all you need to know! Happy map-making and good luck!
