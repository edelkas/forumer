#273906 2 2006-12-20T18:11:38Z jtbandes
For each given episode (in this case episode 40), you have 90 seconds to complete that entire episode. What happened for you is that you used almost all of your time on 40-0 and 40-1, so there's none left for 40-2. The only way around this is to play episode 40 again, making sure to get gold (your timer gains 2 seconds per piece) and not to waste time.


#273907 3 2006-12-20T18:11:41Z dejablue
For each episode, time carries over. So if you completed 40-0 and 40-1, the time you had at the end of 40-1 will be your starting time for 40-2. With 0.374 seconds, you'll have to start the entire episode over again. It just isn't possible to complete this level with that little time to start.
