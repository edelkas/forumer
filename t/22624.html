#471529 (forumer) 5 2008-05-19T06:18:00Z Ignate
XiAH: Well, since I lost Paint, I'll just describe it in words.
In the title at the top of the comic, where it says "TEH NINJA - BY XIAH - COMIC 0-00-0" Look at "NINJA". At the right side of N, one line is 1 pixel wide while the other lines are 2 pixel wide. And now see J. The longer line at the right side is only 1 pixel wide while the others are 2 pixels wide. This is caused by the font size, because in order to make the font size smaller by each unit, it will reduce the width on some lines, making them uneven, and will make a comic seem unprofessional. You can fix this by changing the font size and see which ones have even lines.
