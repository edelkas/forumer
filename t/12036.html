#217724 2 2006-08-20T23:30:42Z Twistkill
Sure is, buddy.

Find your N .sol file by searching your computer for ".sol". When you find it, copy the .sol file. Do not copy the entire folder that its in, because the random series of letters and numbers that its in is different with every computer, so copy just the .sol file. Save the copied .sol file to a CD or another removable storage device, and voila.
