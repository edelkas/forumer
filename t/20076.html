#377612 2 2007-11-08T02:10:23Z Hendor
I've just played a couple levels, but I've taken a look at most of them. Nearly every single level has too many objects where they are not needed at all. You guys do this with three items: mines, launchpads and doors. Sure the latter two look cool and may be kinda fun to go flying through, they greatly increase the loading time of the level, they make the level look messy and frankly noobish and it makes the level not a lot of fun for more experienced players. So, in the levels where you have whole columns and rows full of launchpads (Mars Dungeon 2 is the worst case), take them ALL out. Same goes for the repeating pattern of {door switch - door - door switch - door}. In the above mentioned level however, taking them all out would make for an excrutiatingly boring level. You want to make sure all parts of your level have a point.

To sum up what you need to do: WAYY less objects, more simplicity(no spiraling tunnels). Minimalistic is often better than complex.
