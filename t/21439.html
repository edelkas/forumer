Quote: Ad, March 01, 2008 10:58 pm
I started playing this again yesterday. I've beat BoweserWillyWart combo in one save, Tyson, and ContraBirdo in other. I'm at Dracula now. I've got two of those item things too. What're they for?

Oh, the secret items. They don't actually have any purpose, but they're just fun to collect. Which ones do you have?

Here's some spoilers!
Spoiler: all of the bosses, and how to beat them
(listed in chronological order. You can play most of the bosses in any order, but I'll also give the path that most people take, it's probably the easiest.)

1. Mike Tyson. He's the first boss of the upper path, directly after the room with the crazy plane.
Ok, well, he'll try to uppercut you. In his first stage, you're a lot faster than him, so just run to the left, dodging his uppercuts. When he breaths fire at you (I reckon he looks like he's crying actually. ) shoot him five times in the head, he'll go down. Stay where you are.
Second round- He'll move a little faster. Stay where you are for the first uppercut, then jump over, and run to the right. He'll try to hit you, but I think if you stay on the ground it doesn't kill you, as long as you're a little bit in front of him. Once you're on the right hand side, just shoot at him when he cries again. Easy.
Third Round - Ooooooh... Well, this one's a little hard. Try staying on the right for two uppercuts, and then run over to the left for the third, but sometimes this doesn't work. Be careful, he's faster than you on this one. Just practice, and work out a strategy, I have one, but it's really hard to explain and sometimes doesn't work.
Once you've finished, go to the right, get the orb, and then go back to the left to save. If you don't get the orb first, you'll have to play him again.

2. Mecha Birdo - Directly after the G&G area.
Right, well she's got three stages as well. (I think it's a she.) First, you just aim like crazy for that antenna. Not too hard, just make sure the stupid shy guys don't knock you off your platforms.
Stage 2 - Missiles move faster, and laser eyes as well! This time, aim like crazy for the eyes. About thirty shots should do it. Sorry, not really that much advice I can give, except make sure you get the most out of your jumps, or you won't make the gap between the missiles.
Stage 3 - Oh no, active missiles! Don't touch the fronts, or you'll die. Also, more shy guys, and I think the missiles move faster too. Well, just aim like crazy for the mouth, not really any more advice for ya though. After everything stops, just wait, and you'll go inside mecha birdo automatically, or.. something.

3. Dracula - Ugh. After the Castlevania area.
Ok, it's a two stage fight, but that doesn't make it any easier. It takes a bit of practice, but it becomes a lot easier if you learn all his moves, and how to dodge them. Stupid giant white balls. (ew...)
Well, first stage is fairly easy, but then the brains come along. Well, to avoid them, I try to get the both on one side of me, and then make them merge by shooting them together. Makes them a lot easier to manage.
No advice here, sorry.
Also, the kid's voice is the greatest thing ever. THE GREATEST THING EVERRRRRRRR

4. Hmm.. Kraidgeif would be next, I suppose. Go through the tetris room, and the kirby area.
He's a mix between Zangeif, and something with Kraid in it's name, not sure what.
Three stage battle. Not difficult, once you get the hang of it. Stage one - Aim for the face. In order to dodge his attacks, go down to the bottom left. Aim for the face, again. Dodge. Aim for the- yeah.
Stage TWO - He has a variety of attacks. Aim for the eyes, and he'll step backwards. When he attacks dodge (obviously). Then, he'll crouch, aim for the face again. When he runs forward, he'll smash the blocks, that's the start of...
Stage THREE - Blankas. From the crotch. I'm not joking. Jump on top of them, and shoot the face like crazy. LIKE CRAZY. He should blow up, and you can run across the floor to the right hand side.

5. The next two are pretty interchangable, but let's say you do the Klown Kar first. You can either take the bottom path from the start, or just keep going after Kraidgeif. Three stage fight, AGAIN.
STAGE 1 - Bowser - Easy. Stay on the right hand side, and then, when the bomb appears, shoot it into him. Very easy.
STAGE 2 - Wart - Easy. Dodge the shockwaves, and then shoot like hell when that thing appears.
STAGE 3 - Wily - HARD. Dodge the flames, then, when the giant bowling ball thing appears, stay on the left. Shooting it make the bullets reflect upwards and hit the boss. Then jump over the ball when it's about to crush you (I find this easiest to do when you're on the left hand side, but if you're on the right, then you'll shoot the ball quicker. Up to you, I guess.
VERY IMPORTANT::::::: Grab the orb, and walk back to the left hand side. It seems like you can't save, but you can: Jump upwards on the, and you'll go back to the save point. I beat the boss, and didn't save... had to do him again.

6. Mother Brain. Ok, I'll give you a quick guide on how to get there, this one took me a while to find. When you're in the factory path (with the DUMB BUGZ), and you have to ride a platform up in a column of spikes? (right before the yellow laser room). Well, if you jump down the hole below the platform, after you've ridden it up, then you can do a few more rooms, and end up down at Tourain. Stupid room.
Well, no advice here really. It's only a single stage, but it's damn, damn hard. You have to avoid the circle things and the guns, and you have to shoot the brain too. And there's hundreds of them. The only good thing is that M.B. doesn't take many hits. Well, good luck, I guess.

7. Dragon Devil. This one has to be second last, because you can't get to it until you've beaten the other six. In the palace of the guy, right after the mine carts. Well, he's not too hard. Possibly one of the easier bosses, actually.
Shoot whenever he has his mouth open. Dodge his fireballs. When he turns into the six fireball things (OH GOD AWFUL GRAPHICS), you have to work out the best route to dodge them, it's pretty hard to get a route that works consistantly. He'll do this three times, and then chase you back to the start, where he'll crash and die if you've shot him enough times. WHATEVER YOU DO DON'T MOVE AFTER THIS, OR YOUR GAME WILL GLITCH. You'll be teleported to the Entrance of the guy. Good luck.....

8. The Guy. At the very end. A massive SIX stage battle.
First, he'll shoot a large bullet at you. Dodge it, and drop down to the ground. You can dodge him while he tramples you, but I have a better way: If you stay close to him, right near the pillar on the left hand side, and shoot like crazy, you don't have to dodge him, he'll just go right onto stage two!
STAGE 2 - Ok, now you have to jump up to the ledge on the left. Wall climb, and stay up the top to dodge his stupid bullets. Once he jumps over to the left, jump down to the bottom. Run over to the right, but stay on the ground. When he shoots his GIANT bullets at you, jump over them, and shoot at the same time. If you hit him enough he'll go onto stage three (you'll know, he'll jump down onto the bottom), but more likely, you'll end up having to repeat stage 2, just do the same thing.
STAGE 3 - He should be down on the ground, in the middle. He'll shoot large, but not giant bullets at you. Now, the trick is that these bullets reflect towards you when they hit a wall, so the way to do it is to jump over them, and make them reflect towards him! Do this four times, and you'll kill him.
Victory! But...
STAGE 4 - OH NO HE'S LIKE 50 TIMES BIGGER. Aim for the eyes. Dodge the fire. Not too hard, really.
STAGE 5 - Well, this'll start with him shooting fire jets out of his eyes. They always travel anti-clockwise, so if you can get on the other side, then you're safe. Then, after he's stopped, shoot the eyes, and the white things.
STAGE 6 - Very very hard. His eyebrows block the path between the eyes, so you have to go around them, above the screen, dodging spikes, fire and white thingoes all the time. Shoot both the eyes to win.
And yes, he does say "I had sex with your mother". O_O

The final apple. Yes, I was dumb enough not to dodge this, so I had to do it again. Stupid ending credits.
Spoiler: all of the secrets, and how to get them
(listed from easiest to hardest, in my opinion.)

1. In the game over room. If you head to the left two rooms after the start on the upper path, (instead of riding the spikey platform up and dodging the spikes in the narrow column) you can jump over the pit of spikes by touching invisible platforms. (search) It's probably the easiest one to get.

2. Right after the hall of former guys. (directly after the fake 'game crash', that made me laugh. ) Just before the incinerator, and final tower of the guy. There's a save point you can't get to, so you think it's just there to trick you. But, if you fall directly down on the left hand side, and then jump off the screen to the left below the bridge, there's a secret platform, which will take you to the save point. After that there's a room, with the secret item at the end. This one's meant to be the hardest to get, I reckon it's a lot easier than number 6.

3. In the tourain area. In the first room of that area (right after the invisible block room), wait for a while, and weird thing will come out of the wall. Go in through where it came out, and you'll be in the room. I'll leave you to figure this one out, but it's quite hard to get right.

4. In the G&G area (with the zombies and tombstones). You know the hole where you fall down to start playing Mecha birdo? Well, instead of jumping down there, you can jump up the right hand side of the screen. It's a hard jump to make, but the room you jump into is even harder. The most spikes I've ever seen. ;_;

5. In the kirby area. After getting into the cave, don't save. You only get one chance to do this. The truck will keep driving, and smash a hole in the right hand side of the screen, If you follow it, you can get into the secret cavern. But, if you die on the way, the hole will seal up, so don't save there, save before the truck. (you can also get this item through a glitch in the tetris room, but I haven't pulled that off...)

6. Right after the tourain area. I can't get this one, although I can get to the room where it is. After the 'snake' room, you get to the room with the couches that launch you upwards, right before the palace of the guy. Well, if you hold jump while on those couches, they launch you further, and take you all the way up to the secret room.

Anything else anyone needs any help with, just ask me.

Spoiler: WalkthroughI Wanna be The Guy The movie: The game: The walkthrough
Please note that this walkthrough is designed for the HARD difficulty. It will NOT mention any save points in medium.
Index

1. Walkthrough
This is a detailed walkthrough, explaining how to do, well, everything in the game.
Have fun playing!!!
1.1 Start to Mike Tyson
Ok, in the room that you start in has 4 routes which you can go through. 1 will kill you immediately...there is no point going through it. 1 is a stupid route that is not only difficult, but also skips a boss. One will be used later. One is the desired route to begin on. The first direction you want to head to is straight up. You will reach a room full of apples. The first will fall off, killing you if you touch it, and the solution to this problem is to jump over it. This will also cause the second apple to fly upwards. Dont get hit. The next two apples will fall simultaneously when you jump over them. With the next apples, you have to run under them and dart back, so as to make them fall, but without killing you. For the rest of this flat ground, just run flat out at the steps of ground, then slightly double back when you get near the second last apple. From here, Jump up towards the next platform, but double back when you get near it, so that the apple doesnt kill you when it flies up. Now jump up to the next platform and slowly head toward the edge, and when the apple flies up, step back away from it. Jump across to the next platform, and the two apples that you pass will fly up, but miss you. You also do not need to worry about the last apple...it falls downwards.
In the next room there are 4 clouds, the first does nothing, the second falls down, and the next two fall up. To get through this room, jump from the first cloud to the second one, and quickly jump to the third. As the third cloud rises, drop off the right hand side, and use your air-jump to make it to the last cloud. From here you need to jump up to the platform above, but VERY quickly fall back onto the cloud, as a spiked platform will fall. Wait for it to rise and then jump where it was and save it.
SECRET ROUTE:
From that save point, jump over to the left, and go to the next frame. Now, it appears that there is nowhere that you can go, however, this is not the case, there are actually invisible platforms...you just need to know where they are. The first one is directly above the 2nd hole in the ground and 2 squares below where you start. To reach the next invisible platform, just jump as far as you can forwards. Do this again to get onto the left hand ground.
In the next frame there are more invisible platforms. The first is just above the second volcano, the second is 3 blocks to its left. The next one is on top of the cloud in front of you, and if you shoot your gun whilst standing on this block, you can see the location of the next one. The next invisible block is slightly higher and to the left, and you can see its location by jumping once and shooting. The next platform is above the previous one, and can once again be found by jumping once and shooting. From here, double jump straight up and you will enter the room with the secret. From here, grab the secret and go to the right side of the room to exit, and head back to the save point.
END OF SECRET ROUTE
 
Once back at the save point, you need the spiked platform to fall down again. When you get it to come down, you need to get on top of it, and ride it up to the next frame, dodging the spikes as you rise. Once you are up, jump forward, but pull back once over the block next to you, as lightning will shoot out of the cloud above you. After doing this, jump on the moving platform, and jump over the spikes as you move to the right. Be careful when you get to the right hand platform though, as the star second closest to the right will fall, trying to kill you.
In the next room, the first thing you should do is jump and shoot through the spikes, saving the game. When you move on, the spike above your head will fall, so you have to jump from the moving platform back onto the ground where you started, or the spike will kill you. Next, jump onto the other moving platform as it is moving downwards, and jump when it gets near the spike so that you dont die. As the platform rises again, jump onto the gap between the two spikes on your right, but then jump immediately to the ground on the right, as that block will fall. Save it again now, because in the next frame, you can expect to do battle with none other than the legendary Mike Tyson!
END OF 1.1
1.2 Mike Tyson
Ok, with Mike Tyson, there is one very important thing to remember...he can barf fire. DONT run into it. That is the first thing to remember. The second is that once you have beaten him, you MUST stay in the middle of the floor...Mario appears next to the right hand door, and declares Tyson out for the count. If you are where he appears when he gets there, you WILL die.
Ok, to begin this fight, you must go to the very left side of the frame. He will punch and miss twice, and when he punches the third time, double jump vertically, and you will not be hit by his attack. After this, he will lower his guard, and you have to shoot him in the mouth. When you have shot him enough he will go down. You arent done yet though, you will have to do this another 2 times to win. When he gets back up, stay where you are for the first punch, but when he punches the second time, jump over the punch and run to the right hand side. His next punch will miss, and you just have to jump up to the right hand platform and shoot him in the mouth. When he gets up again, you will need to jump over his first punch, and head to the left, however, when he punches again, you will be halfway, so you need to jump over that. This is VERY hard to accomplish, but if you keep trying you will get it. From here, if you continue running left and jump up to the left hand platform, he will miss his next punch, and you can finish the bout by shooting him in the mouth for the last time.
From here, once Mario has opened the doors go right and collect the orb, THEN go back to save it. If you go to save it before getting the orb, you will have to fight Tyson again. And that sucks.
END OF 1.2
