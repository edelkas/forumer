i think i see the problem: if episode5 is "cheat hilighted", you can't beat episode 5 to change that, you have to beat episode _4_.

this is because the hilight tracks the farthest episode you've _reached_, not beaten.

if 5 is cheat-hilighted, it's because you reached it by cheating. so, beating it doesn't matter -- you have to beat episode 4 to prove you can get to 5 without cheating.

i hope this helps -- trib4lmaniac said the same thing.

if they're all hilighted, starting from the very beginning should work.

an easier method: go to a website with a flash movie.
