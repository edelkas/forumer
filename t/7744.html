I am really annoyed at people, for rating maps down because they are easy. I JUST looked at a map, that got rated a 3/5 because it was easy. Another map of mine was easy, and it got rated lower because of that.

Quote: tl and others
tl
what's so bad about it's short and easy? we can't have only monster maps here.

Dex22
good tileset, but short and easy

kendogg102
Cool tileset, awfully easy

People forget that it is not difficulty that makes a map, but playability. If the map is fun, but easy, why is it worse than a difficult mine jumper? Mine jumpers are great, but they are horribly unplayable. For example, they have the most akward jumps. But they are challenging. In fact, that is the apeal of most mine jumpers. In action maps though, that is not the apeal. It is usually supposed to be a fun, fast paced maps, with lots of room to be flexible in your path. Because of the difference in style, they should be judged in a different way. But that is one of the problems. They expect all of the levels to be judged in the same way, causing fun, easy action maps to be rated lower because they are not as hard as a mine jumper.

Quote: n00b
Great maps cannot be completed on your first try

If this becomes a problem, we can all agree numa is in for even more unfair ratings. Newcomers might also become discouraged, if they make an easy map. If they also get it into their heads that easy maps are bad, then maybe we might see the end of easy maps.

Quote: Mapmaking guidlines
If you cannot beat the map, you probably should not submit it

Newcomers are never that good at N, so they usually make easy maps. But the bad ratings might make them quit a possibly great mapmaking career early. The next all-star mapmaker would quit, because some idiot told them that there easy map sucked.
