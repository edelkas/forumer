Oh, simple problem, my friend.

The forum's accounts and NUMA's accounts are not linked. You need to create a new account to log into NUMA, so we apologize for any inconveniences this somehow causes you.

Go into N's Configure menu. In the bottom right-hand corner, there are fields to fill out for a Username, password, and e-mail. Fill out those fields, then click on "Create New User" underneath them to create a NUMA account. Now you should be able to log into NUMA.
