Since Kablizzy and Keron seem to be too busy doing damage control, I have decided to start the second contest without consultating them because I'm tired of posting in "Remove the Banner" polls. W00t! Go anarchy!

Anyways, the contest:

Goal: Create the best ASCII art picture of a pony.

Points for:
  • Creativity: Don't just make pictures of ponies getting impaled or eating something. We've seen it.
  • Complexity: Basically, how many ASCII characters are used.
  • Aesthetics: Does it look like a good pony? Does it even look vaguely like a pony?
Note: Each criteria will be worth ten points for a possible total of thirty.

Judges:
  • Judge #1: Clovic
  • Judge #2: legiax
  • Judge #3:
  • Judge #4:
Note: I need 2 more judges. Anyone can apply as it no longer matters what side you are on.

Deadline: You will have a week to submit your art. The contest will end on the 29th 30th of July at 10:00PM GMT.

Submitting: Each submission will be sent to me via PM. When I receive a submission, I will make note of the author and then pass it along anonymously to our judges. These steps are taken to assure the lack of voting bias. At the end of the week, I will post all the maps along with their scores.

Important:
  • The number of submissions is limited to one. Sorry.
  • Your submission must be original. I don't care if you generate it with software just so long as it isn't copied.
  • The code tag is useful for submitting ASCII art as it doesn't screw up the indentation.
I think that's it. Good luck!

Edit: And I hadn't actually seen this thread when I made the contest. It is just a really bizarre coincidence.
