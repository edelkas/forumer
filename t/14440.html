#269316 2 2006-12-13T07:52:59Z Decoy
You could try approximating it with a triangle based on the 'oldest' particle (i.e. the one closest to fading out entirely) for each stream, but it's probably easier to do a rectangle based on the 'oldest' and 'youngest'.

Find the two parallel lines that are tangential to the two circles, and extend them such that connecting the end points of either line results in another tangential interval.

Or, more simply:
___________
( )|OOOOOOO|
young^ old^

It won't be perfect, but it will be good enough, and fairly fast to boot.
