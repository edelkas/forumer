Let it be known that I hereby *sign* the decree.

DDA's should be individually rated as misc. category, and test levels should just be, well, test levels: no ratings.

If you want the overall rating to represent the best all-rounder (DDAs inc), you can multiply the overall score by the standard deviation of the the number of maps in each category normalised to 1.

Hence, someone with 8 DDAs and nothing else would have his/her overall rating multiplied by a coefficient less than 1 (maybe as low as 0.2) where as someone with 5 maps in each category would have a standard deviation of 1 hence maximum multiplier and would therefore have a greater score.

It's all about defining some weightings but I'd be glad to provide a mathematical model should anyone be interested. This would also make people attempt categories they may not think is "their thing" and discover hidden talent. After all, this model rewards all-round designers.

Just a thought...
