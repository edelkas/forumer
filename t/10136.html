#182234 19 2006-05-30T11:18:41Z LittleViking
I live by a few simple philosophies.

"Let that which does not matter truly slide." Fight Club quote, I know, I'm a dork. But I've lived by this for a long time, even before Fight Club. The point is to forget the bad things that happen. Don't dwell on them. Your life is only so long, don't spend it greiving the small things. (I could throw in another quote from that movie now, "This is your life, and it's ending one minute at a time," to illustrate that you never get back that wasted time.)

"Don't let the bastards get you down." This goes with the last one, except with people. Nothing is better than annoying us than other people, but you have to let it slide. Carrying grudges around will only pile on the stress. Let them go.

"Live and let live." Live your life, take what you can, but don't hurt others in the process. You might be able to let the little things slide, but not everyone can. Hurting someone else can cause all kinds of stress in their life, even if it's really not a big deal. And you can get by without causing that.

"Ignorance is bliss." This isn't one I live by myself, so much as it is a fact of life. Some people never question what they're given. They follow the religion their parents teach them. They go to school, find a job, get married and have children. It's all tradition, and it's all they know. Some people never think look beyond. But guess what? Those people are happy. The point is that knowledge comes at a price. Anyone who tells you otherwise is among the ignorant.

"I'm here for the free food." What pleasure can you enjoy in life more often than food? You have a fair variety of different foods every week of your life and generally, you enjoy eating them. It's little everyday pleasures like this that make life worth living. Free food samples add even more variety at no cost to you. Absolutely win-win. So the bottom line is to look for variety and enjoy everyday things.


So that's all life is. Nothing more. As an end note, I'd like to live by a philosophy of never procrastinating, but I'm too lazy for that. I could do a lot with my life if I chose to. I could learn music, and dance, and art. I could meet interesting people and see places I've never seen. I could know people in every state and every trade. But I don't. And I don't think you do either.
