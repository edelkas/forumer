Sonic Robo Blast 2. A pretty good Freeware Sonic the Hedgehog game. Basically, it's a 2.5D Sonic game, or, the scenary is 3D, but the sprites are 2D. It's your basic Sonic game, you jump around, find Chaos Emeralds, same old same old, right? Wrong. This game Also has 8-16 player Co-Op, meaning, you can have 8-16 people trying to defeat Dr. Robotnik. Also, whenever you get all the Chaos Emeralds, you unlock more levels in the game, there's even a Super Mario Level set. The game is also pretty moddable. It uses .wads as mods, and, people make some pretty worth while mods for it. I'd go on, but I'd rather you play the game some rather than have me spoil it for you.

Also, kirby, Blazeraid, GamingWolf, and I used to play this all the time.
