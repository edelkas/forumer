This is a cool little thing I found while doing my sublime levels. I noticed that you can zoom in on the level. Anyway, I took it a step further and made a level where you zoom in on a room and then drag the level to the next as you go. It works really well in my opinion so I made a full scale level for the DS ned. It works on the other 2 as well though I am not positive about the mac versions.


Directions for the level:
Start by zooming in 2 times on the starting room with the guass.
As you go, drag the level to accomodate your needs.
Note that my level is for the DS ned. You may need to Download this.

Hopefuly this will spawn some other cool maps. Feel free to post them in this thread.
