#413659 3 2008-03-04T01:29:02Z MrKlorox
Reply from Nick:

Quote
As for the 1080p question, we actually don't have the TV's to test this on either... We're mere indies!
We used the Eets codebase, which renders it to a 720p backbuffer and then scales it up. It does use full screen anti-aliasing to smooth the edges out, but it doesn't do actual 1080p. It probably could totally do 1080p actually, but from a development standpoint it takes a lot less time to just take one resolution and scale it to all different TV formats, and since it was already set up for 720p, that's what we used..

I also asked:
Quote
Also, last night after posting this I booted up N+ in 480p fullscreen and saw black bars at the top and bottom, signifying that N+ was not designed with 4:3 as a primary aspect ratio. Wouldn't it be better to allow full 4:3 aspect ratio support so people forced to play in standard definition (and 1280x1024 via VGA) won't have necessary parts of the screen cut off; or so people that prefer to play with the camera zoomed all the way out won't have a smaller image than necessary with all the extra gray on either side?

To which he replied:
Quote
I already replied in the other thread, but yea, we basically had a system working that was using a 1280x720 backbuffer, and we decided not to change it since it worked. We focussed on this resolution, because it seemed to be the most commonly supported resolution/aspect ratio on most modern TV's.

I do agree with your comment on the 4x3 black bars being annoying, and I can tell you that I experienced this first hand because I moved the 360 to the bedroom, and we have an old CRT in the bedroom. I feel your pain!

For the next game we will do it differently.
