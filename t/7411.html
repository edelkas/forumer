#141326 5 2006-02-09T18:10:23Z Jiggerjaw
Well, first of all, you need to be sure that you've actually downloaded MetaNet's real version of N, and are not playing a rip-off version online called "N-game". When you upload the game, in the main menu, press the key ~ or |, and it'll take you to Ned, or debug mode. Now, copy and paste from the text box in a Numa map into the top text box in Ned. Then click outside the text box and press 'L'. Then press 'P' to play the level. Then turn on caps lock to play without frame by frame mode.
