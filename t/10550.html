#189903 9 2006-06-15T18:09:07Z mr__invisible
if you want to make the level appear as a userlevel and save it, then you have to make both Nv1.4 and the userlevels notepad shortcuts on your desktop. When you are finished making the level, copy the data from the first box and open up the userlevels notepad. Then scroll down and make your level at the end of the notepad. Construct it like this

$level name#your name#level discription (do not make to long)#level data (paste the copied data here)#
