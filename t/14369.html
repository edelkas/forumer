Welcome to the NTTS Season 4!

The NTTS - The N Time Trial Society - is a highscoring/demo-contest and the ultimate challange for everyone who likes playing N!

The contest is quite simple. The NTTS lasts for ten weeks. For this season, during each of these ten weeks, there will be chosen a pool of ten maps from Levelpacks, one map for each of the contest weeks. Each participant plays through the map for that week, and submits a completed demo to the contest. Everyone is allowed to submit one demo for the week, and each demo will be judged in both categories.

Demo submissions last for seven days from the start of the week, after which, the week is over and no more demos are allowed. At that moment, the judges begin deliberation, and rate the demos (see below for more infos about rating).

Judges:
- KinGAleX
- krusch
- spect
- LittleViking (Backup)

Rules:
- All demos will be made in Userlevels, using the map for that week.
- No cheating will be allowed. Any cheating from any player will automatically disqualify that participant from the remainder of the contest.
- You submit your demos to spect using the forum's integrated Personal Message function. Please do not use CODE or QUOTE to separate the demo data. Just copy&paste it!
- Only one demo per week will be allowed. Any demos beyond the first will be disqualified from that week's contest.
- Anyone can join without registering, and participation every week is entirely voluntary (although, participating every week gives you a far better chance of winning).

There are two categories in which your demos are rated:
- Time
- Style


Time will be the Time which is displayed in Userlevels. The top five fastest demos will take the following points for the week.

Best Time: 5P
1st Runner-up: 4P
2nd Runner-up: 3P
3rd Runner-up: 2P
4th Runner-up: 1P

The judges will then rank the demos by Style. This is entirely judges' discretion, but this includes such things as tricks, route-finding, execution, close calls, and so on.

Most Stylish: 5P
1st Runner-up: 4P
2nd Runner-up: 3P
3rd Runner-up: 2P
4th Runner-up: 1P

There's no overall category anymore. Style and Time will be seperated from each other all the time, and so will be the prizes.

Another difference to the last Season: There are prizes for anyone who get's at least FIVE points in one category!

This Week begins with this post, and starts with Cauldron by krusch from the Camisade Collection (see attachment). All demos must be in within one week from this post.

Have fun!
