I prefer St. Petersburg over Moscow. Moscow's just too big and it's the target of all kinds of terrorist attacks and stuff. Petersburg has lots of culture, (Catharine palace, Hermitage, etc.) and if you don't want russian food, just go round the corner and you'll be sure to find a Subway, McDonalds, Pizza Hut or something else. Also if you're looking for Computer Software/Movies, 505.ru is a GREAT store with lots of cheap games, other software and music and movies. (i won't question their legality, they've worked well so far).

Also, the subway (not the fastfood, the underground railway) is one of the deepest in the world. You go like 125 meters on the moving stairs (whaddya call it, an escalator?).

And they sell coca-cola in 4L packages (12 x 0.33L cans).

Anyways, it tasted like nothing I've ever tasted before, but it was actually good!
