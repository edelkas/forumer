My first suggestion is a map listing sorter, with which you could rearrange all of the maps listed in different orders, such as:
  • Newest to oldest / Oldest to newest
  • Highest rated to lowest rated / Lowest rated to highest rated
  • Most ratings to least ratings / Least ratings to most ratings
  • Most comments to least comments / Least comments to most comments
  • Map name ascending / Map name descending
  • Author Name ascending / Author Name descending
and anything else that would be useful. I've always felt that this would make finding some maps much easier, and that it would work well with the tag system.

My second suggestion is a recent comments section. I have a couple of ideas for this suggestion. One possibility could be a "10 Most Recent Comments" section on the front page (or however many), listing a little bit of the newest comments, the people who made them, and a link to the maps in which they were posted. Another possibility could be a "Recent Comments " section (or possibly an "All Comments" page) in every user's profile. These could also be used at the same time as well.

(I'm going to assume that at least one person somewhere is going to say something along the lines of: "People will start commenting on their own maps just to keep it on the recent comments listing if the recent comments thing is implied..." I think that we should be more focused on the positive outlooks of the idea, rather than the negative. There are probably enough users who are mature enough not to do this, and it wouldn't be much of a problem anyways, since it wouldn't be a big part of NUMA itself.)

So..?
