#85620 2 2005-07-23T17:52:32Z Guest
hi,
alternate means that the first time the drone hits a wall, it will turn clockwise; the second time, it will turn counter-clockwise, and then clockwise, then counter-clockwise.

of course.. i don't actually remember which direction the 1st turn is (i.e it might be CCW/CW/CCW/CW/etc. or CW/CCW/CW/CCW/etc..)

quasi-random means that it will turn CW or CCW; the decision is made based on the number of ticks since the start of the level, which is deterministic (i.e will happen the same way each time).

unfortunately both of those algos weren't really all that useful in practise
