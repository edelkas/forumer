1. The words pony, ponies, ponying, ponyer, GLAYYYSHUR, glacier, BAYYYKIN, and anything related to them are officially banned from this game. Using them will result in exclusion from the game.

2. Do not post the story in this thread. I will when it's long enough.

3. If you want to add to the story, post in this thread and I will put you on a list. The people on that list will be the people you can send the story to.

4. Put the member's name that last added to the story in your PM. There should eventually be a small list of member's names at the bottom of PM's.

5. Send a carbon copy of any PM's relating to this game to me. I will post the story when it's around 2 paragraphs long, and guess what? It will have a list of all the members to credit for it. That's why you put the last member's name at the bottom of your PM.
