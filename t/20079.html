Having not seen one of these for a while, I thought I'd start one of those little group map projects, where anybody just comes along and adds a segment to the map.

The rules are simple. Add whatever you like, without drastically changing the theme or feel of the map. Try to add something without taking anything away. (where possible. trim as needed.)
The goal is to get a map that represents everybody who contributed, rather than just the last person.
We might just go until we decide it's done, or maybe we'll do a set number of modifications, ten maybe.

The number of additions has been decided. Ten it is. When ten additions have been made, feel free to start a new map by posting a (very) simple new level. Save it as OpenMapProject(level number)_0.

I'll start off with a very simple map.
And one other thing. When you save your map here, save it as OpenMapProject_(your portion number)
for example, mine is saved OpenMapProject_0

Have fun!

Finished maps can now be found on NUMA!
