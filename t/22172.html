The use of biofuels has been touted as a way to reduce carbon emissions. Unfortunately, it can't possibly help. Nitrous oxide, produced by fertilizer breakdown, is 310 times as potent a greenhouse gas as carbon dioxide, meaning any advantage is destroyed.
