#448467 18 2008-06-25T07:39:29Z Ryzor
Well, I'm with this and not with this at the same time. Here's a harms/benefits list.

BITESIZE:
BENEFITS:
-more maps per day could be bitesized
-Bitesized maps commonly seen by users were the most recent ones.
HARMS:
-Pointless, idiotic and/or trite maps could be bitesized due to who the author was or who the sizer was. (such as when one of Guisepi's maps containing one object was sized)
-Bitesizers could remain anonymous if they wished.

FEATURED:
BENEFITS:
-A wider variety of map types is featured
-Featured maps generally garner more attention than your typical bitesized map
HARMS:
-Old maps are often featured, thereby putting the spotlight on the past and partly preventing NUMA's evolution.
-The small group provides for a narrowed view on NUMA. making a small group of people responsible for what all members of the community will see promotes a faint sense of elitism, as well as taking deserving maps out of the spotlight.
-Featured maps are shoved down your throat, whereas one used to have to click a tab or search for bitesized maps.

Personally, I liked the bitesize system more, but it's up to you to weigh the benefits and harms on your own. Alot of this is based in personal preference, which, as you all should know, is pointless to argue about, as it is not a subject that can be empirically proven.

General opinion forms somewhat of a general qualitative bell curve relative to the number of people providing their individual opinions. With too few people, opinion will most likely be biased (as it currenly is towards old maps). However, with a moderate number of people active in their contributions, you will get a balanced general consensus. And finally, with too many people, there will be too much infighting, and one sind will end up taking over either by numbers or persuasion, and will again dominate the general opinion. (case in point: todays mainstream media).

Top Rated should only come back under a couple of conditions:

If you were to bring back Top Authors, there should be 5 levels of top rated authors, with each level weighted differently.
  • Level 1 : This would belong to the top authors around on NUMA. Maps in this category would belong to authors with 60+ rated maps. This would show who has been the most dedicated to NUMA and who has the most amount of highest rated maps.
  • Level 2 : This would belong to authors with greater than 30 rated maps, but less than 60 rated maps. This would show up-and-coming authors that are getting recognition in the community for their awesome maps.
  • Level 3 : This would belong to authors with greater than 15 rated maps but less than 30 rated maps. It would be for newcomers to earn a recognition spot.
  • Level 4 : This would belong to those with less than 15 rated maps and would essentially be where n00bs start out at. There wouldn't be much recognition here, but it would be a place that some people could browse if they wanted to see who was coming up through the ranks as far as n00bs go.
  • Level 5 : The lowest level would belong to the authors who have disabled ratings. It would be weighted by how many times their maps have been favourited (I know it's a little sketch but it's the only thing I can think of to weight it by). This would essentially be a separate section to see which authors don't want their maps rated but still want the recognition.
This would have to be the only way I would think for Top Authors to come back. Otherwise you'll have Clifty, who is far from the best map maker on NUMA, at the top of the list again and it'd be stupid.

---

As for Top Rated, I think a similar system would have to occur, but ratings should have heavier weight to get to the Top Rated page. In the old system you needed just three 5's and two 4.5's to get a map on the Top Rated page. In any new Top Rated system I would like to see 2 things.
  • 1.) Maps are ordered by the date at which they became Top Rated. Otherwise you'll have Afternoon Dragon sitting at the top of the charts again. I think Top Rated maps should be ordered by the day at which they were put on the list, not by their ratings. In this way the Top Rated page would stay looking fresh and you wouldn't see the same maps over and over again.
  • 2.) Ratings for Top Rated should be weighted more. Increase the number of rates to reach Top Rated to 10 or 15 votes. Any map with an average rating of 4.8 or over after so many votes would get a spot on the Top Rated page.
If these things were not added to the Top Rated and Top Authors list, I probably wouldn't support their return unless somebody had an amazingly convincing argument otherwise.
