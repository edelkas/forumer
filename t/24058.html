The Continued Column

So. It's time for me to release the sequel to my level pack, The Completed Column, and now seems to be the perfect time to do so. I've been working on this sequel for over half a year, and like all things, it comes with a back story that I won't bore you with. This pack has 51 levels, most of which are easy except for the last two episodes. They are hard. Please, post any demos you have.

This pack has one dedication, and it is to Jerrod, aka rocket_thumped. He is an awesome guy and went through two rounds of playtesting this pack for me. Without him, it wouldn't be half as good. So for that, he gains the dedication of 13-2: Roll High Little One. He deserves it.

Enjoy all.
