This thread is for our composers. If you can compose music, both for yourself, and (More importantly) for others, sign in here. If you do more than one (Compose and Edit, for instance), please sign in to both threads.

Please, in this thread, only post once. If you have questions, pose them in general discussion.

Now, for you guys, what you'll be doing for the project is directing. We'll have a head producer that's going to oversee the project, and then we're going to have the rest of you guys work with individual musicians in order to produce differing levels of composition for the project.

You guys will be the visionaries, you guys will be dictating where the project goes, and beyond the individual projects that we'll do, you guys will be overseeing each individual piece. Now, depending upon how many of you will be composing, each of you will be responsible for a number of songs.

The first thing you should do, as a composer, is sign in here! Once we know how many composers, musicians, and editors we have, we'll divide you guys into groups of at least one composer, one musician, and one editor each.

The second thing you'll want to do is put up a thread for your group's project. This is where the individual compositions will be happening. Within the thread, we'll be getting together teams of musicians to create the pieces that you'll be composing for. This will be in addition to the individual projects that we'll be working on. I'm thinking that each team will only have to do a single composition, since it's such a big task, and since we'll likely be doing one or two individual compositions each.

Thirdly, you guys will need to keep in touch with me to be sure your group projects are flowing along nicely. I'll be trying to give you guys themes to follow for the pieces, but it's up to you guys (The composers) to be sure everything is on-beat and it's up to the editors to make sure it all sounds good. I'll allow for a lot of creative freedoms for these things, but I don't want the songs to be dance mixes, or anything like that. This stuff needs to be very up-to-par with my expectations, and if it isn't I'll let you guys know what's going on in my head so that we can get together and fix things.

And finally, it's your job to keep in touch with the other members of the group, and to make sure things are moving along nicely.

Beyond that, keep active, keep in touch with me, and welcome!
