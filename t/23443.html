It all happened last night. We were with some friends and Nuke-Mans older brother Matt offered o drive some of us home. Nuke-man and I sat in the back while his girlfriend Katie sat in the front. So we were on the highway and someone cut us off. At that moment, Nuke-man had his seatbelt off and was adjusting it. Matt swerved to avoid hitting the car and lost control. We flipp I can't believeed over a couple of times and Nuke-Man was thrown out of the car. He landed 10 feet away on his head and was knocked unconsious instantly. We rushed over and called 911. They tranported us to the hospital to treat him and Matt, who broke his arm. Katie and I had cuts and bruises, but we were worried about Nuke-Man. As I said earlier, he died at 4:00AM with his parents by his side.
