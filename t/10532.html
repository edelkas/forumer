I can answer your questions.

1. Maximum velocity is attained by not using the launchpad, but by jumping right at the base of the little slope, and jumping again where the slope hits the wall, and it should propel you upwards enough to get all the gold quickly. It is difficult to pull off, so don't worry if you don't get it first try.

2. You must hit the launchpad and jump at virtually the same time. If done correctly, you will be launched upwards with the velocity of the launchpad and the jump from the wall, resulting in a huge jump.

The secret of the exit switch. The door that holds the drones in is concealed behind the exit switch. If you touch the exit switch directly, you will touch the door switch as well, resulting in the drones being released. It is extremely tricky to pull off, but if you just scrape the exit switch, you can activate it without touching the door switch behind it.

I have only pulled off these moves by fluke, so do not get discouraged if you can't get them right away. Good luck.
#189201 3 2006-06-14T04:50:42Z pokemaniac1342
1) That is a triple jump. Normally, one uses slopes (or inward curves) at the base of a wall to achieve more speed by...erm...how do I explain it...jumping on it? Here you jump onto the slope, jump on the slope, and then kick off the wall. It's hard to explain...I think I do it three times in my 14-0 run, so check it out. Check the lower ones out too to see what I mean by jumping on slopes to boost speed up walls.

2) That's a "super jump". When you have a launchpad right next to a vertical wall, you can get a huge boost by landing on the pad when you are also in contact with the wall, and pressing jump when you hit the pad. If you time it right, you jump hiiigh. Watch any 00-0 run to see what I mean. When you press jump, you can either press the directional key toward the wall, which will result in a 00-0 like jump, or you can press the directional key away from the wall to result in a 28-3 like jump.

3) The door lock is slightly smaller than the Exit switch, and is hidden behind the exit switch in that level, so it's possible to juuust touch the exit switch without activating the lock. It's not easy mind you, but totally possible.

Hope I helped.
