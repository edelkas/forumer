#74821 2 2005-06-14T23:51:18Z great_sea
I had the same problem before I knew how the timer worked. You actually start with 90 seconds at the beginning of each episode, and the same allotment of time carries over for all five maps in the episode. Collecting gold adds two seconds to your total time. Therefore if, for example, you didn't get much gold in levels 22-0 or 22-1, and then you took an unusually long time to beat level 22-2, your time-bank (a term I just coined) will be near depleted by 22-3. I hope this clears things up for you.
