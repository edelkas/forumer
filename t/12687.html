#231063 2 2006-09-20T05:26:59Z Atilla
Approach from the left. Stop in front of the first thwump, and wait for it to come down. Walk underneath it as it goes back up - the thwump on the floor will lift out of your way. Wait in the gap after the 2nd thwump for the 3rd thwump to fall, then walk under like before (or don't bother waiting- just run under it if you're feeling confident). Stand beneath the thwump that was on the ground when it goes up. This will lure the thwump on the roof down. Jump on top of it, then jump into the alcove with the exit switch. To get out, wait until the thwump next to you falls, then stand on it and walk underneath the thwump on the left when it rises. From there you can just repeat the process you used to get in. If you opened the doors on the right, head out them instead.
