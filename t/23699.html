#465571 10 2008-08-30T01:51:55Z Mare
We're both really sorry about this, the hand-held versions are definitely not as close to the original as the XBLA version of N+.

We diligently pointed out problems with the bounceblocks every time we sent in feedback, which was at least once per month and every week or two during the peak few months. They were actually much much worse up until the last couple months if you can believe it.

For some reason a lot of arbitrary changes were made at the start -- like floorchasers being the size of drones, or one-way platforms blocking bullets, or thwumps moving the wrong speed -- which were very hard to get undone. We have no idea why the developer wanted to change so many things, but it was kind of a nightmare. Basically, we fought as hard as we could for every change, and made sure the most important things were fixed -- what remains is a compromise made because everyone ran out of time and patience (for us, I think).

We still have a long list of things we don't like, but to most it will only make us look incredibly nit-picky, and at the end of the day, these games are so much better than how they started out, so it really makes more sense to just let it go. Hopefully you can still find a lot to like about them, but we feel and appreciate your pain, and we tried as hard as we could to minimize it.

- DDAs are almost impossible without launchpads, as things like trapdoor and bounceblock propulsion have been taken out, and thwump propulsion tends to crush the ninja outside of the editor.
