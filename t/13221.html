I found a cool free online MMORPG (massively multiplayer online role playing game) Here

Its completely free, you don't have to download or sign up to anything, just type your name and hit login. You travell round a fantasy world killing strange monsters and progressing through levels. Codes:
/help - list of codes
/Join[#] - join the chatroom (of the number you entered)
/level[#] - goes to dungeon # (you can only teleport down to the dungeon you are equal level with (if you r level 2 you can go to dungeon 2)
