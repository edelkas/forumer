Risk - The Game of Global Domination

For those of you who know the game, I welcome you. For those who do not, I advise you to stay away, unless you feel you can handle it and not be whiny. So, to start, I'd like to see who is interested in starting a good 'ole game of Risk. Now, before you attack me with a bunch of crap stuff, let me explain. First off, this is just to see who is interested for now. Next...

Why?
Well, I was thinking that it would be fun for us to get together and play some classic board games. Besides, the moderators are talking about staring with some new (ish) ideas, with some fresh spirits, and "carving your own legacy." And I say, heck, lets give it to them!

Wouldn't the game take way too long?
Yes, yes it would. But for starters it would make known the real time it takes to carry on a war, but that's a crappy reason. It would be a fun project to tackle, and it would give us something to do besides spamming up some "Count to A million!" game.

But that would be boring after a while
Yes, at some times, it would. But hey, everything takes time, right? (Yes I know that's no excuse)

So, you going to abandon this project like all your others?
I certainly hope not. That is why I want to enlist some "moderators" of the game to help keep it going. I would like an actual moderator or admin to help so they can update the stats when I am not around. Also some others to help do some other activities (will clarify later).

Wouldn't this be easier to do all at one time, on some downloadable internet version?
Unfortunately, yes. But not everyone has the time or resources to do so.

What about chances? Like dice rolls and cards?
You guys will have to trust me and the other moderators of the game to do this. You probably don't have any grounds to trust us, but you just have to. I am looking up some dice rollers on the 'net, but if that does not work I will resort to the die I have at my house (once again, trust is the issue). Same with the game cards.

So, how are you going to keep this up?
I will explain more later, but for now here is the deal: Either myself or the other mods will be rolling the dice, depending on who gets here first. Sadly, you have to roll the die according to the player, and he or she must respond to each roll before the next (i.e. whether or not they wish to retreat if they are on offense). This is where I would like to address another issue. We need players who will not abandon this game! I don't want people signing up and then not being around. This will take time, but I don't want this to die out! That also goes with rules. I don't want to take the time to explain things to you, unless it is a changed set of rules to make up for the fact we are using a forum. Look at the Wikipedia link at the top to learn the basics. If you sign up and don't know everything, it is up to you to find out. Any other questions will be asked in a discussion thread.

How many people can play?
Anywhere between 2-6 players at once.

What are the game types? (i.e. - Capitals, 100% Domination, <80% Domination, etc).
That will be decided once everything is sorted. Most likely it will be 100% (Global) Domination, because that is the most familiar.

Stats?
Once again, moderators will help keep those up in a Statistics Thread

What else?
Anything that needs to be addressed will be as we create our roles.

Oh, what board will we be using and how will it be kept?
I have spent the last while Gimp-ing (???) a board based on the computer version of the game, Risk II. There are some new territories, but through my experiences these make for better (and more fun) game play. The territories can be debated. The board can be found here. More on this later.


- - - - - - - - -

Well, I spent a bit of time thinking about this, and would love to here your opinions. Of course, I will be terribly busy, and am looking for a few trustworthy folks to devote their time and effort into making this an awesome experience. I am eagerly hoping that a moderator or administrator will be available to help out with this to make things go smoother.

So, who's in?
