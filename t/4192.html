#87312 9 2005-07-31T15:44:10Z mooseproduce
I hope none of you have a problem with Newgrounds.

Isometrix- unfinished; I lost the source and had to stop development. It's still the craziest thing I've ever done. (And the preloader is awesome.) The really cool thing about the game is the random map generator; you can travel an infinite distance in the game, but every time you play the map is exactly the same. So for instance, when you start the game walk straight forwards and three maps later you will be in "ferca forest". (There's a random name generator too, which makes for crappy names.) Anyways, I love the game and the graphics, but no one else does.

Moosteroids- It's a blatant Asteroids clone!! However, I added 10 or so different ship classes, with different health, speed, and 2 very different weapons each. Also I made bullettime, because I'm lame like that. (The bullettime effect is the neatest looking thing you will ever see)

Untitled- This was something I started but didn't get terribly far in. READ THE CONTROLS!! It wasn't based on N, it was actually based on Soldat(you can tell by the map system). Anyways, I now realize how much it looks like N... ah well. Mine has a crazy combo attack system and zombies, so there.


I have a bunch of old crap on Newgrounds... a lot is based off of Madness Interactive, which I spent over a year trying to make a sequel for. There are some pretty neat things involving physics, but they're all immature looking and from long ago. Tosho, I'd like to see your games if you have any.
