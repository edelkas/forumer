I posted this on the "N for consoles thread, but thought it was such a good idea (getting big-headed now ) that it should have its own thread.

As everyone knows, the Xbox 360 is now out in America, and coming out shortly in the UK (Yeaah!). The 360 has an online service called Xbox Live, which connects all users together, similar to NUMA. Within this service is a thing called the Xbox Live Arcade, which lets you download various things including small games.
Now seeing as N is the kind of game that is suitable to be put on a disk, it could be offered on the Arcade to download, along with Ned.
XBL would be the equivilent to NUMA, and allow players to upload and download user-made maps (and updates).
The game could use the XBL gamer points system (points are awarded for completing levels on games), giving players points for every episode they complete. There could also be scoreboards.
With XBL you can see how players unlocked certain levels and achievments, and this could be used for N, e.g. players could see how you got the "blue flavour" ninja.

The only downside I can see is criticism from graphics whores. N doesn't look exactly "next-gen", when compare to the arcade games on XBL. Perhaps something could be done about that.

Anyone agree/ disagree or have suggestions?
