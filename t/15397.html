Er... wow, no. That's a misconception.

A furry is a slang term (some might even consider it derogatory) that refers to someone who believes he has a strong connection with a particular animal. Some believe that they were that particular animal in a past life, some believe they have such an animal guardian, some believe they have a soul that is at least part that animal... the explanations for it are many, though all confoundingly arbitrary.
Furries feel most natural taking on personality quirks of that particular animal and like to think of their interactions with people in terms that such an animal, though intelligent, would think. Most furries also think of themselves as unique in this world of humans, and that the rest of humankind doesn't understand their connection with their animal.
(It's the same sort of desperation that would otherwise steer someone to religion, if you ask me.)
But furries are so well known because of the outspoken furries who write erotic fiction about animals. There are also plenty of artists who draw "furry porn," which depicts anthropomorphic animals. When referring to furries in artwork, furries are represented as these anthropomorphic animals.
Most furries are harmless and are very much content thinking to themselves that they have this special connection with an animal. But the more vocal and assertive ones are the ones who made furries infamous, what with their furry porn.

(On 4chan, posting furry porn anywhere outside /b/ is a bannable offense. Every Friday on /b/ is "Furry Friday," in which the furries like to spam the channel with furry porn. On an April Fool's Day a few years back, 4chan created the /fur/ channel, let users post what they would for that day, and on April 2nd they banned every IP that posted an image in /fur/. Furries refer to this day as "the Fursecution," and everyone else thinks it's hilarious.)

I have a natural hate for furries, which is of the same kind and intensity as the hate I naturally have for Scientologists and people who think they're vampires. But I have much less of a problem with furries in general because they're usually a more timid sort of people.
