But I signed on to Kai's account (we both know each other's password) and made the thread about my death, after logging on to IRC with the same account and trying to convince some people like Obby, Dave, and LV that, oh no, Kimmie, was dead. He PMed chad_sxtn13 and told me that my little game was immature. And, damn... I knew it.

My dad said something that made my mom burst out laughing, and it made me so happy to see that they were getting along. So I crept into the doorway, and watched as my mom leaned in and kissed my dad, right there, on the lips.

It was strange, almost. Strange, but true. They're still in love. And after I threw my brother out the 2nd story window for telling John (oundj) my password and ruining everything ('cause, you know, this place was my life), they slowly got together and steadily became friends again. Best friends. My brother's getting his cast off in a week, and we're still the best of friends.

Now, don't go telling me that you knew that fateweaver was someone else, or that he was me, because I know that some of you do. And seriously, by saying that, what are you trying to prove?

Anyway, LV caught me first. He sent me a welcome back PM. So, a little thanks to Kevin for that one.

But here's the thing:
As fateweaver, I made friends here. I kept a good status. And most of all, my grades at school were still good. I aced my sociology test![/giration] I even got a girlfriend![/giration again] And, yet, I escaped to here, and managed to prioritze: homework first, Metanet last. And I can't explain it, but something, something really cool, and somewhat [corny] special [/corny], made me keep my promise of work and then play.

And here's where you come in:
All I want is forgiveness. In fact, you don't have to forgive me... just say you do and then shun me, or whatever. But I just want to be a part of this community again, as me; as Kimmie.
