For all who don't understand game development:
  • Games need someone to oversee the whole project. In small companies like this one, that would be either Mare or Raigan.
  • Games aren't just a program with a logo. They embody what the game development team ideals - the specifications that must come from dense teamwork environments, not just a logo in the corner.
  • Tons of games isn't any better than one awesome one. They've amassed this huge collective of fans that keeps on growing with only one game so far (albeit, an updating one). Quality OVER quantity, for sure.
Thusly, M&R don't need "more and more games" to expand. One awesome one every so often does the trick even better. A pile of crappy games won't play as well as one good one.
