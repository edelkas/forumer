#277466 4 2006-12-26T21:06:40Z Bunniesandsheep
basic colors:
red is mines http://numa.notdot.net/map/73121
yellow is gold http://numa.notdot.net/map/73120
blue is door switches or zap drones

Complex colours:

you can find how to do brown here: http://numa.notdot.net/map/71336
pink is spaced out mines http://numa.notdot.net/map/73020
orange is stripes of red and yellow http://numa.notdot.net/map/71136
green is yellow and blue
purple is red and blue

you can try to get different shades of colours by doing more of one colour and less of another. there are probably plenty more but these are the ones i know

you can see lots of colours here: http://numa.notdot.net/map/73072
