well, we'd like to be making console games sometime, but.. the reason N is delicious is that we made it in our spare time, in ~6 weeks. if we were developing for a console, it would take a year or so, and it would be full time.

the problem with developing for consoles (or for mac/pc in c++) is basically that it requires 100x the effort compared to flash/actionscript... although, it DOES run 1000x faster


right now we're just going to keep trying to make good small freeware games, and not taking too long to make each one. we think that one of the best things about freeware is that, since it's made by a small number of people in a small amount of time, it's more adventurous and creative. when you've been making a game for 4 years with a huge team of people, you can't really change a lot of it just because you come up with a great idea. with N we were changing things the whole time, it wasn't until the last week or so that we knew what the final gameplay would be like.

hopefully we'll at some point be able to quit our crappy office jobs and make games all the time as a living. but we don't want to do that if it means working at a big games company, because most of those games suck, and we'd just be tiny parts of a big machine we couldn't control. we like being able to be creative and stuff... like.. maybe 500 games come out each year, but i'd say less than 10/year are truly great. that sucks.

we're still trying to think of a way of making money off of games which appeals to us. the soldat model is good: the game is delicious, but registration lets you customise things (i.e your player colors, etc.).. so, there's really no reason you HAVE to register, but you end up registering if you like it because it's cheap and you want to support the developer.
