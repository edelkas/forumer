Swingv1.0


Hey guys, I've been working on this game on and off for a while now. My brother was kind enough to make a highscore table for it, which we've just finished. And I figured I'd ask you guys to help me test it.
So, play it, test it, and tell me if you find any bugs.

Click to move the ball's pivot around. It takes some getting used to, but it helps if you try to keep the ball moving smoothly.
Hit space to pause and unpause the game.
Stars are worth 1 point, red stars are worth 10. The clocks get you 10 extra seconds.

There are various other powerups throughout the game, but I'll leave you to discover them on your own.

Good luck!

#169025 5 2006-04-27T22:12:53Z LouDog004
I can give some hints. I've played it five times, and I've gotten progressively better.

First, when you start Click way up in the left corner. When the ball get to it's left apex, click in the top right corner. This will get you velocity. Now as it comes back down from it's right apex, click on the string somewhere. The ball should start swinging around hitting stars. If you practice, you should be able to use this technique to get lots of speed, enough to make several full revolutions.

Now, imagine the string is an infinite line. =focal point, =ball, =mouse.

------- ------- When you click like this as the ball is rotating upwards it will continue around your new focal point in a CC direction. You've moved the ball left but kept the circumference of its rotation the same. You can do this to move the ball up, down, or anywhere.

If you lose speed just go back to the first step. It tall gets more and more intuitive.


EDIT: More tips. Clicking as close to the infinite string line as possible helps maintain the greatest velocity of the ball. If you click away from the string, you force the ball off of it's current velocity vector, and it will slow down. You don't want to change the speed, only the direction.
