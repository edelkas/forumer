Quote: Terminal, June 21, 2007 06:44 pm
In episode 89 i got an highscore for level 4 but not for level 3?!
Haha, that happens all the time. It means your time on that level, if you had started from 90.000, would have been lower than 00.000, and so the run would have a negative score, which is impossible. The effect is also quite common on 83-3 and 37-3.
However, it doesn't matter at all. You'll still get all the secrets, even if your score for 89-3 (or any other level) is negative. Your problem is 99; go back and beat it again.
