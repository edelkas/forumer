#117598 7 2005-11-24T13:57:50Z KinGAleX
Yep. Follow the following path in your directory.

C:\Documents and Settings\<your login here>\Application Data\Macromedia\Flash Player\#SharedObjects\<random numbers and letters>\localhost

In that folder, there should be a file called, "n_v14b_userdata". Copy that file to your memory stick, and paste it in the same place you took it from on your school computer.

#117643 11 2005-11-24T20:42:49Z iangb
It's a hidden folder.

I don't know if your school will let you see into hidden folders, but seeing as you can go into your Documents and Settings, it's worth a try...

In windows explorer/My Computer, click on tools>folder options. Go to the 'view' tab, and select the option 'show hidden files and folders'. Click Apply, and then OK, and the folder should appear in it's right place. From there, you know what to do.
