#18752 1 2008-01-07T17:32:29Z Wasted power
Okay. We've got diagonal launchpads. We've got diagonal tiles. Why can't we have diagonal one-ways and diagonal doors?

Yes, 4 new one ways, 4 new slidy doors, 4 new locked doors, and 4 new trapdoors.

Am I asking for too much here? I think I am. But if you can have diagonal launchpads, why can't you encode 16 new objects here in a day or so? I'm not an Actionscript programmer, so I don't know the technical limitations. If you know there limitations, kindly tell me.

Let's say that you want the ninja to slide on a one-way while being attacked by a chaingun and a laser from below. You have no way to do that (and I don't mean making one ways like stairs, that's so ugly).

Or what if you want the ninja to do a perpendicular jump with a rocket following from below the slope? Again, you can't do that.

I'm sure you can come up with many more ideas if only diagonal one-ways and doors can be implemented.

Now there's another nuisance in mapmaking: You can only stick a door (any kind) on the border of a square. See attatched image to see what I mean. In this image, you can see that I can put the locked door on the top or on the bottom. Why can't I stick the thing in the middle? I can do that with oneways, why not doors? If I stick it on the top, xaelar (or anybody else) will somehow manage to squeeze through at the bottom. If I put it at both sides, the thing gets glitched, and you can pass right through it. No, really!

So why not make doors z-snappable like everything else in the game?
