The Mirage Explosion: The Last Lap

It is complete. Team Mirage's first ever collaboration map-pack is finally finished. After nearly six months of continuous mapping, the end product is here. The mapping is finished. The playtesting is coming to a close. The finishing touches are being finished. The release date has been set. The only thing left is for us to wait. It's been 164 days since we first announced the beginning of this pack. 12 more days will feel like nothing.

The Final Rundown

~Three hundred and ninety four playable maps consisting of actions, puzzles, races, and anything in between.
~Six non-playable maps.
~All four-hundred maps are collaborations.

~Prizes will include dedication maps, signature tags, and a secret prize.

The Deadline
~September 11, 2007.
~It has finally been set in stone.
~Yes, nine-eleven.
~Think of the world.

What is it-
~A level pack.

Who is involved-

~Brttrx.

~June 24, 2007: Seven? Most definitely.

~July 31, 2007: It is now set in stone. The Mirage Explosion will have 400 maps. Deadline changed to late September.
