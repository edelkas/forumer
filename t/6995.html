the problem is... at the moment, the replays are done based on the fabulousness of the physics system: all that are recorded are the keypresses, and the game engine does the rest. It's determinism in action (a certain set of actions allways lead to the same result).

If you were to do a 'ghost run', then things such as the position of bounce blocks would affect the run. Plus doors, etc...

So M&R would have to find a new way of 'recording' map runs, which is a pretty hard thing to do.
