#453041 9 2008-07-15T17:36:45Z tktktk
...Man, that Nintendo conference was pretty disappointing. I guess there's always the hope that they set out all their casual stuff (e.g., Wii Sports Resort, Wii Music, Animal Crossing) for the mainstream reporters and they'll announce some more hardcore projects (e.g., a new Kid Icarus, Pikmin, or F-Zero) at some later date. But for the moment at least, pretty much the only things geared toward more serious gamers were GTA DS and the Wii microphone for voice chat.

Edit: Yeah, from what I've been reading, the more optimistic people seem to agree with me that (1) Nintendo now uses E3 as a way to get gaming news to casuals via the mainstream media and (2) Nintendo is a big enough company that it basically has to have more in the pipeline than what they've shown here. The first post of this thread shows that many of their studios either have already-announced projects that weren't shown today or have not announced projects yet.
