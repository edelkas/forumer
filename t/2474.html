#54263 7 2005-03-15T17:32:05Z Eeyore
The highest scoring demo for each level is stored in the .sol level. By backing up your .sol file and then deleting the original so that a new one is made with no demos in it, the first first demo you played gets stored. You then go into the .sol file with a hex editor and there is the demo data for the level you just beat. You then make an exact replica of the level you just made in ned (not that hard if you know how). You can then watch the level you just won or send it to a friend to show them.

Not the easiest thing in the world, but its possible if you can be bothered.
