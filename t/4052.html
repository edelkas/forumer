N-Filtration is a small series, that is supposed to be some kind of crossover between Metal Gear Solid and N, I'm not too sure how good it'll work out, but I hope you'll like it.

Part 1 - Clifftop

The shrabbing sounds of the blades bruised the night's silence. The Fort was barely visible, giving away it's position with large spotlights searching the cliffs. As they pass along the ground, you can get a short look on their defense system.

"You need to get their Warhead Dummy Data. It is of vital importance to our government. This operation is top secret. Once you're in, we cannot help you. Try to stay in contact for as long as possible. We do not have any data about this base's interior, but we have located a communication room in the big tower right after the outer walls. You should be able to find a map there. To get in, you should use the water system. You have to find a way to lower the water level first. There is an outpost inside the cliff and we expect a control room to be situated inside. Try to stay unnoticed for as long as possible. Good Luck"

The spotlight passes just mere feet in front of the helicopter. You can make out a guard in the tower on the bottom of the cliff. The helicopter quickly turns away after you jumped out. It's another one of those missions.
