Wow. What can I say? It's been a long time coming. This Volume of my episodes has been in construction for most of the year, with general periods of me not working on it. I'm only just starting to get back in the game of mapping, due to my school work getting in the way. So, I'll try to contribute to the community a bit more.
About the maps, I thought that it'd be nice to provide some description behind each one, considering that there are only 15 of them in each Volume. You may want to read them after playing each map for best effect.

15-0: Sans Pants - This is just some of my arty stuff, with the -0 theme in mind of a quick map. The title is a dedication to Martin/Molloy, which was a radio show for a period of time in the 90s.
15-1: Contact Intentions - This one was inspired by Barabajagal's work. I love his maps, and how different each one is from the next. The a.k.a is a reference to a movie.
15-2: How very Estute? The Fall Of Conventionalism - Well, I wanted to make a really unconventional map to show what's going on in Numa. Bear in mind that I made this in about January.
15-3: Funk Shadows... - Designed to be funkay.
15-4: On Fire! - Inspired by Linus/nevermore. Simple ideas applyed in certain ways can extend the length of a map in a good way. I think. Title = Lyric from Bloc Party.
16-0: Tres Eyebrow - A man's face, obviously. Very eybrow.
16-1: Get Busy In The Cold Rizzy Of Space - Cold recesses of space, of course. A space map.
16-2: Long Road To Ruin - LV gave me the inspiration for this number. Simple goals. Foo Fighters reference!
16-3: Roadhouse Blues - I was listening to The Doors when I made this one. The pillars are either chips or tapeworms.
16-4: Complex Delirium - I love Lucidium. He was around when I came into the community, and his maps were everywhere. A Dream Theater lyric title.
17-0: Pace, Or Lack Therof - My friend krusch and I were talking about sarcasm in relation to putting the phrase "or lack thereof" after something, for example "my friend's testosterone, or lack thereof." This map is very slow, and a very -0 map.
17-1: Catch 22 Diseases - Another space-map. The title was in relation to a map that I made a while back called "Catch 22."
17-2: Us And Them - A Pink Floyd fueled mine-jumper. I think that this map reflects the grooves pretty well.
17-3: Yes Please - I really enjoyed making this map. I was surprised at how well it works. The title is from a Muse song called "Yes Please."
17-4: An end To A Means - I made this map in about three minutes. An end to a means, indeed.
