I'd like to add more episodes to my N add-on
http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=19543

Currently I have ideas for 4 columns:

1. A whole column of pure N tricks. (e.g. tktktk simple challenge, St Atilla's College for Ninja: http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=9751 , or other ppl's simple challenge )

2. A whole column of pure racing map, where the ninja keep the momentum and never needs to stop/slow down (is this kind of map called racing map?)

3. A whole column of pure puzzles that allow different routes (maps like blue_tetris "time is on my side")

4. A whole column of extreme metanet levels (pick those very famous metanet levels from epi 0-epi99, and make them extremely hard or extreme weird.... e.g. add 10 laser drones in 00-0, or "pick 5 most difficult levels and remove all the golds and put them in an episode")

so the themes for these 4 columns are:

1. the N learner

2. the N racer

3. the N thinker

4. the metanet fanatics

I have these four categories in mind because I want the new episodes to satisfy different taste of highscores.

I have a lot more ideas tho, but I don't have that much time I still need to code more scripts on my highscores database, so I will need help in picking / building the levels. And I want help from serious highscorers, since if you don't highscores then you can just play the maps in numa with "userlevel mode"

I also want the columns to be made one by one carefully. So let's focus on the column of pure N tricks first.
Atilla has an awesome collection of N tricks here (~45 levels): http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=9751
And tktktk also has his simple challenges (18 levels):
http://metanet.2.forumer.com/index.php?showtopic=19330

I don't have a fast comp (nor time) to test all of these levels yet. It will be great if someone can arrange them into 10 episodes.

Since this column is for learning N tricks, so I think it is okay to keep the level be short and simple.
Also I'd like to know if the Atilla maps have covered all of tktktk simple challenge tricks.

If all the maps of Atilla & tktktk has their own uniqueness, then maybe it will be good to combine 2 levels into 1 level, like the left hand side of the room is for trick 1 and then right hand side of the room is for trick 2.

The idea is to pack ALL tricks into the column, so maybe we should keep a blank episode for tricks that found in the future, and keep the right to edit and change the levels.
i.e. All levels in this column are subjected to change! Like the textbooks in school are updated to catch up the new theories.

If you are interested, please kindly make a reply and tell us your thought, thanks.
