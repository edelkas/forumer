#395189 3 2008-01-01T01:00:52Z George
Hello there, tigerformula. Trekking back to the exit is probably the hardest part of the level. Try following the route in my demo to complete the level. After you trigger the exit switch, jump to the tallest column. Try land on the edge and keep your balance, then make the final jump to the exit. If you can't seem to get enough height to get to the exit in one go, you can always wall climb the leftmost section and reach the exit that way. To view my demo, paste the data into the lower left text box in the userlevels menu. Good luck!
