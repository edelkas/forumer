Quote: TornadoTK
How do you guys get into doing this?
A: Simple. You just stick with an idea, learn what you need to learn, and adapt from there. It'll take some time, though, so be patient.

At what age?
A: Any age can really start making a game. It might take age to comprehend the concepts of making one, but you can start... whenever you feel like it.

Does it cost?
A: It depends on what you use to create the game, because the foundation is everything. As said before, Flash is expensive, but effective. There's always C++, or for starters, Game Maker. You can do it without any cost, but then again, you'll have to sacrifice more time or more quality.

Is it really, really worth it?
A: Freeware games (assuming you're making freeware) are never worth it if you're doing it for the money. If it's for the fans, you will get that feeling of pride when you release a game that people really dig.
