#198984 2 2006-07-05T00:29:40Z SlicesOfBread
Hint: Try to lure the rocket into the big pit with mines on the top, then go in the lil hole with the exit switch. When the rocket goes at you, jump, and the rocket will explode onto the wall.

If your still stuck on the starting point, lure the rocket into that little place, and bring the rocket to a corner. Jump (dont let the rocket explode), and go on the walls, so you have a little headstart against the rocket to climb up the wall.

Hope that helps!
