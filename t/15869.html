#297654 14 2007-02-15T18:41:14Z Topolord
It's simple really.

For beginner dragoneers, I would suggest you find a picture of a dragon to start. Then open up ned and take whatever dragon parts from the picture you like. If you are looking for inspiration, just search 'dragon' on deviant art. they have a massive collection of dragons. If you feel more comfortable, I would suggest you sculpt your dragon without the help of pictures; they're helpful, but they limit you much more. Then add in your objects.

note: this method makes great looking dragons, but the levels are often not as fun. That's probably the best reason to imagine a dragon.

Be original, be creative, and have fun!
