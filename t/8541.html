I dont know if its been discovered yet.. but I found something out in Debug Mode which could be a couple hours of fun if you're a sucker for Ragdoll Physics.
In Debug mode, with Caps Lock on, you can press the Spacebar to make N's body act like it was just killed : Yielding different death animations.
Also - if you hold down CTRL while in debug mode (with N dead), he will slingshot to wherever your mouse cursor is. You can slam him into walls, onto mines, and live-out those great death-falls you've always wanted to see. No matter where N is, he'll return to you, going through walls (and even through the Level Boundary, if he got thrown out there). When he combusts/explodes, however, you'll only get one of the body parts (I'm guessing his head).
Does this work for anyone else? I hope so.
