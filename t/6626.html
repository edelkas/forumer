Okay, this is sort of long and semi complex but I'll try my best (you all know I suck at explaining things).

Here we go,

Okay, so a map has 5 votes: 3.5, 4, 4, 3.5, 5 (avg score 4)

That's the normal system, here is my further more compelx systems:

The voters avg score (respectfully): 3.5, 4, 2.5, 2.5, 5 (total: 49)

and I know that no one has an avg score of 5, but it's an example...

okay, so now you take the average score of the voter and divide by the total of average scores (finding their percentage of influence on the vote)

this comes out to: .2, .23, .14, .14, .29 (again, respectfully)

Now multiply their vote by their percentage of influence: .7, .92, .56, .49, 1.45

And add that together: 4.15 (that is the new map score).

---

Under that system, people with higher avg score (obviously people better able to discern a good map, because they make them) have more weight in the vote. so when someone with a 1.5 avg rating comes and votes a map a .5 (this system would have to get rid of 0 as a possible score, really the only downside I see so far, apart from the confussion) they have much less influence than the other members. Also, people with no average rating could just be considered 1 or 2.5 or something like that so they still have a say, just not as much as say a baraba or nevermore. Also, this system doesn't change scores dramatically because it only shifted this .15 (not much). Only thing is I don't think arachnid tracks who voted what... that might be a problem.

Again, I know this is semi confusing, but I think it's a good solution to snipping because I hate seeing repost after repost, it gets annoying and I hate hearing complaints and stuff. Oppinions?
