#142827 11 2006-02-13T00:13:07Z Atilla
It's been suggested before and shot down. I dislike it. For a start, it would be extremely difficult to make a level which caters for all, or even a substantial portion, of the abilities. You would then end up with levels designed to played with one specific ability, in which case you may as well just do away with the abilities. Most existing levels would also work extremely poorly with the abilities. Secondly, it would prevent people choosing their favourite colour, because that would alter the mechanics - not to mention that some people suffer eye strain with some colours. Third, you'd need to alter replay data to include what flavour was used. Fourth, it requires a fair bit of programming, which might be better spent on other things. It might be acceptable as a mod, but I wouldn't want to see M&R spend their time on it when they could be doing other, better stuff.
