I was planning on leaving this until a few more competitions had died off, but as I was browsing yesterday, I noticed there were no maps that I was supposed to be highscoring! Obviously, that was the cue for me to post this. So whether you're a casual player or a over-competitive-stop-at-nothing-to-win highscorer, a complete novice or a hardened veteran or simply looking for some fun, take a glance at the layout below!

Hey guys, thanks for reading this! Before I go any further, I'd like to point out that this is going to be significantly different from the original 'Weekly Gangwar', in more ways than one. There's still going to be the three old gangs, Cult, Faction and Sect, but I'm also going to introduce The Mob, who are not competing, which I'll explain further on. You can't join up any of the three main gangs without showing your commitment, you have to pass an initiation test, as set by the Boss of that Gang.

Then we'll allow another week for the induction of a few member to each gang and the proceding weeks will be team highscoring, as in the original. However, there is also going to be an elimination aspect, allowing the contest to climax rather than fade away as it did the first time.

I will also start a narrative sometime in the second week.

Read on for more details...

Ground Rules
- Runs must be submitted before the deadline (midnight Saturday 10th/Sunday 11th GMT ). Failure to do so will result in your expulsion.
- All runs are to be submitted by Nreality
- You must develop an obvious persona (e.g. backstabber) throughout the Gangwar. If I deem you boring, you will be first warned, then ejected. You may evade elimination by being too interesting to kill off. This is at my discretion, of course.
Spoiler: Further InformationEvery week will end, and the next start, at midnight between Saturday and Sunday. I have a life, so this time won't always be available for me and then you'll get slightly less than a week to highscore. As we'll be using Nreality, your runs for the week before will be timestamped and if you overstep the deadline, you will be penalised.

The Second Week:
This week will be an opportunity for the bosses to attract members to their Gangs before the real competition starts. However, you may collect Grunts as the competition procedes. The last day will also be available for highscoring by the Gang whose Boss won them this bonus.
The Bosses may set any criteria for joining their gang, as long as it's relevant to N playing ability. If a member passes your test, you must accept him, and if he fails you can't. Bosses won't be allowed to change their criteria for joining unless I see it fit (for example, if no-one can pass at all).

The Mob:
Anyone may join The Mob at any time without having to pass any tests. You may also join if you have been dropped from one of the three main gangs. I will preside over this Gang. The pupose of The Mob is to allow members who lack the skill to join any gang, or have been kicked out, to participate. If you Have the highest score in The Mob for tweo consecuitive weeks, you may rejoin your Gang.

The Gangwar:
The average score for every Gang will be found, and the slowest Gang (not including The Mob) will be forced to drop their slowest Grunt. All Grunts failing to submit a run without a reasonable excuse will also be dropped. At the start of any week, players may attempt to usurp their Boss. The boss will then choose a map and any challenger that gets the fastest run that week will become Boss. There is no penalty to any players for losing this, except less time to highscore the main map.
Gangs are disbanded when they drop to two members (including the Boss). When only one Gang reamains, the war is over.

I retain the right to alter the contest at any time and may add narrative twists as I feel fit.
