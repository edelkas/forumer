Quote: zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz, Dec 17 2005, 08:00 PM
OK ILL START AGAIN
How can I make a map?
Open up N.

See the button that has a "~" on it? Press it to get to debug mode.

Now, press "J" to clear the screen, and press "E" to go into edit mode.

Now, press the "Insert" button to place objects OR the "Delete" button to place tiles.

Now, press the number or letter that corresponds to what you want to place.

Got it?
