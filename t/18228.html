In other words, Tsukatu's Wager:

-If existing is more enjoyable and I exist for as long as I can, afterward failing to exist for the rest of time: enjoyment is maximized
-If existing isn't more enjoyable and I exist for as long as I can, afterward succeeding to not exist for the rest of time: enjoyment is maximized (infinite enjoyment outweighs finite, lesser enjoyment)
-If existing is more enjoyable and I choose to stop making myself exist as fast as possible: enjoyment is minimized
-If existing isn't more enjoyable and I choose to stop making myself exist as fast as possibe: enjoyment is maximized
