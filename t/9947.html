5 tokens / level

50 / 105% = slight jump height increase (enables you to jump, vertically, up to 105% higher)

50 / 110% = wall jump distance increase (enables you to jump off of a wall 110% further)
50 / 150% = metabolism upgrade (each gold piece gives 3 seconds of time (was 2 seconds))

level 2 upgrades
125 / 110% = moderate jump increase (enables you to jump, vertically, up to 110% higher)

125 / 120% = wall jump distance increase (enables you to jump off of a wall 120% further)
