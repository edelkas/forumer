#182115 6 2006-05-30T04:41:16Z Clovic
Okay...First off...

The music thing is a part of it, the term probably wouldn't be nearly as applicable if the music hadn't become as popular. That is not to say that that is the determining factor, or even that there /is/ a determining factor.

Some people cut themselves, but that's not really emo, that could be a number of things. You can listen to things like Dashboard, Death Cab, or Postal Service without actually being emo. You may like different types of clothes, so you wear them. All these things and more doesn't make someone emo, nor does the absence of these things make someone "normal".

Also, I really don't think there is a sub-culture, that's just shit that people go to when seeking attention or affection.

Here's the thing, it really is just an outlook on life. It's not a personality, it's not what you wear, it's not what entertains you, it's not who you associate with, it's not how depressed you are, it's not anything except your outlook on life. Anything can be involved in that, but that's really all it comes down to.
