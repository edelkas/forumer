Typeracer

It's a MOTR! You click Join a Race and within seconds you're in a race with a minimum of one other person (most I've seen is 7.) You each control a little car, which moves across the screen according to how far along you are in typing a paragraph of text. The person who types it all first wins the race. The site keeps track of your average WPM and your record and all that stuff. There's also an option to play a friend and I want to try it.

To play against me, visit http://play.typeracer.com/index?room=dHI6aGVuZG9y at the same time as I'm there to enter a little race/chat room. Get your own room from the main menu. Go to IRC to plan the game
