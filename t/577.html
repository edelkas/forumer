hey,
well, the physics has a much different feel than in N (you dont' move as fast or as fluidly, it's more "human-being" and less fantastic) but inertia/momentum definitely matter.

h -- one thing is to go to the options in-game and turn on the "sniper line" option (i forget what it's called, it's in the center-bottom section of the in-game options), this might help you aim a bit better, it draws a line from your player to the mouse.

in terms of guns, they're all really well balanced so i'd say it's pretty much whatever you prefer.. i'd recommend NOT using the sniper or m79 (grenade launcher) just because they're both "weird" weapons that are hard to get used to.

the automatics are probably good to start with (numbers 2,3,4,9 i think), they're all sort of the same, with different tradeoffs between fire-rate, power, and reload time. for instance the mk5 (#2) shoots 30 bullets VERY fast, but they curve a lot so it's only good at med/close range (or else it's too ahrd to aim well), and it reloads in short time, whereas the minimi (#9) shoots 50 bullets very straight, a bit slower, but takes FOREVER to reload. #3 and #4 are sort of inbetween (i.e more accurate than #2, but reload slower)..

the desert eagles, spas, and ruger (#1, 5 and 6 i think) are "powerful" weapons, but harder to hit with. the first two have a lot of curve/arc which makes them hard to use at a distance; the ruger shoots _very_ straight, but it's very sensative to aiming.. this is hard to explain. basically, if you want to be accurate you ned to keep your cursor steady and not wiggle it around, or the ruger's aim will get very random. plus it only has 4 shots.

i'm really not sure that there's a best weapon; lots of people like the sniper (#8) or m79 (#7) because they're one-hit kills, but they both reload VERY slowly. sadly, some servers are filled with nothing but people running around using the sniper rifle as a normal gun (i.e shooting in midair, etc.).. when you do this it's aim is quite random, however it hits enough for it to be annoying.

um.. well, i don't know. it definitely takes a LONG time to get good at it. try to remember that headshots do more damage, so being above someone and shooting down on them really helps. also, if you're trying to fly high, do a 'high jump" (press up without pressing left/right; insetad of jumping 'forward" you'll jump straight up) and then start jetpacking; the jump's momentum will help you get quite high.

aaand... be sure to check out cause there are LOTS of threads on various thigns you might want to know.

raigan
p.s - if you're playing someone and they keep dissapearing, don't accuse them of cheating -- it just means the server has 'realistic mode' activated (see the manual for details)
p.p.s - if i had to recommed a gun, i'd say #1/deagles (it's one of the hardest to aim but once you get usedto it the other weaps will be easy), #3/ak (it's a medium/all-around type of gun), or #4/aug (basically the same as #3, but shoots straighter and faster and is a bit less powerful).
