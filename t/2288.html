Do you know what the clipboard is? It's part of any Windows OS. It's what you copy stuff to. When you select text or images or something and you "copy" or "cut" it, that text/image is stored on the clipboard. Pasting takes the data from the clipboard and puts it wherever you're pasting it.
So copying a level to the clipboard means that you've effectively selected the level data and copied it. All you need to do is open some text editor (preferably notepad) and paste. Yay! Then you save the file.
