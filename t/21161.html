#402112 6 2008-01-19T21:22:25Z PsychoSnail
These are the top-rated races on NUMA.
This is the N wiki's definition of a race.

Quote: N at Wikia
Race maps are designed so that the various aspects of the map keep the ninja moving quickly. They will often have areas where the ninja can run or jump for an extended period of time to build up speed and while rarely require sharp turns or altogether stopping. For example, there may be enemies to keep a player on his/her toes while moving. Or, there may be a thrilling course in which a player aims for the fastest time.
