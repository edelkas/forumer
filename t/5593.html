#113912 17 2005-11-11T20:13:28Z sweep
To respond to why I don't think the non-action categories can compete:
Puzzle - It's just... well, N isn't built for puzzles. Every now and again someone makes a brilliant puzzle, but the fact is, most puzzles aren't really puzzles. Even good puzzles are quite easily monotonous an uninspiring though - that said, there are a few that I love.
Race - too linear.
Survival - just not as satisfying. For it to be fun, you really have to be competing with someone else. In most maps, the real satisfaction is getting to the exit, but there's always the competing with others side. There's only one survival map I've ever loved, and that's Evil Bob's Fear of Crowds one... enoclophobia or something...
DDA - I'm sorry, but the novelty has worn out for me. You watch it once, you go "wow", then that's it. They've now all converged into one huge mess of the same style DDAs anyway, and I've come to realise that making a really good DDA takes less skill and more dedication. With an action map, it takes a real touch, a real eye for things to make the map great. But with a DDA, as long as you know the basic rules, you can make a great one by just spending ages on it. Don't get me wrong, it's impressive, but... meh. I just don't enjoy watching them anymore. It's oooollld news.
Test Level - Well, I don't think I need to explain why this category pales in comparison to action.


One of the other great things about action is the scope it leaves you with. There are probably so many great potential ideas for action maps that haven't been explored yet... action is king, and always will be.

Amen.
