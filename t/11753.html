#213540 12 2006-08-09T12:05:55Z Teedle
Hey, thanks!

A bit of history- the winner of a random art competition in my school was a really bad painting with angsty writing over the top of it, subtly going on about how nobody understands the artist.

There was a very, very similar painting winning another competition in my girlfriend's school.

This was meant to be a statement about the whole grunge- word- teen depression genre of painting and computer work, which everybody loves but I find tends to be tacky, cliche'd, and entirely without depth. (You know- in the same way I find, say, Simple Plan lyrics without depth.)

Hence, the "It's easier just to pretend we were profound" on the bottom right, which was the original point of the project.

Yeah- then there were a ton of photos I'd wanted to use from a while ago when a friend and I joined in a protest march more- or- less by accident that I'd wanted to use for a while. That was a very cool day.

I ended up just liking this thing too much, though, and it ended up mimicing exactly what I was (artistcally) whinging about. But it's kinda neat anyway.
