Also, as Maestro said, I fairly sure it's impossible for N to wipe your backup. However, many drive-cleanup programs and the like delete .sols, so it's possible your backup was deleted at an earlier date if anyone's been running stuff like that on your computer.

#467393 11 2008-09-05T21:03:54Z the_devil_dodger
Heads up people! N doesn't "randomly" delete your SOL. You cause it, however unwittingly.


Possible ways to loose your .sol:

-Multiple N windows open (I lost my data once that way, but it was only column 2 I lost)

-Editing the file

-Watching Jay Leno

-The NReality mod messed up my data once after running it several times.

-Corrupt N file

-Your backup file was copied with notepad (renders backup useless)

-Data corruption or electromagnetic interference of any form can mess up more than just the .sol or the flash player. Keep magnets and cell phones away from your computers!

-Fragmented data is more unstable than normal, and is more prone to corruption. It is much easier for a small glitch to:
Expand
Cause other glitches
with fragmented data than with contiguous data.


Once you've covered those, you should be fine.
