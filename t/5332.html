Here's another poem I just wrote. It contains some deep themes and messages. Again, no stealing it.

THE ESSENCE OF BEING - by Mark

I think, therefore I am.

I am never bothered to get out of bed,
I am drunk and I smoke and it goes to my head
That the world is my oyster, I'll do what I may,
I am better than you, so you work while I play.
Who cares about others, afraid and alone,
With nothing to eat and no place to call home?
I need my money, for wine and champagne,
For short weekend trips to France or to Spain.
But I do my bit for society,
I work 9 'til 5 and I pay my fees,
I don't need to be told about 'loving' and 'giving',
I have a life, and my life I am living.

... I am.
