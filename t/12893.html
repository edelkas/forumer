#235634 2 2006-09-30T19:53:06Z Twistkill
This is explained in the manuals/readme that came with N...

You must have caps lock on to play normally. When it's not, you are in fbf (Frame-by-frame) mode, which is the situation you just explained. So, turning on caps lock is your solution.
