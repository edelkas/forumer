I've stumbled upon a technique I am currently calling 'strip patterns'. Basically, I take an image (like a photograph or a piece of artwork), isolate a strip that is 1px either high or wide and the full size in the other dimension. Then I just expand that out back to the full dimensions, and the result is a striped pattern that generally looks quite cool. They make great wallpapers, and as a bonus they have very small file sizes even at large dimensions.

Audience participation time: give it a shot and comment on the results--or, comment on the fact that you've already done this sort of thing before me.

Well, I did it in Gimp...

1. Create a new layer and copy your vertical pattern onto it.
2. Create another new layer and copy your horizontal pattern onto it.
3. Set the layer mode for the layer on top to "Multiply."
4. Delete the original image layer and merge the two other layers.
5. Yay.

Note: You may have to experiment with the layer modes, Multiply worked for me because the background was white.
