This is a thread similar to the map rating thread.
However, in this thread, you are allowed to submit a map that is already rated.

In this thread, you post your best map(s) that you want to be looked at. Rated or not. The maximum is three. You may post them over a period of time, or all at once, but only three can ever be posted. The map must exist on numa.
Of course, there's no point in posting a map that has a lot of recognition. That's just wasting a map.
You can't post the map here unless it is off of the front page on numa.

Everybody - please rate and comment the maps you see here. It'll probably be worth it, considering these are *best* maps.

This was a very hard map for me to make because there are so many ways to complete it. Most of my maps have one clear route. It was really a stretch for me, and I think it turned out great.
