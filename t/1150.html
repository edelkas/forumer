#30570 8 2004-10-05T04:19:49Z Tsukatu
Go into N. Press the tilde (~) key. Click in the top left text box. Highlight all of the text there and press delete. Put your cat on the keyboard and let it walk around for a few seconds. Put your keyboard on the ground and jump on it to simulate the ways in which you want N to jump, making sure that before you do so (but after the cat thing, very important) you type in on the next line "//shoe.my_size = " and then your shoe size.
You see, N was made so well that it can interpret your shoe hammerings into level code. When you're done, make sure to press the Caps-Lock button as hard as you can so that it sticks in the On position. Then copy your level data, put it into notepad, save it as a .txt, and post it here as an attachment.

And that's it!
