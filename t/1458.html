Just to let you know, this is alot more welcome in the Off-Topic section of the board.

Parkour, known to America as Freerunning, is basically just improv freestyle of climbing, jumping, and pulling off feats of agility on just about anything. It could be from precision jumping from jumping from ledges, precision jumping, long running jumps, or while doing all of this, maintaining constant flow.

It's really impressive, actually. I've seen videos with people precision jumping (that's with both feet together the whole way, btw) an 8ft rail to rail gap, and pulling a frontflip off of a slightly slanted wall, over a car. I've tried freerunning, and it's fun, yet I can't do anything special.

No 8 foot precision hops for me, please. I'll stick to hopping benches and short rail jumps anyday.
