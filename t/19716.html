#371203 5 2007-10-11T04:40:25Z Mosh
He meant to attach a nameoffile.txt file containing the map data, but that works too.

Solution: If you're using "Left, Right, Left, Right" type of pathing for the drones, don't make it so they can travel in a square. Use your mouse cursor to trace their paths while in Ned. It isn't the tiles, it's the method the Zap Drones use for movement. Try holding different placement-keys while placing the Drone. (W is left,right,left,right, other paths with other keys would be Q,E,A,S, and D; and they are all explained in Ned).

I hope that helped,
