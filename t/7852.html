#148561 2 2006-02-25T20:26:09Z whaleshow527
This level really isn't hard at all. People have trouble with it because they go for all the switches. The level is actually possible by obtaining only one switch.

Here's how to do it: Just go through the top section without getting any of the switches. I recommend doing this part quickly so you can trap both zap drones in there. From here just go and get the switch by the second laser drone, which is the easiest, and just finish the level. This way you won't need to get the switch by the floorguard, which you're having trouble with. To get to the exit, just jump from the left wall to the opposite one and then out through the open door you unlocked and hop safely into the exit.

hope this helps..
